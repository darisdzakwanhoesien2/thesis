## Inovasi dan Pengembangan Produk
Product Innovation and Development
[OJK F.26]

Industri media dan hiburan mengalami perkembangan yang sangat dinamis dalam beberapa tahun terakhir seiring dengan kemajuan teknologi dan perubahan preferensi konsumen untuk mengonsumsi informasi dan konten dari platform OTT dan media digital lainnya. Dalam rangka mengikuti perkembangan tren tersebut, SCM telah melakukan investasi yang memadai pada infrastruktur digital yang akan memfasilitasi berbagai inovasi produk dan layanan kepada para pemirsa. Dukungan teknologi terkini yang diadopsi dalam proses produksi program maupun konten juga memampukan SCM untuk terus berkarya dan menghasilkan program dan konten yang berkualitas sehingga SCM senantiasa dapat memenuhi selera dan kebutuhan pemirsa secara berkelanjutan pada semua platform media yang dimilikinya.

also increased its investment in information security systems as part of IT governance to maintain the confidentiality of all customer data contained in the Company's data centers. The Company also implemented a number of strategies, including:

1. Providing access to customers to update personal data to minimize opportunities for misuse of data by irresponsible parties.
2. Strengthening the data security system in order to conduct a quick and responsive follow-up in the event of data misuse. The Company involves an independent party bound by a data confidentiality agreement to conduct an inspection of the existing IT security system.

As of the end of the reporting period, SCM did not receive any complaints regarding violations of customer privacy in the form of reports of data leakage, theft and loss.

The media and entertainment industry has experienced a very dynamic development in recent years along with technological advancements and changes in consumer preferences towards information and content from OTT platforms and other digital media. In order to keep up with the trend, SCM has made adequate investments in digital infrastructure that will facilitate various product and service innovations to the viewers. The support of the latest technology adopted in the production process of programs and content also enables SCM to continue working and producing quality programs and content so that SCM can always satisfy the tastes and needs of viewers in a sustainable manner on all its media platforms.
