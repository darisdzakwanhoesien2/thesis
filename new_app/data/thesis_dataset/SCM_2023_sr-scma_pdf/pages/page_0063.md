# Ketenagakerjaan

## Employment

[GRI 404-3]

Perseroan menunjuk Divisi Human Resources untuk menjalankan tanggung jawab pengelolaan dan pengembangan kompetensi SDM. Divisi ini menyampaikan laporan pengelolaan SDM kepada Direksi untuk kemudian menjadi bahan pertimbangan dalam menetapkan kebijakan pengelolaan SDM. Kinerja SDM Perseroan dievaluasi oleh Direksi dengan menggunakan kriteria yang dimasukkan ke dalam key performance indicators (KPI). Pada tahun 2023, Perseroan memastikan seluruh karyawan telah memperoleh tinjauan kinerja dan mengikuti seluruh kegiatan pengembangan karier yang direncanakan oleh Perseroan.

In addition, a non-discriminatory policy is also adopted in managing relations with the community, especially during the recruitment of new employees. The Company applies the principles of equality, diversity, and compliance with various applicable regulations in the labor recruitment process.

The Company appoints the Human Resources Division to be responsible for the management and development of HR competencies. The Company's HR performance is evaluated by the Board of Directors using criteria that are incorporated into key performance indicators (KPIs). In 2023, the Company ensures that all employees have received performance reviews and participated in all career development programs planned by the Company.

# Kesetaraan dalam Bekerja

## Equality at Work

[GRI 401-1] [OJK F.18] [SASB] SV-ME-260A.1 [GRI 413-1] [GRI 405-1]

Dalam rangka memenuhi kebutuhan tenaga kerja yang kompeten, Perseroan melakukan proses rekrutmen secara adil, transparan dan tanpa membedakan ras, suku, agama, golongan, gender, warna kulit, afiliasi politik maupun kondisi fisik. Perseroan bahkan berkomitmen untuk menjunjung tinggi keberagaman, termasuk dengan memberikan kesempatan yang setara kepada karyawan laki-laki dan perempuan untuk mengembangkan kariernya, termasuk dengan menduduki jabatan struktural di organisasi Perseroan. Hingga per tanggal 31 Desember 2023, terdapat 4 (empat) orang perempuan yang menduduki jabatan anggota dewan Perseroan, di mana komposisi Direktur laki-laki dan perempuan pada jajaran manajemen adalah sama, yaitu masing-masing tiga orang (50%).

In order to fulfill the needs of a competent workforce, the Company conducts a recruitment process fairly, transparently and without distinguishing race, ethnicity, religion, class, gender, skin color, political affiliation or physical condition. The Company is also committed to uphold diversity, including by providing equal opportunities for male and female employees to develop their careers, as well as to serve in structural positions in the Company's organization. As of December 31, 2023, there are 4 (four) women who occupy the position of board members of the Company, in which the composition of male and female Directors in the management ranks is equal at three people each (50%).
