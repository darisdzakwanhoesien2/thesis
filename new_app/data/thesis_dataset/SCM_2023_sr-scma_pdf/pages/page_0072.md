# Pemberian Penilaian Kinerja
Performance Assessment
[GRI 404-3]

Pelaksanaan penilaian kinerja karyawan dilakukan secara berkala dengan tujuan untuk mengukur ketercapaian hasil kerja dan indikator yang ditetapkan. Penilaian kinerja dilakukan secara transparan tanpa membedakan suku, agama, ras, golongan, status sosial, gender, ataupun hal-hal lain yang dapat memengaruhi penilaian secara objektif. Setiap pekerja akan memperoleh tinjauan atas kinerjanya sesuai Peraturan Perusahaan dan hasilnya akan menjadi dasar penentuan bagi manajemen untuk menentukan kesempatan kenaikan jenjang karier atau mengukur minat dan kebutuhan pelatihan yang sesuai untuk menunjang pekerjaan karyawan yang bersangkutan.

Pada tahun 2023, Perseroan telah melakukan penilaian kinerja seluruh karyawan (100%). Hasil penilaian tersebut digunakan untuk memutuskan promosi, mutasi ataupun rotasi bagi pengembangan karier karyawan serta dalam memberikan penghargaan sesuai dengan penilaian atas kinerja masing-masing.

Employee performance assessment is conducted periodically with the aim of measuring the achievement of performance and indicators that have been set. The assessment is conducted transparently without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, class, social status, gender, or other matters that may affect the objective assessment. The performance of each employee will be reviewed in accordance with the Company's Regulations and the results will serve as a basis for the management to determine career advancement opportunities or measure the appropriate interest and training needs to support the work of the employee concerned.

In 2023, the Company has conducted performance assessment to all employees (100%). The assessment results are used to decide on promotions, transfers or rotations for employee career development and to provide rewards in accordance with the valuation of their respective performance.
