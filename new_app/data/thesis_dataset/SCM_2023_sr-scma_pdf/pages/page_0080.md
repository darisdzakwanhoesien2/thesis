## Panduan Informasi atas Produk dan Layanan
## Product and Service Information Guidelines

Sebagai kelanjutan dari perlindungan hak-hak pemirsa, terutama melindungi anak-anak dari dampak negatif yang mungkin muncul dari pendekatan yang kami ambil dalam penyajian suatu konten atau program, maka kami memberikan panduan informasi dari suatu konten atau program yang ditayangkan kepada pemirsa, di antaranya dalam bentuk:

- Mengirimkan semua film dan serial kami ke Lembaga Sensor Film Indonesia (LSF) untuk kemudian dievaluasi sebelum ditayangkan. Hal ini untuk mengurangi risiko khususnya penonton di bawah umur.
- Menempatkan kategori tayangan dewasa di siaran televisi pada saat anak memasuki jam istirahat atau tidur mereka.
- Mendorong dan mengingatkan orang tua untuk memandu anak-anak mereka dalam memilih konten yang sesuai dengan usia. Kami menyediakan berbagai fitur kontrol pada aplikasi Vidio.com. Fitur tersebut mencakup kontrol orang tua dan pembatasan konten, termasuk penandaan khusus untuk tayangan film dewasa dengan kategori 21+. Lebih lanjut, kami menyediakan fitur "Mode Anak" yang menyaring konten agar aman dan edukatif bagi anak-anak. Dengan adanya fitur ini, para orang tua dapat merasa lebih nyaman saat anak-anak menjelajahi platform Vidio.com.

As a continuance of protecting the rights of viewers, especially protecting children from negative impacts that may arise from the approach we take in presenting a content or program, we provide information guidance of a content or program aired to viewers, including in the form of:

- Submitting all our movies and series to the Indonesian Film Censorship Board (LSF) to be evaluated before airing. This is to reduce the risk especially to underage viewers.
- Placing adult categories on television broadcasts at times when children are entering their rest or sleeping hours.
- Encouraging and reminding parents to guide their children in selecting age-appropriate content. We provide various control features on the Vidio.com application. These features include parental controls and content restrictions, including special tagging for 21+ adult movies. Furthermore, we provide a "Kids Mode" feature that filters content to make it safe and educational for children. With this feature, parents can feel more comfortable when their children are browsing the Vidio.com platform.

Until the end of the reporting period, SCM did not receive any complaints regarding customer privacy infringement which include data leakage, theft, and loss.

Protecting the privacy of viewers', OTT media subscribers' and advertisers' data is crucial. Leakage of this privacy data may constitute a violation of business ethics and integrity, as well as a violation of property rights.

In order to ensure the protection of data privacy of viewers, OTT media subscribers and advertisers, the Board of Directors under the supervision of the Board of Commissioners ensures that the Company has implemented ethical customer data protection in order to build SCM's integrity as an accountable company in the eyes of the wider public.
