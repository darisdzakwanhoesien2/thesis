PT INTRACO PENTA TBK 2023 Integrated Annual Report

# PENGAWASAN DAN PEMBERIAN SARAN/NASIHAT DI BIDANG NOMINASI

Pengawasan dan pemberian saran/nasihat di bidang nominasi diantaranya dilakukan dengan cara melakukan kajian untuk merumuskan saran dan masukan kepada Dewan Komisaris terkait usulan nominasi calon Direksi dan direksi anak perusahaan. Saran dan masukan tersebut menjadi bahan masukan Dewan Komisaris dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasan dan pemberian nasihat di bidang nominasi.

# PENGAWASAN DAN PEMBERIAN SARAN/NASIHAT DI BIDANG REMUNERASI

Sepanjang 2023, Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi melakukan review atas kebijakan remunerasi bagi pengurus Perseroan melalui benchmarking dan berdasarkan kinerja yang dicapai. Selanjutnya merumuskan usulan penyesuaian remunerasi pengurus Perseroan kepada Dewan Komisaris.

# SUPERVISION AND PROVISION OF ADVICE IN THE NOMINATION FIELD

Supervision and Provision of Advice in the nomination field is carried out by conducting studies to formulate suggestions and inputs to the Board of Commissioners regarding the proposal for nomination of candidates for the Board of Directors and directors of subsidiaries. These suggestions and inputs become input for the Board of Commissioners in carrying out supervisory and advisory functions in the nomination field.

# SUPERVISION AND PROVISION OF ADVICE IN THE REMUNERATION FIELD

Throughout 2023, the Remuneration and Nomination Committee reviewstheremuneration policy for the Company's management through benchmarking and based on the performance achieved. Furthermore, formulate a proposal for adjusting the remuneration of the Company's management to the Board of Commissioners.
