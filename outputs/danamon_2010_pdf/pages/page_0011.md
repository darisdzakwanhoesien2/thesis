Laporan Direksi
Report from the Board of Directors

Kami percaya, bahwa sebagai bagian dari masyarakat, Danaman wajib berusaha meraih kinerja finansial tanpa mengabaikan tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungannya.

We believe that as a good corporate citizen, we must seek to deliver financial performance in a manner which is both socially and environmentally responsible.

Laporan Keberlanjutan • 2010 • Sustainability Report • PT Bank Danaman Indonesia Tbk
