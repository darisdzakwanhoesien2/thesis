# Independence Statement

All Independent Commissioners of Allo Bank have prepared a Statement of Independence and have submitted it to the OJK. In addition, members of the Board of Commissioners are also obliged to report if there is a change in status that affects their independence and ensure that this practice has been implemented in accordance with GCG principles.
