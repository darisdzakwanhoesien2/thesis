## Penilaian Kinerja Komite di Bawah Dewan Komisaris

Penilaian kinerja organ dan komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris dilakukan baik secara kolegial maupun individual. Penilaian dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris dengan berdasarkan parameter kriteria kelengkapan dan pelaksanaan tugas komite dan organ pendukung. Sepanjang tahun 2024, organ dan komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris yang terdiri dari Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, serta Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi telah melakukan tugas dan fungsinya dengan baik dalam membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam melakukan pengawasan terhadap pengelolaan Bank.

Penilaian terhadap kinerja ketiga komite pendukung dilakukan secara kualitatif, meliputi keaktifan komite dalam menjalankan tugasnya, proses dokumentasi, dan rekomendasi yang diberikan. Di sepanjang tahun 2024, Komite Audit melakukan 15 (lima belas) kali rapat, Komite Pemantau Risiko melakukan 12 (dua belas) kali rapat, serta Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi melakukan 7 (tujuh) kali rapat. Dengan seluruh pelaksanaan tugas yang telah dilakukan oleh seluruh anggota komite, Dewan Komisaris menyampaikan apresiasi dan rasa terima kasihnya kepada seluruh anggota komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris.

Decisions made in the Bank's management meetings follow a deliberative process to reach a consensus. The Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners hold joint meetings as required by regulations and the Bank's operational needs. Additionally, committee meetings are held regularly, with discussions documented in meeting minutes. In general, we assess that the Compliance Function has been effectively carried out, ensuring business and operational compliance at all levels of the organization. This includes providing compliance opinions on new products and activities, as well as on the Bank's policies and procedures. In preparing the Bank's Business Plan, the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners have involved all levels of the organization, ensuring alignment with the annual work plans of each function and work unit.

## Performance Assessment of Committees under Board of Commissioners

The performance assessment of the committees and supporting organs under the Board of Commissioners is conducted both collegially and individually. The Board of Commissioners evaluates these entities based on specific criteria, including the completeness of their structure and the effectiveness of their duties. Throughout 2024, the committees under the Board of Commissioners—the Audit Committee, the Risk Oversight Committee, and the Remuneration and Nomination Committee—have effectively carried out their roles in assisting the Board of Commissioners in overseeing the Bank's management.

The performance evaluation of these three supporting committees is conducted qualitatively, considering factors such as their level of engagement, the documentation process, and the recommendations provided. In 2024, the Audit Committee held 15 (fifteen) meetings, the Risk Oversight Committee held 12 (twelve) meetings, and the Remuneration and Nomination Committee held 7 (seven) meetings. In recognition of their contributions, the Board of Commissioners expresses its appreciation and gratitude to all committee members for their dedication and efforts in fulfilling their responsibilities.
