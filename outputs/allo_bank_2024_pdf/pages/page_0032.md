# Mekanisme dan Frekuensi Pemberian Nasihat kepada Direksi

Sesuai dengan peraturan dan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, keberadaan Dewan Komisaris memperkuat fungsi pengawasan bagi pengelolaan Bank yang dilakukan oleh Direksi. Untuk itu, hubungan kerja antara Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dibangun melalui kesamaan pandangan untuk mencapai visi Bank.

Dewan Komisaris secara rutin mengadakan pertemuan dengan Direksi untuk membahas berbagai hal terkait pengelolaan Bank yang dijalankan oleh Direksi dalam rapat gabungan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Dalam forum tersebut, Dewan Komisaris meminta penjelasan dari Direksi mengenai pencapaian kinerja Bank dan berbagai kendala yang dihadapi. Dewan Komisaris juga senantiasa menyampaikan pandangan dan memberikan nasihat kepada Direksi terhadap implementasi strategi yang dijalankan dengan senantiasa menempatkan pengawasan atas implementasi strategi Bank sebagai salah satu prioritas.

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Dewan Komisaris telah melaksanakan rapat gabungan dengan mengundang Direksi sebanyak 12 (dua belas) kali, dengan tingkat kehadiran Dewan Komisaris dalam rapat secara keseluruhan adalah 100 persen. Selain forum rapat gabungan, organ Dewan Komisaris, yaitu Komite Audit, dan Komite Pemantau Risiko serta Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi juga melakukan rapat dengan mengundang Direktorat dan Divisi terkait untuk membahas bidang-bidang yang menjadi tanggung jawab masing-masing Komite.

The Board of Commissioners also remains committed to supporting the Bank's continuous development of innovative products and services that cater to evolving customer needs. In line with its role, the Board of Commissioners will continue to provide motivation, guidance, and insights to drive the Bank's sustainable growth. The Board of Directors' performance assessment, conducted by the Board of Commissioners, also takes into account shareholder directives. Overall, the Board of Commissioners is of the opinion that in 2024, the Board of Directors has managed the Company's operations effectively and successfully.

# Mechanism and Frequency of Providing Advice to Board of Directors

In accordance with applicable laws and regulations, the role of the Board of Commissioners strengthens the supervisory function over the Bank's management, which is carried out by the Board of Directors. To this end, the working relationship between the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors is built upon a shared vision to achieve the Bank's objectives.

The Board of Commissioners regularly holds meetings with the Board of Directors to discuss various matters related to the Bank's management. These discussions take place during joint meetings between the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors. In these forums, the Board of Commissioners requests explanations from the Board of Directors regarding the Bank's performance achievements and the challenges encountered. The Board of Commissioners also consistently shares its perspectives and provides guidance to the Board of Directors on strategy implementation, always prioritizing the oversight of the Bank's strategic execution.

Throughout 2024, the Board of Commissioners held 12 (twelve) joint meetings with the Board of Directors, achieving a 100 percent attendance rate. In addition to these joint meetings, the Board of Commissioners' committees—namely the Audit Committee, the Risk Oversight Committee, and the Remuneration and Nomination Committee—also conducted meetings with the relevant Directorates and Divisions to discuss matters within their respective areas of responsibility.
