# Peningkatan Kompetensi Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi

Allo Bank senantiasa melaksanakan program peningkatan kompetensi bagi Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi gunai mengoptimalkan seluruh tugasnya dan terus mengembangkan kemampuannya sesuai dengan kondisi dan dinamika bisnis yang terjadi. Informasi mengenai peningkatan kompetensi Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi sepanjang tahun 2024 dapat dilihat pada Bab Profil Perusahaan dalam Laporan Tahunan ini.

# Laporan Pelaksanaan Tugas Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya melalui sejumlah kegiatan, seperti:

1. Menerima dan mencatat pengangkatan Sdr. Siswandi Pujo Sasongko sebagai anggota Komite Pemantau Risiko dan Sdri. Lista Irna sebagai anggota Komite Audit.
2. Mencatat dan mendukung penyesuaian remunerasi pengurus Bank.

# Competency Development of Remuneration and Nomination Committee

Allo Bank continuously implements competency improvement programs for the Remuneration and Nomination Committee in order to optimize all its duties and continue to develop its capabilities in accordance with the current business conditions and dynamics. Information regarding the competency improvement of the Remuneration and Nomination Committee throughout 2024 can be seen in the Company Profile Chapter in this Annual Report.

# Report on Implementation of Duties of Remuneration and Nomination Committee

Throughout 2024, the Remuneration and Nomination Committee carried out its duties and responsibilities through a number of activities, such as:

1. Accepting and recording the appointment of Mr. Siswandi Pujo Sasongko as a member of the Risk Oversight Committee and Ms. Lista Irna as a member of the Audit Committee.
2. Recording and supporting the adjustment of the Bank's management remuneration.
