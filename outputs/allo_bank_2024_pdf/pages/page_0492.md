Walau mengalami perlambatan, pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2024 masih berada dalam rentang pertumbuhan yang disampaikan berbagai lembaga ekonomi global. Bank Dunia, Dana Moneter Internasional (IMF) dan Asia Development Bank (ADB) memproyeksikan pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2024 sebesar 5%. Adapun Bank Indonesia memproyeksikan pertumbuhan ekonomi sebesar 5,1%, sedangkan Kementerian Keuangan di angka 5-5,2%.

Kinerja Lapangan Usaha Jasa Keuangan 2024

BPS menyebutkan pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2024 ditopang oleh bertumbuhnya semua lapangan usaha, termasuk jasa keuangan yang tumbuh sebesar 4,74%, sedikit melambat dibandingan tahun 2023 dengan pertumbuhan 4,77%. Perlambatan tersebut dipengaruhi oleh berbagai hal antara lain ketidakpastian ekonomi global dan tekanan eksternal seperti fluktuasi nilai tukar serta perlambatan investasi asing turut menekan likuiditas dan minat investasi di dalam negeri. Selain itu, kebijakan moneter yang lebih ketat dan pengetatan regulasi di sektor keuangan membuat lembaga keuangan harus lebih berhati-hati dalam mengelola risiko dan pinjaman sehingga mempengaruhi laju ekspansi produk dan layanan keuangan. Di sisi lain, pergeseran perilaku konsumen yang menjadi lebih selektif dan konservatif dalam mengambil keputusan finansial juga menambah beban industri jasa keuangan dalam mempertahankan pertumbuhan yang sebelumnya lebih dinamis.

Berkaitan dengan kinerja sektor jasa keuangan, Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) menilai stabilitas sektor ini terjaga stabil di tengah dinamika perekonomian global dan domestik. Sejumlah indikator perkembangan sektor perbankan menguatkan sinyalemen tersebut. Kinerja intermediasi perbankan tumbuh positif dengan profil risiko yang terjaga. Pada November 2024, pertumbuhan kredit masih melanjutkan double digit growth sebesar 10,79% yoy (Oktober 2024: 10,92 persen) menjadi Rp7.717 triliun.

Sementara itu, berdasarkan jenis penggunaan, Kredit Investasi tumbuh tertinggi yaitu sebesar 13,77%, diikuti oleh Kredit Konsumsi 10,94%, sedangkan Kredit Modal Kerja 8,92%. Berdasarkan kategori debitur, kredit korporasi tumbuh sebesar 16,19%, sedangkan kredit UMKM juga tetap tumbuh sebesar 4,02%.

Di sisi lain, Dana Pihak Ketiga (DPK) perbankan tercatat tumbuh sebesar 7,54% yoy (Oktober 2024: 6,74 persen yoy) menjadi Rp8.835,9 triliun, dengan giro, tabungan, dan deposito masing-masing tumbuh sebesar 10,97%, 6,55%, dan 5,57% yoy. Sementar itu, likuiditas industri perbankan pada November 2024 tetap memadai dengan rasio Alat Likuid/Non-Core Deposit (AL/NCD) dan Alat Likuid/Dana Pihak Ketiga (AL/DPK) masing-masing sebesar 112,94% (Oktober 2024: 113,64%) dan 25,57% (Oktober 2024: 25,58%) dan masih di atas threshold masing-masing sebesar 50% dan 10%. Adapun Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) berada di level 213,07%.

Despite the slight deceleration, Indonesia's economic growth in 2024 remains within the range projected by various global economic institutions. The World Bank, International Monetary Fund (IMF), and Asian Development Bank (ADB) all forecast Indonesia's economic growth at 5% for the year. Meanwhile, Bank Indonesia projects a 5.1% growth rate, while the Ministry of Finance estimates growth between 5.0% and 5.2%.

Banking Industry Performance in 2024

According to the BPS, Indonesia's economic growth in 2024 was supported by the expansion of all business sectors, including financial services, which grew by 4.74%, slightly slower than the 4.77% growth recorded in 2023. The slowdown was influenced by various factors, including global economic uncertainty and external pressures such as exchange rate fluctuations and a decline in foreign investment, which also impacted liquidity and investment interest within the country. Additionally, tighter monetary policies and stricter financial regulations compelled financial institutions to adopt a more cautious approach in risk and loan management, thereby affecting the pace of expansion in financial products and services. Moreover, shifting consumer behavior, with a more selective and conservative approach to financial decisions, further contributed to the challenges faced by the financial services industry in maintaining its previous growth momentum.

Despite these challenges, the Financial Services Authority (OJK) assessed that the stability of the financial services sector remained intact amid the dynamics of both the global and domestic economy. Several banking sector indicators reinforced this positive outlook. Banking intermediation performance showed continued growth, supported by a stable risk profile. As of November 2024, credit growth remained in double digits at 10.79% year-on-year (yoy), compared to 10.92% in October 2024, bringing total credit disbursement to IDR7,717 trillion.

By type of credit usage, Investment Loan recorded the highest growth at 13.77%, followed by Consumer Loan at 10.94%, while Working Capital Loan grew by 8.92%. Based on the debtor category, corporate loan expanded by 16.19%, while MSME credit also continued to grow, increasing by 4.02%.

Meanwhile, Third-Party Funds (TPF) in the banking sector grew by 7.54% yoy (October 2024: 6.74% yoy) to IDR8,835.9 trillion, with current, savings, and time deposits growing by 10.97%, 6.55%, and 5.57% yoy, respectively. The banking sector's liquidity remained adequate in November 2024, with the ratio of Liquid Assets to Non-Core Deposits (AL/NCD) and Liquid Assets to Third-Party Funds (AL/TPF) standing at 112.94% (October 2024: 113.64%) and 25.57% (October 2024: 25.58%), respectively—well above the minimum thresholds of 50% and 10%. The Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) was reported at 213.07%.
