Sepanjang tahun 2024, Bank berhasil membukukan kinerja yang baik sebagaimana tercermin dari pertumbuhan posisi keuangan Bank. Sepanjang tahun buku, jumlah aset Bank tercatat mengalami peningkatan sebesar 9,67% menjadi Rp 13,98 triliun didorong peningkatan kredit. Sementara itu Ekuitas tumbuh 7,4% menjadi Rp7,27 triliun karena penguatan ekuitas Bank yang dilakukan secara organik dari laba bersih Bank

Throughout 2024, the Bank managed to record good performance as reflected in the growth of the Bank's financial position. Throughout the fiscal year, the Bank's total assets were recorded to have increased by 9.67% to IDR 13.98 trillion driven by increase in lending. Meanwhile, Equity grew 7.4% to IDR 7.27 trillion due to the strengthening of the Bank's equity which was carried out organically from the Bank's net profit.

Per 31 Desember 2024, Bank membukukan jumlah aset sebesar Rp 13,98 triliun, meningkat Rp 1,23 triliun atau 9,7% dibanding tahun 2023 yang sebesar Rp12,75 triliun. Hal ini disebabkan oleh peningkatan kredit yang disalurkan, terutama untuk segmen Retail Banking yang tumbuh pesat.

As of December 31, 2024, the Bank recorded total assets of Rp13.98 trillion, an increase of Rp1.23 trillion or 9.7% compared to 2023 which was Rp12.75 trillion. This was due to an increase in credit disbursed, especially for the rapidly growing Retail Banking segment.
