# Komite ALCO

Bank membentuk Komite Aset dan Liabilitas (ALCO) untuk membantu pelaksanaan tugas Direksi dalam memelihara struktur neraca secara menyeluruh yang meliputi analisis dan perumusan kebijakan, strategi, pengambilan keputusan, serta pengawasan dalam pengelolaan Risiko Likuiditas, Risiko Suku Bunga, Risiko Pasar dan permodalan yang sejalan dengan peraturan regulator dan strategi bisnis Bank.

# ALCO Committee

The Bank has formed an Asset and Liability Committee (ALCO) to assist the Board of Directors in carrying out its duties in maintaining a comprehensive balance sheet structure that includes analysis and formulation of policies, strategies, decision-making, and supervision in the management of Liquidity Risk, Interest Rate Risk, Market Risk and capital in line with regulatory regulations and the Bank's business strategy.

# Tugas dan Wewenang Komite ALCO

1. Menetapkan target keuangan Bank (jangka pendek dan menengah) gunai mempertahankan dan meningkatkan nilai Bank melalui optimalisasi spread antara pendapatan dan biaya pada tingkat risiko yang dapat ditoleransi.
2. Memastikan bahwa target keuangan yang ditetapkan Bank mendapatkan persetujuan dari Dewan Komisaris. Pelaksanaan disiplin Asset and Liabilities Management (ALMA) oleh ALCO secara praktis mengarah kepada pencapaian target-target keuangan tersebut.
3. Menetapkan strategi ALMA untuk mencapai target-target tersebut melalui: (i) Penetapan Rencana Bisnis; (ii) Komposisi neraca dan off balance sheet aktiva/pasiva; (iii) Produk dan jasa yang ditawarkan; (iv) Penentuan tingkat suku bunga dan/atau pricing untuk semua produk dan jasa Bank.

# Duties and Authorities of ALCO Committee

1. Establish the Bank's financial targets (short and medium term) to maintain and increase the Bank's value by optimizing the spread between income and costs at a tolerable risk level.
2. Ensure that the Bank's financial targets are approved by the Board of Commissioners. The implementation of the Asset and Liabilities Management (ALMA) discipline by ALCO practically leads to the achievement of these financial targets.
3. Establish the ALMA strategy to achieve these targets, through: (i) Determination of the Business Plan; (ii) Composition of the balance sheet and off balance sheet assets/liabilities; (iii) Products and services offered; (iv) Determination of interest rates and/or pricing for all Bank products and services.
