Laporan Keberlanjutan

Sementara itu, kepada masyarakat sebagai pemangku eksternal yang lain, Bank mengukuhkan manfaat melalui pelaksanaan berbagai program Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TJSL). Pada tahun 2024 kegiatan tersebut berupa Gerakan Berbagi Pangan dan Sembako ("Gebrag"), program PIJAR sebagai gerakan mengajar di berbagai sekolah di daerah-daerah pelosok Indonesia, dan pengadaan program kebun hidroponik di beberapa lokasi. Implementasi program-program TJSL tersebut, sekaligus merupakan upaya nyata dan dukungan konkret Bank terhadap Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB/SDGs).

Di sisi lain, kepada pemerintah, Bank memenuhi tanggung jawab dengan melakukan pembayaran pajak tepat waktu; kepada mitra/vendor, Bank melakukan pengadaan barang dan jasa yang transparan; sedangkan kepada regulator, Bank senantiasa merealisasikan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi yang berlaku.

## Strategi Pencapaian Target

Bank berkomitmen untuk menjaga keselarasan antara aspek ekonomi, sosial dan lingkungan dalam menjalankan usaha. Dalam hal ini, Bank meyakini bahwa implementasi aspek lingkungan dan sosial berkelanjutan akan dipengaruhi oleh pencapaian dalam mewujudkan target-target kinerja aspek ekonomi yang disampaikan dalam RBB Tahun 2024. Untuk itu, Bank telah menetapkan langkah-langkah strategis tahun 2024 sebagai berikut:

1. Melakukan penetrasi pasar berkelanjutan ke berbagai segmentasi nasabah dengan terus memberikan nilai tambah (added value) melalui implementasi 3 pilar strategi bisnis Bank (acquisition, engagement, and monetization);
2. Meningkatkan layanan digital secara signifikan yang berdampak pada peningkatan aset dan liabilitas;
3. Menjalin kolaborasi dengan mitra strategis (strategic partners) ekosistem utama melalui penerapan model open banking untuk memperkaya dan meningkatkan nilai layanan finansial yang disediakan oleh Bank;
4. Penguatan data driven organization dengan memanfaatkan kapabilitas Artificial Intelligence (AI) dan data analytic untuk meningkatkan keunggulan kompetitif Bank;
5. Penguatan manajemen risiko menggunakan teknologi terkini melalui peningkatan IT Maturity dalam rangka memperkuat operasional Bank;
6. Pengembangan talenta DIGITAL untuk mencapai kinerja Bank yang unggul; dan
7. Penguatan Good Corporate Governance (GCG) di seluruh fungsi organisasi Bank.

Untuk menjaga kinerja ekonomi berjalan sesuai dengan yang ditargetkan, Bank melakukan monitoring atas implementasi inisiatif strategis tersebut secara berkala dalam rapat Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Kebijakan tersebut dilakukan dengan tujuan agar target kinerja ekonomi yang telah ditetapkan dapat tercapai, serta merumuskan solusi alternatif apabila terdapat hambatan di dalam merealisasikan target pada tahun pelaporan.

Meanwhile, when it came to its commitments to the community as another external stakeholder, the Bank provided benefits through its implementation of various Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR) programs. In 2024, these activities included its Food and Grocery Sharing Movement ("Gebrag"), its PIJAR program that served as a teaching movement in various schools in remote areas of Indonesia, and its provision of hydroponic garden programs throughout several locations. The implementation of these CSR programs also served as the Bank's way of supporting the nation's Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

In terms of the Bank's commitment to the government, Allo Bank fulfilled its responsibilities by making tax payments on time. For its partners/vendors, the Bank carried out transparent procurement of goods and services. Finally, for regulators, the Bank always ensured that it was in compliance with all applicable regulations.

## Target Achievement Strategy

The Bank is committed to maintaining a sense of harmony between its economic, social and environmental aspects as it operates its business. The Bank believes that its implementation of sustainable environmental and social aspects will be influenced by any achievements it makes in realizing its economic aspect performance targets that it has stated in its Bank Business Plan. To that end, the Bank has established the following strategic steps for 2024:

1. Carrying out sustainable market penetration to various customer segments by continuing to provide added value through its implementation of the 3 pillars of the Bank's business strategy (acquisition, engagement, and monetization);
2. Significantly increasing digital services that have an impact on increasing assets and liabilities;
3. Establishing collaboration with strategic partners in its main ecosystem through its implementation of an open banking model to enrich and increase the value of the Bank's financial services;
4. Strengthening data driven organizations by utilizing Artificial Intelligence (AI) and data analytics capabilities to increase the Bank's competitive advantage;
5. Strengthening risk management by using the latest technology and increasing IT Maturity in order to strengthen the Bank's operations;
6. Development of DIGITAL talent to achieve even better Bank performance; and
7. Strengthening Good Corporate Governance (GCG) across all of the Bank's organizational functions.

In order to ensure that it can maintain economic performance levels that are in accordance with its targets, the Bank periodically monitors its implementation of its strategic initiatives through Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners meetings. These efforts are done with the aim of guaranteeing that the Bank's economic performance targets can be achieved, as well as to have these meetings serve as a venue for formulating alternative solutions if there are obstacles in realizing its targets in the reporting year.
