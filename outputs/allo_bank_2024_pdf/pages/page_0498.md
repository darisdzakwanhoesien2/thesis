Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) menyebutkan sustainable finance memiliki empat dimensi, yaitu pencapaian keunggulan industri, sosial, dan ekonomi dalam rangka mengurangi ancaman pemanasan global serta pencegahan terhadap permasalahan lingkungan hidup dan sosial lainnya; pergeseran target menuju ekonomi rendah karbon yang kompetitif; promosi investasi ramah lingkungan hidup di berbagai sektor usaha/ekonomi; dan pemberian dukungan pada pelaksanaan prinsip-prinsip pembangunan Indonesia 4P (pro-growth, pro-jobs, pro-poor, dan pro-environment).

# Komitmen Allo Bank

Allo Bank mendukung penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan di Indonesia dengan melakukan penyesuaian sejumlah kebijakan agar selaras dengan komitmen mengutamakan nilai Lingkungan (Environmental), Sosial (Social), dan Tata Kelola (Governance) atas ESG. Penyesuaian yang dilakukan antara lain melakukan pembaruan kebijakan penyaluran kredit, serta pembaruan Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) kredit terkait penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan.

Secara spesifik, Bank juga mulai mencanangkan program-program berkelanjutan terkait aspek environment dan social. Dalam hal ini, Bank menjadikan kelompok-kelompok usaha yang mengedepankan bisnis berkelanjutan sebagai target dan sasaran pemberian kredit. Sejalan dengan itu, secara internal, Bank secara optimal menerapkan operasional kantor yang ramah lingkungan dengan memegang prinsip 3R (Reduce, Reuse, &amp; Recycle). Prinsip ini diwujudkan melalui pengelolaan yang baik terkait penggunaan bahan baku/material kertas, energi (bahan bakar minyak dan listrik), air serta limbah.

The demand for the banking industry to contribute to environmental sustainability is an implementation of Republic of Indonesia Law No. 10 of 1998 concerning Amendments to Law No. 7 of 1992 on Banking, which regulates the principle of prudence in credit distribution; Republic of Indonesia Law No. 32 of 2009 on Environmental Protection and Management, where in Article 43, paragraph (3), letter c, there is a clause stating, "Development of an environmentally friendly financial institution and capital market system"; and the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies (POJK Sustainable Finance), released by the Financial Services Authority.

Based on these regulations, the banking sector in Indonesia is required to implement the principles of sustainable finance. The Financial Services Authority (OJK) states that sustainable finance consists of four dimensions: achieving industrial, social, and economic excellence to reduce the threat of global warming and prevent environmental and social issues; shifting targets towards a competitive low-carbon economy; promoting environmentally friendly investments across various business and economic sectors; and supporting the implementation of Indonesia's 4P development principles (pro-growth, pro-jobs, pro-poor, and pro-environment).

# Allo Bank's Commitment

Allo Bank actively supports the implementation of sustainable finance in Indonesia by adjusting several policies to align with its commitment to prioritizing Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) values. These adjustments include updating credit distribution policies and revising the Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for credit related to the implementation of sustainable finance.

Furthermore, the Bank has launched various sustainability-focused programs related to environmental and social aspects. As part of this initiative, Allo Bank prioritizes business groups engaged in sustainable practices as key targets for credit distribution. Internally, the Bank is also committed to environmentally friendly office operations, adopting the 3R principle (Reduce, Reuse, &amp; Recycle). This principle is applied through efficient management of raw materials such as paper, as well as energy, water, and waste consumption.
