3. Tata Kelola Rencana Aksi Pemulihan

Rencana Aksi Pemulihan yang efektif membantu manajemen mengidentifikasi kapan Bank menghadapi atau mungkin menghadapi peristiwa stres yang parah yang mengancam atau mungkin mengancam kekuatan keuangan dan kelayakannya. Dalam hal ini, manajemen Bank akan mengambil tindakan yang tepat untuk memulihkan kekuatan keuangan dan kelayakan bank. Rencana Pemulihan sangat penting bagi ketahanan Bank, dan terintegrasi ke dalam kerangka kerja tata kelola risiko Bank, serta memainkan peran penting dalam manajemen krisis. Rencana Pemulihan juga mengakui transisi Bank dari operasi bisnis biasa ke peringatan dini tentang stres parah hingga stres parah itu sendiri, dan terhubung dengan rencana penyelesaian jika penurunan keuangan tidak diperbaiki.

Proses perencanaan pemulihan harus bersifat berkelanjutan. Proses ini melengkapi fungsi tata kelola risiko dan mendukung operasinya yang aman dan sehat. Dalam proses pengembangan dan pemeliharaan Rencana Pemulihan, manajemen dan Direksi terus berkomitmen untuk meningkatkan tata kelola risiko dengan tujuan untuk mengurangi dampak keuangan dari peristiwa tak terduga di masa depan.

Di bawah arahan regulator khususnya OJK, Bank terus mengembangkan pemicu atau metrik sepanjang kontinuum business as usual, peringatan dini, pemulihan, dan penyelesaian agar dapat merespons dengan cepat dan pulih dari dampak keuangan peristiwa stres yang parah.

Bank juga akan mengkoordinasikan Rencana Pemulihan dengan upaya perencanaan pemulihan dan penyelesaian oleh perusahaan induknya, sehingga rencana tersebut konsisten dan tidak saling bertentangan.

4. Penyampaian Rencana Aksi

Bank telah menyampaikan Rencana Aksi kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan untuk pertama kalinya pada tahun 2024 dengan mengacu pada Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 5 Tahun 2024 tentang Penetapan Status Pengawasan dan Penanganan Permasalahan Bank Umum dimana pada pasal 14 tercantum bahwa Bank wajib menyusun dan menyampaikan Rencana Aksi Pemulihan kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan.

Additionally, the Bank has developed a crisis communication plan as a standardized guideline for managing financial issues within its corporate communications framework. This plan establishes structured governance, communication protocols, and resource allocation to ensure timely and accurate responses to stakeholders. It also assigns clear accountability to the designated crisis management team, aiming to minimize the impact of financial challenges on the Bank's reputation. The disclosure of the Recovery Action Plan follows a structured approach to enhance stakeholder confidence and regulatory compliance.

3. Recovery Action Plan Governance

An effective Recovery Action Plan enables management to identify situations where the Bank is experiencing or may experience a severe stress event that could threaten its financial strength and viability. In such cases, the Bank's management will take appropriate measures to restore financial stability. The Recovery Plan is essential to the Bank's resilience, as it is integrated into the Bank's risk governance framework and plays a key role in crisis management. It also outlines the Bank's transition from normal business operations to early warning of financial distress and, if necessary, to a severe stress scenario. Additionally, it is linked to the resolution plan should the financial deterioration remain unaddressed.

The recovery planning process is continuous, complementing the Bank's risk governance function and supporting its safe and sound operations. In developing and maintaining the Recovery Plan, management and the Board of Directors remain committed to enhancing risk governance to minimize the financial impact of unforeseen future events.

Under regulatory guidance, particularly from OJK, the Bank continues to refine its triggers and metrics across various stages—ranging from business as usual, early warning, recovery, and resolution—ensuring a swift response to financial stress events and facilitating effective recovery.

Furthermore, the Bank coordinates its Recovery Plan with the recovery and resolution planning efforts of its parent company to ensure consistency and alignment, preventing any potential conflicts between the plans.

4. Submission of the Action Plan

The Bank submitted its Action Plan to the Financial Services Authority for the first time in 2024, in accordance with Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 5 of 2024 on the Determination of Supervision Status and Handling of Commercial Bank Issues, where Article 14 of this regulation mandates that banks must prepare and submit a Recovery Action Plan to the Financial Services Authority.
