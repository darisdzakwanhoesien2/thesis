# Persyaratan Direktur Kepatuhan

Selain memenuhi kriteria umum bagi anggota Direksi, Direktur yang membawahi fungsi kepatuhan juga wajib memenuhi sejumlah persyaratan sebagai berikut:

1. Memenuhi persyaratan independensi.
2. Direktur utama dan/atau wakil direktur utama dilarang merangkap jabatan sebagai direktur yang membawahkan Fungsi Kepatuhan.
3. Dilarang membawahi fungsi: Bisnis, Operasional, Manajemen Risiko yang melakukan pengambilan keputusan pada kegiatan usaha Bank, treasury, keuangan dan akuntansi, logistik dan pengadaan barang/jasa, teknologi informasi dan/atau audit internal.

# Pengangkatan Anggota Direksi

1. Anggota Direksi diangkat berdasarkan keputusan RUPS.
2. Anggota Direksi diangkat untuk masa jabatan tertentu dan dapat diangkat kembali.
3. Satu periode masa jabatan anggota Direksi paling lama 5 (lima) tahun atau sampai dengan penutupan RUPS tahunan pada akhir 1 (satu) periode masa jabatan dimaksud.
4. Usulan penggantian dan/atau pengangkatan anggota Direksi oleh Dewan Komisaris dalam RUPS, harus memperhatikan rekomendasi dari Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi.
5. Persyaratan pengangkatan Direksi yang merupakan tenaga kerja asing harus tunduk kepada ketentuan tentang Pemanfaatan Tenaga Kerja Asing dan Program Alih Pengetahuan sebagaimana diatur dalam Peraturan OJK serta ketentuan instansi terkait lainnya.
6. Honorarium dan/atau tunjangan Direksi ditentukan dalam RUPS.

# Pengunduran Diri Anggota Direksi &amp; Pergantian Anggota Direksi

1. Anggota Direksi dapat mengundurkan diri dari jabatannya sebelum masa jabatannya berakhir.
2. Masa jabatan Direksi berakhir dengan sendirinya apabila Direksi tersebut:
a. Mengundurkan diri dengan pemberitahuan secara tertulis;
b. Tidak lagi memenuhi persyaratan perundang-undangan yang berlaku;
c. Meninggal dunia;
d. Diberhentikan berdasarkan keputusan RUPS;
e. Dinyatakan pailit atau ditaruh dibawah pengampuan berdasarkan suatu penetapan Pengadilan;
f. Terbukti terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan.
3. Direksi berhak mengundurkan diri dari jabatannya dengan memberitahukan secara tertulis kepada Bank selambat lambatnya 90 (sembilan puluh) hari sebelum tanggal pengunduran dirinya.

# Requirements of Compliance Director

Apart from meeting the general criteria for members of the Board of Directors, the Director in charge of the compliance function is also required to fulfill a number of requirements as follows:

1. Fulfill independence requirements.
2. The President Director is prohibited from holding concurrent positions as Director in charge of the compliance function.
3. Prohibited from being in charge of functions: Business, Operations, Risk Management which makes decisions on Bank business activities, treasury, finance and accounting, logistics and procurement of goods/services, information technology and internal audit.

# Appointment of Members of the Board of Directors

1. Board of Directors members are appointed based on the decision of the GMS.
2. Board of Directors members are appointed for a specific term and may be reappointed.
3. The maximum term for a Board of Directors member is 5 (five) years or until the end of 1 (one) term period, which ends with the annual GMS meeting.
4. Proposals for the replacement and/or appointment of Board of Directors members by the Board of Commissioners in GMS, must consider the recommendations of the Remuneration and Nomination Committee.
5. The appointment requirements for foreign workers who serve as Board of Directors members must comply with the provisions on the Utilization of Foreign Workers and Knowledge Transfer Program as stipulated by the OJK regulation and other relevant agencies.
6. The honorarium and/or benefits for Board of Directors members are determined at GMS.

# Resignation and Replacement of Directors

1. Members of the Board of Directors may resign from their positions before their term of office ends.
2. The term of office of a Director ends automatically if the Director:
a. Resign with written notification;
b. No longer meets applicable statutory requirements;
c. Passes away;
d. Dismissed based on GMS decision;
e. Declared bankrupt or placed under guardianship based on a court decision;
f. Proven to be involved in financial crimes.
3. Directors have the right to resign from their positions by notifying the Bank in writing no later than 90 (ninety) days before the date of their resignation.
