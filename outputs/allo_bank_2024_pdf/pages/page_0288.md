# Analisis Kualitatif Qualitative Analysis

2. Dalam rangka meningkatkan efektivitas implementasi Manajemen Risiko Operasional, Bank telah memiliki Unit Kerja Enterprise, Operational, and Market Risk (EOMR) yang salah satu fungsinya terkait Manajemen Risiko Operasional dan berada dibawah koordinasi Satuan Kerja Manajemen Risiko dengan tugas dan tanggung jawab yang telah dirumuskan dengan jelas dan akuntabel. Unit Kerja ini memiliki tugas membantu Direksi dalam mengelola Risiko Operasional, memantau proses pelaksanaan Manajemen Risiko Operasional secara menyeluruh, memastikan kebijakan Manajemen Risiko Operasional berjalan pada seluruh tingkat Organisasi Bank. Dalam pelaksanaannya Unit Kerja EOMR berkoordinasi dengan beberapa Unit Kerja pada setiap kesempatan seperti Unit Kerja Internal Audit, Unit Kerja Cyber Risk, Unit Kerja Legal, Unit Kerja Kepatuhan serta Unit Kerja untuk membahas isu-isu Risiko Operasional yang kritikal agar risiko-risiko tersebut dapat dimitigasi dan dikendalikan secara efektif sejak dini.

Untuk mendukung implementasi Kerangka Kerja Manajemen Risiko Operasional telah dibentuk 3 unsur penanggung jawab pengelola di masing-masing Unit Kerja yang terdiri dari Risk Owner, Operational Risk Manager (Risk Specialist) dan Responsible Person (RP). Risk Owner merupakan pemimpin tertinggi di Direktorat, Unit Kerja Bisnis dan unit kerja pendukung yang bertanggung jawab mengelola Risiko Operasional di unit kerja yang dipimpinnya, termasuk memastikan dipatuhinya peraturan dalam pelaksanaan kegiatan bisnis, operasional, dan transaksional. Operational Risk Manager (ORM) merupakan fungsi manajemen Risiko Operasional yang membantu Risk Owner mengimplementasikan Kerangka Kerja Manajemen Risiko Operasional, Responsible Person (RP) bertugas membantu Kepala Divisinya mengelola Risiko Operasional terutama dalam hal Pelaporan Kejadian Risiko (Incident Report), Risk &amp; Control Self-Assessment (RCSA), Key Risk Indicators (KRI) serta permasalahan risiko operasional lainnya yang tidak tercover pada area kerjanya.

In order to improve the effectiveness of Operational Risk Management implementation, the Bank has an Enterprise, Operational, and Market Risk (EOMR) Work Unit, one of whose functions is related to Operational Risk Management and is under the coordination of the Risk Management Work Unit with clearly defined and accountable duties and responsibilities. This Working Unit has the task of assisting the Board of Directors in managing Operational Risk, monitoring the overall Operational Risk Management implementation process, ensuring Operational Risk Management policies are implemented at all levels of the Bank's Organization. In its implementation, the EOMR Work Unit coordinates with several Work Units on every occasion such as the Internal Audit Work Unit, Cyber Risk Work Unit, Legal Work Unit, Compliance Work Unit and Work Units to discuss critical Operational Risk issues so that these risks can be effectively mitigated and controlled early on.

To support the implementation of the Operational Risk Management Framework, 3 elements of responsible managers have been established in each Work Unit consisting of Risk Owner, Operational Risk Manager (Risk Specialist) and Responsible Person (RP). The Risk Owner is the highest leader in the Directorate, Business Unit and supporting work units who is responsible for managing Operational Risk in the work unit he leads, including ensuring compliance with regulations in the implementation of business, operational and transactional activities. Operational Risk Manager (ORM) is an Operational Risk management function that helps the Risk Owner implement the Operational Risk Management Framework. The Responsible Person (RP) is tasked with assisting the Head of the Division in managing Operational Risk, especially in terms of Incident Reporting, Risk &amp; Control Self-Assessment (RCSA), Key Risk Indicators (KRI) and other operational risk issues that are not covered in their work area.
