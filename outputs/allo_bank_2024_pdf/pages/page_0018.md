## Komposisi Pemegang Saham

Per 31 Desember 2024, jumlah saham PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk adalah sebanyak 21.730.256.442 lembar saham dengan kepemilikan sebanyak 13.228.508.531 lembar saham atau 60,88% dimiliki oleh PT Mega Corpora, 2.497.816.903 lembar saham atau setara 11,49% dimiliki PT Bukalapak.com Tbk, 1.521.117.930 lembar saham atau 7,00% dimiliki oleh Abadi Investment Pte. ## Shareholder's Composition

As of December 31, 2024, PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk had a total of 21,730,256,442 shares outstanding. Among these, PT Mega Corpora held ownership of 13,228,508,531 shares, representing 60.88% of the total shares. PT Bukalapak.com Tbk held 2,497,816,903 shares, equivalent to 11.49% ownership. Abadi Investment Pte. Ltd. Owned 1,521,117,930 shares, accounting for 7.00% ownership. PT Indolife Investama Perkasa held 1,303,815,836 shares, representing 6.00% ownership. The remaining 3,178,997,692 shares, constituting 14.63% ownership, were held by the public, with individual ownership below 5%.
