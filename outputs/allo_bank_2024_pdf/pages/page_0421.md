## Tinjauan terhadap Efektivitas Sistem Manajemen Risiko

Perseroan melakukan peninjauan terhadap efektivitas manajemen risiko secara berkala. Proses ini dilakukan melalui pengkajian atas RAS dan Profil Risiko agar tetap selaras dengan kondisi Perseroan terkini maupun regulasi terbaru. Pada tahun 2024 Perseroan telah melakukan tinjauan terhadap RAS dan metodologi penilaian Profil Risiko, di mana terdapat perubahan yang telah disetujui Direksi dan disampaikan kepada Dewan Komisaris sebagai bagian dari komitmen Perseroan dalam menjaga kerangka manajemen risiko yang relevan dan efektif.

## Profil Risiko dan Tingkat Kesehatan Bank

Peraturan OJK No. 18/POJK.03/2016 tentang Penerapan Manajemen Risiko bagi Bank Umum mengatur bahwa Bank harus melakukan self-assessments melalui Profil Risiko yang dilaporkan ke Regulator secara triwulanan. Proses penilaian Profil Risiko Bank meliputi 8 (delapan) risiko utama yaitu risiko kredit, risiko pasar, risiko likuiditas, risiko strategik, risiko operasional, risiko hukum, risiko kepatuhan, dan risiko reputasi.

Penilaian Profil Risiko tersebut dilakukan dengan parameter pengukuran risiko tidak hanya menggunakan data historis, tetapi juga dengan forward looking dengan mempertimbangkan strategi bisnis dan kondisi makroekonomi. Melalui Profil Risiko, Allo Bank dapat mengelola risiko melalui tindakan yang tepat, dan dilakukan secara menyeluruh dan komprehensif berdasarkan dengan lini bisnis Bank. Berdasarkan pada peraturan OJK No. 4/POJK.03/2016 tentang Penilaian Tingkat Kesehatan Bank, Allo Bank telah melakukan penilaian atas Tingkat Kesehatan Bank yang meliputi 4 (empat) faktor, antara lain Risk Profile, Good Corporate Governance (GCG), Earnings, dan Capital.

Adapun hasil dari self-assessment GCG Bank di tahun 2024 dapat dilihat sebagai berikut:

3. Ensure the Bank's compliance with commitments made by the Bank to the Financial Services Authority and/or other competent supervisory authorities.

## Review of the Effectiveness of the Risk Management System

The Company periodically reviews the effectiveness of its risk management system. This process is conducted through a review of the RAS and Risk Profile to ensure they remain aligned with the Company's current conditions and the latest regulations. In 2024, the Company reviewed the RAS and the Risk Profile assessment methodology, with the proposed changes approved by the Board of Directors and submitted to the Board of Commissioners. This initiative reflects the Company's commitment to maintaining a relevant and effective risk management framework.

## Risk Profile and Bank Soundness Level

OJK Regulation No. 18/POJK.03/2016 concerning the Implementation of Risk Management for Conventional Bank stipulates that Banks must conduct self-assessments through a Risk Profile reported to the Regulator quarterly. The Bank Risk Profile assessment process includes 8 (eight) main risks, namely credit risk, market risk, liquidity risk, strategic risk, operational risk, legal risk, compliance risk, and reputation risk.

The Risk Profile assessment is carried out with risk measurement parameters not only using historical data, but also with forward looking by considering business strategies and macroeconomic conditions. Through the Risk Profile, Allo Bank can manage risks through appropriate actions, and is carried out comprehensively and comprehensively based on the Bank's business line. Based on OJK Regulation No. 4/POJK.03/2016 concerning Bank Health Level Assessment, Allo Bank has conducted an assessment of the Bank Health Level which includes 4 (four) factors, including Risk Profile, Good Corporate Governance (GCG), Earnings, and Capital.
