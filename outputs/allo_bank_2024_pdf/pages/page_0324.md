## Masa Jabatan Dewan Komisaris

Berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Pasal 20 ayat 6, Dewan Komisaris dapat diangkat oleh RUPS untuk jangka waktu terhitung sejak pengangkatannya sampai penutupan RUPS tahunan ketiga berikutnya dengan tidak mengurangi hak RUPS untuk memberhentikan sewaktu-waktu.

## Susunan dan Dasar Pengangkatan Dewan Komisaris Tahun 2024

Sepanjang tahun 2024, susunan dan komposisi Dewan Komisaris tidak mengalami perubahan. Adapun komposisi Dewan Komisaris Allo Bank per 31 Desember 2024 adalah sebagai berikut:

2. The Board of Commissioners is led by a President Commissioner.
3. The Board of Commissioners consists of Commissioners and Independent Commissioners. At least 50% (fifty percent) of the total members of the Board of Commissioners are Independent Commissioners.
4. At least 1 (one) Commissioner must reside in Indonesia.

## Term of Office of the Board of Commissioners

Based on Article 20 paragraph 6 of the Articles of Association, the Board of Commissioners is appointed by the GMS for a period starting from their appointment until the closing of the next third annual GMS without prejudice to the GMS's right to dismiss them at any time.

## Penilaian Kelayakan dan Kepatutan Dewan Komisaris

Bank memastikan bahwa seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris telah dinyatakan lulus dalam uji kemampuan dan kepatutan (fit and proper test), serta memperoleh persetujuan dari OJK sebelum menjalankan tugas dan fungsi sesuai jabatannya. Kebijakan ini juga sesuai sebagaimana diatur dalam Peraturan OJK No. 27/POJK.03/2016 tentang Uji Kemampuan dan Kepatutan (Fit and Proper Test) bagi Pihak Utama Lembaga Jasa Keuangan.

A brief profile of each member of the Board of Commissioners can be seen in the Board of Commissioners Profile section, in the Company Profile chapter of this Annual Report.

## Fit and Proper Assessment of the Board of Commissioner

The Bank ensures that all members of the Board of Commissioners have successfully passed the Fit and Proper Test and obtained approval from OJK before assuming their roles and responsibilities. This requirement is in accordance with OJK Regulation No. 27/POJK.03/2016 concerning the Fit and Proper Test for Main Parties of Financial Services Institutions.
