### Tinjauan Segmen Industri

Bank Indonesia juga menyatakan bahwa ketahanan sistem keuangan terjaga baik, termasuk pada industri perbankan. Likuiditas perbankan tetap memadai, tercermin dari rasio Alat Likuid terhadap Dana Pihak Ketiga (AL/DPK) pada November 2024 yang tinggi sebesar 25,57%. Rasio kecukupan modal (Capital Adequacy Ratio/CAR) perbankan pada Oktober 2024 tercatat sebesar 27,02%, tergolong kuat dalam menyerap risiko dan mendukung pertumbuhan kredit. Sementara itu, rasio kredit bermasalah (Non-Performing Loan/NPL) perbankan pada Oktober 2024 terjaga rendah, sebesar 2,20% (bruto) dan 0,77% (neto).

Ketahanan permodalan dan likuiditas perbankan juga didukung oleh pencapaian profitabilitas dan pengelolaan risiko bank yang baik, serta ditopang oleh kemampuan membayar dan profitabilitas korporasi yang terjaga, sebagaimana hasil stress test perbankan terkini. Ke depan, Bank Indonesia terus memperkuat sinergi kebijakan bersama KSSK dalam menjaga stabilitas sistem keuangan, termasuk memitigasi berbagai risiko yang dapat mengganggu stabilitas sistem keuangan.

Kinerja transaksi ekonomi dan keuangan digital pada November 2024 tetap tumbuh didukung oleh sistem pembayaran yang aman, lancar, dan andal. Dari sisi nilai besar, transaksi BI-RTGS pada November 2024 meningkat 9,82% (yoy) dengan nominal transaksi sebesar Rp14.969,37 trilion. Dari sisi ritel, volume transaksi BI-FAST pada November 2024 tumbuh 69,90% (yoy) mencapai 338,61 juta transaksi. Akselerasi pembayaran digital berlanjut. Volume transaksi digital banking pada bulan yang sama tercatat 2,04 miliar transaksi atau tumbuh sebesar 40,1% (yoy), sementara volume transaksi Uang Elektronik (UE) tumbuh 33,4% (yoy) mencapai 1,44 miliar transaksi.

Volume transaksi QRIS terus tumbuh pesat sebesar 186% (yoy) mencapai 689,07 juta transaksi, dengan jumlah pengguna dan merchant masing-masing mencapai 55,02 juta dan 35,1 juta pada November 2024. Sementara itu, volume transaksi pembayaran menggunakan kartu ATM/D pada November 2024 turun 10,9% menjadi 562,75 juta transaksi. Volume transaksi kartu kredit pada bulan yang sama tumbuh 21,1% (yoy) mencapai 41,15 juta transaksi. Dari sisi pengelolaan uang Rupiah, Uang Kartal Yang Diedarkan (UYD) tumbuh 11,9% (yoy) menjadi Rp1.105,8 trilion secara nominal pada akhir November 2024.

Government spending also increased toward the end of the year, further driving economic activity. However, non-oil and gas exports were projected to slow down due to weak global demand. Sector-wise, growth was primarily supported by the Manufacturing Industry, Construction, and Wholesale and Retail Trade Sectors. Overall, Indonesia's economic growth in 2024 was estimated to be within the range of 4.7%—5.5%, with expectations of further growth in 2025 to 4.8%—5.6%.

### Industry Segment Overview

Bank Indonesia reported that the financial system remained resilient, including within the banking industry. Banking liquidity was well-maintained, as reflected in the Liquid Assets to Third Party Funds (AL/TPF) ratio, which stood at 25.57% in November 2024. Meanwhile, the banking Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) was recorded at 27.02% in October 2024, indicating strong capacity to absorb risks and support credit growth. The Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio remained low, standing at 2.20% (gross) and 0.77% (net) in October 2024.

The stability of banking capital and liquidity was further strengthened by sound profitability and risk management, alongside maintained corporate repayment capacity and profitability, as indicated by the results of the latest banking stress test. Moving forward, Bank Indonesia will continue to enhance policy coordination with KSSK to maintain financial system stability and mitigate risks that could pose threats.

The digital economy and financial transactions continued to expand throughout November 2024, supported by a secure, efficient, and reliable payment system. In terms of large-value transactions, BI-RTGS transactions in November 2024 grew by 9.82% yoy, reaching a transaction value of IDR14,969.37 trillion. On the retail side, BI-FAST transactions surged by 69.90% yoy, totaling 338.61 million transactions. The adoption of digital payments also accelerated significantly. In November 2024, the volume of digital banking transactions reached 2.04 billion transactions, reflecting 40.1% yoy growth. Likewise, Electronic Money (EU) transactions rose by 33.4% yoy to 1.44 billion transactions.

QRIS transactions recorded remarkable growth, increasing by 186% yoy to 689.07 million transactions, with the number of users and merchants reaching 55.02 million and 35.1 million, respectively, in November 2024. Conversely, ATM/Debit card transactions declined by 10.9%, totaling 562.75 million transactions. Meanwhile, credit card transactions grew by 21.1% yoy to 41.15 million transactions. In terms of the management of Rupiah currency, the total Circulating Currency (UYD) grew by 11.9% yoy, reaching IDR1,105.8 trillion at the end of November 2024.
