# Informasi Kelangsungan Usaha Business Continuity Information

## Hal-Hal yang Berpotensi Berpengaruh Signifikan terhadap Kelangsungan Usaha

Kondisi ekonomi dan industri yang masih fluktuatif di tahun 2024 berpotensi mempengaruhi kinerja Bank. Berlanjutnya kebijakan tingkat suku bunga acuan yang tinggi dari Bank Indonesia (BI) untuk menjaga tingkat inflasi nasional menjadi salah satu faktor yang dapat menekan kinerja Bank. Persaingan industri yang ketat juga mengharuskan Bank untuk terus beradaptasi dan berinovasi secara cepat agar dapat menjaga kelangsungan usaha Bank.

Berbekal fondasi bisnis yang solid dan ekosistem CT Corpora yang kuat, Bank optimis bahwa seluruh keunggulan yang dimiliki dapat mengoptimalkan kinerjanya dalam mengatasi berbagai tantangan ke depan. Pengembangan produk dan layanan, serta perluasan jangkauan melalui digitalisasi yang dimiliki diyakini mampu mengakselerasi kinerja Bank agar dapat meningkatkan kinerja bisnis dan menghadirkan solusi perbankan bagi masyarakat.

## Assessment Manajemen atas Hal-hal yang Berpengaruh Signifikan terhadap Kelangsungan Usaha

Bank telah menyusun dan menerapkan manajemen risiko secara cermat, mulai dari penetapan sejumlah risiko utama hingga upaya mitigasinya. Selain itu, manajemen Bank juga melakukan evaluasi dan assessment terkait hal-hal yang berpengaruh signifikan terhadap kelangsungan usaha Bank. Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi turut berperan dalam berbagai upaya untuk mengelola potensi risiko yang berpengaruh signifikan terhadap kelangsungan usaha Bank. Assessment manajemen dilakukan dengan memperhatikan berbagai faktor, seperti analisis kekuatan kondisi keuangan Bank maupun operasional. Berdasarkan hasil assessment tersebut, manajemen tidak mendapati adanya hal-hal yang berpengaruh signifikan terhadap kelangsungan usaha Allo Bank.

## Matters That Have the Potential to Significantly Impact Business Continuity

Ongoing fluctuations in economic and industrial conditions that continued throughout 2024 had the potential to affect the Bank's performance. Bank Indonesia's (BI) High benchmark interest rate policy continued to maintain the nation's inflation rate and served as one of the factors that could have led to a suppression of the Bank's performance. Tight competition within the industry meant that the Bank had to continue to be quick in adapting and innovating in order to maintain its own business continuity.

Thanks to its solid business foundations and its robust CT Corpora ecosystem, the Bank is optimistic that it can optimize its performance in overcoming any challenges ahead. The Bank believes that its efforts toward product and service development, as well as its efforts to expand its reach through digitalization, can help to accelerate its business performance and provide the community with banking solutions.

## Management's Assessment of Matters that Significantly Influence Business Continuity

The Bank has made careful efforts to design and implement proper risk management. Its actions towards this end began with the Bank determining its primary risks to know what it needs to mitigate. The Bank's management has also conducted evaluations and assessments for all matters that could have significantly influenced its business continuity. The Board of Commissioners and Directors also play a role in various efforts designed to manage potential risks that could have significantly influenced the Bank's business continuity. The management's assessments were carried out by taking various factors into consideration. This was done through analysis of the Bank's financial strength and operational conditions. Based on the results of its assessment, the Bank's management did not find any matters that could have significantly influenced Allo Bank's business continuity.
