Jumlah pesangan pemutusan kontrak kerja kepada manajemen kunci pada 31 Desember 2024 dan 2023 masing-masing sebesar Rp821.327.249.877 dan nihil.

Berdasarkan Akta Perpanjangan Sewa Menyewa No. 1 tanggal 1 Oktober 2018 yang dibuat di hadapan notaris Sheila Ersan Sugito, SH., MKn., notaris di Jakarta, antara PT Asean Motor International dengan Bank, kedua belah pihak telah menyetujui untuk melakukan perpanjangan perjanjian sewa menyewa atas gedung yaitu ruangan di dalam gedung yang terletak di lantai 1 (satu), lantai 2 (dua) dan lantai 3 (tiga) yang beralamat di Jl. Kyai Haji Samanhudi No.10-10 A, Jakarta Pusat. Biaya sewa untuk perpanjangan sewa tersebut sebesar Rp243.702.500 per bulan dan biaya service charge/maintenance sebesar Rp103.526.742 per bulan. Perjanjian ini berlangsung selama 5 tahun, terhitung mulai tanggal 19 Desember 2017 sampai dengan 19 Desember 2022.

The amount of the termination benefits for the key management as at December 31, 2024 and 2023 amounted to Rp821.327.249.877 and nil, respectively.

Based on the Deed of Lease Extension No.1 dated October 1, 2018 of Sheila Ersan Sugito, SH., MKn., notary in Jakarta, between PT Asean Motor International and the Bank both parties have agreed to extend lease agreement on the building that is the room in the building located on the 1st floor, 2nd floor and 3rd floor which are located at Jl. Kyai Haji Samanhudi No. 10-10 A, Central Jakarta. The rent expense for the lease extension amounting to Rp243,702,500 for each month and the service charge/maintenance expense amounting to Rp103,526,742 for each month. This agreement lasts for 5 years, starting on December 19, 2017 until December 19, 2022.
