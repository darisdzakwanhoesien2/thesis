# Keanggotaan dalam Asosiasi
## Membership in Associations

Per 31 Desember 2024, Bank tergabung sebagai anggota di sejumlah asosiasi, antara lain:

1. Perhimpunan Bank Swasta Nasional (Perbanas)
2. Asosiasi Sistem Pembayaran Indonesia (ASPI)
3. Forum Komunikasi Direktur Kepatuhan Perbankan (FKDKP)
4. Lembaga Alternatif Penyelesaian Sengketa Sektor Jasa Keuangan (LAPS SJK)

As of 31 December 2024, the Bank is a member of a number of associations, including:

1. National Private Banks Association (Perbanas)
2. Indonesian Payment System Association (ASPI)
3. Communication Forum for Director of Banking Compliance (FKDKP)
4. Alternative Agencies for Dispute Resolution in Financial Services (LAPS SJK)

# Kronologis Penerbitan dan/atau Pencatatan Saham
## Chronology of Issuance and/or Share Listing

1. Rencana Go Public Perseroan bermula dari tahun 2014 dan sejak saat itu, Perseroan mulai melakukan seleksi untuk pemilihan lembaga dan profesi penunjang, termasuk melakukan perubahan Anggaran Dasar.
2. Due Diligence Meeting &amp; Public Expose berlangsung pada tanggal 13 Juli 2015 di Ballroom ASEAN Tower Lantai 9, Pasar Baru - Jakarta Pusat.
3. Pernyataan efektif oleh Pengawas Pasar Modal Otoritas Jasa Keuangan diperoleh tanggal 31 Juli 2015. Sedangkan masa penawaran umum berlangsung pada tanggal 4-6 Agustus 2015.
4. Dalam penawaran penjatahan pasti (fixed allotment) dibatasi sampai dengan jumlah sebesar 98% dari jumlah saham yang ditawarkan dan sisanya sebanyak-banyaknya 2% akan dilakukan penjatahan terpusat (pooling).
5. Pencatatan efektif saham Allo Bank dengan kode saham BBHI di BEI dilaksanakan pada tanggal 12 Agustus 2015, dengan melakukan penawaran saham kepada masyarakat sebanyak 800.000.000 saham dengan harga Rp125 per lembar saham.

1. The Company's plan to go public commenced in 2014 and has since started the selection of supporting institutions and professions, including the amendment of the Articles of Association.
2. Due Diligence Meeting &amp; Public Expose took place on July 13, 2015 at the Ballroom of ASEAN Tower 9th Floor, Pasar Baru - Central Jakarta.
3. An effective statement by the Capital Market Supervisor of the Financial Services Authority was obtained on July 31, 2015. Meanwhile, the public offering period lasted from August 4-6, 2015.
4. Fixed allotment bidding was limited to 98% of the total shares offered and the remaining 2% will be done by pooling.
5. The effective listing of Allo Bank's shares with BBHI's ticker code on the IDX was carried out on August 12, 2015, by offering the public 800,000,000 shares with a price of Rp125 per share.
