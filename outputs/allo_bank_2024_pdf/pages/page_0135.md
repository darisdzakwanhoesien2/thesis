# Tinjauan Perekonomian dan Industri

Economic and Industrial Overview

## Tinjauan Perekonomian

Sebagaimana dikutip dari Tinjauan Kebijakan Moneter Desember 2024 yang dirilis Bank Indonesia, mengungkapkan bahwa Ketidakpastian pasar keuangan global semakin meningkat disertai dengan risiko perlambatan pertumbuhan ekonomi dunia. Rencana kebijakan perdagangan di Amerika Serikat (AS) melalui kenaikan tarif impor, komoditas, dan cakupan negara yang lebih luas telah menyebabkan risiko peningkatan fragmentasi perdagangan dunia.

Inflasi dunia meningkat dibandingkan prakiraan sebelumnya dipengaruhi oleh gangguan rantai suplai. Di AS, penurunan Fed Funds Rate (FFR) diprakirakan lebih lambat akibat inflasi yang lebih tinggi tersebut. Sementara itu, kebijakan fiskal AS yang lebih ekspansif mendorong yield US Treasury tetap tinggi, baik pada tenor jangka pendek maupun jangka panjang. Penguatan mata uang dolar AS secara luas terus berlanjut disertai berbaliknya preferensi investor global dengan memindahkan alokasi portfolionya kembali ke AS. Hal ini meningkatkan tekanan pelemahan berbagai mata uang dunia dan menahan aliran masuk modal asing ke negara berkembang. Perkembangan ekonomi global yang diikuti dengan tetap tingginya ketidakpastian pasar keuangan global tersebut memerlukan respons kebijakan yang kuat untuk memitigasi dampak negatifnya terhadap perekonomian di negara-negara berkembang, termasuk Indonesia.

Namun demikian, Pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tetap terjaga didukung oleh permintaan domestik. Investasi diprakirakan tumbuh positif pada triwulan IV 2024 ditopang oleh penyelesaian berbagai Proyek Strategis Nasional (PSN) dan investasi swasta didukung insentif dari Pemerintah. Konsumsi rumah tangga diprakirakan tetap tumbuh didorong oleh keyakinan konsumen yang terjaga serta dampak positif pelaksanaan Pilkada di berbagai daerah. ## Economic Overview

Uncertainty within global financial markets has been on an uptick, as is evidenced by data from Bank Indonesia's December 2024 Monetary Policy Review. Accompanying this was the risk of slowing global economic growth. The United States' trade policy plan of increasing import tariffs, commodities, and wider country coverage has led to the risk of increased fragmentation of world trade.

Global inflation has meanwhile gone up compared to previous estimates due to supply chain disruptions. In the United States (US), the Fed Funds Rate (FFR) reduction was predicted to be slower due to higher inflation. A more expansive US fiscal policy has pushed US Treasury yields to remain high for both short-term and long-term tenors. The strengthening of the US dollar currency has broadly continued while accompanied by a reversal in global investor preferences that saw them moving their portfolio allocations back to the US. This has increased pressure on the weakening of various world currencies and restrained foreign capital inflows to developing countries. Global economic developments, followed by continued high uncertainty in global financial markets, require a strong policy response to mitigate their negative impact on the economies of developing countries, including Indonesia.

Nevertheless, Indonesia's economic growth has continued to remain stable thanks to the support of domestic demand. Investment grew positively in the fourth quarter of 2024 thanks to the completion of various National Strategic Projects and private investment supported by incentives from the Government. Household consumption continued to grow thanks to maintained consumer confidence and the positive impact of regional elections in various regions.
