Risiko Likuiditas

Risiko likuiditas dapat muncul sebagai akibat dari mismatch antara struktur aset dan liabilitas Bank, dimana Bank berisiko untuk tidak mampu memenuhi kewajiban yang jatuh tempo dari sumber pendanaan arus kas dan/atau dari aset likuid berkualitas tinggi yang dapat dilikuidasi segera dan/atau diagunkan tanpa mengganggu aktivitas keuangan Bank. Dalam hal ini, Bank menjaga rasio-rasio penilaian risiko likuiditas, nilai outstanding asset likuid Bank dan rasio-rasio pendukung seperti, Aset Likuid Primer dan Aset Likuid Sekunder terhadap Total Aset. Rasio LCR dan NSFR dipertahankan di atas batas minimum regulator dan penggunaan dana selalu dilakukan secara optimal dengan tetap memperhatikan ketentuan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan/Bank Indonesia tentang prinsip kehatian serta memiliki contingency funding plan yang memadai sebagai mitigasi potensi risiko likuiditas yang mungkin timbul.

4. Internal Control System

The Bank controls the limitation of losses that arise through its process of determining transaction limits, specifically, dealer transaction limits and cut loss limits. This process is carried out so that treasury activities can be monitored and evaluated. The process of supervising the development of treasury transaction limit activities to ensure that treasury has complied with the established transaction limits (especially for cut-loss limits) is considered a priority.

Independent reviews is carried out by the Internal Audit Unit function. These reviews make use of a methodology and frequency that are both in accordance with established rules. Results of these reviews are reported to the Board of Commissioners and Directors.

Liquidity Risk

Liquidity risk may arise as a result of a mismatch between the Bank's asset and liability structure. The Bank may be at risk of not being able to meet maturing obligations from cash flow funding sources and/or from high-quality liquid assets that can be liquidated immediately and/or collateralized without disrupting the Bank's financial activities. If this happens, the Bank maintains liquidity risk assessment ratios and its outstanding liquid asset value and supporting ratios such as Primary Liquid Assets and Secondary Liquid Assets to Total Assets. The LCR and NSFR ratios are maintained above regulatory minimum limits, while the use of funds is always carried out optimally while also still taking into consideration any provisions of the Financial Services Authority/ Bank Indonesia when it comes to the principle of prudence. The Bank also ensures that it has an adequate contingency funding plan as a way of mitigating any potential liquidity risks that may arise.
