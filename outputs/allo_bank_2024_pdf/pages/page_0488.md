# Hubungan dengan Pemangku Kepentingan [OJK E.4]

Komunikasi dan hubungan yang harmonis antara Allo Bank dengan segenap pemangku kepentingan, baik pemangku internal maupun eksternal, merupakan pondasi yang kokoh untuk mewujudkan kinerja terbaik. Merujuk pada Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (POJK) No. 17 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola Bagi Bank Umum (POJK Tata Kelola), pemangku kepentingan adalah seluruh pihak yang memiliki kepentingan secara langsung atau tidak langsung terhadap kegiatan usaha Bank. Berdasarkan pengertian tersebut, Allo Bank merumuskan pemangku kepentingan sebagai kelompok atau individu yang terpengaruh oleh kegiatan, produk dan jasa yang ditawarkan Bank. Disisi lain, keberadaan mereka juga mempengaruhi Allo Bank dalam mewujudkan keberhasilan penerapan strategi dan pencapaian tujuan.

Untuk mewujudkan hubungan yang harmonis, Allo Bank melibatkan pemangku kepentingan sesuai dengan kepentingan masing-masing. Proses pelibatan pemangku kepentingan mencakup upaya Bank untuk memenuhi harapan dari setiap pemangku kepentingan dengan menggunakan sumber daya yang dimiliki, dengan cara yang tepat, dapat dipertanggungjawabkan, serta tidak bertentangan dengan ketentuan yang diatur dalam peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

Allo Bank secara berkesinambungan melibatkan para pemangku kepentingan sejalan dengan komitmen untuk meningkatkan transparansi, akuntabilitas, serta mendapatkan masukan berharga untuk perbaikan kinerja dan pengembangan produk/layanan yang sesuai kebutuhan nasabah. Lebih dari itu, pelibatan pemangku kepentingan juga penting dalam mendukung implementasi keuangan berkelanjutan dan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik di Bank.

In executing its risk management strategy, the Bank conducts risk profile assessments, covering both inherent risk evaluations and risk management quality assessments. Additionally, the Bank continuously adapts its assessment indicators in response to new regulations, employing the Risk-Based Bank Rating (RBBR) concept.

To further strengthen risk management, Allo Bank is committed to improving composite rating assessments through continuous evaluation and refinement of the four primary risks it faces: credit risk, market risk, liquidity risk, operational risk. By involving all elements within the Bank, the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, who are responsible for ensuring proper risk management implementation, have affirmed that the Bank's risk management framework is adequate given the Bank's complexity and risk profile. Furthermore, they have demonstrated a comprehensive understanding of the types and levels of risks inherent in the Bank's business operations.

# Relations with Stakeholders [OJK E.4]

Harmonious communication and strong relationships between Allo Bank and all stakeholders, both internal and external, serve as a solid foundation for achieving optimal performance. Referring to Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) No. 17 of 2023 concerning the Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank (POJK Governance), stakeholders are defined as all parties who have a direct or indirect interest in the Bank's business activities. Based on this understanding, Allo Bank identifies stakeholders as groups or individuals affected by the Bank's activities, products, and services. At the same time, these stakeholders also play a role in influencing the Bank's ability to successfully implement its strategies and achieve its goals.

To foster harmonious relationships, Allo Bank engages stakeholders based on their respective interests. The stakeholder engagement process involves the Bank's efforts to meet stakeholder expectations by utilizing its resources appropriately and accountably, while ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Allo Bank consistently engages with stakeholders in alignment with its commitment to enhancing transparency and accountability while gaining valuable insights for improving performance and developing products and services that meet customer needs. Furthermore, stakeholder engagement plays a crucial role in supporting the implementation of sustainable finance and good corporate governance at the Bank.
