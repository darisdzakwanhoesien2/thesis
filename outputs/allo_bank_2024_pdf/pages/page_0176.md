Secara umum peringkat komposit Profil Risiko PT Allo Bank Indonesia, Tbk. untuk selanjutnya disebut "Bank" - pada periode Desember 2024 berada pada peringkat 2. Penilaian tersebut sama dengan periode September 2024 yaitu peringkat 2. Sampai posisi Desember 2024, Bank melakukan cukup banyak upaya berkelanjutan dari berbagai aspek untuk memposisikan Profil Risiko secara komposit tetap berada pada peringkat 2.

Dengan pendekatan self assessment tersebut, hasil perhitungan Risiko Inheren, Kualitas Penerapan Manajemen Risiko dan Peringkat Risiko Inheren menunjukkan komitmen pengelolaan risiko Bank. Pada posisi Desember 2024 dari sisi pendapatan, Bank telah meningkatkan pendapatan organiknya dari proses kredit dan juga pendapatan Bank dari aset-aset produktif lain terutama bisnis ritelnya. Kecukupan modal Bank per Desember 2024 telah sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku dan sejalan dengan strategi Bank.

Kualitas Penerapan Manajemen Risiko (KPMR) seluruhnya berada pada peringkat "Satisfactory", dimana Bank secara terus-menerus memperbaiki kelemahan yang ada dan akan terus melakukan perbaikan untuk dapat menjaga peringkat tersebut.

# Risiko Kredit

Risiko Kredit adalah risiko yang timbul sebagai akibat kegagalan counterparty memenuhi liabilitasnya. Bank melakukan pengelolaan risiko kredit dengan menyusun strategi dan kerangka kerja serta menjalankan upaya mitigasi risiko atas aspek bisnis perkreditan mikro, retail, komersial, maupun konsumsi, penempatan antar-bank serta pembelian surat berharga.

# 1. Tata Kelola Risiko

Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi memiliki pemahaman yang baik untuk melakukan pengawasan secara aktif terhadap manajemen perkreditan. Tugas dan wewenang Direksi dioptimalkan melalui tanggung jawab atas penyusunan rencana dan kebijakan perkreditan, memastikan kepatuhan Bank terhadap ketentuan undang-undang dan peraturan yang berlaku, serta melakukan pelaporan secara berkala kepada Dewan Komisaris mengenai pelaksanaan pemberian kredit, memitigasi setiap potensi risiko kredit, pengawasan terhadap penyimpangan dalam pemberian kredit, dan upaya terus meningkatkan kualitas kredit Bank.

Dalam melakukan proses pemberian kredit, Bank mengacu kepada aturan regulator dan prinsip kehati-hatian dalam menilai kualitas debitur sebelum pemberian kredit dilakukan. Proses penilaian tersebut dilakukan dengan melihat faktor prospek usaha debitur, cashflow debitur, dan kemampuan membayar debitur.

In general, the composite rating of PT Allo Bank Indonesia, Tbk., hereinafter referred to as the "Bank," for its Risk Profile as of December 2024 remains at Rank 2. This assessment is consistent with the rating from September 2024, which was also Rank 2. As of December 2024, the Bank has made continuous efforts across various aspects to maintain its composite Risk Profile at Rank 2.

Using a self-assessment approach, the results of the calculation of Inherent Risk, Quality of Risk Management Implementation, and Inherent Risk Rating reflect the Bank's strong commitment to risk management. In December 2024, in terms of income, the Bank increased its organic income from the credit process, as well as its earnings from other productive assets, particularly in the retail business segment. The Bank's capital adequacy as of December 2024 complies with applicable regulations and aligns with its strategic objectives.

The Quality of Risk Management Implementation (KPMR) is entirely rated as "Satisfactory," with the Bank continuously addressing existing weaknesses and committing to further improvements to maintain this rating.

# Credit Risk

Credit Risk is any risk that arises as a result of a counterparty's failure to fulfill its obligations. The Bank manages credit risk by developing various strategies and frameworks and by implementing risk mitigation efforts for aspects of its micro, retail, commercial, and consumer credit business, as well as for its inter-bank placements and purchases of securities.

# 1. Risk Governance

The Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors both understand the importance of actively supervising credit management. The Board of Directors' duties and authorities towards this end are optimized through its efforts in preparing credit plans and policies, through ensuring the Bank's compliance with applicable laws and regulations, and through reporting periodically to the Board of Commissioners on any matter related to the implementation of credit provisions. The board also works to mitigate any potential credit risk, supervises deviations in credit provision, and makes efforts to continuously improve the Bank's credit quality.

In carrying out its credit granting process, the Bank uses various regulations and the principle of prudence as references in its efforts towards assessing the quality of debtors before granting them credit. Its assessment process is carried out by looking at the debtor's business prospects, debtor cash flow, and their ability to pay.
