# Pandangan Dewan Komisaris Terhadap Tata Kelola Perusahaan

Dewan Komisaris berkomitmen untuk mendukung keberlanjutan bisnis Bank dengan mengawasi kinerja Direksi dalam mengelola Bank, salah satunya melalui penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik. Secara umum, Bank senantiasa berupaya menerapkan prinsip-prinsip Good Corporate Governance (transparency, accountability, responsibility, independency, and fairness) dalam menjalankan kegiatan usahanya melalui aspek-aspek governance structure dan governance process yang mendukung governance outcome secara memadai terhadap TI faktor penilaian pelaksanaan GCG sesuai dengan POJK No. 17/POJK.03/2023 mengenai Penerapan Tata kelola bagi Bank Umum dan Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 13/SEOJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum

Terkait aspek governance structure, Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa struktur organisasi Bank sudah cukup memadai untuk mendukung keberlanjutan bisnis secara prudent yang diimbangi dengan penerapan manajemen risiko. Komposisi susunan pengurus Bank telah memenuhi ketentuan. Bank memiliki Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, serta Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi di bawah Dewan Komisaris, serta pembentukan berbagai Komite di bawah Direksi sesuai ketentuan.

Seluruh karyawan telah melakukan penandatanganan Kode Etik yang dimonitor secara berkala untuk dapat memastikan bahwa mereka telah memahami dan mengimplementasikan Kode Etik dengan baik. Terkait sumber daya manusia, Bank telah menyusun program pengembangan dan pelatihan karyawan dengan mempertimbangkan kebutuhan pelatihan bagi pegawai di seluruh jenjang organisasi dan unit kerja, seperti menerapkan program Induction Training bagi seluruh karyawan baru.

Terkait aspek governance process, Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa Direksi telah menerapkan proses-proses yang berjalan secara independen dan efektif, khususnya terkait manajemen risiko dan pengendalian internal agar risiko-risiko yang mungkin timbul dapat dikelola dengan baik.

# Board of Commissioners' Views on Corporate Governance

The Board of Commissioners is committed to supporting the Bank's long-term sustainability by overseeing the Board of Directors' performance in managing the Bank, particularly through the implementation of Good Corporate Governance. In general, the Bank continuously strives to uphold the principles of Good Corporate Governance (transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness) in its business operations. These principles are embedded in the governance structure and governance processes, which adequately support governance outcomes in alignment with the 11 GCG implementation assessment factors, as outlined in POJK No. 17/POJK.03/2023 concerning the Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank and the Financial Services Authority Circular Letter No. 13/SEOJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank.

Regarding governance structure, the Board of Commissioners considers the Bank's organizational structure to be well-designed, ensuring prudent business sustainability while maintaining effective risk management. The Bank's management composition meets all regulatory requirements. The Bank has established an Audit Committee, a Risk Oversight Committee, and a Remuneration and Nomination Committee under the Board of Commissioners, along with various committees under the Board of Directors, all in accordance with applicable regulations.

All employees have signed the Code of Ethics, which is periodically monitored to ensure proper understanding and implementation. In terms of human resource development, the Bank has designed employee training and development programs, considering the training needs at all organizational levels and work units. This includes the Induction Training program, which is mandatory for all new employees.

Regarding governance processes, the Board of Commissioners assesses that the Board of Directors has effectively implemented independent and structured processes, particularly in risk management and internal control, ensuring that potential risks are properly mitigated.
