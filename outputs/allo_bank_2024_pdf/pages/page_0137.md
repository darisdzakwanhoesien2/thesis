# Rencana dan Kebijakan Strategis Perusahaan

Company Strategic Plan and Policy

Semakin tingginya tingkat kesadaran masyarakat untuk menyeimbangkan gaya hidup dan kesejahteraan finansial jangka panjang menjadi katalis positif bagi perkembangan bisnis perbankan. Tingginya generasi muda yang berada di usia produktif dan memiliki kesadaran finansial menjadi salah satu faktor yang mendorong pertumbuhan nasabah.

Melihat kondisi ini, Allo Bank selaku bank umum berbasis digital senantiasa memperkuat fondasi bisnis yang dimiliki dan terus berinovasi agar dapat mengoptimalkan momentum pertumbuhan. Dengan didukung kolaborasi ekosistem bisnis yang kuat, pengembangan produk-produk inovatif, serta keamanan yang kuat, Bank berupaya untuk dapat terus hadir menjadi everyday bank yang dapat menjawab segala kebutuhan nasabah.

Di samping itu, Bank telah menetapkan kebijakan strategis dalam mengoptimalkan pertumbuhan bisnis, diantaranya dengan menetapkan langkah-langkah strategis sebagai berikut:

1. Penetrasi pasar berdasarkan model bisnis Hibrida (Hybrid Business Model) dalam menawarkan produk dan layanan segmen Retail dan Wholesale secara terintegrasi untuk menumbuhkan bisnis Bank melalui aktivitas Acquisition, Engagement dan Monetization.
2. Pengembangan layanan digital untuk mencapai portofolio aset dan liabilitas yang berimbang dan berkualitas antara segmen Retail dan Wholesale.
3. Penguatan kapabilitas Ecosystem Banking dalam rangka kolaborasi yang intensif dengan mitra strategis untuk meningkatkan nilai layanan finansial dan daya saing Bank.
4. Optimalisasi kapabilitas data analytics dan Artificial Intelligence (AI) untuk menghasilkan Customer Insight yang efektif dan manajemen risiko yang efisien dalam memberikan layanan finansial yang kontekstual kepada nasabah.
5. Penguatan sistem manajemen layanan yang didukung oleh teknologi sesuai standar best practice IT Service Management (ITSM).
6. Pengembangan talenta DIGITAL untuk mendukung model bisnis Hibrida.
7. Penguatan Good Corporate Governance yang terintegrasi dan berkelanjutan pada proses bisnis dan operasional sebagai komitmen Bank untuk mendukung prinsip-prinsip Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG).

Increased levels of public awareness for balancing lifestyles and long-term financial well-being serves as a positive catalyst for the development of banking as a business. The high number of young people who are of productive age and have financial awareness is one of the factors that has driven customer growth.

Allo Bank, as a digital-based conventional bank, sees these conditions and has always strengthened the foundation of its business and continues to innovate in order to optimize growth momentum. With the support of its strong business ecosystem collaboration, its development of innovative products, and its strong security, the Bank is working to continue to be present as an everyday bank that can answer all customer needs.

In addition, the Bank has also established a strategic policy for optimizing business growth, including by establishing the following strategic steps:

1. Market penetration based on a Hybrid Business Model in offering integrated Retail and Wholesale segment products and services to grow the Bank's business through Acquisition, Engagement and Monetization activities.
2. Development of digital services to achieve a balanced and quality assets and liabilities portfolio between the Retail and Wholesale segments.
3. Strengthening Ecosystem Banking capabilities in the context of intensive collaboration with strategic partners to increase the value of financial services and the Bank's competitiveness.
4. Optimizing data analytics and Artificial Intelligence (AI) capabilities to produce effective Customer Insight and efficient risk management in providing contextual financial services to customers.
5. Strengthening the service management system supported by technology according to IT Service Management (ITSM) best practice standards.
6. Developing DIGITAL talent to support a Hybrid business model.
7. Strengthening integrated and sustainable Good Corporate Governance in business and operational processes as part of the Bank's commitment to supporting the principles of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG).
