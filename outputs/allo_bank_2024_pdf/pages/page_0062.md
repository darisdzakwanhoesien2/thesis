# 2024

- Mei: Allo Bank menyelesaikan pembangunan Pusat Data Bersertifikat TIA Rated-3 baru sebagai tulang punggung infrastruktur TI untuk mendukung aspirasi pertumbuhan Bank.
- Juni: Bank selenggarakan ALLO BANK FESTIVAL 2024 pada 21-22 Juni 2024 dengan mengusung tema Intimate Concert Experience dengan bintang tamu internasional WAY V di ISTORA SENAYAN JAKARTA bersama berbagai musisi nasional Indonesia.

- November: Allo Bank mencatatkan 11 juta nasabah yang tersebar di seluruh Indonesia.
- May: Allo Bank completed the construction of a new TIA Rated-3 Certified Data Center as the backbone of its IT infrastructure to support the Bank's growth aspirations.
- June: The Bank held ALLO BANK FESTIVAL 2024 on 21-22 June 2024 with the theme Intimate Concert Experience with international guest star WAY V at ISTORA SENAYAN JAKARTA along with various Indonesian national musicians.
- September: Allo Bank achieved the COBIT 2019 assessment with an IT Maturity Level of 4.21 on September 10, 2024.
- November: Allo Bank reached a remarkable milestone with 11 million customers across Indonesia.
