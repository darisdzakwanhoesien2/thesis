# Transparansi Informasi terkait Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Transparency of Information Regarding the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors

Assessment Penerapan GCG untuk Aspek Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi selaku organ utama Bank memiliki peran penting bagi pertumbuhan bisnis Bank ke depan. Bank secara berkala melakukan assessment bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dengan tujuan untuk memberikan evaluasi dan rekomendasi atas kinerja kedua organ, serta melakukan tindak lanjut atas rekomendasi yang muncul dari proses assessment tersebut.

Pada tahun 2024, self assessment penerapan GCG Bank meraih nilai komposit sebesar 2 atau memperoleh predikat "BAIK". Berdasarkan penilaian tersebut, aspek Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi masing-masing dinilai baik.

Assessment of GCG Implementation for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors

As the main organs in the Bank's structure, the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors play a central role in Allo Bank's business growth. For this reason, the Bank consistently conducts evaluations in the form of assessments of these two organs. These assessments aim to evaluate their performance, provide recommendations, and ensure the implementation of follow-up actions based on the recommendations that arise from the assessment process.

In 2024, the Bank's GCG implementation self-assessment achieved a composite score of 2, earning the rating "GOOD". Based on this assessment, both the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors were classified as performing well.
