## Aspek Keberlanjutan dalam Laporan

Laporan keberlanjutan ini menyajikan pembahasan aspek-aspek yang material, yaitu aspek-aspek yang penting dan relevan serta memiliki dampak yang signifikan bagi Bank maupun para pemangku kepentingan selama tahun 2024. Penentuan aspek material merujuk pada Lampiran II POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017 dimana Bank termasuk dalam kategori perusahaan yang proses bisnisnya tidak berkaitan langsung dengan lingkungan hidup.

## Lingkup dan Batasan Pelaporan

Laporan keberlanjutan ini mencakup aspek keberlanjutan Kantor Pusat dan dua kantor cabang Allo Bank di Indonesia.

## Sustainability Aspects in this Report

This sustainability report presents a discussion of various material aspects, specifically, aspects that are important, relevant and have had a significant impact on the Bank and its stakeholders during 2024. The process of determining these material aspects used Attachment II of POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017 as a reference. In it, the Bank was included within the category of companies whose business processes were not directly related to the environment.

## Reporting Scope and Limitations

This sustainability report covers the sustainability aspects of Allo Bank's Head Office and its two branch offices in Indonesia.
