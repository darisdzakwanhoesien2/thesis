# Tindak Lanjut atas Hasil Keputusan Hingga Akhir Tahun 2024:

Bank telah menetapkan penggunaan laba bersih sesuai dengan hasil keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan dalam pembukuan keuangan Bank.

Determining the entire Net Profit of the Company for the 2023 Fiscal year, namely IDR IDR444,566,134,304 (four hundred and forty four billion five hundred and sixty six million one hundred and thirty four thousand three hundred and four rupiah) is used as follows:

1. An amount of IDR6,134,304 (six million one hundred thirty four thousand three hundred and four rupiah) is set aside as reserve funds to fulfill the provisions of Article 70 of the Company Law; And
2. The remaining IDR444,560,000,000 (four hundred and forty four billion five hundred and sixty million rupiah) will be recorded as retained earnings.
