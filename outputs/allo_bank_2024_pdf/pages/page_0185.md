3. Proses Manajemen Risiko

Bank telah melaksanakan evaluasi secara berkala atas realisasi RBB melalui Rapat Koordinasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, sebagaimana dituangkan dalam Laporan Realisasi Rencana Bisnis Bank yang disampaikan ke OJK setiap triwulanan.

Secara umum, Bank berupaya meningkatkan kualitas SDM sesuai dengan nilai perusahaan sehingga sejalan dengan strategi SDM yang mengacu pada strategi jangka panjang perusahaan. Bank tetap mengedepankan pelaksanaan GCG dalam proses pengelolaan risiko strategis dengan tetap memantau pencapaian Bank terhadap RBB. SIM berjalan dengan baik, termasuk laporan risiko strategik kepada Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Perekrutan karyawan dilakukan untuk menunjang kebutuhan bisnis, pendidikan dan pelatihan, serta evaluasi karyawan secara regular akan ditingkatkan untuk memastikan kesesuaian kapabilitas SDM.

4. Sistem Pengendalian Risiko

Dalam pelaksanaan sistem pengendalian risiko strategik, telah dilakukan pemantauan pencapaian realisasi dibandingkan dengan rencana yang telah ditetapkan dan memastikan risiko yang diambil masih dalam batas toleransi. Sistem pengendalian internal tersebut melibatkan seluruh manajemen dan karyawan Bank yang mencakup penetapan kebijakan dan prosedur pengendalian, serta evaluasi untuk memastikan bahwa kebijakan dan prosedur tersebut sesuai arahan Direksi telah dipatuhi dan dilaksanakan secara efektif dan konsisten.

Pengendalian internal merupakan kegiatan yang tidak terpisahkan dari setiap fungsi atau kegiatan Bank sehari-hari dan dapat membantu Direksi dan Komisaris dalam mengelola dan mengendalikan risiko yang dapat mempengaruhi kinerja atau mengakibatkan kerugian Bank.

3. Risk Management Process

The Bank has carried out periodic evaluations of its Bank Business Plan realization through its Coordination Meetings of the Board of Commissioners and Directors, as it has stated in its Bank Business Plan Realization Report submitted quarterly to the Financial Services Authority (OJK).

In general, the Bank strives to improve the quality of human resources in accordance with its company values so that it is in line with its own long-term strategy. The Bank continues to prioritize the implementation of GCG in its strategic risk management process by continuing to monitor its own achievement of the Bank Business Plan. The Bank's SIM runs well and includes strategic risk reports to the Board of Commissioners and Directors. Employee recruitment is carried out to support business needs. Education and training activities, as well as regular employee evaluations, will all see improvements to ensure the suitability of HR capabilities.

4. Risk Control System

In its implementation of a strategic risk control system, the Bank monitors its achievement of results and compares these results to its established plan as a way to ensure that the risks it has taken are still within the tolerance limits. The internal control system involves all management and employees. This system includes the determination of control policies and procedures, as well as evaluations to ensure that the policies and procedures in accordance with the direction of the Board of Directors have been complied with and have been implemented effectively and consistently.

Internal control is an activity that is inseparable from every function or daily activity of the Bank and can assist the Board of Directors and Commissioners in managing and controlling risks that can affect performance or result in losses for the Bank.
