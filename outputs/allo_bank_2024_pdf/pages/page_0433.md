b. Kecukupan dan Efektivitas Kebijakan &amp; Prosedur
Bank telah menyusun Kebijakan dan Prosedur APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM yang secara berkala dilakukan peninjauan dan penyempurnaan sesuai ketentuan yang berlaku. Untuk memperkuat penerapan APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM, maka di setiap prosedur produk dan aktivitas perbankan diatur pula prosedur pelaksanaan APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM guna mengidentifikasi dan melakukan penilaian risiko tindak pidana pencucian uang, tindak pidana pendanaan terorisme dan/atau tindak pidana pendanaan proliferasi senjata pemusnah massal untuk masing-masing produk atau aktivitas. Untuk memastikan implementasi Program APU PPT &amp; PPSPM telah berjalan sebagaimana mestinya maka dilakukan supervisi oleh Divisi Compliance &amp; AML-CFT.

Kebijakan dan prosedur penerapan program APU PPT sejalan dan sesuai dengan Peraturan OJK (POJK) No. 8 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM di sektor jasa keuangan, yang antara lain mengatur:

- Pengawasan Aktif Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris;
- Prosedur Customer Due Diligence dan/atau Enhanced Due Diligence terhadap Calon Nasabah/Nasabah/Beneficial Owner/Walk in Customer termasuk memastikan bahwa mereka tidak masuk dalam Daftar Terduga Teroris dan Organisasi Teroris dan/atau Daftar Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal dan daftar watchlist lainnya (OFAC List, UN List);
- Penerapan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM berdasarkan pendekatan berbasis risiko (Risk-Based Approach);
- Prosedur Pemantauan Profil dan Transaksi Nasabah;
- Pelaksanaan Prosedur Transfer Dana;
- Penatausahaan Dokumen Nasabah dan Transaksi;
- Penerapan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM di Jaringan
- Kantor dan Entitas Anak dalam Konglomerasi Keuangan;

- Approval from the Board of Directors on the appointment of the Compliance &amp; AML-CFT Division as UKK responsible for implementing the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program;
- Approval of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners on the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Policy;
- Periodic reports (monthly, quarterly and semi-annually) regarding reporting obligations and implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program to the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners;
- Approval from the Board of Directors on the Plan and Realization Report on Updating Customer Data;
- AML Forum attended by the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners to discuss the implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program.

b. Adequacy and Effectiveness of Policies &amp; Procedures
The Bank has prepared AML-CFT &amp; CPF Policies and Procedures which are regularly reviewed and refined in accordance with applicable regulations. To strengthen the implementation of AML-CFT &amp; CPF, every banking product and activity procedure also regulates procedures for implementing AML-CFT &amp; CPF to identify and assess the risk of criminal acts of money laundering, criminal acts of financing terrorism and/or criminal acts of funding the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction for each product or activity. To ensure that the implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program is running as it should, supervision is carried out by the Compliance &amp; AML-CFT Division.

The policies and procedures for implementing the AMLCTF program are in line with and in accordance with OJK Regulation (POJK) No. 8 of 2023 concerning Implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program in the financial services sector, which among other things regulates:

- Active Supervision of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners;
- Customer Due Diligence and/or Enhanced Due Diligence procedures for prospective Customers/Customers/Beneficial Owner/Walk in Customers including ensuring that they are not included in the List of Suspected Terrorists and Terrorist Organizations and/or the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction List and other watchlists (OFAC List, UN List);
- Implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program based on a risk-based approach;
- Customer Profile and Transaction Monitoring Procedures;
- Implementation of Fund Transfer Procedures;
- Administration of Customer Documents and Transactions;
- Implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program on the Network
- Offices and Subsidiaries in Financial Conglomerates;
