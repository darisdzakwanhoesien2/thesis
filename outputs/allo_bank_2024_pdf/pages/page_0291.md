# Analisis Kualitatif
## Qualitative Analysis

5. Bank senantiasa menyempurnakan implementasi Manajemen Risiko Operasional dengan meningkatkan kesadaran para Pegawai terhadap risiko serta menyempurnakan Kebijakan dan Prosedur operasional Bank. Berbagai upaya ini ditujukan untuk memitigasi risiko inheren dan terus meningkatkan sistem pengendalian khususnya terhadap Risiko Operasional. Bank juga terus-menerus meningkatkan kesadaran risiko seluruh pegawainya melalui berbagai media termasuk diantaranya email blast dan sosialisasi secara langsung.

Bank dalam menentukan tingkat Risiko yang diambil, Toleransi Risiko, dan Strategi Bank secara keseluruhan selalu memperhatikan kemampuan modal Bank untuk dapat menyerap eksposur risiko atau kerugian yang timbul berdasarkan pengalaman kerugian di masa lalu, kemampuan sumber daya manusia, serta kepatuhan terhadap ketentuan internal dan eksternal yang berlaku. Bank telah menetapkan Limit Risiko secara memadai sesuai sasaran strategis dan strategi bisnis Bank secara keseluruhan yang wajib dipatuhi oleh seluruh lini organisasi, oleh karenanya Bank menerbitkan Kebijakan Risk Limit. Limit Risiko yang ditetapkan oleh Bank secara berkala ditinjau ulang minimal satu tahun sekali untuk memastikan kesesuaiannya dengan arah dan strategi bisnis Bank.

Bank telah menerapkan Budaya Risiko (Risk Culture) antara lain dengan secara konsisten menerapkan proses identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan, pengendalian dan pelaporan atas potensi kerugian yang dapat dialami Bank (Potential Loss), mencegah kejadian risiko (Risk Event) dan meminimalkan kerugian Bank (Risk Loss). Kemudian melaksanakan proses identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan, pengendalian dan pelaporan setiap jenis dan kelompok risiko yang dilakukan secara berkesinambungan. Budaya Manajemen Risiko diinternalisasikan pada seluruh level Organisasi untuk mendorong konsistensi dan efektivitas Penerapan Manajemen Risiko Bank dan merupakan tanggung jawab bersama seluruh karyawan di setiap lini organisasi.

Terdapat Product &amp; Activity Forum yang secara rutin dilakukan untuk mengidentifikasi dan memitigasi risiko yang melekat pada produk dan aktivitas baru. Untuk mempermudah proses mitigasi Risiko Produk oleh Product Owner dan unit-unit kerja yang terkait lainnya, Bank telah menyusun Kebijakan Pengelolaan Risiko Produk Bank. Semua uang tunai yang dikelola oleh Bank telah diasuransikan, jenis asuransi uang tunai pada Bank terdapat dalam Cash In Save (uang dalam khazanah utama) dan Cash In Transit (uang dalam perjalanan).

Sebagai bentuk pengawasan aktif Dewan Direksi atas implementasi Kualitas Penerapan Manajemen Risiko yang salah satunya terkait Risiko Operasional, Bank telah memiliki Komite Manajemen Risiko yang tujuan utamanya adalah untuk eskalasi dan pembahasan risiko-risiko utama di Bank.

The Bank continuously improves the implementation of Operational Risk Management by increasing employee awareness of risk and improving the Bank's operational policies and procedures. These efforts are aimed at mitigating inherent risk and continuously improving the control system, especially for Operational Risk. The Bank also continuously improves the risk awareness of all its employees through various media, including email blasts and direct socialization.

In determining the level of risk taken, risk tolerance, and overall bank strategy, the Bank always pays attention to the Bank's capital capacity to absorb risk exposure or losses arising from past loss experience, human resource capacity, and compliance with applicable internal and external provisions. The Bank has set adequate Risk Limits in accordance with the Bank's strategic targets and overall business strategy that must be adhered to by all lines of the organization, therefore the Bank issues a Risk Limit Policy. The Risk Limits set by the Bank are periodically reviewed at least once a year to ensure their compliance with the Bank's business direction and strategy.

The Bank has implemented a Risk Culture, among others by consistently implementing the process of identification, measurement, monitoring, control and reporting of potential losses that can be experienced by the Bank (Potential Loss), preventing risk events (Risk Event) and minimizing Bank losses (Risk Loss). Then implementing the process of identification, measurement, monitoring, control and reporting of each type and group of risks that are carried out continuously. Risk Management Culture is internalized at all levels of the Organization to encourage consistency and effectiveness of the Implementation of Bank Risk Management and is a shared responsibility of all employees at every level of the organization.

There is a Product &amp; Activity Forum which is routinely held to identify and mitigate the risks inherent in new products and activities. To facilitate the process of Product Risk mitigation by Product Owners and other related work units, the Bank has prepared a Bank Product Risk Management Policy. All cash managed by the Bank has been insured, the type of cash insurance at the Bank is in Cash In Save (money in the main treasury) and Cash In Transit (money in transit).

As a form of active supervision by the Board of Directors over the implementation of the Quality of Risk Management Implementation, one of which is related to Operational Risk, the Bank has a Risk Management Committee whose main purpose is to escalate and discuss the main risks in the Bank.
