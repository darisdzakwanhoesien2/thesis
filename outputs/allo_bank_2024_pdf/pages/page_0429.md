# Kode Etik dan Budaya Perusahaan

# Code of Conduct and Corporate Culture

Bank telah memiliki Kode Etik dan Budaya Perusahaan sebagai prinsip dasar dan pedoman yang harus diikuti dan diimplementasikan oleh Insan Bank dalam menjalankan kegiatan usaha maupun operasi secara beretika dan berintegritas. Bank senantiasa memperbaharui Kode Etik dan Budaya yang dimiliki agar tetap relevan dengan perkembangan regulasi, norma, dan dinamika bisnis Bank.

# Isi Kode Etik dan Pernyataan Kode Etik Berlaku bagi Seluruh Insan Allo Bank

Kode Etik (Code of Conduct) Bank disahkan berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Direksi No. 025/DIRAB/24 tentang Kebijakan Kode Etik (Code of Conduct). Kode Etik Perilaku ini berlaku bagi Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan karyawan Allo Bank. Seluruh karyawan telah menandatangani pernyataan bahwa yang bersangkutan telah memahami dan akan menaati serta menjalankan Kode Etik Bank Allo Bank tersebut.

Secara garis besar, Kode Etik Perilaku Allo Bank mengatur tentang:

1. Mematuhi peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, baik internal maupun eksternal.
2. Menolak penyuapan, korupsi dan aktivitas ilegal lainnya.
3. Dilarang menerima atau meminta hadiah/hiburan dari nasabah dalam bentuk apapun.
4. Menjaga agar tidak terjadi benturan kepentingan, baik dengan nasabah, karyawan Bank, pemegang saham, anggota Dewan Komisaris, anggota Direksi, dan/atau pihak terkait dengan Bank.
5. Mencegah pencucian uang, membocorkan rahasia perusahaan, dan menjaga kerahasiaan informasi nasabah.
6. Tidak menyalahgunakan jabatan dan wewenangnya untuk kepentingan pribadi maupun keluarga.
7. Tidak melakukan perbuatan tercela yang dapat merugikan nasabah maupun Bank.
8. Senantiasa berusaha untuk meningkatkan pengetahuan, khususnya pada industri perbankan.

The Bank has a Code of Conduct and Corporate Culture as a basic principle and guideline that must be adhered and implemented by Bank Personnel in carrying out business activities and operations ethically and with integrity. The Bank continuously updates the Code of Conduct and Culture, is it remains relevant to the development of regulations, norms, and dynamics of the Bank's business.

# Contents of Code of Conduct and Statement of Code of Conduct Applies to All Allo Bank Personnel

The Bank's Code of Conduct was ratified based on the Decree of the Board of Directors No. 025/DIRAB/24 concerning the Code of Conduct Policy. This Code of Conduct applies to the Board of Commissioners, Directors, and employees of Allo Bank. All employees have signed a statement that they have understood and will obey and implement the Allo Bank Code of Conduct.

In general, the Allo Bank Code of Conduct regulates:

1. Compliance with applicable laws and regulations, both internal and external.
2. Rejecting bribery, corruption and other illegal activities.
3. It is prohibited to accept or ask for gifts/ entertainment from customers in any form.
4. Ensuring that there are no conflicts of interest, whether with customers, Bank employees, shareholders, members of the Board of Commissioners, members of the Board of Directors, and/or parties related to the Bank.
5. Preventing money laundering, leak company confidential matter and maintain the confidentiality of customer information.
6. Not abusing individual's position and authority for personal or family interests.
7. Not commit disgraceful acts that could harm customers or the Bank.
8. Striving to enhance knowledge, especially in the banking industry.
