## Pengelolaan Air

Air bersih merupakan salah satu kebutuhan vital dalam operasional Allo Bank, terkhusus untuk memenuhi keperluan domestik karyawan seperti air minum, mencuci peralatan dapur, wudhu, pembilasan di kamar kecil, mencuci kendaraan dan sebagainya. Bank mendapatkan air bersih dari pihak ketiga, yaitu produsen air dalam kemasan serta Perusahaan Daerah Air Minum (PDAM).

Sejalan dengan terus berkurangnya pasokan sumber air bersih, termasuk semakin banyaknya polutan pada air permukaan

Meanwhile, indirect GHG emissions (Scope 2) from electricity consumption are calculated by multiplying total electricity usage (in kWh per year) by the average grid emission factor issued by the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources, which references the 2015-2024 PLN RUPTL. The emission factor used for Java-Bali is 0.929 kgCO2/kWh (2017). Using this formula, Scope 2 emissions in 2024 totaled 361,381 kgCO2eq (361 tons CO2eq), compared to 66,627 kgCO2eq (67 tons CO2eq) in 2023. This increase is in line with the higher electricity consumption.

As a result, as of December 31, 2024, Allo Bank's total GHG emissions amounted to 422,365 kgCO2eq (422 tons CO2eq), a significant increase compared to 104,049 kgCO2eq (104 tons CO2eq) in 2023.

## Water Management

Clean water is a critical resource for Allo Bank's operations, primarily used for employee needs, including drinking water, kitchen sanitation, ablution, restroom use, and vehicle washing. The Bank sources clean water from third parties, including bottled water suppliers and the Regional Drinking Water Company (PDAM).
