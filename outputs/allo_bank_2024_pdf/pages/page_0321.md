# Pengangkatan dan Pemberhentian Anggota Dewan Komisaris

## Ketentuan Pengangkatan

1. Anggota Dewan Komisaris diangkat berdasarkan keputusan RUPS dengan memperhatikan rekomendasi komite yang menjalankan fungsi nominasi.
2. Anggota Dewan Komisaris diangkat untuk masa jabatan tertentu dan dapat diangkat kembali saat masa jabatannya telah berakhir.
3. 1 (satu) periode masa jabatan anggota Dewan Komisaris paling lama 3 (tiga) tahun atau sampai dengan penutupan RUPS tahunan pada akhir 1 (satu) periode masa jabatan dimaksud.

e. Currently not in a legal process and/or a fit and proper test process at a Bank.
f. Does not work in a company that prohibits becoming a member of the Board of Commissioners.
g. Have a commitment not to engage in and/or repeat certain actions or behaviors, for candidates for the Board of Commissioners who previously failed the fit and proper test.

4. The minimum competency requirements that must be fulfilled are:
a. Have knowledge in banking and/or finance that is adequate and relevant to the position.
b. Have experience and expertise in banking and/or finance.
c. Have knowledge about the duties and responsibilities as well as understanding the main business activities and main risks of the Bank.
d. Have a Risk Management Certificate of at least Level 6 (six).

5. The financial reputation requirements that must be fulfilled, among others:
a. Does not have non-performing loans, and
b. Never been declared bankrupt or served as a member of the Board of Commissioners that was found guilty of causing a company to declare bankrupt within the last 5 (five) years before being nominated.

6. Never been a member of the Board of Commissioners who, during their tenure:
a. Failed to hold the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGM).
b. Their accountability as a member of the Board of Commissioners was not accepted by the GMS or they failed to provide accountability as a member of the Board of Commissioners to the GMS.
c. Caused a company that obtained a license, approval, or registration from the Financial Services Authority (OJK) to fail to fulfill its obligation to submit Annual Reports and/or Financial Statements to the OJK.

# The Appointment and Dismissal of Members of the Board of Commissioners

## Appointment Requirements

1. Members of the Board of Commissioners are appointed based on the decision of the GMS.
2. Members of the Board of Commissioners are appointed for a certain term of office and may be reappointed when their term of office has ended.
3. The maximum term of office for a member of the Board of Commissioners is 3 (three) years or until the closing of the annual GMS at the end of the said term of office.
