# Pengawasan Terhadap Kebijakan Strategis Direksi

Dewan Komisaris secara proaktif meninjau dan memonitor implementasi kebijakan dan strategi yang ditetapkan pada awal tahun bersama Direksi, pada pengelolaan Bank. Dewan Komisaris juga secara terus menerus melakukan review atas inisiatif strategis sesuai dengan Rencana Bisnis Bank yang dijalankan Direksi dan kinerja yang dihasilkan Bank. Terkait hal ini, Dewan Komisaris memberikan rekomendasi atas kendala-kendala yang dihadapi untuk merealisasikan target tersebut.

# Supervision of the Board of Directors' Strategic Policies

The Board of Commissioners proactively reviews and monitors the implementation of policies and strategies established at the beginning of the year, working closely with the Board of Directors in managing the Bank. Additionally, the Board continuously evaluates strategic initiatives aligned with the Bank's Business Plan, as executed by the Board of Directors, and assesses the Bank's performance. In this regard, the Board of Commissioners provides recommendations to address any obstacles encountered in achieving the set targets.

Based on the oversight conducted—both through discussions with the Board of Directors regarding the Bank's performance and direct field observations—the Board of Commissioners assesses that the Board of Directors has fulfilled its role in accordance with the annual work plan, the Bank's vision and mission, and the strategic direction set by the Shareholders.
