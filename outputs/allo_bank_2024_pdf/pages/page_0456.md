Keberhasilan Allo Bank semakin bermakna karena diraih pada saat pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia mengalami perlambatan. Badan Pusat Statistik menyatakan pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2024 tercatat sebesar 5,03, turun dibanding tahun sebelumnya yang mencapai  $5,05\%$ . Selain Indonesia, perlambatan pertumbuhan ekonomi juga dialami mitra dagang utama Indonesia yaitu Tiongkok dan Amerika Serikat. Adapun pertumbuhan ekonomi global, menurut proyeksi Dana Moneter Internasional (IMF) tercatat sebesar  $3,2\%$ , melambat dibanding tahun 2023 yang mencapai  $3,3\%$ .

Secara spesifik, merujuk data BPS, lapangan usaha Jasa Keuangan dan Asuransi, di mana industri perbankan termasuk di dalamnya, tumbuh  $4,74\%$  pada tahun 2024. Pertumbuhan tersebut sedikit menurun dibandingkan tahun 2023 dengan pertumbuhan sebesar  $4,77\%$ . Perlambatan pertumbuhan lapangan usaha ini dipengaruhi berbagai faktor diantaranya ketidakpastian ekonomi global dan tekanan eksternalseperti fluktuasi nilai tukar serta perlambatan investasi asing sehingga menekan likuiditas dan minat investasi di Indonesia. Selain itu, kebijakan moneter yang lebih ketan dan pengetatan regulasi di sektor keuangan juga membuat lembaga jasa keuangan lebih berhati-hati dalam mengelola risiko dan pinjaman sehingga mempengaruhi laju ekspansi produk dan layanan keuangan yang dikelolanya. Faktor berikutnya adalah terjadinya pergeseran perilaku konsumen yang menjadi lebih selektif dan konservatif dalam mengambil keputusan finansial. Perubahan tersebut menambah beban bagi industri jasa keuangan dalam mempertahankan pertumbuhan yang sebelumnya lebih dinamis.

Di tengah kondisi yang penuh dinamika di lapangan usaha jasa keuangan itulah, Allo Bank berhasil memanfaatkan peluang yang ada, mengoptimalkan semua sumber daya sehingga mampu meraih kinerja terbaik. Melalui laporan keberlanjutan inilah, pencapaian kinerja aspek ekonomi tersebut kami sampaikan. Selain aspek ekonomi, sesuai dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten dan Perusahaan Publik atau POJK Keuangan Berkelanjutan, laporan ini juga menyampaikan pencapaian kinerja aspek sosial dan lingkungan.

# Dukungan Allo Bank terhadap Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Indonesia berkomitmen untuk melaksanakan pembangunan berkelanjutan sesuai dengan amanat perundang-undangan. Berdasarkan Undang-Undang No. 32 Tahun 2009 tentang Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup, pembangunan berkelanjutan didefinisikan sebagai upaya yang terencana dan sadar untuk mengintegrasikan aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan ekonomi ke dalam strategi pembangunan. Tujuannya adalah untuk memastikan keberlanjutan lingkungan serta kesejahteraan dan kualitas hidup generasi sekarang dan mendatang.

Allo Bank's success is even more meaningful considering it made all of its aforementioned accomplishments during a time when Indonesia's economic growth was experiencing a period of slowed-down activity. The Central Statistics Agency (BPS) has noted that Indonesia's economic growth in 2024 was recorded at 5.03 percent, which was down compared to the previous year's 5.05 percent. In addition to Indonesia, a slowing of economic growth was also experienced by Indonesia's main trading partners, specifically, China and the United States. Meanwhile, global economic growth, according to projections by the International Monetary Fund (IMF), was recorded at  $3.2\%$ , which slowed down compared to 3.3 percent in 2023.

Specifically, when looking at Central Statistics Agency data, the Financial Services and Insurance business sector in Indonesia, which includes the banking industry, grew by 4.74 percent in 2024. This growth was slightly lower than the same sector's 4.77 percent growth in 2023. The slowdown in growth in this business sector was influenced by various factors that included global economic uncertainty, external pressures (such as exchange rate fluctuations) and slowdown in foreign investment, which resulted in a suppression of liquidity and investment interest in Indonesia. Tighter monetary policies and tightened regulations in the financial sector have also made financial services institutions more careful in managing risks and loans in such a way that affected the rate of expansion of all the financial products and services they managed. The next factor was a shift in consumer behavior towards becoming more selective and conservative in making financial decisions. This change added to the financial services industry's burden in maintaining its previously more dynamic growth.

In the midst of these dynamic conditions within the field of financial services as a business, Allo Bank was able to succeed in taking advantage of existing opportunities and optimizing all of its resources so as to achieve its best in performance. We would like to use this sustainability report to convey our achievements when it comes to economic performance. In addition to this economic aspect, we will also use this report to convey our achievements in social and environmental performance. We are doing this in accordance with Financial Services Authority (OJK) Regulation No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers and Public Companies (hereinafter referred to as "POJK on Sustainable Finance").

# Allo Bank's Support for Sustainable Finance

Indonesia is committed to implementing sustainable development in accordance with the mandate of law. The country defines sustainable development as a planned and conscious effort to integrate environmental, social and economic aspects into development strategies. It uses this definition in accordance with Law No. 32 of 2009 concerning Environmental Protection and Management. The nation's goal is to ensure environmental sustainability as well as welfare and quality of life for both its present and its future generations.
