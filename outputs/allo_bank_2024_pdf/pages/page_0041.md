Laporan Keberlanjutan

Dari sisi produk dan layanan, Allo Bank telah merilis Rekening Dana Nasabah untuk mendukung minat masyarakat dalam berinvestasi melalui aplikasi digital. Dalam hal ini, Bank telah mengantongi surat izin dari Kustodian Sentral Efek Indonesia (KSEI) dan menjalin kerja sama dengan beberapa platform investasi digital dalam penyelenggaraan produk tersebut. Dengan masuknya Allo Bank ke segmen ini, Bank akan terus memperluas lingkup layanan pada segmen milenial dan Gen-Z. Tak kalah pentingnya, sejumlah produk dan layanan baru juga diluncurkan untuk semakin memenuhi kebutuhan nasabah meliputi Virtual Debit Card (dengan Visa), Izin Bank Devisa, hingga Internet Banking untuk nasabah korporasi. Selain itu, Bank juga terus memperluas kolaborasi dengan berbagai mitra strategis terkemuka di Indonesia. Didorong semua pencapaian di atas, Bank telah berhasil mencatatkan milestone 11 juta total nasabah pada akhir November 2024.

Dari sisi branding, Bank terus fokus menyasar segmen milenial dan Gen Z sebagai segmen utama Bank, dengan mengadakan berbagai program marketing yang unik dalam industri perbankan Indonesia seperti Allo Bank Festival pada 21-22 Juni 2024 yang mengusung tema Intimate Concert Experience bersama bintang tamu internasional WAY V bersama musisi nasional Indonesia di Istora Senayan Jakarta. Program lainnya termasuk Allo Friends Day pun mendapatkan respons yang sangat positif, tercermin dari tingginya impressi dari setiap event dan capaian media sosial Bank yang menembus 132 juta lebih impressi di tahun 2024.

## Perbandingan antara Hasil yang Dicapai dengan yang Ditargetkan

Sepanjang tahun 2024 iklim usaha nasional memang dipenuhi dengan berbagai tantangan, namun hal itu tak menyurutkan kinerja usaha Bank hingga ke level optimal. Dalam penetapan target, Allo Bank senantiasa memperkuat fondasi bisnis melalui kebijakan strategis yang tepat sasaran. Di tahun 2024, Allo Bank merencanakan perolehan dari beberapa komponen substansial sebagai tolak ukur dalam menilai kinerja usaha. Perbandingan antara rencana dan realisasi adalah sebagai berikut:

### - Aset

Per 31 Desember 2024, Bank membukukan Jumlah Aset sebesar Rp13,98 triliun, mengalami peningkatan sebesar 9,7% dibanding tahun 2023 yang tercatat sebesar Rp12,75 triliun. Kinerja Aset tersebut didorong oleh peningkatan kredit yang disalurkan, terutama untuk segmen Retail yang tumbuh pesat.

### Pendapatan Operasional

Bisnis inti Allo Bank tetap kuat, seperti tercermin pada perolehan Total Pendapatan Bank sebesar Rp1,440 triliun selama tahun 2024, dimana pencapaian tersebut mengalami peningkatan sebesar 25% dibanding tahun 2023 yang tercatat sebesar Rp1,153 triliun, didorong oleh peningkatan yang sehat pada pendapatan bunga bersih dan pendapatan operasional lainnya.

In terms of products and services, Allo Bank introduced a Customer Fund Account to support customers' interest in investing through digital applications. To facilitate this, the Bank secured approval from the Indonesian Central Securities Depository (KSEI) and partnered with several digital investment platforms. With this expansion, Allo Bank aims to broaden its reach within the millennial and Gen Z segments. Additionally, the Bank introduced several new products and services to further meet customer needs, including the Virtual Debit Card (with Visa), Foreign Exchange Bank permits, and Internet Banking services for corporate customers. The Bank also continued to expand its collaborations with leading strategic partners across Indonesia. As a result of these achievements, the Bank successfully reached a significant milestone, recording 11 million total customers by the end of November 2024.

In terms of branding, the Bank remained focused on targeting millennials and Gen Z as its primary customer segments. To engage these groups, Allo Bank organized unique marketing initiatives within the Indonesian banking industry, such as the Allo Bank Festival held on June 21-22, 2024, under the theme "Intimate Concert Experience" featuring WAY V as an international guest star alongside renowned Indonesian musicians at Istora Senayan, Jakarta. Other initiatives, such as Allo Friends Day, also received overwhelmingly positive responses, reflected in the high engagement levels at each event. Additionally, the Bank's social media presence saw exceptional growth, reaching over 132 million impressions in 2024.

## Comparison between Achieved and Targeted Results

Throughout 2024, the national business climate presented various challenges; however, this did not hinder the Bank from maintaining optimal business performance. In setting its targets, Allo Bank continued to strengthen its business foundation through well-targeted strategic policies. In 2024, Allo Bank aimed to achieve several key benchmarks for assessing business performance. The comparison between the planned targets and actual performance is as follows:

### - Assets

As of December 31, 2024, the Bank recorded Total Assets of IDR13.98 trillion, reflecting a 9.7% increase compared to IDR12.75 trillion in 2023. This growth was primarily driven by an increase in loan disbursement, particularly in the rapidly expanding Retail segment.

### Operating Income

Allo Bank's core business remained strong, as reflected in its Total Income of IDR1,440 trillion in 2024, representing a 25% increase compared to IDR1,153 trillion in 2023. This growth was driven by a healthy rise in net interest income and other operating income.
