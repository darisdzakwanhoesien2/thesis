# Pedoman Kerja Direksi

Allo Bank telah memiliki BOD Charter yang merupakan pedoman bagi Direksi dalam melakukan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya. BOD Charter mengatur sejumlah hal, antara lain:

1. Persyaratan Anggota Direksi;
2. Persyaratan Direktur Kepatuhan;
3. Pengangkatan Anggota Direksi;
4. Pengunduran Diri Anggota Direksi;
5. Pemberhentian Sementara Anggota Direksi;
6. Pengangkatan, Pemberhentian, dan Pengunduran Diri Direktur yang Membawahi Fungsi Kepatuhan;
7. Susunan dan Pembagian Tugas Direksi;
8. Rangkap Jabatan Direksi;
9. Transparansi, Independensi, dan Etika Jabatan Direksi;

10. Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Direksi;
11. Kehadiran dan Waktu Kerja Direksi;
12. Tata Tertib Rapat Direksi;
13. Penilaian Kinerja Direksi.

# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Direksi

Secara umum, Direksi bertanggung jawab dalam mengelola Bank secara kolektif sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dalam mencapai tujuan Bank. Adapun rincian tugas dan tanggung jawab Direksi, antara lain:

1. Setiap anggota Direksi wajib melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab dengan itikad baik, penuh tanggung jawab dan kehati-hatian.
2. Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawab atas pengurusan, Direksi wajib menyelenggarakan RUPS tahunan dan RUPS lainnya sebagaimana diatur dalam peraturan perundang-undangan dan Anggaran Dasar.
3. Direksi wajib mempertanggungjawabkan pelaksanaan tugasnya kepada pemegang saham melalui RUPS.
4. Membuat kebijakan strategis terkait pengelolaan perusahaan sesuai dengan kewenangan yang diberikan.
5. Membuat dan menyusun rencana kerja tahunan yang memuat juga anggaran tahunan Bank dan disampaikan kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk memperoleh persetujuan.
6. Melaksanakan prinsip-prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik dalam setiap kegiatan usaha Bank pada seluruh tingkatan atau jenjang organisasi.
7. Memastikan penerapan manajemen risiko telah memadai sesuai dengan Kebijakan Manajemen Risiko dan Strategi Manajemen Risiko yang telah ditetapkan.
8. Mengembangkan budaya anti fraud dan budaya kepatuhan pada seluruh jajaran organisasi.
9. Menindaklanjuti temuan audit dan rekomendasi dari Satuan Kerja Audit Internal Bank, auditor eksternal, hasil pengawasan OJK, BI dan/atau hasil pengawasan otoritas lain.
10. Menyusun kebijakan remunerasi dan mengungkapkan kepada pegawai kebijakan yang bersifat strategis di bidang kepegawaian.

# Board of Directors Charter

Allo Bank has established a BOD Charter, which serves as a guideline for the Board of Directors in performing their duties and responsibilities. The BOD Charter regulates several key aspects, including:

1. Requirements for the Board of Directors;
2. Requirements for Director of Compliance;
3. Appointment of the Board of Directors;
4. Resignation of the Board of Directors;
5. Temporary Dismissal of the Board of Directors;
6. Appointment, Dismissal, and Resignation of Directors in charge of Compliance Functions;
7. Structure and Division of Duties of the Board of Directors;
8. Concurrent Positions of the Board of Directors;
9. Transparency, Independence, and Job Ethics of the Board of Directors;
10. Duties and Responsibilities of the Board of Directors;
11. Attendance and Working Hours of the Board of Directors;
12. Rules of Procedure for Board Meetings;
13. Performance Assessment of the Board of Directors.

# Duties and Responsibilities of the Board of Directors

In general, the Board of Directors is responsible for managing the Bank collectively in accordance with the Articles of Association and applicable laws and regulations to achieve the Bank's objectives. The details of the Board of Directors' duties and responsibilities are as follows:

1. Each member of the Board of Directors must carry out their duties and responsibilities in good faith, full responsibility, and prudence.
2. In carrying out their duties and responsibilities in management, the Board of Directors must hold the annual GMS and other GMS as stipulated in the laws and regulations and the Articles of Association.
3. The Board of Directors must be accountable for the implementation of their duties to shareholders through the GMS.
4. Make strategic policies related to the management of the company in accordance with the authority given.
5. Create and prepare the annual work plan that also includes the Bank's annual budget and submitting it to the Board of Commissioners for approval.
6. Implement the principles of Good Corporate Governance in all Bank business activities at all levels of the organization.
7. Ensure that the implementation of risk management is adequate in accordance with the Risk Management Policy and Risk Management Strategy that have been established.
8. Develop an anti-fraud culture and a compliance culture throughout the organization.
9. Follow up on audit findings and recommendations from the Bank's Internal Audit Unit, external auditors, OJK and/or other supervisory authorities' surveillance results.
10. Develop a remuneration policy and disclosing to employees the strategic policies in the field of personnel management.
