Laporan Keberlanjutan

SBDK yang dipublikasikan oleh Bank belum memperhitungkan komponen estimasi premi risiko mengingat besarannya akan disesuaikan dari penilaian Bank atas risiko masing-masing debitur atau kelompok debitur sehingga besaran suku bunga kredit yang dikenakan belum tentu sama dengan SBDK. Bank senantiasa meninjau dan memperbaharui SBDK sesuai dengan pergerakan suku bunga acuan BI 7-day Reverse Repo Rate (BI7DRR).

Bank mengelompokkan SBDK menjadi 4 (empat) kelompok berdasarkan segmentasi kredit, yaitu Kredit Korporasi, Kredit Ritel, Kredit Mikro, dan Kredit Konsumsi. Seluruh informasi yang lebih rinci mengenai SBDK yang berlaku dapat dilihat pada publikasi di setiap kantor Bank dan/atau situs web resmi Allo Bank di www.allobank.com.

The SBDK published by the Bank has not taken into account the estimated risk premium component. This is due to the fact that the amount will be adjusted from the Bank's assessment of the risk of each debtor or group of debtors so that the amount of credit interest charged is not necessarily the same as the SBDK. The Bank continuously reviews and updates its SBDK in accordance with the movement of the BI 7-day Reverse Repo Rate (BI7DRR) benchmark interest rate.

The Bank groups its SBDK into 4 (four) groups based on credit segmentation, specifically, Corporate Credit, Retail Credit, Micro Credit, and Consumer Credit. More detailed information regarding applicable SBDK can be seen in the publications at each Bank office and/or the official Allo Bank website at www.allobank.com.

## Kinerja Produk Kredit

Pada tahun 2024, kinerja kredit tercatat sebesar Rp 7,48 triliun, mengalami peningkatan 1,2% dari tahun 2023 yang sebesar Rp7,39 triliun. Hal ini terutama disebabkan oleh peningkatan kredit yang disalurkan, terutama untuk segmen Retail Banking yang tumbuh pesat.

## Lending Product Performance

In 2024, loan performance was recorded at IDR 7.48 trillion, an increase of 1.2% from 2023 which was IDR 7.39 trillion. This was mainly due to an increase in credit disbursed, especially for the rapidly growing Retail Banking segment.
