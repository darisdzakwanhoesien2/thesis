Pemenuhan tanggung jawab terhadap lingkungan juga diwujudkan Bank dengan mengoptimalkan operasional kantor yang ramah lingkungan dengan mengedepankan prinsip 3R (Reduce, Reuse & Recycle). Prinsip tersebut diimplementasikan dalam pengelolaan penggunaan kertas, energi listrik, bahan bakar minyak, air/air limbah maupun limbah, baik limbah yang termasuk dalam Bahan Berbahaya dan Beracun (B3) maupun non-B3B sesuai dengan regulasi yang berlaku. Jika pun dalam laporan ini terdapat kenaikan penggunaan kertas, energi, emisi dan air, hal itu dipengaruhi oleh meningkatnya operasional Bank, termasuk di antaranya penambahan karyawan.

Kinerja Aspek Sosial

Tanggung jawab pada aspek sosial dilaksanakan Bank dengan menjawab dan memenuhi berbagai keperluan dan harapan para pemangku kepentingan, baik internal maupun eksternal. Untuk karyawan, Bank dengan konsisten memperlakukan karyawan secara setara sehingga tidak terjadi insiden diskriminasi, memenuhi hak-hak normatif sesuai regulasi ketenagakerjaan, memberikan peluang yang sama untuk maju dan berkembang melalui keikutsertaan berbagai pendidikan dan pelatihan, serta mewujudkan lingkungan kerja yang sehat dan aman sesuai kaidah Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3).

Terkhusus untuk pengembangan kompetensi karyawan, pada tahun 2024, Bank telah menyelenggarakan pengembangan kompetensi sebanyak 179 program, yang diikuti oleh 439 pegawai atau 93% dari total pegawai dengan jumlah jam pelatihan sebanyak 1.472 jam. Adapun biaya pengembangan kompetensi tercatat sebesar Rp4,853 miliar naik dibandingkan tahun 2023, yang mencapai Rp3,886 miliar. Sementara itu, untuk kinerja K3, Bank berhasil mempertahankan pencapaian tahun sebelumnya yaitu tidak tercatat adanya kecelakaan kerja dan penyakit akibat kerja.

Adapun pemenuhan tanggung jawab kepada nasabah sebagai salah satu pemangku kepentingan eksternal utama, Bank secara berkelanjutan melakukan inovasi produk dan jasa agar sesuai dengan harapan terkini. Selain itu, Bank juga mengoptimalkan layanan agar semakin efektif dan efisien, serta memudahkan nasabah bertransaksi di manapun dan kapanpun. Lebih dari itu, sebagai bagian dari layanan terbaik serta perlindungan terhadap nasabah, Bank juga berkomitmen untuk menyelesaikan semua pengaduan nasabah sesuai dengan Service Level Agreement (SLA) yang berlaku. Berdasarkan survei internal yang diadakan Bank kepada nasabah, sebanyak 78,56% nasabah menyatakan puas dengan produk dan layanan Bank.

Komitmen lain yang senantiasa dipegang Bank adalah menjaga dan melindungi kerahasiaan data nasabah agar tidak ada yang bocor, apalagi hilang. Upaya tersebut berhasil dipertahankan sebagaimana tahun sebelumnya, yang ditandai dengan tidak adanya pengaduan berkaitan dengan kebocoran data atau hilangnya data nasabah.

The Bank also realized its environmental responsibility by optimizing environmentally friendly office operations. It did this by prioritizing the “3R” principle (Reduce, Reuse & Recycle). This principle was implemented in the Bank through its management of paper use, electrical energy, fuel oil, waste water and waste, whether it was waste categorized as “Hazardous and Toxic Materials” or non-Hazardous and Toxic Materials. It carried out all of this in accordance with applicable regulations. Any increase mentioned in this report in the Bank's use of paper, energy, emissions and water was influenced by an increase in the Bank's operations, including its addition of employees.

Social Aspect Performance

The Bank carried out its social responsibility by responding to (and fulfilling the various needs and expectations of) both internal and external stakeholders. For employees, the Bank consistently treated its employees equally so that there were no incidents of discrimination. It also fulfilled all normative rights in accordance with employment regulations, provided equal opportunities for employees to advance and develop through participation in various education sessions and training activities, and created a healthy and safe work environment that operated in accordance with Occupational Health and Safety principles.

Specifically, in terms of employee competency development, the Bank organized 179 competency development programs in 2024 that were attended by 439 employees (or 93% of the Bank's total employees) with a total of 1,472 training hours. The Bank's competency development cost was recorded at IDR4.853 billion, an increase compared to IDR3.886 billion in 2023. Meanwhile, in terms of Occupational Health and Safety performance, the Bank managed to maintain its previous year's achievement of having no work accidents or work-related diseases being recorded.

In terms of fulfilling its responsibilities to customers as one of its primary external stakeholders, the Bank worked to continuously innovate its products and services to meet current expectations. In addition, the Bank also optimized services to be more effective and efficient, and made it easier for customers to transact anywhere and anytime. Moreover, as part of its best service and protection for customers, the Bank also committed itself to resolving all customer complaints in accordance with applicable Service Level Agreements (SLA). Based on Bank's own internal survey to customers, some 78.56% of customers expressed satisfaction with the Bank's products and services.

Another commitment that the Bank always upheld was its effort to maintain and protect the confidentiality of customer data so that nothing is ever leaked, let alone lost. The Bank was able to successfully maintain this level of data security as it had in the previous year, which was a year marked by the absence of complaints related to data leaks or any losses of customer data.
