# Tentang Laporan Keberlanjutan About This Sustainability Report

Laporan Keberlanjutan merupakan media untuk menunjukkan akuntabilitas dan transparansi bagi PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk dalam menjalankan usaha beserta dampak operasinya terhadap ekonomi, lingkungan hidup dan sosial. Untuk itu, setiap tahun, Bank menerbitkan Laporan Keberlanjutan sebagai bagian tak terpisahkan dari Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan.

Laporan keberlanjutan tahun buku 2024 ini merupakan penerbitan ketiga, sedangkan laporan tahun sebelumnya terbit pada April 2024. Laporan ini berisi tentang kinerja keberlanjutan yang terdiri dari tiga aspek, yaitu aspek ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial beserta dampak yang ditimbulkannya selama periode 1 Januari-31 Desember 2024. Implementasi keselarasan atas ketiga aspek sekaligus merupakan dukungan Bank terhadap penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan serta pencapaian Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan/TBP (Sustainable Development Goals/SDGs) di Indonesia.

Penerbitan laporan ini merupakan kepatuhan Allo Bank terhadap Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten dan Perusahaan Publik. Bank menerbitkan laporan ini dalam statusnya sebagai Bank Umum Kegiatan Usaha (BUKU) 3 per 1 Januari 2022, atau Kelompok Bank berdasarkan Modal Inti (KBMI) 2 sesuai Peraturan OJK No. 12/POJK.03/2021 tentang Bank Umum. Selain edisi cetak, laporan yang sama dapat diunduh di website resmi Bank, yaitu https://www.allobank.com/.

This Sustainability Report serves as a medium for PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk to demonstrate its accountability and transparency in running its business and in showing the impact of its operations on the economy, on the environment and on society. For this reason, the Bank publishes this Sustainability Report annually as an integral part of its Annual Report.

This 2024 fiscal year's sustainability report marks the third time the Bank has published this report. The previous year's report was published in April 2024. This report contains information on sustainability performance that consists of three aspects, specifically, economic, environmental, and social aspects, alongside any impacts they caused during the period spanning January 1 to December 31, 2024. The Bank's alignment of these three aspects also serves as its way of implementing sustainable finance and achieving the nation's Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

The publication of this report is Allo Bank's way of complying with Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers and Public Companies. The Bank has published this report in its status as an entity categorized as a "Conventional Bank with Business Activities (BUKU) 3" as of January 1, 2022, or as a "Bank Group based on Core Capital (KBMI) 2" in accordance with POJK No. 12/POJK.03/2021 concerning Conventional Bank. In addition to its printed edition, this same report can be downloaded on the Bank's official website located at https://www.allobank.com/.
