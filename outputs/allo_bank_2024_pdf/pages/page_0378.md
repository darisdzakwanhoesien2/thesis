# Pedoman Kerja: Piagam Komite Pemantau Risiko

Komite Pemantau Risiko telah memiliki pedoman kerja yang ditetapkan dalam Piagam Komite Pemantau Risiko. Piagam tersebut mengatur mengenai tugas dan tanggung jawab Komite, wewenang, struktur keanggotaan, persyaratan keanggotaan, mekanisme kerja, etika, waktu kerja, dan mekanisme rapat.

# Masa Tugas dan Kompensasi

1. Masa tugas anggota Komite Pemantau Risiko tidak boleh lebih lama dari masa jabatan Dewan Komisaris sebagaimana diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar dan dapat dipilih kembali untuk 1 (satu) periode berikutnya.
2. Masa tugas anggota Komite Pemantau Risiko yang berasal dari Pihak Independen Non Komisaris paling lama 3 (tiga) tahun dan dapat diperpanjang 1 (satu) kali selama 2 (dua) tahun masa tugas dengan tidak mengurangi hak Dewan Komisaris untuk memberhentikan sewaktu-waktu.
3. Apabila anggota Dewan Komisaris yang menjadi Ketua Komite Pemantau Risiko berhenti sebelum masa tugasnya sebagai Komisaris Bank, maka Ketua Komite Pemantau Risiko digantikan oleh Komisaris Independen lainnya.
4. Apabila masa tugas sebagai Dewan Komisaris berakhir, maka berakhir pula masa tugasnya sebagai Anggota Komite Pemantau Risiko.
5. Anggota Komite Pemantau Risiko yang berasal dari Pihak Independen Non Komisaris diberi honorarium bulanan yang jumlahnya ditetapkan oleh Dewan Komisaris dengan tetap mengacu pada ketentuan perundangan.

# Keanggotaan Komite Pemantau Risiko

Ketentuan keanggotaan Komite Pemantau Risiko Bank ditetapkan sebagai berikut:

1. Komite Pemantau Risiko sekurang-kurangnya terdiri dari 3 (tiga) orang yang berasal dari Komisaris Independen Independen.
2. Komposisi keanggotaan Komite Pemantau Risiko paling kurang 1 (satu) orang Komisaris Independen sebagai Ketua merangkap anggota, 1 (satu) orang Pihak Independen Non Komisaris yang memiliki keahlian di bidang keuangan, dan 1 (satu) orang Pihak Independen Non Komisaris yang memiliki keahlian di bidang manajemen risiko.
3. Anggota Komite Pemantau Risiko yang berasal dari Pihak Independen dinilai memiliki keahlian di bidang keuangan dalam hal memenuhi kriteria:
a. Memiliki pengetahuan di bidang ekonomi, bidang keuangan dan/atau bidang perbankan.

# Charter of the Risk Oversight Committee

The Risk Oversight Committee has work guidelines set out in the Risk Oversight Committee Charter. The charter regulates the duties and responsibilities of the Committee, authority, membership structure, membership requirements, work mechanisms, ethics, working hours and meeting mechanisms.

# Term of Service and Compensation

1. The term of office of members of the Risk Oversight Committee may not be longer than the term of office of the Board of Commissioners as regulated in the Articles of Association and may be re-elected for 1 (one) subsequent period.
2. The term of office of members of the Risk Oversight Committee who come from Independent Non-Commissioner Parties is a maximum of 3 (three) years and can be extended 1 (one) time for a period of 2 (two) years without reducing the right of the Board of Commissioners to dismiss at any time.
3. If the member of the Board of Commissioners who is the Chair of the Risk Oversight Committee resigns before his term of office as Bank Commissioner, then the Chair of the Risk Oversight Committee will be replaced by another Independent Commissioner.
4. If the term of service as a member of the Board of Commissioners ends, the term of duty as a Member of the Risk Oversight Committee also ends.
5. Members of the Risk Oversight Committee who come from Independent Non-Commissioner Parties are given a monthly honorarium whose amount is determined by the Board of Commissioners while still referring to the applicable statutory provisions.

# Membership of Risk Oversight Committee

The provisions for membership of the Bank Risk Oversight Committee are determined as follows:

1. The Risk Oversight Committee consists of at least 3 (three) people from Independent Commissioners and Independent Parties.
2. The composition of the Risk Oversight Committee membership comprises at least 1 (one) Independent Commissioner as Chair and member, 1 (one) Independent Non-Commissioner who has expertise in the field of finance, and 1 (one) Independent Non-Commissioner who has expertise in the field of risk management.
3. Members of the Risk Oversight Committee who come from Independent Parties are considered to have expertise in the financial sector in terms of meeting the following criteria:
a. Have knowledge in the field of economics, finance and/or banking.
b. Have work experience of at least 5 (five) years in the field of economics, finance, and/or banking.
