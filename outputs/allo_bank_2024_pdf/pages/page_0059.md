Laporan Keberlanjutan

Sejak grand launching pada 20 Mei 2022, PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk telah berkembang menjadi bank umum berbasis layanan digital terkemuka dengan melayani lebih dari 11 juta nasabah per akhir tahun 2024. Untuk produk dan layanan, Allo Bank menawarkan berbagai produk dan layanan perbankan digital yang inovatif termasuk Allo Pay, Allo Pay+, Allo Prime, Allo Grow, PayLater dan Instant Cash. Nama "Allo" merupakan singkatan dari All in One, yang mewujudkan aspirasi Bank bagi nasabahnya untuk Experience a Simple Life. Selain itu, Allo Bank adalah anak perusahaan dari CT Corpora, sebuah grup bisnis terintegrasi terkemuka yang berfokus pada konsumen di Indonesia dengan beragam usaha di layanan keuangan, media, ritel, gaya hidup, dan hiburan.

Anggaran Dasar Bank juga telah mengalami beberapa kali perubahan. Di tahun 2022, Anggaran Dasar Bank mengalami perubahan yang ditetapkan berdasarkan Akta Perubahan Anggaran Dasar No. 05 tanggal 15 Oktober 2021 yang dibuat di hadapan Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notaris di Jakarta. Selanjutnya, Bank kembali melakukan perubahan Anggaran Dasar pada tahun 2023 yang telah disahkan melalui Akta No. 01 tanggal 14 Juni 2023 yang dibuat di hadapan Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notaris di Jakarta yang telah memperoleh persetujuan dari Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia dengan Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0039698.AH.01.02.Tahun 2023 tanggal 12 Juli 2023 dan telah didaftarkan dalam Daftar Perseroan No. AHU-0131254. AH.01.11 tanggal 12 Juli 2023. Perubahan tersebut disesuaikan dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan di sektor Perbankan dan Pasar Modal yang berlaku, yaitu Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia No. 4 Tahun 2023 tentang Pengembangan dan Penguatan Sektor Keuangan untuk penyesuaian kegiatan usaha Bank serta Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 17 tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum untuk penyesuaian jumlah komposisi anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris Bank.

Since its grand launch on May 20, 2022, PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk has developed into a leading digital service-based conventional bank serving more than 11 million customers by the end of 2024. Allo Bank offers a variety of innovative digital banking products and services including Allo Pay, Allo Pay+, Allo Prime, Allo Grow, PayLater and Instant Cash. The name "Allo" is an abbreviation of All in One, which reflecting the Bank's aspirations for its customers to Experience a Simple Life. In addition, Allo Bank is a subsidiary of CT Corpora, a leading consumer-focused integrated business group in Indonesia with various businesses in financial services, media, retail, lifestyle and entertainment.

The Bank's Articles of Association have undergone several changes. In 2022, the Articles of Association were amended, as outlined in the Deed of Amendment to the Articles of Association No. 05, dated October 15, 2021, executed before Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notary in Jakarta. Subsequently, further amendments were made in 2023, as ratified through Deed No. 01, dated June 14, 2023, executed before Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notary in Jakarta. These amendments received approval from the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decree No. AHU-0039698. AH.01.02.Year 2023, dated July 12, 2023, and were registered in the Company Register No. AHU-0131254.AH.01.11 dated July 12, 2023. The changes were made to align with prevailing laws and regulations in the Banking and Capital Market sectors, including Law No. 4 of 2023 on the Development and Strengthening of the Financial Sector to adjust the Bank's business activities and Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 17 of 2023 on the Implementation of Governance for conventional bank to accommodate changes in the number of members of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners.
