Dalam menjalankan tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris turut dibantu oleh Komite Dewan Komisaris yang dibentuk untuk membantu dan meningkatkan fungsi pengawasan yang dijalankan oleh Dewan Komisaris, yaitu Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, dan Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi. Sementara, dalam melaksanakan tugasnya untuk mengelola dan mengendalikan operasional bisnis serta bertanggung jawab atas implementasi GCG di Bank, Direksi turut dibantu oleh sejumlah Komite di bawah Direksi, yaitu Komite Pengarah Teknologi Informasi, Komite Kebijakan Perkreditan, Komite Produk dan Proses Penerbitan Produk atau Aktivitas Baru, Komite Manajemen Risiko, Komite Kredit, Komite Aset dan Liabilitas (ALCO), dan Komite Sumber Daya Manusia. Selain itu, Direksi juga dibantu oleh organ pendukung lain yang bersifat struktural, yaitu Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO dan Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI).

The Board of Commissioners also acts as a supervisor and advisor, while the Board of Directors is responsible for the company's daily management.

In carrying out its duties, the Board of Commissioners is assisted by the Board of Commissioners Committees, which are established to support and enhance the supervisory function performed by the Board of Commissioners. These committees include the Audit Committee, Risk Oversight Committee, and Remuneration and Nomination Committee. Meanwhile, in executing its responsibilities to manage and oversee business operations and ensure the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) within the Bank, the Board of Directors is supported by several committees under its authority. These include the Information Technology Steering Committee, Credit Policy Committee, Product and Process Committee for Issuing New Products or Activities, Risk Management Committee, Credit Committee, Asset and Liability Committee (ALCO), and Human Resources Committee. Additionally, the Board of Directors is supported by other structural units, such as the Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO and the Internal Audit Work Unit (SKAI).
