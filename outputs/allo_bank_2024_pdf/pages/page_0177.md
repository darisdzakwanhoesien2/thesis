Pengelolaan kredit pada segmen wholesale dilakukan dengan suatu metode dalam memonitor kinerja debitur, sehingga tindak lanjut (action plan) dapat segera dilakukan untuk mencegah penurunan kualitas kredit debitur. Bank juga melakukan proses monitoring yang bertujuan untuk mengidentifikasi debitur yang berpotensi mengalami kesulitan membayar kewajiban.

Pengelolaan risiko kredit Bank diarahkan untuk meningkatkan keseimbangan antara ekspansi kredit yang sehat dengan pengelolaan kredit yang prudent, sehingga potensi penurunan kualitas debitur dapat dimitigasi sedini mungkin.

Proses kredit telah dilakukan sesuai dengan Pedoman dan SOP per kreditan yang ada. Pedoman dan SOP berfungsi sebagai petunjuk dalam melakukan proses pemberian kredit, mulai dari tahap permohonan melalui proposal, proses analisa, persetujuan, dokumentasi, pengawasan, hingga proses restrukturisasi. Selain itu, penetapan suku bunga kredit selalu dikaji ulang setiap bulan melalui Asset and Liabilities Committee (ALCO).

2. Kerangka Manajemen Risiko

Kerangka manajemen risiko kredit mencakup kecukupan kebijakan, prosedur dan penetapan limit. Kebijakan dan prosedur pengelolaan risiko kredit yang telah disetujui oleh Direksi, telah dikomunikasikan dengan optimal oleh Divisi yang menangani aktivitas fungsional yang memiliki eksposur risiko kredit.

Untuk menjaga agar eksposur risiko kredit sesuai dengan risk appetite, Bank menetapkan batasan (limit), antara lain susunan dan kewenangan kredit yang disesuaikan dengan ukuran dan kompleksitas Bank. Selain itu, Bank juga memiliki kebijakan untuk menjaga risiko kredit sesuai dengan Batas Maksimum Pemberian Kredit (BMPK) yang sesuai dengan ketentuan pengawas.

3. Proses Manajemen Risiko

Proses manajemen risiko kredit bertujuan untuk mengidentifikasi, mengukur, memantau, dan mengendalikan Risiko Kredit. Hal yang menjadi perhatian Bank ke depan adalah mengevaluasi dan meningkatkan efektivitas pelaksanaan unit kerja yang melakukan pemantauan kualitas kredit sejak dini. Konsentrasi terbesar yang dilakukan Bank saat ini adalah memitigasi risiko kredit termasuk terhadap potensi meningkatnya risiko kredit yang lebih besar.

Kecukupan sistem informasi manajemen dinilai memenuhi ekspektasi, hal ini terlihat dari pelaporan kredit kepada Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi mengenai eksposur risiko yang berjalan dengan baik, sehingga data yang disajikan dapat membantu secara optimal dalam pengambilan keputusan. Credit management in the wholesale segment is carried out with a method of monitoring a debtor's performance. This monitoring allows for follow-up action plans to be carried out immediately to prevent a decline in the debtor's credit quality. The Bank also carries out a monitoring process that aims to identify any debtors that have the potential to experience difficulty in paying their obligations.

The Bank's credit risk management is directed at improving the balance between healthy credit expansion and prudent credit management. This ensures that the potential for a decline in debtor quality can be mitigated as early as possible.

The Bank's credit process has been carried out in accordance with existing credit Guidelines and SOPs. There have been some deviations, but they have only been minor. These Guidelines and SOPs serve as instructions for the Bank as it carries out its credit granting process, beginning with the application stage through the proposal, analysis process, approval, documentation, supervision, to the restructuring processes. In addition, the Bank's method of determining credit interest rates is always reviewed every month through its Asset and Liabilities Committee (ALCO).

2. Risk Management Framework

The Bank's credit risk management framework encompasses its adequacy when it comes to policies, procedures and limit setting. Credit risk management policies and procedures that have been approved by the Board of Directors have been optimally communicated by the Division that handles functional activities with credit risk exposure.

In order to ensure that it can maintain its credit risk exposure in accordance with its risk appetite, the Bank has established various limits, including limits on credit composition and authority that have been adjusted to its size and complexity. In addition, the Bank also has a policy to maintain credit risk in accordance with supervisory provisions governing Legal Lending Limits (LLL).

3. Risk Management Process

The Bank's credit risk management process aims to identify, measure, monitor, and control Credit Risk. The Bank's future focus is to evaluate and improve the effectiveness of its work units that monitor credit quality early on. The Bank's greatest area of focus at present is mitigating credit risk, including mitigating any potential for greater credit risk.

Its management information system is considered to have met expectations, as can be seen evidenced by credit reports to the Board of Commissioners and Directors indicating reasonable risk exposures.
