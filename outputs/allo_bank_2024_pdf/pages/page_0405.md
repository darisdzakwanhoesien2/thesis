## Satuan Kerja Audit Internal

Allo Bank memiliki Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI) sebagai third line of defense yang memiliki tugas dan tanggung jawab untuk menjalankan fungsi audit internal di Bank secara berkesinambungan. SKAI bertugas sebagai penjaminan (assurance) dalam mengevaluasi proses tata kelola, pengelolaan risiko dan efektivitas pengendalian internal pada semua aspek kegiatan Bank melalui pendekatan berbasis risiko. Selain itu, SKAI juga bertugas sebagai fungsi konsultatif untuk memberikan masukan atas pengendalian internal kepada divisi lainnya, seperti pemberian rekomendasi atau saran perbaikan dalam berbagai pemeriksaan yang dilakukan, masukan atas draft kebijakan atau prosedur, masukan atas inisiatif Bank, dan lainnya.

## Dasar Hukum

1. Peraturan OJK No. 17 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum.
2. Peraturan OJK No. 1/POJK.03/2019 tentang Penerapan Fungsi Audit Internal pada Bank Umum.
3. Peraturan OJK No. 56/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Penyusunan Piagam Unit Audit Internal.

## Internal Audit Unit

Allo Bank has an Internal Audit Work Unit (SKAI) as the third line of defense that has the task and responsibility to carry out the internal audit function in the Bank on an ongoing basis. SKAI is tasked with providing assurance in evaluating the governance process, risk management and effectiveness of internal control in all aspects of the Bank's activities through a risk-based approach. In addition, SKAI also serves as a consultative function to provide input on internal control to other divisions, such as providing recommendations or suggestions for improvement in various examinations carried out, input on draft policies or procedures, input on Bank initiatives, and others.

## Legal Basis

1. OJK Regulation No. 17 of 2023 concerning the Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank.
2. OJK Regulation No. 1/POJK.03/2019 concerning the Implementation of Internal Audit Function in Conventional Bank.
3. OJK Regulation No. 56/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Establishment and Guidelines for Drafting Internal Audit Unit Charters.

## Appointment and Dismissal of Head of SKAI

President Director may appoint and dismiss the Head of SKAI after obtaining approval from the Board of Commissioners by considering the recommendations of the Audit Committee to be subsequently reported to the Financial Services Authority (OJK).
