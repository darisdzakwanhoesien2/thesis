9. Otoritas Jasa Keuangan berwenang melakukan evaluasi terhadap keputusan pemberhentian atau penggantian anggota Dewan Komisaris yang dilakukan sebelum periode masa jabatan anggota Dewan Komisaris berakhir.
10. Bank wajib melakukan keterbukaan informasi kepada masyarakat dan menyampaikan kepada OJK paling lambat 2 (dua) hari kerja setelah:
a. Diterimanya permohonan pengunduran diri anggota Dewan Komisaris.
b. Dilakukan penyelenggaraan RUPS.

3. Members of the Board of Commissioners may resign from their positions before their term of office ends.
4. The term of office of a member of the Board of Commissioners ends automatically if:
a. Resign with written notification;
b. No longer meets applicable statutory requirements;

c. Passes Away;
d. Dismissed based on GMS decision;
e. Declared bankrupt or placed under amnesty based on a Court decision;
f. Proven to be involved in financial crimes.

5. The Commissioner has the right to resign from his position by notifying the Bank in writing no later than 90 (ninety) days before the date of his resignation.
6. The Bank is required to hold a GMS to decide on the Commissioner's resignation request no later than 90 (ninety) days after receipt of the resignation letter.
7. If the Commissioner resigns, resulting in the number of members of the Board of Commissioners becoming less than 3 (three) people, then the resignation is valid if it has been determined at the GMS and new members of the Board of Commissioners have been appointed so that it meets the minimum requirements for the number of members of the Board of Commissioners.
8. The GMS may appoint another person to fill the position of a Commissioner who is dismissed from office or to fill a vacancy. The term of office of a person appointed to replace a member of the Board of Commissioners who has resigned or filled a vacancy is for the remaining term of office of the member of the Board of Commissioners who has been dismissed or replaced.
9. The Financial Services Authority has the authority to evaluate decisions to dismiss or replace members of the Board of Commissioners made before the term of office of members of the Board of Commissioners ends.
10. Banks are required to disclose information to the public and submit it to the OJK no later than 2 (two) working days after:
a. Acceptance of resignation requests from members of the Board of Commissioners.
b. A GMS was held.

## Composition of the Board of Commissioners

1. The Board of Commissioners consists of at least 3 (three) members and a maximum of the same number of members as the Board of Directors.
