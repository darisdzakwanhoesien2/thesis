# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Sekretaris Perusahaan

1. Mengikuti perkembangan Pasar Modal khususnya peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku di bidang Pasar Modal.
2. Memberikan masukan kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik untuk mematuhi ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan di bidang Pasar Modal.
3. Membantu Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dalam pelaksanaan tata kelola perusahaan yang meliputi:
a. Keterbukaan informasi kepada masyarakat, termasuk ketersediaan informasi pada Situs Web Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.
b. Penyampaian laporan kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan secara tepat waktu.
c. Penyelenggaraan dan dokumentasi RUPS.
d. Penyelenggaraan dan dokumentasi rapat Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris.
e. Pelaksanaan program orientasi terhadap perusahaan bagi Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris.
f. Sebagai penghubung dengan pemegang saham, OJK dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

# Peningkatan Kompetensi Sekretaris Perusahaan &amp; PMO

Allo Bank senantiasa melaksanakan program peningkatan kompetensi bagi Sekretaris Perusahaan &amp; PMO guna mengoptimalkan seluruh tugasnya dan terus mengembangkan kemampuannya, khususnya untuk mengikuti perkembangan isu-isu seputar korporasi dan fungsi di bawah Sekretaris Perusahaan &amp; PMO. Informasi mengenai peningkatan kompetensi Komite Pemantau Risiko sepanjang tahun 2024 dapat dilihat pada Bab Profil Perusahaan dalam Laporan Tahunan ini.

# Duties and Responsibilities of the Corporate Secretary

1. Monitoring the development of the Capital Market, especially the applicable laws and regulations in the field of Capital Market.
2. Providing input to the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of the Issuer or Public Company to comply with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of Capital Market.
3. Assisting the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners in implementing corporate governance, which includes:
a. Information disclosure to the public, including the availability of information on the Issuer's or Public Company's website.
b. Submitting reports to the Financial Services Authority in a timely manner.
c. Organizing and documenting the GMS.
d. Organizing and documenting meetings of the Board of Directors and/or the Board of Commissioners.
e. Implementing the company orientation program for the Board of Directors and/or the Board of Commissioners.
f. Acting as a liaison with shareholders, OJK, and other stakeholders.

# Competency Development of Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO

Allo Bank always implements competency improvement programs for Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO in order to optimize all their duties and continue to develop their capabilities, especially to follow developments in issues surrounding the corporation and functions under the Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO. Information regarding the improvement of the Risk Oversight Committee's competency throughout 2024 can be seen in the Company Profile Chapter in this Annual Report.

# Implementation Report of Duties and Responsibilities of Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO

In 2024, Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO has carried out his duties and responsibilities through a number of activities, such as:
1. Prepare the 2023 Annual Report and Sustainability Report;
2. Organize and document the 2023 Annual GMS on May 31, 2024;
3. Submit periodic reports and information disclosure to the OJK and IDX in accordance with applicable rules and regulations relating to the Capital Market in a timely manner;
4. Organize Board of Directors meetings, Board of Commissioners meetings, Joint Board of Commissioners and Directors meetings, Audit Committee and Risk Oversight Committee meetings as well as preparing and administering minutes of meetings and attendance lists;
5. Supervise the Company's shareholder registration process carried out by the appointed Securities Administration Bureau and report changes to the Board of Directors and regulators;
6. Develop and implement various policies and procedures related to corporate communications, transparency &amp; continuous information disclosure and good corporate governance;
