b. Report on the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Compliance Director to the President Director with a copy to the Board of Commissioners (quarterly).

c. Periodic communication through the Board of Directors meeting forum, Board of Commissioners meeting, and the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners joint meeting.
