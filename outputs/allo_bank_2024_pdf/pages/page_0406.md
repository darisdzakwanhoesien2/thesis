# Struktur dan Kedudukan SKAI

Satuan Kerja Audit Internal Allo Bank dipimpin oleh Kepala SKAI yang bertanggung jawab langsung kepada Direktur Utama dan memiliki akses komunikasi langsung kepada Komite Audit. Kepala SKAI membawahi Audit Non-Digital, Audit Digital dan Audit Information Technology. Hingga 31 Desember 2024, Satuan Kerja Audit Internal Bank beranggotakan 10 (sepuluh) orang, termasuk Kepala SKAI.

# Piagam Audit Internal

Internal Audit memiliki Piagam Audit Internal (Internal Audit Charter) yang disahkan oleh Direktur Utama dan Komisaris Utama yang terakhir kali dimutakhirkan pada tanggal 21 Oktober 2024. Piagam Audit Internal (Internal Audit Charter) dibentuk dengan mengacu kepada Peraturan OJK No. 56/POJK.04/2015 tanggal 23 Desember 2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Penyusunan Piagam Audit Internal dan Peraturan OJK No. 1/POJK.03/2019 tanggal 28 Januari 2019 tentang Penerapan Fungsi Audit Internal pada Bank Umum.

Piagam Audit Internal (Internal Audit Charter) memberikan pedoman mengenai tujuan, kedudukan, wewenang, tanggung jawab dan ruang lingkup pekerjaan internal audit yang dinyatakan secara formal dalam Piagam Audit Internal (Internal Audit Charter) sesuai dengan Peraturan OJK No. 1/POJK.03/2019 tentang Penerapan Fungsi Audit Intern pada Bank Umum.

# Kepala SKAI dan Dasar Pengangkatan

Allo Bank melalui Direksi menunjuk Dani Bayu Angkat sebagai Kepala SKAI berdasarkan Surat Keputusan No. 062/DIRAB/22 tanggal 22 Agustus 2022 yang telah disampaikan kepada OJK melalui surat No. 150/DIR-BIS-REG/22 tanggal 30 Agustus 2022.

# Structure and Position of SKAI

Allo Bank's Internal Audit Work Unit is led by the Head of SKAI who reports directly to the President Director and has direct communication access to the Audit Committee. The Head of SKAI oversees Non-Digital Audit, Digital Audit, Information Technology Audit and Fraud Management. As of December 31, 2024, SKAI Allo Bank has 9 (nine) members, including the Head of SKAI.

# Internal Audit Charter

Internal Audit has an Internal Audit Charter ratified by the President Director and President Commissioner which was lastly updated on October 21, 2024. The Internal Audit Charter was established with reference to OJK Regulation No. 56/POJK.04/2015 dated December 23, 2015 concerning the Establishment and Guidelines for the Preparation of Internal Audit Charters and OJK Regulation No. 1/POJK.03/2019 dated January 28, 2019 concerning the Implementation of Internal Audit Functions in Conventional Bank.

The Internal Audit Charter provides guidelines regarding the objectives, position, authority, responsibilities and scope of internal audit work which are formally stated in the Internal Audit Charter in accordance with OJK Regulation No. 1/POJK.03/2019 concerning the Implementation of Internal Audit Functions in Conventional Bank.

# Head of SKAI and Basis for Appointment

Allo Bank, through the Board of Directors, appointed Dani Bayu as Head of SKAI based on Decree No. 062/DIRAB/22 dated August 22, 2022 which was submitted to OJK via letter No. 150/DIR-BISREG/22 dated August 30, 2022.
