# Tinjauan Industri

Bank Indonesia menyatakan bahwa ketahanan system keuangan terjaga baik, termasuk pada industri perbankan. Likuiditas perbankan tetap memadai, tecermin dari rasio Alat Likuid terhadap Dana Pihak Ketiga (AL/DPK) pada November 2024 yang tinggi sebesar  $25.57\%$ . Rasio kecukupan modal (Capital Adequacy Ratio/CAR) perbankan pada Oktober 2024 tercatat sebesar  $27.02\%$ , tergolong kuat dalam menyerap risiko dan mendukung pertumbuhan kredit. Sementara itu, rasio kredit bermasalah (Non-Performing Loan/NPL) perbankan pada Oktober 2024 terjaga rendah, sebesar  $2.20\%$  (bruto) dan  $0.77\%$  (neto).

Ketahanan permodalan dan likuiditas perbankan juga didukung oleh pencapaian profitabilitas dan pengelolaan risiko bank yang baik, serta ditopang oleh kemampuan membayar dan profitabilitas korporasi yang terjaga, sebagaimana hasil stress test perbankan terkini. Ke depan, Bank Indonesia terus memperkuat sinergi kebijakan bersama KSSK dalam menjaga stabilitas system keuangan, termasuk memitigasi berbagai risiko yang dapat mengganggu stabilitas system keuangan.

# Industry Overview

Bank Indonesia has stated that the financial system's resilience has been well maintained, including in the banking industry. Banking liquidity remained adequate, reflected in the high ratio of Liquid Assets to Third Party Funds of  $25.57\%$  in November 2024. The capital adequacy ratio (CAR) for banking in October 2024 was recorded at  $27.02\%$ . This percentage was considered strong in absorbing risks and supporting credit growth. Meanwhile, the banking non-performing loan (NPL) ratio in October 2024 was maintained at low levels, at  $2.20\%$  (gross) and  $0.77\%$  (net).

The resilience of banking capital and liquidity was also supported by good bank profitability and risk management. It was also supported by the ability to pay and maintain corporate profitability, as shown in the results of the latest banking stress tests. Going forward, Bank Indonesia will continue to strengthen policy synergy with the Financial System Stability Committee in maintaining financial system stability, including by mitigating various risks that could disrupt financial system stability.

The performance of digital economic and financial transactions in November 2024 continued to grow thanks to the support of a safe, smooth, and reliable payment system. In terms of large value, BI-RTGS transactions in November 2024 increased by  $9.82\%$  (yoy) with a nominal transaction of IDR14,969.37 trillion. From the retail side of things, the volume of BI-FAST transactions in November 2024 grew by  $69.90\%$  (yoy) to reach 338.61 million transactions. The acceleration of digital payments continued. The volume of digital banking transactions in the same month was recorded at 2.04 billion transactions, which marked a growth of  $40.1\%$  (yoy), while the volume of Electronic Money (EU) transactions grew by  $33.4\%$  (yoy) to reach 1.44 billion transactions.

The QRIS transaction volume continued to grow rapidly by  $186\%$  (yoy) reaching 689.07 million transactions, with the number of users and merchants reached 55.02 million and 35.1 million respectively in November 2024. Meanwhile, the transaction volume of payments using ATM/D cards in November 2024 fell by  $10.9\%$  to 562.75 million transactions. The volume of credit card transactions in the same month grew by  $21.1\%$  (yoy) to 41.15 million transactions. In terms of Rupiah money management, Circulating Currency grew by  $11.9\%$  (yoy) to IDR1,105.8 trillion in nominal terms at the end of November 2024.
