# Perkembangan Penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik

## Development of Good Corporate Governance Implementation

## Perkembangan GCG Allo Bank di Tahun 2024

Dalam rangka meningkatkan kinerja Bank, melindungi kepentingan para pemangku kepentingan, serta meningkatkan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, Allo Bank berkomitmen untuk terus memperkuat implementasi tata kelola yang baik dan berkelanjutan pada proses bisnis dan operasional dengan mendukung prinsip-prinsip Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG). Allo Bank juga terus memperkuat fondasi bisnis melalui penerapan GCG, sistem yang andal, memenuhi sumber daya manusia yang sesuai dengan arah rencana bisnis Bank, serta menyesuaikan seluruh usaha sesuai dengan perkembangan teknologi dan dinamika bisnis yang terjadi.

Di samping itu, Bank juga berkomitmen untuk menerapkan praktik tata kelola perusahaan yang baik dengan menyempurnakan implementasi GCG di lingkungan Bank. Peningkatan kualitas pelaksanaan tata kelola yang dilakukan Bank pada tahun 2024, antara lain:

1. Komposisi susunan pengurus Bank telah terpenuhi sesuai ketentuan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dan seluruh Dewan Komisaris serta Direksi telah lulus Penilaian Kemampuan dan Kepatutan.
2. Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya secara independen.
3. Setiap keputusan hasil rapat Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dilakukan secara musyawarah untuk mufakat. Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris melakukan rapat gabungan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku dan kebutuhan Bank.
4. Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Bank telah melaksanakan tugasnya dan menyelenggarakan rapat baik secara tersendiri maupun gabungan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku dan mengadministrasikan setiap dokumen notulen Rapat tersebut.

## Development of Allo Bank's GCG in 2024

To improve performance, protect stakeholder interests, and enhance compliance with laws and regulations, Allo Bank remains committed to strengthening the implementation of good and sustainable governance in its business and operational processes by upholding Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles. The Bank also continues to reinforce its business foundation by implementing GCG, establishing a reliable system, developing human resources in alignment with the Bank's business plan, and adapting to technological advancements and evolving business dynamics.

Additionally, the Bank is committed to implementing GCG practices by improving governance implementation within its environment. The improvements in governance quality carried out by the Bank in 2024 include, among others:

1. The composition of the Bank's management has been fulfilled in accordance with applicable laws and regulations and all Board of Commissioners and Directors have passed the Fit and Proper Test.
2. The Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners have carried out their duties and responsibilities independently.
3. Decisions made by the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners are reached through deliberation and consensus. They conduct joint meetings in accordance with applicable provisions and the Bank's needs.
4. The Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners have fulfilled their duties and held both individual and joint meetings as required and have also properly documented the minutes of each meeting.
