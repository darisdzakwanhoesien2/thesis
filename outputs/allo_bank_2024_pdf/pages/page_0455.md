# Penjelasan Direksi Board of Directors' Explanation [OJK D.1]

![img-126.jpeg](img-126.jpeg)

## Pemegang Saham dan Pemangku Kepentingan yang Terhormat,

Puii syukur kami haturkan kepada Tuhan yang Maha Esa, atas kehendak-Nya, PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk berhasil melewati tahun 2024 dengan pencapaian kinerja yang sangat baik. Hingga akhir tahun 2024, jumlah nasabah tercatat lebih dari 11 juta nasabah, naik signifikan dibanding jumlah nasabah pada awal tahun yang sama, yaitu sebanyak 7,6 juta atau naik 44,74%. Kenaikan nasabah tersebut sejalan dengan peningkatan pendapatan bunga, pendapatan operasional neto, laba bersih dan aset. Pencapaian tersebut merupakan cerminan atas ketepatan dan konsistensi segenap manajemen dan karyawan dalam mengimplementasikan kebijakan dan inisiatif strategis di sepanjang tahun pelaporan.

## Dear Shareholders and Stakeholders,

We would like to begin by expressing our gratitude to God Almighty, as it was thanks to His grace that PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk was successfully able to emerge from 2024 with excellent achievements in its performance. By the end of 2024, we recorded more than 11 million customers, a significant increase compared to the 7.6 million customers we had at the beginning of that very same year, or an increase of 44.74%. This increase in customers was in line with increases in interest income, net operating income, net profit and assets. This achievement serves as a reflection of the accuracy and consistency on the part of all the Bank's management and employees in implementing strategic policies and initiatives throughout the reporting year.
