## Wewenang Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi

Dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Komite memiliki wewenang sebagai berikut:
1. Melakukan komunikasi dengan Kepala Unit Kerja dan pihak-pihak lain di Bank untuk memperoleh informasi, klarifikasi serta meminta dokumen dan laporan yang diperlukan.
2. Mengakses catatan atau informasi tentang pegawai, dana, aset serta sumber daya perusahaan lainnya yang berkaitan dengan pelaksanaan tugasnya.
3. Mendapatkan masukan dan atau saran dari pihak luar Bank yang berkaitan dengan tugasnya.
4. Melakukan kewenangan lain yang diberikan oleh Dewan Komisaris.

## Independensi Anggota Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi

Seluruh anggota komite senantiasa bertindak secara independen dalam melaksanakan fungsi dan tugas Dewan Komisaris terkait remunerasi dan nominasi terhadap anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris untuk terus memperkuat sistem dan remunerasi Bank yang berbasis kinerja. Piagam Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi mengatur tentang bagaimana masing-masing anggota Komite dalam menjalankan aktivitas profesionalnya secara terstruktur, sistematis, mudah dipahami dan konsisten, sehingga dapat dijadikan acuan bagi mereka dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab serta meningkatkan kualitas dan efektivitas kerja antara berbagai organ dalam Bank.

## Authority of Remuneration and Nomination Committee

In carrying out its duties, the Committee has the following authorities:
1. Communicate with the Head of Work Unit and other parties at the Bank to obtain information, clarification and request necessary documents and reports.
2. Access records or information about employees, funds, assets and other company resources related to the implementation of their duties.
3. Obtain input and/or suggestions from parties outside the Bank related to their duties.
4. Carry out other authorities granted by the Board of Commissioners.

## Independence of Remuneration and Nomination Committee Members

All members of the committee always act independently in carrying out the functions and duties of the Board of Commissioners related to remuneration and nomination of members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners. This is aimed at strengthening the Bank's performance-based system and remuneration. The Remuneration and Nomination Committee Charter stipulates how each member of the Committee performs their professional activities in a structured, systematic, easy-to-understand and consistent manner, so that it can be used as a reference for them in carrying out their duties and responsibilities and improving the quality and effectiveness of work between various organs within the Bank.
