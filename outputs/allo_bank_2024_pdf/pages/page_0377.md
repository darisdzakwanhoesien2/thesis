# Laporan Pelaksanaan Tugas Komite Audit

Selama tahun 2024, Komite Audit telah melaksanakan tugasnya dalam bentuk penyampaian saran/rekomendasi atas sejumlah kegiatan, di antaranya:

1. Melakukan kajian terhadap akuntabilitas dan transparansi laporan keuangan.
2. Melakukan kajian terhadap pengendalian internal, termasuk efektivitas pencegahan, deteksi, dan investigasi fraud dan tata kelola teknologi informasi, perlindungan data, dan cybersecurity.
3. Penyampaian Rencana kerja Komite Audit selama Tahun 2024.
4. Menyampaikan laporan Evaluasi Komite Audit terhadap Pelaksanaan Pemberian Jasa Audit atas Informasi Keuangan Historis Tahunan.
5. Memberikan rekomendasi mengenai Penunjukan Akuntan Publik dan Kantor Akuntan Publik.

# Komite Pemantau Risiko

Komite Pemantau Risiko merupakan komite yang bertugas untuk membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasan, khususnya evaluasi terhadap kepatuhan antara kebijakan dan parameter manajemen risiko dengan implementasinya.

# Dasar Hukum

Pembentukan Komite Pemantau Risiko berpedoman dan berlandaskan kepada peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku serta best practices yang diterapkan di lembaga perbankan di Indonesia, termasuk:

1. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("POJK") No.33/POJK.04/2014 tanggal 8 Desember 2014 tentang Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.
2. POJK No. 17/POJK.03/2023 tanggal 14 September 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum.
3. SEOJK No. 13/SEOJK.03/2017 tanggal 17 Maret 2017 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum.
4. POJK No. 18/POJK.03/2016 tanggal 16 Maret 2016 tentang Penerapan Manajemen Risiko bagi Bank Umum.
5. POJK No. 38/POJK.03/2016 tanggal 14 Desember 2016 tentang Penerapan Manajemen Risiko dalam Penggunaan Teknologi Informasi oleh Bank Umum.
6. Anggaran Dasar Bank.

# Tujuan Pembentukan Komite Pemantau Risiko

Komite Pemantau Risiko dibentuk sesuai dengan ketentuan OJK dengan tujuan untuk membantu fungsi pengawasan Dewan Komisaris terkait:

1. Evaluasi kepatuhan antara kebijakan dan parameter manajemen risiko dengan implementasinya.
2. Memberikan rekomendasi guna menjaga kualitas penerapan manajemen risiko.
3. Meningkatkan control environment secara keseluruhan.

# Audit Committee Task Implementation Report

Throughout 2024, the Audit Committee has performed its duties by providing suggestions and recommendations on several activities, including:

1. Reviewing the accountability and transparency of financial reports.
2. Reviewing internal controls, including the effectiveness of fraud prevention, detection, investigation, information technology governance, data protection, and cybersecurity.
3. Submitting the Audit Committee's work plan for 2024.
4. Submitting the Audit Committee's evaluation report on the Implementation of the Provision of Audit Services for Annual Historical Financial Information.
5. Providing recommendations regarding the appointment of public accountants and public accounting firms.

# Risk Oversight Committee

The Risk Oversight Committee is a committee tasked with assisting the Board of Commissioners in carrying out its supervisory function, particularly evaluating compliance between risk management policies and parameters and their implementation.

# Legal Basis

The formation of the Risk Oversight Committee is guided by and based on applicable laws and regulations as well as best practices implemented in banking institutions in Indonesia, including:

1. Financial Services Authority Regulation ("POJK") No.33/POJK.04/2014 dated December 8, 2014 concerning the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies.
2. POJK No. 17/POJK.03/2023 dated September 14, 2023 concerning Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank.
3. SEOJK No. 13/SEOJK.03/2017 dated March 17, 2017 concerning Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank.
4. POJK No. 18/POJK.03/2016 dated March 16, 2016 concerning the Implementation of Risk Management for Conventional Bank.
5. POJK No. 38/POJK.03/2016 dated December 14, 2016 concerning the Implementation of Risk Management in the Use of Information Technology by Conventional Bank.
6. Bank Articles of Association.

# Purpose of Establishing a Risk Oversight Committee

The Risk Oversight Committee was established in accordance with the provisions of the Financial Services Authority. The committee aims to assist the supervisory function of the Board of Commissioners regarding:

1. evaluate compliance between risk management policies and parameters and their implementation.
2. provide recommendations to maintain the quality of risk management implementation
3. improve the overall control environment
