# Jejak Langkah
Allo Bank
Allo Bank Milestones

## 1992

Berawal dengan nama PT Bank Arta Griya
The Bank was established with the name PT Bank Arta Griya

## 1993

Berganti nama menjadi PT Bank Harda Griya sebelumnya bernama Bank Harda
Changed its name to PT Bank Harda Griya, which was previously named Bank Harda

## 1994

Memulai operasional bank untuk pertama kalinya
Commenced banking operations for the first time

## 1996

Berganti nama menjadi PT Bank Harda Internasional (BHI)
Changed its name to PT Bank Harda International (BHI)

## 2015

Melakukan Penawaran Saham Perdana (IPO)
Carried out an Initial Stock Offering (IPO)

## 2021

- Maret: PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk resmi diakuisisi oleh PT Mega Corpora
- Juni: Setelah mendapat persetujuan dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, pada Juni 2021, PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk resmi berganti nama menjadi PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk
- Juli: Right Issue (PUT II) Rp750 miliar
- Agustus: Bank berganti logo menjadi Allo Bank yang merefleksikan model bisnis baru, adaptif dan relevan dengan perkembangan saat ini khususnya dalam mendukung pertumbuhan inklusi ekonomi digital di Indonesia.
- November: Soft launch ke 40,000 karyawan CT Corp
- March: PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk was officially acquired by PT Mega Corpora
- June: After receiving approval from the Financial Services Authority, in June 2021, PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk officially changed its name to PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk
- July: Rights Issue (PUT II) IDR 750 billion
- August: Bank changed its logo to Allo Bank which reflects a new business model, adaptive and relevant to current developments, especially in supporting the growth of digital economic inclusion in Indonesia.
- November: Soft launch to 40,000 CT Corp employees

## 2022

- Januari: Right Issue (PUT III) Rp4,8 triliun (Buku 3, KBMI 2)
- Mei: Allo Bank Grand Launching dan Allo Bank Festival
- Juli: Relokasi kantor pusat ke Menara Bank Mega
- Desember: Pencapaian 5 juta nasabah
- January: Rights Issue (PUT III) IDR 4,8 trillion (Book 3, KBMI 2)
- May: Held Allo Bank Grand Launching and Allo Bank Festival
- July: Relocation of head office to Menara Bank Mega
- December: Achievement of 5 million customers
