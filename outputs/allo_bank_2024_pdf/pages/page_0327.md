# Pembagian Tugas Pengawasan Dewan Komisaris

Allo Bank berupaya untuk memastikan seluruh tugas dan tanggung jawab yang dilakukan oleh Insan Bank berjalan secara baik sesuai dengan kemampuan yang dimiliki, tak terkecuali bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris. Untuk itu, Dewan Komisaris melaksanakan fungsi pengawasan sesuai dengan latar belakang yang dimiliki oleh masing-masing anggota agar pengawasan terhadap pengelolaan Bank maupun implementasi strategi manajemen dapat berjalan secara optimal agar dapat mewujudkan tujuan Bank. The termination must be notified to the person concerned along with the reasons.
m. Appoint one of the Directors to carry out the vacant Director's work with due observance of applicable regulations.
n. Hold a GMS no later than 90 (ninety) days after the temporary dismissal which will decide whether the relevant Directors will be permanently dismissed or returned to their original positions. Directors who were temporarily dismissed were given the opportunity to appear to defend themselves.
o. If the GMS cannot make a decision or is not held within a period of no later than 90 (ninety) days after the temporary dismissal, then the temporary dismissal becomes null and void and the person concerned has the right to return to his or her original position.

4. If all members of the Board of Directors are temporarily dismissed and the Bank does not have a single member of the Board of Directors, the Board of Commissioners is temporarily required to manage the Bank. In such cases the Board of Commissioners has the right to grant temporary powers to one or more of them at their collective expense.
