3. Penguatan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM Semakin kompleksnya produk, jasa dan layanan perbankan, serta meningkatnya penggunaan teknologi digital, hal ini berpotensi meningkatkan risiko perbankan untuk digunakannya sebagai sarana pencucian uang, pendanaan terorisme dan pendanaan proliferasi senjata pemusnah massal oleh para pelaku kejahatan. Sehubungan dengan hal tersebut, Bank perlu memastikan bahwa implementasi APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM telah berjalan dengan baik dan sesuai dengan ketentuan, selain itu perlu dilakukan upaya perbaikan dalam pelaksanaannya.

Beberapa inisiatif perbaikan yang dilakukan Bank, antara lain:
a. Meningkatkan awareness dan kompetensi pegawai terkait implementasi APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM;
b. Melakukan evaluasi proses penerimaan nasabah
c. Melakukan updating atas kebijakan dan prosedur internal sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku.
d. Mengembangkan AML system dan aplikasi lainnya untuk mendukung optimalisasi implementasi APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM
e. Meningkatkan kualitas data nasabah dan analisis pelaporan kepada regulator sesuai dengan ketentuan;
f. Menindaklanjuti seluruh hasil audit baik dari internal maupun eksternal terkait dengan implementasi APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM sesuai dengan waktu yang telah ditetapkan;
g. Melakukan koordinasi secara aktif dengan Regulator dan Aparat Penegak Hukum dalam pemenuhan permintaan data dan pemberian keterangan dalam upaya Pencegahan dan Pemberantasan Tindak Pidana Pencucian Uang, Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal.

f. Implementing a program to improve customer data quality
g. Establishing and fostering cooperation and coordination with regulators and Law Enforcement Officials, especially in fulfilling customer data and/or information requests.
h. Playing an active role in the work program and activities of the AML-CFT regime organized by the regulator/government.

3. Strengthening the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program

The increasing complexity of banking products, services and services, as well as the increasing use of digital technology, has the potential to increase the risk of banking being used as a means of money laundering, financing terrorism and financing the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction by criminals. In connection with this, the Bank needs to ensure that the implementation of AML-CFT &amp; CPF has gone well and in accordance with the provisions, in addition to that efforts need to be made to improve its implementation.

Several improvement initiatives carried out by the Bank include:
a. Raising employee awareness and competence regarding the implementation of AML-CFT &amp; CPF;
b. Evaluating the customer acceptance process
c. Updating internal policies and procedures in accordance with applicable regulations.
d. Developing AML systems and other applications to support the optimization of AML-CFT &amp; CPF implementation
e. Improving the quality of customer data and reporting analysis to regulators in accordance with regulations;
f. Follow up on all internal and external audit results related to the implementation of AML-CFT &amp; CPF according to the specified time;
g. Coordinate actively with Regulators and Law Enforcement Officials in fulfilling requests for data and providing information in efforts to Prevent and Eradicate Money Laundering, Prevent Terrorism Financing and Prevent Funding for the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction.
