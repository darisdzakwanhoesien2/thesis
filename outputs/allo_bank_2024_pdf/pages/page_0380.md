# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Komite Pemantau Risiko

Dalam menjalankan fungsinya, Komite harus bertindak secara independen dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab antara lain sebagai berikut:

1. Melakukan pemantauan dan evaluasi atas:

a. Kesesuaian antara kebijakan manajemen risiko dan kebijakan manajemen risiko terintegrasi Bank dengan pelaksanaan kebijakan tersebut.
b. Pelaksanaan rencana kerja dan tugas Komite Manajemen Risiko, Komite Manajemen Risiko Terintegrasi, Satuan Kerja Manajemen Risiko, serta Satuan Kerja Manajemen Risiko Terintegrasi Bank.
c. Kecukupan proses identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan, pengendalian dan sistem informasi manajemen risiko.
d. Kepatuhan Bank terhadap Anggaran Dasar, peraturan Otoritas Pengawas Bank dan Pasar Modal, serta peraturan perundangan lainnya yang terkait dengan manajemen risiko.

2. Melakukan pemantauan dan penelaahan atas:

a. Laporan Tingkat Kesehatan Bank berbasis risiko.
b. Laporan lainnya terkait dengan pengelolaan 10 (sepuluh) jenis risiko yaitu Risiko Kredit, Risiko Pasar, Risiko Operasional, Risiko Likuiditas, Risiko Hukum, Risiko Kepatuhan, Risiko Reputasi, Risiko Strategik, Risiko Transaksi Intra Group, dan Risiko Asuransi.
c. Kebijakan umum perkreditan serta kewajiban lainnya yang diwajibkan Regulator untuk disampaikan kepada Dewan Komisaris oleh Direksi.

# Duties and Responsibilities of Risk Oversight Committee

In carrying out its functions, the Committee must act independently in carrying out its duties and responsibilities, including the following:

1. Monitor and evaluate:

a. Conformity between risk management policies and the Bank's integrated risk management policies with the implementation of these policies.
b. Implementation of work plans and duties of the Risk Management Committee, Integrated Risk Management Committee, Risk Management Work Unit, and the Bank's Integrated Risk Management Work Unit.
c. Adequacy of identification, measurement, monitoring, control processes and risk management information systems.
d. Bank compliance with the Articles of Association, regulations of the Bank and Capital Market Supervisory Authority, as well as other laws and regulations related to risk management.

2. Monitor and review:

a. Risk-based Bank Soundness Level Report.
b. Other reports related to the management of 10 (ten) types of risk, namely Credit Risk, Market Risk, Operational Risk, Liquidity Risk, Legal Risk, Compliance Risk, Reputation Risk, Strategic Risk, Intra Group Transaction Risk, and Insurance Risk.
c. General credit policies and other obligations required by the Regulator to be submitted to the Board of Commissioners by the Board of Directors.
