# Nomination and Remuneration of Board of Commissioners and Directors

Sebagai organ tertinggi dalam organisasi Bank, Pemegang Saham memiliki kewenangan untuk mengangkat dan memberhentikan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi melalui mekanisme RUPS. Pemegang saham juga berwenang untuk mendelegasikan penetapan besaran remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi kepada Dewan Komisaris dengan mempertimbangkan rekomendasi dari Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi.

## Prosedur Nominasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Allo Bank telah menetapkan sejumlah kriteria bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku. Calon anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dapat berasal dari pemegang saham pengendali atau dari sumber-sumber lainnya. Setiap calon yang dinominasikan akan dikaji oleh Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi untuk disampaikan kepada Dewan Komisaris lalu diusulkan kepada Pemegang Saham. Penetapan calon yang dinominasikan dilakukan melalui mekanisme RUPS untuk kemudian mengikuti tahapan selanjutnya, yaitu uji kepatutan dan kelayakan yang dilakukan oleh OJK.

## Kebijakan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Bank menetapkan besaran remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi berdasarkan Keputusan RUPS setiap tahunnya. Besaran remunerasi serta fasilitas lain yang diberikan bagi Dewan Komisaris akan ditetapkan di dalam RUPS Tahunan oleh Pemegang Saham dengan memperhatikan rekomendasi dari Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi. As the highest organ in the Bank's organization, Shareholders have the authority to appoint and dismiss members of the Board of Commissioners and Directors through the GMS mechanism. Shareholders are also authorized to delegate the determination of the amount of remuneration for members of the Board of Commissioners and Directors to the Board of Commissioners by considering recommendations from the Remuneration and Nomination Committee.

## Nomination Procedure for Board of Commissioners and Directors

Allo Bank has set a number of criteria for members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in accordance with applicable provisions. The Board of Commissioners and Directors candidates may come from controlling shareholders or from other sources. The Bank has established a number of criteria for members of the Board of Commissioners and Directors in accordance with applicable regulations. Each nominated candidate will be reviewed by the Remuneration and Nomination Committee to be submitted to the Board of Commissioners and then proposed to Shareholders. The determination of the nominated candidates is carried out through the GMS mechanism and then follows the next stage, namely the fit and proper test carried out by the OJK.

## Remuneration Policy for Board of Commissioners and Directors

The Bank determines the remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors based on the Annual GMS Decision. The remuneration amount and other benefits provided to the Board of Commissioners are determined in the Annual GMS by shareholders, taking into account the recommendations of the Remuneration and Nomination Committee.
