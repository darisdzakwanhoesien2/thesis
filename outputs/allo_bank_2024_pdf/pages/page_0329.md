# Peningkatan Kompetensi Dewan Komisaris

Seluruh Dewan Komisaris Bank senantiasa mendapatkan pelatihan atau program pengembangan kompetensi untuk menunjang dan mengoptimalkan peran serta fungsi Dewan Komisaris. Informasi terkait program peningkatan kompetensi yang dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris pada tahun 2024 dapat dilihat pada Bab Profil Perusahaan dalam Laporan Tahunan ini.

# Pelaksanaan Tugas Dewan Komisaris Tahun 2024

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Dewan Komisaris telah melakukan pengawasan, mengarahkan, memantau, mengevaluasi, dan memberikan nasihat kepada Direksi terkait pelaksanaan kebijakan strategis yang dilakukan melalui:

1. Hasil dan Rekomendasi Pelaksanaan Fungsi Kepatuhan Bank melalui Memo Dinas No. 005/KOMISARIS/24 tertanggal 30 Agustus 2024.
2. Rekomendasi atas Rencana Bisnis Bank melalui Memo Dinas No. 008/KOMISARIS/24 tertanggal 25 November 2024

# Penilaian Kinerja Organ dan Komite Pendukung di Bawah Dewan Komisaris

Dewan Komisaris turut dibantu oleh Komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris yang bertujuan untuk membantu pelaksanaan tugas dan fungsi pengawasan atas pengelolaan Bank. Dewan Komisaris telah memiliki sejumlah Komite pendukung, antara lain Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, serta Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi.

Dewan Komisaris secara berkala melakukan evaluasi atas kinerja masing-masing komite berdasarkan kriteria yang telah ditetapkan oleh Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi. Berdasarkan evaluasi yang telah dilakukan, Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa seluruh Komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris telah menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya secara baik di sepanjang tahun 2024.

# Competency Development of Board of Commissioners

The Board of Commissioners regularly participates in training and competency development programs to enhance their role and effectiveness. Information regarding the competency improvement programs undertaken by the Board of Commissioners in 2024 can be found in the Company Profile Chapter of this Annual Report.

# Implementation of Duties of the Board of Commissioners in 2024

Throughout 2024, the Board of Commissioners supervised, directed, monitored, evaluated, and provided advice to the Board of Directors regarding the implementation of strategic policies through:

1. Results and Recommendations for the Implementation of the Bank's Compliance Function through Service Memo No. 005/KOMISARIS/24 dated August 30, 2024.
2. Recommendations on the Bank's Business Plan through Service Memo No. 008/KOMISARIS/24 dated November 25, 2024.

# Performance Assessment of Supporting Organs and Committees under Board of Commissioners

The Board of Commissioners is assisted by Committees under its authority, which are established to support the implementation of supervisory duties and functions over the Bank's management. The Board of Commissioners has several supporting committees, including the Audit Committee, Risk Oversight Committee, and Remuneration and Nomination Committee.

The Board of Commissioners periodically evaluates the performance of each committee based on the criteria set by the Remuneration and Nomination Committee. Based on the evaluations conducted, the Board of Commissioners has determined that all Committees under its authority effectively carried out their duties and responsibilities throughout 2024.
