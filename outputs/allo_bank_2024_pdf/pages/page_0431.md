# Penerapan Program Anti Pencucian Uang, Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal

Sebagai bentuk pemenuhan ketentuan OJK, Bank Indonesia, maupun peraturan perundang-undangan lainnya, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris berkomitmen penuh untuk memastikan Allo Bank melakukan seluruh kegiatan usahanya dengan tetap mendukung Anti Pencucian Uang (APU), Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme (PPT) dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal (PPPSPM).

Dewan Komisaris melakukan pengawasan secara aktif dalam mengawasi pelaksanaan tanggung jawab Direksi terhadap penerapan program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM, termasuk komitmen yang dibuat oleh Allo Bank kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan/Bank Indonesia serta pelaporan kepada regulator sesuai peraturan yang berlaku. Bank senantiasa meningkatkan kualitas penerapan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM, baik melalui pengawasan aktif dari manajemen, penyempurnaan kebijakan, penyelenggaraan program pelatihan, penyempurnaan system informasi, maupun inisiatif-inisiatif lainnya.

Berdasarkan POJK No. 8 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Program Anti Pencucian Uang, Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme, dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal di Sektor Jasa Keuangan, Allo Bank telah menerapkan program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM yang mencakup sejumlah faktor, seperti:

1. Pengawasan Aktif Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris.
2. Kebijakan dan prosedur.
3. Pengendalian internal.
4. Sistem informasi manajemen.
5. SDM dan pelatihan.

Implementation of Anti-Money Laundering, Counter-Terrorist Financing, and Counter-Proliferation Financing of Weapons of Mass Destruction Program

To comply with the provisions of the OJK, Bank Indonesia, and other laws and regulations, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners are fully committed to ensuring that Allo Bank carries out all its business activities while continuing to support Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Countering the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) and Counter Proliferation Financing of Mass Destruction Weapons (CPF).

The Board of Commissioners actively supervises the implementation of the Board of Directors' responsibilities for the implementation of the AML CFT &amp; CPF program, including commitments made by Allo Bank to the Financial Services Authority/Bank Indonesia and reporting to regulators in accordance with applicable regulations. The Bank continues to improve the quality of the implementation of the AML CFT &amp; CPF Program, both through active supervision from management, policy improvement, implementation of training programs, improvement of information systems, and other initiatives.

Based on POJK No. 8 of 2023 concerning the Implementation of the Anti-Money Laundering, Prevention of Terrorism Financing, and Prevention of Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction Programs in the Financial Services Sector, Allo Bank has implemented the AML CFT &amp; CPF program which includes a number of factors, such as:

1. Active supervision of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners.
2. Policies and procedures.
3. Internal control.
4. Management information system.
5. Human resources and training
