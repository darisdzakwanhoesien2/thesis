# Sosialisasi Kode Etik

Bank secara berkala melakukan kegiatan sosialisasi Kode Etik Perilaku melalui kegiatan internal serta menyediakan kebijakan kode etik yang dapat dibaca semua karyawan di portal intranet MyAllo. Sosialisasi kode etik ini bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kesadaran para karyawan untuk terus menjalankan kegiatan operasional secara beretika dan berintegritas.

# Budaya Perusahaan

Dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasional sehari-hari, seluruh Insan Perseroan juga menerapkan nilai-nilai perusahaan, yakni:

1. Data-driven: Kami mengambil keputusan dan memecahkan masalah berdasarkan data dan informasi yang kredibel;
2. Impact: Kami memberikan solusi dan dampak positif kepada semua stakeholders;
3. Growth: Kami terus menerus tumbuh dan berkembang secara berkesinambungan untuk masa depan yang lebih baik;
4. In harmony: Kami bekerjasama dengan semua pihak secara harmonis yang berorientasi pada hasil;
5. Transparency: Kami berperilaku jujur dan transparan dalam segala aspek pekerjaan;
6. Agility: Kami tangkas beradaptasi terhadap semua perubahan yang terjadi; dan
7. Learning: Kami terus belajar, berbagi dan menerapkan pengetahuan dan keterampilan baru untuk mengembangkan kompetensi diri.

# Dissemination of Code of Conduct

The Bank periodically conducts dissemination activities of the Code of Conduct through internal activities and provides a code of conduct policy that can be accessed by all employees on the MyAllo intranet portal. The dissemination aims to raise employee awareness to carry out operational activities ethically and with integrity.

# Corporate Culture

In carrying out daily operational activities, all employees of the Company adopt the following values:

1. Data-driven - We make decisions and solve problems based on credible data and information;
2. Impact - We provide positive solutions and impact to all stakeholders;
3. Growth - We continuously grow and develop sustainably for a better future;
4. In harmony - We collaborate with all parties harmoniously towards result-oriented;
5. Transparency - We are honest and transparent in all aspects of our work;
6. Agility - We are nimble in adapting to all changes that occur; and
7. Learning - We continuously learn, share, and apply new knowledge and skills to develop our competencies.

# Violations and Sanctions

Throughout 2024, the Bank found any violations of the Code of Conduct in 3 cases. All cases that occurred have been followed up by the Bank in accordance with applicable provisions.
