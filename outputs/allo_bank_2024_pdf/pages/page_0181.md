2. Kerangka Manajemen Risiko

Sebagai strategi pengelolaan risiko likuiditas, Bank mengidentifikasi dan memantau berbagai faktor utama yang mempengaruhi kemampuan Bank untuk memperoleh sumber pendanaan, termasuk sumber alternatif yang dapat memperkuat kapasitas untuk bertahan pada kondisi krisis. Fungsi pengelolaan kebutuhan likuiditas dilakukan oleh ALCO dan secara operasional oleh Divisi Treasury.

Kecukupan secondary reserves, strategi menurunkan dana jangka pendek dan berbiaya mahal masih terus dilakukan dengan mempertimbangkan maturity gap yang terukur dan seimbang. Pemantauan dan pengendalian terhadap kondisi likuiditas terus menjadi perhatian dari manajemen.

Direksi telah mendelegasikan wewenang dan tanggung jawab kepada Divisi Treasury dalam mengelola likuiditas Bank untuk memelihara Giro Wajib Minimum (GWM), Loan to Funding Ratio (LFR), serta memastikan pengelolaan secondary reserves sejalan dengan tujuan strategis, juga peran ALCO dalam menetapkan kebijakan dan strategi likuiditas.

Kebijakan manajemen risiko likuiditas Bank mencakup manajemen likuiditas, pemeliharaan cadangan likuiditas yang optimal, penetapan strategi pendanaan, proyeksi arus kas, profil maturitas, penetapan limit likuiditas, dan Rencana Pendanaan Darurat (Contingency Funding Plan). Kebijakan ini bertujuan untuk memastikan kecukupan dana harian dalam memenuhi kewajiban pada kondisi normal maupun kondisi krisis secara tepat waktu dari berbagai sumber dana yang tersedia.

3. Proses Manajemen Risiko

Dalam upaya mitigasi risiko likuiditas, Bank telah menerapkan Rencana Pendanaan Darurat (Contingency Funding Plan) dalam upaya memitigasi risiko. Bank menjaga cadangan likuiditas dengan mempertahankan jumlah aset likuid berkualitas tinggi yang cukup untuk memenuhi komitmennya kepada para nasabah dan pihak lainnya, baik dalam rangka pemberian kredit, pembayaran kembali simpanan nasabah, pemenuhan kebutuhan likuiditas dan menjaga agar jumlah aset yang jatuh tempo pada setiap periode dapat menutupi jumlah liabilitas yang jatuh tempo.

2. Risk Management Framework

As part of its liquidity risk management strategy, the Bank identifies and monitors various key factors that affect its own ability to obtain funding sources. This includes alternative sources that can strengthen its own capacity to survive in crisis conditions. The function of managing liquidity needs is carried out by ALCO and is operated by the Treasury Division.

The Bank ensures that it maintains adequacy in its secondary reserves. Strategies to reduce short-term and expensive funds are still being carried out by taking measurable and balanced maturity gaps into consideration. The processes of both monitoring and controlling liquidity conditions continue to be a source of concern for management.

The Board of Directors has delegated authority and responsibility to the Treasury Division for managing the Bank's liquidity and maintaining a Minimum Statutory Reserves and a Loan to Funding Ratio (LFR) to ensure that the management of secondary reserves is in line with strategic objectives. There is also the role of ALCO in determining liquidity policies and strategies.

The Bank's liquidity risk management policy includes liquidity management, maintaining optimal liquidity reserves, determining funding strategies, cash flow projections, maturity profiles, determining liquidity limits, and a Contingency Funding Plan. This policy is designed to ensure the Bank's adequacy when it comes to daily funds for meeting obligations under normal conditions and crisis conditions in a timely manner from various available funding sources.

3. Risk Management Process

In an effort to mitigate its own liquidity risk, the Bank has implemented its own Contingency Funding Plan. The Bank maintains liquidity reserves by maintaining a sufficient amount of high-quality liquid assets to meet its commitments to customers and other parties. It does this in the context of providing credit, repaying customer deposits, meeting liquidity needs and ensuring that the amount of assets due in each period can cover the amount of liabilities due.
