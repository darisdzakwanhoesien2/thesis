Risiko Pasar

Risiko pasar merupakan risiko yang timbul dari portofolio yang dimiliki oleh Bank karena adanya pergerakan adverse movement di pasar, termasuk risiko suku bunga dan risiko nilai tukar, yang berpotensi merugikan Bank. Dengan mempertimbangkan posisi Bank sebagai Bank Non-Devisa yang tidak melakukan kegiatan trading baik pada portofolio aset maupun kewajiban terhadap produk derivatif dan Fair Value Option, Bank tetap berkomitmen untuk selalu melakukan pemantauan terhadap potensi Risiko Pasar serta terus meningkatkan Kualitas Penerapan Manajemen Risiko Pasar dengan mengkaji kualitas pemenuhan serta kelengkapan dokumen secara berkala.

1. Tata Kelola Risiko

Pengelolaan risiko pasar Bank dilakukan dengan mempertimbangkan terbatasnya instrumen yang dikelola Bank serta rendahnya potensi risiko instrumen yang dimiliki. Untuk mengelola pergerakan variabel pasar, Bank telah melakukan Stress Testing pada instrumen suku bunga yang terekspos risiko pasar dalam rangka menghitung potensi dampak keuangan yang timbul. Selain itu, pengelolaan risiko pasar didukung oleh kerangka limit dan kebijakan untuk mengontrol risiko yang dapat diterima oleh Bank. Limit risiko pasar dialokasikan pada berbagai tingkatan dan dipantau oleh Divisi Treasury secara harian. Bank tidak terekspos secara signifikan pada risiko nilai tukar. Risiko suku bunga yang dikelola oleh Bank sebagian besar berasal dari posisi banking book dan tidak terdapat posisi trading book. Sebagai bagian dari pengelolaan risiko pasar, Bank melakukan identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan dan pengendalian risiko pasar, yang dilakukan oleh unit bisnis terkait.

4. Internal Control System

The Bank continues to put in efforts to improve its Internal Control System in terms of credit distribution. Operational functions and credit risk management functions have been clearly separated. The initiation process (ex ante) has been optimally reviewed by a team that analyzes credit risk issues that may arise, while the Internal Audit Unit carries out periodic reviews (ex post) to assess the implementation of credit risk management and internal control in functional activities that have credit risk exposure.

Market Risk

Market risk is defined as any risk that arises from the Bank's portfolio due to adverse movements in the market. These risks include interest rate risks and exchange rate risks that have the potential to harm the Bank. The Bank itself is a Non-Foreign Exchange Bank that does not conduct trading activities either in asset portfolios or liabilities for derivative products and Fair Value Options. It remains committed to always monitoring the potential for Market Risk and is always continuing to improve its Quality of Market Risk Management Implementation by periodically reviewing the quality of its documents.

1. Risk Governance

The Bank's market risk management is carried out by taking into consideration the Bank's limited instruments and the low potential risk of these instruments that it owns. To manage the movement of market variables, the Bank has conducted Stress Testing on instruments exposed to market risk in order to calculate any potential financial impact that arises. In addition, market risk management is supported by a framework of limits and policies to control any risks considered acceptable to the Bank. Market risk limits are allocated at various levels and are monitored by the Treasury Division on a daily basis. The Bank is not significantly exposed to exchange rate risk. The interest rate risk managed by the Bank is mostly derived from banking book positions. There are no trading book positions. As part of its market risk management, the Bank identifies, measures, monitors and controls market risk. This process is carried out by relevant business units.

The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors have a good sense of awareness and understanding of Market Risk management.
