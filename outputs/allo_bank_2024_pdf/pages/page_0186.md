2. Kerangka Manajemen Risiko

Strategi manajemen risiko reputasi dilakukan searah dengan risk appetite dan risk tolerance. Bank memiliki berbagai Kebijakan dan prosedur manajemen risiko reputasi Bank saat ini mulai menetapkan berbagai kebijakan dan prosedur yang relevan dengan arah bisnis Bank.

3. Proses Manajemen Risiko

Proses manajemen risiko reputasi berjalan baik dalam mengidentifikasi, mengukur, memantau dan mengendalikan risiko sesuai dengan Kebijakan dan SOP internal yang ada. Identifikasi risiko reputasi dilakukan pada faktor risiko yang melekat pada aktivitas fungsional yang mencakup keterbukaan informasi yang material, keluhan nasabah terhadap pelayanan Bank, sikap karyawan Bank dalam melayani nasabah, dan sistem komunikasi Bank. Pengukuran risiko reputasi dilakukan berdasarkan hasil assessment terhadap faktor-faktor yang berpengaruh pada reputasi Bank.

Bank telah memiliki unit Pengaduan Nasabah untuk mendukung layanan dan penanganan pengaduan nasabah dalam rangka pengendalian Risiko Reputasi. Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO bertanggung jawab atas penerapan kebijakan yang berkaitan dengan penanganan dan penyelesaian pemberitaan negatif atas menghindari informasi kontraproduktif serta menjalankan fungsi kepedulian dalam rangka melaksanakan Tanggung Jawab Sosial Perusahaan (Corporate Social Responsibility/CSR).

Di bidang pengembangan sumber daya manusia, Bank telah melaksanakan program pengembangan SDM di bidang pendidikan dan pelatihan bagi seluruh pegawai (sesuai job specification).

2. Risk Management Framework

The Bank's reputation risk management strategy is carried out in line with risk appetite and risk tolerance. The Bank has various reputation risk management policies and procedures. The Bank is currently starting to establish various policies and procedures that are relevant to the Bank's business direction.

3. Risk Management Process

The Bank's reputation risk management process does well in identifying, measuring, monitoring and controlling risks in accordance with existing internal policies and SOPs. Reputation risk identification is carried out on risk factors inherent in functional activities that include material information disclosure, customer complaints about Bank services, Bank employee attitudes in serving customers, and the Bank's communication system. Reputation risk measurement is carried out based on the results of an assessment of factors that influence the Bank's reputation.

The Bank has a Customer Complaints unit to support customer service and complaint handling in order to control Reputation Risk. The Corporate Secretary and PMO are responsible for implementing policies related to handling and resolving negative news or avoiding counterproductive information and carrying out the Bank's Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) function.

In the field of human resource development, the Bank has implemented a human resource development program in the field of education and training for all employees (that has been designed according to job specifications).

4. Risk Control System

The internal control system for reputation risk is carried out through periodic evaluation efforts of any reputation risk faced by the Bank. These evaluations are stated in media monitoring report by the Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO. The Bank also monitors the level of customer complaints resolved and carries out consistent communication through various conventional channels such as telephone and email. Monitoring is also carried out through various social media channels. One focus point of compliance risk control is to always maintain openness of information and transparency to all stakeholders, as well as to always establish harmonious relationships with the media to maintain the Bank's good name.
