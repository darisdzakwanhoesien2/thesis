|  Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Republik Indonesia No. 15 Tahun 2024 tentang Integritas Pelaporan Keuangan Bank. Regulation of the Financial Services Authority of the Republic of Indonesia No. 15 of 2024 concerning the Integrity of Bank Financial Reporting |  | Bank akan membentuk unit kerja khusus atau penunjukan pejabat eksekutif yang bertanggung jawab terhadap pencegahan kecurangan atau manipulasi dalam informasi keuangan dan/atau laporan keuangan bank dilaksanakan paling lama 6 (enam) bulan sejak POJK diundangkan (9 April 2025). The Bank will establish a special work unit or appoint an executive officer who is responsible for preventing fraud or manipulation in financial information and/or bank financial reports, implemented no later than 6 (six) months after the POJK is enacted (April 9, 2025).  |
