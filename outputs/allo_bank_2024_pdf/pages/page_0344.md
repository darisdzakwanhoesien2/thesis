# Penilaian Kinerja Organ dan Komite Pendukung di Bawah Direksi

Dalam menjalankan fungsi, tugas, dan tanggung jawab terkait pengelolaan Bank, Direksi turut dibantu oleh organ pendukung yang bersifat struktural maupun non struktural. Organ pendukung struktural Direksi terdiri dari Sekretaris Perusahaan &amp; PMO dan Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI), sementara organ pendukung non-struktural Direksi terdiri dari Komite Kredit, Komite Kebijakan Perkreditan, Komite Aktiva dan Pasiva, Komite Manajemen Risiko, Komite Pengarah Teknologi Informasi, Komite Produk dan Komite Sumber Daya Manusia.

Direksi menilai bahwa seluruh organ pendukung Direksi, baik organ pendukung struktural maupun non-struktural telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik. Direksi menilai bahwa seluruh organ pendukung telah membantu fungsi pengelolaan perusahaan dengan baik sehingga Bank mampu melalui dinamika yang terjadi pada tahun 2024.

# Performance Assessment of Supporting Organs and Committees under Board of Directors

In carrying out its functions, duties, and responsibilities related to the management of the Bank, the Board of Directors is assisted by both structural and non-structural supporting organs. The structural supporting organs of the Board of Directors consist of the Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO and the Internal Audit Work Unit (SKAI). Meanwhile, the non-structural supporting organs include the Credit Committee, Credit Policy Committee, Asset and Liability Committee, Risk Management Committee, Information Technology Steering Committee, Product Committee, and Human Resources Committee.

The Board of Directors assesses that all supporting organs, both structural and non-structural, have effectively carried out their duties and responsibilities. Additionally, the Board of Directors recognizes that these supporting organs have significantly contributed to the Bank's management function, enabling the Bank to successfully navigate business dynamics in 2024.
