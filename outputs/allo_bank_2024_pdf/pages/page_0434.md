d. Sistem Manajemen Informasi

Untuk mengoptimalkan pemantauan transaksi dalam pelaksanaan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM, Bank secara berkelanjutan mengembangkan teknologi sistem informasi, antara lain:

- Melakukan penyempurnaan sistem dan aplikasi pendukung untuk Anti Money Laundering Detection System, Filtering &amp; Screening Terrorist (DTTOT List), PEP Screening Detection dan Customer Due Diligence Risk Scoring, baik secara batch maupun realtime. AML System digunakan untuk mendukung pelaksanaan Prinsip Mengenal Nasabah yang lebih Optimal, khususnya dalam pendeteksian/pemantauan transaksi nasabah yang memiliki pola perilaku dan pola transaksi pencucian uang dan pendanaan terorisme.
- Pengembangan aplikasi pemanfaatan data Politically Exposed Person (PEP) PPATK;
- Pengembangan aplikasi KYC untuk melakukan filtering dan screening calon nasabah terhadap Data Teroris/Terrorist List, Daftar Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal, PEP List, OFAC List, UN List dan Fraudulent List;

e. Sumber Daya Manusia dan Pelatihan

Bank secara berkesinambungan menyelenggarakan pelatihan, sosialisasi dan Reminder mengenai penerapan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM kepada seluruh pegawai secara berkesinambungan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi, antara lain meliputi:

- Pelatihan, metode pelatihan dapat dilakukan secara elektronik/online base, maupun melalui tatap muka.
- Pelaksanaan Supervisi dan Sosialisasi penerapan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM.
- Penyampaian Email Blast terkait tipologi, modus terbaru dan lesson learned terkait APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM kepada Pegawai Bank.

- Internal Control; and
- Human Resources and Training.

c. Effective internal control in implementing AML-CFT &amp; CPF includes, among other things, carried out by:

- Having AML-CFT &amp; CPF policies and procedures as well as adequate internal monitoring;
- There are clear boundaries of authority and responsibility for work units related to the implementation of the AMLCTF &amp; CPF program either through the first line of defense (risk taking unit), second line of defense and third line of defense; and
- Conducting inspections to ensure the effectiveness of the implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program by the Internal Audit Work Unit.
- Development of an internal application system to support reporting obligations in accordance with the PPATK GoAML reporting format requirements;
- Development of a GoAML interface system to support reporting obligations that have been adjusted to GoAML provisions;

d. Information Management System

To optimize transaction monitoring in the implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program, the Bank continuously develops information system technology, including:

- Improve the supporting systems and applications for the Anti Money Laundering Detection System, Terrorist Filtering &amp; Screening (DTTOT List), PEP Screening Detection and Customer Due Diligence Risk Scoring, both in batch and real time. The AML System is used to support more optimal implementation of Know Your Customer Principles, especially in detecting/monitoring customer transactions that have money laundering and terrorism financing behavior and transaction patterns.
- Developing an application for utilizing PPATK Politically Exposed Person (PEP) data;
- Developing a KYC application to filter and screen prospective customers against Terrorist Data/Terrorist List, Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction List, PEP List, OFAC List, UN List and Fraudulent List;

e. Human Resources and Training

The Bank continuously provides training, outreach and reminders regarding the implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program to all employees on an ongoing basis to improve competency, including:

- Training, training methods can be carried out electronically/online, or face to face.
- Implementation of Supervision and Socialization of the implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program
- Submission of Email Blast regarding typology, latest modes and lessons learned regarding AML-CFT &amp; CPF to Bank Employees.
