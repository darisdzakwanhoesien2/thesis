## 22. Liabilitas Imbalan Pasca-Kerja

Sesuai dengan Undang-undang Republik Indonesia No. 11/2020 tentang Cipta Kerja, Bank wajib memberikan imbalan pasca-kerja kepada karyawannya pada saat pemutusan hubungan kerja atau pada saat karyawan menyelesaikan masa kerjanya. Imbalan pasca kerja ini diberikan berdasarkan masa kerja dan kompensasi karyawan pada saat pemutusan hubungan kerja atau selesainya masa kerja. Imbalan pasca-kerja ini merupakan program imbalan pasti Liabilitas imbalan pasti atas imbalan pasca-kerja (post-employment benefit) tersebut dihitung dengan menggunakan metode "Projected Unit Credit".

## 22. Post-Employment Benefits Liabilities

In accordance with the law of the Republic of Indonesia No. 11/2020 on job creation, banks are obliged to provide post-employment benefits to their employees upon termination or when the employee completes his / her period of employment. This post-employment benefit is provided based on the employee's length of Service and compensation upon termination or completion of the employment period. This post-employment benefit is a defined benefit plan defined benefit liability for post-employment benefits is calculated using the "Projected Unit Credit" method.
