# Kegiatan Usaha dan Pasar yang Dilayani Business Activities and Markets Served

## Kegiatan Usaha Berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar

Berdasarkan Pasal 3 Anggaran Dasar Bank yang terakhir diubah dan disahkan melalui Akta No. 01 tanggal 14 Juni 2023 yang dibuat di hadapan Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notaris di Jakarta yang telah memperoleh persetujuan dari Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia dengan Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0039698.AH.01.02.Tahun 2023 tanggal 12 Juli 2023 dan telah didaftarkan dalam Daftar Perseroan No. AHU-0131254.AH.01.11 tanggal 12 Juli 2023, maksud dan tujuan Bank adalah melakukan usaha di bidang perbankan sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, serta optimalisasi pemanfaatan sumber daya yang dimiliki Bank untuk menghasilkan barang dan/atau jasa yang bermutu tinggi dan berdaya saing kuat untuk mendapat/mengejar keuntungan guna meningkatkan nilai Bank dengan menerapkan prinsip-prinsip Perseroan Terbatas.

## Produk dan Layanan

Di tahun 2024, Bank senantiasa mengembangkan layanan perbankan digital untuk tabungan dan fitur-fitur pembayaran, meliputi:

1. Produk uang elektronik dengan branding Allo Pay dan Allo Pay+
2. Produk tabungan termasuk Allo Prime dan Allo Grow
3. Deposito
4. Kredit digital dengan branding PayLater dan Instant Cash
5. Layanan Transfer Dana, tarik tunai tanpa kartu, top up uang elektronik, pembayaran QRIS, layanan pembayaran biller dan program loyalty berupa Membership, Point, dan Coupon (MPC).
6. Rekening Dana Nasabah (RDN)

## Business Activities and Markets Served

Based on Article 3 of the Bank's Articles of Association, which was last amended and ratified through Deed No. 01 dated June 14, 2023 which was made before Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notary in Jakarta, and approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia with Decree No. AHU-0039698. AH.01.02.Year 2023 dated July 12, 2023 and has been registered in the Company Register No. AHU-0131254. AH.01.11 dated July 12, 2023. The aim and objective of the Bank is to conduct business in the banking sector in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations. The Bank also aims to optimize the utilization of its resources to produce high-quality goods and/or services with strong competitiveness, aiming to generate profits and increase the value of the Bank by applying the principles of Limited Liability Companies.

## Products and Services

Throughout 2024, the Bank continued to develop digital banking services for savings and payment features, including:

1. Electronic money products with Allo Pay and Allo Pay+ branding
2. Savings products include Allo Prime and Allo Grow
3. Time Deposits
4. Digital credit with PayLater and Instant Cash branding
5. Fund Transfer Services, cardless cash withdrawals, electronic money top ups, QRIS payments, biller payment services and loyalty programs in the form of Membership, Points and Coupons (MPC).
6. Customer Fund Account (RDN)
