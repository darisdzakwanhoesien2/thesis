# Organ Pendukung Dewan Komisaris

## Board of Commissioners Supporting Organ

Dewan Komisaris senantiasa dibantu oleh 3 (tiga) Komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris, yaitu Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, serta Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi. Pembentukan komite tersebut ditujukan untuk membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam melaksanakan tugas pengawasan.

## Komite Audit

Komite Audit merupakan organ pendukung yang bertugas untuk mendukung pelaksanaan tugas Dewan Komisaris dalam memantau dan mengevaluasi perencanaan maupun pelaksanaan audit dan kecukupan proses laporan pelaporan keuangan termasuk memberikan rekomendasi penunjukan akuntan publik serta melakukan pengawasan terhadap efektivitasnya.

## Dasar Hukum

Pembentukan Komite Audit mengacu pada peraturan perundang-undangan dan best practice yang diterapkan di lembaga perbankan di Indonesia, di antaranya:

1. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("POJK") POJK No. 33/POJK.04/2014 tanggal 8 Desember 2014 tentang Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.
2. POJK No. 17/POJK.03/2023 tanggal 14 September 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum.
3. POJK No. 13/POJK.03/2017 tanggal 27 Maret 2017 tentang Penggunaan Jasa Akuntan Publik dan Kantor Akuntan Publik dalam Kegiatan Jasa Keuangan.
4. POJK No. 1/POJK.03/2019 tanggal 28 Januari 2019 tentang Penerapan Fungsi Audit Intern pada Bank Umum.
5. SEOJK No. 36/SEOJK.03/2017 tentang Tata Cara Penggunaan Jasa Akuntan Publik dan Kantor Akuntan Publik dalam Kegiatan Jasa Keuangan.
6. Anggaran Dasar Bank.

The Board of Commissioners is always assisted by 3 (three) Committees, namely the Audit Committee, the Risk Oversight Committee, and the Remuneration and Nomination Committee. The establishment of these committees is intended to assist the Board of Commissioners in performing its supervisory duties effectively.

## Audit Committee

The Audit Committee is a supporting organ of the Board of Commissioners whose task is to support the implementation of the Board of Commissioners' duties in monitoring and evaluating the planning and implementation of audits and the adequacy of the financial reporting process, including providing recommendations for the appointment of public accountants and supervising their effectiveness.

## Legal Basis

The formation of the Audit Committee is guided by and based on applicable laws and regulations as well as best practices implemented in banking institutions in Indonesia, including:

1. Financial Services Authority Regulation ("POJK") POJK No. 33/POJK.04/2014 dated December 8, 2014 concerning the Directors and Board of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies.
2. POJK No. 17/POJK.03/2023 dated September 14, 2023 concerning Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank.
3. POJK No. 13/POJK.03/2017 dated March 27, 2017 concerning the Use of Public Accounting Services and Public Accounting Firms in Financial Services Activities.
4. POJK No. 1/POJK.03/2019 dated January 28, 2019 concerning Implementation of the Internal Audit Function in Conventional Bank.
5. SEOJK No. 36/SEOJK.03/2017 concerning Procedures for Using the Services of Public Accountants and Public Accounting Firms in Financial Services Activities.
6. Bank Articles of Association.
