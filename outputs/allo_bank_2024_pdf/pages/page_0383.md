## Peningkatan Kompetensi Komite Pemantau Risiko

Allo Bank senantiasa melaksanakan program peningkatan kompetensi bagi Komite Pemantau Risiko guna mengoptimalkan seluruh tugasnya dan terus mengembangkan kemampuannya sesuai dengan kondisi dan dinamika bisnis yang terjadi. Informasi mengenai peningkatan kompetensi Komite Pemantau Risiko sepanjang tahun 2024 dapat dilihat pada Bab Profil Perusahaan dalam Laporan Tahunan ini.

## Laporan Pelaksanaan Tugas Pemantau Risiko

Di tahun 2024, Komite Pemantau Risiko telah melaksanakan tugasnya melalui sejumlah kegiatan, di antaranya:

1. Memantau dan melakukan evaluasi terhadap delapan tipe risiko Utama Bank, bukan hanya Risiko Kredit, Risiko Operasional (khususnya cybersecurity) dan Risiko Reputasi yang dilihat material mengingat positioning Bank sebagai bank umum berbasis digital, namun juta risiko Risiko Likuiditas, Risiko Pasar, Risiko Hukum, Risiko Kepatuhan, Risiko Strategik.

## Competency Development of Risk Oversight Committee

Allo Bank always implements competency improvement programs for the Risk Oversight Committee in order to optimize all its duties and continue to develop its capabilities in accordance with the current business conditions and dynamics. Information regarding the improvement of the Risk Oversight Committee's competency throughout 2024 can be seen in the Company Profile Chapter in this Annual Report.

## Implementation Report of Risk Oversight Committee Duties

In 2024, the Risk Oversight Committee implemented its duties through several activities, as follows:

1. Monitor and evaluate the eight types of Main Bank risks, not only Credit Risk, Operational Risk (especially cybersecurity) and Reputation Risk which are considered material considering the Bank's positioning as a digital-based conventional bank, but also Liquidity Risk, Market Risk, Legal Risk, Compliance Risk, Strategic Risk.
