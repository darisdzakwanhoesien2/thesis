4. Sistem Pengendalian Risiko

Pengendalian risiko likuiditas dilakukan oleh Divisi Treasury sebagai tindak lanjut hasil pemantauan kinerja dan kepatuhan unit kerja operasional terhadap limit likuiditas yang telah ditetapkan. Secara khusus, Divisi Treasury memperhatikan indikator peringatan dini (early-warning indicators) dalam memantau posisi likuiditas sebagai bagian dari proses manajemen likuiditas serta untuk mengetahui peningkatan potensi risiko dalam menghadapi kondisi normal maupun kondisi tidak normal, yang meliputi strategi pendanaan mencakup diversifikasi sumber dan jangka waktu pendanaan untuk mendukung rencana bisnis Bank.

Tanggapan terhadap temuan dan hasil kaji ulang sudah dilakukan dengan optimal, dimana dual control system telah diperbaiki dari sisi mekanisme dan prosedur, serta perbaikan pada fungsi pengawasan. Kaji ulang independen dilakukan termasuk adanya perbaikan dalam aspek metodologi.

Risiko Operasional

Risiko operasional adalah risiko yang disebabkan adanya ketidakcukupan dan/atau tidak berfungsinya proses internal (kebijakan, sistem dan prosedur), kesalahan sistem, kesalahan manusia, atau adanya problem eksternal yang mempengaruhi operasional Bank.

1. Tata Kelola Risiko

Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi memiliki awareness dan pemahaman yang baik mengenai manajemen Risiko Operasional. Budaya manajemen Risiko Operasional telah diinternalisasikan dengan baik pada seluruh level organisasi. Penerapan manajemen risiko operasional dilakukan melalui desain struktur organisasi yang menggambarkan keterlibatan seluruh pihak yang terkait manajemen risiko operasional, yaitu Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Komite Manajemen Risiko, Satuan Kerja Manajemen Risiko, Unit Kerja Operasional, Fungsi Manajemen Risiko Operasional, dan Satuan Kerja Audit Internal.

Bank juga telah memiliki Product Forum pada level operasional untuk mendiskusikan semua inisiatif produk dan aktivitas baru sebelum dilakukan eskalasi melalui Komite Produk pada level Direksi. Hal ini dilakukan secara cepat berdasarkan prinsip agile dalam pengembangan produk Bank, sehingga setiap risiko baru dapat diantisipasi dan dilakukan mitigasi risiko sedini mungkin.

The Bank's Early Warning System (EWS) serves as one of the information systems that it uses in the event of a worsening situation that could endanger the Bank's liquidity position. This system is always being improved upon, considering that EWS represents an analysis methodology that can be used to detect a liquidity problem and for evaluating the Bank's funding needs and strategies during a crisis.

4. Risk Control System

Liquidity risk control is carried out by the Treasury Division as a follow-up action to the monitoring results from the performance and compliance of operational work units with the established liquidity limits. The Treasury Division specifically pays attention to early warning indicators in monitoring liquidity positions as part of the Bank's liquidity management process and as a way to determine any increase in potential risks in facing normal and abnormal conditions. This includes funding strategies such as diversification of sources and funding terms to support the Bank's business plan.

Responses made to any review findings and results have been carried out optimally. The Bank's dual control system has also been improved in terms of mechanisms and procedures, as well as in terms of improvements in the supervisory function. Independent reviews are carried out on aspects such as improvements in methodologies.

Operational Risk

Operational risk is defined any risk caused by inadequacy and/or non-functioning of internal processes (policies, systems and procedures), system errors, human error, or external problems that affect the Bank's operations.

1. Risk Governance

The Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors have a good sense of awareness and understanding when it comes to Operational Risk management. The culture of Operational Risk management has been well internalized at all levels of the organization. The implementation of operational risk management is carried out through the design of an organizational structure that describes the involvement of all parties related to operational risk management. These parties encompass the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, Risk Management Committee, Risk Management Work Unit, Operational Work Unit, Operational Risk Management Function, and Internal Audit Work Unit.

The Bank also has a Product Forum at the operational level to discuss all new product initiatives and activities before being escalated through the Product Committee at the level of the Board of Directors. This is done quickly based on an agility principle in the Bank's product development, ensuring that every new risk can be anticipated and that risk mitigation carried out as early as possible.
