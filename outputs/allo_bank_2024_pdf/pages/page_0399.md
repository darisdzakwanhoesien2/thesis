## Laporan Pelaksanaan Tugas Komite Pengarah Teknologi Informasi

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Komite Pengarah Teknologi Informasi telah melaksanakan berbagai kegiatan sesuai tugas dan kewenangannya, antara lain:

1. Persetujuan usulan baik penetapan maupun revisi terhadap rencana pengembangan teknologi informasi pada tahun 2024 dan tahun 2025.
2. Evaluasi realisasi CAPEX dan OPEX TI periode tahun 2024.
3. Persetujuan usulan alokasi CAPEX dan OPEX TI periode tahun 2025.
4. Evaluasi hasil kinerja TI periode tahun 2024.
5. Persetujuan usulan penetapan standar teknologi pengembangan aplikasi, infrastruktur TI, dan keamanan TI.
6. Persetujuan usulan penetapan hasil pelaksanaan dan implementasi standar keamanan ISO 27001 information security management system (ISMS).
7. Persetujuan usulan pengadaan perangkat infrastruktur TI.
8. Persetujuan usulan pelaksanaan uji coba rencana pemulihan bencana (Disaster Recovery Plan/DRP).
9. Persetujuan usulan penetapan hasil penilaian Tingkat Maturitas Digital Bank (DMAB) tahun 2023 dan tahun 2024.

## Implementation Report of Duties of Information Technology Steering Committee

Throughout 2024, the Information Technology Steering Committee carried out various activities in accordance with its duties and authorities, including:

1. Approval of proposals for both determination and revision of the information technology development plan in 2024 and 2025.
2. Evaluation of the realization of IT CAPEX and OPEX for the period 2024.
3. Approval of proposals for allocation of IT CAPEX and OPEX for the period 2025.
4. Evaluation of IT performance results for the period 2024.
5. Approval of proposals for determination of standards for application development technology, IT infrastructure, and IT security.
6. Approval of proposals for determination of the results of the implementation and implementation of the ISO 27001 information security management system (ISMS) security standard.
7. Approval of proposals for procurement of IT infrastructure devices.
8. Approval of proposals for the implementation of the disaster recovery plan (DRP) trial.
9. Approval of proposals for determination of the results of the Bank's Digital Maturity Level (DMAB) assessment in 2023 and 2024.
