# Direksi Board of Directors

Direksi adalah organ utama yang secara kolektif berwenang dan bertanggung jawab penuh atas pengurusan perusahaan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan untuk kepentingan Bank. Direksi juga bertanggung jawab mewakili Bank, baik di dalam maupun di luar pengadilan sesuai dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar dan ketentuan peraturan perundangan-undangan. Direksi juga memiliki wewenang untuk mengatur jalannya Bank, mengambil tindakan dan kebijakan yang dianggap perlu untuk memastikan kelancaran operasional Bank dengan memperhatikan aspek efisiensi dan efektivitas.

Setiap anggota Direksi juga wajib menjalakan fungsi pengurusan perusahaan dengan mengedepankan prinsip-prinsip profesionalisme, efisiensi, transparansi, kemandirian, akuntabilitas, pertanggungjawaban serta kewajaran sesuai dengan prinsip tata kelola yang diadopsi oleh Bank. Setiap anggota Direksi Bank juga wajib melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan itikad baik, penuh tanggung jawab, dan kehati-hatian, sesuai dengan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

## Persyaratan Anggota Direksi

1. Setiap anggota Direksi harus memenuhi persyaratan penilaian kemampuan dan kepatutan sesuai Peraturan OJK mengenai Penilaian Kemampuan dan Kepatutan bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, yaitu persyaratan integritas, kompetensi dan reputasi keuangan. Persyaratan tersebut harus dipenuhi anggota Direksi pada saat diangkat dan selama menjabat.
2. Persyaratan integritas yang harus dipenuhi, antara lain:
a. Memiliki akhlak dan moral yang baik, paling sedikit ditunjukan dengan sikap mematuhi ketentuan yang berlaku, termasuk tidak pernah dihukum karena terbukti melakukan tindak pidana dalam jangka waktu tertentu sebelum dicalonkan.

The Board of Directors is the main organ that collectively holds full authority and responsibility for managing the company in accordance with its objectives and in the best interest of the Bank. The Board of Directors is also responsible for representing the Bank both in and outside the court, in accordance with the provisions of the Articles of Association and applicable laws and regulations. Additionally, the Board of Directors has the authority to regulate the Bank's operations, implement policies, and take necessary actions to ensure the smooth functioning of the Bank while considering aspects of efficiency and effectiveness.

Each member of the Board of Directors is required to manage the company while upholding the principles of professionalism, efficiency, transparency, independence, accountability, responsibility, and fairness, in accordance with the governance principles adopted by the Bank. Furthermore, each member of the Board of Directors must perform their duties and responsibilities with good faith, full accountability, and prudence, in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

## Requirements for Members of the Board of Directors

1. Each member of the Board of Directors must meet the requirements for assessing their ability and suitability according to the OJK Regulation regarding the Assessment of Capability and Suitability for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, including requirements for integrity, competence, and financial reputation. These requirements must be met by the member of the Board of Directors at the time of appointment and throughout their tenure.
2. The integrity requirements that must be met include:
a. Have good ethics and morals, demonstrated at least by adhering to applicable regulations, including not having been convicted of a criminal offense within a certain period before being nominated.
