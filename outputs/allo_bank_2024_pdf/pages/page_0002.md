# Strengthening Foundation for Competitive Sustainable Growth

Iklim usaha di sepanjang tahun 2024 masih masih dihadapkan dengan situasi yang penuh dengan ketidakpastian. Namun demikian, Allo Bank sebagai bank umum berbasis digital senantiasa fokus untuk memperkuat pondasi bisnis yang dimiliki agar mampu mencapai pertumbuhan secara berkelanjutan di masa depan ("Strengthening Foundation for Competitive Sustainable Growth"). Dalam upaya untuk menghadirkan Allo Bank sebagai everyday bank yang dapat menjawab segala kebutuhan nasabah, Bank terus mengembangkan berbagai inisiatif strategis termasuk kolaborasi dengan berbagai ekosistem, mengembangkan produk dan layanan inovatif dan tanpa melupakan Good Corporate Governance dan manajemen risiko yang menjadi disiplin prudential yang menjadi ciri khas Allo Bank. Allo Bank berkomitmen untuk terus mengembangkan produk dan layanan perbankan digital secara optimal. Layanan tersebut dilakukan melalui kolaborasi dengan ekosistem baik di dalam maupun diluar Group CT Corpora, dengan cara memperkuat cakupan infrastruktur physical &amp; digital (phygital) dan mengembangkan kapabilitas teknologi dan data terbaik agar Bank dapat terus meningkatkan akuisisi dan engagement serta kualitas layanan nasabah.

The business climate throughout 2024 remained one that was filled with uncertainty. Nevertheless, Allo Bank (as a digital-based commercial bank) is always staying focused on strengthening its business foundations so that it can to achieve sustainable growth in the future (as part of its "Strengthening Foundation for Competitive Sustainable Growth" slogan). The Bank has continued to develop various strategic initiatives in an effort to present Allo Bank as an everyday bank that can answer all of its customer needs. This includes the Bank's collaborations with various ecosystems and its efforts at developing innovative products and services, all while ensuring that Good Corporate Governance and risk management remained prudential disciplines that served as Allo Bank hallmark qualities. Allo Bank is committed to continuing with its efforts to optimally develop its digital banking products and services through collaborations with ecosystems both within and outside CT Corpora Group. They have also been carried out by the Bank's efforts at strengthening its coverage of "physical and digital" (or "phygital") infrastructure and by its efforts at developing the best in technology and data capabilities so that it can continue to improve its acquisitions and engagement, as well as improve its customer service quality.
