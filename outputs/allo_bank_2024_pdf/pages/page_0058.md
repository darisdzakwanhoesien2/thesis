Setelah lebih dari 2 (dua) dekade, Bank selanjutnya mengambil langkah strategis dengan melakukan Penawaran Saham Perdana di Bursa Efek Indonesia pada tahun 2015 sehingga menjadikan Bank berubah statusnya menjadi perusahaan terbuka dengan nama PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk. Perubahan ini dinyatakan dalam Akta Berita Acara Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa No. 31 tanggal 18 Maret 2015 yang dibuat di hadapan Edward Suharjo Wiryomartani., S.H., M.Kn., Notaris di Jakarta Barat dan telah memperoleh Penerimaan Pemberitahuan Perubahan Anggaran Dasar dari Menkumham melalui Surat No. AHU AH.01.03-0017606 tanggal 19 Maret 2015. Pencatatan saham di BEI ini bertujuan untuk memperkuat struktur keuangan dan meningkatkan daya saing usaha di dunia perbankan.

Pada tahun 2021, PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk resmi diakuisisi oleh PT Mega Corpora. Setelah mendapat persetujuan dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, pada Juni 2021, PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk resmi berganti nama menjadi PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk. Perubahan nama tersebut dinyatakan dalam Akta Perubahan Anggaran Dasar No. 1 tanggal 2 Juni 2021 yang dibuat di hadapan Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notaris di Jakarta, dan akta tersebut telah memperoleh persetujuan dari Menkumham No. AHU-0031692. AH.01.02 tahun 2021 tanggal 2 Juni 2021. Perubahan nama menjadi PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk telah mendapat persetujuan dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan berdasarkan Keputusan Deputi Dewan Komisioner Pengawas Perbankan I Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. KEP-48/PB-1/2021 tanggal 30 Juni 2021 tentang Penetapan Penggunaan Izin Usaha Atas Nama PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk Menjadi Izin Usaha Atas Nama PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk.

After more than 2 (two) decades, the Bank took strategic steps by conducting an Initial Share Offering on the Indonesia Stock Exchange in 2015. Through these offering, the Bank changed its status to a public company under the name PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk. These changes were stated in the Deed of Minutes of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders No. 31 dated March 18, 2015 which was made before Edward Suharjo Wiryomartani., S.H., M.Kn., Notary in West Jakarta and received the Notification of Amendments to the Articles of Association from the Minister of Law and Human Rights through Letter No. AHU-AH.01.03-0017606 dated March 19 2015. The aim of listing shares on the IDX is to strengthen the financial structure and increase business competitiveness in the banking world.

In 2021, PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk was officially acquired by PT Mega Corpora. After obtaining approval from the Financial Services Authority, in June 2021, PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk officially changed its name to PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk. The name change is stated in the Deed of Amendment to the Articles of Association No. 1 dated June 2, 2021 which was made before Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notary in Jakarta, and the deed was approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights No. AHU-0031692.AH.01.02 of 2021 dated June 2, 2021. The name change to PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk has received approval from the Financial Services Authority based on Decree of the Deputy Board of Commissioners for Banking Supervision I of the Financial Services Authority No. KEP-48/PB-1/2021 dated June 30, 2021 concerning Stipulation of Using of a Business License on behalf of PT Bank Harda Internasional Tbk to Become a Business License on behalf of PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk.
