Rencana dan Kebijakan Strategis Bank Tahun 2024

Industri perbankan nasional menghadapi berbagai tantangan di sepanjang tahun 2024. Selain ketepatan dalam memanfaatkan kebijakan pemerintah untuk pemulihan ekonomi pasca pandemi atau post pandemic policy responses, perbankan juga menghadapi tantangan berupa spillover effect tensi geopolitik yang masih tinggi. Sumber resmi Otoritas Jasa Keuangan menyatakan kondisi tersebut mendorong volatilitas harga komoditas dan energi, lonjakan inflasi dan kenaikan suku bunga era higher for longer, serta adanya potensi perlambatan ekonomi.

Tantangan berikutnya adalah perkembangan yang sangat luar biasa dalam konteks digitalization berupa percepatan digitalisasi dan emerging technology serta aspek kesiapan industri baik dari sisi people, dari proses, maupun teknologi. Tantangan yang tak kalah penting adalah risiko keuangan terkait dengan iklim (climate related risks) serta upaya untuk mencapai target net zero emission.

Menghadapi berbagai tantangan tersebut, Allo Bank mengimplementasikan sejumlah strategi inovatif untuk memastikan pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan. Salah satu langkah kunci adalah memperkuat kolaborasi dengan berbagai mitra strategis di dalam maupun di luar ekosistem CT Corpora, yang memungkinkan Bank untuk memanfaatkan basis pelanggan yang luas. Melalui penerapan model open banking, Allo Bank berupaya untuk meningkatkan layanan finansialnya sehingga memberikan kemudahan bagi nasabah dalam bertransaksi di era digital.

Selaras dengan itu, Bank secara konsisten mendayagunakan infrastruktur phygital (physical and digital) yang memberikan berbagai macam keuntungan bagi nasabah. Dalam hal ini, infrastruktur phygital Allo Bank menggabungkan aplikasi Mobile Banking Allo Bank dengan jaringan kanal fisik antara lain TransMart, Metro, Mitra Bukalapak, dan Indomaret.

Sesuai dengan Rencana Bisnis Bank, selama tahun 2024, Allo Bank fokus pada pengembangan beberapa ekosistem utama melalui akselerasi adopsi teknologi & transformasi digital. Melalui OJK also reported that credit quality remained well-managed, with a gross Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio of 2.19% (October 2024: 2.20%) and a net NPL of 0.75% (October 2024: 0.77%). The Loan at Risk (LaR) ratio showed a downward trend, decreasing to 9.82% in November 2024 (October 2024: 9.94%). Notably, this ratio was lower than the pre-pandemic level of 9.93% recorded in December 2019. Overall, banking profitability, as measured by Return on Assets (ROA), stood at 2.69% (October 2024: 2.73%), indicating that the banking industry remains resilient and stable.

Furthermore, the banking sector's capital resilience remained strong, as reflected in the high Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) of 26.92% (October 2024: 27.02%). Although slightly lower due to an increase in ATMR driven by credit growth, the sector's solid capital base continues to serve as a robust risk mitigation buffer amid ongoing global uncertainties.

Bank's Strategic Plan and Policy for 2024.

The national banking industry faced various challenges throughout 2024. In addition to the need for precise utilization of government policies for post-pandemic economic recovery, the banking sector also encountered spillover effects from persistent geopolitical tensions. According to official sources from the Financial Services Authority (OJK), these conditions contributed to volatility in commodity and energy prices, surges in inflation, and prolonged high interest rates, as well as the potential for an economic slowdown.

Another challenge was the rapid advancement of digitalization, including the acceleration of emerging technologies and the industry's readiness in terms of people, processes, and technology. Equally important were climate-related financial risks and efforts to achieve the net-zero emissions target.

To navigate these challenges, Allo Bank implemented a range of innovative strategies to ensure sustainable growth. A key initiative was strengthening collaboration with strategic partners, both within and beyond the CT Corpora ecosystem, allowing the Bank to leverage its extensive customer base. Through the implementation of the open banking model, Allo Bank aimed to enhance financial services, offering customers greater convenience in conducting transactions in the digital era.

Additionally, the Bank continued to optimize its phygital (physical and digital) infrastructure, providing numerous benefits to customers. This approach integrates the Allo Bank Mobile Banking application with a network of physical service points, including TransMart, Metro, Mitra Bukalapak, and Indomaret, ensuring seamless and accessible banking services.
