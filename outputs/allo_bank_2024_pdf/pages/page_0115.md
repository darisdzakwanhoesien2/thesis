7. On July 4, 2018, the Company conducted Limited Public Offering to increase the capital, by issuing Pre-emptive Rights to the Company's shareholders for as many as 169,431,795 shares with a price of Rp200 per share.
8. The Company conducted Limited Public Offering II on July 14, 2021 to increase the capital by issuing Pre-emptive Rights to the Company's shareholders for as many as 7,498,501,776 ordinary shares or $64.18\%$ of the issued and fully paid capital of the Company after Limited Public Offering II at the nominal value of Rp100 per share. Thus, the amount that the Company received in this PMHMETD is Rp749,850,177,600.
9. The Company conducted Limited Public Offering III in 2022 to increase the capital by issuing Pre-emptive Rights to the Company's shareholders for as many as 10,047,322,871 ordinary shares or $46.24\%$ of the issued and fully paid capital of the Company after Limited Public Offering III at the nominal value of Rp100 per share with an exercise price of Rp478 per share. Thus, the amount that the Company received in this PMHMETD III is Rp4,802,620,332,338.
