# Komposisi dan Struktur Keanggotaan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi

Ketentuan keanggotaan Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi Bank ditetapkan sebagai berikut:

1. Komposisi keanggotaan Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi wajib beranggotakan paling sedikit:
a. 1 (satu) orang Komisaris Independen sebagai Ketua merangkap anggota;
b. 1 (satu) orang Komisaris Non-Independen;
c. 1 (satu) orang Pejabat Eksekutif yang membawahi fungsi Sumber Daya Manusia.

2. Anggota Direksi Bank maupun Bank lain dilarang menjadi anggota Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi.

3. Dalam hal Anggota Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi ditetapkan lebih dari 3 (tiga) orang, maka Komisaris Independen paling sedikit berjumlah 2 (dua) orang.

4. Ketua Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi hanya dapat merangkap jabatan sebagai ketua Komite paling banyak pada 1 (satu) Komite lainnya.

# Composition and Membership Structure of Remuneration and Nomination Committee

The provisions for membership of the Bank's Remuneration and Nomination Committee are determined as follows:

1. The membership composition of the Remuneration and Nomination Committee must consist of at least:
a. 1 (one) Independent Commissioner as Chairperson and member;
b. 1 (one) Non-Independent Commissioner;
c. 1 (one) Executive Officer in charge of Human Resources functions.

2. Members of the Board of Directors of the Bank or other Banks are prohibited from being members of the Remuneration and Nomination Committee.

3. In the event that the members of the Remuneration and Nomination Committee are determined to be more than 3 (three) people, the number of Independent Commissioners shall be at least 2 (two) people.

4. The Head of the Remuneration and Nomination Committee can only hold the same position as Committee Chair in a maximum of 1 (one) other Committee.
