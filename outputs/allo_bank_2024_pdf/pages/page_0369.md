3. Independence Requirements:
a. Do not own Bank shares, either directly or indirectly.
b. In the event that a member of the Audit Committee acquires the Bank shares either directly or indirectly as a result of a legal incident, the shares must be transferred to another party within a period of no later than 6 (six) months after acquiring the shares;
c. Not a person in a Public Accounting Firm, Legal Consultant Firm, Public Appraisal Services Firm or other party providing insurance services, non-insurance services, appraisal services and/or other consulting services to the Bank within the last 6 (six) months;
d. Have no affiliation with the Bank, Commissioners, Directors and main shareholders of the Bank.
e. Have no direct or indirect business relationship with the Bank.
f. Former members of the Board of Directors or Executive Officers of the Bank or parties who have a relationship with the Bank that can influence the person's ability to act independently are prohibited from becoming Independent Parties as members of the committee at the Bank concerned before undergoing a waiting period of at least 6 (six) months.
g. The shortest waiting period of 6 (six) months does not apply to former members of the Board of Directors who are in charge of supervisory functions or Executive Officers who carry out supervisory functions at the Bank.

4. Confidentiality:
a. Audit Committee members are required to maintain the confidentiality of all Bank documents and/or information as well as everything related to the Bank and the implementation of the Committee's duties. The classification of confidentiality of Bank documents and/or information refers to internal provisions and applicable laws and regulations.
b. The obligation to keep confidential also applies if the term of office as a member of the Audit Committee or term of office as a member of the Board of Commissioners at the Bank ends.
