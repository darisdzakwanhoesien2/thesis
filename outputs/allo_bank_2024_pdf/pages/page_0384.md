## Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi

Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi adalah komite pendukung Dewan Komisaris yang bertugas untuk melakukan pengawasan dan memberikan pendapat yang independen kepada Dewan Komisaris terkait kebijakan remunerasi dan nominasi.

## Dasar Hukum

Pembentukan Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi berpedoman dan berlandaskan kepada peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku serta best practices yang diterapkan di lembaga perbankan di Indonesia, termasuk:

1. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("POJK") No. 33/POJK.04/2014 tanggal 8 Desember 2014 tentang Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.
2. POJK No. 17/POJK.03/2023 tanggal 14 September 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum.
3. SEOJK No. 13/SEOJK.03/2017 tanggal 17 Maret 2017 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum.
4. POJK No. 34/POJK.04/2014 tanggal 8 Desember 2014 tentang Komite Nominasi Dan Remunerasi Emiten Atau Perusahaan Publik.
5. Anggaran Dasar Bank.

## Persyaratan Keanggotaan Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi

1. Persyaratan umum:
a. Memiliki integritas, akhlak dan moral yang baik.
b. Tidak memiliki kepentingan/keterkaitan pribadi yang dapat menimbulkan conflict of interest terhadap Bank.

2. Monitor and evaluate Risk Management policies and other policies related to Bank risks and evaluate the suitability between risk management policies and their implementation.
3. Monitor Risk Appetite, including risk appetite statements, tolerance limits and risk limits in accordance with the risk matrix that has been set for eight types of risk and capital resilience, including the Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process and the implementation of stress tests.
4. Monitor the Bank's capital by linking the level of risk with capital adequacy to anticipate potential losses that may arise from the Bank's business activities.
5. Evaluate and provide recommendations to the Board of Commissioners regarding the implementation of the duties of the Risk Management Committee and the Risk Management Work Unit; and provide recommendations related to reports to OJK such as Risk Profile Reports, Bank Health Reports and other reports in order to ensure the appropriateness of information delivery to OJK on the risk conditions faced by the Bank.
6. Monitor Bank Soundness Level based on independent assessment.

## Remuneration and Nomination Committee

Remuneration and Nomination Committee is tasked with assisting the Board of Commissioners in carrying out supervision and providing independent advices to the Board, especially on remuneration and nomination committee.

## Legal Basis

The establishment of the Remuneration and Nomination Committee is guided by and based on applicable laws and regulations and best practices implemented in banking institutions in Indonesia, including:

1. Financial Services Authority Regulation ("POJK") No. 33/POJK.04/2014 dated December 8, 2014 concerning the Directors and Board of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies.
2. POJK No. 17/POJK.03/2023 dated September 14, 2023 concerning the Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank.
3. SEOJK No. 13/SEOJK.03/2017 dated March 17, 2017 concerning the Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank.
4. POJK No. 34/POJK.04/2014 dated December 8, 2014 concerning the Nomination and Remuneration Committees for Issuers or Public Companies.
5. Bank Articles of Association.

## Membership Requirement for Remuneration and Nomination Committee

1. General requirements:
a. Have integrity, good morals and morals.
b. Have no personal interests/relationships that could give rise to a conflict of interest towards the Bank.
