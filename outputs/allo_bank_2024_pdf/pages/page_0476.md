Allo Bank memberikan kesempatan seluas-luasnya kepada Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan seluruh pemimpin divisi untuk mengikuti pengembangan kompetensi terkait penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan. Bank meyakini pengembangan kompetensi tersebut akan sangat membantu dalam penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan pada tahun pelaporan maupun pada tahun-tahun berikutnya. 3. Initiating outreach efforts and heightened awareness regarding sustainable finance. To mitigate risks associated with ESG implementation, Allo Bank has set a target to adjust and update the Bank's credit policy and SOP as well as enhancing awareness of sustainable finance in industry analysis. These efforts are expected to strengthen stakeholder trust and contribute to sustainable development.

Allo Bank provides extensive opportunities for the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and all division leaders to participate in competency development programs related to sustainable finance. The Bank believes that enhancing competencies will significantly support the effective implementation of sustainable finance both in the reporting year and in the years to come.
