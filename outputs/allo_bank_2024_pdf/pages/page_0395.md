## Laporan Pelaksanaan Tugas Komite Kredit

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Komite Kredit telah melaksanakan kegiatan sesuai dengan fungsinya yaitu dengan mengevaluasi dan memutuskan proposal kredit yang diajukan oleh petugas Bank atas proposal kredit baru sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku.

## Komite Kebijakan Perkreditan

Bank membentuk Kebijakan Perkreditan untuk membantu Direksi dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab terkait pengelolaan kebijakan perkreditan yang bertujuan untuk mendukung pemberian kredit yang sehat dan mengandung unsur pengendalian internal mulai tahap awal proses kegiatan perkreditan sampai dengan tahap pemantauan perkreditan dengan memperhatikan prinsip kehati-hatian.

2. In making decisions on credit proposals and/or credit restructuring, the Credit Committee is obliged to:

a. Consider studies from the Work Unit in charge of the Credit Review &amp; Restructure function, the Work Unit in charge of the Legal function, and the Work Unit in charge of the Compliance function. The results of this study can be used as a basis for making changes and/or additional requirements to mitigate credit risks that may arise.
b. Ensure that all aspects fulfill the principles of providing healthy credit in accordance with the Bank's Credit Policy Guidelines, do not violate the provisions of the Bank's prudential principles which are guided by external and internal regulations or provisions, and ensure the appropriateness of providing credit and minimize the potential for moral hazard.

## Report on the Implementation of Credit Committee Duties

Throughout 2024, the Credit Committee carried out activities in accordance with its function, namely by evaluating and deciding on credit proposals submitted by bank officers on new credit proposals in accordance with applicable regulations.

## Credit Policy Committee

The Bank establishes Credit Policy to assist the Board of Directors in carrying out duties and responsibilities related to the management of credit policies, aiming to support the provision of sound credit and contain elements of internal control from the initial stage of the credit activity process to the credit monitoring stage by paying attention to the principle of prudence.
