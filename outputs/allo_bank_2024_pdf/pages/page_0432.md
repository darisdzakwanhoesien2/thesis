# Penerapan Program Anti Pencucian Uang (APU), Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme (PPT) dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal (PPPSPM) Tahun 2024

1. Struktur Organisasi dalam Penerapan Program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM

Dalam menerapkan program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM, Bank menetapkan Divisi Compliance dan AML-CFT sebagai Unit Kerja Khusus (UKK) penerapan program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM yang mana Divisi Compliance dan AML-CFT melapor dan bertanggung jawab langsung kepada Direktur yang membawahi fungsi kepatuhan di Allo Bank dalam hal ini Direktur Risiko, Kepatuhan &amp; Hukum. Selain itu penerapan APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM juga diterapkan oleh Kepala Bagian Operasional di setiap Kantor Cabang yang memiliki fungsi supervisi dan memastikan bahwa kegiatan operasional di Kantor Cabang masing-masing telah sesuai dengan penerapan program APU PPT &amp; PPPSPM.

# Implementation of the Anti Money Laundering (AML). Countering the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) and Counter-Proliferation Financing of Weapons of Mass Destruction (CPF)

1. Organizational Structure in Implementing the AML-CFT &amp; CPF Program

In implementing the AML-CFT &amp; CPF program, the Bank designated the Compliance and AML-CFT Divisions as Special Work Units (UKK) for implementing the AML-CFT &amp; CPF programs where the Compliance and AML-CFT Divisions report and are directly responsible to the Director who oversees the compliance function at Allo Bank, in this case the Director of Risk, Compliance &amp; Legal. Apart from that, the implementation of AML-CFT &amp; CPF is also implemented by the Head of Operations at each Branch Office who has a supervisory function and ensures that operational activities in each Branch Office are in accordance with the implementation of the AML-CFT &amp; CPF program.
