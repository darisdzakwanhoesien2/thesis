Wewenang Komite Pemantau Risiko

Dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Komite memiliki wewenang sebagai berikut:
1. Melakukan komunikasi dengan Kepala Unit Kerja dan pihak-pihak lain di Bank untuk memperoleh informasi, klarifikasi serta meminta dokumen dan laporan yang diperlukan.
2. Mengakses catatan atau informasi tentang pegawai, dana, aset serta sumber daya perusahaan lainnya yang berkaitan dengan pelaksanaan tugasnya.
3. Memperoleh Laporan Profil Risiko, Laporan Tingkat Kesehatan Bank, dan Laporan lainnya terkait penerapan manajemen risiko di Bank.
4. Mendapatkan masukan dan atau saran dari pihak luar Bank yang berkaitan dengan tugasnya.
5. Melakukan kewenangan lain yang diberikan oleh Dewan Komisaris.

3. Provide recommendation to the Board of Commissioners regarding:
a. Matters that can support the improvement in the effectiveness of risk management implementation in the Bank.
b. Conformity between the Bank's risk management policies and integrated risk management policies with the implementation of these policies.
c. Ensure that the Bank has managed risks adequately.
d. Implementation of work plans and duties of the Risk Management Committee, Integrated Risk Management Committee, Risk Management Work Unit, and the Bank's Integrated Risk Management Work Unit.

4. Hold regular meetings with related work units to discuss matters within the scope of their supervision.
5. Report the results of monitoring and reviews periodically, as well as provide input on matters that need the attention of the Board of Commissioners.
6. Develop a Risk Oversight Committee Work Charter and conduct reviews as needed at least once every 2 (two) years.
7. The Head of the Risk Oversight Committee is tasked and responsible for coordinating all Committee activities to fulfil the Committee's objectives in accordance with its formation.
8. The head and members of the Risk Oversight Committee are responsible for the following matters:
a. Determine the annual work plan.
b. Determine the annual meeting schedule.
c. Make periodic reports regarding Committee activities and matters deemed necessary for the attention of the Board of Commissioners.
d. Appoint non-Board of Commissioners Risk Oversight Committee members or appoint other third parties as Committee secretaries to record Committee Meetings and prepare Minutes of Committee Meetings.
e. Attend meetings.
f. Play an active role and contribute to every Committee Meeting activity.

Risk Oversight Committee Authority

In carrying out its duties, the Committee has the following authorities:
1. Communicate with the Head of Work Unit and other parties in the Bank to obtain information, clarification and request necessary documents and reports.
2. Access records or information about employees, funds, assets and other company resources related to the implementation of its duties.
3. Obtain Risk Profile Reports, Bank Health Level Reports, and other reports related to the implementation of risk management in the Bank.
4. Obtain input and/or suggestions from parties outside the Bank related to its duties.
5. Carry out other authorities granted by the Board of Commissioners.
