# Hasil Evaluasi Penerapan Sistem Pengendalian Internal

Bank secara konsisten melakukan evaluasi atas pelaksanaan sistem pengendalian internal di setiap jenjang organisasi. Satuan Kerja Audit Internal melakukan evaluasi atas kecukupan dan efektivitas Sistem Pengawasan Internal secara menyeluruh dan pengawasan yang dilakukan untuk mendukung asersi Direksi tentang efektivitas Sistem Pengendalian Internal Bank yang diterapkan.

Hasil evaluasi yang dilakukan oleh SKAI kemudian disampaikan kepada manajemen untuk ditindaklanjuti dan dimonitor pelaksanaannya guna memastikan Sistem Pengendalian Internal Bank telah cukup memadai dalam mendukung pencapaian tujuan dan sasaran yang telah ditetapkan. Berdasarkan hasil pemeriksaan serta paparan hasil evaluasi terhadap efektivitas dan kecukupan pengendalian internal pada Bank, khususnya pada kecukupan sistem, prosedur, struktur organisasi, sumber daya manusia, dan pelaporan, Bank menyimpulkan bahwa sistem pengendalian internal telah berjalan baik, termasuk diantaranya kelengkapan, akurasi, dan ketersediaan data pada sistem informasi.

# Penilaian Kecukupan Penerapan Sistem Pengendalian Internal oleh Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris

Berdasarkan hasil evaluasi atas efektivitas dan kecukupan pengendalian internal Bank, khususnya pada kecukupan sistem, prosedur, struktur organisasi, sumber daya manusia, dan pelaporan, Direksi menilai bahwa penerapan Sistem Pengendalian Internal telah berjalan dengan baik dan memadai, baik dari kecukupan infrastruktur maupun metode. Untuk itu, Bank berkomitmen untuk terus meningkatkan kualitas penerapan sistem pengendalian internal ke depan guna memastikan kinerja usaha Bank tetap sehat dan aman.

# Results of Evaluation of Implementation of Internal Control System

The Bank consistently evaluates the implementation of the internal control system at every level of the organization. The Internal Audit Work Unit evaluates the adequacy and effectiveness of the Internal Supervisory System as a whole and the supervision carried out to support the Board of Directors' assertions regarding the effectiveness of the Bank's Internal Control System implemented.

The results of the evaluation conducted by SKAI are then submitted to management for follow-up and monitoring of its implementation to ensure that the Bank's Internal Control System is sufficient to support the achievement of the goals and objectives that have been set. Based on the results of the examination and the presentation of the evaluation results on the effectiveness and adequacy of internal control at the Bank, especially on the adequacy of systems, procedures, organizational structure, human resources, and reporting, the Bank concludes that the internal control system has been running well, including the completeness, accuracy, and availability of data in the information system.

# Assessment of Adequacy of Implementation of Internal Control System by Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners

Based on the evaluation results of the effectiveness and adequacy of the Bank's internal control, especially on the adequacy of systems, procedures, organizational structure, human resources, and reporting, the Board of Directors assesses that the implementation of the Internal Control System has been running well and adequately, both in terms of the adequacy of infrastructure and methods. To this end, the Bank is committed to continuously improving the quality of the implementation of the internal control system in the future to ensure that the Bank's business performance remains sound and safe.
