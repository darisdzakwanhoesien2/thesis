# Information on the Realization of the Use of Proceeds from the Public Offering

Allo Bank has conducted a Limited Public Offering in 2022 in the context of Capital Increase by Providing Pre-emptive Rights (PMHMETD) III to shareholders. This came in the form of 10,047,322,871 ordinary shares or  $46.24\%$  of the issued and fully paid-up capital after PMHMETD III with a nominal value of IDR100 per share with an Exercise Price of IDR478 per share. As a result, the amount of funds received by the Bank through PMHMETD III was IDR4,802,620 million. The funds from this public offering were used in the following ways as per 31 December 2024:

1. IDR4,080,336 million (85%) for Credit;
