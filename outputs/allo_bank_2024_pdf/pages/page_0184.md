# 4. Sistem Pengendalian Risiko

Sistem pengendalian internal berjalan cukup efektif dalam mendukung pelaksanaan manajemen risiko Operasional. Bank terus berupaya melakukan perbaikan proses dan pengendalian internal yang lebih efisien sesuai dengan best practice dan arah bisnis Bank.

Pelaksanaan kaji ulang independen (independent review) dilakukan oleh Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI) berdasarkan metodologi, dimana hasilnya dilaporkan kepada Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Tindak lanjut atas kaji ulang independen telah dilaksanakan dengan membuat target penyelesaian.

# Risiko Strategik

Risiko strategis adalah risiko antara lain disebabkan adanya penetapan dan pelaksanaan strategi Bank yang tidak tepat, pengambilan keputusan rencana bisnis yang tidak tetap kepada kurang responsifnya Bank terhadap perubahan eksternal.

# 1. Tata Kelola Risiko

Dewan Komisaris melakukan pengawasan aktif dengan memberikan arahan kepada Direksi yang tertuang dari rekomendasi yang disampaikan pada Rapat Komisaris dan Direksi sesuai ketentuan yang berlaku. Bank mengelola risiko strategik melalui proses pertimbangan dan pengambilan keputusan setiap kebijakan strategis secara hati-hati oleh Direksi. Pengelolaan risiko strategik tersebut erat kaitannya dengan kemampuan dalam menyusun rencana kerja yang terkoordinasi, baik untuk jangka panjang maupun jangka pendek oleh karenanya penetapan strategi Bank dirumuskan dengan memperhatikan peraturan Bank Indonesia/Otoritas Jasa keuangan/ketentuan lainnya yang terkait serta memperhitungkan dampak risiko strategik terhadap permodalan Bank.

Rencana Bisnis Bank (RBB) disusun sesuai dengan kondisi dan kemampuan optimal atas pendayagunaan sumber daya, baik sumber daya finansial, infrastruktur dan SDM yang dimiliki. Dengan perencanaan strategis tersebut, Bank berkeyakinan dapat menjaga pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan sehingga akan meningkatkan nilai perusahaan. Untuk meminimalkan terjadinya penyimpangan pada pelaksanaan RBB, dilakukan pemantauan pencapaian RBB melalui Komite Manajemen Risiko, dan telah dilakukan komunikasi kepada setiap jenjang organisasi, baik pada saat penyusunan rencana serta pada saat review pelaksanaan yang dilakukan secara rutin tiap semester.

# 2. Kerangka Manajemen Risiko

Bank memiliki Kebijakan Manajemen Risiko Strategik dan proses manajemen Risiko Strategik berfungsi untuk mengidentifikasi, mengukur, memantau, dan mengendalikan Risiko Strategik. Bank telah menetapkan pencapaian target bank dalam Rencana Bisnis Bank dengan mempertimbangkan risk limit (risk appetite dan risk tolerance) yang telah disepakati.

# 4. Risk Control System

The Bank's internal control system runs quite effectively in supporting Operational risk management. The Bank continues to improve its processes and internal controls so that they are more efficient in accordance with best practices and in accordance with the Bank's business direction.

Independent reviews are carried out by the Internal Audit Work Unit based on a methodology that has results reported to the Board of Commissioners and Directors. Follow-up actions to independent reviews have been carried out through the creation of a completion target.

# Strategic Risk

Strategic risk is defined as any risk caused by things such as inappropriate Bank strategies, inconsistent business plan decision-making or the Bank's lack of responsiveness to external changes.

# 1. Risk Governance

The Board of Commissioners carries out active supervision by providing direction to the Board of Directors by way of recommendations submitted at Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors Meetings in accordance with applicable provisions. The Bank manages strategic risk through a process of careful consideration and decision-making of each strategic policy by the Board of Directors. The management of strategic risk is closely related to preparing a coordinated work plan, both for the long term and the short term. The Bank's strategy is formulated by taking into consideration Bank Indonesia/Financial Services Authority regulations/other related provisions and by taking into account the impact of strategic risk on the Bank's capital.

The Bank's Business Plan is prepared in accordance with conditions and optimal capabilities for the utilization of resources (in terms of financial resources, infrastructure and human resources owned). It is through this strategic planning that the Bank believes that it can maintain sustainable growth that can increase the company's value. To minimize deviations in the implementation of this plan, achievements are monitored through the Risk Management Committee, and communication has been carried out to every level of the organization, both during the preparation of this plan and during reviews carried out routinely every semester.

# 2. Risk Management Framework

The Bank has a Strategic Risk Management Policy and a Strategic Risk Management process that functions to identify, measure, monitor, and control Strategic Risk. The Bank has set the achievement of its targets in the Bank's Business Plan by taking into consideration the agreed risk limits (risk appetite and risk tolerance).
