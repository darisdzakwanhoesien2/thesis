Sustainable development, which maintains economic stability and promotes inclusivity, is currently being implemented in Indonesia. This requires a national economic system that prioritizes harmony between economic, social, and environmental aspects. In this context, the development of an environmentally friendly financial system, as mandated by Law No. 32 of 2009 concerning Environmental Protection and Management, has become increasingly important.
