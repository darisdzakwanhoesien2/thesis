# PT Mega Corpora

PT Mega Corpora merupakan perseroan terbatas yang berkedudukan di Jakarta Selatan, didirikan berdasarkan Akta Pendirian No. 53 tanggal 27 Oktober 1994, yang dibuat di hadapan Enimarya Agoes Suwarko, S.H., Notaris di Jakarta, dan telah disetujui oleh Menteri Kehakiman dengan Surat Keputusan No. C2-18944-HT.01.01.TH.94 tanggal 27 Desember 1994 dan telah diumumkan dalam Berita Negara Republik Indonesia No. 97 tanggal 3 Desember 1999, Tambahan No. 8134. Anggaran Dasar PT Mega Corpora telah mengalami beberapa kali perubahan, di mana perubahan terakhir berdasarkan Akta No. 11 tanggal 21 April 2022, yang dibuat di hadapan Dedy Syamri, S.H., Notaris di Jakarta, antara lain mengenai perubahan modal disetor Perseroan. Perubahan ini telah diberitahukan kepada Direktorat Jenderal Administrasi Hukum Umum Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia ("Dirjen AHU") dengan Surat Penerimaan Pemberitahuan No. AHU-AH.01.03-0229746 tanggal 22 April 2022.

# Kegiatan Usaha Utama PT Mega Corpora

Berdasarkan Pasal 3 Anggaran Dasar PT Mega Corpora, maksud dan tujuan serta kegiatan usaha Perusahaan antara lain:

1. Aktivitas Perusahaan Holding (64200); dan
2. Aktivitas Konsultasi Manajemen Lainnya (70209).

Saat ini kegiatan usaha utama PT Mega Corpora adalah dalam bidang aktivitas perusahaan holding melalui pemilikan saham di Entitas Anak yang bergerak dalam bidang jasa usaha perbankan, pembiayaan, sekuritas dan asuransi.

# PT Mega Corpora

PT Mega Corpora, a limited liability company domiciled in South Jakarta, was established based on Deed of Establishment No. 53 dated October 27, 1994, made before Enimarya Agoes Suwarko, S.H., Notary in Jakarta, and has been approved by the Ministry of Justice with Decree No. C2 18944-HT.01.01.TH.94 dated December 27, 1994 and has been announced in the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 97 dated December 3, 1999, Supplement No. 8134. The Articles of Association have been amended several times, most recently by Deed No. 11 dated April 21, 2022, made before Dedy Syamri, S.H., Notary in Jakarta, regarding changes to the Company's paid-up capital. This change has been notified to the Directorate General of General Legal Administration of the Ministry of Law and Human Rights ("Dirjen AHU") in Notification Acceptance Letter No. AHUAH. 01.03-0229746 dated April 22, 2022.

# Main Business Activities of PT Mega Corpora

Based on Article 3 of the Articles of Association of PT Mega Corpora, the aims and objectives and business activities of PT Mega Corpora are as follows:

1. Holding Company Activities (64200); and
2. Other Management Consulting Activities (70209).

Currently, the main business core of PT Mega Corpora cover the holding company's activities through the ownership of shares in Subsidiaries, which are engaged in banking, financing, securities and insurance business services.
