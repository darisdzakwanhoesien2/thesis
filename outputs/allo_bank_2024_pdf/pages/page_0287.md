|  1. Bank telah mengimplementasikan kebijakan manajemen risiko sesuai dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 18/POJK.03/2016 dan Surat Edaran OJK Nomor 34/SEOJK/032016 tentang Penerapan Manajemen Risiko Bagi Bank Umum serta mengacu juga pada peraturan internal mengenai Kebijakan Manajemen Risiko.  |
|  Beberapa Kebijakan Internal yang terkait Manajemen Risiko Operasional diantaranya SK Manajemen Risiko Operasional; SE Kerangka Kerja Manajemen Risiko; SK Komite Manajemen Risiko; SE Pedoman Penyusunan ATMR Risiko Operasional; SE Pedoman Penyusunan Profil Risiko; SK Kewajiban Penyediaan Modal Minimum (KPMM) sesuai Profil Risiko; SK Kebijakan Risk Limit; SE Pengelolaan Risiko Produk Bank; SE Risk Control & Self Assessment (RCSA); SE Key Risk Indicator (KRI); SE Prosedur dan Panduan Incident Reporting & Management; SK Implementasi Responsible Person (RP) pada Masing-Masing Divisi; SK Crisis Management Plan (CMP); SK Business Continuity Management (BCM); SK Business Continuity Plan (BCP); SE Prosedur dan Penyusunan BIA dan BCP Unit Kerja; dan SK Kebijakan Risk Management Terkait Kegiatan Alih Daya.  |
|  Kebijakan Manajemen Risiko Operasional berperan sebagai panduan bagi Bank dalam mengelola Risiko Operasional yang melekat pada berbagai aktivitas Operasional dan Bisnis Bank, terutama untuk menetapkan tanggung jawab, akuntabilitas, dan menyelaraskan proses kerja. Harapannya, hal ini dapat mengurangi potensi kerugian yang disebabkan oleh gangguan proses internal, kesalahan manusia, kegagalan sistem, dan/atau peristiwa-peristiwa eksternal, dengan mengantisipasi timbulya insiden/ kesalahan akibat kurangnya pengelolaan Risiko Operasional yang efektif.  |
|  The Bank has implemented risk management policies in accordance with the Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 18/POJK.03/2016 and OJK Circular Letter Number 34/SEOJK/032016 concerning the Implementation of Risk Management for Commercial Banks and also refers to internal regulations regarding Risk Management Policies.  |
|  Some of the Internal Policies related to Operational Risk Management include Operational Risk Management Decree; Risk Management Framework Decree; Risk Management Committee Decree; Operational Risk RWA Preparation Guidelines; Risk Profile Preparation Guidelines; Minimum Capital Adequacy Decree (CAR) according to Risk Profile; Risk Limit Policy Decree; SE Bank Product Risk Management; SE Risk Control & Self Assessment (RCSA); SE Key Risk Indicator (KRI); SE Incident Reporting & Management Procedures and Guidelines; SK Responsible Person (RP) Implementation in Each Division; SK Crisis Management Plan (CMP); SK Business Continuity Management (BCM); SK Business Continuity Plan (BCP); SE Procedures and Preparation of BIA and BCP Work Units; and SK Risk Management Policies Related to Outsourcing Activities.  |
|  The Operational Risk Management Policy serves as a guide for the Bank in managing Operational Risk inherent in various Operational and Business activities of the Bank, especially to assign responsibilities, accountabilities, and harmonize work processes. It is expected to reduce potential losses caused by internal process disruptions, human errors, system failures, and/or external events, by anticipating the occurrence of incidents/errors due to lack of effective Operational Risk management.  |
