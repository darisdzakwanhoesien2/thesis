# Penilaian Kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

# Penilaian Kinerja Dewan Komisaris

Peraturan OJK No. 17 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola Bagi Bank Umum dan Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 13/SEOJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum mewajibkan Bank Umum untuk melakukan penilaian terhadap penerapan tata kelola. Untuk itu, Bank senantiasa melakukan evaluasi secara rutin terhadap kinerja Dewan Komisaris yang dilakukan oleh Pemegang Saham dalam forum RUPS.

Evaluasi tersebut dilakukan dengan memperhatikan kompetensi dan keahlian/pengalaman, kinerja Komite-Komite Dewan Komisaris, efektivitas pelaksanaan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris, dan penilaian mandiri maupun penilaian rekan sejawat terhadap pelaksanaan fungsi pengawasan oleh masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris. Hasil penilaian atas aspek-aspek tersebut selanjutnya akan disimpulkan ke dalam laporan dan diajukan kepada pemegang saham melalui mekanisme RUPS.

# Penilaian Kinerja Direksi

Undang-Undang No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas mengatur bahwa dalam hal Direksi terdiri dari 2 (dua) anggota Direksi atau lebih maka tanggung jawab Direksi berlaku secara tanggung renteng. Adapun prosedur penilaian kriteria kinerja Direksi Bank terdiri dari 2 (dua) jenis, yaitu:

1. Penilaian Kinerja oleh RUPS

Masing-masing Anggota Direksi akan dievaluasi setiap tahunnya oleh Dewan Komisaris untuk disampaikan kepada Pemegang Saham dalam RUPS, berdasarkan hasil kinerja keseluruhan dari tugas dan kewajiban Direksi secara kolegial maupun individual. Hal ini menjadi salah satu bahan pertimbangan bagi pemegang saham untuk memberhentikan dan/atau menunjuk kembali anggota Direksi.

2. Prosedur Pelaksanaan Assessment Kinerja Direksi

Self-assessment atau penilaian sendiri dilakukan oleh masing-masing anggota Direksi untuk menilai pelaksanaan kinerja Direksi secara kolegial, dan bukan menilai kinerja individual masing-masing anggota Direksi. Dengan adanya self-assessment ini diharapkan masing-masing anggota Direksi dapat berkontribusi untuk memperbaiki kinerja Direksi secara berkesinambungan.

Prosedur penilaian kinerja Direksi melalui pelaksanaan self-assessment GCG dilakukan pada bulan Juni dan Desember setiap tahunnya yang berdasarkan pengelolaan Bank, pencapaian target, upaya penanggulangan kendala dan manajemen risiko, serta penerapan tata kelola perusahaan yang dilakukan. Dewan komisaris akan mengevaluasi melalui rapat dengan Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi kemudian hasilnya akan dilaporkan kepada pemegang saham dalam RUPS Tahunan.

# Performance Assessment of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors

# Assessment of the Board of Commissioners' Performance

OJK Regulation No. 17 of 2023 concerning the Implementation of Governance for Conventional Bank and Financial Services Authority Circular Letter No. 13/SEOJK.03/2017 require Conventional Bank to conduct assessments of their governance implementation. In compliance with these regulations, the Bank routinely conducts evaluations of the Board of Commissioners' performance, which are carried out by shareholders during the GMS forum.

The evaluation is carried out by considering the competence, expertise/experience of the Board of Commissioners, the performance of the Board of Commissioners' Committees, the effectiveness of the Board of Commissioners' responsibilities, as well as independent assessments and peer assessments of the supervisory function performed by each Board of Commissioners member. The results of this assessment will then be summarized in a report and submitted to shareholders through the GMS mechanism.

# Assessment of the Board of Directors' Performance

Based on Law No. 40 of 2007 regarding Limited Liability Companies, if the Board of Directors consists of two or more members, their responsibility applies jointly and respectively. There are two types of procedures for assessing the Board of Directors' performance as follows:

1. Performance Assessment by GMS

Each member of the Board of Directors will be evaluated annually by the Board of Commissioners to be presented to Shareholders at the GMS, based on the results of the overall performance of the duties and obligations of the Board of Directors both collegial and individual. This is a consideration for shareholders when dismissing and/or reappointing members of the Board of Directors.

2. Procedures for Implementing Directors' Performance Assessments

Self-assessment is carried out by each member of the Board of Directors to assess the implementation of the performance of the Board of Directors in a collegial manner, and not to assess the individual performance of each member of the Board of Directors. With this self-assessment, it is expected that each member of the Board of Directors can contribute to improving the performance of the Board of Directors on an ongoing basis.

The procedure for assessing the performance of the Board of Directors through the implementation of a GCG self-assessment is carried out in June and December each year based on the Bank's management, target achievement, efforts to overcome obstacles and risk management, as well as the implementation of corporate governance. The Board of Commissioners will evaluate it through meetings with the Remuneration and Nomination Committee and then the results will be reported to shareholders at the Annual GMS.
