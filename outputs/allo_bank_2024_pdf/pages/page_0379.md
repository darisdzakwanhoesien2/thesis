5. Members of the Board of Directors of the Bank or other Banks are prohibited from becoming members of the Risk Oversight Committee.
6. Independent Commissioners and Independent Parties who are members of the Risk Oversight Committee constitute at least 51% (fifty one percent) of the total members of the Risk Oversight Committee.
7. The Head of the Risk Oversight Committee can only hold the same position as Committee Chair in a maximum of 1 (one) other Committee.
8. In carrying out its daily duties, the Committee may be assisted by staff and/or the Committee Secretary appointed based on the decision of the Risk Oversight Committee meeting.
