# Jaringan Operasional Bank

Kantor Pusat Bank berkedudukan di Jakarta, selain itu dalam memberikan pelayanan kepada para nasabah Bank memiliki 1 kantor pusat dan 2 kantor cabang. Sepanjang 2024, Bank tidak melakukan ekspansi terhadap jaringan kerja, maupun pembukaan jaringan kantor yang baru.

# Kepemilikan Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, dan Pemegang Saham dalam Kelompok Usaha Bank

Bank telah memiliki Pedoman dan Tata Tertib Kerja bagi Direksi serta Pedoman dan Tata Tertib Kerja bagi Dewan Komisaris yang didalamnya mengatur mengenai kepemilikan saham dalam kelompok usaha Bank terkait aspek transparansi dan larangan bagi Anggota Direksi yang sesuai dengan POJK No. 17 Tahun 2023. Bank mengatur bahwa anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris harus menginformasikan Sekretaris Perusahaan saat memiliki saham Bank agar Bank bisa segera melaporkan perihal tersebut kepada OJK selambat-lambatnya 3 (tiga) hari kerja setelah terjadinya kepemilikan tersebut. Pengungkapan kepemilikan saham dari anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi ada pada bab Profil Perusahaan dari Laporan Tahunan ini.

# Bank Operational Network

The Bank's Head Office is located in Jakarta. In addition to providing customer services, the Bank operates 1 Head Office and 2 branch offices. Throughout 2024, the Bank did not expand its operational network or open any new offices.

# Ownership of Directors, Board of Commissioners, and Shareholders in Bank Business Groups

The Bank has established Guidelines and Work Rules for the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, regulating share ownership within the Bank's business group. These guidelines ensure transparency and compliance with POJK No. 17 of 2023, which outlines restrictions on Board Members' share ownership. Under these regulations, members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners must notify the Corporate Secretary upon acquiring shares in the Bank. The Bank is then required to report this to the OJK within 3 (three) working days. Details regarding the share ownership of members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are included in the Company Profile chapter of this Annual Report.
