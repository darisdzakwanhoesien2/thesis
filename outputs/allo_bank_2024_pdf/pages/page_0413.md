# Akuntan Publik

## Public Accountant

### Kepatuhan terhadap Standar Akuntansi Keuangan Indonesia

Fungsi Audit Eksternal diimplementasikan melalui pelaksanaan audit Laporan Keuangan Bank oleh Kantor Akuntan Publik untuk memastikan bahwa informasi keuangan dimaksud disusun dan disajikan secara berkualitas, membentuk dan menyatakan pendapat atas kewajaran Laporan Keuangan Bank, serta menguji pengendalian internal, termasuk melakukan pengujian kembali atas hal-hal yang sudah diuji oleh Internal Audit dan melakukan observasi dari prosedur yang dilakukan oleh Internal Audit.

### Compliance with Indonesian Financial Accounting Standards

The External Audit Function is carried out by a Public Accounting Firm, which audits the Bank's Financial Statements to ensure they are accurately prepared and presented. This process involves forming and expressing an opinion on the fairness of the financial statements, evaluating internal controls, and reviewing areas already assessed by Internal Audit. Additionally, the external audit includes observing the procedures performed by Internal Audit to ensure their effectiveness.

### Mekanisme Penunjukan Akuntan Publik

Berdasarkan POJK No. 37/POJK.03/2019 tentang Transparansi dan Publikasi Laporan Bank dan SEOJK No. 9/SEOJK.03/2020 tentang Transparansi dan Publikasi Laporan Bank Umum Konvensional, audit atas Laporan Keuangan Bank untuk tahun buku 2024 telah dilakukan oleh Kantor Akuntan Publik yang independen, kompeten, profesional, dan obyektif sesuai dengan Standar Profesional Akuntan Publik, serta perjanjian kerja dan ruang lingkup audit yang telah ditetapkan.

### Public Accountant Appointment Mechanism

Based on POJK No. 37/POJK.03/2019 concerning the Transparency and Publication of Bank Reports and SEOJK No. 9/SEOJK.03/2020 concerning Transparency and Publication of Conventional Conventional Bank Reports, the audit of the Bank's Financial Statements for the 2024 fiscal year has been conducted by an independent, competent, professional, and objective Public Accounting Firm in accordance with the Public Accounting Professional Standards, as well as the work agreement and audit scope that has been determined.
