# Analisis Kualitatif
## Qualitative Analysis

3. Bank mengembangkan metodologi Manajemen Risiko Operasional dalam rangka penyempurnaan ORM tools yang telah ada. ORM Tools adalah perangkat atau sistem yang dirancang untuk membantu Bank dalam mengidentifikasi, menilai, mengendalikan, dan memantau Risiko Operasional. ORM Tools memiliki tiga modul yakni Risk Control Self Assessment (RCSA), Key Risk Indicator (KRI) dan Incident Report. RCSA digunakan untuk membantu risk owner dalam melakukan proses manajemen risiko operasional yang mencakup identifikasi dan pengukuran risiko operasional secara mandiri yang bersifat prediktif dan antisipatif. Selanjutnya, KRI adalah alat bantu yang memberikan informasi sejak dini mengenai gejala maupun risiko yang trennya menunjukkan peningkatan. Sedangkan Incident Report merupakan tools yang berfungsi sebagai database peristiwa risiko, yang digunakan untuk data pembelajaran Bank.

Bank juga membangun database peristiwa risiko, termasuk di dalamnya data kerugian karena risiko operasional, melalui modul Incident Report. Hal ini merupakan bagian dari kegiatan Bank mendata Risk &amp; Loss Event Data (RLED) yang akan digunakan di dalam perhitungan Modal Risiko Operasional dan ATMR Risiko Operasional. Peristiwa yang dicatat meliputi Loss Event dan/atau Near Miss Event. Kejadiannya dapat berasal dari kegiatan yang bersifat transaksional maupun non transaksional. Near Miss merupakan peristiwa risiko yang nyaris menimbulkan kerugian finansial atau yang telah menimbulkan kerugian finansial namun kerugian tersebut sudah recovery seluruhnya, dengan catatan pada saat pencatatan Incident Report peristiwa risiko sudah terselesaikan baik di hari yang sama dengan peristiwa ataupun tidak, sedangkan Loss Event merupakan peristiwa risiko yang berdampak finansial atau hilangnya dana Bank.

Insiden/peristiwa Risiko Operasional wajib dianalisis kronologi, penyebab, dan dampaknya untuk memperoleh informasi yang lengkap dan komprehensif. Seluruh peristiwa risiko yang memiliki dampak kerugian finansial harus dicatat di dalam modul Incident Report berapapun nilai kerugiannya. Terhadap Insiden Risiko yang telah dicatat Unit Kerja terkait memantau kerugian dari Loss Event mengenai update setiap recovery kerugian sampai dengan final serta pembukuan kerugian final dalam buku Bank. Unit Kerja EOMR akan merekonsiliasi Loss yang dicatat pada Incident Report Database dengan yang dicatat di GL Kerugian Bank./

The Bank developed an Operational Risk Management methodology in order to improve the existing ORM tools. ORM Tools are devices or systems designed to assist the Bank in identifying, assessing, controlling, and monitoring Operational Risk. ORM Tools have three modules, namely Risk Control Self Assessment (RCSA), Key Risk Indicator (KRI) and Incident Report. RCSA is used to assist risk owners in carrying out the operational risk management process which includes identifying and measuring operational risks independently which are predictive and anticipatory. Furthermore, KRI is a tool that provides early information on symptoms or risks that show an increasing trend. Meanwhile, Incident Report is a tool that functions as a database of risk events, which is used for Bank learning data.

The Bank also establishes a database of risk events, including data on losses due to operational risks, through the Incident Report module. This is part of the Bank's activities in recording Risk &amp; Loss Event Data (RLED) which will be used in calculating Operational Risk Capital and Operational Risk RWA. The events recorded include Loss Events and/or Near Miss Events. The incidents can originate from transactional or non-transactional activities. Near Miss is a risk event that almost caused a financial loss or has caused a financial loss but the loss has been fully recovered, with the note that at the time of recording the Incident Report the risk event has been resolved either on the same day as the event or not, while Loss Event is a risk event that has a financial impact or loss of Bank funds.

Operational Risk Incidents/Events must be analyzed for chronology, causes, and impacts to obtain complete and comprehensive information. All risk events that have an impact on financial loss must be recorded in the Incident Report module regardless of the value of the loss. For the Risk Incidents that have been recorded, the related Work Unit monitors the losses from the Loss Event regarding updates on each loss recovery until the final and the final loss recording in the Bank's books. The EOMR Work Unit will reconcile the Losses recorded in the Incident Report Database with those recorded in the Bank's Loss GL.
