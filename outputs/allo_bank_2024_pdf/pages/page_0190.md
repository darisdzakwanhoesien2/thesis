# Good Corporate Governance

Bank melakukan penilaian sendiri terhadap seluruh kriteria/indikator penerapan tata kelola, dengan mempertimbangkan faktor yang mencakup baik governance structure, governance process, dan governance outcome, dan pemenuhan atas prinsip GCG seperti keanggotaan Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan Komite. Bank menyadari bahwa perbaikan dan peningkatan kualitas pelaksanaan Tata Kelola, merupakan upaya berkesinambungan, maka dari itu Bank akan melakukan hal-hal sebagai berikut:

1. Melakukan penilaian (self-assessment) terhadap kecukupan pelaksanaan Tata Kelola dan menyusun laporan pelaksanaan secara berkala sehingga dalam hal masih terdapat kekurangan maka dapat segera dilakukan tindakan korektif yang diperlukan.
2. Memprioritaskan komitmen yang telah dibuat oleh Bank kepada Regulator atas perbaikan Penerapan Tata Kelola sebagai tindak lanjut atas hasil pemeriksaan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan di tahun 2024.
3. Bank telah menyediakan sumber daya manusia yang berkualitas. Pemenuhan jumlah SDM dan peningkatan kemampuan dan kualitas SDM pada unit kerja terus dilakukan guna terpenuhinya tata kelola Bank yang baik.
4. Kebijakan, ketentuan, serta sistem dan prosedur yang dimiliki oleh Bank telah diperbaharui sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku. Bank terus memantau implementasi kebijakan dan prosedur tersebut agar sesuai dengan ketentuan dan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

## Rentabilitas

Secara umum Allo Bank telah melakukan upaya mitigasi risiko yang cukup optimal sehingga Rentabilitas Bank tahun 2024 secara umum membaik dibandingkan tahun 2023, terlihat dari perbaikan beberapa rasio rentabilitas seperti ROA, ROE, dan NIM.

## Permodalan

Kewajiban Penyediaan Modal Minimum (KPMM) posisi 31 Desember 2024 adalah sebesar 9,90%. Berdasarkan capaian tersebut, kecukupan modal Bank dinilai masih mampu menyerap potensi kerugian yang dihadapi dan jauh di atas batas minimal yang dipersyaratkan regulator.

## Good Corporate Governance

The Bank conducts its own self-assessment of all criteria/indicators of governance implementation. It does this by considering factors that include governance structure, governance process, and governance outcome, and fulfillment of GCG principles such as membership of the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors and Committees. The Bank realizes that improving and enhancing the quality of Governance implementation represents a continuous effort. Towards this end, the Bank has carried out the following efforts:

1. Conducted a self-assessment on the adequacy of Governance implementation and compiled periodic implementation reports so that the necessary corrective actions can be taken immediately if deficiencies were still found.
2. Prioritized commitments that have been made by the Bank to regulators as way for improving Governance Implementation and as follow-up actions to the results of the Financial Services Authority's inspections in 2024.
3. The Bank has provided quality human resources. Efforts towards having the right amount of HR and increasing the ability and quality the Bank's HR within work units continued to be carried out in order to fulfill good Bank governance.
4. The Bank's policies, provisions, and systems and procedures have been updated in accordance with applicable provisions. The Bank continued to monitor the implementation of these policies and procedures to ensure that they complied with applicable laws and regulations.

## Rentability

In general, Allo Bank has made optimal risk mitigation efforts. This was to ensure that the Bank's Rentability in 2024 generally improved compared to 2023. The results can be seen from the improvement in several rentability ratios such as ROA, ROE, and NIM.

## Capitalization

The s Minimum Capital Adequacy Ratio as of December 31, 2024 was 9.90%. This result means that the Bank's capital adequacy is considered still capable of absorbing potential losses faced and is still far above the minimum limit required by regulators.
