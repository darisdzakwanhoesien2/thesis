# Implementation Report of Product Committee Activities

Throughout 2024, the Product Committee carried out activities in accordance with its function, which is to ensure the development of the Bank's products and services and the activities therein are in line with the Bank's plans and strategies, as well as having identified the risks faced.

# Human Resources Committee

The Bank establishes Human Resources Committee whose task is to assist the Board of Directors in carrying out its duties and responsibilities regarding the determination of strategic plans and management of policies in the field of Human Resources.

# Tugas dan Wewenang Komite Sumber Daya Manusia

1. Memberikan arahan strategi dan membuat kebijakan umum di bidang Sumber Daya Manusia termasuk perubahan-perubahannya, antaralah (namun tidak terbatas) pada bidang-bidang berikut: Strategi Sumber Daya Manusia; Kebijakan Umum tentang Ketenagakerjaan; Kompensasi dan benefit karyawan; Manajemen Penilaian Kinerja, termasuk promosi; Manajemen Talenta dan Perencanaan Suksesi; Struktur Organisasi dan jenjang kepangkatan; Pelatihan dan Pengembangan karyawan; Budaya dan nilai perusahaan serta Keterikatan karyawan; Pengembangan hubungan karyawan dan industri; Perencanaan Tenaga Kerja; dan Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Organisasi.
2. Mendiskusikan dan memutuskan ketentuan remunerasi yang diberikan kepada pegawai.
3. Memberikan usulan terkait ketentuan remunerasi kepada Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi berdasarkan hasil diskusi dan keputusan pada poin diatas.

# Duties and Authorities of the Human Resources Committee

1. Provide strategic direction and make general policies in the field of Human Resources including changes, including (but not limited to) the following areas: Human Resources Strategy; General Employment Policy; Employee compensation and benefits; Performance Assessment Management, including promotions; Talent Management and Succession Planning; Organizational Structure and rank levels; Employee Training and Development; Company culture and values and Employee engagement; Employee and industrial relations development; Workforce Planning; and Organizational Health and Safety.
2. Discuss and decide on the remuneration provisions given to employees.
3. Provide proposals related to remuneration provisions to the Remuneration and Nomination Committee based on the results of discussions and decisions in the points above.
