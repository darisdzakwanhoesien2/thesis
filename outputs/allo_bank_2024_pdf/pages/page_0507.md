# Pelatihan dan Pengembangan Kemampuan Karyawan [OJK F.22]

Allo Bank secara berkesinambungan menyelenggarakan pelatihan dan pendidikan untuk mengembangkan kompetensi pegawai sehingga mereka mampu menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawab secara maksimal. Kegiatan tersebut menjadi fokus karena sebagai bank umum berbasis digital, Bank memerlukan pegawai dengan kualifikasi dan kompetensi yang spesifik di bidang bisnis digital dan teknologi informasi. Adapun aspek kompetensi yang menjadi target pengembangan disesuaikan dengan kekuatan pegawai (strength-based development) dan arahan bisnis Bank.

Selain program pengembangan kepemimpinan, Bank juga akan membuat, menyelenggarakan dan ikut dalam program pengembangan teknis, khususnya di bidang teknologi informasi dan digital, cyber security, data analytics, dan digital marketing. Bank mendukung pengembangan personal dan profesional pegawai untuk mempelajari hal baru sesuai minat dan kompetensi yang dimiliki. Pelaksanaan program yang dilakukan akan menggunakan metode yang efektif, seperti pembelajaran virtual, electronic, maupun tatap muka.

Selama tahun 2024, Bank telah menyelenggarakan pengembangan kompetensi sebanyak 179 program, yang diikuti oleh 439 pegawai atau 93% dari total pegawai dengan jumlah jam pelatihan sebanyak 1.472 jam. Adapun biaya pengembangan kompetensi tercatat sebesar Rp4,853 miliar naik dibandingkan tahun 2023, yang mencapai Rp3,886 miliar. # Employee Training and Development [OJK F.22]

Allo Bank continuously provides training and education to enhance employee competencies, ensuring they can perform their duties and responsibilities optimally. These development programs are a priority, as the Bank, operating as a digital-based conventional bank, requires employees with specialized qualifications in digital business and information technology. The targeted competency development areas are aligned with employees' strengths (strength-based development) and the Bank's strategic business direction.

Beyond leadership development programs, the Bank also initiates, organizes, and participates in technical development programs, particularly in information and digital technology, cybersecurity, data analytics, and digital marketing. The Bank strongly supports employees' personal and professional development, encouraging them to explore new areas aligned with their interests and expertise. These programs are delivered through various effective learning methods, including virtual, electronic, and face-to-face training sessions.

Throughout 2024, the Bank conducted 179 competency development programs, attended by 439 employees—representing 93% of the total workforce, with a cumulative total of 1,472 training hours. The total investment in competency development amounted to IDR4.853 billion, marking an increase from IDR3.886 billion in 2023.
