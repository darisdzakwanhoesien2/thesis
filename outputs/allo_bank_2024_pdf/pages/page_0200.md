# Table LIA: Penjelasan mengenai perbedaan antara nilai eksposur sesuai standar akuntansi keuangan dengan ketentuan kehat-hatian

LIA Table: Explanation of differences between prudential accounting exposures and carrying values

# Indonesia

Bank harus menjelaskan asal perbedaan antara nilai tercatat sesuai standar akuntansi keuangan, sebagaimana dilaporkan pada laporan keuangan dan nilai eksposur sesuai ketentuan kehat-hatian, sebagaimana terdapat pada template LI1 dan LI2.

Banks must explain the origin of the difference between the carrying value according to financial accounting standards, as reported in the financial statements and the exposure value according to prudential provisions, as contained in templates LI1 and LI2.

(a) Bank harus menjelaskan sumber perbedaan signifikan antara nilai pada kolom (a) dan (b) di LI1.

The bank must explain the source of significant differences between the values in columns (a) and (b) in LI1.

(b) Bank harus menjelaskan sumber perbedaan antara nilai tercatat dan nilai yang digunakan untuk tujuan pengaturan yang tercantum pada LI2.

The bank must explain the source of the difference between the recorded value and the value used for regulatory purposes as stated in LI2.

(c) Sesuai dengan implementasi dari panduan pada valuasi prudensial, bank harus menjelaskan system dan kontrol untuk memastikan estimasi valuasi prudensial dan dapat diandalkan. Pengungkapan harus memasukkan:

In accordance with the implementation of the guidelines on prudential valuation, banks should describe systems and controls to ensure prudential and reliable valuation estimates. Disclosures must include:

(i) Metodologi valuasi, termasuk penjelasan sejauh mana penggunaan metodologi mark-to-market dan mark-to-model.

Valuation methodology, including an explanation of the extent of use of mark-to-market and mark-to-model methodologies.

(ii) Deskripsi proses verifikasi harga independen.

Description of the independent price verification process.

(iii) Prosedur untuk penyesuaian valuasi atau cadangan (termasuk deskripsi proses dan metodologi untuk menilai posisi trading dengan tipe instrumen).

Procedures for valuation adjustments or reserves (including a description of the process and methodology for valuing trading positions by instrument type).

Nilai tercatat sebagaimana tercantum dalam publikasi laporan keuangan telah memperhitungkan prinsip standar kehati-hatian sehingga tidak terdapat perbedaan pada kedua nilai tersebut. Nilai tercatat sesuai masing-masing risiko merupakan nilai yang sesuai dengan POJK Perhitungan Kerangka Risiko Kredit, Perhitungan Kerangka Aset Sekuritisasi, Kerangka Counterparty Credit Risk, dan Kerangka Risiko Pasar. The recorded value as stated in the publication of the financial statements has taken into account the principle of prudential standards so that there is no difference in the two values. The recorded value according to each risk is a value that is in accordance with POJK Calculation of Credit Risk Framework, Calculation of Securitization Asset Framework, Counterparty Credit Risk Framework, and Market Risk Framework.
