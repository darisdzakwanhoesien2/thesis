## Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi

Dalam menjalankan fungsinya, Komite harus bertindak secara independen dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab antara lain sebagai berikut:

1. Terkait Kebijakan Remunerasi:

a. Memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris mengenai:

- Struktur remunerasi;
- Kebijakan atas remunerasi; dan
- Besaran atas remunerasi bagi Anggota Direksi dan/atau Anggota Dewan Komisaris.

b. Struktur, kebijakan, dan besaran remunerasi sebagaimana dimaksud di atas wajib dievaluasi oleh Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 1 (satu) tahun.

## Duties and Responsibilities of Remuneration and Nomination Committee

In carrying out its functions, the Committee must act independently in carrying out its duties and responsibilities, including the following:

1. Regarding Remuneration Policy:

a. Provide recommendations to the Board of Commissioners regarding:

- Remuneration structure;
- Policies on remuneration; And
- Amount of remuneration for Members of the Board of Directors and/or Members of the Board of Commissioners.

b. The structure, policies and amount of remuneration as referred to above must be evaluated by the Remuneration and Nomination Committee at least 1 (one) time in 1 (one) year.
