# Organ Pendukung Direksi
Supporting Organ of Board of Directors

Dalam membantu menjalankan tugas pengelolaan Bank, Direksi dapat membentuk organ pendukung sebagaimana diatur dalam ketentuan perundang-undangan. Direksi telah memiliki organ pendukung struktural, yakni Sekretaris Perusahaan &amp; PMO dan Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI) serta organ pendukung non-struktural yang terdiri dari Komite Kredit, Komite Kebijakan Perkreditan, Komite Aktiva dan Pasiva, Komite Manajemen Risiko, Komite Pengarah Teknologi Informasi, dan Komite Produk serta Komite Sumber Daya Manusia.

## Komite Manajemen Risiko

Bank membentuk Komite Manajemen Risiko untuk membantu dan memberikan arahan bagi anggota Direksi dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab terhadap proses implementasi manajemen risiko di Bank dan Untuk mewujudkan penerapan manajemen risiko yang efektif dan pelaksanaan fungsi manajemen risiko dalam mengelola keseluruhan eksposur risiko Bank.

In assisting in carrying out the Bank's management duties, the Board of Directors may form supporting organs as stipulated in the provisions of the law. The Board of Directors has structural supporting organs, namely the Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO and Internal Audit Work Unit (SKAI) as well as non-structural supporting organs consisting of the Credit Committee, Credit Policy Committee, Asset and Liability Committee, Risk Management Committee, Information Technology Steering Committee, and Product Committee and Human Resources Committee.

## Risk Management Committee

The Bank has formed a Risk Management Committee to assist and provide direction to members of the Board of Directors in carrying out their duties and responsibilities regarding the risk management implementation process at the Bank and to realize effective risk management implementation and implementation of risk management functions in managing the Bank's overall risk exposure.
