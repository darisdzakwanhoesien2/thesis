# Survei Kepuasan Nasabah terhadap Produk dan/atau Jasa Keuangan Berkelanjutan [OJK F.30]

Survei kepuasan nasabah memiliki peran penting bagi Allo Bank dalam meningkatkan kualitas produk dan layanan yang ditawarkan. Melalui survei ini, Bank dapat memahami kebutuhan, harapan, dan pengalaman nasabah, termasuk tantangan yang mereka hadapi dalam mengakses produk dan layanan Allo Bank. Lebih lanjut, informasi tersebut menjadi dasar yang kuat bagi Bank untuk merancang strategi peningkatan pelayanan, mengembangkan produk yang lebih relevan, serta memastikan bahwa setiap program yang dijalankan benar-benar memberikan dampak positif bagi nasabah.

Merujuk hasil survei tahun 2024, sebanyak  $78.56\%$  nasabah menyatakan puas dengan produk dan layanan Allo Bank, sedikit menurun  $1,67\%$  dibandingkan tahun 2023 dengan skor kepuasan nasabah (customer satisfaction score) sebesar  $80.23\%$ . Pengukuran tingkat kepuasan nasabah terdiri dari berbagai aspek, seperti ketersediaan produk dan fitur, kemudahan penggunaan aplikasi, keamanan transaksi dan respons layanan pelanggan yang tanggap.

in the Financial Services Sector, Allo Bank has implemented various programs to promote financial literacy and inclusion throughout 2024. One of the flagship initiatives is "Allo Goes to Campus," a program designed to enhance financial literacy among university students. Under this initiative, the Bank conducts campus visits and organizes face-to-face educational sessions as part of university events or in collaboration with academic institutions.

The implementation of financial literacy and inclusion is also carried out through collaborations with universities, which conduct company visits as part of the Allo Goes to Campus initiative, a program that has been running since 2022. This program reflects the Bank's commitment to ensuring that Indonesians, particularly young people, become more financially literate and acquire the right knowledge about financial management. Additionally, the Bank regularly provides financial education through public outreach initiatives, including segments on economic and financial programs such as the Money Talks segment on CNBC TV Indonesia and the Digital Innovation segment on SWA Media's podcast broadcasts. The Bank also supports financial literacy efforts through various activities organized by the OJK Institute. Beyond in-person programs, the Bank consistently delivers financial education online through social media, allowing for broader accessibility and outreach.

# Customer Satisfaction Survey on Sustainable Financial Products and Services [OJK F.30]

Customer satisfaction surveys play a crucial role for Allo Bank in enhancing the quality of its products and services. Through these surveys, the Bank gains valuable insights into customer needs, expectations, and experiences, including any challenges they face in accessing Allo Bank's products and services. This information serves as a strong foundation for the Bank to develop service improvement strategies, introduce more relevant products, and ensure that every program implemented has a meaningful and positive impact on customers.

According to the 2024 survey results,  $78.56\%$  of customers expressed satisfaction with Allo Bank's products and services, reflecting a slight decrease of  $1.67\%$  compared to 2023, when the customer satisfaction score was  $80.23\%$ . The measurement of customer satisfaction levels is based on several key aspects, including product and feature availability, ease of application usage, transaction security, and responsiveness of customer service.
