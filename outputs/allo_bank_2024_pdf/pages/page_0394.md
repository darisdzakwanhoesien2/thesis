# Komite Kredit

Bank membentuk Komite Kredit dengan tujuan untuk membantu Direksi dalam menetapkan keputusan di bidang perkreditan, khususnya dalam rangka memberikan persetujuan/menolak suatu usulan permohonan kredit yang dituangkan dalam bentuk proposal kredit yang diajukan oleh calon debitur/debitur melalui petugas Bank yang telah ditunjuk.

# Komposisi Komite Kredit

Komite kredit terdiri dari Kredit Wholesale, Kredit Treasury, dan Kredit Digital.

1. Susunan Komite Kredit Wholesale
a. Ketua Komite : President Director
b. Anggota Komite : Business Director
c. Undangan Tetap : Risk, Compliance, &amp; Legal Director
d. Sekretaris Komite : Credit Review &amp; Restructure Specialist

2. Susunan Komite Kredit Treasury
a. Ketua Komite : President Director
b. Anggota Komite : Business Director
c. Undangan Tetap : Risk, Compliance, &amp; Legal Director
d. Sekretaris Komite : Credit Review &amp; Restructure Specialist

3. Susunan Komite Kredit Digital
a. Ketua Komite : President Director
b. Anggota Komite : Digital Director
c. Undangan Tetap : Risk, Compliance, &amp; Legal Director

4. Susunan Komite Restruktur Kredit Wholesale
a. Ketua Komite : President Director
b. Anggota Komite : Finance, Operations &amp; Human Capital Director
c. Undangan Tetap : Risk, Compliance, &amp; Legal Director
d. Sekretaris Komite : Credit Review &amp; Restructure Specialist

# Credit Committee

The Bank establishes Credit Committee with the aim of assisting the Board of Directors in making decisions in the credit sector, particularly in the context of approving/rejecting a proposed credit application which is stated in the form of a credit proposal submitted by a prospective debtor/debtor through a designated Bank officer.

# Composition of Credit Committee

The credit committee consists of Wholesale Credit, Treasury Credit, and Digital Credit.

1. Composition of the Wholesale Credit Committee
a. Committee Chair: President Director
b. Committee Member: Business Director
c. Permanent Invitee: Risk, Compliance, &amp; Legal Director
d. Committee Secretary: Credit Review &amp; Restructure Specialist

2. Composition of the Treasury Credit Committee
a. Committee Chair: President Director
b. Committee Member: Business Director
c. Permanent Invitee: Risk, Compliance, &amp; Legal Director
d. Committee Secretary: Credit Review &amp; Restructure Specialist

3. Composition of the Digital Credit Committee
a. Committee Chair: President Director
b. Committee Member: Digital Director
c. Permanent Invitee: Risk, Compliance, &amp; Legal Director

4. Composition of the Wholesale Credit Restructuring Committee
a. Committee Chair: President Director
b. Committee Member: Finance, Operations &amp; Human Capital Director
c. Permanent Invitee: Risk, Compliance, &amp; Legal Director
d. Committee Secretary: Credit Review &amp; Restructure Specialist

# Tugas dan Wewenang Komite Kredit

1. Bertanggung jawab untuk memberikan persetujuan atau penolakan atas permohonan kredit dan/atau permohonan restrukturisasi kredit setelah memperoleh informasi yang cukup dari pengusul.

# Duties and Authorities of the Credit Committee

1. Responsible for approving or rejecting credit applications and/or credit restructuring applications after obtaining sufficient information from the proposer.
