In 2024, Allo Bank released new products in the form of Customer Fund Accounts, Virtual Accounts and Virtual Debit Cards as a way to realize its business sustainability strategy. Meanwhile, within the marketing sector, the Bank continued to develop strategies for establishing strategic partnerships with various other leading ecosystems through its implementation of an Open Banking model in an effort to develop a digital ecosystem and increase the contextual value of its financial services, especially for sectors that stood close to aspects of customer life.

No less important was the Bank's effort to strengthen its branding through its focus on targeting millennial and Gen Z segments as its primary segments. It did this by hosting a variety of unique marketing programs that included the Allo Bank Festival on June 21-22, 2024 at Istora Senayan Jakarta. This event, designed as an Intimate Concert Experience, featured performances from international guest star WAY V and Indonesian national musicians.

In addition to the aforementioned policies, Allo Bank also worked to consistently communicate with all stakeholders. Its efforts towards this end included opening a complaints channel for anyone affected by the Bank's operations. In line with that, the Bank organized various Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs that involved stakeholders, especially the community as one of the Bank's primary external stakeholders. Allo Bank did this in order to maximize the list of benefits available as a result of its presence as a business entity.

The Bank's consistency and seriousness in realizing its sustainability strategy has received various awards from external parties that served as forms of appreciation for its efforts. Throughout 2024, the list of the awards the Bank won included The Highest Digital Index Deposit Conventional Bank 2024 from Infobank &amp; Isentia; The Best Conventional Bank 2024, The Best Digital Bank (KBMI 2) 2024, The 2nd Highest Digital Index Digital Bank 2024, The Best Saving Account Conventional Bank (KBMI 2) 2024, all four of which are from Infobank; 5th Indonesia Top Bank Awards 2024 in KBMI 2 Category from The Economics; Best Bank 2024: KBMI 2 (Core Capital More than IDR6T - IDR14T) from investortrust; Top 100 CEO &amp; The 200 Future Leaders Forum 2024: Indra Utoyo - Banker Of The Year 2024, Sajal Bhatnagar - Future Leaders of the Year 2024 from InfoBank, and a Predicate of "Very Good" for Outstanding Digital Innovation 2024 from SWA Media Group.
