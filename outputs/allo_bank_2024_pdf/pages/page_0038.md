# Macroeconomic Review

According to the December 2024 Monetary Policy Review released by Bank Indonesia, global financial market uncertainty increased alongside the risk of a slowdown in global economic growth. The United States (US) trade policy, which includes raising import tariffs on various commodities and expanding country coverage, has heightened the risk of global trade fragmentation.

Global inflation rose beyond previous estimates due to ongoing supply chain disruptions. In the US, the anticipated cut in the Fed Funds Rate (FFR) is expected to be delayed due to persistently high inflation. Meanwhile, a more expansionary US fiscal policy has driven US Treasury yields to remain high across both short-term and long-term tenors. The continued strengthening of the US dollar has led to a shift in global investor preferences, with portfolios being reallocated back to the US.
