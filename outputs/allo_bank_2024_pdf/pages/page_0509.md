Laporan Keberlanjutan

Selama tahun 2024, Bank menerima pengaduan sebanyak 19.335 kasus, naik dibandingkan tahun 2023 yang mencapai 13.375 kasus. Topik pengaduan yang masuk terbagi dalam beberapa kategori yaitu Kartu ATM/Debit/Mesin ATM, Electronic Banking dan Sistem Pembayaran Lainnya (Transfer Dana), dan semuanya (99,91%) telah ditindaklanjuti sesuai Standard Operating Procedure yang berlaku.

## Kegiatan Tanggung Jawab Sosial Lingkungan (TJSL) [OJK F.25]

Allo Bank menyelenggarakan Program TJSL/CSR sebagai jembatan antara Bank dengan masyarakat. Melalui program tersebut, Bank tidak hanya berkontribusi pada peningkatan kualitas hidup dan pemberdayaan masyarakat, tetapi juga memperkuat reputasi dan kepercayaan publik terhadap Bank. Dalam era digital yang terus berkembang, program CSR membantu Allo Bank untuk menunjukkan komitmennya terhadap tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan, yang pada gilirannya dapat menarik minat investor dan nasabah yang peduli terhadap dampak sosial dan lingkungan.

CSR juga merupakan bentuk komitmen Allo Bank kepada lingkungan dengan tujuan untuk berperan serta dalam pembangunan berkelanjutan selaras dengan prinsip Triple Bottom Line yaitu People, Planet, Profit (3P) serta memberikan nilai tambah kepada semua pemangku kepentingan, termasuk internal perusahaan guna mendukung pertumbuhan Bank.

Di Allo Bank, CSR didefinisikan sebagai tanggung jawab atas dampak dari keputusan dan aktivitasnya pada masyarakat dan lingkungan melalui perilaku yang transparan dan beretika yang bertujuan untuk:

1. Membangun citra dan reputasi Bank yang dapat meningkatkan kepercayaan kepada Allo Bank.
2. Membangun hubungan yang harmonis dengan segenap pemangku kepentingan.
3. Menumbuhkan positive value karyawan terhadap Allo Bank.
4. Konsisten dengan pembangunan berkelanjutan (sustainable development) dan kesejahteraan masyarakat.
5. Mematuhi peraturan dan ketentuan yang berlaku.

Allo Bank terus berupaya memaksimalkan manfaat atas keberadaan Bank di tengah masyarakat melalui berbagai program CSR di bawah branding "Allo Bank Pelita", termasuk program kerja sama dengan CT Arsa Foundation untuk semakin memperkuat dampak sosial kepada semua stakeholders. Pelaksanaan CSR sekaligus merupakan dukungan Bank terhadap 17 Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB) atau Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) yang saat ini sedang dilaksanakan pemerintah. The complaints were categorized into several topics, including ATM/Debit Cards/ATM Machines, Electronic Banking and Other Payment Systems (Fund Transfers), all of which (99.91%) were followed up in accordance with the applicable Standard Operating Procedure.

## Corporate Social and Environmental Responsibility Activities [OJK F.25]

Allo Bank implements the TJSL/CSR Program as a means of fostering relationships with the community. Through this program, the Bank not only contributes to improving quality of life and empowering communities but also enhances its reputation and strengthens public trust. In the ever-evolving digital era, CSR initiatives enable Allo Bank to demonstrate its commitment to social and environmental responsibility, attracting investors and customers who value social and environmental impacts.

CSR is also a reflection of Allo Bank's commitment to environmental responsibility, supporting sustainable development in line with the Triple Bottom Line principle: People, Planet, and Profit (3P). By doing so, the Bank provides added value to all stakeholders, including its internal workforce, to support long-term business growth.

At Allo Bank, CSR is defined as the Bank's responsibility for the impact of its decisions and activities on society and the environment through transparent and ethical behavior. The objectives of Allo Bank's CSR initiatives include:

1. Enhancing the Bank's image and reputation to strengthen public trust.
2. Building and maintaining harmonious relationships with all stakeholders.
3. Cultivating employee positive value towards Allo Bank.
4. Aligning with sustainable development and community welfare.
5. Complying with all applicable laws and regulations.

Allo Bank continuously seeks to maximize the positive impact of its presence in the community through various CSR initiatives under the "Allo Bank Pelita" branding. One of the key initiatives includes a collaboration with the CT Arsa Foundation to further amplify the Bank's social impact across its stakeholder network. Additionally, these CSR programs reflect Allo Bank's support for the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) set by the government. The CSR collaborations undertaken in 2024 and their alignment with the SDGs are as follows:

1. Food and Grocery Sharing Movement ("Gebrag"). This initiative provides free food packages and meals to improve nutrition and meet the dietary needs of underprivileged individuals in the Jabodetabek area during Ramadan 2024. This program aligns with SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being.
