# Keberagaman Komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Allo Bank disusun dengan memperhatikan kebijakan keberagaman berdasarkan ketentuan Pemegang Saham dengan memperhatikan pengetahuan, usia, jenis kelamin, pendidikan dan pengalaman yang sesuai dengan kebutuhan dari Bank, sehingga mendapatkan komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang optimal guna meningkatkan kinerja Bank ke depannya.

Keberagaman komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi merupakan bagian dari upaya dari Allo Bank untuk mendorong proses pengambilan keputusan yang lebih objektif, komprehensif, optimal, dan memiliki dampak positif terhadap pengawasan dan pengelolaan Bank. Keberagaman komposisi ini diharapkan dapat memperkaya sudut pandang dan kepentingan dalam proses pengambilan keputusan, baik di tubuh Dewan Komisaris maupun Direksi, sehingga mampu memberikan nilai tambah bagi aspek operasional dan usaha Bank.

Furthermore, the Board of Commissioners and Directors submit a proposal for approval of the performance achievements of the Board of Directors at the GMS and the GMS will grant full release from responsibility to the members of the Board of Directors for the management carried out during the previous fiscal year, as long as these actions are reflected in the Annual Report and Financial Report, except embezzlement, fraud and other criminal acts.

# Diversity in the Composition of the Board of Commissioners and Directors

The composition of the Board of Commissioners and Directors of Allo Bank refers to the diversity policy based on the provisions of Shareholders by taking into account knowledge, age, gender, education and experience in accordance with the needs of the Bank, so as to obtain an optimal composition of the Board of Commissioners and Directors in order to improve the Bank's performance in the future.

The diversity of the composition of the Board of Commissioners and Directors is part of Allo Bank's efforts to encourage a decision-making process that is more objective, comprehensive, optimal, and has a positive impact on the supervision and management of the Bank. It is hoped that this diversity viewpoints and interests in the decision-making process, both within the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, so as to provide added value to the Bank's operational and business aspects.
