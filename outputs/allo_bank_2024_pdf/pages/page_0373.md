13. Komite Audit wajib memastikan bahwa laporan pelaksanaan dan pokok-pokok hasil audit internal disampaikan kepada OJK secara semesteran paling lambat:

a. tanggal 31 Juli tahun berjalan untuk semester kesatu;

14. Memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris mengenai penunjukan pengendali mutu independen dari pihak eksternal untuk melakukan kaji ulang terhadap kinerja Satuan Kerja Audit Internal. 17. Melaporkan hasil pemantauan dan penelaahan secara berkala, serta memberi masukan atas hal-hal yang perlu menjadi perhatian Dewan Komisaris;

18. Ketua Komite Audit bertugas dan bertanggung jawab untuk melakukan koordinasi atas seluruh kegiatan Komite untuk memenuhi tujuan Komite sesuai dengan pembentukannya;

19. Ketua dan anggota Komite Audit bertanggung jawab untuk hal-hal sebagai berikut:

a. Menentukan rencana kerja tahunan.
b. Menentukan jadwal rapat tahunan.
c. Membuat laporan berkala mengenai kegiatan Komite serta hal-hal yang dirasakan perlu untuk menjadi perhatian Dewan Komisaris.
d. Menunjuk anggota Komite Audit non Dewan Komisaris atau menunjuk pihak ketiga lainnya sebagai sekretaris Komite untuk mencatat Rapat Komite dan membuat Risalah Rapat Komite.

12. The Audit Committee is obliged to ensure that a special report regarding any internal audit findings that are expected to compromise the Bank's business continuity, signed by the President Director and Independent Commissioner who is the Chair of the Audit Committee, is submitted to the OJK no later than 3 (three) working days after the discovery.

13. The Audit Committee is obliged to ensure that the implementation report and main points of internal audit results are submitted to the OJK on a semi-annual basis no later than:

a. July 31 of the current year for the first semester;
b. January 31 of the following year for the second semester.

14. Provide recommendations to the Board of Commissioners regarding the appointment of an independent quality controller from an external party to review the performance of the Internal Audit Work Unit. The Audit Committee is obliged to ensure that the independent external party review report signed by the President Director and President Commissioner is submitted to the OJK no later than 2 (two) months after the review period ends;

15. Hold regular meetings with related work units to discuss matters within the scope of their supervision;

16. Develop an Audit Committee Work Charter and conduct a review as needed at least once every 2 (two) years;

17. Report the results of monitoring and reviews periodically, as well as provide input on matters that need the attention of the Board of Commissioners;

18. The Head of the Audit Committee is tasked with and responsible for coordinating all Committee activities to fulfill the Committee's objectives in accordance with its formation;

19. The Head and members of the Audit Committee are responsible for the following matters:

a. Determining the annual work plan.
b. Determining the annual meeting schedule.
c. Making periodic reports regarding Committee activities and matters deemed necessary for the attention of the Board of Commissioners.
d. Appointing non-Board of Commissioners Audit Committee members or appoint other third parties as Committee secretaries to record Committee Meetings and prepare Minutes of Committee Meetings.
e. Attending meetings.
f. Playing an active role and contribute to every Committee Meeting activity.
