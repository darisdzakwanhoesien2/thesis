# Pejabat Sekretaris Perusahaan dan Dasar Pengangkatan

Berdasarkan Surat Keputusan (SK) PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk ("Bank") No. 074/DIRAB/22 tertanggal 3 Oktober 2022, Bank melalui Direktur Utama menunjukkan Stacey Aryadi Suryoputro sebagai Head Sekretaris Perusahaan &amp; PMO.

# Corporate Secretary Officer and Appointment Basis

Based on the Decree (SK) of PT Allo Bank Indonesia Tbk ("Bank") No. 074/DIRAB/22 dated October 3, 2022, the Bank through the President Director appointed Stacey Aryadi Suryoputro as Head of Corporate Secretary &amp; PMO.
