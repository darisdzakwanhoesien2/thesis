GCG SELF ASSESSMENT

Bank telah melakukan penilaian sendiri (self assessment) terhadap penerapan tata kelola (Good Corporate Governance) yang dilakukan oleh pihak internal dengan mempertimbangkan berbagai faktor penilaian secara komprehensif dan terstruktur, yang mencakup governance structure, governance process, dan governance outcome. Pada tahun 2024, self assessment penerapan GCG Bank meraih nilai komposit sebesar 2 atau memperoleh predikat "BAIK".

The Bank has conducted its self-assessment for its Good Corporate Governance implementation. It has done this by taking various assessment factors into consideration in a comprehensive and structured manner. These factors include the Bank's governance structure, governance process, and governance outcome. In 2024, the Bank's GCG implementation self-assessment process saw it achieving a composite value of 2, or a predicate score of "GOOD".
