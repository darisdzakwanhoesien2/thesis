## Independensi Dewan Komisaris

1. Mayoritas anggota Dewan Komisaris dilarang saling memiliki hubungan keluarga sampai dengan derajat kedua dengan sesama anggota Direksi dan/atau dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris lain.
2. Mantan anggota Direksi atau Pejabat Eksekutif Bank atau pihak-pihak yang mempunyai hubungan dengan Bank, yang dapat mempengaruhi kemampuan yang bersangkutan untuk bertindak independen, wajib menjalani masa tunggu (cooling off) paling singkat 1 (satu) tahun sebelum menjadi Komisaris Independen pada Bank.
3. Ketentuan di atas tidak berlaku bagi mantan anggota Direksi yang membawahi fungsi pengawasan atau Pejabat Eksekutif yang melakukan fungsi pengawasan pada Bank.
4. Anggota Dewan Komisaris wajib menandatangani Surat Pernyataan Independensi yang berlaku sesuai dengan periode atau masa jabatan sebagaimana ditetapkan dalam RUPS.

## Program Orientasi bagi Anggota Dewan Komisaris Baru

Dalam hal terjadi perubahan susunan dan komposisi Dewan Komisaris, maka anggota Dewan Komisaris yang baru diangkat wajib mengikuti program pengenalan perusahaan sebagaimana diatur dalam BOC Charter. Program pengenalan tersebut dilakukan untuk memberikan pemahaman mengenai tugas dan tanggung jawab sebagai anggota Dewan Komisaris agar dijalankan sebaik-baiknya. Kegiatan ini juga bertujuan agar anggota Dewan Komisaris dapat memahami latar belakang dan kegiatan usaha Bank agar dapat mempermudah dan melancarkan pelaksanaan tugas Dewan Komisaris secara efektif.

Sehubungan dengan tidak adanya perubahan susunan dan komposisi Dewan Komisaris sepanjang tahun 2024, maka Bank tidak melaksanakan program pengenalan Dewan Komisaris baru.

## Independence of the Board of Commissioners

1. The majority of the Board of Commissioners members are prohibited from having family relationships up to the second degree with other members of the Board of Directors and/or with other members of the Board of Commissioners.
2. Former members of the Board of Directors or Executive Officers of the Bank, or parties who have relationships with the Bank that can influence their ability to act independently, must undergo a minimum cooling-off period of 1 (one) year before becoming an Independent Commissioner at the Bank.
3. The above provision does not apply to former members of the Board of Directors who are in charge of supervisory functions or Executive Officers who conduct supervisory functions at the Bank.
4. Members of the Board of Commissioners are required to sign a Declaration of Independence letter that applies according to the period or term of office as determined in the GMS.

## Orientation Program for New Members of Board of Commissioners

In the event of a change in the composition of the Board of Commissioners, newly appointed members are required to participate in the company's introduction program, as stipulated in the BOC Charter. This program is designed to provide an understanding of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners, ensuring that they can carry out their roles effectively. Additionally, it aims to familiarize members with the Bank's background and business activities, facilitating the effective execution of their supervisory duties.

Since there were no changes in the composition of the Board of Commissioners in 2024, the Bank did not implement an introduction program for new members.
