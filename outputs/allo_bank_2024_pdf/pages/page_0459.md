# Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan Tahun 2023

Di sepanjang tahun 2024, Allo Bank telah berupaya semaksimal mungkin untuk melaksanakan keuangan berkelanjutan dengan menyelaraskan pencapaian aspek ekonomi, lingkungan hidup, dan sosial. Upaya tersebut meraih hasil sebagai berikut:

1. Kinerja Aspek Ekonomi

Setiap tahun, Allo Bank menyusun Rencana Bisnis Bank (RBB) secara matang dan realistis berdasarkan prinsip kehati-hatian dan penerapan manajemen risiko. Rencana bisnis antara lain berisi tentang rencana Bank untuk meningkatkan kinerja usaha serta strategi untuk merealisasikan rencana tersebut sesuai target dan waktu yang ditetapkan. Penyusunan rencana bisnis senantiasa memperhatikan faktor eksternal dan internal yang dapat mempengaruhi kelangsungan usaha Bank, prinsip kehati-hatian, penerapan manajemen risiko, dan asas perbankan yang sehat.

Segenap manajemen dan karyawan Allo Bank terus mengukuhkan sinergi untuk merealisasikan target-target dalam RBB tahun 2024 dan membuahkan hasil yang membanggakan. Pendapatan Operasional tercatat sebesar Rp1,447 trilion, naik 25% dibandingkan pencapaian tahun 2023, didorong oleh pertumbuhan pendapatan bunga bersih dan pendapatan berbasis biaya. Pendapatan bunga bersih tumbuh 8% menjadi Rp.1,118 trilion ditopang oleh pertumbuhan kredit di tengah kondisi ekonomi makro yang penuh tantangan. Laba Bersih sebesar Rp467 miliar atau naik 5% dibanding tahun sebelumnya.

Pencapaian tersebut sejalan dengan peningkatan jumlah akuisisi/onboarding nasabah hingga akhir tahun 2024 yang mencapai lebih dari 11 juta nasabah. Selain itu, juga selaras dengan keberhasilan Bank meningkatkan engagement sehingga nasabah puas dan sering bertransaksi di Allo Bank. Dalam hal ini, Bank mencatatkan peningkatan signifikan dalam jumlah transaksi khususnya transaksi menggunakan QRIS, transfer melalui BI-Fast maupun pembayaran Bill Payment.

2. Kinerja Aspek Lingkungan

Operasional Bank sebagai lembaga jasa keuangan tidak berdampak langsung bagi lingkungan. Walau demikian, Bank senantiasa merujuk berbagai regulasi yang mengatur hubungan antara industri perbankan dengan lingkungan. Dengan memperhatikan prinsip kehati-hatian, Allo Bank merilis produk dan layanan berupa giro, tabungan, deposito dan pinjaman konsumsi (instant cash dan pay later) yang semuanya tidak berkontribusi negatif terhadap lingkungan.

# Sustainable Finance Implementation in 2023

Throughout 2024, Allo Bank has made every possible effort to implement sustainable finance by aligning its achievements when it came to its economic, environmental, and social aspects. These efforts have bore the following results:

1. Economic Aspect Performance

Every year, Allo Bank prepares a mature and realistic Bank Business Plan (RBB) that it designs based on the principle of prudence and risk management implementation. This business plan includes the Bank's plans to improve business performance, as well as accompanying strategies to realize these plans within an established time frame and with clear goals. The process of preparing this business plan always takes into account any external and internal factors that can potentially affect the Bank's business continuity. The plan also takes into account the principle of prudence and the implementation of risk management, all while being guided by healthy banking as a concept.

All management and employees of Allo Bank continue to strengthen synergy to realize the targets in the 2024 RBB and produce good results. Operating Revenue was recorded at IDR1,447 trillion, up 25% compared to the achievement in 2023, driven by growth in net interest income and fee-based income. Net interest income grew 8% to IDR1,118 trillion supported by credit growth amidst challenging macroeconomic conditions. Net Profit reached IDR467 billion or up 5% compared to the previous year.

These achievements were in line with an increase in Allo Bank's number of customer acquisitions/onboarding until the end of 2024 that reached more than 11 million customers. In addition, these achievements were also in line with the Bank's success in boosting engagement so that customers are satisfied and carried out transactions frequently through Allo Bank. As a result, the Bank was also able to record a significant increase in the number of transactions, particularly those using QRIS, as well as the number of transfers via BI-Fast and Bill Payment payments.

2. Environmental Aspect Performance

The Bank's operations as a financial services institution do not have any direct impact on the environment. At the same time, the Bank still paid heed to various regulations that governed the relationship between the banking industry and the environment. The Bank paid attention to the principle of prudence and released products and services in the form of current accounts, savings, deposits and consumer loans (instant cash and pay later), none of which had any sort of negative environmental contributions.
