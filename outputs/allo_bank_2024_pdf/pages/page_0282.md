## Analisis Kualitatif

## Qualitative Analysis

- NSFR (Net Stable Funding Ratio) as of December 31, 2024 amounted to 115.87%, a decrease of 2.09% compared to NSFR as of September 30, 2024 which amounted to 117.96%. The decrease in NSFR was mainly due to the increase in RSF (Required Stable Funding) which was greater than the increase in ASF (Available Stable Funding). Total RSF increased by 2.21% and total AFS increased by 0.39% compared to the previous quarter.

- The decrease in the Bank's NSFR was due to the following reasons:

ASF:
- Total ASF increased by 0.39% when compared to NSFR position on September 30, 2024.
- Capital in accordance with POJK CAR increased by 1.39% compared to NSFR position on September 30, 2024.
- The increase in ASF is mainly in the component of deposits originating from individual customers and funding originating from micro and small business customers amounting to Rp305.41 billion or 31.77% when compared to the period September 30, 2024.
- Specifically, the Bank's funding is dominated by non-stable funding of Rp1.20 trillion, compared to Rp62.12 billion for the Bank's stable funding.
- The Bank's stable to non-stable funding ratio is 5.16%.

- RSF:
- Total RSF increased by 2.21% compared to NSFR as of September 30, 2024.
- The increase in RSF came from other assets component by 55.13% and administrative accounts component by 11.06% compared to NSFR as of September 30, 2024.
