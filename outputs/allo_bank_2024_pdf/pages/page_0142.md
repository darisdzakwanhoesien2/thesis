Per 31 Desember 2024, kinerja produk kredit tercatat sebesar Rp7,48 triliun yang terutama berasal dari kelompok segmentasi kredit Bank, yaitu Kredit Korporasi dan Kredit Konsumsi. As of December 31, 2024, the Bank's loan product performance was recorded at IDR7.48 trillion, which mainly came from the Bank's lending segmentation groups, specifically, Corporate Lending and Consumer Lending. Meanwhile, deposit product performance was recorded at IDR6.10 trillion, which came from contributions in current accounts, savings, and deposit products.
