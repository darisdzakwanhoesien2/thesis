## Allo Talks: Winning The Challenge To Accelerate Growth

Sesuai dengan values Growth, Agility, dan Learning, Bank mengadakan sharing session dimana Ibu Aviliani membahas kondisi makro ekonomi terkini dan tantangan perbankan digital ke depan dengan semua Pimpinan Kepala Divisi.

Reflecting the values of Growth, Agility, and Learning, the Bank organized a sharing session where Mrs. Aviliani discussed current macroeconomic conditions and the future challenges of digital banking with all Division Heads.

## Town Hall 2024

Direksi menggelar Town Hall dengan semua karyawan. Mengangkat tema Driving Towards Allo Bank 2.0, acara tersebut bertujuan untuk membagikan strategi dan rencana kerja serta meningkatkan koordinasi dan komunikasi demi pencapaian strategic objectives sepanjang tahun 2024.

The Board of Directors held a Town Hall meeting with all employees. With the theme Driving Towards Allo Bank 2.0, the event aimed to share strategies and work plans while improving coordination and communication to achieve strategic objectives for 2024.

## Gebrag Ramadhan

Bank bekerjasama dengan CT Arsa Foundation dalam program Gerakan Berbagi Pangan dan Sembako ("Gebrag") memberikan bantuan kepada kaum dhufa atau yang membutuhkan pada saat bulan Ramadhan.

In collaboration with the CT Arsa Foundation, the Bank conducted the Food and Staple Goods Giveaway ("Gebrag") program to assist underprivileged individuals during the month of Ramadan.
