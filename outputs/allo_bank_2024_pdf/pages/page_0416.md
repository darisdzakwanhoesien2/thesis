# Gambaran Umum Manajemen Risiko

Sejalan dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 18/ POJK.03/2016 tanggal 16 Maret 2016 tentang Penerapan Manajemen Risiko bagi Bank Umum, Allo Bank memandang perlunya untuk membangun kesadaran dan budaya manajemen risiko terpadu (integrated risk culture) guna mencapai tujuan Bank jangka panjang. Fokus penerapan manajemen risiko Bank sendiri lebih diutamakan pada efektivitas penerapan tata kelola dan kerangka kerja manajemen risiko.

Kerangka Manajemen Risiko

Kerangka kerja manajemen risiko Bank menetapkan pendekatan pengelolaan risiko dan kerangka pengendalian di mana risiko dikelola untuk mendapatkan keseimbangan antara risiko (risk) dan pendapatan (return). Penerapan kerangka kerja manajemen risiko dilakukan melalui perumusan tingkat risiko yang akan diambil (risk appetite), dan toleransi risiko (risk tolerance) bagi setiap jenis risiko, pengembangan kebijakan dan prosedur manajemen risiko yang berkesesuaian serta pengembangan struktur pengendalian internal secara terpadu. Selain itu, Bank juga membangun budaya risiko yang menitikberatkan kesadaran seluruh pegawai akan risiko dan efektivitas proses manajemen risiko. Penerapan manajemen risiko di Allo Bank mengacu pada empat pilar manajemen risiko, yaitu:

1. Pengawasan aktif Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.
2. Kecukupan kebijakan, prosedur, dan penetapan limit manajemen risiko.
3. Kecukupan proses identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan, dan pengendalian Risiko serta sistem informasi manajemen risiko.
4. Sistem pengendalian internal yang menyeluruh.

Penerapan manajemen risiko Allo Bank juga berpedoman pada dokumen-dokumen dari Basel Committee on Banking Supervision.

Struktur Organisasi dan Tata Kelola

Dalam menjalankan memastikan pengelolaan risiko, Bank telah membentuk Komite Manajemen Risiko dan Komite Aset dan Liabilitas (ALCO) pada tingkat Direksi serta Komite Pemantau Risiko pada tingkat Dewan Komisaris. Komite Pemantau Risiko bertanggung jawab untuk mengevaluasi keakuratan kebijakan manajemen risiko dan implementasinya, memantau serta mengevaluasi kinerja dari Komite Manajemen Risiko dan Satuan Kerja Manajemen Risiko. Komite Manajemen Risiko menetapkan strategi, kebijakan, dan prosedur manajemen risiko, serta memantau implementasinya, mengembangkan budaya pengelolaan risiko, dan memastikan sumber daya yang memadai telah dikembangkan untuk memastikan pengelolaan risiko di Allo Bank. Sementara, ALCO bertanggung jawab untuk mengelola modal dan menetapkan kebijakan terkait dengan pengelolaan neraca dan kepatuhan terhadapnya, termasuk di dalamnya manajemen risiko likuiditas, kecukupan modal, dan risiko suku bunga.

General Overview of Risk Management

In line with Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 18/POJK.03/2016 dated March 16, 2016 concerning the Implementation of Risk Management for Conventional Bank, Allo Bank acknowledges the urgency of raising awareness and establishing a culture of integrated risk management in order to achieve the Bank's long-term goals. In implementing the risk management, the Bank has put more emphasis on the effectiveness of implementing governance and risk management frameworks.

Risk Management Framework

The Bank's risk management framework establishes a risk management approach and control framework in which risk is managed to achieve a balance between risk and return. The risk management framework is formed through the formulation of the level of risk to be taken (risk appetite), and risk tolerance for each type of risk, the development of appropriate risk management policies and procedures, and the development of an integrated internal control structure. In addition, the Bank also builds a risk culture that emphasizes the awareness of all employees of risk and the effectiveness of the risk management process. The implementation of risk management at Allo Bank refers to four pillars of risk management, namely:

Active supervision of the Board of Commissioners and Directors.
Adequacy of risk management policies, procedures, and determination of limits.
Adequacy of the process of identification, measurement, monitoring, and control of Risk and the risk management information system.
Comprehensive internal control system.

The implementation of Allo Bank's risk management is also guided by documents from the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision.

Organizational Structure and Governance

In ensuring risk management, the Bank has established a Risk Management Committee and an Asset and Liability Committee (ALCO) at the Board of Directors level and a Risk Oversight Committee at the Board of Commissioners level. The Risk Oversight Committee is responsible for evaluating the accuracy of risk management policies and their implementation, monitoring and evaluating the performance of the Risk Management Committee and the Risk Management Work Unit. The Risk Management Committee establishes risk management strategies, policies, and procedures, and monitors their implementation, develops a risk management culture, and ensures that adequate resources have been developed to ensure risk management at Allo Bank. Meanwhile, ALCO is responsible for managing capital and establishing policies related to balance sheet management and compliance with it, including liquidity risk management, capital adequacy, and interest rate risk.
