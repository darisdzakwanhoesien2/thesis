## Produk Simpanan

Allo Bank menyediakan produk simpanan berupa penghimpunan dana baik dari nasabah Retail Banking maupun nasabah Wholesale Banking. Dalam hal penghimpunan dana nasabah Retail, Bank menawarkan berbagai produk-produk simpanan berbasis digital, seperti uang elektronik (AlloPay dan AlloPay+), tabungan (Allo Prime dan Allo Grow), dan deposito yang dilakukan melalui mobile app Allo Bank. Bank juga menyediakan produk giro dan deposito untuk nasabah Wholesale Banking yang sudah ada untuk melakukan transaksi operasional maupun terkait dengan pembayaran kredit.

## Kinerja Produk Simpanan

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Bank berhasil menghimpun Dana Pihak Ketiga (DPK) sebesar Rp 6,10 triliun, meningkat 24,4% jika dibanding tahun 2023 yang sebesar Rp4,90 triliun. Pencapaian ini mencerminkan hasil dari kinerja Bank untuk meningkatkan dana nasabah di tengah tantangan likuiditas yang ketat.

## Deposit Products

Allo Bank provides savings products in the form of fund collection from both Retail Banking customers and Wholesale Banking customers. In terms of fund collection from Retail customers, the Bank offers various digital-based savings products, such as electronic money (AlloPay and AlloPay+), savings (Allo Prime and Allo Grow), and deposits made through the Allo Bank mobile app. The Bank also provides current account and deposit products for existing Wholesale Banking customers to conduct operational transactions or related to credit payments.

## Deposit Products Performance

Throughout 2024, the Bank managed to collect Third Party Funds (TPF) of IDR 6.10 trillion, an increase of 24.4% compared to 2023 which was IDR 4.90 trillion. This achievement reflects the results of the Bank's performance in increasing customer funds amidst tight liquidity challenges.
