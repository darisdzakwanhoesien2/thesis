# Prospek dan Peluang

Pada tahun 2025, perekonomian Indonesia diproyeksikan tumbuh stabil di kisaran 5%. Bank Dunia dalam laporannya pada Oktober 2024 memperkirakan pertumbuhan sebesar 5,1% untuk tahun 2025. Sementara itu, Pemerintah Indonesia sebagaimana disampaikan dalam Anggaran Pendapatan dan Belanja Negara Tahun 2025 menargetkan pertumbuhan ekonomi sebesar 5,2%. Di sisi lain, pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2025 akan menghadapi berbagai kendala dan tantangan di antaranya defisit transaksi berjalan yang diperkirakan melebar antara 0,5% hingga 1,3% dari PDB, serta terjadinya penyusutan kelas menengah yang dapat mempengaruhi konsumsi domestik.

Di tengah proyeksi optimistis, sekaligus tantangan yang patut diwaspadai, industri perbankan Indonesia diharapkan tetap solid dan berperan aktif dalam mendukung pertumbuhan ekonomi tahun 2025. Dalam hal ini, Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) optimistis terhadap kinerja positif sektor jasa keuangan, meskipun menghadapi tantangan likuiditas yang dapat mempengaruhi penyaluran kredit. Bank Indonesia memproyeksikan pertumbuhan kredit perbankan akan meningkat menjadi 11-13% pada tahun 2025, lebih tinggi dibandingkan proyeksi tahun sebelumnya.

Berdasarkan latar belakang dan proyeksi tersebut, Allo Bank telah menetapkan berbagai target dalam RBB Tahun 2025, terkhusus pada aspek ekonomi, serta rencana strategis untuk mencapainya. Target tersebut di antaranya Laba Bersih sebesar Rp466 miliar; Kredit yang Diberikan sebesar Rp9,7 triliun atau 130% dibandingkan realisasi tahun 2024, dan Dana Pihak Ketiga sebesar Rp8,4 triliun atau 138% dibandingkan realisasi tahun 2024.

Selaras dengan penetapan tersebut, Allo Bank telah menetapkan berbagai inisiatif strategis dan berkomitmen untuk melaksanakannya secara konsisten dengan menguatkan sinergi antara manajemen dan karyawan. Berbekal spirit untuk memberikan kontribusi terbaik, Allo Bank optimistis dapat mencapai target-target tersebut demi mewujudkan bisnis yang berkelanjutan.

# Prospects and Opportunities

In 2025, the Indonesian economy is projected to grow steadily in the range of 5%. In its October 2024 report, the World Bank specifically estimated a total growth of 5.1% for 2025. Meanwhile, the Government of Indonesia has stated in its 2025 State Budget that it is targeting an economic growth of 5.2%. At the same time, Indonesia's economic growth in 2025 is set to face various obstacles and challenges, including its current account deficit, which is estimated to widen between 0.5% and 1.3% of GDP, as well as a shrinking middle class that could affect domestic consumption.

In the midst of these optimistic projections, as well as in the midst of challenges that should continue to be monitored, the Indonesian banking industry is expected to remain in solid condition and continue to play an active role in supporting economic growth in 2025. In this regard, the Financial Services Authority (OJK) is optimistic about the positive performance of the financial services sector in the face of liquidity challenges that could affect credit distribution. Bank Indonesia projects that banking credit growth will increase to 11-13% in 2025, higher than the previous year's projections.

With all of these projections in mind, Allo Bank has set various targets in its 2025 RBB, specifically when it comes to the economic aspects. It has also established strategic plans for how to achieve these targets. The targets include Net Profit of IDR466 billion; Lending of IDR9.7 trillion or 130% compared to the realization in 2024, and Third Party Funds of IDR8.4 trillion or 138% compared to the realization in 2024.

In line with these goals, Allo Bank has established various strategic initiatives and is committed to implementing them consistently. It aims to do so by strengthening its synergy between management and employees. Armed with its desire to provide the best in contributions, Allo Bank is optimistic that it can achieve its targets in order to realize its goal of sustainable business operations.
