# Pedoman Kerja Dewan Komisaris

Dewan Komisaris melaksanakan fungsi pengawasan atas pengelolaan Bank dengan mengacu kepada BOC Charter yang berfungsi sebagai panduan dalam memahami peraturan-peraturan terkait tata kerja Dewan Komisaris. BOC Charter disusun berdasarkan prinsip-prinsip hukum korporasi, ketentuan Anggaran Dasar, peraturan dan ketentuan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, arahan Pemegang Saham, serta praktik-praktik terbaik (best practices) GCG.

BOC Charter ini diharapkan dapat mempermudah Dewan Komisaris memahami peraturan-peraturan yang terkait dengan tata kerja Dewan Komisaris, menghilangkan praktik pengambilan keputusan berdasarkan kebiasaan (yang tidak tertulis) serta memperjelas tingkat akuntabilitas dalam pengambilan keputusan. Aspek yang diatur dalam BOC Charter meliputi:

1. Persyaratan Anggota Dewan Komisaris;
2. Persyaratan Komisaris Independent;
3. Pengangkatan Anggota Dewan Komisaris;
4. Pemberhentian Anggota Dewan Komisaris;
5. Komposisi Dewan Komisaris;
6. Rangkap Jabatan Anggota Dewan Komisaris;
7. Transparansi, Independensi, dan Etika Jabatan Dewan Komisaris;
8. Tugas, Tanggung Jawab dan Wewenang Dewan Komisaris;
9. Kehadiran dan Waktu Kerja Dewan Komisaris;
10. Tata Tertib Rapat Dewan Komisaris;

# Work Guidelines of Board of Commissioners

The Board of Commissioners carries out its supervisory function over the management of the Bank by referring to the BOC Charter, which serves as a guide for understanding regulations related to the work procedures of the Board of Commissioners. The BOC Charter is prepared based on the principles of corporate law, the provisions of the Articles of Association, applicable laws and regulations, shareholder directives, and GCG best practices.

The BOC Charter is expected to help the Board of Commissioners understand the regulations related to the work procedures of the Board of Commissioners, eliminate the practice of making decisions based on habits (which are not written) and clarify the level of accountability in decision making. Aspects regulated in the BOC Charter include:

1. Requirements for Members of the Board of Commissioners;
2. Requirements for Independent Commissioners;
3. Appointment of Members of the Board of Commissioners;
4. Dismissal of Members of the Board of Commissioners;
5. Composition of the Board of Commissioners;
6. Concurrent Position as Member of the Board of Commissioners;
7. Transparency, Independence and Ethics of the Board of Commissioners;
8. Duties, Responsibilities and Authorities of the Board of Commissioners;
9. Attendance and working hours of the Board of Commissioners;
10. Rules and Regulations for Board of Commissioners Meetings
