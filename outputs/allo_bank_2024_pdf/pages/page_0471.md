## Significant Changes in the Bank [OJK C.6]

During the reporting year, significant changes took place, including the launch of the Customer Fund Account (RDN) product on July 1, 2024. This product was introduced following PT Kustodian Sentral Efek Indonesia (KSEI)'s approval and the signing of a Cooperation Agreement (PKS) with 23 Customer Fund Account Administrator Banks (RDN Administrator Banks) and Payment Banks, one of which is Allo Bank.

In accordance with the Financial Services Authority's Circular Letter No. 6/SEOJK.04/2019 concerning Guidelines for Opening Customer Securities Accounts and Customer Fund Accounts Electronically Through Securities Companies Acting as Securities Trading Intermediaries, the Customer Fund Account is a fund account registered under the customer's name at an RDN Bank designated by the Depository and Settlement Institution, in compliance with relevant laws and regulations. This account is managed by a Securities Trading Intermediary based on the customer's power of attorney or instructions.

Additionally, the Bank also offers Virtual Account payment services and Virtual Debit Card services, providing customers with a secure, convenient, and reliable financial experience.
