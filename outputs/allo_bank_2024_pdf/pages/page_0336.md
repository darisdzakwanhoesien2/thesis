# Pemberhentian Sementara Anggota Direksi

1. Anggota Direksi dapat diberhentikan untuk sementara oleh Dewan Komisaris dengan menyebutkan alasannya.
2. Pemberhentian sementara wajib diberitahukan secara tertulis kepada anggota Direksi yang bersangkutan.
3. Dewan Komisaris harus menyelenggarakan RUPS untuk mencabut atau menguatkan keputusan pemberhentian sementara tersebut.
4. RUPS harus diselenggarakan dalam jangka waktu paling lambat 90 (sembilan puluh) hari setelah tanggal pemberhentian sementara.

4. The Bank is obliged to hold a GMS to decide on the request for resignation of the member of the Board of Directors no later than 90 (ninety) days after receipt of the letter of resignation.
5. If a member of the Board of Directors resigns resulting in the number of members of the Board of Directors being less than 3 (three) people, then the resignation is valid if it has been determined in the GMS and new members of the Board of Directors have been appointed so that it meets the minimum requirements for the number of members of the Board of Directors.
6. The GMS may appoint another person to fill the position of a Director who is dismissed from his position or to fill a vacancy. The term of office of a person appointed to replace a member of the Board of Directors who has resigned or filled a vacancy is for the remaining term of office of the member of the Board of Directors who has been dismissed or replaced.
7. For the President Director and/or Compliance Director, dismissal, replacement and/or resignation before the end of the term of office must obtain prior approval from the OJK before being decided at the GMS:
a. Submission of the application to the OJK as intended is submitted by the Bank no later than 1 (one) month before the planned implementation of the GMS which contains the agenda for dismissal or replacement of the President Director and/or director in charge of the compliance function, by including: (i) reasons/considerations for dismissal, replacement and/or resignation; (ii) The profile of the replacement candidate who is deemed to meet the requirements for a fit and proper test
b. If OJK assesses that the plan is not feasible then: (i) The plan is not approved by OJK; (ii) Banks are prohibited from including this agenda in the GMS.
8. The Bank is required to submit information to the OJK and disclose information no later than 2 (two) working days after:
a. Acceptance of resignation requests from members of the Board of Directors.
b. A GMS was held.
9. In addition to the Company's Articles of Association, resignation of members of the Board of Directors is also subject to OJK provisions and/or other relevant authority regulations.

# Temporary Suspension of Board of Directors Member

1. A Board of Directors member may be temporarily suspended by the Board of Commissioners by stating the reasons.
2. The temporary suspension must be notified in writing to the respective Board of Directors member.
3. The Board of Commissioners must convene a General Meeting of Shareholders to revoke or confirm the temporary suspension decision.
4. The General Meeting of Shareholders must be held no later than 90 (ninety) days after the date of the temporary suspension.
