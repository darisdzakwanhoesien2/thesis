# Analysis

1. Calculation of Liquidity Coverage Ratio position in the fourth quarter of 2024 Bank Allo Indonesia for the period October 2024 - December 2024 amounted to 172.85% above the provisions required by the Regulator which is 100% (POJK Number 42/POJK.03/2015 concerning Obligations to Fulfill the Liquidity Adequacy Ratio (Liquidity Coverage Ratio) for Commercial Banks). This indicates that the Bank's condition is quite good in managing liquidity needs to support business activities in the last 3 months period.

2. The LCR value for the fourth quarter of 2024 was obtained from the daily average of reports from October 2024 to December 2024. The average value for the fourth quarter decreased by 2.84% compared to the third quarter of 2024 which amounted to 175.69%. The decrease in this ratio was caused by:

a. Total High Quality Liquid Asset (HQLA) in the fourth quarter of 2024 increased by 21.64% compared to the third quarter of 2024. The increase in HQLA was caused by total placements with Bank Indonesia and securities issued by the Central Government and Bank Indonesia in rupiah when compared to the previous quarter.

b. Total average Net Cash Outflow in the fourth quarter of 2024 amounted to Rp2.13 trillion, an increase of 23.64% or equivalent to Rp406.88 billion compared to the third quarter of 2024 which amounted to Rp1.72 trillion. This was due to an increase in funding from corporate customers by 224.36 billion and individual customer deposits and funding from micro and small business customers by 1.90 billion compared to the third quarter of 2024.

3. The composition of Allo Bank's average HQLA during the fourth quarter of 2024 was dominated by Government securities 93.45% (after weighting).

4. The average concentration of funding sources in Q4 2024 position from individual customers amounted to 3.21% and corporate customers amounted to 96.55% (after weighting). This is acknowledged by the Bank, where the Bank continues to strive to increase individual funding sources by conducting various programs to attract retail customers to increase the portion of stable funds.

5. Allo Bank has no derivative exposure as of Q4 2024.

6. In managing liquidity, Allo Bank has identified, measured, monitored and controlled liquidity risk comprehensively, among others by monitoring liquidity risk limits (appetite limit &amp; tolerance limit), calculating cash flow projections, Maturity Profile Behavioural, calculating Liquid Assets against Non-Core Deposits, Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR), liquidity stress testing and liquidity management which are determined in the ALCO committee meeting and implemented by the relevant working units of Funding, Lending, and Treasury. The Bank has also conducted a Table Top Exercise with liquidity scenarios to ensure the Bank's readiness in the event of a liquidity crisis influenced by various internal and external factors.
