# LAYING THE FOUNDATIONS FOR A SUSTAINABLE ECONOMY IN WEST SUMBAWA

The Batu Hijau mine plays a very significant role in West Sumbawa's economy, both as an employer and a consumer of goods and services. In 2023, the mining and quarrying sector, in which AMMAN is by far the biggest player, contributed 82% of the regency's Gross Regional Domestic Product (GRDP).[15]

Our programs are intentionally focused on securing a sustainable future for the communities in the region beyond the lifetime of our mine.
