# Strategi Keberlanjutan [A.1]

Sustainable Strategy [A.1]

Pada RAKB ini, Bank Mandiri memastikan agar kebijakan, proses, serta aktivitas jangka panjang selaras dengan prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan yang meliputi struktur tata kelola, visi/misi/spirit, rencana aksi, serta unit yang terlibat hingga 5 tahun ke depan.

In this Sustainable Finance Action Plan, Bank Mandiri ensures that long-term policies, processes, and activities are in line with the principles of sustainable finance, which include governance structure, vision / mission / spirit, action plans, and units involved for the next 5 years.
