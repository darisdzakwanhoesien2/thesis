Tantangan terberat adalah pandemi COVID-19 yang berdampak sangat besar bagi seluruh sektor kehidupan, termasuk sektor ekonomi. Bahkan, pandemi telah menyebabkan ekonomi Indonesia memasuki zona resesi. Badan Pusat Statistik mencatat, pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2020 adalah -2,07%, turun signifikan dibanding tahun 2019, yang mencatatkan pertumbuhan sebesar 5,02%

The toughest challenge is the COVID-19 pandemic which has a huge impact on all sectors of life, including the economic sector. In fact, the pandemic has caused the Indonesian economy to enter a recession zone. The Central Statistics Agency noted that economic growth was -2.07%, Indonesia in 2020 was down significantly compared to it in 2019 in 2019, which recorded a growth of 5.02%.
