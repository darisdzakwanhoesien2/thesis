# BNI MSMEs' journey

BNI's focus in the MSME journey is the orients toward Go Productive, Go Digital, and Go Global.

# BNI MSME Empowerment Roadmap

Initiation Phase (Go Productive)

## MSME Capability Enhancement

Capital support and mentoring for MSMEs in accordance with the results of mapping needs to improve business capabilities and knowledge of environmentally friendly practices as a driver of MSME graduation.

Acceleration Phase (Go Digital)

## Digitalization of MSME Business Processes

Introduction of technology in MSME business processes to create digitalized MSMEs.

## Online Market Access

Directing the marketing of export oriented MSME products through access to the Xpora.bni.co.id Portal.

Scale-Up Phase (Go Global)

## Export Market Access

Supporting potential export MSMEs to reach the global market, both in terms of product quality, legality/certification, product services, and foreign transaction services.
