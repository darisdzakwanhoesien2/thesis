# Sustainability Portfolio

One of the targets of the sustainable finance implementation is increasing the loan disbursements to the Criteria for Sustainable Business Activities (KKUB), which include disbursing them to eco-friendly sector and MSME segment. Until the end of 2023, BNI recorded total credit of Rp695,085 billion.
