## Renewable Energy

## Mini-Hydro Power Plants (MHPP)

Mini-Hydro Power Plants (MHPP) are power plants that utilize the potential energy from the watershed area of Cikaengan river, Pandeuy Village, Pandeuy District, Garut Regency. Cikaengan MHPP has a capacity of 3 x 1.7 MW (5.1 MW) and is able to electrify households in Taraju, Tasikmalaya and Cibalong, Garut and increase the percentage of new renewable energy (NRE) mix in the area.
