Annual Review

PT Bank Negara Indonesia (Persero) Tbk.

SUSTAINALYTICS

# Conclusion

Based on the limited assurance procedures conducted, $^{4}$  apart from the exception noted in Table 2 below, nothing has come to Sustainalytics' attention that causes us to believe that, in all material respects, the reviewed projects do not conform with the use of proceeds criteria and reporting commitments in the Framework. BNI has disclosed to Sustainalytics that as of December 2022, of the total IDR 5 trillion raised from 2022 Green Bonds,  $77.06\%$  of the proceeds were allocated to refinancing eligible projects as aligned with the Framework while the remaining  $22.94\%$  of the proceeds have been invested in activities beyond the scope of the Framework.
