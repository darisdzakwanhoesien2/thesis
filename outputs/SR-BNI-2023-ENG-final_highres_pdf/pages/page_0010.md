In 2023, BNI has implemented the framework, target, and roadmap as reference for the Bank in implementing and increasing environmental, social, and governance initiatives in the Bank's operations. BNI formulated the target and roadmap for the short-term (2023), medium-term (2024-2026), and long-term (&gt;2027).

# ESG Framework &amp; Roadmap

## Environment

- Conducting climate-related financial risk management by referring to international standards
- Achieving NZE (Net Zero Emission) in Operations by 2028
- Strengthening governance in ESG loans
- Improving employee awareness and capabilities
- Sharpening ESG disclosure

![img-52.jpeg](img-52.jpeg)

## Social

- Increasing employee engagement
- Improving employee capabilities
- Strengthening data and customer financial protection

## Governance

- Increasing diversity at the Board of Directors level
- Strengthening the engagement of the Board of Directors in ESG

## Short-Term Focus (2024)

- Strengthening infrastructure
- Establishing NZE and Carbon Budget

## Mid-Term Focus (2025-2026):

- Strengthening the Risk Acceptance Criteria (RAC) of debtors
- Strengthening the infrastructure of climate change risk management
- Strengthening Board engagement in ESG
- Optimizing data loss prevention

## Long-Term Focus (2027):

- Implementation of climate change risk management in the Bank's operations
