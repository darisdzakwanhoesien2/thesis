# ESG Risk Management in Financing [FS1, FS2, FS3][FN-CB-410a.2]

Risk management in banking activities is carried out prudently and by taking ESG aspects into consideration. The Risk Management Director and Credit Committee perform an assessment to the risks in financing activities. The Credit Committee has officials authorized to decide on credits in stages. For any granted financing, monitoring will follow and an approval will be requested to the credit committee during a periodic review conducted at least once a year.

Prior to granting financing to the prospective debtors, BNI exercises a series of proper tests for loan which involves cross functions. The Internal Audit (IAD) assesses the adequacy of risk management and internal control in respected unit, including in the loan process. Upon reviewing the loan proposal, we identify and monitor the ESG risks. The Bank also has a credit procedure standard that requires AMDAL for mandatory business plans/activities, or Environment Management Efforts (UKL) - Environment Monitoring Efforts (UPL) as per prevailing laws and regulations.

# Risk Unit (CER, CMR, and ERM)

The Risk Unit, by taking ESG factors into consideration, conducts the identification, measurement, mitigation, monitoring, and reporting function of the risks inherent in business activities by referring to the Industry Risk Rating (IRR), Industry Risk Appetite (IRA), and Risk Acceptance Criteria (RAC) established by the ERM Division. The established stipulations have also taken ESG factors into account.

# Credit Committee

Conducts analyses against debtors/prospective debtors, covering all risks and risk mitigation by considering the outcomes of the compliance test, then deciding whether to approve and/or refuse credit.
