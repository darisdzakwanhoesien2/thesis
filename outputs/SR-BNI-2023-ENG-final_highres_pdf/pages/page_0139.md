# Sustainable Natural Resources and Land Use

The Natural Resources and Sustainable Land Use categories are implemented to the forest concession projects that have reforestation with FSC-certified sustainable management plans, which located in Nabire, Central Papua Province and Kaimana, West Papua Province. The project protects and maintains those tree species that able to capture and store carbon dioxide.
