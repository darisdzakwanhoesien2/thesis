## A. Green Bond Framework

Prior to the Green Bond Issue in March 2023, BNI has developed a Green Bond Framework and engaged Sustainalytics to issue a Second-Party Opinion (SPO). BNI has aligned the Green Bond Framework with the Provisions of the Financial Services Authority Regulation (Peraturan OJK, "POJK") No. 60/POJK.04/2017 concerning Issuance and Requirements of Green Debt Securities, Green Bond Principles 2021 (GPB) issued by the International Capital Market Association (ICMA) and the ASEAN Green Bond Standards 2018. The summary of BNI's Green Bond Framework is presented below:
