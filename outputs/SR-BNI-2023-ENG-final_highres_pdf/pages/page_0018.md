# Support for Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Support and contribution towards the achievement of SDGs are aligned with the management of economic and ESG aspects. BNI focuses on supporting SDGs within the context of banking industry business that sustainably contributes positive impacts to the community and environment, presently and future wise.
