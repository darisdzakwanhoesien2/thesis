## Borneo Queen

Borneo Queen is one of the partners assisted by Rumah BUMN Katingan which processes the surrounding natural resources into commodities with economic value and high selling value through jewelry making and seaweed utilization. Borneo Queen is real evidence of a Rumah BUMN MSME that has succeeded in marketing their products by going global through Xpora BNI.

## Cataract Surgery in Eastern Indonesia

BNI supports the cataract prevention program in collaboration with Yayasan Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih (YPP) SCTV Indosiar to perform cataract surgery in four locations in Eastern Indonesia (Sumbawa, Jayapura, Buru Island, and North Toraja) with a target of 450 eyes. During 2023, cataract surgeries have been performed on 350 eyes or 78% of the target.
