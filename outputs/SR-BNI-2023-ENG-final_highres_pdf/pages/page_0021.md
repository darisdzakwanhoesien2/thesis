PT Bank Negara Indonesia (Persero) Tbk

# Material Topics Managed by BNI

Material topics are arranged based on issues and information that were significant in the reporting year. There are changes to material topics from the previous year. Material topics are adjusted to activities and issues that have a significant impact occurring in 2023. We involve management and independent observers in determining topics as priorities. The principles of stakeholder management, sustainability context, materiality, and completeness were taken into account upon determining the material topics. Factors affecting the Bank and stakeholders' decision making have been identified through the material topics mapping process.

# Process of Determining Material Topics [1-1]

# Performing identification and assessment on business impacts by reviewing the sustainable finance principles by involving stakeholders.

# 03

# Understanding BNI's business process

Understanding the process of BNI's business activities to identify the impacts of business activities. This involves discussions with accompanying ESG consultants and independent observers.

# 02

# Identification

Identifying positive and negative impacts along with actual and potential impacts from the Bank's business process by considering references of financial sector standards, such as GRI, SASB, and SUSBA.

# 05

# Assessment

Assessing the importance level of each of the Bank's activities, mainly from the financing and services aspects. In conducting the assessment, BNI management has considered risks, opportunities and challenges related to economic, social and environmental aspects.

# 04

# Priority Topic

Determining the priority based on the most significant impacts to be conveyed in the sustainability report. This activity involves the ERM Division and considers input from investors.
