PT Bank Negara Indonesia (Persero) Tbk

# Sustainability Governance Structure [2-9]

In implementing sustainability governance, BNI is supported by its leaders, which are the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, being directly engaged in the planning and monitoring of Sustainable Finance. The Bank has an ESG Sub-Committee under the Risk Management &amp; Anti-Fraud Committee. The personnel of the ESG Sub-Committee is comprised of cross-function members in accordance with Directors' Decree No. KP/745/DIR/R dated 26 October 2023.
