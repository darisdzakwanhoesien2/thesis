Wastewater generation | • Melakukan pengelolaan air limbah mengacu pada PP No. 82 tahun 2001 tentang Pengelolaan Kualitas Air dan Pengendalian Pencemaran Air.
Carrying out waste water management referring to PP. 82 Year 2001 concerning Water Quality Management and Water Pollution Control.
• Pemantauan kualitas air limbah domestik mengacu pada Peraturan Menteri Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan No. 68 tahun 2016 tentang Baku Mutu Air Limbah Domestik.
Monitoring the quality of domestic wastewater refers to the Minister of Environment and Forestry Regulation No. 68 Year 2016 concerning Domestic Wastewater Quality Standards.

• Melakukan inspeksi, pengelolaan serta pemantauan terhadap kondisi IPAL (pH dan debit) dan sumur pantau.
Inspect, manage and monitor the conditions of WWTP (pH and discharge) and monitor wells.

Cooperating with licensed domestic liquid waste managers.

Perform reporting and coordination regarding the environmental conditions of the Company to the Ministry of Environment and Forestry.
• Program pengungarangan beban pencemar air di kegiatan operasi, perkantoran dan kegiatan pendukung lainnya.
Water pollutant load removal program in operational activities, offices and other supporting activities.  |

Brine/condensate
water generation | • Air terproduksi dari sumur panas bumi berupa air Brine serta air kondensat dialirkan ke pond atau kolam pendingin untuk selanjutnya diinjeksikan kembali ke dalam sumur injeksi panas bumi. Hal ini diperlukan juga dalam rangka menjaga tekanan reservoir panas bumi.
Produced water from geothermal wells in the form of Brine water and condensate water is flowed to the pond or cooling pond for subsequent re-injection into the geothermal injection well. This is also necessary in order to maintain the pressure of the geothermal reservoir.
• Merawat dan memelihara ground pit yang ada untuk dijadikan kolam penyangga apabila terjadi masalah pada sumur injeksi.
Maintain and preserve the existing ground pit to be used as a buffer pond in the event of a problem with the injection well.  |
