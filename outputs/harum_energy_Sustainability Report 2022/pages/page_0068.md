# Penyuluhan dan Pemberian Bantuan Stunting dan Gizi Buruk

# Counseling and Provision of Stunting and Malnutrition Assistance

Pada Pilar Kesehatan, melalui Entitas Anak, yaitu POS, telah dijalankan program yang turut mendukung program pemerintah dalam rangka mencegah dan menurunkan angka stunting di setiap daerah. Peran serta Perseroan dalam program ini adalah memberikan pemahaman melalui penyuluhan kepada orang tua dalam pencegahan dan penanganan stunting pada balita dan turut membantu kebutuhan pokok para orang tua dari anak stunting. Selain itu, POS juga memberikan fasilitas bangunan kamar mandi di Posyandu Desa Tewil (Desa Dampingan) agar pelayanan kesehatan Posyandu dapat lebih baik dan bersih.

In the Health Pillar, through our Subsidiary, namely POS, a program has been implemented that supports government programs in order to prevent and reduce stunting rates in each region. The Company's participation in this program is to provide understanding through counseling to parents on how to prevent and treat stunting in toddlers and to help support the basic needs of parents of stunted children. In addition, POS also provided bathroom facilities at Posyandu Tewil Village (Assisted Village) so that Posyandu health services can be better and cleaner.

# Program Renovasi dan Pembangunan Posyandu

# Posyandu Renovation and Development Program

Melalui Entitas Anak, yaitu MSJ, kami melakukan renovasi Posyandu di Desa Gunung Menangis. Renovasi ini terdiri dari pembuatan pondasi halaman Posyandu dan pengecatan Posyandu. Program ini diharapkan dapat meningkatkan pelayanan kesehatan yang diberikan dan masyarakat yang berobat merasa semakin nyaman. Selain itu, MSJ juga membangun Posyandu An Nur di RT 21 Desa Sebuntal. Pembangunan Posyandu ini diharapkan dapat membantu masyarakat sekitar Desa Sebuntal sehingga semakin mudah mendapatkan akses kesehatan maupun penyuluhan kesehatan dan pemerataan pelayanan kesehatan di desa-desa binaan.

Through our Subsidiary, namely MSJ, we renovated Posyandu in Gunung Menangis Village. This renovation consisted of constructing Posyandu yard foundation and painting Posyandu. This program is expected to improve the health services provided and people who seek treatment feel more comfortable. MSJ also built An Nur Posyandu at RT 21 Sebuntal Village. The development of this Posyandu is expected to help the community around Sebuntal Village for easier access to health, health counseling, and equal distribution of health services in the assisted villages.
