## Program Pelestarian Lingkungan

Kegiatan operasional pertambangan berdampak negatif bagi kelestarian lingkungan, termasuk flora dan fauna yang terdapat di dalamnya. Oleh karena itu, pelestarian lingkungan senantiasa dilakukan sebagai bentuk kepedulian kami terhadap keanekaragaman hayati sekaligus sebagai pemenuhan kewajiban untuk melakukan rehabilitas dan reklamasi lahan pascatambang.

Saat ini kegiatan penambangan batubara masih aktif sesuai dengan izin usaha yang masih berlaku sehingga belum ada lahan yang memasuki status pascatambang. Meskipun demikian, kami tetap melaksanakan program rehabilitasi dan reklamasi lahan di wilayah operasional tambang. [POJK F.9] [GRI 3-3, GRI 304-2]

## Environmental Preservation Program

Mining operational activities have a negative impact on environmental preservation, including the flora and fauna contained therein. Therefore, environmental preservation is always carried out as a form of our concern for biodiversity as well as fulfilling the obligation to carry out rehabilitation and reclamation of post-mining land.

Currently, coal mining activities are still active in accordance with valid business permits, and therefore, no land has entered post-mining status. Nonetheless, we continue to carry out land rehabilitation and reclamation programs in the mining operational area. [POJK F.9] [GRI 3-3, GRI 304-2]
