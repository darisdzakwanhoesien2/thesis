## Operasional Areas

Pada tahun 2022, wilayah operasional pertambangan batubara Perseroan terdapat di Kabupaten Kutai Kartanegara dan Kota Samarinda, Provinsi Kalimantan Timur, serta Kabupaten Barito Utara, Provinsi Kalimantan Tengah. Selain itu, terdapat pertambangan bijih nikel yang berlokasi di Halmahera Timur. Perseroan juga memiliki usaha pelayaran yang terletak di Kalimantan Timur.

In 2022, the Company's coal mining operational areas were in Kutai Kartanegara Regency and Samarinda City, East Kalimantan Province, and North Barito Regency, Central Kalimantan Province. Furthermore, there is a nickel ore mine located in East Halmahera. The Company also has a shipping business located in East Kalimantan.
