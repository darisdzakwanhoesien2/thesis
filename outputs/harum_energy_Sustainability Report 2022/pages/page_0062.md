# Keberlanjutan Pertumbuhan Masyarakat Lokal [GRI 413-1]

Kehadiran Perseroan dan Entitas Anak diantara masyarakat lokal diharapkan dapat berkontribusi dalam peningkatan kesejahteraan dan kemandirian masyarakat. Oleh karena itu, kami melaksanakan berbagai kegiatan peningkatan kualitas sosial-kemasyarakatan melalui program Pengembangan dan Pemberdayaan Masyarakat (PPM). Pelaksanaan program PPM juga ditujukan untuk meminimalkan dampak negatif yang ditimbulkan dari aktivitas operasi Perseroan dan Entitas Anak,seperti gangguan kesehatan, perubahan fungsi lahan, ataupun pencemaran lingkungan. Perumusan dan pelaksanaan program PPM mengacu kepada Keputusan Menteri ESDM No. 1824 K/30/MEM/2018 tentang Pedoman Pelaksanaan Pengembangan dan Pemberdayaan Masyarakat. [POJK F.23] [GRI 3-3]

# Sustainability of Local Community Growth [GRI 413-1]

The presence of the Company and its Subsidiaries among the local community is expected to contribute to community's welfare and independence improvements. Therefore, we conduct various social-community quality improvement activities through the Community Development and Empowerment (CDE) programs. The CDE programs aim at minimizing the negative impacts arising from the Company's and Subsidiaries' operating activities, such as health problems, changes in land use, or environmental pollution. The formulation and implementation of CDE programs refer to the Decree of Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources No. 1824 K/30/MEM/2018 on Guidelines for Community Development and Empowerment. [POJK F.23] [GRI 3-3]
