Selain itu, Perseroan melalui Entitas Anak, yaitu MSJ, telah melakukan pemantauan lingkungan terhadap flora (revegetasi) dan fauna (satwa liar). Pemantauan tersebut dilakukan pada lokasi areal konservasi (hutan alam), blok E (awal reklamasi dilakukan pada tahun 2017), blok D (awal reklamasi dilakukan pada tahun 2017), dan blok K (awal reklamasi dilakukan pada tahun 2010). Indikator pemantauan diambil dari pengukuran pertumbuhan tanaman dan pemeliharaan tanaman. Pada tahun 2022, kami telah melakukan penanaman 38.775 pohon di lahan seluas 93,1 ha. [POJK F.10] [GRI 304-1]

Kami telah mengidentifikasi sebanyak 66 jenis vegetasi, di mana di antaranya terdapat 3 jenis tanaman rehabilitasi; 3 jenis tanaman sisipan, yakni balangerang (Shorea balangeran), karet (Hevea brasiliensis), dan pulai (Alstonia Scholaris); serta 60 jenis vegetasi yang tumbuh alami di kawasan rehabilitasi. Selain itu, fauna yang terdapat pada lokasi pemantauan, yakni:

1. Orangutan (Pongo pygmaeus);
2. Musang (Viverra tangalunga);
3. Kijang (Muntiacus muntjac);
4. Babi berjenggot (Sus barbatus);
5. Bajing (Callosciurus sp);
6. Tupai (Tupaia sp);
7. Tupai ekor kecil (Dendrogale melanura);
8. Tupai tercat (Tupaia picta); dan
9. Kucing hutan (Prionailurus bengalensis). [POJK F.10] [GRI 304-1]

Furthermore, the Company through its Subsidiary, MSJ, has carried out environmental monitoring of flora (revegetation) and fauna (wildlife). The location for monitoring flora and fauna is in conservation areas (natural forest), block E (initial reclamation was in 2017), block D (initial reclamation was in 2017), and block K (initial reclamation was in 2010). Indicators of monitoring are in 2022, we planted 38,775 trees in an area of 93.1 ha. [POJK F.10] [GRI 304-1]

We have identified 66 types of vegetation, among others, 3 types of rehabilitation plants, 3 types of insert plants, namely balangerang (Shorea balangeran), rubber (Hevea brasiliensis), and pulai (Alstonia Scholaris), and 60 types of vegetation that grow naturally in the rehabilitation area. Furthermore, the fauna at the monitoring locations are:

1. Orangutan (Pongo pygmaeus);
2. Weasel (Viverra tangalunga);
3. Deer (Muntiacus muntjac);
4. Bearded pig (Sus barbatus);
5. Squirrel (Callosciurus sp);
6. Treeshrew (Tupaia sp);
7. Little tailed treeshrew (Dendrogale melanura);
8. Ground treeshrew (Tupaia picta); and
9. Jungle cat (Prionailurus bengalensis). [POJK F.10] [GRI 304-1]
