Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan Sustainability Governance

# Membangun Budaya Keberlanjutan [POJK F.1] [GRI 2-24]
## Building Sustainability Culture

Kesadaran insan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak terhadap aspek keberlanjutan merupakan hal yang penting untuk dimiliki dan diimplementasikan dalam pengambilan keputusan terkait pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya. Kami berupaya untuk membangun dasar-dasar keberlanjutan melalui penegakkan Kode Etik serta implementasi GCG secara berkelanjutan. Hal ini disertai dengan sarana pengaduan terhadap pelanggaran yang diatur dalam kebijakan internal.

## Kode Etik [GRI 2-23, GRI 2-27]

Kami telah menetapkan Kode Etik sebagai standar perilaku minimum bagi seluruh insan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak, termasuk Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, karyawan, serta individu lain yang terlibat dalam bisnis perusahaan. Selain itu, kami juga mewajibkan kepatuhan terhadap setiap hukum dan peraturan yang berlaku. Hal ini dilakukan guna membangun lingkungan kerja yang beretika dan bertanggung jawab. Adapun Kode Etik Perseroan telah disahkan oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi pada tanggal 30 November 2015.

Penegakkan Kode Etik dilakukan untuk mencegah terjadinya konflik kepentingan, pelanggaran norma, hukum, serta permasalahan sosial lain yang menimbulkan kerugian bagi pemangku kepentingan ataupun Perseroan dan Entitas Anak. Oleh karena itu, kami senatiasa mensosialisasikan Kode Etik secara berkala sehingga diharapkan seluruh pihak yang bertanggung jawab dapat memahami dan mematuhi secara menyeluruh. Adapun sosialisasi dilakukan melalui media internal maupun melalui situs web Perseroan yang mudah diakses oleh pihak internal maupun eksternal perusahaan.

It is important for the Company's and Subsidiary's personnel to have awareness regarding the sustainability aspect and implement it in making decisions related to the implementation of their duties and responsibilities. We strive to build the foundations of sustainability by upholding the Code of Conduct and implementing GCG in a sustainable manner. This is accompanied by complaint channel against violation as stipulated in the internal policy.

## Code of Conduct [GRI 2-23, GRI 2-27]

We have established a Code of Conduct as a minimum standard of behavior for all employees of the Company and Subsidiaries, including the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, employees, and other individuals involved in the Company's business. We also require compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. This is done in order to build an ethical and responsible work environment. The Company's Code of Conduct was ratified by the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors on November 30, 2015.

The Code of Conduct is enforced to prevent conflict of interest, violation of norms, law, and other social problems that may cause harm to stakeholders or the Company and its Subsidiaries. Therefore, we regularly disseminate the Code of Conduct and expect that all responsible parties can fully understand and comply. The dissemination is carried out through internal media and the Company's website, which is easily accessed by the Company's internal and external parties.
