## Program Beasiswa Pendidikan untuk Siswa SD dan SMP
## Education Scholarship Program for Elementary and Junior High School Students

Pada Pilar Pendidikan, melalui Entitas Anak, telah diselenggarakan program beasiswa untuk siswah tingkat SD dan SMP di sekitar wilayah operasional. Pada tahun 2022, program dilaksanakan di SD dan SMP yang tersebar di beberapa desa, seperti Desa Prangat Baru, Desa Makarti, dan Desa Gunung Menangis. Program ini telah memberikan manfaat kepada 90 siswah. Melalui program ini, Perseroan dapat memberikan nilai kebermanfaatan terhadap peningkatan kualitas pendidikan masyarakat.

In the Education Pillar, through Subsidiary, a scholarship program has been organized for elementary and junior high school students around the operational area. In 2022, the program was applied to elementary and junior high schools in several villages, such as Prangat Baru Village, Makarti Village, and Gunung Menangis Village. This program has benefited 90 students. Through this program, the Company can provide beneficial value to improve the quality of public education.
