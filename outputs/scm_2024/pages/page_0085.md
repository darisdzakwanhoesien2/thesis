# Panduan Informasi atas Produk dan Layanan Information Guideline for Products and Services

Komitmen Perseroan lainnya terkait perlindungan hak-hak pemirsa ditujukan bagi segmen anak-anak dan remaja dari dampak negatif yang mungkin muncul dari pendekatan yang diambil dalam menayangkan suatu konten atau program. Perseroan dalam hal ini memberikan panduan informasi bagi pemirsa dan melakukan sejumlah inisiatif dalam bentuk:

- Mengirimkan semua film dan serial kami ke Lembaga Sensor Film Indonesia (LSF) untuk kemudian dievaluasi sebelum ditayangkan. Hal ini untuk mengurangi risiko khususnya penonton di bawah umur.
- Menempatkan kategori tayangan dewasa di siaran televisi pada saat anak memasuki jam istirahat atau tidur mereka.
- Mendorong dan mengingatkan orang tua untuk memandu anak-anak mereka dalam memilih konten yang sesuai dengan usia.

This also helps the Company in fulfilling compliance aspects, especially the legality of licensing for the production process of programs and content being aired, including programs and content produced by other entities that will be aired on the Company's media platforms.

The following criteria must be met in the broadcasting of programs and content on all media platforms owned by the SCM Group as stated in Law No. 32 of 2002 concerning Broadcasting:

1. Not slanderous, provocative, misleading and/or false;
2. Does not emphasize the elements of violence, pornography/pornoaction, gambling, narcotics and illegal drugs abuse including cigarettes;
3. Does not conflict with ethnicity, religion, race, and intergroup; and
4. Does not ridicule, humiliate, harass, and/or ignore religious values, Indonesian human dignity, or damage international relations.

The Company's commitment to comply with the applicable regulations in the broadcasting industry has helped the Company avoid the potential for significant sanctions due to alleged violations of intellectual property rights, in terms of both program content and stakeholder privacy violations.

The Company is always open to all forms of criticism and suggestions from the public and viewers. Criticism and suggestions can be submitted via whistleblowing@scm.co.id. Until the end of 2024, the Company did not receive any complaints from the public regarding the negative impact of programs and content aired by SCM Group that resulted in the cancellation of programs or the withdrawal of content. [SV-ME-270a.1]

The Company's other commitments related to the protection of viewers' rights are aimed at protecting children and teenagers from the negative impacts that may arise from its approach to broadcasting content or programs. In this regard, the Company provides information guidelines for viewers and carries out the following initiatives:

- Sending all our films and series to the Indonesian Film Censorship Institute (LSF) to be evaluated before airing. This is to minimize the risk, especially for underage audiences.
- Airing adult-rated programs on television during children's nap or bedtime.
- Encouraging and reminding parents to guide their children in choosing appropriate content.
