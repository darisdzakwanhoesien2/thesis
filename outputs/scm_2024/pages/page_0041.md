# Total Kehadiran Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris ke Rapat Dewan
## Total Attendance of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners in the Board Meetings

[GRI 2-16]

Sebagaimana diatur dalam ketentuan Board Manual Direksi maupun Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan berkewajiban menghadiri rapat internal masing-masing dewan yang dilakukan secara berkala. Selain itu, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris juga wajib menghadiri rapat gabungan yang mencerminkan keterlibatan anggota Direksi atau Komisaris secara efektif untuk menghasilkan keputusan strategis yang penting sekaligus untuk menjalankan pengawasan atas pengelolaan perusahaan. Berikut informasi tingkat kehadiran Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris di rapat dewan pada tahun 2024:

As regulated in the Board Manual, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of the Company are required to attend regular internal meetings of respective Boards. Furthermore, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners are also required to attend joint-meetings to demonstrate the effective involvement of members of the Board of Directors or Board of Commissioners in the formulation of key strategic decisions as well as to exercise supervision over the management of the Company.
