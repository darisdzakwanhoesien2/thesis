# Lini Usaha Perseroan
## The Company’s Line of Business
[OJK C.4] [GRI 2-2]

Sebagaimana termaktub dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan Pasal 3, maka maksud dan tujuan pendirian Perseroan adalah untuk menjalankan usaha di bidang khususnya informasi dan aktivitas profesional, perdagangan, serta multimedia. Dalam rangka mewujudkan maksud dan tujuan tersebut, Perseroan menjalankan 4 (empat) bidang usaha utama, yaitu:

1. Televisi dan platform multimedia lainnya
2. Digital
3. Pembuatan konten dan pendukung produksi
4. Jasa pemasaran dan pendukung

As stipulated in the Article 3 of the Company’s Articles of Association, the purpose and objective of the Company’s establishment is to carry out business, especially in information and professional, trade, as well as multimedia activities. In order to realize the purpose and objective, the Company carries out 4 (four) main businesses, including:

1. Television and other multimedia platform
2. Digital
3. Content creation and production support
4. Marketing services and enabler
