# Penentuan Remunerasi
## Determination of Remuneration
[GRI 2-19] [GRI 2-20] [GRI 2-21]

Perseroan menetapkan struktur remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dengan mempertimbangkan berbagai faktor. Berdasarkan rekomendasi dari Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, Dewan Komisaris Perseroan diberikan wewenang untuk merumuskan besaran remunerasi bagi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dengan mempertimbangkan berbagai faktor, termasuk skala usaha dalam industri sejenis, kompleksitas operasional bisnis, tingkat inflasi, kondisi keuangan Perseroan, pencapaian kinerja individu, realisasi tujuan dan kinerja Perseroan, serta faktor relevan lainnya yang sesuai dengan ketentuan Peraturan Perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Usulan tersebut kemudian disampaikan oleh Dewan Komisaris kepada RUPS untuk dimintakan persetujuannya.

Bagi para organ penanggung jawab pelaksanaan kebijakan tata kelola, Perseroan memberikan remunerasi yang terdiri dari komponen gaji atau honorarium, tunjangan, serta manfaat lain yang relevan untuk mendukung pelaksanaan tugas mereka secara optimal. Dalam menentukan remunerasi bagi organ tata kelola keberlanjutan, Perseroan dalam hal ini berpegang pada praktik terbaik di pasar dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Mekanisme dalam menetapkan Remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi telah diatur dalam ketentuan POJK 34/2014, yang meliputi proses berikut ini:

1. Penyusunan struktur remunerasi untuk anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan;
2. Penetapan kebijakan remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan; dan
3. Penetapan besaran remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan.

## Rasio Kompensasi Tahunan
### Annual Compensation Ratio

Perseroan memperhitungkan rasio kompensasi tahunan yang diterima oleh organ tertinggi dalam tata kelola keberlanjutan terhadap kompensasi tahunan yang diterima karyawan untuk mengukur disparitas dalam pembayaran remunerasi dan menjadi acuan dalam penyusunan sistem remunerasi yang adil dan transparan. Berikut rasio kompensasi tahunan yang berlaku di Perseroan:

The Company determines the remuneration structure of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors by considering various factors. Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Company's Board of Commissioners is authorized to formulate the amount of remuneration for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners by considering various factors, including the scale of business in similar industries, the complexity of business operations, the level of inflation, the Company's financial condition, the achievement of individual performance, the realization of the Company's goals and performance, as well as other relevant factors in accordance with the provisions of the applicable laws and regulations. The proposal is then submitted by the Board of Commissioners to the GMS for approval.

For the organs responsible for implementing governance policies, the Company provides remuneration in form of salary or honorarium, allowances, as well as other relevant benefits to support the implementation of their duties. In determining remuneration for sustainability governance organs, the Company adheres to best market practices and applicable laws and regulations. The mechanism for determining remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is regulated in POJK 34/2014, which includes the following processes:

1. Preparation of remuneration structure for members of the Company's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors;
2. Determination of remuneration policy for members of the Company's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors; and
3. Determination of the amount of remuneration for the Company's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.

The Company calculates the ratio of annual compensation for the highest organ in sustainability governance against the annual compensation received by employees to measure disparities in remuneration payments and as a reference in the formulation of a fair and transparent remuneration system.
