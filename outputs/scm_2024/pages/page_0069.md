## Ketenagakerjaan
### Employment
[GRI 404-3]

Pengelolaan SDM merupakan bagian penting dalam kebijakan keberlanjutan Perseroan. Perseroan memberikan perhatian yang besar terhadap upaya pengembangan SDM yang ada agar terjamin ketersediaan tenaga kerja yang profesional dan memiliki kualifikasi yang dibutuhkan oleh Perseroan untuk mendorong pertumbuhan bisnis dan organisasi secara berkelanjutan.

Untuk mencapai sasaran tersebut, Perseroan telah menunjuk Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia yang bertanggung jawab terhadap pengelolaan dan pengembangan kompetensi SDM. Bertanggung jawab langsung kepada Direksi, Divisi SDM merancang kebijakan pengelolaan dan pengembangan SDM dengan berdasarkan pada Key Performance Indicators (KPI) yang harus dipenuhi oleh SDM sebagai indikator penilaian kinerjanya serta ketentuan ketenagakerjaan yang berlaku lainnya. [GRI 3-3]

## Kesetaraan dalam Bekerja
### Equality at Work
[OJK F.18] [GRI 401-1] [SV-ME-260A.1]

Sebagai bagian dari kebijakan ketenagakerjaan, Perseroan membuat perencanaan akan kebutuhan tenaga kerja sesuai dengan kebutuhan pengembangan bisnisnya. Kebutuhan tenaga kerja dipenuhi secara internal maupun dari eksternal, yaitu antara lain dengan melakukan rekrutmen terhadap calon karyawan yang mampu memenuhi kualifikasi yang ditetapkan untuk posisi tertentu. Perseroan memastikan proses rekrutmen dilakukan dengan menjunjung tinggi aspek kesetaraan, keberagaman, transparansi dan non diskriminasi, yaitu tanpa membedakan ras, suku, agama, golongan, gender, warna kulit, afiliasi politik maupun kondisi fisik.

Selain rekrutmen, Perseroan juga menerapkan kebijakan non diskriminatif di internal organisasinya dengan memberikan kesempatan yang adil dan setara kepada karyawan yang ingin mengembangkan kariernya secara struktural. Penerapan kebijakan kesetaraan dalam kesempatan kerja ini mencerminkan komitmen Perseroan untuk meningkatkan kontribusinya secara tidak langsung terhadap peningkatan kesejahteraan masyarakat di mana Perseroan dan entitas anaknya beroperasi.

Pada tahun 2024, untuk memenuhi kebutuhan SDM di Divisi SCM Media dan SCM Digital &amp; Other Business, Perseroan telah merekrut sebanyak 503 karyawan baru, yang sebagian besar terdiri dari karyawan laki-laki (58%).

Not only to employees, the Company also ensures that there is no discriminatory treatment against the community around its business locations and viewers who are the end users of the content or program services broadcast by the Company through various media platforms.

HR management is an integral part of the Company's sustainability policy. The Company pays great attention to the development of existing HR to ensure the availability of a professional and qualified workforce needed by the Company to drive sustainable business and organizational growth.

To achieve this goal, the Company has appointed a Human Resources Division that is responsible for the management and development of HR competencies. Directly responsible to the Board of Directors, the HR Division designs HR management and development policies based on key performance indicators (KPIs) that must be met by employees as indicators of their performance assessment as well as other applicable employment regulations. [GRI 3-3]

As part of its employment policy, the Company develops a plan for its workforce needs in accordance with the needs of its business development. Labor needs are met internally and externally, including by recruiting prospective employees who are able to meet the qualifications set for a particular position. The Company ensures that the recruitment process is carried out with the utmost regard for equality, diversity, transparency and non-discrimination, regardless of race, ethnicity, religion, class, gender, skin color, political affiliation or physical condition.

In addition to recruitment, the Company also implements a non-discriminatory policy within its organization by providing fair and equal opportunities to employees who want to develop their careers structurally. The implementation of this equal employment opportunity policy reflects the Company's commitment to increase its indirect contribution to improving the welfare of the communities in which the Company and its subsidiaries operate.

In 2024, the Company recruited 503 new employees to meet the human resources needs in SCM Media and SCM Digital &amp; Other Business divisions, most of whom were male (58%).
