# 4. Pelaksanaan Simulasi Keadaan Darurat

Pada periode 2023 PT CPM telah melakukan dua (2) kali kegiatan Emergency Drill:

a. Simulasi tanggap darurat penyelamatan korban gempa bumi diarea pabrik pengolahan. Dilaksanakan pada 16 Mei 2023.
b. Simulasi tanggap darurat peyelamatan korban longsor diarea river reef. Dilaksanakan pada 11 Desember 2023.

# 4. Conducting Emergency Preparedness Drills

In the 2023 period PT CPM has conducted two (2) Emergency Drill activities:

a. Simulation of emergency response to rescue earthquake victims in the processing factory area. Held on May 16 2023.
b. Simulation of emergency response to rescue landslide victims in the river reef area. Held on December 11, 2023.
