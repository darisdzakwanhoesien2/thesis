Kami menentukan proses penetapan isi laporan dengan menerapkan empat tahapan, sebagai berikut:

## a. Identifikasi

Identifikasi topik-topik material bagi Perusahaan dilakukan melalui pengumpulan data dan informasi. Pengumpulan data kemudian diikuti oleh analisis dampak dan cakupan dampak oleh masing-masing topik. Meskipun kami belum melakukan uji tuntas di tahun 2023 untuk melihat dampak operasional Perusahaan terhadap aspek ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial, termasuk dampak terhadap HAM namun proses identifikasi dampak tetap melihat aspek kerentanan pemangku kepentingan dan tetap menggunakan pendekatan hak asasi manusia. Hal ini Kami lakukan dengan dua mekanisme. Pertama, Kami melakukan survey kepada pemangku kepentingan tentang topik material Perusahaan dan meminta responden untuk menilai tingkat kepentingan dari topik material dengan skala sangat penting, penting, dan tidak penting. Kedua, Kami melakukan identifikasi isu-isu yang muncul pada media serta keluhan yang disampaikan melalui whistleblowing system (WBS). Data-data ini akan memberikan gambaran tentang dampak aktual dan potensial, negatif dan positif Perusahaan terhadap ekonomi, lingkungan, dan masyarakat, termasuk dampak terhadap HAM.

## b. Prioritas

Setelah identifikasi dampak selesai dilakukan, Kami kemudian mengelompokan dan memilih topik-topik yang teridentifikasi berdasarkan skala prioritas dan signifikansi dampak negatif dan positif. in the entire process of managing sustainable impacts related to economic, environmental and social aspects.

We define the report content determination process by implementing four stages, as follows:

## a. Identification

Identification of material topics for the Company was carried out through data and information collection. The data collection was then followed by an analysis of the impact and scope of impact from each topic. Even though we have not yet conducted a due diligence in 2023 to assess the impact of the Company's operations on economic, environmental and social aspects, including impacts on human rights, the impact identification process still looks at aspects of stakeholder vulnerability and continues to use a human rights approach. We do this with two mechanisms. First, we conducted a survey of stakeholders regarding the Company's material topics and asked respondents to rate the level of importance of material topics on a scale of very important, important and unimportant. Second, we identify issues that arise in the media and complaints submitted through the whistleblowing system (WBS). These data will provide an overview of the Company's actual and potential, negative and positive impacts on the economy, environment and society, including impacts on human rights.

## b. Priority

After the impact identification has been completed, we then group and select the identified topics based on the priority scale and the significance of the negative and positive impacts. The significance of negative impacts is determined by their severity (scale, scope, and irremediability character) and the likelihood of those impacts occurring.
