Kedelapan risiko di atas kami kelola dengan mengedepankan pendekatan manusia (people approach), melaksanakan praktik-praktik baik dalam pertambangan terkait K3 di seluruh unit usaha, dan befokus pada identifikasi, mitigasi, dan remediasi baik untuk risiko maupun dampak negatif K3. Pendekatan manusia dicontohkan dengan memberikan izin kepada karyawan untuk tidak bekerja di tempat yang tidak aman dan tidak tersedia APD. Pada tanggal 19 September 2022 PT CPM mendapatkan Sertifikat SLO pada Instalasi Jaringan Listrik untuk operasional Tambang dan Pabrik Pengolahan 4000TPD dilakukan oleh mitra kerja yang pada saat itu mengerjakan project instalasi gardu hubungan jaringan listrik 15.000Kva. SLO merupakan sertifikasi yang harus dimiliki oleh berbagai instalasi tenaga listrik mengingat operasinya berpotensi mengakibatkan kecelakaan kerja.

We address the eight aforementioned risks by prioritizing a people-centric approach, implementing sound mining practices pertaining to occupational health and safety (OHS) across all business units, and concentrating on the identification, mitigation, and remediation of both OHS risks and associated negative impacts. The human approach is exemplified by giving permission to employees not to work in unsafe places and where PPE is not available. Additionally, OHS risk and hazard mitigation is conducted through feasibility certification. On September 19 2022 PT CPM received an SLO Certificate for the Electrical Network Installation necessary for the operations of the 4000TPD Mining and Processing Factory through collaboration with a work partner. This partner was concurrently involved in the installation project for the 15,000Kva electrical network connection substation. The SLO certification is mandatory for various electrical power installations due to the inherent potential for work accidents associated with their operations.

# OHS Incident Investigation

A. Incident/accident notifications are based on the classification of incidents/accidents as follows:

1) Class I must be reported immediately (maximum 24 hours) by telephone with confirmation via email.
2) Class II must be reported 2 x 24 hours via email.
3) Class III is reported through an internal K3LK performance report.
