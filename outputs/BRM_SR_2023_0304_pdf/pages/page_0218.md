Rencana kegiatan penambangan dan pengolahan tembaga dan mineral pengikutnya di Blok I Komplek Sungai Mak berpotensi menimbulkan dampak signifikan pada keanekaragaman hayati,seperti migrasi fauna, invasi spesies, dan penurunan keragaman biodiversitas. Sebagai upaya GM untuk mengetahuirona awal sebelum adanya kegiatan penambangan dan pengolahan tembaga dan mineral pengikutnya,maka GM telah melakukan berbagai studi keanekaragaman hayati salah satunya adalah dalam studi Analisis Mengenai Dampak Lingkungan Hidup ("AMDAL") yang dilaksanakan pada tahun 2018 dan pemantauan

The planned mining and processing of copper and its minerals derivatives in Block I of the Sungai Mak Complex has the potential to have a significant impact on the biodiversity, such as fauna migration, invasive species, and erosion of genetic diversity. As part of GM's efforts to find out the baseline prior to mining and processing of copper and its minerals derivatives, GM has conducted various biodiversity studies, one of which is the Environmental Impact Analysis ("AMDAL") study conducted in 2018 and monitoring of aquatic biota diversity, which is done every 6 months.
