Pemanfaatan energi terbarukan memiliki peran penting dalam mendorong pertumbuhan ekonomi global yang berkelanjutan dan menciptakan lingkungan bumi yang lebih sehat bagi generasi sekarang dan mendatang. Oleh karenanya, beberapa unit bisnis BRMS telah memulai menggunakan bahan bakar biodiesel B30 yang berasal dari campuran 30% bahan bakar nabati dan 70% solar untuk kendaraan operasional dan kendaraan sehari-hari seperti dumptruck, articulated dumptruck, manhaul dan light vehicle.

Sepanjang tahun 2023 telah terjadi peningkatan signifikan terhadap kenaikan konsumsi energi di tiga unit bisnis yaitu sebesar 266.276,94 GJ dibandingkan dengan tahun sebelumnya yang hanya sebesar 78.870,80 GJ. Peningkatan konsumsi energi ini terjadi karena sudah beroperasinya pabrik kedua CPM dan adanya kegiatan pengembangan proyek pabrik emas

The outcomes of the 2023 environmental assessment conducted by the HSE Division across each business unit have determined that the air, water, and waste management quality aligns with the established government standards, including Decree of the Minister of Environment No. 202 of 2004, Minister of Environment Regulation No. 4 of 2014, Minister of Environment and Forestry Regulation No. 15 of 2019, Minister of Environment and Forestry Regulation No. 11 of 2021, and Government Regulation Number 22 of 2021.

To mitigate adverse effects on the products manufactured, the Company conducts comprehensive assessments on all distributed and sold items, adhering to international standards such as the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System.

# Energy Consumption

Embracing sustainable energy is crucial to powering more sustainable global economy and creating a healthier planet for current and future generations. Therefore, several BRMS business units have started using B30 biodiesel fuel derived from a mixture of 30% biofuel and 70% diesel for operational vehicles and everyday vehicles such as dumptrucks, articulated dumptrucks, manhauls and light vehicles.

Throughout 2023 there has been a significant increase in energy consumption amounted to 266.276,94 GJ compared to the previous year which was only 78.870,80 GJ.
