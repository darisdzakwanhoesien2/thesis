Social Corporate Responsibility ("CSR") yang tertuang dalam Rencana Induk Pengembangan dan Pemberdayaan Masyarakat ("RIPPM") PT DPM bidang Pendidikan serta dalam rangka mendukung tercapainya Sustainable Development Goals ("SDGs") pilar ke-4 yaitu Pendidikan Berkualitas.

Tahapan seleksi dilakukan secara obyektif dan transparan, dengan kolaborasi penilaian akademis dari pihak kampus GDPIC dan evaluasi dari native speaker (penutur asli) atas hasil penguasaan semua aspek komunikasi bahasa Mandarin (pemahaman dalam mengartikan, mendengarkan, bicara, menulis dan membaca huruf Mandarin). Seluruh siswa-siswi calon penerima beasiswa PT DPM tersebut juga telah diinformasikan secara detail dengan menandatangani pernyataan siap mengikuti segala prosedur termasuk evaluasi Bahasa Mandarin selama setahun penuh.

The selection process ensures objectivity and transparency, incorporating academic assessments from GDPIC and evaluations by native Mandarin speakers to assess proficiency in all aspects of Mandarin communication: interpretation, listening, speaking, writing, and reading. Prospective scholarship recipients are thoroughly briefed on the program requirements, including the mandatory Mandarin language evaluation, and are required to sign a statement of readiness to adhere to these procedures for the duration of the scholarship.

Hendra Kurniawan, GM-HR at DPM and head of the PT DPM scholarship program, emphasized, "The PT DPM Scholarship Program for studying in China epitomizes the company's commitment to sustainable human resource development through a 'Talent Acquisition' framework. This approach not only nurtures a 'Pool of Talent' but also ensures the readiness of skilled human resources to meet the future needs of Dairi Prima Mineral.
