# Sustainability Reporting

The 2023 sustainability report articulates the sustainable development performance of our five business units, namely Dairi Prima Mineral (DPM), Citra Palu Minerals (CPM), Gorontalo Minerals (GM), Suma Heksa Sinergi (SHS) and Linge Mineral Resources (LMR) for the period 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2023. The sustainability report has the same reporting period as the financial reports. The company publishes a sustainability report every year.
