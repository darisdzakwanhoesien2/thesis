# Pengetahuan Kolektif Badan Tata Kelola Tinggi (2-17)

Baik Direksi maupun Dewan Komisaris wajib untuk mengikuti program pelatihan professional. Hal ini ditujukan agar badan tata kelola tinggi mendapatkan informasi terkini tentang perkembangan industri dan dapat mengantisipasi peluang dan tantangan yang akan dihadapi Perusahaan. Setiap tahun, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris akan menyusun program pelatihan yang akan diikuti dan memasukan perkiraan anggaran yang dibutuhkan dalam Rencana Kerja Tahunan Perseroan. Program pendidikan dan pelatihan dapat berupa pelatihan eksekutif, seminar, workshop atau short course. Anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris yang telah mengikuti program pelatihan diharapkan dapat berbagi informasi dan pengetahuan (sharing knowledge) kepada anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris lainnya.

# Evaluasi Kinerja Badan Tata Kelola Tertinggi (2-18)

Kinerja anggota Direksi dievaluasi oleh Dewan Komisaris dengan menggunakan mekanisme penilaian yang telah disusun oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Hasil penilaian kinerja Direksi kemudian disampaikan kepada RUPS dalam Laporan Tugas Pengawasan Dewan Komisaris.

# Collective Knowledge Of The Highest Governance Body

Board of Directors and Commissioners are required to attend professional training programs. This is intended so that the high governance bodies obtain the latest information on industry developments and are able to anticipate the opportunities and challenges that will be faced by the Company. Every year, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners will prepare a training program to be participated and includes the estimated budget needed in the Company's Annual Work Plan. Education and training programs can be in the form of executive training, seminars, workshops or short courses. Members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners who have attended the training program are expected to be able to share information and knowledge with other members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners

# Evaluation of the Performance of the Highest Governance Body

The performance of members of the Board of Directors is evaluated by the Board of Commissioners using the evaluation mechanism that has been prepared by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The results of the Board of Directors' performance evaluation are then submitted to the GMS in the Board of Commissioners' Supervisory Task Report.
