# Komite Keselamatan Pertambangan dan Lingkungan

(2-30) (403-4) (403-7) (POJK51-F.21)

Kami melibatkan tenaga kerja ke dalam manajemen K3 melalui pembentukan Komite Keselamatan Pertambangan dan Lingkungan (KPL). Komite ini beranggotakan Kepala Teknik Tambang, Perwakilan dari bagian Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) dan Keselamatan Operasi (KO) Pertambangan, Kepala Satuan Kerja dan Penanggung Jawab Operasional (PJO) Usaha Jasa Pertambangan (UJIP).

Dengan sistem lintas departemen, kami berharap aspek keselamatan dan kesehatan pertambangan dan lingkungan dapat menjangkau seluruh karyawan. Selain tugas tersebut, komite ini juga befungsi sebagai tempat menyalurkan aspirasi pegawai terkait aspek-aspek keselamatan dan kesehatan pertambangan dan lingkungan. Misalnya, karyawan dapat melaporkan kondisi bahaya di area kerjanya kepada anggota Komite KPL di departemen masing-masing. (403-4)

Berdasarkan Keputusan Dirjen Minerba ESDM No. 185.K/37.04/DJB/2019 tentang petunjuk Teknis Pelaksanaan, Penilaian, dan Pelaporan Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan Minerba. Komite Keselamatan Pertambangan mempunyai tugas dan tanggung jawab antara lain:

- Mengidentifikasi, menetapkan, dan mengesahkan tujuan, sasaran dan program keselamatan pertambangan
- Memastikan pelaksanaan dan perkembangan tujuan, sasaran dan program keselamatan pertambangan

In addition to conducting internal audits, during 2023 BRMS has successfully implemented other programs to support mining safety and health such as carrying out the equipment commissioning process, and organizing special and regular training on mining safety which was attended by Company workers and contractors

# Mining and Environment Safety Committee

We engage the workers in occupational health and safety management through the establishment of a Mining and Environmental Safety Committee (KPL). This committee comprises the Chief Mine Officer (KTT), representatives from the Occupational Safety and Health (K3) and Mining Operations Safety (KO) sections, heads of work units, and Persons in Charge of Operations (PJO) from the Mining Services Business (UJIP).

With a cross-departmental system, we hope that aspects of mining and environmental safety and health can reach all departments and employees. In addition to these duties, this Committee also functions as a place to accommodate employees' aspirations regarding aspects of safety and health in mining and the environment. For example, employees can report hazardous conditions in their work area to members of the KPL Committee in their respective departments.

In accordance with Director General of Mineral and Coal Decree No. 185.K/37.04/DJB/2019 on Technical Instructions for the Implementation, Assessment, and Reporting of Mining and Coal Mining Safety Management Systems, the Mining Safety Committee is tasked with the following duties and responsibilities:

- Identifying, establishing, and validating mining safety goals, objectives, and programs.
- Ensuring the implementation and development of mining safety goals, targets, and programs.
