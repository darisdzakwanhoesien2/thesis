# Human Resource Development Performance

We are committed to providing a safe and inclusive workplace while supporting the development of our human resources so that we can attract, reward, develop, and retain high-performing employees.

HR development is not only carried out through training but by evaluating the performance of each BRMS employees. Evaluations are carried out periodically by BRMS to monitor HR developments in an objective, transparent, participatory and non-discriminatory manner. Objective means that each assessment has the intent and purpose for the smooth running of the Company's operations as well as capacity building and character building expected of employees. On the other hand, being transparent and participatory means that the Company provides the information needed by employees in the context of this evaluation. While non-discriminatory emphasizes that performance evaluation is not related to employee background but is purely related to performance.

Furthermore, BRMS is also committed to prioritizing aspects of respecting human rights, including prohibiting all forms of forced labour, modern slavery and child labour. BRMS also provides equal opportunities for all employees
