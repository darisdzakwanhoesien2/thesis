Dalam upaya mempermudah mekanisme partisipasi, konsultasi dan komunikasi antara karyawan/kontraktor/tamu dengan perusahaan, PT GM dan PT CPM telah memiliki prosedur khusus dengan nomor prosedur GM-HSE-PR-008-Communication, Consultation and Participation dan CPM-HSE3-PR-3.03 Prosedur Komunikasi, Konsultasi, Partisipasi dan Motivasi Keselamatam Pertambangan, Pengamanan dan Lingkungan serta GM-HSE-PR-011-Non Conformance, Incident and Management Action, maka karyawan/tamu/kontraktor dapat menyampaikan keluhan, temuan, ataupun berkonsultasi terkait keselamatan pertambangan kepada Departemen HSE &amp; Compliance serta Komite Keselamatan Pertambangan dan Lingkungan. PT GM juga telah mengakomodasikan formulir HIAS (Hazard Information and Suggestion) yang diletakkan pada lokasi-lokasi strategis sehingga karyawan dan pekerja kontraktor dapat melaporkan risiko

In an effort to facilitate participation, consultation and communication between employees, contractors, guests and the Company, PT GM and PT CPM put special procedures i.e. GM-HSE-PR-008-Communication, Consultation and Participation and CPM-HSE3-PR- 3.03 Procedures for Communication, Consultation, Participation and Motivation for Mining Safety, Security and Environment as well as GM-HSE-PR-011-Non Conformance, Incident and Management Action, employees/guests/contractors can submit complaints, findings, or consult related to mining safety to HSE Department &amp; Compliance and Environmental and Mining Safety Committee. PT GM has also provided HIAS (Hazard Information and Suggestion) forms which are placed in strategic locations so that employees and contractor workers can report OHS risks and hazards.
