## Dampak Topik Material

Kami menyadari bahwa setiap insan yang bekerja dalam perusahaan kami memegang peranan penting dalam pertumbuhan dan kesuksesan kami. Namun demikian, dengan operasi perusahaan di berbagai konteks lingkungan dan sosial yang berbeda beserta kerumitan rantai pasok memberikan dampak potensial dan aktual baik positif maupun negatif kepada karyawan dan pekerja kontraktor. Dampak hak asasi manusia (HAM) ini termasuk dalam hal remunerasi, jam kerja, cakupan kesehatan dan keselamatan, pelatihan peluang, perlindungan sosial, keamanan kerja, dan akses terhadap mekanisme pengaduan.

## Management of Material Topics

### Material Topic Impact

BRMS continually work to reduce, and where possible eliminate, the health and safety risks and hazards associated with material movement, heavy equipment, and production processes throughout the mining process. The series of mining processes that we undertake can entail risks and hazards, including chemical, physical (such as noise, ergonomic issues, radiation, heat, and vibration), biological, and other types of dangers, which may affect employees, contractor workers, and the general public at various levels.
