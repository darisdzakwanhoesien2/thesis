# Tingkat Pergantian Karyawan (401-1)

Kami berhasil menjaga tingkat pergantian (turnover) karyawan di angka yang rendah yaitu kurang dari 10%. Sepanjang tahun 2023, sebanyak 27 karyawan BRMS maupun unit usaha yang mengakhiri hubungan kerja. Di tingkat kantor pusat, terdapat 4 karyawan yang mengakhiri hubungan kerja. Sementara itu, tingkat turnover karyawan pada unit usaha yakni CPM 2%, GM 12% dan DPM, SHS &amp; LMR 0%. Rendahnya tingkat turnover karyawan ini, menunjukkan bahwa BRMS mampu menciptakan lingkungan yang aman dan nyaman bagi karyawan. # Employee Turnover

We managed to keep the employee turnover rate at a low level, i.e., below 10%. Throughout 2023, 27 BRMS employees and business units were laid off. At the head office level, there were 4 employees who ended their employment relationship with the company. Meanwhile, employee turnover rates in business units were CPM 2%, GM 12% dan DPM, SHS &amp; LMR 0%. This low employee turnover rate showed that BRMS was able to create a safe and comfortable environment for employees.
