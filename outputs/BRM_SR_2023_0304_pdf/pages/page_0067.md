# 5. Risiko Operasional

Faktor-faktor yang mempengaruhi risiko ini diantaranya sebagai berikut:

- Kondisi geologis dan geoteknis yang tidak diharapkan;
- Kemungkinan terjadinya berbagai macam kecelakaan operasional;
- Kerusakan lingkungan termasuk pencemaran limbah logam, polusi dan berbagai material berbahaya lainnya ke sungai atau sumber air bersih di sekitar lokasi tambang;
- Kejadian alam seperti aktivitas seismik, kebakaran hutan, banjir, perubahan cuaca, dan aktivitas penambangan liar;

- low security and mining safety;
- changes in the prevailing laws and regulations;
- potential delays in handling social and community issues

All of the aforementioned factors may lead to increased project development costs, delayed project implementation and unexpected economic outcomes. These can trigger new risks and the occurrence of risks that have been identified.

Therefore, preparation of activity plans and monitoring of a project's progress status are important to avoiding the above risks. Each variant that occurs becomes the material of analysis by a Project Manager who proposes strategic improvements and reports them to the Board of Directors for approval.

In addition, the implementation of government regulations on Mining Health and Safety is the focus of a separate unit, and is regularly reviewed by the Company's Occupational Health and Safety Internal Auditors.

- Unexpected geology and geotechnical conditions;
- Probability of various operational accidents;
- Environmental damage, including metal waste, pollution and other hazardous materials leaking into reivers or sources of clean water surrounding the mine sites;
