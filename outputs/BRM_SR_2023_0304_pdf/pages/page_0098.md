# Dairi Prima Mineral Melanjutkan Komitmen Pemberian Beasiswa Ke Negeri China Kepada Generasi Muda Sekitar Tambang

Dairi Prima Mineral Remains Dedicated To Offering Scholarships To The Youth In The Vicinity Of The Mine In China, Emphasizing Its Commitment To Fostering Education Among The Younger Generation

![img-100.jpeg](img-100.jpeg)

PT. Dairi Prima Mineral merealisasikan program pemberian beasiswa untuk 7 putra putri terbaik DPM. Program beasiswa PT DPM ke Negeri China ini merupakan komitmen Perusahaan pada pendidikan berkelanjutan dalam rangka membangun dan mengembangkan kualitas generasi muda Dairi, khususnya yang berada di daerah sekitar tambang PT DPM

Program beasiswa PT DPM ke Negeri China ini memberikan kesempatan kepada putra putri penerima beasiswa untuk melanjutkan pendidikan di Guangdong Polytechnic of Industry and Commerce ("GDPIC") dengan Program Studi Diploma in Mechanical Manufacture and Automation di Kota Guangzhou, provinsi Guangdong. Program beasiswa PT DPM ke Negeri China ini adalah salah satu realisasi program

PT. Dairi Prima Mineral has established a scholarship program benefiting the top seven individuals who are children of DPM employees. This initiative reflects the company's commitment to fostering sustainable education, aimed at enhancing the skills and capabilities of the young generation in the vicinity of the PT DPM mine in Dairi.

The PT DPM scholarship program offers recipients the opportunity to pursue their education at Guangdong Polytechnic of Industry and Commerce (GDPIC) in Guangzhou City, Guangdong province, China. The selected candidates will enroll in the Diploma Study Program in Mechanical Manufacture and Automation, aligning with the fourth pillar of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs),
