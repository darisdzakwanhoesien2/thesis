# PILAR KESEHATAN

## Health Pillar

Peningkatan kesehatan masyarakat senantiasa dilakukan perusahaan melalui kegiatan seperti pemeriksaan kesehatan, donor darah, penyuluhan kesehatan gratis pencegahan stunting dan memberikan makanan tambahan untuk balita.

Improving public health is always carried out by the company through activities such as health checks, blood donations, free health counseling on stunting prevention, and providing additional food for toddlers.

![img-102.jpeg](img-102.jpeg)

Bantuan Pemberian Makanan Tambahan (PMT) untuk balita, peduli lansia, penanggulangan stunting dan Screening TB di wilayah lingkar tambang, Kecamatan Silima Pungga-Pungga

Supporting the provision of supplementary food (PMT) for toddlers, elderly care, stunting prevention, and Tuberculosis screening in the Silima Pungga-Pungga District mining area.
