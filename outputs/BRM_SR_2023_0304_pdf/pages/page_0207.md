# Pengelolaan Sumber Daya Air

(303-1) (303-2) (303-3) (303-4) (303-5)

(POJK51-F.8)

Air merupakan bahan baku penting dalam operasi Perseroan, terutama untuk proses pengolahan bijih, pendinginan, pengecoran, pengendalian debu, dan konsumsi manusia. Demi menjaga keseimbangan dengan kebutuhan masyarakat dan ekosistem, kami berkomitmen untuk menggunakan air secara bertanggung jawab, efisien, dan aman.

Pengelolaan sumber daya air oleh Perusahaan bertumpu pada Permen LHK Nomor P.80 Tahun 2021 tentang Perubahan atas Peraturan Menteri Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan Nomor P.93/Menlhk/ Setjen/Kum.1/8/2018 tentang Pemantauan Kualitas Air Limbah Secara Terus-menerus dan dalam Jaringan Bagi Usaha dan/atau Kegiatan guna menjamin kualitas dan ketersediaan air produksi dan domestik yang cukup setiap saat. Lebih lanjut, unit usaha CPM telah mendapatkan izin pengusahaan sumber daya air untuk kegiatan pertambangan di Sungai Pondo, Kelurahan Poboya, Kota Palu dari Kepala Balai Wilayah Sungai Sulawesi III Provinsi Sulawesi Tengah berdasarkan No. 250/KPTS/M/2020 pada tanggal 27 Maret 2020. Di samping itu, CPM juga telah memiliki izin pembuangan air limbah No. 503/16.72/03/I-IPLC/DPMPTSP/IV/2021 tanggal 6 April 2021. (303-1) (303-2)

Untuk memenuhi komitmen tersebut, Perusahaan befokus pada tiga aspek pengelolaan air: (303-1) (303-2)

1. Mengurangi intensitas pengambilan air baku dari alam terutama air tanah dan air permukaan
2. Memaksimalkan penggunaan air hasil resirkulasi dan daur ulang untuk proses produksi dan keperluan lannya seperti penyiraman dan pengendalian debu
3. Memastikan baku mutu telah sesuai untuk air yang akan dialirkan kembali ke badan air umum.

# Water Resources Management

Water plays a pivotal role as a primary raw material in our operations, especially for ore processing, cooling, casting, dust suppression, and potable purposes. In order to maintain a harmonious balance with the needs of communities and ecosystems, we are committed to ensuring responsible, efficient, and safe water usage.

The management of water resources by the Company relies on the Minister of Environment and Forestry Regulation Number P.80 of 2021 concerning Amendments to the Regulation of the Minister of Environment and Forestry Number P.93/Menlhk/Setjen/Kum.1/8/2018 concerning Continuous Monitoring of Wastewater Quality and in the Network for Businesses and/or Activities to ensure the quality and availability of sufficient production and domestic water at all times. Furthermore, the CPM business unit has obtained a permit to exploit water resources for mining activities in the Pondo River, Poboya Village, Palu City from the Head of the Sulawesi River Region III Central Sulawesi Province based on No. 250/KPTS/M/2020 on 27 March 2020. In addition, CPM also has a waste water disposal permit No. 503/16.72/03/I-IPLC/DPMPTSP/IV/2021 dated 6 April 2021.

To realize this commitment, the Company focuses on three aspects of water management:

1. Reducing the intensity of water extraction from nature, especially groundwater and surface water.
2. Maximizing the use of recirculated and recycled water for production processes and other purposes such as watering and dust control.
3. Ensuring that the quality standard is appropriate for water that will be flowed back into public water bodies.
