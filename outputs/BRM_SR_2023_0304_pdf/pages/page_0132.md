# Memfasilitasi Karyawan Dengan Pelatihan

(404-1) (404-2) (POJK51-F.22)

BRMS berkomitmen terhadap pengembangan kompetensi dan profesionalitas karyawan melalui berbagai program pelatihan SDM seperti kepemimpinan, pelatihan keselamatan, pelatihan karyawan baru, pelatihan lintas-fungsi, refresher training, dan transfer pengetahuan.

BRMS tidak hanya berfokus pada peningkatan keterampilan, tetapi juga memprioritaskan pemahaman dan implementasi prinsip HAM dalam setiap aspek operasionalnya. Sebagai bagian dari upaya ini, perwakilan karyawan dikirim untuk mengikuti pelatihan HAM dalam rangkaian acara dari Business and Human Rights (BHR) Academy yang diselenggarakan oleh United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), dengan dukungan dari Pemerintah Jepang, dan difasilitasi oleh Foundation for International Human Rights Reporting Standards (FIHRRST) pada tanggal 1-2 Maret 2023.

Pelatihan dan pendidikan bagi karyawan yang diselenggarakan baik oleh internal Perusahaan maupun eksternal befokus pada penguatan kompetensi perilaku kepemimpinan (soft competency) dan kompetensi teknis (hard competency). Sepanjang tahun 2023, kami telah memfasilitasi pelatihan sebanyak 3.872 jam pelatihan dengan total karyawan sebanyak 228 karyawan.

# Facilitating Employees With Training

BRMS is committed to the ongoing development of competency and professionalism of our people through human resource training, with a focus on leadership development, safety training, new-hire training, cross-training, refresher training and knowledge transfer.

BRMS prioritizes not just skill enhancement, but also the understanding and implementation of human rights principles across all operational facets. As part of this initiative, employee representatives attended human rights training as part of the Business and Human Rights (BHR) Academy series organized by the United Nations Development Program (UNDP), with support from the Government of Japan, and facilitated by the Foundation for International Human Rights Reporting Standards (FIHRRST) on March 1-2, 2023.

Training and education for employees organized both internally and externally focuses on strengthening leadership behavior competencies (soft competency) and technical competency (hard competency). Throughout 2023, we have facilitated 3,872 hours of training with a total of 228 employees.
