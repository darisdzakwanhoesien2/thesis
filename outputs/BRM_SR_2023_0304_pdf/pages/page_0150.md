# Identifikasi Risiko K3 (403-2)

Setiap unit usaha telah melakukan Identifikasi Bahaya dan Penilaian Risiko (IBPR) dan Identifikasi &amp; Penilaian Dampak Lingkungan (IPDL) serta memperbaruinya sesuai dengan tahapan kegiatan, pembaharuan peraturan perundangan dan riwayat insiden.

BRMS dan unit usaha telah membuat prosedur yang mengatur proses IBPR dan IPDL. Khusus untuk LMR, unit usaha baru menyusun Job Safety Analysis (JSA) karena saat ini masih berada ditahap penyelesaian dokumen lingkungan. Dokumen IBPR, IPDL, dan JSA yang dimaksud yaitu:

- BRMS: HSE.01.02 - HSEC Risk Management.
- PT CPM: HSE3-PR-2.02 - Identifikasi Bahaya &amp; Penilaian Risiko (IBPR) dan Identifikasi &amp; Penilaian Dampak Lingkungan (IPDL).
- PT GM: GM-HSE-PR-002 - Hazard Identification and Risk Management.
- PT DPM: DPM-HSE-SAFT-SOP-080 - Hazard Identification, Risks Assessment and Determining Control.
- PT SHS: SHS-HSE-SOP-002 - Identifikasi Bahaya dan Pengendalian Resiko.
- PT LMR: JSA-DRL-001: Bongkar Muat Rig Dari Dan Ke Kendaraan Angkut.
- JSA-DRL-002: Pemboran Geoteknik.
- JSA-DRL-003: Konstruksi Sumur Pantau.

Ensuring a healthy and safe work environment is further reinforced by incorporating Occupational Safety and Health (K3LK) aspects into the Company Regulations. At CPM and GM, K3LK provisions are contained in the 2022-2024 Company Regulations in Chapter VII Article 50 concerning Occupational Safety and Health Rules. Meanwhile, SHS and LMR are enshrined in the 2023-2025 Company Regulations Article 48 concerning Occupational Safety and Health Rules. All employees are comprehensively covered under these regulations and have undergone training facilitated by the HRD Team.

# OHS Risks Identification

Each subsidiary has conducted Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (IBPR) and Environmental Impact Identification &amp; Assessment (IPDL), continually updating them in accordance with the stages of activity, changes in laws and regulations, and incident history.

BRMS and its business units have established procedures to govern the IBPR and IPDL. Specifically for LMR, the business unit is still developing Job Safety Analysis (JSA) because it is currently still at the stage of completing environmental documents. The IBPR, IPDL and JSA documents in question are:

- BRM: HSE.01.02 - HSEC Risk Management.
- PT CPM: HSE3-PR-2.02 on Hazard Identification &amp; Risk Assessment (IBPR) and Environmental Impact Identification &amp; Assessment (IPDL).
- PT GM: GM-HSE-PR-002 - Hazard Identification and Risk Management.
- PT DPM: DPM-HSE-SAFT -SOP-080 HIRDC.
- PT SHS: SHS-HSE-SOP-002 - Hazard Identification and Risk Control.
- PT LMR: JSA-DRL-001: Loading and Unloading Rigs From and To Transport Vehicles.
- JSA-DRL-002: Geotechnical Drilling.
- JSA-DRL-003: Monitoring Well Construction
