Our farms and hatcheries are divided into three zones. Red Zone (security posts, parking lots, generators), Yellow Zone (intermediate areas that consist of, among others, office premises) and Green Zone (barn areas). This is done to ensure that our animals don't come into contact with infectious elements and contaminated objects that can potentially cause diseases.

## Proper Sanitation

Every person, raw materials, equipment and vehicles entering the farms have to go through strict biosecurity procedures such as showering, changing clothes, fumigation, car dipping and spraying. The process of disinfection is conducted upon entry at each zone and again upon exiting farms and hatcheries. Sanitation helps to increase cleanliness and hygiene, which protects the health of our poultry.
