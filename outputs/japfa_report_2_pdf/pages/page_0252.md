# Farmers

[GRI 203-2, 413-1]

We provide support to our poultry farmer partners and aquaculture farmers through technical assistance and skill improvement programmes where we can support them with our expertise and experience. The aim is to develop them so we can grow stronger together and contribute to build a better poultry and aquaculture industry for Indonesia.
