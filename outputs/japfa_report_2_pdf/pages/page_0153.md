# Our Operations

[GRI 2-1, GRI 2-7, 2-8] [POJK C.3]

We were established in 1971 as a copra pellet producer in Surabaya. We have since grown into a recognisable and trusted integrated feed and agri-food company. Our experiences shaped us to become one of the leaders in the agri-food industry with vast operations across Indonesia. Details on the location of our operations can be found in our 2022 Annual Report. [GRI 2-1] [POJK C.3]
