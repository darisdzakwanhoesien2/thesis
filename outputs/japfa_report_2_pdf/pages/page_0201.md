# Collaboration with Universitas Indonesia to Develop Student Nutrition Journal

This year, we participated in the Kedaireka (Kedaulatan Indonesia dalam Reka Cipta) Matching Fund programme initiated by the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research and Technology, which aims to synergise universities and industries.

In this programme, we collaborated with Universitas Indonesia's School of Public Health to improve children's health through the development of student nutrition journals for JAPFA for Kids' assisted schools located in Padang City and Padang Pariaman Regency.
