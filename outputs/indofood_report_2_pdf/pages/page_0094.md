# Empowerment through Collaboration: How Our Agribusiness Group Empowers Smallholder Farmers in Bumi Jaya Village

Pemberdayaan melalui Kolaborasi: Bagaimana Grup Agribisnis kami Memberdayakan Petani di Desa Bumi Jaya

A 60 years old smallholder farmer from Bumi Jaya Village, Kaubun District, East Kutai Regency, East Kalimantan, Sularno eagerly shares his transformative journey as a partner of Agribusiness Group over the past 15 years.

"Previously, our village grappled with marginalization and daunting economic challenges. However, since our partnership with Agribusiness Group in 2007, we have witnessed numerous positive changes," Sularno exclaimed with palpable delight.

At the beginning, Sularno witnessed a remarkable surge in income through his involvement in palm oil plantation activities. The company's consistent harvest results and competitive pricing structure have empowered him to afford his children's college education.

"Through the training programs and technical support provided by Agribusiness Group, we started seeing significant increases in crop yields. This has enabled me to repair my home and provide better educational opportunities for my children," he elaborated.

Furthermore, Agribusiness Group's presence has triggered infrastructural improvements within Bumi Jaya Village. Roads that were previously challenging to navigate have undergone upgrading, facilitating transportation of harvests and expediting distribution processes.

"Today, our village is thriving and expanding. We extend our gratitude to Agribusiness Group for their positive impact on us, the small-scale oil palm farmers," Sularno concluded with a radiant smile.

Petani berusia 60 tahun asal Desa Bumi Jaya, Kecamatan Kaubun, Kabupaten Kutai Timur, Kalimantan Timur, Sularno, dengan penuh semangat berbagi kisah pengalamannya selama 15 tahun terakhir sebagai mitra petani Grup Agribisnis.

"Sebelumnya, desa kami menghadapi marginalisasi dan mengalami kesulitan ekonomi. Namun, semenjak menjalin kemitraan dengan Grup Agribisnis di tahun 2007, kami telah menyaksikan banyak perubahan yang positif," tutur Sularno dengan penuh kegembiraan.

Hal itu didapati Sularno setelah memperoleh peningkatan pendapatan secara signifikan dari kegiatan perkebunan kelapa sawit. Hasil panen kebun yang konsisten dan penetapan harga yang kompetitif telah memberikan manfaat sehingga dapat membiayai pendidikan anak-anaknya sampai jenjang perguruan tinggi.

"Melalui program pelatihan dan dukungan teknis yang diberikan oleh Grup Agribisnis, kami dapat meningkatan hasil panen secara signifikan. Hal ini memungkinkan saya memperbaiki rumah dan memberikan kesempatan pendidikan yang lebih baik bagi anak-anak saya," jelasnya.

Selain itu, kehadiran Grup Agribisnis telah menggerakan perbaikan infrastruktur di Desa Bumi Jaya. Jalan-jalan yang sebelumnya sulit dilalui telah diperbaiki, sehingga memudahkan transportasi hasil panen dan mempercepat proses distribusi.

"Saat ini desa kami berkembang dan sudah mulai maju, kami mengucapkan terima kasih kepada Grup Agribisnis telah memberikan manfaat yang positif kepada masyarakat petani sawit di desa kami," tutup Sularno sambil tersenyum cerah.
