# Manufacturing Innovation in Bogasari Group

The wheat transfer process is a crucial component of raw material handling, extending from the jetty to the wheat silo and eventually reaching the production line. This process involves the utilization of Cross Belt Towers ("CBT") for transferring the product between conveyor belts during the production process. To address the potential generation of flying dust, a de-dusting system is strategically installed in the CBT area to minimize dust particulate scattering. The primary goal is to reduce the dispersion of dust particulate during wheat transfer process. The de-dusting system efficiently captures and absorbs the dust particulate, directing it to the pelletizer plant for further processing and value generation. Additionally, this system contributes to a reduction in water consumption during the cleaning process, particularly when dealing with dust accumulation.

# Inovasi Manufaktur di Grup Bogasari

Proses transfer gandum merupakan salah satu proses penting dalam penanganan bahan baku, mulai dari lokasi dermaga menuju ke silo gandum hingga mencapai lini produksi. Proses ini melibatkan pemanfaatan Cross Belt Towers ("CBT") untuk proses pemindahan produk antar conveyor belt selama proses produksi. Untuk mengatasi potensi timbulnya debu yang beterbangan, sistem de-dusting telah secara strategis dipasang di area CBT guna meminimalkan penyebaran partikulat debu. Tujuan utama upaya ini adalah untuk mengurangi penyebaran partikulat debu selama proses transfer gandum. Sistem de-dusting secara efisien menangkap dan menyerap partikulat debu, menyalurkannya ke unit pengolahan pelet untuk diproses lebih lanjut dan menghasilkan nilai tambah. Selain itu, sistem ini juga berkontribusi terhadap pengurangan penggunaan air selama proses pembersihan, terutama ketika menangani penumpukan debu.
