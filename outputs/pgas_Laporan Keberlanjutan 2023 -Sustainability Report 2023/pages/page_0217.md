# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Tim ESG

Tugas dan tanggung jawab tiap entitas dalam Tim ESG disesuaikan dengan fungsi dan keahlian masing-masing unit.

# Duties and Responsibilities of the ESG Team

The duties and responsibilities of each entity in the ESG Team are adjusted to the functions and expertise of each unit.

|  Project Sponsor | 1. Memberikan arahan strategis dalam implementasi ESG Management sesuai milestone dan target yang telah direncanakan. 2. Memfasilitasi Tim ESG Management dengan Direksi PGN serta fungsi pendukung lainnya. 3. Memonitor pelaksanaan implementasi ESG Management melalui laporan berkala Project Leader. 4. Memberikan arahan terhadap penentuan/pencapaian target dan deliverable dalam implementasi ESG Management. 5. Memberikan support/bantuan strategis terkait pelaksanaan kegiatan Tim ESG Management antara lain kebutuhan resources, bujet dan kebutuhan lain. 6. Melakukan hal-hal lainnya yang diperlukan dalam rangka melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab Project Sponsor. 1. Provide strategic direction in the implementation of ESG Management in accordance with the planned milestones and targets. 2. Facilitate the ESG Management Team with the PGN Board of Directors and other supporting functions. 3. Monitor the implementation of ESG Management through periodic reports from the Project Leader. 4. Provide direction on the determination/achievement of targets and deliverables in the implementation of ESG Management. 5. Provide strategic support/assistance related to the implementation of ESG Management Team activities, including resource needs, budgets and other needs. 6. Perform other matters required in order to carry out the duties and responsibilities of the Project Sponsor.  |
| --- | --- |
|  Project Leader & Vice Project Leader. | 1. Menyampaikan arahan Project Sponsor kepada Tim ESG Management. 2. Melakukan koordinasi seluruh aktivitas yang perlu dilakukan dalam implementasi ESG Management. 3. Memastikan tercapainya target dan deliverable sesuai waktu yang ditentukan. 4. Menentukan hal-hal yang perlu eskalasi ke Project Sponsor untuk pengambilan keputusan. 5. Membantu anggota tim untuk melakukan koordinasi dengan SME dan pihak ketiga lainnya yang terlibat. 6. Memonitor implementasi ESG Management secara berkala. 7. Melaporkan progress status kepada Project Sponsor secara berkala. 8. Memastikan tersedianya support/bantuan strategis terkait pelaksanaan kegiatan Tim ESG Management antara lain kebutuhan resources, bujet dan kebutuhan lainnya. 9. Melakukan hal-hal lainnya yang ditugaskan oleh Project Sponsor serta diperlukan dalam rangka melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab Project Leader dan Vice Project Leader. 1. Convey Project Sponsor directions to the ESG Management Team. 2. Coordinating all activities that need to be carried out in implementing ESG Management. 3. Ensure the achievement of targets and deliverables according to the specified time. 4. Determine matters that need to be escalated to the Project Sponsor for decision making. 5. Assist team members to coordinate with SME and other third parties involved. 6. Monitor the implementation of ESG Management regularly. 7. Report progress status to the Project Sponsor periodically. 8. Ensure the availability of strategic support/assistance related to the implementation of ESG Management Team activities, including resource needs, budget and other needs. 9. Do other things assigned by the Project Sponsor and necessary in order to carry out the duties and responsibilities of the Project Leader and Vice Project Leader.  |
