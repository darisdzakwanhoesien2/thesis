# LAYANAN DAN PROMOSI KESEHATAN
[403-3] [403-6][11.9.4] [11.9.7]

PGN menyelenggarakan berbagai program untuk meningkatkan kesehatan Perwira Subholding Gas meliputi program preventif, kuratif, rehabilitatif, dan promotif yang telah sesuai dengan persyaratan hukum yang berlaku. Program kesehatan kerja yang telah diselenggarakan, mencakup:

1. Preventif:
a. Identifikasi aspek dan dampak faktor lingkungan kerja.
b. Penilaian Risiko Kesehatan atau Health Risk Assessment (HRA).
c. Pelaksanaan Medical Check Up (MCU).
d. Pelaksanaan Daily Check Up (DCU)
e. Penentuan tingkat kesehatan (fitness status).
f. Pelatihan Pertolongan Pertama Pada Kecelakaan (P3K).
g. Pengelolaan hygiene dan sanitasi lingkungan kerja.
h. Pelaksanaan imunisasi dan vaksinasi.
i. Wellness program.

2. Promotif:
a. Seminar kesehatan kerja.
b. Konsultasi khusus.
c. Sosialisasi Kesehatan P2HIV dan P2TB

3. Kuratif:
a. Pelayanan pengobatan di Perusahaan.
b. Pelayanan rujukan.
c. Penanganan kondisi darurat medis.

# HEALTH SERVICES AND PROMOTION
[403-3] [403-6][11.9.4] [11.9.7]

PGN organizes various programs to enhance the health of Subholding Gas Officers, including preventive, curative, rehabilitative, and promotive programs, all in compliance with applicable legal requirements. Occupational health programs include:

1. Preventive
a. Identifying aspects and impacts of the work environment.
b. Health Risk Assessment (HRA).
c. Conducting Medical Check-Up (MCU)
d. Conducting Daily Check-Up (DCU).
e. Determining health status (fitness status).
f. First Aid Training.
g. Managing hygiene and sanitation of the work environment.
h. Implementing immunization and vaccination.
i. Wellness program.

2. Promotive
a. Occupational health seminars.
b. Special consultations.
c. Health socialization P2HIV and P2TB.

3. Curative
a. Company medical services.
b. Referral services.
c. Handling medical emergencies.
