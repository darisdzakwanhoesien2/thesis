PT INDOFOOD SUKSES MAKMUR Tbk • 2022 • Sustainability Report

The Sustainability Coordinator heads the Sustainability Department and reports directly to the BOD. The Sustainability Department is supported by the Strategic Function and Group and division representatives who form part of the Implementation Function.

The Sustainability Department coordinates and integrates relevant value-adding sustainability initiatives and programs throughout the Groups' operating units. These efforts include organization of socialization and training events to ensure that relevant stakeholders remain aware of sustainability-related initiatives and developments.

The Sustainability Department works in partnership with the Strategic Function to monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of Group-wide sustainability strategy and program implementation within the Group.

The Implementation Function manages the daily practicalities involved in the implementation of our sustainability framework throughout the Indofood organization and is comprised of coordinators from each business function across our divisions. Its responsibilities include executing programs, socializing initiatives, and supporting sustainability data collection, including tracking and evaluation.

Members of the BOD and all individuals in our sustainability governance structure attend periodic sustainability training programs, workshops and seminars to build our capacity in sustainable business practices. The training programs attended in 2022 included topics such as Indonesia's Economic Outlook 2023, Global Economic Outlook 2023, the Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD), GHG Emissions Measurement, Supply Chain and Sustainable Procurement, Capacity Building on ESG Series, GRI Standard, Human Rights, Post-Consumer Packaging Waste Management, and more. Koordinator Sustainability memimpin Departemen Sustainability dan melapor secara langsung kepada Direksi. Departemen Sustainability didukung oleh Fungsi Strategis serta perwakilan dari setiap Grup dan divisi yang menjadi bagian dari Fungsi Implementasi.

Departemen Sustainability mengoordinasikan dan mengintegrasikan berbagai inisiatif dan program keberlanjutan yang memberikan nilai tambah di seluruh unit operasional. Upaya yang dilakukan termasuk melaksanakan sosialisasi dan pelatihan tentang keberlanjutan guna memastikan bahwa para pemangku kepentingan yang relevan terus mendapatkan informasi terkait inisiatif dan perkembangan keberlanjutan yang dilakukan.

Departemen Sustainability berkoordinasi dengan Fungsi Strategi dan bertanggung jawab untuk mengawasi dan mengelola efektivitas strategi dan implementasi program di seluruh Grup.

Fungsi Implementasi melaksanakan kegiatan operasional harian yang berkaitan dengan kerangka kerja keberlanjutan di seluruh unit operasional Indofood dan mencakup koordinator dari masing-masing fungsi kegiatan usaha di seluruh divisi. Tanggung jawab Fungsi Implementasi termasuk menjalankan program, melakukan sosialisasi kegiatan dan membantu pengumpulan data keberlanjutan, serta melakukan penelusuran dan evaluasi.

Anggota Direksi dan seluruh individu dalam struktur tata kelola keberlanjutan mengikuti berbagai program pelatihan, workshop dan seminar tentang keberlanjutan secara berkala guna meningkatkan kapasitas praktik keberlanjutan bisnis kami. Program pelatihan yang diikuti diantaranya adalah Indonesia's Economic Outlook 2023, Global Economic Outlook 2023, the Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD), Pengukuran Emisi GRK, Rantai Pasokan dan Pengadaan Berkelanjutan, Peningkatan Kapasitas terkait ESG, Standar GRI, Hak Asasi Manusia, serta Pengelolaan Sampah Kemasan Pasca Konsumsi dan berbagai pelatihan lainnya.
