## Seasonal Contract Workers (2-8)

In our Agribusiness Group, we hired 13,337 seasonal contract workers¹³ as and when required for seasonal jobs, especially during peak season.

## Pekerja Kontrak Musiman (2-8)

Di Grup Agribisnis, kami mempekerjakan 13.337 pekerja kontrak¹³ jika diperlukan untuk pekerjaan musiman, terutama selama musim puncak.

¹² Employee turnover is calculated from the number of employees who leave the organization voluntarily
¹³ Pergantian karyawan dihitung berdasarkan jumlah karyawan yang berhenti atas kemauan sendiri
¹⁴ All of our employees, including seasonal contract workers, are employed on a full-time basis. We have no part-time employees
¹⁵ Semua karyawan kami, termasuk pekerja kontrak musiman, dipekerjakan sebagai pekerja penuh waktu (full-time). Kami tidak mempekerjakan pekerja paruh waktu
