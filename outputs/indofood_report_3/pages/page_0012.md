In the short to medium term, we will maintain our emissions reduction efforts by increasing the efficiency with which we use energy and by continuing to expand our use of renewables. We will also remain abreast of the alignment and development of international sustainability reporting standards. As the field of sustainability reporting continues to grow, we will take measures to extend our climate risk reporting using the TCFD framework beyond our Agribusiness to all areas of the Group.
