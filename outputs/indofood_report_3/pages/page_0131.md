# Energy Consumption and Renewable Energy

Indofood's energy consumption totaled 17.2 million Gigajoules ("GJ") in 2022. A total of 67.7% of Indofood's energy consumption is derived from renewable energy sources such as bagasse, oil palm shell, and fiber. [302-1]

# Konsumsi Energi dan Energi Terbarukan

Konsumsi energi di Indofood mencapai 17,2 juta Gigajoules ("GJ") pada tahun 2022. Sebanyak 67,7% penggunaan energi di Indofood bersumber dari energi terbarukan seperti serat tebu, cangkang dan serat kelapa sawit. [302-1]
