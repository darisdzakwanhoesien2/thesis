# Bogasari Receives Green Port Award Recognition
## Bogasari Menerima Penghargaan Green Port

![img-128.jpeg](img-128.jpeg)
Bogasari Group has been recognized in supporting Green Port

![img-129.jpeg](img-129.jpeg)
Grup Bogasari mendapatkan pengakuan atas upayanya dalam mendukung Green Port

We are proud that Bogasari Port in Tanjung Priok, Jakarta, has been recognized by the Port Authority Office in the Otoritas Pelabuhan Green Port Award. In 2021, our terminal was honored with the "Port Authority Awards" for our excellence across three categories which were "The Most Innovative Terminal to Support Green Port," "Best Terminal for Environmental Management" and "Best Terminal with Zero Accident Achievement". In 2022, we added a new category to our accomplishments, for "Own-Interest Terminal" in supporting Green Port which is given to terminal operators. The awards provide an incentive and motivation for operators to enhance sustainability-related operations and reinforce our commitment to continue with our efforts to operate our port in Tanjung Priok in an environmentally friendly manner.

Kami bangga bahwa Pelabuhan Bogasari di Tanjung Priok, Jakarta, telah mendapatkan pengakuan dari Kantor Otoritas Pelabuhan pada ajang Otoritas Pelabuhan Green Port Award. Pada tahun 2021, terminal kami dianugerahi penghargaan "Port Authority Awards" karena keunggulan kami di tiga kategori, yaitu "The Most Innovative Terminal to Support Green Port," "Best Terminal for Environmental Management" dan "Best Terminal with Zero Accident Achievement". Di tahun 2022, kami menambahkan keunggulan di satu kategori, yakni "Own-Interest Terminal" dalam mendukung terwujudnya Green Port yang diberikan kepada operator terminal. Penghargaan ini tentunya memberikan insentif dan motivasi kepada operator untuk terus meningkatkan berbagai inisiatif terkait keberlanjutan serta menegaskan komitmen kami untuk melanjutkan upaya dalam mengoperasikan pelabuhan kami di Tanjung Priok dengan sistem pengoperasian yang ramah lingkungan.
