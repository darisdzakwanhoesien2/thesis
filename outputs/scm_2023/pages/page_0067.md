Laporan Keberlanjutan 2023 Sustainability Report

Lebih lanjut mengenai pemberian hak cuti melahirkan, cuti melahirkan diberikan untuk masa waktu 90 hari kalender bagi karyawan perempuan dan karyawan yang bersangkutan dapat kembali bekerja usai menjalani masa cuti. Hak cuti juga diberikan kepada karyawan laki-laki yang istrinya melahirkan (paternity leave) selama 2 hari kalender. Pada tahun 2023, sebanyak 48 karyawan perempuan mengambil cuti melahirkan, yang terdiri 19 orang karyawan dari Divisi SCM Media dan 29 orang karyawan dari Divisi SCM Digital &amp; Other Business, dan seluruh karyawan tersebut telah kembali bekerja dengan penempatan posisi yang sama sebelum mereka mengambil cuti melahirkan.

Di samping remunerasi karyawan, Perseroan juga memberikan remunerasi yang menarik bagi anggota Direksi, Dewan Komisaris dan jajaran eksekutif, termasuk memberikan insentif jangka panjang sebagai bentuk apresiasi atas dedikasi jajaran manajemen dan karyawan. Komponen remunerasi bagi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris telah dijelaskan pada Laporan Tahunan 2023 yang disajikan dalam dokumen terpisah.

Perseroan menunjukkan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi untuk membantu merumuskan kebijakan remunerasi bagi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris untuk kemudian diusulkan kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk dimintakan masukannya sebelum disampaikan kepada RUPS untuk dimintakan persetujuannya. Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan ini dipimpin oleh Wakil Komisaris Utama dan Komisaris Independen Perseroan, Suryani Zaini. Informasi lebih lanjut mengenai Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan, termasuk struktur keanggotaan dan lingkup tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, dapat dilihat pada Laporan Tahunan 2023 yang disajikan terpisah dari Laporan Keberlanjutan ini.

# Lingkungan Kerja yang Layak, Sehat, dan Aman
Decent, Healthy, and Safe Working Environment
[GRI 403-1] [GRI 403-5] [GRI 403-9] [OJK F.21]

Untuk mendorong karyawan agar terus berkarya dan bekerja secara produktif, SCM telah menyediakan lingkungan kerja yang layak, sehat dan aman melalui penerapan Kebijakan Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3).

Penerapan K3 di lingkungan Perseroan dilaksanakan antara lain dengan melaksanakan berbagai pelatihan terkait K3, seperti pelatihan evakuasi kebakaran gabungan, simulasi damkar kering, dan pelatihan penggunaan tabung apar. Hingga akhir tahun 2023, Perseroan memastikan pengelolaan K3 telah berjalan baik sebagaimana diindikasikan hanya ada 1 (satu) insiden kecelakaan kerja ringan.

As for the provision regarding maternity leave, it is given for a period of 90 calendar days for female employees and the employee concerned can return to work after their leave period. Leave rights are also given to male employees whose wives give birth (paternity leave) for 2 calendar days. In 2023, a total of 48 female employees took paternity leave, consisting of 19 employees from SCM Media Division and 29 employees from SCM Digital &amp; Other Business Division, and all employees have returned to work in the same position before they took the leave.

In addition to employee remuneration, the Company also provides attractive remuneration for members of the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners and executives, including long-term incentives as a form of appreciation for the dedication of the management and employees. The remuneration components for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners have been elaborated in the 2023 Annual Report which is presented in a separate document.

The Company appoints the Nomination and Remuneration Committee to assist the formulation of remuneration policies for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners to be proposed to the Board of Commissioners for input before being submitted to the GMS for approval. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee is chaired by the Vice President Commissioner and Independent Commissioner of the Company, Suryani Zaini. Further information on the Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee, including its membership structure and scope of duties and responsibilities, can be found in the 2023 Annual Report which is presented separately from this Sustainability Report.

To encourage employees to continue working productively, SCM has provided a decent, healthy and safe working environment through the implementation of Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) policies.

The implementation of OHS in the Company's environment is carried out through various OHS-related trainings, such as joint fire evacuation training, dry firefighting simulation, and training on the use of light fire extinguishers. Until the end of 2023, the Company ensures that its OHS management has been running properly, as indicated there was only 1 (one) minor work accident.
