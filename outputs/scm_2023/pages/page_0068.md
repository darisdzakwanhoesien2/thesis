# Pelatihan dan Pengembangan Kompetensi
Training and Competency Development
[OJK F.22]

## Pendekatan Manajemen [GRI 404-2]

Dalam kegiatan pengelolaan SDM Perseroan, SCM memberikan kesempatan yang adil dan setara kepada seluruh karyawan untuk mengembangkan kemampuan pribadinya melalui keikutsertaan pada program pendidikan dan pelatihan yang sesuai dengan minat serta kebutuhan pengembangan bisnis di unit atau departemen di mana karyawan tersebut ditempatkan.

Sebagaimana disampaikan sebelumnya, tanggung jawab pengembangan kompetensi karyawan menjadi bagian dari pelaksanaan tugas Divisi Human Resources. Divisi tersebut bertanggung jawab untuk menyelenggarakan pendidikan dan pelatihan internal bagi seluruh karyawan ataupun bekerja sama dengan pihak eksternal. Hasil dari pelaksanaan pendidikan dan pelatihan karyawan dilaporkan dan dievaluasi oleh Direksi melalui mekanisme penilaian pencapaian Key Performance Indicator (KPI).

Pada tahun 2023, Perseroan telah mengadakan pelatihan bagi 967 karyawan dengan total jam pelatihan sebanyak 6.798 jam dan realisasi biaya sebesar Rp602,79 juta. Sementara itu, pada tahun 2022, total jam pelatihan karyawan mencapai 4.726 jam dengan realisasi biaya sebesar Rp374,62 juta.

## Management's Approach [GRI 404-2]

In the management of the Company's Human Resources, SCM provides fair and equal opportunities for all employees to develop their personal capabilities through participation in education and training programs that are in accordance with the interests and business development needs of the unit or department in which the employee is assigned.

As previously stated, the responsibility for employee competency development is part of the Human Resources Division's duties. The division is responsible for organizing internal education and training for all employees or collaborating with external parties. The results of employee education and training are reported and evaluated by the Board of Directors through the Key Performance Indicator (KPI) assessment mechanism.

In 2023, the Company has held training for 967 employees with total training hours of 6,798 hours and realized costs of Rp602.79 million. Meanwhile, in 2022, the total employee training hours reached 4,726 hours with realized costs of Rp374.62 million.
