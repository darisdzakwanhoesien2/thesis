# EVALUASI KINERJA BADAN TATA KELOLA TERTINGGI
PERFORMANCE EVALUATION OF THE HIGHEST GOVERNANCE ORGANS

[GRI 2-18]

Sebagai bagian dari upaya membangun budaya keberlanjutan, maka Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi melakukan penilaian terhadap kinerja anggota Direksi dan/atau anggota Dewan Komisaris berdasarkan indikator kualifikasi yang telah ditetapkan dan disepakati bersama pada awal tahun buku. Adapun kebijakan dan proses penilaian tersebut dapat dilihat dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan tahun 2023 yang disajikan dalam dokumen terpisah.

As part of the effort to build a culture of sustainability, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee evaluates the performance of members of the Board of Directors and/or members of the Board of Commissioners based on qualification indicators that have been set and agreed upon at the beginning of the fiscal year. The policy and evaluation process can be seen in the Company's Annual Report 2023 which is presented in a separate document.
