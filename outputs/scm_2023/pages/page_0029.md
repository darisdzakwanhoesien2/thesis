# SEPUTAR USAHA DI BIDANG MEDIA ABOUT MEDIA BUSINESS

## Kepatuhan Perseroan dan Capaian Tahun 2023
### Company Compliance and Achievements in 2023

Kepatuhan Perseroan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku diwujudkan dengan mengikuti perkembangan regulasi dan menerapkannya di segenap aspek usahanya. Komitmen Perseroan ini antara lain direalisasikan dengan mematuhi etika pariwara dan kebijakan oleh Menkominfo dan/ atau Komisi Penyiaran Indonesia (KPI).

The Company's compliance with the prevailing laws and regulations is realized by following the development of regulations and implementing them in all aspects of its business. The Company's commitment is realized, by complying with advertising ethics and policies issued by the Ministry of Communication and Information and/or the Indonesian Broadcasting Commission (KPI).

![img-37.jpeg](img-37.jpeg)

Kepatuhan pada regulasi dan etika pariwara membawa SCM menunjukkan dominasi media miliknya dalam hal total audience share dari 3 televisi nasional FTA (SCTV, Indosiar, dan Mentari TV). Berdasarkan survei tingkat kepemirsaan yang dilakukan oleh pihak independen, total kepemirsaan SCM tercatat sebesar 34,9% pada akhir tahun 2023. SCTV mencatatkan pangsa pasar pemirsa 14,3% dan Indosiar meraih pangsa pasar pemirsa 13,0%. Sementara itu, televisi digital milik Perseroan, yaitu Mentari TV (yang menayangkan konten untuk segmen pemirsa anak-anak dan remaja), berkontribusi sebesar hampir 7,6% terhadap total audience share.

Kemudian, platform video streaming Over-the-Top (OTT), Vidio, mempertahankan keunggulannya di industri Over-the-Top (OTT), termasuk dari pemain OTT global dan regional. Pada kuartal III/2023, Vidio berada di posisi pertama dalam hal Monthly Active Users di antara platform OTT lain di Asia Tenggara. Berdasarkan data Media Partner Asia (MPA), Vidio juga berhasil meraih posisi pertama di Indonesia untuk Total Streaming Minutes pada kuartal III/2023 dan menduduki posisi teratas dalam jumlah pelanggan di Indonesia pada kuartal IV/2023.

Selain produksi konten, SCM juga memiliki produk NexParabola yang membantu Perseroan untuk menjangkau pelanggan di daerah pedesaan dan terpencil.

Compliance with regulations and advertising ethics has led SCM to show its media dominance in terms of total audience share of 3 national FTA televisions (SCTV, Indosiar, and Mentari TV). Based on an independent audience survey, SCM's total audience share was recorded at 34.9% by the end of 2023. SCTV recorded an audience share of 14.3% and Indosiar achieved an audience share of 13.0%. Meanwhile, the Company's digital television, Mentari TV (which airs content for the children and youth audience segments), contributed to nearly 7.6% of the total audience share.

Subsequently, the Over-the-Top (OTT) video streaming platform, Vidio, maintained its lead in the Over-the-Top (OTT) industry, including among global and regional OTT players. In Q3/2023, Vidio ranked first in terms of Monthly Active Users among other OTT platforms in Southeast Asia. Based on Media Partner Asia (MPA) data, Vidio also managed to achieve first place in Indonesia for Total Streaming Minutes in Q3/2023 and topped the number of subscribers in Indonesia in Q4/2023.

In addition to content production, SCM also has a product called NexParabola which helps the Company to reach customers in rural and remote areas.
