In this regard, the Company has an obligation to manage harmonious relationships with the community by providing sustainable benefits to the community through the Implementation of a Social Responsibility (CSR) agenda. This agenda is prepared for a one-year period and reported to the Board of Directors as the highest person in charge of the implementation of the Company's sustainability strategy. The Board of Directors will then evaluate the effectiveness of each CSR program to ensure that the community receives optimal benefits from the implementation of the Company's CSR activities.

On the other hand, the implementation of CSR activities is perceived as the Company's effort to demonstrate that its business presence can have a significant positive impact on the surrounding community and environment. The commitment to invest in the community is expected to not only generate substantial support from the community for the sustainability of the Company's business but also to increase the Company's contribution to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

The Company manages its CSR activities through Yayasan Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih (YPP), which is responsible for channeling donations collected from internal and public to people in need. The donation is distributed through Pundi Amal SCTV and Peduli Kasih Indosiar programs, which are managed directly by YPP. The Company also invites employees as well as the Board of Directors and Commissioners to actively participate and voluntarily manage the Company's CSR activities, for example by becoming a board member of YPP.

# Yayasan Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih (YPP)

Yayasan Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih (YPP) merupakan bentuk kerja sama antara SCTV dan Indosiar untuk melaksanakan program tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan. YPP dalam hal ini bertanggung jawab untuk mengelola dan menyalurkan kembali dana sosial yang dikumpulkan dari Pemirsa, serta berbagai bantuan sosial yang diberikan oleh SCTV dan Indosiar, untuk kemudian disalurkan kembali kepada masyarakat yang membutuhkan.

Fokus penyaluran bantuan sosial dan/atau donasi yang dikelola dan dikumpulkan oleh YPP meliputi empat bidang kegiatan, yaitu Bantuan Bencana, Sosial Kesehatan, Pendidikan, dan Lingkungan. Bantuan sosial yang diberikan berupa kegiatan literasi media, beasiswa, donasi perlengkapan sekolah, pembangunan infrastruktur jembatan, bantuan alat instalasi air minum, pemberdayaan usaha mikro, donasi bagi korban bencana, pengobatan masyarakat, penyediaan sanitasi bersih dan lain-lain.

Yayasan Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih (YPP) is a form of cooperation between SCTV and Indosiar to implement corporate social responsibility programs. In this case, YPP is responsible for managing and redistributing social funds collected from viewers, as well as various social assistance provided by SCTV and Indosiar, to be redistributed to communities in need.

The focus of distribution of social assistance and/or donations managed and collected by YPP covers four areas of activity, namely Disaster Relief, Social Health, Education, and Environment. Social assistance is provided in the form of media literacy events, scholarships, donations of school supplies, bridge infrastructure development, drinking water installation equipment, micro business empowerment, donations for disaster victims, community treatment, provision of clean sanitation and others.
