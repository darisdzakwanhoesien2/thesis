## Kebijakan Antikorupsi
### Anti-Corruption Policy

[GRI 205-1] [GRI 205-2] [GRI 404-2] [GRI 2-27] [205-3]

Perseroan menyusun Kebijakan Antikorupsi, Penyuapan, dan Sumbangan Ilegal sebagai bagian dari upaya kepatuhan terhadap hukum dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Kebijakan anti korupsi ini menjadi bagian dari Pedoman Tata Kelola Perseroan dengan ketentuan yang mengatur pengelolaan hubungan antara Perseroan dengan seluruh pemangku kepentingan.

Selain itu, Perseroan telah melakukan evaluasi dan identifikasi atas praktik-praktik yang berisiko membuka celah bagi terjadinya praktik korupsi. Untuk memastikan efektivitas penerapan kebijakan anti korupsi dan anti suap, Perseroan telah menunjuk Komite Audit dan Departemen Corporate Legal untuk mengawasi pelaksanaan kebijakan anti korupsi di lingkungan perusahaan yang hasilnya dilaporkan langsung kepada Direksi. Komite Audit dan Departemen Corporate Legal bekerja sama dengan Audit Internal juga bertanggung jawab juga untuk melakukan prosedur penelaahan risiko, termasuk risiko yang timbul akibat praktik korupsi dan penyuapan, menentukan kategori risiko, serta mengusulkan rekomendasi untuk langkah mitigasinya. [3-3]

Berikut tindakan yang dinilai mengandung risiko untuk terjadinya praktik korupsi, yaitu:

- Tindakan pemberian dan penerimaan segala bentuk penyuapan, dengan tidak ada pengecualian siapapun penerimanya;
- Tindakan pemberian atau penawaran uang, biaya, komisi, kredit, hadiah, benda berharga, atau kompensasi dalam bentuk apapun, langsung ataupun tak langsung, kepada badan pemerintah, pejabat, kontraktor atau subkontraktor untuk memperoleh suatu kontrak atau perlakuan khusus.

The Company's commitment to realizing the goals and objectives of the sustainability strategy implementation is strengthened by cultivating a culture of sustainability to all employees and stakeholders. The culture of sustainability is socialized regularly and supported by a number of policies designed to encourage each of the Company's personnel to apply the sustainability principles seriously in the Company's environment. Some of the activities to build a culture of sustainability are encouraging environmentally friendly behavior by using energy wisely in the office environment and reducing the use of plastic materials for food or beverage containers.

The Company has established an Anti-Corruption, Anti-Bribery, and Anti-Illegal Donation Policies as part of its efforts to comply with prevailing laws and regulations. These policies are part of the Company's Corporate Governance Guidelines which regulates the management of relationships between the Company and all stakeholders.

In addition, the Company has evaluated and identified practices that are at risk of creating loopholes for corrupt practices. To ensure the effectiveness of the implementation of anti-corruption and anti-bribery policies, the Company has appointed the Audit Committee and Corporate Legal Department to oversee the implementation of anti-corruption policies within the company, the results are reported directly to the Board of Directors. The Audit Committee and Corporate Legal Department in collaboration with Internal Audit are also responsible for conducting risk review procedures, including the risks arising from corruption and bribery practices, determining risk categories, and proposing recommendations for mitigation measures. [3-3]

The following actions are deemed to contain risks of corrupt practices:

- The act of giving and receiving any kinds of bribery, regardless of who the recipient is;
- The act of giving or receiving money, fees, commissions, credits, gifts, valuables, or compensation in any forms, whether directly or indirectly, to the government institution, official, contractor or sub-contractor to obtain a contract or special treatment.
