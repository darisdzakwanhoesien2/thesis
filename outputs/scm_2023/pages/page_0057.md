# Pengendalian Emisi
Emission Control
[OJK F.11] [OJK F.12]

Perubahan iklim membawa dampak yang signifikan terhadap kondisi lingkungan di berbagai belahan dunia. Untuk mendukung upaya pengendalian dampak perubahan iklim, Perseroan menunjukkan perhatian dan dukungannya terhadap penerapan peningkatan praktik akuntansi dan pelaporan Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) yang merupakan wujud kepatuhan Standar Protokol Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) berdasarkan Protokol Gas Rumah Kaca (GHGP) dan Standar GRI.

Hingga akhir periode pelaporan, SCM telah mengidentifikasi emisi GRK langsung (cakupan 1) dan emisi GRK tidak langsung (cakupan 2). Di masa datang, Perseroan berupaya untuk melakukan penghitungan atas emisi cakupan 3.

From the above data, the amount of energy usage shows a decrease, which is supported by a number of energy efficiency programs carried out by the Company, including: [OJK F.7]

- The Company appoints a small team for program production activities to reduce the use of operational vehicles;
- Installation of automation system to control air conditioners at several points around the office;
- Replacement of electrical cables using aluminum material with copper which is able to reduce the resistance of electric current, thus reducing the Company's electricity consumption;
- The use of energy-efficient LED lights in production equipment in the studio to reduce electricity consumption;
- Installation of a coordinated elevator system (smart elevator);
- Efforts to minimize the negative impact of energy usage;
- Efforts to encourage energy efficiency and seek alternative renewable energy sources, such as the use of solar power as an alternative energy source for room lighting.

Climate change has a significant impact on environmental conditions in various parts of the world. To support the efforts to control the impact of climate change, the Company shows its attention and support for the implementation of improved Greenhouse Gas (GHG) accounting and reporting practices as a form of compliance with the Greenhouse Gas Protocol (GHGP) (GHGP) and GRI Standards.

By the end of the reporting period, SCM has identified direct GHG emissions (scope 1) and indirect GHG emissions (scope 2). In the future, the Company strives to quantify its scope 3 emissions.
