Company Profile

Pada Anggaran Dasar Perseroan pasal 3 telah dinyatakan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan adalah bergerak dalam bidang jasa (informasi dan aktivitas profesional), perdagangan serta multimedia. Untuk mencapai maksud dan tujuan yang ditetapkan, Perseroan beroperasi dalam tiga bidang usaha utama, yaitu FTA dan DTH (Free-to-Air dan Direct-to-Home); Digital Business dan OOH Platform (Out-of-Home Platform); dan Content, Talent, and Others (Konten, Artis, dan Lainnya). The Article 3 of the Company's Articles of Association stated that the Company's purposes and objectives are to engage in service industry (information and professional activities), trading and multimedia. To realize these purposes and objectives, the Company operates in three main businesses, including FTA and DTH (Free-to-Air and Direct-to-Home); Digital Business and OOH Platform (Out-of-Home Platform); and Content, Talent, and Others.
