# Kode Etik

## Code of Conduct

[GRI 2-23] [GRI 2-24]

Pokok-pokok Kebijakan Standar Perilaku, yang berlaku sejak 18 Mei 2016, menegaskan kewajiban bagi setiap individu di dalam Perseroan dan mengikat seluruh kegiatan usaha Perseroan untuk mematuhi:

1. Semua hukum yang berlaku;
2. Semua peraturan dan ketentuan yang berhubungan dengan kegiatan usaha Perseroan; dan
3. Standar pelaksanaan usaha dan tata kelola yang baik.

Kebijakan ini menegaskan komitmen Perseroan untuk mematuhi norma hukum dan etika dalam menjalankan operasionalnya, menciptakan lingkungan berbisnis yang patuh, dan memastikan praktik tata kelola yang baik. Dengan demikian, setiap insan Perseroan diharapkan berpartisipasi aktif dalam penerapan kode etik ini.

Seluruh pihak di dalam Perseroan memiliki tanggung jawab untuk menciptakan suasana kerja positif dan lingkungan usaha yang mendukung. Oleh karena itu, Perseroan menekankan bahwa Kode Etik Perseroan memiliki sifat mengikat dan berlaku secara merata bagi semua karyawan, tanpa memandang posisi atau tingkatan dalam struktur organisasi, termasuk bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan anggota Direksi Perseroan. Untuk memastikan efektivitas pelaksanaannya, Perseroan mengadakan sosialisasi kode etik pada setiap kesempatan serta dengan memanfaatkan berbagai saluran komunikasi internal. [GRI 3-3]

Violations that can be reported through this mechanism include allegations of corruption and bribery, violent practices, and violations of the Company's code of conduct and the prevailing laws and regulations. This whistleblowing system is expected to create a safe and transparent work environment that has integrity so that the Company can always maintain the trust of the general public in the management of the Company.

In 2023, the Company recorded no reports of corruption, bribery or violations of the code of conduct that could hinder the Company's progress. More information about the implementation of the whistleblowing system can be seen in the 2023 Annual Report which is presented separately from this sustainability report. [GRI 3-3]

The Code of Conduct Policy, which was effective on May 18, 2016, clarifies the obligations of every individual within the Company and binds all of the Company's business activities to comply with:

1. All the prevailing laws;
2. All rules and regulations related to the Company's business activities; and
3. Standards of business conduct and good governance.

This policy affirms the Company's commitment to comply with legal and ethical norms in conducting its operations, creating a compliant business environment, and ensuring good governance practices. As such, every individual in the Company is expected to actively participate in the implementation of this code of conduct.

All parties within the Company are responsible for creating a positive working atmosphere and a supportive business environment. Therefore, the Company emphasizes that the Code of Conduct is binding and applicable equally to all employees, regardless of position or level in the organizational structure, including members of the Board of Commissioners and members of the Board of Directors. To ensure the effectiveness of its implementation, the Company socializes the code of conduct at every opportunity by utilizing various internal communication channels. [GRI 3-3]
