## YPP also distributes donations for disaster victims, including in the form of: (SDG 1, 2, 3, 4, 9)

Disaster relief programs in 2023 were focused on the recovery after the Kanjuruhan tragedy and the rehabilitation phase of the 2022 Cianjur Earthquake disaster.

1. Recovery assistance after the Kanjuruhan tragedy, Malang, and the earthquake in Cianjur, where the Company through YPP collaborated with the Ministry of Social Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia handed over a house for one of the families of victims of the Kanjuruhan Tragedy, as well as five units of Earthquake Resistant Houses for victims of the Cianjur Earthquake that occurred in 2022 throughout several areas around Cianjur.
2. Cooperation between YPP SCTV Indosiar and Yayasan Cinta Anak Bangsa (YCAB) and RANS Entertainment to provide school furniture assistance for four elementary schools in Cianjur affected by the earthquake in 2022.

## Yayasan Indosiar

Kemudian, dalam rangka menjamin ketersediaan tenaga kerja yang kompeten di bidang penyiaran, maka SCM melalui Yayasan Indosiar telah mendirikan Akademi Televisi Indonesia (ATVI) yang merupakan lembaga pendidikan yang telah terakreditasi. Melalui ATVI, SCM turut berkontribusi untuk lulusan-lulusan yang kompeten dan profesional yang dibutuhkan oleh industri penyiaran ke depannya.

In order to ensure the availability of a competent workforce in the broadcasting field, SCM through Indosiar Foundation has established the Indonesian Television Academy (ATVI), an accredited educational institution. Through ATVI, SCM contributes to the competent and professional graduates needed by the broadcasting industry in the future.
