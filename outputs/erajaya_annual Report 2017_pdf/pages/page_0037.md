# Erajaya Jalin Kerjasama Strategis dengan GoPro
Erajaya Engaged in Strategic Partnership with GoPro

Erajaya menjalin kerjasama strategis dengan GoPro, sebagai importir dan distributor resmi GoPro di Indonesia dalam Ajang Pameran Teknopolis 2017. Erajaya akan mendistribusikan produk-produk GoPro melalui jaringan ritel offline dan online Erajaya Group, meliputi Erafone, Erafone.com, iBox, iBox.co.id, dan Urban Republic.

Erajaya engaged strategic partnership with GoPro, as an official importer and distributor of GoPro in Indonesia at the 2017 Teknopolis Exhibition event. Erajaya will distribute GoPro products through offline and online retail networks under Erajaya Group such as Erafone, Erafone.com, iBox, iBox.co.id and Urban Republic.

# Pembukaan Wonder Photo Shop, Gerai Berkonsep Retro Original Fujifilm
Grand Opening of Wonder Photo Shop, An outlet with Fujifilm Original Retro Concept

Fujifilm Indonesia dan Erajaya meresmikan Wonder Photo Shop, sebuah gerai foto dengan konsep retro dan original pertama di Indonesia. Wonder Photo Shop merupakan hasil kerja sama antara Fujifilm Indonesia dengan Origami, salah satu entitas anak dari Erajaya yang memiliki gerai pertamanya di lantai 3 Mall Kota Kasablanka, Jakarta. Selain Jakarta, Wonder Photo Shop juga sudah hadir di kota-kota besar dunia dan berbagai negara, seperti Tokyo, Barcelona, Bogota, Manila, Shanghai, Singapura, Australia, Brazil, Vietnam, Russia, Turkey, Thailand, Jerman, dan Malaysia.

Fujifilm Indonesia and Erajaya inaugurated Wonder Photo Shop, the first photo outlet with retro and original concepts in Indonesia. Wonder Photo Shop is the result of collaboration between Fujifilm Indonesia and Origami, a subsidiary of Erajaya, having its first shop at 3rd floor of Kota Kasablanka Mall, Jakarta. In addition to Jakarta, Wonder Photo Shop is also present in major cities of the world and various countries, such as Tokyo, Barcelona, Bogota, Manila, Shanghai, Singapore, Australia, Brazil, Vietnam, Russia, Turkey, Thailand, Germany, and Malaysia.
