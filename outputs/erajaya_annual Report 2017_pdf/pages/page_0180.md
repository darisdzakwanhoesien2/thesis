# PELATIHAN DAN PENGEMBANGAN KARYAWAN

Learning Solution and Development Erajaya (LSD Erajaya) merupakan sentra pelatihan dan pengembangan Sumber Daya Manusia Erajaya Group yang berpusat di e-DNA (Erajaya Development and Assessment Center) serta berlokasi di Jakarta Barat. Sasaran pelatihan dan pengembangan yang dituju ialah kompetensi semua karyawan baik dari bisnis Ritel, Distribusi, maupun dari divisi support. Kompetensi karyawan yang dimaksud mencakup kompetensi inti, manajerial maupun teknis. Dalam proses pembelajaran yang dilakukan, LSD Erajaya menggunakan kurikulum dan metode pembelajaran yang mendorong karyawan untuk dapat berkembang secara menyeluruh. Untuk mendukung kebutuhan pelatihan dan pengembangan kompetensi karyawan, LSD Erajaya terbagi menjadi 5 bagian utama yaitu Corporate, Retail, Distribution and Operator, Program Development, serta Learning Technology.

# LSD Corporate

Salah satu program unggulan LSD adalah pengembangan untuk para key talent melalui Management Development Program atau lebih dikenal dengan Mini MBA. Program ini melibatkan karyawan terpilih Erajaya yang akan dikembangkan untuk memimpin bisnis di masa depan. Selain itu, Erajaya juga menjalankan program regular seperti Communication Skill, Project Management, Finance for Non Finance, English Course, Leadership Seminar, Basic Development Program (BDP) dan Supervisory Development Program (SDP) yang bertujuan untuk mempersiapkan kompetensi karyawan untuk jenjang yang lebih tinggi baik dari segi kompetensi inti maupun manajerial.

# EMPLOYEE TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

Erajaya Learning Solution and Development (LSD Erajaya) is a Human Capital training and development center which is centralized at e-DNA (Erajaya Development and Assessment Center) located in West Java. The designated training and development goals is competency of all employees in terms of Retail, Distribution or support divisions. The employee competency covers core, managerial and technical competencies. In the implemented learning process, LSD Erajaya applied learning curriculum and method which encouraged employees to have holistic growth. To support employee competency and training needs, LSD Erajaya is divided into 5 main parts, among others, Corporate, Retail, Distribution and Operator, Program Development and Learning Technology.

# LSD Corporate

One of LSD featured program is key talents development in Management Development Program or broadly known as Mini MBA. The program involved selected Erajaya employees to be developed and lead business in the future. In addition, Erajaya also implements regular program such as Communication Skill, Project Management, Finance for Non-Finance, English Course, Leadership Seminar, Basic Development Program (BDP) and Supervisory Development Program (SDP) aiming to prepare competency of the employees for higher level in terms of core and managerial competencies.
