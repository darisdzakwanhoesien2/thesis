# Sim Chee Ping

Direktur

Director

Warga Negara Singapura, umur 49 tahun, berdomisili di Jakarta Barat. Menjabat sebagai Direktur Perseroan sejak bulan Desember 2010 berdasarkan Akta Berita Acara RUPSLB Nomor 34 tanggal 16 Desember 2010. Beliau meraih gelar Sarjana Akuntansi dari National University of Singapore pada tahun 1992.

Memiliki pengalaman sebagai Direktur Konsultan Bisnis di Arthur Andersen dan Direktur Eksekutif PT AZEC Indonesia Management Service.

Saat ini beliau juga menjabat sebagai Wakil Direktur Utama PT Erafone Artha Retailindo, Direktur PT Inovidea Magna Global, Direktur CG Computers Sdn Bhd, Komisaris PT Amtrust Mobile Solution Indonesia, dan Direktur Erafone Retail Malaysia Sdn Bhd.

Singapore Citizen, 49 years, lives in West Jakarta. He is appointed as Director since December 2010 pursuant to EGMS Minutes of Meeting Deed No. 34 dated December 16, 2010. He earned Bachelor Degree of Accounting from National University of Singapore in 1992.

His career includes serving as Director of Business Consulting in Arthur Andersen and was the Executive Director of PT AZEC Indonesia Management Service.

He, currently, also serves as Vice President Director of PT Erafone Artha Retailindo, Director of PT Inovidea Magna Global, Director of CG Computers Sdn Bhd, Commissioner of PT Amtrust Mobile Solution Indonesia, and Director of Erafone Retail Malaysia Sdn Bhd.
