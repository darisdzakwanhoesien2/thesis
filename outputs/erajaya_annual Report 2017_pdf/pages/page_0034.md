# Xiaomi bersama dengan Erajaya, Sat Nusapersada dan TSM Technologies Mulai Memproduksi Perangkat Lokal di Indonesia

Xiaomi Cooperated with Erajaya, Sat Nusapersada and TSM Technologies To Manufacture Local Device in Indonesia

![img-23.jpeg](img-23.jpeg)

![img-24.jpeg](img-24.jpeg)

Xiaomi mulai memproduksi perangkat lokal di Indonesia. Langkah ini sekaligus mempertegas komitmen Xiaomi terhadap peraturan Tingkat Kandungan Dalam Negeri (TKDN).

Untuk memproduksi perangkat ponselnya di Indonesia, Xiaomi bermitra dengan PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk, PT Sat Nusapersada Tbk, dan TSM Technologies. Pabrik berlokasi di Batam, yang merupakan bagian dari segitiga ekonomi Indonesia – Malaysia – Singapura sekaligus zona perdagangan bebas yang juga dikenal sebagai kota industri serta pusat transportasi.

Xiaomi started to manufacture local devices in Indonesia. This initiative has strengthened Xiaomi's commitment to Domestic Content Level (TKDN) Regulation.

To manufacture its mobile device in Indonesia, Xiaomi is partnered with PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk, PT Sat Nusapersada Tbk and TSM Technologies. The factory is located in Batam, which is part of the Indonesia – Malaysia – Singapore economic triangle as well as a free trade zone also known as an industrial city as well as a transportation hub.

# Pembukaan Gerai Erafone Megastore di Summarecon Mall Serpong, Tangerang

Erafone Megastore Outlet Grand Opening at Summarecon Mall Serpong, Tangerang

Erajaya melalui salah satu entitas anaknya yaitu PT Erafone Artha Retailindo meresmikan gerai Erafone Megastore di lantai 2, Summarecon Mall Serpong, Tangerang. Erafone Megastore Summarecon Mall Serpong merupakan gerai Megastore yang ke 26 yang dioperasikan oleh PT Erafone Artha Retailindo.

Hingga akhir tahun 2017, Erajaya telah membuka 24 gerai Erafone Megastore yang tersebar di Indonesia.

Through one of its subsidiaries, namely PT Erafone Artha Retailindo, Erajaya inaugurated Erafone Megastore outlet at 2nd floor of Summarecon Mall Serpong, Tangerang. Erafone Megastore Summarecon Mall Serpong is the 26th Megatore outlets operated by PT Erafone Artha Retailindo.

By the end of 2017, Erajaya has opened 24 Erafone Megastore outlets located across Indonesia region.
