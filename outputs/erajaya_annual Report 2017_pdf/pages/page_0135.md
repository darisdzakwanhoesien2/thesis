Tata cara pembagian Dividen Tunai Perseroan Tahun Buku 2015 adalah sebagai berikut :

1. Dividen Tunai akan dibagikan kepada Pemegang Saham yang namanya tercatat dalam Daftar Pemegang Saham Perseroan ("DPS") atau recording date pada tanggal 21 Juni 2016 dan/atau pemilik saham Perseroan pada sub rekening efek di PT Kustodian Sentral Efek Indonesia ("KSEI") pada penutupan perdagangan tanggal 21 Juni 2016.
2. Bagi Pemegang Saham yang sahamnya dimasukkan dalam penitipan kolektif KSEI, pembayaran dividen tunai dilaksanakan melalui KSEI dan akan didistribusikan ke dalam rekening perusahaan Efek dan/atau Bank Kustodian pada tanggal 1 Juli 2016. Bukti pembayaran dividen tunai Mechanism of Cash Dividend Fiscal Year 2015 Payment is as follows:

1. Cash Dividend will be paid to Shareholders whose names were listed on Company's Shareholders List ("DPS") or recording date on June 21, 2016 and/or shareholders of the Company in securities account at PT Kustodian Sentral Efek Indonesia ("KSEI") at closing of trading on June 21, 2016.
2. Shareholders whose shares were included in KSEI collective deposit, cash dividend payment was carried out through KSEI and will be distributed to Securities company account and/or Custody Bank on July 1, 2016. The cash dividend payment receipt will be submitted by KSEI to Shareholders
