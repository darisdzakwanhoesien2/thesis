Program ini searah dengan tujuan bisnis untuk memperkuat jalur distribusi di beberapa kota se-Indonesia. # LSD Distribution and Operator

Throughout 2017, LSD Distribution and Operator organized 3 batches Area Brand Coordinator Development Program training with total 69 participants. This program is in line with business purpose to strengthen distribution channel in several cities across Indonesia. As a regular program, further, LSD Distribution and Operator also implemented White Belt, Red Belt and Black Belt program targeting sales technical competency improvement.

# Program Development

Unit yang baru terbentuk di tahun 2017 ini telah menghasilkan beberapa program baru seperti Business Simulation, Joint Business Development Program, update White Belt Training, serta New ERO 2.0. Business Simulation merupakan program yang metode trainingnya 80% merupakan latihan dan simulasi kasus. Kurikulumnya berangkat dari tugas dan tanggung jawab seorang Area Head, dan kasus yang digunakan merupakan kasus real yang terjadi di lapangan. New ERO 2.0 merupakan pembaharuan metode dari training New ERO yang tadinya 5 hari di kelas, menjadi hanya 3 hari, namun terdapat proses self-learning dan in-store training. Dengan penggunaan media belajar online ini, karyawan bisa mendapatkan lebih banyak materi baik dari soft-skill maupun pengetahuan tentang produk. Dari hasil evaluasi yang dilakukan, metode ini menunjukkan peningkatan efektivitas dan efisiensi.

# Development Program

The unit which had been just established in 2017 had delivered new programs such as Business Simulation, Joint Business Development Program, Update White Belt Training and New ERO 2.0 Business Simulation as a program whose 80% of its training method were case training and simulation. The curriculum adapted duty and responsibility of an Area Head and the case applied was real case in the field. New ERP 2.0 is a method renewal from New ERO training which previously done as 5 days in-class training into 3 days, although is still equipped with self-learning process and in-store training. By using this online learning media, the employees will acquire more material either soft-skill or product knowledge material. Based on evaluation report, this method indicated effectiveness and efficiency improvement.
