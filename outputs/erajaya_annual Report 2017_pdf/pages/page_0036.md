# Erajaya Sukses Menyelenggarakan Teknopolis 2017 di Jakarta Convention Center, Jakarta

Erajaya Successfully Organize Teknopolis 2017 at Jakarta Convention Center, Jakarta

Teknopolis adalah sebuah ajang pameran teknologi baru di Indonesia, acara ini secara resmi dibuka oleh Menteri Komunikasi dan Informatika RI, Bapak Rudiantara. Teknopolis yang berlangsung dari tanggal 9 Juni 2017 hingga 11 Juni 2017 merupakan hasil kerjasama antara PT. Erafone Artha Retailindo (Entitas Anak Erajaya) dan PT. Traya Eksibisi Internasional. Berlokasi di Hall A Jakarta Convention Center, Teknopolis menggabungkan pameran teknologi, Internet of Things (IoT), wearable devices dan smartphone. Mengusung tema "Today's Technology and Beyond", Teknopolis menghadirkan puluhan inovasi produk dan teknologi dari beragam merek ternama.

Teknopolis is an exhibition of new technology in Indonesia, the event was inaugurated officially by Mr. Rudiantara, Minister of Communication and Information of Republic of Indonesia. Teknopolis which took place from June 9, 2017 until June 11, 2017 is the result of collaboration between PT Erafone Artha Retailindo (subsidiary of Erajaya) and PT Traya Eksibisi Internasional. The event was located at Hall A, Jakarta Convention Center, Teknopolis incorporates technology exhibitions, Internet of Things (IoT), wearable devices and smartphones. Carrying the theme of "Today's Technology and Beyond", Teknopolis presents dozens of product and technology innovations from leading brands.
