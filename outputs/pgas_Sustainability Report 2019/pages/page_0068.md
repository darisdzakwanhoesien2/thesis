## Emission Intensity

The intensity of GHG emissions reveals the amount of GHG emissions per production volume. That is, to produce 1,474.38 MMSCFD of natural gas during 2019, how much emission is discharged into the air. The emission intensity ratio is one of the Company's environmental performance parameters. We try to reduce the amount of emissions discharged into the air in the production process every year through various efforts to reduce emissions.
