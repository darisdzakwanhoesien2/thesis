## 1984
### PERUSAHAAN UMUM GAS NEGARA
The form of business is changed into Public Company (Perum).

## 1994
### PT PERUSAHAAN GAS NEGARA (PERSERO)
The change in the status of the company was accompanied by the addition of a broader scope of business. In addition to the distribution of natural gas, PGN also took part in the transmission field.

## 1965
### PERUSAHAAN GAS NEGARA
On May 13, 1965, pursuant to Government Regulation No. 19 of 1965, PGN was designated as a State Enterprise.

## 2003
### PT PERUSAHAAN GAS NEGARA (PERSERO) TBK
The shares of PGN have been listed in the Jakarta Stock Exchanges and the Surabaya Stock Exchange on December 15, 2003 with the trading transaction code "PGAS".

## 2018
### PT PERUSAHAAN GAS NEGARA TBK
In line with the initiative of SOE Oil and Gas Holding formation, at 11 April 2018 Government transferred 56.96% of the shares series B at PGN to PT Pertamina (Persero) based on the Government Regulation Number 6 Year 2018.

Next as a series of the SOE Oil and Gas Holding formation process, PGN acquired 51% of shares owned by PT Pertamina (Persero) at PT Pertamina Gas on December 28, 2018 and PGN officially carry out the role as Subholding Gas.
