# AWARDS AND CERTIFICATIONS

## AWARDS

In 2019, PGN and Subsidiaries, as well as Affiliate, managed to obtain several awards from national and international with the following details:

![img-192.jpeg](img-192.jpeg)
4 February 2019 (PGN)
Pertamina EP Consumer Awards
PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk obtain award from PT Pertamina EP as The Best Gas Customer 2018 Platinum Award in the evening's event appreciation achievement of lifting oil and gas 2018. The award is received directly by the Commercial Director PGN'S, Danny Parditya in Intercontinental Hotel, Bandung, West Java.

![img-193.jpeg](img-193.jpeg)
27 March 2019 (PGN)
Top IT &amp; TOP Telco Award 2019
Top IT &amp; TOP Telco is the highest award in Indonesia given to companies, government agencies, and IT Telco vendors, who are considered successful in terms of utilizing IT &amp; telco to improve performance, business competitiveness, and services in Indonesia
PGN successfully won the title of Top Digital Innovation Gas Distribution Services from 100 finalists who were good participants from the business community including BUMN, BUMD, central / regional government agencies, and others.

![img-194.jpeg](img-194.jpeg)
11 April 2019 (PGN)
HR Excellence Award 2019
PGN received the HR Excellence Award Charter from the Management Institute of the Faculty of Economics, University of Indonesia, SWA magazine and Bank Mandiri as the best company for creative HR management in several categories on April 10, 2019. PGN won awards in the categories of Learning &amp; Development Strategy, HR Transformation and HR Technology.

![img-195.jpeg](img-195.jpeg)
22 April 2019 (PGN Group)
2019 National K3 Award
In the 2019 National K3 award event organized by the Ministry of Manpower, PGN successfully received a P2HIV award in the Platinum category. In addition, PGN also received Zero Incident Award for PGN Head Office, GDMR III, GTM, and PMO. Several PGN Group companies also received Zero Incident Award obtained by PT PGAS Solution, PT PGAS Telekomunikasi Nusantara, PT Gagas Energi Indonesia, PT Nusantara Regas, and PT Permata Graha Nusantara.

Business News Awards
From around 400 BUMN, BUMD, private company national and governments / institutions assessed in Business News Award in 2019, PGN success obtained two awards namely The Best Growth Strategy in Energy Industry and The Best Human Capital Management in Energy Industry.
