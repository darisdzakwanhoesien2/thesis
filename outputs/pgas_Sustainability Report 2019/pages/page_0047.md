&gt; Natural gas contributes significantly to the national economy as it is used in a variety of large-scale industries, electricity generation, and households. National and global energy development trends have shifted to more environmentally friendly energy to decrease the effects of greenhouse gases. PGN contributes to providing natural gas energy needs that are relatively more environmentally friendly compared to other fossil fuels, such as petroleum and coal.
