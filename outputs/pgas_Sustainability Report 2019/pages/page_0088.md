b. Fostered Village Program

PGN is committed to maintaining the harmony of the community around the PGN Offstake Station area so that it can create superior and competitive communities with the Fostered Village Program, it is hoped that the communities in the village can be competitive and can improve their economy. PGN's Fostered Village include Panaran and Tambesi fostered villages in Batam, Pagardewa fostered villages in Muara Enim, Bojonegara fostered villages, Serang and Labuan Maringgai fostered villages in East Lampung.
