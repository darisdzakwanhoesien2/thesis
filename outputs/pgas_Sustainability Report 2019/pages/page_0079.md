The Social and Environmental Responsibility policy refers to ISO 26000 on Social Responsibility; it is a guide issued by the International Standardisation Organisation (ISO) and covers 7 aspects, namely: [GRI 103-2]

1. Organizational Governance
2. Human Rights
3. Employment (Labor Practices)
4. Environment
5. Fair Operating Procedures
6. Consumer Issues
7. Community Involvement and Development.
