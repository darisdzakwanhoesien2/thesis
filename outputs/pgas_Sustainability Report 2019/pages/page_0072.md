## ENVIRONMENTAL PROGRAM

In accordance with our commitment to be responsible for meeting the needs of present and future generations while maintaining environmental sustainability, we have undertaken excellent Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs as discussed more fully in the CSR section in this Report.

Related to nature conservation efforts, PGN has a greening program that aims to reduce GHGs and preserve ecosystems. PGN planted 70,035 trees in 2019. The trees planted are mangrove, rubber tree, and local plants. The presence of these trees absorbs the concentration of $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ gas in the atmosphere and replaces it with $\mathrm{O}_{2}$ gas which is beneficial for the habitat and the surrounding environment.
