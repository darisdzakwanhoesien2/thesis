## OHS IN COLLECTIVE LABOUR AGREEMENT

A vital linkage between PGN management and employees, the Collective Labour Agreement is an agreement in which the rights and obligations of management and employee are explained for the sake of maintaining harmonious industrial relations within the Company. Articles related to OHS are seen as having a vital role so their presence is needed. The rules regarding OHS are specifically contained in the Collective Labour Agreement, which is drafted jointly between the employee's representatives who are members of the PGN Workers Union (SP-PGN) and PGN management representatives. Around 80,4% of PGN'S employee became a member of PGN Workers Union. [GRI 403-1]
