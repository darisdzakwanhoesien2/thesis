## 8. Health and Safety Reporting

PGN submits routine Health and Safety Reports to the Ministry of Manpower every three months and to the Directorate General of Oil and Gas (Dirjen Migas) of the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources every month.

Such measurements provide feedback on how the OHSMS has been implemented, areas that require repair, and how to further motivate field employees in OHS implementation, also delivering a basis for continuous improvement.
