# Indonesia Green Award (IGA) 2024
oleh La Tofi School of Social Responsibility
Indonesia Green Award (IGA) 2024
by La Tofi School of Social Responsibility

PT Pertamina Gas meraih dua penghargaan di ajang Indonesia Green Award (IGA) 2024.

Penghargaan diraih Pertagas OCSA melalui kategori pengorganisasian tanggung jawab sosial Perusahaan dengan program Tempat Wisata Ramah dan Sadar Lingkungan "Embung Senja" mampu memberikan dampak peningkatan ekonomi masyarakat dan menjadi destinasi wisata Desa Gajah Mati Kabupaten Musi Banyuasin.

PT Pertamina Gas won two awards at the 2024 Indonesia Green Award (IGA) event.

The award was won by Pertagas OCSA through the category of organizing corporate social responsibility with the "Embung Senja" Environmentally Friendly and Conscious Tourism Site program, which was able to have an impact on increasing the community's economy and become a tourist destination in Gajah Mati Village, Musi Banyuasin Regency.
