# World Safety Organization (WSO)
Indonesia-Pakistan Safety Culture Award (WISCA-WPSCA) 2024 oleh World Safety Organization (WSO)
World Safety Organization (WSO) Indonesia-Pakistan Safety Culture Award (WISCA-WPSCA) 2024 by World Safety Organization (WSO)

WSO Indonesia-Pakistan Safety Culture Award (WISCA-WPSCA) merupakan penghargaan yang diberikan pada perusahaan yang menerapkan nilai – nilai Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) yang kuat.

- PT Nusantara Regas meraih kategori bintang 4
- PT PGAS Solution meraih kategori Gold bintang 4
- PT Pertamina Gas meraih kategori Gold

The WSO Indonesia-Pakistan Safety Culture Award (WISCA-WPSCA) is an award given to companies that implement strong Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) values.

- PT Nusantara Regas achieved a 4-star category.
- PT PGAS Solution achieved a Gold 4-star category.
- PT Pertamina Gas achieved a Gold category.
