## Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Tim ESG

Setiap entitas dalam Tim ESG melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab yang disesuaikan dengan fungsi dan keahlian unit masing-masing.

## Duties and Responsibilities of the ESG Team

Each entity within the ESG Team carries out tasks and responsibilities tailored to the functions and expertise of its respective unit.

## Project Sponsor

1. Memberikan arahan strategis dalam implementasi ESG Management sesuai milestone dan target yang telah direncanakan.
2. Memfasilitasi Tim ESG Management dengan Direksi PGN serta fungsi pendukung lainnya.
3. Memonitor pelaksanaan implementasi ESG Management melalui laporan berkala Project Leader.
4. Memberikan arahan terhadap penentuan/pencapaian target dan deliverable dalam implementasi ESG Management.
5. Memberikan support/bantuan strategis terkait pelaksanaan kegiatan Tim ESG Management antara lain kebutuhan resources, bujet dan kebutuhan lain.
6. Melakukan hal-hal lainnya yang diperlukan dalam rangka melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab Project Sponsor.

2. Facilitate the ESG Management Team with the PGN Board of Directors and other supporting functions.
3. Monitor the implementation of ESG Management through periodic reports from the Project Leader.
4. Provide direction on the determination/achievement of targets and deliverables in the implementation of ESG Management.
5. Provide strategic support/assistance related to the implementation of ESG Management Team activities, including resource needs, budgets and other needs.
6. Perform other matters required to carry out the duties and responsibilities of the Project Sponsor.
