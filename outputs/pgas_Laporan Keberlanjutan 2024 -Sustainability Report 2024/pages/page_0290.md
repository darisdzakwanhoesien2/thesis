|  Stream ESG Corporate Strategy and Implementation | 1. Memimpin dan mengkoordinasikan seluruh aktivitas terkait dengan Pemetaan & Metodologi ESG, Integrated ESG Policy & Data Management, Roadmap, Penyelarasan terhadap RJPP & KPI, Visi dan Misi serta Tata Nilai Perusahaan, Organisasi Sustainability (ESG) dan Sistem Tata Kerja.
2. Menyusun study atas baseline dan data collection, preliminary ESG Diagnostic Report, diagnostic assessment, pemetaan ESG serta framework dan metodologi ESG.
3. Menyusun integrated ESG Policy dan ESG Data Management.
4. Menyusun ESG Management Roadmap dan menyelaraskan ESG Management terhadap Rencana Jangka Panjang Perusahaan (RJPP) dan Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Perusahaan.
5. Memonitor target dan pencapaian pelaksanaan Stream ESG Corporate Strategy Implementation antara lain ESG Committee, ESG Policy, Roadmap RJPP, Framework dan KPI ESG.
6. Mensosialisasikan Integrated ESG Policy dan Data Management yang diperlukan ke seluruh Perusahaan.
7. Memberikan konsultasi dalam penyusunan Sistem Tata Kerja terkait ESG Management.
8. Melakukan hal-hal lainnya yang ditugaskan oleh Stream Leader ESG Corporate Strategy and Implementation dan Project Sponsor serta yang diperlukan dalam rangka melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab Stream ESG Corporate Strategy and Implementation.
9. Melaporkan hasil pekerjaan Stream ESG Corporate Strategy & Implementation kepada Project Leader
1. Lead and coordinate all activities related to ESG Mapping & Methodology, Integrated ESG Policy & Data Management, Roadmap, Alignment with RJPP & KPI, Vision and Mission as well as Company Values, Organizational Sustainability (ESG) and Work System.
2. Prepare baseline studies and data collection, preliminary ESG Diagnostic Report, diagnostic assessment, ESG mapping and ESG framework and methodology.
3. Develop integrated ESG Policy and ESG Data Management.
4. Prepare an ESG Management Roadmap and align ESG Management with the Company's Long Term Plan (RJPP) and the Company's Key Performance Indicators (KPI).
5. Monitor the targets and achievements of the ESG Corporate Strategy Implementation Stream, including the ESG Committee, ESG Policy, RJPP Roadmap, Framework and ESG KPIs.
6. Socialize the necessary Integrated ESG Policy and Data Management throughout the Company.
7. Provide consultation in preparing the Work Procedure System related to ESG Management.
8. Carry out other things assigned by the ESG Corporate Strategy and Implementation Stream Leader and Project Sponsor and as necessary to carry out the duties and responsibilities of the ESG Corporate Strategy and Implementation Stream.
9. Report the results of the ESG Corporate Strategy & Implementation Stream work to the Project Leader  |
