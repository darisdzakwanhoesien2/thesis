MATERNITY LEAVE [GRI 401-3]

Based on the PGN’s Collective Labor Agreement for 2019-2021, Article 33 regulates the provisions of Permit to Leave Work in paragraph 2, workers get permission for following leaves but still receive Basic Wages for maternity as follows:
- Female workers are entitled for 1.5 (one and a half) months leave before maternity date, as predicted by doctor / midwife and 2.5 (two and a half) months after giving birth.
