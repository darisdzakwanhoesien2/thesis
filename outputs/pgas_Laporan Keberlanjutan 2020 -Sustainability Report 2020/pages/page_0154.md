## TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN
Sustainability Governance

Tata kelola perusahaan yang baik tidak sekadar slogan, namun benar-benar dijalankan. Tak hanya satu, PGN melakukan assessment berdasarkan tiga standar sekaligus untuk menjaga tata kelola diterapkan secara baik dan tepat.

Good Corporate Governance is not only a slogan, but should be actually carried out. Not only one. PGN conducts its assessments based on three standards at once so as to maintain good and proper governance.
