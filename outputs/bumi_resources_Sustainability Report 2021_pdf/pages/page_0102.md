# PROGRAM DAN PELATIHAN K3 (403-5) (403-7)

Kegiatan pertambangan memiliki risiko terhadap keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja dari setiap karyawannya, oleh karenanya Kami telah mengidentifikasi tingkat risiko dari setiap pekerjaan dan menyusun program keselamatan pertambangan yang sesuai dengan kaidah pertambangan yang baik sebagai langkah pencegahan dan mitigasi dampak.

Unit usaha telah menyusun program Keselamatan Pertambangan dan Lingkungan selama tahun 2021 sebagai bagian dari proses mitigasi dampak. Biaya yang telah dikeluarkan oleh masing-masing unit usaha sebesar USD 67,917 untuk CPM, USD 60,725 untuk DPM dan USD 20,177 untuk GM.

# OHS PROGRAM AND TRAINING

Mining activities have risks to the occupational safety and health of each of its employees. Therefore, we have identified the level of risk from each job and developed a mining safety program that is in accordance with good mining principles as a preventive and impact mitigation measure.

The business unit has developed a Mining and Environmental Safety program during 2021 as part of the impact mitigation process. The costs incurred by each business unit were USD 67,917 for CPM, USD 60,725 for DPM and USD 20,177 for GM.

The mining safety program is carried out independently by each business unit or in collaboration with a competent and certified third party. The implementation of the mining safety program that has been prepared is presented as follows:
