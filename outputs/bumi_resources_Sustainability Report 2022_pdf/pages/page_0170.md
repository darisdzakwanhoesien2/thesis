# Strategi Pengelolaan Lingkungan 2022 (2-23)

Untuk tahun 2022, BRMS dan unit usaha memfokuskan kinerja lingkungan untuk mencegah pencemaran baik di udara, air, maupun darat. Dalam rangka mewujudkan komitmen dan tanggung jawab terhadap lingkungan, di tahun 2022 Kami telah menyusun beberapa program kerja sebagai berikut:

1. Penerapan prinsip-prinsip sistem manajemen lingkungan sesuai dengan standar internasional ISO 14001.
2. Konsumsi energi.
3. Pengendalian perubahan iklim melalui inventarisasi gas rumah kaca (GRK).
4. Pengelolaan limbah.
5. Pengelolaan sumber daya air.
6. Pelestarian keanekaragaman hayati.

# KOMITMEN LINGKUNGAN DALAM RANTAI PASOKAN (308-1)

Rantai pasok Kami mempunyai peran yang sama kuatnya untuk mewujudkan keberlanjutan Perusahaan dan masyarakat setempat. Dengan adanya pengadaan barang dan jasa dari lingkungan setempat, Kami berperan untuk meningkatkan pertumbuhan ekonomi di daerah tersebut. Namun demikian, hal ini tentunya tidak mengurangi komitmen Kami untuk menerapkan standar pengadaan barang dan jasa yang bertanggung jawab serta menghormati HAM dan kelestarian lingkungan.

Sebelum melakukan kerja sama, setiap pemasok atau mitra kerja akan melalui proses uji tuntas untuk melihat kinerja bisnis dan profil risiko. # Strategy of Environmental Management 2022

In 2022, BRMS and business units focused on environmental performance to prevent air, water, and land pollution. In order to realize our commitment and responsibility towards the environment, all over 2022 we have developed several work programs as follows:

1. Implementation principles of the environmental management system in accordance with the international standard ISO 14001.
2. Energy consumption.
3. Control of climate change through greenhouse gas (GHG) inventories.
4. Waste management.
5. Menagament of water resources.
6. Conservation of biodiversity.

# Environmental Commitment In The Supply Chain

Our supply chain plays a strong role in realizing the sustainability of the Company and local communities. By procuring goods and services from the local environment, we contribute in increasing economic growth in the area. However, this certainly does not reduce our commitment to implementing responsible, respect for human rights and environmental sustainability standards for the procurement of goods and services.

Before entering into business relations, each supplier or business partner will go through a due diligence process to see business performance and risk profile. Here, we look at the track record of potential suppliers or business partners in the aspects of occupational health and safety, environmental management and fulfillment of labor responsibilities. We have included environmental protection provisions in all (100%) business agreements with contractors, mining equipment suppliers, consultants and other business partners.
