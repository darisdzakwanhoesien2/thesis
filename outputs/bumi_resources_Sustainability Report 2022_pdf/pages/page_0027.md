In practice, we continue to ensure that every Company activity complies with all applicable government regulations, fulfills the rights of all our workforce, conforms to operational permits, make mitigation plans concerning impacts on the environment, and ensure the implementation of good mining practices.

In relation to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), we deem that the SDGs will be achieved if all parties understand the value of sustainability. Thus, we are committed to continuing to socialize and internalize the sustainability values as set out in BRMS Sustainability Policy version 1.0, the Code of Conduct, environmental policies, and other policies that support sustainability.

To amplify the impact and reinforce the development of a strong national economy, BRMS strives to support the implementation of Presidential Regulation Number 59 of 2017 concerning Implementation of the Achievement of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by making the regulation one of the considerations in preparing the Company's sustainability strategy.
