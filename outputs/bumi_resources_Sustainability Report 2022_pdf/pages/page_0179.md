Kegiatan operasional dan domestik Perusahaan menghasilan limbah baik padat maupun cair dan baik yang berkategori berbahaya dan beracun (B3) maupun non-B3. # Waste Management

The Company's operational and domestic activities generate both solid and liquid waste as well as hazardous and toxic (B3) and non-B3 categories. Generally, operational waste is generated from unit maintenance and small construction work in the project area, while domestic waste is generated from office activities, domestic, canteens, and plant waste.
