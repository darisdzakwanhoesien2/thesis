Program Keselamatan Pertambangan GM Tahun 2022
GM Mining Safety Program 2022

|  Program | Kegiatan | Activity  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Keselamatan Kerja Pertambangan Mining Work Safety | 1. Mempersiapkan penilaian risiko K3L (HSE risk assessment).
2. Mempersiapkan Job Safety Environment Analysis (JSEA).
3. Menyediakan dan melakukan pemeliharaan Alat Pelindung Diri (APD).
4. Melakukan pertemuan K3 (OHS meeting).
5. Melakukan inspeksi.
6. Melakukan kampanye (bulan K3).
7. Melakukan pemasangan rambu dan tanda keselamatan.
8. Mempersiapkan dan melaporkan kinerja K3 (OHS IPR).
9. Melakukan simulasi tanggap darurat.
10. Mempersiapkan program pencegahan kecelakaan dan investigasinya. | 1. Prepare an HSE risk assessment (HSE risk assessment).
2. Prepare Job Safety Environment Analysis (JSEA).
3. Provide and maintain Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).
4. Conduct OHS meeting (OHS meeting).
5. Conduct inspections.
6. Conducting campaigns (K3 month).
7. Installing safety signs and signs.
8. Prepare and report on OHS performance (OHS IPR).
9. Conduct emergency response simulations.
10. Prepare an accident prevention program and its investigation.  |
|  Kesehatan Kerja Pertambangan Mining Occupational Health | 1. Melakukan pemeriksaan kesehatan
2. Melakukan diagnosis atas Penyakit Akibat Kerja (PAK).
3. Menyediakan obat-obatan untuk tindakan P3K.
4. Mengaktifkan kembali klinik. | 1. Conduct health checks
2. Diagnosing Occupational Diseases (PAK).
3. Providing medicines for first aid measures.
4. Reactivate the clinic.  |
|  Lingkungan Kerja Pertambangan Mining Work Environment | 1. Melakukan pengukuran dan pemantauan di lingkungan kerja | 1. Carry out measurement and monitoring in the work environment  |
|  Keselamatan Operasional (KO) Pertambangan | 1. Melakukan perawatan dan perbaikan infrastruktur, instalasi dan peralatan pertambangan.
2. Melakukan kompetensi tenaga teknik.
3. Melakukan dan mengevaluasi kajian teknis pertambangan. | 1. Carry out maintenance and repair of mining infrastructure, installations and equipment.
2. Carry out the competence of technical personnel.
3. Conduct and evaluate mining technical studies.  |
|  Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan (SMKP) | 1. Develop SMKP in accordance with Appendix IV to Decree of the Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources of the Republic of Indonesia No: 1827K/30/MEM/2018 Guidelines for Implementation of Mineral and Coal Mining Safety Management Systems  |
