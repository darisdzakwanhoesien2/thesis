# Business Opportunities and Prospects

We are optimistic that our business has good prospects amidst inflationary turmoil and uncertainty at the global level. This is because the product we produce, gold, is considered a safe haven investment and has a relatively stable price and even increases from time to time.

In addition, the global decarbonization agenda and energy transition are also opportunities for us. BRMS has copper and lead projects undertaken by our business units, namely Gorontalo Minerals and Dairi Prima Mineral, respectively. Copper and lead are important components in batteries for electric cars that are environmentally friendly. Electric cars have gained momentum to become a solution to reduce emissions and mitigate climate change. Indeed, BRMS also helps achieve the target of the Indonesian government in the latest Enhanced Nationally Determined Contribution (ENDC) in 2022 to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 31.89% independently and by 43.20% with international support.

Seeing bright prospects ahead, we set 2022 as the start of a new era for BRMS to realize our dream of becoming one of the best mineral mining companies in Asia. To realize this goal, we have set short, medium and long term strategies and targets for BRMS. Our short-term strategy is to immediately optimize the first and second gold factories in Palu, which have a total production capacity of 4,500 tons of gold ore per day. Then, in 2024 we plan to complete the construction of a third gold processing plant in Palu with a production capacity of 4,000 tons of gold ore per day and a fourth plant in Gorontalo with a capacity of 2,000 tons of gold ore per day. We are grateful that we managed to secure funding for our short term project.
