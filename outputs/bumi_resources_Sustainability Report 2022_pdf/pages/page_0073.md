# Evaluasi Kinerja Badan Tata Kelola Tertinggi (2-18)

Kinerja anggota Direksi dievaluasi oleh Dewan Komisaris dengan menggunakan mekanisme penilaian yang telah disusun oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Hasil penilaian kinerja Direksi kemudian disampaikan kepada RUPS dalam Laporan Tugas Pengawasan Dewan Komisaris.

Dewan Komisaris juga menjalani penilaian dalam bentuk self-assessment dan peer-to-peer assessment mengacu pada kriteria penilaian kinerja yang disusun oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Selanjutnya, hasil penilaian Dewan Komisaris diungkapkan di dalam Laporan Tugas Pengawasan Dewan Komisaris yang disampaikan kepada RUPS.

Hasil penilaian kinerja Dewan Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris akan menjadi dasar dan pertimbangan RUPS untu memberikan atau tidak memberikan persetujuan dan/atau pengesahan atas Laporan Tahunan Perseroan, Laporan Keuangan Konsolidasian Perseroan, dan Laporan Tugas Pengawasan Dewan Komisaris.

# Mencegah Benturan Kepentingan (2-15)

BRMS terus menerapkan prinsip-prinsip dasar GCG yang salah satunya berkaitan dengan independensi. Artinya, setiap departemen, Direksi, dan Dewan Komisaris melakukan fungsi dan tanggung jawab sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar dan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dan dikelola secara profesional tanpa adanya benturan kepentingan.

Dalam badan tata kelola tertinggi, masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dilarang untuk melakukan transaksi yang memiliki potensi benturan kepentingan dan potensi untuk mengambil keuntungan pribadi.

Berdasarkan hasil audit yang telah dilakukan pada tahun 2022, Kami bangga untuk melaporkan bahwa tidak ada transaksi yang mengandung benturan kepentingan.

# Evaluation of the Performance of the Highest Governance Body

The performance of members of the Board of Directors is evaluated by the Board of Commissioners using the evaluation mechanism that has been prepared by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The results of the Board of Directors' performance evaluation are then submitted to the GMS in the Board of Commissioners' Supervisory Task Report.

The Board of Commissioners also undergoes assessment in the form of self-assessment and peer-to-peer assessment referring to the performance criteria prepared by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. Furthermore, the results of the Board of Commissioners' assessment are disclosed in the Board of Commissioners' Supervisory Task Report which is submitted to the GMS.

The results of the performance evaluation of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners will become the basis and consideration of the GMS to give or not give approval and/or ratification of the Company's Annual Report, the Company's Consolidated Financial Statements, and the Board of Commissioners' Supervisory Task Report.

# Preventing Conflict of Interest

BRMS continues to apply the basic principles of GCG, one of which is the principle of independence. This means that each department, the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners carry out their functions and responsibilities in accordance with the Articles of Association and applicable laws and are managed professionally without any conflict of interest.

In the highest governance body, each member of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is prohibited from engaging in transactions that have a potential conflict of interest and potential for personal gain.

Based on the audit results that were carried out in 2022, we are proud to report that there were no transactions that contained a conflict of interest.
