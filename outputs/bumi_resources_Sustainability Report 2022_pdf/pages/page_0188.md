In addition, dry tailings stored in the Final Stockpiling facility will be utilized in the future after obtaining a B3 Waste Utilization Permit from the Ministry of Environment and Forestry. It is planned that PT CPM will utilize dry tailings in the form of stockpiling materials, brick making, building materials and planting media.
