PT GM dan PT CPM telah memenuhi standar keberhasilan reklamasi dari Kementerian ESDM, Adapun ketidaktercapaian luasan reklamasi karena terdapat gangguan dari pelaku PETI dan masih proses evaluasi kegiatan explorasi dan penggunaan infrastruktur untuk kegiatan penambangan. PT GM menempatkan jaminan reklamasi-nya dalam bentuk Deposito Berjangka sehingga berhak melakukan pencairan dana Jaminan Reklamasi sebesar Rp. 10.244.627.438 dari total jaminan reklamasi yang dijaminkan oleh PT GM sebesar Rp. 14.714.930.670 sejak tahun 2019 sampai 2021.

PT GM and PT CPM have met the reclamation success standards from the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources. While it is laudable, there is still challenge in fully achieving the target of reclamation success due to illegal mining activities (PETI) which has been included during the exploration evaluation and the use of infrastructure for mining activities. PT GM places its reclamation guarantee in the form of a Time Deposit so that it has the right to withdraw the Reclamation Guarantee fund amounted to Rp. 10,244,627,438 of the total reclamation guarantee guaranteed by PT GM of Rp. 14,714,930,670 from 2019 to 2021.
