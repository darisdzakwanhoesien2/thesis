# REKRUTMEN DAN TINGKAT PERPUTARAN KARYAWAN (401-1)

Guna merekrut talenta terbaik, proses rekrutmen dan seleksi dilakukan secara profesional dan menerapkan prinsip kesetaraan dengan memberikan kesempatan yang sama kepada calon karyawan tanpa membedakan suku, agama, ras, jenis kelamin, dan golongan. Lebih jauh, proses rekrutmen juga menekankan pada aspek meritokrasi, adil, terbuka, dan berdasarkan kualifikasi yang dibutuhkan oleh divisi terkait dan sesuai dengan tahapan operasional pertambangan. # Recruitment And Employee Turnover Rate

In order to recruit the best talent, the recruitment and selection process is carried out in a professional manner and applies the principle of equality by providing equal opportunities to prospective employees regardless of ethnicity, religion, race, gender and class. Furthermore, the recruitment process also emphasizes meritocratic aspects, is fair, open, and is based on the qualifications required by the relevant divisions and in accordance with the stages of mining operations.
