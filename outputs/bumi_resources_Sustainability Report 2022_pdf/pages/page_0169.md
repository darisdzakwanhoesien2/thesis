## Manajemen Dampak

Untuk mengelola dan memastikan kelestarian lingkungan, Perusahaan telah membentuk divisi yang bertanggung jawab terhadap pelaksanaan dan monitoring program-program lingkungan hidup yaitu Divisi Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE). Divisi HSE berisikan personel-personel yang memiliki kompetensi dan kepedulian terkait lingkungan menjadi garda terdepan Kami dalam memastikan ketercapaian target pengelolaan lingkungan. Di samping itu, manajemen lingkungan telah mengadopsi standar internasional ISO 14001:2015 – Sistem Manajemen Lingkungan.

Moreover, our operational activities comply with various applicable laws and regulations, including:

1. Law of the Republic of Indonesia No. 30 of 2007 concerning Energy
2. Government Regulation No. 70 of 2009 concerning Energy Conservation
3. Law No. 11 of 2020 concerning Job Creation
4. Government Regulation No. 22 of 2021 concerning the Implementation of Environmental Protection and Management
5. Regulation of the Minister of Environment and Forestry of the Republic of Indonesia No. P.92/MENLHK/SETJEN/KUM.1/8/2018 Concerning Amendments to the Regulation of the Minister of Environment and Forestry No. P.20/MENLHK/SETJEN/KUM.1/6/2018 Concerning Protected Plants and Animals
6. Regulation of the Minister of Environment and Forestry of the Republic of Indonesia No. 3 of 2021 concerning Standards for Business Activities in the Implementation of Risk-Based Business Licensing in the Environment and Forestry Sector
7. Regulation of the Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources No. 26/2018 concerning Implementation of Good Mining Principles and Supervision of Mineral and Coal Mining.

## Management of Impact

To manage and ensure environmental sustainability, the Company has established a Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) Division responsible for implementing and monitoring environmental programs. The HSE Division consists of personnel who are competent and concerned about the environment and are our frontline in safeguarding the achievement of environmental management targets. In addition, environmental management has adopted international standard ISO 14001:2015 – Environmental Management System.
