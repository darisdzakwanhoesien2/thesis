# ANTIKORUPSI DAN SISTEM PELAPORAN PELANGGARAN (2-26) (205-1) (205-2) (205-3)

Sebagai salah satu perusahaan global, Kami berkomitmen untuk melakukan kegiatan bisnis secara beretika dan transparan. Praktik korupsi, penyuapan, dan praktik buruk lainnya tidak hanya merusak citra perusahaan namun juga mengancam ketercapaian target-target kegiatan Perusahaan, Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan, serta pemenuhan hak asasi manusia untuk para pemangku kepentingan. Meskipun Perseroan belum memiliki kebijakan antikorupsi tersendiri, tetapi komitmen untuk tidak menolerir perilaku fraud dan tindakan korupsi secara jelas dituangkan di dalam Peraturan Perusahaan dan Kode Etik yang harus dipatuhi oleh seluruh insan Perseroan. Lebih lanjut, setiap karyawan akan mendapatkan buku Pedoman Etika dan Perilaku serta Peraturan Perusahaan. (205-2)

# Anti-Corruption And Violation Reporting System

As a global company, we are committed to conducting business activities in an ethical and transparent manner. Corruption, bribery and other bad practices not only damage the company's image but also threaten the achievement of the Company's activity targets, the Sustainable Development Goals, and the fulfillment of human rights for stakeholders. Even though the Company does not yet have a separate anti-corruption policy, the commitment to not tolerate fraud and acts of corruption is clearly stated in the Company Regulations and Code of Ethics which must be obeyed by all Company personnel. Anti-corruption provisions also apply to third parties who have a working relationship with the Company through the provisions in the cooperation agreement. Every year we always conduct refresher training related to Company Regulations and Code of Ethics for old employees and induction training for new employees. Furthermore, each employee will receive a book of Ethics and Conduct and Company Regulations.

As part of efforts to prevent and overcome crime in the Company and its subsidiaries, the Company has established a reporting procedure for alleged violations of the Code of Conduct and an investigation process for internal and external fraud. This procedure is expected to become a reference standard for the prevention and control of crime in the Company and its subsidiaries, and can be implemented by all employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.
