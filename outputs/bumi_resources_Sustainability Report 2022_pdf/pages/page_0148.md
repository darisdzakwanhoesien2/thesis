# OHS Risks Identification

Each subsidiary has carried out a Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (IBPR) and an Environmental Impact Identification &amp; Assessment (IPDL) and updated them according to the stages of activity, updating laws and regulations and incident history. On 5 October 2022, IBPR and IPDL in-persons training was carried out thoroughly for BRM and subsidiaries at PT CPM – Palu City and virtually using the zoom meeting application. The procedure governing the IBPR process at BRM is HSE.01.02 – HSEC Risk Management, at PT CPM with procedure number HSE3-PR-2.02 on Hazard Identification &amp; Risk Assessment (IBPR) and Environmental Impact Identification &amp; Assessment (IPDL), at PT GM with procedure number GM-HSE-PR-002 – Hazard Identification and Risk Management, and at PT DPM with procedure number DPM-HSE-SAFT -SOP-080 HIRDC.
