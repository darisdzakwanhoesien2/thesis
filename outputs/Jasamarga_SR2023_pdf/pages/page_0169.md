# Intensitas Energi [F.6]

Dalam beberapa tahun terakhir, konsumsi energi di lingkup Jasa Marga mengalami fluktuasi sejalan dengan restrukturisasi organisasi dan peningkatan kapasitas layanan. Pada periode pelaporan, Perseroan belum menetapkan target, dasar pengukuran dan melakukan audit energi, sehingga penghitung intensitas energi dilakukan secara manual.

Di bawah ini disajikan intensitas energi dengan membandingkan volume pemakaian daya energi listrik (kWh) di kantor pusat dengan luas bangunan (m²). Dengan rumus tersebut diperoleh Intensitas Konsumsi Energi (IKE) bangunan gedung Kantor Pusat Jasa Marga sebagai berikut.

# Energy Intensity [F.6]

In recent years, energy consumption within Jasa Marga has fluctuated in line with organizational restructuring and increased service capacity. In the reporting period, the Company had not set target, measurement basis and conducted energy audit, so energy intensity calculations were carried out manually.

Below is presented the energy intensity by comparing the volume of electrical energy usage (kWh) at the head office with the building area (m²). Using this formula, the Energy Consumption Intensity (IKE) of the Jasa Marga Head Office building is obtained as follows.
