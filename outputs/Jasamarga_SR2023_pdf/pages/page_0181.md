Di tahun 2023 Jasa Marga telah melakukan usaha konservasi keanekaragaman hayati berupa penanaman 14.297 pohon dari berbagai jenis di sisi ruas jalan tol yang dimilikinya. Proses penanaman pohon melibatkan unit kerja Kantor Pusat, Kantor Regional dan RO, entitas anak dan penyedia jasa.

In 2023, Jasa Marga committed to biodiversity conservation by planting 14,297 trees of various species along its toll road sections. The tree planting process included representatives from the Head Office, Regional Offices, subsidiaries, and service providers.
