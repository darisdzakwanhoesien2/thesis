In 2023, AMI calculated Scope 1 emissions using the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) 2006 methodology with the following scope: fuel combustion, refrigerants and fire suppressants, waste incineration, and wastewater treatment plant (WWTP). Meanwhile, AMI uses generators thus does not generate scope 2 emissions. The calculation of Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions is presented in the following table: [F.11] [305-1]

Pada tahun 2023, AMI telah menghitung emisi Cakupan 1 dengan menggunakan metodologi Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) 2006 dengan cakupan: pembakaran bahan bakar, pendinginan dan APAR, pembakaran limbah, dan fasilitas pengolahan limbah cair (WWTP). Sementara itu, AMI menggunakan genset sehingga tidak menghasilkan scope 2. Perhitungan emisi Scope 1 dan Scope 2 disajikan pada tabel berikut: [F.11] [305-1]
