# Milestone [GRI 2-6]
# Jejak Langkah

## 2007
The Company was established under the name PT Jasapower Indonesia.
Perusahaan didirikan dengan nama PT Jasapower Indonesia

## 2010
PT Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk acquired a 25% ownership stake in PT Lahai Coal, PT Maruwai Coal, PT Sumber Barito Coal, PT Kalteng Coal, and PT Juloi Coal.
PT Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk mengakuisisi 25% kepemilikan di PT Lahai Coal, PT Maruwai Coal, PT Sumber Barito Coal, PT Kalteng Coal, dan PT Juloi Coal.

## 2015
Commenced production of high-quality semi-soft coking coal (SSCC) known as Haju through PT Lahai Coal.
Memulai produksi semi soft coking coal (SSCC) berkualitas tinggi yang dikenal dengan nama Haju, melalui PT Lahai Coal.

## 2016
PT Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk acquired the remaining ownership stakes in PT Lahai Coal, PT Maruwai Coal, PT Sumber Barito Coal, PT Kalteng Coal, and PT Juloi Coal.
PT Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk mengakuisisi sisa kepemilikan di PT Lahai Coal, PT Maruwai Coal, PT Sumber Barito Coal, PT Kalteng Coal, dan PT Juloi Coal.

## 2019
Commenced production of hard coking coal (HCC) known as Lampunut Hard Coking Coal and Lampunut Green Coal through PT Maruwai Coal.
Memulai produksi hard coking coal (HCC) yang dikenal dengan nama Lampunut Hard Coking Coal dan Lampunut Green Coal, melalui PT Maruwai Coal.

## 2020
First shipment of Lampunut Hard Coking Coal in May 2020.
Pengiriman pertama Lampunut Hard Coking Coal pada bulan Mei 2020.

## 2021
- PT Jasapower Indonesia changed its name to PT Adaro Minerals Indonesia Tbk.
PT Jasapower Indonesia mengubah nama menjadi PT Adaro Minerals Indonesia Tbk
- Through PT Alam Tri Daya Indonesia, the Company completed the acquisition of a 99% ownership stake in PT Lahai Coal, PT Maruwai Coal, PT Sumber Barito Coal, PT Kalteng Coal, and PT Juloi Coal from PT Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk.
Melalui PT Alam Tri Daya Indonesia, Perusahaan menyelesaikan akuisisi 99% kepemilikan PT Lahai Coal, PT Maruwai Coal, PT Sumber Barito Coal, PT Kalteng Coal, dan PT Juloi Coal dari PT Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk.

## 2022
- PT Adaro Minerals Indonesia Tbk was officially listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange on January 3, 2022.
PT Adaro Minerals Indonesia Tbk resmi tercatat perdagangannya di Bursa Efek Indonesia pada tanggal 3 Januari 2022.
- AMI's parent company, PT Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk, streamlined its businesses into three growth pillars: Adaro Energy, Adaro Minerals, and Adaro Green. As a result, the company leads the Adaro Minerals pillar by managing and coordinating all Adaro Group businesses related to mineral products.
Perusahaan induk AMI, PT Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk merampingkan bisnis-bisnisnya ke dalam tiga pilar pertumbuhan, yaitu Adaro Energy, Adaro Minerals, dan Adaro Green. Dengan ini, Perusahaan memimpin pilar Adaro Minerals dengan mengelola dan mengoordinasikan seluruh bisnis Grup Adaro yang berhubungan dengan produk mineral.
- The Company established PT Adaro Baterai Indonesia (ABI), which later took over PT Adaro Indo Aluminium.
Perusahaan mendirikan PT Adaro Baterai Indonesia yang kemudian mengambil alih PT Adaro Indo Aluminium.

## 2023
- AMI subscribed new shares issuance of PT Alam Tri Cakra Indonesia (ATCI), resulting in AMI owning 90.84% of ATCI shares.
AMI mengambil bagian pada penerbitan saham-saham baru PT Alam Tri Cakra Indonesia (ATCI), sehingga AMI memiliki 90,84% saham ATCI.
- In second quarter of 2023, PT Kalimantan Aluminium Industry secured financing for its 500,000 tpa aluminium smelter.
Pada kuartal kedua tahun 2023, PT Kalimantan Aluminium Industry memperoleh pendanaan untuk smelter aluminiumnya yang memiliki kapasitas 500.000 tpa.

Laporan Keberlanjutan 2023 PT Adaro Minerals Indonesia Tbk
