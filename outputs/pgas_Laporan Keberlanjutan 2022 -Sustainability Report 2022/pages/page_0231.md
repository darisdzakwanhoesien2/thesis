# BENTURAN KEPENTINGAN DAN PENGENDALIAN GRATIFIKASI

Benturan kepentingan adalah situasi di mana seseorang diduga memiliki kepentingan pribadi yang dapat mempengaruhi penggunaan wewenang yang dimilikinya. Untuk mencegah terjadinya benturan kepentingan dan sekaligus pengendalian gratifikasi, PGN telah menetapkan Kebijakan Benturan Kepentingan dan Pengendalian Gratifikasi sesuai Keputusan Direksi No. 018401.K/OT.03/UT/2014 tanggal 1 Oktober 2014 yang telah diperbarui menjadi Pedoman Benturan Kepentingan dan Pengendalian Gratifikasi No. P-004/0.11 dan ditetapkan oleh Direktur Utama pada tanggal 15 September 2021. Kebijakan ini merupakan dasar implementasi pengendalian gratifikasi untuk mendorong pelaksanaan etika usaha dan etika kerja, pencegah benturan kepentingan serta pencegah kecurangan.

Pengelolaan Gratifikasi berada di Internal Audit Group, di mana terdapat Tim Unit Pengendalian Gratifikasi (UPG). UPG akan menyampaikan laporan rekapitulasi penanganan atas pelaporan penerimaan gratifikasi yang dikelola UPG baik yang dalam penanganan maupun tindak lanjut penetapan kepemilikan dan pemanfaatan gratifikasi kepada KPK secara berkala setiap bulan. Selain itu, UPG menyampaikan laporan perkembangan dan evaluasi hasil implementasi Program Pengendalian Gratifikasi di lingkungan perusahaan kepada Direksi secara berkala setiap bulan.

The Company ensures that each function routinely carries out risk identification related to anti-bribery and anti-corruption. Afterward, a review of the implementation of anti-corruption and anti-bribery was carried out, especially related to FKAP reviews, due diligence in the procurement and payment process, evaluation of the implementation of the RKAP and also the implementation of the WBS. As part of the internal control system, once a year an audit process of ABMS implementation is also carried out by the internal auditors.

# BRIBERY AND CORRUPTION REPORTING MECHANISM

Any violation of business and work ethics can be reported through the Company's reporting channel/whistleblowing system at www.wbs.pgn.co.id. In addition, each employee can also report directly to their direct supervisor or through the Ethics Committee. During the reporting period there were no cases related to corruption cases in the PGN Group. [205-3][11.20.4]

# CONFLICT OF INTEREST AND GRATIFICATION CONTROL

A conflict of interest is a situation in which a person is suspected of having a personal interest which may affect the exercise of authority. To prevent conflicts of interest and control gratification, PGN has established a Conflict of Interest and Gratification Control Policy in accordance with Board of Director's Decree Number 018401.K/OT.03/UT/2014 on October 1, 2014 which has been updated to become Guidelines for Conflicts of Interest and Gratification Control Number P-004/0.11 stipulated by the President Director on September 15, 2021. This policy is the basis for implementing gratification control to encourage the implementation of business ethics and work ethics, preventing conflicts of interest and preventing fraud.

Gratification Management is under the domain of the Internal Audit Group, specifically Gratification Control Unit Team. The UPG will submit reports on the recapitulation of the handling of reports on receiving gratification managed by UPG, both in handling and following up on determining the ownership and utilization of gratification, to the KPK periodically every month. In addition, UPG submits progress reports and evaluates the results of the implementation of the Gratification Control Program within the company to the Board of Directors periodically every month.
