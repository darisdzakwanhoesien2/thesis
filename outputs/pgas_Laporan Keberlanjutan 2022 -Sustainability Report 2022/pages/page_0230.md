# PROGRAM ATAU PROSEDUR MENGATASI PRAKTEK KORUPSI, FRAUD, SUAP DAN/ATAU GRATIFIKASI

Berikut program atau prosedur yang dilaksanakan di 2022:

1. Pembuatan Surat Pernyataan Komitmen Perwira Subholding Gas dalam menerapkan GCG dan Etika Usaha/Etika Kerja termasuk komitmen tidak menerima suap/ gratifikasi atau tidak memberikan suap (anti suap) melalui apliasi GCG Online System (GOLS).
2. Uji pemahaman materi gratifikasi/benturan kepentingan yaitu iKnow.
3. Penilaian aspek integritas pada proses rekrutmen bagi calon Perwira Subholding Gas dan fit and proper test pada proses promosi Perwira Subholding Gas.
4. Pembuatan Pakta Integritas bagi Perwira Subholding Gas yang diangkat jabatannya atau dipromosikan.
5. Pembuatan Pakta Integritas pada setiap dilakukan proses Pengadaan Barang dan Jasa bagi Calon Penyedia Barang dan Jasa maupun Pengguna Barang Jasa.
6. Pembuatan Pakta Integritas bagi Pengelola Proses Pengadaan Barang dan Jasa.
7. Pengawasan pelaksanaan pekerjaan pengadaan barang dan jasa yang bebas Korupsi, Kolusi dan Nepotisme serta Penyuapan yang dituangkan dalam Pembuatan Berita Acara Pemeriksaan (BAP) dan Berita Acara Serah Terima (BAST) Pekerjaan.
8. Dalam penerapan SMAP ISO 37001 telah dilaksanakan Tinjauan Fungsi Kepatuhan Anti Penyuapan (FKAP) pada tanggal 22 Desember 2022.
9. Due diligence dalam proses investasi, sponsorship, dan pembayaran.
10. Penerapan pelaporan deklarasi gratifikasi oleh Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan seluruh karyawan secara berkala setiap semester melalui aplikasi GOLS dan apabila memperoleh gratifikasi wajib lapor melalui UPG (www.upg.pgn.co.id) atau ke Komisi Pemberantasan Korupsi (KPK).

# TANGGUNG JAWAB MANAJEMEN TERHADAP PENERAPAN ANTI PENYUAPAN DAN ANTIKORUPSI

Manajemen PGN memastikan penerapan anti penyuapan dan antikorupsi berjalan dengan baik di seluruh lini bisnis. Untuk itu Perusahaan membentuk Tim Penerapan SMAP ISO 37001, di mana kedudukan manajemen sebagai Dewan Pengarah. Selain itu juga dibentuk Tim Fungsi Kepatuhan Anti Penyuapan (FKAP).

Dukungan atau komitmen terhadap anti penyuapan dan antikorupsi juga diwujudkan dalam bentuk penandatanganan komitmen bersama oleh manajemen. Perusahaan telah memiliki pedoman WBS sebagai sarana pelaporan tindakan dugaan penyuapan dan korupsi, pedoman SMAP, serta pedoman gratifikasi yang ditandatangani oleh seluruh manajemen dan dimuat di situs web Perseroan.

# PROGRAMS OR PROCEDURES FOR ADDRESSING CORRUPTION, FRAUD, BRIBERY AND/OR GRATUITY PRACTICES

The following programs or procedures were carried out in 2022:

1. Making a Statement of Commitment for Gas Subholding Employees in implementing GCG and Business Ethics/ Work Ethics including a commitment not to receive bribes/gratuities or not to give bribes (anti-bribery) through the GCG Online System (GOLS) application.
2. Testing the understanding of gratification/conflict of interest material, i.e. iKnow.
3. Assessment of integrity aspects in the recruitment process for Gas Subholding Employee candidates and the fit and proper test in the promotion process for Gas Subholding Employees.
4. Making an Integrity Pact for Gas Subholding Employees who are appointed or promoted.
5. Making an Integrity Pact every time the process of procuring goods and services is carried out for prospective providers of goods and services and users of goods and services.
6. Making an Integrity Pact for Goods and Services Procurement Process Personnel.
7. Oversight on the implementation of goods and services procurement that is free from Corruption, Collusion and Nepotism as well as Bribery as outlined in the Preparation of Minutes of Inspection, and Minutes of Handover of Work.
8. Implementing ABMS ISO 37001 by carrying out Anti-Bribery Compliance Function Review on December 22, 2022.
9. Due diligence in the investment, sponsorship and payment processes.
10. Implementation of gratuities declaration reporting by the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors and all employees periodically every semester through the GOLS application and the reporting obligation through UPG (www.upg.pgn.co.id) or to the Corruption Eradication Commission

# MANAGEMENT RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF ANTI-BRIBERY AND ANTI-CORRUPTION

PGN's management ensures that anti-bribery and anti-corruption practices run well in all business lines. To that end, the Company established an ISO 37001 SMAP Implementation Team, in which the management holds the Steering Committee positions. In addition, an Anti-Bribery Compliance Function Team was also established.

Support or commitment to anti-bribery and anti-corruption is also manifested in the form of signing of a joint commitment by management. The company has WBS guidelines as a means of reporting suspected acts of bribery and corruption, ABMS guidelines, as well as gratuities guidelines signed by all management and posted on the Company's website.
