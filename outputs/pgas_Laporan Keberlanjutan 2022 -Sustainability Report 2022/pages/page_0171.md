Di tengah masa pemulihan pasca pandemi COVID-19, PGN tetap mendukung upaya Pemerintah dalam melakukan pencegahan dan penanganan pandemi COVID-19. Di antaranya, pada tahun 2022 PGN menyalurkan bantuan gas bumi untuk dapur Wisma Atlet dan bantuan tabung oksigen untuk Rumah Sakit di bawah naungan RS IHC Pertamina.

Karena jumlah kasus yang telah menurun, program bantuan PGN terkait COVID-19 memang tidak lagi sebesar pada 2021 di mana total bantuan yang disalurkan oleh PGN Grup kepada masyarakat yang terdampak mencapai IDR15,6 miliar. Bentuk bantuan berupa paket sembako, paket sanitary kit, masker, hand sanitizer, penyemprotan disinfektan hingga penyediaan perlengkapan APD untuk tenaga medis disalurkan ke berbagai daerah.

Subholding Gas Grup juga membantu meningkatkan suplai oksigen medis yang disalurkan di wilayah Operation East Region yang mencakup Provinsi DKI Jakarta, Banten, Jawa Barat, Jawa Tengah, Daerah Istimewa Yogyakarta, dan Jawa Timur. Selain itu PGN juga berperan dalam program vaksinasi bersama BUMN, vaksinasi di Manokwari, Papua, Kelurahan Krukut, serta kepada masyarakat di sekitar Stasiun Pagardewa yang merupakan Desa Binaan Perseroan.

### Pilar Ekonomi

Melalui pilar ekonomi, PGN berinisiatif untuk memberikan dukungan kepada masyarakat dalam bentuk bantuan pengadaan sarana yang dapat menunjang produktivitas, sehingga penerima manfaat memiliki kemampuan untuk meningkatkan taraf hidupnya secara mandiri. Sepanjang periode pelaporan, PGN telah menyalurkan nilai sebesar IDR9.226.675.798 dalam pilar ekonomi.

Bentuk penyaluran dalam pilar ekonomi terdiri dari:

- Pelatihan kewirausahaan untuk pengembangan bisnis UMKM
- Bantuan alat produksi pengolahan sabut kelapa
- Bantuan alat produksi pengolahan batok kelapa menjadi briket
- Bantuan mesin pengolahan kopi
- Pembangunan sarana prasarana kandang ayam
- Bantuan dana pelatihan pembuatan batik
- Bantuan mesin pencacah sampah plastik

Selain itu, PGN menyadari pentingnya melaksanakan program yang berkesinambungan dan mampu menciptakan dampak berkelanjutan. Untuk itu PGN menginisiasi program Desa Binaan yang dirancang secara komprehensif mulai dari tahapan pemetaan pemangku kepentingan dan sosial, survei ke pemangku kepentingan PGN di sekitar lokasi, perencanaan program, implementasi program, monitoring dan evaluasi program, dan penyerahan program kepada kelompok masyarakat (exit strategy).[413-1][11.15.2]

In the midst of the recovery period after the COVID-19 pandemic, PGN continues to support the Government's efforts in preventing and handling the COVID-19 pandemic. Among them, in 2022 PGN distributed natural gas for the kitchen of the Athlete's Village and oxygen tank assistance for the Hospital under the auspices of the Pertamina IHC Hospital.

Because the number of COVID-19 cases has decreased, PGN's assistance program related to COVID-19 is no longer as large as in 2021 where the total assistance distributed by the PGN Group to affected communities reached IDR 15.6 billion. The form of assistance in the form of food packages, sanitary kit packages, masks, hand sanitizers, and spraying disinfectants to the provision of PPE equipment for medical personnel was distributed to various regions.

Gas Subholding Group also helps increase the supply of medical oxygen which is distributed in the Operation East Region which includes DKI Jakarta, Banten, West Java, Central Java, Yogyakarta Special Region and East Java Provinces. In addition, PGN also plays a role in the vaccination program with SOEs, vaccination in Manokwari, Papua, Krukut Village, as well as to the community around Pagardewa Station which is the Company's Fostered Village.

### Economic Pillar

Through the economic pillar, PGN takes the initiative to provide support to communities in the form of assistance in procuring facilities that can support productivity, so that beneficiaries have the ability to improve their standard of living independently. Throughout the reporting period, PGN has channeled a value of IDR9,226,675,798 in the economic pillar.

The form of distribution in the economic pillar consists of:

- Entrepreneurship training for MSME business development
- Assistance of production equipment for processing coconut coir
- Assistance of production equipment for processing coconut shells into briquettes
- Coffee processing machine assistance
- Construction of chicken coop infrastructure
- Funding assistance for batik making training
- Plastic waste chopping machine assistance

In addition, PGN realizes the importance of implementing sustainable programs and creating sustainable impacts. To that end, PGN initiated the Foster Village program which is comprehensively designed, including the stakeholder and social mapping stage, surveys of PGN stakeholders around the site, program planning, program implementation, program monitoring and evaluation, and program delivery to community groups (exit strategy).
