Selaras dengan kebijakan HSSE, PGN berupaya mengurangi risiko serendah mungkin sesuai dengan prinsip As Low as Reasonably Practicable (ALARP) termasuk terhadap major accident hazard dengan memastikan kecukupan mitigasi yang dibutuhkan melalui penggunaan peralatan yang standar, pengendalian proses secara aman, SDM yang kompeten, serta memastikan sistem tanggap darurat dan krisis telah disiapkan sehingga keberlangsungan bisnis dapat terjaga. Di samping itu, Perusahaan juga memastikan pemahaman dan implementasi Corporate Life Saving Rules (CLSR) pada pekerjaan dan mitra kerja serta melakukan intervensi terhadap kondisi maupun tindakan yang dinilai tidak aman. [3-3] [11.9.1]

In line with the HSSE policy, PGN strives to reduce risk as low as possible in accordance with the As Low as Reasonably Practicable (ALARP) principle, including for major accident hazards, by ensuring adequate mitigation required through the use of standard equipment, safe process control, competent human resources, and ensuring emergency and crisis response systems have been prepared to maintain business continuity. In addition, the Company also ensures the understanding and implementation of the Corporate Life Saving Rules (CLSR) for employees and business partners, as well as intervenes in conditions or actions that are considered unsafe. [3-3] [11.9.1]

PGN juga memiliki Pedoman Penanggulangan Keadaan Gawat Darurat No. P-003/A018 yang bertujuan untuk mencegah dampak yang lebih luas dari kondisi Keadaan Gawat Darurat melalui penanggulangan yang cepat, efektif dan maksimal. Pedoman ini berlaku untuk semua area operasi bisnis dan keproyekan Perusahaan di lingkungan Sub Holding Gas. Selain itu, pedoman tersebut memberikan persyaratan minimal prosedur rencana penanggulangan Keadaan Gawat Darurat yang disebabkan bahaya dalam operasi bisnis Perusahaan ataupun bahaya dari luar operasi Perusahaan yang dapat mempengaruhi kelangsungan bisnis.

Sedangkan terkait dengan risiko mitra bisnis, PGN mengembangkan Contractor Safety Management System (CSMS). CSMS ini wajib dipatuhi oleh seluruh mitra kerja/kontraktor PGN yang menyediakan barang dan jasa. Seluruh kontraktor harus menerapkan CSMS sebelum memulai pekerjaan, selama bekerja, hingga setelah menyelesaikan pekerjaan. Pedoman CSMS ini bertujuan untuk mengurangi risiko kerja para kontraktor, sehingga dapat bekerja dengan aman, tanpa cidera, efektif, efisien, dan tepat waktu.

Pedoman ini juga mengatur hal-hal terkait penilaian risiko, prakualifikasi, penyeleksian kontraktor, pra pelaksanaan pekerjaan, pelaksanaan pekerjaan, hingga evaluasi akhir sebagai monitoring dan laporan telah terlaksananya seluruh kegiatan sesuai dengan pedoman yang berlaku. Sebelum melaksanakan pekerjaan di lapangan, PGN memberikan pelatihan HSSE kepada kontraktor dan memberikan HSSE Passport setelah mereka menyelesaikan pelatihan tersebut.

PGN also has Guidelines for Handling Emergency Situations Number P-003/A018 aims to prevent the greater impact of Emergency Situations through fast, effective, and maximum response. This guideline applies to all areas of the Company's business operations and projects within the Gas Subholding environment. Furthermore, the guideline sets out minimum requirements for procedures for handling Emergency Situations caused by hazards in the Company's business operations or hazards from outside the Company's operations that may affect business continuity.

Meanwhile, with regard to business partner risk, PGN has developed a Contractor Safety Management System (CSMS). All PGN partners/contractors who provide goods and services must comply with the CSMS. All contractors must implement CSMS before starting work, during work, and after completing work. This CSMS guideline aims to reduce the work risks of contractors so that they can work safely, without injury, effectively, efficiently, and on time.

This guideline also regulates matters related to risk assessment, pre-qualification, contractor selection, pre-implementation of work, execution of work, the final evaluation as monitoring, and reporting that all activities have been carried out by adhering to applicable guidelines. Before carrying out work in the field, PGN provides HSSE training to contractors and issues HSSE Passports after completing the training.
