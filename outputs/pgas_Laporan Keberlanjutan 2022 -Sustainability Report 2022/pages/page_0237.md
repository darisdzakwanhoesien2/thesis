c. Penelaahan Laporan
Komite Etik melakukan pemeriksaan substantif atas Pelaporan Pelanggaran yang diterima dan telah memenuhi kriteria, untuk melihat kecukupan bukti permulaan untuk dapat dilakukan investigasi lebih lanjut.

d. The Ethics Committee will provide receipts of the Whistleblowing Reports and deliver the receipts to the Whistleblowers or their representatives. For anonymous Whistleblowing, the Ethics Committee will record the reports in the Whistleblowing list/database.

In carrying out its duties, the Ethics Committee will follow up on each Whistleblowing Report received through the following stages:

a. Receipt of Reports
The Ethics Committee receives each whistleblowing report from the whistleblower through various WBS reporting channels that have been provided, mana-ges the reporting database, and conducts monitoring of the status of each Whistleblowing Report received.

b. Verification of Reports
The Ethics Committee carries out administrative checks on Whistleblowing Reports received through the WBS to determine whether or not the criteria for reported violations to be followed up on are met.

c. Review of Reports
The Ethics Committee conducts a substantive check on the Whistleblowing received that has met the criteria, to see the adequacy of preliminary evidence for further investigation.

# FOLLOW-UP OF COMPLAINTS

To follow up on the reports that will be processed, the Company is required to provide receipts of whistleblowing reports to the stakeholders and/or the representatives of the stakeholders who submit the reports. Should there be a violation report that has sufficient preliminary evidence, the Company will form an Investigation Team to conduct an investigation of the reported problems or violations.

The Ethics Committee, which is an independent function or unit of day-to-day operations of the Company, will provide relevant information to the highest management of the Company/Ethics Council and is responsible for the whistleblowing system mechanism in the Company. Another independent function or unit is the Internal Audit Unit, which is assisted by the Legal, GCG, and Human Resources functions. [2-16.a] [2-16.b] [2-26]

Strict sanctions in the form of warnings of dismissal from work and legal proceedings can be imposed on parties who are proven to have committed violations. Throughout 2022, there were 9 reports submitted through the WBS reporting channel with four reports resolved and five reports in ongoing progress.[2-25]
