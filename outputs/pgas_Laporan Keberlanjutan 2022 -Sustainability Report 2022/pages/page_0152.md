# IDENTIFIKASI BAHAYA, PENILAIAN RISIKO DAN INVESTIGASI INSIDEN [403-2][403-9][11.9.3][11.9.10]

PGN senantiasa memitigasi risiko K3 yang ada di seluruh lingkungan kegiatan operasi yang berpotensi timbul dari material, peralatan, area, metode kerja serta perilaku manusia. Untuk itu PGN menerapkan metode identifikasi bahaya dan pengelolaan risiko seperti:

1. Pengelolaan Risiko dan Peluang Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (IBAPR /HIRADC) yang digunakan untuk mengidentifikasi bahaya dan melakukan analisa serta menentukan profil risiko dari aktivitas rutin.
2. Job Safety Analysis (JSA) yang digunakan dalam aktivitas non rutin seperti kegiatan keproyekan.
3. Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) yang digunakan untuk mengkaji bahaya proses yang ada di kegiatan operasional Perusahaan.
4. Serta metode analisis bahaya dan risiko lainnya yang dapat digunakan sebagai studi yang lebih spesifik sesuai kebutuhannya.

Identifikasi bahaya dan pengelolaan risiko yang dilakukan bertujuan untuk menentukan program pengendalian berdasarkan tingkat risiko dan peluang yang telah diidentifikasi. Tingkat risiko dari hasil penilaian menjadi dasar keputusan dalam menetapkan pengendalian yang diperlukan untuk memitigasi atau meminimalkan risiko. Tindakan ditentukan dengan merujuk pada hierarki mulai dari eliminasi, substitusi, pengendalian teknik, administrasi, serta alat pelindung diri.

To ensure that the K3P2L-E Management System is implemented correctly, PGN conducts a good K3P2L-E management system audit which is carried out at least once a year and covers all Gas Subholding Employees and work areas.

The audits carried out included internal audits as well as external audits conducted by independent parties. In 2022, PGN audited the K3P2L-E management system internally and externally. From the results of an external audit conducted by an independent party, PGN has succeeded in retaining the K3P2L-E management system certificate. Here are the details:

1. SMK3 Certificate based on Government Regulation Number 50 of 2012 with an achievement of 89% and for the Advanced Level category (166 criteria).
2. SO 45001:2018 certificate related to the implementation of the K3 Management System and ISO 14001:2015 related to the implementation of the Environmental Management Management System.
3. Security Management System Audit Certificate based on Regulation of the National Police of the Republic of Indonesia Number 7 of 2019 with the title of Gold Reward.

# HAZARD IDENTIFICATION, RISK ASSESSMENT AND INCIDENT INVESTIGATION [403-2][403-9][11.9.3][11.9.10]

PGN always mitigates OHS risks that exist throughout the operating environment that have the potential to arise from materials, equipment, areas, work methods and human behavior. For this reason, PGN applies hazard identification and risk management methods such as:

1. Occupational Safety and Health Risk and Opportunity Management (IBAPR /HIRADC) which is used to identify hazards and carry out analysis and determine the risk profile of routine activities.
2. Job Safety Analysis (JSA) used in non-routine activities such as project activities.
3. Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) which is used to assess process hazards in the Company's operational activities.
4. As well as other hazard and risk analysis methods that can be used as more specific studies according to their needs.

The hazard identification and risk management carried out aims to determine a control program based on the level of risks and opportunities that have been identified. The level of risk from the results of the assessment becomes the basis for decisions in determining the necessary controls to mitigate or minimize risk. Actions are determined by referring to a hierarchy starting from elimination, substitution, technical control, administration, and personal protective equipment.
