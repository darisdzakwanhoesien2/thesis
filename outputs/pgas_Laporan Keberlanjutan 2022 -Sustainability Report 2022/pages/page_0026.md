# SUBHOLDING GAS TANAM 1.003 BIBIT MANGROVE
# Gas Subholding Planted 1,003 Mangrove Seeds

Dalam rangka menyambut Hari Bumi Tahun 2022, Subholding Gas Pertamina melalui afiliasi Pertagas melakukan kegiatan penanaman bibit mangrove di sekitar lokasi ORF Porong-Tambakrejo Pertamina OEJA. Penanaman mangrove di sekitar pemukiman warga ini bertujuan untuk membantu menjaga keseimbangan ekosistem di daerah tersebut.

In commemoration of the 2022 Earth Day, Pertamina Gas Subholding through its affiliate Pertagas carried out activities of planting mangrove seedlings around the area of Pertamina OEJA ORF Porong-Tambakrejo. Planting mangroves around residential areas was aimed at maintaining the balance of the ecosystem in the area.

# SUBHOLDING GAS DUKUNG BIDANG PENELITIAN
# Gas Subholding Provided Research Assistance

PGN melalui afiliasi PT Pertagas Niaga (PTGN), memberikan dukungan bantuan penelitian dalam penggunaan fasilitas laboratorium Fakultas Teknik Kimia Universitas Sumatera Utara. Melalui dukungan ini, Subholding Gas Pertamina mendukung kemajuan bidang penelitian dari institusi pendidikan Indonesia.

PGN through its affiliate PT Pertagas Niaga (PTGN) provided research assistance in the form of laboratory facilities at the Faculty of Chemical Engineering, University of North Sumatra. Through this support, Pertamina Gas Subholding supported research development of Indonesian educational institutions.

# SUBHOLDING GAS TANAM 2.000 POHON MANGROVE DI KOTA DUMAI
# Gas Subholding Planted 2,000 Mangrove Trees in Dumai City

PGN melalui afiliasi Pertagas Operation Dumai Area (ODA), melakukan penanaman 2.000 pohon mangrove di pesisir pantai Kota Dumai dalam rangka memperingati Hari Lingkungan Hidup Sedunia 2022. Penanaman pohon mangrove ini diharapkan dapat memberikan manfaat bagi lingkungan sekitar.

PGN through its affiliate Pertagas Operation Dumai Area (ODA) planted 2,000 mangrove trees on the coast of Dumai City in commemoration of the 2022 World Environment Day. The planting of mangrove trees was expected to bring benefits to the surrounding areas.
