# FOKUS KEBERLANJUTAN DAN TOPIK MATERIAL [3-1-aii][3-2]

Penyusunan Laporan Keberlanjutan ini memperhatikan topik-topik yang relevan bagi para pemangku kepentingan yaitu pihak-pihak yang terpengaruh dan/atau memberi pengaruh kepada kegiatan operasional dan bisnis Perseroan. Para pemangku kepentingan memberikan masukan topik melalui berbagai saluran seperti survei/angket, rapat, pertemuan, Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) dan Focus Group Discussion (FGD).

PGN telah meninjau kembali topik-topik material yang disajikan pada Laporan Keberlanjutan tahun lalu. Tujuannya adalah untuk menentukan informasi material yang paling relevan bagi para pemangku kepentingan. Ini merupakan sebuah proses peningkatan berkesinambungan (continual improvement) atas laporan-laporan sebelumnya.

Proses peninjauan dilakukan secara internal Perusahaan melalui mekanisme FGD dengan melibatkan para penanggung jawab yang relevan dengan topik yang dibahas. Selanjutnya topik material tersebut divalidasi kembali oleh manajemen Perusahaan untuk disajikan dalam laporan mengikuti prinsip-prinsip pelaporan Standar GRI. Perusahaan menentukan topik material yang relevan dengan menyesuaikan kepada fokus dan strategi keberlanjutan Perusahaan.

Step 1. Identification: Identifying the material aspects along with the boundaries. In this step, the Company selects issues related to the sustainability context, which is then adjusted to the decisions made by the stakeholders;
Step 2. Priority: Prioritizing the aspects that have been identified in the previous step. Through materiality tests, the Company determines significant sustainability issues and topics, which can influence stakeholders' assessments and decisions;
Step 3. Validation: Validating material aspects. The Company examines the scope, boundaries and time aspects of sustainability topics by applying the principle of completeness; and
Step 4. Review: Conducting a review of published reports to improve the quality of the next year's report. By involving stakeholders, we ensure that the contents of the report are in accordance with the reasonable expectations and interests of the stakeholders. We use a questionnaire filling facility to obtain input from stakeholders.

# SUSTAINABILITY FOCUS AND MATERIAL TOPICS [3-1-aii][3-2]

The preparation of this Sustainability Report takes into account relevant topics for stakeholders, namely parties who are affected and/or give influence to the Company's operational and business activities. Stakeholders provide input on topics through various channels, including surveys/questions, meetings, forums, General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) and Focus Group Discussion (FGD).

PGN has reviewed the material topics presented in last year's Sustainability Report. The aim is to determine the most relevant material information for stakeholders. This is a process of continual improvement over previous reports.

The review process is carried out internally by the Company through a FGD involving persons-in-charge who are relevant to the topics discussed. Furthermore, these material topics were re-validated by the Company's management to be presented in a report following the reporting principles of the GRI Standards. The Company determines relevant material topics by adjusting it to the focus and sustainability strategy of the Company.
