PGN menyediakan berbagai media untuk mendukung partisipasi Perwira Subholding Gas dalam program-program kesehatan, seperti aplikasi HOLISTIC untuk mengakses hasil Medical Check Up (MCU) dan Health Risk Assessment (HRA), pelayanan klinik Perusahaan selama jam kerja, layanan konsultasi kesehatan secara online.

Pelaksanaan program kesehatan kerja, termasuk perencanaan, monitoring, dan evaluasi, dikoordinasikan oleh HSSE Group yang memiliki tenaga profesional dokter Perusahaan dan bersertifikasi Hiperkes. Selain itu, Perusahaan juga melakukan audit internal dan audit eksternal Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja setiap satu kali per tahun guna memastikan layanan kesehatan kerja terselenggara sesuai standar.

Hasil dari MCU dan layanan kesehatan kerja lainnya digunakan sebagai pendekatan untuk mengidentifikasi dan mengeliminasi bahaya serta memitigasi risiko kesehatan kerja. PGN memastikan riwayat medis Perwira Subholding Gas yang diperoleh dari layanan kesehatan terjaga kerahasiaannya dan tidak digunakan sebagai dasar untuk pengelolaan ketenagakerjaan atau perlakuan yang tidak sesuai dengan hak-hak Perwira.

Pada tahun 2022, program kesehatan yang diselenggarakan masih berfokus pada rangkaian kegiatan pencegahan dan penanggulangan COVID-19. PGN memastikan kegiatan usaha tetap berlangsung demi menjamin pemenuhan kebutuhan pasokan gas masyarakat tetap terpenuhi, namun tidak mengurangi prioritas Perusahaan dalam melindungi aspek kesehatan dan keselamatan Perwira Subholding Gas.

PGN melakukan program pencegahan dan penanggulangan COVID-19 untuk melindungi kesehatan Perwira Subholding Gas sebagai berikut:

1. Mengampanyekan Memakai Masker, Mencuci tangan dengan sabun di air mengalir, Menjaga jarak, Menjauhi kerumunan, Mengurangi mobilitas (5M) secara gencar.
2. Menerapkan program skrining, antara lain: Form absensi dan kesehatan harian Perwira Subholding Gas, Rapid Antigen Test ataupun PCR Test sebelum WFO, Health Alert Form, dan pengukuran suhu tubuh.
3. Membentuk Task Force Penanggulangan COVID-19 yang terdiri dari Penanggung Jawab Bidang K3, Bidang SDM, dan dokter PGN.
4. Membuka jalur komunikasi terbuka untuk saran, masukan, dan tanggapan Perwira Subholding Gas, menyediakan hotline dan point of contact dari Tim Task Force.
5. Berkoordinasi dengan Pemerintah dan stakeholder terkait agar terlaksana vaksin booster COVID-19 bagi Perwira Subholding Gas, Mitra Kerja, dan keluarganya.

PGN provides various media to support the participation of Gas Subholding Employees in health programs, such as the HOLISTIC application to access Medical Check-Up (MCU) and Health Risk Assessment (HRA) results, the Company's clinical services during working hours, 24-hour health consultation services, and online health education seminars.

The implementation of the occupational health program, including planning, monitoring, and evaluation, is coordinated by the HSSE Group, which has professional staff of Company doctors and is Hiperkes certified. Moreover, the Company also conducts internal and external audits of the Occupational Health and Safety Management System once per year to ensure that occupational health services are carried out according to standards.

Results from MCU and other occupational health services are used as an approach to identify and eliminate hazards and mitigate occupational health risks. PGN ensures that the medical history of Subholding Gas Employees obtained from health services is kept confidential and is not used as a basis for managing employment or treatment that is not in accordance with the rights of Employees.

In 2022, the health program being implemented will still focus on a series of activities to prevent and control COVID-19. PGN ensures that business activities continue to ensure that the community's gas supply needs are met, but does not reduce the Company's priority in protecting the health and safety aspects of Gas Subholding Employees.

PGN carries out the COVID-19 prevention and control program to protect the health of Gas Subholding Employees, as follows:

1. Campaigning for Wearing Masks, Washing hands with soap in running water, Keeping distance, Staying away from crowds, Reducing mobility (5M) intensively.
2. Implementing screening programs, including: Attendance and daily health forms for Subholding Gas Employees, Rapid Antigen Tests or PCR Tests before WFO, Health Alert Forms, and body temperature measurements.
3. Establishing COVID-19 Mitigation Task Force consisting of Persons in Charge of K3, HR, and PGN doctors.
4. Opening an open line of communication for suggestions, input, and responses from Subholding Gas Employees, providing a hotline and point of contact from the Task Force Team.
5. Coordinating with the Government and related stakeholders so that the COVID-19 booster vaccine is carried out for Gas Subholding Employees, Work Partners, and their families.
