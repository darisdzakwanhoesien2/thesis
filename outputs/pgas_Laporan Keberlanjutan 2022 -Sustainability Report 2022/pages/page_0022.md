## PENGHARGAAN KESELAMATAN MIGAS TAHUN 2022 DARI DIREKTORAT JENDERAL MINYAK DAN GAS BUMI KEMENTERIAN ESDM

The 2022 Oil and Gas Safety Awards from the Directorate General of Oil and Natural Gas of Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources

PGN mendapatkan penghargaan atas pencapaian dalam melaksanakan lingkungan kerja dan pengelolaan usaha gas bumi secara profesional, berkelanjutan, serta menerapkan budaya Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3) dengan baik. Acara ini merupakan wujud apresiasi Pemerintah terhadap Kontraktor Kontrak Kerjasama (KKKS) dan Badan Usaha (BU/BUT) yang senantiasa menjaga jam kerja aman dan menerapkan Pembinaan Keselamatan Kerja Migas di lingkungan usahanya.

PGN received an award for its achievement in managing the work environment and natural gas business professionally and sustainably, as well as for implementing a good Occupational Health and Safety culture. This event is a form of Government's appreciation for Cooperation Contract Contractors (KKKS) and Business Entities that always maintain safe working hours and implement Oil and Gas Occupational Safety Education in their work environment.
