# DEWAN KOMISARIS

Merupakan pelaksana fungsi pengawasan dan pemberian nasihat kepada Direksi terkait pelaksanaan kegiatan operasional Perseroan. Dalam menjalankan tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris bertindak secara independen dan profesional dibantu oleh Sekretaris Dewan Komisaris, Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Manajemen Risiko dan Pengembangan Usaha (PMRPU) serta Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi GCG.

# DIREKSI

Merupakan organ Perseroan yang bertanggung penuh atas kegiatan usaha Perseroan, termasuk kegiatan terkait keberlanjutan, sesuai dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Masing-masing Direksi memiliki tugas sesuai dengan kewenangan masing-masing, namun tanggung jawab ditanggung bersama (kolegial). Pada jajaran Direksi PGN terdapat 6 Direktur yang terdiri dari Direktur Utama, Direktur Infrastruktur dan Teknologi, Direktur SDM dan Penunjang Bisnis, Direktur Keuangan dan Manajemen Risiko, Direktur Strategi dan Pengembangan Bisnis, serta Direktur Sales dan Operasi. Tugas Direksi dibantu oleh bagian Corporate Secretary, Investor Relations, Corporate Social Responsibility, Internal Audit, Legal Counsel and Compliance serta Risk Management. [2-11-a]

# BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

The Board of Commissioners constitutes implementer of the functions of supervision and advice provision to the Board of Directors regarding the implementation of the Company's operational activities. In carrying out its duties, the Board of Commissioners acts independently and professionally assisted by the Secretary to the Board of Commissioners, Audit Committee, Risk Management and Business Development Monitoring Committee (PMRPU) and the GCG Remuneration and Nomination Committee.

# BOARD OF DIRECTORS

The Board of Directors is the organ of the Company that is fully responsible for the Company's business activities, including activities related to sustainability in accordance with the provisions of the Company's Articles of Association. Each Board of Directors has duties according to their respective authorities, but the responsibility is shared (collegial). There are 6 Directors at PGN's Board of Directors consisting of the Main Director, Director of Infrastructure and Technology, Director of HR and Corporate Services, Director of Finance and Risk Management, Director of Strategy and Business Development, and Director of Sales and Operations. In carrying out its duties, the Board of Directors is assisted by the Corporate Secretary, Investor Relations, Corporate Social Responsibility, Internal Audit, Legal Counsel and Compliance and Risk Management sections. [2-11-a]
