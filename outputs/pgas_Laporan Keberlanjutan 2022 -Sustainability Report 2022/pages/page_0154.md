Pengelolaan program K3 di PGN berada di bawah pengawasan HSSE Group yang dipimpin oleh Direktur SDM dan Penunjang Bisnis. PGN juga memiliki unit-unit pengelolaan K3 pada tingkat operasional yang melekat dan satuan kerja melalui komite K3 yang merepresentasikan manajemen dan Perwira Subholding Gas secara bertingkat mulai dari Korporat sampai Satuan Kerja, yaitu:

1. Executive Central Safety Committee (ECSC) di tingkat korporat.
2. Central Safety Committee (CSC) di tingkat direktorat.
3. Central Safety Committee (CSC) di tingkat satuan kerja dan wilayah operasi PGN seperti Unit Operation and Maintenance Management, Sales and Operation Region, keproyekan, hingga Anak Perusahaan.

Komite ini bertanggung jawab mulai dari perencanaan, pengawasan, pemantauan serta pelaksanaan evaluasi program K3 di Perusahaan. Komite K3 juga menggelar pertemuan rutin yang dilaksanakan minimal 1 kali setiap bulannya dan tertuang dalam Personal Safety Action Plan (PSAP) masing-masing pimpinan untuk membahas isu-isu K3 yang dihadapi.

Selain itu, untuk memastikan keterlibatan seluruh pihak dalam menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan sehat, aturan-aturan mengenai K3 dimuat secara spesifik dalam Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) yang disusun bersama antara perwakilan Perwira Subholding Gas yang tergabung dalam Serikat Pekerja Perusahaan Gas Negara (SP-PGN) dengan perwakilan manajemen PGN. Aspekaspek K3 juga diterapkan sebagai bagian dari Key Performance Indicators (KPI) yang menjadi bagian dari penilaian kinerja setiap Perwira Subholding Gas.

The management of the K3 program at PGN is under the supervision of the HSSE Group, which is led by the Director of HR and Corporate Services. PGN also has K3 management units at the inherent operational level and work units through K3 committees that represent management and Gas Subholding Employees in stages starting from the Corporate to the Work Unit, namely:

1. Executive Central Safety Committee (ECSC) at the corporate level.
2. Central Safety Committee (CSC) at the directorate level.
3. Central Safety Committee (CSC) at the work unit level and PGN operating areas such as the Operation and Maintenance Management Unit, Sales and Operation Region, projects, to Subsidiaries.

This committee is responsible for planning, supervising, monitoring and evaluating the K3 program in the Company. The OHS Committee also holds regular meetings which are held at least 1 time per month and are contained in the Personal Safety Action Plan (PSAP) of each leader to discuss the OHS issues they are facing.

In addition, to ensure the involvement of all parties in creating a safe and healthy work environment, the rules regarding K3 are contained specifically in the Collective Labor Agreement (PKB) which is jointly drawn up between representatives of Gas Subholding Employees who are members of the State Gas Company Workers' Union (SP-PGN) with PGN management representatives. K3 aspects are also implemented as part of the Key Performance Indicators (KPI) which are part of the performance assessment of each Gas Subholding Employees.
