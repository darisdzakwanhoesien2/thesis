Terkait penanganan dampak pandemi COVID-19, hingga saat ini kami masih menerapkan berbagai tindakan preventif dan kuratif, terutama di dalam lingkup Perusahaan. Di antaranya kewajiban memakai masker dan program skrining kesehatan di lingkungan Perusahaan, hingga dukungan layanan dokter bagi pekerjaan, mitra kerja dan keluarga Subholding Gas yang terdampak COVID-19. Perusahaan juga melakukan monitoring implementasi program pencegahan dan pengendalian COVID-19 di lingkungan Subholding Gas setiap 6 bulan sekali melalui program inspeksi lingkungan kerja.

PGN menerima berbagai penghargaan terkait keselamatan kerja di tahun 2022, di antaranya Penghargaan K3 2022 dari Kementerian Ketenagakerjaan untuk penghargaan kecelakaan nihil (zero accident) dan penanggulangan COVID-19 serta Penghargaan Keselamatan Migas Tahun 2022 dari Direktorat Jenderal Minyak dan Gas Bumi Kementerian ESDM. Atas segala inisiatifnya di bidang pemberdayaan Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM), Perusahaan juga menerima berbagai kategori penghargaan dalam Human Capital on Resilience Excellence Award 2022. [A.4.b.2]

Tanggung jawab sosial PGN juga lekat dengan pelanggan kami. Perusahaan memastikan bahwa selain menyediakan layanan barang dan jasa, kami juga memberikan perlindungan terkait keamanan produk dan layanan pelanggan. Di antaranya dengan melakukan kontrol rutin terhadap seluruh infrastruktur agar memenuhi aspek keamanan.

Sementara terkait layanan, PGN menyediakan akses bagi pelanggan untuk menyampaikan kebutuhannya melalui berbagai saluran seperti Pertamina Call Center 135 dan platform digital official PGN termasuk sarana komunikasi terbaru WhatsApp Chatbot untuk melayani permintaan yang bersifat umum.

Dalam kurun lima tahun terakhir, nilai matriks Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) dan Customer Loyalty Index (CLI) PGN berada pada kriteria sangat baik. Tingkat CSI dan CLI PGN pada tahun 2022 masing-masing berada pada level 4,53 dan 4,64, yang mengindikasikan kriteria sangat baik. Pada tahun 2022 juga tidak terjadi pelanggaran terkait pelanggan. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]

Di luar karyawan dan pelanggan, fokus tanggung jawab sosial kami yang tidak kalah penting dan selalu mendapat perhatian penuh adalah program pemberdayaan masyarakat, baik melalui investasi sosial maupun program pengembangan masyarakat. Program Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TJSL) PGN didesain untuk mendukung keberlangsungan usaha, meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat dan berkontribusi terhadap pencapaian SDGs melalui 4 pilar yaitu sosial, lingkungan, ekonomi dan tata kelola.

With regards to the COVID-19 pandemic, to date, we remain committed to enforce various preventive and curative measures, especially within the scope of the Company. These include the obligation to wear masks, run health screening programs within the Company, and provide doctor services for Gas Subholding's employees, work partners, and families affected by COVID-19. The Company also monitors the implementation of the COVID-19 prevention and control program within the Gas Subholding once every six months through a work environment inspection program.

PGN has won several awards related to work safety in 2022, including the 2022 OHS Award from the Ministry of Manpower for a zero accident award and the COVID-19 control program award, as well as the 2022 Oil and Gas Safety Award from the Directorate General of Oil and Gas of the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources. The Company was also awarded various categories in the 2022 Human Capital on Resilience Excellence Award for all initiatives in the field of Human Resources (HR) empowerment. [A.4.b.2]

PGN's social responsibility is also attached to our customers. In addition to providing goods and services, the Company also ensures that we provide protection related to products safety and customer services, including conducting regular controls on all infrastructure to meet security aspects.

Meanwhile, in terms of services, PGN provides access for customers to submit their inquiries through various channels, such as the Pertamina Call Center 135 and PGN's official digital platforms, including the latest communication media WhatsApp Chatbot to respond to general inquiries.

In the past five years, PGN's Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) and Customer Loyalty Index (CLI) matrix values have been in very good criteria. PGN's CSI and CLI levels in 2022 stood at 4.53 and 4.64, respectively, which indicated very good criteria. In 2022 there were also no violations related to customers. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]

Apart from employees and customers, our social responsibility also focuses on community empowerment programs, which are no less important and have always received full attention, be it through social investments or community development programs. PGN's Social and Environmental Responsibility program is devised to promote business sustainability, improve community welfare, and contribute to the achievement of the SDGs through four pillars: social, environmental, economic, and governance aspects.
