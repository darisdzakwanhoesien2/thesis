# KEBIJAKAN ANTI PENYUAPAN DAN ANTIKORUPSI [205]

Definisi penyuapan menurut PGN adalah tindakan memberi uang, barang atau bentuk lain sebagai pembalasan dari pemberi suap kepada penerima suap, yang dilakukan untuk mengubah sikap penerima suap atas kepentingan si pemberi. Sementara korupsi adalah suatu perbuatan yang dilakukan dengan maksud untuk mendapatkan keuntungan yang bertentangan dengan tugas resmi dengan menyalahgunakan kekuasaan atau kesempatan.

3rd place in the AKHLAK LCV Assessment for the GM category, competing with 79 GMs from other entities in the Pertamina Group.

In terms of Gas Subholding as a whole, the average AKHLAK LCV Assessment score is 382 with a Maturity Level Commitment. It shows that the leaders in Gas Subholding have become a role model in encouraging the implementation of culture towards performance achievement in the domain of units/functions.

In addition to the AKHLAK cultural program, PGN has additional programs to strengthen corporate culture, create a positive work environment, and increase employee engagement. The programs are:

1. Circular on Anti Sexual Harassment in the Gas Subholding Institution, in which PGN actively campaigns for the anti-sexual harassment movement to prevent acts of sexual harassment.
2. Cross Talent Mobility: this program aims to obtain experienced talent in the oil and gas holding institutions, strengthen competence, and integrate culture.
3. Millennials Empowering: this program aims to strengthen millennial competencies in order to accelerate millennial employee development programs.
4. Energy For Kids: this program aims to introduce PGN to the sons and daughters of workers and as an effort to increase employee engagement with the Company and improve a culture of harmony among Employees.

In order to measure the effectiveness of the implementation of the Corporate Culture, PGN continuously conducts an Employee Engagement survey every year. In 2022, the employee engagement survey on the corporate culture aspect resulted in a score of 4.08 out of 5. Based on the employee engagement score results on the corporate culture aspect, the AKHLAK Gas Subholding Employee cultural values have been internalized within every personnel and have been implemented consistently.

# ANTI-BRIBERY AND ANTI-CORRUPTION POLICY [205]

The definition of bribery according to PGN is the act of giving money, goods or other items from the bribe giver to the bribe taker, which is carried out to change the attitude of the bribe taker in the interests of the giver. In the meantime, corruption is an act carried out with the intention of gaining profit by violating official duties and abusing power or opportunity.
