- Pelaporan pelanggaran disampaikan kepada Komite Etik baik yang menyangkut Dewan Komisaris, Direksi maupun Perwira Subholding Gas.
- Pelaporan pelanggaran dapat dilakukan pada saat/di luar jam kerja.
- Pelaporan pelanggaran disampaikan melalui e-mail, website, surat, atau datang langsung ke Komite Etik.
- Perusahaan menerima setiap pelaporan pelanggaran yang diajukan oleh stakeholders dan/atau perwakilan stakeholders baik secara lisan maupun tertulis.
- Perusahaan memberikan penjelasan mengenai kebijakan dan prosedur penyelesaian pelaporan pelanggaran pada saat stakeholders dan/atau perwakilan stakeholders yang mengajukan pelaporan pelanggaran.
- Pelaporan pelanggaran yang diduga dilakukan disampaikan secara tertulis atau lisan dengan mekanisme sebagai berikut:

1. Melalui website Perseroan www.pgn.co.id, sub bagian “Pelaporan Pelanggaran”.
2. Melalui e-mail di etik@pgn.co.id.
3. Menyampaikan surat resmi yang ditujukan kepada Perseroan c.q Komite Etik PGN, dengan cara diantar langsung atau pos ke Perseroan dengan alamat: Komite Etik PT PGN (Persero), Gedung Graha PGAS Lantai 3, Jl. KH Zainul Arifin 20, Jakarta Barat.

- Pelaporan pelanggaran secara tertulis disarankan untuk dilengkapi bukti pendukung yang berkaitan dengan bukti indikasi awal terjadinya pelanggaran.
- Jika pelaporan pelanggaran diajukan secara tertulis beridentitas maka Perseroan memberikan tanda terima, dan jika pelaporan pelanggaran tidak dilengkapi dengan identitas maka akan dicatat dalam daftar pelaporan pelanggaran.
- Apabila pelaporan pelanggaran diajukan oleh perwakilan stakeholders, maka selain dokumen di atas juga diserahkan dokumen lainnya yaitu:

1. Fotokopi bukti identitas stakeholders dan perwakilan stakeholders.
2. Surat Kuasa dari stakeholders kepada perwakilan stakeholders yang menyatakan bahwa stakeholders memberikan kewenangan bertindak untuk dan atas nama stakeholders.
3. Jika perwakilan stakeholders adalah lembaga atau badan hukum, maka harus dilampiri dengan dokumen yang menyatakan bahwa pihak yang mengajukan pelaporan pelanggaran berwenang untuk mewakili lembaga atau badan hukum tersebut.

- Whistleblowing reports are submitted to the Ethics Committee be it pertaining to the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, or Gas Subholding Employees.
- Whistleblowing can be carried out during or outside working hours.
- Whistleblowing reports are submitted via email, website, letter, or in-person to the Ethics Committee.
- The Company accepts any whistleblowing report submitted by stakeholders and/or representatives of stakeholders be it verbally or in writing.
- The Company provides an explanation of the policies and procedures for resolving whistleblowing reports when stakeholders and/or representatives of stakeholders submit the reports.
- Whistleblowing reports of violations allegedly committed are submitted in writing or verbally with the following mechanism:

1. Through the Company's website www.pgn.co.id, sub-section "Reporting of Violation".
2. Via email at etik@pgn.co.id.
3. Submitting an official letter addressed to the Company c.q PGN Ethics Committee, be it in-person or via mail to the Company at the address: Komite Etik PT PGN (Persero), Gedung Graha PGAS Lantai 3, Jl. KH Zainul Arifin 20, Jakarta Barat.

- Whistleblowing reports in a written form are recommended to be supplemented with supporting evidence relating to the initial indication of the violation.
- If the whistleblowers of the written reports state their identities, the Company will provide receipts, and if the whistleblowers of the written reports do not state their identities, they will be recorded in the list of reported violations.
- If the whistleblowing reports are submitted by representatives of stakeholders, other documents must also be submitted in addition to the documents above, namely:

1. Copies of proof of identity of stakeholders and representatives of stakeholders.
2. Power of Attorney from the stakeholders to the representatives of stakeholders stating that the stakeholders grant them the authority to act for and on behalf of the stakeholders.
3. If the representatives of the stakeholders are institutions or legal entities, a document stating that the party submitting the whistleblowing report is authorized to represent the institution or legal entity must be attached.
