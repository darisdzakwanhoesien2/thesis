Salah satu program yang kami banggakan adalah Program Desa Binaan atau pembangunan desa-desa di sekitar wilayah operasi menjadi desa yang maju dan mandiri, sehingga dapat menunjang pembangunan berkelanjutan. Inisiatif yang kami lakukan antara lain meningkatkan pengetahuan, pendapatan, kesehatan dan pendidikan masyarakat desa, serta menyediakan akses atau permodalan melalui koperasi dan Unit BUMDes.

Saat ini ada 5 Desa Binaan PGN yaitu Desa Teluk Terate di Serang, Desa Sriminosari di Lampung, Desa Pagardewa di Muara Enim, Desa Tambak Kalisogo di Sidoarjo dan Desa Tembesi Tower di Batam dengan total ribuan penerima manfaat. Adapun selain pengelolaan Program Desa Binaan tersebut, PGN berperan aktif dalam keterlibatan pembentukan Balkondes (Balai Ekonomi Desa) di desa Karangrejo Magelang, dimana hal tersebut memunculkan creating shared value bagi masyarakat sekitar, serta mendukung program Super Destinasi wisata di kawasan Borobudur. Selain meningkatkan kualitas perekonomian, kegiatan Desa Binaan ini juga meningkatkan taraf hidup penerima manfaat secara mandiri. Sepanjang periode pelaporan, PGN telah menyalurkan nilai investasi sebesar IDR9.226.675.798 dalam pilar ekonomi.

Perusahaan menerima berbagai penghargaan atas inisiatifnya di bidang tanggung jawab sosial di antaranya BUMN Awards 2022, Indonesia Diversity Equity Inclusion &amp; Environmental Social Governance Awards (IDEAS) 2022 dan Nusantara CSR Award 2022. [A.4.b.2]

## Aspek Tata Kelola

PGN secara konsisten memastikan penerapan GCG di dalam setiap lini bisnis kami, termasuk melakukan evaluasi terhadap kinerja jajaran Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Kami juga terus menguatkan prinsip tata kelola untuk mencegah pelanggaran kode etik dan tindakan korupsi.

Komitmen kami pada antikorupsi dilakukan dengan mengimplementasikan SMAP ISO 37001:2016 yang menjadi panduan dalam mencegah, mendeteksi, dan merespon tindak penyuapan serta memastikan penegakan aturan anti-penyuapan dalam seluruh kegiatan operasional dan bisnis PGN. Kami terus melakukan kegiatan anti korupsi kepada seluruh Perwira melalui sosialisasi atau pelatihan.

Sosialisasi GCG dilakukan secara proaktif, mulai dari pimpinan tertinggi hingga ke seluruh karyawan melalui berbagai kanal seperti uji pemahaman GCG dan code of conduct bagi setiap pekerja, penerapan aplikasi GCG Online System (GOLS) untuk meningkatkan awareness GCG serta branding GCG lewat flyer yang ditempatkan melalui email blast, website hingga media sosial PGN.

One of the programs we take pride in is the Fostered Villages Program, a development program for villages around the operational area to transform into developed and independent villages to support sustainable development. Our initiatives include improving knowledge, increasing income, enhancing health, and education for village communities as well as providing access or capital through cooperatives and the BUMDes Units.

To date, there are five PGN assisted villages, namely Teluk Terate Village in Serang, Sriminosari Village in Lampung, Pagardewa Village in Muara Enim, Tambak Kalisogo Village in Sidoarjo and Tembesi Tower Village in Batam with a total of thousands of beneficiaries. In addition to managing the Fostered Villages Program, PGN actively engages in establishing Balkondes (Village Economic Hall) in Karangrejo Village in Magelang, which promotes creating shared value for the surrounding communities and supports the super-priority tourism destination program in the Borobudur area. In addition to improving the quality of the economy, these Fostered Villages activities also enable the beneficiaries to improve their standards of living on their own. Throughout the reporting period, PGN has distributed an investment value of IDR9,226,675,798 in the economic pillar.

The Company received various awards for its initiatives in the field of social responsibility, including the 2022 BUMN Awards, the 2022 Indonesia Diversity Equity Inclusion &amp; Environmental Social Governance Awards (IDEAS), and the 2022 Nusantara CSR Award. [A.4.b.2]

## Governance Aspect

PGN consistently ensures the implementation of GCG in each of our business lines, including evaluating the performance of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors. We also continuously strengthen governance principles to prevent violations of the code of ethics and acts of corruption.

Our commitment on anti-corruption is manifested by implementing the Anti Bribery Management System (Sistem Manajemen Anti Penyuan or ABMS) ISO 37001:2016, which serves as a guide in preventing, detecting, and responding to acts of bribery and ensuring the enforcement of anti-bribery rules in all PGN operations and business activities. We continue to conduct anti-corruption activities to all Employees through dissemination or training programs.

The Company also remains proactive in disseminating Good Corporate Governance (GCG), starting from the highest level of leadership to all employees through various channels, including tests on Good Corporate Governance (GCG) and code of conduct understanding for every employee, implementing the GCG Online System (GOLS) application to raise GCG awareness, and GCG branding through flyers in email blasts, PGN websites, and social media.
