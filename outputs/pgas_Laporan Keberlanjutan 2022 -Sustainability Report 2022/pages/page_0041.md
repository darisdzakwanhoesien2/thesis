# PENCAPAIAN KINERJA KEBERLANJUTAN

## Aspek Ekonomi

Penguatan lapangan usaha dua tahun pasca pandemi COVID-19, ikut menyulut pertumbuhan ekonomi nasional tahun 2022 yang mencapai 5,31% (year on year/yoy), jauh lebih tinggi dibandingkan tahun 2021 yang hanya sebesar 3,70%.

PGN turut meraih penguatan kinerja, di mana pada tahun 2022 Perusahaan mencatat peningkatan nilai ekonomi yang diperoleh dengan jumlah Laba Bersih sebesar USD326,23 juta, naik 7,38% dibandingkan tahun 2021 yang senilai USD303,82 juta. Pencapaian Laba Bersih 52,57% di atas target RKAP.

Kami secara konsisten mendistribusikan sebagian nilai ekonomi yang diperoleh kepada para pemangku kepentingan sesuai fungsi dan kebutuhannya. Di antaranya melalui pembayaran pajak dan kontribusi untuk pemerintah, pembagian dividen bagi para pemegang saham, peningkatan kesejahteraan karyawan serta peningkatan kesejahteraan masyarakat sekitar melalui berbagai kegiatan pemberdayaan sosial. Jumlah nilai ekonomi yang didistribusikan sepanjang tahun 2022 mencapai USD3.719.533.692 naik 15,72% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]

Komitmen kami dalam meningkatkan manfaat bagi para pemangku kepentingan mendapatkan apresiasi di antaranya Apresiasi Kenduri Bagimu Negeri BUMN Berprestasi Dividen atas konsistensi Perusahaan dalam menyalurkan dividen dan Penerimaan Negara Bukan Pajak (PNBP) untuk negara. [A.4.b.2]

## Aspek Lingkungan

Pada aspek lingkungan, PGN terus menerapkan sistem manajemen lingkungan berdasarkan standar internasional ISO 14001:2015 meliputi proses identifikasi aspek dan dampak lingkungan, pemenuhan kepatuhan pada peraturan di bidang pengelolaan lingkungan, penyusunan target dan program, kegiatan evaluasi seluruh program lingkungan, serta audit internal maupun eksternal. Sistem ini memberikan arahan dalam mengelola lingkungan untuk mitigasi potensi dampak negatif.

Berbagai inisiatif terkait lingkungan kami lakukan terutama untuk mengurangi konsumsi energi, tingkat emisi dan limbah. Upaya efisiensi energi dilakukan di antaranya melalui pengaturan pola operasi Gas Turbine Compressor, pemasangan soft starter pada after cooler, hingga penggunaan AC dan lampu yang hemat energi.

Inisiatif efisiensi konsumsi energi tersebut berdampak langsung pada berkurangnya emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) yang dihasilkan. Sepanjang tahun 2022, PGN mencatat pengurangan emisi (Ton CO2eq) sebesar 132.092,17, lebih besar dibandingkan tahun 2021 yang mencapai 89.626,76. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]

# SUSTAINABLE PERFORMANCE ACHIEVEMENTS

## Economic Aspect

The strengthening of business fields two years after the COVID-19 pandemic has helped spur national economic growth in 2022 with 5.31% (year-on-year/yoy), much higher than the growth recorded in 2021 which was only 3.70%.

PGN also achieved performance reinforcement in 2022 with the Company recording an increase in economic value obtained by total Net Income of USD326.23 million, higher by 7.38% on a Y-o-Y than that of 2021 of only USD303.82 million. Net profit reached 52.57% above RKAP target.

We consistently distribute some of the economic value obtained to stakeholders according to their functions and needs. These include paying taxes and contributions to the Government, distributing dividends to shareholders, improving employee well-being and improving the welfare of the surrounding communities through various social empowerment activities. The total economic value distributed throughout 2022 reached USD3,719,533,692 15.72% higher compared to the previous year. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]

Our commitment to increasing benefits for stakeholders has received appreciation, including the Appreciation of Kenduri Bagimu Negeri BUMN with Dividend Achievements for the Company's commitment in distributing dividends and Non-Tax State Revenue (PNBP) for the country. [A.4.b.2]

## Environmental Aspect

On the environmental aspect, PGN continues to implement the environmental management system based on the international standard ISO 14001:2015 covering the process of identifying environmental aspects and impacts, fulfilling compliance with regulations in the field of environmental management, setting targets and programs, evaluating all environmental programs, as well as internal and external audits. The system provides direction in managing the environment to mitigate potential negative impacts.

We carry out various environmental initiatives primarily to reduce energy consumption, emission levels and waste. Energy efficiency efforts are made, among others, by adjusting the operating pattern of the Gas Turbine Compressor, installing a soft starter on the after cooler, and using energy-efficient air conditioners and lamps.

These energy consumption efficiency initiatives have a direct impact on reducing the resulting Green House Gas (GHG) emissions. Throughout 2022, PGN recorded emission reductions (Ton CO2eq) of 132,092.17, higher than that of 2021 which reached 89,626.76. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]
