# KOMITMEN DIREKSI DAN MANAJEMEN TERHADAP PENERAPAN KODE ETIK

Komitmen Direksi dan manajemen terhadap penerapan Kode Etik dapat dilihat dari Pedoman Etika Usaha dan Etika Kerja yang ditandatangani langsung oleh Direktur Utama dan harus dipatuhi oleh seluruh Perwira PGN. Selain itu, pedoman ini juga terus diperbarui mengikuti kebutuhan Perusahaan dan perkembangan industri.

Manajemen juga memastikan bahwa penerapan kode etik terinternalisasi ke seluruh lini dan proses bisnis. Hal ini menjadi tanggung jawab Direksi melalui satuan kerja yang ditunjuk sebagai pengelola GCG, di mana Perwira PGN juga wajib terlibat dalam proses internalisasi dan komunikasi pedoman ini. Berbagai proses internalisasi yang dilakukan di antaranya melalui tatap muka, buku saku, rapat koordinasi hingga media informasi dan digital.

Setiap pimpinan satuan/unit kerja wajib memastikan bahwa Pedoman Etika Usaha dan Etika Kerja telah diterapkan dengan baik di unit kerjanya, dengan melakukan monitoring dan review pelaksanaannya. Direksi akan memutuskan pemberian tindakan pembinaan, sanksi disiplin serta pencegahan yang harus dilaksanakan di satuan/unit kerja masing-masing, apabila diperlukan.

Setiap individu yang terlibat dalam proses bisnis Perseroan, baik pihak internal maupun external, dapat melaporkan penyimpangan etika yang terjadi melalui kanal pelaporan atau whistleblowing system, yang dijelaskan secara terpisah di dalam bab Tata Kelola ini.

Perwira PGN yang terbukti melakukan penyimpangan etika, berhak untuk didengar penjelasannya di hadapan atasan langsung sebelum pemberian tindakan pembinaan atau hukuman disiplin yang mengacu kepada Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB).

# COMMITMENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND MANAGEMENT TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE CODE OF ETHICS

The commitment of the Board of Directors and management to the implementation of the Code of Ethics can be seen from the Guidelines for Business Ethics and Work Ethics which are signed directly by the President Director and must be obeyed by all PGN Employees. In addition, these guidelines are continuously updated following the Company's needs and industry developments.

Management also ensures that the implementation of the code of ethics is internalized throughout all business lines and processes. This is the responsibility of the Board of Directors through work units appointed as GCG managers, where PGN Employees are also required to get involved in the process of internalizing and communicating these guidelines. Various internalization processes carried out included in-person meetings, pocket books, coordination meetings to information and digital media.

Each head of the unit/work unit must ensure that the Business Ethics and Work Ethics Guidelines have been properly implemented in their respective work unit, by monitoring and reviewing the implementation. The Board of Directors will decide on the provision of coaching, disciplinary sanctions and prevention actions that must be carried out in the respective work units/units, if necessary.

Any individual involved in the Company's business processes, both internal and external parties, can report ethical deviations that occur through reporting channels or the whistleblowing system, which are explained separately in this Governance chapter.

PGN Employees who are proven to have committed ethical deviations are entitled to be heard their explanations before the direct supervisor prior to being given coaching or imposed disciplinary punishment referring to the Collective Labor Agreement (PKB).
