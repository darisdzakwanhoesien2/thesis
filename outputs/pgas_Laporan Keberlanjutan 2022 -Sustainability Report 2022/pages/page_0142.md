## PERENCANAAN SUKSESI DAN PROMOSI

Jenjang karier termasuk adanya kenaikan level atau promosi jabatan adalah rangkaian proses yang harus dilalui setiap karyawan untuk mencapai tingkatan posisi tertentu dalam organisasi PGN. Secara berkala, Perusahaan memberikan promosi kepada Perwira PGN dengan mempertimbangkan berbagai faktor seperti tingkat pendidikan, kinerja yang dihasilkan serta talenta yang dinilai potensial, setelah terlebih dahulu mengadakan penilaian secara obyektif. Perencanaan suksesi dan promosi disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan perkembangan usaha PGN.

Sesuai hasil penilaian kinerja, selama periode pelaporan, terdapat 302 Perwira PGN yang dipromosikan ke tingkat jabatan atau grade yang lebih tinggi.

## SUCCESSION AND PROMOTION PLANS
A career path, including job advancement or promotion, is a series of processes that each employee must pass through to reach a certain level of position within the PGN organization. Periodically, the Company provides promotions to PGN Employees, taking into account a variety of elements, such as educational level, work performance, and potential skills, after first conducting an objective assessment. Succession planning and promotions are tailored to the needs of PGN's business development.

During the reporting period, 302 PGN Employees were promoted to higher ranks or grades based on performance appraisal results.
