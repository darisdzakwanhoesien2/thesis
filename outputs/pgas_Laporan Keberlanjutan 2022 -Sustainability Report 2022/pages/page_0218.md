# AUDIT DAN PELAPORAN PERUSAHAAN

Company Audit and Reporting

Komite Audit berperan memberikan pendapat kepada Dewan Komisaris terhadap laporan atau hal-hal yang disampaikan Direksi kepada Dewan Komisaris, mengidentifikasi hal-hal yang memerlukan perhatian Dewan Komisaris, dan melaksanakan tugas-tugas lain terkait tugas Dewan Komisaris antara lain dalam hal: menelaah informasi keuangan yang akan dikeluarkan Perusahaan, menelaah ketaatan Perusahaan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, menelaah hasil pemeriksaan auditor internal dan eksternal hingga memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris mengenai penunjukan Akuntan Publik.

Komite Audit diketuai Komisaris Independen, satu orang anggota yang merupakan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan tiga orang anggota profesional dari luar Perseroan. Susunan keanggotaan tersebut telah memenuhi ketentuan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 55/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Pelaksanaan Kerja Komite Audit dan dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Komite Audit telah menjalankan prinsip-prinsip profesionalisme dan independen.

Perusahaan memiliki Satuan Kerja Audit Internal yang bertanggungjawab melaksanakan proses audit Perusahaan sesuai dengan standar audit dan kode etik yang berlaku. Selain itu, Satuan Kerja Audit Internal juga bertanggung jawab untuk mengalokasikan sumber daya audit secara efektif dan efisien, mengembangkan profesionalisme auditor dan melaksanakan program quality assurance dalam tugas dan pengelolaan Satuan Kerja Audit Internal.

Tugas Satuan Kerja Audit Internal di antaranya: mengkoordinasi penyusunan strategi dan rencana/rotasi audit dan advisory di Subholding Gas Group, mengelola temuan audit dan tindak lanjut hasil audit di Perusahaan serta melakukan persiapan dan pendampingan audit eksternal. Penunjukan auditor eksternal sendiri dilakukan berdasarkan RUPS yang memberikan pendelegasian kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk mengusulkan Kantor Akuntan Publik (KAP) yang akan ditugaskan.

The role of the Audit Committee is to provide opinions to the Board of Commissioners on reports or matters submitted by the Board of Directors to the Board of Commissioners, identify matters that require the attention of the Board of Commissioners, and carry out other tasks related to the Board of Commissioners' duties, including in terms of: reviewing financial information which will be issued by the Company, reviewing the Company's compliance with laws and regulations, reviewing the results of inspections by internal and external auditors to providing recommendations to the Board of Commissioners regarding the appointment of a Public Accountant.

The Audit Committee is chaired by an Independent Commissioner, one member who is a member of the Board of Commissioners and three members who are professionals from outside the Company. The composition of the membership complies with the provisions of the Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 55/POJK.04/2015 on the Establishment and Guidelines for the Implementation of the Work of the Audit Committee and in carrying out its duties, the Audit Committee has carried out the principles of professionalism and independence.

The Company has an Internal Audit Work Unit which is responsible for carrying out the Company's audit process in accordance with applicable auditing standards and codes of ethics. Moreover, the Internal Audit Work Unit is also responsible for allocating audit resources in an effective and efficient manner, developing auditor professionalism and implementing a quality assurance program in the duties and management of the Internal Audit Work Unit.

The duties of the Internal Audit Work Unit include: coordinating the preparation of audit and advisory strategies and plans/rotation in the Gas Subholding Group, managing audit findings and following up on audit results in the Company as well as preparing and assisting external audits. The appointment of the external auditor itself was carried out based on the GMS which delegated to the Board of Commissioners to propose a Public Accounting Firm to be commissioned.
