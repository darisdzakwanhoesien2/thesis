# SISTEM PELAPORAN PELANGGARAN (WHISTLEBLOWING SYSTEM) [2-16]

Sejak 2019 PGN telah memiliki sistem pelaporan whistleblowing system yang memungkinkan setiap individu, baik internal maupun eksternal, untuk melaporkan dugaan pelanggaran terhadap aturan Perusahaan, etika bisnis, diskriminasi, maupun tindakan penipuan. Kerahasiaan identitas pelapor dilindungi oleh Perusahaan dan setiap laporan yang diterima akan ditindaklanjuti sesuai dengan peraturan dan ketentuan yang berlaku.

Penerapan whistleblowing system mengacu kepada Pedoman whistleblowing system No. P-002/A011 tanggal 2 Agustus 2021 sebagai pembaruan atas Pedoman No. P-002/0.11 Rev.00 tanggal 25 Juni 2019. Pedoman tersebut telah diunggah di situs web PGN di bagian Tata Kelola.

Whistleblowing system PGN tersedia tidak hanya bagi karyawan PGN namun juga kepada pihak eksternal yang terlibat di dalam proses bisnis Perseroan seperti pemasok, pelanggan, dan masyarakat. Proses tindak lanjut pelaporan diberlakukan terhadap semua laporan yang masuk, baik dari pelapor yang mencantumkan identitasnya maupun tidak (anonim).

Berbagai mekanisme pelaporan yang bisa dilakukan meliputi: melalui situs web Perseroan www.pgn.co.id sub bagian Pelaporan Pelanggaran, melalui email di etik@pgn.co.id serta menyampaikan surat resmi yang ditujukan kepada Perseroan/Komite Etik PGN. Perusahaan gencar melakukan berbagai kampanye kepada para karyawan untuk melaporkan pelanggaran, baik melalui seminar, training maupun kampanye digital email blast dan media sosial.

# PROSES WHISTLEBLOWING SYSTEM

a. Kewajiban Perusahaan menerima dan menyelesaikan Pelaporan Pelanggaran. Dalam hal ini Perusahaan:

- Wajib menerima pelaporan pelanggaran dari pihak internal maupun eksternal.

# WHISTLEBLOWING SYSTEM [2-16]

Since 2019, PGN has established a whistleblowing system that allows every individual, both internal and external, to report suspected violations of the Company's regulations, business ethics, discrimination, or fraudulent acts. The confidentiality of the informant's identity shall be protected by the Company and every report submitted will be followed up in accordance with the prevailing rules and regulations.

The implementation of the whistleblowing system refers to the Whistleblowing System Guideline Number P-002/A011 dated August 2, 2021, as an update to Guideline Number P-002/0.11 Rev.00 dated June 25, 2019. The guidelines have been uploaded on PGN's website in the Governance section.

PGN's whistleblowing system is made available not only to PGN employees but also to external parties involved in the Company's business processes such as suppliers, customers, and the public. The follow-up on these reports is applied to all incoming reports, both from whistleblowers who state their identities and those who do not (anonymous).

Various reporting mechanisms are available, including submitting reports through the Company's website www.pgn.co.id sub-section Reporting of Violation or email at etik@pgn.co.id as well as by submitting an official letter addressed to the Company/PGN Ethics Committee. The Company vigorously conducts various campaigns for employees to report violations, be it through seminars, training, or digital campaigns such as email blasts and social media.

# WHISTLEBLOWING SYSTEM PROCESS

The provisions in the Company's Whistleblowing Management are as follows:

a. The Company is obligated to accept and resolve Whistleblowing reports. In this regard, the Company:

- Must accept reports of violations from internal and external parties.
- Must accept and resolve whistleblowing reports both from whistleblowers who state their identities and those who do not (anonymous).
- Provide a channel for managing whistleblowing reports, namely through the Ethics Committee.
- The Ethics Committee will proceed with the whistleblowing reports in accordance with the context of the parties reported.
