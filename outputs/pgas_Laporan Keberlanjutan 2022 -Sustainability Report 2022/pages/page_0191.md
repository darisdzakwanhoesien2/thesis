# KEGIATAN MEMBANGUN BUDAYA TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN[A.6.a]

Activities to Build a Culture of Sustainability Governance[A.6.a]

Perusahaan secara proaktif juga melakukan berbagai inisiatif untuk meningkatkan kesadaran akan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik dan berkelanjutan, serta menjadikannya budaya yang melekat dalam internal organisasi Perseroan. Beberapa inisiatif yang dilakukan oleh PGN sepanjang 2022 mencakup:

- Sosialisasi dan internalisasi kepada seluruh insan PGN Proses sosialisasi dan internalisasi tersebut diikuti dengan uji pemahaman GCG dan Code of Conduct bagi setiap pekerja, di mana hasil uji pemahaman dinyatakan dalam Surat Pernyataan Perwira Pemahaman Pedoman Etika Usaha dan Etika Kerja serta Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Yang Baik.
- Penerapan aplikasi GOLS (GCG Online System) Aplikasi ini sudah dimulai sejak tahun 2020 lalu dan terus dikembangkan untuk menjadi sarana meningkatkan awareness GCG bagi karyawan PGN. Sepanjang tahun 2022, pengisian aplikasi GOLS oleh Perwira PGN sudah mencapai 100%. Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa kesadaran mengenai GCG telah membudaya di dalam Perwira PGN.
- Branding GCG melalui penyampaian flyer terkait GCG melalui email blast setiap 2 mingguan dan penempatan flyer pada background laptop, media sosial dan website PGN.
- Melakukan pengukuran dan penilaian terhadap penerapan GCG yang baik melalui mekanisme asesmen yang dilakukan oleh konsultan independent dengan mengacu pada Parameter SK MENTERI BUMN No. 16/S.MBU/2012 tanggal 6 Juni 2012, dan self-assessment mengacu pada parameter level negara ASEAN yaitu ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS).
- Penandatanganan Komite Bersama BOD, BOC dalam penerapan GCG sesuai prinsip Transparency, Accountability, Responsibility, Independency dan Fairness dan penerapan Etika Usaha dan Etika Kerja sesuai Budaya AKHLAK : Amanah, Kompeten, Harmonis, Loyal, Adaptif dan Kolaborasi.
- Penyelesaian rekomendasi hasil asesmen GCG secara berkala setiap triwulan dan melaporkan hasilnya kepada Manajemen serta mendiskusikan hasil tersebut kepada Komite NRGCG.
- Pembentukan Komite Keberlanjutan dan Tim Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Management Subholding Gas untuk memastikan penerapan aspek keberlanjutan berjalan baik dan tepat.

The Company also proactively carries out various initiatives to raise awareness of good and sustainable corporate governance, and to make it a culture that is inherent in the Company's internal organization. Some of the initiatives carried out by PGN throughout 2022 are as follows:

- Dissemination and internalization to all PGN employees The process of dissemination and internalization is followed by an understanding test of GCG and the Code of Conduct for each employee, in which the results of the understanding test are stated in the Employee's Statement of Understanding of the Guidelines for Business Ethics and Work Ethics and Guidelines for Good Corporate Governance.
- Implementation of GOLS application (GCG Online System) This application has been in use since 2020 and continues to be developed to become a means of increasing GCG awareness for PGN employees. Throughout 2022, the completion of the GOLS application by PGN Employees has reached 100%. This shows that awareness on GCG has become entrenched among PGN Employees.
- GCG branding via delivery of flyers related to GCG through email blasts fortnightly and placing flyers on laptop backgrounds, social media and the PGN website.
- Conduct measurement and assessment on good GCG implementation through an assessment mechanism carried out by an independent consultant with reference to the Parameters of Minister of State-Owned Enterprises (MSOEs) Decree Number 16/S.MBU/2012 dated June 6, 2012, and the self-assessment refers to the ASEAN country level parameters, namely the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS).
- Signing of the BOD, BOC Joint Committee in the implementation of GCG pursuant to the principles of Transparency, Accountability, Responsibility, Independence and Fairness and the application of Business Ethics and Work Ethics according to the AKHLAK Culture: Trustworthy, Competent, Harmonious, Loyal, Adaptive and Collaborative.
- Completion of recommendations for the GCG assessment results on a quarterly basis and reporting the results to Management and discussing these results with the NRGCG Committee.
- Establishment of a Sustainability Committee and Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Management Gas Subholding Team to ensure that the implementation of sustainability aspects runs well and appropriately.
