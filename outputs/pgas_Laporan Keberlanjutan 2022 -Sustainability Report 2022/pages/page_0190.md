# KOMITMEN
Commitment

PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk (PGN) berkomitmen untuk terus meningkatkan penerapan Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan di seluruh lini bisnis dan operasional Perseroan agar penge-lolaan aspek Lingkungan, Sosial dan Tata Kelola/LST (Environment, Social and Governance/ESG) dapat berjalan baik dan tepat sasaran. Penerapan Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan merupakan bagian dari Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (Good Corporate Governance/GCG) yang bersandar pada kepatuhan hukum dan prinsip-prinsip GCG. [3-3] [2-27]

Pelaksanaan Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan di dalam PGN terutama diterapkan melalui peraturan, kebijakan dan pedoman pendukung, di antaranya:

a. Board Manual
b. Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik
c. Pedoman Etika Usaha dan Etika Kerja
d. Pedoman Benturan Kepentingan dan Pengendalian Gratifikasi
e. Pedoman Whistleblowing System
f. Pedoman Manajemen Risiko
g. Pedoman Piagam Audit Internal
h. Pedoman Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan

i. Pedoman Tata Kelola Teknologi Informasi dan Komunikasi
j. Kebijakan Pengelolaan Informasi Perusahaan
k. Pedoman Sistem Manajemen Anti Penyuaapan (SMAP) ISO 37001
l. Pedoman Pengadaan Barang/Jasa.
m. Pedoman Penyusunan Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Perusahaan (RKAP)
n. Pedoman Pengendalian Kinerja
o. Pedoman SMK3P2LE (Sistem Manajemen K3)
p. Perjanjian Kerja Bersama
q. Pedoman Perencanaan dan Pengembangan KPI Berbasis Kriteria Penilaian Kinerja Unggul
r. Pedoman Akuntansi
s. Pedoman Pengukuran Kepuasan Pelanggan
t. Pedoman Berlangganan Gas
u. Pedoman Penyusunan Laporan Keuangan

PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk (PGN) is committed to continuously improving the implementation of Sustainability Governance in all business lines and operations of the Company so that the management of Environment, Social and Governance (ESG) aspects can run well and right on target. The implementation of Sustainability Governance is part of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) on the basis of legal compliance and GCG principles. [3-3] [2-27]

The Implementation of Sustainability Governance in PGN is mainly achieved through regulations, policies and supporting guidelines, including:

a. Board Manual
b. Guidelines for Good Corporate Governance
c. Guidelines for Business Ethics and Work Ethics
d. Guidelines for Conflict of Interest and Gratification Control
e. Whistleblowing System Guidelines
f. Risk Management Guidelines
g. Guidelines for Internal Audit Charter
h. Guidelines for Social and Environmental Responsibility
i. Guidelines for Information and Communication Technology Governance
j. Company Information Management Policy
k. Guidelines for Anti-Bribery Management System (ABMS) ISO 37001
l. Guidelines for Procurement of Goods/Services.
m. Guidelines for Drafting of Company Work Plans and Budgets (RKAP).
n. Performance Control Guidelines
o. SMK3P2LE Guidelines (K3 Management System)
p. Collective Labor Agreement
q. Guidelines for KPI Planning and Development based on Superior Performance Assessment Criteria
r. Accounting Guidelines
s. Guidelines for Measuring Customer Satisfaction
t. Gas Subscription Guidelines
u. Guidelines for Drafting of Financial Statements
