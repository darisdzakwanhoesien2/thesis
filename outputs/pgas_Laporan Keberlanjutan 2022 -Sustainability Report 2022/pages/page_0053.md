# PERTAMINA GAS NEGARA

PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk (PGN) merupakan perusahaan nasional terbesar di Indonesia yang menjadi pionir dalam bidang transportasi dan distribusi gas bumi. Perjalanan usaha Perusahaan selama lebih dari 6 dekade memberikan dampak signifikan terhadap pemenuhan kebutuhan gas bumi domestik.

Kini di tengah gelombang transisi energi global dari energi fosil ke energi bersih atau terbarukan, PGN berperan besar dalam proses transisi energi di Indonesia. Di antaranya melalui berbagai program konversi energi berbasis gas bumi atau gasifikasi di sektor strategis seperti sektor kelistrikan nasional.

PGN berkomitmen untuk mengawal agenda pembangunan nasional tersebut dengan inovasi dan terobosan dalam meningkatkan keandalan dan perluasan akses gas bumi nasional. Saat ini melalui portofolio usahanya PGN mengelola 95% infrastruktur nasional dan 91% niaga gas bumi nasional.

Selain itu sebagai aggregator gas bumi di Indonesia, PGN akan terus memperluas pembangunan infrastruktur serta meningkatkan sumber pasokan gas bumi melalui sinergi antar Subholding di Holding Migas. Dalam rangka meningkatkan keandalan dan fleksibilitas penyaluran gas bumi ke pelanggan, PGN telah mengintegrasikan infrastruktur gas bumi pada ruas pipa transmisi di Jawa dan Sumatera serta mengembangkan opsi moda transportasi gas bumi melalui virtual pipeline untuk dapat menjangkau pelanggan yang secara lokasi berada jauh dari sumber pasokan maupun infrastruktur gas bumi.

PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk (PGN) is the largest national company in Indonesia pioneering in the transportation and distribution of natural gas. In its journey of more than six decades, the Company has brought a significant impacts on meeting the domestic needs for natural gas.

In today's massive efforts to transition from fossil energy to clean or renewable energy globally, PGN plays a major role in the energy transition process in Indonesia, including through various natural gas-based energy conversion programs or gasification in strategic sectors such as the national electricity sector.

PGN is committed to overseeing the national development agenda through innovations and breakthroughs dedicated to increasing reliability and expanding access to national natural gas. Currently, through its business portfolios, PGN manages 95% of the national infrastructure and 91% of the national natural gas trade.

In addition, as a natural gas aggregator in Indonesia, PGN will continue to expand infrastructures developments and increase natural gas supply sources through synergies between Subholdings in Oil and Gas Holdings. In a bid to improve the reliability and flexibility of distributing natural gas to customers, PGN has integrated natural gas infrastructures in Java and Sumatra transmission pipelines and developed options for natural gas transportation modes through virtual pipelines to reach customers who are located far from the sources of natural gas supply and infrastructures.
