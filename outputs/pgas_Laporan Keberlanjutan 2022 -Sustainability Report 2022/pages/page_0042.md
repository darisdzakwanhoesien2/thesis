Perusahaan mengikuti Program Penilaian Peringkat Kinerja Perusahaan dalam Pengelolaan Lingkungan (PROPER) yang diselenggarakan oleh Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan Republik Indonesia sebagai bentuk evaluasi. Hasilnya sangat memuaskan di mana PT Pertamina Gas selaku anak perusahaan PGN berhasil meraih penghargaan PROPER Emas dan 4 PROPER Hijau. PROPER Emas menunjukkan bahwa Perusahaan telah melakukan pengelolaan lingkungan lebih dari yang dipersyaratkan sekaligus melakukan pengembangan kemasyarakatan secara berkesinambungan.

PGN juga mendapatkan berbagai apresiasi dan penghargaan eksternal terkait pengelolaan lingkungan. ## Aspek Sosial

Pada aspek sosial, fokus utama kami dimulai dari pekerja PGN atau yang biasa disebut Perwira Subholding Gas, sebagai salah satu tonggak utama Perseroan. Kami menerapkan praktik ketenagakerjaan sesuai ketentuan peraturan yang berlaku, memastikan setiap Perwira mendapat lingkungan kerja yang kondusif dan mampu mengeluarkan potensi terbaik mereka, menjamin K3, serta menjunjung keberagaman, kesetaraan dan hak asasi manusia (HAM) di tempat kerja.

Berbagai hal yang kami lakukan di antaranya adalah serangkaian program pengembangan kompetensi dan suksesi Perwira. Jumlah jam rata-rata pelatihan per Perwira mencapai 85 jam/orang dan terdapat 302 orang Perwira yang dipromosikan ke jabatan yang lebih tinggi di sepanjang tahun 2022. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]

Terkait remunerasi, Perusahaan telah mengikuti ketentuan Upah Minimum Provinsi (UMP) yang ditetapkan Pemerintah, serta tidak membedakan pemberian upah dan remunerasi berdasarkan jenis kelamin. PGN juga memastikan bahwa upah yang diterima mampu memenuhi kebutuhan dasar pekerja.

Lingkungan kerja yang sehat dan aman bagi seluruh Perwira juga menjadi perhatian utama Perseroan. Mengingat industri migas memiliki risiko tinggi akan K3, penerapan sistem manajemen keselamatan migas merupakan bentuk tanggung jawab Perseroan sekaligus pemenuhan hak asasi akan keselamatan Perwira. Pada tahun 2022 PGN berhasil mencapai 149.239.721 Jam Kerja Aman, atau naik dibandingkan 2021 yang mencapai 136.293.670. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]

The Company participates in the Corporate Performance Rating Assessment Program in Environmental Management (PROPER) organized by the Ministry of the Environment and Forestry of the Republic of Indonesia as a form of evaluation. The results were very satisfying with PT Pertamina Gas as a subsidiary of PGN winning the Gold PROPER award and 4 Green PROPER awards. Gold PROPER shows that the Company has carried out environmental management more than required while at the same time carrying out community development in a sustainable manner.

PGN has also received various appreciations and external awards related to environmental management, including the 2022 Social and Environmental Responsibility and Corporate Social Responsibility (TJSL and CSR) awards and the 2022 Corporate Emissions Transparency Award from Berita Satu Holding. [A.4.b.2]

## Social Aspect

On the social aspect, our main focus starts with PGN employees, known as Gas Subholding 'Perwira', as one of the Company's main pillars. We implement employment practices in accordance with the prevailing regulations, ensure that every Employee experiences a conducive work environment and is able to bring out their best potential, ensures OSH, and upholds diversity, equality and human rights in the workplace.

Among the things we have done are a series of competencies development and employees succession programs. The average number of hours of training per employee reaches 85 hours/person and there are 302 employees who are promoted to higher positions throughout 2022. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]

Regarding remuneration, the Company has complied with the Provincial Minimum Wage (UMP) regulation set by the Government and does not differentiate wages and remuneration based on gender. PGN also ensures that the earned wages were able to meet employees' basic needs.

A healthy and safe work environment for all Employees has also come into the Company's major focus. Considering that the oil and gas industry poses a high risk of OHS, the application of oil and gas safety management systems serves as the Company's form of responsibility and human rights fulfillment on employees safety. In 2022 PGN successfully achieved 149,239,721 Safe Working Hours, or an increase compared to 2021, which reached 136,293,670. [A.4.a.4][A.4.b.1]
