# TUGAS, TANGGUNG JAWAB DAN KEWENANGAN KOMITE KEBERLANJUTAN [A.5.a] [2-12]

Tugas, tanggung jawab dan kewenangan Komite Keberlanjutan secara umum adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Mengawasi, menetapkan dan memberi arahan terkait ambisi, strategi, roadmap, kebijakan, inisiatif dan ukuran kinerja terkait aspek keberlanjutan.
2. Memonitor pencapaian target dan tingkat pencapaian penerapan aspek keberlanjutan sesuai waktu yang ditentukan.
3. Memonitor pencapaian kinerja Perusahaan di aspek keberlanjutan berdasarkan penilaian pihak ke tiga, namun tidak terbatas pada ESG rating.
4. Mengarahkan dan memonitor komunikasi eksternal terkait aspek keberlanjutan.

# DUTIES, RESPONSIBILITIES, AND AUTHORITIES OF SUSTAINABILITY COMMITTEE [A.5.a] [2-12]

In general, the duties, responsibilities, and authorities of the Sustainability Committee are as follows:

1. Supervising, determining, and giving directions regarding goals, strategies, roadmaps, policies, initiatives, and performance measures related to sustainability aspects.
2. Monitoring the target achievement and the achievement rate of the implementation of sustainability according to the time set.
3. Monitoring the achievement of the Company's performance in the aspect of sustainability based on third-party assessments, but not limited to the ESG rating.
4. Directing and monitoring external communications related to sustainability aspects.
