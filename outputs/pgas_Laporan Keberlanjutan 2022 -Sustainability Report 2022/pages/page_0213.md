Based on the results of the assessments and measurements that have been carried out by the assessors and considering the results of consolidation and validation as well as management’s concerns, it is stipulated that PGN is at “Managed” maturity level or Level 4 out of 5 (five) risk management maturity levels with a *Risk Maturity Index* (RMI) score of 4.18.
