# PELATIHAN K3 [403-5] [11.9.6] [A.6.c.2.d]

PGN berkomitmen untuk memberikan pembinaan dalam rangka meningkatkan kesadaran dan kompetensi Perwira Subholding Gas, sejalan dengan kebijakan SMK3P2L-E dalam menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang sehat, aman, dan ramah lingkungan. Hal ini dilakukan agar Perwira Subholding Gas dapat mengambil tindakan preventif dan proaktif guna mencegah terjadinya penyakit akibat kerja dan kecelakaan kerja. Pelatihan ini didasarkan pada analisis kebutuhan pelatihan atau Learning Need Analysis (LNA) yang telah disusun secara khusus untuk memenuhi kebutuhan Perusahaan. Sepanjang tahun 2022, pelatihan K3 yang dilaksanakan adalah sebagai berikut:

6. Opening consulting services with company doctors so that Subholding Gas Employees feel connected and supported when exposed to COVID-19.
7. Providing dissemination and education about mitigating the transmission of COVID-19 through webinars, banners, leaflets, e-mails, and guidebooks to Gas Subholding Employees, Work Partners, and Families.
8. Implementing a system for monitoring and reporting new cases of COVID-19. Reporting of new cases is followed by contact tracing and testing.
9. Providing a Safe House for Gas Subholding Employees/work partners who are contracting COVID-19 and whose residence does not allow them to carry out independent isolation.

# OHS TRAINING [403-5] [11.9.6] [A.6.c.2.d]

PGN is committed to providing coaching in order to increase the awareness and competence of Gas Subholding Employees, in line with the SMK3P2L-E policy in creating a healthy, safe and environmentally friendly work environment. This is done so that Gas Subholding Employees can take preventive and proactive actions to prevent work-related illnesses and work accidents. This training is based on a training needs analysis or Learning Need Analysis (LNA) that has been specially prepared to meet the needs of the Company.
