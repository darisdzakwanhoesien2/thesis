# IMPLIKASI FINANSIAL SERTA RISIKO DAN PELUANG LAIN AKIBAT DARI PERUBAHAN IKLIM [201-2] [11.2.2]

Dampak perubahan iklim berpotensi mengganggu aktivitas operasional Perseroan. Oleh karena itu PGN berupaya merespon dampak perubahan iklim melalui berbagai inisiatif. Upaya dan inisiatif mitigasi dan adaptasi perubahan iklim dapat dilihat pada Bab Meningkatkan Keberlanjutan Usaha dan Bab Meningkatkan Kinerja Lingkungan pada Laporan Keberlanjutan ini.

Evaluation on risk management is made periodically, starting from the level of function, work unit, up to the top management level. This is reflected in the routine agenda of the Board of Directors' meetings which always include risk management in the discussion agenda. The same goes to the establishment of a Risk Management Committee at the Board of Directors level which serves as a form of strengthening and completeness of risk management implementation across all lines of the company.

# FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS AND OTHER RISKS AND OPPORTUNITIES DUE TO CLIMATE CHANGE [201-2] [11.2.2]

The impact of climate change has the potential to disrupt the Company's operational activities. Therefore, PGN seeks to respond to the impacts of climate change through various initiatives. Efforts and initiatives to mitigate and adapt to climate change can be seen in Chapter on Enhancing Business Sustainability and Chapter on Enhancing Environmental Performance in this Sustainability Report.
