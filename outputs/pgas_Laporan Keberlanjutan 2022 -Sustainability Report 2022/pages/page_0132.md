# KOMITMEN

## COMMITMENT

PGN berkomitmen menjalankan kinerja sosial keberlanjutan yang bertanggung jawab dengan mengintegrasikan aspek sosial, lingkungan, dan ekonomis ke dalam berbagai program yang dijalankan. Sebagai panduan PGN mengadopsi standar internasional ISO 26000 yang berisi acuan dan prinsip-prinsip dasar dalam tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan yang diakui secara global. [3-3]

Dalam penerapan ISO 26000, PGN memperhatikan tujuh prinsip dasar dalam tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan, yaitu:

1. Akuntabilitas.
2. Transparansi.
3. Perilaku etis.
4. Menghormati kepentingan stakeholder.
5. Menghormati aturan hukum.
6. Menghormati norma perilaku yang berlaku internasional.
7. Menghormati hak asasi manusia.

Melalui penerapan ISO 26000, PGN berupaya menciptakan nilai tambah bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Dalam hal pengelolaan ketenagakerjaan, PGN berkomitmen memberikan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan sehat bagi karyawan, serta memastikan setiap karyawan mendapatkan perlakuan yang setara.

Terkait pelanggan, PGN berupaya memberikan layanan pelanggan terbaik dengan memastikan keandalan infrastruktur yang aman dan berkualitas, serta meluncurkan berbagai program yang memudahkan pelanggan.

Perusahaan juga memperhatikan kesejahteraan masyarakat di sekitar wilayah beroperasi dengan menerapkan program-program pengembangan masyarakat yang bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kualitas hidup masyarakat setempat. Melalui program tersebut, Perusahaan membangun dan meningkatkan infrastruktur di sekitar wilayah operasional serta memberikan pelatihan dan dukungan kegiatan ekonomi masyarakat yang bertujuan meningkatkan kesejahteraan mereka secara mandiri.

PGN is committed to carrying out the social performance with responsible sustainability by integrating social, environmental and economic aspects into a host of programs it runs. As a guide, PGN adopts the international standard ISO 26000 which contains references and basic principles of globally recognized corporate social responsibility. [3-3]

In the adoption of ISO 26000, PGN takes into account seven basic principles of corporate social responsibility, namely:

1. Accountability.
2. Transparency.
3. Ethical behavior.
4. Respect the interests of stakeholders.
5. Respect the rule of law.
6. Respect the norms of behavior that apply internationally.
7. Respect human rights.

Through the adoption of ISO 26000, PGN seeks to create added value for all stakeholders. In terms of manpower management, PGN is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment for employees, and ensuring that every employee receives equal treatment.

Regarding customers, PGN strives to provide excellent customer service by ensuring the reliability of safe and quality infrastructure, as well as launching various programs that provide ease for customers.

The Company also pays attention to the welfare of the communities around its operating areas by implementing community development programs that aim to improve the quality of life of the local community. Through this program, the Company builds and improves infrastructure around operational areas as well as provides training and support for community economic activities aimed at improving their welfare independently.
