### PGN GROUP BANTU KORBAN GEMPA BUMI CIANJUR

PGN Group Helps Cianjur Earthquake Victims

PGN Group menunjukkan kepedulian terhadap masyarakat yang terkena musibah gempa bumi di Kabupaten Cianjur, Provinsi Jawa Barat dengan memberikan bantuan sembako. Melalui aksi ini, PGN Group menegaskan komitmennya dalam memberikan kontribusi nyata bagi masyarakat di tengah situasi bencana.

PGN Group shows concern for the people affected by the earthquake in Cianjur Regency, West Java Province by providing basic food assistance. Through this action, the PGN Group emphasizes its commitment to making a real contribution to society in the midst of a disaster.

## 25 November

### SUBHOLDING GAS JALANKAN KONSEP PERTANIAN BERKELANJUTAN

Gas Subholding Executes Sustainable Agriculture

Pertagas Operation Kalimantan Area bersama masyarakat dalam program Pertani Mandiri Jalur Pipa Sidrap (TAMAN Sidrap) fokus menjalankan konsep pertanian holtikultura semi organik yang berkelanjutan dan terintegrasi untuk lahan gambut di sekitar wilayah tersebut. Selain itu, petani Sidrap berinovasi membuat produk pertanian mandiri seperti Pestisida Nabati, Pupuk Organik Cair, Fungisida Buatan Ramah Tanah, Ramuan Organik Nutrisi 3 in 1 dan TB Formula untuk menurunkan pH tanah dan efisiensi biaya pertanian. Pertagas Operation Kalimantan Area together with the community in the Sidrap Pipeline Independent Farming program (TAMAN Sidrap) focuses on carrying out the concept of sustainable and integrated semi-organic horticulture farming for peatlands around the area. In addition, Sidrap farmers are innovating to make independent agricultural products such as Vegetable Pesticides, Liquid Organic Fertilizers, Soil Friendly Artificial Fungicides, 3 in 1 Nutrient Organic Potions and TB Formulas to lower soil pH and improve farming cost efficiency.

## 1 December

### PGN BERDAYAKAN MASYARAKAT KELOLA MINYAK JELANTAH

PGN Empowers Communities to Manage Used Cooking Oil

PGN memberdayakan ibu-ibu Manjalita di Desa Kalitengah untuk mengelola bank minyak jelantah dan mengelola proses pengolahan minyak jelantah bekas tersebut. Bank minyak jelantah ini berfungsi untuk mengumpulkan minyak jelantah bekas yang akan diolah menjadi produk bernilai ekonomi tinggi seperti sabun dan biodiesel. Melalui program ini, diharapkan masyarakat sekitar dapat memanfaatkan limbah rumah tangga secara lebih efektif dan efisien, serta memperoleh manfaat ekonomi dari hasil pengolahan limbah tersebut.

PGN empowered the Manjalita (Communities to Manage Used Cooking Oil) women in Kalitengah Village to manage the used cooking oil bank and manage the processing of the used cooking oil. This used cooking oil bank functions to collect used cooking oil which will be processed into products with high economic value such as soap and biodiesel. Through this program, it is hoped that the surrounding community can utilize household waste more effectively and efficiently, as well as obtain economic benefits from the processing of this waste.
