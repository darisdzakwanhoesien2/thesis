# LAYANAN DAN PROMOSI KESEHATAN
[403-3] [403-6][11.9.4] [11.9.7]

PGN menyelenggarakan berbagai program untuk meningkatkan kesehatan Perwira Subholding Gas meliputi program preventif, kuratif, rehabilitatif, dan promotif yang telah sesuai dengan persyaratan hukum yang berlaku. Program kesehatan kerja yang telah diselenggarakan, mencakup:

1. Preventif:
a. Identifikasi aspek dan dampak faktor lingkungan kerja.
b. Penilaian Risiko Kesehatan atau Health Risk Assessment (HRA).
c. Pelaksanaan Medical Check Up (MCU).
d. Penentuan tingkat kesehatan (fitness status).
e. Pelatihan Pertolongan Pertama Pada Kecelakaan (P3K).
f. Pengelolaan hygiene dan sanitasi lingkungan kerja.
g. Pelaksanaan imunisasi.
h. Wellness program.

2. Promotif:
a. Seminar kesehatan kerja.
b. Konsultasi khusus.

3. Kuratif:
a. Pelayanan pengobatan di Perusahaan.
b. Pelayanan rujukan.
c. Penanganan kondisi darurat medis.

4. Rehabilitatif:
a. Penanganan rehabilitasi.
b. Bekerja kembali sesudah sakit.

# HEALTH SERVICES AND PROMOTION
[403-3] [403-6][11.9.4] [11.9.7]

PGN organizes various programs to improve the health of Gas Subholding Employees including preventive, curative, rehabilitative and promotive programs that comply with applicable legal requirements. Occupational health programs that have been held include:

1. Preventive:
a. Identification of aspects and impacts of work environment factors.
b. Health Risk Assessment (HRA).
c. Implementation of Medical Check Up (MCU).
d. Determination of the level of health (fitness status).
e. First Aid Training in Accidents (P3K).
f. Management of hygiene and sanitation of the work environment.
g. Implementation of immunization.
h. Wellness programme.

2. Promotive:
a. Occupational health seminars.
b. Special consultation.

3. Curative:
a. Medical services at the Company.
b. Referral service.
c. Handling medical emergencies.

4. Rehabilitative:
a. Handling rehabilitation.
b. Back to work after illness.
