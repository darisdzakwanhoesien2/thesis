# KEBIJAKAN REMUNERASI DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI

Penetapan remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris mengacu kepada Peraturan Menteri BUMN No. PER-04/MBU/2014 tentang Pedoman Penetapan Penghasilan Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, dan Dewan Pengawas Badan Usaha Milik Negara sebagaimana terakhir diubah dengan Peraturan Menteri BUMN No. PER-13/MBU/09/2021

President Commissioner of PGN, Mr. Arcandra Tahar and PGN's Board of Directors have expertise, competence and work experience in the Oil and Gas sector pursuant to the Company's core business. The entire PGN Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors do not own Company shares. Further description of the profiles of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors can be seen in the Company Profile Chapter in PGN's 2022 Annual Report.

Detailed information regarding the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, GMS and other Governance organs such as Committees of the Board of Commissioners, including explanations on independence, prevention of conflicts of interest, concurrent positions, job descriptions, work experience, responsibilities and authorities, nomination process, and other information can be seen in the Governance Chapter in PGN's 2022 Annual Report.
