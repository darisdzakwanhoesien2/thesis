# PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI [404-2] [11.10.7] [A.6.c.2.d]

Pengembangan kompetensi PGN dilakukan untuk mempertahankan standar dan kualitas Perwira PGN maupun kebutuhan penguasaan kompetensi baru. Pengembangan kompetensi karyawan dilakukan melalui kegiatan pelatihan dan pendidikan untuk meraih manfaat jangka pendek dan jangka panjang. Manfaat jangka pendek adalah untuk pemenuhan keahlian dan keterampilan guna mendukung produktivitas, keselamatan, dan keberlangsungan operasional PGN. Sedangkan manfaat jangka panjang adalah untuk memastikan pelaksanaan strategi dan keberlanjutan kepemimpinan jangka panjang.

Sesuai tahapan Rencana Jangka Panjang Perusahaan (RJPP) dan inisiatif Holding BUMN Migas dan peran PGN sebagai Subholding Gas, PGN terus meningkatkan program pengembangan pekerja yang komprehensif dan konsisten dengan program-program pendidikan dan pelatihan (diklat) inhouse maupun public training, sertifikasi dan sharing knowledge. [3-3] [11.10.1] [11.11.1]

Sepanjang tahun 2022 PGN menyelenggarakan program-program pengembangan kompetensi teknis yang didesain melalui pendekatan experiential learning, dengan memadukan metode 70% assignment, 20% coaching, dan 10% in-class training yang difokuskan untuk level Staf hingga Manajer pada fungsi-fungsi core business sebagai berikut:

1. Gas Management, Operation and Reliability Fundamental merupakan program pengembangan kompetensi teknis bagi Perwira Subholding Gas level Staf dan Senior Staf pada rumpun jabatan Operation dan Gas System Management.
2. Junior Marketing and Sales Development Program merupakan program pengembangan kompetensi teknis bagi Pekerja level Staf dan Senior Staf pada rumpun jabatan Marketing dan Sales.
3. Special Development to Unleash Performance (SUPER) merupakan program pengembangan kompetensi teknis bagi Pekerja level Staf dan Senior Staf yang bertujuan untuk mendukung pencapaian program strategis Perusahaan serta memperkuat kompetensi inti bisnis gas. Pada pelaksanaan program di tahun 2022, peserta diberikan pembekalan mengenai kompetensi inti komersialisasi gas bumi untuk selanjutnya peserta diterjunkan langsung untuk melaksanakan aktivitas penjualan gas bumi.
4. Gas Academy LNG Project Development, merupakan program pengembangan kompetensi teknis yang fokus pada penguatan kompetensi inti bisnis gas, khususnya terkait dengan LNG. Pada tahun 2022, fokus Gas Academy pada pengembangan kompetensi yang mendukung bisnis LNG.

# COMPETENCY DEVELOPMENT [404-2] [11.10.7] [A.6.c.2.d]

PGN's competency development is carried out to maintain the standard and quality of PGN Employees, as well as the need to master new competencies. Employee competency development is accomplished through training and education activities with the goal of achieving short-term and long-term benefits. The short-term benefits include acquiring expertise and skills to enhance PGN's productivity, safety, and operational sustainability. Meanwhile, the long-term benefits include ensuring strategy implementation and long-term leadership sustainability.

In accordance with the Company's Long Term Plan (RJPP), the Oil and Gas Holding SOEs initiative, and PGN's role as Gas Subholding, PGN continues to improve comprehensive and consistent employee development programs with in-house and public learning and training programs, certification, and knowledge sharing sessions. [3-3] [11.10.1] [11.11.1]

Throughout 2022 PGN organized technical competency development programs designed through an experiential learning approach by combining the 70% assignment, 20% coaching, and 10% in-class training method that focused on Staff to Manager level in the following core business functions:

1. Gas Management, Operation, and Reliability Fundamentals is a technical competency development program for Gas Subholding Employees at Staff level and Senior Staff level in the Operations and Gas System Management position groups.
2. Junior Marketing and Sales Development Program is a technical competency development program for Employees at Staff and Senior Staff level in the Marketing and Sales position group.
3. Special Development to Unleash Performance (SUPER) is a technical competency development program for Employees at Staff and Senior Staff levels aiming to support the achievement of the Company's strategic programs and strengthen the core competencies of the gas business. During the program's implementation in 2022, participants will be equipped with the core competencies of natural gas commercialization, for which participants will be directly deployed to carry out natural gas sales activities.
4. Gas Academy LNG Project Development is a technical competency development program that focuses on strengthening the core competencies of the gas business, particularly in relation to LNG. In 2022, Gas Academy concentrated on developing competency that supports the LNG business.
