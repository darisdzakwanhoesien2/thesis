Program Budaya AKHLAK Subholding Gas secara konsisten dilakukan dan dievaluasi pelaksanaannya setiap 1 (satu) bulan sekali melalui self-assessment. Pelaksanaan self-assessment dilakukan di setiap awal bulan oleh setiap Perwira Ksatriya (Pertamina Wira Komunitas Internalisasi Budaya) yang ada di Satuan Kerja/Anak Perusahaan/Afiliasi. Bagi 3 (tiga) Satuan Kerja/Anak Perusahaan/Afiliasi yang mendapatkan skor self-assessment tertinggi di bulan berjalan akan mendapatkan apresiasi untuk menambah motivasi dan semangat dalam mengimplementasikan program budaya AKHLAK.

Berdasarkan hasil self-assessment yang telah dilakukan, Perusahaan mengadakan Culture Award setiap 1 tahun sekali yang bertujuan untuk memberikan apresiasi bagi Satuan Kerja/Anak Perusahaan/Afiliasi yang telah mengimplementasikan Budaya AKHLAK secara sungguhsungguh dan konsisten. Kegiatan ini juga melibatkan seluruh Satuan Kerja/Anak Perusahaan/Afiliasi di lingkungan Subholding Gas.

Pada tahun 2022 PGN telah mengadakan Culture Award, dengan kategori penghargaan antara lain The Best Perwira Ksatriya 2022, Top 3 Perwira Ksatriya in Working Unit dan Top 3 Perwira Ksatriya in Subsidiary and Affiliation. Kegiatan Culture Award diharapkan menjadi penyemangat dan motivasi bagi seluruh Perwira Subholding Gas dalam mengimplementasikan budaya perusahaan, hal ini pun dapat berdampak bagi perusahaan untuk mempertahankan budaya yang positif dan berkelanjutan.

The AKHLAK Gas Subholding Cultural Program is consistently carried out, and its implementation is evaluated every month through self-assessment. The Self-assessment is carried out at the beginning of each month by each Ksatriya Employee (Pertamina Wira Budaya Internalization Community) in the Work Unit/Subsidiaries/Affiliates. The 3 (three) Work Units/Subsidiaries/Affiliates that obtain the highest self-assessment scores in the current month will receive appreciation aimed to increase motivation and enthusiasm in implementing the AKHLAK cultural program.

Based on the results of the self-assessment that has been carried out, the Company holds a Culture Award once every year which aims to extend appreciation to Work Units/Subsidiaries/Affiliates that have implemented the AKHLAK Culture seriously and consistently. This activity also involves all Work Units/Subsidiaries/Affiliates within the Gas Subholding institution.

In 2022, PGN held a Culture Award, with award categories including The Best Ksatriya Employee in 2022, Top 3 Ksatriya Employees in Working Unit and Top 3 Ksatriya Employees in Subsidiary and Affiliate. The Culture Award is expected to be an encouragement and motivation for all Gas Subholding Employees in implementing the corporate culture. It can also have an impact on the company to maintain a positive and sustainable culture.

In 2022, PGN's Work Units and Subsidiaries also take part in the AKHLAK Living Core Values (LCV) Assessment conducted by Pertamina to see the extent of the leader's commitment to the implementation of the AKHLAK cultural program. The assessment was carried out by all Vice Presidents (VP) and General Managers (GM) in the Pertamina Group.
