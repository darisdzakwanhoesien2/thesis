# TANGGUNG JAWAB TERHADAP LAPORAN KEBERLANJUTAN

Perseroan melalui pelibatan pemangku kepentingan telah menyepakati 21 topik material yang seluruhnya dilaporkan kepada Direksi. Direksi memberikan persetujuan terhadap topik material tersebut dan menyatakan bertanggung jawab atas isi Laporan Keberlanjutan yang dinyatakan dalam Lembar Pernyataan dengan ditanda tangani oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.[2-14]

# KOMITE KEBERLANJUTAN

Untuk mempermudah koordinasi dan komunikasi dalam pengelolaan aspek keberlanjutan, PGN membentuk Komite Keberlanjutan pada 5 September 2022 berdasarkan Keputusan Direksi PGN Nomor 037200.K/OT.00/PD0/2022. Komite ini bertugas mengawasi, menetapkan serta mengarahkan kinerja Perusahaan terkait aspek keberlanjutan, termasuk memastikan penerapannya dan memonitor pencapaian target.

# STRUKTUR ORGANISASI KOMITE KEBERLANJUTAN[2-9-b][A.5.a]

Komite Keberlanjutan dipimpin oleh Direktur Utama dan didukung oleh Pejabat Penanggung Jawab Bidang yang telah ditentukan sesuai keahliannya, untuk memastikan implementasi program keberlanjutan berjalan baik di fungsi dan unit bisnis masing-masing. Komite bersama Pejabat Penanggung Jawab Bidang secara rutin melakukan rapat untuk mengevaluasi kinerja masing-masing bidang. [2-13]

# RESPONSIBILITY FOR SUSTAINABILITY REPORT

The Company through stakeholder engagement has agreed on 21 material topics which were all reported to the Board of Directors. The Board of Directors gives approval for these material topics and states that they are responsible for the contents of the Sustainability Report which is stated in the Statement Sheet signed by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors.[2-14]

# SUSTAINABILITY COMMITTEE

To provide ease of coordination and communication in managing sustainability aspects, PGN established a Sustainability Committee on September 5, 2022 based on Decree of the PGN Board of Directors Number 037200.K/OT.00/PD0/2022. This committee is tasked with supervising, determining and directing the Company's performance related to sustainability aspects, including ensuring its implementation and monitoring target achievement.

# SUSTAINABILITY COMMITTEE ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE[2-9-b][A.5.a]

The Sustainability Committee is helmed by the President Director and supported by the Officials in Charge of Fields who have been determined according to their expertise, to ensure good implementation of the sustainability program in their respective functions and business units. The Committee together with the Officials in Charge of Division holds meetings to evaluate the performance of each division on a regular basis. [2-13]
