# SOSIALISASI KEBIJAKAN ANTIKORUPSI UNTUK KARYAWAN DAN MITRA BISNIS

Sepanjang tahun 2022 Perusahaan telah melakukan sosialisasi dan/atau pelatihan terkait anti korupsi termasuk memastikan bahwa kebijakan dan prosedur anti korupsi juga sampai kepada semua pemasok dan mitra bisnis. Di antaranya dengan mewajibkan penandatanganan pernyataan Pakta Integritas oleh pemasok dan mitra bisnis untuk mencegah terjadinya kecurangan dan ketidaksesuaian dalam proses pengadaan barang dan jasa. [3-3][11.20.1]

Pakta Integritas berisi komitmen untuk beritikad baik dalam pelaksanaan pengadaan barang dan jasa, prinsip kehati-hatian, bebas dari conflict of interest, serta sesuai dengan ketentuan dan perundang-undangan.

Perseroan juga telah melakukan Sosialisasi Aplikasi Vendor Management System serta Penerapan GCG dan Sistem Manajemen Anti Penyuapan (SMAP) ISO 37001 kepada penyedia barang dan jasa. Selama periode pelaporan, PGN telah menyelenggarakan 11 kali sosialisasi/pelatihan terkait anti korupsi yang diikuti oleh total 604 insan PGN dan 1 kali komunikasi kebijakan anti korupsi kepada rekanan yang diikuti oleh 229 peserta dari 150 perusahaan.

Untuk meningkatkan kesadaran antikorupsi dan penyuapan, Perseroan juga memanfaatkan media e-mail dan kanal digital serta situs web Perseroan. Di antaranya dengan melakukan kampanye antikorupsi melalui flyer, konten media sosial dan email blast. Kemudian Perusahaan juga memuat pedoman whistleblowing system (WBS), pedoman gratifikasi/benturan kepentingan dan pedoman SMAP di situs web PGN-bagian Tata Kelola, agar bisa diakses oleh semua pihak.

# DISSEMINATION OF ANTI-CORRUPTION POLICY FOR EMPLOYEES AND BUSINESS PARTNERS

Throughout 2022, the Company has carried out dissemination and/or training related to anti-corruption, including ensuring that anti-corruption policies and procedures reach all suppliers and business partners. The Company requires the signing of an Integrity Pact statement by suppliers and business partners to prevent fraud and non-compliance in the process of procuring goods and services. [3-3][11.20.1]

The Integrity Pact contains a commitment to act in good faith in the implementation of the procurement of goods and services, the principle of prudence, free from conflict of interest, and in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

The Company has also disseminated the Vendor Management System Application and the Implementation of GCG and the ISO 37001 Anti-Bribery Management System (ABMS) to goods and service providers. During the reporting period, PGN has held 11 anti-corruption-related dissemination/training sessions attended by a total of 604 PGN employees and 1 anti-corruption policy communication to partners which was attended by 229 participants from 150 companies.

To improve awareness of anti-corruption and bribery, the Company also utilizes e-mail and digital channels as well as the Company's website. The company carries out anti-corruption campaigns through flyers, social media content and email blasts. The Company also displays guidelines for the whistleblowing system (WBS), guidelines for gratification/conflict of interest and ABMS guidelines on the PGN website - the Governance section, so that they can be accessed by all parties.
