# TIM ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL &amp; GOVERNANCE (ESG)

Demi memastikan bahwa arah perubahan Perusahaan ke depan sesuai dengan manajemen ESG yang baik, berorientasi kepada lingkungan/eco-friendly, serta sesuai dengan kaidah dan framework yang tepat, PGN juga membentuk Tim ESG berdasarkan Keputusan Direksi PGN Nomor 037201.K/OT.00/PD0/2022 pada 5 September 2022. Tim ini terdiri dari berbagai bisnis unit yang dikepalai oleh Project Sponsor yang berisi Direktur Strategi dan Pengembangan Bisnis, Direktur Keuangan dan Manajemen Risiko serta Direktur SDM dan Penunjang Bisnis.

5. Giving directions regarding the implementation of sustainability-based corporate external financing, including but not limited to Sustainability Linked Financing, Green Financing, Transition Financing, and other financing schemes.
6. Coordinating with the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, Directorates, or Sub Holdings in implementing sustainability aspects.
7. Carrying out the functions of the Social and Environmental Responsibility (SER) Committee of SOEs as stipulated in the MSOEs Regulation of the Republic of Indonesia Number PER-05/MBU/04/2021 dated April 8, 2021, as follows:

a. Securing coordination between directorates and/or work units/units to formulate goals and guidelines for the implementation of the SER Programs of SOEs.
b. Mapping and preparing the SER Programs of SOEs.
c. Assisting the Board of Directors in monitoring and evaluating the implementation of the SER Programs of SOEs.

On the other side, the special duties, responsibilities, and authorities of the Sustainability Committee are as follows:

1. The Chairperson and Members of the Sustainability Committee are authorized to decide and give directions on goals, strategies, roadmaps, policies, initiatives, and performance measures related to the implementation of sustainability.
2. The Secretary to the Sustainability Committee carries out the directions of the Committee Chairperson and its Members regarding sustainability management and performs tasks, including planning, monitoring, evaluating the implementation of sustainability, external reporting, external assessment, as well as ensuring PGN's involvement in sustainability initiatives at the national and international levels.
3. Officials in Charge of Division provide suggestions and input according to their respective areas of expertise, as well as implement sustainability programs within their respective functions and business units.

# ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, &amp; GOVERNANCE (ESG) TEAM

To ensure that the Company's future directions of change align with good ESG management, is environmentally oriented/eco-friendly, and complies with the right rules and frameworks, PGN has also formed an ESG Team based on BOD's Decision of PGN Number 037201.K/OT.00/PD0/2022 dated September 5, 2022. This team comprises various business units led by the Project Sponsor, including the Director of Strategy and Business Development, the Director of Finance and Risk Management, and the Director of HR and Corporate Services.
