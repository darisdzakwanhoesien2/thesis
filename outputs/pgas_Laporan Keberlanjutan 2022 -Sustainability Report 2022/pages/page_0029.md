## 18 October

### PGN DAN KIS KEMBANGKAN BISNIS BIOMETHANE

PGN and KIS Develop Biomethane Business

PGN dan KIS Group menandatangani Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) untuk kerja sama penyusunan bersama studi kelayakan terkait dengan pengembangan bisnis Biomethane dari pembangunan sampai pengoperasian pabrik sehingga Biomethane dapat dialirkan hingga ke pelanggan baik dalam bentuk Bio-CNG maupun injeksi ke dalam pipa PGN. Selain itu kedua perusahaan menjajaki investasi bersama dalam rangka pembangunan pabrik Biomethane dan fasilitas pendukung lainnya serta kajian bersama terkait dengan sertifikasi karbon seperti cara memperolehnya dan cara memperdagangkannya. KIS Group adalah perusahaan yang berpengalaman di bidang Biogas dan telah berhasil mengembangkan teknologi ZPHBO (Zero Pollution Higher Biogas).

PGN and KIS Group inked a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) for collaboration in joint preparation of a feasibility study related to the development of the Biomethane business from construction to factory operation so that Biomethane can be channeled to customers both in the form of Bio-CNG and injection into PGN pipes. In addition, the two companies are exploring joint investment in the context of building a Biomethane factory and other supporting facilities as well as joint studies related to carbon certification, such as how to obtain it and how to trade it. KIS Group is a company experienced in the field of Biogas and has successfully developed Zero Pollution Higher Biogas (ZPHBO) technology.

# 19 Oktober

## 19 October

### PGN DAN TOYOTA TSUSHO INDONESIA KEMBANGKAN RNG

PGN and Toyota Tsusho Indonesia Develop RNG

PGN dan Toyota Tsusho Indonesia menandatangani Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) yang berisi kesepakatan tentang pengembangan Renewable Natural Gas (RNG). Kerja sama ini diharapkan dapat meningkatkan pemanfaatan sumber daya energi terbarukan di Indonesia serta mengurangi emisi gas rumah kaca dari sektor transportasi.

PGN and Toyota Tsusho Indonesia inked a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) which contains an agreement regarding the development of Renewable Natural Gas (RNG). This collaboration is expected to increase the use of renewable energy resources in Indonesia and reduce greenhouse gas emissions from the transportation sector.

## 24 November

### PGN MELALUI PERTAGAS RAIH TIGA PENGHARGAAN ISDA

PGN through Pertagas Wins Three ISDA Awards

PGN melalui Pertagas, berhasil meraih tiga penghargaan di ajang Indonesian Sustainable Development Goals Award (ISDA) 2022. Penghargaan ini menjadi bukti bahwa Subholding Gas Pertamina telah melakukan program Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) dan Community Involvement &amp; Development (CID) yang memberikan manfaat bagi masyarakat luas.

PGN through Pertagas, won three awards at the 2022 Indonesian Sustainable Development Goals Award (ISDA). This award is proof that Pertamina Gas Subholding has carried out Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and Community Involvement &amp; Development (CID) programs, which benefits the wider community.
