6. PGN Sektor Darat

Layanan gas bumi melalui program konversi transportasi sektor darat berbahan bakar gas, pengembangan Stasiun Pengisian Bahan Bakar Gas (SPBG) dengan target pertumbuhan sekitar di atas 15% dengan potensi demand kurang lebih 10.000 armada LNG Trucking. 6. PGN Land Sector

Natural gas services through a gas-firedland transportation conversion program, development of Gas Fuel Filling Stations (SPBG) with a growth target of more than 15% with a potential demand of approximately 10,000 LNG Trucking fleets. Currently, PGN, through its subsidiary, Gagas Energi Indonesia, has operated 12 SPBGs and 4 mobile refueling units (MRUs) to support gas utilization for land transportation.

Natural gas services through the provision of alternative energy for communities located outside the reach of PGN's pipeline and non-pipeline infrastructure (empowerment of local resources).

8. Going Global

PGN's business development in LNG trading in Asia.

In overseeing the implementation of the Sapta PGN program, the Company continues to improve the management of natural gas infrastructure in an integrated manner in downstream natural gas business processes starting from procuring natural gas supplies from various sources to transmission/transportation. During the reporting period, PGN initiated a number of programs in order to strengthen downstream natural gas infrastructure as follows:

1. Increase the gas supply portfolio

To ensure the availability of gas supply and continuity of gas supply, PGN is constantly embarking on the potentials of new gas sources such as from HCML originating from 3M and MAC Fields which are currently being developed, Petrochina International Jabung Ltd. from the Jabung Block, Repsol Sakakemang B.V. from the Sakakemang Block, Conrad Petroleum Ltd. from the West Natuna Basin, and other potential sources of gas supply.

PGN is also discussing the extension of the existing supply contracts, which will expire. Furthermore, PGN is currently in the novation process of gas supply contracts from PT Pertamina (Persero) and PEP Cepu from the Jambaran Tiung Biru Field (PEPC JTB) of 172 MMSCFD. This PEPC JTB gas supply will be used to meet the needs of PLN in East Java and Central Java, PT Petrokimia Gresik, as well as industrial customers of PGN Group. PGN also searches for sources of gas supply with the LNG mode to anticipate a decline in the availability of piped gas supplies in the existing PGN operational areas and for the development of new areas that do not have gas sources in the local area.
