# PROFIL LAPORAN

## Report Profile

Laporan Keberlanjutan 2022 PGN merupakan Laporan Keberlanjutan ke-13 yang diterbitkan sejak tahun 2010. Laporan ini diterbitkan setiap tahun bersamaan dengan Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan.

Laporan yang berisi komitmen, kebijakan, program, inisiatif, pencapaian, dan tantangan perusahaan di bidang Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (LST) ini dibuat berpedoman kepada POJK No. 51/POJK .03.2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan Bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten dan Perusahaan Publik serta ‘sesuai dengan’ Standar Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) 2021 dan GRI 11: Oil and Gas Sector 2021.

The 2022 Sustainability Report of PGN is the 13th Sustainability Report published since its first issuance in 2010. This report is issued annually concomitant with the Company’s Annual Report.

This report contains the Company’s commitments, policies, programs, initiatives, achievements, and challenges in the area of Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG). This report was prepared based on Regulation of the Financial Services Authority (POJK) Number 51/POJK.03.2017 on the Application of Sustainable Finance to Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Listed Companies and in accordance with the 2021 Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards, and GRI 11: Oil and Gas Sector 2021 Standards.

## DAFTAR ENTITAS DALAM LAPORAN KEUANGAN TERKONSOLIDASI [2-2]

### LIST OF ENTITIES WITHIN THE CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS [2-2]

Laporan ini memuat kinerja keberlanjutan PGN beserta entitas anak atau entitas terasosiasi. Daftar entitas anak dan afiliasi dapat dilihat pada Bab Profil Perusahaan.

Data keuangan yang terdapat dalam bab Meningkatkan Kinerja Usaha dan data karyawan dalam bab Meningkatkan Kinerja Sosial merupakan data konsolidasian dengan anak perusahaan, sedangkan data lainnya merupakan data yang mencakup entitas induk, kecuali terdapat penjelasan berbeda pada data tersebut.

This report contains the sustainability performance of PGN, along with its subsidiaries or associated entities. The Chapter of the Company Profile exhibits the list of PGN’s subsidiaries and affiliates.

The financial data presented in the Enhancing Business Performance chapter and the employee data presented in the Enhancing Social Performance chapter represent consolidated data with that of the subsidiaries. Meanwhile, other data represents data that covers the holding entity, except where duly stated otherwise within this data.

## RUANG LINGKUP DAN BATASAN LAPORAN SCOPE AND BOUNDARIES OF THE REPORT

Hal yang termasuk dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan ini adalah segala kegiatan terkait operasional PGN di kantor pusat, kantor perwakilan, dan seluruh area operasional entitas yang termasuk dalam konsolidasi Laporan Keuangan Perusahaan.

This Sustainability Report includes all activities related to PGN’s operations at the head office, representative offices, and all operation regions of the entity, which are included in the Company’s consolidated financial statements.
