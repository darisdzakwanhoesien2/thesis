GENET REPORT

Komitmen bersama ini merupakan langkah nyata mewujudkan aspirasi Pertamina menjadi perusahaan energi global yang ramah lingkungan dan berkontribusi dalam target perubahan iklim. Selain itu juga sebagai bentuk dukungan Pertamina terhadap agenda transisi energi Pemerintah Indonesia dalam mencapai target NZE pada 2060 melalui:

## STAKEHOLDER MANAGEMENT

Melakukan Stakeholder Engagement secara aktif untuk bersinergi dalam pencapaian target NZE.

## PENGEMBANGAN BISNIS PASAR KARBON

Pengembangan dan Implementasi Bisnis Pasar Karbon Pertamina (Internal &amp; Eksternal).

## MODEL BISNIS

Inovasi model bisnis yang agile, kooperatif, dan adaptif untuk mendukung upaya dekarbonisasi dan new green business. Membangun kemitraan global yang terbuka dan setara dalam pengembangan green business.

## TEKNOLOGI HIJAU &amp; INOVASI DIGITAL

Akselerasi penggunaan teknologi hijau dan digitalisasi terbaru untuk mendukung efisiensi dan efektivitas serta competitiveness dekarbonisasi serta green business.

## OPERASIONAL

Implementasi green operation secara bertahap dengan tetap memastikan keandalan pasokan dan efisiensi.

## IMPLEMENTASI 2 (DUA) PROGRAM UTAMA

1. Dekarbonisasi Bisnis menjalankan efisiensi energi, loss reduction, elektrifikasi peralatan, penggunaan bahan bakar rendah karbon, green power generation, mobile equipment electrification, carbon capture and storage (own use).
2. Akselerasi Green Business secara agresif dengan membangun bisnis energi terbarukan, baterai dan kendaraan bermotor listrik beserta ekosistemnya, biofuel, blue/green hydrogen, CCS dan CCUS (services), nature-based solution.

## ORIENTASI GREEN STRATEGY

Menanamkan pilar strategis perusahaan ke dalam sistem manajemen kinerja dan manajemen risiko perubahan iklim, melakukan implementasi Carbon Accounting, Pertamina Internal Carbon Pricing (P-ICP) dan Carbon Trading.

## KAPABILITAS ORGANISASI &amp; SDM

Mengembangkan dan memperkuat organisasi untuk dapat mendorong upaya dekarbonisasi dan peningkatan Green Business di Holding dan Subholding Pertamina.

This shared commitment constitutes a concrete step in realizing Pertamina's aspirations to become a global energy company that is environmentally friendly and contributes to the climate change target. In addition, it also serves as a form of Pertamina's support toward the energy transition agenda set by the Indonesian Government in achieving the NZE target by 2060 through:

## STAKEHOLDER MANAGEMENT

Actively conduct stakeholder Engagement to create synergy in the achievement of NZE target.

## CARBON MARKET BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

Development and Implementation of Pertamina Carbon Market Business (Internal &amp; External).

## BUSINESS MODEL

Agile, collaborative, and adaptive business model innovation to promote efforts in decarbonization and new green businesses. Building an open and equal global partnership in developing green businesses.

## GREEN TECHNOLOGY &amp; DIGITAL INNOVATION

Acceleration of the use of green technology and the latest digitization to boost efficiency, effectiveness, and competitiveness in decarbonization and green businesses.

## OPERATIONS

Gradual implementation of green operations while ensuring supply reliability and efficiency.

## IMPLEMENTATION OF 2 (TWO) MAIN PROGRAMS:

1. Business Decarbonization to run energy efficiency, loss reduction, equipment electrification, use of low carbon fuels, green power generation, mobile equipment electrification, carbon capture, and storage (own use).
2. Aggressively accelerating Green Business by building industries in renewable energy, battery, and electric vehicles and their ecosystems, biofuels, blue/green hydrogen, Carbon Capture Storage (CCS) and Carbon Capture Utilization and Storage (CCUS) services, and nature-based solutions.

## GREEN STRATEGY ORIENTATION

Instilling the company's strategic pillars into performance management systems and climate change risk management, implementing Carbon Accounting, Pertamina Internal Carbon Pricing (P-ICP), and Carbon Trading.

## ORGANIZATIONAL &amp; HR CAPABILITIES

Developing and strengthening the organization to promote decarbonization efforts and to improve Green Businesses in Pertamina Holding and Subholding.
