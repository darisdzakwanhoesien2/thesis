# KOMITMEN MANAJEMEN TERHADAP KEBERLANJUTAN

Management Commitment to Sustainability

Komitmen manajemen terhadap keberlanjutan salah satunya ditunjukkan dengan membentuk Komite Keberlanjutan dan Tim Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) pada 2022 (Keputusan Direksi PGN No. 037201.K/OT.00/PDO.2022). Merujuk Keputusan Direksi PGN Nomor 037200.K/OT.00/PDO/2022 tentang Komite Keberlanjutan, pengelolaan keberlanjutan PGN dilakukan oleh Direksi dan Direktorat terkait sesuai fungsi dan tanggung jawab masing-masing, dengan diketuai oleh Direktur Utama. Direksi dapat mendegasikan pengelolaan keberlanjutan kepada setiap fungsi di Subholding Gas dan entitas anak.

Tim ESG yang terdiri dari Direksi dan Kepala Satuan Kerja masing-masing bidang, bertugas memastikan bahwa arah perubahan Perusahaan ke depan sesuai dengan manajemen ESG yang baik, berorientasi kepada lingkungan/eco-friendly, serta sesuai dengan kaidah dan framework yang tepat. Pada 2022, Tim ESG telah menyusun fokus dan strategi keberlanjutan Perseroan. Uraian lebih lanjut mengenai Komite Keberlanjutan dan Tim ESG dapat dilihat pada Bab Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan. [2-23][2-24]

Perusahaan juga terus melakukan sosialisasi budaya keberlanjutan internal secara proaktif, mulai dari pimpinan tertinggi hingga ke seluruh karyawan melalui berbagai kanal seperti uji pemahaman Good Corporate Governance (GCG) dan code of conduct bagi setiap pekerjaan, penerapan aplikasi GCG Online System (GOLS) untuk meningkatkan awareness GCG serta branding GCG melalui flyer pada email blast, website hingga media sosial PGN. [A.6.a]

Management's commitments to sustainability is demonstrated by the establishment of a Sustainability Committee and an Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Team in 2022(PGN Board of Directors Decree Number 037201.K/OT.00/PDO/2022). Referring to Decree of the PGN Board of Directors Number 037200.K/OT.00/PDO/2022 concerning the Sustainability Committee, the Board of Directors and relevant Directorates carry out PGN's sustainability management in accordance with their respective functions and responsibilities, which is led by the President Director. The Board of Directors is entitled to delegate sustainability management to each function in Gas Subholding and subsidiaries.

The ESG team, which consists of the Board of Directors and the Head of Work Unit in respective divisions, is tasked to ensure that the Company's future transformation direction is aligned with good ESG management, oriented to environment/eco-friendly while also complying with the appropriate rules and frameworks. In 2022, the ESG Team formulated the focus and strategy for the Company's sustainability. Further details on the Sustainability Committee and the ESG Team are presented in the Sustainability Governance Chapter. [2-23][2-24]

The Company also remains proactive in disseminating a culture of internal sustainability, starting from the highest level of leadership to all employees through various channels, including tests on Good Corporate Governance (GCG) and code of conduct understanding for every employee, implementing the GCG Online System (GOLS) application to raise GCG awareness, and GCG branding through flyers in email blasts, PGN website and social media platforms. [A.6.a]
