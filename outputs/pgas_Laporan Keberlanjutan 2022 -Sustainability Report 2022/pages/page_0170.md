## PILAR SOSIAL

Melalui program ini, PGN berkomitmen menjadi Perusahaan dengan kepedulian sosial tinggi dan sigap merespons berbagai situasi yang terjadi di Indonesia. Dalam pelaksanaannya, pada beberapa program, PGN SHARE biasanya bekerja sama dengan lebih kurang 3 (tiga) lembaga/organisasi kemanusiaan. Selama tahun 2022, nilai sebesar IDR30.643.121.419 telah disalurkan dalam pilar sosial.

## IMPLEMENTATION OF SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY PROGRAMS

## SOCIAL PILLAR

Through this program, PGN is committed to becoming a company with high social awareness and is responsive to various situations that occur in Indonesia. In its implementation, in several programs, PGN SHARE usually cooperates with at least 3 (three) humanitarian agencies/organizations. During 2022, a value of IDR30,643,121,419 has been distributed in the social pillars.
