# PROGRAM KESELAMATAN DAN KESEHATAN KERJA PGN[403-7] [11.9.8]

PGN merancang peta jalan K3 sebagai panduan untuk membawa PGN mencapai sasaran K3. Peta jalan Program Safety Excellence ini dirancang sejak 2009 dengan sasaran untuk membawa setiap Perwira Subholding Gas memahami aspek K3 dan menyadari pentingnya mitigasi risiko.

# PGN OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY AND HEALTH PROGRAM[403-7][11.9.8]

PGN designed an OHS roadmap as a guide to bring PGN to achieve its OHS goals. The Safety Excellence Program roadmap has been designed since 2009 with the aim of bringing every Gas Subholding Employees to understand the K3 aspect and realize the importance of risk mitigation.
