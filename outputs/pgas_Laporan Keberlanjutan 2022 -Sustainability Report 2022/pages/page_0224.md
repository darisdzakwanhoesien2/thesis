# PENERAPAN AKHLAK DI DALAM KODE ETIK

Penerapan Kode Etik Perusahaan tidak dapat dilepaskan dari implementasi budaya perusahaan yaitu "AKHLAK" (Amanah, Kompeten, Harmonis, Loyal, Adaptif, dan Kolaboratif) yang juga menjadi faktor penting dalam pencapaian Visi Misi Subholding Gas. Core Values AKHLAK telah ditetapkan dalam Keputusan Direksi PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk Nomor 037800.K/OT.04/PDO/2020 sejak tanggal 16 Oktober 2020.

AKHLAK juga didorong sebagai tata nilai dan alat perokat organisasi untuk menumbuhkan semangat kebersamaan dan sinergi dalam memberikan layanan yang lebih baik kepada pelanggan. Sehingga secara bersama sama, AKHLAK menjadikan perilaku seluruh organisasi selaras dengan AKHLAK telah menjadi budaya bagi perilaku setiap Perwira Subholding Gas.

# PENGUATAN BUDAYA PERUSAHAAN [A.6.a]

Sebagai bagian dari Holding Migas, PGN turut mengimplementasikan core values BUMN AKHLAK sebagai budaya Perusahaan. Perwira Subholding Gas memiliki peranan penting sebagai agent of change dalam proses implementasi penguatan budaya Perusahaan.

Penyelarasan budaya Perusahaan dilakukan melalui program-program budaya berdasarkan Nilai Utama AKHLAK dan arahan program Pertamina, yaitu:

1. Ethics Ambassador, yaitu program sosialisasi dan penerapan nilai-nilai Etika Usaha &amp; Etika Kerja di setiap Satuan Kerja/Anak Perusahaan/Afiliasi.
1. Displaying of PGN Code of Ethics on the PGN website so that it can be accessed by all parties.
2. The GCG Online System (GOLS) application, which is a digital information system to increase awareness of GCG and the Code of Ethics as well as a means for Gas Subholding Employees, stating the commitment to implementing GCG principles and the Company's values once a year.
3. Conducting training related to the implementation of GCG and the Code of Ethics.
4. Personnel Application (EASY), which is a digital employment information system as a means for Gas Subholding Employees to obtain information regarding their rights and obligations.
5. The PGN-Q application, which is an information system for a collection of Terms of Reference owned by the Company for the benefit of Gas Subholding Employees in obtaining document information related to the Company's business procedures and operations.
6. The Company's media, including social media, banners, flyers, and other advertising media in the Company's office area.

# APPLICATION OF AKHLAK IN THE CODE OF ETHICS

The implementation of the Company's Code of Ethics is inseparable from the implementation of the corporate culture, namely "AKHLAK" (Trusted, Competent, Harmonious, Loyal, Adaptive and Collaborative) which is an important factor in achieving the Gas Subholding Vision and Mission. AKHLAK's Core Values have been stipulated in the Decree of the Board of Directors of PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk Number 037800.K/OT.04/PDO/2020 on October 16, 2020.

AKHLAK is also integrated as a value system and part of organizational integration to foster a spirit of togetherness and synergy in providing better service to customers. Collectively, AKHLAK ensures the behavior of the entire organization in line with AKHLAK and has become a culture for the behavior of every Gas Subholding Employee.

# STRENGTHENING CORPORATE CULTURE [A.6.a]

As part of the Oil and Gas Holding, PGN also implements the core values of BUMN AKHLAK as a corporate culture. Gas Subholding Employees play an important role as agents of change in the implementation process of strengthening the Corporate culture.

Alignment of Corporate culture is carried out through cultural programs based on AKHLAK's Core Values and Pertamina's program directives, namely:

1. Ethics Ambassador: a dissemination program and implementation of the values of Business Ethics &amp; Work Ethics in every Work Unit/Subsidiary/Affiliate.
