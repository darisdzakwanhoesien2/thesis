# FOKUS DAN STRATEGI KEBERLANJUTAN [A.1]
# Sustainability Strategy and Focus [A.1]

PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk (PGN) meyakini bahwa pertumbuhan bisnis dan keberlanjutan saling terkait dan berjalan beriringan. Oleh karena itu, fokus dan strategi keberlanjutan PGN dirancang selaras dengan kondisi dan lingkungan bisnis Perseroan, dengan mengedepankan komitmen lingkungan, sosial dan tata kelola serta Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan/TPB (Sustainable Development Goals/SDGs). Pada tahun 2022 Perseroan telah merumuskan nilai-nilai keberlanjutan dalam 4 fokus keberlanjutan yang diturunkan ke dalam 14 inisiatif/strategi keberlanjutan dan akan terus dijalankan secara konsisten hingga 2030. [2-22]

PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk (PGN) is of the opinion that business growth and sustainability are interconnected and go hand in hand. Therefore, PGN's focus and sustainability strategy are devised in line with the situations and business environment of the Company, by prioritizing environmental, social, and governance commitments as well as the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). In 2022, the Company has formulated sustainability values within 4 sustainability focuses that are translated into 14 sustainability initiatives/strategies and will continue to be implemented consistently until 2030. [2-22]
