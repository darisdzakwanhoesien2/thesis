MENINGKATANI KERERJANUTAN USAMA
DIPARTAR KANAMAKTEK
Sumberkaitan

Kami mengapresiasi berbagai inisiatif Direksi terkait penerapan Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TJSL) melalui berbagai program yang telah dijalankan secara kontinu. Di antaranya program Desa Binaan yang berhasil membawa manfaat ekonomi bagi masyarakat setempat dan juga manfaat lingkungan yang lebih bersih melalui inisiatif bank sampah dan pengelolaan air bersih.

Secara internal kami juga terus memperkuat penerapan tata kelola Perusahaan melalui praktik bisnis yang bertanggung jawab. Artinya, setiap proses bisnis yang kami lakukan harus sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan yang berlaku dan penerapan etika bisnis. Kami yakin, penerapan tata kelola yang baik akan meningkatkan nilai perusahaan (corporate value) PGN dan memperkuat daya saing perusahaan secara berkesinambungan.

Kesungguhan seluruh insan PGN dalam mewujudkan komitmen keberlanjutan dibuktikan dengan sejumlah penghargaan dan apresiasi eksternal yang kami terima di 2022. Kami bangga dengan pencapaian tersebut, yang terwujud karena hasil kerja keras berbagai pihak terutama Direksi dan seluruh jajaran manajemen serta Perwira Subholding Gas.

Berada di tahun 2022 berarti kita sudah setengah jalan menuju tenggat waktu pencapaian agenda SDGs di 2030. Artinya kita harus bergerak lebih cepat dan tepat untuk melakukan perubahan-perubahan yang diperlukan. Kami percaya Direksi dan segenap manajemen PGN serta Perwira Subholding Gas mampu mewujudkan hal itu, tentunya dengan dukungan dan kontribusi dari para pemangku kepentingan.

We appreciate the various initiatives of the Board of Directors related to the implementation of Social and Environmental Responsibility (TJSL) through numerous programs that have continually been implemented. Among them is Desa Binaan (Foster Villages) program, which has succeeded in bringing economic benefits to local communities as well as cleaner environmental benefits through waste bank initiatives and clean water management.

Internally, we also continue to strengthen the implementation of good corporate governance by means of responsible business practices. This means that every business process we carry out must comply with the provisions of applicable regulation and the application of business ethics. We are certain that the implementation of good governance will increase PGN's corporate value and thereby bolster the company's competitiveness on an ongoing basis.

The determination shown by all PGN ranks in manifesting their commitments to sustainability is evidenced by the number of awards and external appreciation we received in 2022. We are proud of these achievements, which were made possible thanks to the hard work of various elements, especially the Board of Directors and all ranks of management and Gas Subholding Employees.

This has been the year of 2022, which means that we are already halfway toward the deadline for achieving the SDGs agenda in 2030. The point is that we must move faster and precisely to make the necessary changes. We believe the Board of Directors and all PGN management as well as Gas Subholding Employees are able to materialize it, certainly, with the support and contribution of stakeholders.

Jakarta, 28 April 2023
