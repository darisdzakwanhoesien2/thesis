# INNOVATION AWARD PROGRAM

Innovation Award Program adalah salah satu program pengakuan bagi pekerja PGN dan Subholding Gas yang telah memberikan kerja ekstra dalam cara mereka bekerja yang meningkatkan kemampuan perusahaan melalui ide dan inovasi kreatif. Sejak tahun 2019, program ini mengadaptasi metode Continuous Improvement Program Pertamina. Tujuan Innovation Award Program adalah membangun sinergi Subholding Gas, meningkatkan kreativitas dan pemikiran inovatif, menciptakan lingkungan bagi pekerja untuk mengeksplorasi peluang bisnis demi keberlanjutan perusahaan, dan meningkatkan keterlibatan karyawan melalui pengakuan terhadap inovasi mereka. Pekerja terbaik yang telah mengikuti program ini akan diikutsertakan ke tahap Annual Pertamina Quality Award, selanjutnya diikutsertakan dalam tingkat Penghargaan Dharma Karya Energi dan Sumber Daya Mineral di Kementerian Energi dan Sumber Daya Mineral Republik Indonesia.

# INNOVATION AWARD PROGRAM

The Innovation Award Program is one of the recognition programs for PGN and Gas Subholding employees who have put in extra work in their way of working that enhances the Company's capabilities through creative ideas and innovations. Since 2019, this program has adapted Pertamina's Continuous Improvement Program method. The Innovation Award Program aims to build synergies with Gas Subholding, enhance creativity and innovative thinking, create an environment for employees to explore business opportunities for the company's sustainability, and boost employee engagement through recognition of their innovations. The best employees who have participated in this program will be included in the Annual Pertamina Quality Award stage; then, they will be included in the Dharma Karya Energy and Mineral Resources Award level at the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources of the Republic of Indonesia.

# EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (EAP)

Sejak 2013 PGN menyediakan program Employee Assistance Program (EAP) untuk membantu memberikan pendampingan, motivasi, dorongan, dan pengetahuan praktis dalam bidang yang dibutuhkan Perwira Subholding Gas, terutama terkait kesehatan mental. EAP memberikan bantuannya dalam bentuk konseling maupun seminar singkat dengan konselor eksternal.

# EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (EAP)

Since 2013 PGN has developed an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) to assist Gas Subholding Employees by providing mentoring, motivation, encouragement, and practical knowledge in areas of need, especially regarding mental health. EAP provides assistance through counseling and brief seminars with external counselors.
