2. Etika Perseroan dengan Pelanggan
Perseroan mengedepankan layanan prima, sikap proaktif dan cepat tanggap dalam menghadapi dan melayani pelanggan dengan bersikap santun dan menyampaikan informasi secara lengkap dan akurat.

3. Etika Perseroan dengan Penyedia Barang dan Jasa
Perseroan melaksanakan pengadaan barang dan jasa berdasarkan prinsip-prinsip kompetitif, transparan, adil, wajar dan akuntabel berdasarkan pedoman dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dengan menghormati hak dan kewajiban masing-masing pihak serta menjalin komunikasi yang baik.

4. Etika Perseroan dengan Kreditur
Perseroan menerima pinjaman dana dari Lembaga keuangan bank maupun non bank sesuai anggaran dasar Perseroan dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dengan menghormati hak-hak kreditur.

5. Etika Perseroan dengan Pemerintah
Perseroan menjalin hubungan yang harmonis, konstruktif dan saling menghormati dengan pemerintah pusat maupun daerah melalui hubungan dan komunikasi yang baik serta kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

6. Etika Perseroan dengan Masyarakat
Perseroan melaksanakan program sosial dan kemasyarakatan untuk memberdayakan potensi dan peningkatan kualitas hidup masyarakat sekitar, serta bersinergi dengan program-program Pemerintah.

7. Etika Perseroan dengan Pemegang Saham
Perseroan berkomitmen meningkatkan nilai tambah dan melindungi hak-hak pemegang saham sesuai dengan ketentuan perundang-undangan dengan menjamin kesetaraan dan menyediakan informasi yang sama secara akurat, lengkap dan tepat waktu.

8. Etika Perseroan dengan Pemasok Gas Bumi
Perseroan menjalin kerja sama yang harmonis dan saling menguntungkan dengan para pemasok gas bumi sesuai dengan ketentuan perundang-undangan.

9. Etika Perseroan dengan Anak Perusahaan/Afiliasi
Perseroan membentuk Anak Perusahaan/Afiliasi untuk pengembangan bisnis yang dapat memberikan nilai tambah dan mendukung bisnis utama Perseroan dengan melakukan sinergi dan kerja sama untuk pencapaian strategi dan pelaksanaan operasional.

2. Company Ethics with Customers
The Company prioritizes excellent service, being proactive and responsive in dealing with and serving customers by being courteous and conveying complete and accurate information.

3. Company Ethics with Goods and Service Providers
The Company carries out the procurement of goods and services based on competitive, transparent, just, reasonable and accountable principles based on guidelines and applicable laws and regulations by respecting the rights and obligations of each party and establishing good communication.

4. Company Ethics with Creditors
The Company receives loan funds from bank and non-bank financial institutions in accordance with the Company's articles of association and applicable laws and regulations by respecting the rights of creditors.

5. Company Ethics with the Government
The Company maintains a harmonious, constructive and mutually respectful relationship with the Central Government and regional governments through good relations and communication as well as compliance with the applicable laws and regulations.

6. Company Ethics with Communities
The Company carries out social and community programs to empower potential and improve the quality of life of the surrounding communities, as well as create synergy with the Government's programs.

7. Company Ethics with Shareholders
The Company is committed to increasing added value and protecting the rights of shareholders in accordance with provisions of legislation by ensuring equality and providing the same information accurately, thoroughly and on time.

8. Company Ethics with Natural Gas Suppliers
The Company establishes harmonious and mutually beneficial cooperation with natural gas suppliers in accordance with provisions of legislation.

9. Company Ethics with Subsidiary/Affiliate
The Company establishes a Subsidiary/Affiliate for business development that can provide added value and support the Company's main business through synergy and cooperation for the achievement of strategies and operational implementation.
