Pelibatan pemangku kepentingan dilakukan dengan membuat program-program yang sesuai dengan kondisi masyarakat setempat dengan tetap mempertimbangkan kepatuhan terhadap kebijakan hak asasi manusia. Program yang dikembangkan antara lain (namun tidak terbatas pada):

a. Program Pemberdayaan Ekonomi masyarakatseperti penguatan kelembagaan Koperasi, Badan Usaha Milik Desa (BUMDes) dan pembentukan UKM untuk ibu-ibu.
b. Program Kesehatanseperti pelatihan Perilaku Hidup Bersih dan Sehat (PHBS) dan bantuan peralatan di Posyandu.
c. Program Pemberdayaan Lingkunganseperti perlombaan dusun bersih dan sehat dan peningkatan manajemen kelompok bank sampah.
d. Program Kesenian dan Kebudayaanseperti tentang pergelaran seni tahunan.
e. Program Infrastrukturseperti renovasi sarana belajar dan pembuatan sarana airbersih.

Program ini dilakukan di sekitar wilayah stasiun gas (Offstake Station) PGN. Melalui program ini, PGN berupaya menjalin hubungan dan komunikasi yang baik dengan masyarakat di ring satu wilayah operasional. Tujuannya agar dapat memperkuat dukungan warga terhadap Perusahaan, karena Offstake Station yang terdapat di desa ini merupakan obyek vital nasional yang memiliki peran penting dalam jaminan ketersediaan pasokan gas di wilayah yang dilayani. Stakeholder engagement is carried out by creating programs in accordance with the situations of the local community while still considering compliance with human rights policies. Programs developed include (but are not limited to):

a. Community Economic Empowerment Programs such as strengthening cooperative institutions, Village-Owned Enterprise (BUMDes), and establishing SMEs for mothers.
b. Health programs such as Clean and Healthy Behavior (PHBS) training and equipment assistance at Posyandu.
c. Environmental Empowerment Programs such as clean and healthy hamlet competitions and improving the management of waste bank groups.
d. Arts and Culture Programs such as annual art performances.
e. Infrastructure programs such as renovating learning facilities and making clean water facilities.

This program is carried out around the PGN gas station (Offstake Station). Through this program, PGN seeks to establish good relations and communication with the community in the ring of one operational area. The aim is to strengthen residents' support for the Company, because the Offstake Station located in this village is a national vital object that has an important role in ensuring the availability of gas supplies in the areas served.
