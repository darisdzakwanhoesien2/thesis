The 2022 Indonesian SDGs Award (ISDA) is an annual event held by the CFCD and supported by the Coordinating Ministry for Economic Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia, Ministry of the National Development Planning/the National Development Planning Agency (BAPPENAS), and Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources.

ISDA is an award for companies or institutions for their contributions to the attainment of the Sustainable Development Goals. This award is also conferred on individuals who play important roles in companies' internal or external CSR/CID programs.
