# MENINGKATKAN KESELAMATAN DAN KESEHATAN KERJA (K3) [403-7][11.9.8]

Improving Occupational Safety and Health (K3) [403-7][11.9.8]

Meningkatkan budaya Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) merupakan prioritas utama bagi PGN sebagai perusahaan berbasis energi yang memiliki risiko kerja tinggi. Penerapan aspek K3 dalam proses bisnis Perusahaan menjadi tanggung jawab Incident Management Team/ Emergency Management Team/Crisis Management Team.

The utmost priority for PGN, as an energy-based company with a high risk of work, is to improve the Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) culture. The implementation of OSH aspects in the Company's business processes is the responsibility of the Incident Management Team/ Emergency Management Team/Crisis Management Team.
