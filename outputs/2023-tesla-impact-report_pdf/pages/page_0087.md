# Human and Organizational Performance (HOP) Principles Shape Our Safety Journey

The HOP mission is to accelerate progress through operational learning, increased capacity and systems thinking. HOP enables Tesla to learn quickly and develop robust systems that can tolerate errors and fail safely. It prioritizes making it easy for workers to do the right thing. We develop tools and programs to integrate HOP principles into daily work.

In 2023, we rolled out the Essential Safeguards program, a focused effort on fatality and serious injury prevention by strengthening our preventative and mitigative safeguards.
