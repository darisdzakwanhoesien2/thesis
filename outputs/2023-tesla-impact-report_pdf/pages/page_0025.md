# NEDC, WLTP or EPA Testing Cycles Do Not Represent Real-World Fuel or Electricity Consumption

We used real-world energy consumption by Model 3 and Model Y to analyze EV energy consumption. For ICE fuel consumption, we used data provided by Consumer Reports, which reports model year 2023 mid-size premium vehicles achieve 24.9 MPG on average. This translates to approximately 400 grams of CO₂e per mile once we account for emissions generated through the extraction, refining and shipment of oil.
