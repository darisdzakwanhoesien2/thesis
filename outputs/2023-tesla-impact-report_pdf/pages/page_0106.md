# More Primary Data Than Ever to Inform Supply Chain Decarbonization

We're working to accelerate the world's transition to sustainable energy not only through our products and operations, but also through our supply chain. To get there, we need accurate emissions data.

Historically, emissions have been underestimated due to limited data and generalized methodologies. That's why we prioritize gathering high-quality and primary data directly from our suppliers.

See page 147 for a full disclosure of our Scope 3 GHG emissions, including supply chain.

## In 2023:

345
GHG datapoints were collected directly from our suppliers through our GHG survey or LCAs disclosed by suppliers
We used a process-based model for steel and aluminum for the first time
We moved to more regionally representative numbers
We collected significantly more primary data for the battery calculations
