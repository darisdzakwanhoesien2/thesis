# Across Our Lithium Supply Chain

In 2023, our responsible sourcing teams participated in on-site visits with current and prospective Lithium suppliers in Chile and Australia. These visits helped the team better understand suppliers' current environmental and human rights practices, as well as decarbonization roadmaps.

Two of our direct lithium suppliers also completed or are in the process of completing an independent third-party audit against the Initiative for Responsible Mining Assurance (IRMA) Standard, Tesla's preferred mining standard. IRMA is a multi-stakeholder led organization with the most comprehensive mining certification system and transparent reporting of results available. Its focus on continuous improvement allows suppliers to improve their results over time. Tesla has been an IRMA member since late 2021. Our goal is to encourage the uptake of IRMA across our supply chain.
