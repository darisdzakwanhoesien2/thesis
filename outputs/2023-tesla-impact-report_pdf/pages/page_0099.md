# Our Pay Equity Program

Our program is designed to assess whether similarly situated employees are paid in a similar manner after accounting for variables such as:

- Geographic Zone
- Tenure
- Average Performance Score
- Job Function
- Management Level and Role

HR also offers a Pay Equity and Pay Transparency educational course to everyone in the People Organization, with a focus on HR partners and recruiters. This course details what pay equity is, why it is important, how unconscious bias affects pay and hiring, best practices for hiring and compensation and how each person can be an effective partner in helping the company achieve and maintain pay equity.
