# Electrifying Heavy-Duty Trucks is Critical to Our Mission

Combination trucks account for about 16% of U.S. vehicle emissions—Semi helps change that. With less than 2 kWh per mile of energy consumption, Semi can travel up to 500 miles on a single charge, fully loaded. Charging with electricity is approximately 2 times cheaper per mile than refueling with diesel. Operators can see estimated fuel savings of up to $150,000 within their first three years of ownership.* With remote diagnostics, over-the-air software updates and fewer moving parts to maintain, operators will spend less time at service centers and more time on the road.

*Based on average Q1 2024 diesel prices in California and latest electricity rates.
