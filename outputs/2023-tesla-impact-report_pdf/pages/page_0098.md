# Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)

Our employees can purchase Tesla stock at a discount through the ESPP. Enrollment in ESPP is easy with an internal site, stock admin team and support through the administrator. In 2023, we increased efforts around education for how to use this benefit. We want to ensure increased financial health and literacy is something everyone at Tesla can benefit from regardless of role or seniority.
