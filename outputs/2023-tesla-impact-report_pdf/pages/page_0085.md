# We Listen to Our Employees

We empower our employees to identify improvements. Take Charge—our internal improvement suggestion module—allows employees to submit improvements in environment, health, safety, security, people and more. Promoting a company-wide culture of improvement, Take Charge is used across all departments and regions where Tesla operates. Executive leaders have adopted and been trained in Take Charge. Submissions can be made anonymously and be owned at a cross-functional level.

Take Charge works as a leading indicator for safety and engages those closest to the work—as well as their supervisors—to establish a process to address issues proactively. Since its launch in 2021, we've seen a 30% reduction in global injury rate.
