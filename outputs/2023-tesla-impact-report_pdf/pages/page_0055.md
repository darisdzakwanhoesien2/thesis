# Making EVs Even More Affordable

Our goal is to displace fossil fuels by selling as many Tesla products as possible. To achieve this goal, we need to make our products even more accessible. Affordability begins with how much it costs us to produce our vehicles. We were able to reduce the cost to build a single vehicle by almost 50% since 2018 with the introduction of Model 3 and Model Y as well as the deployment of new, more efficient factories. And we aren't done yet. During 2023 Investor Day, we outlined our goal of reducing costs even further with the introduction of new vehicle and manufacturing technologies.
