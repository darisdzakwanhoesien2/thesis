# Grid Stability is Required for Decarbonization

As we decarbonize the economy and electrify everything, grid expansion and stability will be more important than ever. Pairing renewables with energy storage is the best way to stabilize and grow the grid while simultaneously making the required GHG emissions reductions needed to avoid the worst outcomes of climate change. Electrical disturbances in the U.S. are becoming more common, predominantly due to weather and natural disasters. According to the U.S. Department of Energy, electrical disturbances cost businesses $150 billion per year. It is not surprising that homeowners and businesses are increasingly turning to backup power supply options.
