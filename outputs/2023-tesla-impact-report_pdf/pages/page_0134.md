# Grievance Mechanisms

We receive concerns related to responsible sourcing through a variety of grievance channels. 2023 was the first full year the Tesla Integrity Line was available to external parties to raise a potential concern. There is a clear process, detailed and publicly available here. Tesla Integrity Line was added to the Tesla Supplier Portal in 2023 to make it more accessible to supply chain workers with access to the Portal.
