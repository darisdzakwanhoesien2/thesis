# Model Y Total Cost of Ownership per Mile is Similar to Mass-Market ICE Vehicles

While the "sticker price" of Model Y is similar to an equivalent BMW or Audi, the lifetime running costs of EVs are lower than those of ICE vehicles due to lower maintenance costs and cheaper electricity.

Electric vehicles are less expensive to fuel than gasoline powered vehicles. The cost of electricity to power Model Y is up to 3 times lower than a comparable ICE vehicle. This results in approximately $7,000 of fuel savings over 5 years and 60,000 miles.
