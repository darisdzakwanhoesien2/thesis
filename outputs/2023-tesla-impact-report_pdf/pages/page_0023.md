# Air Pollution From Burning Fossil Fuels Leads to Premature Deaths

Pollution from burning fossil fuels leads to eight million premature deaths globally each year—that accounts for one-in-five premature deaths worldwide. Our products are not just about the future of our planet, but also about addressing preventable deaths today. This is a major advantage of zero direct emission products that is often forgotten.
