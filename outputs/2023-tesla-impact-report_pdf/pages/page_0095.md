# Nearly Two-Thirds of Our People Leaders Come From Internal Promotions

We offer internal career development to our employees and the ability to make a meaningful contribution to a sustainable future. As of the end of 2023, 65% of our managers were promoted from an internal, non-manager position and the average tenure of senior management was over nine years.
