# 3TG (Tin, Tantalum, Tungsten and Gold): Risk Identification and Mitigation Summary

Mining communities in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and other upstream 3TG sourcing regions are essential to the production of Tesla products. Mining in conflict-affected contexts has a history of negative impacts on communities, including human rights abuses. Our due diligence process includes supplier engagement, visiting 3TG production regions with the purpose of observing on-the-ground conditions, meeting with local stakeholders and exploring opportunities for positive impact. Through these efforts, we aim to avoid contributing to human rights abuses, conflict, and instability through our sourcing, with the ultimate aim to improve on-the-ground conditions in and around 3TG mining communities.
