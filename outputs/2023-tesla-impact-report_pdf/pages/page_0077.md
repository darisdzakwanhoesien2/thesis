# Preparing the workforce for a sustainable energy future

To sustain our pace of innovation, we must ensure we attract, develop and retain a talented workforce with ample opportunity to contribute to our mission and grow professionally.

We are committed to providing a workplace where our employees feel respected, satisfied and appreciated. Our policies are designed to promote fairness and respect for everyone. We hire, evaluate and promote employees based on their skills and performance.
