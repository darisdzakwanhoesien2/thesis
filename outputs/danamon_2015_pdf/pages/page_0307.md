# Independence of Risk Monitoring Committee Members

To maintain independence and objectivity, the majority (83%) of Committee members are Independent Commissioners and Independent Parties. Members of the Board of Directors may not sit as a member of the Risk Monitoring Committee. The Risk Monitoring Committee is chaired by an Independent Commissioner, Manggi T. Habir.
