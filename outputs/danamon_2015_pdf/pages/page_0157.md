## General and Mandatory Reserves

On December 31, 2015, the Bank set general and compulsory reserves of Rp302,618 (2014: Rp276,578). General and mandatory reserves refer to the Law of the Republic of Indonesia No. 1/1995, which was amended by Law No. 40/2007 effective August 16, 2007 regarding Limited Liability Companies, which requires companies to set aside general reserves of at least 20% of the issued and fully paid capital. The law does not further specify the time period for the reserves.

## EMPLOYEE SHARE OWNERSHIP PROGRAM (ESOP/MSOP)

The Bank has launched the new special program which was awarded to the Directors and Senior Executives selectively with total 7,633,200 shares at the price of Rp3,602 per share and has been granted on 10 September 2015.

Eligible employee is allocated a certain predetermined amount of cash and directly used to purchase the Banks stocks. The stocks are purchased under the individual employee's name with three years holding periode and it is put under an independent custodian.
