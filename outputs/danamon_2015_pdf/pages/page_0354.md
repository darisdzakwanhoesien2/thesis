## Actions Implemented by the Risk Management Committee in 2015

During 2015, the Risk Management Committee conducted meetings with discussion topics which include:

a. Routine discussions:
- Portfolio (bank level, wholesale, retail, and micro business).
- Operational, market, and liquidity risks.
- Portfolio per business unit.
- Credit rating.
- Concentration of business units and industry portfolio.
- Bonds.
- Debtors on watchlist.
- Risk dashboard.

b. Specific Discussions: Improvement on DSP documentation system to accelerate the improvement of credit quality.
