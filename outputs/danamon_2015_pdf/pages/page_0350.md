## Orientation Program for New Directors

An orientation program for new Directors is conducted to provide an understanding of the vision, mission and values of Danamon, code of ethics, organizational structure, lines of business, Board of Directors' charter as well as banking rules and regulations. The orientation program is implemented by providing an On-Boarding Pack and briefing in-person by the business units and other support units.
