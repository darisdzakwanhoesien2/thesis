# Independency of members Board of Commissioners

All members of the Board of Commissioners who currently serve have no family relationship to the second degree with members of the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors as well as the controlling shareholders. The majority of members of the Board of Commissioners do not have any financial relationships with controlling shareholders and all the members of the Board of Commissioners do not have any financial relationship with the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.
