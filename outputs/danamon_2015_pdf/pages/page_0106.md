## 2015 Initiatives

Danamon Syariah carried out a number of initiatives in 2015 including:

1. Optimizing the Syariah branch network and office channeling to increase third party funding.
2. Developing unique Syariah-based products marketable through the parent-bank branch network.
3. Continuing to consolidate branch offices as part of business transformation.
4. Focusing on the growth of its portfolios, particularly the small and medium businesses portfolio, through financing for Employee Cooperatives and Syariah Rural Banks (BPRS).
5. Focusing on the growth of its commercial segment through financing of heavy equipment and trade finance, including structured trade and commodity finance.
6. Optimizing cash flows generated by financing through cash management.
7. Focusing on improving human resources productivity.
