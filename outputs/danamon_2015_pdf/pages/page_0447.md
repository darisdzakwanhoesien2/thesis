## Rapid Disaster Response Target:

### 1. External

- Responding to disasters around Danamon/Adira branch through mobilization of internal volunteers.
- Internal volunteers response to big scale disasters that are beyond the Danamon/Adira branch coverage by partnering with third parties (PMI, ACT, BPBD, etc.)
- Teaming up with the National Disaster Mitigation Agency and the Regional Disaster Mitigation Agency in form of people's market fire disaster mitigation training.

### 2. Internal

- Increase active participation from all units of Danamon and Adira throughout the country;
- Enhance employees involvement as volunteers in the Disaster Quick Response program.
