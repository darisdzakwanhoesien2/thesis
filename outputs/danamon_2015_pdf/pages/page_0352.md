## Directors Succession

Succession of Directors is made through identifying potential executive officers through the Talent Review Program. Each member of the Board of Directors is obliged to submit candidate successors for evaluation, and identify their competencies and skill gaps. Successor candidates are grouped in a talent inventory for recommendation to the Nomination Committee.
