## Human Resources Roadmap

Danamon recognizes that each employee is unique, which is an advantage for each to compete effectively. This becomes a basis for managing human resources so that each employee's potential is continuously being developed and supported as part of the Bank sustainable growth.

The 2015 and 2016 Roadmap for human resources development strategy is explained in the diagram below.

This roadmap supports the Bank's initiative in implementing change to the business model, ensuring clear accountability within each function in organization, and development of employee competency.
