# CHANGES OF TAX REGULATIONS IN 2015

Changes of tax regulations in 2015 that significantly impacted the Bank's performance is the Minister of Finance of the Republic of Indonesia Regulation Number 207/PMK.010/2015 dated November 20, 2015 concerning the requirement of tax id number of debtors for loan amount aboved Rp50 million which can not be collected, then it can be deducted from gross income.
