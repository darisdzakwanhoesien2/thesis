2) Risk measurement at the operating unit level is supported by the Risk/Loss Event Database (R/LED), Risk Control Self Assessment (RCSA), Key Risk Indicator (KRI) and Capital Charge Calculation &amp; Modeling to measure the Bank's risk profile quantitatively and be used to identify the effectiveness of operational risk management.

The measurement of operational risks maintains the use of the basic indicator approach based on the Circular Letter of Bank Indonesia No. 11/3/DPNP dated 27 January 2009. Based on this Circular Letter, the capital costs of operational risk were 15% of average gross income during the last three years.

3) Operational risk monitoring through regular reports to management to identify issues related to weakness or failure of control functions.

The establishment of the Operational Risk Management Committee is designed as a forum to discuss significant operational risk issues and to monitor the implementation of ORM.

Through the ORMC, the BoD can be informed of operational risk issues, and immediate action can be taken.
