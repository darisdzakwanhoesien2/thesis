| Regulation on Integrated Capital Adequacy For the Financial Conglomerates through P0JK No.26/ P0JK.03/2015 dated December 4, 2015 | The principles of this OJK Regulation are: 1. Financial conglomerate shall provide an integrated capital of 100% of Total Capital Adequacy Ratio for Financial conglomerates. 2. Integrated Capital Adequacy shall be done by calculating the Integrated CAR: $$ \text{Integrated CAR} = \frac{\text{TMA (aggregate net equity)}}{\text{TMM (aggregate regulatory capital requirement)}} $$ | Danamon will start to develop Integrated Capital Adequacy Reports for December 2015 position |
| 3. The provisions for TMA and TMM for each LJK (Financial Services Institution): |
| LJK | TMA | TMM |
| Bank | Core Capital plus Additional Capital | Minimum Capital depending on Risk Profile |
| Financing Companies | Adjusted Capital is Actual | Adjusted Capital is Minimum |
| Insurance/Reinsurance Companies | The actual value of the difference between allowable asset with liabilities; | The minimum value of the difference between allowable asset and liabilities |
| Securities Company | Actual Adjusted Net Working Capital (MKBDI) | The minimum value of Adjusted Net Working Capital (MKBDI) |
| The first report was to be submitted for the first time for end of December 2015 position. |
| BI Regulation No. 17/22/PBI/2015, dated December 28, 2015, regarding Mandatory Countercyclical Buffers | Highlights of this regulation are: 1. Countercyclical Buffer (CB) is additional capital that serves as buffer against expected loss arising from excessive bank loan/financing growth that potentially disrupt the stability of the financial system 2. The obligation of the Bank to form CB per January 1, 2016, with the amount set at 0% - 2.5% of risk weighted assets to start with, CB is set at 0% of risk weighted assets from January 1, 2016. 3. The requirement to form CB shall be obliged with Common Equity Tier 1, after CET 1 is allocated to meet the obligation to provide, minimum common equity tier 1, minimum tier 1, minimum Capital Requirement based on Risk Profile. 4. Bank Indonesia to evaluate the amount of CB, at least 1 time in 6 months | No impact yet on Bank's capital |
| Regulation on the Obligation to Meet Liquidity Adequacy Ratio (Liquidity Coverage Ratio) for Commercial Banks based on P0JK No. 42/ P0JK.03/2015 dated December 23, 2015 | Highlights of this regulation are: 1. Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) as follows: $$ \text{LCR} = \frac{\text{HQLA}}{\text{Total net cash out flow within 30 days ahead}} $$ | <to be check with Market Risk> |
| HQLA = High Quality Liquid Asset, are cash and/or financial assets that can be easily converted into cash to meet liquidity needs of the Bank during the period of the next 30 days under a stress scenario. 2. Stages for the fulfillment of minimum LCR ratio is as follows: |
| Minimum LCR ratio | BUKU 2 | BUKU 4 |
| 70% | 30 June 2016 | 31 December 2015 |
| 80% | 30 June 2017 | 31 December 2016 |
| 90% | 31 December 2017 | 31 December 2017 |
| 100% | 31 December 2018 | 31 December 2018 |
| Regulations on Determination of Systemically Important Banks and Capital Surcharge through P0JK No. 46/P0JK.03/2015 dated December 23, 2015 | Highlights of the OJK regulation are: 1. Capital Surcharge for Systemically Important Bank (SIB) is additional capital to mitigate negative impacts on the stability of the financial system and economy in case of failure of SIB through increasing the Bank's ability to absorb losses. 2. Banks designated as SIB by the OJK, shall establish Capital Surcharge for SIB. The determination of SIB and Capital Surcharge by OJK is conducted every semester on: a. In March using data of the position in December the previous year. b. In September using data of the position data in June Determination of SIB and Capital Surcharge was first conducted in January 2016 using data from the position in June 2015. | Currently the bank is waiting for the OJK to determine SIB and Capital Surcharge bucket. |
