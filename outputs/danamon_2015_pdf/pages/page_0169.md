# Risk Management

Risk Management at Danamon is carried out holistically against all risks faced by the Bank. The approach consists of identifying, measuring, monitoring, and controlling many risks in each Danamon business line, support function and Subsidiaries.

Danamon's principles of risk management are implemented proactively to support the achievement of sustainable growth. Danamon's has an integrated risk management policy as a guidance to identify, measuring, monitoring and controlling all risks faced by Danamon and its subsidiaries in carry out their activity.
