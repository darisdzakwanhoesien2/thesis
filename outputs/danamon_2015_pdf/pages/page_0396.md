## Brand Management

Danamon's Brand is a representation of the Bank's Vision and Mission, communicated through TV commercials, print media, radio, outdoor media, and online campaigns. The promise 'We make it happen for you', introduced in 2008, is the core of Danamon's services, products and programs. This promise places Danamon in a unique position compared to its market competitors.

One among the initiatives managed by this unit is Danamon Social Entrepreneur Awards (DSEA), which has been done annually since 2006. Danamon Social Entrepreneur Award has provided the highest appreciation to ordinary individuals who are able to give extraordinary and continuous effort in empowering themselves and their surrounding environment.
