## Duties and Responsibilities of the Committee

a. Review Danamon's compliance framework in order to monitor the level of compliance with the prevailing laws and regulations.
b. Review and evaluate the audit results performed by external parties such as Bank Indonesia, Financial Services Authority, and other relevant parties.
c. Carry out monitoring of correction steps and ensure Danamon's compliance with the prevailing laws and regulations in the banking industries.
