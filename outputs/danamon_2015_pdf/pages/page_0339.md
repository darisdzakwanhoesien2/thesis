## Frequency of Meetings and the Directors' Meetings

In 2015, the Bank held Board of Directors meetings 45 times or has met the minimum requirement of the Board of Directors meetings. All decisions were documented in the minutes of the meetings. Decision-making in all the Board of Directors meeting were conducted by deliberation and consensus, and there were no dissenting opinions.
