|  Regulations on Net Open Position for Commercial Banks through PBI No.17/5/PBI/2015 dated May 29, 2015 regarding the 4th Amendment of PBI No. 5/13/PBI/2003 | The key amendment in the regulation is the removal of the requirement to maintain Net Open Position in every 30 (thirty) minutes.
This Bank Indonesia Regulation came into force on June 1, 2015. | The provision provides adequate space for the Bank to manage foreign exchange exposure  |
|  Regulations on Foreign Exchange Transactions Against Rupiah between Banks and Domestic Parties through BI Regulation No.16/16/PBI/2014 and BI Circular Letter No.16/14/DPM as last amended through BI Regulation No.17/15/PBI/2015 dated October 2, 2015 and BI Circular Letter No.17/49/DPM dated December 21, 2015 | In principle, additions and/or amendments in the regulation include:
1. The addition of activities that must be the underlying for foreign exchange transaction against Rupiah:
a. Transactions of trade in goods and services and investment, as well as income and expense estimation.
b. Ownership of fund in foreign currency in the country and abroad like savings, current accounts, and Negotiable Certificate of Deposit (NCD), specifically for the sale of foreign exchange transactions against the rupiah through forward transactions (forward sale)
2. Investments in Securities of Bank Indonesia in foreign currency cannot be used as the underlying for purchase of foreign currency against Rupiah neither through spot nor other derivative transactions.
3. Amendment in the threshold amount of foreign exchange transactions against the rupiah per Customer to the Bank is required to have the underlying:
a. Foreign currency purchase transactions against the rupiah, which is done through:
- Monthly spot transactions in the amount of USD 25 thousand or its equivalent.
- Monthly derivative Transactions of USD 100 thousand or its equivalent.
b. FX sales transactions against the rupiah, which is done through:
- Forward transaction of USD 5 million or its equivalent per transaction.
- Option Transactions of USD 1 million or its equivalent per transaction.
This BI Regulation came into force on October 7, 2015 | Compliance with the provisions by making and socializing written SOP policies, and socialization
Education to customers is given directly by both the front liners and through coordination with Bank Indonesia  |
