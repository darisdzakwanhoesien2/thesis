## Share Ownership of Commissioners

As required by Personal Investment Policy Decision Number: DIR-CORP.SEC-014 dated 16 December 2013, Members of the Board of Commissioners shall report any transaction involving the Bank's and its subsidiaries' securities transactions to the Corporate Secretary a minimum of 1 (one) business day prior to the transaction and within 1 (one) business day after the transaction is completed. Commissioners shall also periodically to the Corporate Secretary regarding the share ownership in Danamon or other companies.
