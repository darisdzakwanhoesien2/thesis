## Fraud Committee

Danamon has established a Fraud Committee at the Head Office and Regional level. At the central level, the Fraud Committee manages violations with a nominal above Rp1,000,000,000 including material operational risks towards Danamon. Meanwhile, the Fraud Committee on the regional level addresses frauds with a nominal value of Rp10,000,000 and up to Rp1,000,000,000.
