# MAJORITY AND CONTROLLING SHAREHOLDERS

The ultimate shareholder of Danamon is Asia Financial (Indonesia) Private Ltd ("AFI"). AFI is a Singapore-based legal entity established on November 10, 1990 as an investment holding company under the name ENV Corporation (Private) Limited.

The controlling shareholder of Danamon is Temasek Holding Private Limited ("Temasek"), through Fullerton Management Private Limited and Fullerton Financial Holdings Private Limited. Temasek is an investment company domiciled in Singapore and owned by the Singaporean Ministry of Finance.

Detailed information about shareholders include the structure, composition, shareholders above 5% and the 20 largest shareholders, as well as the profile group of shareholders below 5% can be viewed on the Company Profile in the Annual Report of the Bank.
