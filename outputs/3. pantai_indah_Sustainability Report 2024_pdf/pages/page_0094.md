# Remunerasi Karyawan [F.20]

Perseroan memberikan hak dasar karyawan berupa pemberian gaji dan/atau remunerasi yang diberikan sesuai dengan ketentuan dan perturan yang berlaku mengenai Upah Minimum Regional (UMR). Dalam pemberian remunerasi, tidak terdapat perbedaan remunerasi antara karyawan laki-laki dan perempuan. Pemberian remunerasi dilakukan secara kompetitif sesuai dengan level jabatan dan kinerja karyawan. Untuk karyawan dengan tingkat terendah, Perseroan memberikan besaran gaji yang setara dengan kebijakan UMK yang berlaku. Berikut tabel remunerasi karyawan terhadap UMR di tahun 2024:

# Employee Remuneration [F.20]

The Company provides its employees with basic rights in the form of salary and/or remuneration in accordance with the applicable regulations regarding the Regional Minimum Wage (UMR). In terms of remuneration, there is no distinction between male and female employees. Remuneration is provided competitively, based on job level and employee performance. For employees at the lowest level, the Company provides a salary that aligns with the applicable UMR policy. Below is the table showing employee remuneration in relation to the UMK in 2024:

Selain menerima remunerasi berupa gaji bulanan, setiap karyawan juga berhak atas kompensasi atau manfaat non-tunai berupa asuransi dan tunjangan lainnya. Selain itu, Perseroan juga telah mendaftarkan setiap karyawannya ke dalam program Badan Penyelenggara Jaminan Sosial (BPJS) Kesehatan dan BPJS Ketenagakerjaan. Langkah ini dilakukan sebagai bentuk dukungan Perseroan terhadap program pemerintah dalam upaya meningkatkan perlindungan sosial bagi pekerja secara menyeluruh.

In addition to receiving remuneration in the form of monthly salary, each employee is also entitled to compensation or non-cash benefits in the form of insurance and other benefits. In addition, the Company has also registered each of its employees into the Social Security Administration Agency (BPJS) Health and BPJS Employment programs. This step is taken as a form of the Company's support for government programs in an effort to improve social protection for workers as a whole.
