Product Impact [F.28]

Perseroan telah melakukan serangkaian inisiatif dalam mengevaluasi keamanan produk dan jasa yang disediakan untuk memastikan keamanannya. Seluruh produk dan jasa yang dipasarkan telah disertai informasi penting yang perlu diketahui pelanggan, mulai dari deskripsi produk, spesifikasi teknis, fasilitas yang tersedia, serta benetif yang dapat diperoleh. Perseroan juga selalu memberikan informasi atas semua unit yang diserahterimakan, yang semuanya tertulis dalam Perjanjian Pengikatan Jual Beli (PPJB). Selain itu, Perseroan juga selalu memberikan informasi atas produk yang dijual, baik melalui sarana tatap muka seperti acara maupun melalui sarana daring, seperti media sosial.

Hingga akhir 2024, Perseroan tidak menerima keluhan terkait dampak negatif atas produk yang dihasilkan. Sebaliknya, masyarakat justru merasakan berbagai dampak positif, antara lain terpenuhinya harapan untuk memiliki hunian, ruko, maupun kantor, tersedianya alternatif pusat perbelanjaan dan hiburan, serta hadirnya beragam fasilitas umum, termasuk ruang pertemuan yang mendukung aktivitas sosial dan komunitas.

Sebagai hasil dari kualitas produk dan layanan yang konsisten, tidak ada produk Perseroan yang ditarik dari pasar atau dihentikan karena alasan teknis maupun keluhan pelanggan. [F.29]

The Company has undertaken a series of initiatives to evaluate the safety of the products and services provided to ensure their safety. All products and services marketed are accompanied by important information that customers need to know, ranging from Product descriptions, technical specifications, available facilities, and benefits that can be obtained. The Company also always provides information on all units handed over, all written in the Sales and Purchase Agreement (PPJB).In addition, the Company also always provides information on the products sold, both through face-to-face means such as events and through online means, such as social media.

Until the end of 2024, the Company did not receive any complaints related to negative impacts on the products produced. On the contrary, the community has experienced various positive impacts, including the fulfillment of expectations for owning homes, shops, and offices, the availability of alternative shopping and entertainment centers, and the presence of various public facilities, including meeting spaces that support social and community activities.

As a result of consistent product aand service quality, none of the Company's products have been withdrawn from the market or discontinued due to technical reasons or customer complaints. [F.29]
