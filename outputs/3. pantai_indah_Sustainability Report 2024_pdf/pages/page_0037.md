# Sumber Daya Manusia [C.3]

Human Resources [C3]

Per 31 Desember 2024, jumlah karyawan PIK2 tercatat sebanyak 669 orang, yang terdiri dari karyawan Perseroan sebanyak 53 orang dan karyawan Entitas Anak sebanyak 616 orang. Jumlah ini berkurang 49 orang atau 6,8% dibandingkan jumlah karyawan pada 2023 yang mencapai 718 orang. Penurunan jumlah karyawan tersebut disebabkan oleh realokasi karyawan proyek yang telah menyelesaikan project existing.

Berikut demografi karyawan Perseroan dalam 3 (tiga) tahun terakhir.

As of December 31, 2024, the total number of employees at PIK2 was recorded at 669, consisting of 53 employees from the Company and 616 employees from subsidiaries. This represents a decrease of 49 employees or 6.8% compared to the total number of employees in 2023, which was 718. The reduction in the number of employees was due to the realignment of project staff who had completed their existing projects.
