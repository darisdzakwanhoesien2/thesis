Pojok Literasi (Literacy Corner) is an initiative aimed at strengthening the culture of literacy within the community, with a primary focus on children aged 4 to 11. In an effort to create a more knowledgeable and competitive society, Literacy Corner offers a variety of activities designed to foster interest in reading and writing. This program serves as a bridge that connects children with sources of knowledge, including books, articles, and other digital media.
