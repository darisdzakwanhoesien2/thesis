# Program Penanaman Pohon

Dalam rangka memberikan solusi bagi masyarakat yang menghadapi dampak perubahan iklim serta mengurangi polusi udara, Perseroan telah menyediakan lahan untuk hutan kota dan juga taman umum atas ruang terbuka hijau yang nantinya akan ditanami berbagai pepohonan. Selain itu, Perseroan juga berpartisipasi dalam program CSR lingkungan berupa penanaman pohon bakau.

Informasi mengenai penanaman pohon dapat dibaca pada bab Kinerja Lingkungan Berkelanjutan pada Laporan Berkelanjutan ini.

# Tree Planting Program

In order to provide solutions for communities facing the impacts of climate change and reduce air pollution, the Company has provided land for urban forests and also public parks or green open spaces which will be planted with various trees. In addition, the Company also participates in environmental CSR programs in the form of mangrove tree planting.

For more information on tree planting, please refer to the Sustainable Environmental Performance of this Sustainability Report.

# Environmental Financing

The Company is consistently making efforts to preserve the environment to minimize the ecological footprint caused by its operational activities by preventing, mitigating, reducing, and remedying environmental harms. This includes initiatives to decrease pollution, waste, and ecosystem degradation. Details on environmental financing are provided on the Sustainable Environmental Performance section of this Sustainability Report.
