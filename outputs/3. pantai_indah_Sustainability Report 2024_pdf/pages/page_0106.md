# Tanggung Jawab Sosial Perusahaan [F.25], [S-12]

Perseroan melaksanakan program Tanggung Jawab Sosial (CSR) dengan mengacu kepada Kebijakan CSR Perseroan. Program CSR Perseroan didesain untuk mendukung keberlangsungan Perseroan, meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat, dan berkontribusi terhadap pencapaian Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB). Program CSR dilaksanakan dengan menyelaraskan strategi, budaya maupun visi dan misi perusahaan.

Berbagai inisiatif program yang dilakukan dalam program CSR ini merupakan bentuk investasi sosial bagi masyarakat. Perseroan berupaya menerapkan inisiatif program CSR yang efektif dan efisien sehingga dapat memberikan manfaat yang luas, berkelanjutan, serta mendukung peningkatan kualitas hidup masyarakat di sekitar wilayah operasional.

Program CSR Perseroan dilaksanakan dalam 4 (empat) pilar, yaitu: Pendidikan, Kesehatan, Ekonomi dan Lingkungan.

## Pendidikan

Melalui program #PIK2MENDIDIK, Perseroan hadir untuk mengatasi kesenjangan antara Perseroan dengan masyarakat lokal. Melalui konsep #BISAbersamaPIK2, Perseroan mendorong masyarakat lokal untuk berani bermimpi dan merajut asa bersama PIK2 melalui program Community Development di bidang Pendidikan.

Hingga 2024, kegiatan CSR di pilar pendidikan telah mencapai Rp1,6 miliar yang digunakan untuk mendukung perluasan program pendidikan diantaranya dengan memastikan pemerataan terhadap akses pendidikan bagi siswa SD, SMP dan SMA, serta mengurangi angka putus sekolah melalui program kejar paket B &amp; C di beberapa wilayah binaan Perseroan. Selain itu, Perseroan juga telah melakukan pembangunan beberapa sarana penunjang belajar seperti merenovasi PKBM dan mendukung kegiatan keagamaan di beberapa wilayah operasional Perseroan.

## Corporate Social Responsibility [F.25], [S-12]

The Company implements its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) program by referring to the Company's CSR policy. The Company's CSR programs are designed to support the Company's sustainability, improve community welfare, and contribute to the achievement of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The CSR program is implemented by aligning the Company's strategy, culture, and vision and mission.

The various program initiatives implemented under this CSR program represent a form of social investment for the community. The Company strives to apply effective and efficient CSR initiatives that can provide broad and sustainable benefits, while supporting improvements in the quality of life for communities around the operational area.

The Company's CSR programs are implemented in 4 (four) pillars, namely: education, health, economy and environment.

## Education

Through the #PIK2MENDIDIK program, the Company is present to overcome the gap between the Company and the local community. Through the concept of #BISAbersamaPIK2, the Company encourages local communities to dare to dream and knit their hopes together with PIK2 through Community Development programs in the field of education.

By 2024, the Company's CSR initiatives in education have totaled IDR1.6 billion. These funds supported expanded access to education, ensuring equal opportunities for elementary, middle, and high school students and reducing dropout rates through equivalency programs in targeted areas. The Company has also invested in educational infrastructure, including the renovation of community learning centers and support for religious activities in operational regions.
