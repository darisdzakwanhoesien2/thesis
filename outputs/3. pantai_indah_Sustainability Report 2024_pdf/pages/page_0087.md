# 4. Pendekatan Keberlanjutan

Penerapan prinsip keberlanjutan menjadi dasar dalam pengembangan Greenbelt ini. Penggunaan tanaman endemik yang adaptif terhadap iklim tropis serta penerapan teknologi ramah lingkungan menjadi bagian dari upaya efisiensi pemeliharaan dan pelestarian ekosistem. Selain itu, konektivitas ekosistem juga diperhatikan, terutama dalam menjaga habitat bagi spesies burung dan satwa lokal lainnya.

Dengan hadirnya Greenbelt, PIK2 diharapkan dapat menjadi kawasan yang harmonis antara fungsi hunian, komersial, dan pelestarian lingkungan. Inisiatif ini mencerminkan komitmen Perseroan dalam menciptakan ruang hidup yang hijau, sehat, dan berkelanjutan untuk generasi masa depan.

# 4. Sustainability Approach

Sustainability principles are integrated into the Greenbelt's development. This includes the use of native, climate-resilient plant species, and the application of environmentally friendly technologies to ensure efficient maintenance. The design also emphasizes the importance of green connectivity, supporting bird species and other wildlife, thus maintaining a functioning and interconnected ecosystem.

The presence of the Greenbelt in PIK2 reinforces the Company's commitment to developing an environmentally friendly and livable area, harmonizing residential and commercial functions with ecological preservation. It forms part of our broader efforts to provide a greener, healthier, and more sustainable environment for future generations.

The Company's environmental commitment is demonstrated through various initiatives in environmental management. In 2024, the Company allocated IDR 6.19 billion to environmental conservation, representing a  $136.97\%$  increase from the previous year. This rise in environmental expenses was driven by emission testing, in-house maintenance, LED lighting purchases for area illumination, and the launch of the polder system's operations.
