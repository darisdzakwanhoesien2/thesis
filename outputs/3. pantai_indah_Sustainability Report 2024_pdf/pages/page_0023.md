# Strategi Pencapaian Target

Perseroran memandang peluang untuk mencapai target keberlanjutan semakin terbuka lebar melalui penerapan pengelolaan risiko yang terarah dan terintegrasi. Oleh karena itu, Perseroan telah merumuskan strategi pengelolaan peluang dan risiko berbasis prinsip Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola ("LST" atau "ESG").

Perseroan meyakini bahwa capaian kinerja keberlanjutan pada tahun 2024 menjadi landasan penting bagi peningkatan kinerja di tahun-tahun mendatang. Melalui integrasi prinsip keberlanjutan dalam kegiatan operasional, Perseroan optimis bahwa PIK2 memiliki potensi untuk terus meningkatkan nilai tambah dan daya saing di industri properti.

Selain fokus pada pencapaian ekonomi, Perseroan juga berkomitmen terhadap dampak sosial dan lingkungan melalui implementasi berbagai inisiatif hijau serta program pengembangan komunitas yang berkelanjutan. Komitmen ini merupakan bagian dari upaya Perseroan dalam menciptakan ekosistem usaha yang bertanggung jawab dan bernilai jangka panjang.

# Target Achievement Strategy

The Company sees significant opportunities to achieve sustainability targets through the implementation of a focused and integrated risk management approach. Accordingly, the Company has developed a strategy for managing opportunities and risks based on Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles.

The Company believes that its sustainability performance achievements in 2024 form an essential foundation for enhanced performance in the coming years. By integrating sustainability principles into its operational activities, the Company is optimistic that PIK2 will continue to increase its value-added and competitiveness within the property industry.

Beyond its economic objectives, the Company is also committed to delivering positive social and environmental impacts through the implementation of various green initiatives and sustainable community development programs. This commitment is part of the Company's ongoing efforts to create a responsible and long-term value-driven business ecosystem.
