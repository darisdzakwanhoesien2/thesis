## Sapphire Category on ESG Implementation
### By Transparency International Indonesia &amp; Tempo Data Science

PT Pantai Indah Kapuk Dua Tbk (PANI) kembali menerima apresiasi dari Tempo Data Science (Tempo) dan Transparency International Indonesia (TII) di acara Malam Apresiasi Indeks Integritas Bisnis Lestari 2024.

Filosofi indeks bisnis lestari adalah perusahaan yang menginspirasi perubahan dan menyakinkan bahwa bisnis yang berintegritas dan berkelanjutan adalah kunci keberhasilan jangka panjang. Apresiasi telah melalui kurasi ketat dari 934 perusahaan yang terdaftar di BEI serta 5 perusahaan BUMN dengan aset dan valuasi pasar yang besar.

Tempo dan TII menilai PT Pantai Indah Kapuk Dua Tbk sudah tepat memperoleh penghargaan indeks bisnis lestari yang mana menunjukkan rekam jejak PANI mengupayakan praktek bisnis berintegritas, anti-korupsi, bisnis yang menjunjung tinggi HAM dan melestarikan lingkungan hidup demi keberlangsungan ekosistem usaha jangka panjang.

PT Pantai Indah Kapuk Dua Tbk (PANI) once again received recognition from Tempo Data Science (Tempo) and Transparency International Indonesia (TII) at the 2024 Sustainable Business Integrity Index Appreciation Night.

The philosophy behind the Sustainable Business Integrity Index is to spotlight companies that inspire change and reinforce the belief that integrity and sustainability are key drivers of long-term business success. This recognition followed a rigorous curation process involving 934 companies listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) and 5 large state-owned enterprises with substantial assets and market valuation.

Tempo and TII assessed that PT Pantai Indah Kapuk Dua Tbk was well-deserving of this award, which reflects PANI's strong track record in promoting ethical business practices, anti-corruption efforts, respect for human rights, and environmental stewardship for the long-term sustainability of its business ecosystem.
