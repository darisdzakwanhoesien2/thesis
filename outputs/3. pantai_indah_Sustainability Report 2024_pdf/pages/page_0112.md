# Ekonomi

Pilar ekonomi tidak hanya fokus pada kesenjangan, namun melalui #PIK2SEJAHTERA, Perseroan mendorong masyarakat lokal untuk meningkatkan kesejahteraan ekonomi mereka melalui program pemberdayaan masyarakat.

Hingga 2024, total dana yang disalurkan untuk pilar ekonomi adalah sebesar Rp3,38 miliar yang digunakan untuk mendukung pengembangan ekonomi kreatif dan pemberdayaan komunitas. Beberapa program unggulan yang diluncurkan meliputi pelatihan keterampilan ekonomi kreatif, asistensi dalam pengembangan dan pemasaran produk lokal, serta pembuatan dokumen social mapping untuk memahami potensi ekonomi masyarakat. Selain itu, kami memperkuat program kemitraan community development dan membangun gerai UMKM sebagai wadah promosi dan penjualan produk lokal.

Currently, freshwater aquaculture programs have been running in several villages, including Lemo Village and Pangkalan Village, with the main focus on catfish farming. This cultivation has become one of the alternative sources of livelihood for the local community, which not only provides additional economic opportunities, but also encourages community empowerment.

# Economy

The economic pillar does not only focus on the gap, but through #PIK2SEJAHTERA, the Company encourages local communities to improve their economic welfare through community empowerment programs.

Until 2024, the total funds disbursed for the economic pillar is IDR3.38 billion which is used to support the development of the creative economy and community empowerment. Some of the flagship programs launched include creative economy skills training, assistance in the development and marketing of local products, and the creation of social mapping documents to understand the economic potential of the community. In addition, we strengthened the community development partnership program and built MSME outlets to promote and sell local products.

Perseroan bekerjasama dengan seorang Affiliator untuk memberikan pelatihan kepada ibu-ibu PKK mengenai cara mendapatkan penghasilan dari rumah melalui program afiliasi. Pelatihan ini mencakup panduan praktis tentang cara membuat konten yang menarik, mudah dipahami, dan efektif dalam menarik perhatian penonton agar tertarik mengklik tautan afiliasi yang telah disediakan. Dengan demikian, diharapkan para peserta dapat mengoptimalkan platform digital untuk menambah penghasilan secara mandiri.

The Company collaborated with an affiliator to provide training to PKK mothers on how to earn income from home through affiliate programs. The training includes practical guidance on how to create content that is interesting, easy to understand, and effective in attracting viewers to click on the affiliate links provided. Thus, the participants are expected to optimize the digital platform to increase their income independently.
