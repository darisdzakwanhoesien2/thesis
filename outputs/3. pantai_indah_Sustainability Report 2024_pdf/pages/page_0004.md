# Lingkup Laporan

## Scope of the Report

Laporan Keberlanjutan ini mencakup informasi dan data yang dikumpulkan dari Perseroan dan daftar entitas seperti yang termuat dalam Laporan Tahunan 2024 dan Laporan Keuangan yang telah diaudit, pada bagian “Daftar Entitas Anak Perusahaan”. Meskipun begitu, Perseroan masih menetapkan batasan terhadap ruang lingkup dari data kinerja yang disajikan, namun cakupannya diperluas ke unit bisnis lainnya.

Penentuan batasan data di atas didasarkan pada kebutuhan untuk memastikan representasi seimbang dari unit bisnis Perseroan, dengan tujuan memberikan gambaran komprehensif atas aktivitas yang dilakukan oleh Perseroan.

This Sustainability Report includes information and data collected from the Company and a list of entities as contained in the 2024 Annual Report and the audited Financial Statements, in the “List of Subsidiaries” section. However, the Company still sets limitations on the scope of the performance data presented, but the scope is expanded to other business units.

The determination of the above data limits is based on the need to ensure a balanced representation of the Company’s business units, with the aim of providing a comprehensive picture of the activities carried out by the Company.

Approximately 280 local housewives from Teluknaga and Kosambi sub-districts received training and mentoring in cooking food products that have the potential to become viable small businesses. These efforts aim to create economic opportunities and foster high-quality self-reliance aligned with market standards. Among the participants, 80 were housewives, with the program specifically designed to inspire them not to view their roles as limitations, but as opportunities to remain productive and empowered. This initiative is expected to enhance women’s self-reliance and contribute to improving household economic resilience.
