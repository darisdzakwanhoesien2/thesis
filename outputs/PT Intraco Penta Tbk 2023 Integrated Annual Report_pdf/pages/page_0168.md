# Komite Manajemen Risiko

Risk Management Committee

Komite Manajemen Risiko bertanggung jawab kepada Dewan Komisaris dan membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam pelaksanaan tugasnya agar pengelolaan Perseroan dapat berlangsung dengan efisien dan efektif melalui sistem dan pelaksanaan pengawasan yang kompeten dan independen.

The Risk Management Committee is responsible to the Board of Commissioners and assists the Board of Commissioners in carrying out its duties so that the management of the Company can take place efficiently and effectively through a competent and independent supervision system and implementation.
