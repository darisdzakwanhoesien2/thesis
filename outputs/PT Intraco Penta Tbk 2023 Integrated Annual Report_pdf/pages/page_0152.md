## PENILAI

Penilaian kinerja terdiri dari self-assessment oleh anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, yang hasilnya diverifikasi oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, dan evaluasi tahunan oleh Pemegang Saham pada RUPST. Tidak ada pihak independen yang mengevaluasi kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi tahun 2022.

## ASSESSMENT

Performance assessment consists of self-assessment by members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, the results of which are verified by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, and annual evaluation by Shareholders at the AGMS. There is no independent party that evaluates the performance of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors on 2022.
