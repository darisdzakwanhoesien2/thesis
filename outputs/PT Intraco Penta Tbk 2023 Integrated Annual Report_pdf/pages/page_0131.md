# PELAKSANAAN DAN PUTUSAN RUPS LUAR BIASA

Sepanjang tahun 2023, Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan 1 (satu) kali RUPS Luar Biasa pada tanggal tanggal 06 Januari 2023 bertempat di Auditorium INTA Building - Lantai 5, INTA HQ Building, Jl. Raya Cakung Cilincing KM 3.5 Jakarta Utara. RUPS Luar Biasa dilaksanakan Secara elektronik melalui aplikasi eASY.KSEI.

# IMPLEMENTATION AND DECISION OF THE EXTRAORDINARY GMS FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR 2022

Throughout 2023, INTA has held 1 (one) Extraordinary GMS on January 6, 2023, at the INTA Building Auditorium - 5th Floor, INTA HQ Building, Jl. Raya Cakung Cilincing KM 3.5 North Jakarta. The Extraordinary GMS was held electronically through the eASY.KSEI application.
