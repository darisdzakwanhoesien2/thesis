# 2015

Perseroan melalui PT Intraco Penta Prima Servis (IPPS) dipercaya menjadi distributor Dressta, merek alat berat jenis dozer yang dimiliki oleh perusahaan China LiuGong Dressta Machinery. Alat berat Dressta diproduksi di Polandia dan memiliki standar tinggi dengan harga bersaing.

Selain itu Perseroan juga dipercaya dalam penjualan untuk memenuhi beragam jenis suku cadang seperti ban, undercarriage, hidrolik, filter dll. dari merek kelas dunia seperti Techking dan Kotrack Undercarriage.

The company, through PT Intraco Penta Prima Servis (IPPS), is trusted to be the distributor of Dressta, a brand of dozer-type heavy equipment owned by the Chinese company LiuGong Dressta Machinery. Dressta machines are manufactured in Poland and are of a high standard at competitive prices.

Apart from that, the company is also trusted in sales to fulfill various types of spare parts, such as tires, undercarriage, hydraulics, filters, etc., from world-class brands such as Techking and Kotrack Undercarriage.

# 2015

Perseroan memasuki bisnis Pembangkit Listrik (PLTU) dengan mendapatkan Power Purchase Agreement dari PLN untuk membangun pembangkit listrik tenaga batubara di Bengkulu.

- CCL, anak perusahaan INTA, memperoleh kontrak infrastruktur dan fabrikasi dari BUMN antara lain untuk proyek LRT, Oil &amp; Gas, dll.
- The Company entered the Power Plant (PLTU) business by obtaining a Power Purchase Agreement from PLN to build a coal-fired power plant in Bengkulu.
- CCL is subsidiary of INTA, obtained infrastructure and fabrication contracts from SOEs, among others, for LRT, Oil &amp; Gas, etc. projects.
