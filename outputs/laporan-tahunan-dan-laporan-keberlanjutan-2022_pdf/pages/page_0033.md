Sampoerna is committed to leveraging science and technology to provide a range of scientifically-substantiated, smoke-free products as a better choice for adult smokers, compared to continued smoking. This may have a major positive impact on public health.

The HEETS factory in Indonesia focuses on fulfilling demands of the domestic market and Asia Pacific. This factory is PMI's first production facility for smoke-free products in Southeast Asia and the seventh in the world.
