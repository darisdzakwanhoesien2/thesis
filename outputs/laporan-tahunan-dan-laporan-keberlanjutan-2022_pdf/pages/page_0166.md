## Cianjur Disaster Relief

SAR Sampoerna Rescue together with our partner, SUN Foundation, provided assistance to people of Cianjur, West Java, who were affected by the earthquake disaster in November 2022. Under the national coordination by the National Search and Rescue Agency (BASARNAS) and the National Agency for Disaster Countermeasure (BNPB), the team managed to distribute 220 blankets, two platoon tents, 72 tarpaulins, and 25,000 liters of water to several evacuation camps, as well as mobilized SAR vehicles to the affected area.

## GOVERNANCE ASPECT

We believe that governance is crucial in ensuring that all aspects of the Company's business operations comply with our corporate values.
