¹⁹ Workplace Accident Rate (including AFR, Recordable Work-Related Injuries Rate, High-Consequence Work-Related Injuries Rate, and Fatality Rate) is calculated as follows: number of workplace accidents x 1,000,000 divided by total hours worked. Data recorded for work accidents is based on OHS regulations. Definition of AFR, Recordable Injury, High-Consequence Work-Related Injury can be found on Glossary page 202
