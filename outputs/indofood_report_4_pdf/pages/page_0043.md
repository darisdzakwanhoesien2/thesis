SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY AND GOVERNANCE

To ensure we achieve our goals in creating sustained and tangible impacts, we develop KPIs and set quantifiable targets that we continually measure and monitor to understand how we are progressing against these targets. The sustainability governance framework strengthens the current governance approach. It comprises Good Corporate Governance, Risk Management, and the Company's Code of Conduct.

## GOOD CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

Good Corporate Governance ("GCG") determines stakeholders trust in a business. This enables regular, meaningful, and rewarding engagement between companies and stakeholders. Our Board is responsible for establishing sound governance practices which are developed based on Indonesia's regulatory framework and which adhere to key principles of GCG including, transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness.

For more information and details on our GCG, please refer to Indofood 2021 Annual Report that can be downloaded through www.indofood.com.

## RISK MANAGEMENT SYSTEM [102-30]

Indofood has implemented a comprehensive approach in managing risks across the Group. This is done through a systematic, integrated, and coordinated risk management strategy and framework executed by the ERM unit. Risk management is carried out via a top-down approach that involves the BOD in the evaluation of high-level risks, and a bottom-up approach where subsidiaries and business units assess risks specific to their operations. The Corporate ERM team comprehensively analyzes the outputs and consolidates the identified key risks. It then submits periodic reports capturing risk exposures and mitigating measures to the BOD and the Audit Committee. In line with its ERM, Indofood has also identified its sustainability risks.

More details on Indofood's ERM are available in the Company's 2021 Annual Report.

## TATA KELOLA PERUSAHAAN YANG BAIK

Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (Good Corporate Governance atau "GCG") merupakan faktor yang memengaruhi kepercayaan para pemangku kepentingan terhadap suatu bisnis. Hal ini mendorong keterlibatan bersama antara perusahaan dan pemangku kepentingan secara berkala, bermakna serta saling menguntungkan. Direksi kami bertanggung jawab untuk menjalankan praktik tata kelola yang baik dan mengacu pada kerangka peraturan di Indonesia serta sesuai dengan prinsip-prinsip GCG yang mengedepankan aspek transparansi, akuntabilitas, tanggung jawab, independensi dan kesetaraan.

Penjelasan lebih rinci terkait praktik GCG kami dapat dilihat dalam Laporan Tahunan Indofood tahun 2021 dan dapat diunduh melalui www.indofood.com.

## SISTEM MANAJEMEN RISIKO [102-30]

Indofood telah menerapkan pendekatan yang komprehensif dalam mengelola berbagai risiko di seluruh Grup. Pendekatan ini dilakukan melalui strategi dan kerangka kerja manajemen risiko yang sistematis, terintegrasi dan terkoordinasi melalui ERM. Pengelolaan risiko dilakukan melalui pendekatan top-down yang melibatkan Direksi dalam mengevaluasi risiko-risiko utama secara keseluruhan, serta pendekatan bottom-up yang melibatkan anak perusahaan dan unit operasional dalam mengkaji risiko-risiko yang spesifik di masing-masing unit usaha. Tim ERM Perseroan melakukan analisis secara komprehensif dan mengonsolidasi berbagai risiko utama yang teridentifikasi, serta menyampaikan laporan secara berkala mengenai paparan risiko dan tindakan mitigasi yang perlu dilakukan kepada Direksi dan Komite Audit. Sejalan dengan ERM, Indofood juga telah melakukan identifikasi terhadap risiko-risiko keberlanjutannya.

Penjelasan lebih rinci terkait ERM Indofood tersedia dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan tahun 2021.
