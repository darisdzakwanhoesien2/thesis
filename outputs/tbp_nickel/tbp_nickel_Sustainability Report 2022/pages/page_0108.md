There are two techniques in general to multiply and reproduce the seedlings, which are the generative and vegetative techniques. Generative techniques use the seed itself to grow a new seedling where it is planted in the medium and moved from the nursery after seedlings are ready to be planted. Meanwhile the vegetative techniques are a process in which plants are reproduced from stems, roots and leaves, and this can be done through cutting, grafting and tissue culture. Secara umum ada dua teknik untuk memperbanyak dan mereproduksi bibit, yaitu teknik generatif dan vegetatif. Teknik generatif menggunakan benih itu sendiri untuk menumbuhkan bibit baru di mana benih tersebut ditanam di media dan dipindahkan dari persemaian setelah bibit siap ditanam. Sementara itu, teknik vegetatif adalah proses di mana tanaman diperbanyak dari batang, akar, dan daun, dan ini dapat dilakukan melalui stek, okulasi, dan kultur jaringan.
