Brand represents the right to use "iBox" trademark for an indefinite future period as granted by the Certificate issued by Director General of Intellectual Property Rights on behalf of the Minister of Law and Human Rights of Republic of Indonesia, while Licenses represent the agreement with Apple Inc. ("Apple") to distribute and sell its products, and to operate Apple Authorized Retail Stores. The related Licenses have been renewed for several times at little or no cost, therefore the Licenses are determined to have indefinite useful lives.
