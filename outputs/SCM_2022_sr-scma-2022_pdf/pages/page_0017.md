## Sekilas Tentang SCM

Sejak tahun 1999, Perseroan hadir memberikan kontribusi di industri media Indonesia melalui berbagai program, konten, tayangan, dan layanan di bidang media. SCM juga memperluas layanan pembuatan konten berkualitas dan jasa periklanan yang unggul, manajemen artis, serta manajemen fasilitas siaran dan produksi film.

## SCM in Brief

The Company has been contributing to the Indonesian media industry through various programs, contents, shows, and media services since 1999. SCM has also expanded its services to quality content creation and advertising services, artist management, and broadcast and film production facility management.
