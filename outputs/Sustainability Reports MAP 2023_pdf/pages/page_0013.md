# Products, Services, Business Activities, Supply Chains, and Other Relevant Business Relationships [1,4]

MAP is a leading lifestyle retailer in Indonesia. We have a network of over 3,000 retail stores and a portfolio of more than 150 exclusive brands covering sports, fashion, department stores, kids, food &amp; beverage, digital, and lifestyle products.

## Activities in the fiscal year 2023 include: (2-6)

- Department Stores (including supermarkets);
- Specialty Stores:

1. Active (sports, leisure &amp; kids);
2. Fashion (including cosmetics and beauty);
3. Digital;
- Food &amp; Beverage; and
- Others (including bookstores, handicrafts &amp; souvenirs, travel &amp; lifestyle, beauty &amp; health).

# Produk, Jasa, Kegiatan Usaha, Rantai Pasok, dan Hubungan Bisnis Lain yang Relevan [1,4]

MAP adalah peritel gaya hidup terkemuka di Indonesia. Kami memiliki jaringan lebih dari 3.000 gerai ritel dan portofolio lebih dari 150 brand eksklusif yang mencakup sports, fashion, department stores, kids, food &amp; beverage, digital serta produk-produk gaya hidup.

Kegiatan pada tahun buku 2023: (2-6)

- Department Stores (termasuk supermarket);
- Specialty Stores:

1. Active (sports, leisure &amp; kids);
2. Fashion (termasuk cosmetics dan beauty);
3. Digital;
- Food &amp; Beverage; dan
- Lain-lain (termasuk gerai buku, kerajinan tangan &amp; cinderamata, travel &amp; lifestyle, beauty &amp; health).
