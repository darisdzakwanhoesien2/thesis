Our product labels include nutritional information such as carbohydrates, dietary fiber, protein, fat, vitamins, and minerals, along with the percentage of recommended dietary allowance, serving size, and the number of servings per package to help educate consumers on appropriate portion sizes. Additionally, nutrition labels highlight allergen notices within the ingredients list for better consumer awareness.

Our Healthier Choice logo on the front of the product packaging stands out easily to identify the product as a healthier alternative.

As the leading snack food brand, we prioritize understanding consumers' snacking habits and provide clear nutritional information about our products. All our snack food items feature simplified nutrition details on the front of the packaging, making it easy for consumers to understand key information about energy, fat, salt, and sugar.

Before every product launch, we conduct market research to gather consumer insights. Through an iterative process, we adapt the product based on the research findings and publicize product launches to consumers via advertisements, product brands' websites, customer service, and consumer education programs.

Sebagai produsen makanan ringan bermerek yang terkemuka, kami mengutamakan pemahaman konsumen terhadap kebiasaan konsumsi makanan ringan, serta memberikan informasi nilai gizi yang jelas.
