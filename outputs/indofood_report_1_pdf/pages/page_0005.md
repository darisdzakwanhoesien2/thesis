2024 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT

# ABOUT THIS SUSTAINABILITY REPORT [2-3, 2-5, 2-6]

TENTANG LAPORAN KEBERLANJUTAN KAMI

This marks our fourth Sustainability Report, covering the time frame from January 1 to December 31, 2024. The report details Indofood's commitments, strategic initiatives, and approaches during its sustainability journey. We emphasize our commitment to improving our sustainability practices in line with the goals established by the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals ("SDGs"). Furthermore, this report provides thorough insights into our progress toward achieving our sustainability targets.

Our annual Sustainability Report is created in compliance with the Financial Services Authority Regulation (Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan or "POJK") No. 51/POJK.03/2017, which outlines the implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies. It also follows the criteria established in the Circular Letter of the Financial Services Authority (Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan or "SEOJK") No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021, detailing the Form and Contents of the Annual Report of Issuers or Public Companies. Additionally, our report aligns with the Global Reporting Initiatives ("GRI") Sustainability Reporting Standards, incorporating the most recent GRI Universal Standards from 2021. Climate related disclosure in this year's report follows the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures ("TCFD") recommendations, now part of International Financial Reporting Standards ("IFRS") Sustainability Disclosure Standards.

During the reporting period, there were no notable changes in business activities, material topic boundaries, share ownership, supply chain structure, and business scale.

To guarantee the accuracy and reliability of this report, Indofood has retained PT Moores Rowland Indonesia, an independent assurance service provider, to conduct verification and limited assurance. PT Moores Rowland Indonesia did not participate in the preparation of this sustainability report, and there are no declared conflicts of interest. All financial information for 2024 presented in this report is sourced from the Indofood 2024 Annual Report.

Laporan ini merupakan Laporan Keberlanjutan kami yang keempat untuk periode 1 Januari hingga 31 Desember 2024, yang menyampaikan komitmen, pendekatan strategi, serta inisiatif Indofood sepanjang perjalanan keberlanjutannya. Melalui laporan ini, kami ingin menunjukkan komitmen kami yang kuat dalam meningkatkan praktik keberlanjutan, selaras dengan tujuan yang ditetapkan oleh Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (Sustainable Development Goals atau "SDGs") Perserikatan Bangsa-Bangsa. Selain itu, kami juga menguraikan dalam laporan ini gambaran menyeluruh atas kemajuan kami dalam mencapai target-target keberlanjutan.

Laporan Keberlanjutan tahunan ini disusun dengan berpedoman pada Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("POJK") No. 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten dan Perusahaan Publik dan sesuai dengan kriteria pada Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("SEOJK") No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 tentang Bentuk dan Isi Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik. Penyusunan laporan ini juga sesuai dengan Standar Pelaporan Keberlanjutan Global Reporting Initiatives ("GRI") termasuk versi terbaru GRI Universal Standard 2021. Pengungkapan terkait iklim dalam laporan tahun ini mengikuti rekomendasi Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures ("TCFD"), saat ini merupakan bagian dari Standar Pelaporan Keuangan Internasional (International Financial Reporting Standards or "IFRS").

Selama periode pelaporan, tidak terdapat perubahan yang signifikan pada kegiatan usaha, batasan topik material, kepemilikan saham, struktur rantai pasokan dan skala bisnis.
