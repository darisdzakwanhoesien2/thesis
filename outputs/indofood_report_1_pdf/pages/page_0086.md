LAPORAN KEBERLANJUTAN 2024

As 94% of our refinery CPO supply is from our own POMs, we focus our Policy compliance audits on our internal supply chains, including our nucleus estates, plasma estates and our POMs. In 2024, we conducted 502 visits, workshops, and audits on 100% of our POMs and their supplying estates. Besides compliance with our policy, our assessments also center on:

- Agronomy (GAP, yield, soil health, and crop protection);
- Responsible operations (safety, biodiversity, peatland, fire risk, human rights, FPIC, and community engagement);
- Efficiency of operations (energy and water consumption, GHG emissions, waste production); and
- Compliance with Government regulation and ISPO certifications. [308-1, 414-1]

Sekitar 94% pasokan CPO berasal dari PKS kami sendiri, kebijakan kami berfokus pada audit kepatuhan terhadap rantai pasokan internal, termasuk perkebunan inti, lahan petani plasma dan PKS. Pada tahun 2024, kami telah melaksanakan 502 kunjungan, workshop dan audit terhadap 100% PKS termasuk perkebunan-perkebunan para pemasok. Selain kepatuhan terhadap kebijakan, kegiatan penilaian juga difokuskan pada aspek berikut:

- Agronomi (GAP, hasil panen, kesuburan tanah dan perlindungan tanaman);
- Kegiatan operasional yang bertanggung jawab (keamanan, keanekaragaman hayati, lahan gambut, risiko kebakaran, hak asasi manusia, FPIC, serta pelibatan masyarakat);
- Efisiensi operasional (konsumsi energi dan air, emisi GRK, limbah yang dihasilkan); serta
- Kepatuhan terhadap peraturan Pemerintah dan sertifikasi ISPO. [308-1, 414-1]
