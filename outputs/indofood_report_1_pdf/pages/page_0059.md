# PRODUCT SAFETY, QUALITY, AND HALAL
[2-23, 2-24, 3-3]
KEAMANAN PANGAN, MUTU DAN KEHALALAN PRODUK

As a prominent food and beverage manufacturing company in Indonesia, we are committed to provide only Halal products which are safe and high quality for all. Indofood's Food Safety Policy, Quality Pledge, and Halal Commitments, which have been approved by the BOD, guarantees that our operations, products, and suppliers comply with the highest food safety and quality standards, including all applicable regulatory requirements.

Our Food Safety Policy includes these key principles that guide business units across the Group:

1. Commitment to produce safe, quality, and Halal products;
2. Commitment to comply with the relevant requirements and regulations;
3. Commitment to ensure the quality, food safety, and Halal of the products through implementation of relevant food management systems such as ISO 9001, ISO 22000, The Global Food Safety Initiative ("GFSI") recognized Food Safety System Certification ("FSSC") 22000, as well as Halal Standard;
4. Obligation to assign key person in each division and operational unit to oversee food safety, quality, and Halal control implementation;
5. Ensure consistency of the management system implementation through conducting periodic internal audits;
6. Provide knowledge and skills related to food safety to the employees, according to the function and position;
7. Actively monitor and respond to consumer feedback;
8. Ensure food safety commitments throughout the supply chain.

Sebagai perusahaan produk makanan dan minuman yang terkemuka di Indonesia, kami berkomitmen untuk hanya memproduksi produk Halal yang aman dan bermutu tinggi bagi semua konsumen. Kebijakan Keamanan Pangan, Maklumat Mutu dan Komitmen Halal Indofood yang telah mendapat persetujuan Direksi, memastikan bahwa kegiatan operasional, produk dan para pemasok telah memenuhi standar keamanan pangan dan mutu tertinggi, termasuk semua ketentuan peraturan yang berlaku.

Kebijakan Keamanan Pangan kami menetapkan prinsip-prinsip utama yang menjadi pedoman bagi unit-unit usaha di seluruh Grup:

1. Berkomitmen untuk menghasilkan produk-produk pangan yang aman, bermutu dan Halal;
2. Berkomitmen untuk mematuhi berbagai ketentuan dan peraturan yang relevan;
3. Berkomitmen untuk menjamin mutu, keamanan pangan dan kehalalan produk melalui penerapan sistem manajemen pangan yang relevan, termasuk ISO 9001, ISO 22000, Food Safety System Certification ("FSSC") 22000 yang telah diakui oleh The Global Food Safety Initiative ("GFSI"), serta Standar Halal;
4. Berkewajiban untuk menunjukkan penanggung jawab di setiap divisi dan unit operasional guna mengoordinasikan penerapan pengendalian keamanan pangan, mutu dan Halal;
5. Memastikan konsistensi penerapan sistem manajemen melalui pelaksanaan audit internal secara berkala;
6. Berbagi pengetahuan dan keterampilan terkait keamanan pangan kepada karyawan sesuai peran dan fungsinya;
7. Memantau dan merespons secara aktif umpan balik dari konsumen;
8. Memastikan komitmen keamanan pangan di seluruh rantai pasokan.
