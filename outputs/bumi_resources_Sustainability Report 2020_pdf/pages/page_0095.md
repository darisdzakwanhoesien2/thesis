The Company and its Subsidiaries implement Policies and Procedures that have been prepared through a series of work programs with the aim of Protecting Employees Against the Covid-19 Pandemic. The program is regularly communicated and evaluated through the Covid-19 Internal Report mechanism. The work programs carried out are:

# Program Kerja Perlindungan Kepada Karyawan Terhadap Pandemi Covid-19 Work Program to Protect Employees Against the Covid-19 Pandemic

|  Program Kerja Working Program | Jenis Kegiatan Activity  |
| --- | --- |
|  Administratif Administrative | 1) Pelaporan status kesehatan karyawan dan kontraktor Reporting on the health status of employees and contractors 2) Prosedur, Instruksi Kerja dan Panduan lainnya Procedures, Work Instructions and other Guidelines  |
|  Sosialisasi, Promosi dan Kampanye Dissemination, Promotion and Campaign | 1) Sosialisasi terkait Covid-19, Cara Pencegahan dan Penanganannya. Dissemination Related How to Prevent and Manage Covid-19. 2) Pemberlakuan dan promosi protokol Kesehatan 3M (mencuci tangan, memakai masker & menjaga jarak) / pencegahan Covid-19. Enforcement and promotion of the 3M Health protocol (washing hands, wearing masks & maintaining distance) / preventing Covid-19. 3) HSE Talk secara online dan terbatas. HSE Talk online and limited. 4) Pengaktifan Internal Memo Internal Memo Activation  |
|  Pengadaan Alat Pelindung Diri (APD) dan Peralatan Kesehatan Procurement of Personal Protec- tive Equipment (PPE) and Medical Equipment | Pengadaan obat-obatan, peralatan dan APD khusus penanganan Covid-19 Procurement of medicines, equipment and PPE specifically for handling Covid-19  |
|  Pengelolaan Hygiene dan Sanitasi Management of Hygiene and Sani- tation | 1) Kegiatan disinfeksi area kerja secara berkala Periodic disinfection of the work area 2) Pemberian “Pencegahan Covid-19 Kit” kepada para karyawan. Providing employees with the “Covid-19 Prevention Kit”.  |
|  Kesiapsiagaan Tanggap Darurat Emergency Response Preparedness | 1) Pemantauan dan pemeriksaan kesehatan karyawan (pemeriksaan suhu, pemeriksaan kesehatan berkala, rapid test antibody/serology, rapid test antigen dan RT-PCR). Monitoring and medical checks of employees (temperature checks, periodic health checks, rapid antibody / serology tests, rapid antigen tests and RT-PCR). 2) Pelatihan dan simulasi kesiagaan dan tanggap darurat Covid-19. Training and simulation on Covid-19 preparedness and emergency response. 3) Penyediaan tempat isolasi mandiri bagi karyawan yang terkonfirmasi Covid-19. Provision of self-isolation places for employees who have been positively confirmed Covid-19. 4) Pengadaan vitamin dan makanan bergizi untuk karyawan. Provision of vitamins and nutritious food for employees.  |
|  Keamanan Security | 1) Membangun pos-pos keamanan sebelum memasuki area kerja. Pos kemanan ini juga berfungsi sebagai screening awal kondisi kesehatan karyawan, yaitu dengan cara pengecekan suhu tubuh sebelum memasuki area tertentu Establish security posts before entering the working area. This security post also functions as an initial screening of the employee's health condition by checking body temperature before entering certain areas 2) Pendataan kapasitas ruangan dan memasang rambu-rambu jumlah maksimal personil yang dapat mengisi ruangan tersebut dengan tetap memperhatikan jarak aman. Data collection on room capacity and put signs for the maximum number of personnel who can fill the room while still paying attention to maintain safe distances.  |
