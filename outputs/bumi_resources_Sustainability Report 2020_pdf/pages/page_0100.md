Proses pengelolaan risiko Kesehatan, Keselamatan Kerja, Lingkungan dan Komunitas (K3LK) terdiri dari tahapan-tahapan proses yang meliputi:

1. Penetapan lingkup K3LK;
2. Identifikasi dan penilaian risiko K3LK;
3. Perlakuan risiko K3LK; dan
4. Pemantauan dan pelaporan risiko K3LK.

The process of managing Health, Safety, Environment and Community (HSEC) risks consists of process stages which include:

1. Determination of the scope of HSEC;
2. Identification and assessment of HSEC risks;
3. HSEC risks treatment; and
4. Monitoring and reporting of HSEC risks.
