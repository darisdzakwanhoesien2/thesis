## Arus Kas dari Aktivitas Operasi

Arus kas yang diperoleh dari aktivitas operasi terdiri dari penerimaan kas dari pelanggan sebesar Rp65.461,88 miliar (2023: Rp59.792,13 miliar). Selain itu, terdapat penerimaan kas dari pendapatan bunga sebesar Rp38,29 miliar (2023: Rp18,17 miliar).

Sedangkan kas yang digunakan untuk aktivitas operasi terdiri dari pembayaran kas kepada pemasok dan karyawan sebesar Rp60.609,79 miliar (2023: Rp57.308,75 miliar), pembayaran beban bunga sebesar Rp583,92 miliar (2023: Rp490,34 miliar), dan pembayaran pajak penghasilan sebesar Rp2.067,14 miliar (2023: Rp1.128,87 miliar).

Kas neto yang diperoleh dari aktivitas operasi hingga akhir tahun 2024 sebesar Rp2.239,32 miliar (2023: Rp882,34 miliar).

## Arus Kas dari Aktivitas Investasi

Arus kas yang diperoleh dari aktivitas investasi terdiri dari hasil penjualan aset tetap sebesar Rp47,88 miliar (2023: Rp1,67 miliar). Pada tahun 2023 Perseroan menerima deviden dari entitas asosiasi sebesar Rp2,43 miliar. (2024: 0).

The Company's cash and cash equivalents position at the end of 2024 was Rp1,627.64 billion, up 38.2% compared to the position at the end of 2023 of Rp1,177.97 billion.

## Cash Flow from Operating Activities

Cash flows generated from operating activities consist of cash receipts from customers of Rp65,461.88 billion (2023: Rp59,792.13 billion). Additionally, there was cash receipts from interest income of Rp38.29 billion (2023: Rp18.17 billion).

While cash used for operating activities consists of cash payments to suppliers and employees of Rp60,609.79 billion (2023: Rp57,308.75 billion), payment for interest expense of Rp583.92 billion (2023: Rp490.34 billion), and payment for income tax of Rp2,067.14 billion (2023: Rp1,128.87 billion).

Net cash provided by operating activities until the end of 2024 amounted to Rp2,239.32 billion (2023: Rp882.34 billion).

## Cash Flow from Investing Activities

Cash flow provided by investing activities consists of proceeds from the sale of fixed assets amounting to Rp47.88 billion (2023: Rp1.67 billion). In 2023, the Company received dividends from associated entities amounting to Rp2.43 billion. (2024: 0).
