# Aset Lancar

Aset lancar sebesar Rp13.308,10 miliar, naik 2,6% dari Rp12.964,56 miliar pada tahun 2023. Kenaikan aset lancar terutama disebabkan oleh:

- Taksiran tagihan pajak penghasilan naik 4.412,4% dari Rp28,90 miliar menjadi Rp1.304,23 miliar.
- Pajak dibayar di muka naik 51,4% dari Rp745,55 miliar menjadi Rp1.128,57 miliar.

Sebaliknya, terjadi penurunan pada pos-pos berikut:

- Persediaan - neto turun 11,4% dari Rp8.045,60 miliar menjadi Rp7.130,92 miliar.
- Piutang usaha pihak ketiga - neto turun 19,4% dari Rp1.348,15 miliar menjadi Rp1.086,13 miliar.
- Piutang lain-lain pihak ketiga - neto turun 43,8% dari Rp664,04 miliar menjadi Rp373,06 miliar.

# Aset Tidak Lancar

Aset tidak lancar sebesar Rp8.466,29 miliar, naik 13,1% dari Rp7.482,89 miliar pada tahun 2023. Kenaikan aset tidak lancar terutama disebabkan oleh:

- Uang muka pembelian aset tetap dan aset takberwujud naik 85,8% dari Rp100,49 miliar menjadi Rp186,71 miliar.
- Aset tetap - neto naik 12,4% dari Rp2.217,10 miliar menjadi Rp2.492,67 miliar.
- Aset hak guna - neto naik 12,8% dari Rp1.593,84 miliar menjadi Rp1.797,06 miliar.
- Taksiran tagihan pajak penghasilan naik 22,5% dari Rp1,906,17 miliar menjadi Rp2.334,85 miliar.

# Current Assets

Current assets amounted to Rp13,308.10 billion, up 2.6% from Rp12,964.56 billion in 2023. The increase in current assets was mainly due to:

- Estimated claims for tax refund increased by 4,412.4% from Rp28.90 billion to Rp1,304.23 billion.
- Prepaid taxes increased by 51.4% from Rp745.55 billion to Rp1,128.57 billion.

Conversely, there was a decrease in the following items:

- Inventories - net decreased by 11.4% from Rp8,045.60 billion to Rp7,130.92 billion.
- Trade receivables third parties - net decreased by 19.4% from Rp1,348.15 billion to Rp1,086.13 billion.
- Other receivables from third parties - net decreased by 43.8% from Rp664.04 billion to Rp373.06 billion.

# Non-Current Assets

Non-current assets amounted to Rp8,466.29 billion, up 13.1% from Rp7,482.89 billion in 2023. The increase in non-current assets was mainly due to:

- Advances for purchase of fixed assets and intangible assets increased by 85.8% from Rp100.49 billion to Rp186.71 billion.
- Fixed assets - net increased by 12.4% from Rp2,217.10 billion to Rp2,492.67 billion.
- Right-of-use assets - net increased by 12.8% from Rp1,593.84 billion to Rp1,797.06 billion.
- Estimated claims for tax refunds increased by 22.5% from Rp1,906.17 billion to Rp2,334.85 billion.
