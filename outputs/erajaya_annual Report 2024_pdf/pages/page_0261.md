## KEBIJAKAN UNTUK MERESPONS TANTANGAN DALAM PEMENUHAN STRATEGI KEBERLANJUTAN

### Nilai-Nilai Keberlanjutan

Selama 28 tahun di industrinya, Erajaya telah bertransformasi menjadi perusahaan multibisnis yang mencakup ritel elektronik konsumen, gaya hidup aktif, makanan dan minuman, hingga solusi kendaraan listrik pintar (Smart EV). Kami meyakini, perjalanan panjang ini dapat diraih berkat kerja keras dan komitmen yang kuat dari seluruh insan Erajaya untuk menerapkan nilai-nilai keberlanjutan yang telah dikembangkan dan disepakati sebagai Nilai-Nilai Inti (Core Values) Erajaya, yakni Innovation, Leadership, Excellent Customer Service, Agility, dan Digital.

Seiring dengan perkembangan organisasi dan skala usaha, sosialisasi dan internalisasi Core Values dan Pedoman Perilaku (Code of Conduct) Erajaya menjadi penting mengingat banyak kader pemimpin muda di anak perusahaan dan bergabungnya perusahaan baru melalui investasi Erajaya sehingga standpoint mengenai perusahaan terbuka yang prudent, transparan, menjunjung tinggi keterbukaan harus senantiasa terjaga. Oleh karena itu, Erajaya secara berkala melakukan sosialisasi dan internalisasi yang disampaikan melalui portal internal perusahaan dan sebagai materi pada program orientasi karyawan baru.

We fully recognize that long-term business sustainability requires a deep commitment to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles as a foundation for managing risks and creating lasting value. For Erajaya, sustainable growth is intrinsically linked to how we identify, assess, and manage environmental and social risks associated with our operations, factors that can significantly influence our business trajectory.

## POLICY TO ADDRESS CHALLENGES IN FULFILLING THE SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY

### Sustainability Values

Over the course of 28 years in the industry, Erajaya has transformed into a multi-business company spanning consumer electronics retail, active lifestyle, food and beverage, and smart electric vehicle (Smart EV) solutions. We believe this long journey has been made possible by the hard work and strong commitment of all Erajaya personnel in upholding sustainability values, which have been developed and agreed upon as the Company's Core Values: Innovation, Leadership, Excellent Customer Service, Agility, and Digital.

As the organization and business scale continue to grow, the socialization and internalization of Erajaya's Core Values and Code of Conduct have become increasingly important. This is particularly relevant with the rise of young leadership within subsidiaries and the inclusion of new companies through Erajaya's investments, requiring a consistent corporate stance of prudence, transparency, and openness. For this reason, Erajaya regularly conducts internalization efforts through the company's internal portal and includes these values as part of new employee orientation programs.
