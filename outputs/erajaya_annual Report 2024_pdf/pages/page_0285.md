Social &amp; Environmental Responsibility

#

Mendorong pemberdayaan potensi masyarakat lokal untuk menghasilan pendapatan.

Encourage the empowerment of local community potential to generate income.

Mengembangkan kawasan hutan sebagai pusat reboisasi dan model pendidikan berbasis lingkungan dengan melibatkan masyarakat.

Develop forest area as reforestation centers and environmental-based education models by involving the community.

Mendukung proses alur bisnis dan kebutuhan bisnis melalui peningkata kompetensi guru dan siswa dalam bisnis inti kami.

Support business flow process and business needs through competency improvement of teachers and students in our core business.

![img-283.jpeg](img-283.jpeg)

Kami ingin ikut berkontribusi meningkatkan kualitas pendidikan dengan menyediakan akses pendidikan yang lebih luas bagi masyarakat. Sejak beberapa tahun terakhir, kami telah bekerja sama dengan Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset, dan Teknologi dalam program yang bertujuan peningkatan kompetensi siswa Satuan Pendidikan Vokasi (SPV), termasuk menyelenggarakan berbagai kegiatan kerja sama dengan SPVseperti talk show HerVocational, Hiring Corner, dan stand untuk memamerkan karya siswa SMK.

Pada tahun 2024, kami menginisiasi Program Peningkatan SDM Vokasi Regional, Program Guru Merdeka Mengajar, Program Vocapreneur, Program Guru Tamu dan Kunjungan Industri, dan Program Magang Industri (Erajaya Micro Learning/EMiL).

We are eager to contribute to improving the quality of education by providing wider access to education for the community. Since the last few years, we have been collaborating with the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology in a program aimed at increasing the competency of Vocational Education Unit (SPV) students, which included holding various collaborative activities with SPV such as the HerVocational talk show, Hiring Corner, and a stand to showcase the work of vocational school students.

In 2024, we initiated the Regional Vocational HR Improvement Program, Merdeka Teachers Program, Vocapreneur Program, Guest Teacher Program and Industrial Visit, and Industrial Internship Program (Erajaya Micro Learning/EMiL).
