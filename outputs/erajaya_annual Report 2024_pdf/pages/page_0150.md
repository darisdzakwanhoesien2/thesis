# INFORMASI DAN FAKTA MATERIAL YANG TERJADI SETELAH TANGGAL LAPORAN AKUNTAN

1. Pendirian PT Era Dealer Otomotif

Pada tanggal 5 Maret 2025, Perseroan melalui SES mendirikan PT Era Dealer Otomotif (EDO) di mana SES memiliki 99,99% kepemilikan pada EDO dengan modal yang disetorkan oleh SES sebesar Rp24.999.000,000 Sedangkan setoran modal dari kepentingan nonpengendali sebesar Rp1.000.000.

2. Pendirian PT Chagee Era Indonesia

Pada tanggal 12 Februari 2025, Perseroan dan Tea Explorer Pte. Ltd. mendirikan PT Chagee Era Indonesia melalui EBN, di mana EBN memiliki 40% kepemilikan pada PT Chagee Era Indonesia.

3. Perubahan Komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Berdasarkan Keputusan RUPSLB tanggal 19 Maret 2025, susunan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan mengalami perubahan. Susunan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang baru diungkapkan di bagian Profil Perusahaan di dalam Laporan Tahunan ini.

# SUBSEQUENT MATERIAL INFORMATION AND FACT AFTER ACCOUNTANT REPORTING DATE

1. Establishment of PT Era Dealer Otomotif

On March 5, 2025, the Company through SES, established PT Era Dealer Otomotif (EDO), where SES has 99.99% ownership in EDO with capital contributed by SES amounting to Rp24,999,000,000, while capital contribution from non-controlling interests amounted to Rp1,000,000.

2. Establishment of PT Chagee Era Indonesia

On February 12, 2025, the Company and Tea Explorer Pte. Ltd. established PT Chagee Era Indonesia through EBN, where EBN has 40% ownership in PT Chagee Era Indonesia.

Based on the Resolution of the EGMS dated March 19, 2025, the composition of the Company's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors has changed. The new composition of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is disclosed in the Company Profile section in this Annual Report.
