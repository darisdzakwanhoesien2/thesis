Erajaya Digital memperkenalkan Google Android AI Zone di sejumlah gerai erafone untuk memberikan pengalaman baru bagi pelanggan dalam menikmati kecanggihan teknologi terkini melalui perangkat dengan fitur kecerdasan buatan. Google Android AI Zone tersedia di sejumlah gerai erafone seperti Central Park Jakarta, Bintaro Jaya Xchange Mall dan Summarecon Mall Bekasi.

Erajaya Digital introduced the Google Android AI Zone at select erafone stores offering customers a new way to experience cutting-edge technology through devices with artificial intelligence features. Google Android AI Zone is available in several erafone stores such as Central Park Jakarta, Bintaro Jaya Xchange Mall and Summarecon Mall Bekasi.

## 5 Desember | December 5

![img-44.jpeg](img-44.jpeg)

Erajaya Active Lifestyle meresmikan tiga gerai DJI Experience Store masing-masing di Summarecon Mall Bekasi, Pondok Indah Mall 1, dan Senayan City. Kehadiran tiga gerai baru ini untuk melayani pelanggan yang ingin mendapatkan produk-produk drone, kamera, gimbal dan aksesoris dari merek DJI.

Erajaya Active Lifestyle inaugurated three DJI Experience Store outlets at Summarecon Mall, Pondok Indah Mall 1, and Senayan City. The presence of these three new outlets is to serve customers who want to get drone products, cameras, gimbals, and accessories from the DJI brand.
