Beberapa faktor yang mendorong pertumbuhan segmen ini meliputi; meningkatnya kesadaran gaya hidup sehat, terutama pascapandemi COVID-19, yang memicu trenolahraga secara mandiri maupun dalam komunitas, perkembangan komunitasolahraga dan media sosial yang menjadi platform promosi organik bagi gaya hidup aktif, dukungan fasilitas publik dan maraknya penyelenggaraanolahraga massalseperti maraton, turnamen tenis/ golf, dan kegiatan outdoorseperti hiking/ lintas alam ringan yang meningkatkan minat publik. Selain itu, kemajuan teknologi dan tren fashion yang menggabungkan fungsi dan estetika dalam produkseperti sportswear dan sneakers, menjadikan kegiatanolahraga sebagai bagian dari fashion statement sehari-hari (trenathleisure).

Produkseperti kaos lari,legging/shorts,dan sepatu lari premium dari merek internasional memiliki permintaan tinggi terutama di kota besar. Teknologi dalam sepatu lari seperti cushioning systems atau smart tracking menjadi daya tarik konsumen urban. Selain itu, acara tahunan seperti Jakarta Marathon dan Maybank Marathon turut mendorong penjualan produk yang relevan.

Sepatu kets tidak lagi terbatas untukolahraga,melainkan menjadi elemen penting dalam gaya hidup urban. Kolaborasi antara merekolahraga dan fashion,seperti JD Sports dan berbagai lini limited edition menarik perhatian generasi muda. Sementara aktivitas luar ruang juga semakin meningkatseperti aktivitas hiking, camping, dan eksplorasi alam pascapandemi sehingga mendongkrak permintaan pada perlengkapan luar ruang. Produkseperti sepatu hiking,jaket tahan air,dan aksesoris pendukung menjadi kategori yang tumbuh stabil, terutama di segmen milenial dan professional muda.

Pasar Internet of Things (IoT) di Indonesia menunjukkan perkembangan yang signifikan didukung oleh kolaborasi antara pemerintah dan pemangku kepentingan industri untuk meningkatkan produk lokal dan membangun ekosistem digital yang inklusif.

Several factors are driving the growth of this segment, including: the heightened awareness of healthy living, particularly in the post-COVID-19 era, which has fueled trends in both individual and community-based exercise; the development of sports communities and social media platforms that serve as organic promotion channels for the active lifestyle; as well as public infrastructure support and the growing number of large-scale sporting events such as marathons, tennis/ golf tournaments, and outdoor activities like hiking or nature trekking that have spurred public interest. In addition, advances in technology and fashion trends that combine functionality and aesthetics in products, such as sportswear and sneakers, have made physical activity an integral part of everyday fashion (the athleisure trend).

Products such as running shirts, leggings/ shorts, and premium running shoes from international brands are in high demand, particularly in major urban centers. Footwear technologies such as advanced cushioning systems and smart tracking features appeal strongly to urban consumers. Annual events like the Jakarta Marathon and Maybank Marathon further boost sales of relevant products.

Sneakers are no longer limited to sports use; they have become a key element of urban lifestyle fashion. Collaborations between sports and fashion brands, such as JD Sports and various limited-edition lines, attract significant interest from younger generations. At the same time, outdoor activities such as hiking, camping, and nature exploration have grown in popularity since the pandemic, driving up demand for outdoor gear. Products such as hiking shoes, waterproof jackets, and supporting accessories continue to see steady growth, especially among millennials and young professionals.

The Internet of Things (IoT) market in Indonesia has shown significant development supported by collaboration between the government and industry stakeholders to enhance local product development and build an inclusive digital ecosystem.
