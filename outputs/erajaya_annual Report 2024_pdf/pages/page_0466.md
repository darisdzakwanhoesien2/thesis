As of 3 September 2024, CG Computers Sdn Bhd (CG), entered into a loan agreement with MBSB Bank Berhad ("MBSB Bank"). Based on the agreement, CG obtained Letter of Credit (LC), Tawarruq Working Capital Financing (TWCF), Bank Guarantee (BG) and Cashline (CL). The total credit amount utilized shall not exceed RM52,000,000.

The above facilities are charged with the following interest:
