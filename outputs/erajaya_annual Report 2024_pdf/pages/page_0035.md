# Laporan Dewan Komisaris

REPORT OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

Direksi telah memaparkan Rencana Bisnis tahun 2025, termasuk rencana ekspansi gerai baru, serta menambah merek dan produk ritel baru ke dalam portofolionya. Langkah ini bertujuan untuk memperkuat posisi Perseroan di pasar dan memenuhi kebutuhan konsumen yang terus berkembang. Perseroan juga telah menetapkan strategi untuk meningkatkan produktivitas dari gerai yang sudah ada, seperti penguatan layanan dan customer experience, digitalisasi dan integrasi O2O, serta penyesuaian tata letak dan visual merchandising di gerai-gerai yang ada di pusat perbelanjaan. Dewan Komisaris mendukung penuh Rencana Bisnis yang disusun Direksi karena meyakini bahwa langkah-langkah strategis yang diambil Direksi akan menghasilkan pertumbuhan yang solid dan memperkuat posisi Perseroan di industri ritel Indonesia ke depan.

# PANDANGAN ATAS PENERAPAN TATA KELOLA PERUSAHAAN

Dewan Komisaris senantiasa mendorong penerapan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (good corporate governance/ GCG) di Perseroan melalui sistem manajemen yang efektif disertai dengan proses pengawasan, evaluasi, dan perbaikan berkelanjutan untuk menjaga keselarasan praktik GCG dengan best practice. Dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasan, Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh Komite Audit. Komite Audit bertugas menelaah laporan keuangan Perseroan, independensi akuntan publik, kecukupan manajemen risiko dan pengendalian internal, serta kepatuhan Perseroan terhadap perundangan-undangan dan peraturan yang berlaku.

Secara berkala, Dewan Komisaris menerima hasil penelaahan atas efektivitas pengendalian internal dan manajemen risiko yang dilakukan oleh Komite Audit bekerja sama dengan Audit Internal Perseroan. Hasil penelaahan dan rekomendasi perbaikan disampaikan kepada Direksi untuk ditindaklanjuti. Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa sistem pengendalian internal dan manajemen risiko di Perseroan cukup memadai dan efektif untuk memastikan kegiatan operasional Perseroan dapat berjalan dengan baik.

The Board of Directors has presented the 2025 Business Plan, including plans for new store expansions and the addition of new retail brands and products to the Company's portfolio. These efforts aim to strengthen the Company's market position and meet the evolving needs of consumers. The Company also plans to enhance productivity at existing stores by improving services and customer experience, advancing digitalization and O2O integration, and optimizing store layout and visual merchandising, particularly in mall-based locations. The Board of Commissioners fully supports the Business Plan laid out by the Board of Directors, as we believe these strategic actions will result in strong growth and reinforce the Company's leadership in Indonesia's retail industry.

# VIEWS ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF GOOD CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

The Board of Commissioners continues to encourage the application of good corporate governance (GCG) practices through effective management systems, accompanied by oversight, evaluation, and continuous improvement to ensure alignment with best practices. In fulfilling its supervisory role, the Board of Commissioners is supported by the Audit Committee, which is tasked with reviewing the Company's financial statements, the independence of public accountants, the adequacy of risk management and internal controls, and the Company's compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Periodically, the Board receives results of reviews on internal control and risk management effectiveness, conducted by the Audit Committee in collaboration with the Company's Internal Audit. These findings and improvement recommendations are submitted to the Board of Directors for follow-up. The Board of Commissioners believes that the Company's internal control and risk management systems are adequate and effective in ensuring smooth business operations.
