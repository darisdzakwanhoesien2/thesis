It encompasses the Company's consolidated financial position as of December 31, 2024, along with its consolidated financial performance and cash flows for the year concluded on that date, in accordance with Indonesian Financial Accounting Standards.

# Aset

Jumlah aset Perseroan per 31 Desember 2024 sebesar Rp21.774,39 miliar, naik 6,5% dari Rp20.447,45 miliar pada periode yang sama tahun sebelumnya. Kenaikan jumlah aset disebabkan oleh kenaikan aset lancar sebesar 2,6% atau Rp343,54 miliar dan aset tidak lancar sebesar 13,1% atau Rp983,40 miliar.

# Assets

The Company's total assets as of December 31, 2024 were Rp21,774.39 billion, up 6.5% from Rp20,447.45 billion in the same period the previous year. The increase in total assets was driven by a 2.6% increase in current assets (Rp343.54 billion) and a 13.1% increase in non-current assets (Rp983.40 billion).
