# PERANAN DIREKSI DALAM PERUMUSAN STRATEGI DAN KEBIJAKAN STRATEGIS

Sebelum memasuki tahun 2024, Direksi menetapkan Rencana Bisnis yang menjabarkan tujuan, sasaran, dan strategi Perseroan untuk satu tahun ke depan yang diselaraskan dengan Rencana Jangka Panjang Perusahaan. Dalam proses perumusannya, Direksi berkoordinasi dengan Dewan Komisaris dalam rapat bersama Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Direksi memaparkan proyeksi industri dan memberikan penjelasan atas draft Rencana Bisnis kepada Dewan Komisaris. Dewan Komisaris secara aktif memberikan pendapat dan rekomendasi yang menjadi masukan untuk finalisasi Rencana Bisnis Perseroan. Selanjutnya, Dewan Komisaris memberikan persetujuan dan pengesahan Rencana Bisnis sebelum tahun buku dimulai.

# PROSES YANG DILAKUKAN DIREKSI UNTUK MEMASTIKAN IMPLEMENTASI STRATEGI

Rencana Bisnis yang telah disetujui menjadi acuan bagi seluruh direktorat, bisnis vertikal, departemen, hingga ke tingkat individu untuk melakukan penjabaran sasaran strategis dan menetapkan indikator kinerja utama (KPI) yang harus dicapai. Dengan adanya target KPI berjenjang yang jelas, Direksi dapat memantau dan mengevaluasi efektivitas dari semua implementasi strategi yang sedang berjalan melalui rapat

Throughout the year, the Company successfully opened 278 new stores, increasing the total number of retail outlets to 2,194 by the end of 2024, spread across Indonesia, Malaysia, and Singapore. This expansion is supported by 77 Company-owned distribution centers and a distribution network that includes over 54,000 third-party retail stores throughout Indonesia.

Erajaya Digital focused on expanding its retail presence through both monobrand and multibrand electronics stores. Erajaya Active Lifestyle expanded its brand portfolio and new business lines, including Under Armour for fashion and sports apparel. The expansion of monobrand retail stores, especially in the IoT and fashion apparel segments, such as Garmin, DJI, ASICS, and JD Sports, continued to be a strategic priority. Through its Erajaya Active Lifestyle vertical, the Company officially entered the Smart EV business as part of a modern lifestyle offering, through a strategic collaboration with XPENG. This new venture marks a significant move into the EV market, where the Company acts as the Exclusive Brand Holder Agent (ATPM) for XPENG products in Indonesia.

Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment introduced several new retail brands, including Curry Up, Bacha Coffee, and Wetzel's Pretzels, while continuing the retail expansion of Paris Baguette and Grand Lucky. Under Erajaya Beauty &amp; Wellness, a collaboration with CARiNG Pharmacy Retail Management Sdn Bhd from Malaysia brought 14 Wellings pharmacies to Indonesia by the end of 2024.

# THE ROLE OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS IN FORMULATING STRATEGY AND STRATEGIC POLICY

Prior to entering 2024, the Board of Directors established a Business Plan outlining the Company's objectives, targets, and strategies for the upcoming year, aligned with the Company's Long-Term Plan. During its formulation, the Board of Directors coordinated with the Board of Commissioners through joint meetings. In these meetings, the Board of Directors presented industry projections and provided detailed explanations of the draft Business Plan to the Board of Commissioners. The Board of Commissioners actively offered feedback and recommendations, which served as valuable input in finalizing the Business Plan. The Board of Commissioners subsequently approved and ratified the Business Plan before the start of the fiscal year.

# PROCESSES UNDERTAKEN BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS TO ENSURE STRATEGY IMPLEMENTATION

The approved Business Plan is used as a reference by all directorates, business verticals, departments, and even individuals to break down strategic objectives and identify key performance indicators (KPIs) to be achieved. With clearly defined KPI targets, the Board of Directors can monitor and assess the effectiveness of ongoing strategy implementation at regular management and Business Development Committee
