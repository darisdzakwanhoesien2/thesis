# STRATEGI PENCAPAIAN TARGET

## Pengelolaan Risiko dalam Menerapkan Praktik Keberlanjutan

Erajaya memahami bahwa pengelolaan risiko secara menyeluruh merupakan bagian penting dalam mencapai target keberlanjutan. Oleh karena itu, secara berkala kami melakukan proses identifikasi, analisis, mitigasi, evaluasi, pemantauan, dan komunikasi terhadap berbagai risiko operasional yang dapat menghambat pencapaian tujuan bisnis, khususnya di sektor ritel. Selain risiko keuangan dan ekonomi, Erajaya juga memberi perhatian serius terhadap risiko sosial dan lingkungan yang berpotensi memengaruhi keberlangsungan usaha dan reputasi perusahaan.

## Pemanfaatan Peluang dan Prospek Usaha

Permintaan terhadap smartphone, laptop, dan komputer terus meningkat, didorong oleh adopsi perangkat 5G dan percepatan digitalisasi. Pasar Internet of Things (IoT) juga tumbuh pesat, sementara industri active lifestyle menunjukkan tren positif berkat meningkatnya kesadaran masyarakat akan pentingnya kesehatan dan kebugaran. Di sisi lain, pasar kendaraan listrik (EV) di Indonesia masih dalam tahap awal, namun menunjukkan potensi pertumbuhan yang kuat, didukung oleh kebijakan pemerintah, termasuk hilirisasi nikel dan insentif fiskal.

Sektor kesehatan mencatat pertumbuhan stabil dalam beberapa tahun terakhir, dengan bertambahnya apotek independen dan jaringan di area perkotaan dan semi perkotaan. Industri makanan dan minuman juga berkembang, sejalan dengan perubahan gaya hidup konsumen. Pertumbuhan ritel modern, seperti Grand Lucky Superstore dan jaringan minimarket, terus berlanjut seiring meningkatnya permintaan.

Pada 2024, strategi utama Perseroan difokuskan pada ekspansi jaringan ritel dan penambahan merek global dari berbagai principal untuk menjangkau segmen pasar yang lebih luas. Selain itu, peningkatan produktivitas dan profitabilitas gerai yang telah ada juga menjadi prioritas.

Dalam menyusun strategi keberlanjutan, Erajaya mempertimbangkan berbagai faktor seperti strategi bisnis, target kinerja, portofolio, kondisi makroekonomi, isu global, serta kontribusi terhadap Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (SDG). Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris secara aktif terlibat dalam perumusan dan pengawasan strategi keberlanjutan yang mencakup aspek ESG. Prinsip keberlanjutan terus diinternalisasi dalam setiap proses bisnis, dan pemetaan profil bisnis terhadap dampak lingkungan dan sosial dilakukan secara berkala, terutama seiring dengan ekspansi usaha. Untuk mendukung pelaporan dan pengelolaan risiko lingkungan dan sosial, Erajaya membentuk Task Force ESMS Project lintas divisi.

# STRATEGY TO ACHIEVE TARGETS

## Risk Management in Implementing Sustainability Practices

Erajaya understands that comprehensive risk management is a crucial part of achieving sustainability targets. Therefore, we regularly conduct the processes of identification, analysis, mitigation, evaluation, monitoring, and communication of various operational risks that could hinder the achievement of business objectives, especially in the retail sector. In addition to financial and economic risks, Erajaya also pays serious attention to social and environmental risks that could affect business continuity and the company's reputation.

## Exploiting Opportunities and Business Prospects

The demand for smartphones, laptops, and computers continues to rise, driven by the adoption of 5G devices and accelerating digitalization. The Internet of Things (IoT) market is also growing rapidly, while the active lifestyle industry shows positive trends due to increasing public awareness of the importance of health and fitness. On the other hand, the electric vehicle (EV) market in Indonesia is still in its early stages but shows strong growth potential, supported by government policies, including nickel downstreaming and fiscal incentives.

The healthcare sector has experienced stable growth in recent years, with an increasing number of independent and chain pharmacies in urban and semi-urban areas. The food and beverage industry is also expanding in line with changing consumer lifestyles. The growth of modern retail, such as Grand Lucky Superstore and minimarket networks, continues as demand increases.

In 2024, the Company's main strategy is focused on retail network expansion and the addition of global brands from various principals to reach a broader market segment. In addition, increasing the productivity and profitability of existing stores is also a priority.

In developing its sustainability strategy, Erajaya considers various factors such as business strategy, performance targets, portfolio, macroeconomic conditions, global issues, and contributions to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG). The Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners are actively involved in the formulation and supervision of sustainability strategies that include ESG aspects. The principles of sustainability are continuously internalized in every business process, and mapping of the business profile against environmental and social impacts is carried out periodically, especially as the business expands. To support environmental and social risk reporting and management, Erajaya has established a cross-divisional Task Force for the ESMS Project.
