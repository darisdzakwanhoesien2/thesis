# KONDISI EKONOMI GLOBAL DAN DOMESTIK TAHUN 2024

International Monetary Fund (IMF) dalam laporan World Economic Outlook Oktober 2023 meramalkan ekonomi global tahun 2024 sebagai "is expected to continue moving at a slow pace rather than experiencing rapid growth, with increasing divergences across regions". Banyak negara masih menghadapi dampak jangka panjang dari perang dagang, volatilitas harga pangan dan energi, konflik di Ukraina dan Timur Tengah, masalah perubahan iklim, dan inflasi yang tinggi.

Secara keseluruhan, hingga akhir tahun 2024 ekonomi global mengalami perlambatan dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya walaupun lebih baik dari proyeksi awal. Menurut laporan World Bank terbaru, pertumbuhan global tercatat di kisaran 2,6%, lebih baik dari perkiraan sebesar 2,4%, tetapi lebih rendah dari 2,9% pada tahun 2023.

Ketidakpastian ekonomi global menjadi tantangan utama bagi pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia. Perlambatan ekonomi negara mitra dagang utama Indonesia terutama China mengakibatkan turunnya permintaan dan harga komoditas global. Fluktuasi harga komoditas utama seperti batu bara dan minyak sawit mentah (CPO) mempengaruhi neraca perdagangan Indonesia.

Meskipun dihadapkan pada tantangan global dan domestik yang tinggi, Kondisi ekonomi Indonesia pada tahun 2024 menunjukkan perbaikan yang moderat dan terkendali. Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS) mengumumkan, pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2024 sebesar 5,03%, sedikit melambat dibandingkan capaian tahun 2023 sebesar 5,05% dan lebih rendah dari target Anggaran Pendapatan dan Belanja Negara (APBN) 2024 sebesar 5,2%. Faktor yang menahan kinerja ekonomi terutama adalah penurunan ekspor pada paruh kedua tahun 2024. Ekspor Indonesia melemah signifikan akibat penurunan harga komoditas global. Sebaliknya, impor menunjukkan tanda pemulihan yang relatif lebih baik. Ekspor berkontribusi sebesar 22,2% terhadap PDB Indonesia, sementara impor memberikan kontribusi sebesar 20,4%.

# GLOBAL AND DOMESTIC ECONOMIC CONDITIONS IN 2024

The International Monetary Fund (IMF), in its World Economic Outlook published in October 2023, forecasted the global economy in 2024 as "expected to continue moving at a slow pace rather than experiencing rapid growth, with increasing divergences across regions". Many countries continued to face the lingering effects of trade wars, volatility in food and energy prices, ongoing conflicts in Ukraine and the Middle East, climate change issues, and persistently high inflation.

Overall, the global economy experienced a slowdown in 2024 compared to the previous year, although outcomes were better than initially projected. According to the latest World Bank report, global growth stood at approximately 2.6%, surpassing the earlier estimate of 2.4%, yet falling short of the 2.9% growth achieved in 2023.

Global economic uncertainty remained a key challenge for Indonesia's economic growth. The economic slowdown of Indonesia's major trading partners, particularly China, led to decreased demand and falling global commodity prices. Fluctuations in the prices of key export commodities such as coal and crude palm oil (CPO) had a notable impact on Indonesia's trade balance.

Despite these significant global and domestic challenges, Indonesia's economic performance in 2024 showed moderate and controlled improvement. The Statistics Indonesia (BPS) reported a GDP growth of 5.03% in 2024, slightly lower than the 5.05% recorded in 2023 and below the State Budget (APBN) 2024 target of 5.2%. The main factor holding back economic performance was a decline in exports in the second half of the year, primarily due to falling global commodity prices. In contrast, imports showed relatively better signs of recovery. Exports accounted for 22.2% of Indonesia's GDP, while imports contributed 20.4%.
