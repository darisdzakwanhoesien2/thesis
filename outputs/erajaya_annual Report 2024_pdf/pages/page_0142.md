# Liabilitas Jangka Pendek

Liabilitas jangka pendek sebesar Rp10.875,98 miliar, naik 5,3% dari Rp10.330,32 miliar pada tahun 2023. Kenaikan liabilitas jangka pendek terutama disebabkan oleh:

- Utang usaha naik 5,4% dari Rp3.671,37 miliar menjadi Rp3.869,88 miliar.
- Utang lain-lain naik 18,0% dari Rp1.404,29 miliar menjadi Rp1.657,05 miliar.
- Utang bank jangka panjang naik 247,8% dari Rp484,17 miliar menjadi Rp1.683,85 miliar.
- Liabilitas sewa naik 45,7% dari Rp427,95 miliar menjadi Rp623,46 miliar.

Sebaliknya terjadi penurunan pada pos-pos berikut:

- Utang bank jangka pendek turun 31,0% dari Rp3.962,99 miliar menjadi Rp2.733,98 miliar.
- Utang pajak turun 57,0% dari Rp237,20 miliar menjadi Rp101,89 miliar.

# Liabilitas Jangka Panjang

Liabilitas jangka panjang sebesar Rp1.841,02 miliar, turun 7,3% dari Rp1.986,36 miliar pada tahun 2023. Penurunan liabilitas jangka panjang terutama karena utang jangka panjang, setelah dikurangi bagian yang akan jatuh tempo dalam waktu satu tahun - utang bank jangka panjang dan liabilitas sewa, secara total turun 23,2% dari Rp1.158,67 miliar menjadi Rp889,92 miliar. Sebaliknya, terdapat kenaikan liabilitas imbalan kerja jangka panjang yang naik 43,5% dari Rp247,93 miliar menjadi Rp335,76 miliar.

# Liabilities

The Company's total liabilities as of December 31, 2024 were Rp12,316.68 billion, up 3.3% from Rp12,717.00 billion in the same period the previous year. The increase in total liabilities was primarily due to a 5.3% increase in current liabilities (Rp545.66 billion), while non-current liabilities decreased by 7.3% (Rp145.34 billion).

# Current liabilities

Current liabilities amounted to Rp10,875.98 billion, up 5.3% from Rp10,330.32 billion in 2023. The increase in current liabilities was mainly due to:

- Trade payables increased by 5.4% from Rp3,671.37 billion to Rp3,869.88 billion.
- Other payables increased by 18.0% from Rp1,404.29 billion to Rp1,657.05 billion.
- Long-term bank loans increased by 247.8% from Rp484.17 billion to Rp1,683.85 billion.
- Lease liabilities increased by 45.7% from Rp427.95 billion to Rp623.46 billion.

Conversely, there was a decrease in the following items:

- Short-term bank loans decreased by 31.0% from Rp3,962.99 billion to Rp2,733.98 billion.
- Taxes payable decreased by 57.0% from Rp237.20 billion to Rp101.89 billion.

# Non-current Liabilities

Non-current liabilities amounted to Rp1,841.02 billion, down 7.3% from Rp1,986.36 billion in 2023. The decrease in non-current liabilities was mainly due to long-term debt, net of current maturities - long-term bank loans and lease liabilities, in total decreased by 23.2% from Rp1,158.67 billion to Rp889.92 billion. Conversely, there was an increase in long-term employee benefits liabilities, which increased by 43.5% from Rp247.93 billion to Rp335.76 billion.
