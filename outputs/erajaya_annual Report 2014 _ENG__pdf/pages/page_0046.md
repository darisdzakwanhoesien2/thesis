# PROFILE OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

Ardy Hady Wijaya
President Commissioner

Ardy Hady Wijaya is the founder of the Company. He serves as President Commissioner of the Company since 1998.

Currently, he also serves as President Commissioner of PT Data Citra Mandiri (2004 - present), PT Teletama Artha Mandiri (2004 - present), PT Erafone Artha Retailindo (2008-present), PT Multi Media Seluler (2009-present), PT Sinar Eka Selaras (2011-present), PT Data Media Telecommunications (2011-present), and PT Prakarsa Prima Sentosa (2011-present).

In addition, he also served as Commissioner of PT Nusa Gemilang Abadi (2006-present), PT Era Sukses Abadi (2011-present) and PT Eralink International (2011-present). Prior to founding the Company, he was a manager of a family-owned department store in Indonesia.
