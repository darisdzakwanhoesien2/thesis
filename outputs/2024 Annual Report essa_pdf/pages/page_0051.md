Indonesian citizen, born in Jakarta, 49 years old as of December 2024 and currently domiciled in Jakarta. He earned a Bachelor's degree and a Master of Science in Operations Research and Industrial Engineering at Cornell University, Ithaca, New York in 1998. He has held his position as Commissioner of the Company since March 2022 and continues his position as Commissioner of the Company based on Deed of Minutes at the Company's EGMS No. 36 dated October 4, 2023 made before Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., Notary in Jakarta.
