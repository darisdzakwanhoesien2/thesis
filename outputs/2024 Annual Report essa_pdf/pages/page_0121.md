# KEBIJAKAN REMUNERASI DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI

## Prosedur Penetapan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Prosedur penerapan remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan dilakukan dengan memperhatikan rekomendasi dari Dewan Komisaris berdasarkan kinerja, tanggung jawab, dan kontribusi masing-masing anggota terhadap pencapaian tujuan Perseroan. Rekomendasi tersebut diajukan kepada Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) untuk disetujui. Penetapan remunerasi juga mempertimbangkan kondisi keuangan Perseroan, tingkat kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, serta praktik tata kelola perusahaan yang baik.

# REMUNERATION POLICY FOR THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS AND BOARD OF DIRECTORS

## Procedure for Determining the Remuneration of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors

The procedure for implementing remuneration for the Company's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is carried out by taking into account the recommendations of the Board of Commissioners based on the performance, responsibilities, and contribution of each member to the achievement of the Company's goals. The recommendations are submitted to the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) for approval. The determination of remuneration also considers the Company's financial condition, level of compliance with laws and regulations, and good corporate governance practices.
