# TINJAUAN EKONOMI GLOBAL DAN NASIONAL

Perekonomian global pada tahun 2024 menunjukkan pemulihan yang tidak merata, di tengah ketidakpastian akibat inflasi yang tinggi dan konflik geopolitik di beberapa kawasan. Kebijakan moneter ketat yang diterapkan oleh bank sentral di berbagai negara berhasil menekan inflasi, tetapi mengurangi laju pertumbuhan ekonomi di negara-negara maju seperti Amerika Serikat dan Uni Eropa. Sebaliknya, negara-negara berkembang menunjukkan daya tahan yang lebih baik, terutama di Asia, yang terus menjadi motor penggerak utama pertumbuhan global. Harga energi, termasuk gas alam sebagai bahan baku utama dalam produksi amoniak, mengalami fluktuasi karena ketegangan geopolitik dan transisi menuju energi terbarukan.

Di tengah tantangan global, perekonomian Indonesia tumbuh sebesar 5,05% pada 2024, melampaui rata-rata pertumbuhan ekonomi dunia. Sektor energi menjadi salah satu kontributor utama, termasuk melalui penguatan kebijakan hilirisasi sumber daya alam. # GLOBAL AND NATIONAL ECONOMIC OVERVIEW

The global economy in 2024 showed an uneven recovery, amid uncertainty due to high inflation and geopolitical conflicts in some regions. Tight monetary policies implemented by central banks in various countries succeeded in curbing inflation, but reduced the pace of economic growth in advanced economies such as the United States and the European Union. In contrast, emerging economies showed better resilience, especially in Asia, which continued to be the main driver of global growth. Energy prices, including natural gas as the main feedstock in ammonia production, fluctuated due to geopolitical tensions and the transition towards renewable energy.

Amidst global challenges, Indonesia's economy grew by 5.05% in 2024, surpassing the world's average economic growth. This growth is driven by domestic consumption, increased natural resource-based exports, and investment in the energy and infrastructure sectors. The energy sector is one of the main contributors, including through strengthening natural resource downstreaming policies. The government continues to accelerate the development of energy infrastructure to support the growth of strategic industries such as ammonia, which is one of Indonesia's leading commodities in the global market.
