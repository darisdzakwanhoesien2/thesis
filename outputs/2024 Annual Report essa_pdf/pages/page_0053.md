Warga Negara Indonesia, lahir di Bandung, 37 tahun per Desember 2024 dan saat ini berdomisili di Jakarta. Beliau memperoleh gelar Bachelor of Science in Finance and Entrepreneurship dari Carnegie Mellon University USA pada Tahun 2008. Beliau diangkat menjadi Presiden Direktur Perseroan berdasarkan Akta Berita Acara pada RUPSLB Perseroan No. 36 tanggal 4 Oktober 2023 yang dibuat di hadapan Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., Notaris di Jakarta.

- Direktur &amp; Deputy CEO PT ESSA Industries Indonesia Tbk. (2020 – Maret 2022)
- Vice President Business Development PT ESSA Industries Indonesia Tbk. (2018 – 2020)
- Direktur &amp; Chief Project Officer PT Panca Amara Utama (2016 – 2018)

- Head of Investor Relations &amp; Corporate Secretary PT ESSA Industries Indonesia Tbk. (2012 – 2014)

## Beliau juga merangkap sebagai:

- Presiden Direktur &amp; CEO PT ESSA Industries Indonesia Tbk. (Oktober 2023-saat ini)

Indonesian citizen, born in Bandung, 37 years old as of December 2024, and currently lives in Jakarta. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Finance and Entrepreneurship from Carnegie Mellon University USA in 2008. He was appointed as President Director of the Company based on the Deed of Minutes at the Company's EGMS No. 36 dated 4 October 2023 drawn up before Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., Notary in Jakarta.
