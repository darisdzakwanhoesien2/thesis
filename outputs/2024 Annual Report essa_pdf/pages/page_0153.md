|  3 | Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan tentang seleksi dan peningkatan kemampuan pemasok atau vendor. | ESSA telah mengimplementasikan rekomendasi ini | ESSA memiliki Procurement Policy dan Commercial Bid Tabulation sehingga seleksi dan peningkatan kemampuan vendor terlaksana dengan baik.  |
|   | The Public Company has a policy regarding the selection and capacity building of suppliers or vendors. | ESSA has implemented this recommendation | ESSA has a Procurement Policy and Commercial Bid Tabulation so that vendor selection and ability enhancement is carried out properly.  |

|   | The Public Company has a policy regarding the fulfillment of creditor rights. | ESSA has implemented this recommendation | The ESSA's Company Regulation states that all business units are required to maintain commitments to fulfill creditor rights according to the terms agreed in the relevant contract.  |

|   | Public Companies have a whistleblowing system policy. | ESSA has not implemented this recommendation | ESSA is finalizing the violation reporting mechanism.
