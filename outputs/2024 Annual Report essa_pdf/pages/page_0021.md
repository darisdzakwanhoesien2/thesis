Pencapaian ini menunjukkan komitmen Perseroan dalam mengintegrasikan prinsip-prinsip keberlanjutan ke dalam kegiatan operasionalnya.

Inisiatif tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan (CSR) Perseroan terus mencerminkan pendekatan holistik terhadap keberlanjutan dan pengembangan masyarakat. Program Pertanian dan Peternakan di Kabupaten Banggai, Sulawesi Tengah, termasuk di Batui, Kintom, Nambo, dan Batui Selatan, tetap menjadi inisiatif utama yang bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kondisi ekonomi lokal dan mengatasi tantangan pertanian dan peternakan. Selain itu, Perseroan juga tetap fokus pada pemberdayaan kesehatan, pendidikan, dan pembangunan infrastruktur yang menunjukkan komitmennya untuk memberikan dampak positif jangka panjang bagi masyarakat.

Meskipun selalu ada ruang untuk perbaikan dalam kinerja ESG, Dewan Komisaris tetap berkomitmen untuk meningkatkan standar keberlanjutan. Perseroan terus menyelaraskan inisiatif ESG dengan praktik-praktik terbaik global, memastikan bahwa tanggung jawab sosial, pengelolaan lingkungan hidup, dan prinsip-prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (GCG) telah tertanam dalam arahan strategisnya.

Tahun ini, Perseroan memulai babak baru dalam mengembangkan strategi keberlanjutan jangka panjang. Inisiatif ini mencerminkan pendekatan terstruktur untuk mengintegrasikan pertimbangan LST ke dalam kerangka kerja bisnis Perseroan, guna memastikan pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan, ketahanan, dan penciptaan nilai jangka panjang. Dewan Komisaris mendorong Direksi untuk lebih meningkatkan transparansi dalam pengungkapan keberlanjutan, memberikan wawasan yang lebih dalam mengenai kemajuan Perseroan dan memperkuat komitmennya terhadap akuntabilitas dan peningkatan kinerja ESG yang berkelanjutan.

Company's achievements in 2024 include receiving the "Blue PROPER" award from the Ministry of Environment and Forestry, marking nine consecutive years of recognition since 2015 for environmental excellence.

Additionally, the Company's subsidiary, PT Panca Amara Utama (PAU), achieved the prestigious "Gold PROPER" award, the highest standard for environmental management in Indonesia. Further affirming the Company's leadership in sustainability, the President Director &amp; CEO was honored with the Green Leadership Award, recognizing exemplary leadership in advancing sustainability and environmental stewardship. These accomplishments highlight the Company's commitment to integrating sustainability principles into its operations.

The Company's corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives continue to reflect a holistic approach to sustainability and community development. The Agriculture and Livestock Program in Banggai Regency, Central Sulawesi, including in Batui, Kintom, Nambo, and Batui Selatan, remains a key initiative aimed at enhancing local economic conditions and addressing agricultural and livestock challenges. Additionally, the Company remains focused on health empowerment, education, and infrastructure development, further demonstrating its commitment to delivering long-term positive impact for communities.

While there is always room for improvement in ESG performance, the Board of Commissioners remains committed to elevating sustainability standards. The Company continues to align its ESG initiatives with global best practices, ensuring that social responsibility, environmental stewardship, and robust corporate governance (GCG) principles are embedded in its strategic direction.

This year, the Company has embarked on a new chapter in developing a long-term sustainability strategy. This initiative reflects a structured approach to integrating ESG considerations into the Company's business framework, ensuring continued growth, resilience, and long-term value creation. The Board of Commissioners encourages the Board of Directors to further enhance transparency in sustainability disclosures, providing deeper insights into the Company's progress and reinforcing its commitment to accountability and continuous improvement in ESG performance.
