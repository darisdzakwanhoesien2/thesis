# Sorotan

Highlights

Di tahun 2019, kami membangun pabrik pengolahan air limbah di unit operasional kami untuk meningkatkan kualitas air yang dibuang

Kami memprakasai rainwater harvesting system di beberapa unit operasional untuk mengurangi ketergantungan kami terhadap air tanah

We initiated rainwater harvesting systems in several of our operations to reduce the dependency of groundwater

We conducted wastewater treatment plants in many of our operations to improve the quality of water discharged
