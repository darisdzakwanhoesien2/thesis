## Associations

Japfa Group secara aktif terlibat dalam berbagai kegiatan dan asosiasi organisasi. Selama periode pelaporan, Perseroan terdaftar sebagai anggota dari organisasi berikut.

Japfa Group is actively involved in numerous organisational activities and associations. During the reporting period, the Company is registered as a member of the following organisations.
