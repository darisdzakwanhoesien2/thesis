Sustainability Report 2019

The core programme of Japfa for Kids is awareness of a balanced diet. Its programmes consist of several phases including:

## Meningkatkan kapasitas

Para guru menerima pelatihan tentang bagaimana mengembangkan manajemen sekolah yang sehat. Program ini juga melibatkan seleksi siswa untuk menjadi Duta Makanan Sehat, Duta Anak Sehat dan Duta Lingkungan Sehat.

## Membangun kebiasaan

Para guru dan duta diwajibkan untuk melakukan perubahan di sekolah seperti penataan lingkungan sekolah, mempersiapkan kebun sekolah, dan berbagai kegiatan untuk mendukung Gerakan Masyarakat Sehat.

## Membangun sistem di sekolah

Program ini melibatkan pengembangan komite di setiap sekolah yang bertanggung jawab untuk mengembangkan berbagai program seperti LISA BILAH (Lihat Sampah Ambil Pilah), Kantin Sehat, Kebun Sekolah dan Jumat Bersih.

## Integrasi dengan kegiatan belajar mengajar

Program ini meningkatkan pengetahuan dan kapasitas bagi para guru melalui aktivitas pelatihan dan pendampingan untuk membantu mereka mengintegrasikan program dan inisiatif kebersihan ke dalam kegiatan belajar siswa sehari-hari.

## Increasing capacity

Teachers receive training about how to develop healthy school management. This programme also involves the selection of students to be the Healthy Food Ambassador, Healthy Kid Ambassador and Healthy Environment Ambassador.

## Building a habit

This programme engages both teachers and students to make changes at school such as arranging the school environment, preparing school garden and various activities to support the Healthy Community Movement (Gerakan Masyarakat Sehat).

## Developing a system at school

This programme involves the development of a committee in each school who is responsible for developing various programmes such as LISA BILAH (Lihat Sampah Ambil Pilah—See, Take and Sort Garbage), Healthy Canteen, School Garden and Clean Friday.

## Integration with the teaching and learning activities

This programme increases knowledge and capacities for teachers through training and mentoring activities to help them integrate hygiene programmes and initiatives into students' daily learning activities.
