# PELAKSANAAN PROGRAM TANGGUNG JAWAB SOSIAL [GRI 203-2, GRI 413-1]

## Pilar Sosial

Melalui program ini, PGN berkomitmen menjadi Perusahaan yang memiliki kepedulian sosial tinggi dan sigap merespons berbagai situasi yang terjadi di Indonesia. Dalam pelaksanaannya, pada beberapa program, PGN SHARE bekerja sama dengan 3 lembaga/organisasi kemanusiaan. Selama tahun 2021, nilai sebesar Rp42.916.597.763,- telah disalurkan dalam pilar sosial.

## IMPLEMENTATION OF SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY PROGRAM [GRI 203-2, GRI 413-1]

## Social Pillar

Through this program, PGN is committed to be high a socially responsible company that is responsive to the diverse situations that occur in Indonesia. PGN SHARE collaborates with three humanitarian institutions or organizations in the implementation of several programs. A total of Rp42,916,597,763 was channeled into the social pillar in 2021.
