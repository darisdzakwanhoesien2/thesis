# Grievance Mechanism [2-16, 2-25, 2-26]

We have grievance mechanisms in place for internal and external whistleblowers to report any suspected violation of the Code via email to kode.etik@indofood.co.id and/or through the established mechanism within operating units. We also conduct dialogues with communities where we operate. All grievances are handled by designated team appointed by the Company. The details of the report and the identity of the individual(s) raising concerns are treated in strict confidentiality. A violation of the Code of Conduct can constitute a breach of employment and may result in disciplinary action.

In 2022, the Company received two notable reports, one of which was deemed valid and followed up.

# Mekanisme Penyampaian Keluhan [2-16, 2-25, 2-26]

Kami memiliki mekanisme penyampaikan keluhan baik dari pihak internal maupun eksternal guna melaporkan dugaan pelanggaran terhadap Kode Etik yang dapat dilayangkan melalui surat elektronik ke kode.etik@indofood.co.id dan/atau melalui jalur yang tersedia di unit operasional. Kami juga membangun dialog dengan masyarakat tempat kami beroperasi. Rincian terkait laporan dan identitas pelapor akan dijamin kerahasiaannya. Pelanggaran terhadap Kode Etik dapat dianggap sebagai pelanggaran terhadap kontrak kerja karyawan dan dapat berujung pada sanksi disipliner.

Pada tahun 2022, Perseroan menerima dualaporan yang patut diperhatikan, satu dari laporan tersebut valid dan ditindaklanjuti.
