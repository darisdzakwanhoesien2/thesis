# Key Achievements in 2022

As pandemic-era restrictions and complications receded and we began again to enjoy in-person interaction with our colleagues, we were faced with increased macroeconomic resistance and global procurement difficulties. Our commitment to sustainably managed, long-term partnerships with a variety of suppliers contributed to the resilience of our supply chain and enabled us to cope effectively with the disruption. These levels of resilience were enhanced considerably by our inclusive business model promoting partnerships with farmers, and micro, small, and medium enterprises ("MSMEs") throughout our value chain activities. In spite of the headwinds and challenges we have faced, we believe we have made significant progress towards our sustainability goals.

We have continued to reduce the intensity of our GHG emissions and have managed the efficiency of our energy and water use. Our environmental performance efforts have been recognized both internationally and within Indonesia. Our Bogasari Group was recognized during the Global Clean Energy Action Forum in Pittsburgh, USA, for its achievements in decarbonizing its supply chain and implementing a digital automation system to integrate its yield and energy management systems. Bogasari Group now operates the only flour mills in the world to have been awarded the International Energy Management Leadership Award of Excellence. The Group also received a Level 5 Green Industry Award, the highest level of recognition, from the Indonesian Ministry of Industry for production process efficiency innovations implemented to preserve the environment.

We understand the risks posed by the climate crisis and have included climate-related risks in our Group-wide risk management processes for many years. In 2022 our Agribusiness Group, which is more exposed to the impact of such risks, took steps to align its existing structure with the Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures ("TCFD") framework. The results of initial scenario analyses have further informed our understanding of our exposure to climate risk, and we are incorporating additional physical and transition risks into our Enterprise Risk Management ("ERM") process. We will be providing a more comprehensive report in line with TCFD disclosures in our 2023 Sustainability Report. At the same time, we continued to play our part in mitigating the effects of climate risk through implementation of energy efficiency initiatives and by extending the use of renewables such as biomass and solar.

# Pencapaian Utama Tahun 2022

Saat era pembatasan dan situasi buruk pandemi telah mereda dan kami mulai kembali dapat berinteraksi langsung dengan para kolega, kami dihadapkan pada meningkatnya resistensi makroekonomi dan tantangan rantai pasokan global. Komitmen kami terhadap kemitraan jangka panjang yang dikelola secara berkelanjutan dengan para pemasok kami yang beragam, telah berkontribusi pada ketahanan rantai pasokan dan memungkinkan kami mengatasi kendala secara efektif. Ketangguhan ini diperkuat oleh penerapan model bisnis inklusif kami melalui kemitraan dengan petani, serta usaha mikro, kecil dan menengah ("UMKM") di sepanjang rantai nilai kami. Terlepas dari hambatan dan tantangan yang kami hadapi, kami meyakini bahwa kami telah menunjukkan kemajuan yang signifikan terhadap sasaran keberlanjutan kami.

Kami terus menurunkan intensitas emisi GRK dan mengelola efisiensi penggunaan energi dan air. Upaya dalam meningkatkan kinerja lingkungan kami telah mendapatkan pengakuan baik dari dunia internasional maupun di Indonesia. Grup Bogasari kami telah mendapatkan pengakuan dalam Forum Global Clean Energy Action di Pittsburg, Amerika Serikat, atas pencapaian kami melakukan dekarbonisasi di rantai pasokan dan menerapkan sistem otomatisasi digital guna mengintegrasikan hasil produksi dengan sistem manajemen energi. Saat ini, Grup Bogasari merupakan satu-satunya pabrik tepung terigu di dunia yang telah meraih International Energy Management Leadership Award of Excellence. Grup Bogasari juga menerima Penghargaan Industri Hijau Level 5, penghargaan tertinggi dari Kementerian Perindustrian Indonesia atas inovasi efisiensi proses produksi yang diterapkan guna mendukung pelestarian lingkungan.

Kami memahami bahwa risiko yang ditimbulkan oleh krisis perubahan iklim telah menjadi bagian dari risiko terkait iklim dalam proses penilaian risiko di seluruh Grup kami selama bertahun-tahun. Pada tahun 2022, Grup Agribisnis kami, yang lebih terpapar terhadap dampak dari risiko-risiko tersebut, mengambil langkah untuk menyelaraskan struktur analisis risiko iklim kami dengan kerangka kerja dari Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures ("TCFD"). Hasil dari skenario awal telah memberikan pemahaman akan paparan yang mungkin terjadi akibat iklim, selain itu kami juga menambahkan risiko fisik dan transisi dalam proses Enterprise Risk Management ("ERM") kami. Kami akan menyampaikan informasi yang lebih lengkap terkait pengungkapan TCFD dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan 2023. Di saat yang sama, kami melanjutkan upaya dalam memitigasi dampak risiko iklim melalui implementasi program efisiensi energi dan memperluas pemanfaatan energi terbarukan seperti biomassa dan energi surya.
