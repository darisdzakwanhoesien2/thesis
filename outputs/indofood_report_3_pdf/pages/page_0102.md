In some divisions, Indofood has harnessed technology to digitalize our supply chain through innovation initiatives that focus on our manufacturing processes and inventory management. In addition to improving production efficiency, this puts in place mechanisms to mitigate future disruption.

## Manufacturing Innovation through Industry 4.0

Indofood has continued to build on the successful implementation of Industry 4.0. This initiative, entitled "Making Indonesia 4.0", was launched by the Indonesian Government in 2018 with the aim of increasing the levels of digitalization in manufacturing industry production processes.

### a. Manufacturing Innovation in Dairy Division

Through implementation of Industry 4.0 technologies, CBP Group's Dairy Division has increased its operational efficiency and flexibility. We used data analytics to develop a cognitive process known as smart operations. The development of smart operations combines several aspects:

- Smart performance provides real time of Overall Equipment Effectiveness that is able to increase transparency of performance from shop floor to top floor;
- Smart quality develops quality control systems that provide real time tracking and traceability, also speed up the release time of products to the market;
- Smart production optimizes the manufacturing process in order to increase productivity by enhancing line performance efficiency and throughput, and by reducing losses;
- Smart maintenance optimizes maintenance costs and provides better line efficiency;
- Smart eco factory focuses on managing energy, water, waste and uncontrolled usage that reduces water consumption, reduces fuel consumption, reduces steam generation costs, and reduces WWTP costs.

## Inovasi Manufaktur melalui Industri 4.0

Indofood turut berkontribusi melanjutkan keberhasilan pelaksanaan Industri 4.0. Inisiatif, "Making Indonesia 4.0", diluncurkan oleh Pemerintah Indonesia pada tahun 2018 dengan tujuan untuk meningkatkan digitalisasi dalam proses produksi industri manufaktur.

### a. Inovasi Manufaktur di Divisi Dairy

Melalui penerapan teknologi Industri 4.0, Divisi Dairy Grup CBP terbukti mampu meningkatkan efisiensi dan fleksibilitas operasionalnya. Kami menggunakan analitik data untuk mengembangkan proses kognisi yang dikenal sebagai sistem smart operation. Pengembangan sistem operasi pintar mengombinasikan beberapa aspek:

- Smart performance memberikan Overall Equipment Effectiveness secara real time yang mampu meningkatkan transparansi kinerja dari areai operasional hingga manajemen;
- Smart quality mengembangkan sistem kendali untuk memberikan penelusuran secara real time, dan juga mempercepat kehadiran produk yang baru diluncurkan;
- Smart production mengoptimalkan proses manufaktur untuk meningkatkan produktivitas melalui peningkatan kinerja di lini produksi dan dengan mengurangi kerugian;
- Smart maintenance mengoptimalkan biaya pemeliharaan dan mendorong efisiensi produksi yang lebih baik;
- Smart eco factory berfokus pada pengelolaan energi, air, limbah dan penggunaan sumber daya yang tidak terkendali sehingga dapat mengurangi penggunaan air, penggunaan bahan bakar, mengurangi biaya produksi uap dan mengurangi biaya operasional IPAL.
