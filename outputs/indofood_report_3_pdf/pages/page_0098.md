## Partnership with MSMEs

Indofood generates significant indirect economic impact through our broad value chain that spans both upstream and downstream operations. Upstream, we foster inclusive growth in our supply chain by building capacity and resilience in local farmers; downstream, we extend shared value through partnerships with small businesses such as the Warmindo and BMC programs. These positive indirect economic impacts ripple across our value chain to benefit individuals, their families, and the wider community.

## Partnership with Warmindo

Warmindo is a program under the CBP Group Noodles Division that supports owners of small Indomie stalls. The program aims to promote business growth by providing training in food hygiene and stall management, by improving the variety of menu, quality, and desirability of meals, and by promoting customer satisfaction through increased comfort and cleanliness of stalls. Stall owners supported through the Warmindo program also benefit from elevated brand recognition, customer loyalty, and importantly increased income.
