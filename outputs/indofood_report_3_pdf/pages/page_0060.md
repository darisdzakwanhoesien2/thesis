These partnerships with our suppliers and our retailers have generated substantial indirect economic value for our supply chain partners and communities along Indofood's value chain. Notably, more than 68,000 Warmindo entrepreneurs and 73,000 farmers including plasma smallholders now count themselves as part of our value chain community, as well as more than 52,000 BMC members with revenues ranging from millions to billions of Rupiah per year.

More information on the upstream and downstream economic benefits can be found in Resilience in Delivering Food for All chapter, in the Socio-Economic Inclusion section on page 88.
