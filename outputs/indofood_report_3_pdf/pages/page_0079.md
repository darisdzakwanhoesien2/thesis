Before every product launch, we undertake market research to gather consumer insights. Through an iterative process, we make changes based on the research findings and publicize product launches to consumers via advertisements, product websites, customer service, and consumer education programs.

## Receiving and Responding to Customers’ Feedback

Customer feedback forms a critical input to our product design and innovation process. Customers and members of the public can contact the centralized Indofood Customer Service through toll-free telephone lines, by email, and through our social media channels which, in 2022, were augmented by a new instant messaging feedback channel. We respond to each customer enquiry or complaint as soon as practically possible, in line with our Standard Operating Procedures. In 2022, we received and responded to over 178,000 instances of feedback, of which 96% were enquiries on product details and promotions.

## Menerima dan Menanggapi Umpan Balik Konsumen

Umpan balik konsumen merupakan masukan yang penting untuk pengembangan produk dan proses inovasi kami. Konsumen dan masyarakat dapat menghubungi Layanan Konsumen Indofood yang terpusat melalui sambungan telepon bebas pulsa, surat elektronik dan berbagai saluran media sosial kami, di mana pada tahun 2022 ditambahkan sebuah saluran umpan balik baru yaitu pesan instan. Kami menanggapi setiap pertanyaan atau keluhan pelanggan sesegera mungkin, sesuai dengan Standar Prosedur Operasional kami. Pada tahun 2022, kami menerima dan menanggapi lebih dari 178.000 umpan balik di mana 96% diantaranya adalah pertanyaan tentang detail produk dan program promosi.
