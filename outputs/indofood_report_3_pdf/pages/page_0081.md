# Dairy Division Digital Campaigns

During 2022, the Dairy Division of CBP Group conducted a number of digital education campaigns. "The Lower The Sugar, The Yummier" campaign was launched to educate parents on the importance of reducing sugar intake for children from an early age, as high sugar intake during development years could lead children to eat excess sugar as they grow older, putting them at higher risk of obesity. Under these programs, we educated parents on the nutritional content and benefits of milk for children, encouraging them to consume "Healthier Choice" milk to meet their children's developmental needs. A separate informational campaign was conducted and highlighted the nutritional content and benefits of milk for children.

# Kampanye Digital Divisi Dairy

Sepanjang tahun 2022, Divisi Dairy dari Grup CBP menyelenggarakan serangkaian kampanye edukasi digital. Kampanye "Rendah Gula, Lebih Yummy" diluncurkan guna memberikan edukasi kepada orang tua akan pentingnya mengurangi asupan gula pada anak sejak dini, karena asupan gula yang tinggi di masa pertumbuhan akan mendorong anak-anak untuk mengonsumsi gula secara berlebih saat mereka beranjak dewasa, sehingga mereka memiliki risiko tinggi menderita obesitas. Melalui program ini, kami mengedukasi orang tua tentang kandungan gizi dan manfaat susu untuk anak-anak, mendorong mereka untuk mengonsumsi susu "Pilihan Lebih Sehat" guna mendukung kebutuhan perkembangan anak-anak mereka. Kampanye terpisah juga dilakukan untuk memberikan informasi terkait kandungan gizi dan manfaat susu untuk anak-anak.
