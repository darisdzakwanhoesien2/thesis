As of the end of 2024, BRSM owns 5 subsidiaries. First, PT Citra Palu Minerals (CPM) carries out exploration, construction and mineral production in an area of 85,180 hectares in Central Sulawesi and South Sulawesi. Second, PT Dairi Prima Mineral (DPM) operates in a concession area of 24,636 hectares in North Sumatra. Third PT Gorontalo Minerals (GM) holds a Contract of Work concession right for a mining area of 24,995 hectares located in Bone Bolango Regency, Gorontalo Province. Lastly, PT Linge Mineral Resources (LMR) which holds a 36,420 hectares Linge Abong gold mining concession in Aceh Province. Fourth, PT Linge Mineral Resources (LMR) has a Mining Business License to a 36,420 hectares mining concession in Linge, Aceh Province. Lastly, PT Suma Heksa Sinergi (SHS) Kerta gold project, has Mining Business License to a 7,291 hectares in Lebak, Banten Province.
