Subsequently, in the contracting stage, we have incorporated environmental protection clauses into all (100%) contractual agreements with contractors, mining equipment suppliers, consultants, and other business partners. In the monitoring and performance evaluation phase, our Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) and supply chain teams routinely review and assess environmental compliance aspects within the supply chain. Importantly, our commitment to environmental sustainability extends beyond regulatory compliance; we actively support our suppliers and partners in attaining environmental management standards through training and outreach initiatives.

# IDENTIFIKASI DAN MITIGASI DAMPAK LINGKUNGAN HIDUP

Environmental Impact Identification and Mitigation (POJK51-F.28) (2-27)

Kami secara aktif melakukan pemantauan lingkungan untuk mengidentifikasi dan menilai risiko guna mencegah dampak negatif yang dapat timbul akibat kegiatan pertambangan dan produk yang dihasilkan. Proses ini dilakukan dengan pendekatan sistematis yang sejalan dengan Peraturan Pemerintah No. 22 Tahun 2021 tentang Penyelenggaraan Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup serta standar internasional ISO 14001:2015 tentang Sistem Manajemen Lingkungan.

Unit usaha kami, GM, CPM, DPM, LMR, dan SHS menerapkan identifikasi dan penilaian risiko lingkungan pada setiap tahapan aktivitas pertambangan, mulai dari eksplorasi dan perencanaan, konstruksi, operasi produksi, transportasi, hingga tahap pascatambang. Dengan pendekatan ini, kami memastikan bahwa setiap proses operasi dikelola secara bertanggung jawab untuk meminimalkan dampak lingkungan dan meningkatkan keberlanjutan operasional.

Hasil identifikasi lingkungan tahun 2024 yang dilakukan oleh Divisi HSE di masing-masing unit usaha telah menyimpulkan bahwa kualitas udara, air, dan timbulan limbah telah sesuai dengan standar baku mutu dan pengelolaan yang ditetapkan oleh pemerintah yaitu Keputusan Menteri Lingkungan Hidup No. 202 Tahun 2004 tentang Baku Mutu Air Limbah Bagi Usaha dan atau Kegiatan Pertambangan Bijih Emas dan atau Tembaga, Peraturan Menteri Lingkungan Hidup No. 4 Tahun 2014 tentang Baku Mutu Emisi Sumber Tidak Bergerak Bagi Usaha dan/atau Kegiatan Pertambangan, Peraturan Menteri

We actively conduct environmental monitoring to identify and assess risks, preventing potential negative impacts from mining activities and the products we produce. This process follows a systematic approach aligned with Government Regulation No. 22 of 2021 on Environmental Protection and Management, as well as ISO 14001:2015 on Environmental Management Systems.

Our business units, GM, CPM, DPM, LMR, and SHS implement environmental risk identification and assessment at every stage of mining operations, from exploration and planning, construction, production operations, and transportation, to post-mining activities. Through this approach, we ensure that all operational processes are responsibly managed to minimize environmental impact and enhance sustainability.

The results of environmental identification in 2024 conducted by the HSE Division in each business unit have concluded that the quality of air, water, and waste generation is in accordance with the quality and management standards set by the government, namely the Decree of the Minister of Environment No. 202 of 2004 concerning Wastewater Quality Standards for Gold and or Copper Ore Mining Businesses and or Activities, Regulation of the Minister of Environment No. 4 of 2014 concerning Emission Quality Standards for Non-Movable Sources for Mining Businesses and or Activities, Regulation
