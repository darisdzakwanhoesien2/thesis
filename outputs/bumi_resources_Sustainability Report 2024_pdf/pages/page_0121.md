# KOMITE KESELAMATAN PERTAMBANGAN (KOMITE KP)

Mining Safety Committee (2-27) (403-1) (403-8) (POJK51-F.21)

Untuk memastikan keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja serta perlindungan lingkungan dapat diterapkan secara menyeluruh, Kami menerapkan sistem lintas departemen. Kami membentuk Komite Keselamatan Pertambangan dan Lingkungan (KPL), yang beranggotakan Kepala Teknik Tambang, perwakilan dari divisi Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) serta Keselamatan Operasi (KO) Pertambangan, Kepala Satuan Kerja, dan Penanggung Jawab Operasional (PJO) Usaha Jasa Pertambangan (UJIP). Dengan struktur komite yang terdiri dari gabungan manajemen dan pekerja, komite berwewenang dalam pengambilan keputusan yang berkaitan dengan keselamatan dan lingkungan.

Selain menjalankan fungsi tersebut, komite ini juga berperan sebagai sarana bagi pegawai untuk menyampaikan aspirasi mereka terkait aspek keselamatan dan kesehatan pertambangan. Misalnya, pekerja dapat melaporkan kondisi berbahaya di tempat kerja kepada anggota Komite KPL di departemen masing-masing. Dengan melibatkan tenaga kerja dalam manajemen K3, kami berharap keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja di lingkungan pertambangan dapat terjaga dengan optimal. (403-4)

Berdasarkan Keputusan Dirjen Minerba ESDM No. 185.K/37.04/DJB/2019 tentang petunjuk Teknis Pelaksanaan, Penilaian, dan Pelaporan Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan Minerba. Komite Keselamatan Pertambangan mempunyai tugas dan tanggung jawab antara lain:

- Mengidentifikasi, menetapkan, dan mengesahkan tujuan, sasaran dan program keselamatan pertambangan
- Memastikan pelaksanaan dan perkembangan tujuan, sasaran dan program keselamatan pertambangan
- Memastikan diterbitkannya kebijakan, standar dan prosedur keselamatan pertambangan;

We implement a cross-departmental system to ensure that occupational safety, health, and environmental protection can be implemented thoroughly. We established the Mining Safety and Environment Committee (KPL), which consists of the Head of Mine Engineering, representatives from the Occupational Safety and Health (OHS) and Mining Operation Safety (KO) divisions, the Head of the Work Unit, and the Person in Charge of Operations (PJO) of the Mining Services Business (UJIP). With a committee structure consisting of a combination of management and workers, the committee is authorized to make decisions relating to safety and the environment.

In addition to carrying out these functions, the committee also serves as a means for employees to convey their aspirations regarding mining safety and health. For example, workers can report hazardous workplace conditions to OHS Committee members in their respective departments. By involving the workforce in OHS management, we hope that occupational safety and health in the mining environment can be optimally maintained.

Following Director General of Mineral and Coal Decree No. 185.K/37.04/DJB/2019 on Technical Instructions for the Implementation, Assessment, and Reporting of Mining and Coal Mining Safety Management Systems, the Mining Safety Committee is tasked with the following duties and responsibilities:

- Identifying, establishing, and validating mining safety goals, objectives, and programs.
- Ensuring mining safety goals, targets, and programs are implemented and developed.
- Ensuring the issuance of mining safety policies, standards, and procedures.
