Air limbah dari kegiatan pengolahan di PT CPM dikelola melalui fasilitas detoksifikasi dan polishing pond. Pada tahun 2024, PT CPM mengoperasikan IPAL domestik berkapasitas 110 m³/hari. Total air limbah domestik yang dilepaskan ke lingkungan sebanyak 13.815,35 m³. To meet the domestic needs of its employees, PT CPM utilizes groundwater that has been authorized through a Groundwater Utilization Permit (SIPA) No. 912020924101800010008, dated January 18, 2024.

Wastewater from processing activities at PT CPM is managed through detoxification and polishing pond facilities. After meeting the quality standards in accordance with Kepmen LH No. 202 Year 2004 Appendix II, wastewater is released to the environment through the CP 01 Poboya compliance point. In 2024, the volume of wastewater released was recorded at 810 m³, much lower than in 2023, which reached 15,275 m³. This decrease reflects the effectiveness of wastewater management efforts and the company's commitment to environmental protection.

In 2024, PT CPM operates a domestic WWTP with a capacity of 110 m³/day. This domestic WWTP treats wastewater from the canteen, mess, office, mosque, recreation area, and vehicle washing area. The total domestic wastewater released to the environment is 13,815.35 m³. The release of domestic wastewater can only be done if it has met the environmental quality standards in accordance with the Technical Approval for Fulfillment of Wastewater Quality Standards No. S.521/PPKL/PPA/PKL-2/7/2023.

Thus, PT CPM's total clean water consumption reached 418,737 m³, a significant increase compared to 2023, which was recorded at 117,409 m³. This increase is in line with the increase in production capacity that surged in 2024.

Meanwhile, DPM's water withdrawal from the third party, PAM, amounts to 1,646,000 liters during 2024. Currently PT DPM does not discharge wastewater from construction activities.
