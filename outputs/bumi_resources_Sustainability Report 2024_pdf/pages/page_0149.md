## Pengelolaan Keadaan Darurat PT GM tahun 2024

### a. Pembentukan Tim Tanggap Darurat

PT GM telah melakukan pengelolaan keadaan darurat sebagai salah satu implementasi dari identifikasi bahaya dan risiko di area kerja, dimana dari hasil identifikasi bahaya dan risiko.

Pengelolaan keadaan darurat yang PT GM lakukan meliputi beberapa hal:

1. Pengadaan peralatan tanggap darurat
2. Pengelolaan peralatan tanggap darurat
3. Pembentukan/penunjukkan tim tanggap darurat
4. Pelatihan tim tanggap darurat
5. Simulasi keadaan darurat di lokasi PT GM

## PT GM Emergency Management in 2024

### a. Establishment of an Emergency Response

PT GM has conducted emergency management as one of the implementations of identifying hazards and risks in the work area, resulting from identifying hazards and risks.

PT GM's management of emergencies includes several things:

1. Procurement of emergency response equipment
2. Management of emergency response equipment
3. Establishment/appointment of an emergency response team
4. Emergency response team training
5. Simulation of an emergency at the PT GM location
