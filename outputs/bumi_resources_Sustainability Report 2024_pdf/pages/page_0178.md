The management of water resources by the Company refers on the Minister of Environment and Forestry Regulation Number P.80 of 2019 concerning Amendments to the Regulation of the Minister of Environment and Forestry Number P.93/Menlhk/Setjen/Kum.1/8/2018 concerning Continuous Monitoring of Wastewater Quality and in the Network for Businesses and/or Activities to ensure the quality and availability of sufficient production and domestic water at all times. Furthermore, the CPM business unit has obtained a permit to exploit water resources for mining activities in the Pondo River, Poboya Village, Palu City from the Head of the Sulawesi River Region III Central Sulawesi Province based on No. 250/KPTS/M/2020 on 27 March 2020. In addition, CPM also has a waste water disposal permit No. 503/16.72/03/I-IPLC/DPMPTSP/IV/2021 dated 6 April 2021 and Technical Approval for Fulfillment of Wastewater Quality Standards No. S.521/PPKL/PPA/PKL-2/7/2023.

To realize this commitment, the Company focuses on three aspects of water management:

1. Reducing the intensity of water extraction from nature, especially groundwater and surface water.
2. Maximizing the use of recirculated and recycled water for production processes and other purposes such as watering and dust control.
3. Ensuring that the quality standard is appropriate for water that will be flowed back into public water bodies.

In practice, we have implemented this commitment in the business unit. CPM conducts gold processing activities with the Carbon in Leach (CIL) method, namely with a closed-circuit water management system, so that gold ore processing water will be flowed to the filter press facility, where 77% of the wastewater will be flowed back for the gold processing process, while 20% of the processing water is mixed with dry tailings and placed in the waste pile facility. By using water from the filter press, approximately 667 kg of cyanide can be recovered per day. As a result, cyanide consumption in gold ore processing using the CIL method is relatively low, at around 0.6 kg per ton.
