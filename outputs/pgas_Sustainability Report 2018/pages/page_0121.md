## OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH ROAD MAP

PGN pays special attention to the workers' health, as only healthy workers would provide the Company's good performance. PGN holds a variety of programs to improve the employees' health. They are customized according to the workers' risk level and the respective work field. Health education comes in four forms:

- Seminar and road show
- Health data management though E-medical record application
- Industrial hygiene program i.e. work environment checking
- Annual health risk check
