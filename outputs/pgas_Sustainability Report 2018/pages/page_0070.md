# GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE [GRI 102-18]

In accordance with the Law No. 40 Year 2007 on Limited Liability Companies, PGN has three Corporate Bodies, as follows:

1. The General Meeting of Shareholders (Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham, RUPS), the highest authority in a Limited Liability Company and the holder of all authorities that are not delegated to the Board of Commissioners and Directors within the limits under law and/or Articles of Association.
2. The Board of Commissioners, a board that conducts general and/or specific supervision in accordance with the Articles of Association and advises the Directors.
3. The Directors, a Corporate Body responsible for management to achieve the Company's vision and mission in its best interests. The Directors are also responsible to represent the Company both within and outside of a court of law in accordance with the Articles of Association.

In carrying out their duties, the Corporate Bodies' functions are governed by laws, the Company's Articles of Association, as well as other provisions, based on the principle that each body independently carries out its duties, functions, and responsibilities in the Company's interests.
