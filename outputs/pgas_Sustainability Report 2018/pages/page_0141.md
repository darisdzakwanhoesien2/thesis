## PGN EMPLOYEE TURNOVER

In 2018, we did not see significant employee turnover. The majority of employees resigned as they have reached the mandatory retirement age of 56 years. In 2018, we did not recruit new employees.

In order to attract the best talents, we announce recruitment programs in nationally circulated print media and our website. Additionally, competent independent parties has been involved to support such objectives. We also recruit employees in our operating areas to demonstrate our commitments in supporting the self-sufficiency, prosperity, and development of the local population.
