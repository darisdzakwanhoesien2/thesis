## WELFARE OF PGN INDIVIDUALS

In 2018, the total welfare fund was Rp 572.725.152.334. The sum was allocated for remuneration or wages, leave allowances, religious holiday allowance, medical care, overtime payments, old-age security, and pensions. Compared with the previous year, the sum rose by 8.4% from Rp 528.291.052.624,-. We want to ensure that employees are fairly rewarded for their dedication to the company. Only then they are motivated to contribute and be committed to the company. In addition, the competitive welfare package ensures that we attract only the best talents.
