## EMISSIONS

To mitigate global warming, PGN takes active measures to reduce greenhouse gas emissions that contributed to temperature rise. We are committed to improve our operating efficiency and reduce carbon emissions in our operations. From 2012, we have tracked our carbon footprint using a Carbon Calculator to measure:

- Emissions from electricity usage in buildings and stations
- Emissions from fuels for generators

- Emissions from fuels for motor vehicles
- Emissions from natural gas for chillers and compressor turbines
- Emissions from natural gas distribution
- Emissions from business flights

Based on these metrics, we found that carbon emissions from our operations in 2018 was down by 5% to 82,612.63 tons of CO₂eq from 87,099.24 tons CO₂eq in 2017.
