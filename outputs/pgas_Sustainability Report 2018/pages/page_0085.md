PT Perusahaan Gas Negara Tbk 2018 Sustainability Report

Natural gas is an important source of energy for Indonesia and the world. It plays a significant role in the Indonesian economy since it is used in sectors ranging from large-scale industries, power plants, and households. Unlike other energy sources, natural gas is among the cleanest. Indeed, natural gas will enjoy greater importance in time to come from an increased public awareness and the desire of many countries to reduce dependence on expensive and environmentally-adverse energy.
