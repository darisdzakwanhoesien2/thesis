# THE COMPANY'S BRIEF HISTORY

![img-99.jpeg](img-99.jpeg)

## L.J.N. EINDHOVEN CO GRAVENHAGE FIRM

The Company was firstly founded by the Eindhoven family.

![img-100.jpeg](img-100.jpeg)

## NV NETHERLAND INDISCHE GAS MAATSCHAPJJ (NIGM)

The Dutch government took over Endhoven family's business and changed the company name. In 1879, NIGM operated two gas plants in Jakarta and Surabaya.

![img-101.jpeg](img-101.jpeg)

## NV OVERZEESE GAS EN ELECTRICITEIT MAATSCHAPPIJ (NV OGEM)

The Dutch government operated 11 gas plants and 33 power plants, thereby combined NIGM and Dutch Government power companies and changed the company's name.

![img-102.jpeg](img-102.jpeg)

## BADAN PIMPINAN UMUM PERUSAHAAN LISTRIK NEGARA (BPU-PLN)

Consolidation of State gas companies electric companies.
