# CONTRIBUTION TO THE STATE

PGN has changed its share ownership from the government to Pertamina since 11 April 2018. Thus, majority shareholder of PGN is Pertamina with shares of 56.96%, and the remaining 43.04% is owned by the public. However, in 2018 PGN still provides dividends to the state from 2017 gas profits). PGN contributes to the state through dividend payments, taxes, and dues to the Oil and Gas Downstream Regulatory Agency (Badan Pengatur Hilir Minyak dan Gas, BPH Migas). In 2018, the total contribution to the state was US$448,961,822, decrease 4.2% compared with US$468,483,274 in the previous year.
