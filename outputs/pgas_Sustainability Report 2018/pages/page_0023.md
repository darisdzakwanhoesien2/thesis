Natural gas is among the most environmentally-friendly and economical source of energy compared with other fossil fuels, such as crude oil and coal. As Indonesia's population continues to grow and standard of living continues to improve, so will demand for natural gas. More than just Indonesia, it is estimated that by 2030, natural gas will assume coal's position as the second most utilized source of energy after crude oil. In general,

PGN is active in developing Indonesia's economy by supplying a clean and economical source of energy. In addition, natural gas is proven to be more cost-efficient for businesses, enabling them to earn higher profits and reduce production costs, thus automatically improving competitiveness of national industries. It has been shown that fuel costs can be reduced by 30 to 60% when using natural gas. This value offered by natural gas encourages PGN to continue expanding its business and promote the use of natural gas in industrial estates, in electricity generation, and in households.
