# Trans Fat-Free Products

The Dairy Division of the CBP Group has focused on developing trans fat-free products over the years. This commitment begins with sourcing raw materials that are free from trans fats and continues through the production process to ensure that our products maintain this status.

As of 2024, the Dairy Division has made significant strides in expanding its trans fat-free offerings across its portfolio, which includes sweetened condensed creamer ("SCC"), UHT milk, sterilized milk, and ice cream. More than 60 dairy product now feature trans fat-free labeling and 100% of our SCC products are trans fat-free. [416-1]

Additionally, our Agribusiness Group has eliminated partial hydrogenated fats by substituting them with interesterified ("IE") oils, as the former contains trans-fats. We are actively enhancing our research and development in IE oil production and have successfully removed trans fats from our consumer and industrial products.

# Produk Bebas Lemak Trans

Divisi Dairy dari Grup CBP telah mengembangkan produk bebas lemak trans selama bertahun-tahun. Komitmen ini dimulai dengan mencari bahan baku yang bebas lemak trans hingga proses produksi tetap mempertahankan status bebas lemak trans ini.

Hingga tahun 2024, Divisi Dairy telah meraih kemajuan besar dalam memperluas portofolio produk yang bebas lemak trans, di antaranya meliputi produk krimer kental manis (sweetened condensed creamer atau "SCC"), susu UHT, susu steril dan produk es krim. Saat ini, lebih dari 60 produk dairy telah memiliki label bebas lemak trans dan 100% produk SCC kami telah bebas lemak trans. [416-1]

Selain itu, Grup Agribisnis telah menghilangkan lemak terhidrogenasi parsial dan menggantinya dengan minyak interesterified ("IE"), karena lemak terhidrogenasi parsial mengandung lemak trans. Kami terus meningkatkan penelitian dan pengembangan produksi minyak IE dan kami telah berhasil menghilangkan lemak trans dari produk konsumen dan produk industri kami.
