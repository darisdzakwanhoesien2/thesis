LAPORAN KEBERLANJUTAN 2024

Within the CBP Group, our dairy factories are certified with the Veterinary Control Number (Nomor Kontrol Veteriner or "NKV") certification. This validates our compliance with hygiene and sanitation requirements for food safety of animal-based food products, aligning with regulations set forth by the Indonesian Ministry of Agriculture.

Another certification, SMKPO is a system designed by BPOM to guarantee the safety and quality of processed food through independent supervision along the food distribution chain. In 2024, all of Indofood Distribution Group branches have been certified with SMKPO.

Indofood adopts the Good Warehouse Practices ("GWP") guidelines relating to product clustering to minimize food contamination risk. To this end, we carry out hazard identification and risk assessment for our warehousing and logistics operations and maintain internal quality assurance with regular audits of food handling procedures at our distribution facilities. We practice First to Expire First Out (FEFO) stock rotation system to ensure we deliver only fresh products to consumers.

Pabrik dairy Grup CBP kami juga telah tersertifikasi Nomor Kontrol Veteriner ("NKV"). Sertifikat ini menunjukkan kepatuhan kami terhadap standar higiene dan sanitasi keamanan produk pangan berbasis hewani sesuai dengan peraturan Kementerian Pertanian Indonesia.

Selain itu, sertifikasi SMKPO merupakan sistem yang dirancang oleh BPOM untuk memastikan keamanan dan mutu produk pangan olahan melalui pengawasan independen di seluruh rantai distribusi. Di tahun 2024, seluruh cabang Grup Distribusi Indofood berhasil memperoleh sertifikasi SMKPO.

Indofood menerapkan Praktik Pengelolaan Sistem Pergudangan yang Baik (Good Warehouse Practices atau "GWP") yang mencakup pedoman pengelompokan produk guna meminimalkan risiko kontaminasi pangan. Kami juga melakukan identifikasi bahaya dan penilaian risiko terhadap kegiatan operasional pergudangan dan distribusi serta memastikan jaminan mutu internal melalui audit berkala terhadap prosedur penanganan pangan di fasilitas distribusi kami. Selain itu, kami menerapkan sistem rotasi stok First to Expire First Out (FEFO) guna memastikan kami hanya mengirimkan produk segar ke konsumen.
