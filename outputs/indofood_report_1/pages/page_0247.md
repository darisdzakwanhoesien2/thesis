Promote and enforce non-discriminatory laws and policies for sustainable development
Mempromosikan dan menegakkan hukum dan kebijakan yang nondiskriminatif untuk pembangunan berkelanjutan | Labor Practices and People Development

• We uphold workplace diversity and equal opportunities in our hiring and recruitment processes, as well as our talent management and people development programs. Career opportunities are awarded based on competency and experience, regardless of race, religion, gender or other individual characteristics
• Training programs and employee development courses are accessible to all employees
