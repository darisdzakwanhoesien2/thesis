Berdasarkan pendekatan utama yang dirumuskan di atas, Perseroan dan entitas anaknya membangun strategi keberlanjutannya sebagai bagian kerangka kebijakan transformasi yang komprehensif untuk diimplementasikan di berbagai aspek bisnisnya. Transformasi yang didukung oleh pengadopsian teknologi terkini dalam penyediaan konten yang berkualitas telah menjadi kunci bagi keberhasilan Grup SCMA dalam mempertahankan kepemimpinannya di industri media dan hiburan. Transformasi yang berkelanjutan semakin penting dilakukan untuk memastikan bisnis media yang berada di bawah naungan Grup SCMA mampu menavigasi seluruh tantangan bisnis yang ada dan dapat beradaptasi secara cepat terhadap perkembangan industri media saat ini guna menjaga prospek bisnis yang berkelanjutan.

Kegiatan sehari-hari lainnya yang dilakukan termasuk menyediakan tempat-sampah terpisah untuk sampah daur ulang, mengurangi penggunaan kertas dengan menggunakan sistem online untuk seluruh proses persetujuan internal, dan masih banyak lagi langkah-langkah lainnya yang telah kami terapkan dalam kegiatan operasional sehari-hari.

## SEKILAS KINERJA KEBERLANJUTAN

Kebijakan transformasi berkelanjutan yang dilakukan Perseroan pada akhirnya membawa dampak positif terhadap leadership to achieve efficiency targets, both in work processes and in financial aspects, and (b) by investing in environmentally friendly sectors.

Based on the main approaches above, the Company and its subsidiaries build its sustainability strategy as part of a comprehensive framework of transformation policy to be implemented across various aspects of its business. Supported by the adoption of the latest technology in providing quality content, the transformation has been crucial for the success of SCMA Group in maintaining its leadership in the media and entertainment industry. Sustainable transformation is increasingly important to ensure that the media businesses under SCMA Group are able to navigate all existing business challenges and adapt quickly to the evolving media industry trends in order to maintain a sustainable business outlook.

In order to support the Group's goals for sustainable growth, the Company synergizes the advantages of each portfolio company in its media ecosystem, including its national Free-To-Air (FTA) television stations and Over-The-Top (OTT) platform to strengthen the go-to-market alliance so that existing business opportunities can be optimized.

In addition, continuous innovation and investment in the latest Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies also help the Company to provide personalized products and services for each market segment and achieve efficiency in business processes.

At the same time, the Company increases its contribution to the realization of a sustainable environment through a number of initiatives, such as building an environmentally friendly studios and upholding efficiency in the use of energy at its headquarters and at the production sites for content or programs that will be aired on its FTA TV stations or digital media platforms. Other everyday activities include providing a separate garbage can for recycling items, reducing paper usage by using online systems for all its internal approval processes (paperless) and there are many more actions that we have implemented in our daily operational activities.
