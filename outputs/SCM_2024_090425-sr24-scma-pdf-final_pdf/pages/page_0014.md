Sementara itu, SCTV dan Indosiar berbagi peringkat pertama dan kedua dengan tingkat kepemirsaan masing-masing sebesar 14,1% dan 13,5%.

Salah satu pendukung kinerja segmen televisi ini adalah tayangan hiburan berkualitas serta berbagai ajang olahraga berkelas dunia. Melalui kolaborasi antara stasiun televisi FTA dan platform OTT Vidio, tercipta suatu keunggulan tersendiri untuk meraih peluang bisnis yang lebih besar melalui go-to-market alliance, terutama dalam meraih hak siar eksklusif untuk perhelatan olahraga bergengsi, baik skala nasional maupun internasional. Sepanjang tahun 2024, segmen media SCMA telah menayangkan sebanyak 4.487 pertandingan olahraga, termasuk turnamen voli, bulutangkis dan kompetisi sepakbola, yaitu Piala Presiden 2024, Piala AFF U-19 2024 dan Piala AFF U-16 2024, dan perhelatan Olimpiade 2024 Paris di Prancis yang berlangsung pada tanggal 26 Juli-11 Agustus 2024. Tayangan Olimpiade Paris 2024 berhasil menarik minat pemirsa lebih besar dibandingkan tiga edisi Olimpiade sebelumnya.

Sementara itu, menurut data MPA, platform OTT, Vidio, masih menjadi yang terdepan dibandingkan platform OTT lainnya yang berasal dari mancanegara dalam hal jumlah pelanggan (subscriber) dan Monthly Active Users. Keunggulan ini termasuk juga dalam hal jumlah unduhan video. Data dari AppAnnie menunjukkan bahwa Vidio mencatat jumlah tertinggi dibanding platform OTT sejenis dengan konten orisinal lokal menjadi yang paling diminati. Lebih lanjut, berdasarkan Consumer Spending, Google Play Store mencatat Vidio juga menjadi platform dengan jumlah Video Streaming terbanyak diunduh pada tahun 2024.

Company's media and entertainment portfolio has successfully maintained its dominance in the country's media industry. Total audience share contributed by the Company's FTA television station reached 32.9%. The Group also managed to position all of its FTA TV stations in the top 10 of the national media industry based on viewership. Even as a newcomer, Mentari achieved a 5.3% audience rating in 2024, placing it at the 7th of the top 10 FTA TV stations. Meanwhile, SCTV and Indosiar reached the first and seconding with audience ratings of 14.1% and 13.5%, respectively.

One of the supporting pillars of this television segment's performance is quality entertainment programs and various world-class sports events. Through a collaboration between the FTA television station and the Vidio OTT platform, a distinct advantage has been created to achieve greater business opportunities through a go-to-market alliance, especially in obtaining exclusive broadcasting rights for both national and international sports events. Throughout 2024, SCMA's media segment has broadcasted 4,487 sports matches, including volleyball, badminton tournaments and football competitions, including the 2024 Presidential Cup, the 2024 AFF U-19 Cup and the 2024 AFF U-16 Cup. The 2024 Olympics which were held on July 26-August 11 in Paris, France successfully attracted greater viewers' interest than the last three previous Olympics.

Meanwhile, according to MPA data, the OTT platform, Vidio, is still ahead of other foreign OTT platforms in terms of the number of subscribers and Monthly Active Users. This also includes the number of video downloads. Data from AppAnnie shows that Vidio has the highest number of downloads compared to similar OTT platforms with local original content being the most in demand. Furthermore, based on Consumer Spending, Google Play Store reported that Vidio is a platform with the highest number of Video Streaming downloads in 2024.

In 2024, Vidio recorded a 16% increase in the number of paid subscribers and a 41% increase in transactions. Vidio's success was facilitated by extracting synergies with SCTV in the quality of its local series, one of which is 'Asmara Gen Z' which was watched by 1.77 million viewers in just the first 3 weeks of its premier broadcast, making it the most watched drama series.
