## Kebijakan Antikorupsi
### Anti-Corruption Policy

[GRI 205-1] [GRI 205-2] [GRI 404-2] [GRI 2-27] [GRI 205-3]

Kebijakan Antikorupsi, Penyuapan, dan Sumbangan Ilegal disusun dan disosialisasikan sebagai wujud komitmen Perseroan terhadap terwujudnya organisasi yang akuntabel. Kebijakan yang melarang praktik korupsi, suap, gratifikasi, dan balas jasa diintegrasikan ke dalam Kebijakan Standar Perilaku. Merujuk pada kebijakan tersebut, setiap karyawan diwajibkan untuk menyampaikan laporan atas setiap temuan terkait praktik tersebut kepada Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia.

Perseroan telah mengidentifikasi jenis tindakan yang berpeluang memicu terjadinya praktik korupsi, yaitu:

- Tindakan pemberian dan penerimaan segala bentuk penyuapan, dengan tidak ada pengecualian siapapun penerimanya; dan
- Tindakan pemberian atau penawaran uang, biaya, komisi, kredit, hadiah, benda berharga, atau kompensasi dalam bentuk apapun, langsung ataupun tak langsung, kepada badan pemerintah, pejabat, kontraktor atau subkontraktor untuk memperoleh suatu kontrak atau perlakuan khusus.

Perseroan menetapkan bentuk-bentuk tindakan penyuapan di atas di kategorikan sebagai pelanggaran berat dengan potensi risiko tinggi (high risk) dengan potensi dampak yang signifikan terhadap reputasi Perseroan dan prospek keberlanjutan Grup dalam jangka panjang.

In order to obtain adequate support, especially from the Company's internal stakeholders, for the implementation of the sustainability policies in 2024, the Company has been striving to cultivate a culture of sustainability within its organization. The Company has developed a number of policies and strategic initiatives to motivate everyone in the Company to voluntarily implement sustainability principles internally and when interacting with external parties.

Some of the initiatives which are designed as part of the cultivation of sustainability culture throughout 2024 are as follow:

The Anti-Corruption, Bribery, and Illegal Donation Policy is formulated and socialized as a form of the Company's commitment to the realization of an accountable organization. This policy prohibits the practices of corruption, bribery, gratification, and kickbacks, and is integrated into the Code of Conduct. Referring to this policy, every employee is required to submit a report on any findings of such practices to the Human Resources Division.

The Company has identified the following types of actions that potentially trigger corruption:

- The act of giving and receiving all kinds of bribery, regardless of the recipient; and
- The act of giving or offering money, fees, commissions, credits, gifts, valuables, or compensation of any kind, whether directly or indirectly, to government agencies, officials, contractors, or sub-contractors to obtain a contract or special treatment.

The Company categorizes the above forms of bribery as serious violations with high-risk potential that could significantly impact the Company's reputation and the Group's long-term sustainability prospects.
