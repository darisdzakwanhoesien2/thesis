# Evaluasi Kinerja Badan Tata Kelola Tertinggi
Performance Evaluation of the Highest Governance Organ
[GRI 2-18]

Direksi menjalani penilaian kinerja menggunakan metode self-assessment. Hasil penilaian ini akan disampaikan kepada Dewan Komisaris melalui Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi untuk kemudian dipertanggungjawabkan kepada Pemegang Saham pada Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham. Selain itu, Dewan Komisaris juga melaksanakan self-assessment setiap tahun atau secara berkala sesuai kebutuhan. Kewajiban ini telah diatur dalam Piagam Dewan Komisaris yang disusun sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan Peraturan Perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Hasil dari self-assessment tersebut menjadi acuan untuk mengukur pencapaian Dewan Komisaris terhadap kriteria standar kinerja yang telah ditetapkan. Selain itu, berdasarkan hasil penilaian tersebut, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi akan mengevaluasi kinerja dan menyusun Remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris.

The performance of the Board of Directors is evaluated using a self-assessment method. The assessment results will be submitted to the Board of Commissioners through the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and then reported to the Shareholders at the General Meeting of Shareholders. In addition, the Board of Commissioners also conducts a self-assessment annually or periodically as needed. This obligation is regulated in the Board of Commissioners' Charter, which was formulated in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association and applicable laws and regulations. The results of the self-assessment are used as a reference to measure the achievement of the Board of Commissioners against the established standard performance criteria. Furthermore, based on the assessment results, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee will evaluate the performance and determine the remuneration for the Board of Commissioners.

# Pengembangan Kompetensi Penanggung Jawab Penerapan Kebijakan Keberlanjutan
Competency Development of the Person in Charge of Sustainability Policy Implementation
[OJK E.2] [GRI 2-17]

Perseroan memberikan kesempatan kepada seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi untuk senantiasa meningkatkan kompetensi melalui program Pendidikan dan Pelatihan yang sesuai dengan kebutuhan pengembangan kapabilitas manajerialnya. Perseroan memberikan dukungan penuh kepada anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi untuk mengembangkan kompetensinya yang mencakup metode self-study, serta partisipasi dalam pelatihan, seminar, konferensi, workshop, dan kegiatan lainnya. Dengan upaya pelatihan dan pengembangan kompetensi ini, diharapkan dapat memberikan dampak positif dalam peningkatan kinerja Perseroan secara berkelanjutan.

Berikut Pelatihan yang diikuti sepanjang tahun 2024:

The Company provides opportunities for all members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors to continuously improve their competencies through education and training programs that are in line with the development needs of their managerial capabilities. The Company fully supports the members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in developing their competencies, including through self-study methods, as well as participation in training, seminars, conferences, workshops, and other activities. These training and competency development programs are expected to positively impact the Company's performance in a sustainable manner.
