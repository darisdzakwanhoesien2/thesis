## Pendekatan Manajemen

Dalam kapasitas SCM sebagai perusahaan yang bergerak di bidang media dan hiburan, Perseroan memiliki peran penting untuk menegakkan integritas dan tanggung jawab etis dalam menjalankan kegiatan usahanya. Di era informasi yang bergerak seolah tanpa batas, SCM memperhatikan prinsip-prinsip dan etika jurnalistik yang menjunjung tinggi akurasi, independensi, dan akuntabilitas dalam penayangan konten dan program bagi masyarakat maupun pengiklan.

Komitmen untuk menyediakan konten yang berkualitas tentunya menunjukkan upaya SCM untuk menjaga daya saing perusahaan di tengah berbagai tantangan yang dihadapi industri media nasional. Selain itu, komitmen ini menjadi wujud peningkatan peran Perseroan untuk senantiasa mengedepankan praktik-praktik keberlanjutan dalam kegiatan operasinya, baik di seluruh stasiun televisi nasional FTA maupun platform media OTT, Vidio.

Untuk memastikan pelaksanaan praktik bisnis yang sehat dan bertanggung jawab, maka Dewan Komisaris turut memperkuat pengawasannya, terutama terkait aspek kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Perseroan memastikan bahwa hingga akhir periode pelaporan, Perseroan tidak menerima sanksi dalam bentuk apapun yang diakibatkan adanya pelanggaran ketentuan terkait persaingan usaha, baik dari otoritas Indonesia, maupun Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha (KPPU) yang berdampak pada pencabutan izin penyiaran Perseroan. ## Management Approach

In SCM's capacity as a company engaged in the media and entertainment sector, the Company has a vital role to uphold integrity and ethical responsibility in carrying out its business activities. In an era of unrestricted information, SCM pays attention to journalistic principles and ethics that uphold accuracy, independence, and accountability in the broadcasting of content and programs for the public and advertisers.

The commitment to provide quality content has certainly demonstrated SCM's efforts to maintain the Company's competitiveness amid the various challenges faced by the national media industry. In addition, this commitment is a manifestation of the Company's increased dedication to always prioritizing sustainability practices in its operations, whether in the national FTA television stations or the OTT media platform, Vidio.

In order to ensure the implementation of sound and responsible business practices, the Board of Commissioners has enhanced its supervision, especially regarding compliance with applicable laws and regulations. The Company ensures that until the end of the reporting period, no sanctions of any kind were imposed on the Company for violating business competition regulations, either by the Indonesian authorities or the Business Competition Supervisory Commission (KPPU), which could result in the revocation of the Company's broadcasting license. [GRI 3-3]

Journalistic ethics are considered a Code of Conduct for journalists to always prioritize the principles of equality, accuracy, independency and accountability in the presentation of reports. As part of upholding the principle of integrity in all of its operations, SCM upholds the principles of responsible journalistic ethics, especially in the Company's media division. The Company ensures that all information presented to the public has been carefully verified to ensure that the news complies with journalistic standards and does not mix facts with judgmental opinions.

In addition, the enforcement of the principle of integrity also includes a refusal to make discriminatory news of a certain ethnicity, religion, race, gender, skin color, physical condition, or group, containing information that is inaccurate, slanderous, sadistic, or indecent.
