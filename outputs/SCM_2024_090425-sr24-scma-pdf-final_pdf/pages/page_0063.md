Pemasangan system elevator terkoordinasi (smart elevator).

# Initiatives to Achieve Energy Efficiency and Use of Renewable Energy [OJK F.7]

The energy efficiency that has been achieved in 2024 is in line with the Company's target. This achievement is supported by a number of initiatives, including:

- Centralization of content/program/broadcast production activities at the Cijayanti Natural Studio, thus reducing the use of operational vehicles;
- Use of an automatic control system for air conditioning (AC) installed at several points around the office;
- Replacement of aluminum-based electrical cables with copper cables to reduce electrical current resistance which ultimately reduces the Company's electricity consumption level;
- Use of LED lights that are more energy efficient for lighting and production equipment in the studio to reduce electricity consumption; and
- Installation of a coordinated elevator system (smart elevator).

Regarding the use of alternative renewable energy sources, the Company has encouraged the use of solar energy, which is an alternative renewable and environmentally friendly energy source for indoor lighting needs.
