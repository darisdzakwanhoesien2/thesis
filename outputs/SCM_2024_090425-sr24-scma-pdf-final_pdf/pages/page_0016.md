Besides paying attention to environmental preservation, the Company also increases its social contribution to the general public. This social contribution is realized by distributing donations to communities who are affected by disasters in various regions in Indonesia. # RESPONDING TO FUTURE CHALLENGES

The Company realizes that sustainable investment has a positive impact on the Company's performance. Therefore, SCMA continues to adapt to dynamic trends in the media and entertainment industry by strengthening digital infrastructure and applying the latest technology.

The application of AI technology this year has been expanded to SCMA's subsidiaries in order to improve the quality of content production. Through Whisper Media, SCMA enhances its capabilities to produce virtual content with high-quality visual effects which are faster and more cost-efficient.

With the Company's ability to utilize technology to create excellence in all content production and other services, SCMA believes in its ability to navigate the risks arising from competition with existing digital media companies or platforms and maintain its leadership in the country's media and entertainment industry.
