# Penggunaan Energi dan Pengendalian Emisi Energy Usage and Emission Control

# Pendekatan Manajemen

Kebijakan penggunaan energi di lingkungan Perseroan memiliki tujuan untuk mendukung efisiensi konsumsi energi di seluruh operasional dan meminimalisir dampak lingkungan melalui pengurangan tingkat emisi karbon. Kebijakan efisiensi energi yang diterapkan Perseroan meliputi penghematan dalam penggunaan listrik, air maupun bahan bakar.

Perseroan senantiasa mencari alternatif solusi yang efektif untuk dapat menekan konsumsi energinya meskipun pengelolaan energi di sejumlah lokasi bisnis Grup SCM merupakan tanggung jawab dari Unit Pemeliharaan Gedung (Building Management). Unit ini akan menyampaikan telah pemantauan atas penggunaan energi di lingkungan bisnis Perseroan kepada Direksi sebagai bahan evaluasi dan pertimbangan dalam merumuskan kebijakan terkait penggunaan energi. [GRI 3-3]

# Penggunaan Energi [GRI 302-1] [OJK F.6]

Perseroan memperoleh pasokan listrik dari PT PLN (Persero) dan bahan bakar yang dipasarkan oleh PT Pertamina (Persero). Dengan mempertimbangkan frekuensi yang tinggi dalam hal kegiatan penyiaran maupun pembuatan konten/program/tayangan, make potensi pemakaian listrik dan bahan bakar cukup tinggi. Oleh karena itu, Perseroan berupaya mencari solusi untuk mengurangi penggunaan energi listrik dan bahan bakar sebagai bagian dari kontribusi Perseroan terhadap pengurangan emisi karbon.

# Management Approach

The Company's energy usage policy aims to support energy efficiency across all operations and minimize environmental impact by reducing carbon emissions. The Company's energy efficiency policy includes saving electricity, water and fuel.

The Company is always looking for effective solutions alternatives to reduce its energy usage, even though the management of energy in some of SCM Group's business locations is the responsibility of the Building Management Unit. This unit will submit the results of energy usage monitoring in the Company's business environment to the Board of Directors for evaluation and consideration in formulating policies related to energy usage. [GRI 3-3]

# Energy Usage [GRI 302-1] [OJK F.6]

The Company obtains its electricity sources from PT PLN (Persero) and fuel from PT Pertamina (Persero). Considering the high frequency of broadcasting activities and the production of content/programs/shows, the potential for electricity and fuel usage is quite significant. Therefore, the Company searches for solutions to reduce the use of electricity and fuel as part of its contribution to the reduction of carbon emissions.

The realization of these efforts is indicated by the decrease in the level of fuel and electricity usage compared to the amount of energy usage in 2023. Fuel and electricity usage decreased by  $6.0\%$  and  $0.4\%$ , respectively, in 2024. The following is a report on fuel and electricity usage in 2022-2024:
