# Topik Material Material Topics

[GRI 3-1] [GRI 3-2]

Perseroan menyusun topik material berdasarkan pedoman pelaporan yang kami gunakan, yaitu GRI Standards. Topik material yang disampaikan dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan ini merupakan topik-topik yang dinilai penting bagi Perseroan karena dampaknya terhadap nilai dan reputasi organisasi dengan cakupan aspek meliputi lingkungan, ekonomi dan sosial, termasuk dampak pada hak asasi manusia.

Namun demikian, dikarenakan pedoman GRI Standards belum menyediakan rujukan khusus untuk industri yang sesuai dengan kegiatan usaha Perseroan, maka topik material yang disusun oleh Perseroan dihimpun berdasarkan hasil evaluasi dan masukan dari para pemangku kepentingan, dampak atau risiko yang ditimbulkan baik positif maupun negatif terkait kegiatan usaha Perseroan terhadap pemangku kepentingan, serta analisis tren keberlanjutan atau isu yang sedang berkembang dan relevan dengan industri Perseroan.

The Company compiles material topics based on the GRI Standards reporting guidelines. The material topics presented in this Sustainability Report are deemed important to the Company due to their impact on the organization's value and reputation in environmental, economic and social aspects, including human rights.

However, since the GRI Standards guidelines did not provide specific references for industries relevant to the Company's business activities, the material topics were compiled by the Company based on evaluation results and input from stakeholders, the positive and negative impacts or risks caused by the Company's business activities to stakeholders, and an analysis of sustainability trends or issues that occur and are relevant to the Company's industry.
