|  Scope 1, 2 and 3 Emissions Definition | Greenhouse Gas Protocol | Scope 1 emissions are direct emissions from owned or controlled sources.
Scope 2 emissions (location-based) are indirect emissions from the generation of purchased energy.
Scope 3 emissions are all indirect emissions (not included in scope 2) that occur in the value chain of the reporting company, including both upstream and downstream emissions. Emissions from the use of a company's products are included in Scope 3.  |
