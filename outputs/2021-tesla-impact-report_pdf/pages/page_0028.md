# Committed to ensuring pay equity

Tesla HR offers a Pay Equity &amp; Pay Transparency educational course to everyone in the People Organization, with a focus on HR partners and recruiters. This course details what pay equity is, why it is important, how unconscious bias affects pay and hiring, best practices for hiring and compensation, and how each person can be an effective partner in helping the company achieve and maintain pay equity. A recorded version of the course is available to all hiring managers.

We also have an annual pay equity program in place, designed to assess whether similarly situated employees are paid in a similar manner after accounting for a range of variables such as:

- Geographic zone
- Tenure (which determines how many grants are in the process of vesting)
- Average performance score
- Job function
- Management level and role
