# ESP Dept Strategy &amp; Portfolio Management

# Sustainable Framework Policy

1. Membangun Framework, komitmen, roadmap, dan target climate change sesuai target NDC/NZE Nasional termasuk cascading indikatornya di KPI
2. Alignment commitment ESG climate change dengan transformasi long Team Corporate Plan 2032 (Lunar Project), Visi Misi, RBB, Corp. Culture
3. Menyusun Strategi Sustainability Transition yang komprehensif (policies, people, process &amp; data) yang tercermin dalam Visi, Risk Measure &amp; Analysis serta Sustainability Program Monitoring
1. Developing a framework, commitment, roadmap, and climate change targets in accordance with National NDC/NZE targets, including cascading KPIs
2. Aligning ESG climate change commitment with the transformation of Long Team Corporate Plan 2032 (Lunar Project), Vision and Mission, the Bank's Business Plan (RBB), and Corporate Culture
3. Developing a comprehensive Sustainability Transition Strategy (policies, people, process &amp; data) as reflected in the Vision, Risk Measure &amp; Analysis and Sustainability Program Monitoring

# Sustainable Portfolio Management

1. Koordinator monitoring bulanan laporan Sustainable Portfolio THI (incl Laporan tematik) dan Sustainability Portfolio sesuai Peraturan OJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017
2. Alignment strategi Sust. Portfolio dengan Loan Portfolio Guideline
3. Pengembangan Sustainable Produk (suite) &amp; ESG Expertise
4. Pengembangan sistem, proses dan metodologi pengelolaan Sust. Portfolio sesuai Best Practices seperti climate-related financial risk dan climate stress testing
1. Coordinating monitoring on THI's Sustainable Portfolio monthly report (including thematic report) and Sustainability Portfolio in accordance with Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 51/POJK.03/2017
2. Aligning Sustainable Portfolio strategy with Loan Portfolio Guideline
3. Developing Sustainable Product (suite) &amp; ESG Expertise
4. Developing system, process and methodology for Sustainability Portfolio management system, process and methodology in accordance with best practices such, as climate-related financial risk and climate stress testing

# ESG Communication &amp; Operation Department

# Sustainable Communication &amp; Disclosure

1. ESG Desk bagi stakeholder
2. Memastikan disclosure ESG di Sustainability Report sesuai best practices termasuk mendapat opini dari Independent Assurer
3. Monitoring &amp; peningkatan ESG Rating
4. ESG Awareness Bankwide
5. Koordinator &amp; Sekretariat alignment inisiatif ESG Mandiri Group dengan seluruh perusahaan Anak (Tata Kelola Terintegrasi)
1. ESG Desk for stakeholders
2. Ensuring that ESG disclosure in Sustainability Report is in accordance with best practices, including obtaining opinions from Independent Assurer
3. Monitoring &amp; improvement of ESG Rating
4. ESG Awareness Bankwide
5. As Coordinator &amp; Secretariat of alignment of Mandiri Group's ESG initiatives with all subsidiaries (Integrated Governance)

# Sustainable Operational

1. Menyusun RAKB sesuai Peraturan OJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017 dan menyampaikannya ke OJK setelah mendapat persetujuan Direksi dan Komisaris
2. Koordinator monitoring Implementasi seluruh Inisiatif RAKB
3. Reporting system terkait nilai emisi karbon aktivitas bionis &amp; operational (konsumsi listrik, BBM, Avtur, Kertas) sesuai TCFD
4. Cascading KPI ESG level group dengan metric untuk mencapai komitmen ESG (Climate Change realated)
5. Memastikan alignment aspek ESG ke ketentuan internal
6. Menyusun materi terkait ESG untuk kebutuhan regulator &amp; BOD
1. Preparing the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (SFAP) in accordance with Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 51/POJK.03/2017 and submit the SFAP to the Financial Services Authority (OJK) after receiving approval from the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners
2. Coordinating and monitoring the implementation of all SFAP initiatives
3. Reporting carbon emission value of business &amp; operational activities (electricity consumption, fuel, avtur, paper) in accordance with TCFD
4. Cascading ESG KPIs at the group level with metrics to achieve ESG commitments (Climate Change related)
5. Ensuring alignment of ESG aspects to internal provisions
6. Preparing ESG-related materials for regulators &amp; the Board of Directors
