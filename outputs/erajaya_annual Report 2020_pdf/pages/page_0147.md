# Board Of Commissioners' Charter

Pursuant to OJK Regulation No. 33/POJK.04/2014 concerning the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies, the Board of Commissioners' Meeting shall be organized minimum 1 (one) time in 2 (two) months.
