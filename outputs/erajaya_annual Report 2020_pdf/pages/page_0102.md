# CAPITAL STRUCTURE POLICY

The Company has implemented a proportion of equality based on risk (risk-based capital). The Company manages the capital structure and adjusts by considering changing economic conditions and underlying assets risk characteristics. The Company also monitors the capital based on Debt to Equity Ratio (DER). Throughout 2020, Erajaya Debt to Equity Ratio (DER) achieved 0.97 times.
