Liabilitas jangka panjang Perseroan naik 147,40% dari Rp153,46 miliar per 31 Desember 2019 menjadi Rp379,65 miliar per 31 Desember 2020. Kenaikan ini terutama disebabkan oleh kenaikan utang jangka panjang-liabilitas sewa Rp179,41 miliar dibanding tahun 2019.

## Ekuitas

Total ekuitas Perseroan per 31 Desember 2020 tercatat sebesar Rp5,69 triliun, meningkat 14,25% dari Rp4,98 triliun per 31 Desember 2019. Peningkatan ini terutama disumbang oleh kenaikan pada laba ditahan.

## LAPORAN KOMPOSISI KEUANGAN KONSOLIDASIAN

## Penjualan Neto

Total penjualan neto Perseroan pada tahun 2020 mencapai Rp34,11 triliun, yang disumbangkan terutama oleh segmen telepon selular &amp; tablet dengan kontribusi sekitar 76,31%, segmen voucher produk operator dengan kontribusi sekitar 11,74%, dan segmen aksesoris &amp; lain-lain dengan kontribusi sekitar 7,41%.

Total penjualan neto tahun 2020 meningkat Rp1,17 triliun atau naik 3,55% dari Rp32,94 triliun pada tahun 2019.

Non-current liabilities increased by 147.40% from Rp153,46 billion as of December 31, 2019 to Rp379,65 billion as of December 31, 2020. The increase was mainly due to increase in long-term debts-leases by Rp179,41 billion in 2020 compared to 2019.

## Equity

The Company's total equity as of December 31, 2020 was recorded Rp5.69 trillion, increased by 14.25% from Rp4.98 trillion as of December 31, 2019. The increase was mainly contributed by an increase in retained earnings.

## CONSOLIDATED STATEMENTS OF FINANCIAL POSITION

## Net Sales

The Company's total net sales in 2020 reached Rp34.11 trillion, which were contributed mainly by cellular phones &amp; tablet segment with contribution of 76.31%, operator product segment with contribution of 11.74%, and accessories and others segment with 7.41% contribution.

The total net sales 2020 increased Rp1.17 trillion or up by 3.55% from Rp32.94 trillion in 2019.
