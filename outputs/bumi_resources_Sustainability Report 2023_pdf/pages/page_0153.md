# Manajemen K3 di Rantai Pasok (403-7)

Perjanjian Kerja Sama Perusahaan dan Kontraktor telah memuat proses verifikasi dan syarat minimum terkait K3 yang harus dipenuhi oleh kontraktor dan subkontraktor untuk dapat bekerja di BRMS dan unit usaha, diantaranya:
- Mengumpulkan Safety Management Plan yang memuat target dan program kerja K3.
- Penyediaan APD dan stok APD yang lengkap terhadap pekerjaan.
- Jaminan asuransi kesehatan terhadap pekerjaan.
- Melengkapi Contractor Induction List seperti safety induction dan code of conduct.

Lebih lanjut, unit usaha melakukan inspeksi, observasi, dan penilaian berjalan kepada kontraktor dan subkontraktor yang bekerja di area mereka.

# OHS Management in the Supply Chain

The Company and Contractor Cooperation Agreement incorporates a verification process and sets out minimum K3 requirements that contractors and subcontractors must meet in order to operate at BRMS and its business units, including:
- Submission of Safety Management Plan that contains OHS targets and programs.
- PPE provision and PPE stock for its workers.
- Health insurance for its workers.
- Completion of Contractor Induction List includes safety induction and code of conduct.

In addition, the subsidiaries also conducts on-site inspection, observation, and ongoing assessments of contractors and subcontractors working in their areas.
