# PILAR PENDIDIKAN

## Education Pillar

Demi meningkatkan kualitas pendidikan bagi masyarakat yang ada di sekitar tambang, CPM, GM, dan DPM memberikan bantuan melalui dana untuk mendukung kegiatan belajar mengajar.

In order to improve the quality of education for the community around the mine, CPM, GM and DPM provide assistance through funds to support teaching and learning activities.

PT CPM memberikan beasiswa pendidikan kepada tiga mahasiswa di Palu melalui Program Beasiswa Tangguh Batch II. Program beasiswa bertujuan mendukung akses pendidikan, pengembangan bakat, dan meningkatkan peluang bagi mahasiswa berprestasi, serta memperkuat fondasi pendidikan. Melalui inisiatif seperti beasiswa, pelatihan keterampilan, dan penyediaan sumber belajar, kami berkomitmen meningkatkan akses dan kualitas pendidikan di masyarakat lokal.

PT CPM provided educational scholarships to three students in Palu through the Tangguh Batch II Scholarship Program. The scholarship program aims to support access to education, talent development, and increase opportunities for outstanding students, as well as strengthening educational foundations. Through initiatives such as scholarships, skills training and the provision of learning resources, we are committed to improving access and quality of education in local communities.
