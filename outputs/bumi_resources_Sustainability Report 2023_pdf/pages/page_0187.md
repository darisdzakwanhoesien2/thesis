## Identifikasi dan Mitigasi Dampak Lingkungan Hidup

(POJK51-F.28)

Melalui pemantauan lingkungan, kami mengidentifikasi dan menilai risiko untuk mencegah adanya dampak negatif lingkungan hidup dari proses pertambangan maupun produk yang dihasilkan. Proses identifikasi dan penilaian tersebut telah sesuai dengan Peraturan Pemerintah No. 22 Tahun 2021 tentang Penyelenggaraan Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup dan ISO 14001:2015 tentang Sistem Manajemen Lingkungan.

Unit usaha kami yaitu GM, CPM, DPM, dan LMR melakukan identifikasi dan penilaian risiko lingkungan pada setiap tahapan penambangan mulai dari ekplorasi dan perencanaan, konstruksi, operasi produksi, transportasi sampai pada tahap pascatambang. Kami bangga karena sampai dengan tahun 2023, kami berhasil mempertahankan kinerja lingkungan hidup.

During the tendering phase, we not only assess compliance with environmental regulations but also evaluate prospective suppliers' and contractors' track records in areas such as occupational health and safety, environmental stewardship, and labor practices.

Subsequently, in the contracting stage, we have incorporated environmental protection clauses into all (100%) contractual agreements with contractors, mining equipment suppliers, consultants, and other business partners. In the monitoring and performance evaluation phase, our Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) and Supply Chain teams routinely review and assess environmental compliance aspects within the supply chain. Importantly, our commitment to environmental sustainability extends beyond regulatory compliance; we actively support our suppliers and partners in attaining environmental management standards through training and outreach initiatives.

## Environmental Impact Identification and Mitigation

Through environmental monitoring practices, we systematically identify and evaluate potential risks to proactively mitigate adverse environmental effects stemming from both the mining operations and the products. The process of identification and assessment is in accordance with Government Regulation No. 22 of 2021 concerning the Implementation of Environmental Protection and Management and ISO 14001:2015 concerning Environmental Management Systems.

As required by these regulations and standards, our business units, GM, CPM, DPM and LMR, carry out environmental risk identification and assessment at every stage of mining, from exploration and planning, construction, production operations, transportation to the post-mining stage. We are proud because up to 2023, we have managed to maintain environmental performance.
