# Strategi Pengelolaan Lingkungan 2023 (2-23)

Pada tahun 2023, kami telah menyerahkan Laporan Pelaksanaan Rencana Pengelolaan dan Pemantauan Lingkungan kepada Dinas Lingkungan Hidup tempat unit usaha beroperasi. Dalam laporan ini, Kami menyampaikan kinerja pengelolaan dan pemantauan lingkungan, di antaranya:

1. Kualitas udara ambien, kebisingan dan getaran.
2. Perubahan fungsi lahan, gangguan kestabilan lereng dan perubahan iklim mikro.
3. Erosi dan sedimentasi serta laju aliran air permukaan.
4. Kualitas dan kuantitas air.
5. Longsor.
6. Timbulan limbah B3.
7. Gangguan biota perairan serta flora dan fauna.
8. Pengelolaan gangguan kesehatan masyarakat dan sanitasi lingkungan.

# Strategy of Environmental Management 2023

In 2023, we submitted an Environmental Management and Monitoring Plan Implementation Report to the local Environment Agency in the area of operation every six months. This report comprehensively outlines our environmental management and monitoring performance, covering various aspects such as:

1. Ambient air quality, noise, and vibration.
2. Changes in land function, slope stability disturbances, and microclimate alterations.
3. Erosion, sedimentation, and surface water flow rates.
4. Water quality and quantity.
5. Landslides.
6. Generation of hazardous waste (B3 waste).
7. Disturbance of aquatic biota, as well as flora and fauna.
8. Management of public health issues and environmental sanitation.

# Environmental Commitment in the Supply Chain

We firmly believe that our supply chain is instrumental in advancing the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly in sourcing sustainable goods and services. Whenever feasible, we prioritize engagement with local communities as suppliers to the Company. Simultaneously, we ensure that risks within our supply chain are effectively mitigated through robust partnerships and comprehensive assessments of human rights and environmental risks and impacts.

We have integrated environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria into our procurement process for goods and services, spanning from tendering procedures to contracting terms and key performance indicators (KPIs) assessment during business unit operations.
