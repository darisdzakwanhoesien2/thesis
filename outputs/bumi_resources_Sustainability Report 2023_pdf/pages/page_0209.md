In 2023, PT CPM has built a domestic WWTP with a capacity of 110 m3/day. This domestic WWTP treats wastewater from canteens, mess rooms, offices, mosques, recreation areas, and washpad areas. The total amount of domestic wastewater released into the environment was 6.595 m3. The release of domestic wastewater can only be carried out if it meets environmental quality standards according to the Regulation of the Minister of Environment and Forestry No. 68 of 2016.

Thus, the total amount of water consumed by PT CPM was 117,409 m3 during 2023.

In 2023, CPM has also prepared a Technical Study for the utilization of wastewater, both industrial and domestic wastewater, to be submitted for assessment and evaluation at the Ministry of Environment and Forestry.

Meanwhile, water withdrawal by DPM from a 3rd party, namely PAM, was 3,700,000 liters during 2023. Currently PT DPM is not discharging of waste water from construction activities.
