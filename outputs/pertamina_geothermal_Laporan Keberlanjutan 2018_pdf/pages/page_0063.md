- Meningkatkan volume produksi uap panas bumi setara listrik;

## COMMITMENT

- Increasing the production of geothermal steam equivalent to electricity;
- Increasing the Company's performance and revenue;
- Creating employment for the community around the Company's working area;
- Increasing the involvement of local suppliers.

## REALISASI

- Volume produksi uap panas bumi setara listrik 4.182,15 GWh dengan pencapaian 96,13% dari target;
- Pendapatan Usaha USD660,835 ribu dengan pencapaian 104.96% dari target;
- Laba Bersih USD107,316 ribu dengan pencapaian 135.41% dari target;
- Komitmen TKDN pengadaan barang dan jasa sebesar 99,44% dengan pencapaian 331,47% dari target.

## REALIZATION

- The volume of geothermal steam equivalent to electricity is 4,182.15 GWh, which is 96.13% of the target;
- Operating Income of USD660,835 thousand, 104.96% of the target;
- Net Profit of USD107,316 thousand, 135.41% of the target;
- TKDN commitment through procurement of goods and services of 99.44%, which is 331.47% of the target.
