# WHISTLE BLOWING SYSTEM [GRI 103-3]

Whistle blowing system (WBS) adalah sistem yang mengelola pengaduan mengenai perilaku melawan hukum, perbuatan tidak etis/tidak semestinya secara rahasia, anonim dan mandiri (independen) yang digunakan untuk mengoptimalkan peran serta Insan Perusahaan dan mitra kerja dalam mengungkap pelanggaran yang terjadi di lingkungan Perusahaan.

Pengelolaan WBS Perusahaan diatur dalam TKO Nomor B-001/PGE500/2018-S9 Revisi 01 yang telah disahkan pada bulan 5 Desember 2018.

# WHISTLEBLOWING SYSTEM [GRI 103-3]

Whistleblowing System (WBS) is a system that manages complaints about unlawful, unethical/inappropriate behaviors confidentially, anonymously, and independently. WBS functions to optimize the participation of Company's Personnel and partners in disclosing violations which occurred within the Company.

Management of the Company's WBS is regulated in TKO Number B-001/PGE500/2018-S9 Revisi 01, which has been ratified in 5 December 2018.
