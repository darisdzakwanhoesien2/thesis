## 1 Wilayah Operasi Ring 1 Ring 1 Operation Area
Area geografis yang berpotensi terkena dampak kegiatan operasi Perusahaan dengan radius kurang lebih 0-5 km. Geographic area that has potential of affected by Company operation activities with radius of more less 0-5 km.

## 2 Wilayah Operasi Ring 2 Ring 2 Operation Area
Area administrative dari desa/kelurahan yang berpotensi terkena dampak kegiatan operasi Perusahaan. Administrative area from village that has potential of affected by company operation activities.

## 3 Wilayah Operasi Ring 3 Ring 3 Operation Area
Area di luar ring 1 dan 3 berdasarkan penugasan pimpinan. Area outside ring 1 and 2 based on assignment from manager.
