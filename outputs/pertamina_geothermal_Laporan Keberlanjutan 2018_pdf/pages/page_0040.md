## PROPER Awards

The Company won the PROPER Award from the Ministry of Environment and Forestry of the Republic of Indonesia. In this event, the Company won the Gold PROPER for eight times consecutively for Kamojang Area. In addition, the Company also won the Green PROPER for Ulubelu and Sibayak Areas.
