SDGs | 13.2
Integrate climate change measures
Mengintegrasikan kegiatan penanganan perubahan iklim | Climate Change and GHG Emissions
• Initiatives on GHG emissions reduction involves assessing and managing climate risk with the TCFD framework and follows the GHG Management Hierarchy, which includes industrial process optimization, implementation of energy efficiency programs, the utilization of biomass, solar photovoltaic implementation and methane capture technology as energy sources, mangrove conservation, tree planting, and responsible plantation management, such as protection of peatlands and preservation of HCV and HCS areas, fire control and haze prevention
Perubahan Iklim dan Emisi GRK
• Inisiatif terkait pengurangan emisi GRK mencakup penilaian dan pengelolaan risiko perubahan iklim dengan kerangka kerja TCFD dan mengacu pada GHG Management Hierarchy, yang meliputi optimasi proses industrial, penerapan program efisiensi energi, pemanfaatan biomassa, implementasi solar photovoltaic dan teknologi methane capture sebagai sumber energi, konservasi bakau, penanaman pohon dan pengelolaan perkebunan yang bertanggung jawab, termasuk perlindungan lahan gambut serta pelestarian area KBKT dan SKT, pengendalian kebakaran dan pencegahan kabut asap  |
