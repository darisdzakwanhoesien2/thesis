## Anti-Gratification and Anti-Corruption

Indofood is committed to operating its business ethically incorporating anti-gratification and anti-corruption policies in the Indofood Code of Conduct and internal policies and procedures. All employees are required to sign off on the Company Regulation as a commitment to the Indofood Code of Conduct.

## Dissemination

The Indofood Code of Conduct and Company Regulation are communicated to the BOC, the BOD, and all employees within the Indofood Group. This is facilitated through our employee orientation program and accessible on our internal web portal. As part of our efforts to instill a strong understanding of the Company's culture, ethics, and practices, all new employees are mandated to undergo Indofood Code of Conduct and Company Regulation socialization program, which includes anti-corruption measures, upholding human rights, no bullying, and no harassment topics.

## Anti-Gratifikasi dan Anti-Korupsi

Indofood berkomitmen untuk menjalankan usahanya secara etis dengan menerapkan kebijakan anti-gratifikasi dan anti-korupsi dalam Kode Etik Indofood serta kebijakan dan prosedur internalnya. Seluruh karyawan menandatangani Peraturan Perusahaan sebagai bentuk komitmen terhadap Kode Etik Indofood.

## Diseminasi

Kode Etik Indofood dan Peraturan Perusahaan dikomunikasikan kepada Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan seluruh karyawan Grup Indofood. Proses tersebut dijalankan melalui program orientasi karyawan dan dapat diakses melalui situs internal kami. Untuk menanamkan pemahaman terhadap budaya, etika dan praktik Perseroan, seluruh karyawan baru wajib mengikuti program sosialisasi Kode Etik Indofood dan Peraturan Perusahaan, yang mencakup langkah-langkah anti-korupsi, penghargaan terhadap hak asasi manusia, serta kebijakan anti-perundungan dan pelecehan.
