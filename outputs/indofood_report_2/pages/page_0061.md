Our Dairy factories within the CBP Group are certified with the Veterinary Control Number certification (Nomor Kontrol Veteriner or "NKV"). This certification validates compliance with hygiene and sanitation requirements for food safety of animal-based food products, aligning with regulations set forth by the Indonesian Ministry of Agriculture.

Indofood is committed to food safety at all stages, including during distribution. We have adopted Good Warehouse Practices ("GWP") that include guidelines relating to product clustering to minimize food contamination risk. Furthermore, we conduct hazard identification and risk assessment for our warehousing and logistics operations. Food handling procedures are regularly subjected to internal quality assurance audits to ensure compliance at our distribution facilities. We also use a First to Expire First Out (FEFO) stock rotation system to ensure we deliver only fresh products to consumers.

Pabrik Dairy Grup CBP kami juga telah tersertifikasi dengan Nomor Kontrol Veteriner ("NKV"). Sertifikat ini menunjukkan bahwa persyaratan higiene dan sanitasi telah terpenuhi sebagai jaminan keamanan produk pangan hewani, sesuai dengan peraturan Kementerian Pertanian Indonesia.

Komitmen kami terhadap keamanan pangan juga berlaku di semua tahapan, termasuk pada tahap distribusi produk. Kami telah menerapkan Praktik Pengelolaan Sistem Pergudangan yang Baik (Good Warehouse Practices atau "GWP") yang mencakup pedoman terkait pengelompokan produk guna meminimalkan risiko kontaminasi pangan. Kami juga melakukan identifikasi bahaya dan penilaian risiko terhadap kegiatan operasional pergudangan dan distribusi. Audit jaminan mutu dilakukan secara internal dan berkala terhadap prosedur penanganan pangan, guna memastikan kepatuhan di fasilitas distribusi kami. Kami juga telah menerapkan sistem rotasi stok First to Expire First Out (FEFO) untuk memastikan kami hanya mengirimkan produk segar ke konsumen.
