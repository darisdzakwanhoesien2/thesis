We build strong partnerships with our smallholder farmers to ensure a stable supply of some of our raw materials, hence increasing our supply chain resilience. As of 2023, we partnered with over 80,000 farmers and plasma smallholders through an inclusive business model that prioritizes sustainable farming practices. We continue to provide training focused on improving yields and farming techniques to improve farmers' yields and income. Alongside farmers, we continue to expand community partnerships to drive creation of shared value. Our Bogasari Group deepened engagement this year with over 53,000 small and medium entrepreneurs through the Bogasari Mitra Card ("BMC") membership program, which provides insurance coverage, training, and credit referrals. In our ongoing efforts to uplift the community, we expanded vocational education programs to prepare local youths for roles aligned with industry needs. This year's launch of the Teaching Factory program by our CBP Group's Flexible Packaging Unit focused on bridging the classroom and employment through immersive hands-on learning.

## Opportunities and Challenges

The increasing pressure of climate change continue to emphasize the need for urgent action. Over the last two years, our Agribusiness Group deepened analysis of the climate-related risks and opportunities the Group faces, to align with the Task Force for Climate-related Financial Disclosures ("TCFD") assessing both physical and transition risks across our operations under both the 2°C-aligned and the 4°C business-as-usual pathways. The analysis has led to a focus on climate forecasts and the potential impact on crops, with the results being incorporated into our risk management and strategy framework. Similarly, we identified risks brought about by an accelerated transition to a low-carbon economy as well as opportunities linked to new market segments, efficient resourcing, and delivering sustainable products and services.

## Peluang dan Tantangan

Tekanan perubahan iklim yang semakin meningkat, semakin menegaskan perlunya tindakan segera. Dalam dua tahun terakhir, Grup Agribisnis kami telah menyusun analisis risiko dan peluang terkait iklim menggunakan Task Force for Climate-related Financial Disclosures ("TCFD"), termasuk penilaian risiko fisik dan transisi berdasarkan skenario 2°C dan 4°C yang merupakan bisnis pada umumnya. Analisis ini mempertimbangkan dampak iklim pada tanaman perkebunan dan diintegrasikan ke dalam kerangka kerja manajemen risiko dan strategi kami. Kami juga mengidentifikasi risiko dan peluang dari ekonomi rendah karbon, segmen pasar baru, efisiensi sumber daya serta produk dan layanan berkelanjutan.
