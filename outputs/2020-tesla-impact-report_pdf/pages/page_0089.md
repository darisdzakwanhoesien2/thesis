|  5.0 million metric tons of CO2e savings | Tesla estimate | To estimate CO2e savings, we first measured the amount of miles driven and kWh of electricity generated at the state, province, and country level for 2020. We then applied an emissions savings factor (in gCO2e/ml for miles driven and gCO2e/kWh for electricity generated), for each state, province, and country. For miles driven, the emissions savings factor is the net of estimated emissions from our vehicles and an ICE with a real-world fuel efficiency rating of 25 mpg. The emissions savings factor is based on grid emissions intensity in each respective location and includes upstream emissions from the production and transport of fuels. We modeled vehicle emissions savings for the U.S., E.U. + EFTA and China, which account for ~90% of miles driven globally. We then scaled the savings to account for 100% of miles driven by dividing by ~90% and arriving at a global estimate for 2020.  |

|  Scope 1, 2 and 3 Emissions Definition | Greenhouse Gas Protocol | Scope 1 emissions are direct emissions from owned or controlled sources.
Scope 2 emissions are indirect emissions from the generation of purchased energy.
Scope 3 emissions are all indirect emissions (not included in scope 2) that occur in the value chain of the reporting company, including both upstream and downstream emissions. Emissions from the use of a company's products are included in Scope 3.  |
