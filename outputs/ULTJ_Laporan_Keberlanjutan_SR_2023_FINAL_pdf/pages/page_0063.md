# Produk Pemasaran

Sesuai dengan kontemen Perseroan untuk selalu menjamin ketersediaan produk di pasar, Perseroan secara rutin melakukan penelitian dengan memperhatikan kepentingan konsumen. Selain itu, Perseroan juga turut aktif dalam mengadakan berbagai kegiatan dan memberikan pendidikan kepada masyarakat tentang pentingnya manfaat minum susu setiap hari. Perseroan meyakini bahwa dengan melakukan langkah-langkah tersebut, pangsa pasar Perseroan dapat terus terjaga dan tetap memegang peranan sebagai pemimpin di pasar.

Di tahun 2023 ini, kondisi pasar terlihat sudah semakin membaik. Seiringan dengan hal itu, Perseroan telah melakukanBeberapa strategi penjualan, dimulai dari mengadakan promo produk baru hingga promo marketing.

# Marketing Products

In accordance with the Company's commitment to always ensure the availability of products in the market, the Company routinely conducts research with consumer interests in mind. In addition, the Company also actively participates in organizing various activities and provides education to the public about the importance of the benefits of drinking milk every day. The Company believes that by taking these measures, the Company's market share can be maintained and it will continue to play a leading role in the market.

In 2023, the market condition seems to have improved. In accordance with this, the Company has carried out several sales strategies, starting from holding new product promos to marketing promos.
