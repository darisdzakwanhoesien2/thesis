# Menjaga kelestarian alam dan lingkungan

- Mengadopsi teknik pertanian organik dan ramah lingkungan untuk mengurangi penggunaan pestisida dan pupuk kimia yang dapat mencemari tanah dan air.
- Menerapkan rotasi tanaman dan penggunaan tanaman penutup untuk meningkatkan kesuburan tanah dan mengurangi erosi.
- Memiliki sistem pengelolaan limbah yang efisien untuk meminimalkan dampak negatif terhadap lingkungan.
- Mengolah limbah organik menjadi pupuk kompos yang dapat digunakan kembali dalam pertanian.
- Menggunakan teknologi irigasi yang efisien dan sistem pengelolaan air untuk mengurangi konsumsi air dan meminimalkan pembuangan limbah cair ke lingkungan.
- Menerapkan praktik konservasi air seperti penanaman vegetasi penahan air dan pembangunan waduk penampungan air.
- Melindungi habitat alami dan lahan konservasi di sekitar area pertanian untuk mendukung keanekaragaman hayati.
- Memastikan bahwa kegiatan pertanian tidak merusak ekosistem lokal dan mempertahankan keseimbangan ekologis.
- Mengoptimalkan penggunaan energi dengan beralih ke sumber energi terbarukan dan efisiensi energi di seluruh operasi perusahaan.
- Mengurangi emisi gas rumah kaca dengan mengurangi penggunaan bahan bakar fosil dan memperkenalkan praktik transportasi hijau.

- Constructing basic infrastructure like boreholes, sanitation facilities, and road access for communities around the farms.
- Adopting environmentally friendly farming practices to reduce negative impacts on the surrounding environment, such as using organic fertilizers and proper waste management.
- Planting trees and maintaining green areas to preserve the environment and uphold ecosystem balance.
- Collaborating with local governments and non-governmental organizations (NGOs) to design sustainable programs tailored to the needs of the local community.
- Supporting government initiatives in infrastructure development and providing basic services for economically disadvantaged communities.
- Providing regular sustainability reports to the community and other Stakeholders to ensure accountability for the company's activities and their impact on society and the environment.
- Creating space for community participation in company decision-making processes through open forums and consultations.

# Preserving Nature and the Environment

- Adopting organic and environmentally friendly farming techniques to reduce the use of pesticides and chemical fertilizers that can pollute soil and water.
- Implementing crop rotation and cover cropping to enhance soil fertility and reduce erosion.
- Establishing efficient waste management systems to minimize negative environmental impacts.
- Converting organic waste into compost fertilizer that can be reused in farming.
- Using efficient irrigation technology and water management systems to reduce water consumption and minimize discharge of liquid waste into the environment.
- Applying water conservation practices such as planting water-retaining vegetation and constructing water reservoirs.
- Protecting natural habitats and conservation areas around farming areas to support biodiversity.
- Ensuring that agricultural activities do not harm local ecosystems and maintain ecological balance.
- Optimizing energy use by transitioning to renewable energy sources and energy efficiency throughout company operations.
- Reducing greenhouse gas emissions by decreasing fossil fuel use and introducing green transportation practices.
