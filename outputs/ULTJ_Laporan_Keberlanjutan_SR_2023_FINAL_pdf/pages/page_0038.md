#

PT Ultrajaya Milk Industry &amp; Trading Company Tbk

![img-41.jpeg](img-41.jpeg)

Kegiatan utama Perseroan adalah bidang industri pengolahan dan perdagangan besar dan eceran, dengan kelompok produk minuman dan makanan.

The Company's primary activities are in the field of processing and wholesale and retail trading, with a focus on beverage and food products.

# Bidang Usaha

Line of Business [GRI 2-6] [POJK C.4]

Di kelompok produk minuman, Perseroan memproduksi berbagai jenis minuman UHT (Ultra High Temperature), seperti susu cair, teh, minuman kesehatan, dan minuman tradisional. Di kelompok makanan, Perseroan memproduksi susu bubuk (powder milk) dan susu kental manis (sweetened condensed milk).

Perseroan memiliki mesin pengolahan untuk setiap masing-masing jenis produk minuman tersebut. Pengolahan dilakukan dengan menggunakan teknologi UHT, yaitu proses pemanasan dengan suhu 140°C dalam waktu 3-4 detik untuk mematikan semua bakteri dalam susu atau minuman sehingga produk steril dan tahan lama, tanpa menggunakan bahan pengawet.

For the beverage category, the Company produces various kinds of UHT beverages such as milk, tea, health drinks, and traditional drinks. As for the food category, the Company produces milk powder and sweetened condensed milk.

The Company owns processing machines for each type of beverage product. The processing is done by using UHT technology, i.e., That is the process of heating at 140°C for 3-4 seconds to eliminate all bacteria in milk or beverages, making the product sterile and long-lasting, without the use of preservatives.
