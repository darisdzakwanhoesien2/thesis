## Jumlah Produk yang ditarik kembali

Selama tahun 2023, tidak ada produk Perseroan yang ditarik kembali. Beberapa keluhan pelanggan mengenai ketidaksesuaian produk telah ditanggapi oleh Perseroan, yang selanjutnya produk tersebut telah diganti dengan yang baru dan diterima dengan baik.

## Survei Kepuasan Konsumen

Perseroan telah menyediakan pusat layanan konsumen bagi para pelanggan baik konsumen maupun pedagang, sehingga mereka dapat mengajukan pertanyaan, meminta saran, maupun informasi tentang produk yang telah dikonsumsi. Penyediaan pusat layanan konsumen ini juga diharapkan mampu membina hubungan komunikasi yang baik dengan para pelanggan.

Salah satu bentuk praktik tanggung jawab atas produk yang dilakukan adalah penerapan Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) pada proses produksi. Penerapan GMP bertujuan untuk memastikan bahwa semua produk yang dihasilkan Perseroan telah aman, layak konsumsi, tidak terkontaminasi atau rusak, dan terjamin mutunya. Kepercayaan konsumen terhadap keamanan produk telah diperkuat dengan sertifikat FSSC 22000 versi 5.1 yang telah diperoleh Perseroan.

Before being delivered to customers and distributors, finished products will be quarantined in the factory warehouse for approximately one week to undergo the final quality checking process.

As the quality and safety inspection procedures implemented, the Company has obtained FSSC 22000 version 5.1 certificate and Halal certificate, and will continue to be verified by internal quality assurance team as well as external auditors including BPOM, MUI, and the Company's partners.

## Number of recalled products

During 2023, none of the Company's products were recalled. Several customer complaints regarding product non-conformity have been responded to by the Company, and the products have been replaced with new ones and were well received.

## Consumer Satisfaction Survey

The Company has provided a consumer service center for both consumers and merchants, so that they can ask questions, seek advice, and information regarding the products they consumed. Providing this consumer service center is also expected to foster a good communication relationship with customers.

One form of product responsibility practice is the implementation of Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) in the production process. The implementation of GMP aims to ensure that all products produced by the Company are safe, fit for consumption, not contaminated or damaged, and guaranteed quality. Consumer confidence in product safety has been strengthened by the FSSC 22000 version 5.1 certificate that the Company has obtained.
