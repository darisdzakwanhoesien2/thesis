# Manajemen Risiko Keberlanjutan Sustainability Risk Management

M. M. S. S. S. S. (M.S.S.S.)

Dalam rangka mengelola dan mengurangi risiko LST dengan efektif, Direksi Perseroan mengelola manajemen risiko dengan mempertimbangkan masukan dari masing-masing departemen dalam mengidentifikasikan dan memetakan dampak risiko setiap tahun.

Risiko-risiko yang telah dianalisa digunakan sebagai acuan dalam proses mitigasi dampak negatif yang berkaitan dengan lingkungan, Pemangku Kepentingan, dan Perseroan. Beberapa risiko yang teridentifikasi di antaranya ketersediaan bahan baku, kualitas bahan baku, kesehatan dan keselamatan pekerja, jaringan distribusi, kebutuhan energi dan dampak perubahan cuaca. Semua risiko ini tentunya memiliki tingkatan yang berbeda-beda di setiap tahunnya. Untuk itu Perseroan telah melakukan beberapa upaya di antaranya, memantau pasokan, memantau efisiensi penggunaan energi saat produksi, dan pengurangan emisi.

Proses mitigasi risiko LST ini melibatkan seluruh karyawan Perseroan, termasuk Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Perseroan melakukan pendekatan secara berkala kepada seluruh karyawan dalam rangka mengoptimalkan kesadaraan terhadap penghematan energi, mengurangi limbah, meningkatkan produktivitas, serta menjaga kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja. Perilaku ini sudah sejalan dengan visi misi Perseroan dan akan terus ditingkatkan untuk menjunjung hak asasi manusia, kesetaraan, keberagamaan dan menjalin kebersamaan.

In order to effectively manage and reduce LST (Legal, Social, and Technological) risks, the Company's Board of Directors manages risk management by considering input from each department in identifying and mapping the impact of risks annually.

The analyzed risks are used as a reference in the process of mitigating negative impacts related to the environment, stakeholders, and the Company. Some identified risks include raw material availability, raw material quality, employee health and safety, distribution networks, energy needs, and the impact of weather changes. All of these risks undoubtedly have different levels each year. Therefore, the Company has made several efforts including monitoring supplies, monitoring energy efficiency during production, and reducing emissions.

The ESG risk mitigation process involved all employees of the Company, including the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners. The Company has conducted regular address to all employees in order to optimize awareness of energy savings, reduce waste, increase productivity, and maintain occupational health and safety. This efforts are in line with the Company's vision and mission and will continue to be improved to uphold human rights, equality, diversity and togetherness.

One of the important efforts to maximize the Company's positive values is to maintain the trust given by consumers. Therefore the Company is committed to providing products that are safe, nutritious, and in accordance with consumer needs. To fulfill this commitment, the Company has an obligation to provide quality raw materials, as well as timely production circulation while maintaining good working relationships with local suppliers and partner breeders/farmers.

The Company has made a large investment with two cattle farms managed with high technology, namely UPBS and USDF. Through these two cattle farms, the Company can obtain high quality local cow's milk. Some of the positive impacts of utilizing local milk are reducing the need for foreign currency, increasing the competence of local personnel, and reducing production costs. The Company also continues to implement strict standard procedures for all production processes, including efficiency, energy usage, and packaging quality. The Company also ensures product safety and smooth distribution.
