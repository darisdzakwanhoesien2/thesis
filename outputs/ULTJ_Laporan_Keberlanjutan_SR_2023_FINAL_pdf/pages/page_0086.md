## Perlakuan Setara untuk Konsumen

Keberadaan Konsumen sangat menentukan perkembangan dan keberlangsungan Perseroan. Perseroan meyakini bahwa semakin banyak relasi dengan konsumen yang didapat dan dijaga dengan baik, maka keberlangsungan usaha Perseroan akan lebih terjamin begitu pula sebaliknya. Ditambah lagi dengan posisi konsumen yang demikian strategis, maka Perseroan terus berkomitmen untuk memberikan produk dan layanan terbaik serta setara kepada seluruh konsumen tanpa terkecuali.

To date, the Company is still working closely with partners Tetra Pak and Combibloc in developing recyclable straws for all UHT products. In addition, the Company together with Tetra Pak Indonesia also continues to conduct continuous socialization to the public about the recycling program of used aseptic cartons and plastic straws.

Apart from that, the Company also holds public expose activities and participates in various activities related to the Dairy Beverages business sector to convey the latest information to the public and potential consumers regarding the goods and/or services offered by the Company.

## Equal Treatment for Consumers

The existence of consumers determines the development and sustainability of the Company. The Company believes that the more relationships with consumers that are obtained and maintained properly, the sustainability of the Company's business will be even more secure and vice versa. Coupled with the strategic position of consumers, the Company continues to be committed to providing the best and equal products and services to all consumers without exception.
