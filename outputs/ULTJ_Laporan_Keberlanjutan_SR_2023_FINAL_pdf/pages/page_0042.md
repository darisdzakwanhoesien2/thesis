#

PT Ultrajaya Milk Industry &amp; Trading Company Tbk

# Wilayah Operasional

## Operational Area [GRI 2-6] [POJK C.3]

Perseroan berelasi dengan banyak pengecer, mulai dari pengecer modern seperti hypermarket, supermarket dan minimarket, hingga tradisional seperti pengecer independen kecil dan toko kelontong, pedagang grosir, dan beberapa institusi dalam negeri. Dalam pendistribusian produk-produk kepada pengecer, Perseroan telah bekerjasama dengan beberapa distributor. Di Pulau Jawa, Perseroan secara langsung menjual produk-produknya kepada pengecer menggunakan jaringan distribusi dari PT Nikos Distribution Indonesia (NDI) yang merupakan entitas anak Perseroan dengan 70% saham kepemilikan.

Untuk pendistribusian di luar Pulau Jawa termasuk daerah yang sulit dijangkau, Perseroan telah bekerja sama dengan 65 distributor lokal.

Perseroan juga menjual produk-produknya ke luar negeri seperti Australia, Kamboja, Nigeria, Arab Saudi, Korea Selatan dan Amerika Serikat melalui distributor setempat.

The Company has partnerships with many retailers, ranging from modern retailers such as hypermarkets, supermarkets and minimarkets, to traditional ones such as small independent retailers and grocery stores, wholesalers, and some domestic institutions. In distributing products to retailers, the Company has cooperated with several distributors. In Java, the Company directly sells its products to retailers using the distribution network of PT Nikos Distribution Indonesia (NDI) which is a 70% owned subsidiary of the Company.

For distribution outside the island of Java, including remote areas, the company has collaborated with 65 local distributors.

The company also exports its products to countries such as Australia, Cambodia, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, and the United States through local distributors.
