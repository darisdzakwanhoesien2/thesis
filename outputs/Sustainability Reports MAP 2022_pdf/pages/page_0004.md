## First Starbucks Signing Store in Indonesia

On December 3, 2022, the company opened the first Starbucks Signing Store in Indonesia. Two thirds of the signing store employees comes from hearing and speech impaired.

Perusahaan membuka Starbucks Signing Store pertama di Indonesia pada 3 Desember 2022. 2/3 dari karyawan signing store berasal dari komunitas tuna rungu dan tuna wicara.
