# Topik Material

We continue to adjust material topics from our previous reports. The determination of material topics considers the results of discussions with internal and external stakeholders. These discussions were facilitated by an independent party acting as an expert team. Following from this, we have determined nine material topics according to the sustainability context. [3-1][3-2]

Kami melakukan penyesuaian topik material dari pelaporan sebelumnya. Penetapan topik material mempertimbangkan telah diskusi dengan pemangku kepentingan internal maupun eksternal. Diskusi difasilitasi pihak independen yang bertindak sebagai tim ahli. Kami menetapkan sembilan topik material sesuai konteks keberlanjutan. [3-1][3-2]
