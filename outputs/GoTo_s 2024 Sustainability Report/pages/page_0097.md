|  In 2023, Gojek received letters from Indonesian Commission for the Supervision of Business Competition or Indonesia Competition Commission (ICC) regarding an information and data request in relation to the order distribution of GoTo's on-demand service (GoCar) connected to the company's collaboration with a taxi company (Bluebird). The latest information and data request occurred in July 2023. | During 2023, the ICC delivered request letters addressed to GoTo regarding the alleged violation of Law No. 5 Year 1999. The letters requested information from GoTo as part of this preliminary investigation process. | GoTo has always and continues to respond to the requests from the ICC. At the date of this sustainability report, GoTo has not received any updates from the ICC on whether the case will be continued to the hearing stage or dropped, as this is the discretion of the ICC.  |

Permintaan informasi dan data terakhir terjadi pada September 2024. | GoTo Financial menerima surat dari KPPU terkait potensi pelanggaran dalam layanan pinjam meminjam uang berbasis teknologi informasi (Pinjaman P2P). | GoTo selalu dan akan terus merespon terhadap permintaan dari KPPU.
