## Periode Pelaporan

Periode pelaporan GoTo dimulai dari Januari hingga Desember setiap tahun. Kecuali ada pernyataan sebaliknya, seluruh informasi yang disajikan di dalam laporan ini mencakup periode pelaporan dari Januari hingga Desember 2024. Kami menggunakan data historis untuk memberikan konteks dan dukungan tambahan terhadap pengungkapan dalam laporan ini. Laporan Keberlanjutan Perseroan diterbitkan sebelum tanggal 30 April setiap tahunnya, kecuali ditentukan lain.

## Standar Pelaporan

Setiap tahun, GoTo melaporkan kinerja keberlanjutannya dengan mengacu kepada standar pelaporan berikut:

- Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)
- Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB)
- Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Indonesia (POJK)
- International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) Standar Pengungkapan Keberlanjutan
- Taskforce on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD)

## Reporting Timeline

GoTo’s reporting period runs from January to December each year. Unless otherwise stated, all disclosures in this report refer to the period January to December 2024. Historical data may be used in order to provide additional context and support for this report’s disclosures. The Company’s Sustainability Report is published annually, typically prior to 30 April each year, unless specified otherwise.

## Reporting Standards

Every year, GoTo reports its sustainability performance based on the following reporting standards:

- Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)
- Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB)
- Indonesia’s Financial Services Authority Regulations (POJK)
- International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) Sustainability Disclosure Standards
- Taskforce on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD)
