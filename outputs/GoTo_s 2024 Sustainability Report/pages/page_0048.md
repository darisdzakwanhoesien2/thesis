In 2024, the GoTransit service has expanded to more than 100 stations across the Greater Jakarta and Yogyakarta-Solo areas. This network connects two major tourist hubs and facilitates the daily commutes of millions of GoTo users. With GoTransit, users can book a GoRide service to and from a station and purchase Commuter Line tickets in a single transaction. Over 877 million km have been travelled using GoTransit in 2024.

Throughout the reporting year, GoTransit was further enhanced through the integration of Gojek driver-partners shelters at KRL Commuter Line stations in the Greater Jakarta area. These facilities allow users to seamlessly transition their journey from the station to their final destination. Currently, 18 Gojek driver-partners shelters are located at major public transport hubs and the high-traffic stations across the Greater Jakarta area. GoTransit received an award for Online Application Supporting Public Transport Integration by the Jakarta Transportation Council in September 2024.
