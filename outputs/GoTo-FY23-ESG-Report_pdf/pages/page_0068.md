# Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB)

SASB Standards "guide the disclosure of financially material sustainability information by companies to their investors. Available for 77 industries, the Standards identify the subset of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) issues most relevant to financial performance in each industry." The table below contains the relevant sustainability disclosure topics and accounting metrics for GoTo's SASB category, Software &amp; IT Services.

|  Data Privacy & Freedom of Expression | Description of policies and practices relating to behavioral advertising and user privacy | TC-SI-220a.1 | Privacy & Data Protection  |
