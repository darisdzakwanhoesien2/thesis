# GoTo by the numbers.

All data as of December 31, 2023. This section is based on voluntary employee self-identification. Percentages may not add up to 100% due to rounding and employees that have declined to self-identify. It includes regular, full-time, and part-time employees. Leadership reflects Directors and above, including individual contributors and those with direct reports.

Reporting on gender and ethnicity data helps GoTo track progress on workforce diversity; however, we recognize that our current categorization of gender is binary and our ethnicity categories are those required by US government reporting. We understand that these are imperfect categorizations and not necessarily how employees define themselves.

* BIPOC as defined by the Federal EEOC Reporting Guidelines and is self-reported by employees in our internal Workday system.
