# GoTo ESG Strategy

In 2023, due to the growing importance of artificial intelligence, we added Responsible Use of AI to the ESG priority areas identified in a 2021 materiality assessment* conducted by Sustainability Roundtable, Inc., a strategic advisory and support service for ESG program assistance. This assessment guides GoTo's ESG strategy, implementation, and management, ensuring that we continue to focus on the risks and opportunities most relevant to our core business—and most important to our stakeholders.

## Top 11 ESG areas most material to stakeholders:**

### Environmental

- Energy &amp; Carbon Emissions

### Social

- Talent &amp; Culture
- Employee Health &amp; Safety
- Diversity, Equity &amp; Inclusion
- Philanthropy &amp; Volunteering
- Human Rights &amp; Labor
- Customer Experience

### Governance

- Privacy &amp; Data Protection
- Governance &amp; Ethics
- Responsible Use of AI
- ESG Business Alignment

* While we want to make sure we have a core strategy, we recognize that our business and stakeholder expectations are continuously evolving when it comes to ESG. We will continue to monitor and check our assumptions about GoTo's priorities, which could result in re-prioritization in future years.

** Material areas are listed in order of appearance within the report. ESG Business Alignment is not represented in a specific section in the report; however, it is reflected in the overall integration of ESG into our business.
