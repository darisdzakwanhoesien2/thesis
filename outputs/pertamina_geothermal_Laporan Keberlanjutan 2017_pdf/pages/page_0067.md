# KODE ETIK DAN BUDAYA PERUSAHAAN
## COMPANY'S CODE OF CONDUCT

Perusahaan telah menyusun Pedoman Etika Usaha dan Tata Perilaku atau Code of Conduct (CoC) sejak tahun 2008, dan telah beberapa kali mengalami perubahan dan pemutakhiran terakhir pada tahun 2016. Pedoman CoC Perusahaan telah disahkan dan ditandatangani oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi melalui Surat Keputusan Nomor 259/DK-PGE/XII/2016 dan Nomor Kpts-213/PGE000/2016-S0 tanggal 23 Desember 2016. Sosialisasi mengenai CoC dilakukan melalui tatap muka langsung, dan media online seperti broadcast email, portal Perusahaan, dan website. [GRI 102-16, GRI 103-2]

The Company has compiled the Code of Conduct since 2008 and had several changes and updates in 2016. The Company's Code of Conduct is ratified and signed by the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors through Decree No. 259/DK-PGE/XII/2016 and No. KPTS-213/PGE000/2016-S0 dated December 23, 2016. CoC Socialization is conducted through direct face to face, and online media such as broadcast email, Corporate portal, and website. [GRI 102-16, GRI 103-2]

## Standar Etika dan Tata Perilaku Perusahaan
## Standards of Ethics and Code of Conduct

### Etika Usaha Perusahaan
## Business Ethics

### MERUPAKAN STANDAR ETIKA YANG MENGATUR INTERAKSI DAN HUBUNGAN DENGAN STAKEHOLDERS SERTA MENGATUR STANDAR ETIKA TERKAIT ASPEK KHUSUS
- Hubungan dengan pemegang saham
- Hubungan dengan pekerja
- Hubungan dengan pelanggan
- Hubungan dengan pemerintah
- Hubungan dengan masyarakat
- Hubungan dengan mitra usaha
- Hubungan dengan pemasok
- Aspek khusus meliputi: kebijakan akuntansi dan keuangan, pemberian donasi, dan transaksi afiliasi

### IS AN ETHICAL STANDARD THAT REGULATES INTERACTIONS AND RELATIONSHIPS WITH STAKEHOLDERS AND SET ETHICAL STANDARDS RELATED TO SPECIFIC ASPECTS
- Relation with shareholders
- Relation with employees
- Relation with customers
- Relation with government
- Relation with the community
- Relation with partners
- Relation with suppliers
- Special aspects cover: accounting and finance policies, donation, and affiliations' transaction

### Standar Tata Perilaku
## Standards of Conduct

### STANDAR PERILAKU INSAN PERUSAHAAN DALAM MELAKSANAKAN TUGAS DAN TANGGUNG JAWAB PEKERJAANNYA MAUPUN DALAM BERINTERAKSI
- Hubungan kerja antar pekerja
- Hubungan atasan dengan bawahan
- Kesempatan kerja yang adil
- Perilaku etis terhadap sesama pekerja
- Pengaturan benturan kepentingan
- Pengaturan anti korupsi, gratifikasi, penyuapan, Pengaturan aktivitas sosial dan politik

### THE STANDARD OF THE COMPANY'S PERSONNEL IN CARRYING OUT THEIR DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES IN THEIR WORK AND INTERACTION
- Work relation between employees
- Superior and subordinate relationship
- Fair employment
- Ethical behavior toward fellow employees
- Conflict of Interest regulation
- Anti-Corruption, gratification, bribery, social and politic activities regulations.
