# PROGRAM PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI PEKERJA EMPLOYEES' COMPETENCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM

Untuk menciptakan World Class Geothermal People, Perusahaan senantiasa melakukan pengembangan kompetensi pekerja melalui berbagai program pelatihan dan pengembangan dalam rangka meningkatkan kemampuan, pengetahuan, keahlian teknis, operasional, manajemen, soft skills, dan kepemimpinan. Kebijakan terkait pelatihan telah tercantum dalam Tata Kerja Organisasi (TKO) Human Capital No.B-006/PGE900/2015-58. Program pengembangan pegawai dari tingkat manajemen, senior manajer, manajer, supervisor, sampai dengan operator di lapangan yang dilakukan selama tahun 2017 terdiri dari dua kategori, yaitu Leadership/Managerial dan Technical/Functional. Program pelatihan kategori Leadership/Managerial di antaranya yaitu Expand Leadership, Community Development, Effective Working Attitude, Junior Management Development Program (JMDP), Senior Management Development Program (SMDP), Professional Directorship Program (PDP). Sedangkan, program pelatihan pada kategori Technical/Functional diantaranya yaitu Project Management Professional, Well Integrity Management, Monitoring Geokimia Produksi and Manifestasi, Pipe Stress Analysis, Legal School, Risk based Inspection, dan Lainnya. [GRI 103-2, GRI 404-2]

Pada tahun 2017, Perusahaan mengeluarkan Rp8.415,91 juta untuk pelatihan sebanyak 464 pekerja dengan jumlah jam pelatihan mencapai 35.167 jam. Sehingga rata-rata jam pelatihan per peserta mencapai 75,79 jam. [GRI 404-1]

To create World Class Geothermal People, the Company continuously develops the employees' competence through various training and development programs in order to improve skills, knowledge, technical skills, operations, management, soft skills, and leadership. The training-related policies are regulated in the Human Capital Organization Structure (TKO) Human Capital No.B-006/PGE900/2015-58. Employee development programs for management level, senior managers, managers, supervisors, and field operators in 2017 is categorized into two different categories, i.e. Leadership/Managerial and Technical/Functional. The training in the Leadership/Managerial category, among others, are Expand Leadership, Community Development, Effective Working Attitude, Junior Management Development Program (JMDP), Senior Management Development Program (SMDP), Professional Directorship Program (PDP). The training in the Technical/Functional category, among others, are Project Management Professional, Well Integrity Management, Monitoring Geokimia Production and Manifestation, Pipe Stress Analysis, Legal School, Risk based Inspection, and others. [GRI 103-2, GRI 404-2]

In 2017, the Company spent Rp8.415,91 million in training program for 464 workers with approximately 35.167 training hours. Therefore, the average hours of training per participant are 75,79 hours. [GRI 404-1]
