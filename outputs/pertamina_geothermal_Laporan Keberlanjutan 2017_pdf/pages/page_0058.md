# PROGRAM PELATIHAN DAN PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI DEWAN KOMISARIS

Program peningkatan kapabilitas merupakan salah satu program penting bagi Dewan komisaris. Program ini untuk mengakomodasi pengembangan pengetahuan profesional, kompetensi, dan kemampuan kepemimpinan serta pengetahuan-pengetahuan lain terkait tugas Dewan Komisaris. Untuk itu, Perusahaan secara teratur mengadakan program peningkatan kapabilitas secara berkelanjutan bagi Dewan Komisaris seperti, pelatihan eksekutif, program pendidikan, seminar, dan juga konferensi. # TRAINING AND COMPETENCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

Capability improvement program is one of the important programs for the Board of Commissioners. These programs accommodate the development of professional knowledge, competence, and leadership capability, and other knowledge related to the duties of Board of Commissioners. Therefore, the Company regularly holds capability development program in a sustainable manner for Board of Commissioners, such as executive training, education program, seminar, and conference.
