# DIREKSI

Direksi merupakan tokoh sentral dan eksklusif dalam organ tata kelola Perusahaan. Oleh dari itu, pengangkatan anggota Direksi harus dilakukan melalui berbagai mekanisme dan pertimbangan. Calon anggota Direksi harus melalui tahapan uji kemampuan dan kepatutan (fit and proper test) dan harus dinyatakan lulus serta memperoleh persetujuan dari pemegang saham. Selain itu, pengangkatan anggota Direksi dilakukan atas dasar pertimbangan keahlian, integritas, kepemimpinan, pengalaman, jujur, dan berperilaku baik serta berdedikasi tinggi untuk memajukan dan mengembangkan Perusahaan serta persyaratan lain yang diatur dalam Undang-Undang.

# BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Board of Directors is a central and exclusive organ in the Company's governance. Therefore, the appointment of Directors must be conducted through various mechanism and considerations. The candidates of Board of Directors must pass the fit and proper test, and must be declared passed and obtain approval from the shareholders. In addition, the appointment of members of the Board of Directors shall be conducted based on consideration of expertise, integrity, leadership, experience, honesty, good manners, and high dedication to promote and develop the Company and other requirements stipulated in the Law.
