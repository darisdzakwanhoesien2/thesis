# WHISTLE BLOWING SYSTEM (WBS)

Kebijakan Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran atau Whistle Blowing System (WBS) dituangkan dalam Tata Kerja Organisasi Nomor B-001/PGE500/2016-50 yang telah disahkan pada tanggal 28 Juli 2016. Kebijakan tersebut diinternalisasikan pada setiap level jabatan yang ada di Perusahaan melalui sosialisasi intensif dan menyeluruh. Kebijakan tersebut diharapkan mampu menekan kemungkinan risiko yang muncul akibat pelanggaran di internal Perusahaan. Kebijakan Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran Perusahaan mengacu kepada beberapa kebijakan diantaranya: Undang-Undang No. 31 tahun 1999 dan telah diperbaharui dengan Undang Undang No. 20 Tahun 2001 tentang Pemberantasan Tindak Pidana Korupsi; Pedoman Sistem Pelaporan dan Pelanggaran - SPP (Whistle Blowing System), Komite Nasional Kebijakan GCG, 2008; Pedoman Pengelolaan Whistleblowing System PT Pertamina (Persero) No. B-001 /N00010/2011-50 Revisi ke-2; Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan (Code of Corporate Governance) PT PGE; Pedoman Etika Usaha &amp; Tata Perilaku (Code of Conduct) PT PGE; Pedoman Konflik Kepentingan (Conflict of Interest) PT PGE No. A-002/PGE400/2010-50 dan Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) PT PGE. [GRI 103-2]

Whistleblowing System (WBS) is stipulated in the Organizational Work Procedures No. B-001/PGE500/2016-50, ratified by July 28, 2016. The policy is internalized on every level of the Company through intensive and comprehensive socialization. The policy is expected to be able to reduce risk that may arise due to violations of the internal Company. Whistleblowing System Policy refers to several policies, among others: Law No. 31 of 1999, and updated by Law No. 20 of 2001, on the Eradication of Corruption; Whistleblowing System (WBS) of National Committee of GCG Policies, 2008; Whistleblowing System Management Guidelines of PT Pertamina (Persero) No. B-001/N00010/2011-50, 2nd Revision; Code of Corporate Governance of PT PGE; Code of Conduct of PT PGE; Conflict of Interest Guidelines of PT PGE No. A-002/PGE400/2010-50 and Collective Labor Agreement (CLA) of PT PGE. [GRI 103-2]
