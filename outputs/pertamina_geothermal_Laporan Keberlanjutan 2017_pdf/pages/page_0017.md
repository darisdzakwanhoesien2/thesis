Sejak awal berdiri, kami telah aktif menjalankan program keberlanjutan untuk memenuhi kebutuhan dan harapan para pemangku kepentingan. Since the establishment, we have been running sustainability programs to meet the needs and expectations of our stakeholders. We strive to focus on contributing to the development and empowerment of local communities, investing in communities, conserving the environment and biodiversity, improving the internal operations performance and adopting various best practices policies and standards, both domestic and abroad. In 2017, the SDGs implementation increasingly appears to be the discourse and practice of sustainability for the Company, which is realized through the implementation and realization of sustainability programs. These programs are aligned to support the Government in achieving SDGs.

# PENCAPAIAN ATAS KINERJA EKONOMI ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE ACHIEVEMENT

## Para Pemangku Kepentingan yang Terhormat,

Pada tahun 2017, Perusahaan semakin menegaskan komitmennya dalam pengembangan sumber energi panas bumi. Sejalan dengan program Pemerintah, dalam rangka percepatan pembangkitan listrik sebesar 35.000 MW di Indonesia. Perusahaan telah memastikan proses pengoperasian PLTP Ulubelu Unit IV berjalan dengan baik bahkan lebih cepat dari target. PLTP Ulubelu Unit IV telah menyumbang 55 MW atau setara 198.000 Gigajoule. Dengan tambahan kapasitas daya tersebut menjadikan porsi kontribusi panas bumi bagi ketenagalistrikan di Lampung meningkat menjadi 25% terhadap total kebutuhan listrik di Provinsi paling selatan Pulau Sumatera tersebut. Sehingga, total kapasitas terpasang Area Ulubelu mencapai 220 MW.

## Dear Distinguished Stakeholders,

In 2017, the Company affirms its commitment to the development of geothermal energy resources. This is in line with the Government's program to accelerate the electricity generation of 35.000 MW in Indonesia. The Company ensures the process to operate GPP Ulubelu Unit IV runs well, and faster than the target. GPP Ulubelu Unit IV has contributed to 55 MW or equivalent to 198.000 Gigajoule. With the additional power capacity, the geothermal electricity contribution for electricity in Lampung increased to 25% of the total electricity demand in the southernmost province of Sumatra. Thus, the Ulubelu Area has a total installed capacity of 220 MW.
