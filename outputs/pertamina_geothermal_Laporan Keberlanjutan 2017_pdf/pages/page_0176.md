# PROSEDUR PENGADUAN KELUHAN MASYARAKAT TERKAIT PROGRAM TJSL

# GRIEVANCE COMPLAINTS PROCEDURE RELATED TO CSR PROGRAM

Perusahaan meyakini bahwa membangun hubungan yang harmonis harus didasarkan pada komunikasi dua arah dan transparan. Untuk itu Perusahaan telah menyediakan saluran komunikasi yang memiliki prosedur pengelola keluhan yang konsisten dan terkoordinir. Saluran komunikasi tersebut dibuat untuk mengumpulkan, mencatat, dan menangani komentar, keluhan, dan klaim masyarakat yang terkena dampak akibat kegiatan operasional Perusahaan.

The Company believes that building a harmonious relationship must be based on two-ways and transparent communication. Therefore, the Company has provided a communication channel that has consistent and coordinated complaints management procedures. The communication channel is created to collect, record, and handle comments, complaints, and claims of affected people as a result of the Company's operational activities.

Selama tahun 2017, Perusahaan menerima dua pengaduan di Area Lahendong dari masyarakat lokal terkait masalah kerusakan lingkungan akibat kegiatan operasional. Seluruh pengaduan tersebut telah selesai ditindaklanjuti, sehingga tidak mengganggu kegiatan operasional Perusahaan serta tidak ada denda dan sanksi non moneter yang diterima Perusahaan atas ketidakpatuhan terhadap peraturan terkait masyarakat. Selain di Area Lahendong, Perusahaan tidak menerima pengaduan masyarakat lokal terkait aktivitas operasional Perusahaan. [GRI 419-1]

In 2017, the Company received two complaints in Lahendong Area from local communities regarding environmental damage caused by operational activities. All complaints have been settled, so as not to interfere with the Company's operational activities. In addition to Lahendong Area, the Company did not receive local complaints related to the Company's operational activities. [GRI 419-1]
