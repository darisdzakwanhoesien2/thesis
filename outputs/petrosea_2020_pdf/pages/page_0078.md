# Ratio of Basic Salary &amp; Remuneration of Female Towards Male [GRI 405-2]

Petrosea is committed to always providing equal opportunities for female and male employees. Therefore, the determination of remuneration in the Company is carried out based on job title, length of service, workload, work location and performance.
