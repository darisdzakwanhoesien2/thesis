Management Approach

|  Pembangunan menara pada daerah tertentu membutuhkan dukungan infrastruktur, yang dapat dimanfaatkan warga setempat. Construction of towers in certain areas requires infrastructure support, the local residents could take advantage from this. | Realisasi biaya pembangunan infrastruktur pada lokasi menara yang dibangun tahun 2020 mencapai Rp852,84 juta. The realization of infrastructure development costs of tower sites built in 2020 reached IDR852.84 million. | SMN berinisiatif menggunakan karyawan lokal untuk pembangunan infrastruktur pendukung menara. SMN took the initiative to use local workers for the construction of tower supporting infrastructure. | Pelaksanaan pembangunan menara dan infrastruktur pendukung menjadi tanggung jawab Build to Suit. Construction of towers and supporting infrastructure is the Build to Suit responsibility. | Secara berkala kegiatan pembangunan menara dan infrastruktur pendukung dilaporkan kepada Direksi, untuk evaluasi. Activities with regard to in the construction of towers and supporting infrastructure are periodically reported to the Board of Directors for evaluation.  |
