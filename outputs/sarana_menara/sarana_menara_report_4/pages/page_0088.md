## Keanekaragaman Hayati (GEOJK.F.R.F.10)

Perusahaan tidak memiliki kantor operasional, baik kantor pusat maupun kantor cabang yang berdekatan dengan kawasan dilindungi dan/atau konservasi keanekaragaman hayati.

Pembangunan dan penyediaan menara dilakukan berdasarkan metode "Built to Suit" yang berasal dari permintaan dan kebutuhan konsumen serta memperhatikan dampak yang ditimbulkan dari pembangunan menara tersebut terhadap fungsi kawasan, termasuk kawasan lindung. Penentuan seluruh lokasi menara telekomunikasi Grup telah memenuhi persyaratan dalam Surat Edaran Dirjen Penataan Ruang Kementerian Pekerjaan Umum No. 06/ SE/ Dr/2011 tentang Petunjuk Teknis Kriteria Lokasi Menara Telekomunikasi.

Hingga akhir periode Laporan ini, lokasi menara Grup terletak di wilayah dengan status zona enclave yang secara faktual merupakan daerah hunian dan dikuasai oleh warga setempat. ## Biodiversity (GEOJK.F.R.F.10)

The Company does not have operational offices, including head office or branch offices, located near protected areas and/or biodiversity conservation areas.

The construction and provision of towers are carried out based on the "Built to Suit" method, originating from consumer demand and needs, while considering the impact of tower construction on the area's function, including protected areas. The determination of all locations for the Group's telecommunications towers has met the requirements in the Circular from the Director General of Spatial Planning, Ministry of Public Works No. 06/SE/Dr/2011 regarding Technical Guidelines for Telecommunications Tower Location Criteria.

As of the end of this reporting period, the Group's tower locations are situated in enclave zone areas, which are factually residential areas controlled by local residents. As such, the Group's towers construction does not have a direct significant impact on the biodiversity in the surrounding areas.

Nevertheless, the Group fully acknowledges that biodiversity plays a vital role in providing water, clean air, as well as food security, human health, and well-being. In line with this, throughout 2023, the Group continued its environmental preservation efforts through social and environmental responsibility programs ("CSR"), including: [GRI-413-1]

a) Contributions to the procurement and planting of mangrove seedlings in several locations: (i) Komodo National Park - NTT; (ii) Kulon Progo - Yogyakarta; and (iii) Lantebung - Makassar, with an estimated total mangrove land planted by December 2023 covering an area of 10 hectares; and

b) Memberikan dukungan terhadap gerakan konservasi alam di Hutan Lindung Sungai Wain, Balikpapan, Kalimantan melalui Yayasan Pro Natura Sejak tahun 2023, Grup telah berencana untuk memberikan dukungan terhadap proyek konservasi macan tutul Jawa (Panthera pardus melas) yang dijalankan oleh Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan bekerja sama dengan Yayasan SINTAS Indonesia, melalui kegiatan survei dan pengumpulan sampel kotoran macan tutul Jawa untuk mengetahui struktur populasi macan tutul Jawa dan preferensi satwa mangsanya.

Macan tutul Jawa merupakan salah satu subspecies harimau yang endemik di Pulau Jawa yang populasinya terancam punah akibat hilangnya habitat alaminya dan perburuan legal. Macan tutul juga merupakan spesies yang termasuk dalam Trophic Cascade, artinya keberadaan macan tutul akan berdampak luas kepada ekosistem. Dalam jangka panjang, macan tutul memegang peranan penting dalam regenerasi vegetasi hutan, karena mampu membatasi herbivora dalam mengkonsumsi tumbuhan secara berlebihan serta memiliki jelajah yang lebih luas daripada primata dan herbivora penyebar biji, sehingga membantu dalam regenerasi hutan yang lebih efektif.

Upaya konservasi macan tutul Jawa ini secara keseluruhan mencakup beberapa tahapan besar, diantaranya kajian data/survei dasar status populasi dan ancaman. Data dasar tersebut nantinya akan digunakan di dalam kajian kelintasan populasi (Population Viability Analysis) macan tutul Jawa dalam rangka pembaharaan dokumen Strategi dan Rencana Aksi Konservasi Macan Tutul Jawa. Selain macan tutul Jawa sebagai target utama, melalui proyek ini juga akan diperoleh data biodiversitas terestrial lain dan sebarannya di seluruh habitat satwa liar yang bersisa di Pulau Jawa.

Dukungan ini juga merupakan bentuk komitmen nyata yang dilakukan Grup untuk berkontribusi terhadap Tujuan pembangunan Berkelanjutan yang juga menjadi fokus utama Perusahaan.

Uraian lebih lengkap terkait kegiatan konservasi macan tutul Jawa ini akan diungkapkan pada Laporan Keberlanjutan Perusahaan tahun buku 2024.

b) Supporting conservation movements in the Protected Forest of Wain River, Balikpapan, Kalimantan through the Pro Natura Foundation.

In 2023, the Group has planned its support for the Javan leopard (Panthera pardus melas) conservation project carried out by the Ministry of Environment and Forestry in collaboration with the SINTAS Indonesia Foundation, through survey activities and collecting faecal samples of the Javan leopard to determine the population structure of the Javan leopard and the preference of its prey animals.

The Javan leopard is one of the tiger subspecies endemics to Java Island, whose population is threatened with extinction due to the loss of its natural habitat and illegal hunting. The leopard is also a species included in the Trophic Cascade, meaning the existence of the leopard has a wide impact on the ecosystem. In the long term, the leopard plays an important role in the regeneration of forest vegetation because it can limit herbivores from excessively consuming plants and has a wider range than primates and herbivorous seed dispersers, hence assisting in more effective forest regeneration.

The overall Javan leopard conservation effort includes several major stages, among them are the basic data/survey study on the population status and threats. This basic data will later be used in the Population Viability Analysis of the Javan leopard in order to update the document on the Strategy and Action Plan for the Conservation of the Javan Leopard. In addition to the Javan leopard as the main target, this project will also obtain data on other terrestrial biodiversity and its distribution across all remaining wildlife habitats on Java Island.

This support also represents a concrete commitment by the Group to contribute to the Sustainable Development Goals, which are a primary focus of the Company.

A more detailed description of the Javan leopard conservation activities will be disclosed in the Company's 2024 Sustainability Report.
