Kohinoor adalah suatu perusahaan terbatas didirikan di Indonesia

hadapan Luh Made Yogi Mawarwati, SH., Notaris di Denpasar. Akta
Pendirian Kohinoor disahkan oleh Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia
dalam Surat Keputusan No. AHU-31535.AH.01.01.Tahun 2011 tanggal
23 Juni 2011, Anggaran Dasar Kohinoor sebagaimana dimuat dalam akta
pendirian tersebut di atas sejak telah mengalami beberapa kali perubahan,
terakhir dengan Akta Pernyataan Sirkuler Para Pemegang Saham No.
03, tanggal 16 Mei 2023, dibuat di hadapan Caesaria Dhamayanti, S.H.,
M.Kn., Notaris di Kabupaten Tangerang, mengenai perubahan maksud
dan tujuan Kohinoor. Perubahan tersebut telah mendapat persetujuan
dari Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia dengan Surat Keputusan No.
AHU-0029190.AH.01.02.TAHUN 2023 tanggal 26 Mei 2023.  |

On December 19, 2019, Protelindo concluded the acquisition of Kohinoor<br/>through subscription of newly issued shares, making it a subsidiary of<br/>Protelindo.

Kohinoor is a limited liability company established in Indonesia based<br/>on Deed of Establishment No. 13, dated March 7, 2011 drawn up in<br/>the presence of Luh Made Yogi Mawarwati, SH., Notary in Denpasar.<br/>Kohinoor’s Deed of Establishment was ratified by the Minister of Law and<br/>Human Rights through Letter Number No. AHU-31535, AH.01.01.Tahun<br/>2011 dated June 23, 2011. Kohinoor’s Articles of Association as contained<br/>in the above-mentioned deed of incorporation have been amended<br/>several times, the latest amendment of which was stated in the Deed<br/>of Statement of Shareholders’ Resolution No. 03, dated May 16, 2023,<br/>drawn up in the presence of Caesaria Dhamayanti, S.H., M.Kn., Notary in<br/>Tangerang Regency, regarding the amendment of Kohinoor’s purpose<br/>and objectives. This amendment has been approved by the Minister<br/>of Law and Human Rights through Letter No. AHU-0029190.AH.01.02.<br/>TAHUN 2023 dated May 26, 2023.
