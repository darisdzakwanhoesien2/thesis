# INFORMASI ENTITAS ANAK
INFORMATION ON SUBSIDIARIES
[GRI 2–2]

IEN is a limited liability company established in Indonesia based on Deed of Establishment No. 04 dated May 17, 2023 drawn up in the presence of Caesaria Dhamayanti, S.H., M.Kn., Notary in Tangerang Regency. HEN's Deed of Establishment was ratified by the Minister of Law and Human Rights through Letter No. AHU-0040867.AH.01.01.TAHUN 2023 dated June 7, 2023. HEN's Articles of Association as contained in the above mentioned deed of incorporation have been amended several times, the latest amendment of which was stated in the Deed of Statement of Shareholders' Resolution No. 307, dated June 26, 2023, drawn up in the presence of Christina Dwi Utami, S.H., M.Hum., M.Kn., Notary in West Jakarta, regarding the amendment of HEN's capital structure. This amendment has been approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights through Letter No. AHU-0036266.AH.01.02.TAHUN 2023 dated June 26, 2023.
