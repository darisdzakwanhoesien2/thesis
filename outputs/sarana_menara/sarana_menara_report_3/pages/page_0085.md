## Emisi

Sebagaimana digambarkan di atas, emisi yang, dihasikan oleh Grup (cakupan (Scope) 1 dan 2) berasal dari emisi penggunaan diesel generator, mobil operasional Grup, dan penggunaan energi listrik di kantor dan lokasi jaringan POP. Sebagian besar kegiatan operasional Grup (dari hulu sampai dengan hilir) dikerjakan dan dijalankan oleh pihak ketiga yang ditunjuk oleh Grup, termasuk antara lain proses distribusi bahan material ke sites, pembangunan menara dan installment fiber optik, serta proses maintenance peralatan, perlengkapan atau aset Grup lainnya yang digunakan, baik dalam bisnis tower maupun non tower (Scope 3).

Pada tahun 2023, Perusahaan melakukan perhitungan emisi karbon yang berasal dari kegiatan operasional bisnis Grup, yakni meliputi cakupan sebagai berikut:

Scope 1: Perusahaan menghitung emisi penggunaan bahan bakar yang berasal dari diesel generator yang berada di sites menara Grup dan penggunaan mobil operasional Grup.

## Emission

As outlined above, the Group's emissions (Scope 1 and 2) are generated from the use of diesel generators, operational cars, and the utilization of electrical energy in offices and POP network locations. Most of Group's operational business activities (upstream to downstream) are largely carried out and executed by third parties appointed by the Group, including the distribution of materials to sites, construction of towers and optic fiber installation, as well as maintenance process of equipment, accessories, or other Group assets used, both within tower and non-tower businesses (Scope 3).

In 2023, the Company calculated the carbon emissions from the Group's operational activities, which includes the following scopes:

Scope 1: The Company calculates emissions of fuel consumption from diesel generators at the Group's tower sites and operational vehicles.
