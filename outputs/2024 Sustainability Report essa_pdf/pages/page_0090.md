|   |  Competency Transfer for Community Empowerment on Non-Hazardous Waste (Non-B3) Reduction and Utilization in Tangkiang Village | Education and assistance for the community in managing non-hazardous waste to reduce environmental pollution and improve sanitation. | Increased public awareness of waste segregation and non- hazardous waste recycling.  |
