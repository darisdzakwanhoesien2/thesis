|  • Jenis Sumber Air: PAU menggunakan air laut sebagai sumber utama air bersih.
*Type of Water Source:* PAU uses seawater as the main source of clean water.
• Proses Pengambilan Air:
*Water Intake Process:*
- Air laut diambil melalui Sea Water Intake untuk diolah lebih lanjut.
Seawater is taken through Sea Water Intake for further processing.
- Air ini kemudian diolah menjadi air bersih yang digunakan untuk berbagai keperluan di fasilitas produksi, kantor, dan area camp.
*This water is then processed into clean water, which is used for various needs in production facilities, offices, and the camp area.*
- Untuk kebutuhan pendinginan proses produksi, air laut murni langsung digunakan tanpa melalui proses pemurnian lebih lanjut.
*For cooling process requirements, pure seawater is used directly without further purification.* | • Air untuk Produksi dan Kebutuhan Internal:
*Water for Production and Internal Needs:*
- Air laut diolah menggunakan Sea Water Reverse Osmosis (SWRO) untuk menghasilkan Desalinated Water.
Seawater is processed using Sea Water Reverse Osmosis (SWRO) to produce Desalinated Water.
- Desalinated Water kemudian diproses lebih lanjut di unit Demineralization untuk menghasilkan Demineralized Water, yang digunakan untuk:
*Desalinated Water is then further processed in the Demineralization unit to produce Demineralized Water, which is used for:*
- Air proses produksi
Production process water
- Air bersih di perkantoran
Clean water in office areas
- Air bersih di camp pekerja
Clean water in worker camps
• Kebutuhan Air Pendingin:
*Cooling Water Requirements:*
- Air laut yang digunakan untuk pendinginan tidak mengalami proses pemurnian dan langsung dialirkan ke sistem pendinginan sebelum akhirnya dibuang kembali ke laut.
*Water used for cooling processes does not undergo purification and is directly circulated in the cooling system before being discharged back into the sea.* | • Air Limbah dari Proses Pendukung (Utility):
*Wastewater from Utility Processes:*
- Air limbah dari unit utility melewati pretreatment terlebih dahulu, sebelum akhirnya diproses di Final Check Basin untuk memastikan kepatuhan terhadap standar lingkungan.
*Wastewater from utility processes undergoes pretreatment first before being processed in the Final Check Basin to ensure compliance with environmental standards.*
- Setelah final treatment, air limbah ini dibuang ke badan air di Sungai Musolang.
*After final treatment, the wastewater is discharged into the Musolang River.*
• Air Limbah Domestik:
*Domestic Wastewater:*
- Limbah dari kegiatan domestik seperti sanitasi dan perkantoran diolah di Sewage Treatment Plant (STP).
*Wastewater from domestic activities, such as sanitation and office use, is treated in the Sewage Treatment Plant (STP).*
- Air hasil olahan STP kemudian dibuang ke Sungai Musolang.
*The treated water from the STP is then discharged into the Musolang River.*
• Air Pendingin:
*Cooling Water:*
- Air laut yang digunakan dalam sistem pendinginan tidak mengalami pencemaran bahan kimia atau kontaminasi lain.
*Seawater used in the cooling system does not undergo contamination with hazardous materials or other pollutants.*
- Setelah digunakan, air ini dikembalikan ke laut melalui Sea Water Outlet Pit dan Sea Water Outfall.
*After use, the water is returned to the sea through the Sea Water Outlet Pit and Sea Water Outlet.*  |
