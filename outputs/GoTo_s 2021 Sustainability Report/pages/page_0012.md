Through our technology, we connect consumers to driver-partners and merchants, empowering these groups to provide benefits for each other in a complementary and extensive way.

Our ecosystem enhances the way consumers access goods and services, enables our merchants – many of whom are small and medium sized entrepreneurs – to start and grow their business, and provides income-earning opportunities for driver-partners. Together with our driver-partners and merchants, we offer an unparalleled selection of goods and services that improve the daily lives of millions of consumers in Indonesia, Singapore, and Vietnam.
