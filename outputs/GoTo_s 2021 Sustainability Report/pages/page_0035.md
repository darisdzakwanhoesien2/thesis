# Accelerating the Adoption of Electric Vehicles

A key part of our emissions reduction strategy is to shift all vehicles in our ecosystem to electric vehicles (EVs) by 2030, as most of our emissions are from the vehicles used to move people and goods across our ecosystem. Starting in Indonesia, a country with one of the highest motorbike usage in the world, we begin this journey with a focus on two-wheel EVs.
