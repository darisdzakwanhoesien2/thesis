Excluding Russia, Belarus and Ukraine, sales in eastern Europe had an increase for the full-year 2023 of 15 percent in SEK and 5 percent in local currencies. Sales were temporarily resumed in Russia from August until November 2022. Development in eastern Europe should be seen in light of the fact that H&amp;M Group's operations in Russia and Belarus have been fully wound down since 30 November 2022. Operations in Ukraine were paused from the end of February 2022 and 7 stores in the country were reopened during November 2023.
