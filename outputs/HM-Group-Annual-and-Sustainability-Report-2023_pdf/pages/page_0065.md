31. CONTINGENT LIABILITIES

When reporting contingent liabilities, IAS 37 Provision, contingent liabilities and contingent assets is applied.

A contingent liability is reported where there is a possible obligation for which it remains to be confirmed whether the company has an existing obligation that could result in an outflow of resources. Alternatively, there may be an existing obligation that does not fulfil the criteria for reporting in the balance sheet as a provision or other liability since it is not likely that an outflow of financial resources will be required in order to settle the obligation or the amount cannot be reliably estimated.

The group is involved in various types of disputes, but it is assessed that no current disputes will have any significant impact on the group's results. For further information concerning tax disputes see note 12.

Neither the group nor the parent company has any pledged assets.

33. EVENTS AFTER THE CLOSING DATE

On 31 January 2024, Helena Helmersson chose to leave the CEO role and the H&amp;M group, and the board of directors appointed Daniel Ervin, responsible for the brand H&amp;M, as new President and CEO of the H&amp;M group with immediate effect.

On 25 February 2024 the board of directors of Re:NewCall AB, one of the H&amp;M group's investments made via New Growth &amp; Ventures, announced their decision to file for bankruptcy. Re:NewCall AB was declared bankrupt by the Stockholm District Court on 26 February 2024. The H&amp;M group's shareholding as at 30 November 2023 amounted to SEK 34 m and is included in the balance sheet item Other shares and interests. In December 2023 the H&amp;M group issued a loan of SEK 45 m to Re:NewCall AB.

On 6 March 2024 it was announced that the H&amp;M group and Vargas Holding launch Syra, a new venture to scale textile to textile recycled polyester. The H&amp;M group has secured an offtake agreement with Syra worth a total of USD 600 million over seven years, covering a significant share of the H&amp;M group's long-term need for recycled polyester, which is currently primarily sourced from rPET bottle-to-textile.
