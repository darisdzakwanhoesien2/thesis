# Metode Pelibatan dan Frekuensi

Method of Engagement and Frequency

- Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan (RUPST) setiap 1 tahun sekali
- Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa (RUPSLB), diadakan sesuai kebutuhan Perseroan
- Rapat Kerja Dewan Komisaris, minimal 6 kali setahun
- Rapat Kerja Dewan Direksi, minimal 12 kali setahun
- Rapat Gabungan Direksi dan Komisaris, minimal 3 kali setahun
- Transparansi Laporan Keuangan, Laporan Tahunan, dan Laporan Keberlanjutan
- Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGMS) once a year
- Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (EGMS), as needed
- Board of Commissioners' meetings at least 6 times a year
- Board of Directors' meetings at least 12 times a year
- Joint meetings of the Board of Directors and Commissioners, at least 3 times a year
- Transparency of Financial Report, Annual Report, and Sustainability Report

# Respons dan Tindak Lanjut

Response and Follow Up

- Menerbitkan Laporan Tahunan dan Laporan Keberlanjutan setiap tahunnya tepat waktu
- Menyampaikan laporan kinerja keuangan dalam Laporan Tahunan kepada publik
- Memberitahukan update terbaru kepada publik dan media
- Publishing Annual Report and Sustainability Report annually on time
- Submitting financial performance reports in Annual Reports to the public
- Communicating latest updates to the public and media
