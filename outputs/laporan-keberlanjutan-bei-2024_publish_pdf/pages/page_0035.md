|  Information Security Management System (ISMS) Guidelines | 1. Developing information security policy that includes ISMS implementation objectives, scope, responsibilities, and procedures regulating activities related to information security.
2. Identifying information assets in the form of hardware, software, employees, and services.
3. Carrying out risk analysis to identify threats and evaluating the impact in the event of risks on information assets. Risk management from impact evaluation is targeted according to the level of risk accepted by the Company.
4. Implementing technical controls such as encryption, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and anti-virus to protect information.
5. Implementing physical access controls on work locations and information assets.
6. Developing guidelines that are derived into procedures and policies regulating the protection, use, and access to information.
7. Carrying out information security training and raising employee awareness of security threats and applicable procedures.
8. Monitoring systems and networks to detect suspicious activity.
9. Conducting security testing through periodic penetration test to ensure the controls implemented are running effectively and according to the policy.
10. Developing and implementing incident response procedures as a measurable and effective response to information security incidents.
11. Periodically implementing and testing a disaster recovery plan to ensure organizational continuity in the event of a disruption.
12. Implementing ISO 27001 standard to ensure that procedures related to ISMS implemented are in accordance with best practices in information security.
13. Regularly evaluating the effectiveness of ISMS and making adjustments if necessary; and
14. Internal Audit and External Audit related to ISO 27001:2013. | Risk Management and Compliance Division  |
