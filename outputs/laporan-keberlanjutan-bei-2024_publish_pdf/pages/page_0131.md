# PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI DAN KARIR

[OJK F.22, GRI 404-1, 404-2, 404-3]

BEI telah menyelenggarakan berbagai kegiatan pelatihan, pengembangan, dan edukasi guna meningkatkan kompetensi serta keahlian karyawan. Kegiatan yang dilaksanakan pada tahun 2023 mencakup pengembangan kompetensi yang meliputi pendidikan formal (learning through structured learning), pengembangan kompetensi melalui mentoring (learning through others), dan belajar dari keterlibatan langsung dalam suatu proyek/tugas/kegiatan (learning through experience), yaitu:

1. Structured learning. Kegiatan meliputi: public training, program sertifikasi, seminar, in house training, foreign language development program, pemberian beasiswa dan bantuan pendidikan bagi karyawan yang melanjutkan pendidikan ke jenjang yang lebih tinggi, dan e-Learning.
2. Learning through experience &amp; others. Kegiatan meliputi benchmarking ke stock exchanges/perusahaan lain, program magang, mentoring dan coaching.

Selama tahun 2023, BEI telah mengikutsertakan 1.624 karyawan. Rata-rata jam pelatihan setiap karyawan adalah 28,41 jam dalam setahun

# COMPETENCY AND CAREER DEVELOPMENT

[OJK F.22, GRI 404-1, 404-2, 404-3]

IDX has organized various training, development, and education activities for employees to improve their competence and expertise. Activities carried out in 2023 involved competency development including formal education (learning through structured learning), competency development through mentoring (learning through others), and learning from direct involvement in a project/task/activity (learning through experience), as follows:

1. Structured learning. This activity includes: public training, certification program, seminar, in-house training, foreign language development program, scholarship, and educational assistance for employees who continue their education to a higher degree, and e-Learning.
2. Learning through experience &amp; others. This activity includes benchmarking to stock exchanges/other companies, internship, mentoring, and coaching programs.

Throughout 2023, IDX registered 1,624 employees in such activities. The average training hours per employee was 28.41 hours.
