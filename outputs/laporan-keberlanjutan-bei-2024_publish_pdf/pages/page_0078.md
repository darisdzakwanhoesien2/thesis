# BENTURAN KEPENTINGAN [GRI 2-15]

Conflict of Interest

Perusahaan senantiasa menghindari segala bentuk benturan kepentingan pada seluruh tingkatan jabatan. Perusahaan memiliki Pedoman Perilaku dan penerapannya secara konsisten merupakan bagian terpenting dalam upaya meningkatkan penerapan tata kelola keberlanjutan, serta mendukung penguatan nilai dan budaya yang dimiliki BEI. Sosialisasi terhadap penerapan Pedoman Perilaku dilakukan kepada segenap insan BEI di seluruh tingkatan jabatan manajemen dan operasional. Sosialisasi dilaksanakan melalui media teknologi informasi yang dapat diakses setiap saat.

Selain Pedoman Perilaku, Perusahaan juga memiliki Piagam Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang di dalamnya telah mengatur pengelolaan benturan kepentingan bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, di antaranya sebagai berikut:

1. Tidak diperkenankan mewakili Perusahaan apabila anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang bersangkutan mempunyai benturan kepentingan dengan Perusahaan. Oleh karenanya, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang bersangkutan harus menyampaikan adanya benturan kepentingan tersebut kepada seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.
2. Tidak terlibat atau berpartisipasi dalam kegiatan apa pun yang akan menimbulkan benturan kepentingan dengan kepentingan terbaik Perusahaan atau yang akan berdampak negatif terhadap reputasi Perusahaan.
3. Para anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi harus mengungkapkan semua benturan kepentingan yang ada dan potensi konflik kepentingan atau apapun yang dapat mencegah Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi bertindak Independen.

Sepanjang tahun 2023, tidak terdapat hubungan afiliasi terkait hubungan keuangan, kekeluargaan dan kepengurusan antara keanggotaan lintas dewan, lintas saham dengan pemasok dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

The Company always avoids all forms of conflicts of interest at all levels of position. The Company has a Code of Conduct and its consistent application is the most important part in efforts to improve the implementation of sustainable governance, and support the strengthening of the Company's values and culture. The Code of Conduct is disseminated to all IDX personnel at all levels of management and operational positions and is carried out through information technology media accessible at any time.

Apart from the Code of Conduct, the Company also has a Charter for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors which stipulates conflicts of interest handling for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, including the following:

1. Not permitted to represent the Company in the event that the members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors concerned have a conflict of interest with the Company. Therefore, the concerned Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors must convey any conflict of interest to all members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.
2. Not be involved or participate in any activity that will create a conflict of interest with the best interests of the Company or which will have a negative impact on the reputation of the Company.
3. Members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors must disclose all existing conflicts of interest and potential conflicts of interest or anything that could prevent the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors from acting independently.

Throughout 2023, there were no affiliated relationships related to financial, family, or management relationships between cross-board, cross-share membership with suppliers, and other stakeholders.
