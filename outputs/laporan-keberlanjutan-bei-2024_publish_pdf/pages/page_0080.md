# KEBIJAKAN REMUNERASI DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI [GRI 2-19, GRI 2-20, GRI 2-21]

Remuneration Policy for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors

Pemberian remunerasi kepada Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi diatur sesuai dengan ketetapan RUPS serta Anggaran Dasar BEI. Remunerasi yang diberikan telah mempertimbangkan hasil penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi pada tahun buku dan pemenuhan KPI. Remunerasi yang diberikan kepada Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi berupa honor/gaji, tunjangan, dan apresiasi kinerja.

Dalam menerapkan remunerasi, Dewan Komisaris membentuk Komite Remunerasi yang bertujuan untuk mendukung Dewan Komisaris dalam evaluasi dan memberikan pandangan terkait remunerasi yang berlaku di BEI, termasuk prosedur penetapannya. Selain itu, Komite Remunerasi turut membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam meninjau dan memberikan saran terkait kebijakan remunerasi.

Perusahaan berkomitmen dan selalu berupaya untuk adil tanpa membedakan gender dalam pemberian remunerasi. Karyawan Perempuan dan Laki-laki memiliki hak yang sama dalam memperoleh remunerasi yang setara baik dalam bentuk cash maupun benefit sesuai dengan fungsi, level, kontribusi dan kinerja masing-masing.

Implementasi remunerasi di BEI mengikuti ketentuan yang berlaku dan memperhatikan kesetaraan di market guna mempertahankan daya saing BEI dan meningkatkan produktivitas Karyawan. Perusahaan memastikan besaran remunerasi untuk pekerjaan terendah untuk Karyawan BEI lebih besar daripada upah minimum yang ditetapkan Pemerintah di setiap wilayah yang ada.

Sampai dengan saat ini, Perusahaan belum dapat menyampaikan rasio kompensasi antara pendapatan tertinggi dan terendah. Hal ini dikarenakan data tersebut bersifat rahasia dan sensitif.

The provision of remuneration to the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is stipulated in accordance with the GMS resolutions and IDX's Articles of Association. The remuneration provided has considered the performance assessment results of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in the financial year and fulfillment of KPIs. Remuneration given to the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is in the form of honorarium/salary, allowance, and performance appreciation.

In implementing remuneration, the Board of Commissioners established the Remuneration Committee, aiming to support the Board of Commissioners in evaluating and providing views regarding remuneration applicable in IDX, including the procedures for determining the remuneration. In addition, the Remuneration Committee also assists the Board of Commissioners in reviewing and providing advice regarding remuneration policies.

The Company is committed and always strives to be fair regardless of gender in providing remuneration. Female and male employees have the same right to receive equal remuneration in the form of cash and benefits according to their respective functions, levels, contributions, and performance.

The remuneration at IDX is implemented by following the applicable regulations and paying attention to equality in the market to maintain IDX's competitiveness and increase employee productivity. The Company ensures that the amount of remuneration for IDX employees at the lowest position is greater than the minimum wage set by the Government in each existing region.

Until now, the Company has not been able to convey the compensation ratio between the highest and lowest income due to confidential and sensitive nature of the data.
