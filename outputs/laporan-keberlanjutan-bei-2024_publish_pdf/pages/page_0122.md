## ASPEK KEANEKARAGAMAN HAYATI

## DAMPAK DARI WILAYAH OPERASIONAL YANG DEKAT ATAU BERADA DI DAERAH KONSERVASI ATAU MEMILIKI KEANEKARAGAMAN HAYATI DAN USAHA KONSERVASI KEANEKARAGAMAN HAYATI [OJK F.9, F.10]

Wilayah operasi Perusahaan berada di DKI Jakarta dan di kota-kota besar di Indonesia, sehingga tidak ada wilayah operasi yang berada di dekat maupun di dalam kawasan yang dilindungi atau kawasan konservasi. Dengan demikian, kegiatan yang dilaksanakan Perusahaan tidak berdampak negatif terhadap kawasan yang dilindungi maupun kawasan konservasi beserta ekosistem yang ada di dalamnya. Namun demikian, Perusahaan tetap melakukan usaha konservasi untuk melindungi keanekaragaman hayati di Indonesia melalui kegiatan penanaman 1.000 bibit terumbu karang di Bali.

## ASPEK EMISI

## JUMLAH DAN INTENSITAS EMISI YANG DIHASILKAN BERDASARKAN JENISNYA

[OJK F.11, GRI 305-1, 305-2, 305-3, 305-4, TCFD MT-B]

Emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) yang dihasilkan oleh Perusahaan bersumber dari penggunaan BBM untuk genset, listrik, perjalanan dinas, BBM kendaraan operasional, perjalanan karyawan, pembelian barang/jasa, barang modal, aset sewaan, dan limbah yang dihasilkan.

All water used by the Company is directly streamed to a waste water processing installation unit (IPAL) that is managed directly by the building management. The amount of water withdrawn by the Company is the same as the water discharged. However, the Company has not measured the total volume of used water that is streamed to IPAL or to water body as used water is directly managed by the building management.

## BIODIVERSITY ASPECT

## IMPACTS FROM OPERATIONAL AREAS THAT ARE NEAR OR LOCATED IN CONSERVATION AREAS OR HAVE BIODIVERSITY AND BIODIVERSITY CONSERVATION EFFORTS [OJK F.9, F.10]

The Company's operational areas are located in DKI Jakarta and other major cities in Indonesia, so there are no operational areas that are near or within protected areas or conservation areas. Thus, the Company's activities do not have a negative impact on protected areas or conservation areas and their ecosystems. However, the Company continues to carry out conservation efforts to protect biodiversity in Indonesia through 1,000 seeds of coral reef planting activities in Bali.
