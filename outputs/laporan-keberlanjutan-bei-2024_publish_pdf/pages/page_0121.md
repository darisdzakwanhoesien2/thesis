# UPAYA DAN PENCAPAIAN EFISIENSI ENERGI DAN PENGGUNAAN ENERGI TERBARUKAN
[OJK F.7, GRI 302-4, 302-5]

Dalam menjalankan aktivitas operasionalnya, Perusahaan terus melakukan upaya efisiensi penggunaan energi dengan melakukan:

1. Menerapkan pemakaian jenis bahan bakar minyak ramah lingkungan;
2. Mengutamakan barang-barang ramah lingkungan dalam proses pengadaan;
3. Mematikan AC dan lampu setelah jam kerja berakhir;
4. Memberikan himbauan kepada karyawan untuk menghemat penggunaan listrik melalui poster hemat energi di setiap ruangan;
5. Melakukan pemasangan light emitting diode (LED) pada sistem pencahayaan; dan
6. Monitoring pemakaian bahan bakar kendaraan operasional berdasarkan liter.

Meskipun intensitas penggunaan energi per produksi masih mengalami peningkatan yang dikarenakan adanya peningkatan aktivitas operasional yang dilaksanakan secara Work From Office, namun Perusahaan berhasil menurunkan penggunaan BBM penggunaan BBM sebesar 17.395,99 liter.

# EFFORTS AND ACHIEVEMENTS OF ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND USE OF RENEWABLE ENERGY [OJK F.7, GRI 302-4, 302-5]

In carrying out its operational activities, the Company continues to make efforts to efficiently use energy by:

1. Applying the use of environmentally friendly types of gasoline;
2. Prioritizing environmentally friendly goods in the procurement process;
3. Turning off air conditioning and lights when working hours ends;
4. Disseminating saving electricity usage campaign through energy saving campaign posters in each room to employees;
5. Installing light emitting diode (LED) in the lighting system; and
6. Monitoring the use of gasoline for operational vehicles based on liters.

Even though the intensity of energy use per production is still increasing due to increased operational activities carried out by Work From Office, the Company has succeeded in reducing fuel consumption by 17,395.99 liters.
