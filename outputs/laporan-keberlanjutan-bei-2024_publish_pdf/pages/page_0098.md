# HUBUNGAN DENGAN PEMANGKU KEPENTINGAN [OJK E.4, GRI 2-29, F55]

Relations with Stakeholders

Perusahaan telah melakukan identifikasi pemangku kepentingan berdasarkan analisis atas interaksi yang terbangun selama periode pelaporan. Pelibatan pemangku kepentingan menjadi tugas dan tanggung jawab setiap Divisi yang terkait aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan. Hal ini tertuang pada tugas dan tanggung jawab Divisi berdasarkan Struktur Organisasi BEI per tanggal 24 Juli 2023. Adapun pelibatan pemangku kepentingan, diuraikan sebagai berikut.

The Company has identified stakeholders based on an analysis of interactions developed during the reporting period. Stakeholder involvement is the duty and responsibility of each Division related to Sustainable Finance actions. This is stated in the Division's duties and responsibilities based on IDX's Organizational Structure as of July 24, 2023. The involvement of stakeholders is described as follows.
