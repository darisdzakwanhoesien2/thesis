Pencalonan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi BEI dapat dilakukan oleh Anggota Bursa, Perusahaan Tercatat, dan pelaku pasar modal secara umum untuk selanjutnya diseleksi Komite Penilaian Kemampuan dan Kepatuhan yang dibentuk oleh Kepala Eksekutif Pengawas Pasar Modal, Keuangan Derivatif, dan Bursa Karbon Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK). Proses pengangkatan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dilakukan atas persetujuan Pemegang Saham setelah calon anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi lolos penilaian kemampuan dan kepatutan yang dilakukan oleh Komite Penilaian Kemampuan dan Kepatuhan.

Nominations for IDX's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors can be made by Exchange Members, Listed Companies, and capital market players in general to be further selected by the Capability and Compliance Assessment Committee established by the Chief Executive of Capital Market Supervision, Derivative Finance, Carbon Exchange, and Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK). The appointment process for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is carried out with the approval of Shareholders after the prospective members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors pass the fit and proper test conducted by the Capability and Compliance Assessment Committee.

# PENILAIAN KINERJA DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI [GRI 2-18]

Performance Assessment of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors

Evaluasi kinerja keberlanjutan dilakukan oleh OJK dengan menggunakan penilaian pencapaian target dalam indikator kinerja utama (KPI Unggulan), yang diberlakukan untuk seluruh pejabat tata kelola keberlanjutan setiap tahun. Adapun KPI Unggulan yang dinilai yaitu jumlah pencatatan efek, jumlah investor baru, persentase availability sistem, waktu pemulihan layanan perdagangan setelah gangguan, tingkat risiko anggota Bursa berdasarkan aspek kepatuhan, operasional dan finansial, indeks kepuasan pelanggan, pengembangan produk/layanan Bursa, dan seluruh aktivitas transaksi yang terindikasi tidak wajar yang terdeteksi dalam lingkup pengawasan telah ditindaklanjuti. Evaluasi juga dilakukan melalui mekanisme rapat Direksi maupun Dewan Komisaris, dan hasil penilaian akhir disampaikan kepada Pemegang Saham dalam RUPS. Berdasarkan evaluasi yang dilakukan pada tahun 2023, para pejabat tata kelola telah mampu mengelola kinerja keberlanjutan dengan baik.

The evaluation of sustainability performance by OJK uses the target achievement assessment in the key performance indicators (preferable KPI), which is applied to all sustainability governance officials every year. The Preferable KPI assessment are the number of securities listings, number of new investors, system availability percentage, recovery time of trading services after disruption, Exchange members risk level based on compliance, operational, and financial aspects, customer satisfaction, Exchange products/services development, and all transactions activities indicated to be unfair, detected within the scope of supervision followed up. Evaluation is also carried out through the meeting mechanism of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners, and the results of the final assessment are submitted to the Shareholders at the GMS. Based on the evaluation in 2023, the governance officials were able to manage sustainability performance properly.
