# INOVASI DAN TEKNOLOGI

Inovasi dan teknologi berdampak positif pada peningkatan keandalan sistem yang akan menurunkan risiko keamanan. Perusahaan senantiasa melakukan inovasi dan pengembangan teknologi yang digunakan untuk meningkatkan layanan kepada para pemangku kepentingan. Pengelola inovasi dan pengembangan teknologi berada di bawah tanggung jawab Divisi Teknologi Informasi. Evaluasi efektivitas inovasi dan pengembangan teknologi tercermin pada telah diraihnya ISO 27001: 2013 tentang Manajemen Keamanan Informasi. Sepanjang tahun 2023, BEI berhasil melakukan inovasi dan pengembangan teknologi berupa Sistem Carbon Trading, Papan Pemantauan Khusus, Sistem Penerima Laporan Transaksi Efek (PLTE), Pengawasan Transaksi EBUS (Daily Watching – DW) dan Lelang Pembelian Kembali SUN (Ministry of Finance Dealer System – MOFiDS), dan Decision Support System.

Dalam rangka mengantisipasi dan meminimalisir risiko cyber crime ketika menerapkan hybrid working arrangement, BEI telah melakukan kegiatan sebagai berikut:

1. Melakukan sosialisasi untuk meningkatkan kesadaran dan pemahaman karyawan terhadap social engineering kepada karyawan dengan membahas terkait jenis cyber crime, antisipasi cyber crime, prosedur pelaporan jika terjadi cyber crime.
2. Melakukan simulasi social engineering dengan beberapa skenario terjadinya cyber crime.

Seiring dengan berlakunya Undang-undang No 27 tahun 2022 tentang Perlindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP) dan memenuhi kebutuhan stakeholder, selama 2023 BEI telah melakukan sosialisasi UU PDP kepada seluruh karyawan BEI dan Anggota Bursa.

# PELATIHAN DAN PENDIDIKAN

Pelatihan dan pendidikan memiliki dampak positif pada peningkatan keterampilan dan produktivitas karyawan. Hal ini dikarenakan Perusahaan menganggap bahwa karyawan merupakan aset penting Perusahaan yang harus terus dikembangkan. Evaluasi program pelatihan dan pendidikan yang diselenggarakan oleh Perusahaan dilakukan dengan melakukan evaluasi secara berkala yang dikoordinasikan oleh Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia.

Even though in 2023 the emissions produced will still increase, the Company will continue to make efforts to reduce emissions. The resulting increase in emissions is due to increased operational activities. Currently, IDX is conducting emission calculations to serve as a baseline.

# INNOVATION AND TECHNOLOGY

Innovation and technology have a positive impact on improving system reliability that will reduce security risks. The Company constantly innovates and develops its technology, used to improve its services to stakeholders. The management of innovation and technology development is under the responsibility of the Information Technology Division. Evaluation of the effectiveness of innovation and technology development is reflected in obtaining ISO 27001: 2013 on Information Security Management. Throughout 2023, IDX managed to innovate and develop its technology in the form of Carbon Trading System, Special Monitoring Board, Recipients of Securities Transaction Reports (PLTE), EBUS Transaction Monitoring (Daily Watching – DW), SUN Buyback Auction (Ministry of Finance Dealer System – MOFiDS), and a Decision Support System.

To anticipate and minimize cyber-crime risk when applying hybrid working arrangement, IDX has carried out the following activities:

1. Conducting dissemination to increase employee awareness and understanding of social engineering to employees by discussing types of cyber-crime, anticipating cyber-crime, reporting procedures if cyber-crime occurs.
2. Carrying out social engineering simulations with several cyber-crime scenarios.

In line with the enactment of Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (PDP Law) and fulfilling stakeholders' needs, throughout 2023 IDX disseminated PDP Law to all IDX employees and Exchange Members.

# TRAINING AND EDUCATION

Training and education have a positive impact on improving employee skills and productivity. This is due to the Company that considers its employees as the Company's important asset that must continuously be developed. Evaluation of training and education programs organized by the Company is carried out by conducting periodic evaluations coordinated by the Human Capital Division.
