|  Pedoman Perilaku Karyawan
Employee Code of Conduct | 1. Adanya acuan bagi segenap jajaran manajemen dan Karyawan Perusahaan dalam membangun dan membina hubungan kerja yang lebih sehat, harmonis dan wajar dengan sesama Karyawan, Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Anggota Bursa Efek, Perusahaan Tercatat, Rekanan, Pelanggan, Pemerintah, dan masyarakat serta menjadi Insan BEI yang profesional.
2. Membangun budaya yang menjunjung tinggi kerja sama tim, integritas, kejujuran, disiplin, independen, kualitas, tanggung jawab dan layanan prima di seluruh tingkatan organisasi Perusahaan.
3. Manajemen Perusahaan dan karyawan memahami serta menerapkan nilai-nilai Perusahaan secara menyeluruh dan berkesinambungan.
4. Setiap karyawan membaca, memahami, dan menandatangani Surat Pernyataan Pribadi Karyawan setiap dua tahun atau ditentukan lain oleh Direksi.
5. Sosialisasi dan Evaluasi Pedoman secara berkala.
1. There is a reference for all levels of management and employees of the Company in building and maintaining healthier, harmonious, and fair working relationships with fellow employees, the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Stock Exchange Members, Listed Companies, Partners, Customers, Government and the community, as well as being Professional IDX people.
2. Building a culture that upholds teamwork, integrity, honesty, discipline, independence, quality, responsibility, and excellent service at all levels of the Company's organization.
3. Company management and employees understand and apply the Company's values thoroughly and continuously.
4. Every employee reads, understands, and signs the Employee Personal Statement every two years or as otherwise determined by the Board of Directors.
5. Socialization and Evaluation the Guidelines periodically. | Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia

|  Pedoman Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3)
Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Guidelines | 1. Dilakukannya identifikasi dan penilaian risiko terkait K3.
2. Pengembangan kebijakan yang terstruktur dan mencakup komitmen dan tujuan k3.
3. Pengembangan prosedur standar operasional (SOP) terkait K3.
4. Pelaksanaan Pengembangan kompetensi melalui pelatihan K3 yang berkelanjutan dan sesuai dengan perkembangan terbaru dalam praktik dan peraturan K3.
5. Pemantauan dan evaluasi K3
1. Carrying out identification and risk assessment related to OHS.
2. Developing policies that are structured and include OHS commitments and objectives.
3. Developing standard operating procedures (SOP) related to OHS.
4. Implementing competency development through ongoing OHS training and in accordance with the latest developments in OHS practices and regulations.
5. OHS monitoring and evaluation. | Tim Gugus Tugas K3, yaitu:
• Divisi Manajemen Risiko dan Kepatuhan
• Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia
• Divisi Umum
OHS Task Force Team, namely:
• Risk Management and Compliance Division
• Human Capital Division
• General Affairs Division  |
