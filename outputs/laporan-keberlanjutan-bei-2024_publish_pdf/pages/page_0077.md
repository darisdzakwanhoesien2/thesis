Sebagaimana dalam Undang-Undang No. 40 Tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, BEI memiliki Struktur Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang terdiri dari Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS), Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi serta organ pendukung Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Dalam menjalankan tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris didukung oleh organ pendukung meliputi Sekretaris Dewan Komisaris, Komite Remunerasi dan Komite Audit. Sedangkan, Direksi dibantu oleh organ pendukung meliputi Sekretaris Perusahaan, Divisi Hukum, Divisi Pengelolaan Strategi &amp; Anak Usaha, dan Satuan Pemeriksaan Internal.

Adapun struktur tata kelola yang dimiliki oleh BEI, sebagai berikut.

As stipulated in Law No. 40 of 2007 on Limited Liability Companies, IDX has a Corporate Governance Structure which consists of General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), Board of Commissioners, and Board of Directors, and the supporting organs of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors. In performing its duties, the Board of Commissioners is assisted by supporting organs, including the Secretary to the Board of Commissioners, Remuneration Committee, and Audit Committee. Meanwhile, the Board of Directors is assisted by supporting organs, including the Corporate Secretary, Legal Division, Corporate Strategy &amp; Subsidiary Management Division, and Internal Audit Unit.
