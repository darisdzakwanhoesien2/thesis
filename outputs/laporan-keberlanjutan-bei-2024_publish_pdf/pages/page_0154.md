## PRODUK/JASA YANG SUDAH DIEVALUASI KEAMANANNYA BAGI PELANGGAN (OJK F.27)

Seluruh kegiatan dan produk BEI diawasi Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) sebagai otoritas tertinggi jasa keuangan di Indonesia, sehingga memberikan jaminan keamanan terhadap pelanggan. BEI memberikan perlindungan aset investor dengan pembentukan dana perlindungan. Hal ini dilakukan untuk meningkatkan keamanan dalam berinvestasi di pasar modal Indonesia. BEI juga menerapkan kewajiban buyback saham publik bagi emiten yang melakukan penghapusan pencatatan secara sukarela (voluntary delisting), maupun delisting oleh regulator. Penerapan buyback saham bertujuan meningkatkan perlindungan investor, terutama pada investor retail. Dengan ketentuan ini, investor retail memiliki jalur atau wadah untuk mendapatkan uangnya kembali dengan menjual saham yang dimiliki.

## ACCELERATION BOARD INDEX AS OF MAY 31, 2023

Acceleration Board Index is an index that measures the price performance of all stocks listed on IDX Acceleration Board. As of December 29, 2023, there were 40 companies listed on the Acceleration Board.

## IDX-PEFINDO PRIME BANK INDEX ON OCTOBER 4, 2023

IDX-PEFINDO Prime Bank Index is an index that measures the price movement performance of 10 banking shares with investment grade rating ((idAAA to idBBB-) and transaction liquidity and good financial performance. IDX-PEFINDO Prime Bank Index was launched and managed in cooperation with rating company PT Pemeringkat Efek Indonesia (PEFINDO).

## PRODUCTS/SERVICES WITH EVALUATED CUSTOMER SAFETY (OJK F.27)

All IDX activities and products are supervised by the Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) as the highest authority for financial services in Indonesia, thereby providing security guarantees for customers. IDX provides investor asset protection by establishing a protection fund. This is carried out to increase security in investing in Indonesia capital market. IDX also implements a public share buyback obligation for issuers that carry out voluntary delisting or delisting by regulators. The implementation of share buybacks aims to increase investor protection, particularly retail investors. This provision accommodates retail investor to get their money back by selling their shares.
