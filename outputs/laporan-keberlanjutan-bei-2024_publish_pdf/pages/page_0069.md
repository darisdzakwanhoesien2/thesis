# PENERAPAN KEUANGAN BERKELANJUTAN

Sepanjang tahun 2023, BEI mencatatkan beberapa pencapaian kunci atas penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan baik secara internal perusahaan, maupun untuk pasar modal Indonesia, di antaranya BEI pada tahun 2023 dengan mendapat skor 16,9 (low risk) dalam laporan penilaian ESG (ESG Rating) dan melakukan pengembangan ASEAN ESG Core Matrix. Sampai dengan Desember 2023, 97% rencana aksi mendukung penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan yang tercantum dalam RAKB telah terlaksana. Realisasi anggaran kegiatan mendukung penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan sesuai RKAT 2023 mencapai 77% atau Rp115,6 miliar dari total anggaran sebesar Rp149,6 miliar.

Pada aspek ekonomi, BEI berhasil mencapai target waran terstruktur sebesar 587% dan target pencatatan efek sebesar 193% dibandingkan RKAT 2023. BEI juga berhasil mencapai target Kontrak Investasi Kolektif (KIK) baru yang mencatatkan DIRE, DINFRA, ETF, EBA, EBA-SP, EBA Syariah, EBA-SP Syariah sebesar 167%. Di samping itu, BEI juga berhasil mencapai target pencatatan saham baru sebesar 130%, target nilai transaksi harian sebesar 100%, target pendapatan usaha sebesar 103%, dan target laba sebesar 148% dibandingkan RKAT 2023.

Pada aspek lingkungan, Perusahaan berhasil mencapai target di antaranya penyampaian laporan kepada stakeholder dalam bentuk digital, penggunaan digital signature, dan tersedianya aplikasi billing yang dapat mendukung proses penagihan dalam bentuk digital dan melakukan implementasi Smart Office - Flexible Working Arrangement dengan perubahan media komunikasi dari sebelumnya telepon konvensional fisik diganti menjadi berbasis aplikasi. Perusahaan juga telah menggunakan perlengkapan ramah lingkungan di area kerja melalui penggunaan peralatan makan yang bisa digunakan kembali, box makanan berbahan karton food grade sebagai alternatif bagi karyawan yang tidak membawa peralatan makan dari rumah, serta tidak menggunakan air mineral dalam kemasan plastik pada setiap kegiatan. Di samping itu, Perusahaan telah mencapai target penerapan aktivitas ramah

The Company still faces challenges in implementing sustainable finance, which are related to the gaps between applicable reporting policies/standards and implementation in the field, resulting from a lack of understanding by employees and capital market players regarding sustainability principles and adequate resources. IDX's efforts to mitigate these problems include organizing and involving employees in sustainability-related training programs and recruiting Sustainability Expert and External Consultant.

# IMPLEMENTATION OF SUSTAINABLE FINANCE

Throughout 2023, IDX recorded several key achievements in implementing sustainable finance both for the Company's internal and for the Indonesia capital market, including IDX in 2023 with a score of 16.9 (low risk) in ESG assessment report (ESG Rating) and in developing ASEAN ESG Core Matrix. As of December 2023, 97% of the action plans to support sustainable finance's implementation, as stated in RAKB, were already implemented. The actual budget for activities supporting the sustainable finance's implementation in accordance with the 2023 RKAT reached 77% or Rp115.6 billion of the total budget of Rp149.6 billion.

In economic aspect, IDX managed to achieve the structured warrant target of 587% and securities listing target of 193% compared to 2023 RKAT. IDX also managed to achieve the new Collective Investment Contract (KIK) target that listed DIRE, DINFRA, ETF, EBA, EBA-SP, EBA Sharia, and EBA-SP Sharia by 167%. Moreover, IDX also succeeded in achieving the new share listing target of 130%, daily transaction value target of 100%, operating revenue target of 103%, and profit target of 148% compared to 2023 RKAT.

In the environmental aspect, the Company managed to achieve the targets, including reports submitted to stakeholder in digital form, use of digital signature, and there are available billing applications that could support billing process in digital form, as well as implement Smart Office - Flexible Working Arrangement with changes in the communication medium from previously conventional physical telephones to application-based ones. The Company has also used environmentally friendly equipment in working area through the use of reusable cutlery, food boxes made from food grade cardboard as an alternative for employees not bringing cutlery from home, and not using mineral water in plastic packaging for every activity.
