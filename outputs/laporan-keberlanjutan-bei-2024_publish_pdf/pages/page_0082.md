Untuk membantu Direksi dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Komite Keberlanjutan mempunyai fungsi memberikan pertimbangan, arahan, serta rekomendasi kepada Direksi mengenai rencana kerja dan anggaran kegiatan Keberlanjutan Perusahaan, baik dari penyusunan RAKB dan Laporan Sustainability, serta terkait realisasi rencana kegiatan Keberlanjutan Perusahaan. Direksi dapat menggunakan atau tidak menggunakan seluruh arahan, pertimbangan atau rekomendasi yang diberikan oleh Komite Keberlanjutan. Seluruh keputusan terkait dengan rencana dan anggaran kegiatan Keberlanjutan Perusahaan sepenuhnya menjadi tanggung jawab Direksi.

Dalam menjalankan aksi keuangan berkelanjutan, Direksi bertanggung jawab penuh terhadap pengelolaan Program Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan secara keseluruhan. Hal ini tertuang dalam Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan tahun 2023. Direksi mendefisasikan tanggung jawab pengelolaan Lingkungan, Sosial dan Tata Kelola (LST), termasuk pelaksanaan RAKB kepada pejabat perusahaan yang ditunjuk, meliputi Divisi Sekretaris Perusahaan, Divisi Pengelolaan Strategi Perusahaan dan Anak Usaha, Divisi Pengembangan Bisnis, Divisi Pengembangan Pasar, Divisi Pasar Modal Syariah, Divisi Layanan &amp; Pengembangan Perusahaan Tercatat, Divisi Umum, Divisi Manajemen Risiko, Divisi Operasional Teknologi Informasi (Bisnis dan Perkantoran) serta Perdagangan dan Pendukungnya), Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia, Divisi Keuangan dan Akuntansi, Divisi Pengembangan TI, Divisi Inkubasi Bisnis, Divisi Strategi dan Transformasi Digital, serta Divisi Hukum. Selama tahun 2023, Direksi menyelenggarakan 14 rapat dari 44 rapat Direksi, yang membahas terkait LST (terutama terkait Bursa Karbon). Di samping itu, aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan juga dilakukan pemantauan dan evaluasi oleh Dewan komisaris melalui Rapat Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi BEI yang rutin dilakukan pada setiap sebulan.

Adapun tugas dan wewenang tiap-tiap Divisi dalam aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan, sebagai berikut.

To assist the Board of Directors in performing its duties, the Sustainability Committee has the function of providing considerations, direction, and recommendations to the Board of Directors regarding work plan and budget for Company Sustainability activities, both from the preparation of RAKB and Sustainability Reports, and on the realization of Company Sustainability activity plans. The Board of Directors may use or not use all directions, considerations, or recommendations provided by the Sustainability Committee. All decisions related to plan and budget for Company Sustainability activities are fully the responsibility of the Board of Directors.

In performing the sustainable finance actions, the Board of Directors is fully responsible for managing the Sustainable Finance Action Program as a whole. This is stated in the 2023 Sustainable Finance Action Plan. The Board of Directors delegates the responsibility for managing Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG), including the implementation of RAKB to appointed Company officials, including the Corporate Secretary Division, Corporate and Subsidiaries Strategy Management Division, Business Development Division, Market Development Division, Sharia Capital Market Division, Listing Services &amp; Development Division, General Affairs Division, Risk Management Division, Information Technology Operations Division (Business and Office) and (Trading and Support), Human Capital Division, Finance and Accounting Division, IT Development Division, Business Incubation Division, Digital Transformation and Strategy Division, and Legal Division. Throughout 2023, the Board of Directors held 14 of the 44 Board of Directors meetings, discussing ESG related matters (particularly related to the Carbon Exchange). In addition, Sustainable Finance actions are also monitored and evaluated by the Board of Commissioners through IDX's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors Meetings routinely held every month.
