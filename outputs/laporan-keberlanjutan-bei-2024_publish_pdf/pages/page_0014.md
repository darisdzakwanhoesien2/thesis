# KEUANGAN BERKELANJUTAN

Penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan berdampak positif pada keberlangsungan usaha Perusahaan yang mengedepankan aspek lingkungan, sosial dan tata kelola. Komitmen Perusahaan dalam penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan dan sekaligus memenuhi kewajiban Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 3 Tahun 2021 tentang Penyelenggaraan Kegiatan di Bidang Pasar Modal dilakukan dengan upaya peningkatan Modal Dasar dan Modal Disetor. Penyesuaian tersebut perlu dilakukan sehingga BEI memiliki pengelolaan kas yang memadai dengan memperhatikan keberlangsungan usaha selama 18 – 24 bulan. Permodalan perlu diperkuat guna membiayai kegiatan operasional sekaligus memenuhi kebutuhan pengembangan pasar modal.

Penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan juga akan meminimalkan risiko perubahan iklim yang berasal dari aktivitas operasional Perusahaan. Bursa Karbon Indonesia merupakan kontribusi nyata Perusahaan dalam pengendalian emisi gas rumah kaca secara nasional.

Matters carried out by the Company to prevent acts of bribery and corruption are:

1. The Anti-Bribery Management System (ABMS) based on SNI ISO 37001:2016 is implemented at IDX as a form of IDX's commitment and support for preventing acts of bribery and corruption.
2. SMAP dissemination/awareness (including on the prohibition of gratification/bribery/corruption) to the Board of Directors and all IDX employees are carried out directly and through email, while those to IDX stakeholders are carried out through letters and publications on IDX website.
3. Establishment of anti-bribery policies, gratification control, prohibition of bribery, employee personal statements.
4. IDX provides reporting facilities regarding allegations/potential/attempted acts of gratification/bribery/corruption through various means, namely:
a. Direct reports by employees to their respective superiors, the Human Capital Division (HC), or the Anti-Bribery Compliance Function (FKAP); or
b. Reports by employees and external parties through the Whistleblowing System (WBS).

Evaluation of the effectiveness of anti-bribery programs implementation is reflected in the possession of ISO 37001: Anti-Bribery Management System. With such commitment in mind, throughout 2023 there were no proven incidents of bribery and corruption that resulted in employees being dismissed or punished, employment contracts with partners being terminated, and legal cases related to corruption being filed by the public.
