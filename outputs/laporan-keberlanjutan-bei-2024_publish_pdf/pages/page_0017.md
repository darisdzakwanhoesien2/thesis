# KEBERAGAMAN DAN KESETARAAN KESEMPATAN

Perusahaan senantiasa menjunjung keberagaman dan kesetaraan di tempat kerja. Hal ini diwujudkan dengan tidak membedakan perlakuan terhadap pekerja maupun dalam bekerja berdasarkan gender, suku, agama, ras, pandangan politik, maupun hal-hal diskriminatif lainnya. Penghormatan atas keberagaman di tempat kerja akan berdampak positif pada rasa aman dan nyaman para karyawan di tempat kerja.

Komitmen Perusahaan dalam menjunjung keberagaman dan kesetaraan yaitu dengan telah dimilikinya Kebijakan Non Diskriminasi yang diinisiasi dan Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia, serta berkolaborasi dengan berbagai Institusi terkait antara lain Kementerian Pemberdayaan Perempuan &amp; Perlindungan Anak, Indonesia Business Coalition for Women Empowerment (IBCWE), Indonesia Global Compact Network (IGCN), UN Women, Investing in Women, International Finance Corporation (IFC), dan lainnya. Efektivitas penerapan keberagaman dan kesetaraan kesempatan yaitu dengan melakukan evaluasi secara berkala.

Setiap karyawan BEI bertanggungjawab mendukung terciptanya lingkungan kerja yang profesional dan tidak berlaku diskriminatif. Perusahaan akan mengambil tindakan disipliner sampai dengan pemutusan hubungan kerja terhadap setiap Karyawan yang tidak dapat mematuhi Pedoman Perilaku yang mencakup larangan perilaku diskrimintatif.

The Company constantly organizes several competency development programs for employees every year to support the Company's business activities. The Company also guarantees that every employee has the same opportunity to attend education and training program in accordance with the employee's needs and development plans. Throughout 2023, there were an increase in the average employee training for all types of programs, namely public training, in house training, and leadership development compared to 2022.

# DIVERSITY AND EQUAL OPPORTUNITY

The Company always upholds diversity and equality in the workplace. This matter is realized by not differentiating treatment of workers or at work by gender, ethnicity, religion, race, political views, or other discriminatory factors. Respect for diversity in the workplace will have a positive impact on employees' feeling of safety and comfort in the workplace.

The Company's commitment to upholding diversity and equality is realized by having a Non-Discrimination Policy, initiated by Human Capital Division, as well as cooperating with various related institutions, including the Ministry of Women's Empowerment &amp; Child Protection, Indonesia Business Coalition for Women Empowerment (IBCWE), Indonesia Global Compact Network (IGCN), UN Women, Investing in Women, International Finance Corporation (IFC), and others. The effectiveness of implementing diversity and equality of opportunity is by conducting regular evaluations.

Every IDX employee is responsible for supporting the creation of a professional and non-discriminatory work environment. The Company will take disciplinary action up to termination of employment against any employee who cannot comply with the Code of Conduct, which includes prohibition on discriminatory behavior.
