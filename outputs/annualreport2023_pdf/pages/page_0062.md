(c) Liquidity risk

Liquidity risk refers to the Group's ability to meet its financial obligations as and when they fall due.

The Group's exposure to liquidity risk is minimal as it adopts prudent liquidity risk management by regularly reviewing its cash flow needs, maintaining sufficient cash from its internally generated cash flow and putting in place adequate financing arrangements.
