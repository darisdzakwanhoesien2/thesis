## 27. Financial risk management

Financial risk factors

The Group is exposed to market risk (including foreign currency risk and interest rate risk), credit risk and liquidity risk.

The board members have overall responsibility for the establishment and oversight of the Group's risk management framework.

The Group's overall financial risk management approach focuses on the state and the unpredictability of the financial and capital markets and seeks to minimise the potential adverse effects from the exposures to these risks on the financial performance of the Group.

The Group's exposure to foreign currency risk arises from its foreign currency contracts for purchase of goods and services and its operations in Malaysia.
