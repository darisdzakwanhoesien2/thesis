# PENILAIAN TERHADAP KINERJA DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI
## Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors
### Performance Assessment

Penilaian terhadap kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dilakukan melalui metode *self-assessment* oleh masing-masing anggota Direksi/Dewan Komisaris yang dilakukan pada akhir Semester 1 dan akhir Semester 2 setiap tahunnya.

### Prosedur Penilaian Kinerja

#### Assessment to the performance of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is conducted through self-assessment by each member of the Board of Directors/Board of Commissioners at the end of first Semester and at the end of second Semester in every year.

### Performance Assessment Procedures

|  Dewan Komisaris | Board of Commissioners  |
| --- | --- |
|  1. Masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris memberikan penilaian atas masing-masing item. | 1. Each member of the Board of Commissioners carries out assessment on each item.  |
|  2. Mempertimbangkan penilaian atas masing-masing *item*, masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris memberikan kesimpulan atas hasil *assessment*-nya terhadap kinerja Dewan Komisaris secara keseluruhan. | 2. Considering the assessment of each item, each member of the Board of Commissioners provides conclusions on the assessment results on the overall performances of the Board of Commissioners.  |
|  3. Masing-masing Komisaris membuat catatan tentang pandangannya mengenai hal-hal yang perlu diperbaiki oleh Dewan Komisaris. | 3. Each member of the Board of Commissioners takes note of their perception regarding the subjects that need to be improved by the Board of Commissioners.  |
|  4. Dilakukan pembahasan bersama seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris untuk menghasilkan kesimpulan dan tindak lanjut yang disepakati Dewan Komisaris. | 4. Collective discussion with all members of the Board of Commissioners is conducted in order to determine the resolutions and follow up actions that are agreed by the Board of Commissioners.  |
|  Direksi | Board of Directors  |
| --- | --- |
|  1. Masing-masing anggota Direksi memberikan penilaian atas masing-masing *item* penilaian. | 1. Each member of the Board of Directors carries out assessment on each item.  |
|  2. Mempertimbangkan hasil penilaiannya atas masing-masing *item*, masing-masing anggota Direksi memberikan hasil *assessment* kinerja Direksi secara keseluruhan. | 2. Considering the assessment results of each item, each member of the Board of Directors provides the overall assessment results of the Board of Directors.  |
|  3. Masing-masing anggota Direksi membuat catatan tentang pandangannya mengenai hal-hal yang perlu diperbaiki oleh Direksi. | 3. Each member of the Board of Directors takes note of their perception regarding the subjects that need to be improved by the Board of Directors.  |
|  4. Pembahasan bersama seluruh anggota Direksi menghasilkan kesimpulan dan tindak lanjut yang disepakati Direksi. | 4. Collective discussion with all members of the Board of Directors is conducted in order to determine the resolutions and follow up actions that are agreed by the Board of Directors.  |

Selain dengan metode *self-assessment*, Direksi juga melakukan penilaian secara kolegial untuk mengevaluasi efektivitas kerjanya dan mengidentifikasi hal-hal yang memerlukan perbaikan.

In addition to the self-assessment method, the Board of Directors also conducts a collegial assessment to evaluate the effectiveness of its work and identify matters that require improvement.
