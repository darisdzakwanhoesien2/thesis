Berkat upaya Perseroan dalam mendorong setiap karyawan untuk memperhatikan prosedur K3 yang berlaku, maka kecelakaan kerja fatal tidak terjadi di lingkungan Perseroan di sepanjang tahun 2022.

## Hubungan Industrial

Hubungan industrial yang harmonis antara Perseroan dengan karyawan merupakan salah satu faktor penting untuk meningkatkan kondisi kerja, kualitas, produktivitas, maupun daya saing. Untuk mencapai hal tersebut, Perseroan memiliki Peraturan Perusahaan yang berlaku efektif dan diperbarui setiap 2 (dua) tahun yang mengatur hal-hal mengenai:
- Hubungan kerja.
- Hari kerja dan jam kerja.
- Upah dan tunjangan.
- Hari libur dan cuti.
- Jaminan sosial tenaga kerja.
- Program jaminan kesehatan.
- Keselamatan kerja.
- Tata tertib dan disiplin.
- Sanksi pelanggaran tata tertib dan disiplin.
- Perawatan dan pengobatan.
- Etika bisnis.
- Berakhirnya hubungan kerja.

Peraturan Perusahaan ini menjadi pedoman bagi Perseroan dan karyawan untuk memahami dan menjalankan hak dan kewajibannya.

## Penyampaian dan Penyelesaian Keluhan

Setiap karyawan berhak menyampaikan pendapat, memperoleh keterangan mengenai Perseroan, pekerjaan serta hubungan kerja dalam batas-batas yang telah ditetapkan Perseroan baik secara lisan maupun tertulis kepada atasan langsung yang akan meneruskannya ke bagian yang berwenang.

Adapun penyampaian pendapat ataupun saran harus dilakukan dengan mematuhi sejumlah tata cara yang ditetapkan oleh Perseroan sebagai berikut:
1. Setiap keluhan Karyawan harus dibicarakan dan atau diselesaikan dengan atasan langsung yang berwenang.
2. Bila keluhan atau masalah tidak bisa diselesaikan oleh atasan langsung, maka dengan sepengetahuan atasannya, karyawan

- Checking the body temperature of all employees every day prior to entering the work area.
- Carrying out duties COVID-19 Task Force team.
- Spraying disinfectants every day to all work areas, including both inside and outside the office area.
- Providing hand sanitizer facilities in the work area.
- Remote seating system arrangement within the office area.
- Setting up WFH &amp; WHO systems tailored to the current conditions.

Throughout 2022, the Company also organized other activities related to Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) as follows:
- On March 25, 2022, all Department Head of operational department attended OHS socialization held by PT SMS Indonesia.
- In addition, the following 2 activities were held on November:
- Training and simulation of fire fighting, attended by 10 employees and 5 business partners.
- EDGE Team signed ABC and declared EHS for EDGE2.

## Work Accident Rate

Thanks to the Company's efforts in encouraging every employee to pay attention to the applicable OHS procedures, there were no fatal work accidents in the Company's environment throughout 2022.

## Industrial Relationship

A harmonious relationship between the Company and its employees is one of the key factors to improve work condition, productivity, as well as competitiveness. To achieve this purpose, the Company has established Company Regulation which is effective and updated every 2 (two) years and stipulates the following subjects:
- Work relationship.
- Working days and working hours.
- Wages and allowances.
- Holidays and leave.
- Social Security.
- Health insurance program.
- Work safety.
- Code of Conduct and discipline.
- Sanctions for violating the rules and discipline.
- Treatment and medication.
- Business ethics.
- End of employment.

This Company Regulation becomes a guideline for the Company and its employees to understand and execute their rights and obligations.
