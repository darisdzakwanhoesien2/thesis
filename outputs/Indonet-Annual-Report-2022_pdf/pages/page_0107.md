## Independensi Anggota Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi

Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, setiap anggota Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi wajib bertindak independen dan dilarang mengambil keuntungan pribadi baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung dari kegiatan Perseroan.

## Pelatihan dan/atau Peningkatan Kompetensi Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi

Sepanjang tahun 2022, anggota Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi mengikuti program pelatihan awareness pada tanggal 6 dan 8 September 2022 yang diadakan secara internal dan diikuti oleh seluruh anggota komite.

## Pelaksanaan dan Penilaian Kinerja Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi Tahun 2022

Selama tahun 2022, Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi telah menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya secara efektif.

Direksi menilai Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi telah menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik berdasarkan output kerja dan tingkat kehadiran dalam Rapat Komite sepanjang tahun 2022.

## Rapat Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi

Rapat Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi Perseroan wajib diselenggarakan secara berkala setidaknya 2 (dua) kali dalam setahun. Sepanjang tahun 2022, Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi telah menyelenggarakan 2 (dua) kali rapat dengan tingkat kehadiran rapat masing-masing anggota 100%.

## Komite Disaster Recovery and Emergency Response Team

Keberadaan Disaster Recovery and Emergency Response Team bertujuan untuk memastikan peran dan tanggung jawab terkait pemulihan kondisi disaster yang telah ditentukan dan dikomunikasikan dalam rangka penerapan Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi berdasarkan ISO 27001:2013.

## Independency of the Information Security Management System Committee

In carrying out their duties and responsibilities, each member of the Information Security Management System Committee is required to act independently and prohibited to take personal benefits from the Company's activities whether directly or indirectly.

## Training and/or Competency Development of the Information Security Management System Committee

Throughout 2022, members of the Information Security Management System Committee have participated in awareness training on September 6 and 8, 2022 which were held internally and attended by all members of the committee.

## Implementation and Assessment of the Information Security Management System Committee Performance in 2022

Throughout 2022, the Information Security Management System Committee has carried out its duties and responsibilities effectively.

The Board of Directors considers that the Information Security Management System Committee has carried out its duties and responsibilities properly based on the performance output and attendance in the Committee Meeting throughout 2022.

## Information Security Management System Committee Meeting

The Information Security Management System Committee is required to hold regular meetings at least 2 (two) times a year. Throughout 2022, the Information Security Management System Committee has held 2 (two) meetings with 100% attendance rate by each member.

## Disaster Recovery and Emergency Response Team Committee

The existence of the Disaster Recovery and Emergency Response Team Committee is intended to ensure that the duties and responsibilities related to disaster recovery have been determined and communicated in order to implement the Information Security Management System based on ISO 27001:2013.

## Membership and Term of Office of the Disaster Recovery and Emergency Response Team Committee

The Disaster Recovery and Emergency Response Team Committee consists of 15 (fifteen) members who were appointed based on the Board of Directors' Circular Letter No. 001/SE/BOD/SQA/XI/2021 dated November 16, 2021 for the period of November 16, 2021–present.
