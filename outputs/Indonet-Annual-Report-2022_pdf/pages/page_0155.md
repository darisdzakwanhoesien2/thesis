## Kegiatan Mendukung Kelestarian Lingkungan

Bentuk komitmen Perseroan untuk menciptakan lingkungan yang hijau dan asri juga diwujudkan kegiatan pelestarian taman dan perawatan hama di sekitar lingkungan kantor, selain itu EDGE DC juga telah melakukan pengelolaan manajemen sampah untuk data center dengan metode Reduce Waste to Land Fill dengan teknologi Refuse Derived Fuel dan memberikan edukasi secara berkala pada setiap karyawan tentang pengelolaan sampah. Biaya pengelolaan manajemen sampah yang dikeluarkan sepanjang tahun 2022 (dimulai dari Agustus sampai dengan Desember) sebesar Rp4 juta.

Meanwhile, as a form of the Company's commitment through its subsidiary, Ekagrata Data Gemilang in supporting sustainable energy, EDGE1 facility has been utilizing renewable energy solution and has obtained Renewable Energy Certificate, which made EDGE1 as the first data center in Indonesia that utilizes sustainable energy initiative from the State Electricity Company (PLN). By utilizing green energy, the Company is able to reduce greenhouse gas emission from all the operating facilities of EDGE1 data center facilities and expected to support carbon neutrality in 2030.

## Environmental Preservation Activities

The Company's commitment in creating green and beautiful environment is also realized through garden maintenance and pest treatment in office environment. In addition, EDGE DC has also carried out waste management through Reduce Waste to Land Fill method with Refuse Derived Fuel technology and provides regular education to every employee concerning waste management. Waste management costs incurred throughout 2022 (starting from August to December) amounted to Rp4 million.

## Biodiversity

None of the Company's operational activities are located in or adjacent to areas that have rich biodiversity.

## Complaints, Sanctions or Fines Related to Environment

Throughout 2022, there were no complaints, sanctions or fines related to environmental damage or pollution due to the Company's operational activities.
