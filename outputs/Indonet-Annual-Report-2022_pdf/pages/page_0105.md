# KOMITE-KOMITE DI BAWAH DIREKSI
## Committees Under The Board of Directors

## Komite Pengelolaan Portal Internal Perusahaan

Komite yang bertanggung jawab untuk merancang dan mengembangkan Portal Internal perusahaan dengan tujuan untuk dijadikan media sosialisasi peraturan, kebijakan, prosedur, dan dokumen Perseroan lainnya yang efektif sehingga seluruh karyawan bisa mendapatkan informasi terbaru dan terkini yang dikeluarkan oleh Perseroan.

## Keanggotaan Komite Pengelola Portal Internal Perusahaan

Komite Pengelola Portal Internal Perusahaan beranggotakan 6 orang yang diangkat berdasarkan Surat Edaran Direksi No. 002/SE/DIR/SQA/X/2021 tanggal 28 Oktober 2021 untuk periode 28 Oktober 2021-sekarang. ## Corporate Internal Portal Management Committee

This Committee is responsible to design and develop the company's Internal Portal which is intended to be established into an effective socialization media for regulations, policies, procedures, and other company documents in order for all employees to have access to the latest and recent information issued by the Company.

## Membership of the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee

The Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee consists of 6 members which were appointed based on the Board of Directors' Circular Letter No. 002/SE/DIR/SQA/X/2021 dated October 28, 2021 for the period of October 28, 2021–present. ## Independensi Anggota Komite Pengelola Portal Internal Perusahaan

Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, setiap anggota Komite Pengelola Portal Internal Perusahaan wajib bertindak independen dan dilarang mengambil keuntungan pribadi baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung dari kegiatan Perseroan.

## Pelatihan dan/atau Peningkatan Kompetensi Komite Pengelolaan Portal Internal Perusahaan

Sepanjang tahun 2022, anggota Komite Pengelolaan Portal Internal Perusahaan mengikuti program pelatihan dan peningkatan kompetensi secara internal yang diberikan oleh Project Manager pengelolaan portal.

## Independency of the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee

In carrying out their duties and responsibilities, each member of the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee is required to act independently and prohibited to take personal benefits from the Company's activities whether directly or indirectly.

## Training and/or Competency Development of the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee

Throughout 2022, members of the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee have participated in internal training and competency development programs which were organized by the Project Manager of portal management.
