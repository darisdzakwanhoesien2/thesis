Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi berpedoman pada Piagam Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi sebagaimana ditetapkan oleh Dewan Komisaris melalui Surat Ketetapan No. 035/Indonet-Dekom.SK/VII/2021 tanggal 2 Juli 2021. Sebagaimana tercantum dalam Piagam Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, seluruh anggota Komite wajib mematuhi kode etik yang ditetapkan Perseroan dan memenuhi persyaratan independensi serta larangan mengambil keuntungan pribadi baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung dari kegiatan Perseroan.

## Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi

### 1. Bidang Nominasi

» Menyusun, mengusulkan, dan melakukan evaluasi tahunan atas Kebijakan Nominasi.
» Memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris terkait Komposisi Jabatan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, Kebijakan dan standar kriteria yang diperlukan dalam proses Nominasi.
» Memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris terkait Kebijakan Evaluasi Kinerja anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.

In carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee refers to the Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter as stipulated by the Board of Commissioners through Decree No. 035/Indonet-Dekom.SK/VII/2021 dated July 2, 2021. As stated in the Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter, all Committee members shall comply to the Company's code of conduct and fulfill the independency requirements as well as be forbidden to make personal gain whether directly or indirectly from the Company's activities.

## Duties and Responsibilities of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee

### 1. Nomination Aspect

» Compiling, proposing, and performing annual evaluation on Nomination Policies.
» Providing recommendations to the Board of Commissioners regarding the Composition of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, Policies and standard criteria that are required in the Nomination processes.
» Providing recommendation to the Board of Commissioners related to the Performance Evaluation Policy for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.
