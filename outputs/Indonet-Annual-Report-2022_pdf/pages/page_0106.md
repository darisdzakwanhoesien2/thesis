# Pelaksanaan dan Penilaian Kinerja Komite Pengelola Portal Internal Perusahaan Tahun 2022

Selama tahun 2022, Komite Pengelola Portal Internal Perusahaan telah menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya secara efektif.

Direksi menilai Komite Pengelola Portal Internal Perusahaan telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik berdasarkan output kerja dan tingkat kehadiran dalam Rapat Komite sepanjang tahun 2022.

# Rapat Komite Pengelolaan Portal Internal Perusahaan

Rapat Komite wajib diselenggarakan secara berkala dalam rangka pengelolaan Portal Internal Perusahaan. Sepanjang tahun 2022, Komite Pengelolaan Portal Internal Perusahaan telah menyelenggarakan 3 (tiga) kali rapat dengan tingkat kehadiran masing-masing anggota 100%.

# Komite Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi

Sebagai salah satu wujud komitmen manajemen untuk memastikan peran dan tanggung jawab terkait keamanan informasi, Direksi membentuk Komite Keamanan Informasi yang telah dikomunikasikan dalam rangka penerapan Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi berdasarkan ISO 27001:2013.

# Implementation and Performance Assessment of the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee Performance in 2022

Throughout 2022, the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee has carried out its duties and responsibilities effectively.

The Board of Directors considers that the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee has carried out its duties and responsibilities properly based on the performance output and attendance in the Committee Meeting throughout 2022.

# Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee Meeting

The Committee is required to hold regular meetings in order to manage the Company's Internal Portal. Throughout 2022, the Corporate Internal Portal Managing Committee has held 3 (three) meetings with 100% attendance rate by each member.

# Information Security Management System Committee

As a form of the management's commitment to ensure the role and responsibilities related to information security, the Board of Directors established the Information Security Committee which has been communicated in order to implement an Information Security Management System that is based on ISO 27001:2013.

# Membership of the Information Security Management System Committee
