# Pihak yang Melakukan Penilaian

Sebagaimana telah disebutkan sebelumnya bahwa penilaian terhadap kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dilakukan melalui metode self-assessment oleh masing-masing anggota Direksi/Dewan Komisaris.

Pada penilaian Direksi terdapat evaluasi secara kuantitatif atas capaian KPI terhadap target. Dimana yang menetapkan KPI dan Target KPI, serta yang menilai adalah Dewan Komisaris berdasarkan Rencana Bisnis Perseroan. Sementara itu, Direktur Utama melakukan evaluasi kinerja Direktur berdasarkan realisasi Rencana Kerja Fungsional dan menyampaikan hasil evaluasinya kepada Dewan Komisaris.

Selanjutnya, Dewan Komisaris akan melakukan evaluasi kinerja Dewan Komisaris dengan memperhatikan hasil self-assessment Dewan Komisaris. Dewan Komisaris juga melakukan evaluasi kinerja Direktur Utama berdasarkan hasil evaluasi atas kinerja Perseroan dan mempertimbangkan kemajuan/hasil dari penugasan-penugasan khusus yang diberikan kepada Direktur Utama.

# The Assessor

As previously mentioned, the performance assessment on the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is conducted through self-assessment by each member of the Board of Directors/Board of Commissioners.

In the assessment of the Board of Directors, there is a quantitative evaluation of KPI achievements against the target. The Board of Commissioners has the role to set KPIs and KPI Targets, as well as assess them based on the Company's Business Plan. Meanwhile, the President Director performs the assessment towards the Directors based on the realization of the Functional Work Plan and reporting the evaluation results to the Board of Commissioners.

Furthermore, the Board of Commissioners evaluates their own performance based on the self-assessment results. The Board of Commissioners also evaluates the performance of the President Director based on the Company's performance evaluation and the development/achievement of special assignments given to the President Director.
