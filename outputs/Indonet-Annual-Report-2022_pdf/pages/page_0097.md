# Pelaksanaan Kegiatan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Tahun 2022

Sepanjang tahun 2022, pelaksanaan kegiatan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi meliputi:

- Membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam mengevaluasi kinerja tahun 2021 anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris.
- Merekomendasikan besaran remunerasi tahun 2022 bagi anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dengan mempertimbangkan kinerja Perseroan dan Kinerja perorangan anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris.
- Meninjau Kebijakan Remunerasi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris terkait kesesuaian dengan ketentuan regulasi yang berlaku bagi Perseroan Publik dan mengawasi pelaksanaannya.
- Meninjau Kebijakan Pengelolaan Kinerja Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dan mengawasi pelaksanaannya.
- Memantau proses perencanaan dan pembuatan Tabel Suksesie anggota Direksi.
- Menyusun Program Pengembangan kompetensi anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris.
- Melakukan sosialisasi Kebijakan terkait remunerasi, kinerja dan suksesi kepada pihak-pihak terkait dan/atau PIC Kebijakan dan memastikan pemahaman dan pelaksanaan yang benar atas kebijakan.

Komite menyimpulkan, bahwa:

1. Kebijakan Remunerasi untuk anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris telah dilaksanakan sesuai dengan Kebijakan dan Prosedur yang ditetapkan.
2. Kebijakan Pengelolaan Kinerja telah dilaksanakan sesuai dengan Kebijakan dan Prosedur yang ditetapkan.
3. Disiplin proses telah terbentuk untuk kebijakan Remunerasi dan Pengelolaan Kinerja.
4. Kebijakan Pengelolaan Suksesie dan Pengembangan Calon Suksesie Direksi baru pada tahun pertama pelaksanaan. Kandidat suksesi untuk posisi Direksi sangat minim namun sudah menunjukkan teridentifikasinya kandidat berpotensi yang dapat dikembangkan secara terencana dan terarah untuk masing-masing posisi.
5. Dengan pengunduran diri Head of HR, Penyusunan Kompetensi anggota Direksi dan Rencana Pengembangan masih dalam tahap penyusunan dan direncanakan pelaksanaannya pada tahun 2023.

# Kebijakan Remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Kebijakan Remunerasi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris tertuang dalam Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris No. 032/Indonet/Dekom-SK/VI/2021 yang menetapkan, antara lain:

a. Struktur Remunerasi anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris terdiri dari: Komponen tetap (Gaji dan THR) dan Komponen Variabel (Bonus Kinerja).
b. Kepada anggota Direksi dapat diberikan Bonus Kinerja berdasarkan pencapaian KPI Perseroan dan dengan mempertimbangkan kinerja masing-masing anggota Direksi.
c. Indikator pencapaian Perseroan untuk keperluan penilaian kinerja dan penetapan Bonus berdasarkan pencapaian KPI Perusahaan dan Direktorat yang telah ditetapkan sebelumnya.
d. Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi melakukan benchmarking dan mengusulkan besaran Remunerasi anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dengan mempertimbangkan kinerja, kepantasan dan keadilan/kesetaraan dan kemampuan Perseroan.

# Implementation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee Duties in 2022

Throughout 2022, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee has carried out the following duties:

- Assisting the Board of Commissioners in conducting 2021 performance evaluation of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners.
- Making recommendation of the remuneration amount in 2022 for members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners by considering the Company's performance and Individual performance of each member of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners.
- Reviewing the Remuneration Policy for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners related to the compliance with the applicable regulations for Public Companies and supervising the implementation.
- Reviewing the Performance Management Policy of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners and supervising the implementation.
- Monitoring the planning process and compilation of Succession Table of the Board of Directors.
- Compiling Competency Development Program for members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners.
- Socializing the Policies related to remuneration, performance and succession to related parties and/or PIC of the Policy and ensuring proper understanding and implementation of the Policy.

The Committee considers, that:

1. The Remuneration Policy for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners has been implemented in accordance with the applicable Policies and Procedures.
2. The Performance Management Policy has been implemented in accordance with the applicable Policies and Procedures.
3. Discipline process has been established in the Remuneration and Performance Management policies.
4. The Policy of Succession Management and Prospective Director Development in the first year of implementation. The succession candidate of Director is barely minimum but has shown an identification of potential candidate to be developed based on the plan and direction in each position.
5. With the resignation of the Head of HR, the Compilation of Competency for members of the Board of Directors and Development is planned to be implemented in 2023.

# Remuneration Policy for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors

The Remuneration Policy for members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners are stipulated in the Board of Commissioners Decree No. 032/Indonet/Dekom-SK/VI/2021 which stipulate the following:

a. The Remuneration Structure for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners consists of: Fixed Component (Salary and Holiday Allowance) and Variable Component (Performance Bonus).
b. The Board of Directors may receive Performance Bonus based on the Company's KPI achievements and based on the performance of each member of the Board of Directors.
c. The indicators of the Company's achievements for the performance assessment and determination of Bonus are based on the Company's KPI achievements of the Company and the Directorate as previously determined.
d. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee conducts benchmarking and proposing the amount of Remuneration received by the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners based on the performance, appropriateness and fairness/equality and the Company's ability.
