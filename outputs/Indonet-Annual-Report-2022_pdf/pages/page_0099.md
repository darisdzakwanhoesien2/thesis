# Board of Directors

Berdasarkan RUPST tanggal 16 Juni 2022, susunan Direksi Perseroan mengalami perubahan dan terdiri dari seorang Direktur Utama dan 4 (empat) orang Direktur sebagaimana tertuang dalam Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Rapat PT Indointernet Tbk No. 113, tanggal 16 Juni 2022, dibuat di hadapan Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notaris di Jakarta. Penetapan jumlah dan komposisi Direksi tersebut telah mempertimbangkan kondisi Perseroan, efektivitas pengambilan keputusan serta kebutuhan Perseroan akan keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan, dan pengalaman dalam menghadapi perkembangan dan tantangan bisnis ke depan.

Sebagai salah satu organ utama Perseroan, Direksi bertanggung jawab atas pengurusan Perseroan mencakup antara lain efektivitas dan efisiensi sumber daya, pelaksanaan pengendalian internal, manajemen risiko, pengelolaan berkelanjutan, komunikasi dengan para Pemangku Kepentingan, penyusunan laporan pertanggungjawaban dan pelaksanaan tata kelola perusahaan. Pembagian bidang kerja masing-masing anggota Direksi sesuai dengan Struktur Organisasi, disesuaikan dengan latar belakang pendidikan dan/atau pengalaman masing-masing anggota Direksi yang dapat dilihat pada tabel di bawah ini:

Based on the AGMS on June 16, 2022, the composition of the Company's Board of Directors has changed and consists of a President Director and 4 (four) Directors as stipulated in the Deed of Meeting Resolution of PT Indointernet Tbk No. 113, dated June 16, 2022, made before Dharma Akhyuzi, S.H., Notary in Jakarta. The determination of total members and composition of the Board of Directors has considered the Company's condition, effectiveness of decision making as well as the Company's needs for expertise, knowledge, and experience to face future business development and challenges.

As one of the Company's main organs, the Board of Directors is responsible to the Company's management which induces the effectiveness and efficiency of resources, implementation of internal control, risk management, continuous management, communication with Stakeholders, compilation of accountability report and implementation of corporate governance. The division of duties for each member of the Board of Directors is in accordance with the Organizational Structure, based on the educational background and/or experience of each member of the Board of Director which can be seen in the following table:
