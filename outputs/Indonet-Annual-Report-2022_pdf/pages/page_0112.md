# Pelaksanaan Tugas Unit Audit Internal Tahun 2022

1. Memfasilitasi proses identifikasi dan klasifikasi risiko, termasuk risiko baru yang muncul;
2. Menentukan prioritas audit dan membuat Rencana Audit Internal 2022;
3. Melakukan audit terhadap departemen sesuai dengan rencana audit tahunan;
4. Menindaklanjuti pelaksanaan rekomendasi audit yang disepakati, baik rekomendasi dari internal maupun eksternal audit;
5. Peninjauan atas proses bisnis dan sosialisasi prosedur di Perseroan;
6. Melakukan pengawasan dan pelatihan atas Kebijakan Anti-Suap dan Korupsi;
7. Melakukan audit terhadap penerapan Kebijakan Anti-Suap dan Korupsi;
8. Membuat program untuk mengevaluasi mutu kegiatan audit internal yang dilakukan; dan
9. Monitoring atas kecukupan manajemen risiko yang dijalankan oleh Perseroan.

# Duty Implementation of Internal Audit Unit in 2022

1. Facilitating risk identification and classification process, including new and arising risks;
2. Determining audit priority and preparing Internal Audit Plan of 2022;
3. Auditing department based on the annual audit plan;
4. Following up the implementation of the agreed recommendations from both internal and external audit;
5. Reviewing business processes and socializing the Company's procedures;
6. Overseeing and organizing training on Anti-Bribery and Corruption policy;
7. Auditing the implementation of Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption policy;
8. Designing a program to evaluate the quality of internal audit activities; and
9. Monitoring the adequacy of risk management by the Company.

# SISTEM PENGENDALIAN INTERNAL Internal Control System

Sistem Pengendalian Internal merupakan suatu mekanisme kontrol yang terintegrasi pada proses kerja operasional sehari-hari untuk memberikan tingkat keyakinan yang memadai dalam rangka mencapai sasaran kerja Perseroan secara keseluruhan. Dalam rangka mencapai kegiatan operasional Perseroan yang efektif dan efisien, Satuan Kerja Audit Internal bertugas untuk mengevaluasi efektivitas sistem kontrol yang telah dilaksanakan dan mengidentifikasi perbaikan yang diperlukan.

Pengelolaan pengendalian internal di Perseroan dilaksanakan secara berlapis menggunakan model three lines of defense. Di level first line of defense, Business Process Owner melakukan pengendalian internal dimana efektivitasnya akan dievaluasi oleh fungsi kendali mutu di level second line of defense. Selanjutnya Satuan Kerja Internal Audit akan melakukan evaluasi di level third line of defense melalui kegiatan assurance and consulting pada aspek manajemen risiko, pengendalian, dan tata kelola. Satuan Kerja Internal Audit memberikan rekomendasi atas perbaikan yang dipandang perlu pada pengendalian internal di level 3 yang dinilai belum efektif. Business Process Owner selanjutnya bertanggung jawab untuk menindaklanjuti rekomendasi tersebut, sehingga kelemahan pengendalian internal dapat segera diperbaiki dan risiko pencapaian tujuan proses bisnis dapat segera termitigasi. Assurance atas kepatuhan pada peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dilakukan oleh Satuan Kerja Legal &amp; Compliance.

Satuan Kerja Audit Internal akan menyampaikan laporan kepada Komite Audit terkait hasil evaluasinya atas kecukupan kontrol dan pengawasan internal dalam rangka kepatuhan terhadap kebijakan, prosedur serta evaluasi atas kecukupan manajemen risiko.

Internal Control System is a control mechanism which is integrated to daily operational activities to ensure sufficient beliefs in achieving the Company's overall target. In order to achieve an effective and efficient operational activity in the Company, the Internal Audit Unit is responsible to evaluate the effectiveness of internal control system that has been implemented and identify any necessary improvement that are required.

The management of internal control within the Company is conducted through three lines of defense. At the first line of defense, Business Process Owner implements an internal control which the effectiveness is evaluated by the quality control function as the second line of defense. Next, the Internal Audit Unit will act as the third line of defense through assurance and consulting activities in the aspect of risk management, control, and governance. The Internal Audit Unit provides recommendation on necessary improvements on the internal control level 3 that are considered ineffective. Business process owner is then responsible to follow up the recommendation, so that the weakness in internal control can be immediately restored and risks in target achievement can be mitigated immediately. Assurance on compliance to the prevailing law and regulations is conducted by the Legal &amp; Compliance Unit.

The Internal Audit Unit will report to the Audit Committee on the evaluation result on the adequacy of internal control and supervision to comply with the policies, procedures as well as to evaluate the adequacy of risk management.
