# KODE ETIK
## Code of Conduct

Kode Etik Perusahaan adalah tata perilaku yang ditetapkan oleh Perseroan sebagai Pedoman bersikap dan berperilaku bagi jajaran Perseroan dalam menjalankan usaha Perseroan, menjalankan tugas dan kegiatan pekerjaan sehari-hari, serta berinteraksi dengan pemangku kepentingan termasuk para pemegang saham, regulator, pelanggan, karyawan/rekan kerja, rekanan dan masyarakat. Kode Etik Perusahaan berakar pada tata nilai yang diyakini oleh Perseroan yang mencakup pokok-pokok sebagai berikut:

1. Menjaga Integritas (Integrity)
2. Bersikap Profesional (Professionalism)
3. Menjaga Keunggulan (Excellence)
4. Menjaga Kerahasiaan (Confidentiality)
5. Menghindari Benturan Kepentingan (Conflict of Interests)
6. Anti Korupsi (Anti-Corruption)

Kode Etik terakhir kali disempurnakan melalui Keputusan Direksi No. 003/KEP-INDONET.DIR/II/2021 tentang Kode Etik PT Indointernet Tbk dan telah disosialisasikan kepada seluruh karyawan, dan setiap tahun dilakukan penyegaran. Seluruh karyawan Perseroan termasuk Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi diwajibkan untuk menandatangani Pakta Integritas setiap tahunnya. Pakta Integritas karyawan tidak hanya mencakup Kode Etik Perusahaan, namun juga mencakup pernyataan memahami Peraturan Perusahaan, Kebijakan Anti-Suap dan Korupsi, serta Kebijakan Whistleblower Perseroan.

The Company's Code of Conduct was established as a guideline of attitude and behaviour which are applicable to all of the Company's employees in carrying out the Company's businesses, duties and daily activities, as well as in their interaction with stakeholders, including shareholders, regulators, customers, employees/business partners, partners, and the public. The Company's Code of Conduct is rooted on the values that the Company believed which contains the following principals:

1. Maintaining Integrity
2. Professional Behaviour (Professionalism)
3. Maintaining Excellence
4. Maintaining Confidentiality
5. Avoiding Conflict of Interests
6. Anti-Corruption

The Code of Conduct was last updated through the Board of Directors' Decree No. 003/KEP-INDONET.DIR/II/2021 concerning PT Indointernet Tbk's Code of Conduct and has been socialized to all employees, and refreshment is conducted in every year. All employees of the Company, including the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are required to sign an Integrity Pact yearly. The Integrity Pact is not only containing the Company's Code of Conduct, but also statement of understanding the Company's Regulations, Anti-bribery and Corruption Policy, as well as the Company's Whistleblower Policy.

# KEBIJAKAN PENGUNGKAPAN INFORMASI
## Information Disclosure Policy

Sebagaimana tertuang dalam Pedoman dan Tata Tertib Dewan Komisaris Perseroan, Kebijakan Perseroan terkait pengungkapan informasi oleh anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris mewajibkan anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris untuk secara rutin menyampaikan laporan terkait hal-hal di bawah ini kepada Perseroan melalui Sekretaris Perusahaan.

a. Kepemilikan saham pada Perseroan maupun pada perusahaan lain yang berkedudukan di dalam atau luar negeri.
b. Hubungan keuangan dan hubungan keluarga dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris lain serta anggota Direksi dan termasuk keluarganya.
c. Informasi-informasi lain yang menurut peraturan perundang-undangan wajib diungkapkan kepada publik.

Informasi terkait hal tersebut di atas untuk selanjutnya diungkapkan dalam Laporan Tahunan atau situs web Perseroan.

As stipulated in the Board of Commissioners' Manual, the Company's Policy related to information disclosure by members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners require them to routinely submit reports related to the following subjects to the Company through the Corporate Secretary.

a. Share ownership in the Company or other onshore or offshore companies.
b. Financial and family relationship with members of the Board of Commissioners as well as Board of Directors including with their families.
c. Other informations which are required to be disclosed to the public in accordance with the law and regulations.

Information regarding the above-mentioned subjects will be disclosed in the Annual Report or in the Company's website.
