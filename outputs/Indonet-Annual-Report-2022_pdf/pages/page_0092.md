## Piagam Komite Audit

Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, Komite Audit berpedoman pada Piagam Komite Audit yang ditetapkan oleh Dewan Komisaris melalui SK No. 07/Indonet-Dekom.SK/X/2020 tanggal 27 Oktober 2020. Seluruh anggota Komite wajib mematuhi kode etik yang ditetapkan Perseroan dan memenuhi persyaratan independensi, keahlian, pengalaman, dan integritas sesuai ketentuan yang berlaku.

### Tugas, Tanggung Jawab, dan Wewenang Komite Audit

» Penelaahan atas informasi keuangan Perseroan yang akan dipublikasikan dan/atau dilaporkan kepada Otoritas.
» Penelaahan atas kepatuhan Perseroan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan terkait kegiatan Perseroan.
» Penelaahan atas independensi dan objektivitas Akuntan Publik yang ditunjuk untuk mengaudit pembukuan Perseroan, memberikan rekomendasi penunjukan Kantor Akuntan Publik, dan menilai kecukupan pemeriksaan yang dilakukan oleh Akuntan Publik.
» Penelaahan atas pelaksanaan rencana Audit Internal Perseroan, serta pengamatan Audit Internal terkait pelaksanaan Manajemen Risiko.
» Menyampaikan laporan hasil penelaahan kepada seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris.

### Audit Committee Charter

In carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Audit Committee refers to the Audit Committee Charter which was ratified by the Board of Commissioners through SK No. 07/Indonet-Dekom.SK/X/2020 dated October 27, 2020. All Committee members shall comply with the Company's code of conduct and fulfill the requirements of independency, expertise, experience, and integrity in accordance with the applicable regulations.

### Duties, Responsibilities, and Authorities of the Audit Committee

» Review the Company's financial information that will be published and/or reported to the Authority.
» Review the Company's compliance with law and regulations related to the Company's activities.
» Review the independency and objectivity of the appointed Public Accountant engaged to audit the Company's bookkeeping, providing recommendation on the appointment of Public Accounting Firm, and assessing the adequacy of the audit conducted by the Public Accountant.
» Review the implementation of the Company's Internal Audit Plan, as well as observing Internal Audit related to the implementation of Risk Management.
» Submitting report on the result of the reviews to the Board of Commissioners.
