PT Indointernet Tbk
Laporan Tahunan Terintegrasi 2022 Integrated Annual Report

# Aset

Total aset Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022 tercatat sebesar Rp1,61 triliun, meningkat 26,34% dibandingkan dengan tahun sebelumnya sebesar Rp1,27 triliun. Peningkatan aset tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh peningkatan piutang usaha pihak ketiga sebesar Rp70,8 miliar dan kenaikan aset tetap sebesar Rp277,60 miliar.

# Aset Lancar

Total aset lancar Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022 tercatat sebesar Rp542,31 miliar, meningkat 9,57% dibandingkan dengan tahun sebelumnya sebesar Rp494,92 miliar. Peningkatan aset lancar tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh peningkatan piutang usaha pihak ketiga sebesar Rp70,8 miliar.

# Aset Tidak Lancar

Total aset tidak lancar Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022 tercatat sebesar Rp1,06 triliun, meningkat 37,03% dibandingkan dengan tahun sebelumnya sebesar Rp776,21 miliar. Peningkatan aset tidak lancar tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh kenaikan aset tetap sebesar Rp277,60 miliar sehubungan dengan perluasan bisnis data center EDGE2.

# Liabilitas

Jumlah liabilitas Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022 tercatat sebesar Rp394,51 miliar, meningkat 65,01% dibandingkan sebelumnya sebesar Rp239,09 miliar di tahun 2021. Peningkatan liabilitas tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh peningkatan utang usaha pihak ketiga, akural dan adanya pinjaman Bank senilai Rp36,89 miliar yang diperoleh Perseroan.

# Liabilitas Jangka Pendek

Jumlah liabilitas jangka pendek Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022 tercatat sebesar Rp324,14 miliar, meningkat 48,56% dibandingkan sebelumnya sebesar Rp218,19 miliar di tahun 2021. Hal tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh adanya kenaikan Utang usaha pihak ketiga dan akural senilai Rp36,92 miliar.

# Liabilitas Jangka Panjang

Jumlah liabilitas jangka panjang Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022 tercatat sebesar Rp70,37 miliar, meningkat 236,78% dari sebesar Rp20,90 miliar di tahun 2021. Hal tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh adanya utang bank senilai Rp36,89 miliar.

# Ekuitas

Jumlah ekuitas pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022 adalah Rp1,21 triliun, meningkat 17,38% dari Rp1,03 triliun di tahun 2021. Kenaikan ini terutama disebabkan perolehan total penghasilan komprehensif tahun berjalan Rp179,27 miliar.

# Assets

The Company's total assets as of December 31, 2022 was recorded at Rp1.61 trillion, increased by 26.34% compared to the prior year of Rp1.27 trillion. The increase was mainly due to an increase of third-party trade receivables of Rp70.8 billion and an increase in fixed assets of Rp277.60 billion.

# Current Assets

The Company's total current assets as of December 31, 2022 was recorded at Rp542.31 billion, increased by 9.57% compared to the prior year of Rp494.92 billion. The increase was mainly due to the increase of third-party trade receivables of Rp70.8 billion.

# Non-Current Assets

The Company's total non-current assets as of December 31, 2022 was recorded at Rp1.06 trillion, increased by 37.03% compared to the prior year of Rp776.21 billion. The increase was mainly due to the increase in three of fixed assets by Rp277.60 billion in line with the expansion of data center EDGE2.

# Liabilities

The Company's liabilities as of December 31, 2022 was recorded at Rp394.51 billion, increased by 65.01% compared to the previous from Rp239.09 billion in 2021. The increase of liabilities was due to the increase of third party trade payables, accruals and bank loans amounting to Rp36.89 billion were obtained by the Company.

# Short-term Liabilities

The Company's total short-term liabilities as of December 31, 2022 was recorded at Rp324.14 billion, increased by 48.56% compared to the previous of Rp218.19 billion in 2021. The increase was mainly due to an increase in third-party trade payables and accrual of Rp36.92 billion.

# Long-term Liabilities

The Company's total long-term liabilities as of December 31, 2022 was recorded at Rp70.37 billion, an increase of 236.78% from Rp20.90 billion in 2021. This was mainly due to bank loans which amounted to Rp36.89 billion.

# Equity

Total equity as of December 31, 2022 was Rp1.21 trillion, increased by 17.38% of Rp1.03 trillion in 2021. The increase was mainly due to the achievement on total comprehensive income for the year amounted to Rp179.27 billion.
