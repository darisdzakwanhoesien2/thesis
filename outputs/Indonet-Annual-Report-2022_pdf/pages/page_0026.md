# Prospek Bisnis

Meskipun adanya ancaman resesi global seperti yang diprediksi oleh banyak ekonom, kami melihat industri pusat data di Indonesia akan mengalami pertumbuhan yang lebih tinggi di tahun-tahun mendatang. Hal ini didukung oleh perkiraan pertumbuhan perekonomian Indonesia yang berada pada kisaran 4,5%-5,3% pada tahun 2023, dimana pertumbuhan ekonomi tersebut juga akan berdampak pada peningkatan kebutuhan pusat data.

Menurut Statista, pendapatan di pasar Pusat Data di Indonesia diproyeksikan mencapai US$3,01 miliar pada tahun 2023. Sementara itu, pendapatan di pasar Infrastruktur Jaringan juga diproyeksikan mencapai US$2,08 miliar pada tahun 2023.

Untuk menangkap peluang pasar ini, kami akan tetap fokus pada empat segmen bisnis kami termasuk pengembangan pusat data melalui anak perusahaan kami. Selain itu, kami akan melakukan perbaikan terus-menerus dalam banyak aspek dan menjaga prioritas kami tetap pada jalurnya.

Dengan dukungan Digital Edge yang memiliki tim berpengalaman dalam hal desain dan konstruksi pusat data di industri, kami optimis dapat mencapai pertumbuhan yang terus meningkat di tahun-tahun mendatang.

Furthermore, we continued to improve the competencies of our employees through training and development programs tailored to their needs.

In order to ensure that we understand what our employees' need in achieving their objectives, we consistently conduct training evaluations to identify the employee learning gaps.

As a manifestation of our commitment to sustainable energy, the EDGE1 facility began to use renewable energy solutions after receiving the Renewable Energy Certificate and becoming the first data center in Indonesia to utilize sustainable energy initiatives from PLN, a state-owned electricity company. By using green energy, we can help mitigate greenhouse gas emissions, thus reducing the impact on the environment.

In addition, EDGE2 will utilize renewable energy solutions and incorporate green design principles to reduce its carbon footprint. A combination of market leading design and innovative technologies will enable EDGE2 to become a leading green data center and contribute towards Indonesia's renewable energy goals.

Through a dedicated team we have conducted numerous CSR activities, particularly in the field of education. We believe that education is vital to the formation of character and intelligence and achieving long term prosperity. As part of this we provided undergraduate scholarships for five promising students from low income backgrounds in Adong Village - Kalimantan. We hope these students will further contribute to the economic development of their communities after they graduate.

# Business Prospects

Despite the risk of global recession as predicted by many economists, we believe the Indonesian data center industry will experience sustained growth in the coming years. This is underpinned by the Indonesian economy which is expected to grow in the 4.5%-5.3% range in 2023, leading to increasing needs for data centers.

According to Statista, revenue in the Data Center market in Indonesia is projected to reach US$3.01 billion in 2023. Meanwhile, the market's largest segment is Network Infrastructure with a projected market volume of US$2.08 billion in 2023.

To seize this market opportunity, we will continue to focus on our four business segments including the development of data centers through our subsidiary. We will also continue to improve our broader business operations and services and keep our priorities on track.

With the support of Digital Edge which has one of the most experienced teams in data center design and construction in the industry, we are optimistic we will achieve the next level of growth in the coming years.
