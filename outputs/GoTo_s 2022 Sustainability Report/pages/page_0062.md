## Meningkatkan Efisiensi

Dalam beberapa tahun terakhir, kami makin memahami tentang keterkaitan antara keberlanjutan dan kinerja keuangan, dan bagaimana keberlanjutan dapat meningkatkan metrik keuangan dan manfaat biaya. Bagi GoTo, fokus dan prioritas kami pada profitabilitas tidak mengesampingkan upaya keberlanjutan kami. Untuk peta jalan Nol Emisi Karbon, kami telah mengidentifikasi, menguji, dan meluncurkan inisiatif baru yang dirancang untuk mengurangi jejak emisi sekaligus meningkatkan efisiensi biaya serta efektivitas operasional kami.

Tokopedia's HQ continues to utilize the solar panels installed by our partner Xurya, generating supplementary energy for the building. In assessing the feasibility and impact of our solar panels, we acknowledge that rooftop space availability and volatile weather patterns will always constrain how much energy can be generated. This generated electricity may not address all the energy needs of a given location; however, these kinds of projects support the growing share of renewable energy and we hope that over time, our contributions to the mix will only expand.

## Turning to Efficiency

In recent years, there has been an increased understanding of the linkage between sustainability and financial performance, and how sustainability can lead to improved financial metrics and cost benefits. For GoTo, our focus and prioritization of profitability does not take away from our sustainability efforts; in fact, it has reinforced them. For our Zero Emissions roadmap, we have identified, tested, and launched new initiatives designed to reduce our emissions footprint with more efficient and cost-effective operations.
