## Elektrifikasi Jaringan Logistik

Kami juga telah mulai melakukan transisi kendaraan roda empat (4W)²⁸ ke EV mulai dari logistik, di mana ada siklus rutin mengemudi dan waktu rehat untuk pengisian baterai. Pada tahun 2022, kami bermitra dengan Mitsubishi Motors untuk meluncurkan program percontohan yang menggunakan Minicab MiEV blind van²⁹ untuk berbagai keperluan logistik, seperti pengiriman GoSend ke pengguna akhir dan pengiriman dari gudang ke fasilitas pemenuhan pesanan atau fulfillment. Hasil dari proyek percontohan tahun 2022 menjadi sumber informasi bahwa proyek percontohan yang diluncurkan pada tahun 2023, menggunakan EV untuk pengiriman dari gudang ke fasilitas fulfillment. Ini akan dilakukan melalui kerja sama dengan PT Sokonindo Automobile (DFSK) dan PT Krama Yudha Tiga Berlian Motors (Distributor Resmi Mitsubishi FUSO), untuk menguji blind van dan blind truck. Secara paralel, kami akan terus mengeksplorasi opsi baru dan memperluas opsi yang ada sebagai bagian dari transisi kegiatan operasional gudang dan logistik kami ke EV serta listrik yang bersumber dari energi terbarukan.

Proyek percontohan lebih lanjut dengan mitra lain sedang direncanakan untuk tahun 2023.

Another important aspect of Electrum’s plan is the development of software and data analytics tools to optimize our use of EVs. These developments will be essential in identifying the availability of battery swap stations and monitoring the health of Electrum’s EV fleet. Electrum has a dedicated team of software engineers and experts to further develop the best experience for drivers, customers, and the EV ecosystem.

Overall, GoTo’s efforts to transition its mobility fleet to EVs are an important step forward in the race toward a cleaner, electric mobility system in Indonesia. With the size and reach of our ecosystem, we will continue to work closely with our stakeholders, partners, and the Government to ensure the system is sustainable, resilient, and accessible, and can best serve our ecosystem today and for the future.

## Electrifying Our Logistics Network

We have also begun transitioning our four-wheeled (4W)²⁸ vehicles to EVs starting from logistics, where there are routine driving cycles and downtime for charging. In 2022, we partnered with Mitsubishi Motors to launch a pilot program utilizing a Minicab MiEV blind van²⁹ for various logistical purposes, such as last-mile GoSend deliveries and mid-mile deliveries between warehouses. Outcomes from the 2022 pilot informed the pilots we will launch 2023, using EVs for mid-mile deliveries. These will be done in partnership with PT Sokonindo Automobile (DFSK) and PT Krama Yudha Tiga Berlian Motors (Authorized Distributor of Mitsubishi FUSO), to test out their blind vans and trucks. In parallel, we will continue to explore new and expand our existing options as part of our transition of warehouse operations and logistics to EVs as well as renewable energy-sourced power.

Further pilots with other partners are being planned for 2023.
