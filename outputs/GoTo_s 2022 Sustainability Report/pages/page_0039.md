Partnership and collaboration with the government are fundamental to our approach of delivering meaningful impact from the work we do. The primary outcomes of our engagement with the Government in 2022 include:
• Becoming a strategic partner of the Government in empowering and digitizing MSMEs, working with more than 20 government entities through GoNusantara MSME Training, Kelas Maju Digital, and Bangga Buatan Indonesia (BBI)
• Partnerships with public transport operators to connect customers to seamless public transportation options via GoTransit
• Promoting micro and small businesses, with a focus on female entrepreneurs
• Successful collaboration for the Government’s hosting of the Group of 20 (G20) Leaders Summit in Bali, including the promotion of Indonesia’s digital economy and its opportunities, showcasing GoTo’s MSME partners, and mobilizing GoTo’s EVs, as the G20’s official transportation partner
• Facilitating public participation in efforts aligned to GoTo’s Three Zeros and government targets across waste and emissions reduction, and social protection programs.  |
