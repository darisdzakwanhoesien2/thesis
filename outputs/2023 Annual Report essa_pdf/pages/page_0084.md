PT Panca Amara Utama menerima penghargaan dari Pemerintah Kabupaten Banggai Laut atas kegiatan pelestarian dan penangkaran Maleo melalui konservasi Ex Situ.

Piagam penghargaan diserahkan langsung oleh Bupati Banggai Laut, Bapak Sofyan Kaepa pada acara puncak Festival Malabot Tumbe, 4 Desember 2023.

Piagam penghargaan tersebut diberikan sebagai tanda terima kasih Masyarakat Banggai Laut atas upaya PT Panca Amara Utama yang memiliki peran penting sehingga pelaksanaan ritual adat baik Mombowa Tumpe di Batui maupun Malabot Tumbe di Banggai Laut tetap terlaksana.

PT Panca Amara Utama received an award from the Government of Banggai Laut Regency for its Maleo conservation and breeding activities through Ex Situ conservation.

The award certificate was handed over directly by the Regent of Banggai Laut, Mr. Sofyan Kaepa at the Malabot Tumbe Festival, 4 December 2023.

The award certificate was given as a token of gratitude from the people of Banggai Laut for the efforts of PT Panca Amara Utama, which has an important role so that the implementation of traditional rituals both Mombowa Tumpe in Batui and Malabot Tumbe in Banggai Laut is still carried out.
