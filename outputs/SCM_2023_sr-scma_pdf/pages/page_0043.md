## PENERAPAN KEBERLANJUTAN
### CHALLENGES IN THE IMPLEMENTATION OF SUSTAINABILITY

[OJK E.5]

Topik material dalam laporan ini menjadi bagian dari permasalahan yang kami hadapi dalam penerapan keberlanjutan Perseroan.

The Company considers that the expectations we have collected through the stakeholder engagement process become our biggest challenge to respond and fulfill these needs. The material topics in this report are part of the issues we face in implementing the sustainability in the Company.
