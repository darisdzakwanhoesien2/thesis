# DRIVING CONTINUOUS IMPACT
# FOR BRIGHTER TOMORROW

Memasuki era televisi digital, PT Surya Citra Media Tbk menajamkan visinya untuk mempertahankan posisinya yang terdepan di industri media kreatif di Indonesia. Perseroan dalam hal ini meningkatkan kualitas produk dan layanan pada berbagai unit usaha dalam ekosistemnya melalui akselerasi investasi dan inovasi di beberapa konten digital. Hal ini dengan sendirinya meningkatkan daya saing perusahaan sehingga mampu merealisasikan peluang yang ada, salah satunya dengan meraih Hak Siar Eksklusif (Exclusive Live Broadcasts) untuk salah satu event olahraga bergengsi, Piala Dunia U-17 2023. Di saat yang sama, Perseroan juga mendorong penerapan prinsip-prinsip ESG (Environment, Social, and Governance) sehingga dapat membangun keunggulan kompetitif yang akan mendukung pencapaian pertumbuhan bisnis yang berkualitas.

Entering the digital television era, PT Surya Citra Media Tbk sharpened its vision to maintain its leading position in the creative media industry in Indonesia. In this case, the Company improves the quality of products and services in various business units in its ecosystem through accelerated investment and innovation in several digital content. This in itself increases the company's competitiveness so that it is able to realize existing opportunities, one of which is by obtaining Exclusive Live Broadcasts for one of the prestigious sporting events, the 2023 U-17 World Cup. At the same time, the Company also encourages the implementation of ESG (Environment, Social, and Governance) principles so that we can build competitive advantages that will support the achievement of quality business growth.
