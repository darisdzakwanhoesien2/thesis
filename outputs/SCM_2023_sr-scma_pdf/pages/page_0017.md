# SEKILAS TENTANG SCM

## SCM IN BRIEF

[GRI 2-1]

Selama 25 tahun, Perseroan telah membangun reputasi sebagai perusahaan media dan hiburan yang terkemuka di Indonesia dengan total audience share dari tiga stasiun televisi Free-to-Air nasional yang dimilikinya mencapai 34,9% per 31 Desember 2023. Perseroan senantiasa mengikuti perkembangan industri media dan hiburan yang sangat dinamis dengan didukung oleh teknologi maju dan infrastruktur digital yang dimilikinya. Perseroan juga terus bertransformasi agar dapat meningkatkan kualitas program, konten, tayangan, dan layanan di bidang media. Ekspansi bisnis SCM memposisikannya menjadi salah satu penyedia konten berkualitas dan jasa periklanan, manajemen artis, serta manajemen fasilitas siaran dan produksi film yang terkemuka.

For 25 years, the Company has built a reputation as a leading media and entertainment company in Indonesia with a combined audience share of its three national Free-to-Air television stations reaching 34.9% as of December 31, 2023. The Company keeps up with the dynamic development of the media and entertainment industry with the support of its advanced technology and digital infrastructure. The Company also continues to transform in order to improve the quality of its media programs, content, broadcasts and services. SCM's business expansion has positioned itself as one of the leading providers of quality content and advertising services provider of quality contents and advertising services, artist management, as well as broadcasting facility management and a leading film producer.
