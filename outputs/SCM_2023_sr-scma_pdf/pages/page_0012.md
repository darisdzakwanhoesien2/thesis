Strategi Perseroan yang senantiasa mendorong transformasi yang didukung oleh inovasi dan investasi secara berkelanjutan dinilai berhasil meningkatkan kinerja core business SCM untuk menjadi yang terdepan di jajaran televisi Free-To-Air (FTA) nasional. Secara keseluruhan, pada tahun 2023 seluruh televisi FTA milik Perseroan berhasil mencatatkan rata-rata audience share 34,9%, di mana SCTV memimpin dengan rata-rata audience share 14,3% sedangkan Indosiar meraih rata-rata audience share 13,0%. Sementara itu, Mentari TV berhasil menempatkan dirinya di posisi kelima dalam jajaran televisi FTA nasional dengan 7,6% rata-rata audience share. Kinerja TV FTA kami yang membanggkan itu menegaskan bahwa keberadaan TV FTA nasional ternyata masih paling diminati oleh pemirsa di tanah Air.

Prestasi Grup SCM yang cukup signifikan lainnya dicatatkan oleh platform Vidio. Kualitas konten maupun layanan Vidio dengan memanfaatkan Machine Learning Artificial Intelligence dan produksi Original Series-nya berhasil memposisikannya sebagai platform OTT paling popular dengan jumlah pemasangan pada Smart TV mencapai lebih dari 20 juta. Bahkan data Media Partner Asia (MPA) menunjukkan Vidio berhasil menyaingi kepopuleran pemain OTT global dan regional, seperti Netflix dan Disney+, dengan menduduki posisi ke-1, baik dalam hal jumlah Pengguna Aktif Bulanan (Monthly Active Users/MAU) di kawasan Asia Tenggara maupun jumlah durasi streaming pada kuartal III/2023. Pada kuartal IV/2023, Vidio mencapai posisi platform OTT No. 1 dalam hal jumlah pelanggan terbanyak di Indonesia.

Vidio juga dikenal dengan julukan "home of sports" melalui berbagai tayangan konten olahraga lokal seperti: BRI Liga 1, Proliga, Livoli, hingga konten olahraga internasional seperti: Premier League, NBA, UEFA Champions League, Serie A, La Liga, Olimpiade, FIFA World Cup Qatar 2023 hingga yang terbaru, yaitu penyelenggaraan FIFA U-17 World Cup 2023 di mana Indonesia untuk pertama kalinya dalam sejarah ditunjuk sebagai tuan rumah.

Lalu, pada lini usaha content production, Perseroan berhasil mencapai kinerja gemilang melalui penayangan drama mini seri produksi Base Entertainment, 'Gadis Kretek', dan drama berkualitas lain yang diproduksi oleh Sinemart dan Screenplay. 'Gadis Kretek' berhasil meraih posisi No. 1 di Netflix Indonesia dan masuk dalam jajaran Top-10 di empat negara lainnya.

Perseroan berhasil menyengkkan sejumlah event yang digagas bersama RANS Entertainment dengan kapabilitas yang dimiliki internal perusahaan untuk menarik minat para pengiklan. Salah satu program kompetisi hiburan olahraga selebritas Indonesia hasil kolaborasi SCTV dan RANS yang menjadi program unggulan dan mendapat sambutan hangat dari masyarakat adalah Turnamen Olahraga Selebriti Indonesia (TOSI). Turnamen yang melibatkan selebritas papan atas Indonesia itu memainkan empat cabang olahraga, yaitu bulu tangkis, tenis, tenis meja, dan atletik. Melihat animo masyarakat yang begitu besar, TOSI kembali digelar di tahun 2024 dengan mempertandingkan cabang olahraga baru, yakni renang.

The Company's strategy that continues to drive transformation is supported by continuous innovation and investment, which has successfully increased SCM's core business performance to be at the forefront of the national Free-To-Air (FTA) television lineup. Overall, in 2023, all of the Company's FTA televisions recorded an average audience share of 34.9%, with SCTV leading with an average audience share of 14.3% while Indosiar achieved an average audience share of 13.0%. Meanwhile, Mentari TV managed to place itself in fifth position in the national FTA television lineup with 7.6% average audience share. The impressive performance of our FTA TV confirms that national FTA TV remains as the viewers' favourite in the country.

Another significant achievement of SCM Group was recorded by the Vidio platform. The quality of Vidio's content and services that utilize Machine Learning Artificial Intelligence and its Original Series production has positioned it as the most popular OTT platform with more than 20 million installations on Smart TVs. In fact, Media Partner Asia (MPA) data shows that Vidio managed to rival the popularity of global and regional OTT players, such as Netflix and Disney+ by occupying the top position, both in terms of the number of Monthly Active Users (MAU) in the Southeast Asia region and the number of streaming durations in the third quarter of 2023. In the fourth quarter of 2023, Vidio became the No. 1 OTT platform in terms of the highest number of subscribers in Indonesia.

Vidio is also known as the "home of sports" through various local sports content shows such as: BRI Liga 1, Proliga, Livoli, to international sports content such as: Premier League, NBA, UEFA Champions League, Serie A, La Liga, Olympics, FIFA World Cup Qatar 2023 and most recently, the FIFA U-17 World Cup 2023 where Indonesia was appointed as the host for the first time in history.

Then, in the content production business line, the Company managed to record a brilliant performance through the airing of a mini-series drama produced by Base Entertainment, 'Gadis Kretek', and other quality dramas produced by Sinemart and Screenplay. 'Gadis Kretek' secured the No. 1 position on Netflix Indonesia and entered the Top-10 in four other countries.

The Company successfully synergized a number of events in collaboration with RANS Entertainment and its internal capabilities to attract advertisers. One of the Indonesian celebrity sports entertainment competition programs resulting from the collaboration of SCTV and RANS, which has become a flagship program and received rave reviews from the public, is the Turnamen Olahraga Selebriti Indonesia (TOSI). The tournament involves Indonesia's top celebrities to play in four sports, including badminton, tennis, table tennis, and athletics. Seeing the immense public interest, TOSI will be held again in 2024 by adding a new sport, that is swimming.
