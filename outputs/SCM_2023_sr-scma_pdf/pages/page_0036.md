Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi menjabat selama 5 (lima) tahun untuk satu periode dan dapat dipilih kembali setelah masa jabatannya berakhir. Komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan telah memenuhi unsur keberagaman yang diterapkan oleh Perseroan dalam mendukung pengambilan keputusan yang efektif dan adil, serta telah mempertimbangkan peraturan yang berlaku di bidang Pasar Modal.

Dewan Komisaris, sebagai organ pengawasan, bertanggung jawab untuk mengawasi semua kegiatan manajemen Perseroan yang dilakukan oleh Direksi serta menjalankan tugas-tugas khusus sesuai dengan mandat yang diberikan oleh Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) dan berdasarkan ketentuan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Selain itu, Dewan Komisaris berperan dalam memberikan konsultasi dan nasihat kepada Direksi serta komite-komite khusus yang dibentuk oleh Dewan Komisaris. Peran dan fungsi Dewan Komisaris ini telah diatur secara rinci dalam Piagam Dewan Komisaris Perseroan, yang selaras dengan ketentuan yang terdapat dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan Undang-Undang No. 40 Tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas.

Di sisi lain, Direksi Perseroan bertanggung jawab antara lain untuk mengelola aktivitas Perseroan, merumuskan strategi sesuai dengan visi dan misi yang telah ditetapkan dan disetujui oleh Pemegang Saham. Selain itu, Direksi bertanggung jawab atas pelaksanaan kegiatan operasional Perseroan, termasuk kegiatan administrasi dan pendukung lainnya. Pencatatan dan pelaporan hasil kegiatan operasional merupakan bagian integral dari tanggung jawab, yang dilakukan melalui laporan keuangan, laporan manajemen, dan laporan lainnya. Dengan demikian, Direksi memiliki peran kunci dalam memastikan keseluruhan fungsi Perseroan berjalan efisien dan sesuai dengan rencana strategis yang telah ditetapkan.

The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors serve for 5 (five) years for one period and may be reappointed after their term expires. The composition of the Company's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors has fulfilled the element of diversity applied by the Company in supporting effective and fair decision making, and has taken into account the prevailing regulations in the Capital Market.

The Board of Commissioners, as a supervisory organ, is responsible for overseeing all of the Company's management activities carried out by the Board of Directors as well as carrying out special assignments in accordance with the mandate given by the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) and based on the prevailing laws and regulations. In addition, the Board of Commissioners has a role in providing consultation and advice to the Board of Directors and special committees established by the Board of Commissioners. The roles and functions of the Board of Commissioners have been regulated in detail in the Company's Board of Commissioners Charter, which is in line with the provisions contained in the Company's Articles of Association and Law No. 40 of 2007 on Limited Liability Companies.

On the other hand, the Company's Board of Directors is responsible to manage the Company's activities, formulate strategies in accordance with the vision and mission that have been set and approved by the Shareholders. Furthermore, the Board of Directors is responsible for the implementation of the Company's operational activities, including administrative and other supporting activities. Recording and reporting the results of operational activities is an integral part of the responsibility, which is performed through financial reports, management reports, and other reports. As such, the Board of Directors has a key role in ensuring that the overall functions of the Company run efficiently and in accordance with the established strategic plans.
