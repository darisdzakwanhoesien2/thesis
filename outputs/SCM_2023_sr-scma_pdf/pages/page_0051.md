PT Surya Citra Media Tbk
Laporan Keberlanjutan 2023 Sustainability Report

Perseroan menunjuk Direktur Keuangan sebagai pihak yang bertanggung jawab terhadap pengelolaan dan pengawasan kinerja ekonomi Perseroan serta mengambil keputusan penting terkait keuangan keberlanjutan. Hasil pengelolaan dan pengawasan serta pencapaian kinerja ekonomi SCM dilaporkan kepada Direktur Utama Perseroan secara berkala.

Pada tahun 2023, kinerja ekonomi Perseroan menunjukkan penurunan dibandingkan dengan kinerja ekonomi tahun 2022. Sejumlah tantangan ekonomi turut membayangi kinerja ekonomi SCM yang bergantung pada pendapatan iklan yang ditayangkan pada platform media milik SCM. [GRI 3-3]

The Company appoints the Finance Director to be in charge of managing and overseeing the Company's economic performance and making important decisions related to financial sustainability. The results of SCM's economic performance management, oversight and achievements are reported to the President Director of the Company on a regular basis.

In 2023, the Company's economic performance showed a decline compared to the economic performance in 2022. A number of economic challenges also cast a shadow on SCM's economic performance, which is reliant on advertising revenue from SCM's media platforms. [GRI 3-3]

# Perbandingan Target dan Kinerja Produksi, Portofolio, Target Pembiayaan atau Investasi, Pendapatan Laba Rugi

Comparison of Target and Performance of Production, Portfolio, Financing or Investment Targets, Profit or Loss Revenue
[GRI 201-2] [OJK F.2] [OJK F.3]

Secara umum, realisasi kinerja ekonomi Perseroan telah memenuhi target yang ditetapkan. Bahkan untuk mendukung kinerja keberlanjutan, Perseroan berupaya meningkatkan nilai investasi pada infrastruktur maupun fasilitas pendukung kegiatan operasional sehingga setiap karyawan memiliki kelengkapan perangkat kerja untuk menunjang pelaksanaan tugasnya.

In general, the realization of the Company's economic performance has fulfilled the specified targets. In fact, to support sustainability performance, the Company strives to increase the value of investment in infrastructure and supporting facilities for operational activities so that every employee is fully equipped with work tools to facilitate the fulfillment of their duties.
