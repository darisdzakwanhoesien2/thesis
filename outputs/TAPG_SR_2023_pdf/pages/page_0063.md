# Environmental Management System

TAPG's Environmental Management System is a framework that helps TAPG to achieve its environmental goals through several steps, such as identification of environmental aspects and impacts, compliance with regulations related to environmental management, determination of targets and programs, evaluation of environmental programs, and internal and external audits of the Environmental Management System.

TAPG's Environmental Management System refers to the Company's sustainability policies as the basis for achieving its business goals. Through this policy, TAPG succeeded in achieving several targets in the environmental sector as follows:
