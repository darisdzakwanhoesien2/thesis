# AMMAN at a Glance

AMMAN operates the second-largest copper-and-gold mining company in Indonesia, operating the Batu Hijau mine on the island of Sumbawa. AMMAN has a fully integrated operation encompassing exploration and mine development, mining, processing, and smelting in the near future.
