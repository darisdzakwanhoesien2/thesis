# Material Topics

Identifying our material topics is an essential step in managing our sustainability performance, as it helps us identify the issues that matter most to our stakeholders and business. This allows us to focus our resources and efforts where they will have the greatest impact, and to continuously improve our performance.

In 2021, we conducted a materiality assessment to identify, understand and prioritise our material topics. This was subsequently reviewed in 2022. The following diagram summarises our process:

# Desktop Analysis

We conducted a comprehensive desktop analysis of our external environment to identify ESG issues and trends within the mining industry. The objective of the analysis is to develop a long-list of ESG topics, relevant to the industry, based on the perspectives of established reporting frameworks, ESG raters, investors and thought leaders.

For our analysis, we utilised standards and frameworks such as the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) Standards for Metals and Mining, the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards, Coppermark, International Council on Mining and Metals (ICMM)'s Principles and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs).

# Internal and External Stakeholder Engagement

In this step, we engaged with our internal and external stakeholders to identify the issues that are most relevant to our business operations and stakeholders. We conduct interviews with our senior management, customers, local government and representatives from mining associations to gain insights into their perspectives on best practices in the industry.

Through this process, we aim to understand the specific concerns and expectations of our stakeholders and to contextualise the long-list of topics to our business and operations.

# AMMAN's Material Topics

We consolidated the results of our desktop analysis and stakeholder engagement to identify the final prioritised list of material ESG topics for our organisation.

The final topics were reviewed by members of our ESG team and were finalised and approved by our Board of Directors.

The assessment we conducted predominantly used a single materiality approach. In the coming years, we aim to implement a double materiality approach to comprehensively assess both external impacts and internal factors when identifying and evaluating our material topics.
