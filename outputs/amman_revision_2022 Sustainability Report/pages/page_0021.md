# Mining

Higher grade ore is hauled directly to primary crushers as process plant feed, other ores are either directly crushed, placed on stockpiles for blending or processing at a later date. Waste rock is hauled to the waste rock dump where it is progressively contoured, encapsulated by soils, and then seeded to rehabilitate the land.
