## Our Performance

## Water Withdrawal, Discharge, and Consumption

In 2022, we extracted a combined volume of 225 million  $\mathrm{m}^3$  of water $^{34}$  from both groundwater and seawater sources. Overall, including surface water captured and recycled at our operations, our total water consumption amounted to 274 million  $\mathrm{m}^3$ .

According to the Aqueduct tool developed by the World Resources Institute (WRI), the Sumbawa Island is classified as a region with a low to medium level of baseline water stress, ranging from  $10\%$  to  $20\%$ , implying a lower likelihood of a high-stress water situation occurring. Nevertheless, we recognise that Sejorong Well, one of our primary water sources, can become stressed during the dry season, and account for this in our operational plans.
