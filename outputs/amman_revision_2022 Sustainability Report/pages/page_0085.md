# AMMAN Tailings Facility

The tailings at AMMAN consist of a slurry, comprising 25% to 30% of solids, which are first sent to a deaeration tank to eliminate air bubbles. Subsequently, the tailings flow through an overland steel pipeline before being discharged through a seabed pipeline outfall at a water depth of 125 metres.
