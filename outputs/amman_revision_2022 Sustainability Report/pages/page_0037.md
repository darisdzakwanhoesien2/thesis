## Management Approach

At AMMAN, our Code of Business Ethics and Conduct ("Code") is a comprehensive document that outlines our ethical standards and expectations for employees and business partners.

It covers a wide range of topics, including conflicts of interest, compliance with applicable laws and regulations, and respect for human rights. We require strict adherence to our Code from our workforce, and our employees are expected to sign and acknowledge their comprehension on an annual basis. Our Code is accessible on our website here.
