# LAPORAN DIREKSI

Report of the Board of Directors

Invasi Rusia ke Ukraina sejak 24 Februari 2022, membuat Rusia dijatuhi berbagai macam sanksi ekonomi dan non-ekonomi oleh negara-negara Barat. Sanksi ini langsung berpengaruh pada harga-harga komoditas, energi, dan bahan pangan karena Rusia dan Ukraina memiliki posisi penting dalam rantai pasokan pangan dan energi global. Konflik Rusia-Ukraina dan sanksi Barat terhadap Rusia menimbulkan krisis ekonomi baru. Gangguan rantai pasokan yang membuat harga komoditas pangan dan energi naik pada akhirnya mendorong tingkat inflasi global ke level yang tinggi.

Sejumlah bank sentral seperti di Amerika Serikat (AS), Inggris, dan Uni Eropa (UE) berupaya menahan laju inflasi melalui pengetatan kebijakan moneter dengan menaikkan suku bunga acuan. Hingga akhir tahun 2022, Bank Sentral AS (Federal Reserve) menaikkan suku bunga acuan Fed Fund Rate (FFR) tujuh kali pada tahun 2022 hingga mencapai 4,25%-4,5% pada akhir tahun. Secara kumulatif, sejak Maret hingga Desember 2022, FFR telah naik 425 basis points (pbs) hingga mencapai level tertinggi dalam 15 tahun terakhir. Kebijakan The Fed tersebut memicu penguatan Dolar AS terhadap mata uang utama dunia. Indeks Dolar AS sempat menyentuh angka indeks tertinggi 114 pada Oktober 2022.

Inflasi yang tinggi akibat harga komoditas, kenaikan suku bunga, dan volatilitas pasar keuangan telah menyebabkan pertumbuhan ekonomi dunia melambat signifikan. Sebagian negara yang memiliki hubungan ekonomi yang erat, baik dengan Rusia maupun Ukraina, sudah diambang resesi. Sebagian lainnya sudah meluncur ke dalam resesi ditandai dengan pertumbuhan negatif selama dua triwulan sebelumnya.

# Ekonomi Indonesia Tumbuh Impresif

Memasuki tahun 2022, pemulihan kinerja ekonomi Indonesia menghadapi beberapa tantangan. Di dalam negeri, Indonesia masih berada dalam kondisi pandemi COVID-19, di mana kasus varian Omicron menyebar di awal tahun. Di sisi lain, Indonesia jauh lebih siap menghadapi varian Omicron dibanding varian Delta. Omicron memiliki gejala yang lebih ringan, sehingga tidak mengakibatkan kepadatan pasien di rumah sakit. Russia's invasion of Ukraine on February 24, 2022 resulted in economic and non-economic sanctions by Western countries. Commodity, energy, and food prices were directly affected by these sanctions as Russia and Ukraine play essential roles in the global food and energy supply chains. The Russia-Ukraine conflict and Western sanctions created a new economic crisis. Global inflation was pushed to high levels by rising food and energy commodity prices caused by supply chain disruptions.

A number of central banks, including those in the United States (US), Britain, and the European Union (EU), attempted to contain inflation by tightening monetary policy through raising their benchmark interest rates. The US Federal Reserve extended its benchmark Fed Fund Rate (FFR) seven times in 2022 to hit 4.25%-4.5% by the end of the year. The FFR increased by 425 basis points (pbs) from March to December 2022, to reach its highest level in the last 15 years, triggering the strengthening of the US Dollar against major world currencies. The US Dollar Index hit its highest level of 114 in October 2022.

High inflation due to rising commodity prices, increasing interest rates, and financial market volatility caused world economic growth to slow down significantly. Countries with close economic relations with both Russia and Ukraine were already on the verge of recession, while others slipped into recession marked by negative growth for two consecutive quarters.

# Indonesia's Economy Grew Impressively

The recovery of Indonesia's economic performance faced several hurdles as it entered 2022. Domestically, Indonesia was still amidst the COVID-19 pandemic with the emergence of rampant cases of the Omicron variant at the beginning of the year. However, Indonesia was far better prepared to handle the Omicron variant than the Delta variant. Community mobility was not significantly disturbed, with most people more confident about public activities as they had received the third dose of the vaccine.
