Kenaikan harga minyak mentah dunia dan ICP (Indonesian Crude Price) memaksa pemerintah untuk menyesuaikan harga bahan bakar minyak (BBM) pada 3 September 2022. Kenaikan harga BBM menimbulkan dampak lanjutan terutama pada harga bahan pokok dan biaya transportasi sehingga mendorong laju inflasi ke posisi tertinggi 5,95% pada bulan Oktober. Berdasarkan data Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS), laju Inflasi tahunan Indonesia pada tahun 2022 mencapai 5,51%, lebih tinggi dari target pemerintah sebesar 3,0% dan jauh lebih tinggi dibandingkan inflasi tahun 2021 sebesar 1,87%.

Memasuki tahun 2022, di tengah kondisi ketidakpastian perekonomian global. Pertumbuhan ekonomi triwulan I 2022 tetap kuat dengan capaian 5,01%, tidak jauh berbeda dengan triwulan IV 2021 sebesar 5,02%. Kinerja positif ini didorong oleh peningkatan permintaan domestik seiring dengan meningkatnya mobilitas masyarakat dan tetap terjaganya kinerja ekspor. Perbaikan ekonomi nasional terjadi pada mayoritas lapangan usaha di seluruh wilayah.

Pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia semakin impresif dengan capaian 5,44% pada triwulan II 2022. Bahkan produk domestik bruto (PDB) harga konstan (riil) yang menunjukkan laju pertumbuhan ekonomi secara keseluruhan atau setiap sektor jauh lebih tinggi dibandingkan sebelum pandemi. Konsumsi dan pendapatan ekspor menjadi penopang utama pertumbuhan ekonomi. Kinerja ekspor juga berkontribusi signifikan bagi pertumbuhan ditopang oleh kenaikan harga komoditas dan menguatnya kapasitas output di sektor manufaktur.

Impor juga tumbuh seiring dengan permintaan domestik dan ekspor yang tetap tinggi.

The increase in world crude oil prices and the Indonesian Crude Price (ICP) forced the government to adjust the price of fuel oil (BBM) on September 3, 2022. The increase in fuel prices impacted the price of essential commodities and transportation costs, pushing up the inflation rate. Indonesia's annual inflation rate in 2022 was 5.51%, according to Statistics Indonesia (BPS) data, higher than the government's target of 3.0% and much greater than 1.87%. in 2021.

Indonesia's economic recovery continued into 2022, despite the global economic uncertainties. Economic growth in the first quarter of 2022 remained strong at 5.01%, slightly down from 5.02% in the fourth quarter of 2021. This positive performance was driven by increased domestic demand in line with people's increased mobility as well as strong export performance. Improvements in the national economy occurred in many business sectors in all regions.

Indonesia's economy continued its impressive growth at 5,44% in the second quarter of 2022. Real gross domestic product (GDP), which shows the overall economic growth rate for each sector, was much higher than before the pandemic. Consumption and export income were the main pillars of economic growth. The government's strategy of allowing public travel for the Eid al-Fitr homecoming, which was forbidden in the previous two years due to the pandemic, significantly increased domestic consumption. This in turn drove economic turnover across Indonesia. Export performance also contributed substantially to growth, supported by rising commodity prices and strengthening output capacity in the manufacturing sector.

Indonesia's economic performance strengthened to 5.72% in the third quarter of 2022. Export growth increased to 21.64%, supported by strong demand from major trading partners, and policies to accelerate palm oil exports. Imports also grew in line with domestic and export markets, which remained high.
