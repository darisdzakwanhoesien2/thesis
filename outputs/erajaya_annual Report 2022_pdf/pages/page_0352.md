Erajaya Digital Retail Pte. Ltd

On August 30, 2021, the Company established Erajaya Digital Retail Pte. Ltd. through Erajaya Digital Pte. Ltd., in which Erajaya Digital Pte. Ltd. owned 60% ownership interests in Erajaya Digital Pte. Ltd. Total capital contribution paid by Erajaya Digital Pte. Ltd. for the establishment of Erajaya Digital Retail Pte. Ltd. is amounting to Sin$6,000.

On May 30, 2022, the Company has increased its share capital in Erajaya Digital Retail Pte. Ltd. through Erajaya Digital Pte. Ltd., from Sin$6,000 become to Sin$600,000. The increase in share capital is proportionally taken by the shareholders of Erajaya Digital Pte. Ltd., therefore, the percentage of ownership of each shareholders of Erajaya Digital Pte. Ltd. did not change.
