# LENTERA HIJAU

"Fokus kami tertuju pada aspek lingkungan hidup sebagai bagian dari komitmen dan tanggung jawab Erajaya terhadap lingkungan sekitar dan kelestarian alam. Kami menginisiasi program pengelolaan lahan secara tumpang sari tanaman buah oleh 40 keluarga petani lokal di lahan rehabilitasi Wana Erajaya serta program rehabilitasi kawasan hijau di daerah padat penduduk melalui metode organic urban farming."

"Our focus is on environmental aspects as part of Erajaya's commitment and responsibility towards the surrounding environment and natural preservation. We initiated a fruit crop intercropping land management program by some 40 local farming families in the Wana Erajaya rehabilitation area as well as a green zone rehabilitation program in densely populated areas through organic urban farming methods."
