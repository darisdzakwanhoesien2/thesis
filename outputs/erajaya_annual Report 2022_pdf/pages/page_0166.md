# RAPAT DIREKSI

## Kebijakan Rapat Direksi

Sebagaimana diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan Pasal 17 dan Pedoman Kerja Direksi, Direksi wajib mengadakan rapat Direksi secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam setiap bulan dan rapat Direksi bersama Dewan Komisaris secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 4 (empat) bulan.

Rapat Direksi juga dapat diadakan setiap waktu bilamana dianggap perlu oleh seorang atau lebih anggota Direksi atau atas permintaan tertulis dari Dewan Komisaris atau atas permintaan tertulis 1 (satu) pemegang saham atau lebih yang bersama-sama mewakili 1/10 (satu per sepuluh) bagian dari jumlah seluruh saham yang telah ditempatkan oleh Perseroan dengan hak suara yang sah.

Rapat Direksi sah dan berhak mengambil keputusan yang mengikat apabila dihadiri lebih dari 1/2 (satu perdua) bagian dari jumlah anggota Direksi hadir atau diwakili dalam rapat.

## Pelaksanaan Rapat Direksi

Pada tahun 2022, Direksi menyelenggarakan 22 kali rapat Direksi dan 5 kali rapat gabungan bersama Dewan Komisaris. Rapat Direksi terdiri dari 8 kali rapat berkala Direksi, 10 kali rapat Komite Komersial dan 4 kali rapat Komite Pengembangan Usaha Baru yang juga dihadiri Dewan Komisaris. Selain itu, terdapat 6 kali rapat bersama Komite Audit yang dihadiri Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris, termasuk di dalamnya 2 kali sirkuler pengganti rapat Dewan Komisaris dan Komite Audit.

# MEETINGS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

## Policy of the Board of Directors Meeting

As stipulated in Article 17 of the Company's Articles of Association and the Board of Directors Charter, the Board of Directors is required to hold regular Board of Directors meetings at least 1 (one) time a month and regular joint meetings of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners at least 1 (one) time in 4 (four) months.

The Board of Directors meetings may also be held at any time if deemed necessary by one or more members of the Board of Directors or at a written request from the Board of Commissioners or at the written request of 1 (one) or more shareholders who jointly represent 1/10 (one tenth) of the total number of shares issued by the Company with valid voting rights.

The Board of Directors meeting is valid and has the right to make binding decisions if attended by more than 1/2 (a half) of the total members of the Board of Directors present or represented at the meeting.

## The Board of Directors Meeting

In 2022, the Board of Directors held 22 Board of Directors meetings and 5 joint meetings with the Board of Commissioners. The Board of Directors meetings consist of 8 regular meetings of the Board of Directors, 10 meetings of the Commercial Committee and 4 meetings of the Investment Committee which were also attended by the Board of Commissioners. In addition, there were 6 meetings with the Audit Committee attended by the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, included 2 circular resolutions as substitute of meetings of the Board of Commissioners and the Audit Committee.
