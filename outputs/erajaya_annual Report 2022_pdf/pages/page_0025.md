# Pandangan Atas Kondisi Eksternal

Kebangkitan ekonomi global dari dampak pandemi COVID-19 mulai pertengahan tahun 2021 menumbuhkan optimisme bahwa tahun 2022 akan menjadi tahun pemulihan. Tetapi invasi Rusia ke Ukraina pada 24 Februari 2022 menyebabkan ekonomi global kembali berada dalam kondisi ketidakpastian.

Invasi Rusia ke Ukraina membuat Rusia dijatuhi berbagai macam sanksi ekonomi dan non-ekonomi oleh negara-negara Barat. Sanksi ini langsung berpengaruh pada harga-harga komoditas, energi, dan bahan pangan karena Rusia dan Ukraina memiliki posisi penting dalam rantai pasokan pangan dan energi global.

We are grateful for the good performance of PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk (the "Company") in 2022, both from the financial and operational, and business development aspects.

We would like to convey the Board of Commissioners' report on the progress and management of the Company for the financial year ending December 31, 2022.

# External Review

Global economic recovery from the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic since mid-2021 raised optimism that 2022 would be a recovery year. However, Russia's invasion of Ukraine on February 24, 2022 caused a return to uncertainty for the global economy.

Russia's invasion of Ukraine resulted in economic and non-economic sanctions by Western countries. These sanctions directly affected commodity, energy, and food prices as Russia and Ukraine play essential roles in the global food and energy supply chains.
