# PENILAIAN ATAS KINERJA KOMITE PENDUKUNG TUGAS DIREKSI

Performance Evaluation of The Board of Directors Committee

Pada tahun 2021, Perseroan membentuk Komite Pengembangan Usaha Baru (Investment Committee/IC) untuk mendukung pengembangan pilar bisnis baru. Keberadaan IC terutama untuk memastikan bahwa peluang usaha baru yang diusulkan setiap unit bisnis memenuhi semua parameter standar kelayakan investasi yang ditetapkan IC. Setiap usulan pengembangan usaha akan dipresentasikan sebagai *initial introduction* kepada Direksi dalam rapat IC. Selanjutnya, jika IC menilai bahwa usulan tersebut prospektif, tim Business Development akan melakukan studi kelayakan terhadap aspek *target market size*, *business plan* yang akan dijalankan, prospek pengembangan, kecukupan arus kas, dan sebagainya. Hasil *indepth study* tim Business Development dipresentasikan kembali dalam rapat IC untuk dibahas dan diputuskan.

Direksi menilai, sepanjang tahun 2022 keberadaan IC sangat efektif dalam proses pengambilan keputusan investasi yang tepat, sejalan penerapan prinsip kehati-hatian dan manajemen risiko.

In 2021, the Company established the Investment Committee (IC) to support the development of new business pillars. The existence of IC is primarily to ensure that new business opportunities proposed by each business unit meet all the parameters of the investment feasibility standard set by IC. Each business development proposal will be presented as an initial introduction to the Board of Directors at IC meeting. Furthermore, if IC assesses that the proposal is prospective, the Business Development team will conduct a feasibility study on the aspects of target market size, business plan to be implemented, development prospects, adequacy of cash flow and so on. The results of the Business Development team’s in-depth study will be represented at IC meeting to be discussed and decided.

From the Board of Directors’ point of view, during 2022, IC was very effective in making the right investment decisions while implementing precautionary principles and risk management.
