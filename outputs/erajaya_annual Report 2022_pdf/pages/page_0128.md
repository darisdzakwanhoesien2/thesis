# Aset

Jumlah aset Perseroan per 31 Desember 2022 sebesar Rp17,06 triliun, naik 50% dari Rp11,37 triliun pada periode yang sama tahun sebelumnya. Kenaikan jumlah aset terutama disebabkan oleh kenaikan aset lancar sebesar 69,3%, sementara aset tidak lancar juga naik sebesar 23,0%.

# Aset Lancar

Aset lancar sebesar Rp11,22 triliun, naik 69,3% dari Rp6,62 triliun pada tahun 2021. Kenaikan aset lancar terutama disebabkan oleh:

- Kas dan setara kas naik 100,5% dari Rp520,70 miliar menjadi Rp1,04 triliun.
- Piutang usaha pihak ketiga, neto naik 71,3% dari Rp614,65 miliar menjadi Rp1,05 triliun.
- Piutang lain-lain pihak ketiga, neto naik 120,0% dari Rp427,26 miliar menjadi Rp940,00 miliar.
- Persediaan, neto naik 54,3% dari Rp3,93 triliun menjadi Rp6,06 triliun.
- Pajak dibayar di muka naik 254,7% dari Rp375,74 miliar menjadi Rp1,33 triliun.

# Assets

Total assets of the Company as of December 31, 2022 amounted to Rp17.06 trillion, up 50% from Rp11.37 trillion in the same period of the previous year. The increase in total assets was mainly due to an increase in current assets by 69.3%, while non-current assets also increased by 23.0%.

# Current assets

Current assets amounted to Rp11.22 trillion, an increase of 69.3% from Rp6.62 trillion in 2021. The increase in current assets was mainly due to:

- Cash and cash equivalents increased by 100.5% from Rp520.70 billion to Rp1.04 trillion.
- Trade receivables from third parties, net increased by 71.3% from Rp614.65 billion to Rp1.05 trillion.
- Other receivables from third parties, net increased by 120.0% from Rp427.26 billion to Rp940.00 billion.
- Inventories, net, increased by 54.3% from Rp3.93 trillion to Rp6.06 trillion.
- Prepaid taxes increased by 254.7% from Rp375.74 billion to Rp1.33 trillion.
