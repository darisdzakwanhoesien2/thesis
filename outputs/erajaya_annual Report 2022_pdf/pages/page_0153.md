# Meningkatkan Kualitas GCG Improving GCG Quality

![img-107.jpeg](img-107.jpeg)

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk meningkatkan kualitas GCG secara sistematis dan berkesinambungan sejalan dengan pertumbuhan usaha dan perkembangan praktik-praktik terbaik di tataran nasional, regional, maupun global. Setiap Tahun, Audit Internal melakukan audit GCG pada Divisi Corporate Secretary terkait pemenuhan ketentuan GCG terutama implementasi SEOJK Nomor 32 Tahun 2015 tentang Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang diungkapkan pada Laporan Tahunan Perseroan. Jika terdapat temuan audit yang signifikan, Kepala Audit Internal akan melaporkan kepada Komite Audit dan Dewan Komisaris di dalam forum rapat gabungan Komite Audit dan Dewan Komisaris serta Direksi Perseroan. Pada tahun 2022, audit GCG dilaksanakan pada 5 Juni 2022, dan tidak ada temuan material terhadap hasil audit di Divisi Corporate Secretary.

The Company is committed to improving GCG quality systematically and continuously in line with business growth and development of best practices at national, regional, and global levels. Every year, Internal Audit conducts GCG audit on Corporate Secretary Division related to compliance with GCG provisions, especially the implementation of SEOJK Number 32 of 2015 concerning Guidelines for Corporate Governance which is disclosed in the Company's Annual Report. If there are significant audit findings, Head of Internal Audit will report to Audit Committee and the Board of Commissioners in the joint meeting of Audit Committee and the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors of the Company. In 2022, the GCG audit conducted on June 5, 2022, and there was no material findings regarding the audit results in the Corporate Secretary Division.
