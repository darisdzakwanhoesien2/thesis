# DEWAN KOMISARIS

The Board of Commissioners

"Dewan Komisaris adalah organ utama Perseroan yang bertugas dan bertanggung jawab secara kolektif melaksanakan fungsi pengawasan dan pemberian nasihat kepada Direksi serta memastikan pelaksanaan prinsip-prinsip GCG di Perseroan. Kedudukan masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris termasuk Komisaris Utama adalah setara. Tugas Komisaris Utama adalah mengkoordinasikan kegiatan anggota Dewan Komisaris agar pelaksanaan tugas Dewan Komisaris dapat berjalan secara efektif."

"Board of Commissioners is the main organ of the Company which has collective duties and responsibilities in carrying out supervisory and advisory functions toward Board of Directors and ensuring implementation of GCG principles in the Company. The position of each member of Board of Commissioners, including President Commissioner, is equal. President Commissioner's task is to coordinate the activities of members of Board of Commissioners so that the implementation of duties of Board of Commissioners can run effectively."
