# MEMPERKUAT KOMITMEN UNTUK TATA KELOLA TERBAIK

Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, dan seluruh karyawan Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menerapkan prinsip-prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik/good corporate governance (GCG) di setiap proses bisnis sesuai peraturan perundangan dan best practice.

Sejalan dengan perkembangan organisasi dan skala usaha, Perseroan meningkatkan kualitas GCG secara sistematis dan berkesinambungan. Langkah-langkah perbaikan senantiasa dilakukan melalui monitoring secara mandiri dengan mengacu pada POJK Nomor 21/POJK.04/2015 tentang Penerapan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka.

Setiap tahun, Audit Internal melakukan audit GCG pada Divisi Corporate Secretary terkait pemenuhan ketentuan GCG terutama implementasi SEOJK Nomor 32 Tahun 2015 tentang Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang diungkapkan pada Laporan Tahunan Perseroan. Jika terdapat temuan audit yang signifikan, Kepala Audit Internal akan melaporkan kepada Komite Audit dan Dewan Komisaris di dalam forum rapat gabungan Komite Audit dan Dewan Komisaris serta Direksi Perseroan. Pada tahun 2022, audit GCG dilaksanakan pada 5 Juni 2022, dan tidak ada temuan material terhadap hasil audit di Divisi Corporate Secretary.

Transformasi digital juga bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kualitas tata kelola. Sistem teknologi informasi dapat meningkatkan akurasi data, meminimalkan human error, dan memperkuat proses pengendalian internal dimana pengawasan dapat dilakukan jaringan Perseroan di seluruh Indonesia.

Perseroan secara berkala mengadakan sosialisasi dan internalisasi kebijakan GCG kepada seluruh insan Perseroan agar dapat dipahami dan dijalankan dengan konsisten di lingkungan Perseroan. Kepatuhan atas kebijakan GCG yang sudah disepakati merupakan tanggung jawab bersama seluruh pemangku kepentingan baik di internal Perseroan maupun pihak eksternal yang menjalin hubungan kerja dengan Perseroan.

# STRENGTHENING COMMITMENT TO BEST GOVERNANCE

The Board of Directors, the Board of Commissioners, and all Company personnel are committed to implementing good corporate governance (GCG) principles in every business process, in accordance with relevant laws and regulations and best practices.

In line with the development of the organization and business scale, the Company improves the quality of GCG systematically and continuously. Corrective actions are carried out through independent monitoring under POJK Number 21/POJK.04/2015 on Implementation of the Guidelines for Corporate Governance in Public Companies.

Internal Audit conducts a GCG audit annually on the Corporate Secretary Division related to the fulfilment of GCG provisions, especially the implementation of SEOJK Number 32 of 2015 concerning Guidelines for Corporate Governance, which is disclosed in the Company's Annual Report. If there are significant audit findings, the Head of Internal Audit reports to the Audit Committee and the Board of Commissioners in a joint meeting of the Audit Committee and the Board of Commissioners and Directors. The GCG audit was held on June 5, 2022, and there were no material findings regarding the audit results in the Corporate Secretary Division.

Digital transformation also aims to improve the quality of governance. Information technology systems improve data accuracy, minimize human error, and strengthen internal control processes supervised by the Company's network throughout Indonesia.

The Company regularly conducts socialization and internalization of GCG policies with all employees so they can be understood and implemented consistently. Compliance with the agreed GCG policies is a shared responsibility of all stakeholders, both internally and for external parties, throughout the working relationship.
