Corporate Governance

Keputusan RUPS Tahunan tanggal 23 Juni 2022 terkait remunerasi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan untuk tahun buku 2022 adalah menyetujui melimpahkan kewenangan kepada Pemegang Saham Utama untuk menetapkan gaji/honorarium dan tunjangan anggota Dewan Komisaris, dan memberikan kewenangan kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk menetapkan gaji, tunjangan, tugas dan wewenang Direksi Perseroan.

The summary of the resolutions of the Annual GMS on June 23, 2022 regarding the remuneration of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners of the Company for the 2022 fiscal year was to approve on delegating authority to the Company's Major Shareholders to determine the salaries/honorariums and allowances of members of the Board of Commissioners, and give authority to the Board of Commissioners to determine the salaries, benefits, duties and authorities of the Board of Directors of the Company.

## PENGUNGKAPAN BONUS KINERJA, NON-KINERJA, DAN/ATAU OPSI SAHAM

Remunerasi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris pada tahun 2022 sebagaimana disebutkan di atas sudah termasuk tantiem. Pada tahun 2022 Perseroan tidak memberikan bonus kinerja berupa opsi saham kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris.

## DISCLOSURE OF PERFORMANCE, NON-PERFORMANCE BONUSES AND/OR STOCK OPTIONS

Remuneration of Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners in 2022 as mentioned above includes tantiem. In 2022, the Company did not provide performance bonuses in the form of stock options to Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners.
