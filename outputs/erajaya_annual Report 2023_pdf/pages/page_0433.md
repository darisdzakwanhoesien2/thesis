No. 12, pada tanggal 29 Maret 2022, PT Era Blu Elektronik menerbitkan 400.449 saham baru dengan nilai nominal Rp400.449.000 yang diambil bagian oleh EAR dan The Gioi Di Dong Joint Stock Company, pihak ketiga, masing-masing sebesar Rp220.225.000 dan Rp180.225.000. Based on Notarial Deed Raden Mas Dendy Soebangil, S.H., M.Kn. After the capital increase, EAR's ownership interest in PT Era Blu Elektronik changed from 98% to become 55%.
