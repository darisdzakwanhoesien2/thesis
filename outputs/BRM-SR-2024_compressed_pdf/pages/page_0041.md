# KEANGGOTAAN DALAM ASOSIASI

Membership in Associations (2-28) (POJK51-C.5)

Kami terus menjalin dan menjaga kemitraan strategis dengan berbagai organisasi dan asosiasi untuk mengikuti perkembangan dan memperluas hubungan bisnis serta memperkuat eksistensi. Hingga akhir 2024, BRMS tercatat sebagai anggota asosiasi Indonesia Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA), Asosiasi Emiten Indonesia, dan Indonesia Mining Association (IMA).

We continue to establish and maintain strategic partnerships with various organizations and associations to keep up with emerging developments, expand business relationships and strengthen existence. Until the end of 2024, BRMS is registered as a member of Indonesian Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA), the Indonesian Issuer Association, and the Indonesia Mining Association (IMA)
