2. Simulasi Tanggap Darurat HAR (High Angle Rescue)

Dilaksanakan pada 28 September 2024, simulasi ini melatih tim ERT dalam menangani keadaan darurat akibat jatuh dari ketinggian serta penggunaan peralatan penyelamatan seperti tali dan sistem rigging. Kegiatan ini melibatkan 8 peserta, termasuk tim ERT, paramedis, dan volunteer.

2. HAR (High Angle Rescue) Emergency Response Simulation

Conducted on 28 September 2024, this simulation trained the ERT team to respond to emergencies involving falls from heights and to operate rescue equipment such as ropes and rigging systems safely and effectively. The activity involved 8 participants, including ERT, paramedics, and volunteers.
