The dry tailing method also reduces the risk of ecological disasters due to its low toxicity levels. Moreover, the Dry Tailings Management Facility (DTMF) enhances the Company's ability to meet increasingly stringent environmental regulations regarding mine waste management. The advantages of dry tailings include improved safety and stability of tailings storage facilities, reduced land usage, easier reclamation, relatively lower tailings processing costs, and greater environmental friendliness.

# Social Aspects

We have implemented the ESDM Mining Safety Management System and ISO 45001 to create a safe and healthy work environment. We are grateful that throughout 2024, there were no work accidents or work-related illnesses reported across all our business units. This underscores the high level of discipline exhibited by our team in adhering to stringent operational standards.

BRMS values diversity and equal opportunity as part of its commitment in creating an inclusive, fair, and empowered work environment as stated in the PT Bumi Resources Minerals Tbk Diversity Policy established in July 2024. In line with SDG #5 Gender Equality, SDG #10 Reducing Inequality, and the UN Global Compact Principles on Women's Empowerment for employees with diverse backgrounds, provides fair and equal compensation based on meritocracy, maintains diverse workforce representation, and builds a work culture without discrimination.
