Laporan Keberlanjutan 2024 Sustainability Report 183

Konservasi keanekaragaman hayati flora yang dilakukan CPM pada tahun 2024 berupa penanaman tanaman Diospyros celebica (eboni) sebanyak 198 pohon. Selain itu, CPM telah melakukan kegiatan reklamasi pada tahun 2024 seluas 4,03 Ha. Kegiatan revegetasi CPM menggunakan tanaman penutup, tanaman cepat tumbuh (trembesi, sengon, kemiri, ketapang) dan tanaman lokal (eboni dan cendana). Penanaman tanaman penutup menggunakan mesin hydroseeding dengan penguatan lereng menggunakan cocomesh. (304-3) (POJK51-F.10)

CPM telah menjalin kerjasama dengan masyarakat di Ring I sekitar tambang dalam pengadaan bibit tanaman untuk kegiatan reklamasi dan rehabilitasi. Jumlah tanaman yang disimpan di area nursery untuk kegiatan reklamasi sebanyak 9.656 tanaman. Selain itu, upaya perlindungan fauna yang dilakukan CPM adalah mengembangkikan ayam hutan pada area konservasi. (304-3) (POJK51-F.10)

Terkait kegiatan reklamasi, CPM akan melakukan reklamasi secara bertahap dan berkelanjutan dari Tahap Operasi Produksi pada area terbuka yang sudah tidak digunakan. Kegiatan ini akan memberikan dampak positif terhadap lingkungan hidup seperti peningkatan keanekaragaman hayati flora-fauna, pengurangan laju erosi tanah sehingga berdampak perbaikan kualitas air Sungai Pondo.

Dampak kegiatan reklamasi ini tidak dapat dirasakan dalam jangka pendek terhadap area sekitarnya. Namun sejalan dengan kemajuan kegiatan reklamasi dan pemeliharaan yang berkelanjutan maka pertumbuhan tanaman reklamasi semakin optimal sehingga diharapkan keanekaragaman hayati flora-fauna di sekitarnya ikut memberikan dampak positif bagi lingkungan. Nilai keanekaragaman hayati vegetasi di area proyek berkisar 2,54 - 2,94 yang mana dikategorikan keanekaragamanan sedang dengan produktifitas cukup, kondisi ekosistem cukup seimbang dan tekanan ekologis sedang. (304-3) (POJK51-F.10)

Sementara unit usaha GM terletak di Kabupaten Bone Bolango seluas 24.995 Ha yang terdiri atas 2 (dua) blok, yaitu Blok I (Tombulilato) seluas 20.290 Ha dan Blok II (Molotabu) seluas 4.705 Ha berdasarkan Keputusan Menteri ESDM No. 341.K/30/DJB/2015 tentang Penciutan Kedua Tahap Kegiatan Studi Kelayakan pada Wilayah Kontrak Karya PT Gorontalo Minerals.

Conservation of flora biodiversity carried out by CPM in 2024 in the form of planting Diospyros celebica (ebony) plants as many as 198 trees. In addition, CPM has carried out reclamation activities in 2024 covering an area of 4.03 Ha. CPM revegetation activities use cover crops, fast-growing plants (trembesi, sengon, candlenut, ketapang) and local plants (ebony and sandalwood). Planting cover crops using hydroseeding machines with slope reinforcement using cocomesh.

CPM has established cooperation with the community in Ring I around the mine in the procurement of plant seeds for reclamation and rehabilitation activities. The number of plants stored in the nursery area for reclamation activities is 9,656 plants. In addition, CPM's fauna protection efforts include breeding partridges in conservation areas.

Regarding reclamation activities, CPM does reclamation in stages and continuously from the Production Operation Stage in open areas that are no longer in use. This activity will have a positive impact on the environment, such as increasing the biodiversity of flora and fauna, and reducing the rate of soil erosion so that it has an impact on improving the water quality of the Pondo River.

The impact of this reclamation activity cannot be felt in the short term on the surrounding area. However, in line with the progress of sustainable reclamation and maintenance activities, the growth of reclamation plants is increasingly optimal and it is hoped that the flora and fauna living in the surrounding area will have a positive impact on the environment. The value of vegetation biodiversity in the project area ranges from 2.54 to 2.94 which is categorized as moderate diversity with sufficient productivity, fairly balanced ecosystem conditions and moderate ecological pressure.

Meanwhile, GM's business unit is located in Bone Bolango Regency with an area of 24,995 Ha consisting of 2 (two) blocks, namely Block I (Tombulilato) covering an area of 20,290 Ha and Block II (Molotabu) covering an area of 4,705 Ha based on the Decree of the Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources No. 341.K/30/DJB/2015 concerning the Second Downsizing of the Feasibility Study Activity Phase in the Contract of Work Area of PT Gorontalo Minerals.
