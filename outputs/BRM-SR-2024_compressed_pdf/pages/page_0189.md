Kegiatan konstruksi pertambangan saat ini berada di Prospek Anjing Hitam. Prospek ini berada pada area kawasan hutan lindung ("HL") dan Area Penggunaan Lain ("APL"). Pembangunan sarana-prasarana pertambangan dibangun di HL dan APL. Sarana prasarana pertambangan yang akan dibangun pada HL, antara lain Portal, Pabrik atas, Gudang Handak, Pabrik bawah, Camp Tambang, Sedimen Pond. Sedangkan sarana-prasarana yang akan dibangun di APL yaitu TSF (Tailing Storage Facility).

Pemantauan fauna di DPM dilakukan dengan menggunakan alat monitoring camera trap setiap 2 bulan sekali. Pengambilan data fauna dari camera trap pernah dilakukan dengan pendampingan langsung oleh BKSDA. Pemantauan biota air dilakukan oleh Pihak Ketiga (PT Shafera Enviro Lab) yang sudah berakreditasi KAN di 6 (enam) titik lokasi area DPM dengan rata-rata hasil Indeks Keanekaragaman (H') pada bulan Agustus 2024 sebagai berikut:

LSW=2,995; LSIR=2,899; LSB=2,971; LMS=2,874; LBG-02=2,959; LSG=2,838), dan pemantauan benthos: LSW=2,64; LSIR=2,65; LSB=2,77; LMS=1,90; LBG-02=2,20; LSG=2,90).(POJK51-F.10)

PT GM telah melakukan pemantauan fauna sebanyak 2 (dua) kali di tahun 2024 sesuai dengan Dokumen RKL-RPL. Jenis fauna yang ditemukan antara lain burung sebanyak 15 (lima belas) spesies, mamalia sebanyak 6 (enam) spesies, reptil dan amfibi sebanyak 3 (tiga) spesies, serta serangga dan invertebrata sebanyak 3 (tiga) spesies. Dari hasil pemantauan ini, terlihat bahwa keanekaragaman fauna di wilayah kerja PT GM masih cukup tinggi. Oleh karena itu, PT GM berkomitmen untuk terus melakukan pemantauan rutin dan menerapkan langkah-langkah mitigasi gunya meminimalkan dampak terhadap keberlanjutan fauna di area kerja. Langkah yang dilakukan yaitu dengan melakukan pelepasan satwa liar pada area yang telah ditetapkan, seperti satwa liar yang ditemukan seperti musang, ular, kadal, dan lain-lain.

Mining construction activity is currently on the Anjing Hitam Prospect. This prospect is located in the protected forest area ("HL") and the Other Use Area ("APL"). Mining facilities and infrastructure were built in HL and APL. Mining infrastructure facilities to be built at HL include Portal, Upper Factory, Handak Warehouse, Lower Factory, Mining Camp, Sediment Pond. Meanwhile, the infrastructure that will be built at APL is the TSF (Tailing Storage Facility).

Fauna monitoring at DPM is conducted using camera trap every 2 months. Fauna data collection from camera traps was previously carried out with direct assistance from the BKSDA. Water biota monitoring is conducted by a Third-Party (PT Shafera Enviro Lab), accredited by KAN, at 6 locations within the DPM area. The average results of the Diversity Index (H') in August 2024 are as follows:

LSW=2,995; LSIR=2,899; LSB=2,971; LMS=2,874; LBG-02=2,959; LSG=2,838. Benthos monitoring results are: LSW=2,64; LSIR=2,65; LSB=2,77; LMS=1,90; LBG-02=2,20; LSG=2,90)

PT GM has conducted fauna monitoring 2 (two) times in 2024 in accordance with the RKL-RPL Document. The types of fauna found include 15 (fifteen) species of birds, 6 (six) species of mammals, 3 (three) species of reptiles and amphibians, and 3 (three) species of insects and invertebrates. From the results of this monitoring, it can be seen that the fauna diversity in the PT GM work area is still quite high. Therefore, PT GM is committed to continuing to conduct routine monitoring and implement mitigation measures to minimize the impact on the sustainability of fauna in the work area. The steps taken are by releasing wildlife in designated areas, such as wildlife found such as civets, snakes, lizards, and others.
