# PENGHORMATAN BRMS TERHADAP HAK MASYARAKAT ADAT

BRMS' Commitment to Respecting Indigenous People's Rights (411-1)

BRMS berkomitmen untuk menghormati hak-hak kolektif dan individual masyarakat adat sesuai dengan Deklarasi Hak-Hak Masyarakat Adat PBB, Konvensi 169 ILO tentang Masyarakat Hukum Adat, Prinsip Global Compact, dan Standar Kinerja IFC terkait Masyarakat Adat. Salah satu operasi kami di PT CPM, Palu, bersinggungan langsung dengan masyarakat adat Poboya.

PT CPM beroperasi pada wilayah hutan yang tidak dihuni oleh penduduk asli (Indigeonus people). Tidak ada masyarakat adat yang bermukim pada area tambang dan infrastruktur PT CPM, sehingga PT CPM tidak melakukan aktifitas relokasi pemukiman di area penambangan.

Pada tahun 2024, dalam rangka melindungi dan menghormati hak dan budaya masyarakat adat, PT CPM melaksanakan relokasi makam masyarakat adat Poboya sebagai langkah mitigasi dampak sosial dari kegiatan pertambangan. Proses ini dilakukan dengan menghormati nilai budaya setempat melalui kerja sama dengan masyarakat adat, pemerintah daerah, dan pihak terkait lainnya.

PT CPM menanggung seluruh biaya pemindahan, termasuk proses adat serta biaya lainnya yang berkaitan dengan relokasi. Selain itu, PT CPM juga membangun infrastruktur pendukung di lokasi pemakaman baru. Proses relokasi berjalan lancar sesuai kesepakatan bersama masyarakat adat.

BRMS is committed to respecting the collective and individual rights of indigenous people in accordance with the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples, ILO Convention 169 on Indigenous and Tribal Peoples, the Global Compact Principles, and the International Finance Corporation (IFC) Performance Standards on Indigenous Peoples. One of our operations, PT CPM in Palu, is closely connected to the Poboya indigenous people.

PT CPM operates in a forest area that is not inhabited by indigenous peoples. There are no indigenous communities residing within the mining and infrastructure areas of PT CPM; therefore, the company has not conducted any resettlement activities in the mining area.

In 2024, as part of its commitment to protect and respect the rights and culture of indigenous communities, PT CPM carried out the relocation of the graves of the Poboya indigenous people as a social impact mitigation measure related to mining activities. This process was conducted with respect for local cultural values through collaboration with the indigenous community, local government, and other relevant stakeholders.

PT CPM covered all costs associated with the relocation, including traditional ceremonies and other related expenses. Additionally, the company built supporting infrastructure at the new burial site. The relocation process was successfully completed in accordance with agreements made with the Poboya people.
