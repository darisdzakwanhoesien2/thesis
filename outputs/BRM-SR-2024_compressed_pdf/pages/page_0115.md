Management of Material Topics: Occupational Health and Safety (3-3) (2-23)

## Dampak Topik Material

Impact of Material Topic

Proses penambangan menghadirkan berbagai risiko dan bahaya bagi karyawan, pekerja, kontraktor, dan masyarakat karena dapat terpapar bahan kimia, kebisingan, menghirup debu, dan suhu tinggi dalam proses kerja. Tanpa pengelolaan risiko yang efektif, Perusahaan dapat menghadapi kerugian besar, termasuk penundaan operasional dan peningkatan biaya untuk menangani dampak yang dialami oleh pekerja.

Mining process presents various risks and hazards to employees, workers, contractors, and communities due to exposure to chemicals, noise, dust inhalation and high temperatures in the work process. Without adequate risk management, the Company could face substantial losses, including operational delays and increased costs to address worker impacts.

## Pengelolaan Dampak

Management of Impact

Kebijakan ini telah diintegrasikan dalam strategi dan prosedur operasional perusahaan. BRMS's Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) bertanggung jawab atas pengelolaan K3L, menetapkan target tahunan, dan membentuk Komite Keselamatan Pertambangan dan Lingkungan (KPL). Komite ini memastikan pelaksanaan program K3L sesuai regulasi Kementerian Energi dan Sumber Daya Mineral serta ISO 45001:2018.

This policy is integrated into the Company's operational strategies and procedures. BRMS's Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) &amp; Corporate Relations manages HSE management, sets annual targets, and established the Mining Safety and Environment Committee (KPL). This committee ensures the implementation of HSE programs per the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources regulations and ISO 45001:2018.
