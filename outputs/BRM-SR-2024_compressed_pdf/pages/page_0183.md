Sepanjang tahun 2024, kegiatan operasional GM meliputi pelaksanaan tata batas area IPPKH, pembuatan jalan perintis, pemantauan kualitas lingkungan, dan identifikasi kepemilikan lahan di APL. Merujuk pada perizinan GM, yaitu Persetujuan Tekno-Ekonomi (Surat Direktur Pembinaan Pengusahaan Mineral No. 1131/31.02/DBM/2015), Izin Lingkungan (SK Kepala Dinas Penanaman Modal, ESDM, dan Transmigrasi Provinsi Gorontalo Nomor 19/DPMESDM-TRANS/ SK/IL/XII/2018), dan Persetujuan Akhir Laporan Studi Kelayakan (Surat Direktur Jenderal Mineral dan Batubara Nomor 63/31.02/DJB/2019), kegiatan operasional GM akan dikembangkan pada Blok I Komplek Sungai Mak yang meliputi kegiatan penambangan dan pengelolaan tembaga dan mineral pengikutnya pada areal batas proyek seluas 1.794,17 Ha. Area batas proyek tersebut terdiri dari Area Penggunaan Lain (APL) seluas 190,30 Ha dan Hutan Produksi Terbatas (HPT) seluas 1.603,87 Ha.

Pada tahun 2024, PT GM telah melakukan kewajiban penanaman di area rehabilitasi DAS seluas 41,30 Ha. Lokasi rehabilitas DAS terdiri 596 Ha area Hutan Lindung (HL) yang terletak di Kabupaten Bone Bolango dan Hutan Produksi Terbatas (HPT) seluas 623 Ha di Kabupaten Gorontalo Utara. Throughout 2024, GM's operational activities included implementing IPPKH boundaries, building pioneer roads, monitoring environmental quality, and identifying land ownership in APL. Referring to GM permits, i.e. Techno-Economic Approval (Letter of the Director for Development of Mineral Exploitation No. 1131/31.02/DBM/2015), Environmental Permits (Decree of the Head of the Investment, ESDM, and Transmigration Office of Gorontalo Province Number 19/DPMESDM-TRANS/SK /IL/XII/2018) and Final Approval of the Feasibility Study Report (Letter of the Director General of Minerals and Coal No. 63/31.02/DJB/2019), GM's operational activities will be developed in Block I Komplek Sungai Mak which includes mining and refining of copper and minerals derivatives in the Project Boundary area of 1,794.17 Ha. The project boundary area consists of Other Use Area (APL) of 190.30 ha and a Limited Production Forest (HPT) of 1,603.87 hectares.

In 2024, PT GM has undertaken planting obligations in a watershed rehabilitation area of 41.30 Ha. The watershed rehabilitation area consists of 596 Ha of Protected Forest (HL) area located in Bone Bolango Regency and 623 Ha of Limited Production Forest (HPT) in North Gorontalo Regency.
