# Tingkat Pergantian Karyawan (401-1)

Kami berhasil menjaga tingkat pergantian (turnover) karyawan di angka yang rendah yaitu kurang dari 10%. Sepanjang tahun 2024, sebanyak 25 karyawan BRMS maupun unit usaha yang mengakhiri hubungan kerja. Di tingkat kantor pusat, terdapat 2 karyawan yang mengakhiri hubungan kerja. Sementara itu, tingkat turnover karyawan pada unit usaha yakni CPM 3% dan GM 2%. Rendahnya tingkat turnover karyawan ini, menunjukkan bahwa BRMS mampu menciptakan lingkungan yang aman dan nyaman bagi karyawan. # Employee Turnover

We managed to keep the employee turnover rate low, i.e., below 10%. Throughout 2024, 25 BRMS employees and business units were laid off. Two employees ended their employment relationship with the company at the head office level. Meanwhile, employee turnover rates in business units were CPM 3% and GM 2%. This low employee turnover rate showed that BRMS was able to create a safe and comfortable environment for employees.
