## 4. Risiko Pengembangan Proyek

Setiap tahap dalam pengembangan proyek memiliki risiko dan ketidakpastian yang dapat berdampak material terhadap hasil yang diharapkan. Risiko ini meliputi perubahan tonase dan kualitas bijih, lonjakan biaya bahan baku dan tenaga kerja, keterlambatan perizinan, rendahnya kinerja keselamatan tambang, perubahan regulasi, serta tantangan sosial.

Faktor-faktor tersebut dapat meningkatkan biaya, menunda proyek, dan menghambat pencapaian target keuangan. Untuk mengelola risiko ini, perusahaan melakukan perencanaan dan pemantauan ketat terhadap progres proyek. Setiap deviasi dianalisis oleh Project Manager, dengan usulan perbaikan strategis yang dilaporkan ke Direksi.

Selain itu, implementasi peraturan terkait Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Pertambangan menjadi prioritas, dengan pengawasan khusus dari unit terkait dan dan senantiasa direviu oleh Auditor Internal Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja perusahaan.

## 5. Risiko Operasional

Risiko operasional dapat dipengaruhi oleh berbagai faktor, seperti kondisi geologis dan geoteknis yang tidak terduga, kecelakaan kerja, pencemaran lingkungan, bencana alam, ketidakpatuhan terhadap regulasi, konflik sosial, serta gangguan pada mesin produksi. Jika terjadi, risiko ini dapat menyebabkan kecelakaan, penurunan produksi, atau penghentian operasi yang berpotensi mengurangi pendapatan.

One of the biggest challenges in the mining industry is the fluctuation of mineral prices. Therefore, the ability of the business analysis unit to predict mineral prices is crucial. ## 4. Risks Associated with Project Development

Each stage of project development involves risks and uncertainties that could materially impact expected results. These risks include changes in ore tonnage and quality, increases in raw material and labor costs, permitting delays, poor mine safety performance, regulatory changes, and social challenges.

These factors can increase costs, delay projects, and hinder the achievement of financial targets. To manage these risks, the company conducts strict planning and monitoring of project progress. Any deviations are analyzed by the Project Manager, with strategic improvement proposals reported to the Board of Directors.

Furthermore, the implementation of regulations related to Mining Health and Safety is a priority, with special supervision from related units and is constantly reviewed by the company's Internal Occupational Health and Safety Auditor.

## 5. Operational Risks

Operational risks can be influenced by various factors, such as unexpected geological and geotechnical conditions, work accidents, environmental pollution, natural disasters, non-compliance with regulations, social conflicts, and disruptions to production machines. If they occur, these risks can cause accidents, decreased production, or shutdowns that have the potential to reduce revenue.
