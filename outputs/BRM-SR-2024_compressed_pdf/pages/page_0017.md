# Business Opportunities and Prospects

BRMS's second gold processing plant, operated by its subsidiary PT Citra Palu Minerals in Palu, has been operating effectively since early 2023 and reached its full capacity in 2024. This has driven a significant increase in gold production since 2023. In addition, we are currently constructing our third gold processing plant at the same location in Palu using the Heap Leach method, which is expected to strengthen the Company's financial performance going forward. With these developments, BRMS is optimistic about achieving its gold production target of 75,000 troy ounces by 2025.

Ongoing geopolitical tensions remain a challenge for various industries, including the mining sector. BRMS hopes for a resolution to these global uncertainties for the collective benefit. Despite these challenges, BRMS remains optimistic and continues to identify opportunities. From a market perspective, BRMS is not affected by geopolitical conflicts, as 100% of its demand comes from the domestic market.
