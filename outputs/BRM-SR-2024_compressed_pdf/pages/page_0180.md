Selanjutnya, untuk penggunaan air di GM selama 2024, mayoritas digunakan untuk keperluan domestik dan perkantoran di Site Office Leppin, Staging Tombulilato, serta camp di Sungai Mak, Cabang Kiri, dan Tambulilato. Air di Site Office Leppin disuplai oleh PDAM Provinsi Gorontalo dengan konsumsi mencapai 1.634.000 liter. Sementara itu, kebutuhan air di Staging Tombulilato, Mess Tombulilato, dan site lainnya dipenuhi dari mata air dan air tanah setempat yang dialirkan melalui pipa, dengan konsumsi masing-masing sebesar 2.895.030 liter dan 805.200 liter.

Furthermore, for water use in GM during 2024, the majority is used for domestic and office purposes at Site Office Leppin, Staging Tombulilato, and camps in Sungai Mak, Cabang Kiri, and Tambulilato. Water at the Leppin Site Office is supplied by PDAM Gorontalo Province with consumption reaching 1,634,000 liters. Meanwhile, water needs at Staging Tombulilato, Mess Tombulilato, and other sites are met from local springs and groundwater channeled through pipes, with consumption of 2,895,030 liters and 805,200 liters, respectively.
