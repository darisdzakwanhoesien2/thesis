## IDENTIFIKASI RISIKO K3

OHS Risks Identification (403-2)

Setiap unit usaha telah melaksanakan Identifikasi Bahaya dan Penilaian Risiko (IBPR) serta Identifikasi &amp; Penilaian Dampak Lingkungan (IPDL) sebagai bagian dari upaya proaktif dalam mengelola risiko keselamatan dan dampak lingkungan. Proses ini dilakukan secara berkala dan diperbarui secara sistematis, dengan mempertimbangkan perkembangan tahapan operasional, perubahan regulasi terbaru, serta riwayat insiden yang terjadi.

PT BRMS dan unit usaha telah membuat prosedur yang mengatur proses IBPR dan IPDL. Khusus untuk LMR, unit usaha masih dalam tahap penyusunan Job Safety Analysis (JSA). Namun demikian, saat ini LMR sedang ditahap pengumpulan data untuk studi AMDAL.

To facilitate participation, consultation, and communication regarding mining safety, PT GM and PT CPM have established specific procedures, namely GM-HSE-PR-008 Communication, Consultation, and Participation and CPM-HSE3-PR-3.03. GM-HSE-PR-011 Non-Conformance, Incident, and Management Action also accommodates employees, contractors, and guests in submitting complaints or findings to the HSE &amp; Compliance Department and the Mining Safety and Environmental Committee. PT GM also provides HIAS forms strategically for reporting occupational health and safety (OHS) risks. Furthermore, we conduct Employee Induction Mechanisms before commencing work as part of the K3LK communication process. BRMS and its business units continuously encourage the involvement of all parties in ongoing improvements related to mining safety and the environment.

Ensuring a healthy and safe work environment is further reinforced by incorporating Occupational Safety and Health (K3LK) aspects into the Collective Labour Agreement and Company Regulations. At CPM and GM, K3LK provisions are contained in the 2022-2024 Company Regulations in Chapter VII Article 50 concerning Occupational Safety and Health Rules. Meanwhile, SHS and LMR are enshrined in the 2023-2025 Company Regulations Article 48 concerning Occupational Safety and Health Rules. All employees are comprehensively covered under these regulations and have undergone training facilitated by the HRD Team.

Each business unit has conducted Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (HIRA) and Environmental Impact Identification &amp; Assessment (EIA) as part of a proactive approach to managing safety risks and environmental impacts. This process is carried out regularly and systematically updated, considering operational phase developments, the latest regulatory changes, and incident history.

PT BRMS and its business units have established procedures to govern the IBPR and IPDL processes. Specifically for LMR, the business unit is still developing Job Safety Analysis (JSA). However, LMR is working on data collecting for AMDAL study.
