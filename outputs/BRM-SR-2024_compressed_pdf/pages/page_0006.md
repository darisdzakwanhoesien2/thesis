# Produk Ramah Lingkungan

Environmentally Friendly Product

Berdasarkan hasil penelitian, sebanyak 1.215.523,5 ton limbah tailing kering yang dihasilkan sepanjang tahun 2024 memiliki potensi nilai ekonomi sirkular, dengan pemanfaatan akhir yang dapat diolah menjadi berbagai produk, seperti paving block dan backfill material.

Based on research findings, a total of 1,215,523.5 tons of dry tailings waste generated throughout 2024 holds potential for circular economic value, with end-use applications that can be processed into various products, such as paving block and backfill material.

Catatan: Izin pemanfaatan tailing kering masih dalam proses. Sementara itu, kami telah melakukan riset yang bekerja sama dengan Universitas dan uji laboratorium untuk mengeksplorasi potensinya.

Note: The permit for dry tailings utilization is still in progress. In the meantime, we have conducted research in collaboration with universities and carried out laboratory tests to explore its potential.
