|  Nondiskriminasi
Non-discrimination | BRMS continue to realize a workforce consisting of various identities and backgrounds. BRMS believe that the more diverse the composition of employees, the more ideas and innovations can be contributed to the progress of the Company. This diversity is reflected in our governance bodies and employees. BRMS apply the principle of equal opportunity in the recruitment process, opportunities for advancement, and remuneration policies. | ● | ●  |
|  Anti Korupsi
Anti-corruption | Korupsi menjadi ancaman bagi keberlanjutan Perusahaan dan pencapaian kinerja pembangunan berkelanjutan. Untuk mencegah risiko ini, BRMS menanamkan prinsip integritas kepada seluruh insan BRMS dan mitra kerja melalui pelatihan dan sosialisasi Kode Etik serta melakukan audit secara berkala.
Corruption is a threat to the sustainability of the Company and the achievement of sustainable development performance. To prevent this risk, BRMS build a culture of integrity for all BRMS personnel and work partners through training and disseminating the Code of Ethics and conducting regular audits. | ● | ●  |
|  Masyarakat Lokal
Local Communities | BRMS menjaga hubungan harmonis dengan masyarakat sekitar melalui perekrutan tenaga kerja lokal, investasi infrastruktur, serta mekanisme pengaduan dan remediasi berbasis kebutuhan komunitas.
BRMS maintains harmonious relationships with surrounding communities by recruiting local labor, infrastructure investment, and community needs-based grievance and remediation mechanisms. |  | ●  |
