Safety and Health Work (OHS)

PGN formed the Executive Central Safety Committee (ECSC) at the Board of Directors level and the Central Safety Committee (CSC) at both corporate level and the Business Unit and Project level, as well as Subsidiary. This is aimed at effective and efficient OHS implementation and management. The role of CSC as the principal driver of OHS is very important for PGN's success in socializing occupational safety culture to all employees from all parties that work within PGN's operational areas.
