# MISSION

To increase the Company's added values for stakeholders through:

- Customer: Energy needs fulfillment solution that is safe, value-added, economic, and increase competitiveness;
- Society: The improvement of welfare and sustainable economic growth through energy independence and environmental conservation efforts;
- Shareholders/Investors: The creation of the optimal and sustainable Company's values through internal and external synergies.

Vision and mission of PGN which has been renewed based on Director decree: 000303.K/HM.03/UT/2015 issued on 8 Januari 2015
