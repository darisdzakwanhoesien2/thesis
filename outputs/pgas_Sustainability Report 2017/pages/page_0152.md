# GOOD ENERGY EMBRACES PGN PEOPLE

![img-210.jpeg](img-210.jpeg)

Human resources is a determining factor of PGN's performance and success in realizing the Company's vision and mission. Good communication with PGN People by taking into account prosperity level and development of their professionalism and career in line with their talents, is a form of PGN's effort in developing PGN people. This is an effort aimed to achieve the Company's vision of 2020, i.e. becoming a world-class energy company in the gas sector.
