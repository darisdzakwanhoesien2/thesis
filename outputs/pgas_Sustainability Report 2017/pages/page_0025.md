In addition, the Board of Directors has successfully achieved a magnificent sustainability performance in 2017. PGN recorded a revenue of US$2.97 billion, a 1.7% increase from last year of US$2.93 billion. Executing the strategies in the Environmental Transformation Roadmap, PGN demonstrated that the 3P principles –Profit, Planet and People—are in balance, towards the realization of PGN's Vision for 2020.
