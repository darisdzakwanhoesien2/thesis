# PGN'S CONTRIBUTION TO THE ECONOMY OF THE STAKEHOLDERS

As an economic entity and agent of development, PGN is a driving factor of national economy which delivers economic contribution to the stakeholders which comprise: The state, investors, employees, consumers, suppliers and the community. We always seek that these contributions be improved through strategy development, efficiency and effectiveness improvement as well as maintaining good relations with all stakeholders. Thus we hope that our contribution to the economy of the stakeholders would increase consistently.
