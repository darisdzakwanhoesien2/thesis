## B. Health Road Map

PGN aspires to assure that apart from workers being protected in all operational activities, they can also keep their good health. It is projected that the workers productivity be maintained at a satisfactory level, and even the workers' life quality be better, as long as they are in good health.

PGN holds health programs for its workers. Some of programs are provided in line with the risk level of the workers.

Annually PGN also holds health education programs, such as seminar, road show, worker health record management E-Medical Record, industrial hygiene program of work environment assessment, and health risk assessment. PGN has compiled the Work Reference Document which serves as legal basis of rules regarding workers health management.
