# OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH &amp; SAFETY (OHS)

![img-184.jpeg](img-184.jpeg)

Wherever PGN operates, we are committed to executing operational safety, applying high-standard Occupational Health and Safety (OHS). The OHS application is not only for meeting local requirements but also for following international standards and "best feed" of the existing best practices in similar industries.
