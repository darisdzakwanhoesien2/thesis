Message of the President Director

PT Perusahaan Gas Negara (Persero) Tbk

2017 Sustainability Report

Despite the number of PGN People in 2017 decreased 6.1% from 1,426 workers to 1,339 workers, through training and personnel development, the number of female workers at Middle and Top levels of position increased 5.7%. This is part of the commitment to the participation in realizing Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

## PROJECTION

Heading to the future, PGN would continue focusing on efforts to realize the government's plan to increase new and renewable energy business.

On behalf of the Board of Directors, I appreciate and would like to thank all PGN People who have worked rigorously in realizing sustainability agenda throughout 2017. By making more efforts, I am sure that PGN's performance in sustainability would continue increasing in the coming years.

Jakarta, March 2018
