# GOOD ENERGY FOR EARTH CONSERVATION

![img-173.jpeg](img-173.jpeg)

The National Energy Council (DEN) predicted that the national energy needs would increase two folds by 2025. PGN responded to the challenge of meeting increasing energy demand by providing more environmentally friendly natural gas energy supply. Utilization of natural gas produces less emission compared to that of other fossil fuels, such as oil and coal. By supplying natural gas as energy source, PGN has contributed to suppressing of  $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$  emission, a greenhouse emitter that triggers global warming.
