# GOOD ENERGY BRINGS PROSPERITY TO THE NATION

PGN is aware that sustainability concept from economic aspect could only be implemented if the economy grows higher and the target that has been set is achieved. Economic growth where the people and the environment are taken into account would strengthen PGN's existence in a long term and give positive impact on the economy of stakeholders.
