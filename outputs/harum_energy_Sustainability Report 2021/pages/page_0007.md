# 5 Strategi Strategy

Pelaksanaan program Pengembangan dan Pemberdayaan Masyarakat (PPM).
Implementation of Community Development and Empowerment (CDE) programs.

# Target Target

- Peningkatan nilai yang didistribusikan kepada masyarakat lokal melalui program PPM;
- Peningkatan penerima manfaat program PPM;
- Peningkatan jumlah dan kualitas program PPM yang berkelanjutan.
- Increase value distributed to local communities through CDE program;
- Increase CDE program beneficiaries;
- Increase number and quality of sustainable CDE program.

# 6 Pencapaian di Tahun 2021 Achievement in 2021

- Penerima manfaat program PPM mencapai 6.181 orang dan 9 desa;
- Ikut serta memajukan Usaha Mikro, Kecil, dan Menengah (UMKM) yang berada pada desa-desa binaan, seperti Desa Sebuntal dan Desa Santan Ulu; serta
- Penyusunan program PPM yang memberikan dampak berkelanjutan bagi masyarakat lokal.
- CDE program beneficiaries reached 6,181 people and 9 villages;
- Participated in promoting micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) in the fostered villages, such as Sebuntal Village and Santan Ulu Village; and
- Development of CDE programs that will have a sustainable impact on local communities.
