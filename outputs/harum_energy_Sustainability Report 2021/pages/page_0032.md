# Komitmen Keberlanjutan
## Commitment to Sustainability

Kegiatan usaha yang kami jalankan di bidang pertambangan memiliki risiko ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan, terutama di wilayah operasional. Risiko-risiko tersebut perlu dikelola secara terintegrasi agar tidak menimbulkan dampak negatif, melainkan menciptakan nilai tambah bagi pemangku kepentingan serta bagi Perseroan dan Entitas Anak. Pengelolaannya dilakukan berdasarkan prinsip-prinsip keberlanjutan yang disesuaikan dengan peraturan pemerintah yang berlaku serta 7 subjek inti dalam ISO 26000 tentang Tanggung Jawab Sosial, meliputi:

1. Tata kelola keberlanjutan;
2. Prosedur operasi yang adil;
3. Praktik ketenagakerjaan, kesehatan, dan keselamatan kerja;
4. Pemenuhan hak asasi manusia;
5. Lingkungan hidup;
6. Isu produk dan pelanggan;
7. Pelibatan dan pengembangan masyarakat.

Our business activities in the mining sector carry economic, social, and environmental risks, especially in the operational areas. These risks need to be managed in an integrated manner in order not to cause negative impacts, but rather create added value for stakeholders as well as for the Company and its Subsidiaries. Its management is carried out based on the sustainability principles in line with the applicable government regulations and the 7 core subjects in ISO 26000 on Social Responsibility, covering:

1. Sustainable governance;
2. Fair operating procedures;
3. Practices of employment, occupational health, and safety;
4. Fulfillment of human rights;
5. The environment;
6. Products and customers issues;
7. Community engagement and development.

# Struktur Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan
## Structure of Sustainability Governance

Penerapan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (good corporate governance/GCG) secara berkelanjutan sangat diperlukan dalam pengelolaan aspek keberlanjutan di Perseroan dan Entitas Anak. Penerapan GCG tidak hanya difokuskan pada pengelolaan keuangan dan operasional Perseroan saja, tetapi juga terhadap pemenuhan tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan, termasuk kepatuhan terhadap peraturan yang berlaku.

Agar pengelolaan aspek ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan berjalan efektif, kami telah memiliki struktur tata kelola keberlanjutan yang sesuai dengan kompleksitas usaha yang dijalankan. Organ tersebut menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya sesuai prinsip transparansi, akuntabilitas, responsibilitas, independensi, serta kewajaran dan/atau kesetaraan.

The implementation of good corporate governance (GCG) in a sustainable manner is highly important in managing the sustainability aspects of the Company and its Subsidiaries. The GCG implementation focuses not only on the Company's financial and operational management, but also on the fulfillment of social and environmental responsibilities, including compliance with the applicable regulations.

In order for the management of economic, social, and environmental aspects to run effectively, we already have a sustainable governance structure in place that is in line with our business complexity. These organs perform their duties and responsibilities in accordance with the principles of transparency, accountability, responsibility, independency, as well as fairness and/or equality.
