# Biaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup

Perseroan dan Entitas Anak mengganggarkan dana untuk pelaksanaan program-program pengelolaan lingkungan demi menjaga keberlanjutan ekosistem daratan, termasuk kelestarian keanekaragaman hayati di sekitar wilayah operasional tambang.

# Environmental Management Cost

The Company and its Subsidiaries allocate funds for the implementation of environmental management programs in order to maintain the sustainability of terrestrial ecosystems, including the preservation of biodiversity around the mining operational areas.

# Pengaduan terkait Pengelolaan Dampak Lingkungan

Kami telah menyediakan saluran pengaduan terkait masalah lingkungan yang mungkin dirasakan oleh masyarakat atau pemangku kepentingan lain yang berada di sekitar wilayah operasional pertambangan. Laporan pengaduan dapat disampaikan langsung kepada Entitas Anak melalui External Unit. Selanjutnya, laporan yang diterima akan ditindaklanjuti oleh masing-masing pihak terkait secara responsif serta diselesaikan sesuai dengan peraturan yang berlaku.

Pada tahun 2021, kami tidak menerima laporan terkait masalah lingkungan hidup dari pihak manapun.

# Complaints regarding Environmental Impact Management

We have provided a complaint channel regarding environmental issues that may be indicated by the community or other stakeholders around the mining operational areas. The complaint report can be directly submitted to the Subsidiaries through an External Unit. Furthermore, reports received will be followed up by each relevant party in a responsive manner and resolved in accordance with the applicable regulations.

In 2021, we did not receive any reports related to environmental issues from any party.
