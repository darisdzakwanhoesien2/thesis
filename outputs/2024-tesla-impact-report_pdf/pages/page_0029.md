# Preparing the workforce for a world powered by sustainable energy

As of 2024, Tesla has created over 125,000 clean energy-related jobs globally. We are committed to attracting, developing and investing in exceptional talent and supporting their growth, regardless of their background.

We invest in educational programming that inspires the next generation to pursue careers in clean energy and manufacturing. We also invest in trades training and believe you do not need a degree to contribute to our mission.
