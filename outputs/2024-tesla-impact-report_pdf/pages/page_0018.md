# Autopilot technology is nearly 10-times safer than a human driver

Autopilot technology includes the hardware and software necessary to operate active safety features, Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (Supervised). As of 2024, Tesla drivers using Autopilot technology (with active driver supervision) achieved a safety record nearly 10-times higher than the U.S. national average across all automobile makers and models.

We expect to replicate and accelerate this level of safety with the continued development of Autopilot technology, including our unsupervised autonomy features, which we plan to launch in 2025.
