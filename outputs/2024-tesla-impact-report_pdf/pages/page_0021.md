Our vehicles come standard with premium features that create a driving experience rivaling that of luxury vehicles while maintaining a total cost of ownership in-line with that of mass-market, less-premium vehicles. With no oil changes or annual maintenance required, Tesla vehicles can cost less to maintain.

EVs are less expensive to fuel than gas-powered vehicles, and charging at home makes the experience more convenient. The cost of electricity to power Model Y is up to three-times less than the cost of gas for a comparable ICE vehicle.
