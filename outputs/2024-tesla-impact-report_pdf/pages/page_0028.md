Our Production Process

# We build efficient factories

We design our factories to limit waste, water usage and energy consumption. With each new Gigafactory, we aim to find new ways to manufacture our products more sustainably. Where we can not reduce energy consumption, we source renewables as much as possible.

We integrate learnings from all our sites into the development of future sites and the enhancement of existing locations like Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg and Gigafactory Texas.
