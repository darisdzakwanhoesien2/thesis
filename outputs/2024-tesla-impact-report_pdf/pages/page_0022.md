# Autonomy gives you time back to do what you love

## People spend nearly two hours a day on chores

The impact of autonomy will be experienced on the road as well as in the home and factory. When utilizing autonomous transport, our customers will be able to use their time commuting to enjoy the ride instead of focus on the drive.

Optimus, our humanoid robot (which will automate boring, repetitive or unsafe tasks), will bring this same level of convenience into the home and workplace. This gives families and workers time to focus on more fulfilling or impactful activities.
