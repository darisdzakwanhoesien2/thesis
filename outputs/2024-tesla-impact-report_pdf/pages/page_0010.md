# Our mission starts with making our products as affordable, safe and fun as possible

To achieve our mission, we must sell as many of our products as possible. Many people are unlikely to purchase a product just because it has low lifetime emissions. For mass adoption, our products need to be better than their alternatives in every way: more affordable, safer and more fun.

We are not just trying to build sustainable products—we are committed to building the best products, period. We offer our owners a full ecosystem of products and services to ensure they have the best experience possible—from purchase to delivery, and from charging to service.
