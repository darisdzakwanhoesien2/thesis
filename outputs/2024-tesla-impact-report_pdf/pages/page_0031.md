# Inspiring the next generation of Tesla leaders

Our education outreach programs and events aim to inspire young people to pursue STEM (science, technology, engineering and mathematics) careers by sparking curiosity and fostering creativity.

By increasing access to learning opportunities in underrepresented communities, we are preparing even more people for the careers of the future.

To learn more and apply, visit Tesla.com/Careers.
