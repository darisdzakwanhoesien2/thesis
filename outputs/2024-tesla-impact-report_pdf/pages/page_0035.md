# Achieving our mission through our sourcing

Tesla has positive impact on the world not only through the sustainability of our products, but also by making our supply chain more sustainable. Tesla is one of the largest EV and energy storage system (ESS) material buyers in the world. We use our purchasing power to avoid, prevent or mitigate any adverse impacts from our supply chain so that our sourcing supports a sustainable future.

We work directly with our suppliers to reduce the environmental and social impacts of our material sourcing, particularly in the most critical and impactful areas of our supply chain.
