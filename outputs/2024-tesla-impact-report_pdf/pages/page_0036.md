# Minimizing the environmental impact of our supply chain

For the most GHG emission-intensive areas in our supply chain, we work directly with suppliers to develop on-site emissions reduction plans. These plans include advancing decarbonization efforts, increasing renewable energy usage and implementing more efficient processes.

77% of our battery materials suppliers set a net-zero or carbon-neutral goal
