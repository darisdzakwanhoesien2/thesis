# We can't achieve our mission on our own

![img-7.jpeg](img-7.jpeg)

In Master Plan Part III, we call on others to join us in building a sustainable future. Tesla is a do-the-right-thing company. We don't walk away from difficult problems, but that doesn't mean we can achieve a sustainable future on our own.

We need everyone—from consumers to businesses, manufacturers and policymakers—to embrace decarbonization and automation to ensure the benefits are enjoyed by all.
