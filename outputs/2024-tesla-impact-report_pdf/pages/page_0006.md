# We strive to achieve net-zero GHG emissions

Our goal to reach net-zero emissions includes our products' supply chain, manufacturing, use and end-of-life. Today our avoided emissions are the most important metric for Tesla to measure, but our goal is to achieve net-zero emissions across our product lifecycles over time. We aim to make our operational electricity load 100% renewable before reaching our net-zero emissions target. We also match our Supercharger network's electricity use with renewable electricity, reducing our and our customers' GHG emissions for four years in a row.
