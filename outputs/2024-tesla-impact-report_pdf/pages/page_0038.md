# We respect human rights throughout our supply chain

Respecting human rights throughout our supply chain is a top priority at Tesla. We treat workers in our supply chain the same way that we treat our own employees. We take allegations of human rights abuses seriously and work diligently to uphold the right to freely chosen employment.

In 2024, we invested more human and legal resources than ever before to combat forced and child labor throughout our supply chain. We collaborate with our suppliers to remedy harms across our supply chain by using robust tools to identify and assess risks.
