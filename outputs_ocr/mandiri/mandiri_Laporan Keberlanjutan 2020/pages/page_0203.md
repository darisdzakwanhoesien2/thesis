In creating a safe and comfortable workplace, Bank Mandiri does not only pay attention to physical needs, but it is also accompanied by meeting nonphysical needs, such as employee health insurance. For this reason, Bank Mandiri included all of its employees to become Workers Social Security (Jamsostek) participants through BPJS Employment.
