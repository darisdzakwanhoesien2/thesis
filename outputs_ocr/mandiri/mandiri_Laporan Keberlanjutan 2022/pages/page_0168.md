Improving the Quality of Bank Mandiri Personnel

Proses manajemen talenta dan suksesi terbagi menjadi 4 (empat) proses utama, yaitu:

a. Talent Identification: proses identifikasi TC (Talent Classification) berdasarkan kinerja, leadership characteristics, ability, agility serta engagement.
b. Talent Profiling: Proses asesmen talent berdasarkan aspek track record, technical capability, leadership capability dan personality.
c. Talent Development: penyusunan rencana dan menjalankan pengembangan talent berdasarkan gap kapabilitas.
d. Strategic Talent Review: review kemajuan pengembangan dan menyiapkan rencana sukses incumbent.

The talent management and succession process are divided into 4 (four) main processes, specifically:

a. Talent Identification: Identification of TC (Talent Classification) according to performance, Leadership Characteristics, Ability, Agility and Engagement.
b. Talent Profiling: Talent assessment based on track record, technical capability, leadership capability, and personality.
c. Talent Development: Planning and execution of talent development based on capability gap.
d. Strategic Talent Review: Progress review of the talent development and preparation for the succession of incumbents.
