# KEPEMILIKAN DAN DASAR HUKUM

[GRI 2-1] [OJK C.3.c]

Bank Mandiri berdiri pada tanggal 2 Oktober 1998, sebagaimana dikukuhkan dengan akta No. 10 tanggal 2 Oktober 1998, yang dibuat di hadapan Notaris Sutjipto, S.H. Secara legal formal, pendirian Bank Mandiri telah mendapat persetujuan dari Menteri Kehakiman Republik Indonesia No. C2-16561-HT.01.01 TH 98 tanggal 2 Oktober 1998, dan diumumkan dalam Berita Negara Republik Indonesia No. 97 tanggal 4 Desember 1998, Tambahan Berita Negara Republik Indonesia No. 6859.

# OWNERSHIP AND LEGAL BASIS:

[GRI 2-1] [OJK C.3.c]

Bank Mandiri was founded on October 2, 1998, with confirmation by deed No. 10 on the same date, before Notary Sutjipto, S.H. Legally, Bank Mandiri's establishment was approved by the then Indonesian Minister of Justice, with approval No. C2-16561. HT.01.01 TH 98, dated October 2, 1998. The establishment was announced in the State Gazette of Republic of Indonesia No. 97 on December 4, 1998, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 6859.
