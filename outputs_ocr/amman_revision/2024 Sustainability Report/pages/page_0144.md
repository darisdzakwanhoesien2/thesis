# Analisis Pustaka

Desktop Analysis

Proses: Menganalisis isu-isu ESG dan tren di industri pertambangan

Standar dan kerangka kerja yang digunakan meliputi:

- Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) untuk Logam dan Pertambangan
- Standar Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)
- The Copper Mark
- Prinsip-prinsip International Council on Mining and Metals (ICMM)'s
- Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan dari Perserikatan Bangsa Bangsa (TPB)

Hasil: daftar panjang topik-topik ESG yang relevan dengan industry berdasarkan perspektif kerangka pelaporan global, penilai ESG, pakar, dan investor.

Process: Analyzing ESG issues and trends in the mining industry.

Standards and frameworks used include:

- Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB)
- Standards for Metals and Mining
- Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)
- Standards
- The Copper Mark
- International Council on Mining and Metals (ICMM)'s Principles
- United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Outcome: a long list of industry relevant ESG topics based on the perspectives of global reporting frameworks, ESG raters, thought leaders, and investors.

# Pelibatan Pemangku Kepentingan Internal dan Eksternal

Internal and External Stakeholder Engagement

Proses: Melibatkan pemangku kepentingan internal dan eksternal untuk mengidentifikasi isu-isu yang paling relevan dengan mereka dan operasi bisnis kami.

Dikonsultasikan dengan:

- Manajemen senior
- Para pelanggan
- Pemerintah daerah
- Perwakilan asosiasi pertambangan untuk memahami perspektif mereka terhadap praktik-praktik terbaik di industry.

Hasil: pemahaman tentang kekhawatiran dan harapan khusus para pemangku kepentingan; daftar panjang topik-topik ESG dikontekstualisasikan ke dalam bisnis dan operasi kami.

Process: Engaging our internal and external stakeholders to identify the issues that are most relevant to them and to our business.

Consulted with:

- Our senior management
- Our customers
- Local government
- Mining association representatives to understand their perspectives on best practices in the industry.

Outcome: an understanding of stakeholders' specific concerns and expectations; our long-listed ESG topics contextualised to our business and operations.

# Topik Material AMMAN

AMMAN's Material Topics

Proses: Mengkonsulisasikan hasil dari analisis Pustaka, dan keterlibatan pemangku kepentingan untuk mengidentifikasi dan memprioritaskan topik-topik ESG, peningkuan topik oleh tim 222 kami. Finalisasi dan persetujuan dari Direksi.

Hasil: Daftar final topik-topik penting ESG yang diprioritaskan untuk organisasi kami.

Process: Consolidating the results of our desktop analysis and stakeholder engagement to identify and prioritize ESG topics; review of topics by our ESG team; finalisation and approval by the Board of Directors.

Outcome: a final, prioritised list of material ESG topics for our organisation.
