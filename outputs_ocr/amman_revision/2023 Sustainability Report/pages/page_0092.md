atau penghentian proyek karena, misalnya, pandemi. Kami
tidak mengalami penundaan seperti ini pada tahun 2023 atau
2022, seperti yang ditunjukkan pada Tabel 13. Namun pada

pandemi.

From time to time we experience non-technical delays or project shutdowns due to, for example, a pandemic. We did not experience any such delays in 2023 or 2022, as shown in Table 13. However in 2021, we suspended operations for 35 days non-technical delay in early 2021 to control the spread of infections during a surge in the pandemic.
