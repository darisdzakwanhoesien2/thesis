# WILAYAH OPERASIONAL

OPERATIONAL AREAS

[SEOJK C.3]

Secara geografis, wilayah operasional Grup ditandai dengan portofolio menara dan fiber optik yang dimiliki. Grup memiliki ribuan unit menara dan ribuan kilometer jaringan kabel fiber optik yang tersebar di seluruh penjuru Indonesia, menjangkau pulau-pulau dan ujung barat hingga ujung timur wilayah negara, termasuk pulau-pulau terluar dan terpencil.

WILAYAH OPERASIONAL

[SEOJK C.3] OPERATIONAL AREAS

Geographically, the Group's operation is signified by the portfolio of towers and optic fiber. The Group owns thousands of towers and thousands of kilometers of optic fiber network spread across Indonesia, reaching many islands from the westernmost of the country, all the way to the easternmost, including the outermost and remote islands.
