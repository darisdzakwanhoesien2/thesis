## Employee Training and Capacity Building

**Pendekatan Manajemen** [103-1][103-2][103-3]
Management Approach

|  Keahlian dan kompetensi karyawan maupun pekerja vendor, mempengaruhi kelangsungan usaha SMN.
The skills and competencies of employees and vendor workers affect the continuity of SMN’s business. | Selama tahun 2020, jumlah jam pelatihan per karyawan adalah 18 jam.
In 2020, the average number of training hours per employee was 18 hours. | SMN melakukan pelatihan untuk sertifikasi climbing tower, guna menjamin keselamatan karyawan bekerja di ketinggian.
SMN held training for tower climbing certification, to ensure the safety of employees working at heights. | Pengelolaan pendidikan dan pelatihan karyawan menjadi tanggung jawab Divisi HR.
The management of employee education and training is the responsibility of the HR Division. | Secara berkala pelaksanaan kegiatan pendidikan dan pelatihan karyawan dilaporkan kepada Direksi, untuk evaluasi.
The implementation of employee education and training activities is reported periodically to the Board of Directors for evaluation.  |
