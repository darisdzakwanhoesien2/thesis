Formulir Saran

The CDE program consists of 5 Pillars, which include community empowerment activities—covering programs in Economic Development, Education, Health, Socio-culture, and Infrastructure.

Program CDE terdiri dari 5 Pilar yang meliputi kegiatan pemberdayaan masyarakat yang meliputi program-program di bidang Pengembangan Ekonomi, Pendidikan, Kesehatan, Sosial Budaya, dan Infrastruktur.

PTTBP's "Core values"

"Nilai-nilai Dasar"PTTBP

Humility, Achievement-Oriented, Respect of Every Individual, Integrity, Teamwork, Accountability

Kerendahan Hati, Berorientasi pada Prestasi, Menghargai Setiap Indivitas, Integritas, Kerja Sama Tim, Akuntabilitas
