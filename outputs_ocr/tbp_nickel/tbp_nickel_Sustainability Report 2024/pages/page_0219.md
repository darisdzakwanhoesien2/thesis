|  3. | Occupational Health and Safety
Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja | Work accidents and health issues in mines, factories, and power plants
Kecelakaan kerja dan isu kesehatan di tambang, pabrik dan pembangkit listrik | a) Low employee awareness of occupational safety and health
a) Rendahnya kesadaran karyawan/ atas keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja
b) There are facilities and infrastructure that do not meet safety standards
b) Terdapat Fasilitas dan Infrastruktur yang belum sesuai standar keselamatan
c) Unsafe condition due to bad weather (extreme rainfall and/or dry season)
c) Kondisi tidak aman karena cuaca buruk (curah hujan ekstrem dan/atau musim kemarau) | a) Fatalitas
a) Fatality
b) Gangguan operasional
b) Operational Disruption
c) Reputational damage
c) Kerusakan reputasi | a) Implement the Mineral and Coal Mining Safety Management System (SMKP Minerba), Occupational Safety and Health Management System (SMK3), ISO 45001 certification as a guarantee of occupational safety and health, within the scope of process safety and personnel safety for employees and work partners.
a) Menerapkan Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan Mineral dan Batubara (SMKP Minerba), Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (SMK3), sertifikasi ISO 45001 sebagai jaminan keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja, dalam Scope keselamatan proses dan keselamatan personel bagi karyawan, mitra kerja.
b) Carry out operational safety management by implementing a good commissioning system and process hazard analysis (Process Hazard Analysis).
b) Menjalankan pengelolaan keselamatan operasi dengan menerapan sistem commissioning yang baik dan analisa bahaya proses (Process Hazard Analysis).
c) Implement, review the effectiveness and socialize regulations related to occupational health and safety.
c) Menjalankan, meninjau ulang efektivitas dan menyosialisasikan peraturan terkait kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja.
d) Ensuring the availability of necessary resources and reliability of infrastructure, process safety installations, fire extinguishing facilities, Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).
d) Memastikan ketersediaan sumber daya yang diperlukan dan keandalan infrastruktur, instalasi keselamatan proses, saranan pemadam kebakaran, Alat Pelindung Diri (APD).
e) Implement occupational health and safety requirements in critical activities.
e) Menjalankan persyaratan-persyaratan keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja pada aktivitas kritikal. | Physical Risk/
