## Strategi Pelayanan kepada Off-taker

Pertamina merupakan satu-satunya off-taker Perseroan. Untuk menjaga dan meningkatkan mutu pelayanan, Perseroan menempuh langkah-langkah berikut ini:

- Penyelenggaraan Rapat bulanan evaluasi produksi dan pengambilan Integrated Supply Chain (ISC);
- Pemberian Informasi forecasting produksi kilang setiap bulan guna perencanaan lifting dan Stock di off-taker;
- Penyelenggaraan pertemuan berkala untuk mengevaluasi pelayanan ke off-taker dan pengutamaan HSE selama proses lifting.

## Off-taker Service Strategy

Pertamina is the Company's sole off-taker, and therefore, to maintain and improve service quality, the following steps have been taken:

- Organization of monthly meetings for evaluation of production and application of Integrated Supply Chain (ISC);
- Delivery of monthly forecasting of each plant for planning of lifting and stock at the offtaker;
- Organization of regular meetings for evaluation of offtaker service as well as reviews on HSE issues during lifting process.
