December 12, 2014

LG Mobile Brand Shop is Opened at Gandaria City

The opening of LG Mobile Brand Shop is a result of collaboration between LG and PT Erafone Artha Retailindo, one of the subsidiaries of the Company, a leading retailer with a nationwide network of retail outlets. All this time, all LG mobile products have been marketed through a network of Erafone's multi-brand outlets, and its market share shows a promising growth trend year by year. It is expected with the opening of LG Mobile Brand Shop, the contribution of LG products in Erafone sales will rise significantly.
