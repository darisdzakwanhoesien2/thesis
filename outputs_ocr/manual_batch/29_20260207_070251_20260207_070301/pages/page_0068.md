Annual Report 2014 PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk

# Third Party Financing

Erajaya Group provides financing for retail customers through third party. Such financing is provided through credit card issued by third party bank, which generally provide interest-free installment for the repayment period of 8-12 months. Until now, Erajaya Group has established partnerships with 13 banks to provide payment services using debit and credit cards. With this agreement, Erajaya Group is safe from credit risk default from its customers and therefore is secured from bad debt.

Erajaya Group believes that third-party financing brings positive impact on the sales of mobile phones and other IT products because it allows customers with lower incomes to buy it.
