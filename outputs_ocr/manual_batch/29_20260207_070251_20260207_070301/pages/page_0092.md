Annual Report 2014 PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk

# LEGAL CASES

In 2014, one of the Company's subsidiaries, namely PT Data Citra Mandiri ("DCM") again received a lawsuit from PT Multicom Persada International concerning iBox trademark cancelation lawsuit No. 22/PDT.SUS-MEREK/2014/PN.NIAGA.JKT.PST in the District Court of Central Jakarta. The said lawsuit has reached verdict, and the lawsuit from PT Multicom Persada International against the DCM is denied by the Court of Justice.
