## BOARD OF DIRECTORS

The Board of Directors is responsible for managing day-to-day operational activities under the supervision of the Board of Commissioners. Members of the Board of Directors are appointed and dismissed by the shareholders through the AGM. The Board of Directors consists of 6 persons, namely 1 President Director, 5 Directors and one Independent Director.

## REMUNERATION OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS AND DIRECTORS

The GMS on June 26, 2014 approved to the majority shareholders of the Company, namely PT Eralink International, to determine the salaries and allowances and / or other benefits of the Board of Commissioners of the Company for the fiscal year 2014; and to grant authority to the Board of Commissioners to determine the amount of salaries and allowances and / or other benefits for all members of the Board of Directors for the Company for the fiscal year 2014.

Total remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors amounted to Rp 56.47 billion for a period of 12 months for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2014.
