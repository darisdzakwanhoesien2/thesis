# Customer Satisfaction [SEGJK F.30]

As explained in the Complaint Mechanism on page 217, customers can submit complaints related to the Group's services or products, including technical issues, through the designated channels.

The Group continuously monitors and evaluates customer satisfaction levels by measuring the Service Level Agreement Compliance Rate ("SLA Compliance Rate"), which reflects the percentage of timely complaint resolution in accordance with the terms agreed upon in the service agreement.
