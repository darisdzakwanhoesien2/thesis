# AKSI KORPORASI

Pada tahun 2020 Perseroan telah menyelesaikan aksi korporasi Penambahan Modal Tanpa Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu (PMTHMETD) sehingga jumlah saham beredar di pasar menjadi 15.660.887.000 saham.

# SANKSI PERDAGANGAN SAHAM

Pada tahun 2020 Perseroan tidak menerima sanksi berupa penghentian sementara perdagangan atau penghapusan pencatatan saham dari Bursa Efek Indonesia.

# CORPORATE ACTION

In 2020, the Company has completed the corporate action of Capital Increase Without Pre-emptive Rights (PMTHMETD) so that the number of shares outstanding in the market become 15,660,887,000 shares.

# SHARE TRADING SANCTION

In 2020, the Company did not receive sanctions in the form of temporary suspension of trading or delisting of shares from the Indonesia Stock Exchange.
