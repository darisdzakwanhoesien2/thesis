# Program COVID-19

Memasuki tahun 2022, pandemi COVID-19 semakin terkendali. Program vaksinasi COVID-19 yang diselenggarakan pemerintah berhasil meningkatkan imunitas masyarakat sehingga kasus positif COVID-19 semakin berkurang.

Meski demikian, pemerintah meminta masyarakat tetap waspada dan mematuhi protokol kesehatan (prokes) seperti yang selama ini dilakukan. Terlebih lagi kasus varian baru COVID-19 terus bermunculan meski efek dan gejalanya di masyarakat rendah.

CPIN sepanjang tahun 2022 tetap memberlakukan kebijakan Protokol Kesehatan di lokasi operasional CPIN dan anak usaha untuk mencegah dan memutus penularan rantai COVID-19. Seluruh alat kelengkapan dalam merespon pandemi COVID-19 juga masih bertugas dan disiagakan, seperti tim Penanganan COVID-19 atau Satuan Tugas Penanggulangan COVID-19 Perusahaan.

Demikian juga dengan mekanisme terkait skema alur komunikasi darurat dan skenario penanggulangan kondisi darurat COVID-19 yang dibuat sejak tahun 2020 masih tetap berlaku.

Informasi terkait protokol kesehatan dan kegiatan CPIN tersedia di Bab 6 – Informasi Pendukung (halaman 118-119).

# COVID-19 Program

Entering 2022, the COVID-19 pandemic has been effectively controlled. The COVID-19 vaccination program organized by the government has succeeded in increasing public immunity so that positive cases of COVID-19 are decreasing.

Nevertheless, the government urges the public to remain vigilant and comply with health protocols (prokes) that has been done so far. Moreover, cases of COVID-19 new variants continue to emerge, even though the effects and symptoms in the community are low.

Throughout 2022, CPIN continues to implement the Health Protocol policy at CPIN and its subsidiaries' operational areas to prevent and break the transmission of the COVID-19 chain. All equipments in responding to the COVID-19 pandemic, such as the COVID-19 Handling Team or the Company's COVID-19 Response Task Force, are also still on duty and alerted.

Likewise, mechanisms related to emergency communication flow schemes and COVID-19 emergency response scenarios created since 2020 are still valid.

Information on health protocols and CPIN activities can be found in Chapter 6 - Supporting Information (page 118-119).
