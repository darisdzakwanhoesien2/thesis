Corn Partnership Testimony

Karena skema baru, awalnya saya dan teman-teman cukup khawatir. Tapi waktu panen ternyata kami sangat terbantu. Prosesnya mudah, karena jagung dijual dalam bentuk jagung pipil basah, kita tidak perlu melakukan proses pengeringan dulu. Jadi dari dipetik, dipipil dan dikumpulkan langsung dikirim ke pabrik.

Rantai pasok juga pendek dari petani langsung ke kirim ke pabrik, kita jadi dapat harga bagus. Sejauh ini kami sudah menyetor 6.000 ton jagung ke CPIN dan dari penanaman pertama dan penanaman berikutnya pendapatan kami selalu meningkat.

My friends and I were worried at first since it was a new scheme. But it turned out we were greatly helped during harvest time. The process is simple, because the corn is sold in the form of wet corn kernel, we don't need to do the drying process first. So, it is sent directly to the factory after being picked, pickled and collected.

The supply chain is also short, only from the farmers directly to the factory, so we get a good price. So far, we have delivered 6,000 tons of corn to CPIN and from the first planting to the next planting, our income has always increased.

Saya sudah bermitra sejak Agustus 2021 dengan luas lahan yang ditanam per Desember 2022 sudah 700 hektar. Lahan yang sudah dipanen 210 hektar dan sudah mengirim sebanyak 2.275 ton ke pabrik CPIN.

Kelebihan dengan kemitraan ini kami mendapat benih bermutu dari BISI, ada fasilitas pinjaman saprotan dan pasar yang jelas. Selain itu kami juga mendapatkan pembinaan melekat dari petugas BISI dan CPIN.

Varietas jagung yang dipasok BISI disesuaikan dengan stok benih. Kami berharap mitra petani bisa mendapatkan varietas jagung sesuai dengan pilihan anggota CGR kami. Kami juga berharap, CPIN terus meningkatkan kapasitas produksi, sehingga dapat menampung jagung dari mitra plasma dalam jumlah yang lebih besar.

I have been a partner since August 2021, and as of December 2022, we have 700 hectares of planted land. 210 hectares of land have been harvested and 2,275 tons of corn have been sent to the CPIN factory.

The advantages of this partnership include quality seeds from BISI, a loan facility for saprotan and a definite market. In addition, we also get coaching from BISI and CPIN officers.

The corn varieties supplied by BISI are adjusted to the seed stock. We hope that partners farmer can get corn varieties based on the choice of our CGR members. We also hope that CPIN will continue to increase its production capacity, so that it can accommodate corn from plasma partners in larger quantities.
