CPIN belum memiliki unit kerja atau komite khusus keberlanjutan. Praktik keberlanjutan yang diselenggarakan Perseroan merupakan bagian dari tanggung jawab Dewan Direksi yang berwenang menentukan kebijakan keberlanjutan. Sementara pelaksanaan praktik keberlanjutan didelegasikan kepada tim lintas fungsional yang beranggotakan perwakilan dari unit/departemen terkait aspek keberlanjutan.

## Kompetensi Penanggung Jawab Keberlanjutan

Agar mampu melaksanakan tugas dan kewenangan dalam mengelola praktik keberlanjutan dengan baik, CPIN menyelenggarakan program pelatihan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi tim lintas fungsional sesuai dengan kompetensi yang dibutuhkan masing-masing departemen yang mengelola aspek keberlanjutan. Kegiatan pelatihan keberlanjutan yang diselenggarakan selama periode pelaporan dapat dilihat di Bab Pengembangan SDM. Sementara pelatihan terkait tata kelola dapat dilihat di Laporan Tahunan (Annual Report).

CPIN still does not have a dedicated sustainability working unit or committee. The Company's sustainability practices are part of the responsibility of the Board of Directors, which has the authority to establish sustainability policies. Meanwhile, the implementation of sustainability practices is delegated to a cross-functional team consisting of representatives from units/departments related to sustainability aspects.

## Competence of A Person in Charge of Sustainability

To be able to carry out their tasks and authorities in managing sustainability practices properly, CPIN organizes training programs to improve the competencies of cross-functional teams in accordance with the competencies needed by each department that manages sustainability aspects. Organized sustainability training activities during the reporting period can be seen in the HR Development Chapter. Meanwhile, training related to governance can be found in the Annual Report.
