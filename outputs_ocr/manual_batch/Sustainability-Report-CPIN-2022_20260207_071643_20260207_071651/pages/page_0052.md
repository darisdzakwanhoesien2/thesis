Peminjaman paket kemitraan ini dilakukan dengan sistem pembayaran pasca panen. Metode ini berpotensi memberi keuntungan lebih besar bagi petani dan CPIN sebagai off taker karena memperpendek rantai pasok.

Sebelumnya, harga jagung petani ditentukan oleh pengepul yang biasa membeli dengan harga rendah dari petani dan menjual ke konsumen dengan harga tinggi. Melalui kemitraan ini, petani memperoleh harga yang lebih baik.

Selama setahun lebih, Program Kemitraan Penanaman Jagung (Corn Partnership) ini telah merangkul petani jagung yang tersebar di 10 provinsi yang menjadi sentra komoditi jagung. BISI mentargetkan pada tahun mendatang, luas lahan mitra akan bertambah menjadi sekitar 120.000 hektar.

To ensure that the condition and quality of corn kernel remains good and fresh or undamaged when it arrives at the location of the animal feed factory, CPIN and BISI limit the location of partner farms to no more than 200 km or 6 hours by road from the location of CPIN's animal feed factory.

The loan of this partnership package is carried out with a post-harvest payment system. This method has the potential to provide greater benefits for farmers and CPIN as an off taker because it shortens the supply chain.

Previously, farmers' corn prices were determined by collectors who used to buy at low prices from farmers and sell to consumers at high prices. Through this partnership, farmers receive a better price.

For more than a year, the Corn Partnership Program has embraced corn farmers that spread across 10 provinces that serves as corn commodity centers. In the coming year, BISI targets that the partner land area will increase to around 120,000 hectares.

# ADDED VALUE FOR BISI

+ Meningkatkan market share benih jagung hibrida BISI. Increase the market share of BISI hybrid corn seeds.
+ Serapan benih jagung hibrida BISI lebih stabil dan berkelanjutan. BISI's hybrid corn seed uptake is more stable and continuous.

# NILAI TAMBAH BAGI CPIN

# ADDED VALUE FOR CPIN

+ Kepastian pasokan bahan baku pakan ternak berupa jagung pipil segar. Supply guarantee of raw materials for animal feed in the form of fresh corn kernel.
+ Mendapat jagung pipil segar dengan kualitas sesuai standar pabrik pakan ternak CPIN. Obtain fresh corn kernel following CPIN's animal feed factory quality standards.
+ Harga bahan baku bersaing karena tidak melalui perantara atau pedagang. Competitive raw material prices because they do not go through intermediaries or traders.
+ CPIN dapat menyusun prediksi produksi dengan lebih baik karena memiliki data prospek pasokan bahan baku. CPIN can make better production predictions because we have data on raw material supply prospects.
+ Rantai pasok produksi lebih efisien sehingga meminimalisir risiko fluktuasi ekstrim dari harga bahan baku jagung. More efficient production supply chain that minimizes the risk of extreme fluctuations in corn raw material prices.

+ Tersedia skema pinjaman modal saprotan dengan sistem pembayaran setelah panen. There is a loan scheme for saprotan capital with an after-harvest payment system.
