Corn Partnership Testimony

![img-54.jpeg](img-54.jpeg)
Abdul Mujid Efendi
CGR, Mojokerto

Kami mulai bermitra dengan BISI dan CPIN sejak Februari 2022. Awalnya luas lahan kemitraan kami seluas 50 hektar. Kami mendapatkan pinjaman benih jagung yakni BISI 18 beserta obat dan pupuk yang baru akan dibayar setelah panen.

Setelah panen sekitar bulan Juli hasilnya sekitar 10-12 ton, seluruhnya kita bawa ke CPIN dan dibeli dengan harga Rp4.050/kg. Setelah dipotong biaya transportasi, anggota kami mendapat pembayaran Rp 3.850/kg. Ini jauh lebih tinggi ketimbang menjual sendiri.

Kemitraan ini sangat bermanfaat, karena kami petani membutuhkan mitra yang membeli produk secara berkelanjutan, CPIN dan BISI mencari jagung berkualitas bagus. Kami berharap kemitraan ini diperluas, karena banyak petani yang berminat.

We have been partnering with BISI and CPIN since February 2022. Our partnership land area was started with 50 hectares. We received a loan of BISI 18 corn seeds, as well as medicine and fertilizer, which will only be paid after the harvest.

After harvesting around July, the yield was approximately 10-12 tons, we brought all of it to CPIN and was bought at a price of IDR 4,050/kg. After deducting transportation costs, our members get a payment of IDR 3,850/kg. This is much higher than if they sell it on their own.

This partnership is very beneficial because farmers need partners who will continuously buy our products, CPIN and BISI are looking for good quality corn. We hope this partnership is expanded because many farmers are interested.

Rupian
CGR, Lemongan

Awal menjadi mitra inti plasma BISI dan CPIN ini adalah pada September 2021. Saya dan teman-teman di kelompok tani memiliki total lahan 100 hektar. Ketika itu kami mengambil modal benih jagung sebesar 1,6 ton.

Skema ini sangat membantu dan membuat petani bersemangat, karena biasanya perlu modal untuk membeli saprotan sendiri. Sekarang dipinjamkan dulu dan dibayar belakangan.

We started becoming a core plasma partner of BISI and CPIN in September 2021. My friends and I, who are in farmer group, have a total of 100 hectares of land. At that time, we received 1.6 tons of corn seed capital.

This scheme is very helpful and exciting for farmers because they usually need capital to buy their own saprotan. Now it can be loaned first and paid later.
