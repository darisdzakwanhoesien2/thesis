FOOD SECURITY

Sebagai bentuk tanggung jawab dalam memastikan keamanan dan keselamatan pelanggan saat menggunakan produk, CPIN menyertakan informasi mengenai cara penggunaan dan penanganan produk pada setiap kemasan. Labelisasi yang dilakukan mengacu pada ketentuan yang berlaku sesuai persyaratan Badan Pengawas Obat dan Makanan (BPOM) yang juga mengacu pada Undang-undang Perlindungan Konsumen.

## Kepuasan Pelanggan

Kepuasan pelanggan dan konsumen merupakan aspek penting bagi keberlanjutan usaha. CPIN berkomitmen menerapkan prosedur pemeriksaan atau evaluasi keamanan produk yang ketat sebelum produk didistribusikan.

Melalui pendekatan ini kami memastikan produk diterima pelanggan dalam kondisi dan kualitas terbaik serta aman dikonsumsi. Selama periode pelaporan tidak ada catatan produk kami yang ditarik dari pasaran.

Dalam rangka memastikan dan mengetahui persepsi pelanggan dan konsumen saat membeli produk atau terkait pelayanan dalam operasional, CPIN secara berkala melakukan survey kepuasan pelanggan.

Setiap tahun tim Pengembangan Pemasaran Pusat menyusun kuesioner yang diserahkan ke masing-masing Marketing Executive di setiap area untuk ditujukan kepada pelanggan dan konsumen. Hasil survey selanjutnya akan dianalisa dan dilaporkan ke manajemen.

Di unit usaha Feed, hasil pengukuran kepuasan pelanggan (skala 1-20) di 2 pabrik pakan ternak adalah 19,55 dan 18. Sementara pada unit usaha Food hasil pengukuran kepuasan pelanggan mencapai 82,4 (skala 1-100). Sedangkan pada unit usaha Farm, tidak ada survey kepuasan pelanggan.

As a form of responsibility in ensuring the safety and security of customers when using products, CPIN includes information on how to use and handle products on each package. The labeling refers to the applicable provisions in accordance with the requirements of the National Agency for Drug and Food Control (BPOM) which also refers to the Consumer Protection Law.

## Customer Satisfaction

Customer and consumer satisfaction is an extremely important aspect for business sustainability. CPIN is committed to implementing strict product safety inspection or evaluation procedures before products are distributed.

With this approach we ensure that our products are received by customers in its best condition and quality as well as safe for consumption. There were no records of our products being recalled from the market during the reporting period.

In order to ensure and understand the perception of customers and consumers when buying products or related services of our operations, PIN conducts regular customer satisfaction surveys.

Annually, the Central Marketing Development team compiles questionnaires sent to each Marketing Executive in each area to be addressed to customers and consumers. The survey results then will be analyzed and reported to management.

In the Feed business unit, the results of customer satisfaction measurement (scale 1-20) in 2 animal feed factories were 19.55 and 18. Meanwhile, in the Food business unit, the results of customer satisfaction measurement (scale 1-100) were 82.4. Whereas in the Farm business unit, there is no customer satisfaction survey.
