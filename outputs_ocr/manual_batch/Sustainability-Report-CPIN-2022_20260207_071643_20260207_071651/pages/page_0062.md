STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP

Program ini mendorong kolaborasi aktif praktisi ahli dengan dosen dan mahasiswa agar tercipta pertukaran ilmu dan keahlian yang mendalam dan bermakna antar sivitas akademika di perguruan tinggi dan profesional di dunia kerja.

Kolaborasi ini dilakukan dalam mata kuliah yang disampaikan di ruang kelas baik secara luring maupun daring. Harapannya, bimbingan dan materi yang diberikan para praktisi dapat berkontribusi dan menginspirasi para lulusan perguruan tinggi.

Terdapat dua skema kolaborasi yang ditawarkan program Praktisi Mengajar. Pertama, kolaborasi pendek berlangsung selama 4 sampai 10 jam per semester, praktisi hanya terlibat dalam pengajaran. Kedua, kolaborasi Intensif, dosen dan praktisi berkolaborasi secara end-to-end. Artinya, praktisi mengajar 15 sampai 41 jam per semester dan terlibat dalam perencanaan dan evaluasi.

Perseroan memberikan keleluasaan bagi karyawannya yang hendak berkontribusi dalam program ini dengan meluangkan waktu kerjanya untuk mengajar. CPIN sebagai pionir industri peternakan ayam modern di Indonesia, pastinya memiliki pengalaman yang luas dan berharga bagi perguruan tinggi.

This program encourages active collaboration of expert practitioners with lecturers and students to create a deep and meaningful exchange of knowledge and expertise between academicians in universities and professionals in the working world.

This collaboration is carried out through courses that taught in both offline and online classrooms. The guidance and material provided by practitioners are expected to contribute and inspire college graduates.

The Teaching Practitioner program offers two collaboration schemes. First, short collaboration that lasts for 4 to 10 hours per semester where practitioners only involved in teaching. Second, Intensive collaboration where lecturers and practitioners collaborate in end-to-end manner. That means practitioners teach 15 to 41 hours per semester and are involved in planning and evaluation.

The Company provides flexibility for its employees who wish to contribute to this program by volunteering their work time to teach. CPIN, as a pioneer of the modern chicken farming industry in Indonesia, undoubtedly offers extensive and valuable experience for universities.
