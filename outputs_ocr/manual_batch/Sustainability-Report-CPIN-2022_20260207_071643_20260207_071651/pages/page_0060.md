Sejak diinisiasi sejak 2006 ini, total CPIN telah menghibahkan 13 kandang modern dengan sistem closed house senilai lebih dari Rp1,5 miliar setiap kandang kepada 10 perguruan tinggi di seluruh Indonesia. Since its initiation in 2006, CPIN has donated a total of 13 modern cages with a closed house system, each worth more than Rp1.5 billion, to 10 universities throughout Indonesia. There are 2 modern cages under the CSR Teaching Farm Program that are currently under construction.

## Mendukung Kurikulum Kampus Merdeka

Program CSR Pendidikan yang diinisiasi CPIN sejak 2006, sangat berkorelasi dengan kurikulum Merdeka Belajar Kampus Merdeka (MBKM) yang diluncurkan Kemendikbud sejak awal 2020 lalu. Salah satu program dari kebijakan Kampus Merdeka adalah memberikan hak kepada mahasiswa untuk mengambil mata kuliah di luar prodi dan melakukan perubahan definisi Satuan Kredit Semester (SKS).

MBKM sangat menekankan pentingnya keterserapan sarjana lulusan Perguruan Tinggi oleh dunia kerja. Oleh karena itu dalam kurikulum MBKM semua mata kuliah lebih diarahkan pada penguasaan hal-hal teknis kesiapan keterserapan dunia kerja.

Dari total 8 semester yang dijadwalkan selama perkuliahan, tiga semester terdiri dari mata kuliah wajib, dua semester mata kuliah program studi, satu semester mata kuliah lintas, dan dua semester magang. Konsep pendidikan MBKM ini menekankan pentingnya kemitraan antara Perguruan Tinggi dengan industri.

## Supporting the Kampus Merdeka Curriculum

The Education CSR program initiated by CPIN since 2006 is highly correlated with the Merdeka Belajar Kampus Merdeka (MBKM) curriculum launched by the Ministry of Education and Culture since early 2020. One of the programs of Kampus Merdeka policy is to allow students to take courses outside of their study program and change the definition of Semester Credit Units (SKS).

MBKM strongly emphasizes the importance of the absorption of university graduates by the working world. Therefore, all courses in the MBKM curriculum are more directed at mastering technical aspects, readiness of workforce absorption.

Of the total 8 semesters scheduled during lectures, three semesters consist of compulsory courses, two semesters of study program courses, one semester of cross courses, and two semesters of internships. The MBKM education concept emphasizes the importance of partnerships between universities and the industry.
