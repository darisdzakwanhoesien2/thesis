# Praktik Ketenagakerjaan

## Lingkungan Kerja yang Adil dan Setara

Proses pengelolaan SDM oleh Perseroan menjunjung tinggi kepatuhan terhadap peraturan dan ketentuan ketenagakerjaan yang berlaku. CPIN menganut prinsip kesetaraan melalui praktek ketenagakerjaan yang adil, setara dan tidak membedabedakan etnis, suku, agama, jenis kelamin, pendidikan, dan budaya dalam hal pendelegasian tugas, kewenangan, dan kesempatan.

Implementasi kesetaraan ini berlaku pada program rekrutmen, pengembangan dan pelatihan serta pemberian remunerasi. Dalam setiap pengambilan keputusan, CPIN mendasarkan pertimbangannya murni pada kinerja, kompetensi, dan profesionalitas karyawan maupun calon karyawan.

Seluruh karyawan diperlakukan sesuai dengan prinsip keadilan dan kesetaraan untuk mengembangkan potensi dan karir di dalam CPIN.

## Penegakan Hak Asasi Manusia (HAM)

CPIN tidak mentoleransi praktek kerja paksa dan pekerja anak. Setiap karyawan yang kami rekrut akan terlindungi dalam surat perjanjian kerja yang sah secara hukum yang telah disepakati dan menetapkan batas usia minimal karyawan untuk mencegah perekrutan anak di bawah umur.

Komitmen terhadap pencegahan diskriminasi ras, agama, suku, dan gender juga diimplementasikan dalam bentuk kesempatan memperoleh remunerasi dan pelatihan yang sama antara pekerja laki-laki dan perempuan.

## Remunerasi

Berdasarkan kebijakan Perseroan, remunerasi diberikan dalam bentuk gaji tetap yang sesuai dengan ketentuan minimal upah minimum yang berlaku di wilayah operasi. CPIN menerapkan sistem remunerasi yang adil kepada karyawan berdasarkan target kinerja (Key Performance Indicator -KPI).

Seluruh karyawan tetap di tingkat terendah, laki-laki maupun perempuan, memperoleh upah minimum sesuai dengan ketentuan Upah Minimum Provinsi (UMP) di wilayah operasional terkait. Selain remunerasi, kami juga memberikan fasilitas tunjangan kesehatan serta dana pensiun melalui program BPJS Kesehatan dan Ketenagakerjaan.

# Employment Practices

## Fair and Equal Workplace

The Company's HR management process upholds compliance with applicable labor rules and regulations. CPIN adheres to the principle of equality through employment practices that are fair, equal regardless of their ethnicities, races, religions, genders, education and cultures in terms of delegation of duties, authority and opportunities.

The implementation of this equality applies to recruitment, development and training programs as well as remuneration. In every decision making, CPIN bases its consideration purely on the performance, competence and professionalism of employees and prospective employees.

All employees are treated in accordance with the principles of fairness and equality to develop their potential and career within CPIN.

## Human Rights Enforcement

CPIN does not tolerate forced labor and child labor. Every recruited employee will be protected by a legally binding employment agreement letter that has been agreed upon and we also set a minimum age limit for employees to prevent recruiting minors.

Commitment to the prevention of racial, religious, ethnic and gender discrimination is also implemented through equal remuneration and training opportunities for male and female employees.

## Remuneration

Based on the Company's policy, remuneration is given through fixed salary in accordance with minimum wage provisions applicable in the operational area. CPIN implement a fair remuneration system for employees based on performance targets (Key Performance Indicator - KPI).

All permanent employees at the lowest level, male and female, receive minimum wage in accordance with the UMP provisions in the relevant operational area. In addition to remuneration, we also provide health benefits and pension funds through the BPJS Health and BPJS Employment programs.
