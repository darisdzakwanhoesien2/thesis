Multiplier Effect of CPIN's CSR Teaching Farm Program

Institut Pertanian Bogor (IPB) merupakan perguruan tinggi pertama di Indonesia yang menjadi mitra program CSR Teaching Farm CPIN dan menerima hibah kandang ayam modern sejak 2006 lalu. Pada tahun 2021 lalu, IPB kembali menerima hibah kandang ayam closed house kedua berupa kandang modern berukuran 14x85 meter dengan kapasitas 20 ribu ayam yang diserahkan kepada Fakultas Ilmu Peternakan (Fapet) IPB.

The CSR Teaching Farm program pioneered by CPIN has provided many benefits and added value to the poultry farming education process in the country. Beneficiary universities from this program stated that their campuses were greatly helped by the modern chicken cage facilities donated by CPIN.

Bogor Agricultural University (IPB) was the first university in Indonesia to become a partner of CPIN's CSR Teaching Farm program and has received a modern chicken cage donation since 2006. In 2021, once again, IPB received a second closed house cage donation in the form of a modern 14x85 meter cage with a capacity of 20 thousand chickens, which was handed over to IPB's Faculty of Animal Husbandry (Fapet).

The cage, which construction cost approximately IDR 1.7 billion, is equipped with the latest Internet of Things (IOT) technology, ranging from cultivation, feed systems, cage management, to irrigation.

This closed house donation makes it very easy for the campus to implement the mandatory internship curriculum based on the Merdeka Belajar Kampus Merdeka (MBKM) program from the Ministry of Education and Culture (Kemendikbud).
