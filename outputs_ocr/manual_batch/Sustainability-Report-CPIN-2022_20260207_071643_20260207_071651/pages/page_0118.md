# Program CSR CPIN 2022 dan Penerima Manfaat

CPIN 2022 CSR Program and Beneficiaries

CPIN mendukung upaya peningkatan kualitas dan akses pendidikan di Indonesia melalui program bantuan dan hibah sarana pendidikan.

CPIN supports efforts to improve the quality and access to education in Indonesia through aid programs and educational facility grants.
