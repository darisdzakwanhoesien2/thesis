KEMITRAAN STRATEGIS

CPIN telah mempelopori kemitraan seperti ini sejak hampir 2 dekade lalu, ditandai dengan dibangunnya kerjasama program CSR Teaching Farm antara CPIN dan Institut Pertanian Bogor (IPB) dengan pemberian hibah kandang ayam modern (closed house).

Melalui program CSR Teaching Farm berupa hibah kandang ayam modern closed house, perguruan tinggi memiliki sarana belajar yang dapat memberikan pengalaman langsung (hands-on) kepada mahasiswa mengenai cara kerja industri dan penerapan teknologi di dalamnya.

Sehingga mereka menjadi kompatibel dengan industri peternakan ayam modern dari sisi kompetensi maupun keterampilan (skill).

CPIN has pioneered this partnership for over 2 decades, marked by the establishment of the CSR Teaching Farm program collaboration between CPIN and Bogor Agricultural University (IPB) with the donation of modern chicken cages (closed house).

Through the CSR Teaching Farm program in the form of a modern closed house chicken cage donation, universities have a learning tool that can provide hands-on experience to students on how the industry works and how the technology is applied in it.

Allowing them to become compatible with the modern chicken farming industry in terms of competence and skills.

# Praktisi Mengajar

CPIN juga mendorong karyawannya terutama karyawan di level senior untuk mengikuti program Praktisi Mengajar. Bagian dari program Kurikulum MBKM ini mengajak para profesional dan ahli di berbagai bidang untuk turut memajukan pendidikan Indonesia dengan menyediakan waktu untuk berbagi keahlian dan pengalaman kepada mahasiswa dan perguruan tinggi di sektor mereka masing-masing.

# Teaching Practitioner

CPIN also encourages its employees, especially senior level employees, to participate in the Teaching Practitioner program. This part of the MBKM Curriculum program invites professionals and experts from various fields to help advance Indonesian education by taking the time to share their expertise and experience with students and universities in their respective sectors.
