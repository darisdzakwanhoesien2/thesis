KEMITRAAN STRATEGIS

# Pelatihan Pengenalan (Induction Training)

Pelatihan ini berisi materi pelatihan mengenai perusahaan dan budaya perusahaan yang diperuntukan bagi karyawan baru berisi informasi mengenai visi dan misi CPIN, struktur organisasi serta peraturan perusahaan. Pelatihan ini menjadi bekal bagi karyawan untuk beradaptasi di lingkungan Perseroan dan berperilaku sesuai dengan nilai-nilai CPIN.

# Mekanisme Coaching dan Counseling

Selain pelatihan, CPIN juga melakukan pengembangan SDM melalui mekanisme bimbingan atau coaching di internal Perseroan. Karyawan dengan level manajer ditugaskan menjadi coach internal kepada rekan karyawan yang dinilai potensial atau coachee.

Sehingga manajer di seluruh unit bisnis memiliki calon pemimpin penerus (successor). Berbeda dengan training yang sifatnya jangka pendek, coaching diberikan dalam rentang waktu panjang melalui serangkaian pertemuan yang berkesinambungan.

Selama periode tersebut seorang coach membantu coachee mengoptimalkan potensi dirinya dalam mencapai satu tujuan tertentu. Sebaliknya dengan melakukan coaching, manajer juga diharapkan dapat memiliki pengalaman sebagai pemimpin yang mampu mengembangkan orang lain.

Mekanisme coaching ini diharapkan dapat meningkatkan kerjasama, kolaborasi, motivasi antar karyawan (employee engagement) dan tercapainya obyektif kinerja (target bisnis) yang berperan penting dalam mendukung keberlanjutan (sustainability) organisasi dalam jangka panjang.

# Aplikasi Knowledge Power

CPIN juga telah merancang aplikasi Knowledge Power untuk memfasilitasi proses coaching di internal organisasi guna mendukung kaderisasi pemimpin (leaders) di Perseroan.

Melalui aplikasi ini sessi bimbingan atau coaching dapat dilakukan secara digital sehingga lebih memudahkan kedua pihak yang terlibat baik coach dan coachee karena dapat dilakukan dari mana saja di waktu yang disepakati.

# Kunjungan Antar Lini Usaha

Mengingat CPIN memiliki lini usaha yang terintegrasi dari hulu ke hilir, Perseroan juga melakukan kegiatan kunjungan antar unit bisnis. Hal ini dimaksudkan agar karyawan dapat memahami sepenuhnya konektivitas dan operasional di masing-masing unit bisnis kami.

# Induction Training

This training contains materials about the company and corporate culture for new employees, provide information about CPIN's vision and mission, organizational structure and company regulations. This training assists employees in adapting to the Company's environment and behave in accordance with CPIN's values.

# Coaching dan Counselling Mechanism

Aside from training, CPIN also conducts HR development through a coaching mechanism within the Company. Employees at the managerial level are assigned to be internal coaches to their coworkers who are considered to have potential or coachee.

Therefore, managers in all business units have potential successor leaders (successors). In contrast to short-term training, coaching is provided over a long period of time through a series of continuous meetings.

During this period, the coach helps the coachee to optimize his/her potential in achieving a particular goal. On the other hand, by coaching, managers are also expected to gain experience as leaders who are able to develop others.

This coaching mechanism is expected to improve cooperation, collaboration, motivation among employees (employee engagement) and the achievement of performance objectives (business targets) that play an important role in supporting the long-term sustainability of the organization.

# Knowledge Power Application

CPIN has also developed the Knowledge Power application to facilitate the coaching process within the organization to support regeneration of leaders in the Company.

Through this application, coaching sessions can be conducted digitally, making it easier for both parties involved, coach and coachee, as it can be done from anywhere at an agreed time.

# Inter-Business Line Visits

As CPIN has an integrated business line from upstream to downstream, the Company also conducts visits between business units. Therefore, our employees fully understand the connectivity and operations in each of our business units.
