TENTANG CHAROEN POKPHAND INDONESIA (CPIN)

# Sekilas CPIN

CPIN At a Glance

Charoen Pokphand Indonesia Tbk (CPIN) adalah perusahaan terbesar di industri perunggasan Indonesia dengan kegiatan usaha utama yaitu Feed, Farm dan Food. CPIN memproduksi beragam produk seperti pakan ternak, anak ayam usia sehari atau Day Old Chick (DOC), ayam pedaging hingga makanan olahan.

Mulai beroperasi sejak tahun 1972, CPIN memiliki tradisi keunggulan kualitas yang mendorong Perseroan untuk selalu memastikan terpenuhinya kualitas dan mutu produk sesuai standar mutu yang berlaku nasional maupun internasional. Kegiatan operasional CPIN didukung oleh proses produksi yang modern dengan dukungan teknologi dan inovasi termutakhir.

Charoen Pokphand Indonesia Tbk (CPIN) is the largest company in the Indonesian poultry industry, with its main business activities consisting of Feed, Farm and Food. CPIN produces a variety of products such as animal feed, day old chicks (DOC), broilers and processed foods.

Since its first operation in 1972, CPIN has a tradition of quality excellence that encourages the Company to always ensure the fulfilment of product quality and grade according to national and international quality standards. CPIN's operational activities are supported by modern production processes assisted by the latest technology and innovation.

# Lini Bisnis CPIN

CPIN Business Line

|  LINI BISNIS BUSINESS LINE | DESKRIPSI DESCRIPTION  |
| --- | --- |
|  Feed | CPIN memproduksi beragam produk pakan ternak dengan kandungan formulasi yang disesuaikan dengan tahapan usia ternak. Seluruh produk telah dilengkapi dengan sertifikasi jaminan mutu dan keamanan produk.
CPIN produces a variety of animal feed products with formulation content customized to livestock age stages. All products have been certified with quality assurance and product safety.  |
|  Farm | Sebagai perusahaan peternakan ayam terintegrasi, CPIN menjual anak ayam usia sehari atau Day Old Chick (DOC) ber-standard internasional dengan kualitas genetika terbaik di dunia. As an integrated chicken farming company, CPIN sells international-standard Day Old Chick (DOC) with the best genetic quality in the world. CPIN also manages broiler and layer farms to supply broiler and egg needs.  |
|  Food | CPIN memproduksi makanan olahan berbasis ayam dengan beragam varian produk yang ditujukan untuk berbagai segmen pasar di masyarakat. Produk diproses sesuai standar pengawasan mutu (quality control) untuk menjaga mutu produk.
CPIN produces chicken-based processed foods with a variety of product variants aimed at various market segments in the community. Products are processed according to quality control standards to maintain its quality.  |
