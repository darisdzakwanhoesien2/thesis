## Hibah Teaching Farm
Program hibah Teaching Farm ini dirancang sebagai sarana penunjang peningkatan proses belajar mengajar, praktikum, magang dan sekaligus menjadi tempat penelitian bagi dosen/mahasiswa Fakultas Pertanian Jurusan Peternakan. Fasilitas ini diharapkan dapat melengkapi kemampuan (skill) baik yang bersifat teknis mengenai pemeliharaan ayam pedaging, maupun aspek sosial dan bisnis.

Selain sebagai sarana pendidikan, kandang itu juga sekaligus berfungsi sebagai media usaha peternakan ayam yang dikelola secara komersial, fakultas penerima hibah terikat kerjasama kemitraan inti plasma dengan CPIN. Keuntungan yang didapat dari penjualan ayam pedaging dapat digunakan untuk membiayai program peningkatan sarana dan prasarana pendidikan di fakultas.

Pada tahun 2022, CPIN kembali memberikan hibah kandang ayam modern dengan sistem closed house dalam program CSR Teaching Farm kepada Universitas Padjajaran. Selain itu, CPIN juga telah menandatangani MoU dengan Universitas Sumatera Utara (USU) dan Universitas Tanjung Pura untuk memulai program CSR Teaching Farm.

## Teaching Farm Donation
The Teaching Farm donation program is designed to support the improvement of teaching and learning process, practicum, internship and at the same time become a research place for lecturers / students of the Faculty of Agriculture, Department of Animal Husbandry. This facility is expected to complement both technical skills regarding broiler maintenance, as well as social and business aspects.

Aside from being an educational facility, the cage also serves as a medium for commercially managed chicken farming, the beneficiary faculty is bound by a core plasma partnership with CPIN. Profits that obtained from broilers sales can be used to fund programs to improve educational facilities and infrastructure in the faculty.

In 2022, once again, CPIN donated modern chicken cages with a closed house system to Padjajaran University as part of the CSR Teaching Farm program. In addition, CPIN has also signed MoUs with the University of North Sumatra (USU) and Tanjung Pura University to start the CSR Teaching Farm program.
