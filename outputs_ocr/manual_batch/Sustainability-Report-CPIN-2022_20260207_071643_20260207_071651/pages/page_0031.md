KETAHANAN PANGAN

Pandemi COVID-19 dan krisis geopolitik di Eropa telah meningkatkan ancaman krisis pangan global yang ditandai terus meningkatnya harga pangan di tingkat global. Isu ketahanan pangan seperti ketersediaan dan akses pangan menjadi perhatian hampir semua negara di dunia. Di tengah kondisi yang mengkhawatirkan ini, kinerja ketahanan panan Indonesia justru meningkat. Laporan Economist Impact mencatat skor Indeks Ketahanan Pangan Global (Global Food Security Index/GFSI) Indonesia pada tahun 2022 naik 1,7% dan berada pada kategori moderat.

Pencapaian ini menekankan pentingnya kemandirian pangan di dalam negeri. CPIN sebagai produsen produk peternakan ayam terintegrasi terbesar di Indonesia berupaya mendukung ketahanan pangan nasional melalui produk dan kegiatan bisnisnya, yaitu:

1. Meningkatkan ketersediaan (availability);
2. Meningkatkan kemudahan akses (access) pangan; dan
3. Memastikan jaminan mutu dan keamanan pangan produk yang dihasilkan (food safety).

The COVID-19 pandemic and the geopolitical crisis in Europe have increased the threat of a global food crisis characterized by the continuous increasing food prices at the global level. Food security issues such as food availability and food access become concerns for almost all countries in the world. In the midst of these worrying conditions, Indonesia's food security performance has actually improved. According to Economist Impact Report, Indonesia's Global Food Security Index (GFSI) score in 2022 improved by 1.7% and remained in the moderate category.

This achievement emphasizes the importance of food independence in the country. As the largest integrated producer of chicken farming products in Indonesia, CPIN strives to support national food security through its products and business activities, namely:

1. Increasing availability;
2. Increasing food accessibility; and
3. Ensuring quality assurance and food safety.
