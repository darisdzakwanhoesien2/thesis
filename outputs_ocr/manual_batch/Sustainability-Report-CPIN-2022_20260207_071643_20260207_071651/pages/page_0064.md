Hibah closed house mengatasi seluruh kendala tersebut. Kini Fapet IPB dapat menjadwalkan kesempatan magang bagi mahasiswa sesuai dengan kalender akademik dengan kuota yang dapat disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan. Bahkan, menerima mahasiswa magang dari universitas lain.

Pada akhir 2022 lalu, IPB menerima mahasiswa peserta magang dari Universitas Udayana, Bali sebanyak 10 orang. Selama 2 bulan 10 hari, mereka bekerja menjadi anak kandang di closed house Fapet IPB dan belajar mengenai manajemen kandang ayam modern.

"Selama belajar di kampus apalagi kami kuliah pas pandemi, jadi kami hanya bisa membayangkan saja seperti apa pengelolaan peternakan ayam itu. Dengan magang seperti ini, kami benar-benar tahu dan merasakan langsung industri peternakan ayam seperti apa," kata Maria, salah satu mahasiswa Udayana, Bali yang magang di closed house IPB.

Selain hibah Teaching Farm, CPIN juga sebelumnya telah menghibahkan gedung 3 lantai untuk Sekolah Bisnis IPB (program diploma) dan menghibahkan program Entrepreneur Teaching Center (ETC).

The closed house donation overcomes all these obstacles. Now Fapet IPB can schedule internship opportunities for students according to the academic calendar, with quotas that can be adjusted according to needs. It even accepts internship students from other universities.

At the end of 2022, IPB accepted 10 internship students from Udayana University, Bali. For 2 months and 10 days, they worked as cage keepers in the Fapet IPB's closed house and learned about modern chicken cage management.

"During our studies on campus, let alone during the pandemic, we could only imagine what the management of chicken farms was like. With this internship, we get to know and experience firsthand what the chicken farming industry is like," said Maria, one of the Udayana, Bali students who interned at IPB's closed house.

In addition to the Teaching Farm donation, CPIN has also previously donated a 3-storey building for IPB Business School (diploma program) and donated the Entrepreneur Teaching Center (ETC) program.
