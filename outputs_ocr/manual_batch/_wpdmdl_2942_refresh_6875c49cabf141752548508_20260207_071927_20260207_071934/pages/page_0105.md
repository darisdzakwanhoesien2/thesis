Selain itu, Entitas Anak juga telah memiliki prosedur penanganan keadaan darurat dan telah membentuk tim tanggap darurat yang bertujuan untuk memastikan pelaksanaan rencana tindakan tanggap darurat dan implementasi Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan (“SMKP”) berjalan dengan efektif sesuai dengan pedoman pelaksanaan kaidah teknik pertambangan yang baik.

In addition, the Subsidiary also has procedures for handling emergency situations and has formed an emergency response team which aims to ensure the implementation of the emergency response plan and the effective implementation of the Mining Safety Management System (“SMKP”) in accordance with the guidelines for implementing good mining engineering principles.
