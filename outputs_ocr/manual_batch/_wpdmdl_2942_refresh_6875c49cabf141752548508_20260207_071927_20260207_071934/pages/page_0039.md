Further, our business unit PT Citra Palu Minerals (CPM) carries out exploration, construction and mineral production in an area of 85,180 hectares in Central Sulawesi and South Sulawesi. Meanwhile, PT Dairi Prima Mineral (DPM) operates in a concession area of 24,636 hectares in North Sumatra and PT Gorontalo Minerals (GM) holds a Contract of Work concession right for a mining area of 24,995 hectares located in Bone Bolango Regency, Gorontalo Province.
