|  Lingkungan Kerja Pertambangan Work Environment Mining  |   |
|  Pengendalian dan Pengelolaan Control and Management | PT GM telah melakukan uji riksa kaitannya dengan pemantauan lingkungan kerja diantaranya: PT GM has conducted an inspection test related to workplace environmental monitoring, including 1. Uji ergonomi pekerja Test worker ergonomics 2. Uji faktor biologi Biological factor test 3. Uji faktor fisika Physics factor test 4. Uji faktor kimia Chemical factor test Uji pemeriksaan lingkungan kerja ini telah dilakukan di bulan Maret 2024 dan dalam pelaksanaannya bekerja sama dengan pihak ketiga yang telah memenuhi persyaratan perundang-undangan. This work environment inspection test was carried out in March 2024 and its implementation was carried out in collaboration with a third party that had met statutory requirements.  |

Establishment of Mining Safety Committee chaired by KTT  |
