# MANAJEMEN TOPIK MATERIAL: EKONOMI

Management of Material Topics: Economic (3-3) (2-23)

## Dampak Topik Material

Impact of Material Topic

Kinerja ekonomi yang kuat memungkinkan BRMS membawa dampak baik bagi masyarakat melalui pengadaan lokal hingga kesempatan kerja. Perusahaan juga berperan dalam mendorong kesejahteraan masyarakat melalui pembayaran royalti, pajak, dan dukungan sosial. Untuk memastikan pertumbuhan berkelanjutan, BRMS menargetkan pencapaian target bisnis dengan prinsip akuntabilitas.

Strong economic performance enables BRMS to impact communities through local procurement and employment opportunities. The company also plays a role in promoting community welfare through royalty payments, taxes and social support. BRMS achieves our business targets with the accountability principles to ensure sustainable growth.

## Pengelolaan Dampak

Management of Impact

Perusahaan menerapkan Sistem Pengendalian Internal yang mengacu pada Standard IIA dan COSO Framework. Pengendalian ini mencakup aspek keuangan, operasional, kepatuhan hukum, dan informasi pasar. Efektivitas sistem dijaga melalui keterlibatan berbagai komponen perusahaan, termasuk Dewan Komisaris, Komite Audit, Direksi, dan Departemen Audit Internal. Perusahaan juga telah menerbitkan SOP Anti Tindak Kecurangan, SOP Penerimaan dan Pelaporan Gratifikasi, dan SOP Speak-Up System (Whistleblowing).

The Company implements an Internal Control System using the IIA Standard and COSO Framework. These controls cover financial, operational, legal compliance, and market information. The system's effectiveness is maintained through the involvement of various Company components, including the Board of Commissioners, Audit Committee, Board of Directors, and Internal Audit Department. The Company also has an SOP on Anti Fraud, an SOP on Gratification Acceptance and Reporting, and an SOP on the Speak-Up System (Whistleblowing).
