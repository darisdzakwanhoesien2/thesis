# ANTIKORUPSI DAN SISTEM PELAPORAN PELANGGARAN

Anti-Corruption and Violation Reporting System (2-26) (205-1) (205-2) (205-3)

Praktik korupsi membawa konsekuensi serius bagi suatu perusahaan, mulai dari kerugian finansial, hilangnya kepercayaan pemangku kepentingan dan publik, hingga gangguan operasional akibat investigasi hukum yang dapat menunda atau bahkan menghentikan proyek pertambangan. Selain itu, dampak jangka panjangnya mencakup penurunan reputasi yang sulit dipulihkan. Lebih dari sekadar masalah hukum dan ekonomi, praktik korupsi juga dapat menghambat upaya keberlanjutan dan perlindungan Hak Asasi Manusia (HAM), terutama jika korupsi melibatkan dana yang seharusnya dialokasikan untuk perlindungan lingkungan, keselamatan kerja, serta program sosial dan komunitas.

Sebagai bentuk pencegahan, BRMS telah menerapkan kebijakan anti-korupsi yang mengikat seluruh jajaran Perusahaan, termasuk dewan direksi, karyawan, kontraktor, serta pihak dalam rantai pasok. Komitmen ini diwujudkan melalui berbagai inisiatif, seperti pencantuman klausul anti-korupsi dalam perjanjian kerja sama dengan pihak ketiga, pelatihan kepatuhan terhadap Peraturan Perusahaan dan Kode Etik bagi seluruh karyawan, serta distribusi buku Pedoman Etika dan Peraturan Perusahaan. BRMS juga memastikan adanya mekanisme pelaporan pelanggaran yang transparan dan aman, guna mendorong budaya bisnis yang bersih dan berintegritas. (3-3) (205-2)

BRMS telah melakukan risk assessment terhadap risiko korupsi di berbagai tahapan operasional Perusahaan. Kami menyadari pentingnya risk assessment sebagai langkah mitigasi risiko. (205-1)

Sebagai upaya pencegahan dan penanggulangan tindak kejahatan korupsi di perusahaan dan anak usaha, Perusahaan melakukan sosialisasi kepada seluruh (100%) pegawai terkait larangan penerimaan atau pemberian gratifikasi dan anti suap. (205-2)

Selain itu, kami telah menetapkan prosedur pelaporan atas dugaan pelanggaran Pedoman Perilaku, serta proses investigasi terhadap fraud internal dan eksternal. Prosedur ini berfungsi sebagai standar acuan dalam mencegah dan menangani pelanggaran yang wajib dipatuhi oleh seluruh karyawan perusahaan dan anak usaha. (2-26)

Corrupt practices have serious consequences for a company, ranging from financial losses, loss of stakeholder and public trust, and operational disruptions due to legal investigations that can delay or even halt mining projects. In addition, the long-term impacts include reputational damage that is difficult to recover from. More than just legal and economic issues, corrupt practices can also hamper sustainability and human rights protection efforts, especially if they involve funds that should be allocated to environmental protection, occupational safety, and social and community programs.

As a form of prevention, BRMS has implemented an anti-corruption policy that binds all levels of the Company, including the board of directors, employees, contractors, and parties in the supply chain. This commitment is realized through various initiatives, such as the inclusion of anti-corruption clauses in cooperation agreements with third parties, training on compliance with the Company Regulations and Code of Ethics for all employees, as well as the distribution of the Code of Ethics and Company Regulations. BRMS also ensures a transparent and secure whistleblowing mechanism to encourage a clean and integrity business culture.

BRMS has conducted a risk assessment of corruption risks at various stages of the Company's operations. We recognize the importance of risk assessment as a risk mitigation measure.

To prevent and overcome corruption crime in the company and its subsidiaries, the Company conducts socialization to all (100%) employees regarding the prohibition of receiving or giving gratuities and anti-bribery.

In addition, we have established a reporting procedure for alleged violations of the Code of Conduct and an investigation process for internal and external fraud. This procedure serves as a reference standard in preventing and handling violations that must be obeyed by all employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.
