The Company has also developed a complaint system for ethical violations, the Speak-up System, as outlined in the Code of Conduct. Through this system, all Company personnel can report alleged ethical violations through:

- Phone/SMS/Whatsapp to 0811-805-2757
- Sending electronic mail (e-mail) to: speakup@brm.co.id

The Speak-up System SOP includes protection for whistleblowers, including assurance of identity confidentiality and protection from threats, intimidation or other adverse actions. Every report received will be followed up by the Department of Internal Audit (DAI), prioritising reports with sufficient evidence, without ignoring reports that lack strong evidence. The Company is committed to following up every report transparently and responsibly.

Based on the analysis results, throughout 2024 DAI did not find any significant risks related to corruption in the Company's operations and there were no reports of alleged ethical violations or other violations submitted through the Speak-up System.

Provide The Best Products and Services for Consumers

(POJK51-F.17) (POJK51-F.27) (POJK51-F.28) (POJK51-F.29)

Perusahaan berkomitmen untuk meningkatkan kepuasan pelanggan dengan menyediakan produk dan layanan berkualitas tinggi. Dalam penyediaan produk dan jasa, BRMS memastikan bahwa setiap komoditas yang diproduksi telah melalui proses evaluasi keamanan yang ketat, termasuk pengujian kadar dan penentuan kadar mineral sebelum didistribusikan kepada pembeli.
