Human Resource Development Performance

Kebijakan Keberlanjutan dan Pedoman Perilaku mencerminkan komitmen kami dalam menjalankan praktik ketenagakerjaan yang menghormati hak asasi manusia (HAM) bagi seluruh individu, termasuk karyawan, pekerja kontraktor, dan pihak yang terlibat dalam rantai pasok. BRMS berupaya menyelaraskan kebijakan serta program SDM dengan merujuk pada Undang-Undang No. 13 Tahun 2003 tentang Ketenagakerjaan, Peraturan Pemerintah Republik Indonesia No. 36 Tahun 2021 tentang Pengupahan, serta standar internasional seperti International Bill of Human Rights, United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights (UNGPs), International Labour Organization's Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work, dan regulasi terkait lainnya. (2-7)

Kami melarang segala bentuk praktik perbudakan modern termasuk kerja paksa dan pekerja anak. Batas usia bekerja di BRMS dan unit usaha adalah 18 tahun sesuai dengan Undang-Undang Nomor 20 Tahun 1999 tentang Pengesahan Konvensi ILO mengenai Usia Minimum untuk Diperbolehkan Bekerja. Dalam setiap rekrutmen, kami selalu meminta salinan Kartu Identitas Kependudukan untuk memverifikasi umur pekerja. Selain itu, BRMS juga menerapkan zero-tolerance terhadap praktik kerja paksa dengan memastikan setiap karyawan bekerja sesuai bidang dan persetujuannya, memahami isi kontrak kerja, memastikan gaji sesuai dengan standar upah minimum, dan tidak ada penahanan dokumen pekerja. (POJK51-F.19)

BRMS juga memberikan kesempatan yang sama bagi semua karyawan untuk mengembangkan karir, promosi, pelatihan, dan pemberian penghargaan tanpa memandang suku, agama, etnis, jenis kelamin ataupun karakteristik-karakteristik lainnya. Dengan kesetaraan kesempatan ini, setiap karyawan dapat mengaktualkan potensi dan berkontribusi secara maksimal terhadap Perusahaan. (POJK51-F.18) (POJK51-F.19)

Setelah melakukan review pada Speak Up system maupun kanal penyampaian keluhan lainnya, kami bangga bahwa sepanjang tahun 2024 tidak terdapat aduan tentang diskriminasi terhadap karyawan, pekerja anak, dan kerja paksa di BRMS dan unit usaha. (406-1) (408-1) (409-1) (POJK51-F.18) (POJK51-F.19)

The Sustainability Policy and Code of Conduct reflect our commitment to implementing labor practices that respect human rights (HR) for all individuals, including employees, contract workers, and supply chain partners. BRMS strives to align its HR policies and programs with relevant regulations, including Law No. 13 of 2003 on Manpower, Government Regulation of the Republic of Indonesia No. 36 of 2021 on Wages, as well as international standards such as the International Bill of Human Rights, the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights (UNGPs), the International Labour Organization's Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work, and other applicable regulations.

We strictly prohibit all forms of modern slavery, including forced labor and child labor. The minimum working age at BRMS and its business units is 18 years, in accordance with Law No. 20 of 1999 on the Ratification of the ILO Convention on the Minimum Age for Admission to Employment. In every recruitment process, we require a copy of the National Identity Card to verify the worker's age. Additionally, BRMS enforces a zero-tolerance policy against forced labor by ensuring that all employees work within their agreed roles, fully understand their employment contracts, receive wages that meet minimum wage standards, and are not subjected to the withholding of personal documents.

BRMS also provides equal opportunities for all employees to develop careers, promotions, training and awarding regardless of ethnicity, religion, ethnicity, gender or other characteristics. With this equal opportunity, every employee can actualize their potential and contribute optimally to the Company.

After reviewing the Speak Up system and other complaint channels, we are proud that throughout 2024 there were no complaints about discrimination against employees, child labor and forced labor at BRMS and business units.
