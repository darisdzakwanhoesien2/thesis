|  Progra/Programs | Kegiatan/Activities  |
| --- | --- |
|  Kesehatan Kerja Pertambangan Occupational Health Mining | 1. Pemeriksaan kesehatan berkala dilakukan pada Desember 2024 diikuti oleh seluruh karyawan PT DPM baik yang berada di site maupun di Head Office Periodic health checks were carried out in December 2024 and attended by all PT DPM employees both on-site and at the Head Office 2. Penyuluhan kesehatan dengan bekerjasama dengan vendor lab klinik terkait. Health education in collaboration with the relevant clinical lab vendor.  |
|  Lingkungan Kerja Pertambangan Work Environment Mining | Pengelolaan debu sepanjang jalan tambang dengan melakukan penyiraman jalan menggunakan water truck Manage dust along the mining road by watering the road using a water truck.  |
|  Keselamatan Operasional (KO) Pertambangan Operational Safety (KO) Mining | Commissioning peralatan dilakukan pada alat berat Equipment commissioning on heavy equipment  |
|  Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan (SMKP) Mining Safety Management System (SMKP) | Audit internal SMKP dibantu oleh auditor BRM dikarenakan PT DPM belum memiliki auditor SMKP yang teregistrasi. Persiapan audit internal ini dilakukan sejak Maret 2024, dan laporan audit internal SMKP PT DPM dikirimkan pada Juni 2024. BRM auditors assist the SMKP internal audit because PT DPM does not yet have a registered SMKP auditor. Preparations for this internal audit have been carried out since March 2024, and the PT DPM SMKP internal audit report was sent in June 2024.  |
