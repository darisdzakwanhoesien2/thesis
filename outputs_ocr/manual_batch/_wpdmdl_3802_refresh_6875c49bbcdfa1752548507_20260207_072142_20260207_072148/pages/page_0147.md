## c. Kegiatan Tim Tanggap Darurat BRM

Tim Tanggap Darurat BRM akan mengidentifikasi dan menyediakan peralatan serta fasilitas Kondisi Darurat yang dibutuhkan sesuai dengan kemungkinan Kondisi Darurat yang dapat terjadi. Peralatan dan fasilitas Kondisi Darurat tersebut dipelihara, diperiksa secara periodik dan diletakkan pada tempat yang mudah dijangkau. Jenis, jumlah, penempatan dan kemudahan untuk mendapatkan peralatan dan fasilitas Kondisi Darurat harus sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan dan persyaratan lainnya, seperti Keputusan Menteri Tenaga Kerja Nomor KEP.186/MEN/1999 dan Peraturan Menteri Tenaga Kerja dan Transmigrasi Nomor PER.15/MEN/VIII/2008. Jenis peralatan dan fasilitas yang disediakan perlu disesuaikan dengan potensi Kondisi Darurat di tempat kerja dan sekurangkurangnya harus tersedia:

- Alat Pemadam Api Ringan (APAR);
- Kotak Pertolongan Pertama Pada Kecelakaan (P3K); dan
- Sarana peringatan Kondisi Darurat.

Pertemuan rutin juga dilakukan untuk memantau perkembangan dan kebutuhan tim tanggap darurat dilakukan secara berkala.

## c. BRM Emergency Response Team Activities

The BRM Emergency Response Team will identify and provide emergency equipment and facilities needed according to possible emergency conditions that may occur. Emergency equipment and facilities are maintained, checked periodically and placed in easily accessible places. The type, quantity, placement and ease of obtaining emergency equipment and facilities must comply with statutory regulations and other requirements, such as Decree of the Minister of Manpower Number KEP.186/MEN/1999 and Regulation of the Minister of Manpower and Transmigration Number PER.15/MEN/VIII/2008. The type of equipment and facilities provided need to be adapted to potential emergency conditions in the workplace and must at least be available:

- Light Fire Extinguisher (APAR);
- First Aid Kit for Accidents (P3K); And
- Emergency Condition warning facility.

Regular meetings are also held to monitor the progress and needs of the emergency response team periodically.

## d. Simulasi Tanggap Darurat Gedung Bakrie Tower

Simulasi darurat dilakukan untuk melatih kesiapsiagaan Tim Tanggap Darurat yang telah dilatih. Simulasi ini diadakan minimal sekali setahun dengan koordinasi Management Office Bakrie Tower. Pada 24 Desember 2024, simulasi tanggap darurat kebakaran dilaksanakan bersama Management Office Bakrie Tower dan Tim Pemadam Kebakaran DKI Jakarta.

## d. Emergency Response Simulation Bakrie Tower

Emergency simulations are conducted to train the preparedness of the Emergency Response Team. These simulations are held at least once a year in coordination with the Management Office of Bakrie Tower. On 24 December 2024, a fire emergency response simulation was carried out in collaboration with the Management Office of Bakrie Tower and the Jakarta Provincial Fire Department.
