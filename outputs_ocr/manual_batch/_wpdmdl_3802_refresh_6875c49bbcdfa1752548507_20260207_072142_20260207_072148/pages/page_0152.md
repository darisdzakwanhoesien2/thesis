# b. Pelatihan Anggota Tim Tanggap Darurat

1. Pelatihan HAR (High Angle Rescue)

Pelatihan evakuasi korban dari ketinggian atau jurang dilaksanakan pada 27 September 2024 di depan office ERT (Banua 2) selama  $\pm 3$  jam dengan metode slope rescue. Pelatihan melibatkan 5 peserta volunteer dan menggunakan peralatan seperti tali karmantel, full body harness, pulley, dan lainnya.

2. Pelatihan Water Rescue

Bersama Tim Basarnas Kota Palu, pelatihan penyelamatan korban tenggelam dilakukan di kolam renang Sutan Raja pada 23 Desember 2024, berlangsung  $\pm 4$  jam. Dengan 17 peserta, pelatihan mencakup teknik Reach, Throw, Row, dan Go.

3. Pelatihan Medical First Responder

Pelatihan pertolongan pertama bagi korban luka ringan hingga berat dilakukan pada 9 Oktober 2024 di ruang ERT selama  $\pm 3$  jam. Melibatkan 6 peserta dari tim paramedis dan ERT, materi mencakup teknik bidai, penggunaan KED (Kendrick Extrication Device), serta penanganan henti napas dan henti jantung.

# b. Emergency Response Team Training

1. High Angle Rescue Training

A training session for evacuating victims from heights or ravines was conducted on 27 September 2024, in front of the ERT office (Banua 2) for  $\pm 3$  hours using the slope rescue method. The training involved five volunteer participants and utilized kern mantle ropes, full-body harnesses, pulleys, and more.

2. Water Rescue Training

In collaboration with Basarnas Palu, this training focused on rescuing drowning victims and was held at Sutan Raja swimming pool on 23 December 2024, lasting  $\pm 4$  hours. With 17 participants, the training covered the Reach, Throw, Row, and Go techniques.

3. Medical First Responder Training

A first aid training session for handling minor to severe injuries was conducted on 9 October 2024 in the ERT room for  $\pm 3$  hours. Involving 6 participants from paramedics and the ERT team, it covered splinting techniques, using the Kendrick Extrication Device (KED), and handling respiratory and cardiac arrest cases.

# c. Pelaksanaan simulasi keadaan darurat

1. Simulasi Tanggap Darurat Kebakaran

Dilaksanakan pada 29 Mei 2024 di area BBC, simulasi ini bertujuan meningkatkan kesiapsiagaan tim ERT dalam merespons kebakaran serta menguji jalur evakuasi, pintu darurat, dan system alarm. Kegiatan ini melibatkan 6 peserta, termasuk tim ERT, paramedis, dan volunteer.

# c. Implementation of emergency simulations

1. Fire Emergency Response Simulation

This simulation, conducted on 29 May 2024, at the BBC area, aimed to enhance the ERT team's preparedness in responding to workplace fire emergencies and testing evacuation systems, including escape routes, emergency exits, and alarm systems. The activity involved 6 participants, including ERT, paramedics, and volunteers.
