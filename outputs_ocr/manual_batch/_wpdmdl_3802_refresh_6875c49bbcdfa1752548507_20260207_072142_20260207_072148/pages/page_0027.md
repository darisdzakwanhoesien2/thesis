BRMS always apply the precautionary principle in waste management to not pollute the soil, water sources and the sea. |  | ●  |

Business sustainability depends on good and dedicated talent. Therefore, BRMS is committed to creating a fair and comfortable working environment, with remuneration systems, leave, and working hours that meet or exceed labor standards. | ● | ●  |
