Sustainability Governance

Tugas dan tanggung jawab struktur tata kelola tersebut sebagai berikut.

- Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) merupakan pemegang wewenang tertinggi yang mengambil keputusan strategis terkait Perseroan dan Entitas Anak.
- Dewan Komisaris berperan melaksanakan pengawasan dan pemberian nasihat atas kepengurusan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak yang dilaksanakan oleh Direksi.

o Komite Audit berperan mendukung fungsi Dewan Komisaris melalui penelaahan laporan-laporan Perseroan serta mengawasi kepatuhan terhadap perundang-undangan dan peraturan yang berlaku.

Duties and responsibilities of the governance structure are as follows.

- The General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) is the highest authority holder that makes strategic decisions regarding the Company and its Subsidiaries.
- The Board of Commissioners has a role in supervising and providing advice on the management of the Company and its Subsidiaries carried out by the Board of Directors.

o The Audit Committee plays a role in supporting the Board of Commissioners' functions through reviewing the Company's reports and overseeing compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
