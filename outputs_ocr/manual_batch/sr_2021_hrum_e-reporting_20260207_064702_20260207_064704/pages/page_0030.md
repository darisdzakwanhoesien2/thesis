# Wilayah Operasional

## Operational Areas

Wilayah operasional kami tersebar di 6 wilayah yang terletak di Kabupaten Kutai Kartanegara dan Kota Samarinda, Provinsi Kalimantan Timur, serta Kabupaten Barito Utara, Provinsi Kalimantan Tengah. Selain itu, Perseroan juga memiliki pertambangan nikel yang terletak di Weda Bay, Halmahera Timur.

Our operational areas spread over 6 areas located in Kutai Kartanegara Regency and Samarinda City, East Kalimantan Province, and North Barito Regency, Central Kalimantan Province. In addition, the Company owns a nickel mine located in Weda Bay, East Halmahera.
