- Direksi berperan menjalankan pengelolaan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak, termasuk memastikan pengelolaan risiko ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan di grup usaha telah berlangsung dengan efektif. Dalam melaksanakan fungsi tersebut, Direksi mendelegasikan tugas dan tanggung jawab kepada divisi yang dimiliki, terutama:
- Sekretaris Perusahaan yang bertanggung jawab dalam pelaksanaan informasi kepada publik dan bertugas untuk membina terciptanya citra perusahaan yang baik secara konsisten dan berkesinambungan melalui pengelolaan program komunikasi yang efektif kepada segenap pemangku kepentingan;
- Kepala Satuan Audit Internal yang bertanggung jawab dalam melaksanakan pengawasan internal pada seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan, mengevaluasi proses pengendalian manajemen, operasional dan keuangan Perseroan serta memberikan konsultasi dan/atau rekomendasi perbaikan terhadap efektivitas dan efisiensi kinerja, efektivitas pengendalian internal guna meningkatkan kualitas pengelolaan Perseroan.

Direksi juga dibantu oleh divisi-divisi yang bertugas sesuai dengan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya masing-masing. Selain itu, Direksi juga mendelegasikan tugas dan tanggung jawab kepada Direktur Entitas Anak yang berperan langsung mengelola aktivitas operasional.

- The Board of Directors plays a role in managing the Company and its Subsidiaries, including ensuring that the management of economic, social, and environmental risks in the business group has been carried out effectively. In performing these functions, the Board of Directors delegates duties and responsibilities to the divisions, particularly:
- The Corporate Secretary is responsible for performing information disclosure to the public and to consistently and continuously build corporate image through the management of effective communication program to all stakeholders;
- The Internal Audit Unit Head is responsible for conducting internal control over all of the Company's operational activities, evaluating the Company's management, operational, and financial control processes, and providing consultation and/or recommendations for improvements on performance effectiveness and efficiency, effectiveness of internal control in order to improve the quality of the Company management.

The Board of Directors is also assisted by divisions assigned according to their respective duties and responsibilities. In addition, the Board of Directors also delegates duties and responsibilities to the Directors of Subsidiaries who have a direct role in managing operational activities.

# Pengembangan Kompetensi Berkelanjutan
## Sustainable Competency Development

Pengelolaan keberlanjutan usaha memerlukan pengetahuan dan keterampilan yang memadai dari para pelaksananya sehingga pengembangan kompetensi menjadi hal mutlak. Program yang dipilih disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan pekerjaan dan pengembangan ilmu pengetahuan terbaru, khususnya terkait aspek-aspek keberlanjutan. Pelaksanaannya diutamakan bagi penanggung jawab keberlanjutan di Perseroan, kemudian diturunkan kepada seluruh karyawan hingga di Entitas Anak.

Sepanjang tahun 2021, program pengembangan kompetensi terkait keberlanjutan bagi penanggung jawab dan karyawan Perseroan telah diikuti oleh sebanyak 281 orang dan beberapa diantaranya telah mengikuti program pengembangan kompetensi terkait aspek keberlanjutan. Kami senantiasa mengupayakan agar pengembangan kompetensi terkait aspek keberlanjutan dapat mencakup seluruh karyawan, baik karyawan di wilayah operasional tambang maupun kantor pusat.

Business continuity management requires adequate knowledge and skills from the executors, and therefore, competency development becomes an absolute must. The selected programs are tailored to the needs of the work and latest scientific developments, especially related to sustainability aspects. The implementation is prioritized for those in charge of sustainability in the Company, then passed down to all employees up to the Subsidiaries.

Throughout 2021, the competency development programs related to sustainability for the person in charge and employees of the Company were attended by as many as 281 people, and some of them had participated in competency development programs related to sustainability aspects. We always strive for competency development on sustainability aspects to cover all employees, both employees in the mining operational area and at the head office.
