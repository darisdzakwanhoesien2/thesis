Program unggulan di bidang pembangunan infrastruktur dilakukan melalui pembangunan atau perbaikan sarana ataupun prasarana yang menyokong aktivitas masyarakat. Pada tahun 2021, Perseroan melakukan pengurusan dan pelebaran jalan di Desa Separi yang membantu kelancaran mobilitas warga dengan total sepanjang 3,9 km. Melalui program ini, Perseroan dapat membantu aktivitas ekonomi, seperti pertanian masyarakat serta wisata di bendungan di wilayah tersebut, yang berdampak peningkatan pendapatan desa sekitar.

Program lain yang dilakukan yakni bantuan semenasi Gedung Pembinaan Kesejahteraan Keluarga (PKK) dengan melibatkan BUMDes dalam hal pengadaan barang dan pengerjaannya. Melalui pelaksanaan program ini, kami berharap dapat meningkatkan kegiatan-kegiatan yang dapat terselenggara di Gedung PKK yang bermanfaat bagi masyarakat, khususnya bagi anggota PKK.

The featured programs in the field of infrastructure development are carried out through the construction or improvement of facilities or infrastructure that supports community activities. In 2021, the Company backfilled and widened roads in Separi Village which helped smooth the mobility of residents with a total length of 3.9 km. Through these programs, the Company assisted the economic activities, such as community agriculture and tourism in dams in the area, which have an impact on increasing the income of the surrounding villages.

Another program carried out was assistance for cementing Family Welfare Development (PKK) Building by involving BUMDes in terms of procurement of goods and workmanship. Through the implementation of these programs, we hope to increase the activities held at PKK Building that are beneficial to the communities, especially for PKK member.

# Pengaduan Masyarakat

Kami telah menyediakan pihak yang bertanggung jawab di masing-masing wilayah operasional tambang untuk menerima dan mengelola laporan pengaduan dari masyarakat. Adapun sepanjang tahun 2021, Perseroan beserta Entitas Anak tidak menerima laporan pengaduan dari masyarakat terkait permasalahan sosial ataupun pelaksanaan PPM yang dilakukan.

# Community Complaint

We have designated the responsible parties in each mining operational area to receive and manage complaints from the community. Meanwhile, throughout 2021, the Company and its Subsidiaries did not receive community complaints regarding social problems or CDE implementation.
