## Penanganan dan Pencegahan Penularan Virus Covid-19

Sejak tahun 2020 hingga 2021, pandemi Covid-19 masih menjadi permasalahan yang belum dapat diselesaikan sepenuhnya, baik di Indonesia maupun di dunia. Pandemi ini mendorong kami untuk memberlakukan protokol kesehatan yang ketat dalam meminimalkan risiko Covid-19 terhadap karyawan maupun terhadap aktivitas operasional yang dijalankan.

## Handling and Prevention of the Covid-19 Virus Transmission

From 2020 to 2021, the Covid-19 pandemic was still a non-fully resolved problem, in Indonesia and in the world. This pandemic has prompted the Company and its Subsidiaries to enforce strict health protocols in minimizing the risk of Covid-19 to employees as well as to the operational activities.
