Pada Pilar Pendidikan, program ungulian kami meliputi renovasi sekolah yang dilakukan secara berkelanjutan serta pengadaan buku bacaan. Di tahun 2021, program ini dilaksanakan di SMP 3 Filial, Marangkayu Desa Prangat Baru dengan merenovasi seluruh ruang kelas dan ruang guru melalui penggantian keramik dan plafon bangunan. Sedangkan, program pengadaan buku pada tahun 2021 diadakan di SMP SPT Separi. Program ini diunggulkan karena nilai kebermanfaatan yang dianggap cukup besar bagi masyarakat, khususnya bagi guru dan pelajar di daerah tersebut.

In the Education Pillar, our featured programs include ongoing school renovations and procurement of reading books. In 2021, these programs were implemented at SMP 3 Filial, Marangkayu, Prangat Baru Village by renovating all classrooms and teachers' rooms by replacing ceramic tiles and building ceilings. While, the book procurement programs in 2021 were held at SPT Separi Junior High School. These programs are featured because the benefit value is considered quite large for the community, especially for teachers and students in the area.

Pada Pilar Kesehatan, program unggulan kami terkait dengan penanganan pandemi Covid-19. Program pengadaan tabung oksigen ditujukan bagi beberapa Pusat Kesehatan Masyarakat (Puskesmas) di beberapa desa wilayah dampingan. Program ini dilaksanakan guna mendukung kesiapan beberapa Puskesmas dalam rangka penanganan warga yang terpapar Covid-19 sehingga mampu meminimalisir jumlah korban jiwa yang diakibatkan. Selain itu, program lain yang dilakukan adalah pengadaan masker, hand sanitizer, dan alat kebersihan yang diberikan bagi Desa Separi dan Desa Mulawarman untuk mencegah penularan virus Covid-19.

In the Health Pillar, our featured programs are related to the Covid-19 pandemic handling. The oxygen cylinder procurement programs are intended for several Community Health Centers (Puskesmas) in several assisted villages. These programs are implemented to support the readiness of several Puskesmas for the purpose of handling residents exposed to Covid-19 to minimize the number of fatalities. In addition, other programs carried out were the procurement of masks, hand sanitizers, and cleaning tools provided to Separi Village and Mulawarman Village to prevent the Covid-19 virus transmission.

Pada Pilar Tingkat Pendapatan Riil atau Pekerjaan, program unggulan kami meliputi peternakan kambing dan budidaya pisang kepok. Program ini dapat memberikan dampak yang dapat langsung dirasakan oleh masyarakat sekitar, yaitu berupa keterampilan dalam beternak dan menanam, serta hasil dari beternak dan budidaya tersebut dapat langsung dijual sehingga menjadi sumber penghasilan. Pelaksanaan program ini telah dilakukan sejak tahun 2007 di Desa Perangat Selatan dan Desa Merambai dengan melibatkan 10 orang. Adapun dalam pelaksanaannya, kami melibatkan kelompok tani untuk membantu dalam memberikan edukasi yang baik dan benar.

In the Real Income or Employment Level Pillar, our featured programs include goat farming and kepok banana cultivation. These programs can have a direct impact on the surrounding communities, in the form of skills in raising livestock and planting, and the results from raising livestock and cultivation can be directly sold as their sources of income. The implementation of these programs has been carried out since 2007 in South Perangat Village and Merambai Village, involving 10 people. As for the implementation, we involve farmers to assist in providing good and correct education.

Program pengadaan tabung gas elpiji merupakan program unggulan dari Pilar Kemandirian Ekonomi. Melalui program ini, Perseroan bersama Badan Usaha Milik Desa (BUMDes) Desa Separi mendukung usaha demi mempercepat pembangunan desa melalui penyediaan tabung gas elpiji bagi masyarakat desa yang membutuhkan. Pelaksanaan program ini diharapkan dapat membantu meningkatkan perekonomian masyarakat lokal.

The LPG gas cylinder procurement programs are the featured programs of the Pillar of Economic Independence. Through these programs, the Company and Village Owned Enterprises (BUMDes) Separi Village support efforts to accelerate village development through the provision of LPG gas cylinders for rural communities in need. The implementation of these programs is expected to help improve the economy of local communities.

Pelaksanaan program unggulan di bidang keterlibatan masyarakat pada pengelolaan lingkungan di sekitar wilayah tambang dilakukan melalui pemanfaatan lahan Perseroan untuk keperluan masyarakat di bidang pertanian ataupun peternakan serta pengadaan jaminan air bersih bagi masyarakat sekitar. Total penerima manfaat dari program ini yaitu sekitar 3.120 orang dari 3 desa sekitar wilayah operasional tambang. Melalui program ini, Perseroan dapat berkontribusi terhadap pengadaan air bersih serta membantu meningkatkan penghasilan masyarakat melalui pemanfaatan lahan oleh masyarakat sekitar.

These featured programs in community involvement in environmental management around the mining area are implemented through the use of the Company's land for community needs in agriculture or animal husbandry and the provision of clean water guarantees for the surrounding communities. The total beneficiaries of these programs are around 3,120 people from 3 villages around the mining operational areas. Through these programs, the Company can contribute to the provision of clean water and help increase people's income through land use by the surrounding communities.
