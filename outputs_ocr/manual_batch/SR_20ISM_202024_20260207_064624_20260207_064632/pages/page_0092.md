Beyond GAP knowledge, we support our potato and chili farmer partners in the financial aspect, through providing guaranteed price for their produce. Additionally, we also facilitate their loan applications to financial institutions and offer seed loans to potato farmers.

In support of the Indonesian Government's mandate to reduce reliance on imported potatoes, our CBP Group has been implementing an industrial potato seed production program since 2013 in West Java, supplying local potato farmers with domestically cultivated potato seeds. In 2019, we relocated to Humbang Hasundutan, North Sumatra and has produced approximately 75 million seeds for local potato farmers.

We also assist cassava and coconut sugar farmer partners through mentorship programs. Through these programs, we promote knowledge exchange to help our local farmer partners improve product quality and increase productivity. In 2024, we partnered with more than 8,000 local farmers.

Sebagai bagian dari upaya mendukung kebijakan Pemerintah Indonesia dalam mengurangi ketergantungan pada impor kentang, sejak 2013 di Jawa Barat, Grup CBP telah menjalankan program produksi bibit kentang industri, yang memasok bibit kentang hasil budidaya lokal kepada petani kentang di Indonesia. Pada tahun 2019, program ini direlokasi ke Humbang Hasundutan, Sumatra Utara dan menghasilkan sekitar 75 juta butir bibit kentang untuk mendukung petani lokal.

Melalui program ini, kami mendorong program saling berbagi pengetahuan untuk membantu petani meningkatkan mutu produk dan produktivitas hasil panen. Pada tahun 2024, kami bermitra dengan lebih dari 8.000 petani lokal.
