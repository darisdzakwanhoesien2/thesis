# RECEIVING AND RESPONDING TO CUSTOMERS' FEEDBACK

Customer feedback is essential to our product design and innovation process. Customers and the public can reach the centralized Indofood Customer Service through toll-free phone lines, email, instant messaging, and social media channels.

In 2024, we received and responded to over 240,000 feedback, of which 95% were inquiries on product details and promotion.

# CONSUMER EDUCATION

We engage consumers through online and offline communication channels, to increase product knowledge and awareness of how food consumption impacts health and wellness.

With the widespread use of mobile technology, digital learning and online education have become essential for customer engagement. Our Bogasari and Agribusiness Group websites now offer more interactive features to educate consumers about food consumption choices. Additionally, we continue to conduct online education programs for our Integrated Health Post (Pos Pelayanan Terpadu or "Posyandu") volunteers and cadres.

# EDUKASI KONSUMEN

Kami berinteraksi dengan konsumen melalui berbagai saluran komunikasi, baik online maupun offline, guna meningkatkan pemahaman tentang produk dan kesadaran akan dampak konsumsi makanan terhadap kesehatan dan kebugaran.

Seiring dengan semakin luasnya penggunaan teknologi seluler, format pembelajaran digital dan online kini menjadi sarana penting dalam kegiatan pelibatan konsumen. Situs web Grup Bogasari dan Agribisnis kini menampilkan lebih banyak metode yang interaktif untuk mengedukasi konsumen tentang berbagai pilihan konsumsi makanan. Kami juga melanjutkan program edukasi secara online bagi para relawan dan kader Pos Pelayanan Terpadu ("Posyandu").
