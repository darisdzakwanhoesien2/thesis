LAPORAN KEBERLANJUTAN 2024

- Smart production to fine tune the manufacturing process to increase line performance efficiency and throughput and by reducing losses
- Smart maintenance to manage maintenance costs effectively and provide better line efficiency
- Smart eco factory to manage energy, water, and waste that reduces resources consumption and costs related to steam generation and WWTP

One significant innovation in the Dairy Division is the implementation of an automated sorting process for dairy products during production machine downtime. This system utilizes sensors and programmed logic to streamline sorting, enhancing efficiency and reducing manual sorting by approximately 50%.

Additionally, the division has transitioned from inkjet coding to laser coding technology for product packaging labels. This change eliminates the need for consumable ink and solvents, hence reducing hazardous waste and lowers printing costs. The laser-engraved text also improves label clarity for consumers.

Furthermore, we are actively participating in the Indonesian Digital Industry Center (Pusat Industri Digital Indonesia or PIDI) 4.0, collaborating on an automation and robotics ecosystem for Industry 4.0 challenges. This has resulted in the Dairy Division being recognized as a national role model in the food and beverage sector.

Salah satu inovasi yang signifikan di Divisi Dairy adalah penerapan proses sortir otomatis untuk produk dairy selama terjadi downtime pada mesin produksi. Sistem ini menggunakan sensor dan logika pemrograman, yang membantu meningkatkan efisiensi dan mengurangi proses sortir secara manual hingga 50%.

Selain itu, Divisi Dairy telah melakukan transisi teknologi dari pencetakan inkjet coding ke laser coding untuk pelabelan kemasan produk. Peralihan ini menghilangkan penggunaan tinta dan solvent, sehingga mengurangi limbah B3 serta menekan biaya pencetakan. Selain itu, penggunaan laser coding secara signifikan membentuk hasil cetakan label lebih terbaca oleh konsumen.

Kami aktif terlibat dalam Pusat Industri Digital Indonesia (PIDI) 4.0, bekerja sama dalam mengembangkan ekosistem otomatisasi dan robotika untuk tantangan Industri 4.0. Hal ini telah menjadikan Divisi Dairy diakui sebagai model peran nasional di sektor makanan dan minuman.
