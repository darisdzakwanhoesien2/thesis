## Research &amp; Development

Penelitian &amp; Pengembangan

- Innovate new products and variants catering to different target markets
- Only the following are used:
- Ingredients, technologies and processes that are safe, high quality, and Halal
- Ingredients and food additives permitted under the Government's food regulations
- Appropriate packaging that preserves product safety and quality and meets food safety regulations and standards
- Inovasi produk dan varian baru guna melayani berbagai target pasar
- Kami hanya menggunakan:
- Bahan baku, teknologi serta proses pengolahan yang aman, bermutu tinggi serta Halal
- Bahan baku dan bahan tambahan pangan yang diizinkan berdasarkan peraturan pangan Pemerintah
- Kemasan yang baik sehingga dapat menjaga keamanan pangan dan mutu produk selama masa simpannya, serta memenuhi peraturan dan standar keamanan pangan

## Material Sourcing

Pengelolaan Pasokan

- All suppliers and partners must meet the highest standards of food safety and quality
- Periodic supplier audits are performed in accordance with Good Manufacturing Practices ("GMP"), Halal, and food safety and quality standards
- All materials can be traced to their source of origin
- Semua pemasok dan mitra harus memenuhi standar keamanan pangan dan mutu tertinggi
- Pelaksanaan audit terhadap pemasok secara berkala sesuai dengan Cara Produksi yang Baik (Good Manufacturing Practices atau "GMP"), Halal, serta standar keamanan pangan dan mutu
- Semua bahan dapat ditelusuri hingga ke sumber asalnya

## Manufacturing Process

Proses Manufaktur

- Dedicated Quality Assurance and Quality Control teams to ensure food safety of incoming materials, as well as during production and warehousing stages
- All our production facilities are certified with relevant Food Safety or Quality Management System and Halal
- Regular testing of product safety and quality performed by an experienced laboratory team. Eight of our laboratories are ISO 17025 certified
- Annual internal and external audits conducted to ensure compliance with food safety policies and standards
- Tim khusus Penjaminan Mutu dan Pengendalian Mutu ditunjuk guna memastikan keamanan pangan saat bahan baku diterima, serta selama tahapan proses produksi dan penyimpanan di gudang
- Semua fasilitas produksi telah tersertifikasi dengan Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Pangan atau Mutu yang relevan dan Halal
- Pengujian keamanan pangan dan mutu terhadap produk dilakukan secara berkala oleh tim laboratorium yang berpengalaman. Delapan laboratorium kami telah tersertifikasi ISO 17025
- Pelaksanaan audit internal dan eksternal tahunan guna memastikan kepatuhan terhadap kebijakan dan standar keamanan pangan

## Distribution

Distribusi

- Hazard identification and risk assessments performed for warehousing and logistics operations
- 100% Indofood Distribution Group branches have been certified with Processed Food Safety Management System in Distribution Facilities (Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Pangan Olahan di Sarana Peredaran or "SMKPO") by BPOM
- Stay closer to our customers to ensure product freshness and availability
- Identifikasi bahaya dan penilaian risiko dilakukan terhadap operasional pergudangan dan distribusi
- 100% cabang dari Grup Distribusi Indofood telah tersertifikasi Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Pangan Olahan di Sarana Peredaran ("SMKPO") oleh BPOM
- Berada dekat dengan permintaan pasar guna menjamin kesegaran dan ketersediaan produk
