Indofood participated in the Indonesia International Sustainability Forum ("ISF") 2024 held in Jakarta on 5-6 September 2024.

This event was coordinated by Ministry for Maritime Affairs and Investment and Indonesia Chamber of Commerce and Industry ("KADIN"), with the support of major Indonesian companies. ISF 2024 was officially opened by The President of Indonesia and was attended by over 10,000 participants from more than 50 countries.

Indofood was an active participant at the forum and engaged stakeholders from various background (e.g. consumers, business, government, students, Non Governmental Organization ("NGO") through an exhibition booth which showcased the Sustainability Pillars, Key Sustainability Programs, and Key Sustainability Achievements of the company.

Indofood berpartisipasi dalam Indonesia International Sustainability Forum ("ISF") 2024 yang diselenggarakan di Jakarta pada tanggal 5-6 September 2024.

Kegiatan ini dikoordinasikan oleh Kementerian Koordinator Bidang Kemaritiman dan Investasi dan Kamar Dagang dan Industri Indonesia ("KADIN"), dengan dukungan dari perusahaan-perusahaan besar Indonesia.
