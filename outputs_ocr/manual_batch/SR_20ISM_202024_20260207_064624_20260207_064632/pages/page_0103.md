# RESILIENCE AND INNOVATION [3-3]
## KETANGGUHAN DAN INOVASI

Operational adaptability and innovation are a key success factor to boost the resilience of our business. This has been particularly crucial for mitigating potential social and economic impacts due to increasingly volatile geopolitical and economic circumstances.

## INNOVATION WITHIN OUR OPERATIONS

Innovation is crucial for advancing Indofood's business, with a strong emphasis on customer-centric initiatives. Our R&amp;D continue to improve our products, processes, and technologies to meet the changing needs of our customers. This ongoing innovation is essential for enhancing business resilience and effectively addressing future challenges.

At Indofood, we utilize technology to enhance manufacturing and inventory management. Specifically, our CBP Group's Dairy Division and Bogasari Group have successfully implemented Industry 4.0 practices. This initiative aligns with the Indonesian Government's "Making Indonesia 4.0" program, launched in 2018, which aims to boost digitalization in manufacturing processes. The following sections will detail specific examples of innovation in our operations, demonstrating how we leverage technology for greater efficiency and sustainability.

## Manufacturing Innovation in Dairy Division

Industry 4.0 technologies are implemented throughout CBP Group's Dairy Division to increase its operational efficiency and manpower productivity. Using data analytics to create smart operations combining several aspects:

- Smart performance to provide real-time of Overall Equipment Effectiveness that enhances transparency of performance from shop floor to top floor
- Smart quality to improve quality control systems giving real-time tracking and traceability, speeding up the release time of products to the market and to reduce complaints

Kemampuan beradaptasi dalam kegiatan operasional dan melakukan inovasi merupakan faktor kunci guna meningkatkan ketahanan bisnis Indofood. Hal ini sangat penting untuk mengurangi potensi dampak sosial dan ekonomi akibat tidak menentunya kondisi geopolitik dan ekonomi.
