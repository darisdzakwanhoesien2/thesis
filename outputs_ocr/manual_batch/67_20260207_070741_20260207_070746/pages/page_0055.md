Indonesian citizen, 56 years old, domiciled in Jakarta. He earned his Bachelor's Degree in Food Engineering from Institut Pertanian Bogor in 1987 and a Master of Business Administration Degree from Institut Pengembangan Manajemen Indonesia in 1990.

Prior to joining the Company, he worked at Nokia Mobile Phones Indonesia for 12 years as Country Manager, Executive Advisor, and Director. He has over 18 years of experience in Sales and Marketing.
