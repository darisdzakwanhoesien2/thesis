# Environmental Performance

Kinerja Lingkungan

In accordance with its line of business, the Company does not directly affect and have an impact on the environment. However, EMTEK and its subsidiaries remain committed to providing support for preservation of the environment, including reducing the negative impacts of climate change. [POJK51-6.e.2]

Sesuai dengan bidang usaha yang dijalankan, Perseroan tidak secara langsung memengaruhi dan memberikan dampak terhadap lingkungan. Meski demikian, EMTEK bersama entitas anak perusahaannya tetap berkomitmen memberikan dukungan pada pelestarian lingkungan, termasuk mengurangi dampak negatif akibat perubahan iklim. [POJK51-6.e.2]
