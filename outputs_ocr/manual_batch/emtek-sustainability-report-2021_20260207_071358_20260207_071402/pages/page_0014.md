# Whistleblowing System

GRI 102-17, GRI 205-1

Business integrity is imperative in an environment that fosters sustainable development. In an effort to strengthen business awareness and anticipate corruption risks, EMTEK established a whistleblowing system, which has been communicated to all of the employees [GRI 102-17]. Throughout 2021, we did not receive any complaints that were submitted to our whistleblowing system.

We conduct internal audits on all units based on OJK Regulation No. 56/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Establishment and Guidelines for the Preparation of the Internal Audit Unit Charter. EMTEK formed an internal audit team, which is responsible for providing independent assurance and assessing potential risks to the Company. The internal audit team audited 8 out of 15 operational units during the reporting period, accounting for 53% of all operations [GRI 205-1]. In 2021, there were no findings of fraud in our management and tax payment compliance.

# Public Complaints Mechanism

All public complaints about the Company's operations, as well as corporate social and environmental responsibility efforts, are referred to the appropriate subsidiaries for inquiry. The Company ensures that each subsidiary follows up on all complaints received from the public.

# Competitive Behaviour

The Company is highly committed to fair business competition aligning in our effort to gain as many viewers and customers as possible, also being an end-to-end journey for brands. Efforts shown in principle of anti-monopoly, encouraging its subsidiaries to prioritize quality programs, content, and services as our comparative advantage. The commitment to fair business competition has resulted in zero sanctions imposed on the Company from authorities, including Business Competition Supervisory Commission (KPPU). [GRI 206-1]

# Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran

GRI 102-17, GRI 205-1

Integritas bisnis sangat penting dalam lingkungan yang mendorong pembangunan berkelanjutan. Dalam upaya memperkuat kesadaran bisnis dan mengantisipasi risiko korupsi, EMTEK membentuk sistem pelaporan pelanggaran (whistleblowing system) yang telah dikomunikasikan kepada seluruh karyawan [GRI 102-17]. Sepanjang tahun 2021, tidak terdapat pengaduan yang masuk ke dalam sistem pelaporan kami.

Audit internal kami lakukan pada semua unit berdasarkan Peraturan OJK No. 56/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Penyusunan Piagam Unit Audit Internal. EMTEK membentuk tim audit internal, yang bertanggung jawab untuk memberikan jaminan independen dan menilai potensi risiko bagi Perusahaan. Tim audit internal mengaudit 8 dari 15 unit operasional selama periode pelaporan, terhitung 53% dari seluruh operasi [GRI 205-1]. Pada tahun 2021, tidak ada temuan kecurangan dalam pengelolaan dan kepatuhan pembayaran pajak kami.

# Mekanisme Pengaduan Masyarakat

Semua keluhan publik tentang operasi Perusahaan, serta upaya tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan perusahaan, dirujuk ke anak perusahaan yang sesuai untuk dimintai keterangan. Perusahaan memastikan setiap anak perusahaan menindaklanjuti semua pengaduan yang masuk dan masyarakat.

# Perilaku Kompetitif

Perusahaan berkomitmen tinggi terhadap persaingan bisnis yang sehat sejalan dengan upaya kami untuk mendapatkan sebanyak mungkin pemirsa dan pelanggan, juga menjadi perjalanan ujung ke ujung bagi merek. Upaya yang ditunjukkan dalam prinsip anti monopoli, mendorong anak perusahaan untuk memprioritaskan program, konten, dan layanan yang berkualitas sebagai keunggulan komparatif kami. Komitmen terhadap persaingan usaha yang sehat mengakibatkan Perusahaan tidak dikenakan sanksi oleh otoritas, termasuk Komite Pengawas Persaingan Usaha (KPPU). [GRI 206-1]
