## EMTEK Cares for Corona

In order to assist the government in controlling the spread of the COVID-19, YPP supports vaccine activities by holding free vaccination activities for residents in Sukorejo, Kendal for 2,500 people. The total number of vaccine participants is 68,602, consisting of:

### 1,250
people for the Gotong Royong Vaccine from the EMTEK Group.

### 64,787

### 2,565
vaccine participants in 12 locations in the Greater Jakarta area for the SCTV Anniversary.
partisipan vaksin di 12 lokasi di wilayah Jakarta dalam rangka Ulang Tahun SCTV.

In addition, one of YPP's forms of support in the EMTEK Cares for Corona activities is to participate in providing consumption assistance and snacks for 4310 medical personels in the Riau Islands, Jakarta, Central Java, and East Java.

Selain itu, salah satu bentuk dukungan YPP dalam kegiatan EMTEK Peduli Corona adalah dengan berpartisipasi memberikan bantuan konsumsi dan makanan ringan untuk 4310 tenaga medis di Kepulauan Riau, DKI Jakarta, Jawa Tengah, dan Jawa Timur.

## Emergency Cruise Ship Hospital for COVID-19 Rumah Sakit Kapal Pesiar Darurat untuk COVID-19

We work together with the Ministry of Transportation and the Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises of the Republic of Indonesia to create an infomercial that can educate the viewers, such as preventive measures against COVID-19 infections and charity fundraising. In 2021, we conducted a fundraiser for "Kapal Isolasi Terpusat Terapung" (Isoter Terapung) that are stationed as emergency hospitals during the COVID-19 pandemic. These makeshift hospitals are crucial in supporting the health care systems, especially in larger cities that needed a larger number of beds for COVID-19 patients.

Kami bekerja sama dengan Kementerian Perhubungan dan Kementerian Badan Usaha Milik Negara Republik Indonesia untuk membuat sebuah infomercial yang dapat mengedukasi publik, seperti pencegahan penularan COVID-19 dan penggalangan dana amal. Pada tahun 2021, kami melakukan penggalangan dana untuk "Kapal Isolasi Terpusat Terapung" (Isoter Terapung) yang digunakan sebagai rumah sakit darurat selama pandemi COVID-19. Rumah sakit darurat ini sangat penting dalam mendukung sistem perawatan kesehatan, terutama di kota-kota besar yang membutuhkan lebih banyak tempat tidur untuk pasien COVID-19.

## In 2021, we held the "Charity Concert Indonesia Tangguh" and managed to raise a total of Rp 15,838,513,884.

Pada tahun 2021, kami mengadakan "Konser Amal Indonesia Tangguh" dan berhasil mengumpulkan total Rp 15,838,513,884.

This fund is collected by:
Dana ini dikumpulkan dari:

### IDR 1,876,513,884
Donations from SCTV, INDOSIAR, O'Channel viewers, and Vidio users
Donasi dari SCTV, INDOSIAR, pemirsa O'Channel, dan pengguna Vidio

### IDR 13,962,000,000
Proceeds from the sale of memorabilia of the proud athletes of Indonesia, and sponsorship
Hasil penjualan kenang-kenangan para atlet kebanggaan Indonesia, dan sponsorship

The collected donations were immediately handed over to the Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih Foundation (YPP), which is under the auspices of the EMTEK Group. As the main organiser of the funds, YPP distributed the entire donations to help the operational costs of the Isoter Terapung for COVID-19 patients. YPP provides basic necessities, fruits, vitamins and supplements to people who are undergoing self-isolation in the ship.

Donasi yang terkumpul langsung diserahkan kepada Yayasan Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih (YPP) yang berada di bawah naungan Grup EMTEK. Sebagai pengelola dana utama, YPP menyalurkan seluruh donasi untuk membantu biaya operasional Isoter Terapung bagi pasien COVID-19. YPP memberikan sembako, buah-buahan, vitamin dan suplemen bagi masyarakat yang menjalani isolasi mandiri di kapal.
