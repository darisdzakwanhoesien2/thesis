# EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

## Tunjangan Karyawan

GRI 401-2, GRI 405-2

Retaining employees and talents is always a challenge in business. By retaining our employees, we will be able to continue to improve our knowledge and ensure our company's existence in the future. For that, we want to embrace our employees like a family and provide them with benefits that would assure their well-being. We believe that providing benefits will also motivate them which eventually will contribute to the company's productivity.

Mempertahankan karyawan dan talenta selalu menjadi tantangan dalam bisnis. Dengan mempertahankan karyawan, kami dapat selalu meningkatkan pengetahuan kami dan memastikan eksistensi perusahaan di masa mendatang. Untuk itu, kami merangkul karyawan seperti keluarga dan memberi mereka manfaat yang dapat menjamin kesejahteraan mereka. Kami meyakini bahwa pemberian manfaat juga memotivasi mereka yang pada akhirnya akan berkontribusi pada produktivitas perusahaan.

## 1. Basic salary

Decent remuneration is given without differentiating male and female employees, with a salary ratio of female to male employees at:

- Officer level of 1.43;
- Middle management level of 1.19; and
- Senior management level of 0.88.

Each year, the lowest minimum salary is ensured to be 100% equal to the government-set Regional Minimum Wage. EMTEK remunerate, contract and conduct fair business practice with our employees.

Permanent and contract (non-permanent) employees received the same types of benefits. [GRI 401-2]

### 3. Health Insurance and medical facilities

EMTEK's employees are provided with health insurance on top of mandatory BFUS Kesehatan and special healthcare facilities in our hospitals. The benefits also include outpatient and inpatient health facilities.

### 4. Employees remuneration and share ownership program which in accordance with the applicable regulations

The Company provides remuneration regardless of gender for the same level. In 2021, the Company issued 44,233,000 shares through the Management and Employee Stock Ownership Program (MESOP).

### 5. Return to work guarantee after maternity leave.

At EMTEK, female employees are entitled to 90 days maternity leave and guaranteed to work in the same position. In 2021, there were 3 female employees who had taken maternity leaves and 100% returned to the same position.

## Mengutamakan Kesehatan dan Keselamatan

GRI 403-1, GRI 403-3, GRI 403-4, GRI 403-5, GRI 403-6, GRI 403-8

EMTEK commits to putting our people's safety and health first by complying with the applicable law, especially regarding labour regulation. We follow a comprehensive Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Management System that complies with the existing health and safety regulations. All employees are insured by social security for their employment and health. EMTEK and its subsidiaries follow the Company's Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) to handle safety aspects throughout our operations.

To ensure that our company's safety improves over time, we always welcome feedback and reviews from our stakeholders, including employees and securities. Our company will then analyse their feedback to create a safer environment for all our employees.
