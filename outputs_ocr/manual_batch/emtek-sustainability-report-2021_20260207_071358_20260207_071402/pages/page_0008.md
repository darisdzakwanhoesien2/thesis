# 2018

Through KMK Online, EMTEK invested in PT Kapanlagi Dot Com Networks that manages renowned digital portals such as Vidio.com, Liputan6.com, KapanLagi.com, Bola.net, Merdeka.com, Bola.com, Dream.co.id, Brilio.net, and Fimela.com.

Melalui KMK Online, EMTEK berinvestasi di PT Kapanlagi Dot Com Networks yang mengelola portal digital ternama seperti Vidio.com, Liputan6.com, KapanLagi.com, Bola.net, Merdeka.com, Bola.com, Dream.co.id, Brilio.net, dan Fimela.com.

EMTEK Group secured exclusive live broadcasting rights for the 2018 Asian Games in Jakarta and Palembang through SCTV, Indosier, O Channel, Vidio.com, Liputan6.com, bola.com, and bola.net.

Grup EMTEK mendapatkan hak siar eksklusif untuk Asian Games 2018 di Jakarta dan Palembang melalui SCTV, Indosier, O Channel, Vidio.com, Liputan6.com, bola.com, dan bola.net.

EMTEK invested in online job portals such as Gerja and Jobs.id.

EMTEK berinvestasi di portal pekerjaan online seperti Gerja dan Jobs.id.

# 2019

Moreover, EMTEK through its indirect subsidiary PT Mediatama Televisi, launched NEXPARABOLA direct-to-home (DTH) pay television in 2019. NEXPARABOLA is an affordable satellite-based DTH platform able to broadcast to viewers in urban and rural areas. NEXPARABOLA broadcasts Liga 1, Liga 2, UEFA Champions League, UEFA European League, and EFL Championship through Champions TV.

Selain itu, EMTEK melalui anak Perusahaan tidak langsung PT Mediatama Televisi, wipuncurkan NEXPARABOLA direct-to-home (DTH) televisi berbayar pada tahun 2019. NEXPARABOLA adalah platform DTH berbasis satelit yang terjangkau yang dapat disiarkan ke pemirsa di daerah perkotaan dan pedesaan. NEXPARABOLA menyiarkan Liga 1, Liga 2, Liga Champions UEFA, Liga Eropa UEFA, dan Kejuaraan EFL melalui Champions TV.

EMTEK through its subsidiary Vidio.com launched its first original Indonesian drama series namely Heart, I Love You Baby, and Girls in The City.

EMTEK melalui anak perusahaannya Vidio.com meluncurkan serial drama asli Indonesia pertamanya yaitu Heart, I Love You Baby, dan Girls in The City.

Through PT Screenplay Bumilangit Produksi, EMTEK produced Gundala, the first big-budget superhero movie in Indonesia, and the first instalment in the Bumilangit Cinematic Universe (BCU). Gundala is also the only movie from Southeast Asia to be screened at Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF) 2019.

Melalui PT Screenplay Bumilangit Produksi, EMTEK memproduksi Gundala, film superhero beranggaran besar pertama di Indonesia, dan angkuran pertama di Bumilangit Cinematic Universe (BCU). Gundala juga menjadi satu-satunya film dari Asia Tenggara yang diputar di Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF) 2019.

Through SCMA, EMTEK participated in share placement in PT Benson Media Kreasi ("Samara"). Samara is engaged in offline events, influencer marketing, and talent management. In 2019, Samara held IDEAREST 2019 carrying the theme "Age of Pride" from 3 to 6 October 2019. IDEAREST 2019 comprised of 130 sessions and was attended by more than 250 speakers from home and abroad.

Melalui SCMA, EMTEK turut serta melakukan penyertaan saham PT Benson Media Kreasi ("Samara"). Samara bergerak di bidang offline events, marketing influencer dan talent management. Tahun 2019, Samara menyelenggarakan IDEAREST 2019 dengan tema "Age of Pride" pada tanggal 3-6 Oktober 2019. IDEAREST 2019 dibagi ke dalam 130 sesi dan dihadiri oleh lebih dari 250 pembicara baik dari dalam maupun luar negeri.

# 2020

EMTEK acquired 4,241,000,000 shares representing 71.88% of the issued and paid up capital of PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk ("SAME") that owned and operated OMNI Hospitals.

EMTEK mengakuisisi 4,241.000.000 saham yang mewakili 71,88% dari modal ditempatkan dan disetor PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk ("SAME") yang memiliki dan mengoperasikan OMNI Hospitals.

EMTEK Group through EMTEK Peduli Corona signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the Ministry of Research and Technology of the Republic of Indonesia regarding the Procurement and Utilization of COVID-19 Innovative Products.

GRUP EMTEK melalui EMTEK Peduli Corona menandatangani Nota Kesepahaman dengan Kementerian Riset dan Teknologi Republik Indonesia tentang Pengadaan dan Pemanfaatan Produk Inovatif COVID-19.

EMTEK Group held Satu Indonesia Charity Concert amid the large-scale social restrictions on 10 May 2020, involving 300 celebrities and broadcasted live on SCTV, INDOSIAR, O Channel as well as online on Vidio.com, as a form of support for medical personnel in Indonesia who were struggling at the forefront and assisting the government's efforts in mitigating the COVID-19 pandemic.

GRUP EMTEK menggelar Konser Amal Satu Indonesia di tengah pembatasan sosial berskala besar pada 10 Mei 2020 yang melibatkan 300 selebriti dan disiarkan secara langsung di SCTV, INDOSIAR, O Channel maupun online di Vidio.com, sebagai bentuk dukungan bagi tenaga medis di Indonesia yang sedang berjuang di garda terdepan dan membantu upaya pemerintah dalam mitigasi pandemi COVID-19.

Vidio.com was named the 3rd largest OTT platform in Southeast Asia in 2020 by Media Partner Asia and managed to reach the #1 top download position on the Indonesian Google Playstore for all types of categories in April 2020.

Vidio.com dinobatkan sebagai platform OTT terbesar ke-3 di Asia Tenggara pada tahun 2020 oleh Media Partner Asia dan berhasil mencapai posisi top download #1 di Google Playstore Indonesia untuk semua jenis kategori pada April 2020.

EMTEK Group and Smartfren held "Smartfren WOW Virtual Concert 2020" broadcasted live online, the first collaboration carried out by presenting a national TV program featuring artists in 3 countries, namely Indonesia, London and Korea, which generated millions of viewers across TV, OTT, and social media as well as brand application assets.

GRUP EMTEK dan Smartfren menggelar "Smartfren WOW Virtual Concert 2020" yang disiarkan secara live online, kerjasama pertama yang dilakukan dengan menghadirkan program TV nasional yang menampilkan artis di 3 negara yaitu Indonesia, London dan Korea yang menghasilkan jutaan penonton di seluruh TV, OTT, dan media sosial serta aset aplikasi merek.

# 2021

EMTEK through its subsidiary PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk ("SAME") acquired 441,734,500 shares representing 47.52% of the issued and paid up capital of PT Kedoya Adyaraya Tbk ("RSGK") that owned and operated Grha Kedoya Hospital and Grha MM2100 Hospital.

EMTEK melalui anak usahanya PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk ("SAME") mengakuisisi 441.734.500 saham atau setara 47.52% dari modal ditempatkan dan disetor PT Kedoya Adyaraya Tbk ("RSGK") yang memiliki dan mengoperasikan Rumah Sakit Grha Kedoya dan Rumah Sakit Grha MM2100.

EMTEK through its subsidiary PT Elang Media Visitama acquired 9,089,503,800 shares representing 93% of the issued and paid up capital of PT Bank FAMA International.

EMTEK melalui anak usahanya PT Elang Media Visitama mengakuisisi 9,089,503,800 saham yang mewakili 93% dari modal ditempatkan dan disetor PT Bank FAMA International.

EMTEK Group was appointed by the International Olympic Committee (IOC) as the official broadcaster of the largest sporting event namely the Tokyo Olympics 2020 for live broadcasts on several platforms under EMTEK Group namely Indosiar, O Channel, as well as at Vidio.com.

Grup EMTEK ditunjuk oleh International Olympic Committee (IOC) sebagai official broadcaster acara olahraga terbesar yaitu Olimpiade Tokyo 2020 untuk siaran langsung di beberapa platform yang berada di bawah naungan Grup EMTEK yaitu Indosiar, O Channel, dan Vidio.com.

EMTEK Group established a strategic collaboration with FT Grab Teknologi Indonesia (Grab) to accelerate the transformation of Indonesia's Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in the digital ecosystem so that it could have a positive impact on the national economic recovery and encourage equitable distribution of the digital economy.

Grup EMTEK menjalin kolaborasi dengan PT Grab Teknologi Indonesia (Grab) untuk mendorong percepatan transformasi Usaha, Mikro, Kecil, dan Menengah (UMKM) Indonesia di ekosistem digital sehingga dapat memberikan dampak positif bagi pemulihan ekonomi nasional dan mendorong pemerataan ekonomi digital.

EMTEK and PT Surya Citra Media Tbk ("SCMA") were included in the Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) Quality 45 IDX KEHATI as the 45 best stories assessed from the results of the ESG performance assessment and the company's financial quality as well as having good liquidity.

EMTEK dan PT Surya Citra Media Tbk ("SCMA") masuk dalam Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) Quality 45 IDX KEHATI sebagai 45 saham terbaik yang dinilai dari hasil penilaian hasil kinerja ESG dan kualitas keuangan perusahaan serta memiliki likuiditas yang baik.
