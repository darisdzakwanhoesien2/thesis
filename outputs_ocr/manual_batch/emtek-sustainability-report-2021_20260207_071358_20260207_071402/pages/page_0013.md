# Media Ethics, Freedom of Expression, Plurality

Both of our television channels have an internal control team that reviews all programs created for the channels. All of this program follows the guidelines given by the government, especially broadcasting laws, copyright laws and Indonesian Broadcasting Commission (KPI) guidance as well as broadcasting code of conduct and broadcast program standards (P3SPS) and advertising ethics. In accordance with Law No. 32 of 2002 concerning Broadcasting, information broadcast materials, programs, content and other multimedia products must meet the following criteria:

![img-64.jpeg](img-64.jpeg)

- Shall not contain slanderous, inciting, misleading and/or false materials;
- Shall not contain elements of violence, obscenity, gambling, abuse of narcotics and illicit drugs, including cigarettes;
- Shall not polarise ethnicity, religion, race, and intergroup relations (SARA);
- Shall not denigrate, demean, harass, and/or ignore religious values and dignity of Indonesian citizens or harm international relations.

- Tidak boleh memuat materi yang memfitnah, menghasut, menyesatkan dan/atau tidak benar;
- Tidak mengandung unsur kekerasan, kecabulan, perjudian, penyalahgunaan narkotika dan obat-obatan terlarang, termasuk rokok;
- Tidak mempolarisasi suku, agama, ras, dan hubungan antargolongan (SARA);
- Tidak boleh mempermalukan, merendahkan, melecehkan, dan/atau mengabaikan nilai-nilai agama dan martabat warga negara Indonesia atau merugikan hubungan internasional.

This strict adherence is also complemented by multiple management layers of approval before a programme or movie is aired on our channel. All SOP and policies regarding our content are audited every year to ensure that our content is ethically and morally compliant.

Knowing that Indonesia is a nation with various languages, races and religions, EMTEK Group encourages diversity and plurality in our programmes. EMTEK is not and will not be affiliated to any political parties and we uphold this policy for our news coverage and content. All content must not be biased or heavily leaning to a certain group or religion and we aim to cater to the diversity of the nation. As a result, in our channels, we provide religious content for the three main religious groups in Indonesia, while also airing events that strengthen national pride such as cultural singing contests and national sporting events.

# Intellectual Property Rights

EMTEK Group respects intellectual property rights and data security of viewers and other partners. We ensure broadcasting programs and content from outside its own subsidiaries are obtained legally from the parties who hold property rights. During the reporting period, there were no sanctions over alleged violation of intellectual property rights for broadcasting programs, content, or violations of violations of stakeholder privacy that have legally binding court judgments.

# Manajemen Risiko

GRI 102-II

Perusahaan senantiasa menerapkan manajemen risiko untuk memitigasi risiko bisnis, baik risiko lingkungan, sosial maupun tata kelola (ESG). Perusahaan telah mengembangkan sistem manajemen risiko yang mampu mengidentifikasi, mengevaluasi, dan memitigasi risiko yang dapat mempengaruhi kinerja Perusahaan. Selain itu, pengelolaan risiko dilakukan secara sistematis, terstruktur, dan menyeluruh di seluruh struktur organisasi Perusahaan. Penerapan manajemen risiko dievaluasi oleh Unit Audit Internal dan Dewan Komisaris yang diwakili oleh Komite Audit.

# The following are the ESG risks:

1. Risks related to macroeconomic uncertainty, which have an impact on environmental and social issues management.
2. Risks linked with new government policies that have an impact on corporate governance. In particular, in light of the enactment of Law No.11 of 2020 on Job Creation, also known as the Omnibus Law, the Company has made changes to employment standards and plans for terrestrial broadcasting television based on the digital and analog switch off, among other things.
3. Business competitiveness risks, which have an impact on the management of social issues and corporate governance.

# Berikut ini adalah risiko-risiko ESG:

1. Risiko terkait ketidakpastian makroekonomi, yang berdampak pada pengelolaan isu lingkungan dan sosial.
2. Risiko terkait dengan kebijakan baru pemerintah yang berdampak pada tata kelola perusahaan. Secara khusus, dengan berlakunya Undang-Undang Nomor 11 Tahun 2020 tentang Cipta Kerja yang juga dikenal dengan Omnibus Law, Perusahaan telah melakukan perubahan standar ketenagakerjaan dan rencana penyelenggaraan televisi penyiaran terestrial berbasis digital dan analog switch, dan lainnya.
3. Risiko daya saing bisnis, yang berdampak pada pengelolaan isu sosial dan tata kelola perusahaan.
