# Water Management

[GRI 303-1, GRI 303-3, GRI 303-5]

Based on our perspective, we see first-hand how floods and drought routinely happen in Indonesia and affects many people on many occasions. Flood disasters occur a lot in Indonesia and that water is a resource that we must manage. As a result, we are constantly motivated to preserve water resources. We are committed to managing our water consumption responsibly and efficiently. Our initiative includes installing an automatic water tap system in all of our buildings. Additional efforts were also conducted, such as organising campaigns to save water and appealing to employees through posters in various places.

# Pengelolaan Air

[GRI 303-1, GRI 303-3, GRI 303-5]

Berdasarkan perspektif kami, kami melihat secara langsung bagaimana banjir dan kekeringan rutin terjadi di Indonesia dan berdampak pada banyak orang dalam banyak peristiwa. Bencana banjir banyak terjadi di Indonesia dan air merupakan sumber daya yang harus kita kelola. Oleh karena itu, kami selalu termotivasi untuk melestarikan sumber daya air. Kami berkomitmen untuk mengelola konsumsi air secara bertanggung jawab dan efisien. Inisiatif kami termasuk memasang sistem keran air otomatis di semua gedung kami. Upaya tambahan juga dilakukan, seperti mengadakan kampanye hemat air dan himbauan kepada karyawan melalui poster di berbagai tempat.
