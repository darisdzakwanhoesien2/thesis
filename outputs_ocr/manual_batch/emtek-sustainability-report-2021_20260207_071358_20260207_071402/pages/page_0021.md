# Vidio is our streaming service that is accessible on all screen devices that have access to the internet.

Through this application, Various entertaining content are now in the fingertips of everyone with a connection to the internet. To ensure the accessibility of Vidio, we promote the use of this application through mobile devices.

Vidio adalah layanan streaming kami yang dapat diakses di semua perangkat layar yang memiliki akses ke internet. Melalui aplikasi ini, berbagai konten menghibur kini tersedia untuk semua orang dengan koneksi ke internet. Untuk memastikan aksesibilitas ke Vidio, kami mempromosikan penggunaan aplikasi ini melalui perangkat seluler.

In addition to encouraging penetration of mobile applications with many users, we are also expanding to a bigger screen home entertainment through collaboration with many smart TV brands and also OTT services such as Indihome and XL home. Further accessibility is also provided through bandwidth-saving technology, allowing for better and faster viewing experience.

Affordability of VIDIO is greatly enhanced through our partnership with Telkom, which makes quota prices more affordable and accessible for viewers by ensuring availability in all e-commerce sites as a place of purchase that is accessed by many customers.

Selain mendorong agar aplikasi mobile memiliki banyak pengguna, kami juga merambah ke ranah home entertainment yang lebih besar melalui kerjasama dengan banyak brand smart TV dan juga layanan OTT seperti Indihome dan XL home. Aksesibilitas lebih lanjut juga disediakan melalui teknologi hemat bandwidth sehingga memungkinkan pengalaman menonton yang lebih baik dan lebih cepat.

Keterjangkauan VIDIO ditingkatkan melalui kemitraan kami dengan Telkom, yang membuat harga kuota lebih terjangkau dan dapat diakses oleh penonton dengan memastikan ketersediaan di semua situs e-commerce sebagai tempat pembelian yang diakses oleh banyak pelanggan.

# Access to Market

GRI 203-2

EMTEK contributes to improving the livelihood of micro, small and medium enterprises' (MSMEs) by investing in Bukalapak. Various programs and initiatives have been implemented through Bukalapak including:

- Providing laptops to increase their competitive edge in the market

- Providing assistance for MSMEs to help access more affordable sources of goods

# Access to Finance

GRI 203-2

Driven by our belief that financial inclusion is a key part of economic development, EMTEK strives to provide accessibility to affordable financial services. Our company continues to promote financial inclusivity by helping communities access banking services through DANA, a digital e-money and e-wallet company under EMTEK. Our company has also promoted this amongst our own employees by sending a portion of each employee's salary through DANA to not only enforce traceability of funds but to also encourage the shift towards a cashless society.

Meningkatkan cara UMKM mengelola usahanya tidak hanya akan meningkatkan pendapatan dan mata pencaharian mereka, tetapi keberhasilan mereka juga akan mendukung pertumbuhan ekonomi lokal dan mendorong inovasi.
