# OUR JOURNEY
Perjalanan Kami

## 1983
The company was established under the name PT Elang Mahkota Komputer as a computer equipment provider.
Perusahaan ini didirikan dengan nama PT Elang Mahkota Komputer sebagai penyedia peralatan komputer.

## 1997
PT Elang Mahkota Komputer changed its name to PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi (EMTEK).
PT Elang Mahkota Komputer merubah namanya menjadi PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi (EMTEK).

## 2002
EMTEK acquired strategic share ownership in the free-to-air television station PT Surya Citra Televisi (SCTV) through PT Surya Citra Media TBK (SCMA).
EMTEK mengakuisisi kepemilikan saham strategis di stasiun televisi free-to-air PT Surya Citra Televisi (SCTV) melalui PT Surya Citra Media TBK (SCMA).

## 2009
EMTEK obtained an effective statement from the Capital Market and Financial Institution Supervisory Agency (Bapepam-LK) / Financial Services Authority (OJK) to conduct initial public offering (IPO) of $12.730.000 shares.
EMTEK memperoleh pernyataan efektif dari Badan Pengawas Pasar Modal dan Lembaga Keuangan (Bapepam-LK)/Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) untuk melakukan penawaran umum perdana (IPO) sebanyak $12.730.000 lembar saham.

## 2010
EMTEK conducted initial public offering (IPO) amounting to 10% of the total issued shares on the Indonesia Stock Exchange.
EMTEK melakukan penawaran umum perdana (IPO) sebesar 10% dari total saham yang ditempatkan di Bursa Efek Indonesia.

## 2011
EMTEK acquired PT Indosiar Karya Mandiri Tbk (IDKM), the holding company of free-to-air television station PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri (Indosiar).
EMTEK mengakuisisi PT Indosiar Karya Mandiri Tbk (IDKM), induk perusahaan stasiun televisi free-to-air PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri (Indosiar).
EMTEK's production house subsidiary PT Screenplay Produksi (Screenplay Productions) started its operation.
Anak usaha EMTEK yang bergerak di bidang rumah produksi, PT Screenplay Produksi (Screenplay Productions), mulai beroperasi.

## 2013
EMTEK merged SCMA, the holding company of SCTV, with IDKM, the holding company of Indosiar.
EMTEK menggabungkan SCMA, induk perusahaan SCTV, dengan IDKM, induk usaha Indosiar.
EMTEK secured exclusive broadcasting rights for the 2013 SEA Games in Myanmar through SCTV and Indosiar.
EMTEK mendapatkan hak siar eksklusif SEA Games 2013 di Myanmar melalui SCTV dan Indosiar.

EMTEK secured domestic broadcasting rights for seasons 2013/2014, 2014/2015, and 2015/2016 of the Barclays Premier League.
EMTEK mendapatkan hak siar Barclays Premier League untuk musim 2013/2014, 2014/2015, dan 2015/2016.
EMTEK secured domestic broadcasting rights for seasons 2012/2013, 2013/2014, and 2014/2015 of the UEFA Champions League.
EMTEK mendapatkan hak siar UEFA Champions League untuk musim 2012/2013, 2013/2014, and 2014/2015.
EMTEK through subsidiary PT Elang Medika Corpora (EMC) acquired the Usada Insani Hospital in Tangerang.
EMTEK melalui anak perusahaan PT Elang Medika Corpora (EMC) mengakuisisi Rumah Sakit Usada Insani di Tangerang.

## 2014
EMTEK invested in Home Tester Club, BukaLapak.com, Pertamedika Sentul Hospital, and Karir.com.
EMTEK berinvestasi di Home Tester Club, BukaLapak.com, RS Pertamedika Sentul, dan Karir.com.

## 2015
EMTEK Group established content subholding company PT Indonesia Entertainment Grup, a joint venture between EMTEK and SCMA, with subsidiaries engaging in production house, content production and distribution, and production infrastructure businesses.
Grup EMTEK mendirikan perusahaan subholding konten PT Indonesia Entertainment Grup, perusahaan patungan antara EMTEK dan SCMA, dengan anak perusahaan yang bergerak di bidang rumah produksi, produksi dan distribusi konten, dan bisnis infrastruktur produksi.
EMTEK Group provided additional investment in Bukalapak.com.
Grup EMTEK melakukan investasi lanjutan di Bukalapak.com.

## 2016
Through PT Estha Yudha Ekatama (EYE), EMTEK expanded into outdoor advertising.
Melalui PT Estha Yudha Ekatama (EYE), EMTEK merambah ke iklan outdoor.
EMTEK increased its share ownership in Usada Insani Hospital and Pertamedika Sentul Hospital.
EMTEK meningkatkan kepemilikan sahamnya di RS Usada Insani dan RS Pertamedika Sentul.

## 2017
Through KMK Online, EMTEK acquired a digital payment business PT Espay Debit Indonesia Koe (EDIK), a licensed electronic money company registered with Bank Indonesia. This digital payment has launched under the name "DANA".
Melalui KMK Online, EMTEK mengakuisisi bisnis pembayaran digital PT Espay Debit Indonesia Koe (EDIK), sebuah perusahaan uang elektronik berlisensi yang terdaftar di Bank Indonesia. Pembayaran digital ini diluncurkan dengan nama "DANA".
EMTEK through PT Elang Andalan Nusantara invested in TIX.id, a movie ticket booking application in Indonesia.
EMTEK melalui PT Elang Andalan Nusantara berinvestasi di TIX.id, aplikasi pemesanan tiket film di Indonesia.
