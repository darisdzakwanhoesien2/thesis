## Penanganan dan Pencegahan Penularan Virus Covid-19

Pandemi Covid-19 yang belum terselesaikan menjadi tantangan tersendiri bagi Perseroan dan Entitas Anak untuk menjaga kesehatan karyawan sekaligus mempertahankan tingkat produktivitas. Kami tetap memberlakukan protokol kesehatan yang ketat untuk meminimalkan risiko penularan Covid-19 terhadap karyawan ataupun pihak yang beraktivitas di wilayah operasional pertambangan dan kantor pusat.

Protokol kesehatan terkait penanganan Covid-19 dirumuskan dengan mengacu kepada peraturan yang diterbitkan Kementerian ESDM berdasarkan Surat Edaran No. 02.E/04/DJB/2020 tentang Pencegahan dan Penanganan Covid-19. Selain itu, kami melakukan beberapa upaya dalam rangka mencegah penanganan dan pencegahan penularan Covid-19, yakni:

1. Melakukan upaya pencegahan penularan Covid-19 di tempat kerja sesuai Surat Direktur Teknik dan Lingkungan Mineral dan Batubara/Kepala Inspektur Tambang No. 797/37.04/DBT/2020 tanggal 12 Maret 2020 tentang Upaya Pencegahan Covid-19 di Tempat Kerja;
2. Melakukan penelitian dan pengendalian risiko Covid-19 kepada setiap karyawan;
3. Membatasi kegiatan berkumpul, menerapkan social distance, serta physical distance yang aman pada kendaraan angkutan karyawan;
4. Membudayakan dan menyediakan sarana yang diperlukan untuk hidup bersih dan sehat bagi setiap karyawan;
5. Melakukan sterilisasi dan pembersihan lingkungan kerja secara berkala;
6. Melaksanakan sistem deteksi dini;
7. Menyusun dan menjalankan manajemen keadaan darurat Covid-19 yang disesuaikan dengan kebijakan dan kondisi perusahaan;
8. Melaksanakan swab antigen atau PCR, apabila diperlukan; serta
9. Mengalokasikan sumber daya tambahan, antara lain berupa anggaran, sarana, tenaga medis dan non medis untuk pencegahan dan penanganan Covid-19 di wilayah kerja.

## Handling and Prevention of the Covid-19 Transmission

The unresolved Covid-19 pandemic became a separate challenge for the Company and its Subsidiaries to maintain employees' health while maintaining productivity level. We enforce strict health protocols to minimize the risk of Covid-19 transmission to employees or parties who are active in the mining operational areas and head office.

The health protocols related to the Covid-19 are established by referring to regulations issued by the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources based on Circular No. 02.E/04/DJB/2020 on the Prevention and Handling of Covid-19. Furthermore, we conduct the following efforts to handle and prevent the Covid-19 transmission:

1. Making efforts to prevent Covid-19 transmission at workplace in accordance with the Letter of Director of Technical and Environmental of Mineral and Coal/Head of Mining Inspector No. 797/37.04/DBT/2020 dated March 12, 2020, on Efforts to Prevent Covid-19 at Workplace;
2. Assessing and controlling Covid-19 risk for each employee;
3. Restricting gathering activities, implementing social distances, and enforcing safe physical distances on employees' transportation vehicles;
4. Cultivating and providing every employee with the means necessary for a clean and healthy life;
5. Sterilizing and cleaning the work environment regularly;
6. Implementing an early detection system;
7. Preparing and carrying out emergency Covid-19 management according to the Company's policies and conditions;
8. Conducting antigen swab or PCR if necessary; and
9. Allocating additional resources, among others, in the form of budget, facilities, medical, and non-medical personnel for Covid-19 prevention and handling in the work areas.
