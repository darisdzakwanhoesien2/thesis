# Program Pembangunan dan Bantuan Pengadaan Material untuk Beberapa Tempat Ibadah
# Development Program and Assistance in Procurement of Materials for Several Places of Worship

Pada Pilar Sosial Budaya, melalui beberapa Entitas Anak: MSI dan POS, kami ikut berpartisipasi dalam pembangunan musholla di kantor Desa Perangat Selatan dan pengadaan bantuan material pembangunan masjid di Desa Wailukum, serta pengadaan material pembangunan pondasi gereja baru di Desa Wailukum. Berbagai kegiatan tersebut ditujukan agar menciptakan rasa aman dan nyaman dalam menjalankan ibadah.

In the Socio-Cultural Pillar, through several Subsidiaries: MSI and POS, we participated in the construction of a prayer room at South Perangat Village office, procurement of material assistance for the construction of a mosque in Wailukum Village, and procurement of materials for the construction of foundations for a new church in Wailukum Village. These various activities are aimed at creating a sense of security and comfort in carrying out worship.

# Program Pengadaan Perahu untuk Antisipasi Bencana Banjir
# Procurement Program for Boats to Anticipate Flood Disasters

Melalui Entitas Anak, yaitu MSI juga telah diberikan bantuan berupa perahu untuk membantu masyarakat desa yang terdampak bencana banjir. Through its Subsidiary, namely MSI, we provided assistance in the form of boats to help village communities affected by floods.
