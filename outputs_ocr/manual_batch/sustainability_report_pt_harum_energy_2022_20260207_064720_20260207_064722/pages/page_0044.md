# Kebijakan Anti-Korupsi

Kami berkomitmen untuk menjalankan kegiatan usaha yang sehat melalui pencegahan praktik korupsi dan fraud di seluruh lingkungan kerja. Oleh karena itu, kami telah menyusun kebijakan anti-korupsi dan anti-fraud sebagaimana tercantum dalam Kode Etik Perseroan yang wajib dipatuhi oleh karyawan dan pemangku kepentingan yang terlibat dalam kegiatan usaha yang dijalankan. [GRI 3-3, GRI 205-1]

Secara berkala, kami mensosialisasikan kebijakan tersebut bersamaan dengan sosialisasi Kode Etik kepada insan Perseroan. Selain itu, kami tidak menerima sanksi terkait praktik korupsi maupun fraud yang dilakukan oleh insan Perseroan di seluruh lingkungan kerja. [GRI 205-2, GRI 205-3]

# Benturan Kepentingan [GRI 2-11, GRI 2-15]

Kami berkomitmen untuk menyelenggarakan kegiatan usaha yang baik dan bertanggung jawab. Kami menjamin setiap keputusan bisnis Perseroan maupun Entitas Anak dilakukan secara profesional, tanpa adanya pengaruh dari pihak manapun yang berpotensi menimbulkan benturan kepentingan. Selain itu, kami memastikan bahwa tidak terdapat Pemegang Saham yang memiliki hubungan langsung maupun tidak langsung dengan pemasok dan rantai pasokan Perseroan.

Complete information on Code of Conduct can be accessed via (http://www.harumenergy.com/assets/files/kode_etik_perusahaan_(code_of_conduct).pdf).

# Anti-Corruption Policy

We are committed to carrying out healthy business activities by preventing corruption and fraud practices throughout the work environment. Therefore, we have developed anti-corruption and anti-fraud policies as contained in the Company's Code of Conduct that employees and stakeholders involved in the business activities must comply with. [GRI 3-3, GRI 205-1]

Periodically, we disseminate the policies along with dissemination of the Code of Ethics to the Company's personnel. In addition, we did not receive sanctions related to corrupt or fraud practices committed by Company personnel in all work environment. [GRI 205-2, GRI 205-3]

# Conflict of Interest [GRI 2-11, GRI 2-15]

We are committed to conducting good and responsible business activities. We guarantee that every business decision of the Company and Subsidiaries is carried out professionally, without any influence from any party that has the potential to cause a conflict of interest. In addition, we ensure that there are no Shareholders who have a direct or indirect relationship with the Company's suppliers and supply chain.
