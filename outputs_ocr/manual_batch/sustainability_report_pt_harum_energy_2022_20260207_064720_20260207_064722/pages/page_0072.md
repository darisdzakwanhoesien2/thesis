## Kegiatan Penanaman Pohon pada Hari Lingkungan Hidup Sedunia

Tree Planting Activities on World Environment Day

Pelaksanaan program unggulan bidang keterlibatan masyarakat pada pengelolaan lingkungan di sekitar wilayah tambang dilakukan melalui kegiatan penanaman pohon dalam rangka memperingati Hari Lingkungan Hidup Sedunia dengan melibatkan Pemuda Karang Taruna Desa Soagimalaha. Kegiatan ini dilaksanakan Perseroan, melalui Entitas Anak: POS, di pesisir pantai guna meminimalisir potensi erosi akibat perubahan iklim.

Our featured program in the field of community involvement in environmental management around the mining area is implemented through tree planting activities in commemoration of World Environment Day by involving the Youth Group of Soagimalaha Village. This activity is conducted by the Company, through its Subsidiary: POS, on the coast to minimize the potential for erosion due to climate change.

## Bantuan Prasarana English Learning Center

English Learning Center Facility Assistance

Program penyediaan fasilitas belajar serta pendampingan kepada Yayasan Suara Faifiye dilakukan Perseroan melalui Entitas Anak, yaitu POS. Program yang diselenggarakan berfokus pada pembelajaran Bahasa Inggris untuk anak-anak usia dini. Hal ini sejalan dengan program Pendidikan POS Pintar (Pendidikan Inovatif untuk Masyarakat Sekitar).

The program for providing learning facilities and assistance to Suara Faifiye Foundation is carried out by the Company through its Subsidiary, namely POS. The program focuses on learning English for young children. This is in line with POS Smart Education program (Innovative Education for Surrounding Communities).
