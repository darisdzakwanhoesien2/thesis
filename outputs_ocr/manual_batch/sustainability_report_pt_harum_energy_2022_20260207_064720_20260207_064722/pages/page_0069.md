# Pembinaan UMKM Kopi Harum
Harum Coffee MSME Assistance

Pada Pilar Tingkat Pendapatan Riil atau Pekerjaan, melalui Entitas Anak, yaitu POS bekerja sama dengan mahasiswa dari Fakultas Pertanian, Universitas Khairun telah melaksanakan kegiatan pendampingan pengolahan kopi dengan memanfaatkan sumber daya alam dan sumber daya manusia kepada UMKM Kopi Harum di Desa Binaan. Di samping kegiatan pelatihan, kelompok binaan juga diberikan pelatihan pengemasan sampai dengan penjualan produk.

In the Real Income Level or Employment Pillar, through our Subsidiary, namely POS, we collaborated with students from the Faculty of Agriculture, Khairun University, to conduct coffee processing assistance activities by utilizing natural resources and human resources for Harum Coffee MSME in Fostered Villages. In addition to training activities, the fostered groups were also given training on packaging to product sales.

# Program Pembinaan Kelompok Tani Wanita
Women's Farmer Group Development Program

Kami juga menyelenggarakan Program Pembinaan Kelompok Tani Wanita. Program pemberdayaan dengan dua sasaran target, yaitu pemberdayaan wanita dan pertanian. Pelaksanaan program diharapkan dapat meningkatkan kesejahteraan serta kemandirian ekonomi masyarakat, khususnya di desa binaan.

We also organize Women's Farmer Group Development Program. This empowerment program is with two targets, which are women's empowerment and agriculture. This program is expected to increase the welfare and economic self-reliance of the community, particularly in the assisted villages.
