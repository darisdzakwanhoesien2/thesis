# Nominasi dan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi [GRI 2-10, GRI 2-19, GRI 2-20]

Pemberian remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi mengacu pada keputusan kebijakan internal Perseroan dengan mempertimbangkan kondisi Perseroan. Penetapan besaran remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris dilakukan oleh Komisaris Utama setelah mendapat wewenang dari RUPS, sedangkan remunerasi bagi Direksi diberikan atas kebijakan Dewan Komisaris. Adapun dalam proses penentuan besaran remunerasi, Perseroan tidak melibatkan pemangku kepentingan.

Informasi lebih lengkap terkait nominasi dan remunerasi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi disampaikan dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan tahun 2022.

# Nomination and Remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors [GRI 2-10, GRI 2-19, GRI 2-20]

Provision of remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors refers to the Company's internal policy decisions by considering the Company's condition. The remuneration amount for the Board of Commissioners is determined by the President Commissioner after obtaining authority from the GMS, while remuneration for the Board of Directors is given at Board of Commissioners' discretion. In determining the remuneration amount, the Company does not involve stakeholders.

More complete information on nomination and remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is outlined in the Company's 2022 Annual Report.

# Pengembangan Kompetensi Keberlanjutan [POJK E.2] [GRI 2-17]
# Sustainability Competency Development

Dalam rangka mencapai tujuan keberlanjutan, kami senantiasa mengikutsertakan karyawan kepada kegiatan-kegiatan pengembangan kompetensi, khususnya terkait keberlanjutan sosial dan lingkungan yang terkait dengan kegiatan usaha yang Perseroan jalankan. Program pengembangan kompetensi tersebut diprioritaskan bagi penanggung jawab keberlanjutan di Perseroan, lalu selanjutnya akan diikuti oleh seluruh karyawan, baik yang bekerja di wilayah operasional tambang hingga kantor pusat. [GRI 3-3]

Pada tahun 2022, sebanyak 206 orang karyawan atau sekitar $28,1\%$ dari total karyawan telah mengikuti berbagai kegiatan pengembangan kompetensi. Beberapa di antaranya merupakan penanggung jawab penerapan keberlanjutan. Namun, pada tahun ini, kami belum melakukan perhitungan terhadap jam pelatihan. [GRI 404-3]

In order to achieve sustainability goals, we enroll our employees in competency development activities, particularly those related to social and environmental sustainability in connection with the Company's business activities. The competency development programs are prioritized for those in charge of sustainability in the Company. Then, all employees will participate, either those working in the mine operational area or those at the head office. [GRI 3-3]

In 2022, as many as 206 employees or around $28.1\%$ of the total employees participated in various competency development activities. Some of them are persons responsible for implementing sustainability. However, we had not calculated training hours for this year. [GRI 404-3]
