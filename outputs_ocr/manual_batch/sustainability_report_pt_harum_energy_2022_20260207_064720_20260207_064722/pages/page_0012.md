# Pelibatan Pemangku Kepentingan [POJK E.4] [GRI 2-29, GRI 3-1] Stakeholders Engagement

Kehadiran pemangku kepentingan memberikan pengaruh yang penting dalam keberlangsungan usaha, termasuk dalam penyusunan Laporan Keberlanjutan. Proses pengambilan keputusan, pengelolaan isu-isu penting yang relevan terhadap kinerja keberlanjutan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak, serta topik material dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan ini mempertimbangkan sudut pandang para pemangku kepentingan.

Stakeholders' presence has an important influence on business continuity, including in preparing the Sustainability Report. The decision-making process, the management of important issues relevant to the sustainability performance of the Company and its Subsidiaries, and the material topics in this Sustainability Report consider stakeholders' perspectives.
