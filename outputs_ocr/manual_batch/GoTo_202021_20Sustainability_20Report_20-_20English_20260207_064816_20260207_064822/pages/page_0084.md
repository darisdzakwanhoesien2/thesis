# Appendix A:

# Initiatives for Medium and Low Priority Material Topics

|  Material Topic | Initiative | Brief Description  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Community Health and Well-Being | COVID-19 Vaccination Vouchers | Two ride vouchers worth $15 each for all users in Singapore which they can use to book rides to and from COVID-19 vaccination centers around the island.  |
|  Community Health and Well-Being | Gotong Royong Oxygen House | GoTo Group and partners set up Indonesia's first dedicated oxygen facility for COVID-19 patients.  |
|  Community Health and Well-Being | Supporting the Community in Vietnam amid COVID-19 | Cash support for drivers and free delivery on GoFood orders at selected locations in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi.  |
|  Community Health and Well-Being | GoPay and Jago Collaboration | GoPay and Jago collaboration enables Indonesians to open a bank account via the Gojek app.  |
|  Consumer Welfare | #AmanBersamaGojek (#SafewithGojek) | Series of initiatives focusing on education, safety and protection to ensure that the Gojek ecosystem is safe for everyone - users, driver-partners, and merchant partners.  |
|  Consumer Welfare | Legal Assistance Program | Legal assistance for users who fall victim to scams and social engineering frauds on Tokopedia. We help users report their cases to the police.  |
|  Consumer Welfare | Suspicious Card Transaction Alert | Alert sent to issuing bank on suspicious card transactions to reconfirm the transaction with the card holder.  |
|  Consumer Welfare | This is me/not me | An alert that is sent to the user's trusted device if there was an attempt to log in using new device, thus preventing users from falling victim to any kind of scam.  |
|  Consumer Welfare | Routine social media Anti-Scam Education | Regular social media posts to remind users of how to keep their account safe from potentially fraudulent activities.  |
|  Consumer Welfare | Portal POLRI | A web Portal to assist the police for fast track access to official data requests in criminal cases.  |
|  Stakeholder Engagement (Partners) | "Go the Extra Mile" campaign | S$1 per completed trip given back to driver-partners throughout Gojek's three year anniversary week in Singapore.  |
