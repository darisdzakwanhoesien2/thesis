# Appendix H: Customer Satisfaction and Public Complaint Mechanism

## Gojek Customer Satisfaction Survey

To evaluate the level of customer satisfaction with the products provided by Gojek, Gojek conducts customer satisfaction surveys on a quarterly basis. In Q4 2021, the customer satisfaction survey was conducted with 2,277 customers covering five products: GoRide, GoCar, GoFood, GoSend, and GoMart. The survey obtained the Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) for each product.

### Customer Satisfaction Survey on Gojek Products (Q4 2021)

|  Product | CSAT^{a)}  |
| --- | --- |
|  GoRide | 94  |
|  GoCar | 96  |
|  GoFood | 96  |
|  GoSend | 96  |
|  GoMart | 93  |

a) CSAT score: % of respondents who rated "Satisfied" or "Very Satisfied" on their experience with the respective product.
Base : GoRide=507, GoCar=507, GoFood=417, GoSend=520, GoMart=326.
Age group : above 18 years old.
Cities : Jakarta, Bodetabek, Bandung, Surabaya, Makassar, Medan, Yogyakarta, Semarang, Palembang, Bali, Malang.

## Tokopedia Customer Satisfaction Survey

The Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) Survey is conducted to measure user's satisfaction in using Tokopedia products and/or services. Table 1 shows the CSAT survey results conducted for Logistics and Support Service (Care) among buyers in 2021. The survey is conducted in-house with details as follows:

- Logistics: Survey is distributed every W1 of running month to eligible buyers
- Support Service: Survey is distributed to users after complaint status is finished and complaint ticket is closed

### CSAT Survey for Logistics and Support Service (Care) Among Buyers, FY'21

|  Product | CSAT | Base  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Logistics | 85 | 3,606  |
|  Support Service (Care) | 81 | 782,974  |

These insights are valuable, and some highlights that we can draw out are the average ratings of 4.4 and 4.8 (out of 5) on both AppStore and Google Play for Gojek and Tokopedia apps respectively. These numbers indicate the trust and satisfaction that goes beyond consumer's expectation. Analyzing these reviews also show that the best aspects from these apps include: being easy to use, quality of service, having diverse payment methods, and promotions on cashback and free-shipping.
