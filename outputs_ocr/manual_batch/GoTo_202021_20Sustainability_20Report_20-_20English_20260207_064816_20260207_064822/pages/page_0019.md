At GoTo, we use the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) to align our efforts to global targets, as well as measure how our company and ecosystem contributes to resolving society's most pressing challenges. In ensuring the impact we have is measurable, accurate and relevant, we have mapped GoTo's approach and management of our material ESG issues with the relevant SDGs and the corresponding reporting standard disclosures (GRI and SASB for this reporting period).
