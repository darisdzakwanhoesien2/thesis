Our Supplier Code of Conduct lays out clear guidelines to which all our suppliers are required to comply. It does not extend only to our direct suppliers, but also covers the suppliers' employees, agents, assistants, and sub-suppliers.

# Some key elements of our Supplier Code of Conduct

![img-31.jpeg](img-31.jpeg)

We have robust governance mechanisms and zero tolerance for unethical behaviour. We had zero confirmed incidents of corruption and zero new confirmed cases of anti-competitive behavior, antitrust or monopoly practices across any of our businesses in 2021.

As additional information, GoTo Group was identified as a participant in two cases³ related to late reporting of acquisition in 2017 and 2018 which were active in 2021. We have responded to all enquiries from the authorities in a timely manner to meet the expectations of the relevant regulator and both cases have been settled in 2021.

³ Details of the cases are included in Appendix B.
