# Rehabilitation Activities at PT Gane Permai Sentosa

PT Gane Permai Sentosa (GPS) was also involved in rehabilitation activities in the Desa Galala Halmahera Selatan and Kawasan Hutan Lindung Halmahera Selatan areas. GPS planted a total of 387,750 seeds / tree saplings over a 517-hectare area. Harita Nickel produced a video of GPS's rehabilitation efforts, which can be viewed through this link: Video of Rehabilitation in GPS

Kegiatan Rehabilitasi di PT Gane Permai Sentosa

PT Gane Permai Sentosa (GPS) juga terlibat dalam kegiatan rehabilitasi di wilayah Desa Galala Halmahera Selatan dan Kawasan Hutan Lindung Halmahera Selatan. GPS menanam sebanyak 387.750 bibit/anakan pohon di area seluas 517 hektar. Harita Nickel membuat video tentang upaya rehabilitasi GPS, yang dapat dilihat melalui tautan ini: Video Rehabilitasi di GPS
