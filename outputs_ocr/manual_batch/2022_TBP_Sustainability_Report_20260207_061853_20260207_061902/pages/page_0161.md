# Socio-Cultural programs

PTTBP is actively involved in social and cultural activities. PTTBP funded the youth and sports events in 2022, including the Ngibi Cultural Dance Festival in Soligi, the Cakalele Cultural Dance Festival in Kawasi, the Tabligh akbar Festival in Laiwui, and a variety of stakeholder activities. The following diagram depicts the various projects related to social culture development, the investment amount, and the PTTBP beneficiaries.

# Spending on community engagement by targeted region

The Company delivered CSR programs in 10 (ten) villages including Kawasi, Buton, Baru, Akegula, Laiwui and Jikotamo in Obi District. Furthermore in Soligi, Gambaru and Fluk in South Obi District as well as Kelo Village in East Obi District. These villages are located in South Halmahera Regency, North Maluku Province.

# Program Sosial Budaya

PTTBP secara aktif terlibat dalam kegiatan sosial dan budaya. PTTBP mendanai kegiatan kepemudaan dan olahraga pada tahun 2022, termasuk Festival Tari Budaya Ngibi di Soligi, Festival Tari Budaya Cakalele di Kawasi, Festival Tabligh akbar di Laiwui, dan berbagai kegiatan pemangku kepentingan. Diagram berikut menggambarkan berbagai proyek yang terkait dengan pengembangan sosial budaya, jumlah investasi, dan penerima manfaat PTTBP.

# Pengeluaran untuk pelibatan masyarakat berdasarkan wilayah sasaran

Perusahaan melaksanakan program CSR di 10 (sepuluh) desa yaitu Kawasi, Buton, Baru, Akegula, Laiwui dan Jikotamo di Kecamatan Obi. Selanjutnya Soligi, Gambaru dan Fluk di Kecamatan Obi Selatan serta Desa Kelo di Kecamatan Obi Timur. Desa-desa tersebut berada di Kabupaten Halmahera Selatan, Provinsi Maluku Utara.
