The survey in 2022 found a total of 296 species, including mammals, aves, herpetofauna, insects and various flora species. Amongst them, 139 (47%) were endemic. None of the species found were "Critically Endangered", "Endangered", or "Vulnerable" according to the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN). Only one species was considered "Near Threatened" and this is *Pteropus hypomelanus*.

## Total Flora and Fauna Species Count

The number of flora and fauna counts that is observed through the annual monitoring and survey gives a general overview of the biodiversity condition although it may not necessarily depict the real number of species present in the area. Aside from a long interval between two monitoring period, variation in the number of species observed (especially for fauna) relies solely on the encounters during the survey, and hence it may vary depending on the time when the survey was conducted and the movement of the animals during that period of time.

Survei pada tahun 2022 menemukan total 296 spesies, termasuk mamalia, aves, herpetofauna, serangga, dan berbagai spesies flora. Di antara mereka, 139 (47%) adalah endemik. Tak satu pun dari spesies yang ditemukan "Kritis", "Terancam Punah", atau "Rentan" menurut *International Union for Conservation of Nature* (IUCN). Hanya satu spesies yang dianggap "Hampir Terancam" dan ini adalah *Pteropus hypomelanus*.

## Jumlah Spesies Flora dan Fauna

Jumlah flora dan fauna yang diamati melalui pemantauan dan survei tahunan memberikan gambaran umum tentang kondisi keanekaragaman hayati meskipun belum tentu menggambarkan jumlah spesies yang ada di daerah tersebut. Selain karena jarak waktu yang cukup lama antara dua periode pemantauan, variasi jumlah spesies yang diamati (terutama untuk fauna) hanya bergantung pada jumlah spesies yang dijumpai selama survei, dan karenanya dapat bervariasi tergantung pada waktu pelaksanaan survei dan pergerakan satwa selama periode waktu tersebut.
