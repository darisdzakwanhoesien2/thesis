# HUBUNGAN DENGAN PEMANGKU KEPENTINGAN

STAKEHOLDERS ENGAGEMENT

[SEOJK E.4], [GRI 2-29] [ACGS (B) C.1.3]

Perusahaan telah mengidentifikasi kelompok pemangku kepentingan Perusahaan, yaitu internal dan eksternal. Perusahaan memfasilitasi pelibatan pemangku kepentingan, termasuk dalam memberikan masukan dan umpan balik terkait bisnis dan aspek keberlanjutan yang material bagi Perusahaan, melalui kegiatan komunikasi dan koordinasi yang dilaksanakan secara rutin maupun penggunaan metode lainnya. Daftar pemangku kepentingan, serta metode dan frekuensi metode komunikasi dan koordinasi, dapat dilihat pada tabel di bawah ini:

The Company has identified its stakeholders groups, namely internal and external. The Company also facilitates the engagement of stakeholders, including soliciting input and feedback related to business and sustainability aspects that are material to the Company, through communication and coordination activities that are carried out regularly as well as the use of other methods, including in providing their insights and feedback regarding business and sustainability matters that are material to the Company.
