## Kudus Vocational High School Improvement Program

The Kudus Vocational High School Improvement Program is an educational initiative undertaken by the Djarum Foundation for Education since 2011, focusing on improving human resource quality through vocational education in Kudus, Central Java. To date, the Djarum Foundation for Education has nurtured 17 vocational high schools with 20 competency skills.

# PROGRAM TANGGUNG JAWAB SOSIAL DAN LINGKUNGAN
## SOCIAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY PROGRAM
[SEOJK F.25]

1. **Curriculum Alignment**: Aspek ini menekankan pada upaya untuk memastikan kurikulum pendidikan yang dijalankan di SMK sejalan dengan ekspektasi industri dan perkembangannya ke depan. Curriculum alignment melibatkan kontribusi dan masukkan dari para ahli atau akademisi serta pelaku usaha profesional di industri yang relevan.
2. **Teacher Training**: Aspek ini menekankan pada upaya peningkatan kualitas tenaga pendidik melalui pelatihan dan magang di industri guna mendapatkan kualitas keterampilan yang sesuai dengan standar industri.
3. **Physical Learning Environment**: Melalui aspek ini, program berfokus pada upaya untuk menyediakan fasilitas pendikan di sekolah-sekolah agar memiliki standar yang sama sesuai dengan kebutuhan yang dimiliki oleh dunia industri.
4. **Teaching Factory**: Aspek ini bermaksud untuk memberikan kesempatan dan pengalaman kepada peserta didik agar dapat mengaplikasikan keterampilannya dengan mengerjakan proyek pesanan berbayar dari perusahaan-perusahaan besar.
5. **Career Placement Services**: Membantu lulusan-lusan SMK mendapatkan pekerjaan yang sesuai dengan keahlian dan keterampilannya dengan remunerasi yang kompetitif.

Tahun 2021, Grup ikut berkomitmen membantu program ini dengan memberikan bantuan berupa peningkatan kualitas guru melalui pelatihan bersama industri yang kompeten (Teacher Training), menambah kapasitas ruang dan alat praktek berstandar industri (Physical Learning Environment) dan melengkapi kebutuhan Teaching Factory.

Berikut adalah dukungan yang diberikan Grup melalui program pengembangan ini sampai dengan tahun 2023:

1. **Curriculum Alignment**: This aspect emphasizes efforts to ensure the educational curriculum implemented in vocational high schools aligns with industry expectations and future developments. Curriculum alignment involves contributions and input from experts or academics and professional business practitioners in relevant industries.
2. **Teacher Training**: This aspect focuses on enhancing the quality of educators through training and internships in the industry to achieve skills quality that meets industry standards.
3. **Physical Learning Environment**: Through this aspect, the program focuses on providing educational facilities in schools that meet the same standards as required by the industry.
4. **Teaching Factory**: This aspect intends to provide opportunities and experiences for students to apply their skills by working on paid projects from major companies.
5. **Career Placement Services**: Assisting vocational high school graduates in finding employment that matches their expertise and skills with competitive remuneration.

In 2021, the Group committed to supporting this program by providing assistance in improving teacher quality through joint training with competent industries (Teacher Training), increasing the capacity of rooms and practice tools to industry standards (Physical Learning Environment), and fulfilling the needs of the Teaching Factory.
