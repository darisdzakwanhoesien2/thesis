# PENJELASAN DIREKSI

## BOARD OF DIRECTORS REPORT

Grup SMN meningkatkan pengawasan terhadap implementasi kinerja keberlanjutan dalam kegiatan bisnis Grup, dengan fokus utama pada inisiatif digitalisasi dan Information Technology melalui penerapan cyber security, implementasi prinsip tata kelola yang baik, antara lain melalui kebijakan anti-korupsi dan labor management, Improvement of Waste and Energy Consumption, serta pelaksanaan kegiatan CSR yang terarah dan terukur.

SMN Group enhance the oversight of sustainability performance implementation in the Group's business activities, with a main focus on digitalization initiatives and Information Technology through the application of cyber security, implementation of good governance principles, including through anti-corruption policies and labour management, improvement of waste and energy consumption, as well as the execution of directed and measurable CSR activities.
