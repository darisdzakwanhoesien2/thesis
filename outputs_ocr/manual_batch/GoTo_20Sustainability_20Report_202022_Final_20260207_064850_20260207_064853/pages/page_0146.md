|  Gojek (Perseroan) diperiksa oleh Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha (KPPU) terkait keterlambatan pemberitahuan (notifikasi) akuisisi Perseroan yang dilakukannya atas PT Lintas Promosi Global pada tahun 2018. Menurut peraturan pemerintah yang berlaku, Perseroan wajib melakukan pemberitahuan (notifikasi) pengambilalahan saham PT Lintas Promosi Global kepada KPPU selambat-lambatnya 30 (tiga puluh) hari sejak tanggal efektif setiap transaksi. Akan tetapi, Perseroan menyampaikan laporan di luar batas waktu yang diperbolehkan. | Perseroan diberitahu bahwa KPPU telah menghentikan penyelidikan atas keterlambatan pemberitahuan (notifikasi) Perseroan setelah diberlakukannya peraturan KPPU tentang relaksasi penegakan hukum. |  Gojek (the Company) was investigated by the Business Competition Supervisory Commission (KPPU) regarding the late notification of PT Lintas Promosi Global’s acquisition back in 2018. The Company acquired PT Lintas Promosi Global in 2018. Under the relevant Government regulation, the Company is required to report its acquisition of shares in PT Lintas Promosi Global within 30 working days from the effective date of each transaction. However, the Company submitted its report beyond the permissible deadline. | The Company has been informed that the KPPU has ceased the investigation of the Company’s late filing following the enactment of a KPPU regulation on the relaxation of legal enforcement. The Company has received a letter from the KPPU formalizing the dismissal of this investigation in 2022. |   |
