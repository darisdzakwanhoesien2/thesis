# Nol Sampah

# Zero Waste

Mengingat bahwa ekosistem jutaan mitra pedagang dan konsumen GoTo terdesentralisasi, pengembangan dan pengelolaan strategi Nol Sampah menjadi upaya dan proses yang sangat kompleks untuk dioperasionalisasi. Berkaca dari upaya dan praktik terbaik dari industri yang berkaitan dengan industri kami, ini akan menjadi kegiatan yang harus dilakukan secara berkelanjutan dan perlu dinilai kembali dan diulangi seiring dengan berjalannya waktu.

Considering GoTo’s decentralized ecosystem of millions of merchants and consumers, developing and managing our Zero Waste strategy has been an incredibly complex effort and process to operationalize. Scoping the efforts and best practices of related industries to ours, we acknowledge it will be an ongoing effort, and one we will continuously need to reassess and iterate upon as we progress along our journey.

Underpinning our efforts is our Zero Waste roadmap, which is publicly available on our website. As with our Zero Emissions roadmap, we aim to periodically update this live as we learn more from our efforts, partnerships, and peers, and as more credible solutions and alternatives are introduced to the market and industry.

As of today, our multi-pronged approach consists of:
