# Lampiran F: Inventaris Emisi GRK

# Appendix F: GHG Emissions Inventory

Total emisi yang diproduksi oleh GoTo pada tahun 2022 setara dengan 976,952,58⁵⁶ tCO2e.

Kami telah mengacu pada GHG Protocol WBCSD-WRI yang diterima secara luas untuk penghitungan emisi GRK. Kami menggunakan pendekatan pengendalian operasional untuk mengkonsolidasikan data GRK yang mencakup gas CO2, CH4, dan N2O dalam penghitungan jejak karbon yang setara.⁵⁷ Silakan mengacu pada rincian emisi GRK kami dibawah ini:

Total emissions for GoTo in 2022 was measured at 976,952,58⁵⁶ tCO2e.

We have referred to widely accepted WBCSD-WRI GHG Protocol for accounting of GHG emissions. We used the operational control approach to consolidate the data for our GHG inventory which includes CO2, CH4 and N2O gases in the accounting of carbon footprint equivalent.⁵⁷ Please find below the breakdown of our GHG emissions:

56. Gojek dan GTF bersama-sama menyumbang 766,043,25 tCO2e dari total emisi, sedangkan pangsa Tokopedia adalah 190,909,33 tCO2e.
Gojek and GTF together comprised 766,043,25 tCO2e of total emissions, while Tokopedia's share was 190,909,33 tCO2e.

57. Faktor emisi yang digunakan untuk perhitungan bersumber dari sumber yang diakui seperti Department of Environment, Food &amp; Rural Affairs (DEFRA), US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Energy Market Authority (EMA) dan Institute for Global Environmental Services (IGES). Potensi Pemanasan Global (Global Warming Potential) yang telah digunakan berasal dari laporan AIR5 Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC).
The emission factors used for the calculations are sourced from recognized sources like the Department of Environment, Food &amp; Rural Affairs (DEFRA), US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Energy Market Authority (EMA) and Institute for Global Environmental Services (IGES). The Global Warming Potential (GWP) used is sourced from the Sixth Assessment Report (AIR5) by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC).

58. Sebagian besar aktivitas bisnis utama Gojek dan Tokopedia termasuk dalam jejak emisi dan inventaris mereka, kecuali untuk aktivitas yang terkait langsung dengan pedagang dan pelanggan yang tidak dapat mereka pengaruh. Aktivitas kategori 5, 8, 10, 13, 14, dan 15 dari Cakupan 3 telah dikecualikan karena telah dimasukkan dalam perhitungan Lingkup 1 atau 2, berada di luar batas operasi GoTo, atau telah diidentifikasi sebagai tidak material atau tidak ada data yang memadai.
Most of Gojek's and Tokopedia's key business activities are included in their emissions footprint and inventory, except for activities directly associated with merchants and customers that they cannot influence. Category 5, 8, 10, 13, 14, and 15 activities from Scope 3 have been excluded due to them having been included in Scope 1 or 2 calculations, being outside the bounds of GoTo's operations, or having been identified as immaterial or absent of adequate data.

59. Termasuk pembakaran bahan bakar dari kendaraan di gudang Mitra dan emisi fugitif dari kebocoran zat pendingin di kantor.
Includes combustion of fuels from vehicles in Mitra warehouses and fugitive emissions from refrigerant leakages in offices.

60. Emisi GoTo Financial dimasukkan ke dalam Gojek karena kedua entitas tersebut bertagd kantor. Emisi Lingkup 2 Tokopedia termasuk kantor dan gudang.
GoTo Financial emissions are subsumed under Gojek as the two entities share offices. Tokopedia's Scope 2 emissions include both its offices and warehouses.

61. Perhitungan memperhitungkan semua emisi hulu (yaitu, credit to gate) dari produksi atas produk yang dibeli, disewa, atau diperoleh oleh GoTo pada tahun pelaporan. Produk mencakup barang berwujud dan tidak berwujud (misalnya kemasan, peralatan kantor, dan iklan) dari layanan dari pihak ketiga. Emisi GoTo Financial dimasukkan dalam Gojek karena penggunaan sumber daya bersama.
Calculations took into account all upstream emissions (i.e., cradle to gate) from the production of products purchased, rented, or acquired by GoTo in the reporting year. Products include both tangible and intangible goods (e.g., packaging, office equipment, and advertising) and services from third parties. GoTo Financial emissions are subsumed under Gojek due to shared resources.

62. Emisi dihitung dari data jarak dan pengeluaran pengiriman barang internal GoTo dari vendor dan pengiriman barang merchant antar gudang Tokopedia.
Emissions are derived from a combination of distance-based and spend-based data for shipment of GoTo internal goods from vendors and shipment of merchant goods between Tokopedia's warehouses.

63. Mencakup emisi dari transportasi karyawan dari aktivitas terkait bisnis (termasuk perjalanan udara, darat, kereta api, dan laut), serta akomodasi karyawan selama perjalanan terkait bisnis selama tahun pelaporan. Data jarak dan pengeluaran telah digunakan.
Includes emissions from the transportation of employees from business-related activities (including air, road, rail, and marine travel), as well as the accommodations of employees during business-related travel during the reporting year. Both distance-based and spend-based data were used.

64. Data survei digunakan untuk mengukur perjalanan dua arah karyawan dari rumah ke kantor atau gudang.
Survey data was used to measure two-way employee commute from their homes to our offices or warehouses.

65. Emisi dari distribusi dan transportasi hilir dihitung menggunakan data jarak dan pengeluaran biaya untuk pengiriman Tokopedia. Emisi dari pengiriman Tokopedia yang diselesaikan menggunakan layanan Gojek (yaitu GoKlat) termasuk dalam Kategori 11.
Downstream transportation and distribution emissions were calculated using distance-based and spend-based data from shipping of Tokopedia deliveries. Emissions from Tokopedia deliveries that were completed using Gojek services (i.e. GoKlat) are included in Category 11.

66. Emisi dari penggunaan produk yang dijual GoTo termasuk emisi tahap penggunaan langsung dari layanan mobilitas Gojek: GoBluebird, GoCar, GoTaxi, GoRide, GoFood, GoShop, GoMart, GoKlat, GoSend, dan GoBox. Emisi fase penggunaan tidak langsung dari produk yang dijual tidak termasuk.
Emissions from GoTo's use of sold products includes direct use-phase emissions from Gojek's mobility services: GoBluebird, GoCar, GoTaxi, GoRide, GoFood, GoShop, GoMart, GoKlat, GoSend, and GoBox. The indirect use-phase emission from sold products are excluded.

67. Termasuk emisi dari pembuangan limbah dan pengolahan produk yang dijual oleh GoTo. Emisi untuk kategori ini diperoleh dari data pengeluaran untuk pengemasan barang di Tokopedia.
Includes emissions from waste disposal and the treatment of products sold by GoTo. Emissions for this category were derived from spend data for packaging goods in Tokopedia.
