# Rekrutmen &amp; Pergantian Karyawan

Pada tahun 2022, kami merekrut 3.406 karyawan baru (sekitar 32% adalah perempuan dan 7% adalah posisi manajemen senior).

Di Grup GoTo, seperti banyak organisasi di seluruh industri global, kami diharuskan membuat keputusan sulit terkait Perseroan dan karyawan. Kami harus menilai kembali struktur organisasi untuk memenuhi permintaan pasar dan harapan yang baru, dan untuk memastikan kami dapat tumbuh secara berkelanjutan untuk mendukung ekosistem kami dalam jangka panjang. Sebagai hasil dari keputusan ini, kami memperkecil skala organisasi, dan sejalan dengan kebijakan terkait, kami menawarkan program bantuan yang adil untuk mendukung karyawan yang terdampak. Ke depan, kami akan sangat strategis dan fokus dalam hal rekrutmen dan

- DEI pathways curated to women's leadership, allyship, and the basics of DEI to raise awareness and change behaviors;
- The development of a bot to recognize and respond to employee opportunities for micro-learning, and to answer and refer employees to correct points of contact for DEI-related queries;
- In-person, custom training for all People Business Partners on issues related to DEI.

Further baselining and scoping of future activities continue. This is particularly the case for DEI targets and initiatives related to issues outside of gender and beyond our immediate workforce; for example:

- In 2023, establishing a series of roundtables to explore employee interest in establishing ERGs beyond Women @ GoTo, such as a Multicultural ERG;
- Working to integrate existing initiatives such as Tokopedia's NakaParents group into a Company-wide ERG;
- Using aggregated and anonymized data to explore whether an e-commerce sales gap exists between merchants at GoTo.

As such, we will disclose further details and progress on Company targets live on GoTo's Zero Barriers roadmap in the latter part of 2023. Below are some of the key outputs we achieved in 2022:

- Establishment of a centralized DEI function (SIDI) and cross-Company working group
- CEO endorsement of GoTo's first internal DEI Framework and 2030 Roadmap for DEI
- Commencement of GoTo's first gender pay audit, to be completed in Q2 2023
- Evidence- and data-based competitive analysis and maturity assessment of GoTo's DEI position vis-a-vis industry and global peers
- Development of a DEI communications and branding plan to capture the value attributable to GoTo and our brands
- Development of a women's leadership and mentoring program, launched on International Women's Day on 8 March 2023

# Recruitment &amp; Turnover

In 2022, our total recruitment was 3,406 new employees (of which approximately 32% are females and 7% in senior management).

At GoTo Group, like many organizations across the industry and globally, we were required to make difficult decisions regarding our Company and people. We had to reassess the structure of our organization, to meet new market demands and expectations, and to ensure we could grow sustainably to support our ecosystem in the long term. As a result of these decisions, we reduced the size of our organization, and in line with the related policy, offered impacted employees fair and supportive assistance programs. Moving forward, we will be highly strategic and focused in terms of our recruitment and growth from an employee-based perspective.
