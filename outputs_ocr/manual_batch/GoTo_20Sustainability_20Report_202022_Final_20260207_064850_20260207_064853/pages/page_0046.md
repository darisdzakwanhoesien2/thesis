Sepanjang tahun, Fungsi Sustainability bekerja sama dengan setiap unit bisnis utama yang bertanggung jawab atas operasionalisasi peta jalan Tiga Nol Perusahaan dan target terkait. Ini penting untuk memastikan bahwa kerangka, standar, dan praktik terbaik industri dan internasional relevan dan diterapkan dalam konteks realitas operasional masing-masing unit bisnis, agar dapat menghasilkan kemajuan yang kredibel, praktis, dan hemat biaya.

Throughout the year, the Sustainability Function works closely with each of the key business units responsible for the operationalization of the Company's Three Zeros roadmaps and related targets. This is essential in ensuring that industry and international frameworks, standards, and best practices are relevant and applied in the context of the operational realities of each business unit, so as to deliver progress that is credible, practical, and cost-effective.
