# SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN [2-9, 2-12, 2-13, 2-14, 2-17]

A strong governance structure and clear definition of respective roles and responsibilities are crucial to effective implementation of sustainable business practices. Indofood's Sustainability Governance Structure includes members from all four Strategic Business Groups, allowing for integration and consistency of application of sustainability management practices throughout Indofood and its subsidiaries.

The BOD heads our sustainability governance practices and has ultimate responsibility for the management of Indofood's impacts on the economy, the environment, and the communities affected by our operations. The BOD is also responsible for the continual review, evaluation, and monitoring of sustainability matters, including key programs and their progress.

Struktur tata kelola yang kuat serta peran dan tanggung jawab yang jelas dari masing-masing pihak sangat diperlukan guna mengimplementasikan praktik bisnis berkelanjutan yang efektif. Struktur Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan Indofood mencakup perwakilan dari empat Kelompok Usaha Strategis, sehingga mampu mendorong pengelolaan keberlanjutan yang terintegrasi dan konsisten di Indofood dan anak perusahaannya.

Direksi secara langsung memimpin struktur keberlanjutan Perseroan dan bertanggung jawab terhadap pengelolaan dampak Indofood dalam aspek ekonomi, lingkungan serta masyarakat yang terdampak oleh kegiatan operasional kami. Direksi juga bertanggung jawab terhadap pengawasan terus menerus, evaluasi serta pemantauan isu keberlanjutan, termasuk pelaksanaan program-program utama dan perkembangannya.
