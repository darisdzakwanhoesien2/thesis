PT INDOFOOD SUKSES MAKMUR Tbk • 2022 • Sustainability Report

Several of our offerings are formulated to cater to the nutritional needs of consumers including baby, children, pregnant, and lactating mothers, and we have eliminated trans-fat from all Agribusiness Group consumer products. Two noodles products and 25 dairy products meet the National Agency of Drug and Food Control's (Badan Pengawas Obat dan Makanan or "BPOM") guidelines on nutrition and carry the "Healthier Choice" logo. By providing healthier products in economy-sized, affordable packs, we are contributing to the national fight against malnutrition. We recognize that marketing responsibly and clearly labelling our products with information can motivate consumers to improve their dietary habits. All our products display information on nutrition and serving sizes, in accordance with BPOM labelling guidelines.

## Sourcing Ingredients Sustainably

The transparent and responsible partnerships we nurture with our suppliers enable us to maintain the quality of products. Our Responsible Supplier Guidelines ("Guidelines") reflect our commitment to ensuring our suppliers factor environmental and social considerations into their business activities, maintain high labor standards, and continuously evaluate their impact on human rights. We implement traceability across our product lines and the finished products can be traced to their raw inputs. Every oil palm seed is stamped and barcoded, and every ton of palm oil can be traced to its source.

Since 2014 we have implemented an Agricultural Monitoring System ("ARES") to enhance the traceability of potatoes and chilis. These commodities, in addition to all milk we source, can be traced back to our cooperative partners that produce them while all flour can be traced to the country of origin. Our palm oil-related products are ISPO certified with traceability back to plantation and POMs of origin. Our Sustainable Agriculture Policy requires that we align with ISPO-recommended sustainable procurement practices and promotes accountability, transparency, and fair opportunity across our value chain.

Semua produk kami mencantumkan informasi nilai gizi dan takaran saji, sesuai dengan pedoman pelabelan yang dikeluarkan oleh BPOM.

## Mengelola Pasokan Bahan Baku secara Berkelanjutan

Kemitraan yang transparan dan bertanggung jawab yang kami bangun bersama para pemasok membantu kami dalam mempertahankan mutu produk. Pedoman Pemasok yang Bertanggung Jawab ("Pedoman") mencerminkan komitmen kami dalam memastikan bahwa para pemasok turut mempertimbangkan aspek lingkungan dan sosial dalam kegiatan usaha mereka, mempertahankan standar pekerja yang tinggi, serta terus melakukan evaluasi dampak mereka terhadap penegakan hak asasi manusia. Kami menerapkan ketertelusuran pada lini produksi sehingga produk-produk kami dapat ditelusuri hingga ke bahan bakunya. Setiap bibit sawit diberi tanda dan barcode, dan setiap ton minyak sawit dapat ditelusuri hingga ke sumbernya.

Sejak tahun 2014, kami telah menerapkan Sistem Pemantauan Pertanian (Agricultural Monitoring System atau "ARES") untuk meningkatkan ketertelusuran kentang dan cabai. Sama seperti produk susu yang kami hasilkan, komoditas cabai dan kentang juga dapat ditelusuri ke mitra koperasi kami yang memproduksinya, sedangkan semua produk berbahan dasar tepung terigu dapat ditelusuri sampai ke negara asalnya. Produk-produk yang bersumber dari minyak sawit telah memperoleh sertifikat dari ISPO yang dapat ditelusuri hingga ke perkebunan dan PKS asal. Kebijakan Agrikultur Berkelanjutan kami mewajibkan kami untuk memenuhi praktik-praktik pengadaan berkelanjutan, mendorong akuntabilitas, transparansi dan kesempatan yang adil di sepanjang rantai nilai, sesuai rekomendasi ISPO.
