# Indofood Integrated Total Quality Control Management

## Manajemen Kendali Mutu Terpadu Indofood

### Research &amp; Development

**Penelitian &amp; Pengembangan**

Only the following are used:
Kami hanya menggunakan:
- Ingredients, technologies, and processes that are safe, high quality, and Halal
- Ingredients and food additives permitted under Government food regulations
- Appropriate packaging that preserves product quality and safety, and meets food safety regulations and standards
- Bahan baku, teknologi dan proses pengolahan yang aman, bermutu tinggi serta Halal
- Bahan baku dan bahan tambahan pangan yang diizinkan berdasarkan peraturan pangan Pemerintah
- Kemasan yang baik sehingga dapat menjaga mutu dan keamanan produk selama masa simpannya, serta memenuhi peraturan dan standar keamanan pangan

### Material Sourcing

**Pengolahan**
**Pasokan**

![img-97.jpeg](img-97.jpeg)

- All suppliers and partners must meet the highest standards of food safety and quality
- Supplier audits performed in accordance with GMP, Halal, and Indofood food safety and quality standards
- Semua pemasok dan mitra harus memenuhi standar tertinggi keamanan pangan dan mutu
- Pelaksanaan audit terhadap pemasok mengenai GMP, Halal, serta standar keamanan pangan dan mutu Indofood

### Manufacturing Process

**Metode**
**Manufaktur**

- Dedicated Quality Assurance and Quality Control teams to ensure food safety at incoming materials, production, and warehousing stages
- Our production facilities implement international Food Safety or Quality Management System principles
- Regular testing of product safety and quality performed by experienced laboratory team. Our seven laboratories are ISO 17025 certified
- Annual internal and external audits conducted to ensure compliance with food safety policies and standards
- Tim khusus Penjaminan Mutu dan Pengendalian Mutu ditunjuk guna memastikan keamanan pangan saat bahan baku diterima, selama proses produksi serta penyimpanan di gudang
- Fasilitas produksi kami menerapkan prinsip-prinsip Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Pangan atau Mutu Internasional
- Pengujian terhadap keamanan pangan dan mutu produk dilakukan secara berkala oleh tim laboratorium yang berpengalaman. Tujuh laboratorium kami telah tersertifikasi ISO 17025
- Pelaksanaan audit internal dan eksternal tahunan guna memastikan kepatuhan terhadap kebijakan dan standar keamanan pangan
