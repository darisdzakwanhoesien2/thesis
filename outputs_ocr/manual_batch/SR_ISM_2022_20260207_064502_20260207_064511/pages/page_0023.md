## Assessment

### Asesmen

Social, Environmental, and Human Rights Materiality

- Indofood and its stakeholders assess how significant the impact of its materiality issues is on themselves and Indofood's sustainability performance

Materialitas terkait aspek Sosial, Lingkungan dan Hak Asasi Manusia
