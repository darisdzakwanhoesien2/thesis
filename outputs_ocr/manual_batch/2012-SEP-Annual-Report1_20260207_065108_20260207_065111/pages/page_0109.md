## b. Standards in issue not yet effective

Effective for periods beginning on or after January 1, 2013 is PSAK 38 (revised 2012), Business Combination Under Common Control and Amendment to Financial Accounting Standard (PSAK) 60: Financial Instrument Disclosure.

As of the issuance date of the consolidated financial statements, management is evaluating the effect of these standards on the consolidated financial statements.
