## b. Facility 2

Based on Credit Agreement No. 144 dated April 25 2013, Bank UOB provided the investment credit facility for the purchase of the Company's office building with maximum amount of Rp 52,850,000,000, payable in installments over a period of 120 months or 10 years. Such loan was used to settle the purchase of the Company's new office building at DBS Tower 18h floor, Prof. Dr. Satrio street, South Jakarta.
