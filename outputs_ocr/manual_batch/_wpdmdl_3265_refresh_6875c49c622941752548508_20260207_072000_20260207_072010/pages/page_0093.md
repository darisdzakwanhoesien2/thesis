# PRODUKSI DAN KINERJA EKONOMI 2022

BRMS meraih kinerja ekonomi positif selama tahun 2022 salah satunya adalah pendapatan yang diraih sebesar USD 11,644,445. Pendapatan ini mengalami kenaikan sebesar 10.08% dari tahun sebelumnya yang tercatat sebesar USD 10,578,248. Kenaikan pendapatan ini tentunya sejalan dengan kenaikan produksi emas pada tahun 2022 sebesar 174 kg atau mengalami kenaikan 25.2% dari tahun sebelumnya sebesar 139 kg. Lebih lanjut BRMS juga berhasil mencetak pertumbuhan signifikan sebesar 23% pada pos laba usaha menjadi USD 1.063.303 di tahun 2022, dibandingkan pada tahun 2021 yakni sebesar USD 862.976 juta.

Pencapaian kinerja ekonomi ini tidak lepas dari usaha keras yang dilakukan oleh unit usaha dan dukungan dari para pemangku kepentingan. Unit usaha BRMS memproduksi beberapa jenis mineral yang berbeda. PT Citra Palu Minerals (CPM) melaksanakan eksplorasi, konstruksi dan produksi emas di wilayah seluas 85.180 hektar di wilayah Sulawesi Tengah dan Sulawesi Selatan. Sementara itu, PT Dairi Prima Mineral (DPM) beroperasi di area konsesi seluas 24.636 hektar di Sumatera Utara dan mengelola deposit seng, timah hitam, dan perak. Ketiga, PT Gorontalo Minerals (GM) memegang hak konsesi Kontrak Karya untuk pertambangan seluas 24.995 hektar yang berlokasi di Kabupaten Bone Bolango, Provinsi Gorontalo untuk memproduksi tembaga, emas, dan perak. Keempat, PT Linge Mineral Resources (LMR) yang memiliki Izin Usaha Pertambangan (IUP) dengan konsesi pertambangan seluas 36.420 hektar di Linge, Provinsi Aceh. Terakhir adalah PT Suma Heksa Sinergi (SHS) merupakan tambang emas Kerta yang memiliki Izin Usaha Pertambangan (IUP) dengan konsesi seluas 7.291 hektar di lokasi Lebak, Provinsi Banten.

# Production And Economic Performance 2022

BRMS achieved positive economic performance during 2022, one of which was the revenue it earned USD 11,644,445. This income increased by 10.08% from the previous year which was recorded at USD 10,578,248. This increase in revenue is of course in line with the increase in gold production in 2022 of 174 kg or an increase of 25.2% from the previous year of 139 kg. Furthermore, BRMS also managed to record a significant growth of 23% in operating profit to USD 1,063,303 in 2022, compared to USD 862,976 million in 2021.

The achievement of the economic performance cannot be separated from the hard efforts made by the business unit and support from stakeholders. The BRMS business unit produces several different types of minerals. PT Citra Palu Minerals (CPM) carries out exploration, construction and production of gold in an area of 85,180 hectares in Central Sulawesi and South Sulawesi. Meanwhile, PT Dairi Prima Mineral (DPM) operates in a concession area of 24,636 hectares in North Sumatra and manages deposits of zinc, lead and silver. Third, PT Gorontalo Minerals (GM) holds a Contract of Work concession right for a mining area of 24,995 hectares located in Bone Bolango Regency, Gorontalo Province to produce copper, gold and silver. Fourth, PT Linge Mineral Resources (LMR) which has a Mining Business Permit (IUP) with a mining concession area of 36,420 hectares in Linge, Aceh Province. Finally, PT Suma Heksa Sinergi (SHS) is a Kerta gold mine that has a Mining Business Permit (IUP) with a concession area of 7,291 hectares in the Lebak location, Banten Province.

In encouraging growth in economic performance, in 2022 we have successfully completed the second gold factory in Palu which has a daily production of 4,000 tons of gold ore (tpd). Our target in 2023 is for the 4,000 tpd factory, which was completed in October 2022, to be fully operational so that the total operating plants in 2023 will be 4,500 tpd. Furthermore, the target for 2024 is to complete the construction of a third gold processing plant in Palu with a production capacity of 4,000 tons of gold
