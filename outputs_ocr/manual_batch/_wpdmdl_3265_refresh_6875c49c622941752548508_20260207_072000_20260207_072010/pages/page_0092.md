# Manajemen Dampak

Perusahaan telah memiliki Sistem Pengendalian Internal yang mengadopsi Standard IIA (Institute of Internal Auditors) and COSO Framework. Pelaksanaan pengendalian internal antara lain dilakukan melalui pengendalian keuangan, pengendalian operasional, kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, dan informasi pasar. Untuk memastikan efektivitas sistem pengendalian internal, Kami melibatkan lintas departemen dan komponen Perusahaan yaitu Dewan Komisaris, Komite Audit, Direksi, Departemen Audit Internal (DAI), Unit Kerja, Unit Kerja penerbit kebijakan dan/atau prosedur, pejabat dan karyawan, serta pihak eksternal.

Di samping itu, Perusahaan telah menerbitkan beberapa Standard Operational Procedure (SOP) sebagai pelengkap dan penjabaran atas Pedoman Perilaku di antaranya adalah SOP Anti Tindak Kecurangan, SOP Penerimaan dan Pelaporan Gratifikasi, dan SOP Speak-Up System (Whistleblowing).

# Management of Impact

The company has an Internal Control System that adopts the Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA) Standard and COSO Framework. Our internal control is carried out through financial control, operational control, compliance with laws and regulations, and market information, among others. To ensure the effectiveness of our internal control system, we involve cross departments and components of the Company, namely the Board of Commissioners, Audit Committee, Board of Directors, Internal Audit Department (DAI), Work Units, Work Units issuing policies and/or procedures, officials and employees, and external parties.

In addition, the Company has issued several Standard Operational Procedures (SOPs) as a complement and elaboration of our Code of Conduct, including SOPs for Anti-Fraud, SOPs for Receiving and Reporting Gifts, and SOPs for our Speak-Up (Whistleblowing) System.
