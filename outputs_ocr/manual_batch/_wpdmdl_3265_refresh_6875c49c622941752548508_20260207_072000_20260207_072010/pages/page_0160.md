# OHS Performance

Compilation of reports of incidents/accidents and work-related diseases is an important tool for us to ensure that there is an evaluation for each incident, remediation, and ensuring that there is a risk control for the readiness and fitness of workers; environmental risk control around the work site; and risk control from equipment and vehicles used.

We are proud that throughout 2022, BRMS and business units have succeeded in maintaining the target of occupational health and safety, i.e. to be free from mining accidents resulting in fatalities, hazard incidents, occupational diseases and labor disease incidents.
