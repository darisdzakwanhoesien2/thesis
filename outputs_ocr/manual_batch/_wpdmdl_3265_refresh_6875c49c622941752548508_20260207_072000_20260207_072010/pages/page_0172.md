Dengan seiring adanya perhatian para pemangku kepentingan terkait transisi energi, BRMS telah memulai perjalan ini dengan mendikarbonisasi sumber energi tak terbarukan menjadi terbarukan dan ramah lingkungan. # Energy Consumption

Energy is our primary source for the Company's operational activities. With the increasing demand from stakeholders towards energy transition, BRMS has started this journey by de-carbonizing nonrenewable energy sources to be renewable and environmentally friendly. Several BRMS business units have started using B30 biodiesel fuel derived from a mixture of 30% biofuel and 70% diesel for operational vehicles and everyday vehicles such as dumptrucks, articulated dumptrucks, manhauls and light vehicles.

To gauge the achievement of the renewable energy usage and energy consumption as a whole, we always calculate the total energy consumption covering all business units. The calculated energy includes the use of gasoline, diesel, biodiesel and electricity expressed in GigaJoule (GJ) energy units based on the energy unit conversion table.

Throughout 2022 there has been an increase in energy consumption amounted to 78,870.80 GJ compared to the previous year which was only 45,018.41 GJ. This increase in energy consumption was due to the development of the second gold factory project in Palu, which has a production capacity of 4,000 tons of gold ore per day.

In terms of consumption of environmentally friendly fuels/materials, BRMS and business units have used 41,363 GJ of Biosolar B30 throughout 2022 or an increase from 2021 which only consumed around 18,511 GJ of Biosolar.
