# Pencapaian Keberlanjutan 2022

Kami menyadari bahwa aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (ESG-Environment, Social, and Governance) menjadi semakin penting untuk mewujudkan keberlanjutan. Di sepanjang tahun 2022, kami terus berupaya untuk meningkatkan kinerja ESG dengan menerapkan praktik penambangan yang baik dan benar, memastikan keselamatan dan kesejahteraan pekerja dan masyarakat, serta menjamin kelestarian lingkungan melalui insiatif-inisiatif hijau.

Kami telah membentuk strategi keberlanjutan yang tertuang dalam BRM Sustainability Framework. Kerangka Keberlanjutan ini bertujuan mencapai target dalam jangka waktu 5 tahun untuk Zero Fatalities, Zero Serious Injuries, and Zero Serious Environmental Incidents; menyediakan dampak positif bagi masyarakat melalui program berkelanjutan; mencapai 25% keterwakilan perempuan dalam Perusahaan di tahun 2030; menargetkan 90% penggunaan kembali air dan 10% penggunaan air bersih; Net Zero Emission 2050; dan mematuhi Global Industry on Tailing Management (GISTM) melalui pembuatan dry tailing.

# Lingkungan Hidup

Sebagai Perusahaan yang sangat erat kaitannya dengan pemanfaatan sumber daya alam, BRMS melalui unit usaha yaitu CPM, GM, DPM, LMR dan SHS, terus berupaya mewujudkan perannya dalam pengelolaan lingkungan hidup yang berkelanjutan melalui serangkaian upaya pengelolaan lingkungan hidup yang visioner dan inovatif mulai dari penciptaan lingkungan yang mendukung keanekaragaman hayati dan dekarbonisasi, efisiensi penggunaan energi dari bahan bakar tak terbarukan, dan manjemen limbah yang baik dan benar.

To address the risks of climate change, we have mapped the scope of greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and have made progress in using environmentally friendly energy, making efficient use of non-renewable energy, and rehabilitating former drilling areas.

# Sustainability Achievement 2022

We realize that environmental, social and governance (ESG) aspects are becoming increasingly important for achieving sustainability. Throughout 2022, we strived to improve our ESG performance by implementing good and acceptable mining practices, ensuring the safety and welfare of our workers and the community, and ensuring environmental sustainability through green initiatives.

We have developed a sustainability strategy and it is crystalized in the BRMS Sustainability Framework, which aims to achieve these targets within five years: Zero Fatalities, Zero Serious Injuries, and Zero Serious Environmental Incidents while providing positive community impact through sustainable programs; achieve 25% female representation in the Company by 2030; targeting 90% water reuse/recycle and 10% fresh water consumption; Net Zero Emissions 2050, and comply with the Global Industry on Tailings Management (GISTM) by producing dry tailings.

# Environment

As our Company relies on natural resources, BRMS through its business units, CPM, GM, DPM, LMR and SHS, realizes its role in sustainable environmental management through a series of visionary and innovative environmental management efforts such as creating a sustainable environment that underpins biodiversity and decarbonization, the efficient use of energy from non-renewable fuels, and good waste management.
