Dalam upaya mempermudah mekenisme partisipasi, konsultasi dan komunikasi antara karyawan/kontraktor/tamu dengan perusahaan, PT GM dan PT CPM telah memiliki prosedur khusus dengan nomor prosedur GM-HSE-PR-008-Communication, Consultation and Participation dan CPM-HSE3-PR-3.03 Prosedur Komunikasi, Konsultasi, Partisipasi dan Motivasi Keselamatan Pertambangan, Pengamanan dan Lingkungan serta GM-HSE-PR-011-Non Conformance, Incident and Management Action, maka karyawan/tamu/kontraktor dapat menyampaikan keluhan, temuan, ataupun berkonsultasi terkait keselamatan pertambangan kepada Departemen HSE dan Komite Keselamatan Pertambangan dan Lingkungan.

PT GM juga telah mengakomodasikan formulir HIAS (Hazard Information and Suggestion) yang diletakkan pada lokasi-lokasi strategis. BRM dan Anak Usahanya selalu mendukung keterlibatan semua pihak dalam proses perbaikan berkelanjutan tertutama yang terkait dengan keselamatan dan lingkungan pertambangan. Selama tahun 2022, tidak ada temuan ataupun aduan yang diterima oleh Departemen HSE.

In an effort to facilitate participation, consultation and communication between employees, contractors, guests and the Company, PT GM and PT CPM put special procedures i.e. GM-HSE-PR-008-Communication, Consultation and Participation and CPM-HSE3-PR-3.03 Procedures for Communication, Consultation, Participation and Motivation for Mining Safety, Security and Environment as well as GM-HSE-PR-011-Non Conformance, Incident and Management Action, employees/guests/contractors can submit complaints, findings, or consult related to mining safety to HSE Department and Environmental and Mining Safety Committee.

PT GM has also provided HIAS (Hazard Information and Suggestion) forms which are placed in strategic locations. BRM and its subsidiaries always support the involvement of all parties in the process of continuous improvement, especially those related to mining safety and the environment. During 2022, no findings or complaints were received by the HSE Department.
