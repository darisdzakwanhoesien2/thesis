# Tingkat Pergantian Karyawan

Kami berhasil menjaga tingkat pergantian (turnover) karyawan di angka yang rendah. Sepanjang tahun 2022, sebanyak 23 karyawan BRMS maupun unit usaha yang mengakhiri hubungan kerja. Di tingkat kantor pusat, terdapat 5 karyawan yang mengakhiri hubungan kerja. Sementara itu, tingkat turnover karyawan pada unit usaha yakni CPM 3,8% GM 2,4% dan DPM 3,5%. Rendahnya tingkat turnover karyawan ini, menunjukkan bahwa BRMS mampu menciptakan lingkungan yang aman dan nyaman bagi karyawan. # Employee Turnover Rate

We managed to keep the employee turnover rate at a low level. Throughout 2022, 23 BRMS employees and business units were laid off. At the head office level, there were 5 employees who ended their employment relationship with the company. Meanwhile, employee turnover rates in business units were CPM 3.8% GM 2,4% dan DPM 3,5%. This low employee turnover rate showed that BRMS was able to create a safe and comfortable environment for employees.
