# KEBIJAKAN DAN PROSEDUR REMUNERASI (2-19) (2-20)

Remunerasi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan ditetapkan berdasarkan keputusan RUPS Tahunan mempertimbangkan hasil usaha Perseroan pada tahun buku sebelumnya, kondisi keuangan Perseroan, rencana pengembangan usaha, serta faktor-faktor ekonomi dan industri yang relevan. RUPS dapat melimpahkan kuasa dan kewenangan kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk menetapkan besaran honorarium dan/atau tunjangan lainnya bagi seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi sesuai kedudukan, peran, tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenang anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris dengan memperhatikan rekomendasi dari Komite NR. (2-19)

Dalam hal prosedur, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi menjalankan fungsi remunerasi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaries melalui langkah-langkah berikut: (2-20)

a. Mengawasi dan mengevaluasi sistem remunerasi agar dapat menetapkan struktur remunerasi yang transparan, adil dan bersaing dengan pasar sesuai industrinya.
b. Mengevaluasi sistem dan struktur remunerasi paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 1 (satu) tahun.
c. Menyusun kajian usulan perbaikan remunerasi dan besaran tantiem sesuai perkembangan Perseroan.
d. Menyusun penjelasan lengkap kepada RUPS mengenai sistem penggajian, fasilitas dan/atau tunjangan bagi Direksi serta rincian mengenai gaji, fasilitas, dan/atau tunjangan yang diterima Direksi yang sedang menjabat.

# Remuneration Policy And Procedure

Remuneration of the Company's Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners is determined based on resolution of Annual GMS taking into account the results of the Company's operations in the previous financial year, the Company's financial condition, business development plans, as well as relevant economic and industrial factors. GMS may delegate power and authority to Board of Commissioners to determine the amount of honorarium and/or other allowances for all members of Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors according to the positions, roles, duties, responsibilities and authorities of members of Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners by taking into account recommendations of NR Committee.

In terms procedure, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee carries out the following remuneration steps for the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners:

a. Supervise and evaluate remuneration system in order to establish a remuneration structure that is transparent, fair and competitive with the market according to the industry.
b. Evaluate remuneration system and structure at least 1 (one) time in 1 (one) year.
c. Prepare a review of the proposed improvement of remuneration and the amount of tantiem in accordance with the Company's growth.
d. Propose to GMS regarding system for providing service fees and post-service allowances for Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.
