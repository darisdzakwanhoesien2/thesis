# Respon atas Tantangan Keberlanjutan

Keberhasilan mempertahankan dan memperkuat kinerja ekonomi, lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola di tahun 2022 tentunya tidak terlepas dengan adanya tantangan.

Tantangan utama yang kami hadapi di tahun 2022 adalah memastikan keberlangsungan operasional di tengah pandemi Covid-19 yang masih membayangi dan pelemahan ekonomi global sebagai dampak lanjutan dari ketegangan geopolitik antara Rusia dan Ukraina. Kedua peristiwa tersebut memengaruhi kenaikan harga minyak dan gas yang mengakibatkan naiknya biaya transportasi dan rantai distribusi produk serta kelangkaan rantai pasok. Di lain sisi, pandemi Covid-19 masih memberikan risiko dan dampak terutama pada kesehatan dan keselamatan pekerja juga terhambatnya kegiatan ekspor-impor alat-alat produksi pertambangan.

Lebih lanjut, kami melihat bahwa perubahan iklim memberikan tantangan nyata bagi keberlanjutan bisnis kami ke depan. Risiko langsung dan tidak langsung dari perubahan iklim adalah curah hujan dengan intensitas yang sulit diprediksi, banjir, gelombang panas dan kekeringan yang memengaruhi kegiatan operasional dan kesehatan dan keselamatan para pekerja.

Kami bersyukur dapat mengatasi tantangan-tantangan ini dengan strategi-strategi preventif dan kuratif. Keberhasilan kami merupakan buah hasil kerja sama dan koordinasi erat dari unit bisnis kami, PT Citra Palu Minerals ("CPM"), PT Gorontalo Minerals ("GM"), PT Dairi Prima Mineral ("DPM"), PT Linge Mineral Resources ("LMR") dan PT Suma Heksa Sinergi ("SHS"). Untuk melindungi pekerja kami dari paparan Covid-19, sampai saat ini kami menerapkan kebijakan protokol kesehatan seperti tetap memakai masker pada area kerja, penyediaan hand sanitizer, dan menjaga jarak. Selain itu, kami telah melakukan vaksinasi kepada hampir seluruh karyawan dan juga keluarganya serta swab PCR secara berkesinambungan.

# Response to Sustainability Challenges

The success of maintaining and strengthening economic, environmental, social and governance performance in 2022 is indeed inseparable from challenges.

The main challenge we faced in 2022 was how to ensure operational continuity amidst a still looming Covid-19 pandemic and the weakening global economy as a result of the geopolitical tensions between Russia and Ukraine. Both of these events caused an increase in oil and gas prices which resulted in increased transportation costs and product distribution chains as well as supply chain scarcity. In addition, the Covid-19 pandemic still posed risks and impacts, especially on the health and safety of our workers as well as hampering the export-import activities of mining production equipment.

Furthermore, we acknowledge that climate change poses a real challenge to the sustainability of our business going forward. The direct and indirect risks from climate change include unpredictable rainfall, floods, heat waves and droughts which affect operational activities and the health and safety of our workers.

We are grateful to be able to overcome these challenges with preventive and curative strategies. Our success is the result of the close cooperation and coordination of our business units, PT Citra Palu Minerals ("CPM"), PT Gorontalo Minerals ("GM"), PT Dairi Prima Mineral ("DPM"), PT Linge Mineral Resources ("LMR") and PT Suma Heksa Sinergi ("SHS"). To protect our workers from Covid-19, we have implemented health protocol policies such as continuing to wear masks in the working areas, providing hand sanitizers, and maintaining social distance. In addition, we carried out vaccinations for almost all our employees and their families as well as PCR swabs on an ongoing basis.
