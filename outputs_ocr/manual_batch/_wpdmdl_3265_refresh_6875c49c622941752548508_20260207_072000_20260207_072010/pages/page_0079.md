# 6. Risiko Legalitas - Perizinan dan Persetujuan

Risiko yang dihadapi oleh Perseroan salah satunya terkait dengan masalah tumpang tindih lahan (overlapping) baik dengan entitas badan hukum, perorangan dan/atau masyarakat setempat. Untuk menangani masalah ini secara formal, Perseroan melakukan upaya hukum untuk tetap mempertahankan wilayah atau konsensinya baik melalui Pengadilan Negeri dan/atau Pengadilan Tata Usaha Negara.

Selain itu, ketentuan Kontrak Karya, izin eksplorasi dan izin kehutanan mengharuskan Perseroan untuk menghadapi berbagai risiko termasuk antara lain:

- Jika Perusahaan gagal untuk memenuhi kewajibannya berdasarkan Kontrak Karya, izin eksplorasi dan izin kehutanan, pemerintah dapat menghentikan Kontrak Karya dan izin eksplorasi tersebut. Hak untuk mengeksplorasi dan menambang di area konsesi tergantung pada berlakunya masa Kontrak Karya, izin eksplorasi dan izin kehutanan tersebut.
- Semua Kontrak Karya, izin eksplorasi dan kehutanan mempunyai tanggal kadaluwarsa, namun kontrak/izin tersebut dapat dihentukan oleh pemerintah sebelum tanggal kadaluwarsa jika Perseroan tidak dapat memenuhi kewajiban ini meliputi pembayan royalti dan pajak kepada pemerintah dan pemenuhan ketentuan-ketentuan tertentu dalam pertambangan, lingkungan hidup, keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja.

Ultimately, this may cause the closure of the mine and loss of expected revenue.

Among the risk mitigation programs that have been carried out by the Company include conducting the necessary studies, compiling standard operating procedures (SOP) for an activity and conducting reviews on its implementation, periodic training related to HSE and the use of operational tools, carrying out integrated community development activities, and monitoring compliance with regulations strictly, as well as improving coordination between operations departments.

# 6. Legal Risk - Permits and Approvals

One of the risks faced by the Company is related to overlapping land ownership with other legal entities, individuals and/or local communities. To handle this matter formally, the Company makes every effort to safeguard its areas or concessions either through the District Court and/or the Administrative Court.

In addition, the Company's terms of Contracts of Work, exploration permits and forestry permits present numerous risks, including:

- Should the Company fail to fulfill its obligation under a Contract of Work, exploration permits or forestry permits, the Government may terminate the respective Contract of Work or exploration permit. The right to explore and mine in the concession area depends on such contracts and permits.
- All Contracts of Work, exploration permits and forestry permits of the Company have expiration dates, but such contracts/ permits may be terminated by the government prior to their expiry dates should the Company be unable to fulfill its contractual obligations. These obligations include royalties and taxes paid to the government and fulfillment of certain provisions in the areas of mining, the environment, as well as occupational health and safety.
