At GM, the business unit uses water for office necessities at the Leppin and Staging Tombulito site offices as well as water for camp necessities at the Sungai Mak and Tombulilato Sites. At this early stage of construction, clean water is only needed to meet domestic and office needs with an amount of around 900,000 liters per year. The water needs for the Leppin site office are met by the Regional Drinking Water Company (PDAM) of the Gorontalo Province with total consumption in 2022 was 952,000 liters. As for the needs at Staging Tombulilato, Mess Tombulito and the site are met from springs and surrounding groundwater which is then channeled through pipes to the camp in very small quantities because operational activities are only in the form of daily monitoring and general housekeeping.
