The Minutes of the Meeting is stated in the Deed of Minutes of the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders of PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk number 07 dated June 19, 2024, made by RM Dendy Soebangil, S.H., M.Kn., Notary in South Jakarta.
