# Erajaya Active Lifestyle

Fokus pada produk gaya hidup seperti sport fashion apparel untuk olahraga lari, tenis, golf, dan aktivitas luar ruang seperti hiking/lintas alam, yang memadukan fungsi dan estetika dalam produk sportswear dan sneakers, menjadikan kegiatan olahraga sebagai bagian dari fashion statement sehari-hari (tren athleisure) melalui multibrand seperti Urban Republic, JD Sports, MST Golf, Urban Adventure maupun monobrand seperti Asics, DJI, Garmin, 6IXTY8IGHT, dan merek lainnya, serta smart EV.

Focus on lifestyle products such as sport fashion apparel for running, tennis, golf, and outdoor activities such as hiking/cross-country, which combines function and aesthetics in sportswear and sneakers, making sports activities part of everyday fashion statement (athleisure trend) through multibrands such as Urban Republic, JD Sports, MST Golf, Urban Adventure as well as monobrands such as Asics, DJI, Garmin, 6IXTY8IGHT, and other brands, and smart EVs.
