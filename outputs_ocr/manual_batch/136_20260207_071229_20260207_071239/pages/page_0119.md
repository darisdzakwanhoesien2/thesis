Di awal tahun 2024, BI memutuskan untuk mempertahankan suku bunga acuan BI 7-day Reverse Repo Rate (BI-Rate) pada level 6% yang sudah berlangsung sejak Oktober 2023. Keputusan ini bertujuan untuk membantu stabilisasi nilai rupiah, mengendalikan inflasi, dan membantu pertumbuhan ekonomi.

Pada bulan April 2024, BI menaikkan BI-Rate menjadi 6,25% yang bertahan sampai Agustus dan sejak September kembali ke level 6% hingga akhir tahun. Keputusan ini konsisten dengan tetap rendahnya inflasi yang terkendali di bawah target, serta penguatan dan stabilitas nilai tukar rupiah.

Secara rata-rata tahunan, BI-Rate tahun 2024 sebesar 6,03%, lebih tinggi dibandingkan tahun 2023 sebesar 5,81%.

At the beginning of 2024, BI decided to maintain its benchmark interest rate, the BI 7-Day Reverse Repo Rate (BI-Rate), at 6%, a level that had been held since October 2023. This decision aimed to support the stabilization of the rupiah exchange rate, control inflation, and foster economic growth.

In April 2024, BI raised the BI-Rate to 6.25%, a level maintained until August. Starting in September, the rate was lowered back to 6% and remained unchanged until the end of the year. This policy stance was consistent with the continued low inflation, which remained under control and below the target, as well as the strengthening and stabilization of the rupiah exchange rate.

On an annual average basis, the BI-Rate in 2024 stood at 6.03%, higher than the 2023 average of 5.81%.
