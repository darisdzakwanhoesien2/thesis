# Tinjauan Industri
INDUSTRY REVIEW

## INDUSTRI FOOD &amp; NOURISHMENT

Industri makanan dan minuman (mamin) menunjukkan pertumbuhan yang solid. Gabungan Produsen Makanan dan Minuman Indonesia (GAPMMI) memproyeksikan pertumbuhan sektor ini mencapai 5,5% sepanjang tahun, lebih tinggi dibandingkan pertumbuhan 4,47% pada 2023. Kontribusi industri mamin terhadap Produk Domestik Bruto (PDB) industri pengolahan nonmigas juga signifikan, mencapai 40,17% pada triwulan III 2024.

Sektor restoran dan kafe juga mengalami perkembangan positif, didorong oleh meningkatnya kelas menengah dan perubahan gaya hidup masyarakat. Tren bisnis kuliner seperti makanan sehat, organik, dan berbasis tanaman semakin diminati konsumen yang peduli kesehatan.

Sektor ritel modern, termasuk supermarket sekelas Grand Lucky Superstore, mengalami pertumbuhan yang signifikan. Omzet ritel modern tumbuh sekitar 10%, dengan nilai penjualan mencapai Rp162,8 triliun. Pertumbuhan ini didorong oleh meningkatnya jumlah gerai ritel modern, seperti minimarket dan supermarket, yang terus berkembang untuk memenuhi permintaan konsumen yang semakin tinggi.

## FOOD &amp; NOURISHMENT INDUSTRY

The food and beverage (F&amp;B) industry in Indonesia demonstrated solid growth. The Indonesian Food and Beverage Producers Association (GAPMMI) projected the sector to grow by 5.5% throughout the year, a rise from 4.47% in 2023. The F&amp;B industry also contributed significantly to the Gross Domestic Product (GDP) of the non-oil and gas processing industry, reaching 40.17% in the third quarter of 2024.

The restaurant and café sector likewise experienced positive development, driven by a growing middle class and shifting lifestyle trends. Culinary business trends such as healthy, organic, and plant-based foods have gained popularity among health-conscious consumers.

The modern retail sector, including premium supermarkets such as Grand Lucky Superstore, also saw substantial growth. Modern retail revenues rose by approximately 10%, with total sales reaching Rp162.8 trillion. This growth was supported by the increasing number of modern retail outlets, such as minimarkets and supermarkets, expanding to meet the rising demand from consumers.
