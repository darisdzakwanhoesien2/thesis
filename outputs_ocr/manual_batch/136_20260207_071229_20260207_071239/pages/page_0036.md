# PERUBAHAN KOMPOSISI DEWAN KOMISARIS

Pada tahun 2024 tidak ada perubahan komposisi Dewan Komisaris Perseroan. Namun, sebelum batas waktu penyampaian Laporan Tahunan 2024 ini, komposisi Dewan Komisaris Perseroan mengalami perubahan berdasarkan Keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa (RUPSLB) tanggal 19 Maret 2025. RUPSLB menyetujui menerima pengunduran diri Bapak Ardy Hady Wijaya selaku Komisaris Utama Perseroan, Ibu Elly, Ibu Mitchella Ardy Hady Wijaya, dan Bapak Keith Ardy Hady Wijaya masing-masing selaku Direktur Perseroan efektif terhitung sejak tanggal 31 Januari 2025, dan menyetujui untuk mengangkat Bapak Alexander Halim Kusuma sebagai Komisaris Utama Perseroan.

# APRESIASI

Dewan Komisaris menyampaikan apresiasi kepada Direksi, jajaran manajemen, dan seluruh insan Perseroan dan entitas anak, atas kerja keras, dedikasi, dan integritas yang telah ditunjukkan sepanjang tahun 2024. Semoga Perseroan dapat terus tumbuh dan berkembang serta memberikan nilai tambah dan manfaat yang lebih besar bagi para pemegang saham, karyawan, pelanggan, mitra usaha, dan seluruh pemangku kepentingan di masa mendatang.

# CHANGES IN THE COMPOSITION OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

In 2024, there were no changes in the composition of the Board of Commissioners. However, prior to the submission of this 2024 Annual Report, changes occurred based on the resolution of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (EGMS) held on March 19, 2025. The EGMS approved the resignation of Mr. Ardy Hady Wijaya as President Commissioner, and the resignations of Ms. Elly, Ms. Mitchella Ardy Hady Wijaya, and Mr. Keith Ardy Hady Wijaya as Directors of the Company, effective January 31, 2025. The EGMS also approved the appointment of Mr. Alexander Halim Kusuma as the new President Commissioner.

# APPRECIATION

The Board of Commissioners extends its deepest appreciation to the Board of Directors, management, and all employees of the Company and its subsidiaries for their hard work, dedication, and integrity throughout 2024. We hope the Company will continue to grow and thrive, delivering greater value and benefits to shareholders, employees, customers, business partners, and all stakeholders in the years ahead.
