# Tinjauan Makroekonomi
MACROECONOMIC REVIEW

![img-140.jpeg](img-140.jpeg)

"Tahun 2024 ditandai dengan ketahanan ekonomi Indonesia yang tetap terjaga di tengah dinamika global. Pertumbuhan ekonomi nasional yang stabil dan pelaksanaan Pemilu 2024 yang berlangsung aman dan kondusif turut menciptakan sentimen positif bagi dunia usaha, mendorong kepercayaan investor dan pelaku industri."

"The year 2024 was marked by Indonesia's continued economic resilience amid global dynamics. Stable national economic growth and the peaceful, orderly execution of the 2024 General Election helped create a positive sentiment for the business environment, boosting investor and industry confidence."

Pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2024, sedikit melambat dibandingkan capaian tahun 2023 sebesar 5,05%.

Indonesia's economic growth in 2024, slightly lower than 5.05% recorded in 2023.

Annual inflation rate at the end of 2024, lower from 2.61% at the end of 2023.
