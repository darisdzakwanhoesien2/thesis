# Program Guru Merdeka Mengajar
Guru Merdeka Mengajar Program

![img-262.jpeg](img-262.jpeg)

Program yang bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kapasitas dan kualitas tenaga pendidik di SMK. Dirancang untuk memberikan akses kepada guru-guru SMK terhadap pelatihan industri, sertifikasi, serta pengalaman langsung di dunia usaha dan dunia industri (DUDI) seperti Erajaya Group. Kegiatan ini diadaptasi dari program magang siswa yang sudah berlangsung sejak 2021 dengan total peserta mencapai 320 siswa di beberapa bisnis unit erajaya seperti Erafone, Paris Baguette, Wellings.

This program aims to enhance the capacity and quality of vocational high school (SMK) educators. It is designed to provide SMK teachers with access to industry training, certification, and hands-on experience in the business and industrial world (DUDI), including within the Erajaya Group. The initiative is adapted from the student internship program that has been running since 2021, which has involved a total of 320 students across various Erajaya business units such as Erafone, Paris Baguette, and Wellings.
