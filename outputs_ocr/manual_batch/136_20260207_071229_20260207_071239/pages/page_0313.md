# PERESMIAN E-UNIVERSITY

The Innauguration of E-University

Pada April 2024, Erajaya meresmikan Erajaya Corporate University (E-University) yang berlokasi di Plaza Harco Mangga Dua, Jakarta Pusat, sebagai langkah strategis dalam memperkuat pengembangan human capital di sektor ritel. Peresmian ini dihadiri oleh jajaran manajemen Erajaya Group dan perwakilan dari Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset, dan Teknologi, Kementerian Ketenagakerjaan, HIPPINDO, serta mitra bisnis lainnya, menunjukkan sinergi lintas sektor dalam mendukung peningkatan kualitas tenaga kerja nasional.

E-University dirancang untuk menyediakan kurikulum yang relevan dengan kebutuhan industri ritel, dengan pendekatan pembelajaran fleksibel dan hasil pembelajaran yang terukur. Kehadiran fasilitas ini diharapkan dapat menciptakan budaya pembelajaran berkelanjutan, sekaligus memperkuat daya saing karyawan Erajaya. Dilengkapi dengan berbagai fasilitas pendukung seperti ruang kelas, area diskusi, gamifikasi, replika toko ritel, auditorium, studio produksi konten, perpustakaan, hingga perangkat virtual reality, E-University menjadi ekosistem pembelajaran terpadu yang mendukung pertumbuhan bisnis Erajaya secara berkelanjutan.

In April 2024, Erajaya officially launched the Erajaya Corporate University (E-University), located at Plaza Harco Mangga Dua, Central Jakarta, as a strategic initiative to strengthen human capital development in the retail sector. The inauguration was attended by Erajaya Group's management along with guests from the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology, the Ministry of Manpower, the Indonesian Shopping Center Tenant Association (HIPPINDO), and other business partners, demonstrating strong cross-sector collaboration to enhance the quality of the national workforce.

E-University is designed to provide a curriculum tailored to the human resource development needs of the retail industry, offering flexible learning methods with measurable outcomes. Its presence aims to foster a culture of continuous learning while enhancing the competitiveness of Erajaya's employees. Equipped with various learning facilities such as training rooms, focus group discussion areas, gamification modules, a replica of an Erajaya retail store, an auditorium, a content production studio, a library, and virtual reality devices, E-University serves as a comprehensive learning ecosystem that supports Erajaya's sustainable business growth.
