# Tinjauan Keuangan

FINANCIAL OVERVIEW

## Ekuitas

Jumlah ekuitas Perseroan per 31 Desember 2024 sebesar Rp9.057,39 miliar, naik 11,4% dibandingkan Rp8.130,77 miliar pada periode yang sama tahun 2023. Kenaikan ekuitas terutama disebabkan oleh penambahan saldo laba sebesar Rp764,23 miliar.

## LAPORAN LABA RUGI DAN PENGHASILAN KOMPREHENSIF LAIN KONSOLIDASIAN

## Eguity

The Company's total equity as of December 31, 2024 was Rp9,057.39 billion, up 11.4% from Rp8,130.77 billion in the same period in 2023. The addition of Rp764.23 billion in retained earnings accounted for the majority of the equity increase.
