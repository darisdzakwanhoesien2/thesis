# Tinjauan Industri

INDUSTRY REVIEW

Penambahan gerai ini dilakukan pada vertikal Erajaya Digital, Erajaya Active Lifestyle, dan Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment. Ekspansi jaringan ritel bertujuan untuk meningkatkan pangsa pasar dan memperkuat posisi Perseroan sebagai pemain retail di Indonesia.

Perseroan juga terus mengembangkan saluran online untuk menjawab permintaan dari pelanggan melalui layanan e-commerce eraspace, serta hadir di sejumlah platform e-commerce ternama di Indonesia. Platform MyEraspace, program CRM (Customer Relationship Management) sudah memiliki lebih dari 10 juta anggota pada tahun 2024.

Perseroan membangun strategi omnichannel yang menggabungkan pengalaman berbelanja offline dan online tanpa batas. Pelanggan dapat memilih berbagai layanan omnichannel seperti "Click N' Pickup" yakni pelanggan yang merampungkan transaksi secara online dengan pengambilan barang di toko fisik, "Mobile Selling" untuk transaksi melalui layanan WhatsApp dan "EraXpress" yang melayani pengiriman hingga purnajual langsung ke rumah konsumen.

# Erajaya Digital

Vertikal Erajaya Digital fokus untuk memperluas jaringan ritel monobrand store seperti gerai iBox, Samsung, dan Xiaomi. Untuk multibrand elektronik, pengembangan jaringan ritel Erablue terus dilanjutkan hingga mencapai 87 toko pada akhir tahun 2024. Strategi pembukaan toko Erablue difokuskan pada outskirt area Jakarta dan Bodetabek. Sedangkan toko multibrand Erafone difokuskan untuk penetrasi pasar yang sebelumnya belum terjangkau seperti luar pulau Jawa.

Selain menambah sejumlah gerai baru, vertikal Erajaya Digital juga berupaya meningkatkan produktivitas gerai yang telah dibuka dalam dua tahun terakhir melalui optimalisasi produk portofolio yang lebih efektif sesuai dengan lokasi gerai yang dibuka.

# Erajaya Active Lifestyle

Strategi memperkuat portfolio dan mendorong pertumbuhan berkelanjutan pada vertikal Erajaya Active Lifestyle meliputi penambahan merek dan bisnis baru seperti Under Armour untuk produk fashion &amp; sport apparel. Jumlah gerai Under Armour yang diakuisisi hingga akhir tahun 2024 sebanyak 9 gerai. Selain itu, perluasan jaringan ritel terutama untuk saluran ritel monobrand IoT dan fashion apparel seperti Garmin, DJI, ASICS dan JDSport terus dilanjutkan.

The store expansion was carried out across the Erajaya Digital, Erajaya Active Lifestyle, and Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment business verticals. This retail network expansion aims to increase market share and strengthen the Company's position as a leading retail player in Indonesia.

The Company continues to develop online channels to answer customer requests through Eraspace e-commerce services, as well as being present on a number of well-known e-commerce platforms in Indonesia. The MyEraspace platform's CRM (Customer Relationship Management) program had more than 10 million members in 2024.

The Company developed an omnichannel strategy combining seamless offline and online shopping experiences. Customers can choose various omnichannel services, such as "Click N' Pickup," where customers complete transactions online by picking up goods at physical stores, "Mobile Selling," for transactions via WhatsApp services and "EraXpress," which provides delivery to after-sales directly to consumers' homes.

# Erajaya Digital

Erajaya Digital vertical focuses on expanding its monobrand retail network, including iBox, Samsung, and Xiaomi stores. For the electronics multibrand, the development of the Erablue retail network continued, reaching a total of 87 stores by the end of 2024. The store expansion strategy for Erablue is concentrated in the outskirts of Jakarta and the Greater Jakarta (Bodetabek) area. Meanwhile, Erafone multibrand stores are aimed at penetrating previously underserved markets, such as areas outside of Java Island.

In addition to opening new outlets, the Erajaya Digital vertical also seeks to enhance the productivity of stores opened over the past two years by optimizing the product portfolio more effectively based on each store's specific location.

# Erajaya Active Lifestyle

The strategy to strengthen the portfolio and drive sustainable growth within the Erajaya Active Lifestyle vertical includes the addition of new brands and businesses, such as Under Armour for fashion and sport apparel. The number of Under Armour stores acquired by the end of 2024 was 9 stores. In addition, the expansion of the retail network, particularly for IoT monobrand retail channels and fashion apparel brands such as Garmin, DJI, ASICS, and JD Sports, continued to progress.
