# Main Focus ESG Erajaya

## Pilar Lingkungan

Langkah nyata Erajaya dalam mengurangi dampak lingkungan melalui berbagai program yang berfokus pada efisiensi karbon, pengelolaan limbah, dan konservasi energi.

- Efisiensi Karbon: program untuk menghitung dan mengurangi jejak karbon operasional bisnis Erajaya, carbon footprint calculation dan carbon offsetting.
- Waste Management: program pengelolaan limbah B3 dan non B3
- Konservasi Energi: mengembangkan inisiatif penghematan energi

## Program Unggulan 2024:

- Program Wana Erajaya II Taman Hutan Raya.
- Sustainable Gadget: E-Waste Station.
- Carbon Calculating.

## Environment Pillar

Erajaya takes concrete steps to reduce environmental impact through various programs focused on carbon efficiency, waste management, and energy conservation.

- Carbon Efficiency: Programs to calculate and reduce the carbon footprint of Erajaya's business operations, including carbon footprint calculation and carbon offsetting.
- Waste Management: Programs for managing hazardous (IEI) and non-hazardous waste.
- Energy Conservation: Development of energy-saving initiatives.

## Flagship Programs in 2024:

- Wana Erajaya II Program at Taman Hutan Raya.
- Sustainable Gadget: E-Waste Station.
- Carbon Calculating.

## Pilar Sosial

Erajaya berperan aktif dalam meningkatkan kualitas pendidikan, mendukung kesejahteraan karyawan, dan memberdayakan UKM untuk pertumbuhan ekonomi yang inklusif.

## Program Unggulan 2024:

- Pengembangan Vokasi Region: Erajaya Exploration &amp; Collaboration in Education and Learning (EXCEL).
- Pemberdayaan UMKM: Business Matching UMKM.

## Social Pillar

Erajaya actively contributes to improving the quality of education, supporting employee well-being, and empowering MSMEs to drive inclusive economic growth.

## Flagship Programs in 2024:

- Regional Vocational Development: Erajaya Exploration &amp; Collaboration in Education and Learning (EXCEL). Sustainable Gadget: E-Waste Station.
- MSME Empowerment: MSME Business Matching.

## Pilar Tata Kelola

Sebagai perusahaan yang mengutamakan prinsip transparansi dan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi, Erajaya terus memperkuat aspek tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (good corporate governance).

## Governance Pillar

As a company that prioritizes transparency and regulatory compliance, Erajaya continuously strengthens its commitment to good corporate governance (GCG).
