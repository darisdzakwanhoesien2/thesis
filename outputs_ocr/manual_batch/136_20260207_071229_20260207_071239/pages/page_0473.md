24. DIVIDENDS AND GENERAL RESERVES

Based on the Statement of Shareholders' Annual General Meeting Decision which was notarized by Notarial Deed No. 07 of R. M. Dendy Soebangil, S.H., M.Kn., dated on 19 June 2024, the Company's shareholders approved the appropriation of 2023 profit as follows:
