Analysis &amp; Pembahasan Manajemen Management Discussion &amp; Analysis

Pada tahun 2024, vertikal Erajaya Active Lifestyle resmi memasuki bisnis kendaraan listrik (EV) bekerja sama dengan merek XPENG. Bisnis baru ini menjadi langkah strategis memasuki pasar EV sebagai distributor tunggal produk XPENG.

## Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment

Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment membawa merek ritel baru yaitu Curry Up, Bacha Coffee, dan Wetzel Pretzel untuk meningkatkan cakupan pasar. Curry Up merupakan merek ritel lokal dengan spesialisasi pada menu kari, Bacha Coffee merupakan luxury coffee retail &amp; restaurant dari Maroko, dan Wetzel Pretzel fokus pada menu pretzel. Selain itu, ekspansi jaringan ritel Paris Baguette dan Grand Lucky terus dilanjutkan.

## Erajaya Wellness

Erajaya Wellness terus meningkatkan jaringan Apotek Wellings di Indonesia, dengan berkolaborasi dengan CARiNG Pharmacy Retail Management Sdn Bhd dari Malaysia. Hingga akhir 2024 telah hadir 14 apotek Wellings di Indonesia.

## PROSPEK USAHA

Tahun 2025 akan menjadi langkah lanjutan menuju pemulihan pasca pandemi, walaupun pertumbuhan ekonomi global diperkirakan akan melambat dibandingkan dengan sebelum pandemi.

World Bank dalam Laporan Global Economic Prospects Januari 2025 memperkirakan pertumbuhan global akan stagnan di 2,7% pada tahun 2025-2026. Ekonomi global masih dibayangi risiko peningkatan ketidakpastian kebijakan dan pergeseran kebijakan perdagangan yang merugikan, ketegangan geopolitik, inflasi, dan bencana alam sebagai dampak perubahan iklim. Ketidakpastian kebijakan perdagangan dan langkah-langkah pembatasan perdagangan tetap tinggi menyusul hasil pemilihan presiden di AS.

Indonesia berhasil melalui tahun 2024 dengan performa yang kuat pada pertumbuhan ekonomi didukung oleh keberlanjutan reformasi struktural dan stabilitas sektor keuangan. IMF dalam laporan World Economic Outlook Update Januari 2025 memproyeksikan pertumbuhan Indonesia tahun 2025 sebesar 5,1%, meningkat dari 5,03% pada tahun 2024. Sementara pemerintah dalam asumsi APBN 2025 menargetken pertumbuhan sebesar 5,2%.

In 2024, Erajaya Active Lifestyle vertical officially entered the electric vehicle (EV) business through a partnership with the XPENG brand. This new business marks a strategic move into the EV market, with Erajaya serving as the exclusive distributor of XPENG products.

## Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment

Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment introduced new retail brands, Curry Up, Bacha Coffee, and Wetzel Pretzel, to expand its market reach. Curry Up is a local retail brand specializing in curry-based dishes, Bacha Coffee is a luxury coffee retail and restaurant brand from Morocco, and Wetzel Pretzel focuses on pretzel-based menu offerings. In addition, the retail network expansion of Paris Baguette and Grand Lucky continued to progress.

## Erajaya Wellness

Erajaya Wellness vertical continued to expand the Wellings Pharmacy network in Indonesia through a collaboration with CARiNG Pharmacy Retail Management Sdn Bhd from Malaysia. By the end of 2024, there were 14 Wellings pharmacies operating in Indonesia.

## BUSINESS PROSPECT

2025 marks another step toward post-pandemic recovery, although global economic growth is expected to slow compared to pre-pandemic levels.

According to the World Bank's January 2025 Global Economic Prospects Report, global growth is projected to stagnate at 2.7% in 2025-2026. The global economy remains under pressure from policy uncertainty, trade restrictions, geopolitical tensions, inflation, and climate-related natural disasters. Trade policy risks remain high, particularly following the outcome of the U.S. presidential election, which has heightened concerns over potential shifts in global trade dynamics.

Indonesia successfully navigated 2024 with strong economic performance, driven by ongoing structural reforms and financial sector stability. The IMF's January 2025 World Economic Outlook Update projects Indonesia's economic growth at 5.1% in 2025, up from 5.03% in 2024. Meanwhile, the government's 2025 State Budget (APBN) assumptions target a 5.2% growth rate.
