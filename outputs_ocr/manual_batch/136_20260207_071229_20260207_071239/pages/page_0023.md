# Kalender Kegiatan 2024

2024 EVENT CALENDAR

![img-47.jpeg](img-47.jpeg)

Perseroan meresmikan Erajaya Corporate University (E-University) yang berlokasi di Plaza Harco Mangga Dua, Jakarta Pusat. Peresmian dihadiri oleh jajaran manajemen Erajaya Group dan tamu undangan dari Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset, dan Teknologi (Kemenristekdikti), Kementerian Ketenagakerjaan (Kemenaker), Himpunan Peritel dan Penyewa Pusat Perbelanjaan Indonesia (HIPPINDO), serta mitra bisnis lainnya.

The Company inaugurated Erajaya Corporate University (E-University) located at Plaza Harco Mangga Dua, Central Jakarta. The inauguration was attended by Erajaya Group management along with invited guests from the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology (Kemenristekdikti), the Ministry of Manpower (Kemenaker), the Association of Retailers and Mall Tenants of Indonesia (HIPPINDO), and other business partners.

Paris Baguette meluncurkan menu baru, "Hot Off the Pan". Menu ini menyajikan pilihan untuk kategori Hot Meal yang sudah digemari konsumen seperti PB Ultimate Burger dengan lapisan daging sapi empuk, bacon sapi, keju, dan sayuran segar di antara 2 buah roti panggang Kouign Amann.

Paris Baguette launched its new menu, "Hot Off the Pan". This menu serves choices for the Hot Meal category that has been favored by consumers such as the PB Ultimate Burger with layers of tender beef, beef bacon, cheese, and fresh vegetables between 2 pieces of toasted Kouign Amann bread.

Erajaya Digital menyelenggarakan erafone National Tournament 2024, yakni turnamen e-sports game Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. Kompetisi yang telah memasuki tahun ketiga ini menawarkan hadiah dengan total nilai Rp 200 juta. Kompetisi ini juga merupakan ajang tahunan untuk mencari pemain e-sports profesional potensial yang akan menorehkan prestasi di kancah nasional dan internasional.

Erajaya Digital held the erafone National Tournament 2024, an e-sports tournament for the game Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. The competition, which has entered its third year, offered a total prize of Rp200 million. This competition is also an annual event to discover potential professional e-sports players who will make their mark in both the domestic and international arenas.

Erajaya Active Lifestyle melalui merek ritel Urban Republic, menggelar ajang URMazing Run 2024 yang berlangsung pada tanggal 16 Juni 2024 di Jakarta yang diikuti sekitar 350 peserta dari puluhan komunitas. Ajang ini mempertemukan olahraga lari dengan permainan unik yang sekaligus untuk memperkenalkan portofolio produk gaya hidup aktif dari berbagai kategori yang ditawarkan oleh gerai Urban Republic.

Erajaya Active Lifestyle through its retail brand Urban Republic, held the URMazing Run 2024 event on June 16, 2024, in Jakarta, attended by at least 350 participants from dozens of communities. This event combined running with unique games and served to introduce the active lifestyle product portfolio from various categories offered by Urban Republic stores.
