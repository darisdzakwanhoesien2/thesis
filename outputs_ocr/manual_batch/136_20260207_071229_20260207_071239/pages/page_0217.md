Corporate Governance

Rapat IC merupakan bentuk uji kelayakan bisnis yang ketat dan mencakup presentasi oleh bisnis vertikal dan diskusi. Jika IC menilai bahwa usulan tersebut prospektif, tim Business Development akan melakukan studi kelayakan terhadap aspek target market size, business plan yang akan dijalankan, prospek pengembangan, kecukupan arus kas, dan sebagainya. Hasil indepth study tim Business Development dipresentasikan kembali dalam rapat IC untuk dibahas dan diputuskan.

Pada tahun 2024, IC menyelenggarakan 12 (dua belas) kali rapat yang seluruhnya dihadiri oleh anggota Direksi (kehadiran 65%). Agenda utama rapat IC adalah menelaah secara komprehensif apakah peluang usaha yang diusulkan oleh bisnis vertikal Perseroan telah memenuhi semua parameter standar kelayakan investasi yang ditetapkan IC.

## KOMITE KOMERSIAL (CC)

CC membantu Direksi dalam proses pengawasan, pengarahan, dan pengambilan keputusan terkait kinerja dan perkembangan masing-masing bisnis vertikal Perseroan. Keanggotaan CC adalah anggota Direksi Perseroan yang tidak memiliki fungsi manajemen bisnis vertikal.

Pada tahun 2024, CC menyelenggarakan 8 (delapan) kali rapat yang dihadiri oleh seluruh anggota Direksi termasuk CEO di masing-masing bisnis vertikal (kehadiran 100%). Agenda utama rapat CC adalah mengevaluasi perkembangan bisnis vertikal Perseroan.

The IC meeting is a form of strict business feasibility study and includes presentations by vertical businesses and discussions. If the IC considers that the proposal is prospective, the Business Development team will conduct a feasibility study on aspects of target market size, business plan to be implemented, development prospects, cash flow adequacy, and so on. The results of the Business Development team's in-depth study are presented again in the IC meeting to be discussed and decided.

In 2024, the IC held 12 (twelve) meetings, all of which were attended by members of the Board of Directors (65% attendance). The main agenda of the IC meeting was to comprehensively review whether the business opportunities proposed by the Company's vertical businesses had met all the investment eligibility standard parameters set by the IC.

## COMMERCIAL COMMITTEE (CC)

The CC assists the Board of Directors in the process of supervising, directing, and making decisions related to the performance and development of each of the Company's vertical businesses. CC membership is members of the Company's Board of Directors who do not have vertical business management functions.

In 2024, CC held 8 (eight) meetings, all of which were attended by members of the Board of Directors, including the CEO in each vertical business (100% attendance). The main agenda of the CC meeting was to evaluate the development of the Company's vertical businesses.
