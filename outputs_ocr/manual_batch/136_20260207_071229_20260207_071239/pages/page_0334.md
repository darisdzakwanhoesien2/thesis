# COMPETENCY DEVELOPMENT THROUGH REGULAR TRAINING

To maintain consistent service quality, Erajaya regularly conducts training sessions, both online and offline. We offer an online learning platform that enables employees to access training materials anytime and anywhere, complete quizzes, and receive ratings based on their knowledge. This initiative helps us uphold high standards of employee competency in customer service.

# AFTER-SALES SUPPORT AND QUALITY CONTROL

Most brands distributed by Erajaya are supported by official service centers integrated into our network. Additionally, we have a dedicated Quality Control team responsible for verifying the quality of products, whether imported directly from principals or developed under Erajaya's private labels. This quality assurance process is part of our commitment to maintaining customer trust.

# MANAGING CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS AND IMPROVING SERVICES

To date, Erajaya has not encountered any significant customer complaints regarding product failures. The complaints we receive generally vary from product-related issues to dissatisfaction with service quality. To address these concerns effectively, we have established specialized teams, including Customer Care, Quality Control, and supply chain quality testing teams. Furthermore, each of our stores implements strict Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to ensure that services are delivered in accordance with established standards.

# ESCALATION MECHANISM FOR HANDLING COMPLAINTS

All customer complaints are treated with high priority and resolved as quickly as possible. For complaints classified under certain categories, Erajaya implements an escalation mechanism to higher management levels to ensure that decisions on solutions are made swiftly, accurately, and with a focus on customer satisfaction.
