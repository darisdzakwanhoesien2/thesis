# Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi
NOMINATION AND REMUNERATION COMMITTEE

Sampai 31 Desember 2024, Perseroan tidak membentuk Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, karena Perseroan memandang fungsi Nominasi dan Remunerasi dapat dijalankan oleh Dewan Komisaris. Dalam menjalankan fungsi nominasi dan remunerasi, setiap tahun Dewan Komisaris melakukan kajian untuk menyusun usulan remunerasi bagi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris untuk disampaikan kepada RUPS. Dewan Komisaris juga melakukan review kandidat anggota Direksi dan/atau anggota Dewan Komisaris baru untuk disampaikan dan disetujui RUPS.

Until 31 December 2024, the Company did not establish Nomination and Remuneration Committee, due to the Company views that the Nomination and Remuneration function can be managed by the Board of Commissioners. In carrying out the nomination and remuneration function, the Board of Commissioners conducts a yearly review to prepare remuneration proposal of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners to be submitted to GMS. The Board of Commissioners also reviews candidate members of the Board of Directors and/or the Board of Commissioners to be proposed to and approved by the GMS.

# Komite di Bawah Direksi
COMMITTEE UNDER THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Direksi membentuk Komite Pengembangan Usaha (Investment Committee) dan Komite Komersial (Commercial Committee) sebagai Forum Direksi untuk memastikan bahwa semua strategi, kegiatan operasional, dan transaksi bisnis dijalankan secara proper dan prudent, dengan integritas dan etika yang tinggi, serta tata kelola yang kuat untuk memitigasi potensi masalah di masa mendatang. Keanggotaan IC adalah seluruh anggota Direksi.

## KOMITE PENGEMBANGAN USAHA (IC)

IC bertanggung jawab untuk mengevaluasi potensi investasi, melakukan uji tuntas, dan membuat keputusan apakah atas usulan investasi pada bisnis baru dan/atau pengembangan bisnis existing. Keanggotaan IC adalah seluruh anggota Direksi.

Setiap usulan pengembangan usaha akan dipresentasikan sebagai initial introduction kepada Direksi dalam rapat IC. Rapat IC dapat dihadiri oleh Dewan Komisaris.

Transactions Directors established the Investment Committee and the Commercial Committee as a Board Forum to ensure that all strategies, operational activities, and business transactions are carried out properly and prudently, with high integrity and ethics, and strong governance to mitigate potential problems in the future. The IC membership consists of all members of the Board of Directors.

## INVESTMENT COMMITTEE (IC)

The IC is responsible for evaluating potential investments, conducting due diligence, and making decisions on proposed investments in new businesses and/or developing existing businesses. The IC membership consists of all members of the Board of Directors.

Each business development proposal will be presented as an initial introduction to the Board of Directors in an IC meeting. IC meetings may be attended by the Board of Commissioners.
