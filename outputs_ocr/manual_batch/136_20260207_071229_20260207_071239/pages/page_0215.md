# RINGKASAN KEGIATAN KOMITE AUDIT TAHUN 2024

Summary of Audit Committee Activities in 2024

![img-195.jpeg](img-195.jpeg)

Komite Audit telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya yang mengacu pada Pedoman Kerja Komite Audit, arahan Dewan Komisaris, dan ketentuan perundangan yang berlaku, sebagai berikut:

1. Menyelenggarakan 4 (empat) kali rapat yang dihadiri seluruh anggota Komite Audit (kehadiran  $100\%$ ). Rapat Komite Audit juga dihadiri oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Selain itu, Komite Audit mengeluarkan 2 (dua) Keputusan Sirkuler Pengganti Rapat Komite Audit dengan Dewan Komisaris yang ditandatangani seluruh anggota Komite Audit dan Dewan Komisaris.
2. Mengevaluasi kinerja Akuntan Publik (AP)/Kantor Akuntan Publik (KAP) Purwantono, Sungkoro &amp; Surja dalam melakukan audit Laporan Keuangan Konsolidasian Perseroan tahun buku 2023 dan menyampaikan laporan evaluasinya kepada Dewan Komisaris.
3. Melaksanakan kickoff meeting dengan KAP Tanubrata, Sutanto, Fahmi, Bambang &amp; Rekan dan menyampaikan rekomendasi penunjukan AP/KAP untuk tahun buku 2024 kepada Dewan Komisaris.
4. Melaksanakan tugas supervisi atas Audit Internal, antara lain:

a. Menelaah Rencana Audit Tahunan (RAT) 2024.
b. Mengevaluasi temuan hasil audit 2023, memantau tindak lanjut rekomendasi audit internal oleh auditee, dan menyampaikan laporan kepada Dewan Komisaris.

c. Membahas RAT 2025 yang disusun Audit Internal.

5. Komite Audit menelaah Laporan Keuangan Interim Triwulanan yang akan disampaikan ke OJK dan dipublikasikan kepada masyarakat melalui situs web Perseroan dalam rangka keterbukaan informasi.

The Audit Committee performed the following duties and responsibilities referring to Audit Committee Charter, Board of Commissioners directives and the applicable laws and regulations:

1. held 4 (four) meetings attended by all members of the Audit Committee (100% attendance). The Audit Committee meetings were also attended by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors. In addition, the Audit Committee issued 2 (two) Circular Decisions in Lieu of Audit Committee Meetings with the Board of Commissioners signed by all members of the Audit Committee and the Board of Commissioners.
2. Evaluated the performance of the Public Accountant (AP)/ Public Accounting Firm (KAP) Purwantono, Sungkoro &amp; Surja in auditing the Company's Consolidated Financial Statements for the fiscal year 2023 and submitted its evaluation report to the Board of Commissioners.
3. Held a kickoff meeting with KAP Tanubrata, Sutanto, Fahmi, Bambang &amp; Rekan and submitted a recommendation for the appointment of AP/KAP for the fiscal year 2024 to the Board of Commissioners.
4. Carried out supervisory duties over Internal Audit, including:

a. Reviewed the 2024 Annual Audit Plan (RAT).
b. Evaluated the findings of the 2023 audit results, monitoring the follow-up of internal audit recommendations by the auditee, and submitting a report to the Board of Commissioners.
c. Discussed the 2025 RAT prepared by Internal Audit.

5. The Audit Committee reviewed the Quarterly Interim Financial Report which will be submitted to the OJK and published to the public via the Company's website for the purpose of information disclosure.
