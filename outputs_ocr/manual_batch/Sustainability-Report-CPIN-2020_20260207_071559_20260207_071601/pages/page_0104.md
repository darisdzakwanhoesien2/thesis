Untuk memastikan air limbah terkelola sesuai dengan persyaratan perundangan, kami menunjuk penanggung jawab lingkungan di setiap plant kami. Selain itu di setiap plant juga telah disusun prosedur pengelolaan air limbah, operasional IPAL hingga tanggap darurat tumpahan limbah.

Selama periode pelaporan tidak terjadi kasus tumpahan limbah cair. Sedangkan untuk limbah cair yang berasal dari domestik, pengolahannya dilakukan dengan membuat sumur pengolahan limbah domestik sesuai dengan peraturan yang berlaku.

To ensure that wastewater is managed by following the applicable law, we appoint a person in charge of the environmental in each plant. Furthermore, in each plant, procedures for wastewater management, WWTP operations, and waste spill emergency response have been prepared.

During the reporting period, there was no case of liquid waste spill. As for liquid waste from domestic, the handling is carried out by making domestic waste handling wells following the applicable regulations.
