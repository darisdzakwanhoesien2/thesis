Legitimate and binding Directors' decisions can be made without holding a Board of Directors meeting, provided that all members of the Board of Directors have been notified in writing of the proposals concerned. All members of the Board of Directors must also give their approval regarding the proposal submitted in writing and sign the agreement. Decisions made in this way have the same strength as decisions taken legally at a Board of Directors meeting.
