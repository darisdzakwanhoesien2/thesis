# INDEPENDENCE STATEMENT OF AUDIT COMMITTEE

To maintain independence, each member of the Audit Committee has declared their independence through an Independence Statement. The statement of independence of the ESSA Audit Committee is made according to OJK Regulation No.55/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Establishment and Implementation Guidelines of the Audit Committee.

# AUDIT COMMITTEE MEETING

At these meetings, evaluations were carried out, especially on the financial statements preparation system, providing recommendations to auditors, monitoring financial information to be issued, including monitoring the submission of financial statements on a regular basis, monitoring internal control and the implementation of Internal Audit, monitoring the progress of the Company's operational activities and ensuring the Company's compliance with the applicable laws and regulations.

The Audit Committee has also held a meeting with KAP Sigiro &amp; Handayani – a member firm of Grant Thornton International Limited to discuss the Annual Financial Statements for the Period of 31 December 2021, namely on 18 November 2021 and 14 March 2022.

The Audit Committee also reviewed the independence and objectivity of KAP Sigiro &amp; Handayani as a public accountant for PT Surya Esa Perkasa Tbk for the fiscal year ending on 31 December 2020 and the audit report were submitted to OJK on 29 June 2021 with letter number 048/LT-SEP1-OJK/6-2021.
