## 37. FINANCIAL RISK MANAGEMENT (continued)
### Foreign exchange rate risk (continued)

As a result of transactions made with the buyer from abroad, the financial position of the Group may be affected significantly by changes in exchange rate United States dollar/Rupiah, Malaysian Ringgit/Rupiah, and Singapore dollar/Rupiah. Currently, the Group does not have any formal hedging policy for foreign exchange exposure.

Monetary assets and liabilities of the Group denominated in foreign currencies as of December 31, 2023 and 2022 are presented in Note 34.
