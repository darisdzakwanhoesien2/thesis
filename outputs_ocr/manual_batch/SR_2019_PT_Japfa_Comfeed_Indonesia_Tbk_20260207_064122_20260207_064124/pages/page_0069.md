# Pengelolaan Limbah Cair

[GRI 102-11, GRI 306-1, GRI 307-1]

Wastewater Management

Kami berupaya mengurangi dampak lingkungan dengan membangun instalasi pengolahan air limbah (IPAL) sehingga dapat menjamin baku mutu limbah cair selalu terpenuhi.

We strive to reduce our environmental impact through constructing wastewater treatment plants (WWTP) to secure the availability of water for our surroundings.
