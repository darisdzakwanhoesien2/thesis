## Strategi Keberlanjutan

CPIN berkomitmen agar kehadiran kami di tengah masyarakat mampu menghadirkan manfaat dan dampak positif bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Oleh karena itu kami melakukan berbagai inisiatif keberlanjutan yang bertujuan untuk mendukung terciptanya industri peternakan ayam nasional yang efisien dan berdaya saing melalui pengelolaan ketiga pilar keberlanjutan, profit, people dan planet.

## Kinerja Ekonomi (Profit)

Pandemi COVID-19 telah memicu kelesuan ekonomi baik di dalam maupun di luar negeri. Industri peternakan nasional juga turut terimbas, lemahnya daya beli masyarakat memicu turunnya permintaan sehingga daging ayam dan telur melimpah. Kondisi ini berujung dengan jatuhnya harga dua komoditas ini di masyarakat. Meski demikian, CPIN kembali dapat menunjukan kepemimpinannya sebagai market leader di industri ini dengan mencatatkan kinerja usaha yang tetap positif.

Hasilnya, CPIN pada tahun 2021, berhasil meraih laba bersih sebesar Rp3,62 triliun, angka ini turun dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya sebesar Rp3,85 triliun. Keberhasilan CPIN mempertahankan kinerja ini merupakan buah dari kerja keras dan ketekunan CPIN mengelola kegiatan operasionalnya.

Selama periode pelaporan, CPIN masih mempertahankan pendekatan dan strategi yang sama yang dengan yang dilakukan pada tahun sebelumnya. Keputusan ini diambil mengingat situasi pandemi COVID-19 yang belum berakhir dan strategi yang dilakukan sudah terbukti mampu menjaga kinerja usaha Perseroan dengan baik.

Secara mikro, keberlanjutan kinerja ekonomi CPIN didukung oleh komitmen dan kemampuan CPIN untuk selalu beradaptasi dan memitigasi perubahan yang terjadi di believed can boost our immune system, so that it can prevent virus from entering our body.

In relation to this situation, the operational activities of CPIN and its subsidiaries become more significant. In addition to contribute to efforts in supporting food availability in the country and in achieving Sustainable Development Goals, especially Goal 2: Zero Hunger, we indirectly involve in helping physical resilience or public health to deal with the COVID-19 pandemic. In the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic situation, CPIN strive to provide easily accessible nutritious food for all Indonesian people.

## Sustainability Strategy

CPIN is committed that our existence among the community must provide benefits and positive impacts for all stakeholders. Therefore, we carried out various sustainable initiatives that aim to support the creation of an efficient and competitive domestic chicken farming industry through the management of three pillars of sustainability, profit, people and planet.

## Economic Performance (Profit)

The COVID-19 pandemic has triggered economic downturn both domestically and internationally. Domestic livestock industry is also affected, weak public purchasing power triggers a decrease in demand, resulting in an oversupply of chicken meat and eggs. This condition leads to price fall of these two commodities in the community. However, CPIN was again able to demonstrate its leadership as a market leader in this industry by recording positive business performance.

As a result, in 2021 CPIN successfully earned a net profit of Rp3.62 trillion, this figure is less compared to last year of Rp3.85 trillion. CPIN's success in maintaining this performance is the result of CPIN's hard work and perseverance in managing its operational activities.

During the reporting period, CPIN still maintained the same approach and strategy that we did in the previous year. This decision was taken considering the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic situation and the strategy carried out has been proven to be successful in maintaining the Company's business performance favourably.
