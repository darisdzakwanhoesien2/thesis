Bahkan dengan melakukan pelatihan secara daring dapat menjangkau lebih banyak peserta jika dibandingkan pelatihan model konvensional.

The COVID-19 health protocols limit activities that can trigger crowds, including training. However, with technology advancement, it is not an obstacle. By conducting online training, we can even reach more participants if compared to conventional training model.
