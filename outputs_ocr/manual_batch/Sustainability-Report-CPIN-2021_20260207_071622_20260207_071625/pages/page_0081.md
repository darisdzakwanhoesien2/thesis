KEUNGGULAN UNTUK MITRA KAMI

![img-80.jpeg](img-80.jpeg)

"Diharapkan saat penjarangan atau panen raya, mutu atau kualitas ayam sesuai yang diharapkan peternak maupun CPIN. Kami akan lebih berhati-hati mulai dari penangkapan hingga menjaga aspek timbangan," ucapnya.

Kebumen dijadikan pilot project kegiatan ini karena saat ini pertumbuhan peternak ayam di kota ini cukup besar dan mitra plasma CPIN juga cukup banyak. Hasil pemantauan yang dilakukan CPIN menyimpulkan secara umum peternak ayam di Kebumen sudah cukup baik dalam mutu penangkapan ayam.

"It is expected that during thinning or harvesting, the quality of the chickens is as expected by farmers and CPIN. We will be more careful starting from catching to maintaining aspects of the weight scales," he said.

Kebumen is being used as a pilot project for this activity because currently the growth of chicken farmers in this city is quite high and there are quite many CPIN plasma partners. The results of monitoring conducted by CPIN concluded that in general, chicken farmers in Kebumen are quite good in the quality of chicken catching.
