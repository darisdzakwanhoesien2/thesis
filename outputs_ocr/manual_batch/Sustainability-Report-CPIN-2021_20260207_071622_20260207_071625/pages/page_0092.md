# DI TENGAH KRISIS PANDEMI COVID-19, CPIN HIBAHKAN KANDANG MODERN SENILAI LEBIH DARI 5 MILIAR

IN THE MIDST OF THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC CRISIS, CPIN DONATE MODERN CAGES WORTH MORE THAN 5 BILLION

Fokus dalam peningkatan SDM di industri peternakan ayam, tahun 2021 PT Charoen Pokphand Indonesia, Tbk menghibahkan kembali kandang ayam riset closed house untuk 3 universitas di Indonesia.

Universitas Sebelas Maret, Surakarta, Universitas Tanjungpura, Pontianak dan Institut Pertanian Bogor, menjadi penerima hibah program CSR Teaching Farm CPIN pada tahun 2021. Ketiga universitas ini menerima kandang ayam closed house modern beserta peralatannya senilai masing-masing Rp1,7 miliar.

Khusus bagi Institut Pertanian Bogor (IPB) ini merupakan hibah kandang closed house kedua. Sebelumnya, CPIN telah menyumbang closed house untuk IPB pada tahun 2006. CPIN membangun kandang ayam berukuran 14x85 meter dengan kapasitas 20 ribu ayam senilai kurang lebih Rp1,7 miliar.

Mahasiswa akan mendapat keilmuan budidaya dengan teknologi baru yang diharapkan dapat meningkatkan optimasi budidaya. Closed house bertujuan agar budidaya dilakukan lebih efisien dan produktif, lebih aman dari ancaman hewan, dan memberikan hasil yang lebih baik.

Fasilitas closed house ini dianggap penting sebagai upaya meningkatkan kompetensi mahasiswa untuk merasakan suasana industri di dalam kampus. Selain itu juga dapat mendorong minat mahasiswa untuk magang, yang tidak hanya mahasiswaa IPB, mahasiswa dari luar juga dapat menikmati fasilitas ini.

Focusing on improving human resources in the chicken farming industry, in 2021 PT Charoen Pokphand Indonesia, Tbk donated more closed house research chicken cages for 3 universities in Indonesia.

Sebelas Maret University, Surakarta, Tanjungpura University, Pontianak and Bogor Agricultural University, became recipients of CPIN's CSR Teaching Farm donation program in 2021. These three universities received a modern closed house chicken cage and its equipment worth Rp1.7 billion each.

Especially for Bogor Agricultural University (IPB) this is the second closed house cage donation. Previously, CPI had donated closed houses to IPB in 2006. CPIN built a chicken cage measuring 14x85 meters with a capacity of 20 thousand chickens worth approximately Rp1.7 billion.

Students will get cultivation knowledge with new technologies that are expected to improve cultivation optimization. Closed house aims to make cultivation more efficient and productive, safer from animal threats, and provide better results.

This closed house facility is considered important as an effort to improve students' competence to feel the industrial atmosphere on campus. In addition, it can also encourage student interest for internships, which not only for IPB students, students from outside IPB can also enjoy this facility.
