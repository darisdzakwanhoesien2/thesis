## Program CSR: Pendidikan

Program CSR Pendidikan merupakan program prioritas bagi CPIN. Melalui program CSR ini, CPIN berupaya untuk tidak hanya mendukung upaya meningkatkan kualitas pendidikan di masyarakat secara umum, namun juga secara khusus mencetak sarjana peternakan yang siap pakai yang sangat dibutuhkan untuk kemajuan industri peternakan nasional.

CPIN merancang program CSR yang bisa menciptakan dampak ganda di masyarakat. Tidak hanya menyalurkan kontribusi dalam bentuk materi, Perseroan juga berupaya menciptakan nilai ekonomi (economic value) dan nilai sosial (social value) secara bersamaan.

Melalui program CSR yang dilakukan seperti hibah kandang sistem tertutup (Teaching Farm), Entrepreneur Teaching Center dan beasiswa sarjana peternakan, CPIN menggandeng perguruan tinggi menciptakan Pusat Keunggulan (Center of Excellence) mengenai industri peternakan.

Dengan demikian, program ini diharapkan dapat menjadi solusi dalam merespon tantangan klasik industri peternakan nasional, yakni ketersediaan SDM peternakan yang mumpuni dan siap pakai. Bagi CPIN, program CSR ini merupakan bagian integral dari strategi pengelolaan sumber daya manusia yang diberlakukan Perseroan.

In 2021, The Company is working together with several universities dan government institutions in conducting various activities in Stunting Eradication Program. Stunting is a condition which kids' height is shorter than the other kids with the same age because of the lack nutrition on the first 1.000 days of their life. The activity is training for the cadre in educating the community as well as increasing the quality of the nutrition for toddlers and pregnant women. Until the end of 2021, the number of pregnant women and toddlers targeted by this program is 2.302 people in Pandeglang Regency, Brebes Regency, Tangerang Regency, and Semarang City.

## CSR Program: Education

CSR Program: Education is a priority program for CPIN. Through this CSR program, CPIN strives to not only support the efforts to improve the quality of education in society in general, but also specifically develop ready-to-utilize animal husbandry graduates who are greatly needed for the advancement of the national livestock industry.

CPIN designed CSR programs that can create a double impact in society. Not only distributing contributions in material form, but the Company also seeks to create economic value and social value simultaneously.

Through CSR programs carried out such as closed system cage donation (teaching farms), entrepreneur teaching centers and animal husbandry undergraduate scholarships, CPIN collaborates with universities to create a Center of Excellence regarding the livestock industry.

Thus, this program is expected to be a solution in responding to the classic challenges of the national livestock industry, which is the availability of qualified and ready-to-utilize livestock human resources. For CPIN, this CSR program is an integral part of the human resource management strategy implemented by the Company.
