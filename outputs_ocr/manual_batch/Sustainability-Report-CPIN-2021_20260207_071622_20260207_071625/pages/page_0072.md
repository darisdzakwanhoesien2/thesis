# Penerapan Protokol Kesehatan

Health Protocols Implementation

![img-71.jpeg](img-71.jpeg)

Sepanjang tahun 2021, CPIN tetap memberikan komitmen yang tinggi untuk mencegah dan memutus penularan rantai COVID-19. Tim Penanganan COVID-19 tetap bertugas sebagai Satuan Tugas Penanggulangan COVID-19 atau SATGAS COVID-19 di perusahaan. Unit ini bertanggung jawab untuk memastikan penerapan protokol kesehatan dilakukan secara tegas dan konsisten di seluruh wilayah operasional CPIN dan entitas anak perusahaan. Selain itu, SATGAS COVID-19 juga melakukan kegiatan pemantauan dan pembaruan perkembangan informasi tentang COVID-19 di masing-masing wilayah operasional.

Skema alur komunikasi darurat dan skenario penanggulangan kondisi darurat COVID-19 yang dibuat sejak tahun lalu khususnya untuk karyawan yang mengalami gejala virus Corona atau potensi terjadinya penularan tetap dijalankan selama tahun 2021.

Protokol kesehatan dan kegiatan memutus rantai penularan COVID-19 yang diterapkan di CPIN dan entitas anak usaha antara lain:

1. Melakukan vaksinasi kepada seluruh karyawan dengan bekerja sama dengan satgas COVID-19 daerah kota/kabupaten dan fasilitas kesehatan untuk mempercepat target pencapian kekebalan komunal.
2. Untuk memudahkan tracking dan kontrol pembatasan pegawai maka perusahaan mewajibkan karyawan menggunakan aplikasi peduli lindungi sebelum memasuki tempat kerja.
3. Prosedur bagi karyawan melaporkan setiap ada kasus dicurigai COVID-19 (gejala demam/ batuk/ pilek/ nyeri tenggorokan/ sesak nafas) untuk segera dilakukan pemantauan. Karyawan wajib melapor ke atasan yang akan melaporkan ke saluran komunikasi khusus COVID-19. Di pintu masuk pabrik/ kantor/ fasilitas riset dilakukan pengukuran suhu tubuh untuk seluruh karyawan. Dan karyawan diwajibkan menggunakan masker selama berada di area kerja.

Throughout 2021, CPIN continues to provide a high commitment to prevent and break the chain of transmission of the COVID-19. The COVID-19 Handling Team continue to serve as the COVID-19 Countermeasures Task Force or COVID-19 TASK FORCE within the Company. This unit is responsible for ensuring the implementation of health protocols in the Company is carried out assertively and consistently throughout CPIN and its subsidiaries operational areas. In addition, the COVID-19 TASK FORCE also monitors and updates on information developments of COVID-19 in each operational area.

The emergency communication flow scheme and COVID-19 emergency condition handling scenario which developed last year, especially for employees who has the Corona virus's symptoms or any potential for transmission, will continue to be carried out during 2021.

The health protocols and activities to break the chain of transmission of COVID-19 implemented in CPIN and its subsidiaries are as follow:

1. Vaccinate all employees in collaboration with the city / district COVID-19 task force and Health facilities to accelerate the target of achieving communal immunity.
2. To facilitate tracking and control of employees restrictions, the Company requires employees to use PeduliLindungi app before entering the workplace.
3. The procedure the employee to report any suspected cases of COVID-19 (symptoms of fever/cough/cold/sore throat/shortness of breath) for immediate monitoring. Employees are required to report to their supervisor who then will report it to the COVID-19 dedicated communication channel. Body temperature checks for every employee in every entrance of factories / offices / research facilities. And employees are required to wear masks in workplace.
