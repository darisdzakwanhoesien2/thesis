Kami juga berupaya untuk meminimalisir jejak lingkungan yang dihasilkan dari dampak kegiatan operasional dengan cara menggunakan sumber energi ramah lingkungan. Teknologi ramah lingkungan ini kami lakukan bekerja sama dengan perguruan tinggi ternama di Indonesia yang sudah diakui secara Nasional dan Internasional.

We also strive to minimize the environmental footprint resulted from the impact of our operational activities by using environmentally friendly energy sources. This environmentally friendly technology is our collaboration with well-known universities in Indonesia that have been recognized nationally and internationally.

Sejalan dengan strategi bisnis perusahaan, CPIN berupaya mengintegrasikan aspek keberlanjutan melalui pendekatan 3P (people, profit, planet). Kami menyadari bahwa keberadaan kami sebagai perusahaan penghasil pakan ternak, Day Old Chicks (DOC), ayam pedaging dan makanan olahan terbesar di Indonesia haruslah memberikan kontribusi dan nilai terhadap lingkungan, masyarakat dan lingkup kerja di sekitar kami.

In line with the company's business strategy, CPIN seeks to integrate sustainability aspects through a 3P (people, profit, planet) approach. We realize that our existence as the largest company in Indonesia that produce poultry feed, Day Old Chicks (DOC), broilers and processed foods must give contribution and value to the environment, community and scope of work around us.

# Tanggung Jawab Sosial Lingkungan (TJSL)

Perseroan melakukan kegiatan Tanggung Jawab Sosial Lingkungan (TJSL) sebagai bentuk apresiasi terhadap masyarakat dan dukungan untuk meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat. Komitmen ini dimplementasikan dalam bentuk program Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) pada bidang pendidikan dan pengembangan masyarakat.

# Environmental Social Responsibility (TJSL)

The Company conducts Environmental Social Responsibility (TJSL) activities as a form of appreciation for the community and support to improve the community's welfare. This commitment is implemented through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs in the field of education and community development.
