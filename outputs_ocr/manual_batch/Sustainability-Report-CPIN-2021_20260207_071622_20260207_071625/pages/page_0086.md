# Upaya Kami

Untuk memastikan program TJSL yang dilakukan tepat sasaran, CPIN membentuk unit Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR). Unit ini bertugas merancang agenda kegiatan CSR yang dilakukan Perseroan setiap tahunnya. Program CSR yang diselenggarakan difokuskan pada 3 bidang yakni pengembangan masyarakat, pendidikan dan bencana alam.

# Our Efforts

To ensure that the TJSL program is carried out on target, CPIN established a Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) unit. This unit is in charge of designing the agenda of CSR activities carried out by the Company every year. The CSR program is focused on 3 areas, community development, education and natural disasters.

# Evaluasi Kami

Mengingat situasi pandemi COVID-19 yang belum juga berakhir, selama periode pelaporan, CPIN mengurangi pelaksanaan program CSR pengembangan masyarakat karena terkendala kebijakan Pemberlakuan Pembatasan Kegiatan Masyarakat (PPKM). Sementara untuk program CSR bencana alam, CPIN merealokasikan dana CSR bidang ini untuk disalurkan pada kegiatan pencegahan dan penanggulangan dampak pandemi COVID-19. CPIN melanjutkan sejumlah aksi kepedulian sosial yang telah dilakukan pada periode sebelumnya sebagai bentuk partisipasi dan upaya kami mengurangi dampak dari Pandemi COVID-19 di masyarakat.

# Our Evaluation

Given the COVID-19 pandemic situation that has not yet ended, during the reporting period, CPIN reduced the implementation of CSR community development program due to constraints on the Restrictions on Community Activities (PPKM) policy. As for the natural disaster CSR program, CPIN allocated CSR funds in this field to be distributed for prevention and countermeasures activities for the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. CPIN continues several social care actions that have been carried out in the previous period as a form of participation and our efforts to reduce the impact of the COVID-19 Pandemic in the community.
