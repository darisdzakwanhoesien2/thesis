# Kepuasan Pelanggan

Customer Satisfaction

![img-55.jpeg](img-55.jpeg)

Kepuasan pelanggan dan konsumen merupakan aspek yang sangat penting bagi keberlanjutan usaha. Oleh sebab itu CPIN berkomitmen untuk memenuhi kebutuhan para pelanggan dan konsumen dengan menghadirkan produk pakan ternak, DOC, ayam pedaging dan telur serta makanan olahan berkualitas yang terjamin keamanan dan higienitasnya.

CPIN menerapkan prosedur pemeriksaan atau evaluasi keamanan produk yang ketat sebelum dipasarkan. Melalui pendekatan ini kami memastikan produk kami diterima pelanggan dalam kondisi dan kualitas terbaik serta aman dikonsumsi. Selama periode pelaporan tidak ada catatan produk kami yang ditarik dari pasaran.

Customer and consumer satisfaction is an extremely important aspect of business sustainability. Therefore, CPIN is committed to serving the needs of customers and consumers by providing poultry feed products, DOC, broilers and eggs as well as quality processed foods with guaranteed safety and hygiene.

CPIN implements strict product safety inspection or evaluation procedures before being marketed. With this approach we ensure that our products are received by customers in its best condition and quality as well as safe for consumption. There were no records of our products being withdrawn from the market during the reporting period.
