1. Teknologi Genetika Ayam
CPIN berkomitmen untuk hanya menggunakan induk ayam (parental Stock) yang memiliki kualitas genetika terbaik. Sifat unggul itu antara lain, daya hidup (livability) tinggi, pertambahan bobot harian (average daily gain atau ADG) tinggi, dan sangat efisien dalam menggunakan pakan dengan kata lain rasio pakan (feed conversion ratio atau FCR) sangat rendah. Untuk itu, CPIN bermitra dengan perusahaan penyedia bibit atau strain terbaik di dunia agar dapat menghasilkan bibit ayam DOC yang memiliki sifat unggul.

2. Teknologi Pakan Ternak
CPIN mengadopsi teknologi pakan ternak yang mampu memasok kebutuhan protein, lemak dan nutrisi sesuai dengan tingkat tumbuh kembang ayam. Sehingga pakan ternak yang diberikan bisa memenuhi kebutuhan tumbuh kembang ayam agar dapat mencapai bobot tubuh yang maksimal. Keberhasilan CPIN menjadi market leader di sektor pakan ternak menjadi bukti keunggulan produk pakan ternak kami.

3. Teknologi Budidaya Ayam Closed House
Saat ini 100% peternakan CPIN sudah mengadopsi penggunaan teknologi budidaya ayam dengan sistem Closed House. Penggunaan kandang closed house menjadi kunci dari usaha peternakan yang efisien dan berdaya saing. Sistem ini mampu memberikan lingkungan yang diperlukan ayam agar dapat tumbuh dan berkembang secara maksimal sesuai target. Penerapan sistem ini juga membuat sistem manajemen ayam di kandang terkait pemberian pakan menjadi lebih mudah dilakukan dan efisien. Dengan demikian, pemeliharaan ayam menjadi efisien, dengan hasil produksi yang maksimal.

1. Chicken Genetic Technology
CPIN is committed to only use Parental Stock with the best genetic quality. The superior characteristics include high livability, high average daily gain (ADG), and very efficient use of feed or in other words very low feed conversion ratio (FCR). For this reason, CPIN partners with companies that provide the best seed or strain in the world in order to produce DOC seed with superior characteristics.

2. Poultry Feed Technology
CPIN adopts poultry feed technology that is able to supply the needs of protein, fat and nutrition according to the growth and development level of chicken. So that the poultry feed provided can meet the needs of chicken growth and development in order to achieve maximum body weight. CPIN's success as a market leader in the poultry feed sector is proof of the excellence of our animal feed products.

3. Closed House Chicken Farming Technology
Currently 100% of CPIN farms have adopted the use of chicken farming technology with the Closed House system. The use of closed house cages is the key to an efficient and competitive livestock business. This system is capable to provide the environment needed by chickens in order to grow and develop optimally according to the target. The implementation of this system also makes the chicken management system in the cages related to feeding easier and efficient. Thus, the maintenance of chickens becomes efficient, with maximum production results.

Social Performance (People)
CPIN is committed to supporting the management of excellent and competitive Human Resources through an excellent character HR approach, namely human resources who are competent, committed and contribute to the company.

CPIN partners with universities in various regions that have majors in animal husbandry to boost an animal husbandry education climate that is in accordance with the industry needs.
