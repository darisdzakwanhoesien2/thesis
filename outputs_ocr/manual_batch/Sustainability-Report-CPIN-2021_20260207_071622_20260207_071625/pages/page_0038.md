## Evaluasi Kami

Strategi dan pendekatan yang dilakukan sepanjang 2021, berhasil mengukuhkan CPIN sebagai salah satu perusahaan yang memiliki kinerja tangguh di tengah pandemi COVID-19. Meski kinerja industri peternakan nasional sempat diterpa kemelut harga daging ayam dan telur, CPIN tetap mencatatkan kenaikan pendapatan di tahun 2021.

Pada akhir periode laporan CPIN mencatatkan kinerja ekonomi yang positif dengan membukukan total penjualan sebesar Rp51,70 triliun atau meningkat 21,59% dari tahun 2020 yang tercatat sebesar Rp42,52 triliun. Sepanjang periode pelaporan, CPIN meraih laba bersih sebesar Rp3,62 triliun.

## Our Evaluations

The strategies and approaches carried out throughout 2021 has successfully established CPIN as one of the companies that have a strong performance in the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic. Although the performance of the national livestock industry was hit by a sharp decline in the price of chicken and eggs, CPIN still recorded an increase in revenue or profit since the first half of 2021.

At the end of the report period, CPIN recorded a positive economic performance by recording total sales of Rp51.70 trillion, this figure is bigger by 21.59% than the same period in 2020, which was recorded at Rp42.52 trillion. Throughout the reporting period, CPIN achieved a net profit of Rp3.62 trillion.
