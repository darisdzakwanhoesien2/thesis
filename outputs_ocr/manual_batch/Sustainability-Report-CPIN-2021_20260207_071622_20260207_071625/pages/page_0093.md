## Hibah Kandang Modern Pertama di Borneo

Program CSR Teaching Farm pada tahun 2021 berhasil menjangkau Pulau Kalimantan. Universitas Tanjungpura, Pontianak menjadi penerima hibah pertama di bumi Borneo.

CPIN membangun Kandang sistem closed house berukuran 120 x 12 meter dengan kapasitas bisa diisi 20 ribu ekor dan maksimal bisa 25 ribu ekor ayam yang berlokasi di dalam kampus Untan. Nilai kandang ayam yang dibangun diperkirakan mencapai Rp2,3 miliar.

CPIN berharap keberadaan kandang ayam modern ini dapat meningkatkan kualitas pengetahuan dan ketrampilan mahasiswa peternakan Untan mengenai budidaya ayam modern. Dan nantinya SDM yang sudah terlatih itu dapat diserap CPIN dan perusahaan sejenis lainnya di Pulau Kalimantan.

Hingga akhir tahun 2021, CPIN telah menyumbang 13 kandang closed house untuk 10 universitas di Indonesia. Selain hibah kandang closed house, program CSR pendidikan juga memiliki program hibah pusat kewirausahaan dengan menghibahkan toko Prima Freshmart (PFM) beserta peralatannya kepada sejumlah universitas. CPIN juga membuka kesempatan magang bagi sarjana peternakan melalui program Work Based Academy (WBA).

Semua kegiatan ini merupakan wujud dari komitmen CPIN untuk berbagi pengetahuan dan nilai ekonomi sosial kepada perguruan tinggi. Sebagai perusahaan peternakan ayam terintegrasi terbesar di Indonesia dengan pengalaman produksi lebih dari 40 tahun di Indonesia, CPIN berkomitmen untuk menjadi Pusat Keunggulan (Excellence Center).

## First Modern Cage Donation in Borneo

CSR Teaching Farm program in 2021 successfully reached Kalimantan Island. Tanjungpura University, Pontianak became the first recipient in Borneo.

CPIN built a closed house system cage measuring 120 x 12 meters with a capacity of 20 thousand chickens and a maximum of 25 thousand chickens located within the Untan campus. The value of chicken cage built is estimated at Rp2.3 billion.

CPIN hopes that the existence of this modern chicken cage can improve the quality of knowledge and skills of Untan animal husbandry students regarding modern chicken cultivation. And later the trained human resources can be absorbed by CPIN and other similar companies on the island of Kalimantan.

Until the end of 2021, CPIN has donated 13 closed house houses to 10 universities in Indonesia. In addition to closed house donation, the educational CSR program also has an entrepreneurship center donation program by donating Prima Freshmart (PFM) stores and its equipment to several universities. CPIN also opens internship opportunities for animal husbandry graduates through the Work Based Academy (WBA) program.

All these activities are a manifestation of CPIN's commitment to share knowledge and social economic value with universities. As the largest integrated chicken farming company in Indonesia with more than 40 years of production experience in Indonesia, CPIN is committed to becoming an Excellence Center.
