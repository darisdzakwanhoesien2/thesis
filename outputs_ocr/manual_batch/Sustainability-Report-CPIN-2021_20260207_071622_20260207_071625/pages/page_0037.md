Sepanjang tahun 2021, kinerja usaha seluruh sektor usaha masih dibayang-bayangi oleh krisis multidimensi akibat Pandemi COVID-19 yang belum berakhir. Pandemi yang terjadi sejak akhir 2019 telah memicu pelambatan ekonomi yang berimbas pada menurunnya target pendapatan mayoritas perusahaan di tanah air.

Kebijakan Pemberlakuan Pembatasan Kegiatan Masyarakat (PPKM) oleh pemerintah untuk mencegah penyebaran penyakit COVID-19 telah memicu kelesuan pasar akibat daya beli masyarakat yang menurun. Seluruh sektor usaha dipaksa untuk beradaptasi untuk tetap bertahan ditengah situasi Pandemi COVID-19 ini.

## Pendekatan Kami

Menyikapi situasi ekonomi di tengah pandemi COVID-19, CPIN memutuskan untuk melanjutkan pendekatan dan strategi pada tahun 2020 lalu yang sudah terbukti berhasil menjaga kinerja CPIN tetap positif. Perseroan memfokuskan kinerja usaha dengan terus memperkuat dan mempertahankan keunggulan CPIN di seluruh unit bisnisnya yang terdiri dari pakan ternak (feed), anak ayam usia sehari (Day Old Chicks/DOC) &amp; ayam pedaging (farm) dan makanan olahan (food). Selain itu, CPIN juga menerapkan strategi dengan berpegang pada prinsip kehati-hatian dan menyesuaikan dengan kondisi pasar.

Throughout 2021, the business performance of all business sectors is still overshadowed by the multi-dimensional crisis caused by the ongoing COVID-19 Pandemic. The pandemic that occurred since the end of 2019 has triggered an economic slowdown that has resulted in a decrease in the revenue target of most companies in the country.

The government's Policy of Implementation of Restrictions on Community Activities (PPKM) to prevent the spread of the COVID-19 disease has triggered market downturn due to the declining public purchasing power. All business sectors are forced to adapt to survive in the midst of the COVID-19 Pandemic situation.

## Our Approaches

Responding to the economic situation in the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic, CPIN decided to continue the approach and strategy in 2020 which has been proven to be successful in maintaining CPIN's performance positive. The Company focuses its business performance by continuing to strengthen and maintain CPIN's excellence in all its business units consisting of poultry feed (feed), day-old chicks (DOC) &amp; broilers (farm) and processed foods (food). Furthermore, CPIN also implements the strategy by adhering to the precautionary principle and adapting to market conditions.
