# Evaluasi Kami

CPIN selalu berusaha menyelenggarakan kegiatan tata kelola perusahaan yang terintegrasi dan konsisten. CPIN setiap tahun melakukan evaluasi kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Dewan Direksi baik secara kolegial maupun individu berdasarkan tingkat pencapaian Perseroan dibandingkan dengan target (Key Performance Indicator) yang telah disepakati. Mekanisme evaluasi ini dilakukan untuk memastikan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris dan Dewan Direksi sesuai dengan peraturan perundangan dan/atau Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.

# Our Evaluations

CPIN always strives to organize integrated and consistent corporate governance activities. Annually, CPIN evaluates the performance of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors both collegially or individually based on the Company's level of achievement compared to the agreed-upon target (Key Performance Indicator). This evaluation mechanism is also carried to ensure that duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are in accordance with the laws and regulations and/or the Articles of Association of the Company.

# Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan

Hingga saat ini, Presiden Direktur merupakan penanggung jawab penerapan keberlanjutan, yang bertugas menentukan kebijakan keberlanjutan, mengkoordinir praktik keberlanjutan yang dilaksanakan dan mengelola informasi terkait keberlanjutan. Dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Presiden Direktur dibantu oleh tim lintas fungsional yang terdiri dari beberapa karyawan dari beberapa departemen di Perseroan.

# Kompetensi Penanggung Jawab Keberlanjutan

Kompetensi yang dimiliki oleh tim lintas fungsional tentunya tergantung dari kompetensi yang dimiliki anggota tersebut di departemen asal mereka. Pelatihan yang diikuti juga pelatihan yang sesuai dengan tupoksi mereka di departemen asal.

# Sustainability Governance

Until now, the President Director is the person in charge of implementing sustainability, determining sustainability policies, coordinating sustainability practices implemented and managing information related to sustainability. In carrying out his duties, the President Director is assisted by a cross-functional team consisting of several employees from several departments in the Company.

# Competence of the person in charge of sustainability

The competence possessed by cross-functional teams certainly depends on the competence of these members in their origin department. The training that followed is also training that corresponded to their main tasks and functions (tupoksi) in the origin department.
