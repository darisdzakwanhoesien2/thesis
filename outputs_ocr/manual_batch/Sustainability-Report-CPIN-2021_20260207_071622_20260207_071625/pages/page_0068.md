Work Objectives:
a. Update the latest knowledge and application of Good Laboratory Practices in the workplace
b. To improve employee's knowledge and competence about GLP and practice GLP in their work area.
c. Improve expertise and ability in laboratory organization in the process of conducting tests, facilities, labour and conditions that can ensure that testing can be carried out in accordance with national and international standards.
d. Improve the ability of laboratory organization, to meet safety and health requirements and emphasize environmental sustainability.
e. Introduction to international standards related to GLP, organization of quality management systems and documentation, personnel management, facilities, conditions and accommodations, equipment and chemical management, selection, verification and validation of testing methods, return and sampling, testing execution, reporting of testing results, quality assurance and results validity, audits, inspections, K3 and the application of B3 Waste in the laboratory. | 5  |
