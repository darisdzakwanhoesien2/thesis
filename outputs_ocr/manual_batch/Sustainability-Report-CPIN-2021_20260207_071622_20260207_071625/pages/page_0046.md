# KEUNGGULAN MAKANAN OLAHAN KAMI

Selama periode pelaporan kinerja lini usaha produk makanan olahan CPIN meningkat signifikan dan menjadi salah satu motor kinerja perusahaan. Selain ditunjang pergeseran pola konsumsi masyarakat di masa pandemi, lini usaha CPIN ini juga berhasil memperluas jaringan pemasarannya di dalam dan luar negeri. Pada periode pelaporan, segmen produk makanan olahan memberikan kontribusi sebesar 13,42% kepada total penjualan perusahaan.

Memanfaatkan dan mengoptimalkan momentum ini, pada tahun 2021 CPIN terus meningkatkan kinerja lini daging ayam olahan dengan membangun beberapa rumah pemotongan hewan unggas. Penambahan fasilitas ini diharapkan dapat memperluas kapasitas produksi di segmen makanan olahan yang terletak di Cikande (Banten) sebanyak 3 unit, Salatiga (Jawa Tengah) sebanyak 2 unit, Medan (Sumatera Utara), Sidoarjo (Jawa Timur), Mojokerto (Jawa Timur), Bandung (Jawa Barat) dan Tabanan (Bali).

Hingga saat ini CPIN memiliki beberapa merek untuk produk makanan olahan yang melayani kebutuhan keluarga Indonesia untuk berbagai macam kebutuhan yaitu Golden Fiesta, Fiesta, Champ, Okey, Akumo dan Asimo.

CPIN berkomitmen untuk memproduksi produk makanan yang aman dan berkualitas tinggi. Untuk itu CPIN mengimplementasi secara ketat penerapan standar pengawasan mutu (quality control) yang terintegrasi dalam seluruh proses. Mulai dari pengadaan bahan baku hingga proses distribusi. Untuk memastikan kualitas makanan tetap terjaga, semua bahan diproses dalam suhu terkontrol dengan mesin pengolahan makanan berteknologi canggih dan modern. Selama proses pengolahan berlangsung kami meminimalisasi peran tangan karyawan dan langsung dibekukan dengan sempurna untuk menjaga higienitas.

# EXCELLENCE OF OUR PROCESSED FOOD

During the reporting period, the performance of CPIN processed food product business line increased significantly, becoming one of the engines of the Company's performances. Apart from being supported by a shift in people's consumption patterns during the pandemic, this CPIN business line also succeeded in expanding its marketing network domestically and internationally. During the reporting period, the processed food product segment contributed 13,42% to the Company's total sales.

Utilizing and optimizing this momentum, in 2021 CPIN continues to improve the performance of processed chicken lines by building several poultry slaughterhouses. The addition of these facilities, which located in Cikande (Banten) by 3 units, Salatiga (Central Java) by 2 units, Medan (North Sumatra), Sidoarjo (East Java), Mojokerto (East Java), Bandung (West Java) and Tabanan (Bali), is expected to expand production capacity in the processed food segment.

Until now CPIN has several brands for processed food products that serve the needs of Indonesian families for various needs, namely Golden Fiesta, Fiesta, Champ, Okey, Akumo and Asimo.

CPIN is committed to producing safe and high-quality food products. For this reason, CPIN strictly implements the quality control standards that are integrated throughout the entire process. From the procurement of raw materials to the distribution process. To ensure food quality is maintained, all ingredients are processed in controlled temperatures with technologically advanced and modern food processing machines. During the processing procedure we minimize the involvement of employees' hands and products are immediately frozen perfectly to ensure hygiene.
