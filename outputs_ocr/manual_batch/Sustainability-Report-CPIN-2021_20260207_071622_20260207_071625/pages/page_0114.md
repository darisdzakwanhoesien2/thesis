EXCELLENCE FOR OUR SUSTAINABLE GOVERNANCE

Sementara Direksi adalah organ Perseroan yang berwenang dan bertanggung jawab penuh atas pengurusan Perseroan untuk kepentingan Perseroan, sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan serta mewakili Perseroan baik di dalam dan di luar pengadilan sesuai dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar. Dalam pelaksanaan tugasnya Direksi dibantu oleh Unit Audit Internal dan Sekretaris Perusahaan.

## Upaya Kami

Untuk memastikan penyelenggaraan tata kelola perusahaan berjalan sesuai dengan prinsip-prinsip dasar GCG, persyaratan perundangan dan norma yang berlaku, CPIN telah menyusun alat kelengkapan berupa perangkat dokumen sebagai pedoman. Alat kelengkapan dokumen itu terdiri dari piagam Komite Audit, Pedoman Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi serta Piagam Internal Audit.

Rincian lebih lanjut mengenai tugas, tanggung jawab dan wewenang masing-masing organ tata kelola dapat dilihat pada Laporan Tahunan Perseroan pada Bab Tata Kelola. Sementara pengelolaan aspek keberlanjutan di CPIN di delegasikan ke setiap fungsi kerja yang relevan, dan hasil kinerja akan ditinjau oleh Direktur.

Meanwhile, the Board of Directors is a CPIN organ that is authorized and entirely responsible in managing CPIN for its benefit, following CPIN's purposes and objectives as well as representing CPIN both inside and outside the court in accordance with the provisions in the Articles of Association. In carrying out its duties, the Board of Directors is assisted by the Internal Audit Unit and the Corporate Secretary.

## Our Efforts

To ensure the implementation of good corporate governance is implemented following the basic principles of GCG, applicable law and norms. CPIN has compiled equipment in the form of document as guidelines. The document equipment consists of Audit Committee Charter, Remuneration and Nomination Committee Guidelines, and Internal Audit Charter.

Further details regarding the duties, responsibilities, and authorities of each governance organ can be seen in the CPIN's Annual Report in the Governance Chapter. Meanwhile, the management of sustainability aspects at CPIN is delegated to each relevant work function, and the performance results will be reviewed by the Director.
