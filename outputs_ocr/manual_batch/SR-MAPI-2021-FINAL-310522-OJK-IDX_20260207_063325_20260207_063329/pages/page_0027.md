# OUR BUSINESSES

## BIDANG USAHA

### Our Businesses
### Bidang Usaha

The Company runs the following businesses:

- Department Stores (including supermarket);
- Specialty Stores:
1) Active (sports, leisure &amp; kids);
2) Fashion (including cosmetics and beauty);
3) Digital;
- Food &amp; Beverage; and
- Others (including book stores, handicrafts &amp; souvenirs, travel &amp; lifestyle, beauty &amp; health).

For complete information, please refer to the 2021 Annual Report.

Perusahaan menjalankan usahanya di bidang:

- Department Stores (termasuk supermarket);
- Specialty Stores:
1) Active (sports, leisure &amp; kids);
2) Fashion (including cosmetics and beauty);
3) Digital;
- Food &amp; Beverage; dan
- Lain-lain (termasuk gerai buku, kerajinan tangan &amp; cinderamata, travel &amp; lifestyle, beauty &amp; health).

Untuk informasi selengkapnya, dapat merujuk ke Laporan Tahunan 2021.
