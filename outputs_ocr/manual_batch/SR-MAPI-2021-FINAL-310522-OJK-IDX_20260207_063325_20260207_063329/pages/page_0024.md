# COMPANY IN BRIEF

## SEKILAS PERUSAHAAN

Since its establishment in 1995, MAP has vast development. As a leading lifestyle retailer in Indonesia, MAP has more than 2,700 retail outlets in 78 cities and is supported by more than 20,000 employees.

Sejak didirikan tahun 1995, MAP terus mengalami pertumbuhan pesat. Sebagai peritel gaya hidup terkemuka di Indonesia, MAP memiliki lebih dari 2.700 gerai ritel yang tersebar di 78 kota dan didukung oleh lebih dari 20.000 karyawan.

MAP's business segments consist of department stores, active (sports &amp; leisure, kids), fashion, food &amp; beverage, as well as lifestyle products. A number of leading brands managed by MAP among which are Starbucks, Zara, Marks &amp; Spencer, SOGO, SEIBU, Oshkosh B'Gosh, Reebok.

MAP won a number of awards for its achievements, among which were "TOP 50 Companies" – Forbes Indonesia, 2020.

Segmen usaha MAP terdiri dari department stores, active (sports&amp;leisure, kids), fashion, food &amp; beverage serta produk-produk lifestyle. Beberapa merek terkemuka yang dikelola oleh MAP antara lain Starbucks, Zara, Marks &amp; Spencer, SOGO, SEIBU, Oshkosh B'Gosh, Reebok.

Serangkaian penghargaan telah diraih MAP atas prestasinya antara lain "Top 50 Companies" – Forbes Indonesia, 2020.
