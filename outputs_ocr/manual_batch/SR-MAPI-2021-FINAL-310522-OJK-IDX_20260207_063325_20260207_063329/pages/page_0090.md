# Rumah Belajar Anak Bintang (RAJAB)

![img-133.jpeg](img-133.jpeg)

RAJAB is a community development program that provides character-building education for children aged 10-15 years who come from less prosperous families. This program was initiated in 2014, and we cooperate with Youth Organization (Karang Taruna) of West Java Province in managing this program.
