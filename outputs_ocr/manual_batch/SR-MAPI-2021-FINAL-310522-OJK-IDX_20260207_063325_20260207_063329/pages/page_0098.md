# Working with Key Opinion Leaders to Spread Active Lifestyle

Our Company supports national athletes to spread healthy and active lifestyle. In 2021, Hoka One One, a brand under our Active segment, supports several athletes that compete in the National Sports Competition (PON) held in Papua. The athletes' achievements are as follows:

- Sapwaturrahman
- Gold Medal – Long Jump

- Bronze Medal – Men's Relay 4 x 100 m
