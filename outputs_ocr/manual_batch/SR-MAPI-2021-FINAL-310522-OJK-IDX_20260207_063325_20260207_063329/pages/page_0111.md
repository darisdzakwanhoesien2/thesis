As a retailer, most of the wastes is not of B3 category, but we have one garment factory that produces B3 waste. The garment factory is located in Gunung Putri Bogor, and is managed by PT Mitra Garindo Perkasa (MGP). This garment factory occupies an area of 33,000 sqm and has 507 employees.
