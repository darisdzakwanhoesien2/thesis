Pada tahun 2023, GM telah melakukan pembukaan lahan seluas 10,329 Ha untuk keperluan fasilitas penunjang seperti jalan non tambang, perumahan karyawan, kantor, bengkel, drillpad, akses drilling, fuel station, dan nursery sebagai persiapan dalam kegiatan konstruksi yang telah dilakukan penatagunaan lahan untuk mencegah terjadinya longsor. Bertujuan untuk mendukung kegiatan revegetasi pada area bukaan lahan, GM telah membangun Nursery Tombulilato untuk melakukan pembibitan tanaman lokal, covercrop, serta tanaman cepat tumbuh. Penyediaan bibit dilakukan secara swadaya dengan mengambil bibit dari area bukaan lahan dan melalui Kerjasama dengan vendor lokal CV Forest Modelidu serta Kebut Bibit Desa ("KBD") Mopuya milik BP DAS Provinsi Gorontalo.

Dari hasil studi keanekaragaman hayati yang telah dilakukan sampai dengan tahun 2023, beberapa fauna yang ditemukan merupakan fauna endemik Sulawesi, seperti monyet yaki (Macaca nigra), babirusa (Babyrousa babyrousa), anjing hutan (Macrogalidia musschenbroekii), anoa besar (Bubalus depresicornis), tarsius (Tarsius speetrum), meliputi rangkong (Rhyticeros cassidix), nuri Sulawesi (Eos histrio) dan kumkum hijau Sulawesi (Ducula aenea). In 2023, GM has cleared a 10,329 Ha of land to build supporting facilities such as non-mining roads, mess, offices, workshops, drillpads, access drilling, fuel stations, and nurseries as preparation for construction activities which have been carried out in land use stewardship to prevent the occurrence of landslide. To support revegetation activities in land clearing areas, GM has built the Tombulilato Nursery to carry out local plant nurseries, cover crops, and fast growing plants. Seeds procurement is done independently by taking seeds from land clearing areas and through collaboration with local vendors CV Forest Modelidu and Kebut Seeds Village ("KBD") Mopuya owned by BP DAS Gorontalo Province.

The biodiversity study that has been conducted up to 2023 revealed that some of the fauna found are endemic to Sulawesi, such as the yaki monkey (Macaca nigra), babirusa (Babyrousa babyrousa), forest dog (Macrogalidia musschenbroekii), large anoa (Bubalus depressornis), tarsier (Tarsius speetrum), including hornbill (Rhyticeros cassidix), Sulawesi parrot (Eos histrio) and Sulawesi green kumkum (Ducula aenea).
