# Pencapaian Keberlanjutan 2023

Pada tahun 2023, BRMS mencapai tonggak penting dalam upaya keberlanjutannya, yang menunjukkan komitmennya terhadap prinsip-prinsip lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST). Masuknya BRMS ke dalam indeks-indeks ternama seperti indeks FTSE dari Inggris, IDX Sharia Growth, IDX 80, dan Kompas100 dari Indonesia, membuktikan semakin besarnya pengakuan perusahaan dalam komunitas investasi baik di dalam maupun luar negeri. Inklusi ini tidak hanya meningkatkan visibilitas saham BRMS namun juga mencerminkan positif likuiditas perdagangannya.

According to data from the World Bank, the average price of 99.5% pure gold at the London Fix Afternoon Price reached USD 1,942.67 per troy ounce in 2023, marking a substantial increase of approximately 7.9% compared to the previous year—setting a record high that surpassed earlier projections.

These fluctuations in gold prices surpass initial estimates, as evident from the October 2023 edition of the World Bank's Commodity Markets Outlook report, which projected a lower average gold price of USD 1,800 per troy ounce for the year. Despite these unforeseen market dynamics, economic growth persists, as indicated by data from the Central Statistics Agency (BPS). The agency reported a year-on-year growth of 5.04% in the fourth quarter of 2023, marking an increase from the previous quarter's growth of 4.94%.

Amidst this challenging situation, BRMS has successfully reached its targets set for 2023. This accomplishment underscores our commitment to adaptability, strategic planning, and effective management practices, all of which contribute to our ability to navigate and thrive amid uncertainty.

# Sustainability Achievement 2023

In 2023, BRMS reached a significant milestone in its sustainability endeavors, showcasing its dedication to environmental, social, and governance (ESG) principles. The company's inclusion in renowned indices such as the FTSE index in England, the IDX Sharia Growth, IDX 80 and Kompas100 in Indonesia, underscores its growing recognition in the investment community, domestically and internationally. This inclusion not only enhances the visibility of BRMS shares, but also positively impacts its trading liquidity.
