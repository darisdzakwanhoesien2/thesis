BRMS menekankan komitmennya terhadap praktik-praktik berkelanjutan dalam mengelola lingkungan. Pengelolaan limbah menggunakan teknologi dry tailing yang telah beroperasi di pabrik pengolahan emas di Palu merupakan salah satu bentuk dari komitmen BRMS untuk melaksanakan praktik berkelanjutan ini.

Selama tahun 2023, kami telah menggunakan Biosolar (B30) sebesar 131,296.39 GJ, sebuah peningkatan signifikan sebesar 68,4% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya. Kami juga melaksanakan berbagai program inovasi teknologi terkait proses produksi, dengan mengupayakan penggunaan kembali sumber daya alam dalam kegiatan sehari-hari untuk mendukung Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB) dan merespons isu pemanasan global.

Di bidang proses pengolahan bijih emas, kami telah melakukan renovasi unit dan mengadopsi penggunaan bahan bakar dengan nilai oktan yang lebih tinggi, dengan tujuan agar pembakaran bahan bakar dapat dilakukan secara efektif dan efisien. Langkah ini bertujuan untuk mengurangi tingkat polusi udara dan emisi gas rumah kaca yang dihasilkan.

BRMS underscores its dedication to sustainable practices in environmental management. The utilization of dry tailings technology, currently operational at the gold processing factory in Palu, exemplifies BRMS' commitment to implementing such sustainable practices.

Throughout 2023, we utilized Biosolar (B30) totaling 131,296.39 GJ, marking a substantial increase of 68.4% compared to the previous year. Additionally, we initiated various technological innovation programs aimed at optimizing production processes, with a focus on reusing natural resources in our daily activities to bolster support for the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and address the global warming issue.

In our gold ore processing process, we have refurbished the unit and transitioned to using fuel with a higher octane value, intending to burn the fuel more effectively and efficiently. This measure is aimed at reducing air pollution levels and subsequently minimizing greenhouse gas emissions.

Furthermore, in our efforts to address Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) concerns, one of our business units, PT CPM, underwent an external audit for ISO 14001 and 45001 from May 2nd to May 5th, 2023, conducted by BV. The results of this audit were favorable, leading to the attainment of satisfactory status and the issuance of ISO 14001 and 45001 certificates. As a company that prioritizes OHS, demonstrated through the implementation of ISO 45001, we are grateful that throughout 2023, there were no work accidents or work-related illnesses reported across all our business units. This underscores the high level of discipline exhibited by our team in adhering to stringent operational standards.
