# ACTIVITIES TO ESTABLISH SUSTAINABILITY CULTURE

Kegiatan Membangun Budaya Keberlanjutan

# [F.1] [GRI 2-24]

The Company's sustainability transformation is carried out by providing an understanding of the holistic scope of sustainability throughout the operational value chain. The internalization method used is in the form of socialization, training, and capacity development for all employees.

As its commitment to strengthening governance implementation, EMTEK has organized various trainings to increase awareness of anti-corruption and anti-bribery practices as well as build understanding of personal data protection and the importance of facing challenges on the increasingly complex data security. This initiative is in line with EMTEK's ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard ("KCGS") score of 97.79 in 2023. The score has posed EMTEK as one of the companies with the predicate of "Very Good" or 4th Level (90.00-99.99).

In addition to the employees, the Company also conducts improvement to all its business partners, including the supplier selection process. All suppliers have been required to fill out a form and provide a statement of their commitment to carrying out the collaboration process by upholding transparency and anti-corruption principles.

The following are the activities carried out by the Company in establishing sustainability culture: [GRI 2-24]

Transformasi keberlanjutan Perseroan dilakukan dengan memberi pemahaman terkait ruang lingkup keberlanjutan secara holistik dalam seluruh rantai nilai operasional. Metode internalisasi yang dilakukan berupa sosialisasi, pelatihan, dan pengembangan kapasitas kepada seluruh karyawan.

Sebagai komitmen terhadap penguatan pelaksanaan governansi, EMTEK telah mengadakan berbagai pelatihan guna meningkatkan kesadaran mengenai praktik anti-korupsi dan anti-suap serta membangun pemahaman terhadap pelindungan data pribadi serta menghadapi tantangan terhadap pentingnya keamanan data yang semakin kompleks. Langkah tersebut sejalan dengan perolehan skor ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard ("KCGS") EMTEK sebesar 97,79 pada tahun 2023. Skor dari hasil penilaian tersebut memposisikan EMTEK dalam jajaran perusahaan dengan predikat "Sangat Bagus" atau Level 4 (90,00-99,99).

Selain kepada karyawan, Perseroan juga melakukan pembenahan kepada seluruh mitra bisnisnya, di antaranya dengan proses seleksi pemasok. Seluruh pemasok telah diminta mengisi formulir dan harus memberikan pernyataan komitmennya untuk melaksanakan proses kerja sama dengan menjunjung tinggi prinsip transparansi dan anti-korupsi.
