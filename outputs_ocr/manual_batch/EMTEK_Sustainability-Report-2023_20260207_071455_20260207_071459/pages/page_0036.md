# Responsible for Sustainability Implementation [8.1]

Penanggung Jawab Penerapan Keberlanjutan

Provided opinions and advice to the Company's Board of Directors on the establishment of Anti-Sexual Violence Task Force in the Company's Work Place which was effective since 14 August 2023 to ensure the appropriate anti-sexual harassment and discrimination in accordance with the Minister of Manpower Regulation of the Republic of Indonesia No. 88 year 2023 concerning the Anti-Sexual Violence in Work Place Guidelines.

Memberikan opini dan arahan kepada Direksi Perseroan atas pemberitukan Satuan Tugas Pencegahan dan Penanganan Kekerasan Seksual di Tempat Kerja Perseroan yang berlaku efektif sejak tanggal 14 Agustus 2023 untuk menjamin pencegahan dan penanganan kekerasan seksual sesuai dengan ketentuan pada Peraturan Menteri Ketenagakerjaan Republik Indonesia No. 88 tahun 2023 tentang Pedoman Pencegahan dan Penanganan Kekerasan Seksual di Tempat Kerja.

Provided opinions and advice to improve the quality of ESG implementation by the Company's group, including encouraging the Company to achieve ISO standards. The Committee also provided instructions and opinions on the standardization process of ISO 37001:2016 concerning Anti-Bribery Management System of the Company.

Memberikan opini dan arahan yang bertujuan meningkatkan kualitas penerapan ESG yang dilakukan oleh grup Perseroan, termasuk mendorong grup Perseroan untuk mencapai standar ISO. Komite juga memberikan arahan dan opini atas proses standarisasi ISO 37001:2016 terkait Sistem Manajemen Anti-Penyuapan Perseroan.

Provided opinions and advice in each implementation of GCG principles and the Company's compliance towards the prevailing laws and regulations including in the selection of suppliers for the Company's operational activities through the Supplier Statement Form and Integrity Pact.

Memberikan opini dan arahan dalam setiap pelaksanaan prinsip GCG dan implementasi kepatuhan Perseroan sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku termasuk dalam melakukan pemilihan pemasok terhadap kegiatan operasional Perseroan melalui pengisian Formulir Pernyataan Pemasok dan Fakta Irma.

Provided recommendation to the Company's Board of Directors to attend risk management training so that in 2023: (a) Ms. Titi Maria Rusli as a Director obtained Level 4 Certified Risk Governance Professional (ATKRT-BNGP)/(CRGP) (Certified Risk Governance Professional) (GIRMA) from the Risk Management Professional Certification Agency; and (b) Mr. Jay Geoffrey Wacher as a Director obtained Dual Certification: CERG QRGP (Qualified Risk Governance Professional) which was held by CRMS (Center for Risk Management and Sustainability). Specifically, the Company's Director who is responsible for ensuring the smooth running of the Company's Risk Management is Mr. Jay Geoffrey Wacher.

Memberikan rekomendasi kepada Direktur Perseroan untuk mengikuti pelatihan manajemen risiko sehingga pada tahun 2023: (a) Direktur Ibu Titi Maria Rusli telah mendapatkan sertifikasi Ahli Tata Kelola Risiko Terintegrasi (ATKRT-BNGP)/(CRGP) (Certified Risk Governance Professional) (GIRMA) Level 4 dari Lembaga Sertifikasi Profesi Manajemen Risiko; dan (b) Direktur Bapak Jay Geoffrey Wacher telah mendapatkan Dual Certification: CERG QRGP (Qualified Risk Governance Professional) yang diadakan CRMS (Center for Risk Management and Sustainability). Secara spesifik, Direktur Perseroan yang bertanggung-jawab untuk memastikan Risk Management Perseroan berjalan baik adalah Bapak Jay Geoffrey Wacher.

Disclosed the quantitative sustainability targets as well as the development of sustainability related performance that have been set in the previous period as disclosed in this Sustainability Report.

Mengungkapan target keberlanjutan secara kuantitatif serta perkembangan kinerja terkait keberlanjutan terhadap target yang ditetapkan pada periode sebelumnya yang mana tercantum dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan ini.

Actively involving internal and external stakeholders to provide inputs related to significant sustainability issues through public communication channels, including the Company's website and official social media, as well as feedback sheet in the Sustainability Report.

Melibatkan pemangku kepentingan dari internal dan eksternal secara aktif untuk memberikan masukan terkait isu penting keberlanjutan, melalui saluran komunikasi terbuka, seperti situs web dan media sosial resmi Perseroan, serta formulir umpan balik dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan.

Throughout 2023, Board of Directors considers that the ESG Committee has carried out its duties and responsibilities properly, including assisting the Board of Directors in preparing sustainability plans and implementing various sustainability initiatives in the environmental, social, and governance aspects, as well as accommodating the demands and needs of all stakeholders in accordance with the prevailing laws and regulations.

Sepanjang tahun 2023, Direksi menilai Komite ESG telah melakukan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik seperti membantu Direksi dalam menyiapkan rencana berkelanjutan dan mengimplementasikan berbagai inisiatif keberlanjutan pada aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan governansi, serta mengakomodir kebutuhan dan kepentingan seluruh pemangku kepentingan sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

Komunikasi Masalah Penting Terkait ESG
