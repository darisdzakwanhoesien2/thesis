# Product Innovation and Development (P.26)

Inovasi dan Pengembangan Produk

# Others Division (Technology, Digital Infrastructure and Financial Services)
Divisi Lainnya (Teknologi, Infrastruktur Digital, dan Layanan Keuangan)

In procuring telecommunication and networking devices, EMTEK Group, through PT Abhimata Citra Abadi, has supported PT Jasa Marga Toll Road Maintenance (JMTM) with an innovative smart system for Public Street Lighting (PJU). By replacing conventional PJU lights with smart PJU systems, this system has a crucial role for drivers in ensuring visibility, guiding the road, and reducing the risk of accidents, especially at night.

This smart PJU is equipped with a controller on each PJU, all information such as the lifetime of the lamp, position, and disturbances that occur will immediately appear and be displayed on the computer screen. PJU is an innovative lighting technology that combines energy efficiency, convenience and sustainability to save more energy and maintenance.

# Pelindungan dan Privasi Konsumen

# [GRI 418-1]

Personal data and information are fundamental rights and privacy of an individual that always need to be protected to avoid undesirable circumstances. Especially in the midst of technological advances, where the digitalization system has been implemented in almost all aspects of life. Therefore, the risk of data abuse is potentially very high and will certainly be detrimental to the personal data owner (consumers).

The Company has a responsibility to manage data/information to safeguard and protect the privacy and data/information within the Company by implementing: [GRI 3-3]

- commitment to provide timely notification to the data/information subject in the event of policy changes and data or policy breaches;
- commitment to implement reliable data/information protection standards;
- commitment to obtain data/information by lawful and transparent means, with consent;
- commitment to collect and process data/information in a limited manner in accordance with the stated purposes;
- provisions and guidelines for data/information collection, use, distribution and destruction, among others;
- obligation for third parties including the Company's partners who receive the data/information to comply with the policy.

# Addressing Customer Security, Health, Safety, and Convenience

# Memperhatikan Faktor Keamanan, Kesehatan, Keselamatan, dan Kenyamanan Pelanggan

[GRI 416-1] [GRI 416-2] [GRI 417-1] [GRI 417-2] [GRI 417-3] [P.27]

The interests and fulfillment of customers' needs are an absolute concern for the Company to ensure its long-term business growth. Obviously, providing benefits and added values to customers are our main goals, but we are also mostly concerned about the importance of protecting customers from any potential harmful risks. This protection includes all aspects of customer security, safety, and health. Therefore, we provide guidance and warnings on the products and services that we provide in a transparent manner so that customers can be aware of the things that need to be considered regarding the possible negative impacts that can arise in using these products. To fulfill and protect the interests of customers, the Company also complies with all applicable provisions and regulations, this will certainly give assurance and comfort to the customers.

The customers can access, read complete information related to our products and services offered by EMTEK Group including the specifications, benefits, usage procedures, and risks that may arise through each subsidiary's website. The customers can also directly contact customer service in each EMTEK Group to submit questions, feedback, grievances, and complaints related to the products and services that we provide. Through this facility, all customers are expected to be aware of the benefits and risks of the products or services we offer to avoid disappointments or losses. [GRI 3-3]
