# Performance Achievement

Throughout 2023, the Company has carried out the governance, social, and environmental strategies that have been previously set, including the capacity development programs regarding the implementation of sustainable business.

EMTEK Group's sustainability transformation continues with various achievements. PT Abhimata Citra Abadi, one of the Company's subsidiaries in technology and digital infrastructure sector that provides services in the procurement of telecommunication devices and networking systems has successfully obtained numerous certifications, including ISO 45001:2018 for Occupational Health and Safety Management System, and ISO 9001:2015 for Quality Management System, ISO 37001:2016 for Anti-Bribery Management Systems. Furthermore, by utilizing the expertise of PT Abhimata Citra Abadi in information and technology, we have managed to present Smart Public Road Lighting (PJL) in collaboration with other related institutions. The presence of this Smart PJL is not only increasing visibility and safety of road users during night time, but also providing energy efficiency and optimal maintenance.

In the healthcare services sector, under the auspices of PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk (SAME), EMC Alam Sutera Hospital has managed to acquire international accreditation from Joint Commission International (JCI). In 2023, EMC Alam Sutera Hospital also acquired a prestigious award from ACHS International with the ACHS International Quality Improvement Award for its innovative product, YourVOICE, a digital platform for customers' to submit advices and grievances in order to improve the patients' safety. We are currently operating 8 fully equipped hospitals, providing a total of 1,397 beds and supported by 892 doctors consist of 716 specialist-subspecialist doctors and 165 general practitioners high quality and trusted doctors with international standard medical facilities. One of our top facilities is the Advanced Medical Rehabilitation that has a robot-assisted therapy, LEKO and DIEGO, as well as 'Eagle Eye' technology that uses Augmented Reality for spine surgery.

In addition, EMTEK Group's comprehensive ecosystem in Indonesia's digital economy which is supported by three highly successful applications: Grab, Bukalapak, and Superbank, has demonstrated its strong commitment to cultivate inclusive economic growth, including by empowering Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), as well as increasing the roles of female as business players.

In the social aspect, we uphold equality and Human Rights. This equality applies to every policy, starting from employee recruitment, training, to career development.

# Pencapaian Kinerja

Sepanjang tahun 2023, Perseroan telah melaksanakan strategi yang telah ditetapkan sebelumnya baik dalam aspek governance, sosial, dan lingkungan, termasuk di dalamnya kegiatan pengembangan kapasitas terkait penerapan bisnis yang berkelanjutan.

Transformasi keberlanjutan di tubuh Grup EMTEK terus berlangsung dengan berbagai pencapaiannya. PT Abhimata Citra Abadi, salah satu entitas Perseroan di bidang teknologi dan digital infrastruktur yang menyediakan jasa pengadaan perangkat telekomunikasi dan networking system telah berhasil memperoleh beberapa sertifikasi, yaitu: dan ISO 45001:2018 untuk Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja, ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System, ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management Systems. Terlebih lagi, dengan memanfaatkan keahlian PT Abhimata Citra Abadi dalam teknologi informasi, kami berhasil menghadirkan Penerangan Jalan Umum (PJU) Pintar yang bekerja sama dengan institusi terkait lainnya. Kehadiran PJU Pintar ini bukan hanya meningkatkan visibilitas dan keselamatan pengguna jalan pada malam hari, tetapi juga memberikan efisiensi energi dan pemeliharaan yang optimal.

Di bidang jasa kesehatan, di bawah naungan PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk (SAME), Rumah Sakit EMC Alam Sutera telah meraih akreditasi internasional dari Joint Commission International (JCI). Pada tahun 2023, Rumah Sakit EMC Alam Sutera juga meraih penghargaan yang membanggakan dari ACHS International dengan penghargaan ACHS International Quality Improvement Award atas program inovatif YourVOICE, sebuah platform digital untuk memberikan saran dan keluhan pelanggan guna meningkatkan keselamatan pasien. Kami saat ini mengoperasikan 8 rumah sakit yang dilengkapi dengan baik, menyediakan total 1.397 tempat tidur serta diperkuat oleh 892 dokter terdiri dari 716 dokter spesialis-subspesialis dan 165 dokter umum dan terpercaya dengan fasilitas medis berstandar internasional. Salah satu fasilitas unggulan kami adalah Rehabilitasi Medis Lanjutan dengan terapi berbantu robot LEXO dan DIEGO, serta teknologi Eagle Eye yang menggunakan Augmented Reality untuk bedah tulang belakang.

Selain itu, ekosistem Grup EMTEK yang komprehensif dalam ekonomi digital Indonesia, yang ditopang oleh tiga aplikasi super yang sangat sukses: Grab, Bukalapak, dan Superbank telah menunjukkan komitmen kuat untuk memberdayakan pertumbuhan ekonomi yang inklusif diantaranya mendorong Usaha Mikro, Kecil, dan Menengah (UWKM), serta menguatkan peran perempuan sebagai pelaku usaha.
