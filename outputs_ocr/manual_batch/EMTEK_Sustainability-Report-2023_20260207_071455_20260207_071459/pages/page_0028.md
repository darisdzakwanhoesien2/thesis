# Lajcoran Divekol

In the environmental sector, considering that our business activities, especially EMTEK Holding, are not directly related to the utilization or processing of natural resources, so it can be sure that the Company does not have a significant impact on the environment and social issues. However, we remain committed to supporting environmental sustainability, especially in addressing the risks of climate change. Regarding the risks, impacts and mitigation of climate change which are currently receiving a lot of global attention from all parties, the Company, based on its identification, currently has no actual physical impacts. The actual risks that need to be anticipated by the Company are limited to employee health conditions due to extreme weather (air pollution) and as of now, these risks are not in the category of dangerous. Currently, climate change mitigation is entering a transition period which has given rise to new regulations, environmentally friendly technologies, and market shifts due to awareness on the importance of mitigating climate change. The Company will be faced with the risks of fund to purchase environmentally friendly technologies as well as the Company's image will decline if it does not support the climate change mitigation programs.

Our main approach is to implement an environmentally friendly culture through the principle of Reduce, Reuse, Recycle. For example, we have taken concrete steps in 2023 by procuring 2 electric cars as a commitment to reduce emissions. In our daily operational activities, we also encourage all employees to have a responsible attitude towards the use of energy, water, paper, and materials as part of our efforts in reducing our carbon footprint.

Noya which functioned to support the activities of Benih Baik Indonesia Foundation as an organizer of the Charity Crowdfunding.

# Management of Risks and Opportunities

Risk management is an integral part to the implementation of EMTEK Group's sustainable business to ensure the company's long-term growth and protect all stakeholders. Through the ISO 31000:2018 guidelines, we manage risks starting from identification, measurement of potential and impact, preventive measures, monitoring and evaluation. This commitment is further reinforced by the establishment of the Company's Risk Oversight Committee under the Board of Commissioners on 30 August 2023.

# Pengelolaan Risiko dan Peluang

Pengelolaan risiko menjadi bagian penting dalam pelaksanaan bisnis berkelanjutan Grup EMTEK guna memastikan pertumbuhan perusahaan dalam jangka panjang dan melindungi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Melalui pedoman ISO 31000:2018 kami melakukan pengelolaan risiko mulai dari identifikasi, pengukuran potensi dan dampak, langkah pencegahan, pemantauan dan evaluasinya. Komitmen ini semakin dipertegas dengan pembentukan Komite Pemantau Risiko Perseroan yang berada di bawah Dewan Komisaris pada tanggal 30 Agustus 2023.
