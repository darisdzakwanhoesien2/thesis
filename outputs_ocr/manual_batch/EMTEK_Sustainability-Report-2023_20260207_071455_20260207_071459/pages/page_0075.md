## PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk (SAME)

- Penerapan TrakCare dan Intersystems sebagai bagian dari transformasi digital. Penerapan ini mampu meningkatkan layanan lebih cepat dan efisien, yang pada akhirnya mengarah pada peningkatan produktivitas dan operasional yang lebih lancar.

- Sistem DoMInsS — Sistem Informasi dan Manajemen Dokumen. Sistem ini mampu mengelola dokumen secara memadai dan memberikan informasi yang

needed by employees to deliver quality services that prioritize patients' safety. This innovation also facilitates patients and maintains the confidentiality of patients' data.

- EMC Tangerang Hospital's Spine Center has become a pioneer of innovation in Asia by introducing Augmented Reality (AR) technology in the treatment of spinal disorders. Through the use of AR, the Spine Center utilizes 3D holograms of patient imaging data to perform precise spinal surgery. This technology not only increases safety and reduces radiation, but also improves efficiency, accuracy, and is relatively more affordable compared to other navigation systems as it does not depend on a single type of implant.

- In 2023, EMC Alam Sutera Hospital was awarded with the ACHS International Quality Improvement Award for its innovative YourVOICE program to give patients the opportunity to provide feedback and submit their complaints directly through the platform via QR code.

We also introduced a smart hospital environment, where inpatients can control the room environment through a hospital tablet, including temperature, lighting, TV channel selection, and access to real-time billing information. This user-friendly setup provides convenience without the need for additional physical movement. In addition, patient vital signs are automatically integrated into the hospital's information system.
