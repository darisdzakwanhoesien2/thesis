# Sustainability Performance Highlights

Ikhitsar Kinerja Keberlanjutan

## Social Aspect | Aspek Sosial (R 3)

### Health Kesehatan

#### 2023

- EMTEK helped to facilitate employees for outpatient treatment at EMC Healthcare Group Hospital &amp; RSGK Group Hospital by issuing guarantee letters.
- EMTEK coordinated with insurance company for outpatient treatment EMC Healthcare Group Hospital &amp; RSGK Group Hospital or other Hospitals.
- EMTEK established a partnership with EMC Healthcare Group Hospital, namely EMC Pekayon Hospital for the medical examination of all employees. The examination includes blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, and uric acid. This partnership is also supported by the socialization of Healthy Lifestyles.
- EMTEK established a partnership with EMC Healthcare Group Hospital &amp; RSGK Group Hospital to provide benefits in the form of special discounts for EMTEK employees and/or their main families.

- EMTEK membantu memfasilitasi karyawan untuk rawat jalan di Rumah Sakit Grup EMC Healthcare &amp; Rumah Sakit Grup RSGK dengan menerbitkan surat penjaminan.
- EMTEK berkoordinasi dengan pihak asuransi untuk rawat inap baik di Rumah Sakit Grup EMC Healthcare &amp; Rumah Sakit Grup RSGK maupun Rumah Sakit lainnya.
- EMTEK menjalin kerja sama dengan Rumah Sakit Grup EMC Healthcare, yaitu Rumah Sakit EMC Pekayon dalam hal pemeriksaan kesehatan bagi seluruh karyawan. Pemeriksaan meliputi tekanan darah, gula darah, kolesterol, dan asam urat. Kerja sama ini juga didukung melalui adanya sosialisasi mengenai Pola Hidup Sehat.
- EMTEK menjalin kerjasama dengan Rumah Sakit Grup EMC Healthcare &amp; Rumah Sakit Grup RSGK untuk memberikan benefit berupa potongan biaya yang diberikan khusus kepada Karyawan EMTEK dan/atau keluarga inti.

### Vaccination Distribution: Pemberian Vaksin:

#### 2023

Employees can receive vaccination both from Hospital and/or medical clinic, and can claim the vaccination expenses using the medical benefits given by the Company.

Karyawan dapat melakukan vaksinasi melalui Rumah Sakit dan/atau klinik kesehatan, serta dapat melakukan klaim biaya vaksinasi tersebut menggunakan benefit kesehatan yang sudah didukung oleh Perseroan.

### Education Sector Bidang Pendidikan

#### 2023

- EMTEK Career &amp; Education Festival 2023, a collaboration program of EMTEK and PT Qerja Manfaat Bangsa (Karir.com) in collaboration with PT Semesta Integrasi Digital (Karier.mu) and Kartu Prakerja Program. Other than the job fair, talkshow, and workshops were also presented with various industrial themes.
- The Company collaborated with the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology of the Republic of Indonesia to participate in Internship Program &amp; Certified Independent Study (MSIB) “Kampus Merdeka” batch 5. The MSIB program provides opportunities for students to hone and gain skills, knowledge and attitudes in the industry by working and learning directly on real projects or problems. Learning experience outside of university through structured learning activities that will be converted to Semester Credits Units (SKS) of students who participated in this MSIB program.
- EMTEK Goes to Faculty of Computer Science, University of Indonesia (Fasilkom UI), EMTEK collaborated with Vidio to organize an event titled “Unlock the Secrets of Tech-Driven Advertising” which was held on 8 December 2023. Vidio, represented by Miftah Fathudin as Software Engineering Manager to present the technology theme of “How Vidio is Using Tech to Bolster Indonesian Advertising Industry”. EMTEK, represented by Pieter Adrian who is Vice President of EMTEK’s HR Group, also brought knowledge and insight regarding “How to Build Powerful CV’s and Personal Branding for Tech Professionals”. This event was attended by college students from Fasilkom UI, especially excellent aspiring students.

- EMTEK Career &amp; Education Festival 2023, program kerja sama antara EMTEK dan PT Qerja Manfaat Bangsa (Karir.com) yang kolaborasi dengan PT Semesta Integrasi Digital (Karier.mu) dan Program Kartu Prakerja. Selain job fair, kegiatan talkshow, dan workshop juga disajikan dengan banyak tema dengan lintas industri.
- Perseroan bekerja sama dengan Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset, dan Teknologi Republik Indonesia berpartisipasi dalam Program Magang &amp; Studi Independen Bersertifikat (MSIB) Kampus Merdeka batch 5. Program MSIB Kampus Merdeka memberikan kesempatan kepada mahasiswa untuk mengasah dan mendapatkan kemampuan, pengetahuan dan sikap di dunia industri dengan cara bekerja dan belajar secara langsung dalam proyek atau permasalahan riil. Pengalaman belajar di luar perguruan tinggi dengan aktivitas pembelajaran yang terstruktur akan dikonversi ke Satuan Kredit Semester (SKS) Mahasiswa yang berpartisipasi dalam program MSIB ini.
- EMTEK Goes to Fakultas Ilmu Komputer Universitas Indonesia (Fasilkom UI), EMTEK menggandeng Vidio menyelenggarakan acara yang bertajuk “Unlock the Secrets of Tech-Driven Advertising” yang diadakan pada tanggal 8 Desember 2023. Vidio yang diwakili oleh Miftah Fathudin sebagai Software Engineering Manager menyaikannya tema teknologi yaitu “How Vidio is Using Tech to Bolster Indonesian Advertising Industry”. EMTEK yang diwakili oleh Pieter Andrian yang merupakan Vice President HR Grup EMTEK, juga membawakan pengetahuan dan wawasan mengenai “How to Build Powerful CV’s and Personal Branding for Tech Professionals”. Acara ini dihadiri oleh mahasiswa-mahasiswa dari Fasilkom UI terutama calon mahasiswa berprestasi.
