# Risk Management

# Manajemen Risiko

[E.3]

The essence of sustainability for the Company is achieving its strategic goals and creating long-term value while at the same time reducing negative impacts for all its stakeholders. Therefore, the Company considers it important to managing risk, acting to reduce or (if possible) prevent adverse surprise event(s) faced by the Company. In a systematic and structural manner, the Company has developed a risk management system with capabilities to identify, measure, treat/mitigate, monitor and evaluate risks that may hinders growth for the Company.

The Company implements risk management by referring to ISO 31000:2018 which is supported by accurate and timely procedures, reports, information systems, and analysis to assist the management in determining the steps to deal with potential and actual impacts. The Company continues to increase the understanding and awareness of all EMTEK personnel on the importance of risk management implementation culture as an important aspect of good corporate governance, thus, the Company always strives to build a risk culture within the organization by:

- Encouraging employee participation to be actively involved and concerned about creating a work environment with integrity through a whistleblowing system mechanism.
- Implementing a Key Performance Indicator (KPI) to assess the extent to which employees are actively involved in supporting Good Corporate Governance, matters relating to the performance of ESG aspects, providing rewards for the assessment.

# Review of Risk Management Effectiveness Peninjauan Efektivitas Manajemen Risiko

The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are responsible to determine the direction of the Company through sustainability policies, strategies, and targets in the social, economic, and environmental aspect, including the issue of climate change. The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are also actively responsible to identify and evaluate the impacts to sustainability aspect and climate change by involving stakeholders, including shareholders, regulators, public, and other parties through all forms of consultation, such as offline meeting, online meeting, and through the presented reports. The meeting frequency is adjusted to the current needs.

This year, the Board of Commissioners assessed that the risk management system overseen by the Risk Oversight Committee has conducted an effective evaluation of the suitability between risk management policies and their implementation.

Perseroan terus meningkatkan pemahaman dan kesadaran seluruh insan EMTEK akan pentingnya budaya penerapan manajemen risiko sebagai salah satu aspek penting dalam governansi korporat yang baik, maka Perseroan senantiasa berupaya untuk membangun budaya risiko dalam organisasi yang dilakukan dengan cara:

- Mendorong peran serta karyawan untuk terlibat aktif dan memiliki kepedulian untuk menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang berintegritas melalui mekanisme whistleblowing system.
- Penerapan Key Performance Indicator (KPI) untuk menilai sejauh mana karyawan terlibat aktif dalam mendukung Governansi Korporat yang Baik, dan hal yang berkaitan dengan performa aspek ESG, memberikan penghargaan atas penilaian tersebut.
- Perseroan juga membudayakan tiap karyawan untuk memberikan masukan dan umpan balik mengenai masalah keberlanjutan yang bersifat material bagi bisnis Perseroan. Hal ini dilakukan dengan turut mempertimbangan faktor risiko termasuk dalam hal memutuskan pengembangan bisnis atau produk baru. Divisi Pengembangan Bisnis (Business Development) selanjutnya akan menyampaikan proposal kepada Direksi untuk kemudian mendapat persetujuan Dewan Komisaris dengan memperhatikan faktor materialitas dan berbagai aspek risiko yang ada di dalamnya.
- Mengadakan sesi berbagi manajemen risiko atau kegiatan yang setara kepada seluruh personil Perseroan. Pada bulan Desember 2023, kami berhasil mengundang konsultan pihak ketiga untuk melakukan sesi berbagi tingkat tinggi tentang manajemen risiko prinsip &amp; pedoman bagi seluruh personil.
