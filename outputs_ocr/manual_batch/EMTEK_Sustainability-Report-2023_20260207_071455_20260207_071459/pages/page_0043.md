# Risk Profile and Mitigation Efforts Profil Risiko dan Upaya Mitigasinya [8,3]

At the beginning of 2023, the Risk Management Unit re-identified risks in the Company's environment by providing a worksheet to each relevant division to carry out a self-assessment by outlining the potential risks and the extent of the impact if the risk occurs and the mitigation measures to reduce or anticipate the impacts. From the 9 divisions within EMTEK Holding, we identified 72 risks with various scales of impact that may arise at risk levels of High, Medium, or Low categories. In this report, we only present 12 risks with 2 new risks compared to the previous year. Based on the risk identification, the Company takes concrete steps to manage and mitigate these risks. Based on the identification of these risks, the Company has taken concrete steps to manage and mitigate them. The final impact assessment results are then categorized into High, Medium, or Low-risk levels. The risks included in the High category will be the main concern in risk management.
