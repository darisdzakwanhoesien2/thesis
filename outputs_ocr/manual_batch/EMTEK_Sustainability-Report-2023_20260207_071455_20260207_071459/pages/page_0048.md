# Challenges in the Implementation of Sustainable Business

Tantangan Terhadap Penerapan Bisnis Berkelanjutan

[E.5]

The sustainability transformation within EMTEK Group continues to progress significantly. Internalization has been quite effective, as indicated by the various socialization and training programs that have been held, including the implementation of our policies on business partners that encourage sustainable business processes. We are also currently adopting various provisions or best practice guidelines in the context of sustainability, including trends in sustainability performance reporting guidelines and their relation to the impact of climate change risks and opportunities for company growth.

In addition to the above mentioned transformation process, we face a number of concrete challenges as we carry out our business activities, and we believe that addressing these challenges can make a positive contribution. Some of the challenges we identified and are working to address include:

Transformasi keberlanjutan dalam tubuh Grup EMTEK terus berjalan secara signifikan. Internalisasi berjalan cukup efektif ditandai beragam sosialisasi dan pelatihan yang telah dijalankan, termasuk penerapan kebijakan kami terhadap mitra bisnis yang mendorong proses bisnis yang berkelanjutan. Saat ini kami juga sedang mengadopsi berbagai ketentuan atau pedoman praktik terbaik yang berkaitan dengan konteks keberlanjutan, di antaranya tren pedoman pelaporan kinerja keberlanjutan serta kaitannya dengan dampak risiko dan peluang perubahan iklim bagi pertumbuhan perusahaan.

# Business activities that are free from corruption/Kegeatan bisnis yang bersih dari korupsi

It is important to ensure that corporate governance is implemented through the principles of transparency, accountability, ethics, and integrity.

Penting untuk memastikan bahwa governansi perusahaan yang dilaksanakan melalui prinsip-prinsip transparansi, akuntabilitas, etis, dan integritas.

# Maintain data confidentiality/Menjaga kerahasian data

Our line of business is closely related to the collection and use of consumer data therefore, managing data confidentiality is very important to avoid detrimental.

Bidang usaha kami sangat erat kaitanya dengan pengumpulan dan penggunaan data konsumen sehingga pengelolaan kerahasiaan data sangat penting dilakukan untuk menghindarkan hal-hal yang merugikan.

# Media Literacy/Literasi Media

We consider the lack of awareness and understanding of media literacy among the public therefore, we are committed to contributing to increasing media literacy, especially since we have the special capacity and expertise to carry out this initiative.

Kami melihat masih kurangnya kesadaran dan pemahaman literasi media di kalangan masyarakat sehingga kami berkomitmen untuk memberikan kontribusi dalam meningkatkan literasi media, terutama karena kami memiliki kapasitas dan keahlian khusus untuk menjalankan inisiatif tersebut.

# Health and environment/Nesehatan dan lingkungan

Importual: there is limited access to a clean environment and adequate health services in vulnerable or remote areas. Secara khusus, terbatasnya akses terhadap lingkungan bersih dan layanan kesehatan yang memadai di daerah rentan atau terpencil.

# Inclusive education/Pendidikan yang inklusif

We recognize that inequality of infrastructure and resources in supporting inclusive education is a challenge. Therefore, we continuously strive to reduce this gap through inclusive education initiatives.

Kami mengakui bahwa ketidaksetaraan infrastruktur dan sumber daya dalam mendukung pendidikan inklusif adalah suatu tantangan. Oleh karena itu, kami terus berusaha untuk mengurangi kesenjangan ini melalui inisiatif pendidikan yang inklusif.

# Support for MSMEs/Dukungan terhadap UMKM

Limited business management skills and limited access to digital platforms for people in remote areas.

Keterbatasan keterampilan manajemen usaha dan terbatasnya akses terhadap platform digital bagi masyarakat di daerah terpencil.

# Reduce carbon footprint/Mengurangi jejak karbon

Our awareness of the importance of contributing to environmental sustainability encourages us to continue working to reduce our carbon footprint through managing natural resources efficiently, effectively and responsibly. In this way, we ensure that our business not only provides economic benefits, but is also positive for the environment.

Kesadaran akan pentingnya kontribusi terhadap kelestarian lingkungan mendorong karbo rutin, serta terus berupaya mengurangi jejak karbon melalui pengelolaan sumber daya alam secara efisien, efektif, dan bertanggung jawab. Dengan demikian, kami memastikan bahwa bisnis kami tidak hanya memberikan manfaat ekonomi, tetapi juga positif bagi lingkungan.
