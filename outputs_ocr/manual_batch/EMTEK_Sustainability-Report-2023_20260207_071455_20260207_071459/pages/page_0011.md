# Company in Brief

Sekilas Perusahaan

PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk, further referred to as the “Company” or “EMTEK”, is a reputable holding company that manages a broad ecosystem of various investment portfolios and services including media, entertainment, content, online, telecommunication, technology, e-commerce, healthcare services as well as banking and financial services.

PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk, selanjutnya disebut “Perseroan” atau “EMTEK”, merupakan perusahaan holding yang menaungi ekosistem dari beberapa entitas bisnis yang mengelola berbagai portfolio investasi dan jasa yang meliputi industri media, hiburan, konten, daring, telekomunikasi, teknologi, e-commerce, layanan kesehatan hingga jasa perbankan dan keuangan.

![img-28.jpeg](img-28.jpeg)

Established on 3 August 1983, EMTEK previously known as PT Elang Mahkota Komputer, began its business in the media, information, and technology industry. Currently as it enters its fourth decade, EMTEK and its subsidiaries continue transforming their ecosystems to realize a solid, adaptive, and responsive organization against the increasingly dynamic business condition.

Through its sustainable investment, both in technology and supporting infrastructures, EMTEK strengthens each of its subsidiaries to continue to innovate in order to improve their comparative benefits and competitiveness. Starting from its media business, the Company through its subsidiary, PT Surya Citra Media Tbk (SCMA), in 2002 realized a strategic acquisition for the ownership of free-to-air television station, PT Surya Citra Televisi (SCTV). The initiative was followed by other significant corporate actions, including the implementation of Initial Public Offering (IPO) on 12 January 2010 and the settlement of the acquisition of PT Indosiar Karya Mandiri Tbk (IDKM), which is a parent company of television station of PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri (Indosiar).

Strengthening its position in the national media industry is done by combining the superior competencies of SCMA, the parent company of SCTV, with IDKM, the parent company of Indosiar.
