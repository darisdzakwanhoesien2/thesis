# Efforts on Energy Efficiency and Emissions Reduction Upaya Efisiensi Energi dan Upaya Pengurangan Emisi

# [F.7] [F.12]

The policy to use energy efficiently, as previously stated, is in line with the efforts to reduce the impact of Green House Gas (GHG). Our actions include efficiency measures in the use of electricity, fuel, and the use of technology and digitalization to reduce reliance on fuel for transportation.

For example, we optimize the use of information technology to hold online meetings and training. This approach does not only reduce costs, but also contributes to reducing the use of fuel that can cause emissions. We remain committed to developing more effective strategies and actions in dealing with challenges related to environmental impact.

Realizing that the use of energy for our operations contributes to GHG emissions as one of the factors causing climate change, we strive to use existing energy sources responsibly and efficiently through the following approaches:

1. EMTEK socializes the policy related to energy efficiency in the work environment, including reducing consumption of electricity, water, and fuel.
2. Replacement of tungsten lights with LED lights in our production studios to increase the efficiency of electricity usage and support environmentally friendly practices.
3. Utilizing office interior design to maximize the use of natural lighting and reduce the needs for artificial lighting.
4. Replacing all lifts with integrated intelligent elevators, resulting in energy savings through centralized control and ensuring that lifts are only used when needed.
5. Implementation of digitalization, including non-physical recording and reducing the needs for transportation.

Kebijakan untuk menggunakan energi secara efisien, sebagaimana telah diungkap sebelumnya, sejalan dengan upaya untuk mengurangi dampak Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK). Tindakan yang telah diambil mencakup langkah-langkah efisiensi dalam penggunaan energi listrik, BBM, dan pemanfaatan teknologi serta digitalisasi untuk mengurangi ketergantungan pada BBM dalam transportasi.

Sebagai contoh, kami mengoptimalkan penggunaan teknologi informasi untuk mengadakan rapat dan pelatihan secara online. Pendekatan ini tidak hanya membantu mengurangi biaya, tetapi juga berkontribusi pada pengurangan penggunaan bahan bakar yang dapat menyebabkan emisi. Kami terus berkomitmen untuk mengembangkan strategi dan tindakan yang lebih efektif dalam menghadapi tantangan dampak lingkungan.

Memahami bahwa penggunaan energi untuk operasional kami berkontribusi pada emisi GRK sebagai salah satu faktor penyebab perubahan iklim, kami berupaya menggunakan sumber energi yang ada secara bertanggung jawab dan efisien dengan mengambil langkah-langkah:

1. EMTEK turut menyesialisasikan kebijakan terkait efisiensi energi di lingkungan kerja termasuk dalam pengurangan konsumsi energi listrik, air, dan bahan bakar minyak.
2. Penggantian lampu tungsten dengan lampu LED di studio produksi bertujuan meningkatkan efisiensi penggunaan listrik dan mendukung praktik ramah lingkungan.
3. Memanfaatkan desain interior kantor untuk memaksimalkan penggunaan pencahayaan alami dan mengurangi kebutuhan pencahayaan buatan.
4. Penggantian semua lift dengan intelligent elevators yang terintegrasi memberikan penghematan energi melalui kontrol terpusat serta memastikan penggunaan lift hanya saat diperlukan.
5. Penerapan digitalisasi termasuk rekaman tanpa bentuk fisik serta mengurangi kebutuhan transportasi.

6. Utilizing automation systems to control and monitor air conditioning in certain office areas as a concrete approach to energy efficiency.
7. Utilizing electronic signatures to strengthen sustainable initiatives and minimize paper usage.
8. EMTEK actively uses electric vehicles as a form of support for renewable energy and more efficient energy use.
9. EMTEK encourages employees to turn off equipment and electronic devices not being used actively outside working hours.
10. Providing used office uniforms to those in need as an effort to reduce demand for new clothing production which can cause emissions during the production process.

# [F.9] [F.10]
