Through this event, all parties can understand their rights and responsibilities in establishing a sexual harassment-free work environment, thereby providing a sense of security, especially for female employees.

The efforts to realize gender equality and prevent sexual violence are not only implemented within EMTEK, but also at our educational institution, namely Indonesian Television Academy (ATVI). On 28 November 2023, ATVI openly held a Public Test of Candidates for the Selection Committee (Capansel) of the Task Force (Satgas) for the Sexual Violence Prevention and Control (PPKS) which was broadcast live via YouTube. The process of forming the PPKS Task Force begins with the establishment of a Candidate Selection Committee (Capansel). Capansel then attended training on sexual violence from the Character Development Center (Puspeka) of the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research and Technology of the Republic of Indonesia. After attending the training, Capansel took a post-test, and for those who passed, they took a Public Test to assess their skills in selecting the PPKS Task Force. It is expected that the PPKS Task Force can complement and strengthen ATVI in the effort to prevent and handle sexual violence in the campus environment.

We are also open to complaints regarding allegations or behavior of sexual violence and gender inequality. All employees can submit complaints via the Whistleblowing System e-mail: wbo@emtek.co.id or Employee Grievance e-mail: hrd@emtek.co.id. [GRI 2-25]. Throughput 2023, there were no complaints or incidents of sexual harassment that occurred within the Company.

# Testimony for Employees with Disabilities

# Testimoni Karyawan Disabilitas

![img-74.jpeg](img-74.jpeg)
Priyono
PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri

My name is Priyono, I am 46 years old, have worked for 24 years in EMTEK Group for PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri. When I graduated from high school, I felt deep fear about my life in the future because of the limitations I had. However, I finally got a golden opportunity to work at EMTEK Group. The work environment at EMTEK Group is very supportive and inclusive for all employees, where all employees are respected and treated equally fairly without any discrimination. While working at EMTEK Group, I felt that I was given space to continue learning and developing myself. And now, I have been trusted as Head of MIR (Master Inventory Keeper) and Show List Maintenance. This is an appreciation for me, as a sign that I was accepted and appreciated while working at the EMTEK Group, and shows that EMTEK provides equal opportunities for career advancement for all employees, including employees with disabilities. I have experienced many benefits by working at the EMTEK Group, I can realize my dream of being able to pursue higher education and have good financial conditions, such as buying property. I can even pay for my sister's college. I am proud, EMTEK has created an inclusive and supportive work environment for disabled employees like me by making special adjustments and ensuring that office facilities can be used by us. With an inclusive work environment, the EMTEK Group has succeeded in providing space for all employees, including employees with disabilities, to grow and develop.

Saya, Priyono, bersala 46 tahun, telah bekerja selama 24 tahun di Grup EMTEK untuk PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri. Ketika lulus SMA, saya sempat merasakan ketakutan yang mendalam akan hidup saya di masa depan karena keterbatasan yang saya miliki. Namun, akhirnya saya mendapat peluang emas untuk bekerja di Grup EMTEK. Lingkungan kerja di Grup EMTEK sangat mendukung dan inklusif bagi seluruh karyawan, di mana semua karyawan dihargai dan diperlakukan secara secara adil tanpa ada diskriminasi. Selama bekerja di Grup EMTEK, saya merasa diberikan ruang untuk terus belajar dan mengembangkan diri. Hingga kini, saya dipercaya sebagai Head of MIR (Master Inventory Keeper) and Show List Maintenance. Hal ini merupakan sebuah apresiasi bagi saya, sebagai tanda bahwa saya diterima dan dihargai selama bekerja di Grup EMTEK, serta menunjukkan bahwa EMTEK memberikan kesempatan yang sama dalam peningkatan karier bagi seluruh karyawan, termasuk karyawan difabel. Banyak manfaat yang telah saya rasakan dengan bekerja di Grup EMTEK, saya dapat mewujudkan mimpi saya untuk dapat menempuh pendidikan tinggi serta memiliki kondisi finansial yang baik, seperti membeli properti. Bahkan, saya juga dapat membiayai perguruan tinggi adik saya. Saya bangga, EMTEK telah menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang inklusif dan mendukung bagi karyawan difabel seperti saya dengan melakukan penyesuaian khusus dan memastikan bahwa fasilitas kantor dapat digunakan oleh kami. Dengan lingkungan kerja yang inklusif, Grup EMTEK berhasil memberikan ruang bagi seluruh karyawan, termasuk karyawan difabel untuk dapat tumbuh dan berkembang.
