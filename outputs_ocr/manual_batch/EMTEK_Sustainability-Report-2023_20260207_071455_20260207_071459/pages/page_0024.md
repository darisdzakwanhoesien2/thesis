# Sertifikasi/Lisensi

1. PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk

PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk obtained ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System Certification from CBQA Global which was valid from 17 November 2023 until 16 November 2026 with the scope of providing an anti-bribery management system for the vendor registration process at the head office.

PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk memperoleh Sertifikasi Sistem Manajemen Anti-Penyuapan ISO 37001:2016 dari CBQA Global dengan masa berlaku dari 17 November 2023 hingga 16 November 2026 dengan lingkup penyediaan sistem manajemen anti-penyuapan untuk proses registrasi vendor di kantor pusat.

2. PT Vidio Dot Com

PT Vidio Dot Com obtained an ISO 27001:2013 Certificate on 29 September 2021. The certification issued by ACM-CCAE Limited was valid from 20 September 2021 until 27 September 2024 for the scope of information security management in providing customer data management for both web and cellular applications.

PT Vidio Dot Com meraih Sertifikat ISO 27001:2013 pada tanggal 29 September 2021. Sertifikasi yang diterbitkan oleh ACM-CCAE Limited tersebut berlaku mulai dari 28 September 2021 hingga 27 September 2024 dengan lingkup sistem manajemen keamanan informasi dalam menyediakan manajemen data pelanggan untuk aplikasi video web dan seluler.

3. PT Abhimata Citra Abadi

PT Abhimata Citra Abadi earned an ISO 37001:2016 Certification for Anti-Bribery Management Systems from CBQA Global for the scope of Anti-Bribery Management Systems for Integrator Solution for ICT and Digital Infrastructure. The certification is valid from 24 November 2022 until 23 November 2025.

PT Abhimata Citra Abadi meraih ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management Systems dari CBQA Global untuk lingkup "Anti-Bribery Management Systems for Integrator Solution for ICT and Digital Infrastructure." Sertifikasi ini berlaku dari 24 November 2022 hingga 23 November 2025.

4. PT Indopay Merchant Services

PT Indopay Merchant Services on 1 Juni 2023 was awarded with a Certificate of Compliance on the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) Version 3.2.1 Level-1. The certification valid through 31 May 2024, was issued by ControlCase, the assessor of security that met the requirements for certification by PCI Security Standards.

PT Indopay Merchant Services pada tanggal 1 Juni 2023 berhasil meraih Certificate of Compliance on the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) Version 3.2.1 Level-1. Sertifikasi ini berlaku hingga 31 Mei 2024 dan diterbitkan oleh ControlCase, penilaik keamanan yang memenuhi syarat dan telah disertifikasi oleh PCI Security Standards Council.

5. Akademi Televisi Indonesia (ATVI)

Pursuant to the Decree BAN-PT No. 5835/96/BAN-PT/Ab-PfyTdat-03/04/2020, ATVI obtained B accreditation from National Accreditation Agency for Universities for Diploma ID Program of Mass Communication Studies Program. The certification is valid for the period of 20 September 2020 until 20 September 2025.

Berdasarkan Keputusan BAN-PT No. 5835/96/BAN-PT/Ab-PfyTdat-03/04/2020, ATVI meraih akreditasi B dari Badan Akreditasi Nasional Perguruan Tinggi untuk Program Diploma ID Program Studi Komunikasi Masia. Sertifikasi berlaku untuk 20 September 2020 hingga 20 September 2025.

Akademi Televisi Indonesia (ATVI) memperoleh izin pembukaan Program Studi Media Program Sarjana Terapan pada 6 Juli 2022 dari Menteri Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset dan Teknologi.

PT Abhimata Citra Abadi meraih Sertifikasi ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System pada tanggal 17 October 2022.
