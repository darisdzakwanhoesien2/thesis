# 1. Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner

Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner

Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner functions to facilitate the implementation of the Company's strategic planning to achieve goals. This function also facilitates the Company in developing, improving the effectiveness of structures and processes through work mechanisms that have undergone adjustments so that the organization can move adaptively, efficiently and effectively. This function also supports the Company's strategic plans is through Future of Work research with the theme of "Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Employees and Work Environment". Future of Work research was carried out in a project-based internship program for University of New South Wales (UNSW) students. Through this research, the Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner function strives to prepare and develop human resources in order to be adaptable and responsive to opportunities and challenges as well as changes in technology and ways of working.

Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner berfungsi untuk memfasilitasi implementasi rencana strategis Perusahaan (strategic planning) untuk mencapai tujuan. Fungsi ini juga memfasilitasi Perusahaan dalam mengembangkan, meningkatkan efektivitas struktur dan proses melalui mekanisme kerja yang telah mengalami penyesuaian agar organisasi dapat bergerak adaptif, efisien, dan efektif. Fungsi ini juga mendukung rencana strategis Perusahaan telah satunya melalui riset Future of Work dengan tema "Dampak Artificial Intelligence terhadap Pekerja dan Lingkungan Kerja". Riset Future of Work dilakukan dalam project based internship program dari mahasiswa University of New South Wales (UNSW). Melalui riset ini, fungsi Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner berupaya mempersiapkan dan mengembangkan sumber daya manusia agar mampu beradaptasi dan bersikap responsif terhadap peluang maupun tantangan serta perubahan teknologi dan cara kerja.

# 2. Talent Acquisition &amp; Employer Branding

# Talent Acquisition &amp; Employer Branding

Talent Acquisition &amp; Employer Branding responsible in implementing High Quality Talent Acquisition &amp; Recruitment. Given the highly dynamic and competitive media industry, the Company certainly needs to build reliable, adaptive and responsive employee competencies to the development of information technology and modern lifestyles. In line with this, EMTEK is committed to carrying out a transparent selection process and upholding aspects of equality, non-discrimination, bias-free, and inclusive.

Employee recruitment activities within EMTEK and its subsidiaries are carried out in line with the Company's sustainability policy by considering the future demand of business development. EMTEK provides opportunities for internal and external talents to join the Company's group through the recruitment process. The internal recruitment process is carried out through promotions and rotations, while the external recruitment process is carried out through a selection process based on the qualifications and expertise determined by the company for prospective employees both fresh graduates and/or experts/professionals.

As a form of recruitment process development, in 2023 EMTEK Group used assessment instruments to measure cognitive capabilities, personality inventories, and situational assessments, as well as to measure the competency and suitability of the candidates for various roles and positions. The aim of using this instrument is to make sure that the employees can contribute and develop with the Company in the long run.

In terms of employee recruitment, in 2023, EMTEK held the EMTEK Career &amp; Education Festival 2023 in collaboration with Karir.com and Karier.mu. This is a continuation of the EMTEK Career Festival 2022 which this time involves several educational institutions, including Pokerja programs. The event, which was held offline at EMTEK City on 30-31 December 2023, was attended by 20 companies belonging to EMTEK Group and non-EMTEK Group and succeeded in connecting more than 4,000 job seekers with the companies. The involvement of educational institutions in this activity enabled the job seekers to obtain information related to the opportunities for continuing their education both formally and informally. The Company expects that the event can help job seekers in Indonesia to obtain career opportunities and information about further education, as well as help the companies and educational institutions to obtain talents and students in order to support realizing common goals. [GRI 3-3]

In addition, EMTEK Group also supports the implementation of Independent Campus (Kampus Merdeka) program by the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology. In 2023, EMTEK Group accepted 1,272 students to take part in internship and Certified Independent Study programs with placements at EMTEK and its subsidiaries. The opening of this internship opportunity represents EMTEK's commitment to providing meaningful learning and supporting lecture programs according to the goals and interests of each student.

EMTEK also organized the Employer Branding event as an effort to build and manage the reputation of EMTEK and its subsidiaries to become the Employer of Choice for professionals. In 2023, this event was held through online platforms (such as LinkedIn) and offline activities on campuses, such as the University of Indonesia and Pelita Harapan University. In the event, the Company provided the prospective employees with an overview of the working situation in EMTEK Group, as well as providing learning and encouraging a positive work culture for college students.

Talent Acquisition &amp; Employer Branding bertugas melaksanakan High Quality Talent Acquisition &amp; Recruitment. Mengingat industri media yang sangat dinamis dan kompetitif, Perseroan tentunya perlu membangun kompetensi karyawan yang andal, adaptif, dan responsif terhadap perkembangan teknologi informasi dan gaya hidup modern. Sejalan dengan hal tersebut, EMTEK berkomitmen untuk melakukan proses seleksi secara transparan dan menjunjung tinggi aspek kesetaraan, nondiskriminatif, bebas bias, dan inklusif.

Kegiatan rekrutmen karyawan di lingkungan EMTEK dan entitas anaknya dilakukan dalam koridor kebijakan keberlanjutan perusahaan dengan memperhatikan kebutuhan pengembangan bisnis ke depan. EMTEK membuka kesempatan bagi talenta internal maupun eksternal untuk bergabung di grup Perseroan melalui proses rekrutmen. Proses rekrutmen internal dilakukan melalui promosi dan rotasi, sementara proses rekrutmen eksternal dilakukan melalui proses seleksi berdasarkan kualifikasi dan keahlian yang ditetapkan oleh perusahaan terhadap calon karyawan yang merupakan lulusan baru dan/atau tenaga ahli/professional.

Sebagai bentuk pengembangan dari proses rekrutmen, pada tahun 2023 Grup EMTEK menggunakan instrumen assessment guna mengukur kemampuan kognitif, inventori kognibadian, dan penilaian situasional, serta untuk mengukur kompetensi dan kesesuaian kandidat untuk berbagai peran dan posisi. Tujuan dari penggunaan instrumen ini adalah untuk memastikan agar karyawan dapat berkontribusi dan berkembang bersama perusahaan dalam jangka waktu panjang.

Terkait rekrutmen karyawan, di tahun 2023 ini, EMTEK telah menyelenggarakan EMTEK Career &amp; Education Festival 2023 yang bekerja sama dengan Karir.com serta Karier.mu. Ini adalah kegiatan lanjutan dari penyelenggaraan EMTEK Career Festival 2022 yang kali ini melibatkan beberapa institusi pendidikan, termasuk program Prakerja. Acara yang diadakan secara luring di EMTEK City pada tanggal 30-31 Desember 2023 ini diikuti oleh 20 perusahaan yang merupakan jajaran Grup EMTEK maupun non-Grup EMTEK dan berhasil menghubungkan lebih dari 4.000 pencari kerja dengan perusahaan. Dengan bergabungnya institusi pendidikan dalam kegiatan tersebut, para pencari kerja juga dapat memperoleh informasi terkait peluang melanjutkan pendidikan baik secara formal maupun informal. Perseroan berharap penyelenggaraan acara ini dapat membantu pencari kerja di Indonesia untuk memperoleh kesempatan berkaitan maupun informasi mengenai pendidikan lanjutan, serta membantu perusahaan dan institusi pendidikan mendapatkan talenta maupun siswa untuk mendukung tercapainya tujuan bersama. [GRI 3-3]

Selain itu, Grup EMTEK juga mendukung penyelenggaraan program Kampus Merdeka oleh Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset, dan Teknologi. Di tahun 2023 ini, Grup EMTEK menerima 1.272 mahasiswa untuk mengikuti program magang dan Studi Independent Bertertifikat dengan penempatan pada EMTEK dan entitas anak perusahaan. Pembukaan kesempatan magang tersebut mewakili komitmen EMTEK untuk memberikan pembelajaran yang bermakna dan mendukung program perkuliahan sesuai dengan tujuan dan minat dari setiap mahasiswa.

EMTEK juga melakukan kegiatan Employer Branding diselenggarakan oleh EMTEK sebagai upaya membangun dan mengelola reputasi EMTEK dan entitas anaknya agar menjadi perusahaan pilihan bagi para profesional untuk membangun karier (Employer of Choice). Pada tahun 2023, kegiatan ini antara lain dilakukan melalui platform daring (seperti LinkedIn) maupun kegiatan luring di kampus-kampus, seperti Universitas Indonesia dan Universitas Pelita Harapan. Pada pelaksanaan kegiatan ini, Perseroan memberikan gambaran kepada calon karyawan tentang bagaimana situasi kerja di Grup EMTEK, serta memberikan pembelajaran dan mendorong budaya kerja yang positif kepada para mahasiswa.
