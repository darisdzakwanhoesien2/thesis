# Promoting Environmental Preservation Mendorong Kelestarian Lingkungan

# Environmental Policy Kebijakan Lingkungan

All living beings are highly reliant on the balance of the environmental ecosystem, including human beings that utilize natural resources to support their activities. Responsible use of natural resources and eco-friendly behavior are the key to preserving the environment. Likewise, the Company is also committed to preserving the environment amidst the rising environmental issues such as climate change and its impacts, plastic waste, air pollution, deforestation, and biodiversity. Our main approach is to act responsibly and eco-friendly by implementing the Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (3R) principle. [GRI 3-3]

# Person in Charge of Environmental Management

The implementation of environmental policy in every aspect of the Company is coordinated, monitored, and evaluated by the ESG Committee which is fully responsible for managing and ensuring compliance with sustainable environment practices. As the main supervisor, the ESG Committee ensures that the implemented policy is in accordance with the applicable regulations and standards, while playing a key role in preparing environmental strategies and initiatives in order to provide positive impacts for the Company and the community.

# Targets

The Company has set a target to save energy by reducing the consumption of energy and water in the office as well as in every operational activity of the Company by 10-15% gradually until 2025 and reducing the use of paper in the office by implementing paperless policy in every operational activity of the Company, including for payment process and e-memo preparation by 20% gradually until 2025. This initiative is taken to positively impact the environment and consolidate resource efficiency.

# Policy Effectiveness Evaluation

The implementation and performance of Environmental Policy is monitored by ESG Committee which regularly reports to the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners, and all stakeholders of the Company through the Company's Sustainability Report. This Environmental Policy is routinely evaluated by ESG Committee, and if necessary, will be revised based on the development in the environmental aspect and the Company's business community. To ensure transparency, the Company actively discloses the Environmental Policy to all stakeholders, including Shareholders, Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Management, Employees, Investors, Regulators, Business Partners, Suppliers, Media, and Public through the Company's website, social media, and other relevant publications.

# Penanggung Jawab Pengelolaan Lingkungan

Pelaksanaan kebijakan lingkungan dalam setiap aspek ruang lingkup Perseroan dikondinir, dimonitor, dan dievaluasi oleh Komite ESG yang bertanggung jawab penuh dalam mengelola dan memastikan kepatuhan terhadap praktik-praktik lingkungan berkelanjutan. Sebagai pengawas utama, Komite ESG memastikan implementasi kebijakan sesuai dengan regulasi dan standar yang berlaku, serta memainkan peran kunci dalam menyusun strategi dan inisiatif lingkungan yang memberikan dampak positif bagi Perseroan dan masyarakat.

# Target Sasaran

Perseroan telah menetapkan target sasaran untuk menghemat energi, yaitu mengurangi konsumsi energi dan air di kantor serta dalam setiap kegiatan operasional Perseroan sebanyak 10-15% secara bertahap hingga tahun 2025 dan menurunkan penggunaan kertas di kantor dengan menerapkan kebijakan tanpa kertas (paperless) dalam kegiatan operasional Perseroan seperti proses pembayaran dan pembuatan e-memo sebanyak 20% secara bertahap hingga tahun 2025. Langkah ini diambil untuk mencapai dampak positif terhadap lingkungan, sekaligus memperkuat efisiensi penggunaan sumber daya.

# Evaluasi Efektivitas Kebijakan

Pelaksanaan dan kinerja Kebijakan Lingkungan dipantau oleh Komite ESG yang secara berkala melaporkan hasilnya kepada Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, dan seluruh pemangku kepentingan Perseroan melalui Laporan Keberlanjutan Perseroan. Kebijakan Lingkungan ini secara rutin dievaluasi oleh Komite ESG, dan jika diperlukan, direvisi sesuai dengan perkembangan aspek lingkungan dan konteks bisnis Perseroan. Untuk memastikan transparansi, Perseroan secara aktif mengungkapkan Kebijakan Lingkungan kepada semua pemangku kepentingan, termasuk Pemegang Saharan Komisaris, Direksi, Manajemen, Karyawan, Investor, Regulator, Mitra Kerja, Pemasok, Media, dan Masyarakat melalui situs web Perseroan, media sosial, dan publikasi terkait lainnya.
