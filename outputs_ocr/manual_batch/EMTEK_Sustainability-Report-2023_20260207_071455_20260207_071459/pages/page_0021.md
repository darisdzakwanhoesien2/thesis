# 12. ContentAsia Awards 2023

Of 22 original series released by PT Vidio Dot Com (Vidio.com) this year, three series ("Serigala Terakhir 2", "Kataruis" and "Pertaruhan The Series") won prestigious awards at ContentAsia Awards 2023. "Pertaruhan The Series" won Silver Award for Best Director of Scripted TV Programme whereas "Serigala Terakhir 2" won Bronze Award in the category of Best Asian Drama for a Single Market in Asia and Gold Award in the category of Best Supporting Actor in a TV Programme/Series Made in Asia. "Kataruis" won Silver Award in the category of Best Sound Design for an Asian TV Programme/Series.

Dari sebanyak 22 serial drama original yang dirilis Vidio tahun ini, tiga serial drama ("Serigala Terakhir 2", "Kataruis" dan "Pertaruhan The Series") berhasil memenangkan penghargaan bergengsi di ajang ContentAsia Awards 2023. "Pertaruhan The Series" meraih Silver Award untuk kategori Best Director of Scripted TV Programme sedangkan "Serigala Terakhir 2" meraih Bronze Award untuk kategori Best Asian Drama for a Single Market in Asia dan Gold Award untuk kategori Best Supporting Actor in a TV Programme/Series Made in Asia. "Kataruis" meraih Silver Award untuk kategori Best Sound Design for an Asian TV Programme/Series.

Presenting Institutions

Nama Lembaga yang Memberikan

ContentAsia

Achievement Date

Tanggal Perolehan

24 August/Agustus 2023
