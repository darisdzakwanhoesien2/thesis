# Environmental Compliance Kepatuhan Lingkungan

EMTEK is committed to implementing sustainable finance principles in each of its operational activities by complying with the applicable provisions related to ESG practices. This commitment is in line with EMTEK's support to the accomplishment of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Until the end of 2023, EMTEK did not receive any complaints from the public or other stakeholders related to environmental issues, so that there was no sanction or fine applied due to non-compliance with the environmental regulations.

Managing Environmental Risks Related to Operational Activities

In carrying out its operational activities, the Company actively identifies, considers, and assesses businesses that have ESG risks. In addition, the Company also sets an annual operational target by continuously considering ESG aspects in order to achieve sustainability goals in all lines of business.

Managing Direct Impacts of the Company's Operations to the Environment

The Company is strictly committed to managing the direct impacts of its operations to the environment through a series of actions that demonstrate social and environmental responsibilities. In an effort to improve environmental performance, the Company routinely monitors, reduces, and reports energy usage, greenhouse gas emissions, paper usage, waste, and water usage in all of its operational processes, including the activities carried out by its suppliers. Furthermore, the Company actively uses environmentally friendly renewable energy, contributes to the preservation of the environment and biodiversity, as well as considers the impact of its operations to biodiversity in every investment decision.

Paying Attention to the Environmental Impact in Supply Chain

In collaboration with suppliers, the Company's main focus is to develop a sustainable process and to minimize negative impacts (the community, environment, and natural resources. The requirements for suppliers include environmental permits, risk assessment, as well as the efficiency of resources and energy to mitigate climate change. We will emphasize sustainable procurement by prioritizing infrastructure vendors with zero-carbon technology and remain neutral during the vendor selection process.

EMTEK berkomitmen untuk menerapkan prinsip-prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan dalam setiap kegiatan operasional yang dijalankan (menutuh/ketentuannya) serkat dengan aspek ESG yang berlaku. Komitmen ini sejalan dengan dukungan EMTEK terhadap pencapaian Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Hingga akhir tahun 2023, EMTEK tidak mendapatkan pengaduan dari masyarakat atau pihak pemangku kepentingan lainnya terkait permasalahan lingkungan, sehingga tidak ada sanksi atau denda yang diterapkan akibat ketidakpatuhan terhadap peraturan lingkungan.

Mengelola Risiko Lingkungan yang Terkait dengan Kegiatan Operasional

Dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasionalnya, Perseroan secara aktif mengidentifikasi, mempertimbangkan, dan menilai usaha-usaha yang memiliki risiko ESG. Selain itu, Perseroan menetapkan target kegiatan operasional tahunan dengan mempertimbangkan aspek ESG secara berkelanjutan dalam mencapai tujuan keberlanjutan di semua lini bisnisnya.

Mengelola Dampak Langsung Operasi Perusahaan terhadap Lingkungan

Perseroan secara tegas berkomitmen untuk mengelola dampak langsung operasionalnya terhadap lingkungan melalui serangkaian tindakan yang mencerminkan tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan. Dalam upaya untuk meningkatkan kinerja lingkungan, Perseroan rutin memantau, mengurangi, dan melaporkan penggunaan energi, emisi gas rumah kaca, penggunaan kertas, limbah, dan penggunaan air dalam seluruh proses operasionalnya, termasuk kegiatan yang dilakukan oleh pemasoknya. Selain itu, Perseroan aktif mengupayakan penggunaan energi terbarukan yang ramah lingkungan, berkontribusi pada pelestarian lingkungan dan keanekaragaman hayati, serta mempertimbangkan dampak operasionalnya terhadap keanekaragaman hayati dalam keputusan investasi.

Memperhatikan Dampak Lingkungan dalam Rantai Pasok

Dalam kerja sama dengan pemasok, fokus utama Perseroan adalah mengembangkan proses berkelanjutan dan mengurangi dampak negatif terhadap masyarakat, lingkungan, dan sumber daya alam. Persyaratan bagi pemasok mencakup perizinan lingkungan, penilaian risiko, serta efisiensi penggunaan sumber daya dan energi untuk mengatasi perubahan iklim. Kami akan mengedepankan pengadaan berkelanjutan melalui penetapan prioritas vendor infrastruktur teknologi karbon nol dan netral selama proses pemilihan vendor.

# Awareness and Socialization

The Company continues its environmental awareness efforts and continuous socialization by expanding technology-based initiatives. Currently, the implementation of e-memo and e-signature is a concrete approach to reduce paper usage, including the e-mail campaign with a slogan of "think before you print". Payment processes have also used digital applications in order to reduce paper usage and create efficiency in financial transactions. The Company also prioritizes energy saving by adopting the policy of electric vehicles usage for operational activities.

# Kesadaran dan Sosialisasi

Perseroan melanjutkan upaya kesadaran lingkungan dan sosialisasi keberlanjutan dengan memperluas inisiatif berbasis teknologi. Saat ini, implementasi e-memo dan e-signature menjadi langkah konkrit dalam mengurangi pemakaian kertas, melakukan kampanye melalui e-mail dengan slogan "think before you print." Proses pembayaran juga telah menggunakan aplikasi digital sehingga mengurangi penggunaan kertas dan menciptakan efisiensi dalam transaksi keuangan. Perseroan juga memprioritaskan hemat energi termasuk mengadopsi kebijakan penggunaan mobil listrik untuk kegiatan operasional.
