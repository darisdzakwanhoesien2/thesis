# Guidelines for Report Quality

# Pedoman Kualitas Laporan

[GRI 2-4] [GRI 2-14][G.1]

The highest governance body, in this case the Independent Commissioner, has the responsibility to review and approve the process of preparing this report, including approving the parties involved as stakeholders and determining material topics. To maintain the quality, the presentation of this Sustainability Report has applied the principles of:

- Accuracy
- Balance
- Clarity
- Comparability
- Completeness
- Sustainability context
- Timeliness
- Verification

This Sustainability Report is prepared by the Corporate Secretary Division with the help of external consultants, then submitted to the ESG Committee and Board of Directors for review and approval. Furthermore, the report is verified by an independent external assurance, PT TUV Rheinland Indonesia, which has no affiliation with the Company, which is then submitted to the Board of Commissioners. The use of external assurance is based on the Company's desire to ensure transparency and quality reporting to stakeholders. [GRI 2-5]

In this report, we will provide a special statement if there is information that we restate or correction of data/information that we presented in the previous report and we also explain the reasons for the restatements. [GRI 2-4]
