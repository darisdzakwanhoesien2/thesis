# Media Ethics, Freedom of Expression, and Pluralism
Etika Media, Kebebasan Berekspresi, dan Pluralisme

[SABB] SV-HE-2004.2, [SABB] SV-HE-2704.3

Media is one of the key pillars for a nation's development and is a crucial foundation as a source of information, entertainment, a democation channel to deliver ideas, opinions, and thoughts, as well as a liberation channel to improve the knowledge and intelligence of the community. Other than the benefits, media is inseparable from its risks that potentially bring negative impacts. To reduce the negative impacts on media users, guidelines or codes of conduct are required to regulate media to fully provide comprehensive benefits.

The Company pays close attention to the applicable provisions and regulations in the media industry, including the Broadcasting Law, Copyright Law, the Guidelines of the Indonesian Broadcasting Commission (KPI), the Broadcasting Code of Conduct and Broadcast Program Standards (P3SPS) as well as advertising ethics. In line with Law No. 32 of 2002 concerning Broadcasting, including broadcast information, programs, content, and other multimedia products, the Company must fulfill certain criteria.

These criteria prohibit any materials that are defamatory, inflammatory, misleading, and or untrue. In addition, the Company also ensures that the produced contents do not contain violence, obscenity, gambling, abuse of narcotics, including cigarettes. Protection of children, teenagers, and females are a priority, and respect for religious views as well as prohibition to polarize ethnicity, religion, race, and intergroup relations (SARA) is emphasized.

The Company also strictly forbids content that can embarrass, degrade, harass, and/or ignore the religious values and dignity of Indonesian citizens, as well as avoiding content that could potentially harm international relations. With an understanding that Indonesia has a large population and consists of various religious, ethnic, and cultural elements, we also continue to be committed to upholding tolerance and maintaining pluralism as a wealth value that must be maintained. The absence of any affiliation with certain political parties also confirms that the Company has independence and objectivity in carrying out broadcasting activities.

Media merupakan salah satu pilar penting bagi pembangunan bangsa dan menjadi landasan penting sebagai sumber informasi, hiburan, saluran demokrasi baik untuk menyampaikan ide, pendapat, gagasan, maupun sebagai sarana pendidikan (literasi) yang meningkatkan wawasan dan kecerdasan masyarakat. Selain manfaatnya, media tidak lepas dari risiko yang berpotensi memberi dampak negatif. Untuk mengurangi dampak negatif terhadap pengguna media, dibutuhkan pedoman atau kode etik yang mengatur agar media sepenuhnya dapat memberikan manfaat secara komprehensif.

Perseroan sangat memperhatikan ketentuan dan peraturan yang berlaku di bidang media di antaranya Undang-Undang Penyiaran, Undang-Undang Hak Cipta, Pedoman Komisi Penyiaran Indonesia (KPI), Pedoman Perilaku Penyiaran dan Standar Program Saran (P3SPS) serta etika pariwara. Sejalan dengan Undang-Undang No. 32 Tahun 2002 tentang Penyiaran, termasuk materi siaran informasi, program, konten, dan produk multimedia lainnya Perseroan harus memenuhi kriteria tertentu.

Kriteria tersebut mencakup larangan terhadap materi yang bersifat memfitnah, menghasut, menyesatkan, atau tidak benar. Pelindungan terhadap anak-anak, remaja, dan perempuan menjadi prioritas, dan rasa hormat terhadap pandangan keagamaan serta larangan mempolarisasi suku, agama, ras, dan hubungan antar golongan (SARA) ditekankan.

Perseroan juga menegaskan larangan terhadap konten yang dapat mempermalukan, merendahkan, melecehkan, dan/atau mengebaikan nilai-nilai agama dan martabat warga negara Indonesia, serta menghindari konten yang dapat berpotensi merugikan hubungan internasional. Memahami bahwa Indonesia memiliki jumlah penduduk yang besar dan terdiri dari beragam unsur agama, suku dan budaya, kami juga terus berkomitmen untuk menjunjung tinggi toleransi serta menjaga pluralisme sebagai nilai kekayaan yang harus dijaga. Tidak adanya hubungan afiliasi dengan partai politik tertentu juga menegaskan bahwa Perseroan memiliki independensi dan objektivitas dalam melaksanakan kegiatan penyiaran.

# Media Integrity

# Integritas di Bidang Media

# [SV-HE-270A.1]

In carrying out our duties as a journalist in our media division, we prioritize the ethical principles of journalism as our guideline. We are committed to maintaining independency, producing accurate, balanced, and free of ill-intention news. We constantly put forward a professional approach in each of our journalism duties, carefully review information, and broadcast news fairly without mixing facts and judgmental opinions.

Integrity is the foundation of every step we take, where we refuse to create fake, slanderous, sadistic, or obscene news. We uphold the principle of the presumption of innocence and firmly refuse to reveal the identities of victims of sexual crimes and children who are perpetrators of crimes. In addition, there is zero tolerance for professional abuse and accepting bribes.

We always respect the rights of source persons with the right to refuse in order to protect the identity of source persons. We respect the embargo, background information, and off-the-record provisions in accordance with the agreements. Attitudes of prejudice or discrimination against individuals based on ethnicity, race, color, religion, gender, or language have never been the basis of our reporting. We refuse to dehumanize the weak, poor, sick, mentally handicapped, or physically disabled.

We are committed to taking sides with the privacy rights of sources unless the information is of public interest. When errors or inaccuracies occur in the news, we are committed to immediately retracting, rectifying, and correcting them accompanied by an apology to readers, listeners and/or viewers. We also always serve the right of reply and right of correction proportionally, prioritizing truth and justice in the information we convey.

In 2023, EMTEK's subsidiary which operates in the media sector did not receive any lawsuits or sanctions resulting in monetary losses because of legal processes related to defamation or slander. Even our flagship program, Liputand.com in 2023 won the prestigious award in the Mafindo Anti-Hoax Award as Anti-Hoax Digital Media and Adyaksa Vidi Wisawan, FactChecker Liputand.com, succeeded in winning the 2023 Mafindo Anti-Hoax Award for ter-anti Hoaks (the most anti-Hoax) journalist category. This success continued at the 2024 Adam Malik Awards held by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia. Liputand.com was also named Best Online Media. This achievement is the result of consistency in fighting misinformation and disinformation through consistency in fact-checking all articles that will be presented.
