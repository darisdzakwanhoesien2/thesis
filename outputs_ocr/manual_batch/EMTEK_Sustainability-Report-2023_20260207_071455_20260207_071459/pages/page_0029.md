# Board of Directors' Report (681.3-22) [0.1]
Laporan Direksi

Overall, based on the implemented risk identification and mitigation measures, we assess that we have been able to anticipate or minimize existing risks. The Company has mitigated risks that were identified to pose major impact on the Company's performance by establishing a risk profile or risk matrix for each division at EMTEK. This approach allows all divisions to understand the risks related to their job and develop appropriate mitigation strategies. The identified risks include changes in economic conditions, changes in government regulations, as well as challenges in maintaining and improving the Company's brand and reputation. Furthermore, we have carried out mitigation efforts such as socialization and training on anti-corruption to build a work environment that has integrity, participation in various seminars or workshops to keep abreast of regulatory changes, as well as implementing social and environmental responsibility activities to gain support from stakeholders. Complete information regarding risk identification can be seen in the Risk Management section. [E.3]

The Company also understands the importance of assessing risks and opportunities related to climate change for our long-term business growth. Currently, at least in the short term, we assess that there was no physical impact experienced by EMTEK Group. However, we recognize that the threat of climate change has brought us into a transition towards a low-carbon economy. This is a challenge for the Company to continue monitoring every potential risk and opportunity in the medium and long-term period. Government regulations, customers behavior and market demands, costs of low emission technology transition, as well as reputation factors during this transition period will have a financial impact on the Company. We assess that our business activities are not in a vulnerable position in terms of climate change risks, especially when referring to the Indonesian Green Taxonomy classification.

The rapid growth of technology has changed the landscape of the media industry in Indonesia. This dynamic encourages the Company to take the right steps by preparing investments in digital infrastructure and technology in order to strengthen its lines of business. On the other hand, we see bright prospects in the hospital business. Demand for health services continues to increase along with population growth and public awareness on the importance of health. We are committed to continue developing medical infrastructures and technologies in order to strengthen our services and provide added value for patients.

Looking at the future prospects, we look forward to continuing our sustainability transformation by adopting the latest practices that are in line with the global trends and overcoming the ESG challenges faced by the Company. One of our main strategies is realized through careful investment in sustainable businesses, by utilizing artificial intelligence (AI) technology to improve the efficiency and sustainability of the Company's operations.

Secara keseluruhan, berdasarkan identifikasi risiko dan langkah mitigasi yang diambil, kami menilai telah mampu mengantisipasi atau mengurangi risiko yang ada. Langkah ini memungkinkan para divisi memahami risiko yang terkait dengan area kerjanya dan mengembangkan strategi mitigasi yang sesuai. Risiko-risiko yang diidentifikasi termasuk perubahan kondisi perekonomian, perubahan regulasi pemerintah, serta tantangan dalam mempertahankan dan meningkatkan merek serta reputasi Perseroan. Informasi lengkap mengenai identifikasi risiko dapat dilihat di bagian Manajemen Risiko. [E.3]

Namun demikian, kami menyadari bahwa ancaman perubahan iklim telah membawa kita ke dalam transisi menuju perekonomian rendah karbon. Hal ini menjadi tantangan tersendiri bagi Perseroan untuk terus memantau setiap potensi risiko dan peluang dalam jangka menengah dan jangka panjang.
