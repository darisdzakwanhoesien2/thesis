# Product, Services, and Business Activities Operated

Produk, Layanan, dan Kegiatan Usaha yang Dijalankan

[GRI 2-6] [C.4]

EMTEK is a modern and integrated holding company that oversees the ecosystem of several entities with three key businesses, including Media and Digital Division, Health Division, and Others Division (Technology, Digital Infrastructure and Financial Services). The following are the details of EMTEK Group's business activities.

EMTEK merupakan perusahaan holding modern dan terintegrasi yang menaungi ekosistem dari beberapa entitas dengan tiga usaha utama, yaitu Divisi Media dan Digital, Divisi Kesehatan, dan Divisi Lainnya (Teknologi, Infrastruktur Digital, dan Layanan Keuangan). Berikut ini adalah rincian kegiatan usaha yang dilakukan Grup EMTEK.

## Media and Digital Division

## Divisi Media dan Digital

Media and Digital Division manages Free-to-Air (FTA) television stations: SCTV, Indosiar, PT Surya Citra Pesona, Mentari TV, Ajwa TV and MOJI TV, as well as satellite television subscription broadcaster Nexparabola, along with a content production and distribution company, movies (Video), as well as a website megaportal and digital publications.

Divisi Media dan Digital mengelola stasiun televisi Free-to-Air (FTA): SCTV, Indosiar, PT Surya Citra Pesona, Mentari TV, Ajwa TV dan MOJI TV, serta penyiaran berlangganan televisi satelit yaitu Nexparabola, beserta perusahaan produksi dan distribusi konten, film dan video (Video), serta megaportal website serta publikasi digital.

## Divisi Kesehatan

## Others Division

## (Technology, Digital Infrastructure and Financial Services)

## Divisi Lainnya

## (Teknologi, Infrastruktur Digital, dan Layanan Keuangan)

Others (Technology, Digital Infrastructure, and Financial Services) manages a suite of information infrastructure and services, communications and information services and technology solutions for the telecommunication, banking, and retail payment industries, including solutions for integrated Very Small Aperture Terminal (VSAT), and services of Internet of Things (IoT) and related telecommunications services, also accommodates various initiatives and new breakthroughs in the areas of banking and financial services, technology and digital, as well as other areas in accordance with the Company's business directions.

Lainnya (Teknologi, Infrastruktur Digital, dan Layanan Keuangan) mengelola serangkaian infrastruktur dan jasa informasi, layanan komunikasi dan informatika serta solusi teknologi untuk industri telekomunikasi, perbankan, dan pembayaran ritel, termasuk solusi jasa untuk Very Small Aperture Terminal (VSAT) terintegrasi, dan layanan Internet of Things (IoT) dan layanan telekomunikasi terkait, serta mengakomodasi berbagai inisiatif dan terobosan baru dalam bidang jasa perbankan dan keuangan, teknologi dan digital, serta bidang-bidang lain yang sesuai dengan arah usaha Perseroan.

## Production House

Managing a production house that produces original TV content and animated content for FTA TV channels, pay TVs, and video streaming.

## Rumah Produksi

Mengelola kegiatan bisnis rumah produksi yang menghasilkan konten acara TV untuk saluran TV FTA, TV berbayar berlangganan, dan video streaming.

## PT Screenplay Produksi (Screenplay Productions)

A production house that produces drama series, soap operas, and films for television stations, SCTV and Indosiar. The relationship between this production house and the two TV stations forms a synergic collaboration that is expected to promote efficiency in producing quality products for viewers.

Rumah produksi yang menghasilkan serial drama, sinetron, dan film televisi untuk SCTV dan Indosiar. Hubungan rumah produksi ini dan kedua stasiun TV tersebut membentuk suatu kolaborasi sinergis yang diharapkan meningkatkan efisiensi dalam menghasilkan produk yang berkualitas bagi pemirsa.

## PT Screenplay Sinema Film (SSF)

SSF conducts business in the areas of film distribution services, program filmmaking services, production of event programs, film marketing, and other related business activities.

SSF menjalankan usaha di bidang jasa peredaran film, jasa pembuatan film program, produksi program-program acara, pemasaran film, serta kegiatan usaha terkait lainnya.

## PT Indonesia Entertainment Produksi (IEP)

A subsidiary of IEP that provides services in areas of management and managing internal production of content, films, TV drama series, music, and events to be broadcasted on TV.

Entitas anak IEP yang menyediakan jasa di bidang manajemen dan pengelolaan produksi internal konten, film, sinetron, musik, serta acara untuk disiarkan di televisi.
