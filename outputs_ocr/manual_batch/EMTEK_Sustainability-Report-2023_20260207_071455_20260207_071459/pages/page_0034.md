# Sustainable Governance Structure and Governance Composition [GRI 2-9]

Struktur Governansi Berkelanjutan dan Komposisi Governansi

To realize improvements in the assessment of the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS), the Company has several strategic steps planned. Firstly, the Company has formed a Risk Oversight Committee to enhance supervision of the risks faced. Secondly, the Company has restructured the members of the Nomination Committee and the Remuneration Committee so that the majority of members come from Independent Commissioners, who have independence in decision-making. Additionally, the Company also plans that the next Annual GMS will explicitly mention the name of the auditor who will audit the Company's financial statements.

In response to the ESG Assessment from S&amp;P Global, we have established various policies related to ESG and published them on the Company's website. These policies include: (i) Information Technology Governance Policy; (ii) Editorial Policy; (iii) Advertising Code of Ethics; (iv) Child Protection Policy; and (v) Supplier Statements and Integrity Pacts. [GRI 2-12] [GRI 412-2].

# Process to Determine Remuneration

# Proses untuk Menentukan Remunerasi

[GRI 2-20]

The amount of remuneration for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners is determined by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee based on the Company's key performance indicator and financial conditions. The Annual GMS grants authority to the Board of Commissioners to determine the remuneration for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners by considering the recommendations from the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The Board of Commissioners will then propose the remuneration to shareholders to be approved in the Annual GMS. In addition, the Company may also use consultants or other third parties in determining the amount of remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.

In determining remuneration for the Board of Directors and/or the Board of Commissioners, the Remuneration Committee considers the following aspects:

a. Financial performance and fulfilment of reserves as regulated in the prevailing laws and regulations;
b. The applicable remuneration in industries similar to the Company's business activities and the Company's scale of business in the industry;
c. Duties, responsibilities, and authorities of the Board of Directors and/or the Board of Commissioners related to the achievement of targets and performance of the Company including the achievement of ESG aspects;
d. Performance target or performance of each member of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners in order to achieve a balance between performance achievement and the remuneration received. The assessment is conducted through self-assessment and performance appraisal at least 1 (once) a year; and
e. The Company's targets and long-term strategies.

Untuk menelajisakan perbaikan penilaian atas skor ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS), Perseroan memiliki beberapalangkah strategis yang telah direncanakan. Pertama, Perseroan telah membentuk Komite Pemantau Risiko gunai meningkatkan pengawasan terhadap risiko-risiko yang dihadapi. Kedua, Perseroan telah melakukan restrukturisasi anggota Komite Nominasi dan Komite Remunerasi agar mayoritas anggotanya berasal dari Komisaris Independen, yang memiliki kemandirian dalam pengambilan keputusan. Selain itu, Perseroan juga merencanakan RUPS Tahunan selanjutnya akan menyebutkan secara eksploit nama auditor yang akan mengaudit laporan keuangan Perseroan.

Untuk menanggapi Penilaian ESG dan S&amp;P Global, kami telah menetapkan berbagai kebijakan terkait ESG dan mempublikasikannya pada situs web Perseroan. Kebijakan-kebijakan tersebut meliputi: (i) Kebijakan Tata Ketola Teknologi Informasi; (ii) Kebijakan Editorial; (iii) Kode Etik Periklanan; (iv) Kebijakan Pelindungan Anak; dan (v) Pernyataan Pemasok dan Pakta Integritas. [GRI 2-12] [GRI 412-2].

Penetapan besaran remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dilakukan oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi berdasarkan indikator kinerja utama (key performance indicator) dan kondisi kesehatan keuangan Perseroan. RUPS Tahunan memberikan wewenang kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk menentukan remunerasi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dengan mempertimbangkan rekomendasi dari Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Dewan Komisaris kemudian mengajukan proposal remunerasi kepada pemegang saham untuk disetujui pada RUPS Tahunan. Selain itu, Perseroan juga dapat menggunakan konsultan atau pihak ketiga lainnya dalam menentukan jumlah remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.

Dalam proses penentuan remunerasi Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris, Komite Remunerasi mempertimbangkan beberapa hal, sebagai berikut:

a. Kinerja keuangan dan pemenuhan cadangan sebagaimana diatur dalam peraturan perundanganan yang berlaku;
b. Remunerasi yang berlaku pada industri sesuai dengan kegiatan usaha Perseroan sejenis dan skala usaha Perseroan dalam industrinya;
c. Tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenang anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris dikaitkan dengan pencapaian tujuan dan kinerja Perseroan termasuk pencapaian aspek ESG;
d. Target kinerja atau kinerja masing-masing anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris agar tercapai kesetaraan antara pencapaian kinerja dengan imbalan yang diterima. Penilaian tersebut dilakukan melalui self-assessment (penilaian diri sendiri) dan performance appraisal (penilaian kinerja) setidaknya 1 (satu) kali dalam 1 (satu) tahun; dan
e. Pertimbangan sasaran dan strategi jangka panjang Perseroan.

# Remuneration Structure

# Struktur Remunerasi

[GRI 2-19]

The remuneration structure for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors consists of:

a. Short-term remuneration which consists of basic salary, medical allowance, transportation and communication allowance, company car in the form of car ownership program and/or annual bonus; and
b. Long-term remuneration consists of Company's share options granted varies in amount from year to year taking into account the Company's performance.

The Independent Commissioner is prohibited to own the Company's shares and/or stock option plan in order to maintain their independence. Furthermore, there is no Clowback rule for the Board of Commissioners' and Board of Directors' bonuses because the bonus is paid after the end of the relevant book year and after the financial audit reports and tax reports are reported. Bonus payments for the previous year are paid several months after the following year. [GRI 2-15]

Struktur remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi terbagi atas:

a. Remunerasi jangka pendek yang terdiri dari gaji pokok, tunjangan kesehatan, tunjangan transportasi dan komunikasi, mobil dinas dalam bentuk program kepemilikan mobil (car ownership program) dan/atau bonus tahunan; dan
b. Remunerasi jangka panjang yang terdiri dari opsi kepemilikan saham Perseroan yang diberikan bervariasi jumlahnya dari tahun ke tahun dengan mempertimbangkan kinerja Perseroan.

Komisaris Independen tidak diperbolehkan memiliki saham Perseroan dan/atau opsi kepemilikan saham Perseroan dengan tujuan agar independensinya tetap terjaga. Selain itu, tidak ada aturan Clowback untuk bonus Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi karena pembayaran bonus dilaksanakan setelah tahun buku yang bersangkutan berakhir dan laporan audit keuangan serta laporan perpajakan dilaporkan. Pembayaran bonus untuk tahun sebelumnya dibayarkan beberapa bulan pada tahun berikutnya. [GRI 2-15]

The amount of remuneration for each member of the Board of Directors awaits approval from shareholders to be disclosed to the public. Total salaries and compensation to EMTEK's Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners in 2023 amounted to Rp153,202,005,638, decreased by 16% compared to the previous year of Rp182,880,366,805. The Company does not disclose the ratio of the highest-paid individual compensation to the median compensation of all employees because it is confidential. [GRI 2-21]

Besaran masing-masing remunerasi anggota Direksi menunggu persetujuan dari pemegang saham untuk disampaikan kepada publik. Total gaji dan kompensasi kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris EMTEK selama tahun 2023 adalah sebesar Rp153,202.005.638, yang turun sebesar 16% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya sebesar Rp182.880.366.805. Perseroan tidak mengungkapkan rasio kompensasi individu dengan bayaran tertinggi terhadap median (nilai tengah) kompensasi seluruh karyawan karena bersifat rahasia. [GRI 2-21]

# Statement of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners on the Adequacy of the Company's Capital and Debt Structure Pernyataan Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris atas Kecukupan Struktur Modal dan Utang Perseroan

Throughout 2023, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners maintained and managed the Company's assets including conducting periodic reviews of the Company's capital and debt structure to ensure alignment with strategic goals and risk appetite.

Sepanjang tahun 2023, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris memelihara dan mengurus kekayaan Perseroan termasuk melakukan perintauan berkala terhadap struktur modal dan utang Perseroan guna memastikan keselarasan dengan tujuan strategis dan tingkat toleransi risiko.
