# Topik Material

# [GRI 3-1] [GRI 3-2]

In accordance with the reporting guidelines that we (GRI Standards), we determine what topics are important to the Company (affecting organizational value) to be presented in this report. The Company prioritizes reporting on topics that reflect the most significant impact on the economy, social, and environment, including the impact on human rights. Considering that the GRI Standards guidelines do not yet provide specific references for industries that are in line with the Company's business activities, we determine material topics by considering input from stakeholders, impacts or risks arising both positive and negative related to the Company's business activities on stakeholders, and analysis of sustainability trends or issues that are developing and relevant to the Company's industry, including support for sustainable development.

This year, we have specifically distributed questionnaires to stakeholders to get an idea on what their main concerns are. Through this questionnaire and the stakeholder engagement described above, the highest governance organ and the ESG Committee conducted an analysis to determine the material topics in this report.

It is necessary to inform that the questionnaire distributed was filled in by 87 respondents consisting of: 39 men and 48 women who were divided into several stakeholder categories.

In determining materiality, we do not make exceptions (reason of omission).

After collecting all responses from respondents, the data is processed and sorted based on the topics most frequently chosen by stakeholders. In the next stage, the sustainability report preparation team together with the ESG Committee and management carries out mapping and provides assessments on various topics. These topics are then sorted based on their priority scale and relevance to the Company. At the final stage, the Company's highest governance organ considers and approves the determination of this material topic.

Sesuai dengan pedoman laporan yang kami gunakan (GRI Standards), kami menentukan topik apa saja yang menjadi penting bagi Perseroan (memengaruhi nilai organisasi) untuk disampaikan dalam laporan ini. Perseroan memprioritaskan pelaporan mengenai topik-topik yang mencerminkan dampak paling signifikan terhadap ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan, termasuk dampak terhadap hak asasi manusia. Mengingat pedoman GRI Standards belum menyediakan rujukan khusus untuk industri yang sesuai dengan kegiatan usaha Perseroan, maka kami menetapkan topik material dengan mempertimbangkan masukan dari pemangku kepentingan, dampak atau risiko yang ditimbulkan baik positif maupun negatif terkait kegiatan usaha Perseroan terhadap pemangku kepentingan, dan analisis tren keberlanjutan atau isu yang sedang berkembang dan relevan dengan industri Perseroan termasuk dukungan terhadap pembangunan berkelanjutan.

Secara khusus, tahun ini kami telah menyebarkan kuesioner kepada pemangku kepentingan untuk mendapatkan gambaran tentang hal apa yang menjadi perhatian utama mereka. Melalui kuesioner ini dan pelibatan pemangku kepentingan yang telah diuraikan di atas, badan tata kelola tertinggi beserta Komite ESG melakukan analisis untuk menentukan topik material dalam laporan ini.

Compared to the previous year's report, there are some changes that are not significant, such as:

- Unlike last year, this year the topics of Human Rights and Inclusiveness/Non-Discrimination are combined into one topic.
- The topic of local suppliers is combined into the topic of "Providing economic benefits to stakeholders".
- The topic of customer confidentiality data (418-1) is now included in the topic "Conducting business with integrity and complying with all regulations including professional codes of conduct".
- Disclosure of new supplier selection using social (414-1) and environmental (308-1) criteria is included in the topic "Conducting business with integrity and complying with all regulations including professional codes of conduct".
- Employee recruitment and turnover disclosures (401-1) are no longer material in this report, but we will still present the information in this report.

Dibandingkan laporan tahun sebelumnya, terdapat beberapa perubahan yang sifatnya tidak signifikan, seperti:

- Berbeda dengan tahun lalu, tahun ini topik mengenai Hak Asasi Manusia dan Inklusivitas/Non-Diskriminasi digabungkan dalam satu topik.
- Topik pemasok lokal digabungkan kedalam topik "Memberikan manfaat ekonomi kepada pemangku kepentingan".
- Topik data kerahasiaan pelanggan (418-1) saat ini dimasukkan kedalam topik "Menjalankan bisnis yang berintegritas dan mematuhi seluruh regulasi termasuk kode etik profesi".
- Pengungkapan seleksi pemasok baru dengan menggunakan kriteria sosial (414-1) dan lingkungan (308-1) dimasukkan kedalam topik "Menjalankan bisnis yang berintegritas dan mematuhi seluruh regulasi termasuk kode etik profesi".
- Pengungkapan perekrutan dan pergantian karyawan (401-1) tidak lagi menjadi material dalam laporan ini, namun kami tetap akan menyajikan informasi tersebut dalam laporan ini.
