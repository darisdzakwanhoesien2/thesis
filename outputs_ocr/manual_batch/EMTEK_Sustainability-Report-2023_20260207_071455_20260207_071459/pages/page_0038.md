# Commitment to Sustainable Business Practices

Komitmen Menjalankan Bisnis Berkelanjutan

[FJ] [GRI 2-24]

To lead EMTEK Group to sustainable growth, we are committed to upholding the best governance principles in all of our operational aspects. The basic principles, such as transparency, accountability, social and environmental responsibility, and independence are implemented comprehensively and strictly supervised. EMTEK Group implements a supervision system in order to ensure ethical and responsible decision-making. This system monitors the integrity of financial reporting, thoroughness of risk management, as well as enforcing proper behavior from all employees and subsidiaries. The following are the policies that we have implemented in order to ensure that the Company's governance practices run well.

Untuk membawa Grup EMTEK tumbuh secara berkelanjutan, kami berkomitmen untuk menjunjung tinggi prinsip governansi yang baik di seluruh aspek operasionalnya. Prinsip-prinsip dasar, seperti keterbukaan, akuntabilitas, tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan, dan independensi, diterapkan secara komprehensif dan diawasi dengan ketat. Grup EMTEK menerapkan sistem pengawasan untuk menjamin pengambilan keputusan yang etis dan bertanggung jawab. Sistem ini mencakup pemantauan integritas dalam pelaporan keuangan, manajemen risiko yang cermat, serta menegakan perilaku yang layak dari seluruh karyawan dan entitas perusahaan. Berikut ini adalah kebijakan yang kami terapkan guna memastikan praktik governansi di Perseroan berjalan dengan baik.

# Standard Policy of Code of Conduct

[GRI 2-23] [GRI 2-24]

The standard policy of code of conduct in EMTEK is the basic guideline of policies that are applicable to all individuals within EMTEK Group in carrying out its business operations. All policies made by the Board of Directors of the Company have been approved and signed by all members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners including all members of the Independent Commissioners.

In our internal policymaking, we also use several mechanisms to obtain advice and input from stakeholders, including regulators and governments. These measures involve identifying relevant stakeholders, providing open opportunities for consultation, holding formal meetings and discussions, using surveys or questionnaires, building partnerships and collaborations, hiring experts or consultants, and conducting analysis and evaluation of various viewpoints and recommendations provided. Through this process, the Company ensures that the mechanisms or methods used in developing policies can reflect the various perspectives and needs of the various parties involved, so that the suggestions provided can obtain adequate and relevant responses.

Comprehensively, this code of conduct ensures that each individual understands the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, to employees and can be accessed through the Company's website. It also stipulates the responsibility to report any deviations, careful handling of confidential information, and providing equal career opportunities to all. # Initiative on External Policy

Inisiatif terhadap Pedoman Eksternal

As a good corporate citizen, EMTEK Group strives to adopt various sustainability-based principles. Including the Women Empowerment Principles (WEPs) which in principle encourage fair treatment to all individuals in the workplace without discrimination, both women and men. In addition, EMTEK Group is also committed to the 10 (ten) principles contained in the United Nations Global Compact (UNGC) which oversees the aspects of human rights, workforce, environment, and anti-corruption. The details of the principle have been attached in the report index section. Not only that, EMTEK Group also implements international standards in all of its chain values, including the ISO 27001:2013 concerning information security management system, ISO 9001:2015 concerning quality management system, ISO 37001:2016 concerning anti-bribery management system, and ISO 45001:2015 concerning occupational health and safety management system.

Sebagai warga korporasi yang baik, Grup EMTEK berusaha mengadopsi berbagai prinsip berbasis pada bisnis berkelanjutan. Di antaranya adalah Women Empowerment Principles (WEPs) yang secara-prinsip mendorong perlakuan adil pada terhadap semua individu di tempat kerja tanpa adanya diskriminasi, baik pada perempuan maupun laki-laki. Selain itu, Grup EMTEK juga berkomitmen pada 10 (sepuluh) prinsip yang terdapat dalam United Nations Global Compact (UNGC) yang memperhatikan aspek hak asasi manusia, tenaga kerja, lingkungan, dan anti-korupsi. Rincian-prinsip tersebut telah terlampir pada bagian indeks laporan. Tidak hanya itu, Grup EMTEK juga menerapkan standarisasi berskala internasional untuk meningkatkan standar atau kualitas di seluruh rantai nilai perusahaan seperti, ISO 27001:2013 tentang manajemen sistem keamanan informasi, ISO 9001:2015, tentang sistem manajemen mutu, ISO 37001:2016 tentang sistem manajemen anti-penyuapan, dan ISO 45001:2018 tentang sistem manajemen keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja.
