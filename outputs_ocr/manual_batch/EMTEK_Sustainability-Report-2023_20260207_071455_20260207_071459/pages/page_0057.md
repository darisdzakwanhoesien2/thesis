# Mechanism of Waste and Effluents Management Mekanisme Pengelolaan Limbah dan Effluent

[F.14]

As a holding company, EMTEK does not produce industrial waste or hazardous waste which can significantly impact the surrounding environment. The waste generated from EMTEK's operations at its head office is limited to non-hazardous office waste. Waste management from head office operations is handled by building management. Further information regarding waste and waste management from subsidiaries can be found in the Sustainability Report for each subsidiary which can be accessed widely. EMTEK is committed to maintaining sustainability principles and continuing to improve responsible environmental practices.

Totaal Generated Waste and Effluents in 2023 [F.13]

# Effluents

Considering the scope of EMTEK Holding, we do not produce liquid waste, so we do not manage any liquid waste. The generated liquid waste is household and the management is carried out by the building management.

On the other hand, at Indosiar's office in Daan Mogot, West Jakarta, we have a Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) to ensure that wastewater from our operations is treated first before being discharged into the sewer. The total effluents discharged in 2023 were 7,282 M3. All effluents have undergone a treatment process before being discharged to fulfill the specified water quality standards.

In 2023, we did not receive complaints or sanctions due to wastewater discharge.

# Effluent

Mengingat ruang lingkup EMTEK Holding, kami tidak menghasilkan limbah cair sehingga tidak ada pengelolaan limbah cair yang kami lakukan. Adapun limbah cair yang dihasilkan bersifat rumah tangga dan pengelolaannya dilaksanakan oleh pihak pengelola gedung.

Sementara itu, di kantor Indosiar, Daan Mogot, Jakarta Barat, kami memiliki Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL) untuk memastikan air limbah dari operasional kami diolah terlebih dahulu sebelum dialirkan ke saluran pembuangan. Total efluent yang dibuang pada tahun 2023 adalah 7.282 m³. Seluruh efluent tersebut sudah melalui proses pengolahan sebelum dibuang sehingga memenuhi baku mutu air yang ditetapkan.

Selama tahun 2023, kami tidak menerima pengaduan atau sanksi yang disebabkan pembuangan limbah air.
