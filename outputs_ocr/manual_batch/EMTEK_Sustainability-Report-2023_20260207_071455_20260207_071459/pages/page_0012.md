Taking advantage of its rapid growth in the digital economic sector in Indonesia, the Company continues to drive its expansion in the digital industry, including through the investment of its subsidiary, PT Kreatif Media Karya (KMK), in one of Indonesia's leading marketplaces, Bukalapak.com, in 2015. Then, EMTEK Group realized a series of corporate actions in the digital sector, such as logistics technology, education technology, insurance technology, and online transportation through our investment in Grab Indonesia, one of Indonesia's leading ride-hailing companies. Strengthening the digital technology-based business portfolio is also done by realizing investment in PT Kapan Lagi Dot Com Networks (KLY) which manages several well-known digital portals with diverse target segments, namely Liputan6.com, Merdeka.com, Dream.co.id, Fimela, Brilio.com, Kapanlagi.com, Bola.com, Bola.net, and Otosia.com.

The Company at the same time develops an Over-the-Top (OTT) streaming platform, Vidio, which excels in providing engaging entertainment and sports content, including on demand videos. Carrying the concept of originality of content and information, Vidio now secures the position of #1 streaming platform in terms of Total Streaming Minutes and Total Monthly Active Users in Indonesia in Q2/2023. Leading in the Mobile Video Streaming Segment with 67% users for all user categories, by Eureka AI in June 2023, won Best Millennials Brand Choice 2023 for Movie Streaming Category, Google Cloud released global case study discussing Vidio as #1 OTT Streaming App in Indonesia &amp; #1 Top Grossing Android App in Entertainment in Indonesia, even achieved Great Place to Work certification in 2023 for the period of February 2023 – February 2024 by achieving a score of 92% for the Great Place to Work Statement and 84% for the Average Trust Index Score.

The Company continued to put its stronger grip on the media industry as we welcomed on board MOJI TV, initially the result of a rebranding of O Channel to target the youth of aged 15-40 years old with its sport, lifestyle, and entertainment TV programs, and Mentari TV aimed at targeting kids and youth segments. Following the implementation of Analog Switch Off (ASO) in Indonesia starting in November 2022 which led to migration from analog TV to digital TV, EMTEK's media portfolios through December 2023 led across market segments with 37.1% market shares (SCTV 14.3%, IVM TV 13.0%, Mentari TV 7.6%, MOJI TV 2.2%).

Perseroan di saat yang sama juga mengembangkan sebuah platform streaming Over-the-Top (OTT), Vidio, yang memiliki keunggulan dalam penyediaan konten hiburan dan olahraga yang menarik, termasuk on demand video. Perseroan terus memperkuat posisinya di industri media digital dengan meluncurkan MOJI TV, yang merupakan hasil rebranding terhadap televisi O Channel untuk menyasar-segmen anak muda usia 15-40 tahun dengan tayangan program olahraga, gaya hidup, dan hiburan yang sesuai, serta Mentari TV yang menyasar-segmen anak-anak dan anak muda. Seiring dengan penerapan kebijakan Analog Switch Off (ASO) di Indonesia mulai November 2022 yang memicu peralihan penyiaran program televisi dari analog ke digital, portofolio EMTEK di segmen media hingga Desember 2023 masih menunjukkan yang terdegan dalam hal penguasaan pangsa pasar, yaitu 37,1% (SCTV 14,3%, IVM TV 13,0%, Mentari TV 7,6%, MOJI TV 2,2%).

Meanwhile, in the digital finance sector, the expansion was conducted by our subsidiary, PT Elang Media Visitama, which acquired 93% ownership of PT Bank Fama International (Bank FAMA) at the end of December 2021. In order to strengthen its position in the digital banking industry, Bank FAMA in early 2023 changed its name to PT Super Bank Indonesia (Superbank) followed by strengthening its competency through a strategic collaboration with KakaoBank which acquired 10% of Superbank's shares through the issuance of new shares. This partnership will improve Superbank's competitiveness in the development of its digital financial products and services as well as bolster its regional penetration, thus indirectly contributing to the positive prospect of EMTEK Group's long-term development in serving the needs of underbanked customers, especially MSMEs and retail customers.
