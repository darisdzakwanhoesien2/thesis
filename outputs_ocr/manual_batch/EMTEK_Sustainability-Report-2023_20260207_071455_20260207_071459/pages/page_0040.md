# Protection of Intellectual Property Rights

# Pelindungan Hak Atas Kekayaan Intelektual

# [SASB] SV-ME-520A.1, [GRI 2-24]

Intellectual creativity and innovation are abilities that must be protected, respected, and appreciated. Because intellectual creativity has provided benefits, it is appropriate and for the sake of justice, that we also provide economic benefits to copyright owners. This approach will certainly motivate each party to continue producing their best work.

EMTEK Group is committed to respecting intellectual property rights as well as ensuring that broadcast programs and content from non-subsidiaries are legally obtained from parties who hold the property rights. We always provide economic incentives for creators and prevent counterfeit goods or works. The Company has also registered the Copyright, Trademark Rights, and Patent Rights of every work and product. Currently, EMTEK Group has 160 Copyright Rights, 1,080 Trademark Rights, and 1 Patent Rights which have been registered in the Directorate General of Intellectual Property, Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia.

Daya kreasi dan inovasi intelektual merupakan sebuah kemampuan yang perlu dilindungi, dihormati, dan dihargai. Karena kegiatan kreatif dari daya pikir tersebut telah memberi manfaat sehingga sudah selayaknya dan demi keadilan, kita juga memberi keuntungan ekonomi kepada pemilik hak cipta. Pendekatan ini tentunya akan memberikan motivasi bagi setiap pihak untuk terus menghasilkan karya terbaiknya.

Grup EMTEK berkomitmen menghormati hak kekayaan intelektual serta memastikan program siaran dan konten dari luar anak perusahaannya dipersileh secara sah dari pihak yang memegang hak milik. Kami senantiasa memberikan insentif ekonomi bagi pencipta, serta mencegah pembuatan barang atau karya tiruan. Perseroan juga telah mendaftarkan Hak Cipta, Hak Merek, dan Hak Paten atas setiap karya dan produk yang dimilikinya. Saat ini Grup EMTEK memiliki 160 Hak Cipta, 1.080 Hak Merek, dan 1 Hak Paten yang telah didaftarkan di Direktorat Jenderal Kekayaan Intelektual, Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia.

Throughout 2023, the Company did not receive material sanctions for violations of intellectual property rights for broadcast programs and content which have legally binding court decisions.

Sepanjang tahun 2023, Perseroan tidak menerima sanksi material atas pelanggaran hak kekayaan intelektual untuk program siaran dan konten yang memiliki keputusan pengadilan yang mengikat secara hukum.

# Policy on Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Terrorism Financing

# Kebijakan Anti-Korupsi, Anti-Penyuapan, dan Anti-Gratifikasi

# [GRI 205-2]

As a form of good governance practices implementation and as a strong foundation for the implementation of sustainable business, the Company continues to instill a culture of integrity and is free from all conflicts of interest aimed directly or indirectly to make individual benefits. The Company consistently socializes Anti-Corruption, Bribery and Illegal Donations Policy to all employees, aiming to ensure understanding and compliance at all levels of the organization. Meanwhile, the Company continues to actively update the policy in accordance with the developments of applicable laws and regulations in Indonesia.

In order to strengthen this commitment, the Company has held various socialization and training activities related to Anti-Corruption, bribery and gratification, including training with the title of "Enable Business Agility with Anti-Corruption &amp; Anti-Bribery Practices" which was attended by the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors and employees of EMTEK, as well as the Board Commissioners and Board of Directors of EMTEK's subsidiaries on 8 May 2023. In addition, the "Awareness Training of ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System" training on 19 September 2023 was attended by the Board of Directors and all employees of EMTEK. On this occasion, the Company reiterated that there is no tolerance for anyone who commits acts of corruption, bribery, and gratification. Collective awareness is needed to create a work environment that has integrity; therefore, we also provide a complaint channel for employees to submit reports if there are indications of corruption acts within the organization.

Other than anti-corruption socialization and training programs, the Company continues to encourage all subsidiaries to adopt the ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System. Currently, EMTEK as a holding company has also succeeded in achieving ISO 37001:2016 certification issued by CBQA Global on 17 November 2023, valid until 16 November 2026. In line with the commitment to ensure the implementation of high anti-bribery standards, several EMTEK subsidiaries have obtained this certification, including PT Abhimata Citra Abadi and PT Estha Yudha Ekatama.

Communication and training on anti-corruption policies and procedures. [GRI 205-2]

Sebagai perwujudan melaksanakan praktik governansi yang baik dan sebagai dasar kuat pelaksanaan bisnis berkelanjutan, Perseroan terus menanamkan budaya integritas dan bebas dari segala konflik kepentingan yang bertujuan menguntungkan diri pribadi secara langsung atas tidak langsung. Perseroan secara konsisten mersosialisasikan Kebijakan Anti-Korupsi, Penyuapan, dan Sumbangan Illegal kepada seluruh karyawan secara rutin, bertujuan untuk memastikan pemahaman dan kepatuhan di seluruh tingkat organisasi. Sementara itu, Perseroan terus aktif memperbarui kebijakan tersebut sesuai dengan perkembangan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku di Indonesia.

Dalam rangka memperkuat komitmen tersebut, Perseroan telah mengadakan beragam kegiatan sosialisasi dan pelatihan terkait Anti-Korupsi, penyuapan dan gratifikasi diantaranya pelatihan bertajuk "Enable Business Agility with Anti-Corruption &amp; Anti-Bribery Practices" yang dihadiri oleh Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan karyawan EMTEK, serta Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi anak perusahaan EMTEK pada tanggal 8 Mei 2023. Selain itu, pelatihan "Awareness Training ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System" pada tanggal 19 September 2023 diikuti oleh Direksi dan seluruh karyawan EMTEK. Dalam kesempatan tersebut, Perseroan kembali menegaskan tidak ada toleransi bagi siapapun juga yang melakukan tindakan korupsi, penyuapan dan gratifikasi. Diperlukan kesadaran bersama untuk menciptakan lingkungan kerja berintegritas, oleh karena ini kami juga menyediakan saluran pengaduan bagi karyawan untuk menyampaikan laporan jika terdapat indikasi tindakan korupsi di tubuh organisasi.

Selain sosialisasi dan pelatihan terkait anti-korupsi, Perseroan terus mendorong agar seluruh anak perusahaan mengadakan aturan ISO 37001:2016 Sistem Manajemen Anti-Suap. Saat ini, EMTEK sebagai perusahaan holding juga telah berhasil merah sertifikasi ISO 37001:2016 yang diterbitkan oleh CBQA Global pada tanggal 17 November 2023 berlaku hingga 16 November 2026. Sejalan dengan komitmen untuk memastikan penerapan standar anti-suap yang tinggi, beberapa anak Perusahaan EMTEK yang telah memperoleh sertifikasi ini termasuk PT Abhimata Citra Abadi dan PT Estha Yudha Ekatama.
