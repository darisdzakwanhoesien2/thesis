## Pension Program

### Program Pensiun

[GRI 201-3] [GRI 404-2] [GRI 2-23] [GRI 401-2]

The Company provides a pension program to plan the long-term economic welfare of employees. This benefit provides peace of mind for employees in preparing for their old age and increase their motivation and performance. The following is the pension program for employees aged 55 years old:

[GRI 3-3]

1. BPJS Pension Program, which is paid by employees from 1% of their wages per month
2. BPJS JHT, which is paid by employees from 2% their monthly wages

In addition to the pension program above, the Company provides training to prepare employees approaching retirement age so they can remain productive. This program is prepared one year before retirement with a variety of interesting topics such as entrepreneurship, presentation techniques, and other relevant topics. Our pension program is fully funded using internal company funds without assistance/funding from external parties. [GRI 3-3]
