# Equal Work Opportunities and Human Rights [GRI 2-23] [GRI 406-1] [GRI 408-1] [GRI 409-1] [1 16] [1 19]

Kesetaraan Kesempatan Bekerja dan Hak Asasi Manusia

Providing clarity and appropriate rewards in any suspension, termination of employment (PHK) and accelerated retirement/early retirement;

Memberikan kejelasan dan imbalan yang sesuai dalam setiap tindakan skorsing, pemutusan hubungan kerja (PHK) dan pensiun dini;

Providing facilities for workers to convey and resolve grievances;

Menyediakan fasilitas bagi pekerja untuk menyampaikan dan menyelesaikan keluh kesah;

The Company is committed to providing a safe environment for all its employees that is free from discrimination on any basis and from harassment in the workplace including sexual harassment. Sexual harassment is unwanted sexual behavior that makes someone feel offended, humiliated and/or intimidated. It includes situations in which a person is asked to engage in sexual activity as an employment requirement, as well as situations that create a hostile, intimidating or humiliating environment. Sexual harassment can consist of one or more incidents and acts that constitute harassment and can be either physical, verbal or non-verbal;

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menyediakan lingkungan yang aman bagi seluruh karyawannya yang bebas dari diskriminasi atas dasar apapun dan dari pelecehan di tempat kerja termasuk pelecehan seksual. Pelecehan seksual adalah perilaku seksual yang tidak diinginkan yang membuat seseorang merasa tersinggung, terhina dan/atau terintimidasi. Ini termasuk situasi di mana seseorang diminta untuk melakukan aktivitas hubungan seksual sebagai syarat pekerjaan orang tersebut, serta situasi yang menciptakan lingkungan yang bermusuhan, mengintimidasi atau mempermalukan penerima. Pelecehan seksual dapat terdiri dari satu atau lebih kejadian dan tindakan yang merupakan pelecehan dan dapat berupa baik fisik, verbal ataupun nonverbal;

The Company intolerances with any form of sexual harassment in the workplace, treats all cases seriously and promptly investigates all allegations of sexual harassment. Any person who found to sexually harass another person will get disciplinary action, and will be dismissed from work, and will be reported as a criminal offense in accordance with applicable laws and regulations;

Perseroan tidak membelaransi untuk segala bentuk pelecehan seksual di tempat kerja, memperlakukan semua kejadian dengan serius dan segera menyelidiki semua dugaan pelecehan seksual. Setiap orang yang ditemukan melakukan pelecehan seksual terhadap orang lain akan menghadapi tindakan disipliner, hingga dan termasuk pemecatan dari pekerjaan, dan dilaporkan sebagai tindak pidana sesuai dengan Peraturan Perundang-undangan yang berlaku;

All complaints of sexual harassment will be taken seriously and treated with respect and confidential. No one will be victimized for making such a complaint.

Semua keluhan terhadap pelecehan seksual akan ditanggapi dengan serius dan diperlakukan dengan hormat dan rahasia. Tidak ada yang akan menjadi korban karena membuat keluhan seperti itu.

The commitment to respect and uphold human rights is not limited to the Company internally but also applies in the relation with business partners and the communities. The implementation of this Policy is coordinated, monitored and evaluated by the ESG Committee and then reported annually to the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners and all stakeholders through the Company's Sustainability Report. As a mutual commitment to establishing an inclusive work environment, the Company held "Diversity &amp; Inclusivity" training delivered by Erika Swarandani as Chief Equality, Diversity and Inclusivity Officer. This training was attended by all EMTEK's employees and Board of Directors.

As a form of our commitment and transparency, we encourage the participation of all parties to submit complaints of any indications of human rights violations through the available complaint channels, such as the whistleblowing system, customer or community complaint channels. Commitment to uphold human rights, equality and anti-discrimination encourages the creation of an inclusive and harmonious work environment. For this commitment, the Company through its subsidiary Vidio received the Great Place to Work certification.

In 2023, there were no complaints or incidents related to human rights violations committed by the Company, whether within the Company, business partners or the public.

# Gender Equality and Anti-Sexual Harassment Kesetaraan Gender dan Anti-Pelecehan Seksual

The Company pays close attention and strives to ensure that the gender equality policy is carried out consistently in its internal operations. This policy focuses on:

- Recruitment, selection, and promotion
- Professional development
- Gender equality for leadership in workplace
- Wage equality
- Safe and inclusive workplace
- Flexible work arrangements
- Preventing, addressing harassment and discrimination
- Anti-discriminatory procurement practices
- Gender-responsive product design and development and marketing

Specifically for preventing sexual violence within the workplace, the Company has established an Anti-Sexual Violence in Work Place Task Force. The structure of the Task Force consists of management and employees, namely: Titi Maria Rusli (Chair), Erika Swarandani (Secretary), Andre William Kurnia (Member), Nyayu Noor Natasha Dewi Akiel (Member), Bella Christella (Member), Beverly Gunawan (Member), Member), Eunike Agustin (Member), and Eay Azhary Putra (Member). It is expected that the diversity of individuals in the Task Force who have different skills and backgrounds will further enrich the Task Force's perspective. In the previous year, EMTEK also appointed Erika Swarandani as Chief Equality, Diversity, and Inclusivity Officer ("CEDI") to promote diversity within the Company.

Perseroan sangat memperhatikan dan berupaya memastikan bahwa kebijakan kesetaraan gender dijalankan secara konsisten di internal operasional Perseroan. Kebijakan ini berfokus pada:

- Rekrutmen, seleksi, dan promosi
- Pengembangan profesional
- Kesetaraan gender di tempat kerja untuk kepemimpinan
- Kesetaraan upah
- Tempat kerja yang aman dan inklusif
- Pengaturan kerja yang fleksibel
- Mencegah, mengatasi pelecehan, dan diskriminasi
- Praktik pengaduan yang anti-diskriminasi
- Desain dan pengembangan serta pemasaran produk yang responsif terhadap gender

# The Handling of Complaints Penanganan Pengaduan

The complaint process begins with reporting to the Task Force via a form that must be filled in by the reporter or via whistleblowing e-mail. Task force members are also tasked with monitoring and being able to report suspected violence or harassment that occurs. Task Force meetings are held four times a year to discuss various issues related to employee safety and welfare. Furthermore, follow-up actions are carried out if there is a potential violation or violation that occurs. This action will be processed in accordance with applicable regulations or Company regulations. [GRI 406-1]

In an effort to improve the competence of the Head of the Task Force and CEDI, both of them actively participate in relevant trainings. One of them is the training "Breaking the Bias: Women and Artificial Intelligence to Bridge Gender Inequality" on 8 March 2023, which was organized by IBM Indonesia together with Microsoft Indonesia and Bisnis Indonesia. The training discussed the importance of involving women in the development of AI technology to overcome the gender gap.
