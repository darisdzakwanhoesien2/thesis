# Literasi

Providing a series of insights, abilities, skills and certain expertise needed in everyday life to the general public is part of EMTEK Group initiative to contribute to improving the quality of society.

Memberikan serangkaian wawasan, kemampuan, keterampilan, keahlian tertentu yang diperlukan dalam kehidupan sehari-hari kepada masyarakat umum menjadi bagian dari inisiatif Grup EMTEK dalam berkontribusi bagi peningkatan kualitas Masyarakat.

On various occasions we have held the following literacy activities:

Dalam berbagai kesempatan kami telah melakukan kegiatan literasi, di antaranya sebagai berikut:

On 13 June 2023, the Company held a Company Visit for 2023 Asian Financial Leaders Program, attended by 53 Top Executive Leaders (CEO or CFO) from various financial institutions in Singapore. The event aimed at introducing the profile of EMTEK and Vidio to the company executives, providing critical insight into leadership and company development, as well as integrating and strengthening participants' capabilities in understanding the complexity of regional and global economic landscape. The Director of EMTEK and CEO of Vidio, Sutanto Hartono, gave opening remarks before presenting the material, followed by a question-and-answer session. The event was closed by a media visit to the Vidio office and SCM newsroom, presenting an in-depth experience regarding media literacy implemented by EMTEK.

On 9 November 2023, the Company held a Media Visit to EMTEK, SCM, and Vidio which were attended by 30 students from Bogor Agricultural Institute. On this occasion, we introduced Media Relations in the realm of Public Relations in the world of work to the younger generation. This event is an educational means to provide experience related to the work atmosphere and provide an overview of the job market to the participants.

Pada tanggal 13 Juni 2023, Perseroan menggelar Company Visit for Asian Financial Leaders Program 2023, dihadiri oleh 53 Top Executive Leaders (CEO or CFO) dari berbagai lembaga keuangan di Singapura. Kegiatan ini bertujuan untuk memperkenalkan profil EMTEK dan Vidio kepada para eksekutif perusahaan, memberikan wawasan kritis dalam kepemimpinan dan pengembangan perusahaan, serta mengintegrasikan dan memperkuat kemampuan peserta dalam memahami kompleksitas lanskap ekonomi regional dan global. Direktur EMTEK dan CEO Vidio, Sutanto Hartono, memberikan sambutan pembukaan sebelum memberikan pemaparan materi, diikuti oleh sesi tanya jawab. Acara ditutup dengan media visit ke kantor Vidio dan newsroom SCM, menyajikan pengalaman mendalam mengenai literasi media yang diterapkan oleh EMTEK.

Pada 9 November 2023, Perseroan mengadakan kegiatan Media Visit to EMTEK, SCM, dan Vidio yang diikuti oleh 30 Mahasiswa Institut Pertanian Bogor. Dalam kesempatan tersebut kami memperkenalkan Media Relation dalam ranah Public Relation di dunia kerja kepada generasi muda. Kegiatan ini merupakan sarana edukasi untuk memberikan pengalaman terkait suasana kerja dan memberikan gambaran lapangan kerja kepada para peserta.

In addition, on 6 December 2023, we hold Media Visit event to EMTEK, SCM, and Vidio which were attended by 102 Bogor Agricultural Institute students. This event aimed at introducing and exploring the potential and interest in the world of journalism in the digital era to the younger generation.

Sementara itu pada 6 Desember 2023, kami kembali mengadakan kegiatan Media Visit to EMTEK, SCM, dan Vidio yang diikuti 102 Mahasiswa Institut Pertanian Bogor. On 24-25 February 2023, Ms. Titi Maria Rusli, a representative of the Board of Directors, participated in a cataract surgery and grocery distribution social service in Manado, North Sulawesi, in conjunction with the 28th anniversary of Indosiar. The event was organized by YPP in collaboration with PERDAMI North Sulawesi at the Main Eye Clinic of North Sulawesi Province, with a total of successfully operated beneficiaries and 100 grocery packages distributed.

Pada 24-25 Februari 2023, Ibu Titi Maria Rusli, perwakilan Direksi, berpartisipasi dalam bakti sosial operasi katarak dan pembagian sembako di Manado, Sulawesi Utara, dalam rangka HUT Indosiar ke-28. Acara ini diselenggarakan oleh YPP bekerja sama PERDAMI Sulawesi Utara di Klinik Mata Utama Provinsi Sulawesi Utara, dengan total penerima manfaat orang berhasil dioperasi dan 100 paket sembako dibagikan.

In addition, on 1 December 2023, Ms. Titi Maria Rusli also served as a speaker at the Birama Hukum Batch V Alumni Meeting at the Faculty of Law, Parahyangan University, Bandung. During this activity, Ms. Titi Maria Rusli provided literacy and motivation, especially to new students, by sharing insights and experiences regarding the relevance of the chosen legal discipline and its benefits for the student's future. Dalam kegiatan tersebut Ibu Titi Maria Rusli memberikan literasi terkait dan motivasi khususnya kepada mahasiswa baru dengan memberikan pandangan dan pengalaman terkait relevansi disiplin ilmu hukum yang telah dipilih dan manfaatnya terhadap masa depan mahasiswa.
