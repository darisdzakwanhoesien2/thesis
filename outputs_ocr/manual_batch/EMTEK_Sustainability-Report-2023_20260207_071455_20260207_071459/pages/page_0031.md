# Corporate Governance Principle

Prinsip Governansi Korporat

# Accountability

The Company implements the Accountability principle by ensuring the availability of charters needed by the Company's main bodies to provide clarity of function, implementation, and accountability pertaining to Shareholders, Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Committees, and Corporate Secretary in order to ensure effective implementation of corporate governance.

The Company consistently implements the directives, policies, guidelines, and technical instructions of a consistent supervision system by taking into account the shareholders' and stakeholders' interests. The aforementioned implementation comprises managerial supervision, evaluation, and control to ensure that the management acts in accordance with the interests of the Company, shareholders, and other stakeholders.

# Akuntabilitas

Perseroan menerapkan prinsip Akuntabilitas dengan memastikan telah tersedianya piagam-piagam yang diperlukan bagi masing-masing organ utama Perseroan guna memberikan kejelasan fungsi, pelaksanaan, dan pertanggungjawaban Pemegang Saham, Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Komite-Komite, dan Sekretaris Perusahaan demi memastikan efektivitas governansi korporat.

Perseroan menerapkan pedoman, kebijakan, panduan, dan petunjuk teknis sistem pengawasan secara teratur dengan mempertimbangkan kepentingan para pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan. Penerapan tersebut meliputi pemantauan, evaluasi, dan pengendalian terhadap manajemen untuk memastikan bahwa manajemen bertindak sesuai dengan kepentingan Perseroan, pemegang saham, dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

# Responsibility

The Company implements the Responsibility principle by observing applicable capital market provisions that include taxation, healthy competition, industrial relations, occupational health and safety, payroll standards, as well as other relevant regulations.

![img-58.jpeg](img-58.jpeg)

# Tanggung Jawab

Perseroan menerapkan prinsip Tanggung Jawab dengan mematuhi seluruh ketentuan pasar modal yang mencakup perpajakan, persaingan yang sehat, hubungan industri, kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja, standar penggajian, dan peraturan relevan lainnya.

# Independence

The Company implements the Independency principle by explicitly including the provisions on Company decision-making authority in Board Charters and the Article of Association. The Company also implements various additional policies in the Guidelines on Good Corporate Governance, such as the Policy on Transactions with Conflict of Interest, Prohibition of Donations to Political Parties, Prohibition of Giving and Receiving Gifts, and Donations, as well as several other similar policies.

The Company ensures that each business unit operates independently without dominating each other and without interference from other parties. Every decision is made professionally and objectively, free from conflicts of interest, and in a respectful relationship between business units.

# Tanggung Jawab

Perseroan menerapkan prinsip Kemandirian dengan mencantumkan secara tegas aturan mengenai wewenang pengambilan keputusan Perseroan dalam Piagam Dewan maupun Anggaran Dasar. Perseroan turut pula menerapkan berbagai kebijakan tambahan dalam Pedoman Governansi Korporat yang Baik seperti Kebijakan Transaksi Benturan Kepentingan, Larangan Donasi Partai Politik, Larangan Pemberian, dan Penerimaan Hadiah dan Donasi, serta kebijakan sejenis lainnya.

Perseroan memastikan bahwa setiap unit usaha beroperasi secara mandiri tanpa dominasi satu sama lain dan tanpa campur tangan dari pihak lain. Seluruh keputusan dibuat secara profesional dan objektif, bebas dari konflik kepentingan, dan dalam hubungan yang saling menghargai antar unit usaha.

# Kewajaran dan Kesetaraan

The Company implements the Fairness principle in various operational aspects, such as by honoring the rights of minority shareholders, prohibiting insider trading, and implementing performance management based on a balanced scorecard.

The Company implements the Equality principle by ensuring that the rights of shareholders and stakeholders can be fulfilled properly in accordance with the prevailing agreements and laws. Shareholders and stakeholders have the right to obtain the same information about the Company's performance and activities. The Company also provides equal opportunities in recruitment and human resources management without discrimination against ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, sexual orientation, and physical condition.

Perseroan menerapkan prinsip Kewajaran dalam berbagai aspek operasional, antara lain dengan menghormati hak pemegang saham minoritas, melarang insider trading, dan menerapkan manajemen kinerja berdasarkan balanced scorecard.

Perseroan menerapkan prinsip Kesetaraan dengan memastikan hak-hak pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan dapat terpenuhi dengan baik sesuai dengan kesepakatan dan peraturan yang berlaku. Pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan berhak memperoleh informasi yang sama mengenai kinerja dan aktivitas Perseroan. Perseroan juga memberikan kesempatan yang sama dalam perekrutan dan pengelolaan sumber daya manusia tanpa diskriminasi suku, agama, ras, golongan, jenis kelamin, orientasi seksual, dan kondisi fisik.

# Guidelines of Corporate Governance Implementation

# Landasan Penerapan Governansi Korporat

# [GRI 2-23]

Other than the Company's Articles of Association, Governance Guidelines, Code of Conduct, Charters, and other internal policies, the Company refers to several regulations as well as international guidelines and best practices in GCG implementation, including:

1. Law No. 40/2007 concerning Limited Liability Company (LUPT);
2. Indonesian General Guidelines for Corporate Governance issued by the National Committee of Governance Policies (KNKG);
3. 2023 ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS);
4. Financial Services Authority (OJK) Circular Letter No. 16/SEQK.04/2021 concerning the Form and Content of Annual Report of Issuers and Public Companies; and
5. Relevant regulations of the Financial Services Authority and Indonesia Stock Exchange, such as the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 15/POJK.04/2020 concerning the Plan and Implementation of General Meeting of Shareholders in Public Companies, Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 33/POJK.04/2014 concerning the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies, and Financial Services Authority Circular Letter No. 32/SEQK.04/2015 concerning the Corporate Governance Guidelines for Public Companies.

Selain Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, Pedoman Governansi, Kebijakan Standar Perilaku, Piagam, dan kebijakan internal lainnya, Perseroan berpedoman pada sejumlah regulasi serta pedoman internasional serta praktik-praktik terbaik dalam menerapkan GCG, di antaranya adalah:

1. Undang-Undang No. 40 Tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas (LUPT);
2. Pedoman Umum Governansi Korporat Indonesia yang dikeluarkan oleh Komite Nasional Kebijakan Governansi (KNKG);
3. ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) 2023;
4. Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) No. 16/SEQK.04/2021 tentang Bentuk dan Isi Laporan Tahunan Emiten dan Perusahaan Publik; dan
5. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan dan Bursa Efek Indonesia yang relevan, seperti Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 15/POJK.04/2020 tentang Rencana dan Penyelenggaraan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perusahaan Terbuka, Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 33/POJK.04/2014 tentang Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik, dan Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 32/SEQK.04/2015 tentang Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka.
