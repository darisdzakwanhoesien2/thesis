# Leave Entitlement

# Hak Cuti

# [GRI 401-3]

The Company is pleased to welcome new members of the employees' family who have just given birth or those who are expecting a baby. In order to support this, the Company provides maternity leave in accordance with government regulations and ensures that employees can return to work after the leave ends in their previous positions. In this reporting year, 100% of female employees who took maternity leave successfully returned to work, including those who had taken leave in the previous year. This reflects the Company's commitment to creating a supportive and inclusive work environment for all employees. Furthermore, the Company provides leave rights to male employees whose wives give birth in accordance with applicable company regulations. [GRI 3-3]
