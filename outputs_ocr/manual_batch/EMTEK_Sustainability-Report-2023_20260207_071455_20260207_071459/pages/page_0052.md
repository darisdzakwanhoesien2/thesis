# Taxation Policy

# Kebijakan Perpajakan

[GRI 207-1]

The Company has a taxation policy that includes responsibility and the principles of transparency and accountability in handling all applicable tax provisions issued by the tax authority. The Company understands the importance of compliance with applicable tax laws and regulations as a form of corporate social responsibility and commitment to sustainability. The Company ensures that all tax matters are handled proactively, transparently and responsibly, while still considering the interests of our stakeholders and not using tax havens in tax planning.

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan perpajakan yang meliputi tanggung jawab serta prinsip transparansi dan akuntabilitas dalam menangani seluruh ketentuan pajak yang dikeluarkan oleh otoritas perpajakan. Perseroan memahami pentingnya kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan perpajakan yang berlaku sebagai bentuk tanggung jawab sosial dan komitmen keberlanjutan perusahaan. Perseroan memastikan bahwa semua masalah perpajakan ditangani secara proaktif, transparan, dan bertanggung jawab, dengan tetap menyeimbangkan kepentingan para pemangku kepentingan kami serta tidak menggunakan negara suaka pajak (tax haven) dalam perencanaan pajak.

The Company has a strong governance system to comply with taxation principles and manage tax risks in line with the tax risk management framework that is overseen by the Board of Directors of the Company. Furthermore, the responsibilities of the Audit Committee have been set out in the Audit Committee Charter. [GRI 3-3]

The Company operates only in Indonesia, so this report is specifically focused on the responsibilities within the area, therefore, GRI 207-4 is not applicable for the Company. In 2023, the Company paid Rp200 billion for taxes to the Indonesia's government, reflecting the Company's commitment to support the development and sustainability in Indonesia. Tax policies related to management of tax strategies, stakeholders' engagement, and management of concerns related to taxes are further disclosed in the 2023 Annual Report of the Company. [GRI 207-2]

Perseroan memiliki sistem governansi yang kuat untuk mematuhi prinsip perpajakan dan mengelola risiko pajak sejalan dengan kerangka manajemen risiko pajak yang diawasi oleh Direksi Perseroan. Sementara itu, salah satu tanggung jawab Komite Audit Perseroan yaitu berperan dalam memberikan gambaran tentang masalah-masalah yang terkait dengan kebijakan dan strategi perpajakan. Kebijakan Perpajakan mengenai manajemen dalam strategi pajak, keterlibatan pemangku kepentingan, dan pengelolaan kepedulian yang berkaitan dengan pajak dibahas lebih lanjut dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan 2023.
