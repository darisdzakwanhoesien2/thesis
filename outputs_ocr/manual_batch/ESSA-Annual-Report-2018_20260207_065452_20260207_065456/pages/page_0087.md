Therefore, members of the Board of Directors must carry out their duties and responsibilities in good faith, full of responsibility, and prudence, by heeding applicable laws. The existence of the Board of Directors is governed by the Indonesian Company Law and POJK 33/2014, where in principle the Board of Directors must prioritize professionalism, efficiency, transparency, independence, accountability, accountability and fairness.
