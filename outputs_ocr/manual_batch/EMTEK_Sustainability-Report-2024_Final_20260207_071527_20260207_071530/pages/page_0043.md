Any worker who receives the offering or proposal to do or receive any kind of payment or gratification shall immediately report the same to the Board of Directors, Audit Committee, and Corporate Legal Department of the Company for legal consultancy.

Karyawan yang menerima tawaran atau usulan untuk melakukan atau menerima segala bentuk pembayaran atau gratifikasi harus segera melaporkannya kepada Direksi, Komite Audit, dan Departemen Corporate Legal Perseroan untuk mendapatkan konsultasi hukum.

Any violation, failure or breach of the policy, shall be sanctioned in accordance with the Company Regulations and can be penalized under the prevailing laws and regulations, either under the provisions of the criminal law or the civil law. In the event of a serious violation of this policy, the individual who committed the violation must be willing to resign. [GRI 3-3]

The implementation of the corruption and bribery prevention system in the Company's environment shall be complemented by monitoring and evaluation mechanisms in order to evaluate its effectiveness and success. In this case, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners are also actively involved as tone at the top and improve management oversight in order for the Company to avoid corruption and bribery practices. This policy is also continuously being updated in accordance with updates in the applicable laws and regulations in Indonesia.

Apabila terjadi penyimpangan, kelalaian, dan atau pelanggaran terhadap kebijakan tersebut, maka Perseroan mengenakan sanksi sesuai dengan Peraturan Perusahaan yang berlaku dan termasuk pengenaan sanksi berdasarkan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, baik secara pidana maupun perdata. Apabila terjadi pelanggaran berat terhadap kebijakan ini, maka individu yang melanggar tersebut harus bersedia mengundurkan diri. [GRI 3-3]

Pelaksanaan sistem pencegahan korupsi dan penyuapan di lingkungan Perseroan harus dilengkapi dengan mekanisme pemantauan dan evaluasi untuk menilai efektivitas dan keberhasilannya. Dalam hal ini, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris juga berperan aktif sebagai tone at the top dan meningkatkan pengawasan manajemen agar Perseroan terhindar dari praktik-praktik korupsi dan suap. Kebijakan tersebut juga terus-menerus diperbarui sesuai dengan perkembangan peraturan perundang-undangan dan hukum yang berlaku di Indonesia.

# Anti-Corruption Training/Socialization to EMTEK's Employees Pelatihan/Sosialisasi Anti-korupsi kepada Karyawan EMTEK

For the Company's employees to have a thorough understanding and be able to properly implement this policy in their daily operational activities, the Company routinely socializes this policy to all employees at all levels of the organization.

In order to emphasize EMTEK's strong commitment to providing constant support in strengthening integrity in all operational aspects of EMTEK Group, on August 9, 2024, the Company held an annual training on Anti-Bribery Management System ("ABMS") attended by the Board of Directors and all EMTEK employees, which reinforced EMTEK's commitment to ethical business practices and compliance. The training was presented by Anton Dewantoro, a consultant from Aktuator, a leading information security management system consulting and certification agency. The training also features interactive sessions and case studies, enabling participants to apply the knowledge gained to their daily practice. This is to ensure that each employee not only understands the theoretical aspects of ISO 37001:2016, but also how to effectively implement these practices in their respective roles.

Agar para karyawan Perseroan mendapat pemahaman menyeluruh dan mampu menerapkan kebijakan ini dengan baik dalam kegiatan operasional sehari-hari, Perseroan secara rutin melakukan sosialisasi kebijakan ini kepada seluruh karyawan di seluruh tingkatan organisasi.

Dalam rangka menegaskan komitmen kuat EMTEK untuk memberikan dukungan yang tanpa henti dalam memperkuat integritas dalam semua aspek operasional Grup EMTEK, pada tanggal 9 Agustus 2024, Perseroan menyelenggarakan pelatihan tahunan tentang Sistem Manajemen Anti-Suap ("ABMS") dihadiri oleh Direksi dan seluruh karyawan EMTEK, yang memperkuat komitmen EMTEK terhadap praktik bisnis yang etis dan kepatuhan. Pelatihan ini dibawakan oleh Anton Dewantoro, seorang konsultan dari Aktuator, sebuah lembaga konsultasi dan sertifikasi sistem manajemen keamanan informasi terkemuka. Pelatihan ini juga menyajikan sesi interaktif dan studi kasus, memungkinkan peserta untuk menerapkan pengetahuan yang diperoleh ke dalam praktik sehari-hari. Hal ini untuk memastikan bahwa setiap karyawan tidak hanya memahami aspek teori ISO 37001:2016, tetapi juga cara menerapkan praktik ini secara efektif dalam peran mereka masing-masing.

During the last 5 (five) financial years, no cases of corruption and bribery have occurred within EMTEK.

Seama 5 (fifteen) tahun buku terakhir, tidak ada kasus korupsi dan penyuapan yang terjadi di lingkungan EMTEK.

EMTEK also encourages all subsidiaries and associated companies to adopt the ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System, as indicated by the receipt of ISO 37001:2016 certification by subsidiaries PT Abhimata Citra Abadi, PT Etsha Yudha Ekatama, and PT Bukalapak.com Tbk.

EMTEK actively follows the developments and activities related to the eradication of corruption. On September 25, 2024, EMTEK participated in the Tech 4 AC (or Tech 4 Integrity) Roundtable, a discussion event with the title "Leveraging Technology for Integrity: Advancing Anti-Corruption Efforts in Indonesia through Innovation". The event was facilitated by the Indonesian Institute for Corporate Directorship (IICD) and the Indonesian Anti-Corruption Committee (KAKI), institutions with expertise in corporate governance. EMTEK was represented by the Head of Corporate Legal EMTEK, who participated in an exchange of views and input with BAPPENAS and BRIN as representatives of Indonesian government institutions.

As a company that focuses on technology and innovation, EMTEK has a vision to create solutions that not only encourage economic growth but also support sustainable social development. Through the development of technology that supports transparency and integrity, EMTEK plays an active role in strengthening public trust. EMTEK's participation in this roundtable reflects its dedication as a company and the entire EMTEK team to eradicating corruption in the technology industry, with the aim of driving the implementation of technologies that fully supports anti-corruption efforts.
