# Risks and Opportunities Arising from Climate Change Risiko dan Peluang Akibat dari Perubahan Iklim [GRI 201-2]

Climate change carries risks that impact the environmental ecosystem which affects human life, including the side effects of business and global economy. Proper management and adaptation are needed to mitigate financial losses from climate change. On the other hand, there are also opportunities associated with climate change. Business owners must be able to understand and even simulate or prepare their business for the most difficult positions due to climate change.

EMTEK has and continues to manage the risks and opportunities related to climate change. Through the ESG Committee as the committee in charge, EMTEK recognizes the importance of analyzing the risks and opportunities of climate change on the Company's cash flow. As an investment holding company, EMTEK understands the importance of having all its entities manage this issue properly since early stages to maintain business continuity.

Perubahan iklim membawa risiko yang memiliki dampak bagi ekosistem lingkungan yang mempengaruhi kehidupan manusia, termasuk di dalamnya efek samping atas bisnis dan ekonomi secara global. Dibutuhkan pengelolaan dan adaptasi yang tepat dalam menghadapi perubahan iklim untuk mengurangi kerugian secara finansial. Di sisi lain ada juga kesempatan untuk memanfaatkan peluang terkait perubahan iklim.

Pelaku usaha harus dapat memahami dan bahkan dapat melakukan simulasi atau mempersiapkan bisnisnya pada posisi tersulit terkait perubahan iklim. EMTEK telah dan terus melakukan pengelolaan terhadap risiko dan peluang terkait perubahan iklim. Melalui Komite ESG sebagai penanggung jawab, EMTEK memandang penting analisis risiko dan peluang perubahan iklim terhadap arus kas Perseroan. Sebagai holding yang bergerak dibidang investasi, EMTEK memahami pentingnya membawa seluruh entitas yang dimilikinya mengelola isu ini dengan baik sejak dini untuk menjaga kesinambungan bisnis.

## Governance Tata Kelola [GRI 2-12]

As the committee in charge of sustainability, the ESG Committee is also responsible for the implementation, supervision, and evaluation of the actions taken in the adaptation and mitigation of climate change. The ESG Committee is supported by the Risk Management Department in identifying risks, especially those related to climate change. The ESG Committee periodically submits the latest information or trends regarding risks and opportunities related to climate change to the Board of Directors.

Komite ESG sebagai penanggung jawab atas keberlanjutan juga bertanggung jawab dalam pelaksanaan, pengawasan, serta evaluasi terhadap langkah-langkah yang diambil dalam adaptasi dan mitigasi perubahan iklim. Komite ESG mendapat dukungan yang sederhana. Manajemen Risiko dalam identifikasi risiko khususnya terkait perubahan iklim. Secara berkala Komite ESG menyampaikan informasi atau tren terbaru seputar risiko dan peluang terkait perubahan iklim kepada Direksi.

The Risk Management Department simultaneously monitors climate change-related risks and other risks. The Risk Management Department periodically reports and coordinates with the Board of Commissioners through the Risk Monitoring Committee during meetings that have been held 2 (two) times throughout 2024.

Pemantauan risiko terkait perubahan iklim dilakukan secara bersamaan dengan pengawasan risiko lainnya yang dilakukan oleh Departemen Manajemen Risiko. Departemen Manajemen Risiko secara berkala melakukan pelaporan dan berkoodinasi dengan Dewan Komisaris melalui Komite Pemantau Risiko dalam rapat yang telah diadakan sebanyak 2 (dua) kali sepanjang tahun 2024.

## Strategy Strategi

The Company maps the risks and opportunities related to climate change by identifying risks through self-assessment conducted by all divisions. A continuous process is carried out at EMTEK to obtain and update the analysis of the climate change risks and opportunities for the Company in the short, medium, and long term. We need to carry out simulations or scenarios by including other factors, such as: physical risks of varying severity, transition risks including new policies and regulations, shifts in consumers' markets and technology, and reputation. Awareness of the importance of climate change mitigation in society can also be used as an opportunity for the Company to provide the products and services that are needed.
