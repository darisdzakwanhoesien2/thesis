The Impact of Operational Areas that are Near or Located in Conservation Areas or Areas that are Rich in Biodiversity

Dampak dari Wilayah Operasional yang Dekat atau Berada di Daerah Konservasi atau Kaya akan Keanekaragaman Hayati

[OJK F.9]

The Company and all of its subsidiaries are not located in or near conservation areas or areas that are rich in biodiversity. Therefore, we can ensure that there are no negative impacts caused by our operational activities.

Perseroan beserta seluruh entitas anak usahanya tidak berada atau dekat dengan daerah konservasi atau kawasan yang memiliki kekayaan keanekaragaman hayati. Sehingga dipastikan tidak ada dampak negatif yang ditimbulkan oleh kegiatan operasionalnya.

Biodiversity Conservation Efforts/Uutra Konservasi Keanekaragaman Hayati [OJK F.10]

However, EMTEK remains to actively participate in initiatives that promote environmental ecosystem balance, including tree planting initiatives. By 2024, the Company together with YPP had planted 3,100 trees in several locations in Karangmalang Beach at Kendal Region, Sigempol Coastal Region at Brebes Region, Jabungan Village at Semarang City, and Cijayanti Village at Bogor Region. This initiative is also presented to help reduce the greenhouse effect and mitigate climate change.

As part of its efforts to protect the environment, the Company, through CASS, has planted mangrove trees in the mangrove forest area of North Jakarta. Through this initiative, the Company expects to contribute to prevent seawater intrusion and protect the surrounding living creatures and organisms.

Meskipun demikian, EMTEK tetap ikut berpartisipasi secara aktif dalam kegiatan yang mendorong keseimbangan ekosistem lingkungan, di antaranya melalui kegiatan penanaman pohon. Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan bersama dengan YPP telah menanam 3.100 pohon di beberapa lokasi yaitu Pantai Karangmalang di Kabupaten Kendal, Pesisir Sigempol di Kabupaten Brebes, Desa Jabungan di Kota Semarang dan Desa Cijayanti di Kabupaten Bogor. Melalui kegiatan ini diharapkan juga dapat membantu mengurangi efek rumah kaca dan memerangi perubahan iklim.

Masih dalam rangkaian melindungi ekosistem lingkungan, Perseroan melalui CASS melakukan penanaman pohon Mangrove di kawasan hutan bakau Jakarta Utara. Melalui kegiatan ini, Perseroan berharap bisa berkontribusi dalam mencegah intrusi air laut ke daratan, serta melindungi makhluk hidup dan organisme yang ada di sekitarnya.

Waste and Effluent Management Mechanism/Mekanisme Pengelolaan Limbah dan Efluen [OJK F.14]

The Company's waste management in SCTV Tower - Senayan City, is carried out by the building owner through authorized third parties. EMTEK Holding does not produce large-scale of waste which significantly impact the surrounding environment. Waste generated from EMTEK operations at headquarters is limited to non-hazardous household waste. EMTEK uses bins that are categorized by waste type to facilitate waste sorting at the final disposal site (plastic, organic, and inorganic waste). Bins are placed on each floor and in strategic locations in the office environment.

Effluent

Given the scope of EMTEK Holding, we do not produce liquid waste, which means that we do not manage liquid waste. Our liquid waste is household waste, and the building management manages it on a regional basis, so we do not have data on the volume of wastewater managed. The management of liquid waste from operational activities is very important before the waste is released into the environment. The Company ensures that all subsidiaries understand and comply with the obligation to meet water quality standards to avoid pollution. In our previous sustainability report, we presented liquid waste data from the Wastewater Treatment Plant (IPAL) at the Indosier office in Daan Mogot, West Jakarta. In this report, we have not included this data as it relates to the operations of a subsidiary and is also reported in the sustainability report of PT Surya Citra Media Tbk.

Waste Spills [OJK F.15]

During 2024, there was no spillage of solid or liquid waste, or pollution of the environment and the community around the Company's operations.

Effluent

Mengganti ruang lingkup EMTEK Holding, kami tidak menghasilkan limbah cair sehingga tidak ada pengelolaan limbah cair yang kami lakukan. Adapun limbah cair yang dihasilkan bersifat rumah tangga dan pengelolaannya, dilaksanakan oleh pihak pengelola gedung secara kawasan sehingga kami tidak memiliki data terkait volume air limbah yang dikelola. Pengelolaan limbah cair yang berasal dari aktivitas operasional sangat penting dilakukan sebelum limbah tersebut dilepasi ke lingkungan. Perseroan memastikan bahwa seluruh entitas anak usaha telah memahami dan mematuhi kewajiban memenuhi baku mutu air untuk menghindari pencemaran. Pada laporan keberlanjutan sebelumnya kami menyampaikan data limbah cair yang berasal dari Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL) di kantor Indosier, Daan Mogot, Jakarta Barat. Pada laporan kali ini kami tidak memasukkan data tersebut mengingat hal tersebut merupakan operasional di entitas anak perusahaan dan dilaporkan juga pada laporan keberlanjutan PT Surya Citra Media Tbk.
