# ASSESSMENT OF GCG COMMITTEE PERFORMANCE

# PENILAIAN KINERJA KOMITE ESG

The Board of Directors considers that the ESG Committee has performed its duties and responsibilities properly, for example by assisting the Board of Directors in preparing a sustainability plan and implementing various sustainability initiatives in Environmental, Social, and Governance aspects, as well as accommodating the needs and interests of all stakeholders in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

EMTEK's successful implementation of sustainability is indicated by its inclusion in the five prestigious ESG and sustainability indexes on the IDX. These indexes include:

1. SRI KEHATI: June - November 2024 and December 2024 - May 2025.
2. ESG Sector Leaders IDX KEHATI: June - November 2024 and December 2024 - May 2025.
3. ESG Quality 45 IDX KEHATI: June - November 2024 and December 2024 - May 2025.
4. IDX ESG Leaders: June - September 2024; and September 2024 - March 2025.
5. IDX LQ45 Low Carbon Leaders: May - August 2024.

In addition, EMTEK also received a positive score from independent rating agencies, S&amp;P Global (score of 60/100 with "very high" data availability) and Sustainalytics (score of 14.2), placing us above the industry average in terms of Environmental, Social, and Governance aspects.

At the Indonesia Regulatory Compliance Awards (IRCA) 2024 organized by HukumOnline, EMTEK received recognition and awards for its outstanding achievements in 4 (four) categories of regulatory compliance:

1. Mrs. Titi Maria Rusli, Legal Director of EMTEK, as Most Inspiring Leader in Regulatory Compliance.
2. Mrs. Titi Maria Rusli, Legal Director of EMTEK, as Leader of the Year in Regulatory Compliance.
3. EMTEK won the award for Best Holding Company in Regulatory Compliance.
4. EMTEK won the "Sapphire Award" for Best Enterprise in Regulatory Compliance within the Technology, Media &amp; Telecommunication Sector.

The Sapphire Award for Best Enterprise in Regulatory Compliance is the highest ranking. This ranking is given to companies that consistently demonstrate excellence in complying with all applicable regulations and policies. Companies nominated for this ranking not only comply with regulations, but also set high standards in their industries, becoming role models for other companies in terms of regulatory compliance.

Direksi menilai Komite ESG telah melakukan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik seperti membantu Direksi dalam menyiapkan rencana berkelanjutan dan mengimplementasikan berbagai inisiatif keberlanjutan pada aspek Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Governansi, serta mengakomodir kebutuhan dan kepentingan seluruh pemangku kepentingan sesuai dengan hukum dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

Keberhasilan penerapan keberlanjutan EMTEK ditandai dengan masuknya EMTEK ke jajaran 5 (lima) indeks ESG dan keberlanjutan yang bergengsi di BEI. Indeks-indeks tersebut meliputi:

1. SRI KEHATI: Juni - November 2024 dan Desember 2024 - Mei 2025.
2. ESG Sector Leaders IDX KEHATI: Juni - November 2024 dan Desember 2024 - Mei 2025.
3. ESG Quality 45 IDX KEHATI: Juni - November 2024 dan Desember 2024 - Mei 2025.
4. IDX ESG Leaders: Juni - September 2024; dan September 2024 - Maret 2025.
5. IDX LQ45 Low Carbon Leaders: Mei - Agustus 2024.

Selain itu, EMTEK mendapatkan penilaian baik dari lembaga rating independen yaitu S&amp;P Global (Skor 60/100 dengan ketersediaan data "sangat tinggi") dan Sustainalytics (skor 14,2) yang menempatkan kami berada di atas rata-rata industri sejenis, baik dari sisi aspek Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Governansi.

Kemudian, di ajang Indonesia Regulatory Compliance Awards (IRCA) 2024 yang diselenggarakan HukumOnline, EMTEK mendapat pengakuan dan penghargaan atas pencapaian luar biasa dalam kepatuhan terhadap peraturan di 4 (empat) kategori:

1. Ibu Titi Maria Rusli, Legal Director EMTEK sebagai Most Inspiring Leader in Regulatory Compliance.
2. Ibu Titi Maria Rusli, Legal Director EMTEK sebagai Leader of The Year dalam Regulatory Compliance.
3. EMTEK meraih penghargaan sebagai Best Holding Company in Regulatory Compliance.
4. EMTEK meraih penghargaan "Sapphire Award" untuk Best Enterprise in Regulatory Compliance within the Technology, Media &amp; Telecommunication Sector.

Sapphire Award untuk Best Enterprise in Regulatory Compliance merupakan peringkat tertinggi. Peringkat ini diberikan kepada perusahaan yang diakui secara konsisten menunjukkan keunggulan dalam mematuhi semua peraturan dan kebijakan yang berlaku. Perusahaan yang terpilih untuk peringkat ini tidak hanya mematuhi peraturan, tetapi juga menetapkan standar yang tinggi dalam industri mereka, menjadi panutan bagi perusahaan lain dalam hal-kepatuhan terhadap peraturan.

The recipient of the Leader of the Year in Regulatory Compliance award earned the highest score from the judges for his leadership in the company. All winners (leaders) in each industry sector were assessed, and a single winner was selected, namely Mrs. Titi Maria Rusli, Legal Director of EMTEK, who was chosen as the best leader.

All of this achievements emphasizes EMTEK's strong commitment to running its business in a sustainable manner.

Penerima penghargaan Leader of the Year in Regulatory Compliance mendapatkan nilai tertinggi dari para Juri atas kepemimpinannya di Perusahaan. Penilaian dilakukan terhadap seluruh pemenang (leader) di setiap sektor industri, kemudian dipilih satu pemenang terbaik yaitu Ibu Titi Maria Rusli, Direktur Legal EMTEK, terpilih sebagai pemimpin terbaik.

Semua ini mempertegas komitmen kuat EMTEK dalam menjalankan bisnisnya secara berkelanjutan.

# KOMUNIKASI MASALAH PENTING TERKAIT ESG

All critical information related to actual or potential negative impacts on stakeholders is recorded by the ESG committee through available mechanisms or channels such as the whistleblowing system. The ESG committee will then communicate any significant issues faced in the Economic, Social, and Environmental aspects to the highest governance organs.
