# Tax Policy

## Kebijakan Perpajakan

[GRI 207-1]

The Company's Tax Policy is formulated based on its business processes and refers to national and international tax regulations and standards. The Company's compliance in fulfilling its tax obligations is based on its responsibility to uphold the principles of transparency and accountability. [GRI 3-3]

The implementation of Tax policy by each sector is coordinated, monitored and evaluated by the Unit/Division in charge of taxation. The performance of Tax Policy implementation is reported by the ESG Committee to the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners every year. The Company also collaborates with the local Tax Office to obtain information and input regarding the latest tax regulations. [GRI 207-2] [207-3]

Since the Company only operates in Indonesia, this report specifically focuses on obligations in Indonesia. In 2024, the Company paid Rpf,053 billion in taxes to the Indonesian government. The Company's compliance in fulfilling its tax responsibilities has supported the government in terms of infrastructure development, public facilities, and other programs that have a direct impact on community welfare and sustainability. [GRI 207-4]

Kebijakan Pajak Perseroan yang disusun sesuai dengan proses bisnis perusahaan dan mengacu pada regulasi dan standar terkait Pajak baik nasional maupun internasional. Kepatuhan Perseroan dalam menjalankan kewajiban perpajakan didasari tanggung jawab yang memegang teguh prinsip transparansi dan akuntabilitas. [GRI 3-3]

Pelaksanaan kebijakan Pajak untuk masing-masing ruang lingkup dikoordinir, dimonitor dan dievaluasi oleh Unit/Divisi yang membidangi perpajakan. Based on this evaluation, the Company will only collaborate with suppliers who demonstrate commitment to existing regulations, including compliance with ESG principles.
