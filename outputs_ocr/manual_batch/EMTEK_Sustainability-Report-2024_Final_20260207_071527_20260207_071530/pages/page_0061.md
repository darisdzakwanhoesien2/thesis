## Economic Performance

## Kinerja Ekonomi

The Company's economic performance is essential to its long-term business sustainability and provides added value for all stakeholders. As a holding company, EMTEK oversees an ecosystem of business entities that manage a range of investment and service portfolios including media, entertainment, content, online, telecommunications, technology, e-commerce, healthcare, banking and financial services. Most recently, in 2024, EMTEK expanded its line of business into the aviation support services sector by finalizing the acquisition of CASS through its subsidiary, PT Roket Cipta Sentosa (PT RCS).

The acquisition of CASS, which is engaged in aviation support services, catering, facilities management, and aviation training services, reflects the Company's concrete efforts to pursue strategic expansion that can provide added value for shareholders and secure the sustainability of its business.
