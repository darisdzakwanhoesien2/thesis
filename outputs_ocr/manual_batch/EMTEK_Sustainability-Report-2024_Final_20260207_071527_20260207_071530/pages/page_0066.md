# Initiative to Use Environmentally Friendly Materials

# Inisiatif Penggunaan Material Ramah Lingkungan

[OJK F.5]

As part of its commitment to sustainability, EMTEK implements various initiatives to use environmentally friendly materials in all its operations. At the 2024 AGMS and Public Expose, EMTEK distributed stainless steel tumblers to shareholders and stakeholders in an effort to reduce single-use plastics. To support this initiative, EMTEK also provides reusable water bottles for employees and campaigns the use of personal lunch containers to reduce single-use packaging waste.

In addition, EMTEK through its subsidiary, PT Elang Media Karya, also optimizes the use of its Natural Studio in Cijayanti, Sentul, as a production center for drama series and other content produced under the SCMA Group. The use of this natural studio not only increases production efficiency, but also contributes to reducing our carbon footprint. By reducing the need to transport crews, artists, and equipment to different and distant filming locations, EMTEK has succeeded in minimizing carbon emissions and increasing overall operational efficiency by centralizing activities in one place.

In the healthcare business unit, EMC Hospital Group has replaced the use of single-use plastics with more environmentally friendly materials. Paper bags are now used as an alternative to plastic bags in hospitals, especially in the pharmacy department. In addition, plastic medicine clips have been replaced with paper packs, while plastic medicine bags have been switched to paper bags to reduce medical plastic waste.

As part of its efforts to support the circular economy principle and to reduce textile waste, EMTEK also organizes an ex-wardrobe shooting bazaar, where wearable clothes from film and drama productions are resold to employees and the public. This initiative not only extends the life cycle of clothing, but also encourages awareness of reusing fashion products to reduce the environmental impact of the textile industry.
