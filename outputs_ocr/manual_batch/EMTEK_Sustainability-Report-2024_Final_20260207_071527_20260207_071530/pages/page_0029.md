# Environment

We continue to apply the Reduce, Reuse, Recycle principles to support an environmentally friendly culture, even though our operational activities are office-based, occupying a space of 50TH Tower, and our scope as a holding company does not have a serious impact on environmental aspects.

Furthermore, as a commitment to achieving Net Zero Emissions for scope 1 and 2 by 2050, EMTEK has taken concrete steps by using energy-efficient LED lights, intelligent elevators, electric vehicles, and natural lighting. Although these actions may seem simple, in 2024, we successfully reduced electricity consumption by 11.8% (from 21.3 GJ to 18.8 GJ).

Meanwhile, the energy consumption of motor vehicles remains stable. We have also started processing indirect emission data (Scope 3) from employee business trips by plane as a form of transparency to all stakeholders as part of our sustainability performance actions. Additionally, EMTEK supports environmental conservation by planting 3,100 trees in Karangmalang Beach at Kendal Region, Sigempol Coastal Region at Brebes Region, Jabungan Village at Semarang City, and Clayanti Village at Bogor Region.

# Social

We are committed to providing social benefits to employees, the community, and supplier partners. We create a work environment that supports productivity by considering skills, health, safety, and employee well-being. We provide equal opportunities for education, training, performance evaluation, and career development, and apply human rights principles to human resource management. Quantitatives, 50% of the Board of Directors and 25% of the Board of Commissioners are women, and we employ 14 disabled employees at EMTEK Group, reflecting our gradual commitment to inclusivity.

In 2024, the Company conducted 43,096 hours of training for parent company employees. We can ensure that there were no workplace accidents, violence, sexual harassment, child labor, or forced labor in our work environment.

# Lingkungan

Kami tetap menerapkan prinsip Reduce, Reuse, Recycle untuk mendukung budaya ramah lingkungan meskipun kegiatan operasionalnya berupa kegiatan perkantoran yang menempati salah satu ruang di 50TH Tower dan ruang lingkup kami sebagai suatu holding company tidak menimbulkan dampak serius terhadap aspek lingkungan.

Lebih jauh, sebagai komitmen mencapai Net Zero Emission untuk scope 1 dan 2 pada tahun 2050, EMTEK secara tegas mengambil langkah kontrat dengan menggunakan LED hemat energi, intelligent elevators, kendaraan listrik, dan pencahayaan alami. Meskipun tindakan ini dirasa sederhana, namun ternyata, pada tahun 2024, kami berhasil mengurangi pemakaian listrik sebesar 11,8% (dari 21,3 GJ menjadi 18,8 GJ).

Sementara itu, dalam pemakaian energi kendaraan bermotor tetap stabil. Kami juga mulai mencoba mengolah data emisi tidak langsung (Scope 3) dari perjalanan dinas karyawan dengan pesawat sebagai bentuk transparansi kepada setiap pemangku kepentingan sebagai bentuk tindakan kinerja keberlanjutan kami. Selain itu, EMTEK mendukung kelestarian lingkungan dengan menanam lebih dari 3.100 pohon di Pantal Karangmalang di Kabupaten Kendal, Pesisir Sigempol di Kabupaten Brebes, Desa Jabungan di Kota Semarang dan Desa Cijayanti di Kabupaten Bogor.

# Sosial

Kami senantiasa berkomitmen memberikan manfaat sosial kepada karyawan, masyarakat, dan mitra pemasok. Kami menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang mendukung produktivitas dengan memperhatikan keahlian, kesehatan, keselamatan, dan kesejahteraan karyawan. Kami memberikan kesempatan antara untuk pendidikan, pelatihan, penilaian prestasi, dan pengembangan karier, serta menerapkan prinsip hak asasi manusia dalam pengelolaan sumber daya manusia. Secara kuantitatif, 50% Direksi dan 25% Dewan Komisaris adalah perempuan, dan lebih lanjut, kami mempekerjakan 14 karyawan disabilitas di Grup EMTEK dan hal ini adalah bentuk cerminan komitmen inklusivitas kami secara bertahap.
