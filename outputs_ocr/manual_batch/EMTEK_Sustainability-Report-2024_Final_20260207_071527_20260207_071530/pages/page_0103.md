# INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE STATEMENT

The 2024 Sustainability Report of PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk Statement No: 340/TRID-A02/3/2025

PT TUV Rheinland Indonesia (hereinafter "TÜV Rheinland" or "we") has been engaged by the management of PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk (hereinafter "EMTEK" or "the Reporting Company") to provide an independent assurance of the PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk Sustainability Report 2024 (hereinafter, "the Report"). TÜV Rheinland, headquartered in Germany, is a global independent Environmental, Social, and Governance ("ESG") &amp; Sustainability Assurance service provider with qualified ESG practitioners worldwide.

This assurance statement applies only to the relevant information included in the scope of the assurance. The entire content and information of the Report is in the responsibility of EMTEK. The responsibility of the assurance team (the "Assurance Team" or the "Assurer") is to provide EMTEK and its stakeholder with an independent assurance statement based on our expert opinions by applying the verification methodology for the specified assurance scope. The overall objective is to provide EMTEK's stakeholders with assurance on the reliability and objectivity of the information contained in the Report. The intended users of this assurance statement are stakeholders who are interested in EMTEK's overall sustainability performance and the impacts of its business activities.

# SCOPE OF ASSURANCE AND SUBJECT MATTER

The scope of assurance engagement agreed upon with EMTEK includes the following:

1. The reporting period: 1st January 2024 to 31st December 2024.
2. The assurance covers the Report against the reporting criteria including evaluation of quality, accuracy and reliability of performance information.
3. Material topics identified and reported by EMTEK consist of 12 material topics (the "Subject Matter"). These material topics include the following:

- Providing economic benefits to stakeholders.
- Engaging and providing opportunities for business partners.
- Infrastructure investments and services supported.
- Responsibility for business practices, anti-corruption and integrity.
- Paying attention to employees' safety and health.
- Respecting human rights and creating an inclusive working environment.
- Paying attention to employees' welfare.
- Human resources training and development.
- Providing the best products and services for customers.
- Concerns for the Community
- Environmentally friendly behavior through Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (3R) approach.
- Conducting business with integrity, transparency and compliance with all laws and regulations, professional codes of ethics, and the application of other good corporate governance principles (including anti-corruption and bribery policies and consumers' data protection and privacy).

# REPORTING CRITERIA AND FRAMEWORK

- Reporting in accordance with the Global Reporting Initiative Standards 2021 ("GRI 2021").
- The Regulation of Financial Services Authority Number 51/POJK.03/2017 on the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies.

# ASSURANCE STANDARD AND LEVEL OF ASSURANCE

The independent assurance was conducted in accordance:

- AA1000AP (2018) Principles, covering the accountability principles of inclusivity, materiality, responsiveness, and impact (the "AA1000AP (2018) Principles").
- AA1000AS v3 with assurance engagement: Type 2 and level of assurance: Moderate.

# LIMITATIONS

The assurance was performed at a moderate assurance level in accordance with AA1000AS. The information and performance data subject to assurance is limited to the content of the Report and supporting data provided by the reporting organization. It implies that no significant errors were found during the assurance process and that there are limitations related to the unavoidable risks that may exist.

# ASSURANCE METHODOLOGY

We have evaluated the overall sustainability strategy, targets and commitments disclosed in the Report. We have performed an objective review of the reported information and evidence gathered against the criteria defined in the assurance standards, i.e. the accountability principles of inclusivity, materiality, responsiveness and impact. The processes and system controls applied by the Reporting Company in the disclosure of quantitative and qualitative information on sustainability performance, from source to aggregation, were evaluated. Analytical procedures, interviews, and data testing were performed on a sample basis to test the generation and collection of data for defined sustainability key performance indicators in accordance with EMTEK's corporate sustainability strategy, governance, and management systems as disclosed in the Report. Our work included interviews with EMTEK representatives, including senior management at the executive and functional levels, as well as relevant employees responsible for collecting, aggregating and reporting the data. The assurance work was performed by our multidisciplinary team of experienced professionals in the areas of corporate sustainability, climate change, environmental, social, governance and stakeholder engagement. The opinion expressed is based on a moderate level of assurance and at the level of materiality of the Assurer's professional judgment.
