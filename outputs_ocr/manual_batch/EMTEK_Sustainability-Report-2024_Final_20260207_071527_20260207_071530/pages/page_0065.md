# Evaluation of Policy Effectiveness

The Company's policies and initiatives regarding environmental responsibility are communicated to stakeholders through sustainability reports and publications on the Company's website. The Company will receive inputs from stakeholders through the feedback form available in the sustainability report. In addition, the ESG Committee also conducts joint evaluations with the Board of Directors through regular meetings. Based on the input and evaluation results, the Company believes that the approaches or policies taken are in accordance with applicable regulations, the Company's capacity and its business context.

# Material Impact and its Mitigation

Currently, the Company has no actual negative impacts, hence there are no mitigation activities to address negative environmental impacts. However, our mitigation and management procedures are ready to address any negative impacts, starting from early detection through regular internal audit practices, continuous training/socialization for employees, and the establishment of emergency response procedures as a proactive measure taken by the Company.

The Company also encourages all subsidiaries to act responsively on any environmental incidents and ensures that corrective actions are carried out in accordance with applicable mechanisms and regulations. [GR 3-3]

# Compliance in Environmental Aspects Kepatuhan di Bidang Aspek Lingkungan

Throughout 2024, the Company did not receive any warnings or sanctions related to negative impacts caused by its operational activities. This demonstrates the Company's compliance with all applicable environmental regulations and provisions.

We manage and support the creation of environmental sustainability in our internal, for example, our culture of natural resource efficiency, and in our external, such as planting trees, providing clean sanitation, and using energy-friendly electronic devices and electric vehicles. These initiatives also contribute to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals program.

# Managing Actual and Potential Risks in the Environmental Aspect

The Company constantly identifies environmental risks and assesses the scale of the impact or the extent to which it affects the Company's operations and business growth. The identification and assessment of impacts are carried out thoroughly, including in the Company's supply chain. This management is important and is a consideration for achieving sustainability goals in all lines of business.

# Evaluasi Efektivitas Kebijakan

Kebijakan dan inisiatif tanggung jawab lingkungan Perseroan dikomunikasikan kepada pemangku kepentingan melalui laporan keberlanjutan serta publikasi yang dilakukan melalui situs web Perseroan. Perseroan akan mendapat masukan dari pemangku kepentingan melalui teman umpan balik yang tersedia dalam laporan keberlanjutan. Selain itu, Komite ESG juga melakukan evaluasi bersama dengan Direksi melalui rapat rutin secara berkala. Berdasarkan masukan dan evaluasi yang dilakukan, Perseroan menilai bahwa pendekatan atau kebijakan yang diambil telah sesuai ketentuan yang berlaku dan sesuai dengan kapasitas serta konteks bisnis Perseroan.

# Dampak Nyata dan Penanganannya

Saat ini, Perseroan tidak menghadapi dampak negatif yang aktual, sehingga tidak ada kegiatan penanganan terhadap dampak negatif lingkungan. Namun demikian kami memiliki prosedur mitigasi dan penanganan jika terjadi dampak negatif, dimulai dari deteksi awal melalui praktik audit internal berkala, pelatihan/sosialisasi berkelanjutan untuk karyawan, serta pembentukan prosedur penanganan akurat sebagai langkah proaktif yang dilakukan oleh Perseroan.

Perseroan juga mendorong seluruh entitas anak usaha bertindak responsif jika terdapat insiden dampak lingkungan dan memastikan tindakan perbaikan dilaksanakan sesuai mekanisme dan peraturan yang berlaku. [GR 3-3]

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Perseroan tidak mendapat teguran, sanksi terkait adanya dampak negatif yang ditimbulkan akibat kegiatan operasionalnya. Ini menunjukkan kepatuhan Perseroan terhadap seluruh ketentuan dan peraturan yang berlaku terkait aspek lingkungan hidup.

Pengelolaan dan dukungan kami untuk menciptakan kelestarian lingkungan dalam ruang lingkup internal sebagai contoh budaya efisiensi sumber daya alam dan lain-lain maupun di eksternal Perseroan seperti penanaman pohon, bantuan sanitasi bersih, penggunaan alat elektronik ramah energi dan kendaraan listrik juga merupakan dukungan terhadap program Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan Perserikatan Bangsa Bangsa.

# Mengelola Risiko Aktual dan Potensial di Bidang Lingkungan

Perseroan senantiasa melakukan identifikasi risiko terkait lingkungan hidup dan mengukur skala dampak atau sejauh mana hal tersebut memberi dampak bagi operasional dan pertumbuhan usaha Perseroan. Identifikasi dan penilaian dampak dilakukan secara menyeluruh termasuk di dalamnya rantai pasokan Perseroan. Pengelolaan ini penting dan menjadi pertimbangan untuk mencapai tujuan keberlanjutan di semua lini bisnisnya.

# Awareness and Socialization

The Company continues to raise environmental awareness and socialize its sustainability principles by developing technology-based initiatives and encouraging all employees to behave responsibly towards the environment. Throughout 2024, the Company held 8 (eight) socialization and training sessions on various ESG topics, including the environment. The sessions were attended by the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners, and employees of EMTEK, and business unit leaders of EMTEK Group, as part of the effort to build a culture of sustainability (further details in the Building a Culture of Sustainability sub-chapter). In addition, as a form of commitment in addressing the increasingly urgent challenge of climate change, the Company has established a Climate Strategy Policy which has been published on its website. The Company also actively promotes energy and water conservation campaigns in all employees, emphasizing the importance of concrete actions in supporting environmental sustainability.

# Kesadaran dan Sosialisasi

Perseroan terus meningkatkan kesadaran lingkungan dan menyesialisasikan prinsip keberlanjutan dengan mengembangkan inisiatif berbasis teknologi serta mendorong seluruh karyawan untuk berperilaku bertanggung jawab terhadap lingkungan. Sepanjang tahun 2024, telah dilaksanakan 8 (dengan) sesi sosialisasi dan pelatihan mengenai berbagai topik ESG, termasuk lingkungan, yang diikuti oleh Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, serta karyawan EMTEK, dan pimpinan unit bisnis di Grup EMTEK, sebagai bagian dari upaya membangun budaya keberlanjutan (selengkapnya pada subbab Kegiatan Membangun Budaya Keberlanjutan). Selain itu, sebagai wujud komitmen dalam menghadapi tantangan perubahan iklim yang semakin mendesak, Perseroan telah menetapkan Kebijakan Strategi iklim yang telah dipublikasikan di situs web EMTEK. Perseroan juga tidak surut menggalakkan kampanye penghematan energi dan air kepada seluruh karyawan, menegaskan pentingnya langkah konkret dalam mendukung keberlanjutan lingkungan.
