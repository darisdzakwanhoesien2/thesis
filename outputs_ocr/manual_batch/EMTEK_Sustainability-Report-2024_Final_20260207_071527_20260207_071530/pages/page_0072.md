# 5. Performance Management &amp; Total Rewards
## Performance Management &amp; Total Rewards

The function of Total Rewards &amp; Personnel Management aims to plan and manage compensation and rewards for employees. This function supports the development of a Performance Based High Performing Culture by innovating benefits that can be provided to employees, which are not only monetary but also non-monetary in the form of long-service awards, employee activities such as sports clubs, team building, and competency development programs, employee and family health service cooperation programs from EMC and JEC subsidiaries, as well as employee CSR activities. This function is also in charge of managing all personnel operational activities, most of which have been automated. By providing fair and equal compensation, rewards, and personnel services in accordance with applicable regulations, employees will become motivated to deliver their best performance.

Fungsi Total Rewards &amp; Personnel Management adalah membuat perencanaan, pengelolaan kompensasi dan penghargaan bagi karyawan. Fungsi ini berperan untuk mendukung pengembangan Performance Based High Performing Culture melalui inovasi terhadap manfaat yang dapat diberikan kepada karyawan, yang tidak hanya moneter tetapi juga non-moneter dalam bentuk penghargaan masa kerja, kegiatan karyawan seperti klub olahraga, team building, dan program pengembangan kompetenci, program kerja sama pelayanan kesehatan karyawan dan keluarga dari entitas anak perusahaan EMC dan JEC, serta kegiatan CSR karyawan. Fungsi ini juga bertugas mengelola semua kegiatan operasional personalia yang sebagian besar telah di formalisasi. Dengan memberikan kompensasi, penghargaan maupun layanan personalia yang aldi, setara dan sesuai peraturan yang berlaku, maka karyawan akan termotivasi untuk mempersembahkan kinerja terbaik.

In addition, this function also plays a role in conducting employee performance appraisals through the online performance appraisal (e-PA) system. The Company has also incorporated a feature in the EMTEK Hub that can facilitate the process of determining work targets and employee performance appraisals. Regular development is still being carried out on the e-PA system, as well as socialization of the system to support employees in establishing individual work targets that are in line with the Company's business targets.

Selain itu, fungsi ini juga berperan dalam penyelenggaraan evaluasi kinerja (performance appraisal) karyawan melalui sistem online performance appraisal (e-PA). Perseroan juga menyematkan fitur pada EMTEK Hub yang dapat memfasilitasi proses penetapan target kerja dan penilaian kinerja karyawan. Pengembangan secara berkala masih terus dilakukan pada sistem e-PA demikian juga dengan kegiatan sosialisasi sistem tersebut untuk mempermudah karyawan ketika menyusun target kerja individual (karyawan) yang setaras dengan target bisnis Perseroan.

The implementation of the Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is designed to support employees in improving their performance quality. Through a structured process starting from the identification of performance issues, the preparation of a performance improvement plan, implementation, and evaluation which is structured over a certain time frame, this program is expected to support employees in improving their performance to enable them in fulfilling or exceeding the expected standards.

Pelaksanaan Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) dirancang untuk mendukung karyawan dalam meningkatkan kualitas kinerjanya. Melalui proses yang terstruktur mulai dari identifikasi masalah kinerja, penyusunan rencana perbaikan kinerja, pelaksanaan, serta evaluasi yang disusun dalam rentang waktu tertentu, maka program ini diharapkan dapat mendukung karyawan perbaikan kinerjanya sehingga mampu memenuhi atau melampaui standar yang diharapkan.

# 6. HR Information System &amp; Analytics
## HR Information System &amp; Analytics

The HR Information System &amp; Analytics function plays a role in improving employee performance and services by facilitating various employee needs and requests, such as daily attendance, leave requests, performance appraisals, and medical functions, through the Employee Self Service module. This function also enables the measurement of employee engagement through employee surveys using Tech-based chat lists.

Fungsi HR Information System &amp; Analytics berperan dalam meningkatkan kinerja dan pelayanan kepada karyawan dengan memfasilitasi berbagai macam keperluan dan permintaan karyawan, seperti fungsi absensi harian, pengajuan studi, penilaian kinerja, medis, melalui modul Employee Self Service. Fungsi ini juga membantu mengukur employee engagement melalui employee survey dengan menggunakan Tech-based chat list.

HR Information System &amp; Analytics also developed Power BI to design MS Teams utilization dashboard, obtain information in the form of data analytics from every employee who uses MS Teams as one of the collaboration tools.

HR Information System &amp; Analytics juga mengembangkan Power BI untuk merancang dashboard MS Teams utilization, memperoleh informasi dalam bentuk data analytics dari setiap karyawan dalam penggunaan MS Teams sebagai salah satu collaboration tools yang digunakan.

Subsequently, the Company developed Tableau, a dashboard to present analytics related to the demographics of EMTEK and its subsidiaries. This dashboard also presents accurate data analytics related to the demographics of EMTEK Group in real time. This data is then used as a reference for designing future HR development strategies and plans. The utilization of the HR Information System &amp; Analytics and Tableau helps the Company provide optimal services to employees and enables the Company to design better and more effective HR development strategies.

Remudian, Perseroan mengembangkan Tableau, yaitu dashboard untuk menyajikan analytics terkait demografi EMTEK dan entitas anak. Dashboard ini juga menyajikan informasi data analytics terkait demografi EMTEK Group secara real time dan akurat. Data ini kemudian dijadikan acuan untuk merancang strategi maupun rencana pengembangan SDM ke depan. Pemanfaatan HR Information System &amp; Analytics dan Tableau membantu Perseroan memberikan layanan yang optimal kepada karyawan dan Perseroan pun dapat merancang strategi pengembangan SDM yang lebih baik dan efektif.

# Upholding Human Rights
## Menjunjung Tinggi Hak Asasi Manusia
[GRI 2-23] [GRI 406-1] [GRI 408-1] [GRI 409-1] [DJK F.18] [DJK F.19]

The Company demonstrates its commitment to ethical business practices by upholding and respecting human rights, including those who work in the Company or parties related to the Company's activities, such as consumers, suppliers, and the community around the Company. Not only a moral or legal obligation, the management of human rights in the Company is a key strategic approach for sustainability and long-term success.

EMTEK believes that good human rights management can prevent bad practices such as child labor, modern slavery, discrimination, or harassment in the workplace. This belief is certainly in line with the Company's principles and commitments. The following is the Company's human rights employment policies: [GRI 3-3]

EMTEK percaya pengelolaan Hak Asasi Manusia yang baik dapat mencegah praktik buruk seperti pekerja anak, pekerja paksa dan perbudakan modern, diskriminasi, atau pelecehan di tempat kerja. Hal ini tentunya sejalan dengan prinsip dan komitmen Perseroan. Berikut ini adalah kebijakan Hak Asasi Manusia Perseroan terhadap ketenagakerjaan, meliputi: [GRI 3-3]

- Fulfilling basic labor rights, including wages and remuneration (equal pay for equal work), social protection, occupational health and safety, social security and retirement, benefits, and assistance.
- Memenuhi hak dasar ketenagakerjaan, seperti dalam hal pengupahan dan remunerasi (equal pay for equal work), perlindungan sosial, kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja, jaminan sosial, jaminan hari tua, serta tunjangan dan bantuan.

- Providing equal pay based on the Regional Minimum Wage set by the Government and even exceed the minimum wage set by the Regional Government to fulfill a living wage.
- Membeli Remuveri yang sama berdasarkan Ujian Remuveri yang sama, berdokter Diarah yang ditetapkan Pemerintah bahkan melebihi upah minimum yang ditetapkan oleh Pemerintah Daerah untuk memenuhi upah yang layak.

- Respecting the employment relationship with employees, especially in terms of clarity of employee status, job placement, acceptance of new employees, transfers, relations, promotions, and demotions.
- Menghormati hubungan kerja dengan karyawan, khususnya dalam hal kejelasan status karyawan, penempatan kerja, penerimaan karyawan baru, mutasi, rotasi, promosi, dan demasi.

- Respecting the rights of employees to have good health, including the provision of medical examination and health-care insurance; clear working days and hours, including overtime, shifts and services on weekends; and rights for sick leave, menstrual leave, childbirth, miscarriage, shared leave, and annual leave.
- Menghormati hak karyawan untuk memiliki kesehatan yang baik, meliputi penyediaan jaminan pemeriksaan pengobatan dan perawatan kesehatan; hari dan jam kerja yang jelas, termasuk waktu lembur, shift dan layanan pada saat weekend; serta kesempatan untuk ruti sakit, ruti haid, melahirkan, keguguran, ruti bersama dan tahunan.

- Providing equal and non-discriminatory opportunities, as well as providing development resources for employees in the aspects of education, training, performance appraisal and employee career development.
- Memberikan kesempatan yang setara dan non-diskriminatif, serta menyediakan pengembangan sumber daya bagi karyawan dalam aspek pendidikan, pelatihan, penilaian prestasi, dan pengembangan karier karyawan.

- Respecting employees' rights to organize and assemble.
- Menghormati hak karyawan untuk berserikat dan berkumpul.

- Ensuring that there is no child labor and forced labor in the Company's employment practices.
- Memastikan tidak ada pekerja anak dan pekerja paksa dalam praktik ketenagakerjaan/Personalis.

- Providing social facilities to all employees, which include worship facilities, recreational and sports facilities.
- Memberikan fasilitas sosial kepada seluruh pekerja, yang mencakup fasilitas peribadatan, fasilitas rekresid dan olahraga.

- Providing clarity and appropriate rewards in any suspension, termination of employment and early retirement.
- Memberikan kejelasan dan imbalan yang sesuai dalam setiap tindakan skorong, pemutusan hubungan kerja (PHK) dan pensiun dini.

- Providing facilities for employees to submit and resolve complaints.
- Menyediakan fasilitas bagi pekerja untuk menyampaikan dan menyelesaikan keluh kesah.

- The Company is committed to providing a safe environment for all employees that is free from discrimination on any basis and from harassment in the workplace, including sexual harassment. Sexual harassment is unwanted sexual behavior that makes someone feel offended, humiliated and/or intimidated. This includes situations where a person is asked to engage in sexual activity as a condition of that person's movement, as well as situations that create a hostile, intimidating or humiliating environment for the recipient. Sexual harassment can comprise one or more incidents and become the conditions that are not met by a person, verbal or non-verbal.

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menyediakan lingkungan yang aman bagi seluruh karyawannya yang bebas dan diskriminasi atas dasar apapun dan dari pelecehan di tempat kerja termasuk pelecehan seksual. Pelecahan seksual adalah perilaku seksual yang tidak diinginkan yang membuat seseorang merasa tersinggung, terhina dan/atau terintimidasi. Ini termasuk situasi di mana seseorang diminta untuk melakukan aktivitas hubungan seksual sebagai syarat pekerjaan orang tersebut, serta situasi yang menciptakan lingkungan yang bermusuhan, mengintimidasi atau memperhatikan pececima. Pelecahan seksual dapat terdiri dari satu atau lebih kejadian dan tindakan yang merupakan pelecehan dan dapat berupa baik fisik, verbal atau pun nonverbal.
