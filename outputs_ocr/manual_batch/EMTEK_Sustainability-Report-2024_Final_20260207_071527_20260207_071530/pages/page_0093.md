# Report Quality Guidelines

## Pedoman Kualitas Laporan

[GRI 2-4] [GRI 2-14][OJK G.1]

The ESG Committee is responsible and directly involved in the preparation of this report, including the determination of relevant stakeholders and material topics. In addition, this Sustainability Report has also been approved by the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners as the highest governance organs of the Company which compositions are presented in the Company's Annual Report.

We strive to present a high-quality sustainability report by applying the reporting principles set forth by the OJK and GRI Standards, which enable users of the information to make objective assessments and decisions. These reporting principles include:

- Accuracy
- Balance
- Clarity
- Comparability
- Comprehensiveness
- Sustainability context
- Timeliness
- Verification

In order to maintain quality, we have invited an independent party to verify this sustainability report. Through a transparent process, we have appointed PT TÜV Rheinland Indonesia to provide assurance on this sustainability report that we have published with Standard AA1000AS Reporting Criteria GRI 2021 Type: 2 Level: Moderate (category/type of assurance, the assured). The Company has no affiliation with the external assurance. The use of this external assurance is solely based on the Company's intention to ensure transparency and reporting quality to stakeholders. [GRI 2-5]

In this report, we will provide specific statements on any restatements or corrections to the data/information presented in our previous reports and the reasons for such restatements.
