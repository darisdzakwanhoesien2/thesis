# Infrastructure Investment and Service Support

## Investasi Infrastruktur dan Dukungan Layanan

[GRI 203-1]

The Company continues to improve its facilities and infrastructure to support its operations and resilience in providing the best service to all customers. This infrastructure investment also aims to fulfill the needs of employees and including infrastructure support required by the community through CSR programs.

The Company continues to invest and develop infrastructure to provide tangible benefits to the community. Through Yayasan Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih, the Company has built ASA Bridges in Boyolali, Garut, and Soe (NTT), which now facilitate the mobility of around 1,200 villagers. In addition, sanitation and clean water facilities have also been built in Jonggol, Bogor, and Haumeni Ana (NTT), providing better access to the basic needs of the community. All of this infrastructure was built pro bono, as a form of care for communities in need. [GRI 3-3]

On the other hand, the Company also continues to improve the quality of broadcasting by presenting advanced digital transmitting antennas. With this technology, the public can enjoy SCTV and Indosiar broadcasts with clearer and higher quality images. [GRI 3-3].

Perseroan terus memperlengkapi sarana dan prasarana untuk mendukung operasional serta ketangguhannya dalam memberi pelayanan terbaik kepada seluruh pelanggan. Dukungan dalam bentuk investasi infrastruktur ini juga untuk memenuhi kebutuhan karyawan dan termasuk di dalamnya dukungan infrastruktur yang dibutuhkan masyarakat melalui program CSR.

Perseroan terus berinvestasi dan mengembangkan infrastruktur demi memberikan manfaat nyata bagi masyarakat. Melalui Yayasan Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih, Perseroan telah membangun Jembatan ASA di Boyolali, Garut, dan Soe (NTT), yang kini mempermudah mobilitas sekitar 1.200 warga desa. Selain itu, fasilitas sanitasi dan air bersih juga telah dibangun di Jonggol, Bogor, serta Haumeni Ana (NTT), memberikan akses lebih baik terhadap kebutuhan dasar masyarakat. Seluruh infrastruktur ini dibangun secara pro bono, sebagai wujud kepedulian terhadap komunitas yang membutuhkan. [GRI 3-3]

Di sisi lain, Perseroan juga terus meningkatkan kualitas penyiaran dengan menghadirkan antena pemancar digital canggih. Dengan teknologi ini, masyarakat dapat menikmati tayangan SCTV dan Indosiar dengan gambar yang lebih jernih dan berkualitas. [GRI 3-3].

# Comparison of Target and Performance of Sustainable Finance Portfolio

## Perbandingan Target dan Kinerja Portofolio Keuangan Berkelanjutan

[OJK F.3]

The Company has no specific targets related to financing or investment in financial instruments that are in line with sustainable finance. However, EMTEK's strategic share investment in Melchor Group with a business field related to carbon credit trading indirectly supports the development of sustainable finance. In addition, EMTEK also invested in benihbaik.com, a charity crowdfunding and CSR marketplace platform developed by PT Teman Baik Sejahtera.

Perseroan tidak memiliki target khusus terkait pembiayaan, atau investasi pada instrumen keuangan yang sejalan dengan keuangan berkelanjutan. Namun demikian, investasi saham strategis EMTEK di Melchor Group dengan bidang usaha yang berkaitan dengan perdagangan karbon kredit secara tidak langsung mendukung perkembangan keuangan berkelanjutan. Selain itu, EMTEK juga berinvestasi pada benihbaik.com, sebuah platform charity crowdfunding dan CSR Marketplace yang dikembangkan oleh PT Teman Baik Sejahtera.

# Distribution of Economic Value to Stakeholders

## Distribusi Nilai Ekonomi Kepada Pemangku Kepentingan

[GRI 201-1]

The Company's business performance has provided economic added value to all stakeholders. The Company contributes to economic growth by providing employment and other business opportunities for business partners or suppliers, and contributes to national development by paying taxes and levies. EMTEK Group's business activities have become a source of livelihood for thousands of its employees and provide benefits for shareholders, while also benefiting the community through its CSR activities. The Company aspires to continue growing and improving the quality of life of all stakeholders.

Kinerja usaha Perseroan telah memberi nilai tambah secara ekonomi kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Perseroan berkontribusi pada pertumbuhan ekonomi melalui lapangan pekerjaan dan terbukanya peluang usaha lainnya bagi mitra bisnis atau pemasok, berkontribusi bagi pembangunan bangsa melalui pembayaran pajak dan retribusi.
