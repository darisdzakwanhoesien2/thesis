# PRODUCTS, SERVICES, AND BUSINESS ACTIVITIES
## PRODUK, LAYANAN, DAN KEGIATAN USAHA YANG DIJALANKAN
[GRI 2-6] [OJK C.4]

Based on Article 3 of the Company's Articles of Association as outlined in the Deed No. 69 dated August 12, 2020 concerning the Purpose and Objectives of Establishment, EMTEK operates as a holding Company to a number of subsidiaries that primarily manages Media and Digital Division, Healthcare Division, Aviation Support Services Division, and Others Division (Connectivity: Internet Service Provider, Solutions, Investments, and Other Businesses).

Berdasarkan Pasal 3 dari Anggaran Dasar Perseroan No. 69 tanggal 12 Agustus 2020 terkait Maksud dan Tujuan Pendirian Perusahaan, maka EMTEK beroperasi sebagai perusahaan induk (holding) yang mengelola sejumlah entitas anak dengan usaha utama, yaitu Divisi Media dan Digital, Divisi Kesehatan, Divisi Jasa Dukungan Penerbangan, dan Divisi Lainnya (Konektivitas: Pengadaan Jasa Internet, Solusi, Investasi, serta Bisnis-bisnis Lain).

# Media and Digital Division
## Divisi Media dan Digital

Media and Digital Division manages Free-to-Air (FTA) television stations: SCTV, Indosiar, PT Surya Citra Pesona, Mentari TV, Ajwa TV and MOJI TV, as well as satellite television subscription broadcaster Nesparabola, along with a content production and distribution company, movies (Video), as well as a website megaportal and digital publications.

Divisi Media dan Digital mengelola stasiun televisi Free-to-Air (FTA): SCTV, Indosiar, PT Surya Citra Pesona, Mentari TV, Ajwa TV dan MOJI TV, serta penyiaran berlangganan televisi satelit yaitu Nesparabola, beserta perusahaan produksi dan distribusi konten, film dan video (Video), serta megaportal website serta publikasi digital.

# Healthcare Division
## Divisi Kesehatan

Healthcare Division manages health services through hospitals in order to meet the needs of the community for quality healthcare services.

Divisi Kesehatan mengelola layanan kesehatan melalui rumah sakit dalam rangka memenuhi kebutuhan masyarakat dengan layanan kesehatan yang berkualitas.

# Aviation Support Services Division
## Divisi Jasa Dukungan Penerbangan

Aviation Support Services Division manages air transportation support services, food solutions and other related services.

Divisi Jasa Dukungan Penerbangan yang mengelola layanan penunjang transportasi udara, solusi makanan dan jasa terkait lainnya.

# Others Division/Divisi Lainnya
(Connectivity: Internet Service Provider, Solutions, Investments, and Other Businesses)
(Konektivitas: Pengadaan Jasa Internet, Solusi, Investasi, serta Bisnis-bisnis lain)

Others Division (Technology, Digital Infrastructure, and Financial Services) manages a suite of information infrastructure and services, communications and information services and technology solutions for the telecommunication, banking, and retail payment industries, including solutions for integrated Very Small Aperture Terminal (VSAT), and services of Internet of Things (IoT) and related telecommunications services, also accommodates various initiatives and new breakthroughs in the areas of banking and financial services, technology and digital, as well as other areas in accordance with the Company's business directions.

Divisi Lainnya (Teknologi, Infrastruktur Digital, dan Layanan Keuangan) mengelola serangkaian infrastruktur dan jasa informasi, layanan komunikasi dan informatika serta solusi teknologi untuk industri telekomunikasi, perbankan, dan pembayaran ritel, termasuk solusi jasa untuk Very Small Aperture Terminal (VSAT) terintegrasi, dan layanan Internet of Things (IoT) dan layanan telekomunikasi terkait, serta mengakomodasi berbagai inisiatif dan terobosan baru dalam bidang jasa perbankan dan keuangan, teknologi dan digital, serta bidang-bidang lain yang sesuai dengan arah usaha Perseroan.
