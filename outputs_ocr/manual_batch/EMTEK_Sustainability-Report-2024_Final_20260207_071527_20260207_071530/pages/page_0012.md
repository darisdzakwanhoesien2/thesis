# COMPANY IN BRIEF SEKILAS PERUSAHAAN

Four decades into its business journey, PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk, hereinafter referred to as the "Company" or "EMTEK", has transformed and continues to reinforce its business in a sustainable manner. Business evolution and portfolio expansion have marked the long journey of EMTEK's business.

Established on August 3, 1983, the Company was formerly known as PT Elang Mahkota Komputer and focused on the procurement of computer equipment. In its development, the Company changed its name to PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi (EMTEK) in line with its continued expansion in the media, information, and technology industries. The name change was ratified under Deed No. 45 dated March 10, 1997, and approved by the Minister of Justice of the Republic of Indonesia under Decree No. C2-2694.HT.01.04.Th.97 dated April 15, 1997, and the Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 70 dated September 2, 1997.

As a holding company, EMTEK has a solid reputation for managing an ecosystem of business entities that oversee a variety of investment portfolios and services, including media, entertainment, content, online, telecommunications, technology, e-commerce, healthcare, banking, and financial services. Most recently, in 2024, the Company expanded into the aviation support services sector.

Through sustainable investment in both technology and supporting infrastructure, the Company and its subsidiaries are constantly expanding and innovating to strengthen their comparative advantages and competitiveness. Simultaneously, the Company is also continuously transforming its organization and human resources in order to build a solid and integrated organization that is adaptive to existing business dynamics.

In the media business segment, through its subsidiary, PT Surya Citra Media Tbk (SCMA), the Company concluded a strategic acquisition of shares in the free-to-air television station, PT Surya Citra Televisi (SCTV), in 2002. Thereafter, the Company carried out a series of other strategic corporate actions, including an Initial Public Offering (IPO) on January 12, 2010, and the acquisition of PT Indosiar Karya Mandiri Tbk (IDKM), a holding company of PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri (Indosiar).

In order to consolidate EMTEK Group's competitiveness in the national media industry, the Company decided to synergize the outstanding competencies of SCMA, the holding company of SCTV, with IDKM, the holding company of Indosiar, in 2013. This synergy has proven to be highly effective, especially in increasing the scale of EMTEK Group's economy in procuring television programs as well as supporting the efficiency strategies and related synergies, including the acquisition of exclusive broadcasting rights for various prestigious national and international sports programs, such as BRI Liga-1 Football, Liga-2, Menpora Cup 2021, Copa America 2021, Tokyo Olympics 2020, Indonesian National Team Matches, UEFA Champions League/Europa League, English Premier League, Official Broadcaster for the FIFA World Cup Qatar

Empat dekade sudah perjalanan bisnis PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk, yang selanjutnya disebut "Perseroan" atau "EMTEK" telah bertransformasi dan terus memperkuat bisnisnya secara berkelanjutan. Evolusi bisnis dan ekspansi portofolio telah mewarnai perjalanan panjang bisnis EMTEK.

Didirikan pada tanggal 3 Agustus 1983, awalnya Perseroan yang bernama PT Elang Mahkota Komputer fokus dengan bisnis penyediaan peralatan komputer. Dalam perkembangannya, Perseroan mengubah nama perusahaan menjadi PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi (EMTEK) seiring dengan ekspansi yang terus dilakukan pada industri media, informasi, dan teknologi. Perubahan nama Perseroan tersebut ditatikan melalui Akta No. 45 tanggal 10 Maret 1997 dan disetujui oleh Menteri Kenakiman Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. C2-2694.HT.01.04. Th.97 tanggal 15 April 1997 dan Tambahan Berita Negara RI No. 70 tanggal 2 September 1997.

Sebagai perusahaan holding, EMTEK memiliki reputasi kuat yang menaungi suatu ekosistem yang terdiri dari entitas bisnis yang mengelola berbagai portofolio investasi dan jasa yang meliputi industri media, hiburan, konten, daring, telekomunikasi, teknologi, e-commerce, layanan kesehatan hingga jasa perbankan dan keuangan. Terkini, di tahun 2024 Perseroan telah merambah di sektor bisnis jasa dukungan penerbangan.

Melalui investasi berkelanjutan, baik dari sisi teknologi dan infrastruktur pendukungnya, Perseroan dan entitas anaknya senantiasa melakukan ekspansi dan inovasi yang memperkuat keunggulan komparatif maupun daya saing mereka. Secara bersamaan, Perseroan juga melakukan transformasi yang berkesinambungan pada sisi organisasi dan sumber daya manusia dalam rangka membangun suatu organisasi yang solid dan berintegritas serta adaptif terhadap dinamika bisnis yang ada.

Di segmen bisnis media, Perseroan melalui entitas anaknya, PT Surya Citra Media Tbk (SCMA), pada tahun 2002 menuntaskan akuisisi strategis atas kepemilikan saham di stasiun televisi free-to-air, PT Surya Citra Televisi (SCTV). Kemudian, serangkaian aksi korporasi strategis lainnya dilakukan oleh Perseroan, termasuk melakukan Penawaran Umum Perdana Saham (IPO) pada tanggal 12 Januari 2010 dan merampungkan aksi akuisisi atas PT Indosiar Karya Mandiri Tbk (IDKM), yang merupakan induk usaha stasiun televisi PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri (Indosiar).

Untuk memperkuat daya saing Grup EMTEK di industri media nasional, pada tahun 2013 Perseroan memutuskan untuk menyirergikan kompetensi unggul dari SCMA, induk usaha SCTV, dengan IDKM, induk usaha Indosiar. Sinergi tersebut terdukti sangat tepat, terutama untuk meningkatkan skala ekonomi Grup EMTEK dalam pengadaan program acara televisi serta mendukung strategi efisiensi dan sinergi terkait, yaitu antara lain dalam memperoleh hak siar eksklusif untuk berbagai program olahraga bergengsi skala nasional maupun internasional, seperti Sepakbola BRI Liga-1, Liga-2, Piala Menpora 2021, Copa America 2021, Olimpiade Tokyo 2020, Laga Timnas Indonesia, UEFA Champions League/Europa League, English Premier League, Official Broadcaster untuk FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022, Official Broadcaster untuk Piala Dunia U-17 tahun

2022, Official Broadcaster for the U-17 World Cup in 2023, the 2024 President's Cup and the 2024 U-19 AFF Cup, as well as Official Broadcaster for the 2024 Paris Olympics.

Subsequently in 2015, by taking advantage of opportunities amid the rapid growth of Indonesia's digital economy, the Company, through its subsidiary, PT Kreatif Media Karya (KMK), invested in one of Indonesia's leading marketplaces, Bukalapak.com. This expansion into the digital sector marked EMTEK Group's entry into various fields, starting from logistics technology, education technology, insurance technology to online transportation through an investment in Grab Indonesia, one of Indonesia's leading ride-hailing companies.

The Company also expanded its portfolio in the digital media business by realizing an investment in PT KapanLag Dot Com Networks (KLY) which manages a number of renowned digital portals, including Liputan6.com, Merdeka.com, Dream.co.id, Fimela, Brillo.com, Kapanlagi.com, Bola.com, Bola.net, and Otosia.com, which have diverse target segments.

At the same time, the Company is developing an Over-the-Top (OTT) streaming platform called Video. The Video platform is ahead of other competitors because it opens up opportunities and access for all levels of society through Advertising-based Video on Demand or AVOD business model. This strategy benefits every user, regardless of their economic background, to access quality local original movie and drama series content, Korean dramas, famous Hollywood series, and even premium sports content. This has made Video known as the "home of premium sports content".

Video is a world-class platform that was proudly built by more than 200 local engineers. As of December 2024, Video has released more than 90 local Original Series, emphasizing its commitment to content originality, its success is further reinforced by its achievement as the number one OTT platform in Indonesia, both in terms of the highest number of subscribers and total monthly active users in the third quarter of 2024, according to a report by Media Partners Asia (MPA). In addition, according to the Google Play Store Top Grossing Entertainment 2024 report, Video also tanks first as the video streaming platform with the highest consumer spending in Indonesia.

The Company's position in the digital media industry is also supported by the presence of MOJI, which is a rebrand of O Channel television that focuses on sports, lifestyle, and entertainment programs that are relevant to its target segment of youngsters aged 15-40 years old. The Company also provides entertainment and educational programs for children and mothers with young children through Mentari TV. Through aggressive investment in various media portfolios, the Company has successfully maintained its leadership in the media segment in spite of the gradual implementation of the Analog Switch Off (ASO) policy in various regions in Indonesia since November 2022, which has encouraged the transition of television broadcasting from analog to digital. By the end of 2024, EMTEK Group's total media market share stood at 36.1%.

Eksponsi di sektor digital tersebut menandai langkah awal Group EMTEK untuk merambah berbagai bidang, mulai dari teknologi logistik, teknologi pendidikan, teknologi asuransi hingga transportasi daring melalui penanaman modal di Grab Indonesia, yang merupakan salah satu perusahaan ride-hailing terkemuka di Indonesia.

Portal digital tersebut yaitu Liputan6.com, Merdeka.com, Dream.co.id, Fimela, Brillo.com, Kapanlagi.com, Bola.com, Bola.net, dan Otosia.com membuat KLY memiliki target segment yang beragam.

Di saat yang sama, Perseroan mengembangkan sebuah platform streaming Over-the-Top (OTT) yaitu Video. Platform Video menjadi lebih unggul ketimbang kompetitor lainnya karena membuka kesempatan dan akses bagi setiap lapisan masyarakat dengan menggunakan model bisnis, salah satunya melalui Advertising-based Video on Demand atau AVOD. Strategi ini menguntungkan setiap pengguna, terlepas dari latar belakang ekonominya, untuk mendapatkan konten film dan serial drama orisini lokal yang berkualitas, drama Korea, serial Hollywood terkenal hingga layangan konten olahraga premium. Hal ini menjadikan Video dikenal dengan sebutan "home of premium sports contents".

Video merupakan platform kelas dunia yang dengan bangga dibangun oleh lebih dari 200 engineer lokal. Hingga Desember 2024, Video telah merilis lebih dari 90 judul Original Series lokal, menegaskan komitmennya terhadap orisinalitas konten. Keberhasiannya semakin diperkuat dengan pencapaian sebagai platform OTT nomor satu di Indonesia, baik dalam jumlah subscriber terbanyak maupun total pengguna aktif bulanan pada kuartal ketiga 2024, berdasarkan laporan Media Partners Asia (MPA). Selain itu, menurut laporan Google Play Store Top Grossing Entertainment 2024, Video juga menempati peringkat pertama sebagai platform video streaming dengan nilai belanja konsumen tertinggi di Indonesia.

Posisi Perseroan di industri media digital juga didukung oleh keberadaan MOJI yang merupakan hasil rebounding terhadap televisi O Channel yang berfokus pada tayangan program olahraga, gaya hidup, dan hiburan yang sesuai dengan target segmennya, yaitu anak muda dengan rentang usia 15-40 tahun. Perseroan juga menyediakan tayangan hiburan dan edukatif untuk segmen anak-anak dan ibu yang memiliki anak kecil melalui Mentari TV. Investasi yang agresif di berbagai portofolio media tersebut berhasil mempertahankan kepemimpinan Perseroan di segmen media bahkan di saat pemirsa di Tanah Air harus menghadapi penerapan kebijakan Anotog Switch Off (ASO) secara bertahap di berbagai wilayah di Indonesia sejak November 2022, yang mendorong peralihan penyiaran program televisi dari analog ke digital. Pada akhir tahun 2024, total penguasaan pangsa pasar media Grup EMTEK adalah 36,1%.
