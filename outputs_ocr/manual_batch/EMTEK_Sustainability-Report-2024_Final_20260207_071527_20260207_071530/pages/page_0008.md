# EXPLANATION ON SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY AND APPROACH
## PENJELASAN STRATEGI DAN PENDEKATAN KEBERLANJUTAN
[GRI 2-22] [OJK A.1]

The Company and all of its subsidiaries are strongly committed to uphold the principle of sustainability as the main guideline for running its business in the long term. The Company's policies are based on the principles of good corporate governance, responsibility, integrity, transparency, upholding human rights, non-discriminatory practices, and always listening to the expectations of stakeholders in order to continue growing and providing added value to all of its stakeholders.

We also ensure that our business activities are sustainable by minimizing the negative impact of any risk that has the potential to adversely affect the Company and its stakeholders. Our risk management is based on ISO 31000:2018, which includes identification, measurement of potential and impact, prevention, monitoring, and evaluation. Through this approach, we aim to continuously monitor and reduce the negative impacts of all aspects (economic, social, environmental) in a measurable way.

The Company has an Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) committee that is responsible for making policies, supervising, and evaluating each sustainability program. This committee continues to strengthen sustainability within the organization and throughout its business chain by implementing policies that promote sustainability values. As a form of our sincerity and commitment to carry out the sustainability transformation, this Committee is comprised of the Board of Directors. This Committee is chaired by Mrs. Titi Maria Rusli and assisted by Mrs. Sutiana Ali and Mr. Jay Geoffrey Wacher as members.

In order to provide benefits and added value to all stakeholders, including employees, shareholders, customers, business partners, the community, and the government, as well as to support the improvement of environmental quality, the Company has established a sustainability strategy that integrates our business activities with the principles of good corporate governance. ## Short to medium-term targets (Sasaran jangka pendek – menengah:

1. Gradually until 2025, reduce energy and water consumption in the office and in all operational activities by 10-15%.
Mengurangi konsumsi energi dan air di kantor serta dalam setiap kegiatan operasional Perseroan sebanyak 10-15% secara bertahap hingga tahun 2025.

2. Gradually until 2025, reduce paper use in the office by implementing a paperless policy in operational activities such as payment processes and e-memo making by 20%.
Menurunkan penggunaan kertas di kantor dengan menerapkan kebijakan tanpa kertas (paperless) dalam kegiatan operasional Perseroan seperti proses pembayaran dan pembuatan e-memo sebanyak 20% secara bertahap hingga tahun 2025.

3. Gradually until 2025, a 100% comprehensive evaluation of the Sustainability Due Diligence (SDD) and understanding of the Company's new partners, suppliers, and business associates (Know Your Customers or KYC) will be carried out.
Secara bertahap hingga tahun 2025, akan dilakukan evaluasi secara menyeluruh (100%) terhadap uji tuntas keberlanjutan (Sustainability Due Diligence – SDD) serta pemahaman mengenai mitra kerja, pemasok, dan rekan usaha baru Perseroan (Know Your Customers atau KYC).

4. Implement a hybrid work system (a mix of work from office and work from home) to provide opportunities and flexibility, especially for every female worker who has just given birth, male worker who has just had a child or every worker or has a family member with special needs who requires additional attention or responsibility. Furthermore, in our office there is space for breastfeeding mothers as a form of our concern for the exclusive breastfeeding movement.
Menerapkan sistem kerja hybrid (sistem kerja gabungan antara bekerja di kantor dan di rumah) sehingga memberikan peluang dan fleksibilitas secara khusus kepada setiap pekerja perempuan yang baru melahirkan, pekerja laki-laki yang baru memiliki anak atau setiap pekerja atau memiliki anggota keluarga berkeluruhan khusus yang memerlukan perhatian atau tanggung jawab lebih. Lebih lanjut, di kantor kami terdapat ruang untuk ibu menyusui sebagai bentuk kepedulian kami terhadap gerakan ASI eksklusif.

5. Increase employee participation in donating usable goods to people in need through Yayasan Amat Peduli Kasih Donation Box in SCTV Tower Building. This initiative indirectly supports waste reduction through recycling by distributing usable goods to people in need as well as educating every employee about the importance of implementing recycling principles in everyday life.
Meningkatkan partisipasi karyawan dalam mendinasikan barang-barang yang layak pakai kepada masyarakat yang membutuhkan melalui Kotak Donasi Yayasan Amat Peduli Kasih di Gedung SCTV Tower. Hal ini secara tidak langsung sebagai langkah nyata mendukung pengurangan limbah melalui praktik daur ulang dengan menyalurkan barang layak pakai kepada masyarakat yang membutuhkan sekaligus melakukan edukasi terhadap setiap karyawan mengenai pentingnya untuk melakukan prinsip daur ulang dalam kehidupan sehari-hari.
