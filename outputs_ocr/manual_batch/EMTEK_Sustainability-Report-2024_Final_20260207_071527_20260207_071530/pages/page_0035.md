# NOMINATION PROCESS, SELECTION OF THE HIGHEST GOVERNANCE ORGANS AND ITS SUPPORTING COMMITTEES

PROSES PENCALONAN, PEMILIHAN BADAN GOVERNANSI TERTINGGI SERTA KOMITE PENDUKUNGNYA

[GRI 2-10]

The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are appointed and dismissed by the GMS, taking into account the recommendations from the Nomination Committee and the Remuneration Committee. The Company may also hire an independent professional executive search firm to identify and recommend suitable candidates.

The Company's Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are selected based on their experience/competence in the relevant field, diversity of background, alignment with the Company's vision, mission, and strategy, as well as other criteria, including: individuals who possess good character, integrity, and morals; committed to complying with laws and regulations; have not been convicted of crimes that cause financial losses to the state and/or crimes related to the financial sector. Other technical requirements and criteria that must be met are determined by the Board of Commissioners based on recommendations from the Committee Nomination and Remuneration Committee, taking into account the needs to achieve the Company's vision, mission, and strategy.

To avoid conflicts of interest, Mrs. Marianna Sutadi, who is an Independent Commissioner and Lead Commissioner, is assigned to be in charge of the Company's governance organs. She also concurrently serves as Chairwoman of the Nomination Committee, the Remuneration Committee and the Risk Monitoring Committee of the Company. [GRI 2-11]

The procedures and criteria for the nomination and selection of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners, as well as the formation of committees and other supporting bodies, which refer to the Financial Services Authority Regulations, can be found in the Company's 2024 Annual Report.

Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi diangkat dan diberhentikan oleh RUPS dengan mempertimbangkan rekomendasi dari Komite Nominasi dan Komite Remunerasi. Perseroan juga dapat menggunakan jasa lembaga pencarian eksekutif/professional (professional executive search firm) yang independen guna mengidentifikasi dan merekomendasikan kandidat yang memenuhi persyaratan.

Pemilihan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan didasari oleh pertimbangan pengalaman/kompetensi di bidang terkait, keragaman latar belakang, kesesuaian dengan kebutuhan untuk pencapaian visi, misi, dan strategi Perseroan, serta beberapa kriteria yang mengikat di antaranya meliputi: Individu yang mempunyai akhtak, integritas, moral yang baik; memiliki komitmen untuk mematuhi peraturan perundangan; tidak pernah dihukum karena melakukan tindak pidana yang merugikan keuangan negara dan/atau tindak pidana yang berkaitan dengan sektor keuangan. Persyaratan dan kriteria teknis yang wajib dipenuhi lainnya ditentukan oleh Dewan Komisaris berdasarkan rekomendasi dari Komite Nominasi dan Komite Remunerasi dengan mempertimbangkan kebutuhan untuk pencapaian visi, misi, dan strategi Perseroan.

Penanggung jawab tertinggi badan governansi Perseroan dijabat oleh Ibu Marianna Sutadi yang merupakan Komisaris Independen dan Lead Commissioner sehingga terhindar dari konflik kepentingan yang juga merangkap sebagai Ketua Komite Nominasi, Komite Remunerasi serta Ketua Komite Pemantau Risiko Perseroan. [GRI 2-11]

Prosedur serta kriteria dalam nominasi dan seleksi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris, serta pembentukan komite-komite dan organ pendukung lainnya yang mengacu pada Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, selengkapnya dapat dilihat dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan tahun 2024.

# DIVERSITY IN GOVERNANCE COMPOSITION AS AN ADDED VALUE FOR THE COMPANY'S GROWTH

KERAGAMAN KOMPOSISI GOVERNANSI SEBAGAI NILAI TAMBAH BAGI KEMAJUAN PERSEROAN

As management and supervisory organs, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors consist of members with diverse backgrounds in terms of expertise/experience, age, gender, disciplines and education, and other aspects. This indicates that the Company is extremely open-minded and considers diversity as a unique asset that drives its continuous growth.

Sebagai organ pengelola dan pengawas, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi terdiri dari berbagai latar belakang keahlian/pengalaman, usia, jenis kelamin, disiplin ilmu dan pendidikan, serta latar belakang lainnya. Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa Perseroan sangat terbuka dan memandang keragaman sebagai suatu kekayaan tersendiri yang dijadikan sebagai kekuatan untuk terus berkembang.
