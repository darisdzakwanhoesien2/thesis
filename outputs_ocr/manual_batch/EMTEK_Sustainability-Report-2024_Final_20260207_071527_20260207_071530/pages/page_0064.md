# Kebijakan

A balanced environmental ecosystem provides resources for the sustainability of humans and other living beings. This balance can be disrupted by changes, including the loss of functions or components that can sever the chain of an ecosystem. Commitment, responsibility and participation of all parties are needed to maintain and create environmental sustainability. In the context of preserving the environment, EMTEK Group has taken numerous initiatives in accordance with its capacity. These initiatives include: employing with environmental laws and regulations, managing the direct impact of operations on the community and supply chain, raising awareness and providing training to employees, and continuously conducting monitoring and evaluation.

All of our subsidiaries that are engaged in several business sectors, including media, hospitals, air transportation support services, technology, digital infrastructure, and financial services, have complied with all environmental regulations. # Person in Charge of Environmental Management

The ESG Committee is responsible for managing, monitoring, evaluating and ensuring compliance with sustainable environmental practices. In carrying out its responsibilities, the ESG Committee is assisted by the Procurement &amp; Corporate Affairs Division, the Human Resources Division, and the Sustainability Function to ensure that policies are implemented in accordance with the applicable regulations and standards. The ESG Committee periodically submits reports/holds meetings with the Board of Directors to evaluate sustainability policies, including policies and issues in environmental management. In 2024, the ESG Committee held 7 (seven) meetings and 3 (three) joint meetings with the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners to discuss, among others, the supervision of ESG practices in the Company.

# Targets

Some of the Company's targets are to reduce energy and water consumption in the office and in all of its operational activities by 10-15% gradually until 2025, and then set net zero emissions by 2050 for scopes 1 and 2, as well as gradually increase paper efficiency in the office by 20% until 2025 through the implementation of a paperless policy in operational activities such as payment processes and e-memo making. These initiatives will positively impact the environment, while also strengthening the efficient use of resources.

# Penanggung Jawab Pengelolaan Lingkungan

Komite ESG bertanggung jawab untuk mengelola, memantau, mengevaluasi dan memastikan kepatuhan terhadap praktik-praktik lingkungan berkelanjutan. Dalam pelaksanaannya Komite ESG dibantu oleh Divisi Pengadaan &amp; Corporate Affairs, Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia, serta Fungsi Sustainability untuk memastikan implementasi kebijakan sesuai dengan regulasi dan standar yang berlaku. Secara berkala Komite ESG memberi laporan/mengadakan rapat dengan Direksi untuk melakukan evaluasi terhadap kebijakan keberlanjutan, termasuk kebijakan dan masalah di pengelolaan lingkungan hidup. Selama tahun 2024, Komite ESG telah mengadakan 7 (tujuh) kali rapat serta 3 (tiga) kali rapat bersama Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris yang membahas, antara lain, mengenai pengawasan terhadap praktik ESG di Perseroan.

# Target Sasaran

Beberapa sasaran yang ingin dicapai Perseroan yaitu mengurangi konsumsi energi dan air di kantor serta dalam setiap kegiatan operasional Perseroan sebanyak 10-15% secara bertahap hingga tahun 2025 untuk kemudian menetapkan net zero emissions pada tahun 2050 untuk scope 1 dan 2, serta efisiensi kertas di kantor dengan menerapkan kebijakan tanpa kertas (paperless) dalam kegiatan operasional seperti proses pembayaran dan pembuatan e-memo sebanyak 20% secara bertahap hingga tahun 2025. Inisiatif ini akan membawa dampak positif terhadap lingkungan, sekaligus memperkuat efisiensi penggunaan sumber daya.
