# ACTIVITIES TO ESTABLISH A SUSTAINABILITY CULTURE

# KEGIATAN MEMBANGUN BUDAYA KEBERLANJUTAN [OJK F.1]

The Company requires support from all of its employees to implement sustainability transformation. Therefore, through ESG Committee, the Company actively socializes and involves employees in various sustainability materials, which include economic, social, environmental, and governance aspects.

The Company has organized various trainings to reinforce the culture of sustainability throughout the organization, including socialization and training for the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and employees. These trainings aim to improve the understanding and skills of each individual, so that they can contribute to the realization of the Company's sustainability vision. Thus, every member of the organization is expected to implement the principles of sustainability in their daily activities, so that the culture of sustainability becomes a core value that drives the Company's operations towards a more sustainable future.

The following trainings were carried out to build the sustainability culture within EMTEK in 2024:

Perseroan membutuhkan dukungan seluruh karyawan untuk melaksanakan transformasi keberlanjutan. Untuk itu Perseroan melalui Komite ESG secara aktif melakukan sosialisasi dan menyertakan karyawan dalam berbagai materi keberlanjutan yang terdiri dari aspek ekonomi, sosial, lingkungan, dan governansi.

Perseroan telah melaksanakan berbagai kegiatan untuk memperkuat budaya keberlanjutan di seluruh organisasi, termasuk sosialisasi dan pelatihan bagi Komisaris, Direksi, dan karyawan. Kegiatan ini bertujuan untuk meningkatkan pemahaman dan keterampilan setiap individu, agar mereka dapat berkontribusi dalam mewujudkan visi keberlanjutan perusahaan. Dengan demikian, diharapkan setiap anggota organisasi dapat mengimplementasikan prinsip-prinsip keberlanjutan dalam aktivitas sehari-hari, sehingga budaya keberlanjutan menjadi nilai inti yang mendorong operasional perusahaan menuju masa depan yang lebih berkelanjutan.
