## Governance

To ensure the implementation of sustainable performance, EMTEK has taken various steps to support the application of transparent, accountable, and responsible Good Corporate Governance ("GCO") principles at every organizational level.

At the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners level, we believe that discussions with an expert, accompanied by reading materials on the application of GCO principles, are necessary. Therefore, we have received input from Indra Saffiri, SH., MM., CRIMP, QIA., a leading capital market legal expert in Indonesia, in the form of books titled "Risk Governance of Public Companies" and "Legal Risk Management, ISO 31022, COSO-ERM, Basel Framework," which were provided to every member of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners. Furthermore, at every organizational level, we feel the need to conduct various socialization and continuous education sessions related to personal data protection, Anti-Bribery Management System training, and the initiation of ISO 37001:2016 certification for corruption prevention.

Quantitatively, throughout 2024, EMTEK achieved a score of 101.34 points in the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS), placing it in the "Leadership in Corporate Governance" (Level 5) category. This achievement led EMTEK to receive the "Best Responsibility of the Board" and "Top 50 Big Capitalization Public Listed Company" awards at the 15th ICD Corporate. EMTEK's sustainability transformation also bore fruit with its inclusion in several ESG Indices such as SRI-KEHATI, ESG Quality 45 (DX KEHATI), ESG Sector Leaders (DX KEHATI), and ESG Leaders. EMTEK is also listed in the MSCI Small Cap Index and scored 60/100 from S&amp;P Global CSA with "Very High" data availability. Additionally, Sustainalytics gave a score of 14.9, indicating EMTEK as a "Low Risk" company.
