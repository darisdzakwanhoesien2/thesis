# Occupational Health Services
## Layanan Kesehatan Kerja
[GRI 403-3]

The Company has a healthcare unit for employees who are unwell. The healthcare unit is supported by adequate facilities and trained medical personnel. These facilities are provided for employees who are ill so that they can receive medical treatment on time and effectively.

The person authorized to decide whether an employee is sick or in an emergency at work is the Head of Human Resources Division (HRD) of the Company. As additional information, while we have employee health data, we highly respect and maintain the confidentiality of this information as it is the employee privacy.

Perseroan memiliki unit kesehatan untuk para pegawai yang sedang dalam kondisi tidak sehat. Unit kesehatan tersebut dilengkapi oleh fasilitas yang memadai beserta tenaga medis yang terlatih. Fasilitas tersebut disediakan untuk pegawai yang sedang sakit agar mendapatkan perawatan medis dalam waktu yang tepat dan efektif.

Pihak yang berwenang untuk memutuskan karyawan sakit atau dalam keadaan darurat di tempat kerja adalah Kepala Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia (HRD) Perseroan. Sebagai informasi tambahan, dalam kami memiliki data kesehatan karyawan, kami sangat menghormati dan menjaga kerahasiaan informasi tersebut sebagai hak privasi karyawan.

# Employee Participation, Consultation, and Communication on Occupational Health and Safety
## Partisipasi, Konsultasi, dan Komunikasi Pekerja tentang Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja
[GRI 403-4]

The ESG Committee delegates the Head of the Procurement &amp; Corporate Affairs Division to oversee the OHS aspects. In this context, the Head of the Procurement &amp; Corporate Affairs Division is responsible for the management of Occupational Health and Safety in the Company. As such, the Company remains committed to ensuring employee participation, consultation, and communication related to OHS aspects. Employees can provide their input through channels established by the Head of the Procurement &amp; Corporate Affairs Division, and the Company continues to encourage their active contribution in maintaining and improving Occupational Health and Safety standards in the workplace.

Komite ESG memberikan delegasi kepada Kepala Divisi Pengadaan &amp; Corporate Affairs untuk mengurusi aspek K3. Dalam konteks ini, Kepala Divisi Pengadaan &amp; Corporate Affairs bertanggung jawab atas pengelolaan K3 di Perseroan. Dengan demikian, Perseroan tetap berkomitmen untuk memastikan partisipasi, konsultasi, dan komunikasi pekerja terkait dengan aspek K3. Karyawan dapat memberikan masukan mereka melalui saluran yang telah ditetapkan oleh Kepala Divisi Pengadaan &amp; Corporate Affairs, dan Perseroan terus mendorong kontribusi aktif para pekerja dalam menjaga dan meningkatkan standar Keselamatan serta Kesehatan Kerja di lingkungan kerja.

# Employee Training on Occupational Health and Safety
## Pelatihan Pekerja tentang Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja
[GRI 403-5]

Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) training is essential to ensure that employees have the knowledge and skills necessary to work safely. This training is expected to provide insight into potential workplace hazards, understand what to do in emergency situations and raise awareness of applicable OHS regulations and standards.

EMTEK also has SOPs regarding the Guidelines for Maintaining Security and Order within the EMTEK Environment, which provide guidelines for all security &amp; safety officers (100%). All security officers (100%) in our company have been trained by a third party in performing their duties. Each security &amp; safety officer is instructed to always follow the established safety procedures, communicate openly, and always respect human rights

EMTEK juga telah mempunyai SOP tentang Pedoman Penyelenggaraan Keamanan dan Ketertiban di Lingkungan EMTEK yang mengatur tentang pedoman atau petunjuk bagi seluruh petugas security &amp; safety (100%). Saluruh petugas keamanan tersebut (100%) di perusahaan kami sudah dilatih oleh pihak ketiga dalam menjalankan tugas. Setiap petugas security &amp; safety tersebut diinstruksikan untuk selalu mengikuti prosedur keselamatan yang telah

# Conducting safety training for employees, such as evacuation drills once a year. EMTEK also collaborates with the National Disaster Management Agency (BNPA) to conduct earthquake evacuation drills.

Mengadakan pelatihan bagi karyawan dalam aspek keselamatan, seperti simulasi evakuasi setahun sekali. EMTEK juga bekerja sama dengan Badan Nasional Penanggulangan Bencana (BNPA) untuk melakukan simulasi evakuasi gampis.

Conducting regular inspections of electrical and electronic equipment in all workspaces together with the relevant building management, security, and engineering teams.

Melakukan pemeriksaan listrik dan perlengkapan elektronik yang menyala secara berkala ke seluruh ruangan kerja dengan tim management building, security, dan engineering terkait.

Appointing an experienced security team on each floor as a floor warden to identify and investigate occupational health and safety hazards in the office environment as well as a form of hierarchy control where the floor warden is also the main point of contact for every employee of the Company to report OHS incidents so that investigations of related OHS incidents can be carried out in accordance with the applicable OHS handling mechanism to find the root cause of the incident so that it does not happen again in the future, and provide socialization of this to all employees of the Company.

The OHS control hierarchy consists of five levels, namely: Elimination, Substitution, Engineering controls, Administrative controls, Personal protective equipment (PPE). These levels are ranked based on their effectiveness in controlling risks, as follows:

1. Elimination: eliminate the source of the hazard, such as spilled oil.
2. Substitution: replacing something dangerous with something safer, such as replacing a slippery floor.
3. Engineering control: performing technical engineering to reduce potential hazards, such as using specialized work equipment to reduce the risk of falls.
4. Administrative control: controlling risks and hazards with regulations related to occupational safety and health.
5. Personal protective equipment (PPE): providing PPE to workers, such as safety glasses, hearing protection, face shields, respirators and gloves.

Providing shuttle facilities for employees in certain positions.

Menyediakan fasilitas antar jemput bagi karyawan di posisi tertentu.

Organizing health training for employees, the Company strives to raise awareness of the importance of healthy living and maintaining work-life balance. This training includes education on healthy diet, stress management, regular exercise, and other health aspects aimed at creating a work environment that supports holistic well-being, both physically and mentally.

Melakukan pelatihan kesehatan untuk karyawan, Perseroan berupaya meningkatkan kesadaran akan pentingnya hidup sehat dan menjaga keseimbangan antara pekerjaan dan gaya hidup. Pelatihan ini mencakup edukasi tentang pola makan sehat, manajemen stres, olahraga rutin, dan aspek-aspek kesehatan lainnya yang bertujuan untuk menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang mendukung kesejahteraan holistik, baik secara fisik maupun mental.

Health facilities such as medical check-ups, health insurance, and BFUE Health insurance are provided for permanent and contract employees. In addition, eyewear and dental health care facilities are also provided to permanent and contract employees. The Company also provides various welfare support facilities, including a health clinic, lactation room, place of worship facilities, employee parking, and other facilities.

Fasilitas kesehatan seperti medical check-up, asuransi kesehatan, dan jaminan BFUE Kesehatan disediakan untuk karyawan tetap dan karyawan kontrak. Selain itu, fasilitas kacamata dan perawatan kesehatan gigi juga diberikan kepada karyawan tetap dan karyawan kontrak. Perseroan juga menyediakan berbagai fasilitas penunjang kesejahteraan, termasuk Klinik kesehatan, ruang Laktasi, sarana tempat ibadah, parkir khusus karyawan, dan fasilitas lainnya.

The Company's office lobby has been designed to be disability friendly, making it easier for those with special needs to enter the high-rise area. In addition, there are also nap pods for employees who need a break, and lounge facilities that provide space to interact with fellow employees.

Lots kantor Perseroan telah dirancang dan didesain secara ramah disabilitas, memudahkan mereka dengan kebutuhan khusus untuk memasuki area gedung bertingkat. Selain itu, nap pod tersedia bagi karyawan yang membutuhkan istirahat, dan fasilitas lounge menyediakan ruang untuk berinteraksi dengan sesama karyawan.

This approach has effectively reduced the risk of work accidents. Throughout 2024, we recorded no work accident incidents.
