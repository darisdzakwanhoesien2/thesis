# HR Management Structure

## Struktur Pengelolaan SDM

In line with the industrial dynamics in which the Company and its subsidiaries operate, the Company designs an effective EMTEK Group HR Management structure. The structure supports the Company in producing, developing, and maintaining employee productivity and motivating employees to work effectively and creatively in producing solutions and implementing best practices throughout EMTEK Group.

Seiring dengan dinamika di industri di mana Perseroan dan entitas anaknya beroperasi, Perseroan merancang struktur pengelolaan SDM Grup EMTEK yang efektif. Struktur tersebut mendukung Perseroan dalam menghasilkan, mengembangkan, dan mempertahankan produktivitas karyawan dan memotivasi karyawan untuk bekerja lebih efektif dan kreatif dalam menghasilkan solusi serta menerapkan praktik-praktik terbaik di seluruh Grup EMTEK.

# 1. Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner

## Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner

The Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner function is instrumental in facilitating the implementation of the Company's strategic plan by developing and improving the effectiveness of the organizational structure and processes. The customized work mechanism enables the organization to move adaptively, efficiently, and effectively. In addition, this function also supports the Company's strategic plan, specifically through its research titled 'Future of Work', which explores the impact of Artificial Intelligence on the workforce and work environment. This research was conducted as part of a project-based internship program for students of the University of New South Wales (UNSW). Through this research, the Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner function seeks to prepare and develop human resources to be able to adapt and respond to opportunities, challenges, and changes in technology and ways of working so that they can continue to support the Company's growth.

Fungsi Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner berperan dalam memfasilitasi implementasi rencana strategis perusahaan melalui pengembangan dan peningkatan efektivitas struktur serta proses organisasi. Mekanisme kerja yang telah disesuaikan memungkinkan organisasi bergerak secara adaptif, efisien, dan efektif. Selain itu, fungsi ini juga mendukung rencana strategis perusahaan, salah satunya melalui penelitian bertajuk 'Future of Work' yang mengeksplorasi dampak Artificial Intelligence terhadap tenaga kerja dan lingkungan kerja. Penelitian ini dilakukan dalam kerangka program magang berbasis proyek dari mahasiswa University of New South Wales (UNSW). Melalui riset ini, fungsi Organization Development &amp; HR Business Partner berupaya mempersiapkan dan mengembangkan SDM agar mampu beradaptasi dan responsif terhadap peluang, tantangan, serta perubahan teknologi dan cara kerja sehingga dapat terus mendukung pertumbuhan perusahaan.

# 2. Talent Acquisition &amp; Employer Branding

## Talent Acquisition &amp; Employer Branding

Talent Acquisition &amp; Employer Branding is a function that carries out High Quality Talent Acquisition &amp; Recruitment. In accordance with EMTEK's highly dynamic and competitive industry characteristics, the Company considers it necessary to have human resources who are competent, reliable, adaptive, and responsive to information technology trends and modern lifestyles.

Talent Acquisition &amp; Employer Branding merupakan fungsi yang menjalankan High Quality Talent Acquisition &amp; Recruitment. Sesuai karakteristik industri EMTEK yang sangat dinamis dan kompetitif, Perseroan memandang perlu untuk memiliki SDM yang kompeten, andal, adaptif, dan responsif terhadap tren teknologi informasi dan gaya hidup modern.

To acquire capable human resources in supporting the Company's sustainable growth, EMTEK and its subsidiaries are committed to conducting selection and recruitment processes that uphold the aspects of transparency, equality, non-discrimination, non-bias, and inclusiveness. EMTEK always provides opportunities for internal and external talents to build their careers in the Company group. For the internal recruitment process, the Company implements promotion and rotation, while for the external recruitment process, the Company conducts workforce selection for prospective employees who are fresh graduates and/or experts/professionals with the qualifications and expertise specified by the Company.

Untuk memperoleh SDM yang mampu mendukung pertumbuhan Perseroan secara berkelanjutan, EMTEK dan entitas anaknya berkomitmen untuk melakukan proses seleksi dan rekrutmen yang menjunjung aspek transparansi, kesetaraan, non-diskriminasi, menghindari bias, dan inklusif. EMTEK senantiasa membuka kesempatan bagi talenta internal maupun eksternal untuk membangun karirnya di grup Perseroan. Untuk proses rekrutmen internal, Perseroan melakukan promosi dan rotasi sedangkan proses rekrutmen eksternal, Perseroan melakukan seleksi tenaga kerja, terhadap calon karyawan yang merupakan lulusan baru dan/atau tenaga ahli/professional, dengan kualifikasi dan keahlian yang ditetapkan oleh perusahaan.

EMTEK Group has utilized assessment instruments to assess cognitive abilities, personality inventory, and situational judgment, as well as to evaluate the competence and aptitude of candidates to occupy roles and positions. These instruments are expected to help employees contribute to the long-term development of the Company.

Grup EMTEK telah memanfaatkan instrument-assessment untuk menilai kemampuan kognitif, inventaris kepribadian, dan penilaian situasional, serta untuk mengevaluasi kompetensi dan kesesuaian kandidat untuk mengisi peran dan posisi. Penggunaan instrumen ini diharapkan membantu karyawan untuk berkontribusi memajukan Perseroan dalam jangka panjang.

# 3. Talent Learning &amp; Development

## Talent Learning &amp; Development

Talent Learning &amp; Development is one of the pillars in the Company's human resource management structure. This pillar is responsible for improving the capabilities of our excellent human resources. The Company has launched various employee development programs, starting from the staff lever-up to leadership positions, such as EMTRACKS, EMTEK Leadership Program, EMTEK Executive Forum, HR Forum, and the development of technical skills such as Presentation Skills, Retirement Preparation Program, as well as other trainings relevant to the work functions of each Division. The development program is designed and organized to ensure that employees continue to develop in accordance with business developments and demands.

Talent Learning &amp; Development adalah adalah salah satu pilar dalam struktur pengelolaan SDM Perseroan yang bertanggung jawab meningkatkan kapabilitas SDM unggul. Perseroan telah meluncurkan berbagai program pengembangan karyawan, mulai dari level staff sampai dengan posisi pimpinan, seperti EMTRACKS, EMTEK Leadership Program, EMTEK Executive Forum, HR Forum, dan pengembangan kemampuan teknis seperti Presentation Skill, Program Persiapan Pensiun, serta dan pelatihan-pelatihan yang relevan dengan fungsi kerja masing-masing Divisi. Program pengembangan dirancang dan diselenggarakan guna memastikan para karyawan terus berkembang sesuai dengan perubahan &amp; tuntutan bisnis.

In managing talents, this function carries out talent profiling which involves assessment of employee competencies and performance. The results of this assessment are used to compile a targeted Individual Development Plan to support their future career development until retirement. With this holistic approach, Talent Learning &amp; Development not only contributes to individual development but also ensures alignment with the Company's strategic objectives, thus creating an effective, productive, and sustainable work environment.

Dalam mengelola talenta, fungsi ini melakukan talent profiling yang melibatkan asesmen terhadap kompetensi dan kinerja karyawan. Hasil dari asesmen ini digunakan untuk menyusun rencana pengembangan karyawan atau individual Development Plan yang terarah, guna mendukung perjalanan karier mereka ke depan sampai dengan purnaliskt. Dengan pendekatan holistik ini, Talent Learning &amp; Development tidak hanya berkontribusi terhadap pengembangan individu tetapi juga memastikan kesetaraan dengan tujuan strategis Perseroan, sehingga menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang efektif, produktif, dan berkelanjutan.

# 4. Employee Relations &amp; Communication

## Employee Relations &amp; Communication

The function of Employee Relations &amp; Communication is responsible for creating harmonious industrial relations through the development of a conducive and productive work environment. The Company conducts weekly socialization of the latest industrial relations provisions and practices, and initiates activities and communication forums called "Enteractive" which were held 4 times in 2024. Through Enteractive, employees receive the latest information about the company, programs, and company policies. It also serves as a forum for dialogue between employees and management, encouraging open and harmonious communication.

Fungsi Employee Relations &amp; Communication bertanggung jawab untuk menciptakan hubungan industrial yang harmonis melalui pengembangan lingkungan kerja yang berdual dan produktif. Perseroan mengadakan sosialisasi ketentuan dan praktik-hubungan industrial terkini setiap minggu, dan menginsiasi kegiatan dan forum komunikasi bernama "Enteractive" yang diadakan 4 kali selama tahun 2024. Melalui Enteractive, karyawan mendapatkan informasi terkini mengenai perusahaan, program-program, serta kebijakan perusahaan. Selain itu juga berperan sebagai ruang dialog antara karyawan dan manajemen, mendorong komunikasi yang terbuka dan harmonis.
