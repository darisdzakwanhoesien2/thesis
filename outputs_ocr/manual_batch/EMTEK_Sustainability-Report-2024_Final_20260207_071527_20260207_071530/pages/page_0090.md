# MAINTAINING DATA CONFIDENTIALITY AND CONSUMER PRIVACY

# MENJAGA KERAHASIAAN DATA DAN PRIVASI KONSUMEN [GRI 418-1]

Protection of internal and external Data/Information is extremely vital for EMTEK. Our main objective is to protect consumers from identity theft, financial loss, and criminal acts. This is important to improve the Company's trust and reputation.

The following are the Company's responsibilities to manage data/information in order to maintain and protect privacy and data/information throughout the Company's environment: [GRI 3-3]

- Commitment to provide timely notification to the subject of the data/information in the event of a change in policy and violation of the data or policy;
- Commitment to implement reliable data/information protection standards;
- Commitment to obtain data/information in a lawful and transparent manner, with consent;
- Commitment to collect and process data/information strictly in accordance with the stated purpose;
- Provisions and guidelines for the collection, use, distribution, and destruction of data/information; and
- Obligation for third parties including Company Partners who receive data/information to comply with the policy.

The above policy is supported by the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2013 certification, which is the international standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS) that has been adopted by a number of companies in the EMTEK group. In order to manage data or information confidentiality, the Company has appointed Dwi Delia Wulandari T. S.H. as Personal Data Protection Officer who is responsible for implementing the above policy.

To emphasize its commitment to personal data protection, EMTEK organizes annual training and socialization on Personal Data Protection and Information Security. The training took place on April 3, 2024, and was attended by the Board of Directors and all employees. The training aims to improve their understanding and ensure compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law in Indonesia. The training materials are tailored to the needs of each department at EMTEK, given the different types and volumes of personal data they manage. This initiative is a crucial step in maintaining the trust of customers and stakeholders.
