# 1. EMTEK Education &amp; Career Festival/Festival Pendidikan dan Karier EMTEK

In 2024, the Company organized the EMTEK Education &amp; Career Festival 2024 in collaboration with PT Gerja Manfaat Bangsa (Kark.com) and invited a number of universities. Besides the job fair, the event also featured talkshows and workshops that covered many cross-industry themes. Furthermore, collaboration was also established with the Ministry of Education and Culture in order to participate in the Internship Program and Certified Independent Study (MDB) batch 6 and batch 7. For information, MDB is a comprehensive career preparation program that provides opportunities for students to learn outside of their study programs with guaranteed credit conversion recognized by universities.

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan menyelenggarakan EMTEK Education &amp; Career Festival 2024 bekerja sama dengan PT Gerja Manfaat Bangsa (Karin.com) dan mengundang sejumlah universitas. Selain kegiatan-job fair, acara ini juga diisi dengan kegiatan talkshow dan workshop yang mengangkat banyak tema dari lintas industri. Kemudian, kerja sama juga dilakukan dengan Kemdebud dalam rangka berpartisipasi di Program Magang dan Studi Independent Bersertifikat (MDB) batch 6 dan batch 7. Sebagai informasi MDB merupakan program persiapan karier yang komprehensif dan memberikan kesempatan bagi mahasiswa untuk belajar di luar program studi dengan jaminan konversi SKS yang diakui perguruan tinggi.

# 2. HR Automation &amp; Analytics Projects/Otomatisasi HR &amp; Analitik Data

The HR Division utilizes the EMTEK Hub as a Human Resource Information System (HRIS) and Analytics platform to effectively manage HR. The platform simplifies HR management processes, such as attendance, leave requests, and medical claims, in an automated and integrated manner within a single system. HRIS also helps the Company to optimize employee performance appraisal, increase employee engagement, satisfaction, and retention, through the collection and implementation of data analysis from various sources, such as surveys, feedback, and reviews. In 2024, the implementation of the EMTEK Hub platform has been expanded to subsidiaries.

Divisi SDM memanfaatkan EMTEK Hub sebagai Human Resource Information System (HRIS) dan platform Analytics untuk mendukung pengelolaan SDM secara efektif. Platform tersebut membantu menyederhanakan proses pengelolaan SDM, seperti absensi kehadiran, pengajuan cuti, dan pengajuan klaim medis, secara terotomatisasi dan terpadu dalam satu sistem. HRIS juga membantu Perseroan untuk mengoptimalkan penilaian kinerja karyawan, meningkatkan keterlibatan, kepuasan, dan retensi karyawan, melalui pengumpulan dan pelaksanaan analisis data dari berbagai sumber, seperti survei, umpan balik, dan ulasan. Pada tahun 2024, penerapan platform EMTEK Hub diperluas ke level entitas anak.

# 3. Education Unit Transformation Support/Fendampingan Transformasi Unit Pendidikan

In terms of competency development, the Company always prioritizes the improvement of the organization's capacity and capability by honing the soft skills and hard skills of its workforce. For example, the HR Division has established a partnership with EMTEK Group's Education Unit, the Indonesian Television Academy (ATVI), to provide opportunities for ATVI students to participate in internship programs at various companies within EMTEK Group's ecosystem. The HR Division also provides a series of training courses, including the internalization of ATVI BISA Culture to all lecturers and educators at ATVI, as well as supporting their competency development through cooperation with Global and National Universities. This project also includes the improvement of HR systems and procedures, as well as the support and assistance of ATVI's transformation into EMTEK Digital Media Institute (IMDI).

Dalam hal pengembangan kompetensi, Perseroan senantiasa memberikan perhatian terhadap peningkatan kapasitas dan kapabilitas organisasi dengan mengasah soft skill dan hard skill tenaga kerja. Salah satunya Divisi SDM telah membangun kerja sama dengan Unit Pendidikan Grup EMTEK Group, yaitu Akademi Televisi Indonesia (ATVI), untuk memberikan kesempatan yang luas kepada para mahasiswa ATVI untuk mengikuti program magang di berbagai perusahaan dalam eksistensi Grup EMTEK. Divisi SDM juga memberikan serangkaian pelatihan, antara lain untuk menginternalisasikan Budaya ATVI BISA kepada seluruh dosen dan tenaga pendidik di ATVI, serta membantu meningkatkan kompetensi mereka melalui kerja sama yang dibangun dengan Perguruan Tinggi Global dan Nasional. # 4. EMTEK LEAD (EMTEK Leadership Empowerment &amp; Development)

EMTEK LEAD is a program designed to develop the leadership skills of EMTEK Group leaders. Participants in this program will be involved in various development activities such as self-assessment, reading, training, business simulations, role play, case studies and group discussions. Various development activities are carried out to encourage the establishment of a culture of learning, innovation, and continuous improvement as well as the acceleration of knowledge, skills, and abilities of leaders and employees. EMTEK LEAD also focuses on the internalization of company culture, innovation, and continuous improvement, as well as reinforcement of collaboration, with the aim of motivating leaders and employees to increase productivity and achieve top performance.

EMTEK LEAD adalah sebuah program yang dirancang untuk mengembangkan kemampuan kepemimpinan para propinan di Untek Group. Peserta program ini akan terlibat dalam berbagai aktivitas pengembangan seperti self-assessment, membaca, pelatihan, simulasi bisnis, role play, studi kasus dan diskusi kelompok. Berbagai aktivitas pengembangan yang dilakukan untuk mendorong terciptanya budaya belajar, inovasi, dan peningkatan berkelanjutan serta akselerasi pengetahuan, keterampilan, dan kemampuan para pemimpin dan karyawan. EMTEK LEAD juga berfokus pada internalisasi budaya perusahaan, inovasi, dan perbaikan berkelanjutan, serta penguatan kolaborasi, dengan tujuan memotivasi para pemimpin dan karyawan untuk meningkatkan produktivitas dan mencapai kinerja unggul.

In 2024, the Company has held the EMTEK Leadership Program titled "The Journey of Leader-Ship". In addition to the Leadership Program, other development programs were also held, such as the "EMTEK Executive Forum", "EMTEK HR Forum", "Emtrackix", and "Visualizing the Data into Business Presentation". These programs are not only aimed at improving leadership skills, but also at ensuring business growth and sustainability throughout EMTEK Group.

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan telah melaksanakan EMTEK Leadership Program yaitu The Journey of Leader-Ship. Selain Leadership Program, juga diselenggarakan program pengembangan lainnya seperti "EMTEK Executive Forum", "Emtek HR Forum", "Emtrackix", dan "Visualizing the Data into Business Presentation". Program-program ini tidak hanya bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kemampuan kepemimpinan, tetapi juga untuk memastikan kesimambungan pertumbuhan bisnis dan keberlanjutan di seluruh Grup EMTEK.

Through this program, the goals and objectives of the Company, division, team, and individual are aligned and disseminated, so that every employee is motivated to work with the same direction and vision. This program also facilitates the provision and collection of feedback and regular evaluations, including the use of online tools, surveys, and reviews, to monitor and measure employee progress and results. The program also includes rewards and appreciation for performance, providing non-monetary benefits to recognize and motivate employees.
