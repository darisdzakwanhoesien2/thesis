# Environmental Complaints

Pengaduan Terkait Lingkungan Hidup [GRI 2-25] [GRI 2-27] [OJK F.16]

As a form of monitoring and evaluation of the Company's performance in environmental responsibility, we accept input and complaints regarding environmental management. The Company will respond and resolve any incoming complaints. The public can submit their complaints via email: wbs@emtek.co.id.

Meanwhile, for other publicly listed subsidiaries, information regarding environmental complaint channels can be found in their respective Corporate Sustainability Reports.

Sebagai bentuk pengawasan dan evaluasi terhadap kinerja Perseroan dalam tanggung jawab lingkungan, kami menerima masukan dan pengaduan terkait pengelolaan lingkungan. Perseroan akan merespon dan menyelesaikan setiap kendala yang ada. Masyarakat dapat menyampaikannya melalui email: wbs@emtek.co.id.

Mearwhile, for other publicly listed subsidiaries, information regarding environmental complaint channels can be found in their respective Corporate Sustainability Reports.

Semantara itu, untuk anak perusahaan terbuka lainnya, informasi terkait saluran pengaduan terhadap lingkungan dapat dilihat dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan Perusahaan masing-masing.

Throughout 2024, EMTEK Holding did not receive any complaints, sanctions and/or fines due to failure in managing environmental aspects.

Sepanjang tahun 2024, EMTEK Holding tidak menerima adanya pengaduan, sanksi dan/atau denda akibat kesalahan dalam pengelolaan aspek lingkungan.

# PROSPEROUS AND RESILIENT HUMAN RESOURCES

# SUMBER DAYA MANUSIA SEJAHTERA DAN TANGGUH

![img-133.jpeg](img-133.jpeg)

Employee management greatly impacts the Company's sustainability and success. Human Resource ("HR") management is an investment that provides long-term benefits.

Having competent and trustworthy human resources provides a competitive advantage that supports the Company in achieving its vision, mission, and business objectives. In the context of sustainability, the management of human resources requires a holistic approach based on compliance with labor-related regulations and upholding human rights. The Company has taken various initiatives to create a fair and safe work environment, including recruitment and selection policies, employee welfare, training and development, retention and succession programs, as well as the utilization of technology for human resource development.

Pengelolaan karyawan memiliki dampak besar terhadap keberlanjutan dan keberhasilan Perseroan. Pengelolaan Sumber Daya Manusia ("SDM") merupakan investasi yang memberikan manfaat dalam jangka panjang.

Memiliki SDM yang kompeten serta berintegritas memberikan keunggulan kompetitif sekaligus mendukung Perseroan dapat mencapai visi dan misi serta tujuan bisnisnya. Mengelola SDM dalam konteks keberlanjutan memerlukan pendekatan holistik yang didasari kepatuhan terhadap ketentuan dan peraturan terkait ketenagakerjaan serta menjunjung tinggi hak asasi manusia. Berbagai inisiatif telah diambil Perseroan untuk menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang adil dan aman mencakup kebijakan rekrutmen dan seleksi, kesejahteraan karyawan, pelatihan dan pengembangan pelatihan, program retensi dan suksesi serta pemanfaatan teknologi untuk pengembangan SDM.

# Establishing a Highly-Performing Human Resource Culture

# Membangun Budaya SDM Berkinerja Tinggi

As a key asset of the Company that provides added value, the Company strives to establish a conducive work environment that will facilitate a culture of innovation and creativity to develop the Company. EMTEK's Human Resources (HR) Division is responsible for the management, development, and optimization of Human Capital.

Sebagai aset penting perusahaan yang memberikan nilai tambah, Perseroan berupaya membangun suatu lingkungan kerja yang kondusif yang akan memfasilitasi budaya inovasi dan kreativitas untuk memajukan Perseroan. Divisi SDM EMTEK bertanggung jawab untuk melakukan pengelolaan, pengembangan dan optimalisasi SDM atau Human Capital.

Throughout 2024, the Company's human resources competency management is aligned with the implementation of sustainability strategies, especially in terms of employment, in which the Company conducts training, socialization, and encourages human resources participation in various initiatives.
