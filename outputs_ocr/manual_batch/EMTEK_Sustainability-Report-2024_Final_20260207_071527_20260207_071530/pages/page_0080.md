# Pension Program

# Program Pensiun

# [GRI 201-3] [GRI 404-2] [GRI 2-23] [GRI 401-2]

The Company provides pension programs to plan the long-term economic welfare of employees. These programs provide a sense of security for employees in preparing their retirement days, which in turn improve their motivation and performance. The following are the pension programs for employees who have reached 55 years old:

1. BFUS Pensiun, which is paid by employee with 1% of their monthly wages.
2. BFUS JHT, which is paid by employee with 2% of their monthly wages.

Other than the programs above, the Company also provides trainings for employees to remain productive when they have reached pension age. This program is prepared one year prior to the retirement and covers various attractive topics such as entrepreneurship, presentation technique, and other relevant topics. Our pension programs are fully funded by the Company's internal funds without assistance/ funding from external parties. [GRI 3-3]

Perseroan memberikan program pensiun untuk merencanakan kesejahteraan ekonomi karyawan dalam jangka panjang. Tunjangan ini memberikan ketenangan bagi karyawan dalam mempersiapkan hari tua sehingga dapat meningkatkan motivasi dan kinerja karyawan. Berikut adalah program pensiun bagi karyawan yang memasuki usia 55 tahun:

1. BFUS Pensiun, yang dibayarkan oleh karyawan 1% dari upah per bulan.
2. BFUS JHT, yang dibayarkan oleh karyawan 2% dari upah sebulan.

Selain program pensiun di atas, Perseroan memberikan pelatihan untuk mempersiapkan karyawan ketika mereka memasuki pensiun dapat tetap produktif. Program ini dipersiapkan satu tahun sebelum masa pensiun dengan beragam topik yang menarik seperti kewirausahaan, teknik presentasi, dan topik-topik lain yang relevan. Program pensiun kami sepenuhnya didanai menggunakan dana internal perusahaan tanpa bantuan/dana dari pihak eksternal. [GRI 3-3]
