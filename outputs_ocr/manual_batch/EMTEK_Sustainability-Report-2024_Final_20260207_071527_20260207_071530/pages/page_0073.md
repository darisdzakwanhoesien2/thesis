# Gender Equality and Anti-Sexual Harassment Kesetaraan Gender dan Anti Pececehan Seksual

Every individual, both men and women, has their own unique potential. Gender equality ensures that this potential can develop without constraints from restrictive stereotypes or social norms. The Company consistently ensures that gender equality policies are implemented, including in:

Setiap individu, baik laki-laki maupun perempuan, memiliki potensi yang masing-masing mempunyai keunggulan tersendiri. Kesetaraan gender memastikan bahwa potensi ini dapat berkembang tanpa hambatan karena stereotipe atau norma sosial yang membatasi. Secara konsisten Perseroan memastikan bahwa kebijakan kesetaraan gender diterapkan meliputi:

- Recruitment, selection, and promotion.
- Professional development.
- Gender equality in the workplace for leadership.
- Equal pay.
- Safe and inclusive workplaces.
- Flexible working arrangements.
- Preventing and addressing harassment and discrimination.
- Anti-discriminatory procurement practices.
- Gender-responsive product design, developments, and marketing.
