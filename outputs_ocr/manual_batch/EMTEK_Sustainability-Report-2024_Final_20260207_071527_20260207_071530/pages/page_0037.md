# External Assessment

## Penilaian Eksternal

The performance evaluation of the highest governance organs and the Company's overall ESG performance also involves independent external parties, including the Indonesia Institute for Corporate Directors (IICD), KEHATI Foundation, and S&amp;P Global. The positive evaluation results are demonstrated by various awards and recognitions, including the nomination of EMTEK as a constituent in several ESG Indices, namely SRI-KEHATI, ESG Quality 45 (DX KEHATI), ESG Sector Leaders (DX KEHATI), and ESG Leaders.

Based on this evaluation results and as a form of the Company's commitment to continuously improve ESG performance, we have established a number of ESG-related new policies in 2024 and published them on the Company's website. These policies include: Occupational Health and Safety Policy (OHS); Climate Strategy Policy; and Policy on the Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the Work Environment.

# REMUNERATION STRUCTURE

## STRUKTUR REMUNERASI

[GRI 2-19]

In order to support the performance of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, the Company provides remuneration, benefits and facilities which are classified into:

a. Short-term remuneration, consisting of basic salary and several benefits, namely: welfare benefits, health insurance and benefits, transportation and communication benefits, company cars in the form of a car ownership program, pension program, and/or annual bonuses.

b. Long-term remuneration, consisting of Company stock ownership options, which amount varies from year to year, taking into account the Company's performance.

Independent Commissioners are not allowed to own Company shares and/or receive share ownership options in order to maintain their independency. In addition, there is no clawback rule for Board of Commissioners and Directors bonuses because bonus payments are made after the end of the relevant financial year and the financial audit report as well as tax report are submitted. Bonuses for the previous year are paid a few months into the following year. [GRI 2-15]

The amount of remuneration for each member of the Board of Directors is still awaiting shareholder approval to be disclosed to the public. The total salary and compensation for the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of EMTEK in 2024 is Rp152,761,808,007, an increase of 0.29% compared to the previous year's figure of Rp153,202,005,638. In this report, the Company does not disclose the ratio of the highest individual compensation to the average compensation of all employees due to its confidential nature. [GRI 2-21]

[GRI 2-20]

The amount of remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is determined by the Committee Nomination and Remuneration Committee based on the key performance indicators and the Company's financial health. The Annual GMS authorizes the Board of Commissioners to determine the remuneration of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners by considering the recommendations of the Committee Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The Board of Commissioners then submits a remuneration proposal to the shareholders for approval at the Annual GMS.
