# FOUNDATION OF CORPORATE GOVERNANCE IMPLEMENTATION LANDASAN PENERAPAN GOVERNANSI KORPORAT

# [GRI 2-23]

Other than the Company's Articles of Association, Governance Guidelines, Code of Conduct, Charters, and other Internal Policies, the Company refers to a number of regulations as well as international guidelines and best practices in the implementation of GCG, including:

1. Laws and regulations related to limited liability companies and public limited companies.
2. Indonesian General Guidelines for Corporate Governance issued by the National Committee of Governance Policies (KNKG).
3. ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS).
4. Indonesian Corporate Governance Roadmap issued by the Financial Services Authority.
5. Financial Services Authority (OJK) Circular Letter No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 concerning the Form and Content of Annual Reports of Issuers and Public Companies.
6. Relevant regulations of the Financial Services Authority and Indonesia Stock Exchange, such as the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 15/POJK.04/2020 concerning the Plan and Organization of the General Meeting of Shareholders in Public Companies, Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 33/POJK.04/2014 concerning the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies, and Financial Services Authority Circular Letter No. 32/SEOJK.04/2015 concerning the Corporate Governance Guidelines for Public Companies.

# GCG ASSESSMENT PENILAIAN GCG

The Company consistently evaluates the implementation of governance based on international standards adopted from the corporate governance principles issued by the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development ("OECD"), and contained in the GCG principles stipulated in the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard ("ACGS").

Secara konsisten Perseroan melakukan evaluasi terhadap pelaksanaan governansi berdasarkan standar internasional yang diadopsi dari prinsip-prinsip corporate governance yang dikeluarkan oleh the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development ("OECD"), dan tertuang dalam prinsip-prinsip GCG yang diatur dalam ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard ("ACGS").

Based on the assessment conducted by IICD on GCG practices carried out in 2023, the Company obtained a score of 101.34 points, earning the predicate of "Leadership in Corporate Governance" or Level 5 (more than 100), this means that the Company's GCG practices have been able to exceed the performance required by the Level 1 Assessment (ACGS Structure) and is the leading issuer (Leader) in the implementation of GCG based on ACGS Standards in Indonesia. The assessment results will be used by the Company as evaluation material to further improve the implementation of GCG practices in the next period. Detailed information regarding this GCG assessment can be found in the Company's 2024 Annual Report.

Berdasarkan penilaian yang telah dilakukan IICD atas praktik-praktik GCG yang dijalankan selama tahun 2023, Perseroan memperoleh skor 101,34 poin, termasuk dalam predikat "Leadership in Corporate Governance" atau Level 5 (lebih dari 100), artinya praktik GCG yang dilakukan Perseroan telah mampu melampaui kinerja yang disyaratkan oleh Penilaian Level 1 (Struktur ACGS) dan merupakan emiten terdepan (Leader) dalam penerapan GCG berdasarkan ACGS Standard di Indonesia. Hasil penilaian tersebut nantinya akan digunakan Perseroan sebagai bahan evaluasi untuk lebih meningkatkan implementasi praktik GCG di periode selanjutnya. Penjelasan lengkap terkait penilaian GCG ini dapat dilihat dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan tahun 2024.

# CORPORATE GOVERNANCE SUPPORTS THE COMPANY'S SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANSI KORPORAT MENDUKUNG KEBERLANJUTAN PERSEROAN

# [GRI 2-9]

Referring to Law No. 40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies, the corporate governance structure consists of three organs, namely: the General Meeting of Shareholders ("GMS"), the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners.

As an ownership organ, the GMS is a company organ that provides a forum for shareholders to make important decisions with exclusive authority not given to the Board of Directors or Board of Commissioners within the limits specified in the Law and/or Articles of Association. On the other hand, the GMS is used as a forum of accountability for management activities by the Board of Directors and supervisory activities by the Board of Commissioners, which also fulfills some of the rights of shareholders. In carrying out its supervisory function, the Board of Commissioners is assisted by 4 (four) supporting organs, namely the Audit Committee, the Nomination Committee, the Remuneration Committee, and the Risk Monitoring Committee, while the Board of Directors is supported by 3 (three) supporting organs to carry out its management function, namely the Corporate Secretary, the Internal Audit and Risk Management Unit, and the Environment, Social, &amp; Governance (ESG) Committee.

Merujuk pada Undang-Undang No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas menyatakan bahwa struktur governansi korporat meliputi tiga organ, yaitu: Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham ("RUPS"), Direksi, dan Dewan Komisaris.

Sebagai organ kepemilikan, RUPS merupakan organ perusahaan yang menjadi wadah bagi Pemegang Saham untuk mengambil keputusan penting yang kewenangannya tidak diberikan kepada Direksi atau Dewan Komisaris dalam batas yang ditentukan dalam Undang-Undang dan/atau Anggaran Dasar. Di lain sisi, RUPS digunakan sebagai forum untuk mempertanggungjawabkan aktivitas pengelolaan oleh Direksi dan aktivitas pengawasan oleh Dewan Komisaris, yang sekaligus untuk memenuhi sebagian hak-hak pemegang saham. Dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasannya, Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh 4 (empat) organ pendukung yaitu Komite Audit, Komite Nominasi, Komite Remunerasi, dan Komite Pemantau Risiko yang, sedangkan Direksi dalam menjalankan fungsi pengurusannya didukung oleh 3 (tiga) organ pendukung yaitu Sekretaris Perusahaan, Unit Audit Internal dan Manajemen Risiko, serta Komite Lingkungan, Sosial, &amp; Tata Kelola (Environmental, Social, &amp; Governance/ESG).
