# CORPORATE GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE

# PRINSIP GOVERNANSI KORPORAT

The commitment to governance culture is binding and applicable to all EMTEK personnel at all levels through the implementation of the 4 (four) principles of Good Corporate Governance, namely Ethical Behavior, Accountability, Transparency, and Sustainability. These principles serve as the Company's guidelines in interacting with stakeholders and influencing the decision-making process in order to maintain integrity and trust in all aspects of the Company's business.

Komitmen budaya governansi berlaku mengikat bagi seluruh insan EMTEK di seluruh tingkatan dengan menerapkan 4 (empat) prinsip Governansi Korporat yang Baik, yaitu Perilaku Beretika, Akuntabilitas, Transparansi, dan Keberlanjutan. Prinsip-prinsip ini menjadi pedoman Perseroan dalam berinteraksi dengan para pemangku kepentingan serta memengaruhi proses pengambilan keputusan guna menjaga integritas dan kepercayaan dalam semua aspek bisnis Perseroan.

# Ethical Behavior

Perilaku Beretika

The Company applies the Ethical Behavior principle in various operational aspects, among others by respecting the rights of minority shareholders, prohibiting insider trading, and implementing performance management based on the balanced scorecard.

The Company ensures that the rights of shareholders and stakeholders can be fulfilled properly under applicable agreements and laws. Shareholders and stakeholders have the right to obtain the same information regarding the Company's performance and activities.

The Company also provides equal opportunities in the recruitment and management of human resources without discrimination of ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, sexual orientation, and physical condition.

Perseroan menerapkan prinsip Perilaku Beretika dalam berbagai aspek operasional, antara lain dengan menghormati hak pemegang saham minoritas, melarang insider trading, serta menerapkan manajemen kinerja berdasarkan balanced scorecard.

Perseroan memastikan bahwa hak-hak pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan dapat terpenuhi dengan baik sesuai dengan kesepakatan dan perundangan yang berlaku. Pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan berhak memperoleh informasi yang sama mengenai kinerja dan aktivitas Perseroan.

Perseroan juga memberikan kesempatan yang sama dalam perekrutan dan pengelolaan sumber daya manusia tanpa diskriminasi suku, agama, ras, golongan, jenis kelamin, orientasi seksual, dan kondisi fisik.

# Accountability

Akuntabilitas

The Company implements the principle of Accountability by ensuring the availability of the necessary charters for each of the Company's main organs to provide clarity of functions, implementation, and accountability of Shareholders, Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Committees, and Corporate Secretary to ensure the effectiveness of Corporate Governance.

The Company implements the guidelines, policies, references, and technical instructions for the supervisory system regularly by considering the interests of shareholders and stakeholders. The implementation includes monitoring, evaluation, and controlling management to ensure that management acts in the interests of the Company, shareholders, and other stakeholders.

Perseroan menerapkan prinsip Akuntabilitas dengan memastikan telah tersedianya piagam-piagam yang diperlukan bagi masing-masing organ utama Perseroan guna memberikan kejelasan fungsi, pelaksanaan, dan pertanggungjawaban Pemegang Saham, Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Komite-Komite, dan Sekretaris Perusahaan demi memastikan efektivitas Governansi Korporat.

Perseroan menerapkan pedoman, kebijakan, panduan, dan petunjuk teknis sistem pengawasan secara teratur dengan mempertimbangkan kepentingan para pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan. Penerapan tersebut meliputi pemantauan, evaluasi, dan pengendalian terhadap manajemen untuk memastikan bahwa manajemen bertindak sesuai dengan kepentingan Perseroan, pemegang saham, dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

# Transparency

Transparansi

The Company implements the principle of Transparency by providing relevant, accurate, and timely information to stakeholders. The Company takes the initiative to convey not only matters required by laws and regulations, but also matters important for decision-making by shareholders and stakeholders.

The Company regularly and periodically publishes financial information in the form of Financial Statements, Annual Reports, and other material information, and provides a means for investors to easily access the Company's important information on the Company's website, the Indonesia Stock Exchange website, print media and press releases, investor and analyst meetings, public exposes, and press conferences and encourages its shareholders, both institutional and retail shareholders to attend Annual General Meetings of Shareholders held regularly by the Company.

Perseroan memerlukan prinsip Transparansi dengan menyediakan informasi yang relevan, akurat, dan tepat waktu kepada para pemangku kepentingan. Perseroan berinsiatif menyampaikan tidak sebatas hal-hal yang diwajibkan oleh peraturan perundang-undangan, tetapi juga hal-hal penting untuk pengambilan keputusan oleh pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan.

Perseroan memapublikasikan informasi keuangan melalui Laporan Keuangan secara berkala dan teratur, Laporan Tahunan, serta informasi material lainnya, serta menyediakan sarana bagi investor untuk mengakses informasi penting Perseroan dengan mudah melalui situs web Perseroan, situs web Bursa Efek Indonesia, media cetak dan press release, pertemuan investor dan analis, paparan publik, dan jumpa pers serta mendorong para pemegang sahamnya, baik pemegang saham institusi ataupun ritel untuk menghadiri Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan yang diadakan secara rutin oleh Perseroan.

# Sustainability

Keberlanjutan

The Company views sustainability as an essential approach for generating benefits for all stakeholders while minimizing adverse impacts on economic, social, and environmental aspects. The Company asserts that the implementation of sustainability practices is vital for becoming a resilient and long-term organization, and the Company is committed to this endeavor. The Company's contributions are designed to align with the specific industries in which we operate, as well as the capabilities of the Company. Furthermore, effective risk management encompassing identification, measurement, and mitigation plays a crucial role in safeguarding both the Company and its stakeholders.

To cultivate sustainability value for the organization and all stakeholders, the Company has established sustainability strategies that integrate our business activities with sound corporate governance principles. These strategies also aim to create positive recovery on social and environmental fronts. The Company's sustainability policies and strategies are structured around three fundamental pillars that are pertinent to its industry:

- Corporate social responsibility in the areas of community engagement, education, and environmental stewardship;
- Corporate social responsibility concerning employment practices, Occupational Health, and Safety; and
- Commitment to consumer and viewer responsibilities.

Untuk menciptakan nilai yang berkelanjutan bagi organisasi dan seluruh pemangku kepentingan, Perseroan telah menetapkan strategi keberlanjutan yang mengintegrasikan kegiatan bisnis dengan prinsip-prinsip Governansi Korporat yang Baik. Strategi ini juga bertujuan untuk menciptakan dampak positif di bidang sosial dan lingkungan. Kebijakan dan strategi keberlanjutan Perseroan disusun berdasarkan tiga pilar fundamental yang sesuai dengan industrinya:

- Tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan di bidang pelibatan masyarakat, pendidikan, dan pengelolaan lingkungan;
- Tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan terkait praktik Ketenagakerjaan, Kesehatan, dan Keselamatan Kerja; dan
- Komitmen terhadap tanggung jawab konsumen dan pemirsa.
