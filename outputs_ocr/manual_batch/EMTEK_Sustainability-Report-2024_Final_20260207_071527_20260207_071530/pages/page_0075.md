# Testimony from Disabled Employee

## Testimoni Karyawan Disabilitas

![img-136.jpeg](img-136.jpeg)
**Bagus Suryo Utomo**

PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri - PIC Group Leader Broadcast Facility

"During my 23-year career at PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri, part of the EMTEK Group, I have experienced how inclusive and supportive the work environment here is. As a disabled employee, I have never been treated differently or restricted in my development, but rather been given equal opportunities to contribute. With my educational background in electronics, I was able to get involved in the development of production support tools and was eventually trusted to serve as PIC Group Leader Broadcast Facility. EMTEK also provides disability-friendly infrastructure, which really helps me to carry out my daily work comfortably and optimally. The support from my colleagues and the company culture that values diversity makes me even more motivated to keep growing. EMTEK has provided equal career opportunities and opportunities, regardless of my physical condition. For me, EMTEK is not just a place to work, but also a space where every individual, without exception, can grow, contribute, and feel the positive impact of an equal and inclusive work environment."

"Selama 23 tahun berkarir di PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri, bagian dari Grup EMTEK, saya merasakan betapa inklusif dan suportifnya lingkungan kerja di sini. Sebagai karyawan difabel, saya tidak pernah diperlakukan berbeda atau dibatasi dalam berkembang, melainkan diberikan kesempatan yang sama untuk berkontribusi. Dengan latar belakang pendidikan di bidang Elektronika, saya bisa terlibat dalam pengembangan perangkat pendukung produksi hingga akhirnya dipercaya menjabat sebagai PIC Group Leader Broadcast Facility. EMTEK juga menyediakan infrastruktur yang ramah disabilitas, yang sangat membantu saya dalam menjalankan pekerjaan sehari-hari dengan nyaman dan optimal. Dukungan dari rekan kerja serta budaya perusahaan yang menghargai keberagaman membuat saya semakin termotivasi untuk terus berkembang. EMTEK telah memberikan kesempatan dan menyediakan peluang karir yang setara, tanpa adanya diskriminasi terhadap kondisi fisik saya. Bagi saya, EMTEK bukan sekadar tempat bekerja, tetapi juga ruang yang mana setiap individu, tanpa terkecuali, dapat bertumbuh, berkontribusi, dan merasakan dampak positif dari lingkungan kerja yang setara dan inklusif."

[GRI 400-1]

EMTEK fosters an inclusive work environment to ensure that all individuals, regardless of their gender, race, religion, age, disability, or other background, feel respected, accepted, and have equal opportunities for growth. Diverse backgrounds are a gift and a driving force for the Company's progress.
