These partnerships with our suppliers and our retailers have generated substantial indirect economic value for smaller supply chain partners and communities along Indofood's food value chain. Notably, more than 66,000 Warmindo entrepreneurs and not less than 20,000 farmers now count themselves as part of our value chain community, as well as more than 49,000 BMC members with revenue ranging from millions to billions of Rupiah per year.

More information on the upstream and downstream economic benefits can be found in Chapter 4: Resilience in Delivering Food for All, in section: Socio-Economic Inclusion, on page 70.
