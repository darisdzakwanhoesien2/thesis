We regularly assess our nucleus, plasma estates, and POMs to ensure their compliance with our Policy. As more than 70% of our refinery CPO supply is from our own mills, we focus our Policy compliance audit on our internal supply chains. In 2021, we conducted 88 visits, workshops, and audits on 100% of our POMs and their supplying estates. Besides compliance to our Policy, our assessments also focus on:

- Agronomy (GAP, yield, soil health, crop protection);
- Responsible operations (safety, biodiversity, peatland, fire risk, human rights, community engagement, Free Prior and Informed Consent ("FPIC"));
- Efficiency of operations (energy and water consumption, GHG emissions, waste production); and
- Compliance with Government Regulation and ISPO certifications. [308-1,414-1]

Secara berkala kami memantau perkebunan inti, perkebunan plasma, serta PKS guna memastikan kepatuhan terhadap Kebijakan kami. Mengingat bahwa lebih dari 70% dari pasokan CPO berasal dari pabrik kami sendiri, Kebijakan kami difokuskan pada audit kepatuhan dari rantai pasokan internal. Di tahun 2021, kami melakukan 88 kegiatan kunjungan, workshop dan mengaudit 100% dari PKS kami beserta perkebunan-perkebunan pemasoknya. Selain kepatuhan terhadap Kebijakan, kegiatan penilaian juga difokuskan pada:

- Agronomi (GAP, hasil panen, kesehatan tanah, perlindungan tanaman);
- Kegiatan operasional yang bertanggung jawab (keamanan, keanekaragaman hayati, lahan gambut, risiko kebakaran, hak asasi manusia, pelibatan masyarakat, Pelaksanaan Persetujuan atas Dasar Informasi di Awal Tanpa Paksaan (Free Prior and Informed Consent atau "FPIC"));
- Efisiensi operasi (konsumsi energi dan air, emisi GRK, produksi limbah); dan
- Kepatuhan terhadap Peraturan Pemerintah serta sertifikasi ISPO. [308-1,414-1]
