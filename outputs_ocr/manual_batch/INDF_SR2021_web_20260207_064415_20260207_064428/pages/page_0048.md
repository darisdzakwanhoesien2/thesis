STRATEGI DAN TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN

To build our sustainable business practice capacity, the BOD and all individuals in our sustainability governance structure attend periodic sustainability training programs, workshops, and seminars. The training programs included topics such as Indonesia Economic Outlook 2022, Global Economic Outlook 2022, Energy Management, Climate Change, GRI Standard, Human Rights, Sustainable Supply Chain, Post-Consumer Packaging Waste Management, and more.

Guna meningkatkan kapasitas praktik keberlanjutan bisnis kami, Direksi dan seluruh individu dalam struktur tata kelola keberlanjutan mengikuti program pelatihan, workshop dan seminar tentang keberlanjutan secara berkala. Program pelatihan diantaranya adalah Indonesia Economic Outlook 2022, Global Economic Outlook 2022, Manajemen Energi, Perubahan Iklim, Standar GRI, Hak Asasi Manusia, Rantai Pasokan yang Berkelanjutan, serta Pengelolaan Sampah Kemasan Pasca Konsumsi dan berbagai pelatihan lainnya.
