### Foreign exchange rate risk

Foreign exchange rate risk is the risk that the fair value or future cash flows of a financial instrument will fluctuate because of changes in foreign exchange rates. The Company and its subsidiaries' exposure to exchange rate fluctuations results primarily from cash and cash equivalents, trade receivables, other receivables, trade payables, other payables, short-term and long-term bank loan, and finance lease payables denominated in United States dollar, Malaysian Ringgit, and Singapore dollar.
