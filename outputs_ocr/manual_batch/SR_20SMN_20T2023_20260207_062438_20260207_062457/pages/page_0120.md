## Kepuasan Pelanggan [SEOJK F.30]

Sebagaimana disampaikan sebelumnya mengenai Mekanisme Pengaduan pada halaman 205, pelanggan dapat menyampaikan pengaduan-pengaduan terkait jasa/layanan atau produk Grup melalui mekanisme tersebut, termasuk pengaduan-pengaduan yang berkaitan dengan technical matter. Grup terus memonitor dan mengkaji tingkat kepuasan pelanggan melalui pengukuran Service Level Agreement Compliance Rate ("SLA Compliance Rate") yang menggambarkan persentase ketepatan waktu Grup dalam menanggapi pengaduan sebagaimana yang telah disepakati dalam perjanjian.

## Customer Satisfaction [SEOJK F.30]

As previously mentioned regarding the Complaint Mechanism on page 205, customers can submit complaints related to Group services or products through this mechanism, including complaints related to technical matters. The Group continues to monitor and assess customer satisfaction levels through the measurement of Service Level Agreement Compliance Rate ("SLA Compliance Rate"), which depicts the percentage of the Group's timeliness in responding to complaints as agreed upon in the agreement.
