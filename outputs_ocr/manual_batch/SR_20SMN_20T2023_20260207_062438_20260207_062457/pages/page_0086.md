Scope 2: The Company calculates emissions from electricity consumption at the Group's head office, branch offices, and representative offices, and at POP network locations from the Group's non-tower business.

Scope 3: The Company calculates emissions from employee commuting to the office and business travel, as well as electrical energy consumption from the Group's tower assets leased by the Company to operators/customers.

The Company is committed to periodically reviewing and considering the emissions calculation report scope, particularly Scope 3, which originates from business processes carried out by third parties, considering the availability of accurate and relevant information from related third parties. This review process is a form of the Group's commitment to providing transparency in the Group's sustainability performance and tangible evidence of the Group's contribution to sustainability aspects.

To calculate direct greenhouse gas ("GHG") emissions (Scope 1), the method used in Indonesia and non-Annex 1 countries (developing countries) is Tier-1, which is based on energy consumption data multiplied by the default emission factor from the IPCC 2019 (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change) with the following formula:

$$
\text{Emisi GRK} = \frac{\text{Konsumsi Energi}}{\text{Energy Consumption}} \times \frac{\text{TJ}}{\text{Thn}} \times \text{Faktor Emisi} \times \text{Emission Factor} \times \text{kg}
$$

Untuk penghitungan emisi GRK Scope 3 dari data perjalanan kantor karyawan dari rumah ke tempat kerja, Perusahaan menggunakan distance-based method Green House Gas ("GHG") Protocol. Sedangkan data perjalanan bisnis dengan menggunakan pesawat, Perusahaan menggunakan metode penghitungan dari International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) Carbon Emissions Calculator ("ICEC").

Tio calculate Scope 3 GHG emissions from employee commuting data from home to the workplace, the Company uses the distance-based method from the GHG Protocol. Meanwhile, for business travel data involving flights, the Company utilizes the calculation method from the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) Carbon Emissions Calculator ("ICEC").
