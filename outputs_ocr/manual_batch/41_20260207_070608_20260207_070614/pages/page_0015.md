## Kondisi Makro Ekonomi dan Industri Tahun 2017

Dengan capaian pertumbuhan secara umum sebesar 3,6% selama tahun 2017, perekonomian dunia mulai mengindikasikan tren pemulihan dibandingkan tahun 2016 dengan realisasi hanya sebesar 3,2%. Kondisi tersebut ditandai dengan perbaikan ekonomi di beberapa negara maju dunia seperti Amerika Serikat, kawasan Eropa serta Cina.

Pada tingkat domestik, Indonesia berhasil mencapai tingkat pertumbuhan ekonomi sebesar 5,07% yang membaik dibandingkan tahun 2016 yaitu hanya 5,02%. Pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia pada tahun 2017 sejalan dengan meningkatnya ketahanan fundamental perekonomian yang tercermin dari angka inflasi yang terjaga pada level 3,61%, surplus neraca pembayaran yang berlanjut dan stabilitas nilai tukar Rupiah yang terjaga.

After experiencing multiple challenges in the last few years, Indonesian economy has started to show a positive sign of recovery as we can observe from the significant improvement in the industrial sector in 2017. As the Management of PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk. ("Company") kindly allow us to convey the Company's Management Report for the fiscal year of 2017.

## Macroeconomics and Industry Outlook 2017

With overall growth of 3.6% throughout 2017, global economy began to indicate a recovering trend compared to the previous 3.2% realization in 2016. This condition was indicated by the economic recovery of some advanced economic powers, such as the United States, Eurozone and China.

At domestic level, Indonesia has managed to achieve a 5.07% for its economic growth, slightly higher than the previous year's growth at the level of 5.02%. In 2017, Indonesia's economic achievement, in the increased growth as well as its ability to increase its economic resilience, is also reflected from a stable inflation rate around 3.61%, a surplus in the continued payment balance sheet and a more stable Rupiah exchange rate.
