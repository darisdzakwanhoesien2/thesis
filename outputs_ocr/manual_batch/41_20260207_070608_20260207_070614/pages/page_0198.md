# LENTERA KASIH

## a. Religious Holiday Celebration

A program dedicated to participate in Religious Holiday celebration such as Eid al Fitr, Eid al Adha, Christmas Day and others.

Throughout 2017, Erajaya Group also participated in Ramadhan 1436 H celebration by organizing fasting break event and giving donation for 100 orphanages and dhuafa. However, during the Eid al Adha Day, Erajaya Group donated at least 500 Qurban Meat packages to marginal people in the Company's operational area.

## b. Social Charity

A social program which was done as free grocery package giveaway to the society in the Company's operational area.

Throughout 2017, Erajaya Group successfully implemented social charity event at Megastore Bengkulu, Bogor, Budi Mulia Nursing Home (West Jakarta), and Murni Foundation Orphanage (North Jakarta).

In this activity, Erajaya Group had donated 250 groceries package to the society, used clothes in good condition for nursing home residents and other donations.

## c. Philanthropy Program

A program which aimed to empower society based on social welfare improvement and disaster relief in Indonesia. In 2017, Erajaya Group had implemented disaster recovery activity in Aceh and donated 50 groceries packages to the refugees.

In addition, Erajaya Group also gave donation for Rohingya refugees which was donated via Ministry of Industry. The donation provided by Erajaya Group included used clothes in good condition, praying equipment, underwear and toddler foods.
