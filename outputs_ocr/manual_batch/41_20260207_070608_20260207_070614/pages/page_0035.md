# Pembukaan Gerai Urban Republic di Summarecon Mall Serpong, Tangerang

Opening of Urban Republic Outlet at Summarecon Mall Serpong, Tangerang

Melalui salah satu entitas anaknya yaitu PT Erafone Artha Retailindo Erajaya membuka gerai Urban Republic keempat yang berlokasi di lantai dasar Summarecon Mall Serpong, Tangerang. Urban Republic hadir untuk memenuhi kebutuhan urban untuk produk lifestyle dengan mode terkini dan produk berbasis Internet of Things (IOT).

Hingga akhir tahun 2017, Erajaya telah memiliki 5 gerai Urban Republic yang tersebar di Indonesia.

Through one of its subsidiaries, namely PT Erafone Artha Retailindo, Erajaya opened the fourth Urban Republic outlet located at Ground Floor of Summarecon Mall Serpong, Tangerang. The opening of Urban Republic is to fulfill the urban needs for lifestyle products with the latest mode as well as Internet of Things (IOT) based products.

By the end of 2017, Erajaya owned 5 Urban Republic outlets located across Indonesia region.

# Erajaya Sukses Menyelenggarakan Gadget Invasion Week 2017 di Mall Taman Anggrek, Jakarta

Erajaya Successfully Organize Gadget Invasion Week 2017 at Taman Anggrek Mall, Jakarta

Erajaya menggelar event Gadget Invasion Week ("GIW") yang berlokasi di Mall Taman Anggrek, Main Atrium, Jakarta. GIW merupakan event tahunan yang diselenggarakan oleh Erajaya untuk memberikan pengalaman atas semua teknologi yang terbaru dari mobile, wearables dan ekosistem Internet of Things secara keseluruhan.

Acara ini akan berlangsung mulai hari Selasa, 4 April 2017 sampai Minggu, 9 April 2017. Pada saat kegiatan ini berlangsung, konsumen diundang untuk menikmati rangkaian acara yang penuh dengan teknologi terkini dan hiburan.

Erajaya organize Gadget Invasion Week (GIW) event located at Main Atrium of Taman Anggrek Mall, Jakarta. GIW is an annual event organized by Erajaya to provide the experience of all the latest technologies starting from mobile, wearables and ecosystem of Internet of Things.

The event was held from Tuesday, April 4, 2017 until Sunday, April 9, 2017. During the event, customers were invited to enjoy a series of events full of the latest technology and entertainment.
