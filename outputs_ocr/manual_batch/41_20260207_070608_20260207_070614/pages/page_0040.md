# Erajaya Group Menyelenggarakan
Erajaya Champion Day 2017

Erajaya Group Organize
Erajaya Champion Day 2017

Erajaya Champion Day adalah kegiatan yang diselenggarakan oleh Erajaya Group yang bertujuan untuk membangun kerjasama seluruh karyawan Erajaya Group dengan tema "Winning as One". Acara ini diselenggarakan selama 2 (dua) hari dari tanggal 8 September 2017 dan berlokasi di Bogor Raya Sport Club.

Erajaya Champion Day is an activity organized by Erajaya Group which aims to build cooperation of all employees Erajaya Group with the theme "Winning as One". The event is held for 2 (two) days from September 8, 2017 and located at Bogor Raya Sport Club.

# Erajaya Group Menyambut Nokia Android di Pasar Indonesia

Erajaya Group Welcomes Nokia Android in Indonesian Market

Erajaya Group mengumumkan kesiapannya menyambut ponsel Nokia berbasis Android, Nokia, 3, 5 dan 6 di Indonesia. Kehadiran Nokia yang berbasis Android akan menjawab kerinduan para penggemar smartphone Nokia di Indonesia, sekaligus menjawab rasa penasaran penggemar gadget mengenai ponsel ini.

Jaringan ritel Erafone (Entitas Anak Erajaya) siap untuk memasarkan jajaran produk smartphone Nokia. Untuk menyambut kehadiran smartphone Nokia, Erafone telah menyiapkan berbagai program di outlet, seperti branding, event brand activation, dan program promosi menarik.

Erajaya Group announced its readiness to welcome Nokia phones Android based, namely Nokia 3, 5 and 6 in Indonesia. The presence of Nokia – Android based answered the longing of Nokia fans in Indonesia, as well as answering the curiosity of gadget fans about this phone.

Erafone's Retail network (subsidiary of Erajaya) is ready to distribute Nokia's smartphone product lineup. To welcome the presence of Nokia smartphones, Erafone has prepared various programs at the outlets such as branding, brand activation events and interesting promotion program.
