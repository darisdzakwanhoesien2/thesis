Pilar ketiga adalah Lentera Kasih, yaitu sebuah bentuk komitmen Erajaya Group terhadap masyarakat yang berbasis kepada peningkatan kesejahteraan sosial (philantrophy program) dan penanggulangan bencana di Indonesia. Third pillar is Lentera Kasih, manifestation of Erajaya Group’s commitment to the society based on social welfare improvement (Philanthropy Program) and disaster relief in Indonesia. The Philanthropy Program held by Lentera Erajaya Peduli including participation in Religious Holiday (Eid al Fitr, Eid al Adha and Christmas Day) as well as donation for natural disaster victims who live in the inside or outside operational area of the Company.
