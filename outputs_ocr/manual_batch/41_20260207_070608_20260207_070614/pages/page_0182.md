# LSD Retail

LSD Retail melayani kebutuhan pengembangan karyawan Erafone, iBox, PPP. Sepanjang tahun 2017, di Erafone telah diadakan 403 batch pelatihan dengan total peserta 3.422 karyawan. Sementara di iBox telah diadakan 200 batch pelatihan dengan total 2.089 karyawan. Untuk PPP, yang menangani kerjasama ritel dengan Indosat Ooredoo, telah diadakan 47 batch training, dengan total peserta 780 karyawan. Diluar kegiatan pelatihan reguler, LSD Retail juga menangani beberapa project seperti Online Training Weekly Quiz, Non-Promotor, Online-to-Offline, serta SLDP internal.

# LSD Retail

LSD Retail serves employee development plan at Erafone, iBox, PPP. Throughout 2017, there were 403 batches training held at Erafone with total participants reached 3,422 employees. However, iBox held 200 batches training with total 2,089 participants. PPP, which handled retail partnership with Indosat Ooredoo held 47 batches training with total 780 participants. Besides regular training activities, the LSD Retail also handled other projects such as Online Training Weekly Quiz, Non-Promotor, Online-to-Offline and Internal SLDP.
