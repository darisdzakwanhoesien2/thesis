# Perubahan Komposisi Dewan Komisaris

# Penilaian Atas Prospek Usaha

Ke depan, Dewan Komisaris melihat prospek dan peluang bisnis perangkat alat telekomunikasi masih sangat menjanjikan. Adanya perkembangan pesat teknologi digital yang diiringi oleh berbagai perubahan di masyarakat akan terus memberikan potensi bisnis baru yang dapat dioptimalkan oleh Perseroan. Oleh karena itu, Dewan Komisaris menggarisbawahi pentingnya upaya untuk membangun Perseroan sebagai perusahaan yang memiliki prospek usaha yang baik dalam aspek digitalisasi.

Potensi pertumbuhan juga akan didorong oleh kondisi ekonomi yang diproyeksi akan tetap stabil di tahun 2018 sekalipun pada tingkat domestik akan diwarnai oleh beberapa dinamika terkait penyelenggaraan perhelatan akbar seperti Pilkada serentak, dan Asian Games 2018. Dengan fundamental ekonomi yang lebih kuat, Dewan Komisaris melihat Indonesia akan tetap kondusif dalam melalui dinamika tersebut di tahun mendatang.

Secara keseluruhan, Dewan Komisaris menilai prospek usaha dan strategi yang telah dipersiapkan oleh Manajemen sudah tepat dan telah mempertimbangkan berbagai faktor yang dapat mempengaruhi kinerja Perseroan. Melalui langkah strategis yang tepat serta tetap mengedepankan prinsip kehati-hatian, Dewan Komisaris optimis Manajemen akan dapat membawa Perseroan meraih setiap peluang bisnis yang ada di masa depan.

As Supervisory Body in the Company, the Board of Commissioners had also provided recommendations and suggestions to the Board of Directors while implementing supervisory activity and considering recommendation from the Audit Committee. The Board of Commissioners also has organized various meetings actively in 2017, either Internal Meeting or Joint Meeting with the Board of Directors and Audit Committee.

The Board of Commissioners hopes the GCG practice can be improved to stay relevant with the Company's needs and always be aligned with every change in regulation issued by the Government. GCG Commitment becomes a sole foundation for the sustainable growth in the future.

# Change in Board of Commissioners Composition

In the context of Board of Commissioners composition, there was no change in Board of Commissioners composition in 2017.

# Evaluation on Business Prospect

The Board of Commissioners views telecommunication device business prospect and opportunity are promising in the future. With rapid growth of digital technology accompanied by major transformations in the society, the condition will create new business potential to be optimized by the Company. Accordingly, the Board of Commissioners highlights the importance of any initiative to develop the Company as a promising Company in digitalization aspect.

The growth potential will be also driven by economic condition which is projected to be stable in 2018 despite couple of dynamics at domestic levels due to some major events such as simultaneous local election (Pilkada) and Asian Games 2018. With stronger economics fundamental, the Board of Commissioners believes Indonesia will remain conducive in passing these challenges in the next year.

Overall, the Board of Commissioners evaluates that the business prospect and strategy prepared by the Management had been appropriate and has considered various factors which may influence the Company's performance. Through effective strategy and upholding prudent principle, the Board of Commissioners believes the Management will lead the Company to pursue every business opportunity in the future.
