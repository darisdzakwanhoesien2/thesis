## 27. Jakarta

### iBox Apple Premium Reseller Flagship Store Ketiga Resmi Dibuka di Central Park, Jakarta

Grand Opening of The 3rd iBox Apple Premium Reseller Flagship Store at Central Park, Jakarta

PT Data Citra Mandiri, entitas anak Erajaya meresmikan gerai iBox Apple Premium Reseller ("APR") Flagship Store di Mal Central Park, Jakarta. Gerai ini merupakan toko ketiga dengan konsep flagship store yang memiliki luas toko lebih dari 300m² yang terletak di lantai 1 di Central Park Mall, Jakarta.

Hingga akhir 2017, Erajaya telah membuka 46 gerai iBox yang tersebar di Indonesia.

![img-21.jpeg](img-21.jpeg)

PT Data Citra Mandiri, subsidiary of Erajaya inaugurated iBox Apple Premium Reseller ("APR") Flagship Store at Central Park Mall, Jakarta. This outlet is the third outlet with flagship store concept covering 300 m² area located at 1st floor of Central Park Mall, Jakarta.

By the end of 2017, Erajaya has opened 46 iBox outlets located across Indonesia region.
