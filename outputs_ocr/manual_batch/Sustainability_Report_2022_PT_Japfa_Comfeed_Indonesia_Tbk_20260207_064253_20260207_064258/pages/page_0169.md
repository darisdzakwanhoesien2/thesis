# Economic Performance

[GRI 2-27, 201-1] [POJK B.1, F.2, F.3, F4]

Good economic performance is essential to maintain our business. Moreover, our financial performance is supported by the application of sustainable principles in our business to drive long-term economic success: Profit, People and Planet.

In 2022, the Company was able to achieve positive performance in accordance with the targets amidst various challenges. We were able to book net sales of Rp49.0 trillion. This figure increased by 9.1% from last year's Rp44.9 trillion.
