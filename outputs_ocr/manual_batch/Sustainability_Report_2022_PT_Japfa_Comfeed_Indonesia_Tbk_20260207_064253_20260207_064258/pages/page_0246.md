# Embracing and Developing Our Employees

[GRI 2-30]

Employee engagement and training are vital to help our employees stay motivated and developed so that they can contribute towards our vision.

# Synergising with Employees

In this post-pandemic era, we held various activities to support their physical and mental health. It showed our appreciation to our employees and increased the bonding between employees. Our employee engagement activities includes:

- Japfa Conversation
- Photography and Poster Competition
- Living Our Values Award held by the Aquaculture Division
- Routine exercise facilitated by the Company, such as chess, basketball, gymnastics, badminton, table tennis, etc.
- Chess and table tennis tournaments
- Religious prayer groups
