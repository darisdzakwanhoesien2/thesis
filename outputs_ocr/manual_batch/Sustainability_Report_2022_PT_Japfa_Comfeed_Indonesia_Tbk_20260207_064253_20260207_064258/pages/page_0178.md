# Understanding Our Stakeholders

[GRI 2-29] [POJK E.4]

Japfa fosters positive working relationships with stakeholders from various sectors. Stakeholders are identified as individuals, groups and organisations who affect or are affected by our business, ranging from employees, customers, financial institutions, shareholders, the government, distributors, associations, surrounding communities, farmer partners and suppliers.

By engaging our stakeholders, we can identify areas where we can collaborate, ensuring stakeholder inclusiveness to create a positive impact for people and the planet. [GRI 2-29] [POJK E.4]
