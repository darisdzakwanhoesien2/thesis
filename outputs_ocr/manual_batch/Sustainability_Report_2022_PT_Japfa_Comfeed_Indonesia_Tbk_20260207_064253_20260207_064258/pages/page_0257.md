# Teaching Farms

We facilitate teaching and learning about poultry farming in various animal husbandry departments by establishing Teaching Farms at Gadjah Mada University, Syiah Kuala University and Brawijaya University. Teaching Farms are useful for applying the theories students get in class to real, controlled situations.

# SAIL (Synergized Aquaculture Innovative Learning)

SAIL is an internship programme held in collaboration with the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research and Technology to increase the quality of university graduates. Through this programme, 128 university students are given the chance to experience an actual work environment at STP and experts from STP will guide them as mentors as well. SAIL places the students in strategic fields that are not limited to shrimp and fish cultivation but also manufacturing and human resources.
