Pertumbuhan pendapatan mitra pengemudi roda dua dihitung berdasarkan pendapatan mitra pengemudi yang memiliki performa tinggi, dengan mempertimbangkan kriteria seperti jam kerja, hari aktif, tingkat penerimaan, dan tingkat penyelesaian. Perhitungan ini mencakup seluruh mitra pengemudi roda dua di Indonesia, kecuali mitra pengemudi GoKlat.
Growth in 2W driver-partners' earnings is calculated based on earnings from high-performing driver-partners, taking into account criteria such as supply hours, active days, acceptance rate, and completion rate. This calculation includes all 2W driver-partners in Indonesia, excluding GoKlat driver-partners.

18. Pertumbuhan pendapatan mitra pengemudi roda empat dihitung berdasarkan pendapatan mitra pengemudi yang memiliki performa tinggi, dengan mempertimbangkan kriteria seperti jam kerja, hari aktif, tingkat penerimaan, dan tingkat penyelesaian. Perhitungan ini mencakup seluruh mitra pengemudi roda empat di Indonesia, kecuali mitra pengemudi GoBox dan GoBlueBird.
Growth in 4W driver-partners' earnings is calculated based on earnings from high-performing driver-partners, taking into account criteria such as supply hours, active days, acceptance rate, and completion rate. This calculation includes all 4W driver-partners in Indonesia, excluding GoBox and GoBlueBird driver-partners.

Pengguna Bertransaksi Bulanan (MTU) mengacu pada pengguna produk On-Demand Services (di Indonesia, Singapura, dan Vietnam) atau Financial Technology Services GoTo, baik di dalam maupun di luar platform, selama periode yang ditentukan. Indikator mengasumsikan Tokopedia dan usaha pengiriman dan fulfillment di bawah GoTo Logistics telah didekonsolidasi dari Grup GoTo sejak 1 Januari 2023.
Monthly Transacting Users refers to users of GoTo's On-Demand Services products (in Indonesia, Singapore, and Vietnam) or Financial Technology Services, either on-platform or off-platform, over the stated period. The number assumes Tokopedia and its related delivery and fulfillment businesses under GoTo Logistics were deconsolidated as of January 1, 2023.
