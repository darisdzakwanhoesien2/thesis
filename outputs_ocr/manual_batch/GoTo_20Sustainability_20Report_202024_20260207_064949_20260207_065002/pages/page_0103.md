# Lampiran I: Kepuasan Pelanggan dan Mekanisme Penanganan Pengaduan Masyarakat

Appendix I: Customer Satisfaction and Public Complaint Mechanism

## Survei Kepuasan Pelanggan

Kami melakukan survei rutin setiap kuartal di seluruh unit bisnis perusahaan untuk mengevaluasi tingkat kepuasan pelanggan (CSAT) terhadap produk dan layanan kami. Pada Kuartal 4 tahun 2024, survei ini dilakukan terhadap 7.172 pelanggan yang mencakup lima produk—GoRide, GoCar, GoFood, GoSend, dan GoMart—untuk memperoleh Skor Kepuasan Pelanggan (CSAT).

## Customer Satisfaction Survey

We conduct routine surveys on a quarterly basis across the company's business units in order to evaluate the level of customer satisfaction (CSAT) within our products and services. In Q4 2024, this survey was conducted with 7,172 customers covering five products GoRide, GoCar, GoSend and GoMart to obtain the Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT).

|  Produk Product | CSAT^{64}  |
| --- | --- |
|  GoRide | 92%  |
|  GoCar | 89%  |
|  GoFood | 89%  |
|  GoSend | 94%  |
|  GoMart | 88%  |
