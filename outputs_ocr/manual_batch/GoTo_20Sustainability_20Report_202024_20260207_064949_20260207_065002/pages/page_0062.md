# Mengelola Karyawan Kami

Di GoTo, karyawan merupakan aset terbesar. Kami tidak dapat melangkah sejauh ini tanpa ketekunan, dedikasi, dan motivasi karyawan yang telah memberdayakan kemajuan pada ekosistem GoTo. Oleh karena itu, mendukung kesehatan dan kesejahteraan mereka sangat penting untuk memastikan keberlangsungan jangka panjang organisasi kami. Kami yakin agar karyawan kami berkembang secara optimal, kami harus mendukung seluruh aspek kesehatan dan kesejahteraan mereka. Bagi kami, kesehatan adalah fondasi dari segalanya, dan kesejahteraan melampaui kesehatan fisik, tetapi juga mencakup keseimbangan mental, emosional, dan sosial.

# Taking Care of Our People

At GoTo, our people are our biggest asset. We would not have come this far without the perseverance, dedication and motivation of our employees, who keep our ecosystem moving forward. As such, ensuring their wellbeing is not only a priority, it is essential to the long-term sustainability of our organization. We believe that for our employees to truly thrive, we must support all aspects of their health and wellbeing. To us, health is the foundation for everything, and true wellbeing goes beyond physical health, to include mental, emotional, and social balance.

In 2024, this commitment was reflected in a range of programs and services offered to our employees, as detailed below:
