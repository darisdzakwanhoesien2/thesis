## Keselamatan di Jalan Raya

Dengan rata-rata lebih dari tiga juta pesanan per hari, mitra pengemudi GoTo melayani banyak pesanan untuk transportasi orang, barang, dan jasa setiap harinya. Mitra pengemudi kami diharapkan dapat menjunjung tinggi standar keselamatan di jalan raya untuk memastikan keselamatan dan kesejahteraan mereka sendiri, juga termasuk para penumpang dan masyarakat.

Pada tahun 2024, kami terus melakukan kampanye dan pelatihan bagi para mitra pengemudi, membagikan pengetahuan dan menerapkan kebijakan yang ketat untuk memperkuat praktik-praktik keselamatan di jalan raya maupun di luar raya. Selain itu, kami juga menyediakan materi pelatihan lain terkait keselamatan bagi para mitra pengemudi, termasuk pelatihan anti-pelecehan seksual dan pelatihan pertolongan pertama. Berbagai upaya ini diawasi oleh Driver Care Unit (DCU) Gojek, yang bertanggung jawab untuk menetapkan dan memantau standar keselamatan serta syarat wajib untuk melindungi pengemudi dan penumpang. Komponen utama dari hal ini adalah sistem manajemen Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) kami, yang terdiri dari berbagai langkah proaktif untuk menyediakan dukungan dalam aplikasi kepada para mitra pengemudi saat aktif di platform kami.

GoTo’s safety measures are also included in-app, and are designed to ensure the protection of all users, including minors. This includes a robust Know Your Customer (KYC) verification process for crucial financial features to prevent fraud and reduce risk.

During the reporting period, no products or services were recalled.

## Road Safety

With an average of over three million orders per day, GoTo’s driver-partners transport a significant volume of people, goods and services. As such, they are expected to uphold the highest standards of road safety to ensure their safety and wellbeing, as well as that of their passengers and the public.

In 2024, we continued to run driver-partners campaigns and training, disseminating knowledge and implementing strict policies to reinforce safe practices on and off the road. In addition, we provide driver-partners with other safety-related educational materials, including anti-sexual harassment training and first aid training. These efforts are overseen by Gojek’s dedicated Driver Care Unit (DCU), responsible for establishing and monitoring safety standards and requirements to safeguard both driver-partners and riders. A core component of this is our Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) management system, which includes proactive measures to offer driver-partners essential in-app support on our platform.
