# Inventarisasi Emisi 2024²⁹

GoTo mencatat peningkatan emisi terutama dari Cakupan 3 Kategori 11: Penggunaan Produk yang Dijual seiring dengan meningkatnya permintaan dan penawaran baru dari bisnis layanan on-demand. Namun, GoTo mencatat penurunan intensitas emisi GRK yang menunjukkan kemajuan kami dalam upaya mengurangi emisi pada setiap kilometer yang ditempuh oleh mitra pengemudi kami saat melayani pesanan. Pengurangan intensitas emisi terutama didukung oleh upaya transisi mitra pengemudi kami untuk menggunakan kendaraan listrik dan peningkatan efisiensi.

- Intensitas emisi GRK per kilometer ditempuh berkurang 1,11% dibandingkan tahun lalu
- Pengurangan sebesar 7,14% pada intensitas emisi GRK per rupiah pendapatan bersih

Akibat dekonsolidasi Tokopedia dari GoTo, inventarisasi emisi GRK dan polutan udara GoTo tahun 2024 tidak lagi memasukkan Tokopedia dari cakupan emisi sehingga mungkin tidak dapat dibandingkan secara langsung dengan inventarisasi emisi pada periode pelaporan sebelumnya.

Near-Term Targets: GoTo commits to reduce absolute Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions 83% by 2030 from a 2022 base year. GoTo also commits to reduce absolute Scope 3 GHG emissions 51% within the same timeframe.

- The transition to 100% electric vehicles in our active driver-partners fleet.
- Leverage Indonesia's state-owned electrical company (PLN) Green Energy-as-a-Service to transition all our Indonesian offices to renewable energy.
- Responsible maintenance and disposal of our office cooling systems to prevent refrigerant leakages.

Long-Term Targets: GoTo commits to reduce absolute Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions 95% by 2050 from a 2022 base year. GoTo also commits to reduce absolute Scope 3 GHG emissions 90% within the same timeframe.

- Maintain composition of low-emission vehicles in tandem with growth or adjustments to the company's business.
- Support and leverage national decarbonization targets for the electric grid in the countries we operate in.

In line with SBTi's Net-Zero standard, GoTo commits to reducing emissions according to its verified targets, and any plans for the Company to use carbon credits will only be to counterbalance the impact of any unabated emissions that remain once GoTo has achieved its long-term science-based target.

# 2024 Emissions Inventory²⁹

GoTo recorded an increase in emissions particularly from Scope 3 Category 11: Use of Sold Products adjacent to growing demand and newer offerings from the company's on-demand services business. However, GoTo recorded a reduction in GHG emissions intensity, demonstrating our progress in reducing emissions for each kilometer travelled by our driver-partners when fulfilling orders. This reduction in emissions intensity was primarily driven by supporting our driver-partners' transition to EVs and improving efficiencies.

- GHG emissions intensity per kilometer travelled reduced 1.11% year-on-year
- 7.14% GHG emissions intensity reduction per rupiah of net revenue

Due to the deconsolidation of Tokopedia from GoTo, the 2024 GHG and air pollutant emissions inventory excludes Tokopedia and may not be directly comparable to emissions inventories from previous reporting years.
