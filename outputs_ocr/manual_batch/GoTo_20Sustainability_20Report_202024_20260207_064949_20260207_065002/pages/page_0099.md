55. Mencakup emisi dari transportasi karyawan dari aktivitas terkait bisnis (termasuk perjalanan udara, darat, kereta api, dan laut), serta akomodasi karyawan selama perjalanan terkait bisnis selama tahun pelaporan. Data jarak dan pengeluaran telah digunakan.
Includes emissions from the transportation of employees from business-related activities (including air, road, rail, and marine travel), as well as the accommodations of employees during business-related travel during the reporting year. Both distance-based and spend-based data were used.
56. Data survel digunakan untuk mengukur perjalanan dua arah karyawan dari rumah ke kantor.
Survey data was used to measure two-way employee commute from their homes to our offices.
57. Emisi dari penggunaan produk yang dijual oleh GoTo mencakup emisi langsung pada tahap penggunaan dari layanan mobilitas on-demand, pengantaran makanan, belanja kebutuhan pokok, serta layanan pengiriman dan variasinya. Emisi tidak langsung pada tahap penggunaan produk yang dijual dikecualikan.
Emissions from GoTo’s use of sold products includes direct use phase emissions from the Company’s on-demand mobility, food delivery, groceries, and delivery services and their derived varieties. The indirect use-phase emissions from sold products are excluded.
