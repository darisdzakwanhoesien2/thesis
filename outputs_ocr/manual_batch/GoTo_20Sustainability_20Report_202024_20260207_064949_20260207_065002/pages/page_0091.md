# Langkah 3: Pengumpulan Masukan Pemangku Kepentingan

Pada Oktober hingga November 2024, kami melibatkan 260 pemangku kepentingan, termasuk karyawan, mitra pengemudi dan pedagang, pengguna, investor, dan masyarakat sipil, melalui survei, wawancara, dan diskusi kelompok terarah (FGD).

# Langkah 4: Penilaian Masukan Pemangku Kepentingan

Kami menilai masukan dari pemangku kepentingan menggunakan Skala Materialitas 4 poin (0-3) untuk mengevaluasi tingkat kepentingan tiap topik bagi GoTo: 0 = Tidak penting; 1 = Cukup penting; 2 = Penting, dan; 3 = Sangat penting. Agar seimbang, bobot dibagi rata: 50% untuk pemangku kepentingan internal dan 50% eksternal (dengan eksternal dibagi rata antara pengguna, mitra, masyarakat sipil, dan investor).

Topik material kemudian dipetakan dalam matriks prioritas dengan ketentuan:

- Sumbu X: Dampak terhadap Bisnis (dari masukan C-suite)
- Sumbu Y: Kepentingan bagi Pemangku Kepentingan (gabungan nilai internal dan eksternal dengan bobot setara)

# Langkah 5: Penetapan Prioritas Topik Material

Kami memfinalisasi topik prioritas menggunakan Matriks Materialitas 3x3 berdasarkan rata-rata skor dari pemangku kepentingan (sumbu Y) dan C-suite (sumbu X). Topik kemudian dikelompokkan ke dalam prioritas tinggi, sedang, dan rendah. Hasilnya, terdapat 7 topik LST prioritas tinggi yang ditampilkan dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan 2024.

# Step 3: Gathering Stakeholder Input

Between October and November 2024, we engaged 260 stakeholders—including employees, driver-partners, merchants, users, investors, and civil society—through surveys, interviews, and focus group discussions.

# Step 4: Scoring Stakeholder Responses

We scored stakeholder input using a 4-point Materiality Scale (0-3) to evaluate the importance of each topic for GoTo: 0 = Not important; 1 = Somewhat important; 2 = Important, and; 3 = Critical. To ensure balance, stakeholder groups were given equal weight—50% internal and 50% external (with external input equally distributed across users, partners, civil society, and investors).

The material topics were then plotted on a prioritization map with the following axis specifications:

- X-axis: Impact on Business (based on C-suite input)
- Y-axis: Importance to Stakeholders (combined internal and external scores with equal weight)

# Step 5: The Prioritization of Material Topics

We finalized the priority topics using a 3x3 Materiality Matrix based on average scores from stakeholders (Y-axis) and the C-suite (X-axis). Topics were grouped into high, medium, and low priority. This process resulted in 7 high-priority ESG topics featured in the 2024 Sustainability Report.
