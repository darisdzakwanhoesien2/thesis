A total of 120 participants received training in intrapersonal communication in the coffee class from MBA employees to become creative and skilled agroforestry entrepreneurs.

Sebanyak 120 peserta mendapat pelatihan komunikasi intrapersonal hingga Coffee Class dari karyawan MBA agar dapat menjadi wirausaha agroforestri yang kreatif dan terampil.
