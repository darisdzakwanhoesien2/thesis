# Sejarah Keberlanjutan Kami

OUR SUSTAINABILITY JOURNEY

1971
Konsep keberlanjutan sudah mengakar bersama kami sejak awal pendirian Perusahaan
Sustainability was envisioned since the establishment of our Company

2018
Memulai proses pemantauan dan pelaporan untuk praktik keberlanjutan
Commenced process of monitoring and reporting our sustainability practices

2019
- Membentuk Komite Keberlanjutan
Established a Sustainability Committee
- Mengembangkan rencana strategi keberlanjutan 2019-2021
Developed sustainability strategy plan for 2019-2021
- Meluncurkan Japfa Sustainability Reporting System (JSRS), platform untuk memonitor kinerja lingkungan, sosial dan keuangan Perusahaan
Launched Japfa Sustainability and Reporting System (JSRS), a platform to monitor the environmental, social and financial performance of the Company
- Menginisiasi kajian awal Penilaian Daur Hidup (LCA) untuk operasi perunggasan dengan lingkup cradle-to-gate
Initiated a pilot project of Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) for poultry operations from cradle-to-gate

2020
Berpartisipasi sebagai road tester dalam proyek LCA sosial yang diinisiasi oleh United Nations Environment Life Cycle Initiative dan Social LC Alliance untuk menilai kontribusi kami terhadap para peternak mitra peternakan komersial
Participated as a road tester in the Social LCA Project initiated by the United Nations Environment Life Cycle Initiative and Social LC Alliance to assess our contribution to our poultry farmer partners
