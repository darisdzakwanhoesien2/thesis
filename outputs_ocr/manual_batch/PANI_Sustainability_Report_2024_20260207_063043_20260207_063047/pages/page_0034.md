# Pengembangan Properti

Unit bisnis ini membangun proyek properti untuk dijual, seperti rumah, ruko, rukan, SOHO, kawasan pergudangan, dan kaveling tanah komersial. Proyek pengembangan properti merupakan bagian integral dari pengembangan kota terpadu (townships) menjadi kawasan perumahan dan komersial, lengkap dengan seluruh fasilitas, termasuk bank, sekolah, universitas, rumah sakit, ritel, pusat perbelanjaan, pusat konvensi, fasilitas olahraga, kawasan hunian, pilihan kuliner, beach club, dan taman untuk menunjang kebutuhan masyarakat.

# Property Development

This business unit develops property projects for sale, such as houses, shophouses, shop-offices, SOHO, warehouse area, and commercial land plots. Property development projects are an integral part of the development of integrated townships into residential and commercial areas, complete with all facilities, including bank, school, university, hospital, retail, shopping center, convention center, sport facilities, residential, culinary, beach club, and gardens to support the community's needs.
