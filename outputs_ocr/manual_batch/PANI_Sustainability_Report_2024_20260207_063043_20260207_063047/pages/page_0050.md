# Kode Etik
## Code of Conduct

Perseroan menjunjung tinggi standar etika perilaku, baik dalam etika perilaku karyawan dalam lingkungan kerja maupun etika perilaku bisnis, serta mendorong semua karyawan Perseroan untuk berperilaku adil, jujur, dan hormat. Kode etik Perseroan menguatkan budaya dan nilai-nilai agar Perseroan dapat tumbuh secara sehat dan berkelanjutan (sustainable). Kode etik Perseroan memuat etika bisnis Perseroan, etika kerja, dan pedoman pelaksanaan di mana kode etik ini berlaku bagi seluruh insan Perseroan, baik Manajemen, karyawan, Direksi, Dewan Komisaris dan mitra kerja yang bekerja bersama Perseroan.

Kode etik Perseroan senantiasa dikaji ulang dan disesuaikan dengan perkembangan hukum, sosial, norma yang berlaku di masyarakat, kepentingan Perseroan, dan perjalanan bisnis Perseroan. Perseroan juga terus melakukan sosialisasi kode etik kepada seluruh karyawan melalui berbagai media, seperti situs internal, surat pernyataan, dan surat elektronik. Hingga akhir tahun 2024, Perseroan tidak mendapati adanya pelaporan terkait pelanggaran kode etik yang dilakukan oleh karyawan Perseroan.

Untuk memastikan kesadaran dan pemahaman yang baik tentang kode etik, Perseroan terus melakukan sosialisasi kepada seluruh karyawan melalui berbagai media, seperti situs internal, surat pernyataan, dan surat elektronik. Dengan demikian, seluruh karyawan dapat memahami dan mengimplementasikan kode etik dalam aktivitas sehari-hari.

Hingga akhir tahun 2024, Perseroan tidak mendapati adanya pelaporan terkait pelanggaran kode etik yang dilakukan oleh insan Perseroan. Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa upaya Perseroan dalam menegakkan kode etik dan membangun budaya kerja yang baik telah berjalan efektif. Dengan komitmen ini, Perseroan dapat memastikan bahwa operasional bisnis dijalankan dengan integritas dan profesionalisme yang tinggi.

The Company upholds high standards of ethical behavior, both in the ethics of employee behavior in the work environment and ethics of business conduct, and encourages all employees of the Company to behave fairly, honestly, and respectfully. The Company's Code of Conduct strengthens the culture and values for the Company to grow in a healthy and sustainable manner. The Company's Code of Conduct contains the Company's business ethics, work ethics, and implementation guidelines where this Code of Conduct applies to all of the people in the Company, including management, employees, Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners, and partners who work with the Company.

The Company's Code of Conduct is constantly reviewed and adjusted to the development of legal, social, norms prevailing in society, the interests of the company and the Company's business journey. The Company also consistently socializes the Code of Conduct to all employees through various media, such as trainings, internal websites, statement letters, and electronic mail. Until the end of 2024, the Company did not find any reports related to violations of the Code of Conduct committed by the Company's employees.

To ensure awareness and understanding of the Code of Conduct, the Company continuously conducts training and awareness programs for all employees through various media, such as internal websites, statements, and electronic letters. This enables all employees to understand and implement the Code of Conduct in their daily activities.

As of the end of 2024, the Company has not received any reports of Code of Conduct violations committed by its employees. This demonstrates that the Company's efforts to uphold the Code of Conduct and build a good work culture have been effective. With this commitment, the Company can ensure that its business operations are conducted with high integrity and professionalism.
