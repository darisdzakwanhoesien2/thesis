# Identifikasi Bahaya, Penilaian Risiko dan Pengendalian Dampak

Perseroan telah memiliki kebijakan SMK3 yang didalamnya terdapat prosedur K3 yang memuat mengenai prosedur identifikasi bahaya, penilaian risiko dan pengendalian dampak yang terjadi di Perseroan. Proses identifikasi, analisa, dan pengendalian potensi bahaya serta risiko K3 dilakukan oleh para personil K3 sebagai unit yang bertanggung jawab mengelola K3 di Perseroan. Adapun alur dari proses identifikasi, analisa, dan pengendalian bahaya serta risiko K3 dapat digambarkan sebagai berikut:

1. Safety Induction;
2. Safety Talk;
3. Inspeksi APD;
4. Inspeksi Pos Jaga (akses keluar masuk proyek);
5. Inspeksi Rambu, Simbol dan Label K3;
6. Inspeksi Pengaman Tepi Bangunan/Lubang dan Barikade;
7. Inspeksi Tempat Merokok, Makanan &amp; Minuman;
8. Inspeksi APAR;
9. Safety Patrol;
10. Safety Training;
11. Safety Meeting;
12. Monitoring Kebersihan Proyek;
13. Pengangkutan Sampah Keluar Proyek;
14. Job Hazzar Analysis;
15. Kebersihan dan Penerapan 5R.

Setiap tahapan dari proses tersebut akan didokumentasikan untuk kemudian dianalisis sehingga terdapat beberapa potensi bahaya dan risiko K3 yang teridentifikasi dan dimitigasi.

# Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment and Impact Control

The Company has a SMK3 policy which includes Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) procedures that contain procedures for hazard identification, risk assessment and impact control that occur in the Company. The process of identifying, analyzing, and controlling potential hazards and OHS risks is carried out by OHS personnels as the unit responsible for managing OHS in the Company. The flow of the process of identifying, analyzing, and controlling OHS hazards and risks can be described as follows:

1. Safety Induction;
2. Safety Talk;
3. Inspection of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE);
4. Inspection of Guard Posts (Access Control);
5. Inspection of Safety Signs, Symbols and Labels;
6. Inspection of Building Edge Protection/Holes &amp; Barricades;
7. Inspection of Smoking, Food and Drink Areas;
8. Inspection of Fire Extinguishers;
9. Safety Patrol;
10. Safety Training;
11. Safety Meeting;
12. Monitoring of Project Cleanliness;
13. Waste Transportation out of the Project;
14. Job Hazzar Analysis;
15. Cleanliness and Implementation of 5R.

Each stage of the process will be documented and then analyzed so that there are several potential OHS hazards and risks identified and mitigated.
