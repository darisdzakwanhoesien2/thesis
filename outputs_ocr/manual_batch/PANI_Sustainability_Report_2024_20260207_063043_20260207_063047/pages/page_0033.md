# Kegiatan Usaha [C.4]

Business Activities [C.4]

Perusahaan ini didirikan berdasarkan Akta No. 37 tanggal 8 Juni 2022 dan memiliki tujuan serta kegiatan usaha utama yang sesuai dengan Klasifikasi Baku Lapangan Usaha Indonesia (KBL) dan telah mendapatkan persetujuan dari Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia. Perusahaan ini bergerak di bidang aktivitas perusahaan Holding dan industri kemasan kaleng, serta melalui Entitas Anak, perusahaan ini juga bergerak di bidang real estate, industri pengolahan hasil perikanan, dan jasa pembekuan/penyimpanan di kamar pendingin (cold storage).

Hingga Laporan Keberlanjutan ini diterbitkan, Perseroan menjalankan kegiatan usaha sebagai entitas induk yang menjalankan aktivitas holding melalui entitas anak yang bergerak di bidang industri real estat. Dengan demikian, kegiatan usaha Perseroan saat ini berfokus pada segment utama, yaitu pengembangan properti.

The company was established based on Deed No. 37 dated June 8, 2022 and has objectives and main business activities in accordance with the Indonesian Standard Industrial Classification (KBL) and has received approval from the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia. The company is engaged in the activities of Holding company and can packaging industry, and through its subsidiaries, the company is also engaged in real estate, fishery products processing industry, and cold storage services.

As of the issuance of this report, the company's business activities are focused on holding company activities through its subsidiaries, which are engaged in the real estate industry. Consequently, the Company focuses on its main segment, namely property development.
