Sustainable Environmental Performance

# Keanekaragaman Hayati [F.9], [F.10]

Biodiversity [F.9], [F.10]

Wilayah operasional Perseroan terletak di Kawasan PIK2, Tangerang yang berdekatan dengan kawasan konservasi mangrove yang berbatasan dengan proyek Perseroan. Meskipun begitu, Perseroan memastikan jika setiap kegiatan operasional yang dijalankan di wilayah tersebut tidak membawa dampak negatif terhadap keberadaan keanekaragaman hayati di lingkungan sekitar.

Sebagai pengembang dan pengelola properti, Perseroan menyadari bahwa setiap aktivitas operasional dan rantai pasokan membawa dampak terhadap lingkungan. Dalam rangka mewujudkan komitmen terhadap pelestarian alam, Perseroan mengambil langkah nyata untuk menjaga dan meningkatkan keanekaragaman hayati melalui penyediaan ruang hijau berupa hutan kota dan pembangunan Greenbelt di Kawasan PIK2. Sepanjang tahun 2023, Perseroan telah menanam sebanyak 11.713 batang pohon pelindung. Upaya ini dilanjutkan pada tahun 2024 dengan penanaman 10.694 batang pohon pelindung tambahan yang diperkirakan mampu menyerap emisi karbon sebesar 4.277,60 ton  $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ .

Berikut adalah Jenis-jenis pohon yang telah ditanam di kawasan Perseroan antara lain:

The Company's operational area is located in the PIK2 area, Tangerang, which is adjacent to a mangrove conservation area that borders the Company's project. Despite this, the Company ensures that every operational activity carried out in the area does not have a negative impact on the biodiversity in the surrounding environment.

As a property developer and manager, the Company recognizes that every operational activity and supply chain has an impact on the environment. To realize its commitment to nature conservation, the Company takes concrete steps to preserve and enhance biodiversity by providing green spaces in the form of urban forests and developing green belts in the PIK2 area. Throughout 2023, the Company planted 11,713 shade trees. This effort was continued in 2024 with the planting of an additional 10,694 shade trees, which are estimated to absorb carbon emissions of approximately 4,277.60 tons of  $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ .
