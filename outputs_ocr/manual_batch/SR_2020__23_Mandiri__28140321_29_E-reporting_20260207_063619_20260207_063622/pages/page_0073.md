# MSME Empowerment Plan through Mandiri SOEs Creative Homes

Providing guidance to Bank Mandiri fostered MSMEs with a focus on the fashion sector (weaving, songket, batik), catering, and handicrafts. Coaching for fostered partners covers 3 (three) aspects, namely Go Modern, Go Digital, Go Online such as Digital Marketing training, Product Design training, Financial Management training, Digipreneur Training, Copywriting Training.
