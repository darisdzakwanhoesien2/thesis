The Company's total assets as of December 31, 2013 was Rp5,001.64 billion, or a 28.66% decrease compared with the total assets valued at Rp3,887.42 billion as of December 2012.

The Company's current assets increased 33.04%, so does with the Company's fixed assets that increased 16.79%. The increase in the assets was due to the increase in the goods procurement in relation to the change in sales structure of Samsung brand at PT Teletama Artha Mandiri (TAM), to MD. The increase was also due to the opening of new Megastore in retail business, and the advance purchase goods.
