PROFILE OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

# Ardy Hady Wijaya

President Commissioner

Ardy Hady Wijaya is the Company's founder. He has served as the President Commissioner of the Company since 1998.

Currently, he also holds the position as the President Commissioner of PT Erafone Artha Retalindo (2008 – present), PT Multi Media Seluler (2009 – present), PT Sinar Eka Selaras (2011 – present), PT Data Media Telekomunikasi (2011 – present) and PT Prakarsa Prima Sentosa (2011 – present). In addition, he is a Commissioner of PT Era Sukses Abadi (2011 – present) and PT Eralink International (2011 – present).

Prior to establishing the Company, he was a manager of a family owned department store in Indonesia.
