## Dewan Komisaris

### Board of Commissioners

Dalam menjalankan kegiatan usaha, Perseroan membutuhkan kontrol yang tepat agar kebijakan dan pengelolaan tetap sejalan dengan visi, misi, dan strategi yang telah ditetapkan. Sesuai dengan UUPT dan POJK 33/2014, pengawasan terhadap kebijakan dan pengelolaan Perseroan menjadi tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris. Selain itu, Dewan Komisaris juga berperan memberikan nasihat kepada Direksi demi kepentingan Perseroan dan memastikan penerapan GCG berjalan sesuai standar.

Tugas pengawasan Dewan Komisaris mencakup pengarahan, pemantauan, dan evaluasi pelaksanaan kebijakan strategis. Namun, Dewan tidak terlibat dalam pengambilan keputusan operasional kecuali untuk hal-hal tertentu yang diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar atau peraturan perundang-undangan. Dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris mengedepankan independensi, itikad baik, kehati-hatian, serta tanggung jawab penuh.

## KOMPOSISI DEWAN KOMISARIS

Komposisi dan jumlah anggota Dewan Komisaris ditentukan oleh RUPS dengan mempertimbangkan kondisi perusahaan dan keragaman keahlian, pengetahuan dan pengalaman para anggota. Rekomendasi untuk komposisi anggota Dewan Komisaris dibuat oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi perusahaan, yang perannya dilaksanakan oleh Dewan Komisaris.

In carrying out business activities, the Company requires proper control so that policies and management remain in line with the vision, mission and strategies that have been set. In accordance with the Company Law and POJK 33/2014, supervision of the Company's policies and management is the responsibility of the Board of Commissioners. In addition, the Board of Commissioners also has the role of providing advice to the Board of Directors for the benefit of the Company and ensuring that GCG implementation is in line with standards.

The Board of Commissioners' supervisory duties include directing, monitoring and evaluating the implementation of strategic policies. However, the Board is not involved in operational decision-making except for certain matters stipulated in the Articles of Association or laws and regulations. In carrying out its duties, the Board of Commissioners emphasizes independence, good faith, prudence and full responsibility.

## BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS COMPOSITION

The composition and number of members of the Board of Commissioners is determined by the GMS taking into account the condition of the company and the diversity of expertise, knowledge, and experience of the members. Recommendations for the composition of the members of the Board of Commissioners are made by the company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee, whose role is carried out by the Board of Commissioners.
