## Prosedur Penilaian Kinerja Komite Audit

Prosedur penilaian kinerja Komite Audit dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris dengan mengacu pada tugas dan tanggung jawab yang tercantum dalam Piagam Komite Audit.

## Kriteria dalam Penilaian Kinerja Komite Audit

Kriteria penilaian kinerja Komite Audit meliputi efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas pengawasan terhadap laporan keuangan, kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, serta kemampuan memberikan rekomendasi yang konstruktif untuk perbaikan tata kelola perusahaan. Penilaian juga mempertimbangkan kualitas komunikasi dan koordinasi antara Komite Audit, Dewan Komisaris, serta auditor eksternal, serta kontribusi Komite Audit dalam mengidentifikasi dan mengelola risiko yang dapat mempengaruhi kinerja perusahaan.

## Direksi

### Board of Directors

Pengelolaan Perseroan dilakukan oleh Direksi secara kolegial, dengan tanggung jawab bersama dalam pengambilan keputusan, termasuk melalui rapat Direksi, serta pelaksanaan keputusan tersebut sesuai dengan tugas dan wewenang masing-masing. Tindakan individu anggota Direksi di luar keputusan rapat Direksi menjadi tanggung jawab pribadi hingga disetujui oleh rapat Direksi. Dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Direksi berpegang pada prinsip profesionalisme, efisiensi, transparansi, kemandirian, akuntabilitas, tanggung jawab, dan kewajaran sebagaimana diatur dalam UU No. 40/2007 dan POJK 33/2014.

Tanggung jawab utama Direksi adalah menciptakan nilai tambah bagi pemangku kepentingan dan memastikan keberlanjutan usaha Perseroan. Setiap anggota Direksi memiliki tanggung jawab dan kewenangan yang diatur sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar serta peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Dalam menjalankan tugasnya, Direksi wajib memberikan dedikasi penuh dengan mencurahkan tenaga, pikiran, dan perhatian demi mencapai tujuan Perseroan.

## Audit Committee Performance Assessment Procedure

The Audit Committee performance assessment procedure is conducted by the Board of Commissioners with reference to the duties and responsibilities stated in the Audit Committee Charter.

## Criteria in the Assessment of Audit Committee Performance

The criteria for assessing the performance of the Audit Committee include the effectiveness of the implementation of supervisory duties on financial statements, compliance with laws and regulations, and the ability to provide constructive recommendations for improving corporate governance. The assessment also considers the quality of communication and coordination between the Audit Committee, the Board of Commissioners, and the external auditor, as well as the Audit Committee's contribution in identifying and managing risks that may affect the company's performance.

The management of the Company is carried out by the Board of Directors in a collegial manner, with shared responsibility in decision-making, including through Board of Directors meetings, as well as the implementation of such decisions in accordance with their respective duties and authorities. The actions of individual members of the BOD outside the decisions of the BOD meeting are their personal responsibility until approved by the BOD meeting. In performing its duties, the Board of Directors adheres to the principles of professionalism, efficiency, transparency, independence, accountability, responsibility and fairness as stipulated in Law No. 40/2007 and POJK 33/2014.

The primary responsibility of the BOD is to create added value for stakeholders and ensure the sustainability of the Company's business. Each member of the BOD has responsibilities and authorities that are regulated in accordance with the Articles of Association and prevailing laws and regulations. In carrying out their duties, the Board of Directors must give full dedication by devoting their energy, thoughts and attention to achieve the Company's objectives.
