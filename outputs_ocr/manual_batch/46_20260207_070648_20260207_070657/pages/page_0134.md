Komposisi Dewan Komisaris Perseroan telah mencerminkan keberagaman anggotanya dalam pengalaman dan keahlian. Setiap anggota Dewan Komisaris memiliki kompetensi tinggi yang mendukung peningkatan fungsi pengawasan di Perseroan.

- Receive or provide cash borrowing with an amount exceeding the limit over periods as stipulated in the BOC Meeting, as long the loan facility does not require approval from the GMS;
- Engage in partnership, establish a new business or join with other companies both domestic and overseas, with an amount exceeding the limit over periods as stipulated in the Board of Commissioners Meeting, as long the condition does not require approval from the GMS.
