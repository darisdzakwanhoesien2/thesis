Beban pajak penghasilan adalah jumlah agregat pajak kini dan pajak tangguhan yang diperhitungkan dalam menentukan laba rugi pada suatu periode.

Beban Pajak Penghasilan Erajaya di tahun 2018 meningkat sebesar 136,82% dari Rp133 miliar di tahun 2017 menjadi Rp314 miliar. Kenaikan Beban Pajak Penghasilan dipengaruhi oleh kenaikan Laba sebelum Pajak.

Income tax expense is an aggregate amount of current tax and deferred tax calculated to determine profit or loss for the current period.

In 2018, the Company experienced increased income tax expense by 136.82% from Rp133 billion in 2017 to Rp314 billion. The increasing income tax expense was driven by increasing income before income tax realization.
