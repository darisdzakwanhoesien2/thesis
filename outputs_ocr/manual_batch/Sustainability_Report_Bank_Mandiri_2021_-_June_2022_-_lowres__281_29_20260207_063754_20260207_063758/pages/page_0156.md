# PROGRAM TJSL NON PENDANAAN UMK
## MSE NON-FUNDING PROGRAM
[OJK F.25]

Tahun pelaporan, Bank Mandiri melakukan program TJSL Non Pendanaan UMK berupa: [GRI 203-1]
In the reporting year, Bank Mandiri conducted MSE Non-Funding Program in the form of: [GRI 203-1]

|  Bantuan terkait Bencana Alam
Natural Disaster Assistance | 01 | Pemberian kebutuhan pokok bagi para masyarakat terdampak bencana berupa paket sembako, pembangunan kontainer bencana sebagai buffer stock dalam aksi cepat tanggap bencana di wilayah Pekanbaru, Palembang, Denpasar, Makassar & Manado, dengan total senilai Rp5.468.404.373.
For disaster-affected communities in the form of basic food packages, construction of disaster relief containers as buffer stock for more rapid response to disasters in Pekanbaru, Palembang, Denpasar, Makassar & Manado, with a total value of Rp5,468,404,373.  |
