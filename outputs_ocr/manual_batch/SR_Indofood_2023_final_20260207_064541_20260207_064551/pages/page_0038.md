# Whistleblowing and Grievance Mechanism [2-16, 2-25, 2-26]

Indofood has grievance mechanisms in place for internal and external whistleblowers to report any suspected violation of the Indofood Code of Conduct via email to kode.etik@indofood.co.id and/or through the established mechanism within operating units. We also conduct dialogues with communities where we operate. All grievances are handled by a designated team appointed by the Company. The details of the report and the identity of the whistleblowers are treated in strict confidentiality. A violation of the Indofood Code of Conduct can constitute a breach of employment and may result in disciplinary action. In 2023, the Company received three notable reports, which were deemed valid and followed up.

# Mekanisme Whistleblowing dan Penyampaian Keluhan [2-16, 2-25, 2-26]

Indofood memiliki mekanisme penyampaian keluhan baik bagi pihak internal maupun eksternal guna melaporkan dugaan pelanggaran terhadap Kode Etik Indofood yang dapat disampaikan melalui surat elektronik ke kode.etik@indofood.co.id dan/atau melalui mekanisme yang tersedia di unit-unit operasional. Kami juga membangun dialog dengan masyarakat di mana kami beroperasi. Seluruh keluhan ditangani oleh tim yang ditunjuk oleh Perseroan. Rincian terkait laporan dan identitas pelapor akan dijamin kerahasiaannya. Pelanggaran terhadap Kode Etik Indofood dapat dianggap sebagai pelanggaran terhadap kontrak kerja karyawan dan dapat berujung pada sanksi disipliner. Pada tahun 2023, Perseroan menerima tiga laporan yang patut diperhatikan, di mana seluruh laporan tersebut dianggap memadai dan ditindaklanjuti.
