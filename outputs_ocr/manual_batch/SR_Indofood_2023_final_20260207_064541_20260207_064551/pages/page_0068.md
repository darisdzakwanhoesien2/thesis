# Trans-Fat Free Products

The Dairy Division of CBP Group has been developing trans-fat free products over the years. From the sourcing of raw material where we ensure we use trans-fat free raw material, to the production process that maintains the trans-fat free status. Our Dairy Division is committed to deliver more trans-fat free products to consumers.

As of 2023, Dairy Division has made continuous progress in developing trans-fat free products across the dairy products portfolio, includes SCC, ultra-high temperature ("UHT") milk, sterilized milk, and ice cream products. Furthermore, 100% of our SCC are trans-fat free. [416-1]

Our Agribusiness Group initiated an effort to eliminate partial hydrogenated fat and substitute it with interesterified (IE) oil. As partial hydrogenated fat contains trans-fat, we are actively advancing our research and development capabilities in IE oil production and have completed the elimination of trans-fat from all our consumer products. Moreover, we conduct ongoing trans-fat elimination trials for our industrial product.

# Produk Bebas Trans-Fat

Divisi Dairy Grup CBP telah mengembangkan produk bebas trans-fat di mana kami memastikan hanya menggunakan bahan baku bebas trans-fat dan menjalankan proses produksi yang akan tetap mempertahankan status bebas trans fat pada produk kami. Divisi Dairy kami berkomitmen untuk menghadirkan lebih banyak lagi produk bebas kandungan trans-fat bagi para konsumen.

Hingga tahun 2023, Divisi Dairy terus meraih kemajuan dengan mengembangkan produk bebas trans-fat di portofolio produk dairy, di antaranya meliputi produk SCC, susu ultra-high temperature ("UHT"), susu steril dan produk es krim. SCC kami telah 100% bebas kandungan trans-fat. [416-1]

Grup Agribisnis telah memulai upaya untuk menghilangkan lemak terhidrogenasi parsial dan menggantinya dengan minyak interesterified (IE). Karena lemak terhidrogenasi parsial mengandung trans-fat, kami terus mengembangkan kemampuan tim penelitian dan pengembangan kami dalam produksi minyak IE, dan kami telah berhasil menyelesaikan eliminasi trans-fat dari seluruh produk konsumen kami, dengan terus melanjutkan uji coba eliminasi trans-fat untuk produk industri.
