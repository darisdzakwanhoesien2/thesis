PT INDOFOOD SUKSES MAKMUR Tbk

- Smart performance delivers real-time of Overall Equipment Effectiveness that provides transparency of performance from shop floor to top floor
- Smart quality enhances quality control systems to reduce complaints, giving real-time tracking and traceability, and also speed up the release time of products to the market
- Smart production fine tunes the manufacturing process to increase productivity by enhancing line performance efficiency and throughput, and by reducing losses
- Smart maintenance manages maintenance costs to provide better line efficiency
- Smart eco factory is about energy, water, waste management that reduces resources consumption and costs related to steam generation and WWTP

We are pleased that the successful implementation of these Industry 4.0 initiatives has also been recognized by the Ministry of Industry, through the various accolades that we have received over the years. In 2019, we received the INDI 4.0 Award, in 2020, the National Lighthouse 4.0 Award, and in 2021, the Special INDI 4.0 Award. Furthermore, in 2023, we were also invited to be part of the Indonesia delegation at Hannover Messe to demonstrate the implementation of Industry 4.0 technology internationally.

- Smart performance memberikan Overall Equipment Effectiveness secara real-time dengan meningkatkan transparansi kinerja dari level operatif hingga level manajemen
- Smart quality untuk meningkatkan sistem kendali mutu produk guna menurunkan jumlah keluhan melalui proses penelusuran secara real-time, serta mempercepat waktu peluncuran produk ke pasar
- Smart production mengoptimalkan proses manufaktur untuk meningkatkan produktivitas melalui peningkatan efisiensi kinerja lini produksi dan penurunan tingkat kerugian
- Smart maintenance mengoptimalkan biaya pemeliharaan dan mendorong efisiensi produksi yang lebih baik
- Smart eco factory berfokus pada pengelolaan energi, air dan limbah sehingga dapat mengurangi penggunaan sumber daya dan biaya yang terkait dengan pembangkitan uap dan IPAL

Selanjutnya pada tahun 2023, kami juga diundang menjadi bagian dari delegasi Indonesia di Hannover Messe untuk mendemonstrasikan penerapan teknologi Industri 4.0 di tingkat internasional.
