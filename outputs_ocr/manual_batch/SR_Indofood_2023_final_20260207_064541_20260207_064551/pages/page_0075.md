# RECEIVING AND RESPONDING TO CUSTOMERS' FEEDBACK

Customer feedback forms a critical input to our product design and innovation process. Customers and members of the public can contact the centralized Indofood Customer Service through toll-free telephone lines, by email, instant messaging, and through its social media channels.

In 2023, we received and responded to over 260,000 pieces of feedback, of which 96% were inquiries on product details and promotion.

# CONSUMER EDUCATION

We maintain communication channels with consumers, both online and offline, to increase product knowledge and awareness of how food consumption impacts health and wellness.

A key initiative is our Indofood Nutrition Care program, which has resumed physical engagements, such as through our nutritions care units, alongside online education programs.

With the prevalent use of mobile technology, digital learning and online education are now taking center-stage as a key customer engagement avenue. The websites of our Bogasari and Agribusiness Groups now feature more engagement and interactive methods to educate our consumers about food consumption choices. We also continue to run online education programs for our Integrated Health Post (Pos Pelayanan Terpadu or "Posyandu") volunteers or cadres.

# MENERIMA DAN MENANGGAPI UMPAN BALIK KONSUMEN

Umpan balik konsumen merupakan masukan yang penting untuk pengembangan produk dan proses inovasi kami. Konsumen dan masyarakat dapat menghubungi pusat Layanan Konsumen Indofood melalui saluran telepon bebas pulsa, surat elektronik, pesan instan, maupun melalui berbagai saluran media sosial.

Di tahun 2023, kami menerima dan menanggapi lebih dari 260.000 umpan balik di mana 96% diantaranya berupa pertanyaan tentang detail produk dan program promosi.

# EDUKASI KONSUMEN

Kami memelihara saluran komunikasi dengan kosumen, baik secara online maupun offline, guna meningkatkan penyampaian informasi dan pemahaman mereka tentang bagaimana dampak pola konsumsi makanan terhadap kesehatan dan kebugaran.

Inisiatif utama kami yaitu program Layanan Gizi Indofood telah melanjutkan kegiatan offline, melalui unit-unit layanan gizi kami, serta berbagai program edukasi online.

Seiring dengan semakin luasnya penggunaan teknologi seluler, format pembelajaran secara digital dan online kini menjadi sarana penting dalam kegiatan pelibatan konsumen. Situs web Grup Bogasari dan Agribisnis kini menampilkan lebih banyak metode yang interaktif untuk mengedukasi konsumen tentang berbagai pilihan konsumsi makanan. Kami juga melanjutkan program edukasi secara online untuk para kader Pos Pelayanan Terpadu ("Posyandu").
