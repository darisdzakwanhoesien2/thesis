| 16.b
Promote and enforce non-discriminatory laws and policies for sustainable development
Mempromosikan dan menegakkan hukum dan kebijakan yang non diskriminatif untuk pembangunan berkelanjutan | Labor Practices and People Development
• Our non-discrimination policy as stated in Company Regulation and CLAs is aligned with the ILO’s Discrimination (Employment and Occupation) Convention, 1958 (No. 111)
• We uphold workplace diversity and equal opportunities in our hiring and recruitment processes, as well as our talent management and people development programs. Career opportunities are awarded based on competency and experience, regardless of race, religion, gender or other individual characteristics
• Training programs and employee development courses are accessible to all employees
Praktik Ketenagakerjaan dan Pengembangan Karyawan
• Kebijakan non diskriminasi yang tercantum dalam Peraturan Perusahaan dan PKB sejalan dengan Konvensi ILO tentang Diskriminasi (Pekerjaan dan Jabatan), 1958 (No. 111)
• Kami menjunjung tinggi keberagaman dan kesetaraan peluang pada proses penerimaan karyawan dan rekrutmen, begitupun dalam talent management dan program pengembangan karyawan. Peluang karier diberikan berdasarkan kompetensi dan pengalaman, tanpa memandang ras, agama, gender ataupun karakteristik individu lainnya
• Program pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan dapat diakses oleh seluruh karyawan |
