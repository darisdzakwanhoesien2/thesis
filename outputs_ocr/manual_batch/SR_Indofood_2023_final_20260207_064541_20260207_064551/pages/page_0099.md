## INNOVATION WITHIN OUR OPERATIONS

Innovation plays a pivotal role in propelling Indofood's business forward, where Indofood prioritizes a customer-centric in managing its innovative initiatives. Our research and development team is dedicated to the ongoing enhancement of products, processes, and technologies to align with the evolving needs of our customers. This continuous pursuit of innovation is integral to strengthening business resilience to navigate future challenges effectively.

At Indofood, we leverage technology improved manufacturing and inventory management. In particular, our CBP Group's Dairy Division and Bogasari Group have continued to build on the successful implementation of Industry 4.0. Our initiative is aligned and contributed to "Making Indonesia 4.0" program, launched by the Indonesian Government in 2018 with the aim of increasing the levels of digitalization in manufacturing industry production processes.
