# PERANAN DIREKSI DALAM PERUMUSAN STRATEGI DAN KEBIJAKAN STRATEGIS

Direksi menyusun dan menetapkan kebijakan, prosedur dan pedoman penyusunan rencana jangka panjang Perseroan dan menyiapkan Rencana Bisnis Tahunan termasuk di dalamnya target-target kinerja jangka pendek, strategi pencapaian target, rencana investasi dan pengembangan usaha, dan anggaran.

Direksi menyampaikan draf Rencana Bisnis kepada Dewan Komisaris sebelum dimulainya tahun buku yang akan datang. Dewan Komisaris mempelajari draf Rencana Bisnis dan meminta Direksi memberikan penjelasan atas draf tersebut secara komprehensif kepada Dewan Komisaris di dalam forum rapat bersama Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Rencana pengembangan bisnis juga dibahas bersama Dewan Komisaris di dalam rapat Komite Pengembangan Usaha Baru (Investment Komite/IC).

Di dalam rapat bersama, Dewan Komisaris secara aktif memberikan pendapat dan masukan konstruktif sesuai pengalaman, kapasitas dan latar belakang kompetensi masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris. Tanggapan Dewan Komisaris menjadi masukan untuk finalisasi Rencana Bisnis. Selanjutnya, Dewan Komisaris memberikan persetujuan dan pengesahan Rencana Bisnis Perseroan sebelum tahun buku dimulai.

Dalam merumuskan strategi dan rencana bisnis Perseroan, Direksi mempertimbangkan fondasi bisnis, resiliensi dalam menghadapi tantangan, dan kapasitas internal Perseroan. Pada tahap selanjutnya, Direksi menetapkan target-target operasional bisnis Perseroan ke dalam target-target kinerja setiap anak usaha, divisi, departemen, hingga ke tingkat individu yang dituangkan di dalam alat ukur Indikator Kinerja Utama (IKU).

# BOARD OF DIRECTORS' ROLE IN STRATEGY AND STRATEGIC POLICY FORMULATION

The Board of Directors compiles and establishes policies, procedures, and guidelines for the Company's long-term plans and prepares the Annual Business Plan, including short-term targets, target achievement strategies, investment and business development plans, and budgets.

The Board of Directors submits the draft Business Plan to the Board of Commissioners before the beginning of the next financial year. The Board of Commissioners examined the draft and asked the Board of Directors to provide a comprehensive explanation in a joint meeting of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners. The business development plan was also discussed with the Board of Commissioners in a meeting of the Investment Committee (IC).

In joint meetings, the Board of Commissioners actively provides opinions and constructive feedback according to each member's experience, capacity, and competency. The Board of Commissioners' response is the input to finalize the Business Plan, and it then approves and ratifies the Plan before the beginning of the next financial year.

In formulating the Company's strategy and business plan, the Board of Directors always considers its business foundation, resilience, and internal capacity. The Board of Directors compiles business operational targets into the performance targets of subsidiaries, divisions, departments, and on the individual level as outlined in the Key Performance Indicators (KPI).
