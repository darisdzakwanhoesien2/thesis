Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment, vertikal bisnis Perseroan, membuka gerai terbaru Paris Baguette di Pantai Indah Kapuk (PIK), di PIK Avenue Mall, lantai GF. Dengan toko ini, Perseroan telah meresmikan 8 gerai Paris Baguette di Jabodetabek dalam waktu kurang dari 1 tahun, yang menunjukkan keseriusan Perseroan dalam mengembangkan pasar cafébakery di Indonesia.

Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment, a Company's business vertical, opened the latest Paris Baguette outlet at Pantai Indah Kapuk (PIK), at PIK Avenue Mall, GF floor. With this store, the Company has inaugurated 8 Paris Baguette outlets in Greater Jakarta in less than 1 year, which shows the Company seriousness in developing the cafébakery market in Indonesia.

PT Mitra Belanja Anda ("MBA"), perusahaan patungan Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment dan PT Perjuangan Anak Muda ("PAM") membuka outlet GrandLucky ke-5 yang berlokasi di Central Market PIK Golf Island, Lantai Dasar, Pantai Indah Kapuk, Utara Jakarta.

MBA telah membuka dan mengoperasikan GrandLucky Superstore di Sanur, Bali, Mall of Indonesia (MOI), Jakarta Utara, Old Shanghai Sedayu City Kelapa Gading, Jakarta Utara, dan di Sumbawa, Nusa Tenggara Barat. MBA berencana membuka superstore di Pakuwon City dan Citraland Surabaya, serta BSD di Tangerang.

PT Mitra Belanja Anda ("MBA"), a joint venture company between Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment and PT Perjuangan Anak Muda ("PAM") opened its 5th GrandLucky outlet located at Central Market PIK Golf Island, Ground Floor, Pantai Indah Kapuk, North Jakarta.

MBA has opened and operated GrandLucky Superstores in Sanur, Bali, Mall of Indonesia (MOI), North Jakarta, Old Shanghai Sedayu City Kelapa Gading, North Jakarta, and in Sumbawa, West Nusa Tenggara. MBA plans to open superstore in Pakuwon City and Citraland Surabaya, and BSD in Tangerang.

PT Era Blu Elektronik, perusahaan patungan antara Erajaya Digital dan The Gioi Di Dong dari Vietnam, membuka toko EraBlue pertama di Indonesia yang berlokasi di Ciledug, Tangerang. Toko EraBlue menawarkan produk terbaru dan terbaik dari merek elektronik terkemuka termasuk Samsung, Sony, LG, OPPO, Apple, dan lainnya.

PT EraBlu Elektronik, a joint venture company between Erajaya Digital and The Gioi Di Dong from Vietnam, opened the first EraBlue store in Indonesia, located in Ciledug, Tangerang. The EraBlue store offers the latest and greatest products from top consumer electronics brands including Samsung, Sony, LG, OPPO, Apple, and more.
