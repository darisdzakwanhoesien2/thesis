# LENTERA KASIH

"Komitmen terhadap pemberdayaan masyarakat kami tunjukkan melalui program-program perbaikan infrastruktur, peningkatan keahlian pada kelompok masyarakat sasaran, peningkatan kemampuan ekonomi masyarakat, serta pengembangan dan pelestarian potensi budaya lokal. Beberapa kegiatan yang telah dilaksanakan adalah memfasilitasi pemasaran dan pameran UMKM sepanjang tahun 2022 dan menyelenggarakan studi dan persiapan program keterlibatan UMKM dalam rantai bisnis Erajaya."

"Our commitment to community empowerment is demonstrated through infrastructure improvement programs, expertise enhancement of target community groups, increased community economic capacity, as well as the development and preservation of local cultural potential. Several activities that have been carried out included facilitating MSMEs marketing and exhibitions throughout 2022 and conducting studies and preparation of MSME involvement programs in Erajaya business chain."
