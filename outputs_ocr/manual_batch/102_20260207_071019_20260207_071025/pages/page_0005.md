## ERAJAYA DIGITAL

Fokus pada produk consumer electronic, handset, laptop, komputer, bisnis operator dan voucher, serta produk relevan lainnya dengan beragam konsep toko dari multibrand seperti Erafone maupun monobrand seperti iBox, Samsung, Mi-store, dan lainnya dengan beberapa brand principal.

Focusing on consumer electronics, handsets, laptops, computers, operator business and vouchers, as well as other relevant products with a variety of store concepts, from multibrand concepts like Erafone store to monobrand concepts like iBox, Samsung, Mi-store, and other several brands.

## ERAJAYA BEAUTY &amp; WELLNESS

Fokus pada produk perawatan kecantikan, kesehatan serta produk-produk farmasi dengan bisnis ritel yang didukung toko The Face Shop dan Wellings dan bisnis distribusi peralatan medis yang bekerja sama dengan beragam brand.

Focusing on beauty, health care and pharmaceutical products with a retail business supported by The Face Shop and Wellings stores and a medical devices distribution business in collaboration with various brands.

## ERAJAYA ACTIVE LIFESTYLE

Fokus pada produk lifestyle seperti accessories, Internet of Things (IoT), sport fashion apparel serta produk aktivitas outdoor dengan beragam konsep toko dari multibrand seperti Urban Republic &amp; JD Sports maupun monobrand, seperti Asics, DJI, Garmin, dan lainnya dengan beberapa brand principal.

Focusing on lifestyle-related products such as accessories, Internet of Things (IoT), sport fashion apparel, as well as other outdoor activity-related products with a variety of stores, from multibrand concepts like Urban Republic &amp; JD Sports stores to monobrand concepts like Asics, DJI, Garmin, and other several brands.

## ERAJAYA FOOD &amp; NOURISHMENT

Fokus pada bisnis food &amp; beverages dengan beragam konsep toko seperti chain restaurant, café &amp; bakery, serta supermarket termasuk restoran Sushi Tei, Paris Baguette, dan supermarket Grand Lucky.

Focusing on food &amp; beverages business with a variety of store concepts such as chain restaurants, cafés &amp; bakeries, as well as supermarket including Sushi Tei restaurant, Paris Baguette, and Grand Lucky supermarket.
