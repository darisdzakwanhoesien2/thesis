# BERTUMBUH BERSAMA HUMAN CAPITAL YANG UNGGUL

Growing with Excellent Human Capital

## UNIT CHANGE MANAGEMENT

Unit Change Management berfungsi untuk memastikan komunikasi untuk karyawan yang dihubungkan melalui teknologi yang bertujuan agar dapat mengubah atau meningkatkan nilai-nilai perusahaan yang baru, cara kerja baru, dan cara pembelajaran yang baru, serta memastikan dalam setiap perubahan bisnis yang terjadi, karyawan dapat menyesuaikan diri dengan baik.

Proyek Change Management antara lain implementasi sistem baru System Application and Processing (SAP), serta sosialisasi dan implementasi nilai-nilai luhur ILEAD: Innovation, Leadership, Excellent Customer Service, Agility, Digital.

## HC DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY

Divisi HC mengembangkan sistem teknologi digital untuk mendukung sarana akselerasi dengan misi dapat diakses kapanpun dan dimanapun oleh karyawan.

Di tahun 2022, dengan adanya pertumbuhan serta penambahan bisnis baru, aplikasi QLEAP beradaptasi menjadi aplikasi yang komprehensif dengan fokus memudahkan pengelolaan administrasi serta menjadikan QLEAP sebagai sumber data dan data analitikal, sehingga dapat berfokus pada proses pengembangan karyawan, produktivitas karyawan serta digitalisasi.

Fitur-fitur yang dikembangkan di dalam aplikasi QLEAP pada tahun 2022 adalah:

Performance Management: sistem penilaian kinerja karyawan.

- Erajaya Achievement Award: sistem pengelolaan program improvement dari ide karyawan.
- Checklist Monitoring System: untuk membantu operasional dalam melakukan pemantauan toko.
- LinkedIn Learning Integration: kerja sama dengan LinkedIn untuk peningkatan sistem pembelajaran.
- Management Trainee: program pelatihan karyawan dengan kurun waktu tertentu.
- Talent Management System & Dashboard Sistem: program karyawan berprestasi dan berpotensi.
- Virtual Job Fair: informasi proses pencarian kandidat karyawan.
- Managerial Dashboard Tools: penyajian laporan terkait data karyawan dan kinerja.

## CHANGE MANAGEMENT UNIT

Change Management Unit functions is to ensure communication for employees connected through technology with the aim of being able to change or enhance new corporate values, new ways of working, and new ways of learning, as well as to ensure that employees can adapt well in every business change that occurs.

Change Management project includes the implementation of the new System Application and Processing (SAP) system, as well as socialization and implementation of ILEAD's noble values: Innovation, Leadership, Excellent Customer Service, Agility, Digital.

## HC DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY

HC Division develops digital technology systems to support acceleration facilities with the mission of being accessible anytime and anywhere by employees.

In 2022, with the growth and addition of new businesses, QLEAP application adapted to become a comprehensive application with a focus on facilitating administrative management and making QLEAP a source of data and analytical data, so that it can focus on employee development processes, employee productivity, and digitization.

The features developed in QLEAP application in 2022 were:

Performance Management: an employee performance appraisal system.
- Erajaya Achievement Award: management of improvement programs from employee ideas.
- Checklist Monitoring System: to assist operations in monitoring stores.
- LinkedIn Learning Integration: collaboration to improve learning systems with LinkedIn.
- Management Trainee: employee training program for a certain period.
- Talent Management System and Dashboard System: program for high achieving and potential employees.
- Virtual Job Fair: information on the process of finding employee candidates.
- Managerial Dashboard Tools: providing reports related to employee data and performance.
