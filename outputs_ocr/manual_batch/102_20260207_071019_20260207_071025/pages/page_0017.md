# PERISTIWA PENTING 2022

2022 Event Highlight

Erajaya Digital, vertikal bisnis Perseroan, dan ZTE Corporation, penyedia solusi teknologi telekomunikasi, perusahaan dan konsumen global terkemuka untuk seluler internet, secara resmi menandatangani perjanjian kerja sama. ZTE Corporation diwakili oleh ZTE Indonesia sebagai representative company.

Melalui kerja sama ini, ZTE mempercayakan Erajaya Digital sebagai distributor utama produk smartphone yang akan diluncurkan ZTE di Indonesia. Kolaborasi ini juga menjadi milestone bagi ZTE dalam menghadirkan kembali smartphone berkualitas tinggi untuk pelanggan di Indonesia.

Erajaya Digital, a Company's business vertical, and ZTE Corporation, a leading global provider of telecommunications, enterprise and consumer technology solutions for the internet cellular, officially signed a cooperation agreement. ZTE Corporation is represented by ZTE Indonesia as a representative company.

Through this collaboration, ZTE entrusts Erajaya Digital to be the main distributor of smartphone products that ZTE will launch in Indonesia. This collaboration is also a milestone for ZTE in bringing back high-quality smartphones for customers in Indonesia.

Erajaya Active Lifestyle, vertikal bisnis Perseroan, dan ASICS Indonesia secara resmi mengumumkan kerja sama yang ditandai dengan pembukaan gerai pertama ASICS di bawah bendera Erajaya Active Lifestyle di Summarecon Mall Kelapa Gading lantai 2 GF, Jakarta Utara.

Gerai ini memiliki beragam fitur canggih untuk menemukan sepatu lari yang paling cocok bagi calon pelanggan, dengan tersedianya fasilitas running form, 3D foot scan, dan foot pronation analysis.

Erajaya Active Lifestyle, a Company's business vertical, and ASICS Indonesia officially announced a partnership marked by the opening of the first ASICS outlet under the Erajaya Active Lifestyle banner at Summarecon Mall Kelapa Gading 2 floor GF, North Jakarta.

This outlet has a variety of advanced features to find the most suitable running shoes for potential customers, with the availability of running form, 3D foot scan, and foot pronation analysis facilities.

Erajaya Beauty and Wellness, vertikal bisnis Perseroan, secara simultan membuka 4 gerai pertama Wellings Pharmacy di Jakarta.

Apotek Wellings menyediakan layanan resep, konsultasi obat, nutrisi, suplemen dan produk kesehatan lainnya, konsultasi dan rehabilitasi kesehatan dengan memberikan pengalaman berbelanja yang nyaman dengan harga bersaing, didampingi oleh apoteker yang siaga untuk memberikan konsultasi aktif kepada konsumen.

Gerai Apotek Wellings yang baru diresmikan adalah Apotek Wellings Pantai Indah Kapuk Jakarta Utara, Apotek Wellings Green Ville Jakarta Barat, Apotek Wellings Citra Garden Jakarta Barat, dan Apotek Wellings Teluk Gong Jakarta Utara.

Erajaya Beauty and Wellness, a Company's business vertical, simultaneously opened 4 first outlets of Wellings Pharmacy in Jakarta.

Wellings Pharmacy provides prescription filling services, drug consultation, nutrition, supplements and other health products, health consultation and rehabilitation, provided with a comfortable shopping experience at competitive prices, escorted by pharmacists who are on standby to provide active consultation on consumers.

The newly inaugurated Wellings Pharmacy outlets are Apotek Wellings Pantai Indah Kapuk, North Jakarta, Apotek Wellings Green Ville, West Jakarta, Apotek Wellings Citra Garden, West Jakarta, and Apotek Wellings Teluk Gong, North Jakarta.
