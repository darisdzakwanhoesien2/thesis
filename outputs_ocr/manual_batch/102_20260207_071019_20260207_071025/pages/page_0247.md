# LENTERA CERDAS

"Kami berkomitmen untuk ikut berkontribusi dalam meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat di bidang pendidikan dengan menyediakan akses pendidikan yang lebih luas bagi masyarakat. Komitmen kami telah diaktualisasikan melalui program Magang On-store dan Online Siswa Sekolah Menengah Kejuruan (SMK) untuk mengenal bisnis retail Grup Erajaya Batch 3 &amp; 4, program bantuan sarana pendidikan untuk menunjang kegiatan belajar-mengajar, dan program kerja sama SMK dengan lini bisnis baru di Grup Erajaya."

"We are committed to contributing in improving community welfare in the field of education by providing wider access to education for the community. Our commitment has been actualized through the On-store and Online Internship Programs for Vocational High School (SMK) Students to introduce retail business of Erajaya Group Batch 3 &amp; 4, educational facility assistance programs to support teaching and learning activities, and SMK collaboration programs with new business lines in Erajaya Group."
