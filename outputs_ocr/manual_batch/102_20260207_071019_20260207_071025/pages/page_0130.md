# Ekuitas

Jumlah ekuitas Perseroan pada 31 Desember 2022 sebesar Rp7,20 triliun, naik 11,5% dibandingkan Rp6,46 triliun pada periode yang sama tahun 2021. Kenaikan ekuitas terutama disebabkan oleh penambahan saldo laba belum ditentukan penggunaannya sebesar Rp649,54 miliar.

# LAPORAN LABA RUGI DAN PENGHASILAN KOMPREHENSIF LAIN KONSOLIDASIAN

# Equity

Total equity of the Company as of December 31, 2022 amounted to Rp7.20 trillion, an increase of 11.5% compared to Rp6.46 trillion in the same period in 2021. The increase in equity was mainly due to the addition of unappropriated retained earnings of Rp649.54 billion.
