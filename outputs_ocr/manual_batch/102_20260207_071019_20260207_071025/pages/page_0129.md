# TINJAUAN KEUANGAN

Financial Overview

## Aset Tidak Lancar

Aset tidak lancar sebesar Rp5,84 triliun, naik 23,0% dari Rp4,75 triliun pada tahun 2021 menjadi Rp5,84 triliun. Kenaikan aset tidak lancar terutama disebabkan oleh:

- Aset tetap, neto naik 84,7% dari Rp851,91 miliar menjadi Rp1,57 triliun.
- Aset tak berwujud, neto naik 13,8% dari Rp767,85 miliar menjadi Rp874,12 miliar.
- Aset hak guna naik 70,1% dari Rp815,73 miliar menjadi Rp1,39 triliun.
- Investasi pada entitas asosiasi dan ventura bersama naik 278,8% dari Rp105,85 miliar menjadi Rp400,92 miliar.

Sebaliknya, terdapat penurunan yang signifikan pada:

- Uang muka pembelian aset tetap dan aset tak berwujud turun 85,9% dari Rp334,88 miliar menjadi Rp47,24 miliar.
- Taksiran tagihan pajak turun 22,2% dari Rp1,64 triliun menjadi Rp1,28 triliun.

## Liabilitas

Jumlah liabilitas Perseroan per 31 Desember 2022 sebesar Rp9,85 triliun, naik 100,7% dari Rp4,91 triliun pada periode yang sama tahun sebelumnya. Kenaikan jumlah liabilitas terutama disebabkan oleh kenaikan liabilitas jangka pendek sebesar 110,8%, sementara liabilitas jangka panjang juga naik sebesar 32,3%.

## Liabilitas Jangka Pendek

Liabilitas jangka pendek sebesar Rp9,02 triliun, naik 110,8% dari Rp4,28 triliun pada tahun 2021. Kenaikan liabilitas jangka pendek terutama disebabkan oleh:

- Utang bank jangka pendek naik 171,2% dari Rp1,29 triliun menjadi Rp3,49 triliun.
- Utang usaha pihak ketiga naik 58,7% dari Rp1,50 triliun menjadi Rp2,38 triliun.
- Utang lain-lain pihak ketiga naik 53,3% dari Rp677,45 miliar menjadi Rp1,04 triliun.
- Utang pajak naik 685,8% dari Rp135,58 miliar menjadi Rp1,07 triliun.

## Liabilitas Jangka Panjang

Liabilitas jangka panjang sebesar Rp834,13 miliar, naik 32,3% dari Rp630,41 miliar pada tahun 2021. Kenaikan liabilitas jangka panjang terutama disebabkan oleh kenaikan liabilitas sewa sebesar 91,8% dari Rp244,91 miliar menjadi Rp431,35 miliar.

## Non-Current Assets

Non-current assets amounted to Rp5.84 trillion, an increase of 23.0% from Rp4.75 trillion in 2021 to Rp5.84 trillion. The increase in non-current assets was mainly due to:

- Fixed assets, net increased by 84.7% from Rp851.91 billion to Rp1.57 trillion.
- Intangible assets, net, increased by 13.8% from Rp767.85 billion to Rp874.12 billion.
- Right-of-used assets increased by 70.1% from Rp815.73 billion to Rp1.39 trillion.
- Investments in associates and joint ventures increased by 278.8% from Rp105.85 billion to Rp400.92 billion.

Conversely, there was a significant decrease in:

- Advances for purchases of fixed assets and intangible assets decreased by 85.9% from Rp334.88 billion to Rp47.24 billion.
- Estimated tax claims for tax refund decreased by 22.2% from Rp1.64 trillion to Rp1.28 trillion.

## Liabilities

Total liabilities of the Company as of December 31, 2022 amounted to Rp9.85 trillion, increased by 100.7% from Rp4.91 trillion in the same period of the previous year. The increase in total liabilities was mainly due to an increase in current liabilities of 110.8%, while non-current liabilities also increased by 32.3%.

## Current liabilities

Current liabilities amounted to Rp9.02 trillion, increased by 110.8% from Rp4.28 trillion in 2021. The increase in short-term liabilities was mainly due to:

- Short-term bank loans increased by 171.2% from Rp1.29 trillion to Rp3.49 trillion.
- Trade payables to third parties increased by 58.7% from Rp1.50 trillion to Rp2.38 trillion.
- Other payables to third parties increased by 53.3% from Rp677.45 billion to Rp1.04 trillion.
- Taxes payable increased by 685.8% from Rp135.58 billion to Rp1.07 trillion.

## Non-current Liabilities

Non-current liabilities amounted to Rp834.13 billion, an increase of 32.3% from Rp630.41 billion in 2021. The increase in non-current liabilities was mainly due to an increase in lease liabilities of 91.8% from Rp244.91 billion to Rp431.35 billion.
