Secara berkala, Dewan Komisaris menerima hasil evaluasi atas efektivitas sistem manajemen risiko dan pengendalian internal yang dilaksanakan oleh Komite Audit bekerja sama dengan Audit Internal. Hasil evaluasi dan rekomendasi perbaikan telah disampaikan kepada Direksi melalui rapat bersama Dewan Komisaris, Komite Audit, dan Direksi atau tim manajemen untuk ditindaklanjuti.

Dalam pengamatan Dewan Komisaris, Perseroan telah mengembangkan sistem manajemen risiko dan pengendalian internal di Perseroan sehingga menjadi sistem yang komprehensif, sistematis, dan terukur dalam mengantisipasi, mendeteksi, mengidentifikasi, dan merespons berbagai perubahan risiko yang dinamis di lingkungan bisnis.

# Penilaian Dewan Komisaris atas Kinerja Komite Dewan Komisaris

Dewan Komisaris melaksanakan tugas pengawasan dan pemberian nasihat dibantu oleh Komite Audit. Dewan Komisaris berpendapat bahwa sepanjang tahun 2022 Komite Audit telah bekerja dengan baik sesuai ketentuan GCG.

Komite Audit memberikan rekomendasi yang dibutuhkan Dewan Komisaris dalam rangka melaksanakan keputusan RUPS Tahunan tahun buku 2021 untuk menunjuk Kantor Akuntan Publik yang akan mengaudit Laporan Keuangan Konsolidasian Perseroan dan Entitas Anak untuk tahun buku 2022 dan menetapkan persyaratan-persyaratan lain, termasuk honorarium, sehubungan penunjukan Kantor Akuntan Publik.

Komite Audit juga membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam menyiapkan tanggapan Dewan Komisaris terkait permintaan persetujuan, dukungan, saran/nasihat dari Direksi setelah melakukan proses penelaahan yang mendalam.

Pada tahun 2022, Komite Audit mengadakan 4 kali rapat Komite Audit dan 2 sirkuler pengganti rapat Komite Audit yang seluruhnya dihadiri Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Rapat Komite Audit juga dapat dihadiri oleh manajemen, Audit Internal, dan auditor eksternal.

Semua pencapaian atas pelaksanaan tugas Komite Audit selama 2022 menjadi dasar pertimbangan bagi Dewan Komisaris dalam memberikan penilaian kinerja yang positif.

The Board of Commissioners periodically receives evaluation results on the effectiveness of risk management and internal control systems implemented by the Audit Committee in collaboration with Internal Audits. Evaluation results and recommendations for improvement are delivered to the Board of Directors through joint meetings of the Board of Commissioners, the Audit Committee, and the Board of Directors or the management team for follow-ups.

The Board of Commissioners is of the view that the Company has developed risk management and internal control systems Which are comprehensive, systematic and measurable to anticipate, detect, recognize and respond to dynamic risk changes in the Company's business environment.

# Performance Assessment of Board of Commissioners' Committees by Board of Commissioners

The Board of Commissioners performs supervisory and advisory duties assisted by the Audit Committee. The Board of Commissioners believes the Audit Committee performed well under GCG provisions throughout 2022.

The Audit Committee provided the recommendations needed by the Board of Commissioners to implement the 2021 fiscal year Annual GMS resolution to appoint a Public Accounting Firm to audit the Consolidated Financial Statements of the Company and Subsidiaries for the 2022 fiscal year, and determine other requirements, including honorariums, with the appointment of the Public Accounting Firm.

The Audit Committee also assists the Board of Commissioners in preparing its responses to requests for approval, support, and advice/recommendations from the Board of Directors, after conducting a thorough review process.

The Audit Committee held four Audit Committee meetings and two circulars to substitute Audit Committee meetings in 2022, all attended by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors. Audit Committee meetings may also be attended by management, Internal Audit, and external auditors.

The Board of Commissioners provided a positive performance assessment of the Audit Committee in 2022, due to all of its achievements in carrying out its duties.
