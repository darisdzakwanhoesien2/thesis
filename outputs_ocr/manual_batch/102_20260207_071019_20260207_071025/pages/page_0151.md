# Ekspositor

Ekspansi merupakan salah satu kunci memperluas cakupan bisnis Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment. Kami berupaya untuk selalu jeli melihat setiap peluang bisnis. Selain itu, standar kualitas produk dan layanan di setiap outlet harus konsisten supaya kekhasan produk dan layanan kami mendapat tempat di hati masyarakat. Kami terus berinovasi untuk menghadirkan produk-produk yang sesuai selera lokal, namun tetap premium.

Expansion is one of the keys to expanding Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment's business scope. We strive to always be observant in seeing every business opportunity. In addition, product and service quality standards at each outlet must be consistent so that the uniqueness of our products and services has a place in the hearts of the public. We continue to innovate to present premium products that suit local tastes.

GABRIELLE HALIM

CEO Erajaya Food &amp; Nourishment
