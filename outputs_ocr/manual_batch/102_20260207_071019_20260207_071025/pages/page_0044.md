LAPORAN MANAJEMEN
Management Reports

Karena kelangkaan chipset, beberapa prinsipal lebih fokus pada produk middle-end dan high-end untuk konsumen yang lebih mengutamakan kualitas, fitur, kenyamanan, dan dapat mewakili gaya hidup mereka. Hal ini selaras dengan fokus pasar Perseroan sebagai modern retail yang menyediakan beragam produk untuk seluruh segmen memberikan customers experience dan product-led demo sehingga average selling price (ASP) Perseroan dapat meningkat dari Rp3 juta per Desember 2021 menjadi Rp4,3 juta per Desember 2022.

Pertumbuhan jumlah gerai yang pesat dan persaingan bisnis ritel yang semakin tinggi membuat kebutuhan sumber daya manusia (SDM) yang berkualitas juga meningkat. Perseroan membutuhkan SDM yang mampu memenuhi kebutuhan di industri existing dan juga untuk pengembangan bisnis baru. Untuk itu Perseroan berinvestasi mengembangkan pusat pelatihan untuk memberikan berbagai pelatihan pengembangan kompetensi. Setiap karyawan, terutama yang bertugas di frontliner, harus memiliki standar product knowledge, soft selling skill, leadership skill, service excellence sehingga Perseroran dapat terus-menerus meningkatkan service level kepada konsumen.

Sepanjang tahun 2022 Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan pelatihan dengan total 2.821 modul yang diikuti 10.611 karyawan (termasuk outsources). Selain itu Perseroan juga mengirimkan karyawannya untuk mengikuti training eksternal serta mengadakan kegiatan workshop dan seminar seperti program Zoom Pakar yang menghadirkan pembicara tamu terkemuka dari industri Agile dan Digital Technology.

Sejak tahun 2021, Perseroan melaksanakan transformasi digital internal dengan mengimplementasikan sistem aplikasi kelas dunia yang dapat mengintegrasikan berbagai proses bisnis operasional Perseroan. Dengan sistem ini, seluruh alur kerja menjadi transparan sehingga memantu proses pengawasan dan pengambilan keputusan secara tepat dan akurat. Proses transformasi digital menjadi tantangan tersendiri karena migrasi proses bisnis ke platform digital dan kegiatan operasional Perseroan harus tetap berjalan secara simultan.

Due to the chipset shortage, some principals focused on middle-end and high-end products for consumers who prioritize quality, features, and convenience which represent their lifestyles. This aligns with the Company's market focus as a modern retailer providing products for all segments, customer experiences, and product-led demos, the average selling price (ASP) increased from Rp3 million as of December 2021 to Rp4.3 million as of December 2022.

The rapid growth in the number of outlets and increasing competition in the retail business also increased the need for quality human resources. The Company requires human resources capable of meeting the needs of the existing industry and for new businesses. We have invested in developing a training center to provide competency development training. Every employee, especially those on the front line, must have a high standard of product knowledge, soft selling skills, leadership skills, and service excellence so the Company can continuously improve its service level to consumers.

The Company conducted training with 2,821 modules attended by 10,611 employees (including outsourced) in 2022. Employees also participated in external training and workshops and seminars, such as the Expert Zoom program, with prominent guest speakers from the Agile and Digital Technology industries.

The Company has carried out an internal digital transformation since 2021, by implementing a world-class application system to integrate its operational business processes. This system makes the entire workflow process transparent, facilitating the monitoring and decision-making process in a timely and accurate manner. Digital transformation is challenging because the migration of business processes to digital platforms and operational activities must continue simultaneously.
