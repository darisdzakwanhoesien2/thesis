# BOARD OF DIRECTORS COMPETENCY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM 2021

In 2021, the Board of Directors has participated in seminars/trainings. The Board of Directors can continuously participate in professional education and training programs to gain more insights into the latest issues in the industry, the economy, regulatory changes, or GCG developments that affect the Company's business.

Education and training programs can be in the form of executive trainings, seminars, workshops or short courses. The Board of Directors prepares programs they plan to participate and include them in the Annual Work Plan. Participating members are expected to share the knowledge gain with other members of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners.
