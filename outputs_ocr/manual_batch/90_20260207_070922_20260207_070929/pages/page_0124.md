|  PT Sinar Eka Seleras ("SES") and PT Mitra Internasional Indonesia ("MIT") | On June 2, 2021, SES as one of the Company's Controlled Companies whose shares are 99% owned by the Company, entered into an affiliated transaction with MIT, also one of the Company's Controlled Companies whose shares are 99% owned by the Company, in the form of equity participation in MIT. | The Company's internal restructuring with the aim of business efficiency and share ownership in the Company's group. | This transaction was made fairly in accordance with adequate affiliate transaction procedures and referred to the generally accepted business practices as stipulated in Article 6 paragraph (1) letter b1 and Article 6 paragraph (2) PGDK No. 42/2020. |  |   |
