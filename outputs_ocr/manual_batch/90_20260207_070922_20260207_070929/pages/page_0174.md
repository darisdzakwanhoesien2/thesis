# MEMBERSHIP AND TERM OF OFFICE

Referring to POJK 55/012015, members of the Audit Committee serve for a term that may not be longer than the term of office of the Board of Commissioners as stipulated in the Articles of Association and may be reappointed only for the next 1 (one) period.
