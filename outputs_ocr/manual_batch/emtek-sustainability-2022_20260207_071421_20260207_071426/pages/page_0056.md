# Clean Water Reservoir Assistance Bantuan Reservoir Air Bersih

![img-105.jpeg](img-105.jpeg)

To help communities that lack clean water, in collaboration with the Paroki Hati Kudus Yesus Laktutus, YPP has inaugurated the installation of water pipes, reservoirs, and spring wells in Fohseka Village, Kupang, NTT. This activity includes the construction of pipeline installations, new wells, reservoirs, and the repair of abandoned water aid facilities. It is known that the local community must draw water in a distant place, crossing 3 hills in order to get clean water. Another option is to subscribe to a water tank for Rp300,000/month. The quality of the water source closest to the settlement is not good and many residents suffer from skin diseases, kidney stones, coughs and digestive problems. The clean water installation has eased the burden of 150 households in the village to enjoy clean water. The water from the facility can also be used to water the villagers' hydroponic plants.

Untuk membantu masyarakat yang kekurangan air bersih, bekerja sama dengan Paroki Hati Kudus Yesus Laktutus, YPP telah meresmikan instalasi pipa air, reservoir, dan sumur mata air di Desa Fohseka, Kupang, NTT. Kegiatan ini meliputi pembuatan instalasi pipa, sumur baru, reservoir, dan perbaikan kembali fasilitas bantuan air yang terbengkal. Untuk diketahui masyarakat setempat harus menimba air di tempat jauh, melintasi 3 bukit agar mendapatkan air bersih. Pilihan lainnya adalah berlangganan air tangki seharga Rp300.000/bulan. Pasalnya kualitas sumber air terdekat dari pemukiman warga tidak bagus dan membuat banyak warga terkena penyakit kulit, batu ginjal, batuk dan masalah pencernaan. Adanya instalasi air bersih ini telah meringankan beban 150 kepala keluarga di desa tersebut agar dapat menikmati air bersih. Bahkan air dari fasilitas tersebut juga dapat digunakan untuk menyiram tanaman hidroponik warga.

Since 2015 through donations collected by YPP, EMTEK Group has consistently built 29 SCTV ASA Bridges. Especially in 2022, a total of 3 ASA Bridges have been inaugurated. The three bridges consist of:

- The 27th SCTV Asa Bridge.
This SCTV Asa Bridge has a total length of 52 meters connecting two villages and two sub-districts, Kertajaya Village, Rumpin Sub-district and Cikuda Village, Parung Panjang Sub-district, West Java Province.

- The 28th SCTV ASA Bridge
This bridge is located in Batu Benawa District, Hulu Sungai Tengah Regency, South Kalimantan Province and has a total length of 100 meters and a width of 1.2 meters built using a suspension bridge structure.

- The 29th ASA SCTV Bridge
This bridge is located in Busang District, East Kutai Regency, East Kalimantan Province and has a total length of 100 meters and a width of 1.2 meters built using a suspension bridge structure.

SCTV's ASA Bridge construction program has greatly benefited the communities that use it. This infrastructure assistance has opened road access which has indirectly opened access to the economy and improved the quality of life of the community.
