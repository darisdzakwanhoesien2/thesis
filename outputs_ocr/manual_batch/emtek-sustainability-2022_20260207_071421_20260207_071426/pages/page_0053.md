Earthquake Disaster in Cianjur/Bencana Gempa Bumi di Cianjur

On 21 November 2022, the Cianjur area was shaken by a 5.6 magnitude earthquake triggered by the Cugenang Fault. The Cugenang Fault stretches for approximately 9 kilometers and crosses at least 9 villages, namely, Ciherang Village, Ciputri Village, Cibeureum, Nyalindung, Mangunkerta, Sarampad, Cibulakan, and Benjot Village. The last village, Nagrak, is located within Cianjur Sub-district.

According to the National Disaster Management Agency (BNPB), at least 1,362 houses were damaged, 343 of which were severely damaged. A shopping center collapsed, two government office buildings, three schools, a hospital, worship facilities, and a boarding school were damaged. 335 people were recorded as dead, however, data from the Cianjur Regency government shows that the number of fatalities reached 635 people or almost double the official BNPB data. Another 2,046 people were injured and up to 39 people are still missing.

In the middle of the disaster, YPP was present to entertain the children of Cianjur earthquake victims by inviting Kak Resha to tell stories and sing together to help restore the psychological condition of the Cianjur earthquake victims who were forced to live in refugee camps. This activity was carried out in Sukamanah Village, Sub-district Cugenang, Cianjur Regency and Public Elementary School (SDN) Palasari, Neighborhood Bojongherang, Sub-district Cilaku, District Cianjur Kota. The YPP team also provided a Weli car (Mobile Education Vehicle) which attracted the enthusiasm of the children to read the books they wanted. The children also received gifts provided by the YPP Indosiar SCTV team.

Pada tanggal 21 November 2022, daerah Cianjur digunakan gempa bumi yang berkekuatan 5,6 Magnitudo yang dipicu oleh patahan atau Sesar Cugenang. Sesar Cugenang membentang sepanjang kurang lebih 9 kilometer dan melintasi sedikitnya 9 desa yakni, Desa Ciherang, Desa Ciputri, Cibeureum, Nyalindung, Mangunkerta, Sarampad, Cibulakan, dan Desa Benjot. Satu desa terakhir, Nagrak, lokasinya di dalam wilayah Kecamatan Cianjur.

Berdasarkan catatan Badan Nasional Penanggulangan Bencana (BNPB) setidaknya 1.362 rumah rusak, 343 di antaranya rusak berat. Sebuah pusat perbelanjaan runtuh, dua gedung kantor pemerintah, tiga sekolah, rumah sakit, fasilitas ibadah, dan pesantren rusak. Tercatat 335 orang tewas, namun data dari pemerintah Kabupaten Cianjur justru menunjukkan jumlah korban tewas mencapai 635 orang atau hampir dua kali lipat dari data resmi BNPB. Sebanyak 2.046 orang lainnya tertuka dan hingga 39 orang masih hilang.

Di tengah bencana tersebut YPP hadir untuk menghibur anak-anak korban gempa Cianjur dengan mendatangkan Kak Resha untuk berdongeng dan bernyanyi bersama guna membantu memulihkan kondisi psikologis anak-anak korban gempa Cianjur yang terpaksa tinggal di pengungsian. Kegiatan ini dilakukan di Desa Sukamanah Kecamatan Cugenang, Kabupaten Cianjur dan Sekolah Dasar Negeri (SDN) Palasari, Kelurahan Bojongherang, Kecamatan Cilaku, Kabupaten Cianjur Kota. In addition, the Pundi Amal Peduli Kasih SCTV Indosiar Foundation also collaborated with the Cianjur Care Navy Task Force to distribute basic food assistance to residents affected by the Cianjur Earthquake.

Mount Semeru Eruption Disaster/Bencana Alam Erupsi Gunung Semeru

Mount Semeru, experienced another eruption marked by hot clouds on 4 December 2021 at 15:20 WIB. The total number of displaced residents was 9,417 people spread across 402 points. The concentration of refugees is centered in 3 Sub-districts, namely in Pasirian 15 points 1,657 people, Candipuro 22 points 3,897 people and Pronojiwo 7 points 1,136 people.

EMTEK Peduli Semeru YPP organized free medical treatment in collaboration with Alpha Omega Foundation or AO Care for residents affected by the eruption of Mount Semeru for residents of Kamar Kajang Hamlet, Sumberwuluh Village, Candipuro Lumajang, 14 March 2022. A total of 129 patients were well served with various complaints including cough and cold, myalgia, ulcer, high blood pressure, headache, itching, and rheumatism.

On that occasion, YPP also distributed 101 basic food packages (5 kg of rice, 1 kg of sugar, 1 liter of cooking oil, 2 cans of 425 gr sardine, 5 pcs of spices, 225 ml of soy sauce, 560gr of sweetened condensed milk) to each family. In addition, YPP also distributed 75 packages of school supplies (backpacks, 10 notebooks, pens, drawing books, 12 color pencils, rulers, pencils, sharpeners, erasers) to students of SDN Jugosari 03. Some students of SDN Jugosari 03 live in Sumberlangsep Hamlet.

EMTEK Peduli Semeru distributed kitchen utensils to residents affected by the eruption of Mount Semeru in Kamar Kajang Hamlet, Sumberwuluh Village, Candipuro Sub-district, Lumajang Regency, 8 February 2022. Kamar Kajang Hamlet is one of the hamlets that has been severely affected by the eruption of Mount Semeru. The Kitchen Equipment packages distributed included 50 gas stoves, 50 regulator hoses, 50 LPG gas cylinders 3 kg, 250 pots, and 250 pans.

Gunung Semeru, kembali mengalami erupsi ditandai dengan adanya guguran awan panas pada tanggal 4 Desember 2021 pada pukul 15.20 WIB. Total warga mengungsi berjumlah 9.417 jiwa yang tersebar di 402 titik. Konsentrasi pengungsian terpusat di 3 Kecamatan, yakni di Pasirian 15 titik 1.657 jiwa, Candipuro 22 titik 3.897 jiwa dan Pronojiwo 7 titik 1.136 jiwa.

EMTEK Peduli Semeru YPP menyelenggarakan pengobatan gratis bekerja sama dengan Yayasan Alpha Omega atau AO Care bagi warga terdampak erupsi Gunung Semeru untuk warga Dusun Kamar Kajang, Desa Sumberwuluh, Kecamatan Candipuro Lumajang, 14 March 2022. Sebanyak 129 pasien terlayani dengan baik dengan berbagai keluhan antara lain batuk pilek, mialgia, maag, darah tinggi, sakit kepala, gatal-gatal, dan rematik.

Dalam kesempatan itu, YPP juga membagikan 101 paket sembako (5 kg beras, 1 kg gula pasir, 1 liter minyak goreng, 2 kaleng sardencis 425 gr, bumbu racik 5 pcs, kecap manis 225 ml, susu kental manis 560gr) kepada masing-masing keluarga. Selain itu YPP juga mendistribusikan 75 paket perlengkapan sekolah (tas ransel, 10 buku tulis, pulpen, buku gambar, pensil warna 12, penggaris, pensil, rautan, penghapus) kepada siswa siswi SDN Jugosari 03. Sebagian siswa SDN Jugosari 03 tinggal di Dusun Sumberlangsep.

EMTEK Peduli Semeru mendistribusikan peralatan dapur bagi warga terdampak Erupsi Gunung Semeru di Dusun Kamar Kajang, Desa Sumberwuluh, Kecamatan Candipuro, Kabupaten Lumajang, 8 Februari 2022. Dusun Kamar Kajang merupakan salah satu dusun yang mengalami dampak cukup parah akibat Erupsi Gunung Semeru. Paket Peralatan Dapur yang didistribusikan antara lain 50 kompor gas, 50 selang regulator, 50 tabung gas elpiji 3 kg, 250 panci, dan 250 wajan.
