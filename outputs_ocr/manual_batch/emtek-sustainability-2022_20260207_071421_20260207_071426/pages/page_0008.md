# COMPANY IN BRIEF
## SEKILAS PERUSAHAAN

&gt; With a vision to become a leading provider of media, technology, digital infrastructure, healthcare services, and financial services and become the main choice of people in Indonesia, the Company has established an excellent reputation as an investment holding company overseeing an ecosystem consisting of business entities with a diverse portfolio of products and services, ranging from media, entertainment, content, online, telecommunications, technology, e-commerce, healthcare to banking and financial services.

&gt; Mengemban visi untuk menjadi penyedia jasa media, teknologi, infrastruktur digital, pelayanan kesehatan, dan jasa keuangan yang terkemuka dan menjadi pilihan utama masyarakat di Indonesia, Perseroan membangun reputasi unggul sebagai perusahaan holding investasi yang menaungi ekosistem yang terdiri dari entitas bisnis dengan beragam portofolio produk dan jasa, mulai dari media, hiburan, konten, daring, telekomunikasi, teknologi, e-commerce, layanan kesehatan hingga jasa perbankan dan keuangan.

The Company manages a variety of businesses as the result of trusted business management which is based on a commitment to always encouraging relentless innovation supported by the latest technology in order to create new business growth opportunities for the Company. Moreover, the Company also continues to transform to realize an organization that is solid, adaptive and sustainable to the existing business dynamics.

Considering business journey of the Company, the spirit of transformation has been echoed since the name change of the Company from the previous name PT Elang Mahkota Komputer when it was established on 3 August 1983 with a business focus on providing computer equipment, to PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi (EMTEK). The Company changed its name through the Deed No. 45 dated 10 March 1997 and was approved by the Minister of Justice of the Republic of Indonesia through Decree No. C2-2694 HT.01.04.Th.97 dated 15 April 1997 and Appendix to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 70 dated 2 September 1997.

The Company continues its innovation and expansion, specifically in the context to strengthen the managed media business, by completing the acquisition of a strategic shareholding in the free-to-air television station PT Surya Citra Televisi (SCTV) through its subsidiary, PT Surya Citra Media Tbk (SCMA), in 2002. followed by other important corporate actions, such as the Company entering the stock exchange through the implementation of an initial public offering (IPO) on 12 January 2010 and completing the acquisition of PT Indosiar Karya Mandiri Tbk (IDKM), the parent company of television station PT Indosiar Visual Mandiri (Indosiar).

Furthermore, in order to strengthen its position in the domestic media industry, EMTEK decided in 2013 to enhance group synergies through the merger of SCMA, the parent company of SCTV, with IDKM, the parent company of Indosiar. This corporate action has successfully strengthened the Company's competitive advantage, gained a better scale of economy in the procurement of television programs and various related efficiencies and synergies. The Company has gained the trust to broadcast various important national and international sports programs, including BRI Liga 1 Football, Liga 2, Menpora Cup 2021, Copa America 2021, Tokyo Olympics 2020, Indonesian National Team Matches, UEFA Champion League/Europa League, English Premier League up to becoming the Official Broadcaster for FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022.

Furthermore, the Company continuously strengthened its business portfolio by venturing into the digital industry to seize opportunities from the rapid growth of the digital economy sector in Indonesia. The Company, through its subsidiary, PT Kreatif Media Karya (KMK), expanded into the e-commerce sector through investment in the shares of one of Indonesia's leading marketplaces, Bukalapak.com, in 2015.

This step was followed by a number of other corporate actions in the digital sector such as logistics technology, education technology, insurance technology and online transportation following the investment in Grab Indonesia, which is one of the leading ride-hailing companies in Indonesia. The strengthening of digital technology-based business is also conducted by realizing investment in PT Kapan Lagi Dot Com Networks (KLN) which oversees several well-known digital portals with diverse target segments, namely Liputan6.com, Merdeka.com, Dream.com, Fimela, Brilio.com, Kapanlagi.com, Bola.com, Bola.net, and Otosia.com.

At the same time, the Company developed an Over-the-Top (OTT) streaming platform, Vidio, which provides a variety of exciting entertainment and sports content, including on-demand video. Carrying the concept of originality of content and information, Vidio in 2022 succeeded in becoming the No.1 OTT streaming platform in the Southeast Asia region in terms of subscriber growth and No.1 in Indonesia in terms of Monthly Active Users and Total Streaming Minutes. In addition, the Company has also rebranded O'Channel television into the MOJ-TV brand with the target segment of young people. Selanjutnya, Perseroan secara berkesinambungan juga memperkuat portofolio bisnisnya dengan merambah industri digital guna meraih peluang dari pertumbuhan yang pesat pada sektor ekonomi digital di Indonesia. Perseroan melalui anak usahanya, PT Kreatif Media Karya (KMK), melakukan ekspansi ke bidang e-commerce melalui investasi pada saham salah satu marketplace terkemuka Indonesia, Bukalapak.com, pada tahun 2015.

Langkah tersebut diikuti dengan sejumlah aksi korporasi lainnya di sektor digital seperti teknologi logistik, teknologi pendidikan, teknologi asuransi hingga transportasi daring menyusul investasi saham di Grab Indonesia, yang merupakan salah satu perusahaan ride-hailing terkemuka di Indonesia. Penguatan bisnis berbasis teknologi digital juga dilakukan dengan merealisasikan investasi pada PT Kapan Lagi Dot Com Networks (KLN) yang menaungi beberapa portal digital ternama dengan target segment yang beragam, yaitu Liputan6.com, Merdeka.com, Dream.com, Fimela, Brilio.com, Kapanlagi.com, Bola.com, Bola.net, dan Otosia.com.

Di saat bersamaan, Perseroan mengembangkan platform streaming Over-the-Top (OTT), Vidio, yang menyuguhkan berbagai konten hiburan dan olahraga yang menarik, termasuk on-demand video. Mengurung konsep orisinalitas konten dan informasi, Vidio pada tahun 2022 berhasil menjadi platform streaming OTT No.1 di kawasan Asia Tenggara dalam hal pertumbuhan pelanggan (subscriber) serta No.1 di Indonesia dalam hal Monthly Active Users dan Total Streaming Minutes. Perseroan selain itu juga telah melakukan rebranding terhadap televisi O'Channel menjadi brand MOJ-TV dengan target segment anak muda.
