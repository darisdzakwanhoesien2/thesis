# FACILITIES, BENEFITS, AND REMUNERATION [GRI 401-2]

# FASILITAS, TUNJANGAN, DAN REMUNERASI [GRI 401-2]

To meet the needs and welfare of employees and support operations, EMTEK provides the same number of facilities, allowances, and remuneration between permanent and non-permanent employees, which includes: (GRI 3-3)

- Basic salary
Providing salaries by considering the applicable regulatory provisions, namely the Regional Minimum Wage, remuneration of similar industries, and the Company's financial condition without discriminating between male and female employees.

- Fixed and non-fixed allowances

- Holiday allowance (THR)
Employees who have 12 (twelve) months of continuous service or more, are given 1 (one) month's wage.

- Employment Social Security
Employment Social Security at the Employment Social Security Organizing Agency (BPJS), consists of: Work Accident Insurance, Death Insurance, Old Age Insurance, Pension Insurance, and Job Loss Insurance.

- Official vehicle
Providing vehicles for certain workers in the form of cars, motorcycles or other types of replacement vehicles to support the smooth running of their duties.

- Maternity leave
Female employees receive 90 days maternity leave.

- Share ownership
Management and Employee Share Ownership Program (MESOP) is implemented in accordance with applicable regulations.

- Health Insurance and health facilities
In addition to BPJS Kesehatan, EMTEK employees are provided with other health insurance and specialized healthcare services at our hospitals. The insurance covers both outpatient and inpatient facilities.

Untuk memenuhi kebutuhan dan kesejahteraan karyawan serta mendukung operasional, EMTEK memberikan sejumlah fasilitas, tunjangan, dan remunerasi yang sama antara karyawan tetap dan karyawan tidak tetap, yaitu meliputi: (GRI 3-3)

- Gaji Pokok
Memberikan gaji dengan mempertimbangkan ketentuan peraturan yang berlaku yakni Upah Minimum Regional, remunerasi industri sejenis, dan kondisi keuangan Perseroan tanpa membeda-bedakan karyawan laki-laki dan perempuan.

- Tunjangan Tetap dan Tidak Tetap

- Tunjangan Hari Raya (THR)
Karyawan yang telah mempunyai masa kerja 12 (dua belas) bulan secara terus menerus atau lebih, diberikan sebesar 1 (satu) bulan upah.

- Jaminan Sosial Ketenagakerjaan
Jaminan Sosial Ketenagakerjaan pada Badan Penyelenggara Jaminan Sosial (BPJS) Ketenagakerjaan, terdiri dari: Jaminan Kecelakaan Kerja, Jaminan Kematian, Jaminan Hari Tua, Jaminan Pensiun, dan Jaminan Kehilangan Pekerjaan.

- Kendaraan Dinas
Menyediakan kendaraan bagi Pekerja tertentu berupa mobil, sepeda motor atau jenis kendaraan pengganti lain untuk menunjang kelancaran dalam menjalankan tugasnya.

- Cuti Melahirkan
Karyawan perempuan mendapat cuti hamil 90 hari.

- Kepemilikan Saham
Program Kepemilikan Saham Manajemen dan Karyawan (MESOP) dilaksanakan dengan peraturan yang berlaku.

- Asuransi Kesehatan dan Fasilitas Kesehatan
Selain BPJS Kesehatan, karyawan EMTEK difasilitasi asuransi kesehatan lain dan pelayanan kesehatan khusus di rumah sakit kami. Asuransi tersebut mencakup fasilitas rawat jalan dan rawat inap.
