## LEMBAR UMPAN BALIK [G.2]

This Sustainability Report has been externally verified by PT TUV Rheinland Indonesia which is an independent party and the Company does not have any affiliation with the external assurance. The use of external assurance is based on the Company's desire to ensure transparency and quality reporting to stakeholders. Meanwhile, the Company's Financial Statements are consolidated reports of all subsidiaries from 1 January to 31 December 2022 which have been audited by public accountants, unless otherwise stated. For detailed information on subsidiaries' sustainability policies and activities that have not been contained in this report, please refer to their respective 2022 Sustainability Reports.
