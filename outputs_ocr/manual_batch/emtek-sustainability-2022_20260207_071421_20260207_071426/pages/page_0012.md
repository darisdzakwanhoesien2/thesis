# PRODUCTS, SERVICES, AND BUSINESS ACTIVITIES OPERATED [GRI 2-6] [C.4]
# PRODUK, LAYANAN, DAN KEGIATAN USAHA YANG DIJALANKAN [GRI 2-6] [C.4]

EMTEK is a holding company that engaged in the Services (professional activities, media services, solutions, information technology, connectivity, healthcare, banking) and Trading sectors through its Subsidiaries. EMTEK's vast business is divided into Media Division, Solution Division, Healthcare Division, and Other Divisions.

By the end of 2022, EMTEK had 29 direct subsidiaries and 106 indirect subsidiaries that were consolidated into the Company's Financial Statements.

EMTEK adalah perusahaan induk yang bergerak di bidang Jasa (aktivitas profesional, jasa media, solusi, teknologi informasi, konektivitas, layanan kesehatan, perbankan) dan Perdagangan melalui Anak-Anak Perusahaan. Bisnis EMTEK yang luas terbagi menjadi Divisi Media, Divisi Solusi, Divisi Kesehatan, dan Divisi Lainnya.

Sampai dengan akhir tahun 2022, EMTEK memiliki 29 anak perusahaan langsung dan 106 anak perusahaan tidak langsung yang terkonsolidasi ke Laporan Keuangan Perseroan.

## Media Division/Divisi Media

Media Division manages free-to-air (FTA) television stations: SCTV, Indosiar, MOJI (formerly O'Channel) with national coverage as well as pay television and various other digital media and content providers.

Divisi Media mengelola stasiun televisi free to air (FTA): SCTV, Indosiar, MOJI (sebelumnya O'Channel) dengan jangkauan nasional serta televisi berbayar dan berbagai media digital dan penyedia konten lainnya.
