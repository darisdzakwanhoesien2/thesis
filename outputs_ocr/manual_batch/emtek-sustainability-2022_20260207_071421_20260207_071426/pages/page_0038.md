# KEPENTINGAN [GRI 201-1]

Economic performance is one of the important parameters and factors to ensure EMTEK Group's capability in maintaining its business growth and providing added value, especially economic value to all its stakeholders. In this material topic, we present the economic benefits provided by the Company through a description of the distribution of economic value to employees, shareholders, government and supplier partners.

Kinerja ekonomi merupakan salah satu parameter dan faktor penting untuk memastikan Grup EMTEK dapat menjaga pertumbuhan usahanya dan memberikan nilai tambah khususnya nilai ekonomi kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingannya. Dalam topik material ini kami menyampaikan manfaat ekonomi yang diberikan Perseroan melalui gambaran distribusi nilai ekonomi kepada karyawan, pemegang saham, pemerintah dan mitra pemasok.

Through a series of strategies, the policies taken by EMTEK managed to book revenue of Rp15,525 billion increased by 21% compared to 2021 which recorded revenue of Rp12,841 billion.

# TAXATION POLICY [GRI 207-1] KEBIJAKAN PERPAJAKAN [GRI 207-1]

The Company has a taxation policy that includes responsibility and the principles of transparency and accountability in handling all applicable tax provisions. The Company understands the importance of compliance with applicable tax laws and regulations as a form of corporate social responsibility and sustainability commitment. The Company is committed to ensuring that all tax matters are handled proactively, transparently and responsibly, while still considering the interests of our stakeholders and not using tax havens in its tax planning. The Company's full taxation policy can be found on our website https://www.emtek.co.id/files/Charter/Tax-Policy_ENG.pdf.

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan perpajakan yang meliputi tanggung jawab serta prinsip transparansi dan akuntabilitas dalam menangani seluruh ketentuan pajak yang berlaku. Perseroan memahami pentingnya kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan perpajakan yang berlaku sebagai bentuk tanggung jawab sosial dan komitmen keberlanjutan perusahaan. Perseroan berkomitmen untuk memastikan bahwa semua masalah perpajakan ditangani secara proaktif, transparan, dan bertanggung jawab, dengan tetap menyambangkan kepentingan para pemangku kepentingan kami serta tidak menggunakan negara suaka pajak (tax haven) dalam perencanaan pajaknya. # Policy on Selection and Increase of Capacity of Supplier or Vendor

EMTEK opens the widest possible opportunity to all potential suppliers to cooperate as long as they meet the specified criteria such as price, quality, timeliness, and other benefits. To ensure this, EMTEK has established a policy regarding supplier or vendor selection in the Company's Good Corporate Governance Guidelines. On the other hand, the supplier or vendor capability improvement policy is beneficial to ensure that the supply chain runs efficiently and effectively. The ability of suppliers or vendors to supply/fullfil the goods or services required by the Company will affect the smooth operation and overall performance of the Company.

# Kebijakan Mengenai Seleksi dan Peningkatan Kemampuan Pemasok atau Vendor

EMTEK membuka ruang seluas-luasnya kepada seluruh calon pemasok untuk bekerja sama sepanjang memenuhi kriteria yang ditentukan seperti harga, kualitas, ketepatan waktu, serta manfaat lainnya. Untuk memastikan hal tersebut, EMTEK telah menetapkan kebijakan mengenai seleksi pemasok atau vendor dalam Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik Perseroan. Di sisi lain, kebijakan peningkatan kemampuan pemasok atau vendor bermanfaat untuk memastikan bahwa rantai pasokan berjalan dengan efisien dan efektif.
