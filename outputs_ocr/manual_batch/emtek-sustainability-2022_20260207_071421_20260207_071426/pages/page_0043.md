## MEKANISME PENGELOLAAN LIMBAH DAN EFLUEN [F.14]

EMTEK as a holding company does not produce industrial waste or waste that is hazardous and has a significant impact on the surrounding environment. ## Effluent/Efluen

As our commitment not to pollute the community and the environment, we conduct waste and effluent management including wastewater which we treat according to the provisions of applicable standards before being discharged into the surrounding environment (river). For example, at Indosier's Daan Mogot office in West Jakarta, we have a Wastewater Treatment Plant (IPAL) to ensure that wastewater from our operations is treated before being discharged into the sewer. We utilize the treated wastewater to water the trees around the office and studio areas.

Sebagai komitmen kami untuk tidak mencemari masyarakat dan lingkungan sekitar, kami melakukan pengelolaan sampah dan limbah termasuk limbah air yang kami olah sesuai ketentuan standar yang berlaku sebelum dibuang ke lingkungan sekitar (sungai). Seperti contoh di kantor Indosier, Daan Mogot di Jakarta Barat, kami memiliki Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL) untuk memastikan air limbah dari operasional kami diolah terlebih dahulu sebelum dialirkan ke saluran pembuangan. Kami memanfaatkan air limbah yang telah diolah untuk menyirami pepohonan di sekitar area kantor dan studio.
