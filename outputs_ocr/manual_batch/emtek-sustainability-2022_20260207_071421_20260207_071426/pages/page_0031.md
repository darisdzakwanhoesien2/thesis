# MANAJEMEN RISIKO [E.3]

EMTEK Group's business objective is to create and deliver value to all its stakeholders sustainably. At the same time we strive to protect the Company and its stakeholders from any losses that may arise in its operational activities. Therefore, the Company has developed a risk management system that identifies, evaluates and mitigates risks that could negatively impact its performance. This is also important to increase the confidence of shareholders and investors in investing their capital, so that the Company can continue to grow. The Company's policy on Risk Management is also available at https://www.emtek.co.id/Ries/Charter/Risk-Management-Policy_ENG.pdf.

The implementation of risk management duties and responsibilities rests with the Internal Audit Unit which then reports to the Board of Commissioners. For information, the head of the Internal Audit Unit is Sylvia Hermawan, who is registered as an Individual Member of the Indonesia Risk Management Professional Association (IRMAPA) with Member No. 2303-003P, validity period until 31 December 2025. (SWi 2-13)

To continuously improve its performance, the Internal Audit Unit always adopts risk management best practices, including attending the "Enterprise Risk Management" training by the Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA) Indonesia which took place on 12-13 October 2022. Also a training entitled "Effective Company Legal Risk Management based on ISO 31022" which was attended by the Legal Division with the organizer HukumOnline.

All EMTEK employees understand the importance of a culture of risk management implementation as one of the important aspects of good corporate governance. The Company's way to build a risk culture in the organization is done through:

- Encouraging employee participation to be actively involved and concerned about creating a work environment with integrity through a whistleblowing system mechanism.
- Implementing Key Performance Indicator (KPI) to assess the extent to which employees are actively involved in supporting Good Corporate Governance, and provide rewards for the assessment.

The Company has a Risk Management policy that refers to ISO 31000:2018 on Risk Management Guidelines. We carry out the process of identifying, analyzing, measuring the impact, evaluating, controlling, and trying to avoid, minimize, or even eliminate unacceptable risks. The existence of a Risk Management System is believed to assist the Company in managing risks and ensuring the availability of policies and the establishment of risk limits supported by procedures, reports, and information systems that provide accurate and timely information and analysis to management including setting steps to deal with changes in market conditions and operational impacts on stakeholders.

More detailed information on the risk management system can be found in the Company's Annual Report 2022. Based on the review and evaluation that has been conducted, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners stated that throughout 2022, the Risk Management System has been effective and adequate.

In identifying risks, the Internal Audit Unit has provided worksheets to each relevant division to conduct self-assessment by describing the potential risks that exist and the extent of the impact if the risk occurs and what mitigation steps are taken to reduce or anticipate the impact. The final results of the impact measurement, we categorize into risk levels that are High, Medium, or Low. The High category will be the main concern in risk management.

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan Manajemen Risiko yang mengacu pada ISO 31000:2018 tentang Panduan Manajemen Risiko. Kami melakukan proses identifikasi, analisis, mengukur dampak, mengevaluasi, mengendalikan, dan berusaha menghindari, meminimalkan, atau bahkan menghilangkan risiko yang tidak dapat diterima. Keberadaan Sistem Manajemen Risiko dipercaya dapat membantu Perseroan dalam mengelola risiko dan memastikan tersedianya kebijakan dan penetapan limit risiko yang didukung oleh prosedur, laporan, dan sistem informasi yang menyediakan informasi dan analisis secara akurat dan tepat waktu kepada manajemen termasuk menetapkan langkah menghadapi perubahan kondisi pasar serta dampak operasional terhadap pemangku kepentingan.

Informasiebih detail mengenai sistem manajemen risiko dapat dilihat dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan 2022. Berdasarkan hasil penelaahan dan evaluasi yang telah dilakukan, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris menyatakan bahwa sepanjang tahun 2022, Sistem Manajemen Risiko telah berjalan efektif dan memadai.

Dalam mengidentifikasi risiko, Satuan Unit Audit Internal telah memberikan lombar kerja kepada masing-masing divisi terkait untuk melakukan self-assessment dengan menjabarkan potensi risiko yang ada serta seajum mana dampak yang ditimbulkan jika risiko itu tersebut terjadi dan langkah mitigasi apa yang diambil guna mengurangi atau mengantisipasi dampak. Hasil akhir pengukuran dampak, kami ketegorikan menjadi tingkatan risiko yang bersifat Tinggi, Sedang, atau Rendah. Kategori Tinggi akan menjadi perhatian utama dalam pengelolaan risiko.
