EMTEK Employees Based on Recruitment and Employee Turnover (EMTEK Holding) [GRI 2-7, GRI 401-1]
Karyawan EMTEK Berdasarkan Rekrutmen dan Pergantian Karyawan (EMTEK Holding) [GRI 2-7, GRI 401-1]

Along with the Company's business development, EMTEK requires an increase in human resources in terms of quality and quantity. Recruitment is conducted fairly and without discrimination as the Company's effort to uphold EMTEK's values and promote diversity and inclusiveness in the work environment. Equal opportunities provide opportunities to develop together regardless of differences in individual backgrounds so that EMTEK can become a melting pot for various ideas and abilities.

The recruitment process consists of 5 stages: (1) candidate search, (2) HR interview, (3) online assessment, (4) reference check, and (5) salary negotiation. The entire process is managed directly by EMTEK and accepted candidates will then follow the orientation process with other new employees in the EMTEK Track for Success (EMTRACKS) program. (GRI 0-3)

During the pandemic in 2022, the Company maintained so that the pandemic conditions did not impact the situation where the Company was forced to reduce the number of employees, and until the end of the year, this matter was successfully maintained. The Company always strives to keep the turnover rate under control by creating a productive work atmosphere, supporting the development of potential and career advancement of employees, taking into account employee welfare and fulfilling fair and appropriate compensation. The employee turnover rate in the Company in 2022 was 14.49%, the majority of which occurred due to resignation, as well as retirement and termination of Specific Time Work Agreement (PKWT) contracts for temporary work types.

Selama masa pandemi di tahun 2022, Perseroan menjaga agar kondisi pandemi tidak berdampak kepada situasi dimana Perseroan terpaksa melakukan pengurangan jumlah karyawan, dan sampai dengan akhir tahun hal ini berhasil dijaga dengan baik. Perseroan senantiasa berupaya untuk menjaga tingkat perputaran tetap terkendali dengan menciptakan atmosfer kerja yang produktif, mendukung pengembangan potensi serta peningkatan karier karyawan, memperhatikan kesejahteraan karyawan dan memenuhi kompensasi yang adil dan sesuai. Tingkat perputaran karyawan di Perseroan pada Tahun 2022 sebesar 14.49% yang sebagian mayoritasnya terjadi karena pengunduran diri, serta adanya pensiun dan pengakhiran kontrak Perjanjian Kerja Waktu Tertentu (PKWT) untuk jenis pekerjaan sementara.
