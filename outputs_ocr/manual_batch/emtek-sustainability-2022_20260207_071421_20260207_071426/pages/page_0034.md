## Role of the Board of Directors in Risk Management (SIR 2-12)

The Board of Directors formulates strategies related to risk management, including risk avoidance, risk mitigation, and protection against some or all consequences of certain risks. The formulation of these strategies is a form of the Board of Directors' responsibility in carrying out the task of managing the Company's risks. In this case, Sylvia Hermawan as the Head of Internal Audit is responsible for monitoring the Company's risk management by coordinating with all members of the Board of Directors according to their respective fields.

On an ongoing basis, monitoring and evaluation related to risk management is carried out continuously through various forums of the Board of Directors' meetings with related departments. Every month the Board of Directors conducts a meeting in which it monitors mitigation and evaluates each risk and its impact. Information can be seen in the Company's 2022 Annual Report. (SIR 2-16)
