## DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM (DOMINOS)

## DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM (DOMINOS)

DoMInoS is an internal company system developed by PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk (SAME) which is useful for managing documents and information in the EMC Healthcare environment digitally by all employees including company management and medical personnel.

DoMInoS adalah suatu sistem internal perusahaan yang dikembangkan sendiri oleh PT Sarana Meditama Metropolitan Tbk (SAME) yang berguna untuk melakukan pengelolaan dokumen dan informasi di lingkungan EMC Healthcare secara digital oleh seluruh karyawan termasuk pihak manajemen perusahaan dan tenaga medis.

![img-108.jpeg](img-108.jpeg)

The innovations that SAME has made through the implementation of information management systems and internal documents resulted in The IDX Channel Indonesia Innovation Award 2022 (ICAI 2022) -IDX Channel on 24 August 2022.

Inovasi yang telah SAME lakukan melalui implementasi sistem manajemen informasi dan dokumen internal menghasilkan penghargaan IDX Channel Indonesia Innovation Award 2022 (ICAI 2022) -IDX Channel pada tanggal 24 Agustus 2022

The advantages from this innovation include:

- Facility for patient data management without having to print documents, thereby reducing paper usage or being environmentally friendly.
- Efficient management of patient data in terms of administrative processes and ease of updating patient profiles without having to have direct contact with patients.
- High-level security to maintain patient's confidential data.

Manfaat yang dirasakan dari inovasi ini di antaranya:

- Memudahkan manajemen data pasien tanpa harus melakukan pencetakan dokumen sehingga mengurangi pemakaian kertas atau ramah lingkungan.
- Pengelolaan data pasien yang efisien dalam hal proses administrasi dan kemudahan update profil pasien tanpa harus ada kontak langsung dengan pasien.
- Aplikasi ini memiliki tingkat keamanan untuk menjaga kerahasiaan data pasien.

## CONSUMER PROTECTION AND PRIVACY (GRI 418-1) PERLINDUNGAN DAN PRIVASI KONSUMEN (GRI 418-1)

EMTEK Group is committed to protecting consumers' personal privacy and data where consumers will be notified and given the opportunity via website, email, or mail regarding the use of such data by EMTEK Group. In this case, 'Personal Data' means any personal data that identifies and points to an individual, identified either (a) from such data, or (b) from other data and/or information in our possession or to which we may have access, including data in our records which may be updated at any time.

Grip EMTEK berkomitmen untuk melindungi privasi pribadi dan data konsumen di mana konsumen akan diberi tahu dan diberi kesempatan lewat situs, email, atau surat mengenai penggunaan data tersebut oleh Grup EMTEK. Dalam hal ini, yang dimaksud dengan 'Data Pribadi' adalah setiap data personal yang mengidentifikasikan dan menunjuk pada seseorang, yang teridentifikasi dari (a) dari data tersebut, atau (b) dari data dan/atau informasi lainnya yang kami miliki atau yang mungkin akses terhadapnya kami miliki, termasuk data dalam catatan kami yang dapat diperbaharui setiap saat.

Confidential Company Information obtained by Employees of the Company shall remain confidential after their employment status ends with the Company.

Informasi Rahasia Perseroan yang diperoleh Karyawan Perseroan harus tetap diirahasiakan setelah masa kerjanya di Perseroan berakhir.

In establishing relationships with consumers, EMTEK Group has policies as outlined below:

a. Customer confidentiality assurance following applicable regulations.
b. In order to maintain the image of EMTEK Group, employees are required to protect all forms of information belonging to customers. For this reason, employees are not entitled to disseminate information before it is known/formally agreed by the parties involved, or based on commitments with other parties that must be kept confidential.
c. EMTEK Group shall serve customers with a reasonable attitude and try to understand the needs and find alternative solutions to customer problems as well as possible and while taking into account the interests of EMTEK Group.
d. Time management and efficiency are factors that support quality service.
e. Principally, EMTEK Group does not approve any gift or acceptance (including offering or requesting) from other parties, including customers, related to the Group's business.

EMTEK is committed to protecting customers or audiences from misuse of their personal data or data leakage in any form. During the reporting period, we did not receive any complaints regarding misuse of customers' or audiences' personal data.
