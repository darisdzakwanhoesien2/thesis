# ISSUES ON SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS IMPLEMENTATION [E.S] PERMASALAHAN TERHADAP PENERAPAN BISNIS BERKELANJUTAN [E.S]

The Company is committed to conducting its business responsibly while providing value to all its stakeholders. Specifically referring to the implementation of the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 51/PDJK.03/2017 concerning the implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies, we have conducted socialization and internalization of a number of sustainability policies, including the formation of the ESG Committee which is responsible for determining the Company's sustainability strategy.

In particular, EMTEK Group currently has no significant issues related to the implementation of sustainability in its business ecosystem. However, we realize that the evolving scope of ESG requires adaptation across EMTEK Group, including the development of various guidelines for writing sustainability reports, ranging from mandatory provisions in Indonesia to voluntary international standards. Understanding the guidelines for writing standards, procedures for collecting and processing data is a challenge in itself. We see that the increasing need of investors for ESG-based reports is a separate motivation for EMTEK Group to continuously develop.

Perseroan berkomitmen menjalankan bisnisnya secara bertanggung jawab sekaligus memberi nilai kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingannya. Secara khusus terhitung implementasi Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 51/PDJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik, kami telah melakukan sosialisasi dan internalisasi sejumlah kebijakan keberlanjutan, di antaranya pembentukan Komite ESG yang bertanggung jawab dalam menentukan strategi keberlanjutan Perseroan.

Secara khusus sampai saat ini Grup EMTEK tidak memiliki kendala yang berarti terkait penerapan keberlanjutan dalam ekosistem bisnisnya. Namun demikian kami menyadari ruang lingkup ESG yang terus berkembang membutuhkan adaptasi di seluruh Grup EMTEK, termasuk adanya perkembangan akan pedoman penulisan laporan keberlanjutan yang sangat beragam, mulai dari ketentuan wajib di Indonesia maupun standar internasional yang bersifat sukarela. Pemahaman pedoman standar penulisan, tata cara pengumpulan dan pengolahan data menjadi tantangan tersendiri. Kami melihat semakin tingginya kebutuhan investor akan laporan berbasis ESG menjadi motivasi tersendiri bagi Grup EMTEK untuk terus berbenah diri.

# MATERIAL TOPICS [GRI 3-1] [GRI 3-2] TOPIK MATERIAL [GRI 3-1] [GRI 3-2]

In this report, we present important or material topics that are relevant to all stakeholders. In line with our commitment to conduct business responsibly and add value to all stakeholders. Therefore, we need to know what are the concerns and expectations of stakeholders towards EMTEK.

## Process to Determine Material Topics

The Company conducts due diligence by collecting data and information and comprehending the expectations of all stakeholders that are relevant to the Company's business and by comparing the results of external surveys that have been conducted in the 2021 Sustainability Report where the relevant ones are still taken. We have various ways both formally and informally including information from customers, risk management identification results, meetings with employees, surveys, regulations and interactions with the community.

Dalam laporan ini, kami akan menyampaikan topik penting atau yang bersifat material dan relevan bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Sejalan dengan komitmen kami untuk menjalankan usaha secara bertanggung jawab dan memberi nilai tambah bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Karenanya kami perlu mengetahui hal apa saja yang menjadi perhatian dan harapan pemangku kepentingan terhadap EMTEK.

## Proses untuk Menentukan Topik Material

Perseroan melakukan due diligence dengan mengumpulkan data dan informasi serta mendengar harapan dari seluruh pemangku kepentingan yang relevan dengan bisnis Perseroan serta dengan membandingkan hasil survey eksternal yang telah dilakukan dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan 2021 dimana yang masih relevan kami ambil.
