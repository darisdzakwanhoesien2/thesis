# EMPLOYEE TRAINING AND SKILL DEVELOPMENT [GRI 404-1] [F.22]

# PELATIHAN DAN PENGEMBANGAN KEMAMPUAN PEGAWAI [GRI 404-1] [F.22]

For EMTEK, employees are very important asset to drive business growth and face various challenges in its industry sector. We continue to explore the potential of EMTEK's human resources to the fullest extent. Various training and talent development programs to hone skills are provided to employees. For new employees, the Company provides induction program to help adapting to the Company's vision, mission and culture. Likewise, at the management level, EMTEK has the Eagle Program.

In 2022, the Company has run a competency improvement program in the form of training for workers. The training attended by Human Resources Division employees aims to improve competencies that are aligned with the company's business and needs, as well as to obtain the latest information related to Human Resources that can provide insight for Human Resources Division strategies and initiatives. The realization of employee competency development funds spent for the current year reached Rp87,590,000. In 2022, there were 2,000 employees who participated in training and skills development with an average of 12 hours of training per employee.

In order to support its business expansion, especially inorganically, EMTEK has designed a human resource competency development program that is aligned with the Company's vision and mission. The HR competency development program is inclusive and non-discriminatory, and is implemented continuously through the program. The Company is able to produce capable and visionary leaders and reliable and professional employees who can drive quality growth in the performance and business of EMTEK Group. Regarding HR development, education and training programs held throughout 2022 can be seen in the Company's 2022 Annual Report. [GRI 3-3]

# EMPLOYEE PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT [GRI 404-3]

# PENILAIAN KINERJA KARYAWAN [GRI 404-3]

The Company conducts regular assessment for all employees including senior management to improve employee performance and welfare. In 2022, 15.94% of all EMTEK Holding employees received promotions. There was an increase compared to 2021 of 9.79%. For employees with good performance based on the indicators, the Company has promoted 11 employees, consisting of 5.80% male employees and 10.14% female employees in 2022. [GRI 3-3]

Perseroan melakukan evaluasi rutin kepada seluruh karyawan termasuk senior manajemen guna meningkatkan kinerja dan kesejahteraan karyawan. Di tahun 2022, 15,94% dari seluruh karyawan EMTEK Holding mendapatkan promosi. Ada peningkatan dibandingkan dengan tahun 2021 sebesar 9,79%. Kepada karyawan yang telah menunjukkan kinerja yang baik sesuai dengan parameter yang ditetapkan, pada tahun 2022 Perseroan telah memberikan promosi kepada 11 karyawan, terdiri dari 5,80% karyawan laki-laki dan 10,14% karyawan perempuan.
