# ENCOURAGING ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY
# MENDORONG KELESTARIAN LINGKUNGAN

## ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY
## KEBIJAKAN LINGKUNGAN

EMTEK Group through all its subsidiaries that run businesses in services (professional activities, media services, solutions, information technology, connectivity, health services, banking) and trading realizes that the earth as a place where we live together must be protected in order to provide the resources we need. Therefore following the business context and capacity, EMTEK Group has made policies in the environmental field with the scope of implementing green office, digital work, using environmentally friendly products, reducing carbon footprint and greenhouse gas emissions and continuous improvement aimed at preserving the environment and ensuring that each of its operational activities does not cause negative impacts on the surrounding environment in all of its operational areas. (ORI 3-5)

The preparation of the Company's Environmental Policy is based on applicable environmental regulations and standards both nationally and internationally. Details can be seen on our Company website at https://www.emtek.co.id/files/Charter/Environmental-Policy_ENG.pdf.

## ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE
## KEPATUHAN LINGKUNGAN

The Company is committed to complying with environmental regulations and laws relevant to the company in all areas of operation.

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk mematuhi regulasi dan peraturan perundang-undangan lingkungan yang relevan bagi perusahaan di semua area operasi.

## Managing the Direct Impact of Company Operations on the Environment
## Mengelola Dampak Langsung Operasi Perusahaan terhadap Lingkungan

The Company is committed to managing the direct impact of its operations on the environment, through:

1. Monitoring, reducing and reporting energy use, greenhouse gas emissions, paper use, waste and water use in operational processes and even in activities carried out by suppliers;
2. Pursuing the use of environmentally friendly renewable energy;
3. Contributing to preserving the environment and biodiversity; and
4. Considering the impact of operations on biodiversity in investments.

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk mengelola dampak langsung operasi perusahaan terhadap lingkungan, melalui:

1. Memantau, mengurangi dan melaporkan penggunaan energi, emisi gas rumah kaca, penggunaan kertas, limbah dan penggunaan air dalam proses operasional bahkan dalam kegiatan yang dilakukan oleh pemasok;
2. Mengupayakan penggunaan energi terbarukan ramah lingkungan;
3. Berkontribusi untuk menjaga pelestarian lingkungan dan keanekaragaman hayati; dan
4. Mempertimbangkan dampak operasi terhadap keanekaragaman hayati dalam investasi.

## Considering Environmental Impact in Supply Chain
## Memperhatikan Dampak Lingkungan dalam Rantai Pasok

The Company pays attention to environmental aspects in the management of logistics, IT sourcing &amp; procurement, and the arrangement of building infrastructure/facilities. In terms of suppliers, we will strive to create regenerative processes and will minimize adverse effects on society, the environment, and natural resources as well as maintain public health and safety including environmental permitting and reporting as well as resource efficiency and green energy.

Perseroan memperhatikan aspek lingkungan dalam pengelolaan logistik, IT sourcing &amp; procurement, serta penataan prasarana/sarana gedung. Dalam segi pemasok, kami akan berusaha menciptakan proses regeneratif dan akan meminimalkan efek yang merugikan bagi masyarakat, lingkungan, dan sumber daya alam serta menjaga kesehatan dan keselamatan masyarakat termasuk mengatur tentang perizinan dan pelaporan lingkungan serta efisiensi sumber daya dan energi ramah lingkungan.

## Managing Environmental Risks Associated with Operational Activities
## Mengelola Risiko Lingkungan yang Terkait dengan Kegiatan Operasional

The Company is committed to managing ESG risks in all aspects of the Company's business. This includes, (but not limited to):

1. Implementing an ESG-Related Operational Policy;
2. In operational activities, the Company identifies, considers and assesses businesses that have ESG risks, and prioritizes profitable business activities that can manage environmental, social and governance risks well; and
3. Determining the target of operational activities that pay attention to sustainable ESG aspects every year.

Perseroan memiliki komitmen untuk dapat mengelola risiko ESG pada semua aspek usaha Perseroan. Ini termasuk, (namun tidak terbatas pada):

1. Menerapkan Kebijakan Operasional Berwawasan ESG;
2. Dalam kegiatan operasional, Perseroan mengidentifikasi, mempertimbangkan dan menilai usaha-usaha yang memiliki risiko ESG, serta memprioritaskan pada kegiatan usaha yang menguntungkan dan sekaligus dapat mengelola risiko lingkungan, sosial dan tata kelola dengan baik; dan
3. Menentukan target kegiatan operasional yang memperhatikan aspek ESG berkelanjutan setiap tahunnya.

## Awareness and Training
## Kesadaran dan Pelatihan

The Company is committed to ensuring that employees and stakeholders understand the importance of paying attention to the environment in daily business activities through the following activities:

1. Providing training for employees related to environmental issues and risks within the scope of the Company's business;
2. Providing knowledge on environmental conservation (including, but not limited to green lifestyle and green building); and
3. Creating and managing a workplace that reflects the commitment to preserve and care for the environment.

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk memastikan bahwa karyawan dan pemangku kepentingan memahami pentingnya memperhatikan lingkungan dalam kegiatan bisnis sehari-hari melalui kegiatan sebagai berikut:

1. Memberikan pelatihan bagi karyawan terkait isu dan risiko lingkungan dalam lingkup bisnis Perseroan;
2. Memberikan pengetahuan mengenai pelestarian lingkungan (termasuk, namun tidak terbatas pada green lifestyle dan green building); dan
3. Menciptakan dan mengelola tempat kerja yang mencerminkan komitmen untuk melestarikan dan merawat lingkungan.

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION
## PEMANTAUAN DAN EVALUASI

All of the Company's environmental responsibility policies and activities are monitored and evaluated by the Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Committee. Furthermore the ESG Performance Committee then report the performance of environmental aspects to the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners and all stakeholders of the Company in the Company's Sustainability Report. The results of the evaluation of environmental performance will guide future policies.

Seluruh kebijakan dan kegiatan tanggung jawab lingkungan Perseroan, dipantau dan dievaluasi oleh Komite Lingkungan, Sosial dan Tata Kelola (ESG). Selanjutnya komite ESG Kinerja akan melaporkan kinerja aspek lingkungan kepada Direksi, Dewan Komisaris dan seluruh pemangku kepentingan Perseroan dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan Perseroan. Hasil evaluasi kinerja aspek lingkungan akan menjadi panduan terhadap kebijakan selanjutnya.
