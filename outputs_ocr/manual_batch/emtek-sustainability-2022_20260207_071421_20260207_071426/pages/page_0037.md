# ACTIVITIES TO ESTABLISH SUSTAINABILITY CULTURE [F.1]

# KEGIATAN MEMBANGUN BUDAYA KEBERLANJUTAN [F.1]

The realization of running business sustainably is marked by the establishment of a structure responsible for sustainability, namely the ESG Committee. With this committee, it is expected that the Company's sustainability policies and strategies can be more focused. Furthermore to instill sustainability values, the Company has also encouraged the implementation of the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies throughout EMTEK Group. EMTEK Group's sustainability culture, in general, continues to be built through socialization and internalization of policies including the issuance of several regulations relevant to the Group's sustainability strategy including socialization of anti-corruption policies, bribery, illegal donations, fraud, related party transaction policies and conflicts of interest, respect for Human Rights (HAM) and anti-discrimination.

In addition, in the environmental aspect, we constantly remind all employees of the importance of environmentally friendly behavior, including by conducting energy, water, paper efficiency and by maintaining cleanliness in the work environment. Similarly with our approach to reducing plastic waste is by socializing restrictions on the use of single-use plastic materials. We encourage the use of stainless steel drinking water tumblers and reusable individual meal boxes to reduce packaging bags and plastic drinking bottles.

Perwujudan menjalankan kegiatan usaha secara berkelanjutan ditandai dengan pembentukan struktur penanggung jawab keberlanjutan yaitu Komite ESG. Dengan pembentukan komite ini diharapkan kebijakan dan strategi keberlanjutan Perseroan lebih terarah. Selanjutnya untuk menanamkan nilai-nilai keberlanjutan, Perseroan juga telah mendorong implementasi Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik di seluruh Grup EMTEK. Budaya keberlanjutan Grup EMTEK secara keseluruhan terus dibangun melalui kegiatan sosialisasi dan internalisasi kebijakan di antaranya dengan diterbitkannya beberapa peraturan yang relevan dengan strategi keberlanjutan Grup di antaranya sosialisasi kebijakan anti korupsi, penyuapan, sumbangan ilegal, fraud, kebijakan transaksi afiliasi dan benturan kepentingan, menghormati Hak Asasi Manusia (HAM) dan anti diskriminasi.

Selain itu, dalam aspek lingkungan kami senantiasa mengingatkan seluruh karyawan akan pentingnya perilaku ramah lingkungan di antaranya dengan melakukan efisiensi energi, air, kertas serta dengan menjaga kebersihan di lingkungan kerja. Demikian pula dengan pendekatan kami dalam mengurangi sampah plastik dengan cara menyosialisasikan pembatasan pemakaian material plastik sekali pakai. Kami mendorong penggunaan tumbler air minum berbahan stainless steel dan kotak makan bagi setiap individu yang dapat dipakai secara berulang (reusable) untuk mengurangi kantong kemasan dan botol minum plastik.
