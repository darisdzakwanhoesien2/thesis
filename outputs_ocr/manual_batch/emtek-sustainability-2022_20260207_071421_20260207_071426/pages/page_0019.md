## Main Issues and Implementation of EMTEK Sustainability

As manifestation of EMTEK's commitment to implementing sustainable business and as a response to the provisions of the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 51/PGJK/26/2017 concerning the implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies, the Company has established Environmental, Social &amp; Governance (ESG) Committee as a task force responsible for EMTEK's governance and sustainability strategy. Furthermore, EMTEK expects to be able to make a real contribution to the Government's program, namely Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), in order to improve the quality of life of many people and protect the natural resources and their ecosystems.

According to our business activities and the capacity we have, we have understood and identified major and relevant issues to be responded in terms of EMTEK's business continuity.

## Environmental Aspect

As a holding company investing in the service sector, EMTEK runs business activities without significant negative influences or impacts on the surrounding environment. Our daily operational activities are conducted in a leased multi-storey office in the city of Jakarta and have no direct contact with the community and the surrounding environment.

However, we realize and are willing to contribute to a clean and sustainable environment. One of them is related to the global warming issue and its impacts which have been major issues for all parties. Through the Paris Agreement, every country has its own commitment and strategy in reducing the rate of Green House Gas (GHG) emissions, including Indonesia. As manifestation of its support to handle global warming, EMTEK has used electricity efficiently and responsibly, including using energy-efficient equipment such as LED lights in offices. We also use automation systems to control and monitor air conditioning (AC) systems, as well as utilize digital applications to reduce energy consumption. In terms of emissions caused by fuel oil (BBM), the Company has no substantial fuel needs for daily operations due to the limited number of vehicles, trips and workers directly employed.

Likewise, the amount of garbage and waste resulted from our daily operations at the head office is very small and tends to be organic waste. Waste management is handled by the building management in collaboration with the third parties in the waste cleaning sector.

## Aspek Lingkungan

Sebagai perusahaan induk yang berinvestasi di bidang jasa, kegiatan usaha EMTEK tidak memiliki pengaruh atau dampak negatif yang signifikan terhadap lingkungan sekitar. Kegiatan operasional kami sehari-hari dilakukan di gedung perkantoran bertingkat yang disewa di ibu kota Jakarta dan tidak bersentuhan langsung dengan masyarakat dan lingkungan sekitar.

Namun, kami menyadari dan bersedia berkontribusi untuk lingkungan yang bersih dan berkelanjutan. Salah satunya terkait isu pemanasan global dan dampaknya yang menjadi isu besar bagi semua pihak. Melalui Paris Agreement, setiap negara memiliki komitmen dan strategi masing-masing dalam menurunkan laju emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK), termasuk Indonesia. Sebagai bentuk dukungannya untuk mengatasi pemanasan global, EMTEK telah menggunakan listrik secara efisien dan bertanggung jawab, termasuk menggunakan peralatan hemat energi seperti lampu LED di kantor. Kami juga menggunakan sistem otomasi untuk mengontrol dan memantau sistem pendingin udara (AC), serta memanfaatkan aplikasi digital untuk mengurangi konsumsi energi. Dalam hal emisi yang disebabkan oleh bahan bakar minyak (BBM), Perseroan tidak memiliki kebutuhan bahan bakar yang besar untuk operasional sehari-hari karena terbatasnya jumlah kendaraan, perjalanan dan orang yang dipekerjakan secara langsung.

Begitu pula dengan sampah dan limbah yang dihasilkan dari operasional sehari-hari di kantor pusat jumlahnya sangat sedikit dan cenderung merupakan sampah organik. Pengelolaan sampah ditangani oleh pengelola gedung bekerja sama dengan pihak ketiga di bidang pembersihan sampah.
