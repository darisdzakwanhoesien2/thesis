# Materiality

EMTEK presents sustainability information that describes the sustainability context as a holding company that manages subsidiaries engaging in Service Company (professional activities, media, information technology, solution, connectivity, healthcare, banking services) and Trade through Subsidiaries. These data enable EMTEK's stakeholders to make informed judgments about the company's management and performance. In our professional opinion, the report covers the organization's material issues by using EMTEK's materiality matrix and boundary mapping.

# Responsiveness

EMTEK has implemented the management practice to respond to the expectations and concerns of its stakeholders. It includes communication on stakeholder expectations and different feedback mechanisms to external and internal stakeholders. In our professional opinion, the report covers EMTEK's responsiveness to issues. Our inputs for the improvement of the report were adopted by EMTEK before the issue of this opinion statement.

# Impact

EMTEK has made steps to identify, measure, monitor, and be accountable for how their actions affect their broader ecosystems including Environmental, Economic and Social aspects. In our professional opinion, the report covers EMTEK's impacts on the broader ecosystems. Our improvement report inputs have been adopted by EMTEK before the issue of this opinion statement.

# In Accordance with GRI Standards 2021

The Report follows the GRI Standards 2021 where all disclosures of each material topic are presented and discussed in the Report. The management approach disclosure for each material topic in general is fairly disclosed.

The Management has applied the Principles for Defining Report Content (stakeholder inclusiveness, sustainability context, materiality, and completeness) and the Principles for Defining Report Quality (balance, comparability, accuracy, timeliness, clarity, and reliability). When applicable, the report presents data and information clarification that is undisclosed due to Management discretion or system and data administration issues.

The energy and emission calculation presented in the report is computed based on the methodology chosen by EMTEK.
